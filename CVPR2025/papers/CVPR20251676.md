## Problem

Learning-to-optimize deep visual odometry methods suffer from three interrelated weaknesses: unstable pose initialization in challenging (low-texture, repetitive) environments, insufficient inter-frame matching refinement due to limited feature interaction, and training instability caused by gradient variance in nested differentiable bundle adjustment. No prior method combines robust geometric initialization with sequential, history-aware matching refinement and a principled training stabilization strategy.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/CVPR20251676/cropped_page2_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The system consumes a monocular RGB image sequence and maintains a sliding window of W=10 keyframes organized as a Point-Frame Graph (PFG). At each new frame, it queries Dino-v2 for context features, EfficientLoFTR for semi-dense pixel correspondences, and Metric3D for monocular depth to bootstrap the PFG. The output is a set of SE(3) camera poses and 3D map point coordinates, refined through differentiable bundle adjustment; MambaVO++ additionally produces a globally consistent pose graph via DBoW2-based loop closure and global optimization.

## Method

MambaVO introduces three coupled components. The Geometric Initialization Module (GIM) performs semi-dense matching via EfficientLoFTR and solves initial poses with PnP, then fuses geometric and Dino-v2 context features into per-pixel matching tokens. The Geometric Mamba Module (GMM) sequentially processes these tokens across the PFG using vanilla Mamba blocks: a History Fusion stage applies cross-attention between current matching features and a persistent history token, and the Mamba blocks output refined pixel offsets (Δp) and per-edge matching weights (w) via a RefineHead, with the history token updated through a GRU. A Trending-Aware Penalty (TAP) stabilizes training by dynamically balancing the pose loss and matching loss using both a gradient-norm ratio (updated every 50 iterations) and a trend-based exponential parameter that emphasizes whichever loss is decreasing more slowly. The refined matching and weights feed a differentiable Gauss-Newton BA layer that jointly optimizes poses and map points, and the entire pipeline is trained end-to-end with supervised pose and matching losses on TartanAir only.

## Results

On EuRoC (without loop closure), MambaVO achieves 0.094 m average ATE, outperforming DPVO (0.105 m), V2V (0.117 m), and DROID-VO (0.186 m). With loop closure, MambaVO++ reaches 0.021 m average ATE on EuRoC, slightly beating DROID-SLAM (0.022 m) and DPV-SLAM (0.024 m). On TartanAir, MambaVO achieves 0.14 m ATE (vs. 0.17 m for DPVO and 0.18 m for V2V), and MambaVO++ achieves 0.10 m with loop closure (vs. 0.16 m for DPV-SLAM). On KITTI, MambaVO attains 50.21 m ATE without loop and 21.84 m with loop, the best among all compared methods in both settings.

## Baselines & Comparisons

MambaVO consistently outperforms all deep VO baselines (DROID-VO, DPVO, V2V) across all four datasets without loop closure, with the largest gains on TartanAir (0.14 m vs. 0.17 m for DPVO, ~18% improvement) and EuRoC (0.094 m vs. 0.105 m for DPVO, ~10% improvement). With loop closure, MambaVO++ achieves the best average ATE on EuRoC (0.021 m), TartanAir (0.10 m), and KITTI (21.84 m), narrowly beating DROID-SLAM and DPV-SLAM on EuRoC and significantly outperforming them on TartanAir (0.10 m vs. 0.16 m for DPV-SLAM). On KITTI with loop closure, MambaVO++ (21.84 m) beats DPV-SLAM++ (25.76 m) and LDSO (22.42 m), though it trails on individual sequences like 08 and 09 where all methods struggle. On TUM-RGBD, MambaVO (0.076 m) and MambaVO++ (0.034 m) achieve the best results in both settings, outperforming DPVO (0.089 m) and GO-SLAM (0.035 m) respectively. Traditional methods like ORB-SLAM3 and SVO fail on multiple sequences (marked 'X'), while MambaVO and MambaVO++ complete all sequences successfully, highlighting superior robustness in challenging conditions.

## Relation to Prior Work

MambaVO extends the learning-to-optimize visual odometry paradigm established by DROID-SLAM, DPVO, and V2V, which combine neural matching with differentiable bundle adjustment. Its key architectural departure is the introduction of Mamba (selective state space model) blocks for sequential matching refinement within a Point-Frame Graph, replacing the patch-based or dense-flow matching strategies of prior work. The Geometric Initialization Module repurposes EfficientLoFTR's semi-dense matching and PnP for robust pose initialization, while the Trending-Aware Penalty extends V2V's gradient weighting with a trend-based balance parameter to further stabilize nested optimization training.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

This is the first application of state space models to visual odometry, demonstrating that linear-complexity sequence modeling can effectively refine inter-frame correspondences across a sliding window, yielding SOTA accuracy with real-time throughput (22 Hz on EuRoC) and lower GPU memory than competing methods. The >50% improvement from loop closure (MambaVO++) on long outdoor sequences highlights that even strong learned frontends still benefit substantially from classical global optimization. Open questions include whether the reliance on multiple pre-trained components (Dino-v2, Metric3D, EfficientLoFTR) limits end-to-end adaptability, and whether the Mamba-based refinement generalizes to stereo, RGB-D, or dynamic scenes beyond the monocular setting evaluated here.
