## Problem

Most human customization models focus exclusively on preserving facial identity, leaving full-body appearance (clothing, accessories, body structure) largely unaddressed. Compounding this, obtaining large-scale paired human data with consistent full-body identities across multiple images per individual is notoriously difficult, causing prior works to compromise by training on a single image per person and struggling with the identity-preservation vs. text-alignment trade-off.

## Input / Output

The method takes a single in-the-wild RGB human image (shortest side ≥ 1024 px) and a free-form text prompt describing pose, action, expression, and surroundings. The input image is internally decomposed into N=5 body-region crops (full body, face, torso, legs, shoes) via body parsing before encoding. Output is a set of diverse full-body human images at 1024+ resolution that preserve the input's complete appearance while conforming to the text description.

## Method

Visual Persona introduces a body-partitioned transformer encoder-decoder that conditions a frozen pre-trained T2I diffusion model (SDXL) for full-body identity transfer. The input image is parsed into 5 body regions, each encoded by DINOv2 into dense local features (using all spatial tokens rather than a single CLS token). A per-body-region transformer decoder then projects each feature set into dense identity embeddings of length l_H = h×w (vs. l_H=16 in prior work) via cross-attention, self-attention, and MLP layers, which are concatenated into a stacked identity embedding. This embedding conditions the diffusion model through a learnable identity cross-attention module, decoupled from the text cross-attention module, so that identity and text guidance operate independently. Training updates only the body-partitioned decoder and identity cross-attention module on the curated Visual Persona-500K dataset (580k paired images across 100k identities), where paired images of the same individual with different poses/scenes prevent overfitting to identity-irrelevant elements.

## Results

On SSHQ, Visual Persona achieves the best identity preservation score (D-I = 7.10) and the highest harmonic mean (D-H = 6.99), outperforming the closest full-body competitor StoryMaker (D-I = 6.74, D-H = 6.71). On PPR10K, Visual Persona again leads in D-I (7.30) and D-H (6.85), while StoryMaker trails at D-I = 6.80 and D-H = 6.63. Face-only baselines (IP-Adapter-FaceID, InstantID, PhotoMaker) score below 2.1 on D-I, confirming their inability to preserve full-body appearance. In human evaluation, Visual Persona also achieves the highest overall score O = √(SC × PQ), surpassing all baselines in both semantic consistency and perceptual quality.

## Baselines & Comparisons

Visual Persona decisively outperforms all face-only baselines (IP-Adapter-FaceID, InstantID, PhotoMaker) on identity preservation, with D-I scores of 7.10/7.30 versus 1.5–2.0, confirming that face-domain methods cannot transfer full-body appearance. Against the full-body IP-Adapter, Visual Persona improves D-I by approximately 1.7 points on SSHQ (7.10 vs. 5.44) and 2.3 points on PPR10K (7.30 vs. 5.04), while maintaining comparable text alignment. The closest competitor, StoryMaker, is outperformed on D-I by 0.36 (SSHQ) and 0.50 (PPR10K) points, and on D-H by 0.28 and 0.22 points respectively; however, StoryMaker edges out Visual Persona on D-T for PPR10K (6.77 vs. 6.67), suggesting a slight trade-off in text alignment on that dataset. In the human evaluation, Visual Persona achieves the highest overall score O, beating StoryMaker and IP-Adapter on both semantic consistency and perceptual quality, while face-only methods score lowest due to their inability to preserve clothing and body details.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the encoder-based T2I customization paradigm (IP-Adapter, InstantID, PhotoMaker, StoryMaker) from face-only identity preservation to full-body human customization. The key architectural departure is replacing compact global identity embeddings (typically 16 tokens from CLIP or face-recognition encoders) with a body-partitioned transformer decoder that produces dense per-region identity embeddings (16×16 tokens per body part) from DINOv2 features, conditioned via a decoupled cross-attention mechanism inherited from IP-Adapter. The data curation pipeline—using LLaVA with a single binary prompt to verify full-body clothing consistency across image pairs—is a novel contribution that addresses the long-standing difficulty of obtaining paired full-body human data, which prior works circumvented by training on single images per identity.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

Full-body human customization is a practical bottleneck for applications in film, virtual try-on, and character design that face-only methods cannot address, and the Visual Persona-500K dataset (580K images, 100K identities) is a significant open resource that the community can leverage. The ablation showing that dense per-body-part embeddings are essential for full-body preservation (while compact embeddings suffice for faces) provides a clear architectural principle for future multi-region appearance transfer. Open questions include extending the approach to video/temporal consistency, multi-person scenes with interacting identities, and whether the VLM-based curation pipeline generalizes to other domains (e.g., animals, objects) where paired data is similarly scarce.
