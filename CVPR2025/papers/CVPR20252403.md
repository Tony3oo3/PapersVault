## Problem

Feed-forward 3D reconstruction models like DUSt3R accept only RGB images and have no mechanism to exploit auxiliary information (camera intrinsics, sparse/dense depth, relative pose) that is routinely available in real-world applications. This limitation prevents them from leveraging known priors to improve accuracy and blocks new capabilities such as native high-resolution inference and sparse-to-dense depth completion.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/CVPR20252403/cropped_page0_idx2.webp)

## Input / Output

The network takes two RGB images (W×H×3) plus any arbitrary subset of five auxiliary modalities: two intrinsic matrices (3×3), one relative pose (4×4), and two depth maps (W×H) with binary sparsity masks. It outputs three pointmaps — X^{1,1}, X^{2,1}, and X^{2,2} — each with a per-pixel confidence map, where X^{2,2} (the second image's pointmap in its own camera frame) is the key addition over DUSt3R. Depth inputs can be arbitrarily sparse, and intrinsics enable processing of non-centered crops at any resolution via sliding-window inference.

## Method

Pow3R extends the DUSt3R ViT encoder-decoder with lightweight conditioning modules that inject auxiliary information via block-specific MLPs in the first transformer block (the 'inject-1' strategy). Intrinsics are converted to per-pixel camera rays and patchified; depth maps are normalized and patchified jointly with their sparsity masks; relative pose is embedded as a global token added to the decoder CLS token. During training, a random subset of modalities is fed at each iteration so the model learns to operate under any combination of priors at test time. An additional head predicts X^{2,2} (the second image's pointmap in its own coordinate frame), enabling single-pass relative pose estimation via Procrustes alignment instead of the slower RANSAC+PnP pipeline used by DUSt3R. The model uses a linear head (replacing DUSt3R's DPT head) and a confidence-aware scale-invariant pointmap regression loss, trained on 8.5M image pairs across 8 datasets at 224px then 512px resolution.

## Results

On the multi-view depth benchmark (Table 3), Pow3R with both intrinsics and relative pose (K+RT) achieves an average Absolute Relative Error of 3.2 and Inlier Ratio of 73.3 across KITTI, ScanNet, ETH3D, DTU, and Tanks & Temples, outperforming DUSt3R (3.7 / 68.2) and all prior MVS methods. On multi-view pose estimation (Table 5), Pow3R with known intrinsics and Procrustes alignment reaches 82.2 mAA(30) on Co3Dv2 and 72.5 mAA(30) on RealEstate10K at 30.1 fps, surpassing DUSt3R+PnP (77.2 / 61.7 at 3.2 fps) and PoseDiffusion (66.5 / 48.0). On the DTU MVS benchmark, adding both intrinsics and pose yields a 36% relative improvement in overall score over DUSt3R (1.115 mm vs 1.741 mm).

## Baselines & Comparisons

Pow3R with K+RT outperforms DUSt3R (its direct predecessor) on average multi-view depth (3.2 vs 3.7 rel, 73.3 vs 68.2 τ) and on every individual dataset except KITTI where they are tied (5.3 vs 5.4 rel). Against traditional MVS pipelines, Pow3R w/K+RT dominates: COLMAP achieves 9.3 rel / 67.8 τ, CER-MVS 11.5 / 51.5, and MVS-Former++ 14.9 / 46.6, all substantially worse. On pose estimation, Pow3R w/K(Pro) beats DUSt3R+PnP by 5.0 mAA points on Co3Dv2 (82.2 vs 77.2) and 10.8 on RealEstate10K (72.5 vs 61.7), while running ~10× faster (30.1 vs 3.2 fps). It also surpasses PoseDiffusion (66.5 mAA on Co3Dv2) and RayDiffusion (93.3 RRA@15 only, no RTA reported) on both accuracy and speed. Notably, Pow3R without any auxiliary input (3.6 rel / 68.0 τ) matches DUSt3R closely, confirming no performance regression from the added conditioning modules.

## Relation to Prior Work

Pow3R builds directly on DUSt3R's pointmap regression paradigm and ViT encoder-decoder architecture, extending its input space from RGB-only to any combination of camera intrinsics, relative pose, and depth maps via lightweight block-specific MLP injection. It departs from the broader SfM/MVS optimization pipeline tradition (COLMAP, MVSNet) by maintaining a single feed-forward network, and from prior 'guidance' methods (UniDepth for optional intrinsics, CompletionFormer for sparse depth) by unifying all modalities in one model trained with random subset sampling. The additional prediction of X^{2,2} (second image's pointmap in its own frame) is a practical departure from DUSt3R that enables single-pass focal and pose recovery via Procrustes alignment instead of PnP.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

By making a widely-adopted 3D foundation model (DUSt3R) accept arbitrary subsets of priors without retraining, Pow3R removes a significant practical barrier: real-world pipelines almost always have at least some camera or depth information available. The emergent capabilities—native-resolution sliding-window inference and zero-shot sparse-to-dense depth completion—open applications that were previously infeasible with the base model. Open questions include whether the pairwise formulation scales gracefully to longer sequences without the global alignment step, whether the conditioning approach generalizes to dynamic scenes (as in MonST3R), and whether the model's learned 'judgment' of input quality (observed in the controllability study) can be made more explicit or reliable under adversarial noise.
