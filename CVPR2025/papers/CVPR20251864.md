## Problem

Existing automatic parallelism (AP) methods for distributed deep learning optimize inter-layer parallelism (pipeline parallelism) and intra-layer parallelism (data, tensor, and fully sharded data parallelism) either separately or hierarchically, which inherently yields sub-optimal parallel strategies. No prior method jointly optimizes both categories of parallel strategies within a single optimization framework to guarantee a globally optimal solution.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/CVPR20251864/cropped_page1_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

UniAP consumes a computation graph, hardware profiling results (all-reduce efficiency per device subset, P2P speeds, computation-communication overlap coefficients, per-layer forward time, and memory usage at various TP sizes), a mini-batch size, the total GPU count, and a strategy dictionary enumerating candidate intra-layer configurations. It outputs a complete execution plan: the optimal pipeline parallel size, micro-batch count, per-layer pipeline stage assignment, per-layer intra-layer strategy (DP/TP/FSDP combination), and the corresponding minimum training time per iteration. The MIQP is solved via Gurobi with a configurable time limit, and the Unified Optimization Process iterates over all factor pairs of the GPU count and mini-batch size to cover the full search space.

## Method

UniAP formulates the joint selection of pipeline parallelism, data parallelism, tensor parallelism, and FSDP as a single Mixed Integer Quadratic Programming (MIQP) problem whose objective minimizes training time per iteration under a GPipe-style pipeline decomposition. The MIQP encodes computation-stage costs, cross-stage communication costs, per-device memory limits, order-preserving (contiguity) constraints on layer placement, and binary strategy-selection variables, with quadratic terms arising from pairwise resharding costs between adjacent layers. The Unified Optimization Process enumerates all valid pipeline parallel sizes (factors of the GPU count) and micro-batch counts (factors of the mini-batch size), solving one MIQP per combination and retaining the globally best solution. This joint formulation is the key distinction: unlike hierarchical methods that fix inter-layer partitioning before optimizing intra-layer strategies, UniAP lets the MIQP solver co-optimize layer placement and intra-layer strategy simultaneously, accessing a strictly larger feasible space that yields up to 3.80× higher throughput and up to 107× faster strategy optimization compared to state-of-the-art baselines.

## Results

Across five Transformer-based models (BERT-Huge, T5-Large, ViT-Huge, Swin-Huge, Llama-7B) on four hardware environments, UniAP achieves up to 3.80× higher training throughput than Galvatron (4.63 vs. 1.22 samples/s on Llama-7B with 8 A100 GPUs) and up to 1.71× higher than Alpa (10.77 vs. 8.95 samples/s on BERT-Huge with 8 TITAN Xp GPUs). Strategy optimization time is reduced by up to 17.29× versus Galvatron and >107× versus Alpa (0.37 min vs. 6.44 min and >40 min on BERT-Huge). UniAP also finds feasible strategies where baselines fail: it trains Swin-Huge on ENVA where Galvatron encounters CUDA OOM, and identifies valid strategies on ENVD-32 where DeepSpeed ZeRO-3 cannot launch training at all.

## Baselines & Comparisons

Against Galvatron (hierarchical AP optimizing DP/TP/FSDP within fixed PP stages), UniAP matches throughput on ENVA for BERT-Huge, T5-Large, and ViT-Huge, but achieves a decisive 3.80× throughput advantage on Llama-7B (ENVC) by discovering an 8-stage PP with micro-batch size 1 instead of Galvatron's 4-stage PP with 2-way TP. UniAP also finds a feasible strategy for Swin-Huge on ENVA where Galvatron fails with CUDA OOM. Against Alpa (hierarchical AP over DP/TP/PP), UniAP wins on throughput for BERT-Huge (10.77 vs. 8.95) and ViT-Huge (45.58 vs. 38.74) on ENVB-8, while Alpa encounters OOM or has no official implementation for several models. Against Megatron (manual TP+PP) and DeepSpeed ZeRO-3 (manual FSDP) on the 32-DCU cluster (ENVD-32), UniAP matches Megatron's best throughput (2.01 samples/s for Llama-7B) but completes strategy optimization in 3.07 min versus >8 hours for Megatron (~157× faster), while DeepSpeed ZeRO-3 fails to produce any solution due to a divisibility constraint on mini-batch size. The ablation study (Figure 6) confirms that restricting the search to inter-layer-only or intra-layer-only strategies degrades throughput or causes OOM, validating the joint optimization approach.

## Relation to Prior Work

UniAP extends the automatic parallelism (AP) literature by formulating the joint optimization of inter-layer (pipeline) and intra-layer (data, tensor, FSDP) parallel strategies as a single Mixed Integer Quadratic Program, departing from the hierarchical approach used by Alpa, Galvatron, PipeDream, and Piper, which first fix inter-layer partitioning and then optimize intra-layer strategies per stage. The MIQP objective is structured around GPipe's pipeline time decomposition, and the order-preserving (contiguity) constraint is adapted from DNNpartitioning. The key conceptual departure is treating both categories of parallelism as co-occurring decision variables in one optimization rather than a two-stage search, which the authors argue eliminates the sub-optimality inherent in hierarchical methods.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The joint-optimization formulation is practically significant: the ablation study confirms that restricting to inter-layer-only or intra-layer-only strategies leads to OOM or measurably lower throughput, validating that the two categories are genuinely coupled. The 3.80× throughput gain on Llama-7B (8× A100) and up to 107× faster strategy search are compelling for practitioners deploying large models on constrained hardware. Limitations include reliance on GPipe-style (non-interleaved) pipeline parallelism, evaluation limited to Transformer-based models, and dependence on a commercial MIQP solver (Gurobi); whether the formulation scales to models with thousands of layers or to more advanced PP schedules (1F1B, interleaved) remains open.
