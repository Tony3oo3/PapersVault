## Problem

Self-supervised structure-from-motion and monocular depth estimation methods rely heavily on rich textures for feature correspondence, causing them to fail on low-texture indoor scenes such as white walls and uniform floors where photometric error remains near zero even with incorrect depth estimates. No prior framework simultaneously exploits shape-from-shading (which captures surface geometry from illumination cues on textureless regions) and structure-from-motion (which excels on textured regions) to complement each other's weaknesses.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/CVPR20250202/cropped_page1_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The network consumes monocular RGB video sequences in fixed 3-frame windows (indoor images resized to 640×480, outdoor to 512×256) and jointly produces per-pixel dense depth maps, relative camera ego-motion, albedo and shading decompositions, surface normal maps, and back-projected 3D point clouds. The SfS pathway operates on individual frames while the SfM pathway processes the sequence, and both share a ResNet-18 encoder-decoder backbone with weights tied across frames.

## Method

Shading-SfM-Net is a dual-pathway self-supervised framework: the SfS pathway performs inverse rendering (Lambertian model with 2nd-order spherical harmonics basis) to estimate albedo, normals, shading, and light source, supervised by a CIELAB-space reconstruction loss; the SfM pathway estimates depth and ego-motion using BRISK-keypoint-restricted photometric consistency with learnable brightness affine parameters, geometric depth consistency, and a layout consistency loss that aligns LayoutNet/RoomNet-detected structural lines between input and back-projected images. Two cross-network consistency terms bridge the pathways: a surface shape consistency that enforces cross-normal agreement between SfM depth gradients and SfS normals plus curved-surface fitting on albedo superpixel segments, and a scene registration consistency that minimizes Chamfer distance between the 3D point clouds from both networks at the same and adjacent timesteps. The entire framework is trained end-to-end without ground-truth depth, with all losses backpropagating through both branches simultaneously.

## Results

On NYUv2, Shading-SfM-Net achieves AbsRel 0.129, RMS 0.520, and δ₁ 0.828, setting the best unsupervised results across all error and accuracy metrics. On ScanNet, it reaches AbsRel 0.151, RMS 0.352, and δ₁ 0.798, again leading all unsupervised baselines. These unsupervised results are competitive with the strongest supervised methods (e.g., Trap Attention: AbsRel 0.090, δ₁ 0.927 on NYUv2), closing the gap to within ~0.03 AbsRel. The ablation confirms each component contributes: removing all joint consistencies degrades AbsRel from 0.129 to 0.178.

## Baselines & Comparisons

Shading-SfM-Net outperforms all unsupervised baselines on both NYUv2 and ScanNet. On NYUv2, it improves AbsRel from 0.152 (RA-Depth, the closest competitor) to 0.129 (−15%) and δ₁ from 0.775 to 0.828 (+5.3 pp), while on ScanNet it reduces AbsRel from 0.169 to 0.151 and raises δ₁ from 0.737 to 0.798. The gap over earlier unsupervised methods is substantial: MonoDepth2 [24] (AbsRel 0.176) and MovingIndoor [88] (AbsRel 0.208) are 28% and 61% worse in AbsRel respectively. GeoNet [77] fails entirely on NYUv2 (training collapsed). Among supervised methods, the best (Trap Attention, AbsRel 0.090) still leads by ~0.039, but Shading-SfM-Net matches or exceeds several supervised baselines (DORN, SharpNet, Adabins) in δ₁ and δ₂ without any ground-truth depth labels. The cross-dataset generalization test (trained on NYUv2, tested on ScanNet) further confirms that the learned shading and motion representations transfer without fine-tuning.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the unsupervised monocular depth and ego-motion learning lineage initiated by SfM-Learner (Zhou et al., 2017) and refined by MonoDepth2 (Godard et al., 2019), but departs from the standard photometric-consistency-only formulation by jointly training a shape-from-shading (inverse rendering) branch alongside the SfM branch. It also generalizes the piece-wise planar prior from PlanerCNN (Liu et al., 2019) to a curved-surface fitting constraint, and replaces full-pixel photometric loss with BRISK keypoint-driven spectral consistency to avoid degradation on textureless regions. The layout consistency term borrows from room layout estimation (LayoutNet, RoomNet) as an auxiliary geometric regularizer, a connection not previously exploited in self-supervised depth pipelines.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The paper addresses a practically important gap: standard self-supervised SfM methods collapse on low-texture indoor surfaces (white walls, bare floors) where photometric constraints become vacuous, yet such scenes dominate real-world robotics and AR applications. The joint SfS+SfM formulation and the cross-network 3D registration loss offer a principled way to exploit shading cues that are otherwise discarded, and the cross-dataset generalization (NYUv2→ScanNet) suggests the learned shading prior transfers. Open questions include whether the Lambertian assumption and ResNet-18 backbone limit scalability to larger or more complex scenes, whether the external layout detectors (LayoutNet/RoomNet) introduce a dependency that undermines the self-supervised claim, and whether the approach extends to dynamic or multi-room sequences without additional temporal modeling.
