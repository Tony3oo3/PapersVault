## Problem

Vision-centric 3D semantic scene completion suffers from two fundamental limitations: (1) 2D-to-3D view projection inevitably duplicates features from occluding objects into voxels behind them, corrupting the semantic context of occluded regions; and (2) static query embeddings in Mask2Former-style decoders lack the adaptability to handle the high appearance variability of semantic classes across diverse driving environments (e.g., urban vs. highway road textures).

## Input / Output

The method consumes a front-view RGB image (1280×384 on SemanticKITTI, 1408×384 on SSCBench), P=4 previous RGB frames, camera intrinsics/extrinsics, and a stereo depth map from an off-the-shelf MobileStereoNet. It produces a 256×256×32 semantic voxel grid (covering 51.2 m × 51.2 m × 6.4 m) where each voxel carries an occupancy probability and a semantic class label (21 classes on SemanticKITTI, 20 on SSCBench). Internally, voxel features are computed at 128×128×16 resolution and upsampled via trilinear interpolation for evaluation.

## Method

SOAP introduces two components. The Occluded Region-Aware View Projection (OAP) lifts multi-scale 2D features (from EfficientNetB7 at 1/4–1/32 scales) into 3D voxel space via Lift-Splat-Shoot, then identifies occluded voxels by thresholding the depth distribution behind the argmax depth bin per pixel. Occluded and invisible voxels are refined by 3D deformable cross-attention against historical voxel features warped from P=4 previous frames using ego-pose alignment, while visible voxels retain their original features. The Scene-Adaptive Decoder is a 9-iteration Mask2Former-style transformer that adds a cross-attention to a semantic repository (K=10 tokens per class) maintained by a novelty-based selection criterion combining classification scores with negative cosine similarity to class-average tokens, enabling query embeddings to capture diverse driving contexts. A Swin-style windowed self-attention voxel encoder processes the combined multi-scale voxel features on BEV planes before the decoder produces final class and mask predictions via Hungarian matching.

## Results

On the SemanticKITTI test set, SOAP achieves 46.09 SC IoU and 19.09 SSC mIoU, surpassing the previous best temporal method HTCL (44.23 IoU, 17.09 mIoU) by +1.86 IoU and +2.0 mIoU. On SSCBench-KITTI360, SOAP reaches 48.12 SC IoU and 20.92 SSC mIoU, outperforming SGN (47.06 IoU, 18.25 mIoU) by +1.06 IoU and +2.67 mIoU. In terms of efficiency, SOAP with EfficientNetB7 uses only 85.2M parameters and 705.3 GFLOPs—less than half the parameters of HTCL (181.4M) and OccFormer (202.7M)—while achieving the highest accuracy.

## Baselines & Comparisons

SOAP outperforms all 11 compared baselines on both datasets. On SemanticKITTI, the closest competitor is HTCL (ECCV 2024), which SOAP surpasses by +2.0 mIoU (19.09 vs 17.09) and +1.86 SC IoU; SGN (IEEE TIP 2024) trails by +3.33 mIoU. On SSCBench-KITTI360, SOAP beats SGN by +2.67 mIoU (20.92 vs 18.25) and Symphonies (CVPR 2024) by +2.34 mIoU. Non-temporal methods (MonoScene, TPVFormer, OccFormer, NDC-Scene, SurroundOcc) lag significantly, with mIoU scores between 11.08 and 13.81, roughly 5–8 points below SOAP. Notably, SOAP also achieves the best per-class scores on most categories (sidewalk, building, car, vegetation, terrain, fence, pole, traffic-sign) on SemanticKITTI, while HTCL retains a slight edge on road (64.4 vs 63.6) and parking (33.8 vs 36.8). In the efficiency comparison (Table 3), SOAP with EfficientNetB7 uses 85.2M params / 705.3 GFLOPs versus HTCL's 181.4M / 2622.4 and OccFormer's 202.7M / 973.1, while still achieving the highest IoU and mIoU.

## Relation to Prior Work

SOAP builds directly on the Lift-Splat-Shoot (LSS) framework for 2D-to-3D view projection and the Mask2Former-style query-based decoder used by OccFormer and LowRankOcc. It departs from prior temporal methods (HTCL, SGN) by explicitly identifying occluded voxel regions via a depth-based occlusion map and refining them with deformable cross-attention against warped historical features, rather than applying temporal fusion uniformly. The scene-adaptive decoder introduces a training-time semantic repository with novelty-based token selection, extending the static query embedding paradigm of Mask2Former-style SSC decoders toward scene-adaptive semantic representation.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

SOAP addresses two fundamental limitations of vision-centric 3D SSC—occlusion-induced feature duplication in single-view projection and appearance variability across driving environments—yielding SOTA results on both SemanticKITTI and SSCBench-KITTI360 with notably lower compute (85M params, 705 GFLoPs) than competing temporal methods. The occlusion-aware projection is a practical, interpretable solution that could transfer to other single-view 3D reconstruction tasks. Open questions include whether the semantic repository can be updated at inference time for truly online adaptation, how the method generalizes to multi-camera or surround-view settings, and whether the reliance on an off-the-shelf stereo depth estimator limits robustness in adverse weather.
