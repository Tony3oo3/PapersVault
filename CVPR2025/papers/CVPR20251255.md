## Problem

Camera-based 3D semantic scene completion methods suffer from non-unique 3D-to-2D correspondences and lack a long-range global perspective, making them unable to recover complete scene semantics in occluded regions. No prior work has exploited the complementary bird's-eye-view information available in low-cost satellite imagery to address this occlusion bottleneck.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/CVPR20251255/cropped_page0_idx1.webp)

## Input / Output

The method takes a single ground RGB image (1226×370 on SemanticKITTI, 1480×376 on KITTI-360) paired with a 512×512 satellite image at 0.2 m/pixel resolution centered on the ego-vehicle, and produces a 256×256×32 voxel grid (0.2 m cubes over a 51.2 m × 51.2 m × 6.4 m volume) with per-voxel semantic class labels and occupancy. Internally, a pre-trained MobileStereoNet depth estimator generates sparse voxel proposals that gate which voxels are actively queried from the ground image, reducing computation to visible regions only.

## Method

SGFormer employs a dual-branch architecture: a ground branch (EfficientNetB7 + FPN) encodes the ground image and uses depth-based sparse proposals with deformable cross-attention to populate visible voxels, followed by deformable self-attention diffusion and a 3D-UNet; a satellite branch (ResNet-50 + FPN) encodes the satellite image into BEV space via deformable cross-attention. The key satellite correction strategy initializes BEV queries with learnable embeddings and iteratively refines them through deformable self-attention against vertically max-pooled ground-view features, warming up the satellite queries to counteract localization misalignment before cross-attention queries the satellite image. An adaptive fusion module then computes per-voxel, per-channel weights via a combined channel-attention (in 3D) and spatial-attention (in BEV) path plus an MLP, blending the two branches' features to preserve satellite's global layout while retaining ground's detail on small and dynamic objects. A final entropy-based refinement step re-queries high-uncertainty voxels from ground features, and training uses weighted cross-entropy, scene class affinity, BEV, and coarse losses.

## Results

On SemanticKITTI, SGFormer achieves 45.01 IoU for occupancy prediction and 16.68 mIoU for semantic prediction, outperforming all camera-based baselines (next best: VoxFormer at 44.05 IoU / 12.30 mIoU; Symphonies at 41.92 IoU / 14.89 mIoU). On SSCBench-KITTI-360, SGFormer reaches 46.35 IoU and 18.30 mIoU, surpassing the previous best camera-based method Symphonies (43.41 IoU / 17.82 mIoU) and approaching LiDAR-based SSCNet (53.58 IoU / 16.95 mIoU). Gains are most pronounced on scene layout classes (road, building, vegetation) where satellite observations provide complementary global context, while the adaptive fusion module preserves accuracy on small and dynamic objects.

## Baselines & Comparisons

SGFormer outperforms all 8 camera-based baselines on both IoU and mIoU across both datasets. On SemanticKITTI, it leads VoxFormer by +0.96 IoU and +4.38 mIoU (45.01 vs 44.05; 16.68 vs 12.30), and Symphonies by +3.09 IoU and +1.79 mIoU (45.01 vs 41.92; 16.68 vs 14.89). On SSCBench-KITTI-360, the gap over OccFormer is the largest at +6.08 IoU (46.35 vs 40.27) and +4.49 mIoU (18.30 vs 13.81), while the margin over Symphonies narrows to +2.94 IoU and +0.48 mIoU. Against LiDAR-based methods, SGFormer surpasses JS3C-Net (38.98 IoU) and LMSCNet (28.61 IoU) on SemanticKITTI, and on SSCBench-KITTI-360 it is the only camera-based method to approach LiDAR-level scene layout accuracy (road 61.04 vs SSCNet 65.70; building 43.05 vs SSCNet 44.41). The adaptive fusion module is critical for maintaining strong performance on small/dynamic objects (car, fence, pole) where satellite observations are less informative, as confirmed by the ablation showing the 'detail' category mIoU drops from 9.29 to 8.26 without it.

## Relation to Prior Work

SGFormer extends the camera-based SSC lineage (MonoScene, TPVFormer, VoxFormer, OccFormer) by introducing satellite imagery as a second input modality, creating a dual-branch architecture that encodes ground and satellite views in parallel. The feature transformation mechanism is built directly on deformable attention from Deformable DETR, while the sparse voxel querying strategy with depth-based proposals follows VoxFormer. The satellite-ground fusion concept draws from the satellite-assisted perception literature (cross-view localization, SG-BEV, SNAP), but this is the first work to apply such cooperative perception to 3D semantic scene completion rather than 2D localization or map construction.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

This work demonstrates that a low-cost, widely available satellite image can meaningfully complement ground-view cameras to resolve the long-standing occlusion bottleneck in SSC, achieving SOTA on both SemanticKITTI and SSCBench-KITTI-360. However, the method's sensitivity to localization noise (±5 m causes ~4.5% mIoU drop) and the inherent staleness of pre-captured satellite imagery (missing dynamic objects, temporary changes) limit immediate deployment. Open questions include whether the approach generalizes to regions with poor satellite coverage, how it performs under more severe localization errors, and whether the dual-branch overhead can be reduced for real-time autonomous driving applications.
