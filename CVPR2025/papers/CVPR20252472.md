## Problem

Existing multimodal LLMs handle at most two modalities (vision-language or speech-language) and rely on external TTS tools for speech generation, preventing real-time emotional spoken dialogue. No prior omni-modal model achieves state-of-the-art performance on both vision-language and speech benchmarks simultaneously while supporting flexible speech style control (emotions, pitches). The paper asks how to build an end-to-end omni-modal LLM that sees, hears, and speaks with vivid emotions without sacrificing vision-language quality.

## Input / Output

Inputs are RGB images (encoded by QwenViT with 4× downsampling), speech waveforms (discretized at 25 tokens/sec into a 4096-entry codebook via FSQ), and text instructions. Outputs are structured as JSON containing a text response, explicit style labels (one of 24 combinations: 2 genders × 3 pitches × 4 emotions), and discrete speech units that are decoded into a speech waveform by a VITS-based U2S detokenizer conditioned on a style prototype embedding. The chain-of-modality design decomposes speech response into ASR → text generation → style selection + speech unit generation.

## Method

EMOVA uses a Qwen-2.5 LLM backbone with a continuous QwenViT vision encoder (MLP projector) for images and a discrete SPIRAL+FSQ speech tokenizer for audio, where semantic content is quantized while acoustic style is kept as a continuous embedding. The key architectural insight is semantic-acoustic disentanglement: only semantic embeddings are quantized into the LLM vocabulary, while style embeddings feed a lightweight U2S detokenizer (conditional VAE built on VITS) that synthesizes speech with controllable emotion and pitch via style prototypes selected from Emotion2Vec. Training follows a three-stage paradigm: (1) vision pre-alignment of the projector, (2) joint omni-modal text-centric alignment on 7.4M image-text and speech-text samples simultaneously (not sequentially), and (3) omni-modal instruction tuning on 4.4M multi-task samples. A surprising empirical finding is that joint omni-modal alignment outperforms both bi-modal (vision-only or speech-only) and sequential alignment strategies, suggesting mutual enhancement across modalities through text as a bridge.

## Results

EMOVA-7B achieves 2317 on MME, 83.0 on MMBench, and 814 on OCRBench, outperforming GPT-4V (1927, 75.0, 656) and Gemini Pro 1.5 on 11 of 15 vision-language benchmarks. On speech, EMOVA-72B achieves 2.9 WER on LibriSpeech, surpassing Whisper-Large (3.0) and Mini-Omni2 (4.8), while EMOVA-7B reaches 4.1 WER. EMOVA-7B also leads VITA-8x7B by +220 points on MME (2317 vs. 2097) and by +136 on OCRBench (814 vs. 678), and exceeds Baichuan-Omni-7B on nearly all evaluated benchmarks.

## Baselines & Comparisons

Against GPT-4V, EMOVA-7B wins on 12 of 15 benchmarks, with the largest margins on MME (+390), OCRBench (+158), and MathVista (+15.6), but loses on MM-Vet (59.4 vs. 67.7) and MMMU (49.8 vs. 56.8). Against GPT-4o, EMOVA-72B leads on 10 of 15 benchmarks (e.g., MME +92, OCRBench +107, MathVista +6.1) but trails on MMMU (59.7 vs. 69.2) and RealWorldQA (71.0 vs. 75.4). Against Gemini Pro 1.5, EMOVA-7B surpasses it on 10 of 15 benchmarks, with notable gains on DocVQA (+7.7) and MathVista (+13.4), while losing on RealWorldQA (67.5 vs. 68.7) and MMMU (49.8 vs. 58.5). Among omni-modal LLMs, EMOVA-7B dominates VITA-8x7B across nearly all vision benchmarks (e.g., MME +220, OCRBench +136, MathVista +20.6) and Baichuan-Omni-7B on most benchmarks (e.g., MME +130, OCRBench +114, MathVista +13.6), though Baichuan-Omni edges ahead on MM-Vet (65.4 vs. 59.4). On speech ASR, EMOVA-72B (2.9 WER) surpasses Whisper-Large (3.0) and Mini-Omni2 (4.8), while EMOVA-7B (4.1) trails Whisper-Large but beats Mini-Omni2 and VITA-8x7B (3.4).

## Relation to Prior Work

EMOVA builds on the semantic-acoustic disentanglement framework of ToneUnit [77] (using SPIRAL encoder + FSQ quantization) for speech tokenization, and on VITS [35] for the unit-to-speech detokenizer. It departs from prior omni-modal LLMs in two key ways: (1) it uses a continuous vision encoder (QwenViT) paired with a discrete speech tokenizer, avoiding the information loss of fully-discrete approaches like AnyGPT while enabling end-to-end speech generation unlike continuous-speech approaches like VITA; and (2) it introduces a lightweight style module for emotion and pitch control, which no prior omni-modal or speech LLM has explored. The joint omni-modal text-centric alignment strategy also departs from the sequential alignment used by VITA and InternOmni.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

EMOVA provides the first open-source omni-modal LLM that simultaneously achieves state-of-the-art on both vision-language and speech benchmarks, offering a practical alternative to closed models like GPT-4o. The empirical finding that joint omni-modal alignment improves both vision-language and speech capabilities over their bi-modal counterparts challenges the assumption that adding modalities creates interference, and suggests a general principle for multi-modal pre-training. Open questions include whether the mutual-benefit effect holds at larger scales or with additional modalities (e.g., video, music), the limited Chinese speech synthesis quality, and whether the emotion control generalizes beyond the four discrete emotions tested.
