## Problem

Mask-based virtual try-on methods destroy spatial and lighting information in the person image, break foreground/background coherence, and require additional parsers—defects that become severe in complex in-the-wild scenarios with occlusions and varied poses. Existing mask-free approaches rely on knowledge distillation from mask-based teachers, inheriting their limitations and failing to generalize to wild settings.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/CVPR20250762/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

At inference, the model takes only an unmasked person image and a garment image (no parser, no mask, no densepose), producing a 1024×768 try-on result via 30 DDIM steps. During training, the model consumes pseudo-triplets {P′, G, P} where P′ is a mask-free person image generated by a two-stage mask-based pipeline, augmented with synthetic foreground and background layers stacked in the order B–P/P′–F. The garment is encoded by both IP-Adapter (cross-attention) and SDXL-Reference-Net (self-attention), with a text prompt restricted to garment categories (upper/lower/dresses).

## Method

BooW-VTON fine-tunes an SDXL U-Net in a mask-free regime by replacing masked person inputs with high-quality pseudo person images generated through a two-stage inference pipeline (coarse-mask try-on → refined-mask try-on) using IDM-VTON. In-the-wild data augmentation synthesizes diverse backgrounds via inpainting and transparent foreground objects via LayerDiffusion, then stacks them with person images to teach the model to preserve non-try-on content under complex interference. A novel try-on localization loss regularizes cross- and self-attention scores in non-try-on regions (using the complement of the try-on mask) to suppress garment feature leakage outside the target area. The final loss combines standard LDM denoising with this attention regularization (λ_ar = 1), applied to attention blocks 5–64 of the 70-block SDXL U-Net, with only U-Net parameters unfrozen during training.

## Results

On VITON-HD (paired), BooW-VTON achieves LPIPS 0.1080, SSIM 0.8618, PSNR 21.80, FIDu 8.809, and KIDu 0.8176, outperforming the strongest baseline IDM-VTON (LPIPS 0.1223, SSIM 0.8547, PSNR 21.06, FIDu 9.265, KIDu 1.272). On the in-the-wild StreetVTON test set, BooW-VTON achieves FIDu 20.50 and KIDu 4.494, and on WildVTON achieves FIDu 32.53 and KIDu 4.509, both substantially better than all mask-based baselines. On DressCode, BooW-VTON leads all three garment categories (Upper, Lower, Dresses) on every metric, e.g., FIDp 8.941 for Upper vs. 16.25 for IDM-VTON.

## Baselines & Comparisons

BooW-VTON outperforms all seven baselines on every VITON-HD paired and unpaired metric, with the closest competitor being IDM-VTON (LPIPS 0.1223 vs. 0.1080; KIDu 1.272 vs. 0.8176). On StreetVTON, BooW-VTON achieves the best FIDu (20.50 vs. 23.02 for TPD and 23.15 for StableVITON) but is slightly behind DCI-VTON on KIDu (4.494 vs. 3.470), which the authors attribute to DCI-VTON's mask-based pixel preservation at the cost of overall image coherence. On WildVTON, BooW-VTON leads on both FIDu (32.53 vs. 38.77 for IDM-VTON) and KIDu (4.509 vs. 5.606 for OOTD), showing the largest margins over mask-based methods in the most challenging setting. On DressCode, BooW-VTON dominates all three garment categories; for example, on Dresses it achieves FIDp 8.434 and KIDu 0.5599 versus 25.96 and 9.739 for IDM-VTON, a gap of roughly 3× in FID. The ablation further confirms that the in-the-wild data augmentation is the single largest contributor to wild-scenario gains (StreetVTON FIDu drops from 27.26 to 21.70), while the try-on localization loss provides the final refinement (StreetVTON KIDu drops from 5.170 to 4.494).

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper builds directly on IDM-VTON (ECCV 2024) as its architectural backbone, reusing its SDXL-based U-Net, IP-Adapter, and Reference Net components, but fundamentally departs from the mask-based training paradigm that dominates the field. While earlier mask-free methods (PFAFN, PFDM) relied on knowledge distillation from mask-based teachers—thereby inheriting their spatial-information loss—this work instead generates high-quality pseudo training pairs via a refined two-stage inference pipeline and augments them with synthetic in-the-wild foreground/background, training the model to learn try-on localization intrinsically. The try-on localization loss, which constrains attention maps to suppress garment feature leakage into non-try-on regions, is a novel regularization not present in prior mask-free or mask-based approaches.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

By eliminating the need for a human parser at inference time, this work removes a significant pipeline dependency and failure mode for consumer-facing try-on applications, particularly in complex real-world scenes with occlusions, accessories, and varied lighting. The ablation revealing that naive distillation from a mask-based teacher actually degrades wild-scenario performance (FID 57.5 vs. 38.8 on WildVTON) provides a useful cautionary result for the community. However, the paper acknowledges a key open limitation: without a reference garment for non-try-on body regions, the model generates random content, limiting user controllability over complete outfits—a challenge that remains unresolved for practical deployment.
