## Problem

Mamba's autoregressive, sequential formulation is poorly suited to vision tasks where spatial relationships are local and parallel rather than order-dependent, and where global context must be captured in a single forward pass. Prior Mamba-based vision backbones (Vim, VMamba) attempt to address this with bidirectional or cross-scan strategies but introduce significant latency and still underperform comparably-sized ViT and CNN backbones. No prior work has systematically studied how to integrate Mamba with self-attention in a hybrid vision architecture.

## Input / Output

The model consumes standard RGB images (224×224 for classification; 1280×800 crops for detection/segmentation) and produces task-specific outputs: class probabilities for ImageNet, bounding boxes and instance masks via a Cascade Mask R-CNN head on MS COCO, or pixel-wise semantic masks via a UperNet head on ADE20K. Internally, the input is converted to overlapping patches at H/4 resolution by a two-layer 3×3 convolutional stem, then progressively downsampled by stride-2 3×3 convolutions across four hierarchical stages (H/4 → H/8 → H/16 → H/32).

## Method

MambaVision redesigns the Mamba mixer for vision by (1) replacing the causal 1D convolution with a regular (non-causal) convolution, (2) adding a symmetric non-SSM branch (conv + SiLU) that bypasses the selective scan, and (3) concatenating the outputs of both branches (each projected to C/2 dimensions) before a final linear projection, rather than using Mamba's original gating. The architecture is a four-stage hierarchical design: stages 1–2 use lightweight CNN residual blocks for fast high-resolution feature extraction, while stages 3–4 alternate between MambaVision mixer blocks (first N/2 layers) and windowed multi-head self-attention blocks (last N/2 layers). The key empirical finding is that placing self-attention exclusively in the final layers of each stage yields the best accuracy–throughput tradeoff, as it recovers the long-range spatial dependencies that the sequential SSM processing inherently limits. This hybrid design achieves a new SOTA Pareto front on ImageNet-1K (e.g., MambaVision-B: 84.2% Top-1 at 3670 img/s) while using 56% fewer FLOPs than MaxViT-B.

## Results

On ImageNet-1K, MambaVision-B achieves 84.2% Top-1 accuracy at 3,670 images/sec throughput, outperforming ConvNeXt-B (83.8%), Swin-B (83.5%), and VMamba-B (83.9%) while delivering substantially higher throughput. The largest variant, MambaVision-L2 (241.5M params), reaches 85.3% Top-1, surpassing DeiT3-L (84.8%) and MaxViT-L (85.1% at lower throughput). On MS COCO, MambaVision-B achieves 52.8 box AP and 45.7 mask AP with Cascade Mask R-CNN, and on ADE20K it reaches 49.1 mIoU with UperNet, both leading among comparably sized backbones.

## Baselines & Comparisons

MambaVision consistently outperforms all Mamba-based baselines: MambaVision-B (84.2%) beats VMamba-B (83.9%) by +0.3% Top-1 while achieving 5.7× higher throughput (3,670 vs. 645 Img/s), and MambaVision-S (83.3%) surpasses SiMBA-B (83.5%) in throughput by 7.5×. Against CNN baselines, MambaVision-B exceeds ConvNeXt-B (83.8%) by +0.4% with 2.5× higher throughput. Against Transformer baselines, MambaVision-L (85.0%) edges out MaxViT-B (84.9%) and FasterViT-3 (84.9%) while using 56% fewer FLOPs than MaxViT-B. On MS COCO detection, MambaVision-T gains +0.7 box AP over both Swin-T and ConvNeXt-T, and MambaVision-B leads ConvNeXt-B by +0.1 box AP. On ADE20K segmentation, MambaVision-T achieves 46.0 mIoU, beating Swin-T (44.5) by +1.5 and Focal-T (45.8) by +0.2, with similar margins at S and B scales.

## Relation to Prior Work

MambaVision builds directly on the Mamba selective state space model (Gu & Dao, 2023), re-designing its mixer for vision by replacing causal convolution with regular convolution, adding a symmetric non-SSM branch (conv + SiLU), and switching from gating to concatenation. It departs from prior Mamba-based vision backbones (Vim, VMamba, EfficientVMamba, SiMBA) that rely on bidirectional or cross-scan SSMs to recover global context, instead introducing a hybrid Mamba-Transformer architecture where self-attention blocks are placed in the final layers. The hierarchical CNN-SSM-attention structure also draws on the CNN-Transformer hybrid tradition (CoaT, NextViT, FasterViT), but is the first to combine Mamba's linear-complexity SSM with self-attention in a single vision backbone.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

MambaVision establishes that a simple hybrid of Mamba and self-attention—rather than more complex bidirectional or cross-scan SSM variants—can match or exceed pure Mamba-based models while delivering substantially higher throughput (e.g., ~5× over VMamba-B at comparable accuracy). Being the first to successfully scale a Mamba-based vision architecture to ImageNet-21K pretraining opens the door to larger, more capable SSM-based backbones for industrial deployment. Open questions include whether the N/2 Mamba-to-attention split generalizes to other modalities (video, 3D), whether the specific windowed attention sizes are optimal, and whether the approach extends to generative or multimodal settings where the sequential nature of SSMs might be more naturally aligned with the data.
