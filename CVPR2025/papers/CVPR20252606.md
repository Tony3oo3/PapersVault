## Problem

Video-LLMs can temporally ground language queries to video moments, but whether their predictions are robust and trustworthy remains unexplored. Specifically, it is unclear whether models maintain consistent predictions when queries are rephrased, visual content is temporally shifted, or when the model is asked to verify its own grounding. This paper reveals that current Video-LLMs exhibit severe consistency deficiencies, often performing at near chance-level when confirming their own predictions.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/CVPR20252606/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The evaluation takes video clips (30s for Charades, 120s for ActivityNet) sampled at model-specific frame rates (8–100 frames) paired with a natural-language query, and produces a timestamp interval [t_s, t_e] for grounding probes or a Yes/No answer for verification probes. Consistency is measured as IoU between the original and probe predictions for grounding, and as QA accuracy (with GPT-4o-mini judging indirect responses) for verification. Only correctly grounded predictions (IoU ≥ 0.5) are included in relative consistency scores, ensuring the probes test true comprehension rather than blanket response patterns.

## Method

The paper introduces VTune (Event Temporal Verification Tuning), which extends standard instruction tuning by reformulating temporal grounding as a verification task. Rather than simply mapping a query to a timestamp, the model is trained to confirm aligned (rephrased) queries against positive moments, identify and correct misaligned queries paired with positive moments, and reject aligned queries paired with negative (non-ground-truth) moments. This explicitly optimizes for consistency in the training objective, which standard instruction tuning does not address. The evaluation framework comprises four probes—Rephrased Grounding, Shifted Grounding, Holistic Verification, and Compositional Verification—supported by newly curated datasets (Charades-CON, ActivityNet-CON) where queries are rephrased via word replacement, voice conversion, and word-order modification, and ground-truth moments are shifted to new temporal positions. VTune is applied on top of Video-LLaMA and TimeChat, yielding substantial gains in both grounding accuracy and consistency across all four probes.

## Results

On the proposed Charades-CON and ActivityNet-CON consistency benchmarks, open-source Video-LLMs achieve near chance-level (≈50%) relative scores on holistic and compositional verification probes, while shifted grounding scores drop to 1.0–8.3 (IoU) on Charades-CON, indicating severe inconsistency. Closed-source models substantially outperform: Gemini 1.5 Flash reaches 34.6/37.8 grounding IoU and 22.8/22.4 holistic verification on Charades-CON/ActivityNet-CON, while GPT-4o achieves 28.5/26.8 grounding and 17.8/16.5 verification. The proposed VTune method yields the largest gains: TimeChat+VTune improves grounding from 30.5 to 76.2 (Charades-CON) and holistic verification from 14.0 to 44.8, while Video-LLaMA+VTune lifts grounding from 14.2 to 54.4 and verification from 7.5 to 30.7.

## Baselines & Comparisons

Among general-purpose open-source models, Video-LLaMA (14.2 grounding IoU on Charades-CON) and Video-ChatGPT (14.4) lead, but both score near 50% relative on verification probes, confirming chance-level consistency. Time-aware models TimeChat (30.5) and VTimeLLM (27.3) achieve the best open-source grounding on Charades-CON, yet their holistic verification relative scores (45.9% and 43.7%) remain far below GPT-4o (62.4%) and Gemini (65.8%). VTG-LLM, despite its specialized timestamp design, shows the worst verification consistency among time-aware models (23.1% relative H-Verify on Charades-CON), suggesting over-specialization at the cost of QA ability. Closed-source models dominate all consistency probes: Gemini 1.5 Flash leads every metric (e.g., 24.8 S-Ground IoU vs. 5.6 for TimeChat on Charades-CON), while GPT-4o is a close second. On the original grounding benchmarks (Table 3), VTune-finetuned TimeChat reaches 58.4 R@1@0.5 on Charades-STA and 41.0 on ActivityNet-Captions, surpassing the original TimeChat (46.7/28.0) and HawkEye (58.3/34.7) but still trailing task-specific Mr.BLIP (69.3/53.9) and BM-DETR (59.4/49.6). The key takeaway is that no open-source model matches closed-source consistency, and VTune is the only method that simultaneously improves both grounding and verification consistency across all probes.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper positions itself at the intersection of video temporal grounding (a well-studied task with proposal-based, proposal-free, and transformer-based methods) and the emerging Video-LLM paradigm, but carves new ground by introducing *consistency* as a first-class evaluation dimension for temporal comprehension. While prior analyses of Video-LLMs (e.g., MVBench, Video-Bench, TempCompass) focus on accuracy or temporal concept understanding via QA, this work repurposes the consistency notion from NLP and image-text literature and applies it specifically to moment predictions. The proposed VTune method extends standard instruction tuning by adding verification queries (event and temporal), drawing on the generator-validator consistency literature but adapting it to the video temporal domain.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The finding that all open-source Video-LLMs achieve near chance-level (~50%) verification consistency—regardless of whether they are general-purpose or explicitly time-aware—raises serious questions about the reliability of temporal grounding in deployed systems. The demonstration that common remedies (CoT prompting, description prompting, standard instruction tuning) fail to close this gap is practically important for practitioners who assumed these techniques would suffice. Open questions include whether consistency can be improved at scale, whether the gap between closed-source (GPT-4o, Gemini) and open-source models reflects architectural differences or training data quality, and whether the VTune paradigm generalizes to other fine-grained video understanding tasks beyond temporal grounding.
