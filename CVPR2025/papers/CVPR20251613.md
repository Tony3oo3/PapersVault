## Problem

3D diffusion policies for robotic manipulation capture geometric structure but lack the semantic understanding needed for pose-aware tasks (e.g., identifying a shoe's toe) and cross-object generalization. Existing semantic field approaches like D³ Fields and GenDP require manual keypoint selection, multi-view setups, and degrade under the occlusions that inevitably occur during manipulation, leaving a gap between geometric precision and robust semantic comprehension in real-time closed-loop control.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/CVPR20251613/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The policy consumes three modalities at each timestep: a real point cloud downsampled to 1024 points, robot joint states, and a 3D semantic flow of 1024 points each carrying 5 PCA-compressed DINOv2 features (shape K×(3+D_feat)). The semantic flow itself is constructed offline per object via active multi-view exploration and a 3D generative model, then updated online purely through pose transformation—no per-timestep feature re-extraction is needed. The output is a robot action vector produced by a DDIM denoising network over K iterations.

## Method

G3Flow operates in two phases. In the initialization phase, a robot actively explores the object from multiple viewpoints, a 3D generative model (Rodin) reconstructs a complete digital twin that fills in occluded regions using learned priors, and DINOv2 features extracted in virtual space are compressed via PCA and sampled to a canonical semantic point cloud via FPS. In the control phase, FoundationPose tracks the object's 6-DoF pose in real time, and the pre-computed semantic flow is rigidly transformed to align with the physical object (Eq. 1), preserving completeness under occlusion without re-detecting or re-extracting features. The enhanced diffusion policy conditions a DDIM denoising network on three parallel MLP-encoded streams—semantic flow features, real point cloud features, and robot joint states—trained with a standard noise-prediction MSE loss. This design decouples expensive semantic extraction (done once per object) from cheap per-timestep pose updates, achieving 34 Hz decision frequency versus ~7 Hz for prior semantic-field baselines.

## Results

On the RoboTwin benchmark, G3Flow achieves an average success rate of 68.3% on terminal-constrained manipulation tasks (Shoe Place, Dual Shoes Place, Tool Adjust, Bottle Adjust), outperforming the strongest baseline 3D Diffusion Policy (46.2%) by 22.1 percentage points. On cross-object generalization tasks, G3Flow reaches 50.1% average success rate versus 31.7% for 3D DP, a 18.4-point improvement. The 2D Diffusion Policy baseline lags significantly at 16.8% and 11.4% on the two task suites respectively. G3Flow also achieves a decision-making frequency of 34.04 Hz, nearly 6× faster than GenDP (6.89 Hz).

## Baselines & Comparisons

G3Flow consistently outperforms all three baselines across both task suites. Against 2D Diffusion Policy (DP), G3Flow gains 51.5 points on terminal-constrained tasks and 38.7 points on generalization tasks, confirming that 2D image-based policies lack the geometric and semantic precision needed for pose-aware manipulation. Against 3D Diffusion Policy (3D DP), G3Flow improves by 22.1 points (terminal) and 18.4 points (generalization), demonstrating that adding semantic flow on top of 3D geometry yields substantial gains. The 3D DP w/ color variant, which augments point clouds with RGB, performs inconsistently (e.g., 70.3% on Tool Adjust but only 7.0% on Dual Shoes Place), whereas G3Flow maintains strong performance across all tasks. In the ablation against GenDP (D³ Fields), G3Flow further improves by 9.3% and 3.7% on Shoe Place (T) and Dual Shoes Place (T), attributed to object-centric feature extraction that avoids background noise and occlusion-induced semantic corruption.

## Relation to Prior Work

G3Flow extends the 3D diffusion policy line (DP3, 3D Diffuser Actor) by addressing their lack of semantic understanding, and departs from prior semantic-field approaches (D³ Fields, GenDP) that require manual keypoint selection and multi-view setups. The key architectural departure is replacing hand-crafted or annotation-dependent semantic fields with a fully automated pipeline that chains a 3D generative model (Rodin) for digital twin creation, a vision foundation model (DINOv2) for feature extraction, and a pose tracker (FoundationPose) for real-time flow maintenance. This positions G3Flow as a foundation-model-driven successor to geometry-centric 3D policies, where semantic completeness under occlusion is achieved through virtual-space reconstruction rather than direct multi-view sensing.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

By eliminating manual annotation and multi-view hardware requirements while maintaining real-time performance (34 Hz vs. ~7 Hz for GenDP), G3Flow makes semantic-aware manipulation practically deployable with a single camera. The large performance gaps (22+ points on terminal-constrained tasks, 18+ on generalization) suggest that the bottleneck in current 3D policies is indeed semantic rather than geometric. However, all evaluation is in simulation (RoboTwin), and the authors acknowledge open challenges with articulated objects, multi-object interactions, and continuous 3D model updates during interaction—areas where the digital-twin assumption may break down in the physical world.
