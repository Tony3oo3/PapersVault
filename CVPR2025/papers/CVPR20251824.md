## Problem

Recorrupted-to-Recorrupted (R2R) demonstrated self-supervised denoising equivalent to supervised learning, but only for Gaussian noise. Its extension to non-Gaussian noise distributions—Poisson, Gamma, log-Rayleigh, and Binomial—that are prevalent in photon-limited imaging, SAR, and medical applications remained unexplored, leaving a gap between the theoretical promise of recorruption-based training and its practical applicability to real-world noise models.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/CVPR20251824/cropped_page1_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method consumes a single noisy image (no clean reference or independent noisy pair required) and outputs a denoised estimate via Monte Carlo averaging over J recorrupted samples at test time. It operates on standard RGB images at 512×512 (DIV2K, SAR) or 256×256 (fastMRI) resolutions and is architecture-agnostic, tested with DnCNN and DRUnet backbones. The recorruption parameter α (typically 0.1–0.5) controls the trade-off between input SNR and target noise level of the generated pair.

## Method

GR2R generalizes the R2R recorruption strategy to two noise classes: (i) non-Gaussian additive noise, where the recorruption noise ω is matched to higher-order moments of the original noise (e.g., third moment for log-Rayleigh), and (ii) Natural Exponential Family (NEF) distributions, where y is decomposed as y = (1−α)y₁ + αy₂ with y₁ and y₂ drawn from distribution-specific conditional distributions (e.g., Beta for Gamma, Binomial for Poisson). The training loss is either MSE between f(y₁) and y₂ or a distribution-aware negative log-likelihood, both proven to be unbiased estimators of the supervised loss. A key theoretical result shows that as α→0, the GR2R-MSE loss recovers the SURE loss without requiring explicit divergence computation, unifying the two frameworks. The approach extends naturally to general inverse problems y ≈ Ax by replacing f(y₁) with Af(y₁) in the loss, and can be combined with equivariant imaging losses to handle rank-deficient operators.

## Results

GR2R achieves near-supervised performance across all tested noise distributions. On Gaussian noise (fastMRI, σ=0.05), GR2R reaches 35.38 dB PSNR / 0.933 SSIM versus 35.41 / 0.935 for supervised MSE. On Poisson noise (DIV2K, γ=0.01), GR2R-MSE achieves 33.92 dB / 0.935 SSIM versus 33.96 / 0.933 supervised. On Gamma noise (SAR, ℓ=30), GR2R-MSE reaches 31.58 dB / 0.901 SSIM versus 31.86 / 0.906 supervised. On log-Rayleigh noise (DIV2K), GR2R achieves 29.47 dB PSNR, a 4.15 dB improvement over the original R2R (25.32 dB) and only 0.46 dB below supervised (29.93 dB).

## Baselines & Comparisons

Against the original R2R [22], GR2R with third-moment matching achieves a 4.15 dB PSNR gain on log-Rayleigh noise (29.47 vs. 25.32 dB), closing the gap to supervised learning from 4.61 dB to just 0.46 dB. On Poisson noise, GR2R-NLL and GR2R-MSE consistently outperform PURE [17] by 1.2–5.7 dB across all noise levels (e.g., 33.92 vs. 32.69 dB at γ=0.01; 23.69 vs. 17.94 dB at γ=1.0), and surpass Neigh2Neigh [9] by 0.5–0.6 dB while requiring only a single forward pass per step. On Gamma noise, GR2R-MSE beats Neigh2Neigh by 0.8–1.0 dB across all look numbers (e.g., 31.58 vs. 30.34 dB at ℓ=30). On Gaussian noise, GR2R outperforms Noise2Score [13] by 0.96–2.87 dB, SURE [24] by 0.07–0.29 dB, and Neigh2Neigh [9] by 0.27–0.46 dB, while remaining within 0.12–0.29 dB of the supervised baseline. For the inpainting extension, GR2R+EI [4] simultaneously addresses denoising and inpainting with Poisson noise, whereas the EI framework with the standard measurement-consistency loss (L_MC + L_EI) fills missing pixels but retains noise artifacts.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper directly extends Recorrupted-to-Recorrupted (R2R) [22], which demonstrated self-supervised denoising via synthetic recorruption for Gaussian noise, to the full natural exponential family (Poisson, Gamma, Binomial) and non-Gaussian additive noise (log-Rayleigh). A key theoretical contribution is showing that SURE [27] emerges as the α→0 limit of the GR2R MSE loss, unifying two previously separate self-supervised frameworks without requiring divergence computation. The work also subsumes two recent R2R-to-Poisson extensions [16, 21] within a broader NEF formulation, and contrasts with GSURE [8], which targets a different estimator (η(x) vs. x).

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

GR2R provides a practical, architecture-agnostic recipe for self-supervised denoising under Poisson (photon-limited imaging), Gamma (SAR speckle), and other NEF noise without clean reference data, using only a single forward pass and a single-term loss. The NLL loss variant addresses a practical gap where MSE is suboptimal for discrete or skewed noise (e.g., low-count Poisson). Open questions include how the framework handles mixed or unknown noise families, the sensitivity to the α hyperparameter in very low-SNR regimes, and whether the unbiased-estimator guarantee degrades with finite Monte Carlo samples (J) at test time. The extension to general inverse problems with rank-deficient operators still requires auxiliary invariance assumptions, limiting its generality.
