## Problem

Monocular full-body 3D human reconstruction remains ill-posed due to complex self-occlusions and limited observed geometry. Existing approaches that predict only a back view or rely on implicit functions struggle with high-fidelity face details, anatomically inconsistent novel views, and fine geometric features like fabric wrinkles. Directly applying multiview diffusion to full-body human images further exacerbates face distortion because the face occupies a small, low-resolution region after VAE encoding.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/CVPR20250121/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method takes a single in-the-wild RGB image of a clothed person as input and produces a textured 3D triangle mesh in approximately one minute. As intermediate outputs, it generates six multiview color images and corresponding normal maps (front, front-left, left, back, right, front-right) that supervise the explicit carving stage. The final mesh is initialized from an estimated SMPL-X template and refined via differentiable remeshing, with optional hand replacement from SMPL-X for improved visual quality.

## Method

PSHuman fine-tunes a pre-trained latent diffusion model (SD2.1-unclip) with two key architectural additions: (1) a body-face cross-scale diffusion that jointly denoises global full-body and local high-resolution face latents, using a noise blending layer that injects face hidden states into the front-view body branch at each self-attention block to preserve identity and cross-view consistency; and (2) SMPL-X conditioning that concatenates VAE-encoded renderings of the estimated SMPL-X with the input image to constrain novel-view generation to anatomically plausible poses. For reconstruction, the method performs SMPLX-initialized explicit human carving: it first aligns the SMPL-X template to generated multiview normals and silhouettes, then iteratively optimizes vertex positions and face topology via differentiable rasterization (700 steps) supervised by multiview normal and silhouette losses, followed by Poisson reconstruction for minor hole filling and per-vertex color fusion for texture.

## Results

On THuman2.1 with SMPL-X body prior, PSHuman achieves a Chamfer Distance of 0.44 cm and P2S of 0.41 cm, outperforming the best baseline (SiFU: 0.58 cm CD, 0.56 cm P2S) by ~24%. On CAPE-NFP, PSHuman reduces Chamfer Distance to 0.97 cm versus 1.55 cm for ICON (37% improvement), and on CAPE-FP achieves 0.78 cm CD versus 1.00 cm for ICON. For appearance on THuman2.1, PSHuman attains PSNR 20.85 dB, SSIM 0.864, and LPIPS 0.076, surpassing the best baseline (GTA: 19.61 dB, 0.834, 0.099). The full pipeline completes in approximately one minute per subject.

## Baselines & Comparisons

PSHuman outperforms all nine baselines across every geometry metric (CD, P2S, NC) on all three test splits in both the w/ and w/o SMPL-X settings. With SMPL-X prior, the largest margins appear on CAPE-NFP where PSHuman's 0.97 cm CD beats ICON (1.55 cm) and SiFU (1.57 cm) by 37–39%, and on THuman2.1 where 0.44 cm CD surpasses SiFU (0.58 cm) and GTA (0.58 cm) by ~24%. Without SMPL-X prior, PSHuman still leads with 2.16 cm CD on CAPE-NFP versus the next-best HiLo (2.65 cm) and SiTH (2.87 cm), demonstrating template-free robustness. For appearance, PSHuman achieves the best PSNR (20.85 dB), SSIM (0.864), and LPIPS (0.076) on THuman2.1, exceeding GTA (19.61 dB) and SiFU (19.44 dB) by 1.2–1.4 dB in PSNR. On face-specific metrics, PSHuman's 0.423 cm CD and 0.924 NC surpass SiFU (0.535 cm, 0.853 NC) and SiTH (0.610 cm, 0.858 NC), validating the cross-scale diffusion design. ECON and PIFu/PIFuHD/PaMIR are only competitive in the w/o SMPL-X setting and are consistently outperformed by PSHuman by 15–50% in Chamfer Distance.

## Relation to Prior Work

PSHuman departs from the dominant implicit-function paradigm (PIFu, ICON, ECON, SiFU, SiTH) by replacing MLP-based surface regression with SMPLX-initialized explicit mesh carving via differentiable rasterization. It extends the multiview diffusion line (SyncDreamer, Wonder3D, Era3D) to the human domain by introducing a body-face cross-scale diffusion and SMPL-X conditioning to address self-occlusion and face distortion—two failure modes that prior front/back-view methods (SiFU, SiTH) could not resolve. The explicit remeshing strategy also contrasts with ECON's bilateral normal integration (BiNI) and Chupa's optimization-based carving, positioning PSHuman as a direct, non-iterative alternative to SDS-based methods like TeCH that require hours of per-sample optimization.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

PSHuman's one-minute inference time (vs. ~6 hours for TeCH) makes high-fidelity single-image human reconstruction practically deployable in interactive pipelines (gaming, AR/VR, fashion), and its template-free training option broadens applicability beyond SMPL-X-fitting scenarios. However, the method inherits cascading sensitivity to pose estimation errors and still struggles with subtle details like loose hair and hands, leaving open the question of whether diffusion-based generation can reliably handle arbitrary fine-grained topology. The explicit carving module's reliance on 700 optimization steps per sample also suggests room for further acceleration if the field moves toward real-time applications.
