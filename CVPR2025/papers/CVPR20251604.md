## Problem

Existing image restoration methods (deblurring, super-resolution) process each degraded image independently, ignoring the complementary scene information available in multi-view captures. This independence leads to view-inconsistent outputs that degrade downstream 3D tasks such as reconstruction, pose estimation, and feature matching, where geometric consistency across views is essential.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/CVPR20251604/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The model takes a sparse set of N degraded images (typically 4) of the same scene at 640×480 resolution, where degradation is either motion blur (kernel size ~85px, intensity [0,1]) or 4× downsample-then-upsample low-resolution. Degraded views are encoded with the same frozen SD2.1 VAE encoder as the target latents and injected via channel-wise concatenation. The output is a restored, 3D-consistent image set, and the architecture is scalable to a variable number of views at inference despite being trained on fixed-size sets.

## Method

SIR-Diff extends the SD2.1 latent diffusion UNet with three key modifications for joint multi-view denoising: (1) a hybrid Spatial-3D ResNet where 2D convolution layers are initialized from SD2.1 weights and 3D convolution layers from Stable Video Diffusion weights, with outputs blended via a sigmoid-gated mixture; (2) a 3D self-attention Transformer that patches latents from all N views into N×p tokens and performs full cross-view self-attention, enabling each token to access information from every other view; and (3) removal of all cross-attention modules and restriction of 3D self-attention to low-resolution UNet layers for efficiency. Training uses the standard diffusion noise-prediction loss with frozen SD2.1 VAE encoder/decoder, and the model is trained on synthetic datasets (Hypersim, TartanAir) with random view ordering for permutation invariance.

## Results

On sparse-view motion deblurring, SIR-Diff achieves FID of 40.09 (ScanNet++), 67.31 (ETH3D), and 85.67 (CO3D), outperforming the best single-image baseline Restormer (49.72/73.47/144.8) and the video method VRT (134.5/160.4/170.4) across all three datasets. On 4× sparse-view super-resolution, SIR-Diff reaches PSNR of 27.38 dB (ScanNet++), 22.58 dB (ETH3D), and 24.53 dB (CO3D), surpassing OSEDiff (25.22/21.93/24.50) and Upscale-A-Video (23.23/20.39/22.73). The proposed Visual Consistency (VConsis.) metric confirms SIR-Diff produces the most 3D-consistent outputs, scoring 5.75/9.43/9.30 on deblurring versus 6.52/9.59/11.00 for Restormer.

## Baselines & Comparisons

SIR-Diff outperforms all single-image deblurring baselines (Restormer, PromptIR) by a wide margin on FID and LPIPS, with the gap most pronounced on CO3D (FID 85.67 vs. 144.8 for Restormer). Against the video-based VRT, SIR-Diff wins decisively because VRT relies on continuous temporal motion estimation that breaks down under sparse, non-sequential views. In super-resolution, SIR-Diff beats OSEDiff by ~2.2 dB PSNR on ScanNet++ and reduces LPIPS from 0.154 to 0.099, while also outperforming the video method Upscale-A-Video by ~4 dB PSNR, attributed to not requiring optical flow conditioning. The single-frame ablation (SIR-Diff 1 Frame) confirms the multi-view attention contributes substantially: it scores FID 81.58 vs. 40.09 for the full model on ScanNet++ deblurring. For 3D reconstruction (Table 3), SIR-Diff + BAD-GS achieves PSNR 26.11/25.33 under medium/hard blur, far exceeding vanilla 3DGS (13.47/10.42) and BAD-GS alone (10.06/8.06). On depth estimation (Table 6), SIR-Diff matches or beats DepthAnythingV2 on AbsRel (9.011 vs. 9.270 on ScanNet++) and significantly outperforms all baselines on geometric consistency (GConsis. 0.129 vs. 0.162 for Marigold, 0.751 for DepthCrafter).

## Relation to Prior Work

SIR-Diff repurposes the multi-view diffusion architecture originally developed for 3D generation (MVDream, CAT3D, Zero123++) toward the restoration task, combining SD2.1's 2D UNet backbone with SVD's 3D convolutional layers and a 3D self-attention Transformer for cross-view token interaction. This departs from prior restoration work (Restormer, OSEDiff, VRT) that processes images independently or as continuous video, and from classical multi-image super-resolution that relies on known sub-pixel shifts. The key architectural novelty is the spatial-3D ResNet that blends 2D (SD2.1) and 3D (SVD) convolution outputs via a learned sigmoid gate, enabling the model to capture both spatial and cross-view geometric structure without training from scratch.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The paper demonstrates that jointly denoising sparse multi-view images produces 3D-consistent restorations that meaningfully improve downstream 3D reconstruction (Gaussian Splatting, novel-view synthesis) and feature matching, addressing a practical gap where single-view restoration introduces geometric inconsistencies that propagate into 3D pipelines. Limitations include training exclusively on synthetic data (Hypersim, TartanAir) with zero-shot evaluation, a fixed 4-view setting in most experiments, and the absence of explicit geometric constraints (e.g., epipolar geometry) in the attention mechanism. Open questions the authors identify include efficient cross-view attention along epipolar lines and formal guarantees of 3D consistency, both of which would be needed for deployment in real-time SLAM or large-scale scene reconstruction.
