## Problem

Existing 3D generative models face a fundamental quality-efficiency tradeoff: SDS-based methods are slow and produce inconsistent geometry, sparse-view reconstruction models are limited by parameter-inefficient triplane representations that cap spatial resolution, and native 3D diffusion models largely lack the ability to generate physically-based rendering (PBR) materials. No prior method efficiently produces high-resolution 3D assets with complete PBR properties (geometry, albedo, metallic, roughness) in a scalable, native generative framework.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/CVPR20250020/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The model accepts either a text prompt or a single RGB image as conditioning signal and outputs a complete GLB file containing a textured mesh with 1024×1024 albedo and metallic-roughness texture maps, ready for direct import into graphics engines. Geometry is extracted via Marching Cubes on the SDF zero-level set, and texture/material values are sampled in a high-resolution UV space with dilation and inpainting for anti-aliasing. The underlying representation uses N=2048 primitives at local resolution a=8 (≈1.05M parameters), enabling high-frequency detail without super-resolution or post-hoc mesh refinement.

## Method

The core contribution is PrimX, a primitive-based 3D representation that encodes SDF, RGB albedo, and metallic-roughness material into a compact N×D tensor, where each primitive is a tiny a³ voxel anchored on the mesh surface with a 3D position, global scale, and 6-channel spatially-varied payload. A 3D VAE (Primitive Patch Compression) independently compresses each primitive's payload at rate f=48 into latent tokens, deferring global semantic modeling to the diffusion stage. The generative model is a 28-layer Diffusion Transformer (DiT) that treats each latent primitive as a token, using cross-attention for condition injection, self-attention for inter-primitive correlation, and adaptive layer normalization for timestep modulation, trained with v-prediction and Classifier-Free Guidance. The tensorial and permutation-equivariant nature of PrimX eliminates the need for positional encodings and enables direct scaling of the Transformer to high-resolution 3D generation without architectural modifications.

## Results

On the representation fitting benchmark (Table 1, 1.05M parameter budget), PrimX achieves the lowest Chamfer Distance (1.310×10⁻⁴) and highest PSNR across all channels (SDF: 41.74, RGB: 21.86, Mat: 16.50), while converging in 1.5 min—nearly 7× faster than the next-best method (Dense Voxels at 10 min). On text-to-3D generation (Table 4), 3DTopia-XL achieves a CLIP Score of 24.33, outperforming Shap-E (21.98) and 3DTopia (22.54). Qualitative image-to-3D comparisons (Figure 5) show 3DTopia-XL producing the best visual and geometric quality with spatially varied PBR materials, including specular highlights and glossiness under environment-map rendering.

## Baselines & Comparisons

Against the Triplane representation [Chan et al., CVPR 2022], PrimX reduces Chamfer Distance by 7.3× (1.310 vs 9.678) and improves PSNR-RGB by 3.6 dB (21.86 vs 18.28) while fitting 10.7× faster (1.5 min vs 16 min). On text-to-3D, 3DTopia-XL outperforms Shap-E [Jun et al.] by 2.35 CLIP Score points (24.33 vs 21.98) and 3DTopia [Hong et al.] by 1.79 points (24.33 vs 22.54). In image-to-3D qualitative comparisons (Figure 5), 3DTopia-XL surpasses all sparse-view reconstruction baselines—LGM [Tang et al.], InstantMesh [Xu et al.], Real3D [Jiang et al.], and CRM [Wang et al.]—which suffer from multi-view inconsistency and bumpy surfaces due to triplane parameter inefficiency. Among diffusion-based methods, CraftsMan [Li et al.] achieves comparable surface quality but cannot generate textures or PBR materials, while LN3Diff [Lan et al.] produces objects less visually aligned with the input condition. 3DTopia-XL is the only method that simultaneously produces high-resolution geometry, spatially varied albedo, and metallic-roughness materials in a single GLB output.

## Relation to Prior Work

3DTopia-XL builds directly on the Diffusion Transformer (DiT) architecture [36] and the latent diffusion paradigm (VAE + diffusion), but departs from the dominant triplane-NeRF representation that underpins most prior 3D generative and reconstruction models. Its core novelty is the PrimX primitive-based representation, which extends the mosaic-voxel idea from M-SDF [58] from shape-only to a unified encoding of SDF, albedo, and PBR material in a compact N×D tensor. Unlike triplane or dense-voxel approaches, PrimX is inherently tensorial (each primitive is a token, no positional encoding needed), parameter-efficient at high resolution, and differentially renderable, positioning it as a bridge between implicit-field methods and mesh-based pipelines.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The paper addresses a practical gap: most 3D generative models produce shape or vertex-colored geometry that degrades significantly when exported to production GLB files, while 3DTopia-XL outputs full PBR assets (geometry + albedo + metallic-roughness) ready for graphics engines. The 7× speedup in representation fitting (1.5 min vs. 10+ min) is critical for scaling to large training datasets. Open questions include whether the fixed N=2048 primitive count generalizes to highly complex or multi-object scenes, whether the 28-layer DiT can be further scaled, and how the method handles open-world or non-rigid content beyond the single-object setting evaluated here.
