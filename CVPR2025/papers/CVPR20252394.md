## Problem

Mamba-based image restoration methods rely on 1D unidirectional scanning of the SSM, which distorts local pixel relationships and fails to establish direct multi-directional spatial connections; multi-directional scanning variants (MambaIR, VMambaIR) mitigate but do not resolve this, and the recursive computation of the forget matrix further limits efficiency. The paper exploits the mathematical congruence between linear attention and SSM to replace the SSM core with linear attention, enabling true global multi-directional modeling at linear complexity.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/CVPR20252394/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The model takes a degraded RGB image (256×256 training patches, 3 channels) and produces a restored RGB image of the same spatial dimensions. Internally, the encoder-decoder operates at three resolution scales (H×W, H/2×W/2, H/4×W/4) with 32 base channels, and the decoder outputs intermediate restorations at each scale before D1 yields the final full-resolution result. Loss is computed jointly in spatial (L1) and frequency (FFT L1) domains across all three scales.

## Method

ACL's core module, LAMA, embeds linear attention inside Mamba's structural skeleton: a main branch applies a linear projection, a 1×1 convolution with SiLU, then linear attention (Q·(KᵀV) with a kernel function φ and learnable position embedding), while a residual branch (Linear + SiLU) gates the output via element-wise multiplication before a final linear layer and FFN. This replaces the recursive SSM scan, eliminating the need for sequential forget-matrix computation and enabling simultaneous global multi-directional pixel dependencies at O(N) complexity. A complementary MDC module (5×5 conv d=2 and 3×3 conv d=2, concatenated and 1×1-projected with a skip connection) is appended after the first two encoder/decoder units to recover local detail at multiple granularities. The overall architecture is a 3-scale U-Net where each encoder/decoder stage stacks N_i LAMA blocks (N=(6,3,3) chosen by ablation), trained with Adam, cosine annealing (lr 1e-3→1e-6), batch size 8, for 800 epochs.

## Results

On deraining, ACL achieves 39.18 PSNR / 0.983 SSIM on Rain100L and 32.22 PSNR / 0.920 SSIM on Rain100H, outperforming all compared methods including Restormer (38.99/0.978 and 31.46/0.904) and MambaIR (38.78/0.977 and 30.62/0.893). Across the broader deraining evaluation (Rain200L, Rain200H, DID-Data), ACL attains an average of 35.33 PSNR / 0.947 SSIM, surpassing the next-best Uformer (34.96/0.943). On GoPro deblurring, ACL reaches 33.25 PSNR / 0.964 SSIM with only 55G FLOPs and 4.6M parameters, the lowest computational cost among all compared methods, though slightly below LoFormer (33.73/0.966) in peak quality.

## Baselines & Comparisons

ACL outperforms all 13 compared deraining methods on the average PSNR/SSIM across Rain200L, Rain200H, and DID-Data (35.33/0.947 vs. Uformer's 34.96/0.943 and Restormer's 34.87/0.942). On Rain200L, ACL leads by 0.16 dB over Restormer (40.74 vs. 40.58) and by 0.54 dB over Uformer (40.74 vs. 40.20); on DID-Data it surpasses the next-best ESDNet by 0.29 dB (34.81 vs. 34.52). However, on Rain200H, Restormer retains a 0.38 dB edge (30.83 vs. 30.45), the only dataset where ACL does not achieve the top PSNR. For GoPro deblurring, ACL (33.25 PSNR) trails LoFormer (33.73) and SSAMAN (33.53) by 0.48 and 0.28 dB respectively, but uses only 55G FLOPs and 4.6M parameters versus LoFormer's 47G/16.4M and Restormer's 140G/26.1M, making it the most parameter-efficient method in the comparison. Against the Mamba-based MambaIR (38.78 PSNR on Rain100L), ACL gains 0.40 dB, confirming that replacing SSM with linear attention within the Mamba structure improves restoration quality.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper builds directly on Mamba's selective state-space model architecture, but departs from the multi-directional scanning strategy adopted by MambaIR and VMambaIR. Instead of augmenting SSM with additional scanning directions, the authors exploit the mathematical equivalence between linear attention and SSM (formalized in Han et al. [15]) to replace the SSM core with a linear attention layer, yielding the LAMA module that naturally captures global multi-directional pixel dependencies without recursive computation. The overall encoder-decoder skeleton follows the standard IR paradigm (cf. Restormer, MPRNet), and the local enhancement via multi-scale dilated convolutions (MDC) is a lightweight alternative to window-based attention strategies used in IPT and SwinIR.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

ACL offers a practical efficiency win for IR: 4.6M parameters and 55G FLOPs on GoPro deblurring, substantially below Transformer-based competitors (Restormer: 26.1M, 140G), while matching or exceeding their quality on deraining. However, the paper itself acknowledges that ACL does not outperform CNN-based methods on large images and still trails SOTA Transformers on deblurring (33.25 vs. LoFormer's 33.73 PSNR), limiting its immediate impact. An open question is whether the LA-in-Mamba formulation generalizes to more complex degradations (denoising, super-resolution) or whether the modest MDC contribution (≈0.3 dB) suggests the local module is a stopgap rather than a principled design choice.
