## Problem

Directly applying binary CNN techniques to Vision Transformers causes severe performance degradation due to two structural incompatibilities: (1) the attention module's back-propagation is corrupted by multiple clip functions and the non-differentiable sign operator, causing vanishing gradients, and (2) binary attention matrices cannot represent graded token similarities, introducing noise that reduces the signal-to-noise ratio. No existing binarization strategy adequately addresses these ViT-specific challenges.

## Input / Output

The model consumes standard RGB images (224×224 for ImageNet-1K, 32×32 for CIFAR-10) and aerial bird's-eye-view images for road segmentation. Input is split into a token sequence via a 4×4 strided convolution patch embedding, producing a 64-channel feature map at quarter resolution. For classification, the output is a single class label; for segmentation tasks, a U-Net decoder attached to the BHViT encoder produces pixel-wise masks (binary road labels or 150-class ADE20K semantic labels).

## Method

BHViT is a 4-stage hierarchical hybrid ViT that replaces the token mixer with multi-scale grouped dilated convolutions (MSGDC) in early stages and a window-based multi-scale multi-head attention module (MSMHA) in later stages, reducing redundant token interactions that degrade binary attention. For the attention matrix, a Quantization Decomposition (QD) scheme decomposes the full-precision softmax output into s binary matrices via threshold-based logical operations, preserving graded token importance without full-precision storage. The binary MLP is augmented with learnable shift operations (horizontal, vertical, and mixed) that add spatial mixing at negligible cost. Training uses knowledge distillation from DeiT-Small plus an L1 regularization loss activated in the final 10% of epochs to counteract weight oscillation caused by the Adam optimizer's interaction with binary sign operations.

## Results

On ImageNet-1K, BHViT-Small† achieves 70.1% top-1 accuracy with only 3.5 MB model size and 0.815×10⁸ OPs, surpassing the best binary CNN (ReCU† on ResNet-18, 65.5%) by 4.6% and the best binary ViT (BiViT on Swin-Tiny, 63.8%) by 6.3%. On CIFAR-10, BHViT-Small reaches 95.0% top-1 accuracy, exceeding the strongest BNN baseline ReCU (92.8%) by 2.2% and the best binary ViT GSB (91.2%) by 3.8%. For semantic segmentation on ADE20K, BHViT achieves 14.87% mIoU, outperforming the best binary baseline BiSRNet (9.74%) by over 5 points. On the RS-LVF road segmentation task, BHViT attains 85.1% mIoU, surpassing even the full-precision ResNet-34 U-Net (77.8%).

## Baselines & Comparisons

BHViT-Small† (70.1%) outperforms all binary ViT baselines on ImageNet-1K: it beats BiViT on Swin-Tiny (63.8%) by 6.3%, BinaryViT (67.7%) by 2.4%, Si-BiViT (55.7%) by 14.4%, and ReActNet on DeiT-Small (49.5%) by 20.6%. Against the strongest binary CNN baselines, BHViT-Small† exceeds ReCU† on ResNet-18 (65.5%) by 4.6% and ReActNet-A* on MobileNet (70.1%) by matching performance with smaller model size and lower OPs. On CIFAR-10, BHViT-Tiny (93.3%) surpasses the best BNN ReCU (92.8%) by 0.5% and BHViT-Small (95.0%) beats GSB on DeiT-Small (91.2%) by 3.8%. For segmentation, BHViT dominates all binary baselines on both ADE20K (14.87% vs. 9.74% mIoU for BiSRNet) and RS-LVF (85.1% vs. 63.6% mIoU for ReActNet), and even exceeds the full-precision ResNet-34 U-Net on RS-LVF (85.1% vs. 77.8% mIoU). The ablation in Table 7 confirms the hybrid token-mixer design (70.1%) outperforms both pure ViT (MSMHA only, 68.8%) and pure CNN (MSGDC only, 67.2%) configurations.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the binary neural network lineage from CNNs (XNOR-Net, Bi-Real Net, ReActNet) to Vision Transformers, but deliberately departs from prior binary ViT work (Bi-ViT, BiViT, GSB, BVT-IMA) by proposing a hybrid CNN+ViT architecture rather than binarizing a pure transformer. It builds on Bi-Real Net's residual connection strategy and piecewise-polynomial backward pass, but reframes the residual's purpose from enhancing representational capacity to alleviating gradient mismatch in attention modules. The Quantization Decomposition for attention binarization and the shift-operation MLP module are novel contributions not present in prior binary ViT or binary CNN literature.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

As LLMs and VLMs increasingly adopt transformer architectures, efficient binary deployment on edge devices becomes practically critical, and this paper demonstrates that a hybrid architecture can close the gap between binary and full-precision ViTs more effectively than pure transformer binarization. The finding that excessive tokens degrade binary ViT performance (motivating MSGDC in early stages) and that Adam amplifies weight oscillation in binary layers are actionable insights for future compression work. Limitations include the reliance on full-precision downsampling layers for best results and the use of knowledge distillation from DeiT-Small, which may not transfer to settings without a strong teacher. Open questions include whether the hybrid architecture principle generalizes to larger-scale models and whether QD can be extended to multi-bit attention without combinatorial cost growth.
