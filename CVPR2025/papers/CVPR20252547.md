## Problem

Existing multi-view generative novel view synthesis methods depend on external alignment processes—explicit pose estimation or pre-reconstruction via dense stereo models—before generation. These alignment steps become unreliable when conditional views have small overlaps or occlusions, causing performance to degrade as more views are added, which contradicts the expectation that additional views should improve synthesis quality.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/CVPR20252547/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The model accepts M unposed RGB conditional images (1–4 views) and N target camera poses specified as 3×4 camera-to-world matrices, with a fixed sequence length T = N + M = 16. Images are encoded to 4-channel VAE latents at H/8 × W/8 resolution, and target poses are represented as 6-channel Plücker ray embeddings plus a learned MLP embedding of the extrinsic sequence. The model outputs N novel view images at the target poses and M predicted Plücker ray embeddings for the conditional images, enabling implicit pose recovery. Training resolutions are 512×512 (all data) and 576×1024 (RealEstate10K and DL3DV only).

## Method

NVComposer introduces an image-pose dual-stream diffusion model initialized from DynamiCrafter that jointly denoises novel view latents and condition camera pose Plücker embeddings in a single generative pass, eliminating the need for any external alignment at inference. A dedicated pose decoding head (reduced to 1/10 the channel width of the image decoder, with attention layers removed) handles the low-frequency pose modality separately to avoid interference with the complex image latent denoising. A geometry-aware feature alignment adapter distills 3D geometric priors from DUSt3R point maps during training by aligning internal U-Net features (after spatial-temporal self-attention layers) with DUSt3R point maps via MSE loss, requiring no explicit reconstruction at test time. The model is trained on ~200K samples from Objaverse, RealEstate10K, CO3D, and DL3DV using 1–4 randomly sampled unposed condition views, with a combined diffusion loss and alignment loss.

## Results

On RealEstate10K with 4 unposed input views, NVComposer achieves PSNR of 22.55 (easy), 19.53 (medium), and 17.82 (hard), substantially outperforming the best baseline ViewCrafter (18.48, 14.74, 15.19 respectively). On DL3DV with 4 views, NVComposer reaches PSNR 17.92 and LPIPS 0.2277, compared to ViewCrafter's 15.06 and 0.4549. On the Objaverse object benchmark, NVComposer with 4 views achieves PSNR 17.72 and LPIPS 0.1889, surpassing SV3D's single-view PSNR of 13.89. In distribution-level evaluation, NVComposer achieves the best FVD (425.44) and KVD (8.04) while matching ViewCrafter on FID (46.19 vs. 46.08).

## Baselines & Comparisons

NVComposer consistently outperforms all five baselines across every difficulty level and dataset. Against ViewCrafter (the strongest generative NVS baseline), NVComposer gains +4.07 PSNR on easy scenes and +2.63 PSNR on hard scenes with 4 views, while reducing LPIPS by roughly 50% (e.g., 0.1188 vs. 0.3068 on easy). On DL3DV, the gap widens further: NVComposer achieves 17.92 PSNR vs. ViewCrafter's 15.06, and LPIPS drops from 0.4549 to 0.2277. Against reconstructive DUSt3R, NVComposer leads by +3.58 PSNR on hard RealEstate10K scenes (17.82 vs. 14.24) and +3.05 PSNR on DL3DV (17.92 vs. 14.87). On the Objaverse object benchmark, NVComposer with 4 views (PSNR 17.72) surpasses SV3D's single-view result (PSNR 13.89) by nearly 4 dB. Notably, NVComposer is the only method whose performance monotonically improves as more unposed views are added, whereas ViewCrafter degrades on hard scenes when going from 1 to 2 views due to unstable external alignment.

## Relation to Prior Work

NVComposer builds on the video diffusion backbone of DynamiCrafter and the geometric priors of DUSt3R, but departs fundamentally from prior multi-view generative NVS methods (ViewCrafter, CAT3D, iFusion) that require external multi-view alignment—either explicit pose estimation or pre-reconstruction—before generation. The key architectural departure is the image-pose dual-stream diffusion design, which jointly denoises image latents and Plücker ray pose embeddings, treating pose estimation as a generative sub-task rather than a preprocessing step. The geometry-aware feature alignment adapter further distinguishes this work by distilling 3D knowledge from DUSt3R's point maps into the diffusion model's internal features during training, eliminating the need for explicit reconstruction at inference.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

By removing the external alignment pipeline, NVComposer makes multi-view generative NVS substantially more accessible and robust, particularly in sparse-view or low-overlap scenarios where pose estimation and stereo matching are unreliable. The finding that performance consistently improves with more unposed views—while alignment-dependent methods degrade—suggests that the bottleneck in prior multi-view NVS was the alignment step, not the generative model itself. Open questions include whether the implicit pose estimation generalizes to very large view counts or highly dynamic scenes, whether the DUSt3R dependency during training can be replaced with lighter geometric supervision, and whether the approach extends to video-length sequences or interactive real-time settings.
