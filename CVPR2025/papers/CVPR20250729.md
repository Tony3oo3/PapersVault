## Problem

Existing generalizable novel view synthesis methods rely on intermediate 3D representations (NeRFs, 3D Gaussians, voxel grids) to enforce multi-view consistency, limiting scalability and generalization especially with many input views. The key challenge is how to impose multi-view geometric consistency when generating novel views and depth maps directly as a conditional generative task, without any explicit 3D scene model.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/CVPR20250729/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method takes an arbitrary number N of posed RGB images (2 to thousands) with known camera intrinsics and extrinsics, resized to a longest side of 256 pixels, and produces a novel-view RGB image and a scale-consistent depth map from a target camera. Depth is parameterized in log-scale over a non-metric range [0.1, 200] after scene scale normalization, and predictions are refined via test-time ensembling over 5 diffusion samples. The model can process 100+ conditioning views on a single GPU, with an incremental conditioning strategy for long sequences.

## Method

MVGD uses a Recurrent Interface Network (RIN) backbone to perform pixel-level diffusion without auto-encoders, where a fixed set of 256–2048 latent tokens cross-attend with input tokens, keeping computation independent of resolution and view count. Conditioning is provided via raymap embeddings (Fourier-encoded camera origins and viewing directions) concatenated with EfficientViT image features, while learnable task embeddings steer the same latent tokens toward RGB or depth generation. A Scene Scale Normalization (SSN) procedure divides all camera translations and ground-truth depth by the maximum absolute translation, enabling training across metric and non-metric datasets while producing scale-consistent depth at inference. The model is trained on over 60M multi-view samples from 19 diverse datasets using DDIM (1000 training / 10 evaluation steps), and a novel incremental fine-tuning strategy duplicates latent tokens to scale model capacity from 256 to 2048 with only 50k additional steps per stage, cutting training time by up to 70%.

## Results

On the 2-view novel view synthesis benchmark, MVGD achieves 28.41 PSNR / 0.891 SSIM / 0.107 LPIPS on RealEstate10K and 29.98 PSNR / 0.875 SSIM / 0.131 LPIPS on ACID, outperforming the previous best (MVSplat: 26.39/28.25 PSNR) by over 2 dB. Extending to 3–9 conditioning views, MVGD reaches 32.89 PSNR on RE10K (9-view) and 26.88 PSNR on DTU (9-view), surpassing CAT3D by 0.69 and 0.96 dB respectively. For stereo depth estimation, MVGD achieves 0.065 Abs.Rel on ScanNet (vs. 0.086 for EPIO), and for video depth it reaches 0.041 Abs.Rel (vs. 0.047 for NeuralRecon), establishing new state-of-the-art on both tasks.

## Baselines & Comparisons

MVGD outperforms all 23 baselines across every evaluated benchmark and task. In 2-view novel view synthesis, it surpasses the strongest 3DGS-based methods (MVSplat, PixelSplat) by ~2 dB PSNR and the NeRF-based MuRF by ~2.3 dB, without requiring any intermediate 3D representation or explicit geometric alignment. In the 3–9 view setting, MVGD beats the diffusion-assisted NeRF pipelines CAT3D and ReconFusion by 0.6–2.0 dB PSNR across RE10K, LLFF, DTU, CO3D, and MIP-NeRF360, despite being trained with only 2–5 conditioning views. For stereo depth, MVGD improves over the previous best (EPIO, 0.086 Abs.Rel on ScanNet) by 24% relative, and for video depth it outperforms NeuralRecon (0.047) by 13% relative. Notably, methods relying on explicit 3D representations (PixelSplat, MVSplat, CAT3D, ReconFusion) degrade or fail to scale beyond 2 views due to geometric alignment requirements, whereas MVGD generalizes seamlessly to 100+ conditioning views via its incremental multi-view generation strategy.

## Relation to Prior Work

MVGD builds on Recurrent Interface Networks (RIN) for efficient pixel-level diffusion and on raymap-based camera conditioning from prior multi-view depth work (DeFiNe, EPIO), but departs fundamentally from the dominant paradigm of constructing an intermediate 3D representation (NeRF, 3D Gaussians) before rendering novel views. Where prior diffusion-based NVS methods (ZeroNVS, ReconFusion, CAT3D) use diffusion as a 2D prior to generate conditioning images for a separate 3D model, MVGD performs joint RGB and depth generation directly from an implicit latent space, with multi-view consistency emerging from the training objective rather than from explicit geometric constraints. The scene scale normalization and learnable task embeddings are novel technical contributions that enable unified training across 19 heterogeneous datasets spanning metric and non-metric scales.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

By demonstrating that scale-consistent, multi-view consistent novel view and depth synthesis is achievable without any intermediate 3D representation, MVGD challenges the field's working assumption that explicit geometric structure is necessary for consistency. The incremental fine-tuning strategy (duplicating latent tokens to scale model capacity with only 50k additional steps) offers a practical recipe for scaling diffusion-based generative models that could transfer beyond this specific task. Key limitations include the requirement for posed inputs, the absence of dynamic scene modeling, and dependence on camera extrinsics for scale normalization; open questions include whether the approach extends to unposed or single-view settings and whether the RIN bottleneck can be further relaxed without the quadratic cost of full self-attention.
