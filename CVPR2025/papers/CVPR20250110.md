## Problem

Existing 3D-awareness probes for visual foundation models rely on 2.5D tasks (depth, normal) or sparse 2D matching, which ignore texture awareness and require 3D ground-truth, limiting evaluation to small, curated datasets. There is no unified framework that simultaneously probes both geometry and texture awareness of VFMs in a dense, pixel-wise manner without 3D labels.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/CVPR20250110/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method takes 2–7 sparse, uncalibrated, casually captured RGB images as input. Frozen features from pre-trained VFMs are extracted per-pixel, unified to 256 channels via PCA, and spatially aligned via bilinear upsampling. A 2-layer MLP (256 units, ReLU) reads out per-pixel 3D Gaussian parameters (position, opacity, covariance, and 3rd-order SH coefficients). Camera poses are initialized by DUSt3R and jointly refined. The output is a set of 3D Gaussians rendered into novel views, evaluated by PSNR, SSIM, and LPIPS on held-out viewpoints.

## Method

Feat2GS decodes 3D Gaussian attributes from VFM features through a deliberately minimal 2-layer MLP readout, trained with multi-view photometric loss against the input images. The key design choice is the disentanglement of 3DGS parameters into geometry (x, α, Σ) and texture (c), enabling three probing modes: Geometry (read out geometry, free-optimize color), Texture (read out color, free-optimize geometry), and All (read out everything). A warm-start stage initializes the readout layer toward DUSt3R's point cloud to avoid local minima from sparse views. Unlike InstantSplat, which freely optimizes all Gaussian parameters and overfits on sparse views, Feat2GS constrains the representation through the shallow feature-to-Gaussian mapping, preventing overfitting while preserving VFM-derived structure. The paper further shows that concatenating features from multiple VFMs (e.g., RADIO + MAE + IUVRGB) and applying PCA yields a strong NVS baseline that outperforms InstantSplat across all metrics.

## Results

Across seven diverse multi-view datasets (LLFF, DTU, DL3DV, Casual, MipNeRF360, MVimgNet, T&T), RADIO achieves the best Geometry-mode NVS quality, while MAE leads in Texture mode (T-LPIPS), and MASt3R/DUSt3R perform best in the All mode. In the application setting (Table 6), Feat2GS with fine-tuned DUSt3R* features achieves 19.75 PSNR, 0.6561 SSIM, and 0.2928 LPIPS on casual sparse images, outperforming InstantSplat (18.87 PSNR, 0.6044 SSIM, 0.3039 LPIPS) on all three metrics. The paper also validates that 2D NVS metrics strongly correlate with 3D point-cloud accuracy on DTU (Table 4), supporting NVS as a proxy for 3D assessment without requiring 3D ground truth.

## Baselines & Comparisons

The paper's primary baseline comparison is against InstantSplat [23], the current SOTA for sparse-view pose-free Gaussian splatting. Feat2GS with RADIO features improves PSNR by +0.86 dB (19.73 vs. 18.87) and SSIM by +0.047 (0.6513 vs. 0.6044), but slightly worsens LPIPS (0.3143 vs. 0.3039). Concatenating all VFM features further boosts PSNR to 19.80 and SSIM to 0.6545, though LPIPS remains above InstantSplat. The strongest variant, Feat2GS with fine-tuned DUSt3R* features, achieves the best score on all three metrics (19.75 PSNR, 0.6561 SSIM, 0.2928 LPIPS), beating InstantSplat by +0.88 dB PSNR, +0.052 SSIM, and −0.011 LPIPS. The paper attributes InstantSplat's inferior geometry to overfitting high-frequency details when freely optimizing millions of 3D Gaussians for sparse viewpoints, whereas Feat2GS's lightweight 2-layer MLP readout from VFM features acts as an implicit regularizer that prevents this overfitting.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the 3D-awareness probing line initiated by Probe3D [22] and R2D3 [75], which relied on 2.5D depth/normal estimation or sparse two-view matching, by replacing these with dense novel-view synthesis as the evaluation proxy and disentangling geometry from texture awareness via 3DGS parameter groups. It builds on 3D Gaussian Splatting [41] as the output representation and DUSt3R [95] for unconstrained camera initialization, but departs from InstantSplat [23] by regressing 3DGS parameters through a lightweight readout MLP from frozen VFM features rather than freely optimizing all parameters, which the authors argue prevents overfitting on sparse views. The PCA-based channel unification and consistent 2-layer MLP architecture across all VFMs is a methodological contribution that enables fair cross-model comparison absent in prior probing work.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The paper provides the first unified, 3D-GT-free framework for simultaneously probing both geometry and texture awareness of VFMs at scale, revealing that current VFMs are systematically weak on texture (often worse than trivial IUVRGB encoding) and that pointmap regression (DUSt3R/MASt3R) yields better cross-view consistency than depth regression (MiDaS) despite identical architecture. The finding that simple feature concatenation outperforms multi-teacher distillation (RADIO) challenges the assumption that distillation is necessary for combining VFM knowledge, suggesting ensembling strategies may be underexplored. Open questions include whether texture-aware pre-training objectives can be integrated without sacrificing geometry, whether the NVS-as-proxy correlation holds for more complex dynamic scenes, and whether the lightweight readout constraint generalizes to higher-resolution or video inputs.
