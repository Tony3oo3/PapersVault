## Problem

High-fidelity text-to-3D generation is bottlenecked by the scarcity of large-scale, high-quality 3D caption datasets. Existing automated annotation pipelines rely on single-view VLMs or expensive proprietary models like GPT-4, producing inconsistent, low-detail captions that lack the geometric, textural, and domain-specific information needed for fine-grained 3D reconstruction, while also suffering from severe scalability and cost constraints.

## Input / Output

The annotation pipeline consumes 3D models rendered as four 512×512 multi-view images (front, back, left, right at 30° elevation) along with filtered human metadata from source datasets, and produces five hierarchical text descriptions per asset ranging from ~200-word comprehensive descriptions to ~10-word semantic tags. The downstream MARVEL-FX3D pipeline takes a free-form text prompt as input and outputs a textured 3D mesh in 15 seconds, with the intermediate image generated at SD 3.5 resolution before background removal via DIS and mesh reconstruction via SF3D.

## Method

MARVEL is a five-stage annotation pipeline: (1) Blender-based multi-view rendering at fixed azimuth angles, (2) Mistral-Nemo-Instruct-2407 filters noisy human metadata to inject domain-specific priors and reduce VLM hallucinations, (3) InternVL2-40B generates a single dense description from all four views simultaneously (avoiding separate view aggregation), (4) Qwen2.5-72B (8-bit quantized) applies hierarchical prompting to progressively compress the description into five levels of detail, and (5) Qwen2.5-14B performs ethical filtering. For text-to-3D generation, MARVEL-FX3D fine-tunes Stable Diffusion 3.5 with LoRA (rank 4) on the MARVEL-40M+ annotations to bridge the 2D-3D domain gap, then feeds the generated image through pretrained SF3D for single-view mesh reconstruction, achieving 15-second end-to-end generation.

## Results

On the text-to-3D generation task (Table 4), MARVEL-FX3D achieves the highest prompt fidelity (7.71/10) and overall preference (6.94/10) in human evaluation, while completing generation in just 15 seconds—orders of magnitude faster than DreamFusion (30 min), Lucid-Dreamer (45 min), and HiFA (>1 h). For annotation quality (Table 2), MARVEL-40M+ (Level 4) attains a 72.41% win rate by GPT-4 and 73.40% by human evaluators in image-text alignment, with an MTLD score of 47.43 (≈83% higher than Kabra, 19% higher than Cap3D). In caption accuracy (Table 3), MARVEL Level 1 achieves 84.70% (GPT-4) and 82.80% (human) correctness at 170 words average length, outperforming all baselines despite being 3–34× longer.

## Baselines & Comparisons

Against the four TT3D generation baselines, MARVEL-FX3D wins on prompt fidelity (7.71 vs. 6.62 for Lucid-Dreamer, 6.88 for HiFA, 4.22 for DreamFusion, 2.65 for Shap-E) and overall preference (6.94 vs. 6.59, 6.44, 4.09, 2.41 respectively), while matching Lucid-Dreamer on geometric consistency (7.20 vs. 7.25) and slightly exceeding it on visual quality (6.58 vs. 6.47). The speed advantage is dramatic: 15 s versus 45 min (Lucid-Dreamer), >1 h (HiFA), 30 min (DreamFusion), and 5 s (Shap-E, which is faster but far inferior in quality). For annotation quality, MARVEL-40M+ dominates all three captioning baselines: it achieves a 72.41% GPT-4 win rate versus 14.55% (Cap3D), 10.80% (3D-Topia), and 2.24% (Kabra), and a 73.40% human win rate versus 9.50%, 14.00%, and 3.10% respectively. In caption accuracy, MARVEL Level 1 (84.70% GPT-4, 82.80% human) edges out Kabra (83.40%, 78.20%) and substantially surpasses Cap3D (76.00%, 72.80%) and 3D-Topia (54.60%, 44.80%), despite being 3–34× longer in word count. The ablation in Table 5 further confirms that fine-tuning SD 3.5 on MARVEL captions outperforms both the pretrained model and the Cap3D-finetuned variant across all four evaluation criteria.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper carves new ground in 3D captioning by repurposing open-source multi-view VLMs (InternVL2) and LLMs (Qwen 2.5) in a five-stage pipeline, departing from prior datasets (CAP3D, 3D-Topia, Kabra) that relied on single-view VLMs or proprietary GPT-4 for annotation. Two conceptual departures stand out: (1) a hierarchical five-level annotation structure ranging from ~200-word descriptions to ~10-word tags, versus the single-level captions in all prior 3D datasets, and (2) the deliberate retention and filtering of human metadata from source datasets (Objaverse, Objaverse-XL) as domain-specific priors, whereas prior works discarded this information. For the TT3D generation component (MARVEL-FX3D), the paper follows the multi-stage pipeline paradigm (TTI → image-to-3D) established by Instant3D and AssetGen, but substitutes SDS-based optimization with a fine-tuned SD 3.5 + pretrained SF3D combination for speed.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

MARVEL-40M+ is the largest 3D caption dataset to date (40M+ annotations over 8.9M assets), and its multi-level structure directly addresses a practical gap: prior datasets forced a single granularity that was either too verbose for rapid prototyping or too terse for fine-grained reconstruction. The 15-second generation time of MARVEL-FX3D makes high-fidelity TT3D practically accessible, though the pipeline inherits limitations from its components—VLMs still struggle with numerical precision, directional reasoning, and thin-object identification, and SF3D occasionally produces flat geometry. Open questions include whether the multi-level annotation hierarchy provides measurable benefits for downstream 3D generation beyond what a single well-tuned level would achieve, and whether the annotation quality gains generalize to 3D models outside the seven source datasets' distribution.
