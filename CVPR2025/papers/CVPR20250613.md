## Problem

Existing egocentric datasets (Ego4D, EPIC-KITCHENS) are limited by short recording durations and single-person perspectives, making them inadequate for capturing multi-person social dynamics and over-day habit patterns needed for a truly useful life assistant. The paper addresses three intertwined gaps: the absence of week-long, multi-person egocentric data with rich multimodal sensing, the lack of benchmarks testing ultra-long-context (up to 44+ hours) life-oriented QA, and the absence of models that jointly handle egocentric video, audio, and identity-aware reasoning.

## Input / Output

EgoGPT consumes 30-second egocentric video clips (sampled at 1 FPS for EgoLifeQA, 32 frames for standard benchmarks) paired with synchronized audio from Meta Aria glasses, producing dense visual-audio captions. EgoRAG then takes a text question and retrieves top-k relevant clips from a hierarchical memory bank (organized at clip, hourly, and daily granularity) using a weighted visual-textual similarity score, feeding the retrieved evidence to a language model for final answer generation. The EgoLifeQA benchmark questions require look-back times ranging from 5 minutes to over 24 hours, with 2,003 of 3,000 questions demanding context beyond 2 hours.

## Method

EgoButler combines two subsystems: EgoGPT, a 7B omni-modal model built on LLaVA-OneVision (Qwen2 backbone) with an audio branch using Whisper Large v3 and a projection module trained on LibriSpeech, fine-tuned on EgoIT-99K (99K QA pairs curated from 9 egocentric datasets) and optionally on EgoLife Day-1 for identity-aware personalization; and EgoRAG, a retrieval-augmented generation module that constructs a multi-level memory bank of clip features, textual descriptions, and timestamped summaries, then performs hierarchical retrieval (day→hour→clip) with a relevance score s_i = Sim(q, c_i) + λ·Sim(q, d_i) to select top-k evidence clips. The key architectural insight is separating clip-level omni-modal understanding (EgoGPT) from long-context memory retrieval (EgoRAG), enabling the system to answer questions spanning weeks of continuous egocentric footage without requiring the language model to process the full video sequence.

## Results

On established egocentric benchmarks, EgoGPT (EgoIT-99K+D1) achieves 75.4% on EgoSchema, outperforming all commercial (Gemini-1.5-Pro: 72.2%, GPT-4o: 72.2%) and open-source baselines (best: Qwen2-VL at 66.7%). On EgoPlanBench, EgoGPT scores 33.4%, slightly below Qwen2-VL (34.3%), and on EgoThink it reaches 61.4%, trailing GPT-4o (65.5%). On the new EgoLifeQA benchmark, EgoGPT (EgoIT-99K+D1) achieves a 36.0% average, marginally below Gemini-1.5-Pro (36.9%) and GPT-4o (36.2%), but notably outperforms LLaVA-OV (30.8%) and the non-personalized EgoGPT (33.1%).

## Baselines & Comparisons

EgoGPT (EgoIT-99K+D1) clearly wins on EgoSchema, scoring 75.4% versus 72.2% for both Gemini-1.5-Pro and GPT-4o, and 66.7% for the strongest open-source model Qwen2-VL — a 3.2-point margin over the best commercial model. However, EgoGPT trails on EgoPlanBench (33.4% vs. Qwen2-VL's 34.3%) and EgoThink (61.4% vs. GPT-4o's 65.5%), indicating that egocentric fine-tuning does not uniformly transfer to all reasoning sub-tasks. On the new EgoLifeQA benchmark, EgoGPT (EgoIT-99K+D1) achieves 36.0% average, slightly below Gemini-1.5-Pro (36.9%) and GPT-4o (36.2%), but substantially above LLaVA-OV (30.8%) and the non-personalized EgoGPT (33.1%). The personalized variant gains the most on RelationMap (33.6% vs. 30.4% for both commercial models), confirming that identity-aware fine-tuning on Day-1 data is critical for interpersonal interaction questions. Among open-source 7B models, EgoGPT dominates EgoSchema by 6.5–11.3 points over LLaVA-OV, Oryx, and LLaVA-Videos, validating the benefit of the EgoIT-99K egocentric instruction-tuning dataset.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the egocentric video understanding lineage from Ego4D and EPIC-KITCHENS (which provide short, single-person clips) to a week-long, multi-person, multi-view setting, filling a gap the authors explicitly identify in prior datasets. Architecturally, EgoGPT builds on LLaVA-OneVision (itself on Qwen2) with an added Whisper-based audio branch, while EgoRAG applies a hierarchical retrieval-augmented generation strategy analogous to long-context LLM RAG but adapted to temporal video memory. The EgoLifeQA benchmark extends the video QA task from the sub-2-hour certification lengths of EgoSchema, EgoMemoria, and HourVideo to questions requiring up to 44+ hours of context, introducing life-oriented categories (habit tracking, entity logging, relation mapping) absent from prior egocentric QA benchmarks.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The 300-hour, six-participant, multi-view, multimodal EgoLife dataset is the first resource enabling study of prolonged behavioral patterns and interpersonal dynamics in egocentric settings, and the EgoLifeQA benchmark provides a concrete evaluation target for life-assistant AI. However, the system's overall accuracy remains low (~36% average), and the ablation revealing a ~10-point gap between human captions and model-generated captions identifies perception quality as the dominant bottleneck rather than reasoning. Key open questions include scaling to diverse languages and populations, improving speech/emotion understanding in egocentric audio, and developing multi-step retrieval reasoning—limitations the authors acknowledge but leave for future work.
