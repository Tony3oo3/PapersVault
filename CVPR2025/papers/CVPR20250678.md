## Problem

Existing sparse-view 3D reconstruction methods either require accurate camera poses and more than 10 views, or when applied to unbounded 360° scenes with only 3–4 unposed views, they suffer from noisy stereo geometry, severe multi-view inconsistency in diffusion-generated views, and spatial ambiguity between near-camera and distant structures. Performing high-quality novel view synthesis and surface reconstruction under these extreme constraints remains largely unsolved.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/CVPR20250678/cropped_page0_idx2.webp)

## Input / Output

The method takes 3–4 unposed RGB images of an unbounded 360° scene as the sole input; camera poses and an initial point cloud are recovered internally via DUSt3R/Mast3R rather than being provided. The output is a layered 2D Gaussian Splatting representation that supports both real-time novel view rendering and complete surface reconstruction (including previously unseen regions), evaluated with PSNR/SSIM/LPIPS for rendering and F1-score against ground-truth meshes for geometry.

## Method

Free360 partitions the scene into a front layer (inside the smallest bounding sphere enclosing the intersection of all camera frustums) and a back layer, each modeled as an independent set of 2D Gaussian primitives. A layer-specific bootstrap optimization refines the noisy DUSt3R point cloud: the front layer is optimized with photometric, monocular-normal, and depth-regularization losses plus voxel-downsampled initialization, while the back layer is completed via ray-box depth inpainting for occluded regions. An iterative fusion loop then alternates between (i) conditioning ViewCrafter on GS-rendered images of known cameras to generate novel views and (ii) appending the most reliable generated frames (selected by minimum distance to known poses) back into the training set, progressively expanding coverage. To prevent error propagation, an uncertainty-aware training scheme computes epistemic uncertainty as the pixel-wise variance across multiple diffusion samples from perturbed conditions, and uses this uncertainty map to down-weight photometric and perceptual losses in under-constrained regions while deactivating Gaussian densification during the generation-informed training phase.

## Results

On Mip-NeRF 360 with 3 input views, Free360 achieves PSNR 16.20, SSIM 0.378, and LPIPS 0.499, outperforming all baselines. On Tanks and Temples with 4 views, it reaches PSNR 14.69, SSIM 0.476, and LPIPS 0.409. For 3D surface reconstruction (F1-score on Tanks and Temples), Free360 achieves 0.423 at 4 views versus 0.284 for 2DGS, a 49% relative improvement. Performance scales consistently with more input views, reaching PSNR 17.35 (Mip-NeRF 360, 9 views) and 16.73 (Tanks and Temples, 9 views).

## Baselines & Comparisons

Free360 outperforms all five baselines across every metric and view count on both Mip-NeRF 360 and Tanks and Temples. Against the closest competitor ViewCrafter (a video diffusion model), Free360 gains +0.57 dB PSNR and −0.016 LPIPS at 3 views on Mip-NeRF 360, and +1.13 dB PSNR and −0.095 LPIPS at 4 views on Tanks and Temples, with the gap widening as scene complexity increases. FSGS and InstantSplat, which rely solely on DUSt3R point clouds without generative priors, trail by 1.5–2.1 dB PSNR and suffer from needle-like artifacts and incomplete backgrounds. ZeroNVS, adapted from single-view to multi-view, is the weakest baseline, lagging by over 1 dB PSNR and showing the highest LPIPS (0.632 vs. 0.499 at 3 views), indicating severe multi-view inconsistency. For surface reconstruction, Free360's F1-score of 0.423 at 4 views substantially exceeds 2DGS (0.284), demonstrating that the layered bootstrap optimization and iterative fusion produce geometrically complete surfaces even in unseen regions.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper builds on three pillars: DUSt3R for pose-free dense stereo initialization, 2D Gaussian Splatting as the geometric representation, and ViewCrafter (a video diffusion model) as a generative prior for missing views. It departs from prior sparse-view 3DGS methods (FSGS, InstantSplat) that assume known poses or more views, and from generative NVS approaches (ViewCrafter, ZeroNVS) that suffer from multi-view inconsistency under low overlap, by introducing a layered front/back Gaussian decomposition with layer-specific bootstrap optimization and an iterative reconstruction-generation fusion loop with uncertainty-aware filtering. The key conceptual departure is treating the unbounded 360° scene's spatial ambiguity as a layering problem rather than a single-representation problem, and making generation and reconstruction mutually conditioning rather than one-directional.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The paper addresses a practically important and previously underexplored setting—reconstructing unbounded 360° scenes from only 3–4 unposed views—which is relevant for applications like AR/VR content capture and autonomous navigation where dense multi-view data is unavailable. The modular framework (layered GS + bootstrap + iterative fusion + uncertainty) is complementary to video diffusion models and could benefit directly from future improvements in generative priors. A stated limitation is failure on scenes with extensive repetitive textures where DUSt3R itself breaks down, and the approach's reliance on a specific generative model (ViewCrafter) raises open questions about generalizability to other diffusion architectures or text-conditioned generation.
