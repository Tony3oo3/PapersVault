## Problem

Parameter-efficient fine-tuning (PEFT) for Mamba architecture remains largely unexplored, as existing PEFT methods are designed for Transformers and prior work assumed the SSM module (analogous to attention) would be the primary target for transfer learning. This paper reveals that Projectors, not SSMs, are the predominant contributors to downstream task adaptation in Mamba, and that the transformation between pretrained and fine-tuned Projector weights is a near-diagonal linear map.

## Input / Output

ProDiaL takes a pretrained Mamba model (Mamba LLM for text or Vision Mamba for images) and produces task-specific outputs: text completions for reasoning benchmarks (HellaSwag, Winogrande, ARC) or class labels for image classification (StanfordCars, Caltech-101, Flowers-102). During training, the pretrained Projector weight W remains frozen; only a block-diagonal matrix D_b, a low-rank off-diagonal matrix ε (via LoRA), and a per-output-channel scaling vector s are optimized. After training, only the transformed W' = s·W·D_b + ε is stored, incurring no inference-time overhead.

## Method

ProDiaL exploits the observation that the linear transformation T = W⁻¹W' between pretrained and fine-tuned Projector weights is nearly identity with strong diagonal entries and minimal off-diagonal components. It decomposes T into a diagonal matrix D and a small off-diagonal residual ε, then implements D as a block-diagonal matrix D_b (parameterized as I − relu(I∘D_a) + (1−I)∘D_a to encourage near-identity initialization) and ε as a LoRA low-rank product B_εA_ε. A learnable scaling vector s is applied at the output to enable subtle rotations beyond pure scaling. The block size r_b and LoRA rank r_ε provide knobs to control the number of learnable parameters, which stays below 1% of the total model. This design is applied selectively to the Input Projector, Output Projector, or both, and is compatible with any Mamba-based architecture.

## Results

On Mamba LLM (130M), ProDiaL targeting both Projectors achieves 44.06% average accuracy across HellaSwag, Winogrande, ARC-E, and ARC-C with only 2.42M learnable parameters, surpassing Full Fine-Tuning (43.43% with 130M params) and the Strong baseline (43.64% with 3.80M params). On Vision Mamba (Vim-tiny), ProDiaL achieves 89.87% average accuracy across StanfordCars, Caltech-101, and Flowers-102 with 0.65M parameters, outperforming Strong (89.08% with 0.94M params) and approaching Full-FT (91.66% with 7M params). ProDiaL consistently outperforms LoRA and DoRA applied to the same Projector targets across all datasets and both model families, while training less than 1% of total model parameters.

## Baselines & Comparisons

ProDiaL (Both-Proj) outperforms all four baselines on the Mamba LLM average: 44.06% vs. LoRA (43.71%), DoRA (43.47%), Strong (43.64%), and BitFit (42.14%), while using comparable or fewer parameters than LoRA/DoRA. On Vision Mamba, ProDiaL achieves 89.87% average, beating LoRA (89.46%), DoRA (89.29%), Strong (89.08%), and BitFit (79.35%) by 0.4–10.5 points respectively. The most notable individual gains are on ARC-E (55.18% vs. 53.87% for LoRA) and Flowers-102 (88.00% vs. 87.32% for LoRA) in the language and vision settings. ProDiaL also matches or exceeds Full Fine-Tuning on the Mamba LLM (44.06% vs. 43.43%) while training only 1.9% of the parameters, and approaches Full-FT on Vision Mamba (89.87% vs. 91.66%) with 9.3% of the parameters. The paper further shows that targeting only the Output Projector with ProDiaL (44.40% LLM avg, 85.15% Vis avg) still outperforms LoRA and DoRA on the same target, confirming the diagonal-centric design is the key differentiator rather than simply targeting more parameters.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper departs from the natural analogy to Transformer PEFT (where attention modules are the primary target) by empirically showing that in Mamba, the linear Projectors—not the SSM—carry most of the transferable task knowledge. Methodologically, ProDiaL builds on LoRA and DoRA but reframes the fine-tuning objective from an additive update (W' = W + ΔW) to a multiplicative linear transformation (W' = WT), then exploits the observed near-diagonal structure of T via a block-diagonal matrix plus a low-rank off-diagonal residual. The closest prior work is Halloran et al. [17] ('Strong'), which applied LoRA to SSM parameters and Projectors jointly; this paper shows that targeting Projectors alone with a diagonal-centric decomposition outperforms that broader strategy.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The finding that Projectors dominate over SSMs for Mamba PEFT redirects where practitioners should invest fine-tuning effort, and the <1% parameter budget makes ProDiaL practical for resource-constrained adaptation of Mamba-based LLMs and vision models. A key limitation is that the off-diagonal component ε still relies on standard LoRA, and the authors acknowledge that a purpose-built optimizer for ε could yield further gains. Open questions include whether the diagonal-centric transformation property generalizes beyond Mamba to other recurrent or hybrid architectures, and whether the approach scales to much larger Mamba models (e.g., 7B+) where the Projector parameter fraction may shift.
