## Problem

Existing 3D generative models face a fundamental trade-off: those trained on 3D or multi-view data achieve good geometry but suffer from limited generalizability due to scarce supervision, while those leveraging 2D priors through per-scene optimization (score distillation, inpainting) are slow and produce multi-view inconsistencies. The paper addresses how to achieve feed-forward, generalizable text-to-3D scene generation in seconds by directly generating 3D representations within a latent diffusion framework that inherits the scale of 2D training data.

## Input / Output

At inference, the model takes a text prompt and a set of N camera poses (encoded as Plücker coordinate ray maps of shape H×W×6 per view) and produces a scene-level 3D Gaussian representation in approximately 8 seconds. Each pixel-aligned Gaussian is parameterized by 12 channels (depth, 4D rotation quaternion, 3D scale, opacity, and 3D spherical harmonics), and multi-view Gaussians are aggregated into a global coordinate system. During training, the model additionally consumes monocular depth maps estimated on-the-fly by DepthAnything-V2-S, forming an RGB-D latent space that disentangles appearance from geometry.

## Method

Prometheus follows a two-stage latent diffusion pipeline. Stage 1 trains a GS-VAE that encodes multi-view RGB-D images into a joint latent space using a frozen Stable Diffusion encoder, fuses cross-view context via a transformer conditioned on Plücker ray maps, and decodes pixel-aligned 3D Gaussians using a repurposed SD decoder (only first/last conv layers adjusted). Stage 2 trains a multi-view latent diffusion model (UNet initialized from SD 2.1) that jointly denoises multi-view RGB-D latent codes conditioned on text (via cross-attention) and camera poses (via channel concatenation), with 3D cross-view self-attention blocks replacing standard self-attention to capture inter-view correlations. The key architectural insight is the RGB-D latent space, which provides geometric cues to the Gaussian decoder and improves both fidelity and geometry. For inference, a hybrid sampling guidance strategy separately weights text and pose guidance terms to balance multi-view consistency against prompt fidelity, combined with CFG-rescale to prevent over-saturation. Training spans 9 datasets (single-view SAM-1B plus 8 multi-view datasets covering objects, indoor, outdoor, and driving) to achieve generalizability comparable to Stable Diffusion.

## Results

On the T3Bench text-to-3D generation benchmark, Prometheus achieves a BRISQUE of 49.63 and CLIP-Score of 0.370 on scene-level prompts, outperforming Director3D (50.88 BRISQUE, 0.357 CLIP-Score) while being nearly 3× faster (≈8 s vs ≈22 s). On object-level prompts, Director3D retains a lead in BRISQUE (49.91 vs 59.43) and CLIP-Score (0.397 vs 0.329), but Prometheus still surpasses the optimization-based GaussianDreamer (107.8 BRISQUE) and MVDream+LGM (74.64 BRISQUE) by a wide margin. For feed-forward 3D reconstruction on TartanAir (Hard), Prometheus achieves δ1 = 0.505 and AbsRel = 0.526, improving δ1 by 64% over PixelSplat (0.307) and 86% over MVSplat (0.272).

## Baselines & Comparisons

Against the feed-forward baseline Director3D, Prometheus wins on scene-level generation across all three metrics (BRISQUE 49.63 vs 50.88, NIQE 14.01 vs 14.97, CLIP-Score 0.370 vs 0.357) and is 2.75× faster (8 s vs 22 s), but loses on object-level BRISQUE (59.43 vs 49.91) and CLIP-Score (0.329 vs 0.397), which the authors attribute to failure cases in object-centric settings. Against the optimization-based GaussianDreamer, Prometheus dominates on image quality (BRISQUE 59.43 vs 107.8) and is roughly 11× faster (8 s vs 15 min), though GaussianDreamer scores slightly higher on CLIP-Score for single objects (0.386 vs 0.329). The MVDream+LGM pipeline is outperformed on BRISQUE and NIQE across all settings but trails on CLIP-Score for single objects (0.379 vs 0.329). For Stage-1 reconstruction on TartanAir, Prometheus clearly surpasses both PixelSplat and MVSplat on geometry metrics (δ1: 0.505 vs 0.307 and 0.272 on Hard), while PixelSplat retains a small edge on PSNR/SSIM for image fidelity, suggesting Prometheus trades a minor image-quality gap for substantially better geometric accuracy and robustness to sparse views.

## Relation to Prior Work

Prometheus sits at the intersection of latent diffusion models (Stable Diffusion), 3D Gaussian Splatting, and feed-forward 3D reconstruction. It departs from optimization-based text-to-3D methods (SDS, inpainting) by generating 3D Gaussians directly in a single forward pass, and from prior feed-forward 3DGS models (PixelSplat, MVSplat) by leveraging large-scale 2D image data and pretrained diffusion priors for generalizability. The closest prior work, Director3D, also generates 3D scenes from text in a feed-forward manner, but Prometheus operates in latent space rather than image space, reducing computational overhead and better exploiting the 2D latent prior. The RGB-D latent space for disentangling appearance and geometry, combined with hybrid sampling guidance from HarmonyView, distinguishes the architectural and inference design from both Director3D and MVDream+LGM pipelines.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

Achieving text-to-3D scene generation in approximately 8 seconds while maintaining multi-view consistency and reasonable geometry quality is a significant practical advance over optimization-based methods (15+ minutes) and even other feed-forward approaches (~22s). The RGB-D latent space design and the finding that single-view data substantially improves generalizable reconstruction offer reusable insights for future 3D generation systems. However, the method's CLIP scores remain below Director3D for object-centric prompts, and reliance on monocular depth estimation as pseudo-geometry ground truth introduces a quality ceiling that may limit fidelity in complex scenes. Open questions include whether the approach scales to larger scene extents, whether the depth prior can be replaced by learned geometry, and how to improve text alignment without sacrificing multi-view consistency.
