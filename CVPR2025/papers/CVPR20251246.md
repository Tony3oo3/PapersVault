## Problem

State-of-the-art 3D generation models are upper-bounded by the scale and diversity of their training data, yet high-quality 3D datasets with camera pose annotations remain prohibitively expensive to collect at web scale. Existing multi-view diffusion (MVD) models rely on 3D-inductive conditions (camera poses, Plücker rays, or warped renders) that cannot be obtained for the vast majority of Internet videos, creating a fundamental barrier to scalable 3D prior learning.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/CVPR20251246/cropped_page0_idx1.webp)

## Input / Output

At inference, See3D accepts a single RGB image or 3–9 sparse views as reference, and iteratively consumes warped images (produced by depth-based reprojection) concatenated with binary occlusion masks as the visual-condition channel. The model outputs a sequence of 3D-consistent novel-view images along a user-defined camera trajectory, which are then fed into 3D Gaussian Splatting to produce a renderable scene or extractable mesh. During training, the visual-condition is constructed purely from the video frames themselves—randomly masked target images corrupted with time-dependent noise—requiring no external pose or geometry annotations.

## Method

See3D is a pose-free visual-conditional MVD model built on Stable Video Diffusion (with the time embedding removed and frames shuffled to eliminate temporal bias). Its key mechanism is a time-dependent visual-condition: target frames are first corrupted via random irregular masking and noise at a reduced timestep t′ < t (Eq. 2), then blended with the standard noisy latent X_t using a monotonically decreasing weight W_t (Eq. 3), so the model relies on pixel-space hints at high noise and transitions to the latent at low noise. This design makes the condition task-agnostic, allowing the same trained model to accept warped images, masks, or other 2D cues at inference without fine-tuning. For 3D generation, an iterative warping pipeline uses MoGe monocular depth, sparse keypoint matching (SuperPoint + LightGlue) for pixel-wise depth scale alignment, and Locally Weighted Linear Regression for global metric depth recovery, producing warped hints that guide See3D to synthesize the next batch of views before 3DGS reconstruction.

## Results

On single-view-to-3D, See3D achieves PSNR of 23.76 / 25.36 / 24.28 on Tanks-and-Temples, RealEstate10K, and CO3D respectively, outperforming the strongest baseline ViewCrafter (19.66 / 21.93 / 20.17) by 4.1–4.6 dB in PSNR. On sparse-view (3-view) reconstruction, See3D reaches PSNR of 23.23 / 28.04 / 17.35 on LLFF, DTU, and MipNeRF-360, surpassing the best prior method CAT3D (21.58 / 22.02 / 16.62) by 1.65 / 6.02 / 0.73 dB. LPIPS scores are also consistently the lowest across all six benchmarks, confirming superior perceptual quality. The ablation on data scaling shows PSNR rising from 19.32 (10% data) to 25.01 (100% data) on RealEstate10K, underscoring the importance of the WebVi3D dataset scale.

## Baselines & Comparisons

See3D wins decisively across all 11 baselines on every metric and dataset. In single-view generation, the gap over the strongest warping-based baseline ViewCrafter is 4.1–4.6 dB in PSNR (e.g., 25.36 vs. 21.93 on RealEstate10K), and the gap over camera-conditional MotionCtrl is even larger (~9 dB). In sparse-view (3-view) reconstruction, See3D outperforms CAT3D by 1.65 dB on LLFF, 6.02 dB on DTU, and 0.73 dB on MipNeRF-360 in PSNR, while also achieving the lowest LPIPS (0.135 / 0.073 / 0.422). The pose-conditional baselines ZeroNVS and ReconFusion trail by 5–10 dB, confirming that the pose-free visual-condition approach is not only competitive but superior. Notably, MuRF and BGGS achieve competitive SSIM on DTU (0.885 and 0.862 vs. 0.884) but are limited to that dataset and do not support MipNeRF-360, whereas See3D generalizes across all three sparse-view benchmarks without dataset-specific tuning.

## Relation to Prior Work

See3D departs from the dominant paradigm of pose-conditional multi-view diffusion (MVD) models like CAT3D, ZeroNVS, and ViewCrafter, which require expensive camera pose annotations to guide 3D-consistent generation. Instead, it introduces a purely 2D-inductive 'visual-condition'—time-dependent noise applied to masked video frames—that implicitly encodes camera movement without any 3D geometry or pose labels. The architecture builds on Stable Video Diffusion, and the downstream 3D generation pipeline follows the warping-inpainting lineage of LucidDreamer and ViewCrafter, but replaces their depth-based warping with the learned visual-condition. The WebVi3D dataset (320M frames from 16M clips) is orders of magnitude larger than prior 3D datasets like DLV3D, RealEstate10K, and Objaverse, shifting the training data source from curated 3D assets to raw Internet video.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

This work directly attacks the fundamental scalability bottleneck in 3D generation: the cost and infeasibility of annotating web-scale data with camera poses. By demonstrating that a pose-free visual-condition trained on video can match or exceed pose-conditional models on standard benchmarks, it opens a path to continuously expanding 3D generation capabilities as Internet video grows. The zero-shot transfer to warping-based generation and 3D editing without fine-tuning suggests the learned 3D priors are genuinely general rather than overfit to a specific conditioning format. Open questions include whether the warping-based iterative pipeline (with its accumulated geometric errors) can be replaced by a more direct approach, whether the method handles dynamic or non-rigid scenes, and whether the 4.63 dB advantage over ViewCrafter holds on truly out-of-distribution open-world content beyond the evaluated benchmarks.
