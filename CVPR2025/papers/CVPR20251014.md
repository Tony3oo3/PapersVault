## Problem

Existing 3D LLM-based methods for scene understanding rely on global-viewpoint datasets and ignore the egocentric perspective, where spatial relationships (e.g., left vs. right) change with the observer's position and orientation. Current data generation pipelines further depend on labor-intensive 3D instance labels and fixed relationship templates, limiting scalability and open-vocabulary coverage.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/CVPR20251014/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The model takes a scene-level point cloud P ∈ R^{N×6} (xyz + RGB per point), a free-form situation description s, and a task instruction t as input. It produces a textual task answer a, a 3D situation position s^pos = (x,y,z), and a yaw rotation s^rot encoded as a quaternion. The point cloud is segmented into K object instances, each subdivided into local spatial patches processed by a ViT encoder; during situation grounding, only anchors within a distance threshold D of the ground-truth position receive supervision.

## Method

The paper contributes two components: (1) View2Cap, an automatic dataset generation pipeline that exploits RGB-D video scanning trajectories as natural egocentric viewpoints, using LLaVA-OneVision to produce situation-conditioned captions and QA pairs from individual frames while recording camera poses and extracting region point clouds via depth—requiring no 3D labels; and (2) a Situation Grounding (SG) module that reframes pose estimation as a classification problem by treating each scene object as an anchor, predicting a 3D position offset Δp_k and a discretized rotation bin (B bins over −π to π) relative to the anchor via an MLP f_grd that concatenates the LLM's [GRD] token hidden state with the anchor's visual token hidden state. The full architecture uses a Uni3D point cloud encoder with spatial attention, an MLP connector projecting into LLaMA 3.1's embedding space (fine-tuned with LoRA), and a three-stage training procedure: region-text alignment, situation grounding with position/rotation/confidence losses, and downstream instruction tuning.

## Results

On Scan2Cap, the method achieves 75.2 CIDEr and 56.3 sentence similarity, surpassing the strongest generalist baseline LEO (72.4 CIDEr, 55.3 Sim) by 2.8 CIDEr. On SQA3D, it reaches 54.0 EM@1 (56.0 with refined matching), outperforming LEO (50.0/52.4) and SIG3D (52.6). On ScanQA, LEO retains a lead in CIDEr (101.4 vs. 89.8) and EM@1 (24.5 vs. 22.9), though the gap narrows under refined matching (47.6 vs. 40.2). For situation grounding, the method achieves 17.4% Acc@0.5m and 24.1% Acc@15°, exceeding SIG3D (16.8%/23.4%) and 3D-VisTA (11.7%/16.9%).

## Baselines & Comparisons

Against the strongest generalist baseline LEO [18], the method wins on Scan2Cap by 2.8 CIDEr (75.2 vs. 72.4) and on SQA3D by 4.0 EM@1 (54.0 vs. 50.0), but loses on ScanQA in both CIDEr (89.8 vs. 101.4) and EM@1 (22.9 vs. 24.5). Compared to task-specific models, the method substantially outperforms ScanQA [1] (47.2 EM@1 on SQA3D) and SIG3D [27] (52.6 EM@1) on the situated QA benchmark. On situation grounding (Table 3), the method leads SIG3D by 0.6% Acc@0.5m and 0.7° Acc@15°, and surpasses 3D-VisTA [46] by 5.7% Acc@0.5m and 7.2° Acc@15°, confirming that the anchor-based grounding module provides meaningful spatial localization gains. The ablation in Table 6 further shows that adding the SG module to LEO improves ScanRefer Acc@0.5 from 30.8 to 32.9, and adding View2Cap data pushes it to 38.4, indicating complementary benefits of the grounding module and the situational dataset.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper builds directly on LEO's embodied 3D LLM architecture, adding a Situation Grounding module and a new egocentric dataset (View2Cap) generated from RGB-D video trajectories. It departs from prior scene-graph-based data generation methods (SceneVerse, LEO, 3D-LLM) that rely on ground-truth 3D instance labels and global viewpoints, instead leveraging the natural camera trajectories in RGB-D scans to capture dynamic observer perspectives. The anchor-based formulation of pose estimation as a classification problem (discretizing rotation into bins relative to object centers) is a novel architectural choice not seen in prior 3D grounding work like SQA3D or SIG3D, which either condition on situation text without predicting it or use separate regression heads.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The View2Cap dataset (231K situations, 553K QA pairs, 10× larger than SQA3D) provides a much-needed resource for egocentric 3D understanding, and the automatic generation pipeline via 2D VLMs significantly reduces annotation cost. However, the situation grounding accuracy remains modest (17.4% Acc@0.5m, 24.1% Acc@15°), suggesting the core challenge of predicting precise observer pose from language is far from solved. Open questions include whether the 2D-to-3D distillation approach generalizes to outdoor or large-scale environments, whether the anchor mechanism scales to scenes with hundreds of objects, and whether the modest improvements over LEO (2.8 CIDEr, 4% EM@1) will compound as base models improve.
