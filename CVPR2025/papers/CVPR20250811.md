## Problem

Under limited training data, GANs cannot adequately populate the continuous input latent space, causing adjacent latent variables to map to images with large realism gaps. This undermines consistency regularization (CR), which forces the discriminator to score such mismatched pairs identically, degrading both discriminator robustness and generation quality. The paper asks how to restructure the latent space so that CR operates over semantically coherent, equally authentic image pairs even with very few training samples.

## Input / Output

The generator consumes a random noise vector z ~ N(0,1) of dimension d_z, maps it through a StyleGAN2-style mapping network f_W into an intermediate style space W, then segments w into s sub-vectors of dimension 4 each and quantizes them against a learnable codebook before feeding the concatenated discrete code w^q into the synthesis network. The output is a 256×256 RGB image. During the separate codebook initialization phase, the method additionally consumes the full training dataset (images) and CLIP text-encoder features of the quantized codes, aligning them via optimal transport before GAN training begins.

## Method

SQ-GAN replaces the continuous style space W with a compact discrete proxy space W^q constructed as the Cartesian product of s codebooks, each of size k with entries of dimension 4. Each sub-vector of w is assigned to its nearest codebook entry (straight-through estimator for backpropagation), and a uniformity loss on a learnable hyperspherical projection prevents codebook collapse. The consistency regularization is reformulated: a perturbed z+ε is mapped to W, quantized to w'^q, and the discriminator is penalized for inconsistent scores on g(w^q) vs. g(w'^q), ensuring the two images are drawn from the same discrete vocabulary and thus equally authentic. A knowledge-enhanced codebook initialization (CBI) step encodes training images with CLIP's visual encoder and codebook codes with CLIP's text encoder, then minimizes a Sinkhorn optimal-transport loss to align the two feature distributions, pre-populating the codebook with a semantically rich vocabulary of the dataset before adversarial training starts.

## Results

On Oxford-Dog, SQ-GAN+CBI achieves FID 35.01 and IS 12.44, outperforming the best baseline (RaHinge, FID 38.68 / IS 11.14) and the original StyleGAN2 (FID 64.26 / IS 9.69). On FFHQ-2.5K, SQ-GAN+CBI reaches FID 22.04 and IS 4.20, a 10.3 FID improvement over the strongest baseline (RaHinge, FID 32.34). On the extremely small MetFaces and BreCa-HAD datasets, SQ-GAN+ADA achieves the best FID of 24.77 and 22.61 respectively, surpassing CR+ADA (29.91 and 22.69) and standalone ADA (28.10 and 23.90).

## Baselines & Comparisons

SQ-GAN+CAN outperforms all 14 baselines on both Oxford-Dog and FFHQ-2.5K. The most direct comparison is with CR [58,62], the consistency regularization method that SQ-GAN modifies: SQ-GAN+CBI reduces FID from 48.73 to 35.01 on Oxford-Dog (−13.72) and from 41.43 to 22.04 on FFHQ (−19.39), while also improving IS. Against the strongest GAN-loss variant (RaHinge, FID 38.68 / 32.34), SQ-GAN+CBI still leads by 3.67 and 10.3 FID points respectively. On the extremely small MetFaces (1,203 images) and BreCa-HAD (1,750 images) datasets, SQ-GAN+ADA achieves the best FID (24.77 and 22.61) and KID (0.008 and 0.010), beating CR+ADA by 5.14 and 0.08 FID and ADA alone by 3.33 and 1.29 FID. Architecture comparisons (Table 3) confirm that SQ-GAN on StyleGAN2 (FID 18.96) substantially outperforms DCGAN+Dropout (77.35) and SNGAN+Dropout (48.54) on Oxford-Dog at 64×64, validating that the quantization strategy is architecture-agnostic but most effective with the StyleGAN2 mapping network.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper repurposes StyleGAN2's intermediate style space W as the target for vector quantization, adapting the VQ-VAE codebook mechanism to the GAN training loop rather than to a standalone autoencoder. It modifies the consistency regularization (CR) framework of Zhang et al. and Zhao et al. by replacing direct perturbation in the prior space Z with perturbation followed by quantization in a discrete proxy space W^q, arguing that the compactness of the codebook makes CR meaningful under limited data. The codebook initialization via CLIP features and Sinkhorn optimal transport draws on knowledge-distillation and alignment techniques from the limited-data GAN literature but applies them to the codebook rather than the discriminator, creating a semantically grounded discrete vocabulary for the generator.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The paper identifies a concrete failure mode of CR under limited data—adjacent latent codes producing images of wildly different realism—and offers a principled fix through discretization, which is a useful conceptual tool for any setting where continuous latent exploration is underpowered. However, the approach is tested only at 256×256 resolution on four small datasets, and the interaction between the discrete proxy space and higher-resolution synthesis or more complex conditioning remains unexplored. An open question is whether the codebook's finite vocabulary becomes a bottleneck for expressiveness as dataset size grows, and whether the CLIP-based initialization generalizes beyond the specific domain of each dataset without manual tuning.
