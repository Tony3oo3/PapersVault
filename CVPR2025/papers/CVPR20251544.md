## Problem

Generating temporally evolving 3D object intrinsics (geometry, reflectance, texture) for natural processes like a rose blooming requires extensive manual effort, and existing score-distillation methods fail to maintain 4D consistency—producing both spatial Janus artifacts and repetitive temporal states across timestamps. No prior method can distill full 4D intrinsic sequences (shape + PBR materials) from 2D foundation models while supporting arbitrary viewpoint and lighting queries.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/CVPR20251544/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method takes a text prompt describing a natural process (e.g., 'a rose blooming') and a reference video sampled from a 2D video diffusion model to bootstrap a coarse 4D reconstruction. It produces a hybrid 4D implicit representation encoding time-varying SDF geometry and per-point PBR material parameters (albedo, roughness, metallic), which can be rendered from any camera pose, under any environment map, at any timestamp—effectively a '3D time-lapse volumetric video' with full relighting and novel-view synthesis capability.

## Method

The pipeline has two stages: first, a coarse dynamic mesh is reconstructed from a reference video via 3D reconstruction plus deformation-field optimization with ARAP regularization, and a Neural Template is built that maps (viewpoint ξ, timestamp t) to a Neural State Map—DINOv2 features after PCA and spatial downsampling, denoised by a consistency model to bridge the distribution gap with natural images. Second, a hybrid 4D representation (K-Planes for low-frequency temporal consistency + per-keyframe NGP hash grids for high-frequency detail) is optimized via SDS gradients from a ControlNet-conditioned diffusion model, where the Neural State Map serves as the 4D control signal anchoring each view-timestep to its correct temporal state. A differentiable Disney Principled PBR renderer decomposes appearance into physically-based material parameters, and a video-diffusion-based temporal regularization term enforces frame-to-frame coherence.

## Results

In a user study with 95 participants across 7 natural processes, the proposed method achieves 76.84% first-place preference for motion alignment, 69.77% for motion realism, and 61.05% for visual quality, substantially outperforming all baselines. On the objective CLIP score, the method scores 30.83, marginally ahead of 4D-Fy (30.11), DreamGaussian4D (29.65), and STAG4D (28.01). The method is the first to generate full temporal object intrinsics (geometry, albedo, roughness, metallic) from text prompts alone, whereas all baselines produce only 4D renderings without decomposed material properties.

## Baselines & Comparisons

The proposed method decisively outperforms all three 4D generation baselines in the user study: 4D-Fy (4.21% motion alignment, 11.13% motion realism, 21.95% visual quality), DreamGaussian4D (2.26%, 5.26%, 11.28%), and STAG4D (16.69%, 13.83%, 5.71%) all trail far behind the proposed method's 76.84%, 69.77%, and 61.05% respectively. On CLIP score, the gap is smaller (30.83 vs. 30.11 for 4D-Fy), suggesting the baselines produce visually plausible static frames but fail to capture the correct temporal dynamics. The paper notes that 4D-Fy and DreamGaussian4D produce 'very small or unrealistic motions,' while STAG4D shows noticeable artifacts in novel views due to its reliance on a 3D reconstruction model. Against the static 3D baseline Fantasia3D, the proposed method avoids the severe Janus problem and texture artifacts observed in the baseline, while additionally providing full PBR material decomposition (albedo, roughness, metallic) that none of the 4D baselines support.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the score distillation lineage (DreamFusion, 4D-fy, DreamGaussian4D) from static 3D or limited 4D generation to the full problem of generating temporally evolving object intrinsics—geometry, albedo, roughness, and metallic parameters—simultaneously. It departs from prior 4D generation methods by introducing a Neural Template built from DINOv2 self-supervised features (after PCA) as a ControlNet-style conditioning signal, which anchors distillation gradients to specific temporal states and viewpoints, addressing the 4D consistency problem that plagues SDS-based approaches. The hybrid K-Planes + NGP representation and the PBR material decomposition via a differentiable Disney Principled renderer are borrowed from the 3D intrinsic decomposition literature (Fantasia3D, NeRF/NeuS) but are the first to be applied in a temporally evolving 4D setting.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

This is the first work to generate full 4D object intrinsics (geometry + PBR materials) from 2D diffusion models, enabling relighting and novel-view rendering of dynamic natural processes like blooming or withering—capabilities absent in all prior 4D generation baselines. The Neural Template concept, using self-supervised image features as a 4D control signal, is an elegant and potentially generalizable mechanism that could extend to other dynamic processes beyond the seven tested phenomena. Open questions include scalability to more complex or multi-object processes, whether the approach handles processes with non-monotonic temporal evolution, and the computational cost of the two-stage pipeline (video diffusion sampling + iterative SDS optimization). The close CLIP scores (30.83 vs. 30.11) despite large user-study gaps suggest the objective quality advantage is more modest than the perceptual one.
