## Problem

Existing matching-based image-to-point cloud registration methods suffer from three interrelated weaknesses: point-wise frustum classification ignores the global geometric constraint that the overlapping region must form a camera frustum, direct cross-modal feature alignment produces many mismatches due to inherent 2D-3D data disparity, and the PnP-RANSAC post-processing step decouples pose estimation from the learning objective, preventing end-to-end optimization.

## Input / Output

The method takes an RGB image (downsampled to 160×512 on KITTI or 160×320 on nuScenes) and a 3D point cloud of 40,960 points from the same scene, along with the known camera intrinsic matrix. It outputs a camera pose as a 3×3 rotation matrix in SO(3) and a 3D translation vector, regressed as a residual (ΔR, Δt) relative to a coarse frustum pose estimated in the detection stage. Intermediate outputs include a per-point frustum mask, N_q=128 paired 2D-3D keypoints, and 2D/3D keypoint heatmaps.

## Method

The proposed pipeline comprises three modules. The Geometric Prior-guided Detection Module (GPDM) first classifies each point as in/out-of-frustum via a binary classifier, then regresses the frustum pose (6D rotation + translation) from the classification probabilities and point coordinates, and finally re-derives the mask by projecting points through the predicted frustum, exploiting the continuous geometric structure to reduce boundary noise. The Implicit Correspondence Learning Module (ICLM) initializes 128 learnable correspondence queries and iteratively (L=3 times) applies alternating Attention-Pixel and Attention-Point operations to aggregate 2D (ResNet-FPN) and 3D (KPFCNN) features; the updated queries are then converted into 2D and 3D keypoint detectors, whose similarity with the respective features yields paired keypoint heatmaps and thus implicit 2D-3D correspondences without explicit cross-modal feature alignment. The Pose Regression Module (PRM) fuses the paired keypoint detectors and keypoints via MLP, pools a pose-sensitive feature, and regresses the residual pose (ΔR, Δt) relative to the coarse frustum pose, enabling fully end-to-end supervised training with a joint loss over classification, frustum pose, correspondence, diversity, and camera pose terms.

## Results

On KITTI, the proposed method achieves 0.20±0.21 m RTE, 1.24±2.34° RRE, and 97.49% registration accuracy (Acc), substantially outperforming the previous SOTA VP2P-match (0.75±1.13 m RTE, 3.29±7.99° RRE, 83.04% Acc). On nuScenes, it reaches 0.63±0.44 m RTE, 2.13±3.75° RRE, and 90.94% Acc, improving over VP2P-match by 0.26 m in RTE and 2.61% in Acc. The method reduces RTE by 0.55 m and RRE by 2.05° on KITTI relative to the prior best, while also achieving the lowest variance across all metrics.

## Baselines & Comparisons

The proposed method decisively outperforms all three baseline families on both datasets. Against VP2P-match (the previous SOTA), it reduces KITTI RTE from 0.75 m to 0.20 m (−73%) and RRE from 3.29° to 1.24° (−62%), while boosting Acc from 83.04% to 97.49%; on nuScenes the gains are 0.26 m in RTE and 2.61% in Acc. Against CorrI2P, the improvement is even more dramatic: KITTI RTE drops from 3.78 m to 0.20 m and Acc rises from 72.42% to 97.49%, with CorrI2P showing extremely high variance (±65.16 m) indicating frequent catastrophic failures. The matching-free DeepI2P variants (Grid Cls.+PnP, DeepI2P 2D/3D) are far behind, with Acc ranging from 2.45% to 38.10%, confirming that fine-grained 2D-3D correspondences remain essential for accurate registration. The paper attributes its advantage to three design choices: geometric-prior-guided frustum detection (GPDM), implicit correspondence learning via shared queries (ICLM), and end-to-end pose regression (PRM) replacing PnP-RANSAC.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper departs from the dominant matching-based paradigm in image-to-point cloud registration (2D3D-MatchNet, CorrI2P, VP2P-match), which relies on explicit cross-modal feature alignment via contrastive or triplet losses followed by PnP-RANSAC. Instead, it repurposes the detection-free correspondence query mechanism from LoFTR (image-to-image registration) to the cross-modal setting, using shared learnable queries to implicitly identify 2D-3D correspondences without forcing 2D and 3D features into a common alignment space. The geometric prior-guided frustum detection extends the coarse frustum classification of DeepI2P by regressing an explicit frustum pose to refine the overlapping region boundary, and the learned pose regression module replaces the non-differentiable PnP-RANSAC post-processing step.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The large accuracy gap over VP2P-match (97.5% vs 83% Acc on KITTI) suggests that the standard recipe of explicit cross-modal feature matching followed by RANSAC may be fundamentally limiting, and that implicit correspondence learning with end-to-end pose regression is a more effective formulation. However, the method is validated only on driving-scene datasets (KITTI, nuScenes) with a pinhole camera model, and its reliance on ground-truth camera poses for supervised training limits applicability to unsupervised or novel-view settings. Open questions include whether the implicit correspondence approach generalizes to non-driving 3D scenes, whether the frustum geometric prior is necessary in more general camera models, and whether the method can be extended to online or real-time registration.
