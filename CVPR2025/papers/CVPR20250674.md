## Problem

Estimating full-body motion from sparse VR tracking signals (head + two hands) is inherently ill-posed: the same sparse input maps to multiple plausible full-body poses, especially for unobserved lower-body joints. Existing approaches either add more body-worn sensors (degrading user experience) or ignore the rich environmental context that could disambiguate the solution. No prior method jointly models the multi-hypothesis uncertainty of sparse-to-dense motion mapping and leverages both semantic and geometric environmental constraints to resolve the ambiguity.

## Input / Output

The method consumes 36-channel sparse tracking signals per frame (positions, 6D rotations, and linear velocities of the HMD and two hand controllers), a pre-scanned environment point cloud cropped to a 1-meter radius around the body and uniformly sampled to 1000 points, and autoregressive historical motion states (132-dim SMPL joint rotations). It outputs a full-body SMPL pose sequence (22 joints in 6D rotation, 132 dims per frame), per-joint heteroscedastic uncertainty estimates, and per-joint contact probabilities. The autoregressive design means the model conditions on its own previous-frame predictions, enabling real-time sequential inference.

## Method

EnvPoser is a two-stage framework. Stage I (trained on AMASS) uses an autoregressive transformer encoder over concatenated sparse-signal and historical-motion embeddings, followed by a pose regression head and a heteroscedastic uncertainty head; the reparameterization trick samples multi-hypothesis poses from N(θ̃, δ) to explicitly represent the one-to-many ambiguity. Stage II (fine-tuned on motion-environment interaction datasets) refines these samples via an environment-aware module: a PointNet++ encoder produces environment embeddings that feed a cross-attention network with spatial salience (distance-aware) to capture semantic motion-environment correlations, while a COAP-based collision loss enforces geometric non-penetration constraints. A contact-probability head (trained with BCE) predicts per-joint contact states, which are concatenated with the refined motion representation to regress the final pose. The combined loss integrates motion L2, uncertainty, joint-position, hand-alignment, foot-contact, ground-height, ground-penetration, contact-classification, and COAP collision terms.

## Results

EnvPoser achieves the best performance across all four metrics on both EgoBody and GIMO datasets. On EgoBody, it reaches 6.00° MPJRE, 74.7 mm MPJPE, 174.0 mm/s MPJVE, and 6.6 Jitter. On GIMO, it achieves 4.38° MPJRE, 57.6 mm MPJPE, 234.6 mm/s MPJVE, and 8.9 Jitter. These results represent improvements of 0.42° (6.5%) in MPJRE and 17.0 mm (18.5%) in MPJPE over the best non-environment baseline (AvatarJLM) on EgoBody, and 5.8% MPJRE reduction over the best environment-aware baseline (S2Fusion) on GIMO.

## Baselines & Comparisons

EnvPoser outperforms all four baselines on every metric across both datasets. Against the strongest non-environment method, AvatarJLM [55], EnvPoser reduces MPJRE by 0.42° (6.5%) and MPJPE by 17.0 mm (18.5%) on EgoBody, and by 0.57° (11.5%) and 13.1 mm (18.5%) on GIMO, while also improving Jitter from 7.2 to 6.6 (EgoBody) and 10.7 to 8.9 (GIMO). Against the closest environment-aware competitor, S2Fusion [35], EnvPoser achieves a 5.8% MPJRE reduction on GIMO (4.38° vs. 4.65°) and a 9.8% MPJRE / 16.3% MPJPE reduction on EgoBody (6.00° vs. 6.65°, 74.7 mm vs. 89.2 mm). The advantage over S2Fusion is most pronounced in motion smoothness, with an 11.9% Jitter reduction on GIMO (8.9 vs. 10.1) and a 47.2% reduction on EgoBody (6.6 vs. 12.5). AGRoL [6] and AvatarPoser [14] are consistently the weakest baselines, with AGRoL showing the highest MPJVE (370.3 mm/s on EgoBody) and Jitter (21.1), indicating that diffusion-based generation without environmental constraints produces less stable motion.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the sparse-observation motion estimation line (AvatarPoser, AGRoL, AvatarJLM) by introducing a two-stage framework that first models multi-hypothesis uncertainty via heteroscedastic regression (building on Kendall & Gal's formulation) and then refines estimates using pre-scanned environment point clouds. The architectural backbone draws from Transformer Inertial Poser's auto-regressive design with historical motion embedding, while the geometric collision constraint repurposes COAP's occupancy-based collision detection. The key departure from the closest competitor S2Fusion is the explicit uncertainty-aware multi-hypothesis representation in Stage I, which S2Fusion lacks, and the dual semantic-plus-geometric environmental constraint rather than S2Fusion's primarily lower-body-focused interaction modeling.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The paper demonstrates that explicitly modeling the one-to-many ambiguity of sparse tracking and then resolving it with environmental context yields consistent gains over both signal-only and environment-only baselines, offering a practical recipe for VR/AR motion capture pipelines. However, the static-environment assumption and lack of dynamic multi-user or object-motion handling limit real-world applicability, and the reliance on pre-scanned meshes of variable quality remains an open practical concern. Future work on incorporating third-person perspectives, dynamic scene understanding, and 2D semantic contact inference from raw images would be needed to close the gap to fully general interactive scenarios.
