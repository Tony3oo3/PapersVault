## Problem

Diffusion-based video super-resolution suffers from two coupled challenges: the inherent randomness of diffusion sampling introduces temporal flickering across frames, and unknown complex degradations in real-world LR videos make it difficult to extract clean, stable features for reconstruction. Existing methods either prioritize temporal consistency at the cost of spatial detail (e.g., MGLD) or produce high-quality single frames without coherent temporal structure, and no prior work has combined state-space models with self-supervised degradation-robust feature learning for VSR.

## Input / Output

The method takes a low-resolution video sequence of T=8 frames at 512×512 resolution with unknown real-world degradations (blur, noise, compression, downsampling) and produces the corresponding high-resolution video at a scaling factor s. Text prompts generated by the Panda-70M model condition the diffusion process during both training and inference, and inference segments longer videos into overlapping sequences due to memory constraints, using 20 denoising steps.

## Method

SCST builds on a pretrained Stable Diffusion V2.1 latent diffusion model and introduces two key modules. First, a Spatial-Temporal Continuous Mamba (STCM) block replaces standard attention in the U-Net, using 3D depth-wise convolutions followed by K=6 continuous scanning paths (three patterns × original/flipped) that maintain uninterrupted pixel-level continuity both within and across frames, achieving global 3D receptive fields at linear complexity. Second, a MoCoCtrl module applies patch-level contrastive learning (adapting MoCo's momentum encoder framework) to align LR ControlNet features toward HR features, extracting degradation-insensitive representations. Training follows a three-stage HR-LR hybrid strategy: Stage 1 trains the ControlNet with a decaying HR/LR mixture (ratio 1→0.3), Stage 2 introduces MoCoCtrl at a fixed 1:1 ratio, and Stage 3 integrates STCM with LR-only training while freezing the ControlNet.

## Results

SCST achieves the best LPIPS and DISTS scores across all four synthetic test datasets (e.g., LPIPS 0.2518 / DISTS 0.1094 on REDS4; LPIPS 0.2156 / DISTS 0.1328 on UDM10), indicating superior perceptual quality over all baselines. On the real-world VideoLQ benchmark, SCST attains the best no-reference metrics: CLIP-IQA 0.4859, MUSIQ 59.20, NIQE 3.566, and DOVER 0.7443. However, SCST trails RealViformer and RealBasicVSR on pixel-level PSNR/SSIM (e.g., PSNR 23.02 vs. 24.28 on REDS4), reflecting the typical trade-off between generative realism and pixel fidelity in diffusion-based VSR.

## Baselines & Comparisons

SCST consistently outperforms all seven baselines on perceptual metrics (LPIPS, DISTS) across every synthetic dataset, with the largest margins over DBVSR (e.g., LPIPS 0.2156 vs. 0.4685 on UDM10) and Upscale-A-Video (LPIPS 0.2518 vs. 0.3639 on REDS4). On the real-world VideoLQ benchmark, SCST leads all methods on all four no-reference metrics, surpassing the closest competitor RealBasicVSR by a notable margin (CLIP-IQA 0.4859 vs. 0.3881; MUSIQ 59.20 vs. 55.61). However, SCST trails RealViformer and RealBasicVSR on PSNR/SSIM (e.g., PSNR 23.02 vs. 24.28 on REDS4; 26.42 vs. 27.18 on UDM10), a gap attributable to the generative nature of diffusion-based reconstruction. Among diffusion-based VSR methods specifically, SCST clearly surpasses MGLD (LPIPS 0.2518 vs. 0.2660 on REDS4) and Upscale-A-Video (LPIPS 0.2156 vs. 0.2799 on UDM10), while also achieving better temporal consistency as measured by Warping Error in ablation studies. On YouHQ40, SCST achieves the best SSIM (0.6759) among all methods, slightly edging out Bicubic (0.6787) and RealViformer (0.6730), and its PSNR (24.31) is within 0.13 dB of RealViformer (24.44).

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper sits at the intersection of three research threads: diffusion-based VSR (StableSR, Upscale-A-Video, MGLD), selective state-space models (Mamba), and self-supervised contrastive learning (MoCo). It departs from prior diffusion-based VSR methods that rely on 3D convolutions or optical-flow alignment for temporal consistency by introducing Mamba's selective scanning as a linear-complexity global 3D attention mechanism, and from prior ControlNet-based SR by adding a MoCo-style patch-level contrastive objective to make the LR encoder degradation-robust. The continuous spatial-temporal scan strategy is a deliberate departure from the standard 3D sweep scan used in prior Mamba-for-vision work (e.g., SegMamba), maintaining pixel-level continuity across frame boundaries rather than resetting at each frame.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

SCST is the first application of Mamba/SSM to video super-resolution, demonstrating that linear-complexity selective scanning can match or exceed quadratic-complexity 3D attention for temporal modeling in VSR. The MoCoCtrl module offers a practical recipe for making diffusion-based SR robust to unknown degradations without requiring paired real-world data, which is a persistent bottleneck in the field. Open questions include whether the continuous scan advantage persists at longer sequence lengths (beyond 8 frames) and higher resolutions, whether the three-stage training protocol generalizes to other degradation regimes, and whether the linear-complexity benefit of Mamba translates to meaningful speedups at the 20-step diffusion sampling cost that still dominates inference time.
