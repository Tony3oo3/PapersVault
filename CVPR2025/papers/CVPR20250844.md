## Problem

Existing 3D-aware image editing methods (e.g., E3DGE, VIVE3D) lack reference-based capabilities, while 2D reference-based editing approaches (GAN inversion, diffusion inpainting) do not support 3D consistency or local part-level control. No prior work provides a unified framework that faithfully copies specific attributes from a reference image onto a source image while maintaining 3D coherence across viewpoints.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/CVPR20250844/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method requires only a single source image and a single reference image (no paired data, no text prompts, no masks from the user). Internally, both images are encoded into triplane representations via a pre-trained EG3D encoder, and the volumetric renderer is queried at multiple camera poses to generate multi-view renderings for segmentation-based localization. The output is a fused triplane that can be rendered from any arbitrary camera pose, yielding a 3D-consistent edited image rather than a single 2D result.

## Method

The pipeline has three key stages: (1) triplane part localization, where 2D segmentation masks are backpropagated through the differentiable volumetric renderer to accumulate gradients on the triplane, producing a binarized 3D mask via mean-clipping, normalization, and thresholding; (2) implicit fusion, where the naively stitched triplane is rendered at the canonical pose, re-encoded with a W+ encoder (high-frequency branch disabled to avoid encoding seams), and re-decoded to project the result onto the generator's natural image manifold, eliminating stitching artifacts; (3) encoder fine-tuning, where the fusion encoder is jointly optimized with masked LPIPS losses on the reference region and ArcFace identity losses on the source region across multiple random viewpoints. The final triplane blends source, reference, and implicitly-fused features using morphologically eroded masks with Gaussian blurring at transition boundaries.

## Results

On CelebA, the proposed method achieves the best FID for eyeglasses addition (66.68) and hair editing (64.59), outperforming all 12 baselines on FID for eyeglasses and matching or exceeding the best hair-editing baseline (Barbershop, 62.80) while simultaneously supporting both edit types. Source preservation is also superior, with masked SSIM of 0.9818 (eyeglasses) and 0.9720 (hair) and masked L2 of 0.0021 and 0.0029 respectively, the best values across all methods. Inference time is 6.0 s on a Tesla T4, competitive with GAN-based methods (0.6–5.1 s) and substantially faster than diffusion-based baselines (9.1–25.9 s).

## Baselines & Comparisons

The method achieves the best FID for eyeglasses (66.68) over all 10 methods capable of that edit, with the closest competitor being VecGAN++ (71.47) and Paint by Example (74.18). For hair editing, Barbershop attains a marginally lower FID (62.80 vs. 64.59) but cannot perform eyeglasses edits and degrades source preservation (MSSIM 0.8756 vs. 0.9720; M_L2 0.0182 vs. 0.0029). Among GAN latent-direction methods (InterFaceGAN, StyleCLIP, SFE, E3DGE), the proposed method leads on FID by 11–40 points and on masked L2 by 2–18×, while also being the only one that is truly reference-based. Diffusion-based baselines (LEDITS++, InfEdit, NoiseCLR, Paint by Example) are 1.5–4× slower and produce substantially worse FID (74–115 for eyeglasses) and more source distortion. The 2D image-to-image methods (HiSD, VecGAN++) are fast but lack 3D consistency and show the worst reference fidelity, particularly for glasses where their low-rate latent spaces cannot capture fine structural details.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper builds directly on EG3D's triplane-based 3D-aware GAN architecture and its inversion encoders (TriplaneNet, Make Encoder Great Again), repurposing the triplane representation as an editable spatial canvas analogous to 2D image compositing. It departs from prior 3D-aware editing methods (E3DGE, VIVE3D) that rely on latent directions without reference images, and from 2D reference-based methods (Barbershop, HairCLIPv2, Paint by Example) that lack 3D consistency, by introducing spatial disentanglement via backpropagated segmentation gradients and an implicit fusion step that re-encodes the naively stitched triplane through the GAN manifold. The fine-tuned fusion encoder, trained with masked LPIPS and ArcFace identity losses, extends the standard inversion encoder into a task-specific component that resolves color and detail inconsistencies at edit boundaries.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

This work fills a concrete gap: no prior method simultaneously supports reference-based attribute transfer and 3D-consistent multi-view rendering within a single framework, a limitation the authors document across 12 baselines. The demonstrated generality across EG3D, PanoHead, AG3D, LN3Diff, and InstantMesh suggests the triplane stitching + implicit fusion recipe is a reusable primitive for any triplane-based generator. Key limitations include dependence on the underlying generator's reconstruction quality (background artifacts) and the need for per-attribute post-processing parameters. The authors identify extending the approach to DiT-based and large-reconstruction-model representations as a promising but unexplored direction, where attention mechanisms could potentially eliminate the need for canonical-space alignment.
