## Problem

Existing audio-visual saliency prediction models for omnidirectional videos treat audio location (via audio energy maps) and audio content attributes as separate entities, leading to misalignments between audio and visual content and undermining temporal consistency across frames. The interaction between spatial audio and panoramic visuals in ODVs remains underexplored, with no prior work holistically integrating both modalities through a unified, query-driven mechanism.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/CVPR20252183/cropped_page0_idx1.webp)

## Input / Output

The model consumes a sequence of T equirectangular-projection (ERP) video frames at 8K resolution paired with 4-channel ambisonic audio (W, X, Y, Z). Audio is spatially anchored by applying farthest point sampling to select N points on the spherical sound field, converting the continuous sound field into discrete spatially sensitive audio queries. The output is a per-frame audio-visual saliency map at 1/4 resolution (H/4 × W/4), predicted via a transformer decoder that fuses encoder features with refined audio queries.

## Method

CASP comprises three components: a multi-modal encoder (Find-CMP-pretrained EPSNet for ERP-aware visual features + SoundNet or ResNet-18 for audio features at N spatially anchored points), an audio-visual interaction module performing spatial fusion via self-attention on concatenated flattened features and temporal fusion via decoupled cross-attention (Audio-to-Video and Video-to-Audio), and an audio-visual transformer where a decoder uses N anchor audio queries augmented with Nq/N learnable queries to aggregate visual cues and produce saliency predictions. The key architectural insight is a query-based framework in which sparse, audio-constrained learnable queries dynamically align with visual content during decoding, rather than relying on AEMs to isolate sound source locations. A novel adaptive inter-frame consistency loss, weighted by the exponential of cosine similarity between consecutive saliency maps, enforces temporal coherence. The visual backbone is self-supervised pretrained via the Find-CMP proxy task to handle ERP geometric distortions before supervised fine-tuning on saliency.

## Results

On the AVS-ODV dataset (Ambisonics modality), CASP achieves SIM 0.444, CC 0.604, AUC-J 0.949, sAUC 0.813, and NSS 4.545, outperforming the closest competitor AVS360 (SIM 0.439, CC 0.595, NSS 3.477) by a notable margin, especially on NSS (+30.7%). On the D-SAV360 dataset, CASP attains CC 0.472 and SIM 0.390, surpassing AVS360 (CC 0.439, SIM 0.367) and DAVE (CC 0.427, SIM 0.368). CASP also maintains its lead under the Mute modality (no audio), confirming robustness of the query-based framework even without auditory cues.

## Baselines & Comparisons

CASP outperforms all nine baselines across every metric and audio modality on both datasets. The closest competitor is AVS360 [5], which also integrates spatial audio via an audio energy map; CASP surpasses it by +0.005 SIM and +1.068 NSS on Ambisonics, and by +0.033 CC and +0.023 SIM on D-SAV360. DAVE [20] is competitive on SIM (0.439 vs. 0.444) but lags substantially on NSS (3.498 vs. 4.545), indicating weaker spatial localization of salient regions. SVGC [29] and AT-Sal [10] trail by 0.05 and 0.054 in SIM respectively on Ambisonics. The 2D-video baselines TASED [16] and STSA [25] are far behind (SIM ≈ 0.17), confirming the difficulty of transferring flat-video models to the panoramic setting. Even under the Mute condition where no audio is available, CASP retains the top SIM (0.457) and NSS (3.667), demonstrating that the query-based architecture generalizes well beyond pure audio-visual fusion.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper builds on the EPSNet/Find-CMP self-supervised pretraining framework [33] for ERP-distortion-aware visual encoding, and departs from prior audio-visual ODV saliency methods (AVS360, AVPS, SVGC) that decompose audio into a separate energy map for localization and a convolutional network for content attributes. The key conceptual shift is a query-based decoder where sparse, spatially-anchored audio queries simultaneously encode both location and semantic content, replacing the AEM-plus-attribute pipeline. The temporal consistency loss further distinguishes CASP from prior work, which treats each frame's saliency independently.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

Accurate audio-visual saliency in ODVs directly enables bandwidth-efficient VR streaming, adaptive encoding, and personalized content delivery, making this practically relevant as 360° content adoption grows. The paper's main limitation is evaluation on only two datasets (162 and 85 videos) with a single GPU, and the efficiency gains, while notable, come at the cost of a 159M-parameter model that is still heavier than lightweight baselines like SVGC. Open questions include whether the query-based audio-visual alignment generalizes to longer sequences, real-time inference on edge devices, and whether the consistency loss transfers to other temporal prediction tasks beyond saliency.
