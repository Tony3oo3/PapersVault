## Problem

Existing diffusion-based NVS methods suffer from three coupled limitations: they are trained primarily on synthetic object-centric data, lack explicit 3D geometric priors that ensure multi-view consistency (especially out-of-domain), and cannot flexibly handle arbitrary combinations of reference and target views without iterative anchor-based generation, dataset updates, or per-scene optimization. No prior work unifies metric depth warping with a multi-view diffusion model to simultaneously address all three issues.

## Input / Output

MVGenMaster accepts a variable number of reference images (1–3+), their camera poses encoded as Plücker rays, and metric depth maps (predicted by Depth-Pro for single-view or Dust3R for multi-view at inference; monocular depth aligned to SfM during training). It produces up to 100 novel-view images at 768×320 resolution in a single forward pass, with no temporal positional encoding constraining view ordering. The 3D priors fed to the model are warped RGB pixels and warped Canonical Coordinate Maps projected from reference to target views via the metric depth and relative camera transformation.

## Method

MVGenMaster extends Stable Diffusion 2 (v-prediction) with 3D full self-attention across all reference and target views, replacing the text branch with Plücker ray camera embeddings and incorporating 3D priors (warped RGB + CCM) by adding lightweight CNN-encoded features directly to the latent input rather than via cross-attention. A key-rescaling technique multiplies reference-view key features by a constant γ (≈1.2) in every self-attention layer to counteract attention dilution when target view counts exceed the training range, enabling coherent 100-view generation without iterative refinement. Training on the newly curated MvD-1M dataset (1.6M scenes across 12 domains with aligned metric depth) employs a noisier ZeroSNR schedule, qk-norm, a domain switcher to harmonize heterogeneous sources, multi-scale cropping, and EMA, all contributing to the final performance gains.

## Results

On the in-domain object-centric benchmark (CO3Dv2+MVImgNet), MVGenMaster with 3 reference views achieves PSNR 18.96 / SSIM 0.583 / LPIPS 0.306 (ordered) and PSNR 21.47 / SSIM 0.653 / LPIPS 0.220 (unordered), substantially outperforming CAT3D* and ViewCrafter. On the scene-level benchmark (DL3DV+Real10k), MVGenMaster(3-view) reaches PSNR 16.18 (ordered) and 18.30 (unordered). On the zero-shot benchmark (DTU, MipNeRF-360, Tanks-and-Temples, ETH3D), MVGenMaster(3-view) achieves PSNR 13.72 / SSIM 0.418 / LPIPS 0.417 (ordered) and PSNR 15.53 / SSIM 0.491 / LPIPS 0.319 (unordered), maintaining a clear lead over both baselines.

## Baselines & Comparisons

MVGenMaster consistently outperforms both baselines across all three evaluation settings (object-centric, scene-level, and zero-shot) and both ordered/unordered viewpoint configurations. Against CAT3D* (re-implemented without 3D priors), MVGenMaster(3-view) gains approximately 1.7–4.6 dB in PSNR depending on the dataset, with the largest margin on the unordered zero-shot setting (15.53 vs. 10.88 PSNR), demonstrating the critical role of metric-depth-warped 3D priors for generalization. Against ViewCrafter, MVGenMaster(3-view) leads by 3.6–4.5 dB PSNR on in-domain benchmarks and by 2.3–4.5 dB on zero-shot, while also handling unordered viewpoints that ViewCrafter's sequential video-diffusion design cannot support. In the 3DGS reconstruction evaluation (Table 3), MVGenMaster further surpasses MVSplat by 3.1–6.2 dB PSNR in the 3-view/100-frame setting, where MVSplat fails due to insufficient view overlap. The 1-view variant of MVGenMaster already exceeds both baselines on all metrics, confirming that the 3D-prior mechanism provides gains even with minimal reference information.

## Relation to Prior Work

MVGenMaster extends CAT3D's multi-view diffusion architecture with 3D full attention by incorporating metric-depth-based geometric warping (warped RGB pixels and Canonical Coordinate Maps from SweetDreamer) as explicit 3D priors, a key departure from prior diffusion-based NVS methods that rely solely on 2D generation. It also builds on Stable Diffusion 2's latent diffusion backbone, adopts Plücker ray encoding for dense camera pose representation, and repurposes the domain switcher from Wonder3D to handle heterogeneous training data. Unlike video-based NVS approaches (ViewCrafter, ReconX) that are constrained by temporal positional encoding and sequential generation, MVGenMaster operates without temporal encoding, enabling unordered and arbitrary view synthesis in a single forward pass.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The MvD-1M dataset (1.6M scenes with aligned metric depth across 12 domains) is a substantial resource that addresses the data limitation bottleneck in scene-level NVS training. The key-rescaling technique—a training-free constant multiplier on reference-view attention keys—offers a simple, FlashAttention2-compatible solution to the attention dilution problem in long-sequence generation, potentially applicable beyond NVS. Limitations include dependence on depth estimation quality (Depth-Pro/Dust3R), a 158-view upper bound on 80G GPUs, and still-modest zero-shot PSNR gains (~1–2 dB over baselines). Open questions include whether the approach generalizes to even longer sequences, whether 3D priors can be made robust to significant depth errors, and whether the single-forward-pass paradigm can be extended to video-length generation.
