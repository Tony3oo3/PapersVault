## Problem

Large-scale bi-temporal semantic change detection (SCD) in Very High Resolution imagery remains poorly addressed: no publicly available dataset combines sufficient size, multi-class semantic labels, and VHR resolution. Existing synthetic or hybrid datasets are limited to single-class (building) changes, restricted geographies, or lack semantic consistency between image pairs, preventing effective transfer to real-world SCD tasks.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/CVPR20251127/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The pipeline consumes a single-temporal VHR image (512×512 px, 0.2 m GSD, 5-band: RGB + infrared + elevation), its 16-class semantic segmentation map, and ~300k open-source instance masks. It outputs a bi-temporal pair (original image I₁ and inpainted image I₂), corresponding semantic maps M₁ and M₂, and a derived change map C, packaged as the FSC-180k dataset (~80 billion pixels, 180k synthetic images). The 5-band output is notable as the first satellite image synthesis to incorporate infrared and elevation channels alongside RGB.

## Method

HySCDG fine-tunes a Stable Diffusion 2 Inpainting model in three stages: (1) adapting the VAE with added L2, Focal, and local color losses to compress multi-band VHR textures without oversaturation; (2) fine-tuning the diffusion U-Net for VHR inpainting with geographically and semantically informed prompts; and (3) training a ControlNet conditioned on semantic class maps to enforce label-consistent generation. The 'Select and Inpaint' mechanism randomly picks instances from the land cover map, reassigns their class labels, and inpaints the corresponding regions with SD+CN, while simultaneously inpainting random unchanged areas to suppress high-frequency inpainting signatures. A spatial buffer around each footprint and weighted class selection (inverse-frequency within the change zone) ensure diverse, realistic multi-class changes at a ~5% prevalence matching European real-world scenarios.

## Results

Pretraining on FSC-180k consistently outperforms both the no-pretraining baseline and the fully synthetic SyntheWorld across all five target datasets and four transfer scenarios. In sequential semantic change detection, FSC-180k pretraining yields SCS of 0.89 on SECOND and 0.78 on HiUCD-mini (vs. 0.83/0.73 baseline and 0.84/0.73 SyntheWorld), and Chg.mIoU of 0.19 on HiUCD-XL (vs. 0.17 for both baselines). In the binary case, FSC-180k provides 2–3% IoU gains over SyntheWorld on all targets. Under zero-shot evaluation, FSC-180k achieves IoU 0.37 on SECOND and 0.36 on HiUCD-mini, while SyntheWorld struggles to transfer effectively without target exposure.

## Baselines & Comparisons

Against SyntheWorld [53], the main competing pretraining dataset, FSC-180k wins on every dataset and every metric tested. On HiUCD-mini, FSC-180k pretraining achieves SCS 0.78 versus 0.73 for SyntheWorld (and 0.73 for the no-pretraining baseline), a relative gain of 8% over SyntheWorld; on SECOND, FSC-180k reaches SCS 0.89 versus 0.84 for SyntheWorld; on HiUCD-XL, FSC-180k improves Chg. mIoU from 0.17 to 0.19 while SyntheWorld shows no improvement over the baseline. In the binary setting, FSC-180k consistently yields 2–3% higher IoU than SyntheWorld across all five targets. Against SCanNet [13] as an alternative architecture, the comparison is mixed: SCanNet slightly edges out the Dual UNet on SECOND without pretraining (SCS 0.84 vs. 0.83), but the Dual UNet clearly outperforms SCanNet on HiUCD-mini (SCS 0.73 vs. 0.66 without pretraining; 0.78 vs. 0.71 with FSC-180k), suggesting the Dual UNet's multi-task design is better suited to semantically diverse scenes.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the hybrid data generation paradigm for change detection (Changen, Changen2, IAug) by replacing GAN-based or copy-paste inpainting with a fine-tuned Stable Diffusion + ControlNet pipeline, enabling semantically controlled multi-class modification of real VHR images. It departs from fully synthetic 3D-rendering approaches (SyntheWorld, SynCW, SMARS) by grounding generation in actual aerial imagery from the FLAIR dataset, preserving realistic textures and geographic context. The change detection model itself (Dual UNet) follows the multi-task architecture of Daudt et al. (2019), keeping the focus squarely on the generation pipeline and dataset rather than architectural innovation.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

FSC-180k is the first open-access, large-scale (80B pixels, 0.2 m GSD) hybrid semantic change detection dataset covering 16 land-cover classes, filling a critical gap where prior synthetic datasets offered at most 8 classes and were often restricted to building-only changes. The consistent outperformance of hybrid over fully synthetic pretraining across all four transfer scenarios (sequential, mixed, low-data, zero-shot) provides strong evidence that realism in the source imagery matters more than sheer synthetic diversity. Open questions include whether the pipeline generalizes beyond European landscapes, whether the 5% change prevalence assumption holds globally, and whether the inpainting signature can be further mitigated for downstream forensic or detection tasks.
