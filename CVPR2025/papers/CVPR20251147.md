## Problem

NeRF and 3DGS reconstructions produce persistent artifacts—spurious geometry, missing regions, and blurriness—when rendering from extreme novel viewpoints or underconstrained areas, and existing methods that leverage 2D diffusion priors either query the diffusion model at every training step (making them >10× slower) or are limited to object-centric scenes. The paper addresses how to efficiently distill 2D generative priors into 3D reconstruction for large, in-the-wild scenes while preserving multi-view consistency.

## Input / Output

DIFIX takes a single rendered novel view (containing NeRF/3DGS artifacts) and a set of clean reference views (selected as the closest training views) as input, operating in the VAE latent space of a U-Net. It outputs an enhanced, artifact-reduced novel view in a single forward pass (76 ms on an A100). The broader DIFIX3D+ pipeline additionally consumes a pretrained 3D representation and camera poses, and produces both an improved 3D representation (via progressive distillation of DIFIX-enhanced pseudo-views) and a final real-time post-processed rendered view at inference.

## Method

The core insight is that the distribution of images degraded by neural rendering artifacts closely matches the distribution of Gaussian-noised images at a moderate noise level (τ=200) in a pretrained diffusion model, enabling DIFIX to be trained as a single-step 'denoiser' that directly maps artifact-laden renders to clean images. DIFIX is fine-tuned from SD-Turbo using a frozen VAE encoder, a LoRA decoder, and a novel reference mixing layer that reshapes the view axis into the spatial axis for self-attention, allowing cross-view conditioning while inheriting all 2D self-attention weights. Training uses L2 + LPIPS + Gram matrix (VGG-16) losses on paired artifact-clean image pairs curated via sparse reconstruction, cycle reconstruction, model underfitting, and cross-reference strategies. The DIFIX3D pipeline then progressively perturbs camera poses toward target views, renders and enhances novel views with DIFIX, and distills them back into the 3D representation over multiple 1.5k-iteration cycles, gradually expanding the spatial extent of well-conditioned 3D cues. DIFIX3D+ adds a final single-step DIFIX pass at render time as a real-time neural enhancer to remove residual artifacts.

## Results

On the Nerfbusters dataset, DIFIX3D+ (3DGS backbone) achieves the best PSNR of 18.51 dB, SSIM of 0.6858, LPIPS of 0.2637, and FID of 41.77, outperforming all baselines across every metric. On DL3DV, DIFIX3D+ (3DGS) reaches PSNR 17.99, SSIM 0.6015, LPIPS 0.2932, and FID 40.86. The headline improvement is a ~2× reduction in FID (e.g., 41.77 vs. 113.84 for raw 3DGS on Nerfbusters) and a ~1 dB PSNR gain, while LPIPS drops by ~0.1 relative to the unenhanced backbones. The same DIFIX model works for both NeRF (Nerfacto) and 3DGS representations without modification.

## Baselines & Comparisons

DIFIX3D+ outperforms all five external baselines (Nerfbusters, GANeRF, NeRFLiX, Nerfacto, 3DGS) on every metric across both Nerfbusters and DL3DV. The most dramatic gap is in FID: DIFIX3D+ (3DGS) achieves 41.77 on Nerfbusters versus 113.84 for raw 3DGS and 116.83 for Nerfbusters, a roughly 2.7× improvement. On PSNR, DIFIX3D+ (3DGS) leads by 0.85 dB over NeRFLiX (17.91) and 0.85 dB over Nerfbusters (17.72) on Nerfbusters. GANeRF and NeRFLiX, which use per-scene GANs or deterministic view aggregation respectively, fail to close the FID gap (81–116 range) and also trail on LPIPS. The paper also reports results on an in-house automotive RDS dataset (Table 3), where Nerfacto+DIFIX3D+ achieves PSNR 21.75 and FID 73.08, again beating Nerfacto (19.95 / 91.38) and Nerfacto+NeRFLiX (20.44 / 116.28) on all four metrics.

## Relation to Prior Work

DIFIX3D+ builds on the single-step diffusion distillation line (SD-Turbo, Pix2Pix-Turbo) by repurposing a text-to-image model as a 3D artifact fixer, and on Instruct-NeRF2NeRF's progressive 3D update scheme to ensure multi-view consistency when distilling diffusion outputs back into the scene. It departs from concurrent work (Deceptive-NeRF, 3D-Gaussian-Enhancer) in two key ways: a progressive (rather than one-shot) 3D update pipeline that preserves long-range consistency, and dual deployment of the same model both during optimization and as a real-time inference-time enhancer. Unlike methods that query a diffusion model at every training step (ReConfusion, DreamFusion, Zero-1-to-3), the single-step formulation reduces overhead by >10× while retaining enough generative prior to hallucinate plausible geometry in underconstrained regions.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The practical impact is significant: a single 76 ms post-processing step on an A100 GPU yields ~2× FID improvement over both NeRF and 3DGS baselines while preserving 3D consistency, making diffusion-based enhancement viable for real-time rendering pipelines. The generality across representations (NeRF and 3DGS) and scene types (in-the-wild, driving) suggests broad applicability. Open questions include how the progressive update scales to very large or dynamic scenes, whether the artifact-noise distribution similarity (τ=200) holds for other degradation types (e.g., motion blur, lighting changes), and whether the approach can be extended to video-level temporal consistency without per-frame flickering.
