## Problem

Existing MLLMs can perform either spatial localization (e.g., referring expression comprehension) or temporal grounding (e.g., temporal video grounding), but none can simultaneously handle both dimensions or spatial-temporal interleaved tasks in an end-to-end manner. This limitation stems from two compounding difficulties: the combinatorial explosion of coordinate space when both spatial and temporal axes are involved makes cross-modal alignment hard, and standard video feature compression (Q-Former, pooling) inevitably destroys the fine-grained spatial-temporal relationships needed for precise localization.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/CVPR20251718/cropped_page0_idx2.webp)

## Input / Output

The model accepts video clips (100 uniformly sampled frames) or single images, encoded by SigLIP-400M at 384x384 resolution, alongside a free-form text prompt. Outputs are generated as natural language sequences in which spatial coordinates and timestamps are expressed as discrete special tokens (100 anchor points per dimension for width, height, and time), so a spatiotemporal tube is emitted as a sequence of coordinate tokens rather than continuous numeric values. The same 7B model handles all task types (REC, TVG, STVG, ELC, SVG, VideoQA) by simply changing the prompt, with no task-specific heads.

## Method

LLaVA-ST introduces two key architectural modules. Language-Aligned Positional Embedding (LAPE) repurposes the row vectors of the LLM's output projection matrix corresponding to coordinate special tokens as positional embeddings added directly to visual features, so the model aligns text-space coordinate representations with visual-space positions without learning a separate cross-modal mapping. The Spatial-Temporal Packer (STP) compresses video features in two stages: first a point-to-region attention reduces spatial resolution (k1=9 patches), then separate packers compress spatial (k2=3) and temporal (sigma=20) dimensions independently, preserving fine-grained context better than naive pooling. Training follows a three-stage progressive schedule: content alignment (freeze LLM, train STP on captions), coordinate alignment (LoRA on LLM plus STP on spatial/temporal grounding data), and multi-task instruction tuning (new LoRA on 39 datasets including 228K newly collected spatial-temporal interleaved samples from VidSTG), all within the LLaVA-OneVision 7B backbone using LoRA (alpha=128, r=256) on 48 A100s for 72 hours.

## Results

On the novel ST-Align benchmark, LLaVA-ST achieves m_tIoU of 43.8 and m_sIoU of 22.8 on Spatial-Temporal Video Grounding (STVG), outperforming Grounded-VideoLLM by 10.8 in m_tIoU and GroundingGPT by 13.6 in m_sIoU. On Charades-STA for Temporal Video Grounding, LLaVA-ST reaches 42.4 mIoU, surpassing the previous best (Grounded-VideoLLM, 36.8) by 5.6 points. On MVBench multi-choice video QA, LLaVA-ST attains the best average score of 64.2, improving by 4.8 over Grounded-VideoLLM (59.4), and achieves the highest VCG-Bench average of 3.32 on open-ended video QA.

## Baselines & Comparisons

LLaVA-ST consistently outperforms all baselines across every evaluated benchmark. Against Grounded-VideoLLM [59] (the strongest prior MLLM for temporal grounding), LLaVA-ST gains +10.8 m_tIoU on STVG, +3.6 m_tIoU on ELC, +5.6 mIoU on Charades-STA TVG, and +4.8 average on MVBench. Against GroundingGPT [37] (the strongest prior model for spatial grounding), LLaVA-ST achieves dramatically larger spatial improvements: +13.6 m_sIoU on STVG, +27.1 m_sIoU on ELC, and +14.6 m_sIoU on SVG, confirming that prior models cannot handle spatial-temporal interleaved tasks end-to-end. On REC (RefCOCO/RefCOCO+/RefCOCOg), LLaVA-ST (90.1/86.0/86.7 val) edges out Groma-7B [42] (89.5/83.9/86.3) by 0.6–2.1 points, matching the best non-full-resolution model. On open-ended VideoQA, LLaVA-ST (VCG-Bench avg 3.32) narrowly surpasses VideoGPT+ [46] (3.28) and Grounded-VideoLLM (3.24), showing that fine-grained grounding training does not degrade general video understanding. No baseline is beaten by LLaVA-ST on any reported metric; the closest margin is on VCG-Bench where the gap over VideoGPT+ is only 0.04.

## Relation to Prior Work

LLaVA-ST builds directly on LLaVA-OneVision 7B as its backbone, extending the LLaVA family of MLLMs from general visual understanding to fine-grained coordinate-based tasks. It departs from prior spatial-only MLLMs (Shikra, Groma, GroundingGPT) and temporal-only MLLMs (VTimeLLM, Grounded-VideoLLM, Momentor) by unifying both modalities of coordinate output within a single end-to-end model. The key architectural departure is LAPE, which repurposes the LLM's output-layer weight vectors as visual positional embeddings to bypass the combinatorial difficulty of cross-modal coordinate alignment, and STP, which decouples temporal and spatial compression into separate point-to-region attention streams rather than using monolithic Q-Former or pooling-based compression.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

This is the first MLLM to handle spatial, temporal, and spatial-temporal interleaved grounding tasks in a unified end-to-end framework, and the accompanying ST-Align dataset (4.3M samples) and benchmark fill a notable gap in training and evaluation data for spatiotemporal tube localization. The ablation finding that spatial-temporal interleaved data improves pure temporal grounding performance (mIoU +3.9 on Charades-STA) suggests a productive direction: cross-dimensional grounding signals are mutually reinforcing rather than competing. Open questions include whether the 100-frame sampling and 384×384 resolution generalize to longer or higher-resolution videos, whether the discrete coordinate tokenization (M=100 per axis) is sufficient for truly fine-grained localization, and whether the progressive three-stage training recipe transfers to other base architectures.
