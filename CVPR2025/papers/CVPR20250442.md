## Problem

Designing effective 4D point cloud video backbones is challenging due to the irregular, unordered spatial distribution of points and temporal inconsistencies across frames. Existing transformer-based 4D backbones suffer from quadratic computational complexity, making them impractical for long video sequences, while CNN-based alternatives have locally constrained receptive fields that limit long-range temporal modeling. No prior work has explored a pure State Space Model backbone for generic 4D point cloud video understanding.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/CVPR20250442/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method consumes raw 4D point cloud video sequences where each of T frames contains N points with 3D coordinates (x,y,z) and C-dimensional features, partitioned into short clips around stride-sampled anchor frames. Spatial anchor points are selected via Farthest Point Sampling at a downsampling rate s_s, and KNN neighbors within a spatial radius k_s form tubelike spatio-temporal intervals. Outputs are task-specific: a single video-level action class, per-frame action labels for segmentation, or per-point semantic class labels, all produced by lightweight task heads on top of the shared Mamba4D backbone.

## Method

Mamba4D disentangles space and time into two hierarchical Mamba modules: an Intra-frame Spatial Mamba (12 blocks) that encodes local geometric structures within short-term point tubes using 4D positional encoding (x,y,z,t) and a temporal max-pooling layer, and an Inter-frame Temporal Mamba (4 blocks) that integrates long-range temporal dependencies across all anchor frames with linear complexity. The inter-frame module employs two scanning strategies—Temporally Sequential Scanning (spatial order within each frame, then temporal order) and Cross-temporal Scanning (spatial order across all frames simultaneously)—to capture 4D causality despite Mamba's unidirectional modeling limitation. The architecture replaces both the Point 4D Convolution and transformer layers in P4Transformer, achieving 87.5% GPU memory reduction and 5.36× speedup while improving accuracy on all three benchmark tasks.

## Results

On MSR-Action3D action recognition, Mamba4D achieves 93.23% accuracy with 36 input frames, outperforming the P4Transformer baseline (82.81%) by over 10 points and steadily improving with longer sequences while P4Transformer degrades. On HOI4D action segmentation, Mamba4D reaches 85.5% frame-level accuracy and 92.6 F1@10, surpassing the prior SOTA X4D-SceneFormer (84.1% Acc, 92.5 F1@10). On Synthia4D semantic segmentation, Mamba4D achieves 83.35 mIoU with 3 frames, a 0.19 mIoU gain over P4Transformer. In terms of efficiency, Mamba4D reduces GPU memory by 87.5% and achieves a 5.36× speedup over P4Transformer.

## Baselines & Comparisons

Against the primary transformer baseline P4Transformer [8], Mamba4D wins decisively on MSR-Action3D: at 24 frames it gains +1.74% (92.68 vs 90.94), and the gap widens to +10.42% at 36 frames (93.23 vs 82.81), demonstrating superior long-sequence modeling. Against the stronger PST-Transformer [10] (93.03% at 24 frames), Mamba4D still leads by +0.35% (93.38%) when integrated into that backbone. On HOI4D, Mamba4D (built on X4D-SceneFormer [17]) edges out the prior SOTA by +1.4% Acc and +0.1 F1@10, and substantially outperforms PPTr+C2P [52,57] by +4.4% Acc and +7.2 F1@10. On Synthia4D, Mamba4D (83.35 mIoU) beats P4Transformer (83.16) and all CNN baselines (MeteorNet-l at 81.80, 4D MinkNet14 at 77.46), though the margin over P4Transformer is modest since segmentation is less time-dependent. The efficiency comparison is the most striking: replacing P4Transformer's CNN+transformer with Mamba blocks yields 87.5% GPU memory reduction and 5.36× faster inference, a gap that grows with sequence length.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper replaces the dominant CNN-local + Transformer-global paradigm in 4D point cloud video backbones (P4Transformer, PST-Transformer) with a purely SSM-based architecture, drawing the intra-frame block design from PointMamba and the stride-based anchor-frame partitioning from PST-Net. The key conceptual departure is the disentangled spatial-temporal Mamba design: an Intra-frame Spatial Mamba handles local geometric structure within short clips, while an Inter-frame Temporal Mamba captures long-range temporal dependencies with linear complexity. Unlike prior Mamba-for-vision work (2D images, 3D point clouds, RGB video), this is the first application of SSMs as a generic 4D point cloud video backbone spanning multiple downstream tasks.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The 87.5% GPU memory reduction and 5.36× speedup over transformer-based 4D backbones make long-sequence point cloud video processing practically feasible, addressing a key bottleneck that has limited real-world deployment in robotics, AR/VR, and SLAM. However, the accuracy gains over strong transformer baselines are modest (+0.7 F1 on HOI4D, +0.19 mIoU on Synthia4D), suggesting SSMs match but do not yet surpass transformers in representational quality for 4D understanding. Open questions include whether the scanning strategies generalize to more complex dynamic scenes, whether deeper Mamba stacks close the remaining gap, and whether the approach scales to very long (hundreds of frames) sequences where the linear-complexity advantage should be most pronounced.
