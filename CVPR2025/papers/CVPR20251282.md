## Problem

Existing continual test-time adaptation (CTTA) methods for 3D LiDAR segmentation rely on computationally expensive backpropagation and large batch sizes, limiting their practicality for real-time autonomous driving. Moreover, they largely ignore the unique density distributional properties of LiDAR point clouds and the high inter-class feature correlations that cause category confusion under domain shift.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/CVPR20251282/cropped_page2_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method consumes a single unlabeled LiDAR point cloud scan (one frame) from a continuously shifting target domain in a strict one-pass online setting, with no access to source domain data. The pre-trained MinkowskiNet backbone extracts 96-dimensional point features, which are projected to 1024 dimensions for decorrelation. The point cloud is spatially partitioned into three distance-based regions (within 0–50 m) to account for density variation. Output is per-point semantic class predictions, with the final label determined by the domain-specific decorrelated classifier rather than the original linear head.

## Method

D³CTTA is a backpropagation-free framework with two core modules. The Distance-Aware Prototype Learning (DAPL) module constructs separate prototypes per class per distance region, updated via exponential moving average, and selects reliable pseudo-labels using KNN spatial consistency (ratio of neighbors sharing the same label > 0.8) rather than entropy or confidence thresholds. The Domain-Specific Decorrelation (DSD) module applies a fixed random projection (RanPAC-style) followed by cumulative Gram-matrix decorrelation, maintaining distinct Gram matrices and prototypical features for each detected domain. Domain shifts are detected by comparing BN statistics of consecutive frames via cosine similarity (threshold 0.85) and matched to known domains via Wasserstein distance, enabling the model to retain and reuse domain-specific decorrelation parameters across repeated encounters.

## Results

On the primary Synth4D→SemanticKITTI-C benchmark, D³CTTA achieves a mean mIoU of 30.82%, improving by 27.1% over the unadapted source model (24.25%) and 5.7% over the previous SOTA CTTA method EATA (29.15%), all without backpropagation. On the sparser Synth4D→nuScenes-C benchmark, the method reaches 24.15% mIoU, exceeding the source model by 16.4% and EATA by 1.6%. In the repeated-domain setting (3 rounds), D³CTTA shows steady improvement (31.14→31.19→31.27 mIoU) while backpropagation-based baselines like EATA degrade (29.35→28.99→28.34).

## Baselines & Comparisons

D³CTTA outperforms all five baselines on both benchmarks. Against the strongest CTTA baseline EATA [28], it gains +1.67 mIoU on SemanticKITTI-C (30.82 vs 29.15) and +0.39 on nuScenes-C (24.15 vs 23.76), while also showing stable or improving performance across repeated domain rounds where EATA degrades. The 3D TTA method GIPSO [33] (25.55 mIoU) is surpassed by 5.27 points, with D³CTTA particularly strong in crosstalk (+5.67) and motion blur (+16.12) domains. ViDA [23] (24.47) and the backpropagation-free T3A [14] (24.00) trail by 6.35 and 6.82 points respectively, with D³CTTA excelling in fog, motion, and snow domains where these methods struggle. TENT [40] performs worst among all baselines (12.22 mIoU), confirming that naive entropy minimization is ineffective under large 3D domain shifts. Notably, D³CTTA achieves these gains without any backpropagation, whereas EATA, GIPSO, ViDA, and TENT all require gradient-based updates.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the prototype-based TTA framework of T3A (Iwasawa & Matsuo, 2021) by introducing distance-aware prototypes that account for LiDAR point density variations across sensor ranges, and adapts the RanPAC (McDonnell et al., 2024) decorrelation mechanism into a domain-specific variant for 3D point clouds. It departs from the dominant backpropagation-based CTTA line (CoTTA, EATA, ViDA) by proposing a fully online, optimization-free pipeline, and from prior 3D TTA work (GIPSO) by addressing continual rather than single-domain adaptation. The key conceptual departure is making both prototype learning and decorrelation domain-aware, motivated by the observation that different corruption domains induce distinct feature correlations that a global decorrelation matrix cannot handle.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The backpropagation-free design makes the method practical for real-time autonomous driving where large batch sizes and gradient computation are infeasible, and the domain-specific parameter retention enables stable performance across repeated domain visits without catastrophic forgetting. A notable open question highlighted by the authors is that pseudo-label accuracy does not reliably predict final segmentation quality under large domain shifts, suggesting that spatially coherent but slightly inaccurate labels may be more useful than confident but biased ones. The method still relies on a simple distance-based geometric prior rather than richer 3D structure, and performance degrades in sparser point cloud settings (nuScenes), leaving room for more sophisticated geometric reasoning.
