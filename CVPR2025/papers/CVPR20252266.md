## Problem

Concept-based post-hoc XAI methods for deep vision models rely on manual annotation to construct concept explanation datasets, making them inflexible and labor-intensive for new models or scenarios. More critically, the intrinsic polysemanticity of visual concepts—where a single channel encodes multiple coupled semantics (e.g., ~13 concept atoms per channel in ResNet-152)—is severely underestimated, leading to ambiguous explanations and no systematic way to quantify or resolve this uncertainty.

## Input / Output

The method consumes a pre-trained DVM (ResNet-152 or CLIP), an image dataset (ImageNet), and a single input image for local explanation. It produces two structured outputs: a global concept explanation dataset mapping each channel to its visual concept, 15 representative image patches, disentangled monosemantic concept atoms with probability distributions, and a CPE score; and a local explanation chain that traces the concept circuit bottom-up across key layers, presenting filtered concept atoms with relevance values in a CoT-style natural language narrative. The CPE score is computed at three granularities (channel, layer, model) as a normalized entropy over atom frequencies.

## Method

CoE is a three-step pipeline: (1) ACD automatically decodes VCs via CRP and uses GPT-4o as a describer to generate linguistic descriptions, replacing manual annotation; (2) CPDF disentangles each polysemantic VC into orthogonal concept atoms by prompting an LVLM with 13 semantic directions (object, scene, color, texture, material, position, etc.) across all 15 image patches, removes semantically redundant atoms via bidirectional NLI entailment checking (DeBERTa), and contextually filters the most relevant atom per input using an LLM with image captioning; (3) A local explanation chain is constructed by identifying top-k relevant concepts per layer via CRP relevance values, assembling filtered atoms into a bottom-up circuit path, and synthesizing a CoT-style natural language explanation via GPT-4. The CPE score formalizes polysemanticity as normalized Shannon entropy over the atom probability distribution, upgrading deterministic concept modeling to uncertain atom distributions.

## Results

On ImageNet Validation (500 samples for GPT-4o, 100 for human evaluation), CoE achieves total explanation scores of 5.06/6 (GPT-4o) and 5.04/6 (human), substantially outperforming the MILAN ANNOTATION + Description baseline (3.14 and 2.62 respectively). The average absolute improvement over the baseline is 36%. The CPE metric achieves 75% agreement with human scorers on polysemanticity ranking across 300 randomly sampled VC pairs.

## Baselines & Comparisons

The primary baseline is MILAN ANNOTATION + Description [Hernandez et al., ICLR 2022], which uses manually annotated concept descriptions without automatic disentanglement or contextual filtering. CoE outperforms this baseline by 1.92 points (GPT-4o) and 2.42 points (human) in total explanation score, a 36% average absolute improvement. The gain is driven primarily by the CPDF mechanism: the ACD dataset alone (CoE w/o Filtering) yields only a modest 0.11–0.72 point increase over the baseline, while adding the full polysemanticity disentanglement and contextual filtering step contributes an additional 1.81 (GPT-4o) and 1.70 (human) points. CoE wins on all three sub-metrics (Accuracy, Completeness, User Interpretability) under both evaluators, with the largest relative gains in Accuracy (1.69 vs. 1.03 for GPT-4o; 1.73 vs. 0.87 for human), indicating that contextual atom filtering is critical for producing factually correct explanations.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the concept-based post-hoc XAI lineage (MILAN, CRP, concept circuits) by replacing manual concept annotation with LVLM-driven automatic decoding and description, and by introducing a systematic polysemanticity disentanglement and quantification mechanism (CPDF + CPE) that prior methods either ignored or addressed only indirectly through predefined subspaces. It builds on the concept circuit framework for local explanations but departs from it by upgrading deterministic concept nodes to uncertain atom distributions and wrapping the entire pipeline in a Chain-of-Thought-style linguistic synthesis. The 13 semantic directions prompted for disentanglement substantially exceed the 5 concept types used in prior annotation efforts, and the NLI-based redundancy filtering is a new component not present in earlier concept explanation pipelines.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The CPE metric offers the first systematic, multi-granularity quantification of concept polysemanticity as an interpretability indicator, filling a gap the authors argue has been 'underestimated severely' in the XAI literature. However, the framework's practical utility is constrained by its heavy dependence on proprietary LLMs (GPT-4o for description, DeBERTa for entailment, GPT-4 for synthesis), which limits reproducibility and scalability. Evaluation is limited to two architectures (ResNet-152, CLIP) on ImageNet, and the 75% CPE–human agreement, while reasonable, leaves room for improvement. Open questions include whether the 13 prompted semantic directions generalize to domains like medical imaging or autonomous driving, and whether the CPE metric holds for transformer-based vision models beyond CLIP.
