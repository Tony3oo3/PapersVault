## Problem

Existing text-guided 3D human generation methods rely solely on text prompts, cannot handle image inputs like user portraits for identity-preserving avatar creation, and suffer from prolonged training times with results that lack fine facial and garment details. While 2D human-centric diffusion models (e.g., IP-Adapter, VTON models) achieve strong visual fidelity, their potential for generating detailed identity-preserving 3D humans from multi-modal inputs remains largely untapped.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/CVPR20251087/cropped_page2_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method takes a text prompt describing the desired clothing and a single user portrait image providing facial identity as input. It produces a 3D human avatar represented as 3D Gaussian Splatting, initialized by densely sampling from a neutral SMPL-X mesh, which supports real-time rendering. The portrait is consumed through IP-Adapter-FaceID-PlusV2's identity embedding, while the text prompt conditions the diffusion prior alongside a pose-conditioned ControlNet that provides view-dependent skeleton visibility cues to mitigate the Janus problem.

## Method

GaussianIP is a two-stage framework. In stage 1, Adaptive Human Distillation Sampling (AHDS) guides 3DGS training: the Human Distillation Sampling (HDS) component decomposes the SDS score difference into a rectifier term and a conditional term, replacing the noisy residual with an identity-preserving score difference that incorporates the face image via IP-Adapter, while an adaptive timestep scheduling strategy maps a dual-piecewise Gaussian PDF to three training phases (coarse geometry → middle textures → fine facial/garment details), reducing training steps by ~30% over standard SDS. In stage 2, the View-Consistent Refinement (VCR) mechanism denoises four main views first, then refines key views via mutual attention with main-view features, and propagates refinement to intermediate views through relative-distance-guided weighted attention fusion, ensuring cross-view texture consistency. The refined multi-view images are then used to optimize the 3DGS via L1 + LPIPS reconstruction losses for 800 steps, yielding a polished avatar without sacrificing 3D coherence.

## Results

GaussianIP achieves the highest scores across all five user-study criteria: Facial Detail (4.71/5), Clothing Texture Richness (4.50/5), Overall Visual Quality (4.17/5), Text Prompt Alignment (4.62/5), and ChatGPT-4o Score (4.52/5). Training completes in approximately 40 minutes on a single V100 GPU, compared to 1–3 hours for all baselines. Identity preservation is verified via Face++ with an average confidence exceeding 83%, a capability none of the compared methods support.

## Baselines & Comparisons

GaussianIP outperforms all five baselines on every quality metric. The closest competitor, HumanGaussian [34], is surpassed by 0.42 on Facial Detail, 0.33 on Clothing Texture, 0.21 on Visual Quality, 0.20 on Text Alignment, and 0.44 on GPT Score, while also training roughly 3× faster (40 min vs. 1.2 h). X-Oscar [39] achieves the second-best Clothing Texture (3.83) and Text Alignment (3.63) but requires 3 h of training—nearly 5× longer than GaussianIP. DreamWaltz [17] and TADA [32] lag substantially on all criteria, with GPT scores of 1.82 and 3.24 respectively. Critically, GaussianIP is the only method that accepts a user portrait as input and preserves facial identity (verified at >83% Face++ confidence), a capability entirely absent from all baselines, which are text-only.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the Score Distillation Sampling paradigm (DreamFusion) and its noise-free decomposition (Katzir et al.) to the 3D human domain by replacing general-purpose diffusion priors with a human-centric stack (IP-Adapter-FaceID-PlusV2 + pose ControlNet), and by redesigning the score difference to explicitly encode identity conditions. It adopts 3D Gaussian Splatting as the 3D representation, following the recent shift from NeRF-based methods (DreamWaltz, AvatarVerse) toward 3DGS for efficiency, and initializes Gaussians on a SMPL-X mesh as in HumanGaussian. The key conceptual departure is the adaptive timestep scheduling that mirrors the coarse-to-fine structure of 2D human denoising, and the view-consistent refinement stage that uses cross-view attention fusion rather than independent per-view denoising.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

GaussianIP is the first method to jointly handle text and image (portrait) prompts for 3D human avatar generation, opening a practical pathway for identity-preserving avatars in AR/VR and virtual try-on applications. The 30% reduction in training steps and the >83% Face++ identity confidence demonstrate that human-centric priors can be distilled more efficiently than general priors for this task. However, the authors acknowledge limitations with complex poses and extreme garment textures, and the framework does not yet handle human-object or human-human interactions, leaving significant room for extension. The VCR mechanism, while effective, is a relatively narrow refinement strategy whose generalizability to other 3D generation tasks remains untested.
