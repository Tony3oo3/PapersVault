## Problem

Single-view 3D scene reconstruction is ill-posed due to heavy occlusions and complex multi-object arrangements. Existing feed-forward methods reconstruct objects independently, ignoring scene context and inter-object relationships, which produces intersecting or floating objects and noisy, incomplete shapes. The paper addresses this by formulating reconstruction as a conditional generative process that jointly models all objects with a learned scene prior.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/NEURIPS20240738/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method takes a single RGB image of an indoor scene as input, processed through a frozen Mask2Former (Swin Transformer backbone) to extract per-object 2D bounding boxes, 8×8×256 image feature patches, and semantic class labels. It outputs 7-DoF poses (3D center via 2D offset + distance, spatial size, and yaw orientation) and 3D shapes (16 anisotropic Gaussians with 512-dim latent features, decoded to occupancy fields and meshes via Marching Cubes) for all detected objects simultaneously. Ground-truth depth maps and instance masks are used only during training for the surface alignment loss, not at inference.

## Method

The core contribution is an image-conditioned 3D scene diffusion model that simultaneously denoises poses and shapes of all objects, framed as a conditional generation process with classifier-free guidance (p=0.8). A key architectural element is Intra-Scene Attention (ISA), a multi-head attention module applied at every denoising step that allows all objects to exchange relational information, learning a joint scene prior over the full object set rather than per-object marginals. Shapes are represented via a SPAGHETTI-style Gaussian scaffolding (16 oriented anisotropic 3D Gaussians + per-Gaussian latent features), denoised by a Transformer, while poses are denoised by a 1D UNet. A novel surface alignment loss (L_align) computes a 1-sided Chamfer Distance between points sampled directly from the intermediate Gaussian scaffolding (transformed by the predicted pose into camera space) and instance-segmented ground-truth depth surface points, enabling joint pose-shape training even when full 3D annotations are unavailable. The total loss combines standard diffusion noise-prediction losses for pose and shape with the alignment loss weighted by λ=0.01.

## Results

On SUN RGB-D, the method achieves 36.10 IoU3D, 58.18 AP3D^15, and 0.99 L_align, outperforming all baselines by significant margins. On Pix3D, it achieves 15.05 Chamfer Distance and 58.71 F-Score, representing a 13.43% F-Score improvement over the strongest shape baseline (InstPIFu) and a 9.6% CD reduction. The headline gains are +12.04% AP3D^15 over Im3D on scene reconstruction and +13.43% F-Score over InstPIFu on shape reconstruction.

## Baselines & Comparisons

The method outperforms all three main baselines (Total3D, Im3D, InstPIFu) on every reported metric across both SUN RGB-D and Pix3D. Against Im3D (the strongest scene-level baseline), it gains +7.79 IoU3D, +12.04% AP3D^15, and +0.25 L_align on SUN RGB-D, while on Pix3D it reduces CD by 36.26 and increases F-Score by 37.26. Against InstPIFu (the strongest shape-level baseline), it improves F-Score by 13.43 points (58.71 vs. 45.28) and reduces CD by 9.6 (15.05 vs. 24.65). Against Total3D, the margins are largest: +27.62 AP3D^15 and +29.27 CD reduction. On ScanNet, the method also beats the retrieval-based ROCA baseline by +3.1% in class-wise alignment accuracy (24.8 vs. 21.7) and +2.1% in instance-level accuracy (29.5 vs. 27.4), attributed to its generative scene prior reasoning about inter-object relationships rather than retrieving from a fixed shape database.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper departs from the regression-based paradigm of prior holistic scene reconstruction methods (Total3D, Im3D, InstPIFu) by framing single-view 3D scene reconstruction as a conditional diffusion process. It builds on SPAGHETTI's Gaussian scaffolding shape representation and SALAD's part-level latent diffusion architecture for shape denoising, while adopting the 7-DoF pose parameterization from Huang et al. The key conceptual departure is the Intra-Scene Attention module, which allows all objects to exchange relational information throughout the entire denoising process, learning a joint scene prior rather than marginal per-object distributions. The surface alignment loss further distinguishes this work by enabling joint pose-shape training under the partial ground-truth annotations common in public datasets like SUN RGB-D.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The 12% AP3D and 13% F-Score improvements over strong baselines, combined with zero-shot generalization to ScanNet, suggest that the generative formulation captures scene structure in ways regression cannot. The surface alignment loss addresses a practical pain point for the community: most public 3D scene datasets lack full joint pose-shape annotations, and this loss provides a clean solution. However, the method remains dependent on accurate 2D detection, does not generalize to unseen object categories, and assumes static scenes—limiting its applicability to dynamic or novel-category settings. Future work on integrating articulation, affordance, and open-vocabulary shape priors could extend the approach toward more immersive applications.
