## Problem

Existing news-driven multi-stock movement prediction methods model inter-stock relations as undirected correlations (via attention or graph networks), failing to capture the inherently unidirectional information flow between stocks (e.g., supplier→consumer). Additionally, the massive noise in financial news text—arising from unreliable sources, information overload, and ambiguous language—makes it difficult for conventional encoders (GRU, LSTM, BERT) to extract the signals relevant to price movement.

## Input / Output

The model consumes L=5 days of historical price features (open, high, low, close, adjusted close, volume) for D stocks plus per-stock daily news text. The news is pre-processed by GPT-3.5 via structured prompts into five scalar scores per article (correlation 0–10, sentiment −1 to 1, importance 0–10, impact 0–10, duration 0–10), which are then embedded into a fixed-size vector. The model outputs a simultaneous binary rise/fall prediction for all D stocks on the target day, along with a learned temporal causal graph of shape L×D×D (one DAG per lag) and a separate causal weight graph encoding the strength of each discovered link.

## Method

CausalStock comprises three jointly trained modules. (1) A Market Information Encoder embeds price features and, critically, an LLM-based Denoised News Encoder that replaces raw text embeddings with LLM-evaluated multi-dimensional scores, reducing noise while injecting external financial knowledge. (2) A Lag-dependent Temporal Causal Discovery module performs variational inference over the posterior p(G|X_<T), factorizing the graph distribution so that each lag's edge probabilities are conditioned on the previous lag's edges via trainable MLPs (the key novelty over lag-independent methods like Rhino/DECI); edges are modeled as Bernoulli variables with Gumbel-softmax reparameterization, and a separate learnable weight graph captures causal degree. (3) An additive-noise Functional Causal Model predicts y_T^i = f_i(parents) + z_T^i, where f_i is a shared-weight MLP that aggregates only the causal parents' price and news representations, gated by the product of the causal existence graph and weight graph. Training maximizes an ELBO (Gaussian log-likelihood + graph sparseness/domain-knowledge prior + posterior entropy) plus a binary cross-entropy term, with the gradient from news to the causal graph detached to satisfy the Causal Stationary assumption.

## Results

On the news-driven multi-stock movement prediction task, CausalStock achieves the best ACC and MCC across all three datasets: 63.42% ACC / 0.2172 MCC on ACL18, 54.64% / 0.0481 on CMIN-US, and 56.19% / 0.1417 on CMIN-CN. On the multi-stock movement prediction task (without news), CausalStock similarly leads on all three datasets: 56.09% ACC / 0.1235 MCC on KDD17, 53.01% / 0.0640 on NI225, and 52.88% / 0.0534 on FTSE100. In the investment simulation, CausalStock achieves the highest Sharpe Ratio (0.369 on ACL18, 0.192 on KDD17, 0.259 on NI225) and Accumulated Portfolio Value (1.32, 1.49, 1.52 respectively) compared to all baselines and market indices.

## Baselines & Comparisons

CausalStock outperforms all eight baselines on every dataset and metric. On the news-driven task, the closest competitor is CMIN [23], which CausalStock surpasses by 0.73 ACC points on ACL18 (63.42 vs. 62.69), 1.21 on CMIN-US (54.64 vs. 53.43), and 0.91 on CMIN-CN (56.19 vs. 55.28); the gap widens substantially against PEN [21] (3.53 ACC on ACL18) and HAN [15] (5.78 ACC on ACL18). On the multi-stock movement prediction task, CausalStock leads the strongest baseline DTML [44] by 2.56 ACC on KDD17 (56.09 vs. 53.53), 0.25 on NI225 (53.01 vs. 52.76), and 0.80 on FTSE100 (52.88 vs. 52.08), with the MCC gap being more pronounced (e.g., 0.1235 vs. 0.0733 on KDD17). StockNet [42] is consistently among the weakest baselines on both tasks, trailing CausalStock by 5+ ACC points on ACL18 and 4+ on KDD17. The RNN-based baselines (LSTM [25], ALSTM [28], Adv-ALSTM [8]) cluster near 50–52% ACC on the multi-stock task, well below CausalStock's 52.88–56.09% range.

## Relation to Prior Work

CausalStock extends the deep end-to-end causal discovery lineage initiated by DECI [9] and Rhino [14], adapting their variational inference framework with Bernoulli edge distributions and additive-noise FCMs to the stock prediction domain. The key methodological departure is the introduction of a lag-dependent temporal causal discovery mechanism, where each lag's causal graph is conditioned on the previous lag's graph, making the model more natural for temporal stock data than the lag-independent formulation in DECI. On the text side, the paper departs from the standard practice of using GRU/LSTM or pre-trained encoders (BERT, RoBERTa, FinBERT) for news representation by proposing an LLM-based Denoised News Encoder that scores each news item across five dimensions (correlation, sentiment, importance, impact, duration), effectively using the LLM as a structured evaluation oracle rather than a sequence encoder.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The paper addresses a practical gap in financial NLP: news data is heavily noisy, and correlation-based stock relation modeling misses the unidirectional information flow inherent in supply-chain and investment relationships. The causal graph output provides interpretable explanations of which stocks influence which, validated by a strong Spearman correlation (0.65–0.89) between discovered causal strength and company market value across four markets. However, the variable-dependent TCD variant that captures inter-edge dependencies increases complexity from O(L×D²) to O(L×D⁴), limiting scalability to large stock universes. Open questions include whether the causal graph should be updated incrementally over time (the authors note this as future work), whether more complex distributions than Bernoulli are needed for multi-level causal strength, and whether the LLM evaluation scores are robust to prompt sensitivity and model version changes.
