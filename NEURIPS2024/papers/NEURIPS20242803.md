## Problem

First-order methods for equilibrium computation in extensive-form games require a distance-generating function (DGF) whose diameter-to-strong-convexity ratio governs convergence rates, but no prior analysis could establish that the weight-one dilated entropy (DilEnt) regularizer—known to be iterate-equivalent to the state-of-the-art KOMWU algorithm—achieves the best possible ratio through standard OMD machinery. The paper asks whether this optimal ratio can be recovered directly from DGF properties, and whether it is in fact the best achievable by any regularizer.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/NEURIPS20242803/cropped_page13_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The analysis operates on a tree-form sequential decision process (TFSDP) derived from an n-player EFG, where the decision space is the compressed sequence-form polytope parameterized over terminal observation points. Reward vectors are assumed bounded in [0,1] at terminal nodes, and the treeplex norms are computed recursively bottom-up over the tree structure. The key quantitative output is the diameter-to-strong-convexity ratio ln|V| (where |V| is the number of reduced normal-form strategies), which directly yields regret bounds of O(√(ln|V|)·√T) for online learning and O(n·ln|V|·log T / T) for CCE computation in n-player games.

## Method

The paper introduces a pair of primal-dual treeplex norms (‖·‖_{H,1} and ‖·‖_{H,∞}) defined as support functions over the transition kernel space and the sequence-form decision space respectively, with recursive decompositions at decision points (sum/max) and observation points (max/sum) that mirror the tree structure. Using these norms, the authors prove that the weight-one DilEnt regularizer is 1-strongly convex with respect to the treeplex ℓ₁ norm by decomposing it into a y-weighted negative entropy over terminals (which is strongly convex in a y-weighted ℓ₁ norm) plus a convex residual, and bound the Bregman diameter by ln|V| via induction on the tree. This yields a diameter-to-strong-convexity ratio of ln|V|, recovering KOMWU's regret bound through the standard OMD template without any kernelization trick. A matching lower bound of Ω(‖Q‖_⊥·log|A₀|·√T) is established via a fully random hard instance, showing the ratio is optimal up to logarithmic factors in the number of sequences.

## Results

The paper establishes that the weight-one dilated entropy (DilEnt) regularizer achieves a diameter-to-strong-convexity ratio of ln|V| with respect to the newly introduced treeplex norms, where |V| is the number of reduced normal-form strategies. This yields a regret upper bound of O(√(ln|V|) √T) for OMD, matching the state-of-the-art KOMWU bound, and a CCE convergence rate of O(n log|V| log T / T) for Clairvoyant OMD in n-player games. A matching lower bound of Ω(√(||Q||_⊥ log|A_0|) √T) is proven, establishing that DilEnt is optimal up to logarithmic factors.

## Baselines & Comparisons

Against Kroer et al. [27] (Dilated Entropy with ℓ₁/ℓ∞ norms), the proposed DilEnt with treeplex norms improves the diameter-to-strong-convexity ratio from O(2^D ‖Q‖₁² log|A|) to ln|V|, eliminating the exponential depth dependence and the quadratic tree-size dependence. Against Farina et al. [16] (Dilated Global Entropy), the ratio improves from O(‖Q‖₁² log|A|) to ln|V|, again removing the quadratic tree-size factor. Against KOMWU (Farina et al. [20]), the OMD+DilEnt regret bound O(√(ln|V|) √T) matches the state-of-the-art, and the CCE rate improves from O(n log|V| log⁴ T / T) to O(n log|V| log T / T), a polynomial improvement in the log T factor. Against Farina et al. [18], the CCE rate improves from O(n·|J×A|·‖Q‖₁² log T / T) to O(n log|V| log T / T), replacing the quadratic tree-size dependence with a logarithmic one. The new lower bound (Theorem 6.1) confirms that no regularizer can achieve a ratio better than Ω(‖Q‖_⊥ log|A_0|), making DilEnt optimal up to at most logarithmic factors.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper sits at the intersection of the dilated regularization framework for EFGs (Hoda et al. 2010), prior strong-convexity analyses of dilated entropy variants (Kroer et al. 2020, Farina et al. 2021), and the kernelized OMWU algorithm (Farina et al. 2022) whose iterate-equivalence to weight-one DilEnt was established by Bai et al. 2022. The key departure is the introduction of primal-dual treeplex norms, which replace the standard ℓ₁/ℓ∞ norm pair used in all prior DGF analyses and enable a direct OMD-based proof of the state-of-the-art regret bound that previously required the KOMWU equivalence. The paper also refines the Clairvoyant OMD analysis (Farina et al. 2022, Piliouras et al. 2022) by plugging in the new DilEnt properties, improving the CCE convergence rate from O(n log|V| log⁴T/T) to O(n log|V| log T/T).

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The result that weight-one DilEnt achieves a near-optimal diameter-to-strong-convexity ratio (up to logarithmic factors) resolves two open questions: whether the KOMWU regret bound is information-theoretically tight, and whether it can be recovered through standard OMD machinery without the kernelization trick. This gives practitioners a principled, minimal regularizer choice for FOMs in EFGs and unifies the analysis of multiple algorithms under one norm framework. The main limitation is that the optimality gap (logarithmic factors) cannot be fully closed in general TFSDPs, and the lower bound applies only to full-information feedback, leaving bandit and external-sampling settings open. The treeplex norms are likely to become a standard analytical tool for future work on structured decision spaces.
