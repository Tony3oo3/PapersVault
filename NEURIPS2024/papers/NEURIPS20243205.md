## Problem

Existing semantic scene completion methods rely on single-pass feature propagation via convolutions or attention, which fails to model the multiple possible semantic labels a voxel may carry due to occlusion and information loss in LiDAR point clouds. These approaches also assume static scenes and cannot leverage the complementary information in adjacent frames to resolve ambiguity, leading to incomplete geometry and inaccurate semantics in dynamic environments.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/NEURIPS20243205/cropped_page2_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method takes a single LiDAR point cloud frame (N_i points with x, y, z, reflectivity) as input, which is voxelized with additional per-point offsets relative to voxel centers to preserve sub-voxel geometry. It produces a dense 3D voxel grid of size L×W×H with per-voxel semantic labels across C'' categories, completed jointly from a Bird's-Eye-View (2D U-Net) perspective and a 3D perspective. During training, a multi-frame teacher network additionally consumes K aligned point cloud frames to generate distillation targets for the single-frame student.

## Method

VPNet employs a dual-branch architecture where a BEV 2D U-Net ensures global geometric completeness and a 3D branch (Cylinder3D-based segmentation subnetwork + completion subnetwork) refines local semantic details. The core innovation is the Confident Voxel Proposal (CVP) module, which learns Q groups of 3D offsets from occupied voxel coordinates, features, and random noise to propose multiple sets of confident voxel positions; features are propagated to these positions with distance-based pruning, and the Q resulting confident feature maps are fused via learned weighted fusion to implicitly model per-voxel semantic uncertainty. A second innovation is Multi-Frame Knowledge Distillation (MFKD), which builds a teacher network processing K aligned frames through separate CVPs, then distills in two stages: stage-1 uses super-voxel-partitioned KL divergence to align each CVP branch with its corresponding frame's augmented features, and stage-2 uses direct voxel-wise KL divergence to align the single-frame output with the multi-frame fused output. Training combines completion loss, segmentation loss, Chamfer Distance geometry loss on proposed coordinates, and the two-stage KD loss.

## Results

On the SemanticKITTI online testing set, VPNet with MFKD achieves 60.7% IoU and 25.6% mIoU, outperforming the previous best method SSA-SC (58.8% IoU, 23.5% mIoU) by 1.9% IoU and 2.1% mIoU. Without MFKD, VPNet still reaches 60.4% IoU and 25.0% mIoU, a 1.6% IoU and 1.5% mIoU improvement over SSA-SC. On the SemanticPOSS validation set, VPNet with MFKD achieves 57.3% IoU and 23.3% mIoU, surpassing SSA-SC (53.3% IoU, 21.6% mIoU) by 4.0% IoU and 1.7% mIoU.

## Baselines & Comparisons

VPNet outperforms all ten baselines on both SemanticKITTI and SemanticPOSS. On SemanticKITTI, the closest competitor SSA-SC [5] achieves 58.8% IoU / 23.5% mIoU, while VPNet (w/ MFKD) reaches 60.7% / 25.6%, a gain of 1.9% IoU and 2.1% mIoU. JS3D-Cet [6] (56.6% IoU, 23.8% mIoU) and Local-DIFs [53] (57.7% IoU, 22.7% mIoU) trail by 4.1% and 3.0% IoU respectively. The LMSCNet variants [2] (55.3–56.7% IoU) and SSCNet variants [1] (29.8–50.0% IoU) are substantially behind, with VPNet exceeding them by 4–31% IoU. In the distillation-method ablation (Table 4b), MFKD outperforms standard KD [51] (58.9% IoU), PVKD [26] (59.1%), and DSKD [16] (59.3%) by 0.7–1.7% IoU, confirming the multi-frame distillation design. On SemanticPOSS, VPNet (57.3% IoU, 23.3% mIoU) leads SSA-SC [5] (53.3%, 21.6%) by 4.0% IoU and LMSCNet-SS [2] (52.6%, 16.4%) by 4.7% IoU, with the largest per-category gains on pole (+3.1%) and fence (+7.2%) over SSA-SC.

## Relation to Prior Work

VPNet builds on Cylinder3D's segmentation backbone and DDCM module for the 3D branch, borrows the offset-based long-range feature propagation paradigm from Tagnet, and adapts the weighted fusion strategy from Selective Kernel Networks for multi-branch integration. It departs from the dominant convolution/attention-based completion paradigm by introducing Confident Voxel Proposal, which learns per-voxel coordinate offsets to explicitly propose occupied locations rather than relying on dense convolutions to fill gaps. The Multi-Frame Knowledge Distillation component extends prior multi-to-single-frame distillation work (M2SKD, SMF-SSD) by aligning distillation branches with individual frames and using super-voxel partitioning to respect feature sparsity, rather than simply fusing aligned point clouds.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The paper addresses a real gap in SSC: most prior methods assume static single-frame scenes and ignore the semantic uncertainty that arises from occlusion and dynamic motion in LiDAR sequences. By modeling voxel-level uncertainty through multiple proposal branches and distilling multi-frame complementary information into a single-frame model, VPNet offers a principled way to improve completion without requiring temporal inference at test time. However, the single-round offset learning limits fine-grained geometric reconstruction (acknowledged by the authors), and the gains over strong baselines like SSA-SC are modest (~2% mIoU), leaving open whether iterative proposal or larger multi-frame windows could yield substantially larger improvements.
