## Problem

Existing single image-to-3D methods face a fundamental trade-off: SDS-based approaches produce diverse results but require hours of per-case optimization with multi-face artifacts, while feed-forward reconstruction models (LRMs) and multi-view diffusion pipelines run fast but produce coarse meshes lacking intricate textures and complex geometric details due to limited generation resolution and multi-view inconsistency. No prior method simultaneously achieves high fidelity, multi-view consistency, and real-time efficiency for textured mesh output.

## Input / Output

The method takes a single in-the-wild RGB image (no camera parameters or text prompts required) and produces a textured 3D mesh with tens of millions of faces in under 30 seconds on a single RTX4090. Internally, it generates four orthographic multi-view images (front, back, left, right) at 256×256, progressively upscales them to 2048×2048, and produces corresponding normal maps at the same resolutions. The output mesh is a standard triangle mesh with per-vertex colors, compatible with common 3D engines like Blender and Maya.

## Method

Unique3D combines a multi-level generation pipeline with a novel mesh reconstruction algorithm (ISOMER). For generation, a multi-view diffusion model (initialized from Stable Diffusion Image Variations with view-index class embeddings and cross-view self-attention) produces four 256×256 orthographic views, which are upscaled to 512 via a fine-tuned ControlNet-Tile and to 2048 via Real-ESRGAN; a parallel normal diffusion model (with a reference U-Net providing pixel-wise attention) generates corresponding normal maps at the same resolutions. ISOMER reconstructs the mesh in three stages: (1) initial mesh estimation by integrating front/back normal maps into depth maps (with random-rotation averaging to handle non-irrotational pseudo-normals) and joining them via Poisson reconstruction; (2) coarse-to-fine differentiable-rendering optimization with mask and normal losses plus an expansion regularization to prevent surface collapse; (3) an ExplicitTarget refinement that assigns each vertex a unique optimization target as a visibility-weighted average of its colors/normals across views (weighted by squared cosine of the angle between vertex normal and view direction), avoiding the wave-pattern artifacts that arise from naively minimizing multi-view differences on inconsistent OOD inputs.

## Results

On the GSO dataset, Unique3D achieves the best PSNR (20.06), SSIM (0.9222), LPIPS (0.1070), Volume IoU (0.5416), and F-Score (0.6696) among all compared methods. The closest competitor, CRM, trails by 1.62 dB in PSNR and 0.0198 in Volume IoU. The full pipeline from single input image to high-precision textured mesh completes in under 30 seconds on a single RTX 4090, with the ISOMER reconstruction step alone taking less than 10 seconds.

## Baselines & Comparisons

Unique3D outperforms all seven baselines on the majority of metrics. Against the strongest competitor CRM, Unique3D gains +1.62 dB PSNR, +0.0134 SSIM, −0.0296 LPIPS, +0.0198 Volume IoU, and +0.0122 F-Score, while CRM holds a negligible 0.0002 edge in Chamfer Distance. Against InstantMesh, the gap is even larger: +1.23 dB PSNR, +0.0111 SSIM, −0.0213 LPIPS, +0.0333 Volume IoU, and +0.0205 F-Score, though InstantMesh edges out by 0.0008 in Clip-Similarity. The older SDS-based and feed-forward methods (One-2-3-45, OpenLRM, Wonder3D) are substantially behind, with PSNR gaps of 2–4 dB and Volume IoU gaps of 0.08–0.15. In the user study (Table 3), Unique3D also leads all baselines on multi-view consistency (9.26 vs. 8.67 for Wonder3D), subject fidelity (8.74 vs. 8.53 for CRM), prompt fidelity (8.52 vs. 8.03 for CRM), and overall quality (9.02 vs. 8.43 for InstantMesh). The paper further demonstrates that ISOMER can be plugged into other pipelines (e.g., Wonder3D+ISOMER improves PSNR from 18.09 to 18.61), confirming the reconstruction algorithm's generality.

## Relation to Prior Work

Unique3D builds on the multi-view diffusion paradigm established by Wonder3D and SyncDreamer, which fine-tune 2D diffusion models (initialized from Stable Diffusion) to generate consistent novel views, and on ControlNet for conditional image generation. It departs from prior work in two key ways: (1) a multi-level upscale pipeline (256→512→2048) that progressively increases resolution rather than generating at a fixed low resolution, and (2) ISOMER, a direct mesh-based reconstruction algorithm whose computational cost scales quadratically with resolution rather than cubically as in field-based methods (SDF, occupancy). The ExplicitTarget mechanism for resolving multi-view inconsistency is a novel departure from the standard practice of minimizing per-view losses simultaneously.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The 30-second end-to-end generation of high-resolution (2048px) textured meshes is practically significant for real-world 3D content pipelines in gaming, animation, and design, where per-case optimization of hours (SDS methods) or limited-resolution outputs (prior LRM/multi-view methods) are prohibitive. The ISOMER algorithm's quadratic scaling and its ability to improve other methods' reconstructions (e.g., Wonder3D+ISOMER) suggest it could be adopted as a general-purpose reconstruction backend. Open questions include robustness to non-frontal or skewed inputs, support for texture maps rather than vertex colors, and whether the multi-view diffusion component can be further generalized beyond the ~50k Objaverse training set.
