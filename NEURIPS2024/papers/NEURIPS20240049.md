## Problem

Existing sparse-to-dense approaches for vision-based Semantic Scene Completion use shared context-independent queries across all input images, which fail to capture the distinct focal regions of different views and lead to undirected feature aggregation in cross-attention. Additionally, projecting 3D points onto the 2D image plane causes multiple points at different depths to share the same 2D coordinates, creating a critical depth ambiguity that degrades feature lifting accuracy.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/NEURIPS20240049/cropped_page1_idx1.webp)

## Input / Output

The method takes a stereo RGB image pair (1226×370 on SemanticKITTI, 1408×376 on KITTI-360) and produces a 3D semantic voxel grid at 256×256×32 resolution (0.2 m per voxel) covering a 51.2 m × 51.2 m × 6.4 m volume around the ego vehicle. The internal 3D feature volume is computed at 128×128×16 and trilinearly upsampled to match the ground-truth resolution. A pretrained MobileStereoNet provides the stereo depth map used both for defining visible voxel query proposals and for refining the depth probability that drives the 3D feature lifting.

## Method

CGFormer's core module, the Context and Geometry Aware Voxel Transformer (CGVT), replaces shared learnable queries with context-dependent queries generated by a voxel-pooling-based query generator that conditions on per-image context features and depth probability. It extends deformable cross-attention from 2D to 3D pixel space by taking the outer product of context features and depth probability to form a 3D key-value volume, allowing points with similar image coordinates to be distinguished by their depth coordinate. A lightweight depth refinement block fuses monocular and stereo depth features via 5×5 neighborhood attention to improve depth probability accuracy without the heavy correlation cost of StereoScene. After cross-attention aggregates features for visible voxels, deformable self-attention diffuses information to the full 3D volume. The 3D Local and Global Encoder (LGE) then processes the volume through a parallel voxel-based branch (3D ResNet + FPN for local detail) and a TPV-based branch (group spatial-to-channel pooling + 2D Swin backbone + FPN for global semantics), dynamically fusing the four resulting feature maps with learned per-voxel weights before a decoding head predicts the final semantic occupancy.

## Results

On the SemanticKITTI test set, CGFormer achieves an IoU of 44.41 and mIoU of 16.63, setting a new state-of-the-art in mIoU among all camera-based methods. On SSCBench-KITTI-360, CGFormer attains an IoU of 48.07 and mIoU of 20.05, surpassing all camera-based baselines by 3.95 IoU and 1.67 mIoU over the previous best (Symphonize). Notably, CGFormer even outperforms LiDAR-based methods on mIoU for SSCBench-KITTI-360 (20.05 vs. 16.95 for SSCNet) while using only stereo RGB inputs.

## Baselines & Comparisons

CGFormer outperforms all camera-based baselines on mIoU across both benchmarks. On SemanticKITTI, it leads the previous best mIoU (MonoOcc-L at 15.63) by 1.00 points and Symphonize (15.04) by 1.59 points, while achieving the best IoU (44.41) tied with MonoOcc-L but with a substantially higher mIoU. The only method that edges out CGFormer on IoU is DepthSSC (44.58 vs. 44.41, a 0.17 gap), but CGFormer surpasses it by 3.52 mIoU (16.63 vs. 13.11). Compared to temporal-stereo methods (VoxFormer-T, HASSC-T, H2GFormer-T, MonoOcc-S) that use additional temporal image pairs, CGFormer using only a single stereo pair achieves 1.2–3.2 mIoU gains. On SSCBench-KITTI-360, CGFormer (48.07 IoU, 20.05 mIoU) beats the prior best camera method Symphonize (44.12, 18.58) by 3.95 IoU and 1.47 mIoU, and even exceeds the LiDAR-based SSCNet on mIoU (20.05 vs. 16.95) despite using only RGB inputs.

## Relation to Prior Work

CGFormer extends the VoxFormer sparse-to-dense paradigm for camera-based semantic scene completion, which itself builds on the deformable attention mechanisms from Deformable DeTR and the depth-probability lifting from BEVDepth. The key conceptual departure is replacing VoxFormer's shared, context-independent voxel queries with per-image context-dependent queries generated via a lightweight voxel-pooling-based generator, and extending deformable cross-attention from 2D to 3D pixel space to resolve depth ambiguity. The 3D Local and Global Encoder draws on the TPV representation from TPVFormer and the voxel-pooling lifting from LiftSplat, combining them with dynamic fusion to capture both fine-grained and global scene structure.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

CGFormer sets a new SOTA on both SemanticKITTI and SSCBench-KITTI-360 while using a single stereo image pair and a standard EfficientNetB7 backbone, outperforming methods that rely on temporal image sequences or substantially larger backbones (e.g., InternImage-XL in MonoOcc-L). The depth refinement block achieves results comparable to StereoScene's dense epipolar correlation with roughly 34M fewer parameters and less training memory, offering a practical path to accurate depth without heavy computational cost. Open questions remain around performance on small, sparse objects (person, bicyclist, other-vehicle) and generalization to multi-view or surround-camera settings, which the authors acknowledge as limitations.
