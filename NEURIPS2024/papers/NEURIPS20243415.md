## Problem

Continuous entropic OT barycenter estimation for general (non-Euclidean) cost functions remains largely unsolved: existing solvers either restrict to the quadratic ℓ² cost, require a fixed prior for the barycenter, fail to recover the transport plans, or lack theoretical guarantees. This paper closes that gap by providing a unified framework that handles arbitrary costs while recovering conditional OT plans and establishing generalization and universal approximation bounds.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/NEURIPS20243415/cropped_page1_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method consumes finite empirical samples {x_k^n} from each source distribution P_k, a set of general continuous cost functions c_k(x_k, y), and a regularization parameter ε; in the manifold-constrained variant it additionally requires a pretrained StyleGAN generator G: Z → Y to define the target manifold. The output is a set of trained neural network potentials f_{θ*,k} that, via MCMC (ULA) sampling, produce conditional transport plans π_k*(·|x_k) mapping any new source sample to the barycenter, as well as barycenter distribution samples (either in ambient space or pushed through G onto the manifold). Notably, the potentials are constrained by the congruence condition Σ_k λ_k f_k ≡ 0, which is enforced by construction rather than as a penalty.

## Method

The core reformulation combines the weak OT dual (via the entropic c-transform f^C(x) = -ε log Z_c(f,x)) with a congruence condition Σ_k λ_k f_k = 0, yielding a pure maximization over K potentials on Y with no min-max or distributional optimization. Neural networks g_{θ_k} parameterize the potentials as f_{θ,k} = g_{θ_k} - Σ_{k'} λ_{k'} g_{θ_{k'}}, automatically satisfying congruence. Training proceeds by stochastic gradient ascent on the dual objective L(θ) = -ε Σ_k λ_k E[log Z_{c_k}(f_{θ,k}, x_k)], where the gradient (Theorem 4.3) is estimated by drawing x_k from the empirical source and then sampling y from the conditional energy distribution μ_{x_k}^{f_{θ,k}} via Unadjusted Langevin Algorithm. For manifold-constrained barycenters, the problem is solved in the StyleGAN latent space with modified costs c_{k,G}(x_k, z) = c_k(x_k, G(z)), which are general (non-ℓ²) due to the nonlinear generator. The paper further proves that the estimation error scales as O(Σ_k N_k^{-1/2}) for feature-based quadratic costs and that multi-layer perceptrons of width D+4 achieve vanishing approximation error, providing the first such guarantees for a continuous barycenter solver.

## Results

On the Ave, celeba! barycenter dataset, the proposed method achieves FID scores of 8.4, 8.7, and 10.2 for the three source-to-barycenter transport plans, dramatically outperforming SCWB (56.7, 53.2, 58.8) and WIN (49.3, 46.9, 61.5). The method also recovers the EOT barycenter of MNIST 0/1 digits both in data space (with expected entropic noise) and on a StyleGAN-constrained manifold (noise-free), and qualitatively matches the analytically known unregularized barycenter on a 2D twister toy problem. The manifold-constrained setup is the key differentiator, as competing solvers are restricted to ℓ² costs in ambient data space and cannot exploit a pretrained generative manifold.

## Baselines & Comparisons

The paper compares against two continuous OT barycenter solvers: SCWB (Fan et al., ICML 2021) and WIN (Korotin et al., NeurIPS 2022). On the Ave, celeba! dataset, the proposed method achieves FID scores roughly 5–7× lower than both baselines across all three source distributions (e.g., 8.4 vs. 56.7 for k=1), a gap attributable to the StyleGAN manifold-constrained setup that the baselines cannot replicate. Both SCWB and WIN are restricted to the unregularized ℓ² barycenter in ambient data space (ε=0), whereas the proposed method learns the entropic (ε>0) barycenter on a pretrained generative manifold, yielding cleaner and more faithful transport plans. On MNIST 0/1, the baselines produce the expected pixel-wise average barycenter images, while the proposed method additionally demonstrates noise-free barycenter recovery when constrained to the StyleGAN manifold. The paper notes that the FID advantage is partly due to the manifold constraint (a setup advantage rather than a pure algorithmic one), but the method's generality to arbitrary OT costs and non-Euclidean domains (e.g., the sphere experiment) has no direct counterpart in either baseline.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the energy-guided entropic OT framework of Mokrov et al. (ICLR 2024) from single-pair transport to the multi-source barycenter setting, leveraging the weak OT dual formulation of Gozlan et al. (2017) to derive a clean maximization objective over congruent potentials. It departs from prior continuous barycenter solvers (Fan et al. 2021, Korotin et al. 2022, Noble et al. 2023) that are restricted to ℓ² costs, and from Li et al. (2020) which handles general costs but requires a fixed barycenter prior. The manifold-constrained variant—solving the barycenter in a pretrained StyleGAN's latent space with modified costs—is a novel practical technique not present in any prior continuous barycenter solver.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The paper provides the first continuous EOT barycenter solver with general cost functions, generalization bounds, and universal approximation guarantees, filling a gap where prior methods either restricted costs to ℓ² or lacked theoretical analysis. The manifold-constrained approach (solving in StyleGAN latent space) elegantly addresses the well-known blurring bias of entropic barycenters in image spaces, yielding dramatically lower FID scores on CelebA (8.4–10.2 vs. 46.9–61.5 for baselines), though the comparison is partially confounded since baselines solve the unregularized ℓ² problem without manifold constraints. Key open questions include the MCMC bottleneck (ULA is slow and can get stuck in local minima), the absence of optimization-error analysis, and whether more efficient samplers (neural HMC, diffusion-based) can make the approach scalable to larger image resolutions.
