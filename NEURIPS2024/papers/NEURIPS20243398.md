## Problem

Feed-forward 360° novel view synthesis of diverse real-world scenes from extremely sparse observations (≤5 views) remains unsolved: existing methods either achieve 360° NVS only at the object level or produce scene-level results only for nearby viewpoints. The task is inherently ill-posed because widely displaced input views have minimal overlap and lack sufficient information to recover the full 3D scene, making both geometric reconstruction and appearance generation highly ambiguous.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/NEURIPS20243398/cropped_page1_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The model takes 5 sparse RGB images at 256×480 resolution with known camera poses (intrinsics, rotation, translation), selected via farthest point sampling over candidate viewpoints, and produces photorealistic 360° or wide-sweeping novel views along arbitrary camera trajectories in a single feed-forward pass. During training, 14 target views are sampled along natural camera trajectories to match SVD's video format; at inference, up to 56 views can be rendered by switching temporal attention to a local window of 14. The model is evaluated on both a new DL3DV-10K benchmark (140 scenes, 7,840 test views) and the existing RealEstate10K benchmark.

## Method

MVSplat360 combines a feed-forward 3DGS backbone (MVSplat) for coarse geometry with a fine-tuned Stable Video Diffusion (SVD) model for appearance refinement. The key architectural innovation is Gaussian feature rendering: each 3D Gaussian predicts an additional 4-channel latent feature that is rasterized directly into SVD's latent space, bypassing the frozen image encoder and enabling SVD's denoising gradients to backpropagate into the geometry backbone for end-to-end training. SVD receives hybrid conditioning—CLIP-embedded tokens from all input views provide global semantic context via cross-attention, while the rendered Gaussian features are channel-concatenated with the noised latent to supply spatial pose and texture cues. A latent space alignment loss regularizes the rendered features toward the distribution of SVD's image-encoded latents, and structural Gaussian parameters (mean, opacity, covariance) have their gradients stopped during SVD backpropagation to maintain training stability. The backbone further uses local-group cross-view attention and cost volumes (restricted to the nearest 2 views) to handle the minimal overlap among widely displaced 360° input views.

## Results

On the new DL3DV-10K 360° NVS benchmark (n=300, 5 input views), MVSplat360 achieves PSNR 16.81, SSIM 0.514, LPIPS 0.418, DISTS 0.175, and FID 17.01, outperforming all baselines across every metric. The most dramatic gain is in FID (17.01 vs. 37.68 for latentSplat and 78.95 for MVSplat), reflecting the diffusion-based refinement's ability to produce outputs aligned with the real image distribution. On RealEstate10K extrapolation, MVSplat360 reaches FID 1.79 (vs. 3.83 for MVSplat and 2.79 for latentSplat) while matching or slightly exceeding MVSplat on PSNR (23.16 vs. 23.04).

## Baselines & Comparisons

MVSplat360 outperforms all four baselines on every metric in both the DL3DV-10K and RealEstate10K evaluations. Against pixelSplat [6], the improvement is most pronounced: +1.98 dB PSNR and a 8.4× reduction in FID (142.83→17.01) on DL3DV-10K, confirming that pure regression-based 3DGS cannot handle 360° extrapolation. Against MVSplat [10] (its own backbone), MVSplat360 gains +1.09 dB PSNR and reduces FID from 78.95 to 17.01, demonstrating the critical contribution of the SVD refinement stage. Against latentSplat [57], the closest competitor (also combining 3DGS with a generative decoder), MVSplat360 leads by +0.13 dB PSNR and more than halves the FID (37.68→17.01), showing that a pre-trained video diffusion model provides substantially stronger generative priors than a GAN-based decoder. On RealEstate10K extrapolation, MVSplat360 edges out MVSplat by +0.12 dB PSNR and nearly halves FID (3.83→1.79), while surpassing latentSplat by +0.54 dB PSNR and −1.0 FID. The paper notes that pixel-aligned gains (PSNR/SSIM) are modest by design, since the diffusion module intentionally generates plausible content for unobserved regions rather than pixel-matching ground truth; the large perceptual and distributional gains (LPIPS, DISTS, FID) are the intended signal.

## Relation to Prior Work

MVSplat360 unifies two previously separate research threads: feed-forward 3DGS scene reconstruction (MVSplat, pixelSplat, latentSplat) that handles nearby viewpoints but fails at wide-sweeping angles, and diffusion-based 360° synthesis (Zero-1-to-3, SV3D, Free3D) that achieves full rotation but only at the object level. The key architectural departure is rendering multi-channel Gaussian features directly into SVD's latent space rather than feeding RGB images through a frozen encoder, which enables end-to-end gradient flow from the diffusion loss back into the geometry backbone. This design contrasts with concurrent per-scene methods (Reconfusion, CAT3D, ViewCrafter) that rely on iterative optimization, and with latentSplat's GAN-based decoder, which the authors show provides insufficient prior knowledge for diverse real-world scenes.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

This is the first feed-forward system to achieve 360° novel view synthesis of unbounded real-world scenes from as few as 5 sparse views, and the new DL3DV-10K benchmark (140 scenes, 7,840 test views) provides a much harder evaluation than the existing RealEstate10K protocol. The dramatic FID reduction (78.95 → 17.01) demonstrates that a video diffusion prior is essential for plausible content generation in disoccluded regions, not merely a quality polish. Open questions include the slow multi-step diffusion inference, the oversaturation and hallucination artifacts inherited from SVD's training distribution, and whether the approach generalizes to even sparser inputs (below 3 views) or to scenes with extreme geometric complexity.
