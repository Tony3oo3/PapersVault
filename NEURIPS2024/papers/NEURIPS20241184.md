## Problem

Protein structure prediction via AlphaFold2 relies heavily on high-quality Multiple Sequence Alignments (MSA), but ~20% of metagenomic proteins and ~11% of eukaryotic/viral proteins are 'orphan' sequences lacking sufficient homologs. Existing virtual MSA generation methods (MSA-Augmentor, PoET, EvoGen) use decoupled axial attention that is inefficient at capturing high-order co-evolutionary correlations across all homologs simultaneously, or depend on external oracle models for guidance.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/NEURIPS20241184/cropped_page1_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The model takes a query protein sequence (1×L amino acid string) and, in few-shot mode, an initial natural MSA (n×L) as in-context prompt; in zero-shot mode only the query is provided. It autoregressively generates a virtual MSA (m×L) by flattening the 2D alignment into a 1D token sequence of length N×L, with special [S]/[E] tokens delimiting generation. The generated MSA is concatenated with the initial MSA and fed to AlphaFold2, which produces the final 3D structure prediction (evaluated by TM-Score, GDT-TS, LDDT).

## Method

MSAGPT is a 2.8B-parameter transformer decoder that replaces axial attention with a 2D evolutionary positional encoding: each amino acid token receives two RoPE-based positional vectors encoding its column index (residue site) and row index (homolog sequence), enabling a single self-attention layer to simultaneously capture row-wise, column-wise, and cross-axis co-evolutionary patterns without decoupled attention passes. The 2D MSA is flattened along the row axis into a 1D sequence, reducing generation to standard next-token prediction and making the architecture compatible with Flash Attention and KV-cache for efficient inference. Training proceeds in three stages: (1) self-supervised pre-training on ~16M UniClust30 MSAs with cross-entropy loss, (2) Rejective Fine-tuning (RFT) on ~60K AF2-validated high-quality MSA subsets to suppress hallucinated generations, and (3) Reinforcement Learning from AF2 Feedback (RLAF) via DPO, where AF2's TM-Score serves as the implicit reward to align generation with structural accuracy.

## Results

On the natural MSA-scarce benchmarks (few-shot setting), MSAGPT+RFT+DPO achieves TM-Scores of 76.4 on CAMEO, 53.6 on CASP, and 60.1 on PDB, outperforming the strongest baseline EvoGen (75.3, 48.5, 55.4) by +1.1, +5.1, and +4.7 respectively. The largest gain is on CASP where MSAGPT improves TM-Score by up to +8.5 over EvoGen. On the artificial MSA-scarce zero-shot benchmark, MSAGPT reaches 50.7 TM-Score and 76.1 LDDT, surpassing MSA-Augmentor (45.8 TM, 71.7 LDDT) and EvoGen (44.6 TM, 70.5 LDDT) by a wide margin. Statistically, MSAGPT improves prediction accuracy for 91.0% of sequences in zero-shot and 88.9% in few-shot scenarios compared to AF2 with natural MSA alone.

## Baselines & Comparisons

MSAGPT+RFT+DPO outperforms all four baselines across all three benchmarks in both zero-shot and few-shot settings. Against EvoGen (the strongest MSA-generation baseline), MSAGPT gains +1.1 TM on CAMEO few-shot, +5.1 on CASP few-shot, and +4.7 on PDB few-shot, with the CASP gap being the most pronounced given the extremely low average MSA depth (4.6). Compared to MSA-Augmentor, MSAGPT leads by +4.3 TM on CASP few-shot and +4.8 on PDB few-shot, while MSA-Augmentor slightly edges out on CAMEO zero-shot (59.2 vs. 62.7 for MSAGPT, but 72.1 vs. 76.4 on few-shot where MSAGPT still wins). EvoDiff, the diffusion-based approach, is consistently the weakest MSA-generation baseline, underperforming even AF2 with natural MSA on CASP (29.5 vs. 32.6 zero-shot TM), suggesting its generation quality degrades severely in low-homology regimes. The RFT+DPO post-training stages contribute an additional +1.2 TM on CAMEO few-shot and +2.6 on CASP few-shot over the base MSAGPT, confirming that AF2-feedback alignment reduces hallucinated MSA and improves structural fidelity.

## Relation to Prior Work

MSAGPT extends the line of MSA generation for protein structure prediction (EvoGen, MSA-Augmentor, PoET) by replacing the dominant axial-attention architecture with a 2D evolutionary positional encoding (adapted from RoPE) that captures row- and column-wise co-evolutionary patterns in a single unified attention, followed by a 1D autoregressive decoding that enables flexible zero-/few-shot in-context generation. The post-alignment stages draw on DPO (Rafailov et al., 2023) and use AlphaFold2 as a reward signal, departing from EvoGen's reliance on an external oracle model for guidance. The 1D formulation also unlocks standard LLM acceleration techniques (Flash Attention, KV-cache) that are difficult to apply to stacked axial-attention MSA models.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The work addresses a practically critical gap: ~20% of metagenomic and ~11% of eukaryotic/viral proteins are 'orphans' with insufficient homologs for reliable MSA-based structure prediction. The 2D positional encoding is a clean, reusable architectural idea, and the DPO-with-AF2-feedback pipeline offers a general recipe for aligning generative protein models to downstream task quality. Open questions include scaling behavior beyond 2.8B parameters, whether the approach transfers to broader protein tasks beyond structure prediction, and the risk that generated MSAs could contaminate natural MSA databases if not properly distinguished.
