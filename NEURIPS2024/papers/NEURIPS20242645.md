## Problem

Existing virtual try-on methods suffer from ill-fitted garment styles, quality degradation during training, and an inability to handle multi-garment combinations. GAN-based approaches produce overly smooth transformations that fail on complex poses, while diffusion-based methods lack vivid fabric textures and photorealistic lighting, and none natively support free mixing of tops and bottoms or layered outfits.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/NEURIPS20242645/cropped_page0_idx1.webp)

## Input / Output

The method takes a human image and one or more target garment images (flat-lay or cropped), along with a text prompt, at 1024×768 resolution. An OpenPose-derived agnostic mask (not human parsing) and pose map are computed as auxiliary inputs, and the VAE-encoded masked person, noisy latent, and resized mask are concatenated into a 9-channel input for the MainNet. The output is a photorealistic try-on image where the garment is fitted to the person's body with correct length, texture, and lighting; for multi-garment mode, upper and lower garments are injected as separate conditions through parallel Hydra branches.

## Method

AnyFit employs two isomorphic U-Nets on an SDXL backbone: a HydraNet that encodes garment features via parallelized self-attention modules (Hydra Encoding Blocks) shared across conditions, adding only ~8% parameters per extra garment, and a MainNet that performs the actual denoising. A Hydra Fusion Block injects HydraNet key/value features into MainNet's self-attention layers via concatenation with learnable positional embeddings to distinguish garment sources. For robustness, a Prior Model Evolution strategy synthesizes initial weights by adding scaled residuals from SDXL-inpainting and DreamshaperXL to SDXL-base (coefficients α=1.0, β=1.1 found by discrete greedy search on CLIP score), and an Adaptive Mask Boost strategy uses parsing-free OpenPose-only masks with random elongation during training and aspect-ratio-adaptive mask extension at inference to prevent clothing-shape leakage and enable autonomous garment-length determination.

## Results

On VITON-HD, AnyFit achieves the best FID (8.60) and KID (0.55), outperforming the next-best methods DCI-VTON (FID 8.76) and OOTDiffusion (KID 0.68) respectively, while also attaining the highest SSIM (0.893). On DressCode, AnyFit leads on LPIPS (0.044), FID (4.51), and KID (0.48), with the KID margin over IDM (1.24) being particularly large. For multi-garment try-on on DressCode-multiple, AnyFit achieves FID 20.43 and KID 7.10 with only 9% additional inference time, surpassing VTON-concat (FID 21.11, KID 7.30) and IDM-2Stage (FID 21.47, KID 7.85). On the challenging proprietary e-commerce dataset, AnyFit trained on proprietary data achieves FID 43.97 and KID 0.69, substantially outperforming IDM (FID 48.76, KID 4.35).

## Baselines & Comparisons

AnyFit outperforms all seven single-garment baselines on the unpaired metrics (FID and KID) across both VITON-HD and DressCode, with the largest margins over IDM on DressCode (FID 4.51 vs. 5.32; KID 0.48 vs. 1.24) and over DCI-VTON on VITON-HD (FID 8.60 vs. 8.76). On paired metrics, AnyFit achieves the best SSIM on VITON-HD (0.893) and the best LPIPS on DressCode (0.044), though DCI-VTON edges it slightly on VITON-HD LPIPS (0.072 vs. 0.075) and IDM leads on DressCode SSIM (0.923 vs. 0.904). For multi-garment try-on, AnyFit beats VTON-concat by 0.68 FID and 0.20 KID at a comparable 9% inference overhead, and substantially outperforms IDM-2Stage (FID 20.43 vs. 21.47) which suffers from sequential dressing artifacts. On the proprietary e-commerce dataset, AnyFit(proprietary) dominates all baselines by a wide margin (KID 0.69 vs. 4.35 for IDM), demonstrating strong generalization to complex real-world scenarios with layered garments and diverse backgrounds.

## Relation to Prior Work

AnyFit extends the diffusion-based VTON lineage (StableVTON, OOTDiffusion, IDM) by replacing the single-condition parallel U-Net (ReferenceNet/GarmentNet) with a HydraNet that parallelizes only self-attention layers across multiple garment branches, enabling arbitrary multi-condition injection at ~8% parameter cost per added branch. It departs from prior work in two further ways: a Prior Model Evolution strategy that linearly merges weight residuals from SDXL-base, SDXL-inpainting, and DreamshaperXL to recover capabilities lost during inpainting pretraining, and an Adaptive Mask Boost that eliminates reliance on parsing-derived masks by using pose-only masks with random elongation. The Hydra Fusion Block generalizes the cross-attention injection used in OOTDiffusion and IDM to handle variable-length condition sequences via concatenation with positional embeddings.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

AnyFit is the first VTON method to natively support arbitrary combinations of attire (tops, bottoms, layered garments) in a single forward pass, addressing a key gap for e-commerce applications where users want to mix and match outfits. The HydraNet's parallelized-attention design and the Prior Model Evolution weight-merging strategy are potentially transferable to other multi-condition generative tasks beyond VTON. Limitations include inherited instability in generating complex hand structures from the base diffusion model and only preliminary text-control capability, leaving open questions about full controllability and extension to video try-on or 3D-consistent generation.
