## Problem

Existing 3D indoor scene generation methods either add objects autoregressively (failing to capture complex inter-object spatial relationships) or use diffusion models that jointly predict all attributes (categorical, spatial, shape) in a single high-dimensional space, which is less data-efficient and does not prioritize spatial reasoning. No prior score-based method focuses exclusively on accurately placing oriented 3D bounding boxes within a bounded floor plan while conditioning on object semantics.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/NEURIPS20243466/cropped_page1_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The model takes a 2D floor plan polygon (encoded via PointNet on 100 sampled contour points) and a set of one-hot semantic category vectors as conditioning, and outputs an 8-dimensional spatial vector per object: 3D center position, 2D rotation encoding (cos θ, sin θ), and 3D dimensions. Notably, the model does not predict semantic categories or shape codes—only spatial attributes. At test time, a binary mask over the 8 spatial dimensions enables inpainting for completion, re-arrangement, and coarse-specification generation, while the initial noise level σ_max controls the degree of freedom in the output.

## Method

DeBaRA adapts the EDM score-based framework to a lightweight transformer denoiser that predicts only 3D spatial attributes (oriented bounding boxes) conditioned on a floor plan and object categories. The architecture uses a shared sinusoidal-encoding object encoder, a PointNet floor encoder, a noise-level token, and a 3-layer transformer (4 heads, no positional ordering) followed by a shared MLP decoder. Training employs a novel semantic-aware Chamfer distance loss that penalizes permutation of objects with mismatched categories, combined with conditioning dropout (p_drop=0.2) to jointly model conditional and unconditional layout densities. A Self Score Evaluation (SSE) procedure leverages the unconditional density to score and select the most plausible conditioning candidate from a set of externally generated (e.g., LLM) semantic sets, enabling 3D scene synthesis without manual category specification.

## Results

On 3D-FRONT living rooms, DeBaRA achieves FID 18.89, KID 3.57, SCA 68.3%, and OBA 167.8 m², outperforming all baselines on every metric. On dining rooms, it reaches FID 22.04, KID 4.41, SCA 52.4%, and OBA 132.8 m². The model uses only 12.2M parameters and generates a layout in 0.49 s (50 EDM steps), compared to 32.8 s for DiffuScene (89.7M params) and 0.16 s for ATISS (36.1M params). On scene re-arrangement, DeBaRA beats the purpose-built LEGO-Net baseline (FID 24.92 vs 26.81, KID 9.47 vs 13.18, Distance Moved 0.082 vs 0.094).

## Baselines & Comparisons

DeBaRA outperforms all four baselines on every reported metric for both living and dining rooms. Against the autoregressive ATISS, DeBaRA reduces FID by 26% (18.89 vs 25.67) and OBA by 80% (167.8 vs 857.3 m²) on living rooms, while also improving SCA (68.3 vs 71.8, closer to the ideal 50%). Against the denoising-based DiffuScene, DeBaRA achieves a 12% FID reduction (18.89 vs 21.54) and halves the out-of-bounds area (167.8 vs 341.1 m²), with a 44% KID improvement (3.57 vs 6.40). The training-free LayoutGPT baseline is substantially worse on all spatial validity metrics (OBA 2913.6 vs 167.8 m²), confirming that LLMs alone struggle with precise 3D spatial reasoning. On scene re-arrangement, DeBaRA surpasses LEGO-Net—which was specifically trained for this task—on both realism (FID 24.92 vs 26.81) and fidelity to the initial configuration (Distance Moved 0.082 vs 0.094), demonstrating that a single spatial-focused denoiser can handle multiple downstream tasks without task-specific training.

## Relation to Prior Work

DeBaRA builds on the EDM score-based diffusion framework (Karras et al., 2022) for its continuous noise parameterization and 2nd-order stochastic sampler, and adopts classifier-free guidance (Ho & Salimans, 2022) via conditioning dropout to jointly model conditional and unconditional layout densities. It departs from prior 3D scene synthesis methods in a key architectural decision: rather than modeling all object attributes (categorical + spatial) in a single high-dimensional space as DiffuScene does, or generating objects autoregressively as ATISS does, DeBaRA restricts its prediction to oriented 3D bounding boxes conditioned on floor plan and semantic categories. The Repaint inpainting formulation (Lugmayr et al., 2022) is repurposed to enable multi-task capabilities (completion, re-arrangement, coarse-specification generation) from a single trained network, and the novel semantic-aware Chamfer distance loss and Self Score Evaluation procedure represent the paper's primary methodological departures from the diffusion literature.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The paper demonstrates that a lightweight (12.2M parameter) score-based model focused exclusively on spatial reasoning can outperform larger, multi-attribute diffusion models on bounded 3D layout generation while enabling real-time inference (<1s) and unifying generation, completion, re-arrangement, and LLM-guided synthesis under one framework. The SSE procedure offers a practical bridge between LLM-generated semantic conditioning and 3D spatial validity, addressing a known weakness of LLMs in spatial reasoning. Key limitations include the absence of physical collision constraints, reliance on a fixed finite category set, and no style consistency enforcement. Open questions include whether the spatial-only prediction strategy generalizes to open-vocabulary or multi-modal (text, image) conditioning, and whether physical constraints can be incorporated without sacrificing the model's data efficiency.
