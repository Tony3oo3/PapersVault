## Problem

Existing social relation recognition methods train dedicated networks end-to-end on labeled image data, limiting their generalizability to unseen domains and providing no interpretable explanations for their predictions. This paper asks whether a modular pipeline combining vision foundation models for perception and large language models for reasoning can match or exceed supervised baselines in a zero-shot setting while producing human-readable justifications.

## Input / Output

The method takes an RGB image and two person bounding boxes as input. The image is first decomposed into object-level masks via SAM, then each masked object is captioned by BLIP-2, and the resulting fragments are fused by an LLM into a single coherent 'social story' paragraph where objects are referenced by symbols (P_i for people, O_i for other objects) rather than raw coordinates. The output is a free-form text response whose first sentence follows a fixed template ('The final answer is [class]') followed by a natural-language explanation of the reasoning.

## Method

SocialGPT follows a 'perceive with VFMs, reason with LLMs' paradigm: SAM segments the image into object masks, BLIP-2 generates both dense per-object captions and task-oriented queries (age, gender, social scene), and an LLM fuses all fragments into a coherent social story using symbol-based referencing to enable unambiguous object queries. For reasoning, a structured SocialPrompt with four segments (System rules, Expectation/role assignment, Context/category definitions, Guidance/in-context example) is concatenated with the social story and a symbol-based query, and a frozen LLM (GPT-3.5, Vicuna, or Llama2) generates the relation label and explanation. To address the long-prompt optimization challenge (prompts exceed 2K tokens, making token-level search intractable), the authors propose Greedy Segment Prompt Optimization (GSPO), which treats each of the four prompt segments as a discrete variable with a candidate set, computes gradients over one-hot segment indicators to select top-K promising substitutions per segment, and greedily evaluates K×M single-segment replacements per iteration over N rounds to minimize a next-token-prediction loss on the first-sentence answer template.

## Results

On the PIPA dataset (16 social relation categories), SocialGPT with Vicuna-13B achieves 66.7% zero-shot accuracy, surpassing the previous state-of-the-art supervised method TRGAT (65.3%) by 1.4%. On the PISC dataset (6 categories), SocialGPT (Vicuna-13B) reaches 65.12% accuracy, edging out GR2N (64.70%). With the proposed GSPO prompt optimization, PIPA accuracy further improves to 69.23% (+2.53%) and PISC to 66.19% (+1.07%). SocialGPT also outperforms the end-to-end VLM GPT-4V by 7.03% on PIPA (66.7% vs. 59.67%).

## Baselines & Comparisons

SocialGPT with Vicuna-13B (66.7%) outperforms all eight prior supervised baselines on PIPA, including the previous SOTA TRGAT (65.3%), MGR (64.4%), and GR2N (64.3%). On PISC, SocialGPT (65.12%) beats GR2N (64.70%) and GRM (64.18%), while the weakest baseline Pair CNN (46.30%) lags by nearly 19 points. Against end-to-end vision-language models queried directly on PIPA, SocialGPT (66.7%) surpasses GPT-4V (59.67%), LLaVA (45.12%), and BLIP-2 (35.84%), demonstrating that the modular perceive-then-reason pipeline with structured prompting is more effective than monolithic VLM inference. The GPT-3.5 variant (64.1%) is slightly below TRGAT but still competitive, while Vicuna-13B consistently yields the strongest results across both datasets and all LLM backbones tested (Vicuna-7B/13B, Llama2-7B/13B).

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper departs from the dominant end-to-end learning paradigm for social relation recognition (GRM, GR²N, TRGAT, MGR), which trains dedicated networks on labeled image data, and instead adopts a modular 'perceive with VFMs, reason with LLMs' framework. It repurposes existing foundation models—SAM for segmentation, BLIP-2 for dense captioning, and LLMs for reasoning—by introducing a novel textual bridge (the 'social story' with symbol-based object referencing) that converts a visual classification problem into a text-based generative reasoning task. The GSPO algorithm extends discrete prompt optimization methods like AutoPrompt to handle the unique long-prompt challenge (>2K tokens) inherent in visual reasoning, where the prompt must encode comprehensive scene descriptions alongside task instructions.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

SocialGPT is the first zero-shot method for social relation recognition, demonstrating that structured perception-plus-reasoning pipelines can match or slightly exceed fully supervised end-to-end models while providing interpretable explanations—a capability end-to-end networks fundamentally lack. The ablation showing a 16.27% drop when bypassing social story fusion highlights that coherent narrative integration of visual facts is critical for LLM-based reasoning, a finding with implications beyond this task. However, performance is tightly coupled to the quality of upstream VFMs (segmentation errors, caption hallucinations), the approach inherits biases from foundation models, and the modest margins (1.4% over TRGAT on PIPA) on small datasets limit confidence in broad generalizability. Open questions include whether GSPO scales to even longer prompts, whether the framework handles multi-person scenes with many concurrent relations, and how to mitigate compounding errors across the modular pipeline.
