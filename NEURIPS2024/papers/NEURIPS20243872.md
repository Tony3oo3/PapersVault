## Problem

Existing image-to-3D methods rely on indirect pipelines that first generate multi-view images via a 2D diffusion model and then reconstruct 3D geometry through sparse-view reconstruction or SDS optimization, introducing efficiency bottlenecks and detail loss. No native 3D generative model has been demonstrated to scale to in-the-wild input images while producing high-fidelity geometry directly from a single view.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/NEURIPS20243872/cropped_page1_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method takes a single 512×512 RGB image as conditioning input, which is processed by DINO-v2 (ViT-L/14) for pixel-level structural tokens and CLIP (ViT-L/14) for semantic-level tokens. Internally, the VAE consumes 81,920 surface points with normals from the 3D model, and the diffusion model operates on a compact 32×32×16 triplane latent (three planes, 16 channels each). The final output is a 3D mesh extracted from a 256×256 triplane-decoded occupancy grid, with textured meshes optionally produced via SyncMVD.

## Method

Direct3D comprises two stages: a D3D-VAE that encodes point clouds into an explicit triplane latent via a transformer encoder with cross-attention to learnable tokens, then decodes it through a CNN upsampler to a 256×256 triplane and an MLP occupancy head, supervised by a semi-continuous surface sampling strategy (linear interpolation within a 1/512 SDF band) to avoid abrupt gradients; and a D3D-DiT (DiT-XL/2, 28 layers) that denoises the triplane latent conditioned on the input image. Each DiT block injects pixel-level DINO-v2 tokens via concatenation into self-attention and semantic-level CLIP tokens via cross-attention, while using adaLN-single for time and class conditioning. This dual-level image conditioning and the explicit 3D triplane structure (as opposed to a 1D implicit latent) enable the model to capture both fine geometric detail and semantic consistency with the conditional image, trained with classifier-free guidance at 10% dropout.

## Results

On the Google Scanned Objects (GSO) dataset, Direct3D trained on Objaverse+internal data achieves the best Chamfer Distance (0.0271), Volume IoU (0.4323), and F-Score (0.5624) among all compared methods. Even when trained solely on Objaverse (160K assets), it already outperforms all baselines on every metric. In a user study with 46 volunteers rating mesh quality and consistency on a 1–5 scale, Direct3D scores 4.41 (quality) and 4.35 (consistency), roughly doubling the scores of the next-best method (InstantMesh at 2.53/2.66).

## Baselines & Comparisons

Direct3D outperforms all four baselines on every metric in Table 1. Against the closest competitor InstantMesh (a multi-view diffusion + sparse reconstruction pipeline), Direct3D reduces Chamfer Distance from 0.0327 to 0.0271 (−17%), improves Volume IoU from 0.4105 to 0.4323, and raises F-Score from 0.5058 to 0.5624. Against Shap-E (a 3D diffusion model on an implicit 1D latent), the gap is largest: Chamfer Distance drops by 54% (0.0585→0.0271) and Volume IoU nearly doubles (0.2347→0.4323). Michelangelo, despite competitive F-Score (0.4371), suffers from the lowest Volume IoU (0.1260), indicating poor volumetric fidelity. One-2-3-45, which relies on SparseNeuS reconstruction, produces the coarsest geometry (F-Score 0.3149). The user study confirms these quantitative gaps: Direct3D's quality score of 4.41 is nearly 2× that of InstantMesh (2.53) and nearly 4× that of Shap-E (1.18), and its consistency score (4.35) similarly dominates all baselines.

## Relation to Prior Work

Direct3D extends the latent diffusion paradigm from LDM (Rombach et al.) and the DiT architecture (Peebles & Xie) into the 3D domain, but makes two key departures from prior 3D generation work. First, it replaces the implicit 1D latent spaces used by Shap-E, Michelangelo, and 3DShape2VecSet with an explicit triplane latent (inspired by RODIN's plane concatenation), supervised directly via semi-continuous occupancy rather than rendered-image losses. Second, it bypasses the dominant multi-view diffusion + SDS/reconstruction pipeline (DreamFusion, MVDream, One-2-3-45, InstantMesh) in favor of a single native 3D diffusion step conditioned on one image, using DINO-v2 for pixel-level and CLIP for semantic-level alignment within each DiT block (with adaLN-single from PixArt for efficient conditioning).

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

By demonstrating that a native 3D latent diffusion model can outperform multi-view pipelines on both quality and generalization to in-the-wild images (from Flux, SDXL, Hunyuan-DiT) without any 2D training data, Direct3D challenges the prevailing assumption that multi-view consistency is a necessary intermediate step for image-to-3D. The explicit triplane latent with direct geometry supervision offers a cleaner, more scalable inductive bias than implicit latents, and the dual-level image conditioning (pixel + semantic) is a reusable design pattern. However, the evaluation is limited to 30 GSO models and four baselines, the method is restricted to individual objects (not scenes), and the reliance on a proprietary 'internal data' set for the best results limits full reproducibility. Open questions include whether the approach scales to scene-level generation and whether the triplane resolution (256×256) is sufficient for very high-frequency geometry.
