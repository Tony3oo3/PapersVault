## Problem

Existing LLM reasoning methods (CoT, ToT, GoT) focus on refining the reasoning process itself but neglect the critical step of first identifying and making explicit the logical relationships within the context. In contextually aware multi-hop reasoning, this oversight leads to superficial interaction with the context, where implicit logical structures and multi-hop connections remain buried in plain text, undermining the quality and reliability of reasoning outcomes.

## Input / Output

The method takes a contextual passage (document or paragraph) and a multi-hop question as input, and produces a final answer. The context is first transformed into a structured MindMap in strict JSON format that encodes hierarchical logical relationships and multi-hop connections. The pruning model receives concatenated logical relationship strings (e.g., '[CLS] Producer: Education: Clifton College [SEP]') along with the question, and the final reasoning step feeds the pruned MindMap (optionally alongside the original text) to the LLM with a zero-shot prompt.

## Method

InfoRE re-organizes context before reasoning through two operations. First, extraction uses an LLM to convert plain text into a MindMap structure that explicitly surfaces logical relationships (parallelism, causality, contrast) and multi-hop connections (e.g., Julius Caesar → Director → Education). Second, pruning employs a BERT-base policy model trained with PPO reinforcement learning to selectively remove irrelevant or distracting logical relationships; the reward is the F1 score between the LLM's answer and the reference, with a clipped policy term (ε=0.2) to stabilize training. The pruned MindMap then serves as the re-organized context for the reasoning step, where the LLM answers the question in a zero-shot setting, optionally augmented with Chain-of-Thought prompting. This approach is complementary to existing reasoning methods and can be stacked with CoT for additional gains.

## Results

Using a zero-shot setting, InfoRE achieves an average absolute F1 improvement of 4% across all contextually aware multi-hop reasoning tasks compared to the Standard baseline. On GPT-4, InfoRE reaches 76.52% F1 on 2WikiMultiHopQA, 83.22% on HotpotQA, and 95.62% on FEVEROUS, while InfoRE+CoT further pushes 2WikiMultiHopQA to 78.60% and HotpotQA to 84.26%. The largest single-task gain is +6.33% F1 on 2WikiMultiHopQA with GPT-3.5 (58.25→64.58), and improvements are consistent across Llama2-70B, GPT-3.5, and GPT-4.

## Baselines & Comparisons

The paper compares InfoRE against two baselines: Standard (direct reasoning over raw context) and CoT (appending 'Let's think step by step'). InfoRE consistently outperforms both baselines across all eight datasets and all three LLMs (Llama2-70B, GPT-3.5, GPT-4). On GPT-4, InfoRE improves over Standard by +3.83% F1 on 2WikiMultiHopQA, +3.89% on HotpotQA, and +3.29% on FEVEROUS, while also surpassing CoT by +2.44% on 2WikiMultiHopQA and +2.56% on HotpotQA. The combination InfoRE+CoT yields the strongest results, e.g., 78.60% F1 on 2WikiMultiHopQA and 95.67% on FEVEROUS with GPT-4, indicating the two strategies are complementary. Gains are largest for weaker models (Llama2-70B sees up to +5.06% on 2WikiMultiHopQA) and smallest for GPT-4 (typically +2.5–4%), suggesting the re-organized context is most beneficial when the model's inherent reasoning is less robust.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper departs from the dominant line of work on improving LLM reasoning (CoT, ToT, GoT) that focuses on refining intermediate reasoning steps, instead proposing a pre-reasoning context re-organization step. Conceptually, it is closest to GraphRAG and SG, which also restructure documents into graph-like representations, but differs by using a centralized MindMap structure (rather than scattered triples or community hierarchies) and by adding an RL-trained BERT pruning module to remove question-irrelevant relations. The method is positioned as complementary to prompting strategies like CoT rather than a replacement, and the RL-based pruning draws on PPO in a way that is somewhat unusual for a text-filtering sub-task.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The consistent ~4% zero-shot improvement across three LLMs and seven datasets, and the demonstrated complementarity with CoT, suggest that explicit logical-relationship extraction before reasoning is a useful and underexplored axis for improving context-aware multi-hop reasoning. However, the gains are modest, no error bars are reported, and the RL-based pruning only marginally outperforms a simple cosine-similarity baseline (1.26%), raising questions about whether the added complexity is justified. Open questions include whether the re-organization step can be performed by smaller/cheaper models, whether other structures (tables, timelines) would be equally or more effective, and whether the approach generalizes beyond the relatively short contexts used in the evaluated benchmarks.
