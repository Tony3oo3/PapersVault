## Problem

Existing automated n-ary relation extraction methods are locked to a single NKG schema, assume a fixed entity order, and output a fixed number of entities, making them unable to handle the diversity of real-world n-ary facts. No prior method can flexibly perform fine-grained n-ary RE across the four major NKG schemas (hyper-relational, event-based, role-based, hypergraph-based) while jointly resolving entity ordering and variable arity.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/NEURIPS20240861/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method takes a single natural language sentence with pre-annotated entity spans (m entities) as input and produces a set of n-ary relational facts where n is not fixed a priori. Each extracted fact is an ordered span-tuple of entities paired with a schema-specific relation label-list: n−1 relations for hyper-relational, n+1 for event-based, n for role-based, and 1 for hypergraph-based schema. The arity of the final output facts is determined dynamically through unsupervised merging of 3-ary atomic facts, so the system can emit facts of any arity without explicit arity specification.

## Method

Text2NKG reformulates n-ary RE as a span-tuple multi-label classification problem over all ordered triples of entities, using packed levitated markers to encode each entity separately within a single BERT forward pass, reducing training-item complexity from O(m³) to O(m). For each triple, n_r linear classifiers (one per relation slot, where n_r depends on the chosen schema) predict relation labels via a weighted cross-entropy loss with a null-label bias hyperparameter α to handle the heavy class imbalance. A key architectural insight is the hetero-ordered merging strategy: during decoding, the predicted probabilities from all 6 permutations of the same three entities are combined (with inverse-relation swapping where applicable) to resolve the correct entity order and filter out non-conforming facts. Finally, an output merging step unsupervisedly combines 3-ary atomic facts sharing a common anchor (main triple, event type, key-value pair, or hyperedge) into higher-arity n-ary facts, enabling variable-arity extraction without explicit arity supervision. Data augmentation further trains the model on all 6 entity-order permutations of each fact with correspondingly transformed relation labels, reinforcing order-awareness.

## Results

On the HyperRED benchmark (hyper-relational schema), Text2NKG with BERT-base achieves an F1 of 84.89 (dev) and 83.63 (test), surpassing the previous best method CubeRE (65.23 dev / 65.04 test) by approximately 19.7 and 18.6 percentage points respectively. With BERT-large, Text2NKG further improves to 85.21 (dev) and 83.81 (test) F1. Across the three additional NKG schemas (event-based, role-based, hypergraph-based), Text2NKG with BERT-base achieves F1 scores of 82.58, 82.64, and 85.03, outperforming the strongest baselines (LasUIE) by 3.8–10.1 points. Unsupervised LLM baselines (ChatGPT, GPT-4) achieve only 11–16% F1 across all four schemas, far below the supervised Text2NKG.

## Baselines & Comparisons

Text2NKG decisively outperforms all supervised baselines in the hyper-relational schema: it beats CubeRE (the only prior hyper-relational extraction method) by ~19.7 F1 points on dev and ~18.6 on test with BERT-base, and by ~16.4 / 17.1 with BERT-large. The Pipeline Baseline (UniRE + BERT-Tagger) and Generative Baseline (BART) trail further behind at 63.24 and 61.80 F1 (dev) respectively. In the three additional schemas (event-based, role-based, hypergraph-based), Text2NKG surpasses the strongest event/unified extraction baselines—LasUIE (BERT-large) achieves 80.40, 77.97, and 76.20 F1, while Text2NKG (BERT-large) reaches 84.19, 83.76, and 85.89—gains of 3.8 to 10.1 points. Unsupervised LLM baselines (ChatGPT at ~11–12% F1, GPT-4 at ~14–16% F1) are far outperformed, confirming that fine-grained multi-label relation classification over a large relation vocabulary remains beyond current LLM prompting capabilities. Ablation results confirm that hetero-ordered merging is the most critical component (removing it drops F1 from 84.89 to 68.52), followed by data augmentation and the null-label weight α.

## Relation to Prior Work

Text2NKG builds on the packed levitated marker encoding from Ye et al. (ACL 2022) to efficiently represent entity span-tuples, reducing training complexity from O(m³) to O(m). It departs from prior n-ary RE methods (Jia et al. 2019, Zhuang et al. 2022) that treated extraction as fixed-arity binary classification, and from CubeRE (Chia et al., EMNLP 2022) which handles only the hyper-relational schema via cube-filling. The key conceptual departure is reframing n-ary RE as span-tuple multi-label classification with hetero-ordered merging, enabling a single framework to handle four distinct NKG schemas (hyper-relational, event-based, role-based, hypergraph-based) with variable arity, rather than requiring schema-specific architectures.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

This is the first unified framework for fine-grained n-ary RE across all four major NKG schemas, and the extension of HyperRED to four schemas provides a much-needed multi-schema benchmark for the community. The ~19-point F1 improvement over CubeRE and the demonstration that LLMs (ChatGPT, GPT-4) achieve only ~10–15% F1 on this fine-grained task highlight both the value of task-specific supervised models and the gap in LLM capabilities for structured multi-label extraction. Open questions include whether the approach generalizes beyond HyperRED to other domains, how to effectively combine supervised extraction with LLMs for cold-start scenarios, and whether the 3-ary atomic unit assumption holds for all real-world n-ary facts.
