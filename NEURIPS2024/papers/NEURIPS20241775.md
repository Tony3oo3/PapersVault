## Problem

Dynamic regret for adversarial linear mixture MDPs with unknown transition had not been resolved without prior knowledge of the non-stationarity measure. Prior methods either required knowing the non-stationarity measure in advance, suffered suboptimal dependence on the episode length H, or incurred an undesirable polynomial dependence on the state space size S. This paper closes the gap by achieving near-optimal dynamic regret simultaneously in d, H, K, and P̄_K without any prior knowledge of non-stationarity.

## Input / Output

At each episode k, the learner receives the initial state s_{k,1}, selects a policy, and interacts with the environment for H steps, observing the full reward function {r_{k,h}}_{h∈[H]} only after the episode ends (full-information feedback). The known feature mapping φ(s'|s,a) ∈ R^d parameterizes the unknown transition via θ*_h, and the learner must estimate θ*_h from observed trajectories. The output is a per-state action distribution π_k and an occupancy measure q̂_k, with the theoretical guarantee being a dynamic regret bound of Õ(√(d²H³K) + √(HK(H+P̄_K))).

## Method

The proposed OOPE algorithm combines two complementary components bridged by a novel conversion. First, an occupancy-measure-based global optimization uses online mirror descent over a confidence-set-constrained decision set Δ(C_k, α), with a two-layer structure (a step-size pool of O(log K) base-learners tracked by a Hedge meta-algorithm) to adapt to unknown non-stationarity. Second, a policy-based variance-aware value-targeted regression estimates the transition parameter θ*_h via weighted ridge regression that exploits the variance of the value function, constructing an optimistic Q-function Q_{k,h} = [r_{k,h} + max_{θ∈C_{k,h}} ⟨θ, φ_{V_{k,h+1}}⟩]_{[0,H]}. The key technical insight is a conversion that decomposes the occupancy-measure approximation error into an occupancy-policy gap (shown non-positive by induction using the optimism of the value estimator) plus a value estimation error, thereby avoiding the need to bound occupancy measure discrepancies directly—which would introduce an S-dependence—and instead reducing the problem to bounding value estimation error via the variance-aware regression.

## Results

The paper establishes a dynamic regret upper bound of Õ(d√(H³K) + √(HK(H+P̄_K))) for adversarial linear mixture MDPs with unknown transition and full-information feedback (Theorem 1), where d is the feature dimension, H is the episode length, K is the number of episodes, and P̄_K is the non-stationarity measure. A matching lower bound of Ω(d√(H³K) + √(HK(H+P̄_K))) is proven (Theorem 2), establishing minimax optimality up to logarithmic factors. This is the first result achieving near-optimal dynamic regret in d, H, K, and P̄_K simultaneously without prior knowledge of the non-stationarity measure.

## Baselines & Comparisons

The paper's method strictly improves upon all three prior works in Table 1. Compared to Zhong et al. [2021], it achieves a better dependence on H (H^{7/4} → H^{3/2}), K (K^{3/4} → K^{1/2}), and the non-stationarity measure (P_K^{1/3} → P̄_K^{1/2}), while also removing the need for prior knowledge of P_K. Compared to Li et al. [2023], it improves the H-dependence in the second term from H²√((K+P_K)(1+P_K)) to √(HK(H+P̄_K)) and eliminates the requirement of knowing P_K in advance. The most direct comparison is with Li et al. [2024b], which also handles unknown non-stationarity but incurs a polynomial dependence on the state space size S (dHS√K); the present work removes this S-dependence entirely, replacing it with the feature dimension d (d√(H³K)), which is the key statistical improvement. The matching lower bound confirms that no algorithm can improve upon these rates beyond logarithmic factors.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper sits at the intersection of two well-studied paradigms for adversarial MDPs: occupancy-measure-based optimization (Zimin & Neu 2013, Jin et al. 2020a) and policy-based value-targeted regression (Ayoub et al. 2020, He et al. 2022). For dynamic regret specifically, it builds on the two-layer ensemble framework from online learning (Zhang et al. 2018, Yan et al. 2023) and the tabular dynamic regret results of Zhao et al. 2022, while extending the linear mixture MDP setting of Li et al. 2023 (known non-stationarity) and Li et al. 2024b (unknown non-stationarity but S-dependent). The key departure is a novel occupancy-to-policy conversion that replaces the traditional ℓ₁-occupancy-discrepancy bound with a value-difference argument, thereby exploiting the linear structure of the transition to eliminate the undesirable state-space-size dependence.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

This is the first work to achieve minimax-optimal dynamic regret (up to log factors) for adversarial linear mixture MDPs with unknown transition and unknown non-stationarity, closing a gap that prior methods left open either by requiring knowledge of the non-stationarity measure or by incurring polynomial dependence on the state space size S. The main limitation acknowledged is computational: the occupancy-measure-based component requires solving a projection problem at each step, making the algorithm significantly more expensive than purely policy-based alternatives. An important open question is whether the same near-optimal statistical guarantees can be attained by computationally efficient policy-based methods alone, and whether the results extend to other function-approximation classes such as generalized linear or multinomial logit models.
