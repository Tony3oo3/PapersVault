## Problem

Existing methods for modeling single-cell perturbation responses either restrict analysis to a single biological context (e.g., iVAE, sVAE) or rely on additive latent models that cannot capture how treatments differentially affect specific cell types or genetic backgrounds. This paper addresses the gap by proposing a framework that explicitly disentangles treatment effects, covariate effects, and their interactions while providing formal identifiability guarantees for all three components.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/NEURIPS20243865/cropped_page2_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method consumes single-cell RNA-seq gene expression vectors (k-dimensional), discrete treatment/intervention labels, and biological context covariates (cell line, tissue, patient), all assumed to be observed without measurement noise in the generative model. It produces three disentangled latent blocks: z_x (covariate-specific, invariant to treatment), z_t (treatment-specific, invariant to covariate), and z_tx (interaction-specific, dependent on both). The z_tx prior is structured via a Hadamard product of learned covariate and treatment embeddings, and the decoder g maps the concatenated latent vector back to gene expression. For prediction tasks, the model recombines control z_x^0 with treated z_tx and z_t to generate predicted expression profiles.

## Method

FCR is a variational autoencoder with a factorized latent space [z_x, z_tx, z_t] where the interaction prior p(z_tx|t,x) is parameterized through a Hadamard product of covariate and treatment embeddings to explicitly encode interactions. The paper proves component-wise identifiability of z_tx under a novel Experimental Sufficiency assumption (requiring 2n_tx+1 cross-referenced [t,x] experimental conditions) and block-wise identifiability of z_x and z_t via their invariance properties, extending Khemakhem et al. (2020) to interacting auxiliary variables. Training combines the standard ELBO with two additional regularizers: a causal structure loss that enforces z_x similarity and z_t dissimilarity between control and treated groups (plus a treatment classifier on [z_t, z_tx]), and adversarial permutation discriminators that enforce conditional independence z_x ⊥ z_tx | x and z_t ⊥ z_tx | t by training binary classifiers to distinguish permuted from original interaction samples.

## Results

On the conditional cellular responses prediction task (R² score for predicting 2000-gene expression in control cells under drug treatment), FCR achieves 0.87±0.02 on sciPlex, 0.90±0.03 on multiPlex-Tram, 0.83±0.03 on multiPlex-7, and 0.78±0.03 on multiPlex-9, outperforming all baselines on the first three datasets. FCR also achieves the highest NMI scores in clustering analysis for covariates (z_x), treatments (z_t), and combined features (z_tx) on sciPlex. In conditional independence testing (KCI), FCR is the only method whose p-values exceed 0.05 for all four required independence statements, confirming proper disentanglement.

## Baselines & Comparisons

FCR outperforms VCI (Wu et al., 2023) and CPA (Lotfollahi et al., 2023) by 0.01–0.03 R² on sciPlex, multiPlex-Tram, and multiPlex-7, with the gap narrowing to a 0.01 R² loss to CPA on multiPlex-9 (0.78 vs. 0.79), which the authors attribute to fewer covariate-specific responses in that dataset. sVAE (Lopez et al., 2023) trails FCR by 0.02–0.05 R² across all four datasets. scGEN (Lotfollahi et al., 2019) and CINEMA-OT (Dong et al., 2023), both designed for binary perturbations, underperform substantially (R² 0.32–0.56), confirming their limitation for multi-dose/multi-treatment settings. In clustering (NMI), FCR's z_x, z_t, and z_tx blocks each outperform the corresponding latent variables from scVI, iVAE, βVAE, and factorVAE. In KCI conditional independence testing, all nine baselines produce p-values below 0.05 (rejecting independence), while FCR's p-values generally exceed 0.05, validating its disentanglement guarantees.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the identifiable deep generative model framework of Khemakhem et al. (2020, iVAE) from single-context to multi-context settings with interacting covariates, proving component-wise identifiability of interaction terms and block-wise identifiability of main effects (the latter adapted from Kong et al., 2022). It departs from prior causal representation learning methods for single-cell data (sVAE, VCI) that are restricted to a single biological context, and from additive latent models (CPA, Hetzel et al.) that handle multiple contexts but cannot capture treatment-by-context interactions. The permutation discriminator mechanism for enforcing conditional independence is borrowed from Bellot and van der Schaar (2019), repurposed here as an adversarial regularizer within a VAE training loop.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The identifiability guarantees are the paper's core contribution: they ensure that the learned interaction embedding z_tx reflects true biological interactions rather than spurious correlations, which is critical for drug discovery where cell-type-specific responses determine therapeutic efficacy. A key limitation is that FCR cannot predict responses to unseen treatments or cell types, restricting its utility in the most practically important scenario of generalizing to novel compounds. The experimental sufficiency assumption (requiring cross-referenced treatment-context experiments) is a practical constraint that may not always be met in high-throughput screening. Future work on incorporating interpretable gene-level components into the latent blocks would bridge the gap between abstract representations and actionable biological insight.
