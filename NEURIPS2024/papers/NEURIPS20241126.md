## Problem

Most protein language models ignore 3D structure information despite protein function being determined by structure. Existing structure-aware PLMs (SaProt, ProstT5) rely on Foldseek for structure tokenization, which encodes each residue using only its immediate neighbors, missing subtle local structural features like catalytic sites and binding pockets. Additionally, their standard Transformer attention cannot explicitly model the relationship between residue sequences and structure token sequences.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/NEURIPS20241126/cropped_page3_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

ProSST takes a protein amino acid sequence and its 3D Cα coordinates as input. The structure is decomposed into per-residue local structures (each residue plus up to 40 nearest neighbors within 10 Å), encoded by a GVP auto-encoder, and quantized into a discrete token sequence of the same length as the sequence (default vocabulary size K=2048, with [SOS]/[EOS]/[PAD] tokens). The model outputs contextual residue hidden states (110M parameters) that can be used directly for zero-shot mutation scoring via log-likelihood ratios or fine-tuned for classification/regression downstream tasks.

## Method

ProSST combines two key modules: (1) a structure quantization pipeline where a 6-layer GVP encoder (trained with a denoising objective on CATH, perturbing Cα coordinates with 3D Gaussian noise and Brownian motion on rotation matrices) embeds each residue's local structure into a dense vector, which is then assigned a discrete token via k-means clustering (K=2048 centroids); and (2) a sequence-structure disentangled attention mechanism inspired by DeBERTa that decomposes pairwise attention into five explicit components—residue-to-residue, residue-to-structure, residue-to-position, structure-to-residue, and position-to-residue—replacing standard self-attention to directly model cross-modal interactions. The model is pre-trained with masked language modeling (15% masking: 80% mask, 10% random, 10% unchanged) on 18.8 million AlphaFoldDB structures, using the structure token sequence as an additional conditioning signal alongside the corrupted amino acid sequence.

## Results

On the ProteinGYM zero-shot mutation effect prediction benchmark, ProSST achieves a Spearman rank correlation (ρs) of 0.504, NDCG of 0.777, and Top-recall of 0.239, outperforming all 20 baselines. On supervised fine-tuning tasks, ProSST (110M parameters) attains 94.32% accuracy on DeepLoc, 76.37% on Metal Ion Binding, 0.726 Spearman on Thermostability, and 0.682/0.492/0.501 F1-Max on GO-MF/BP/CC, achieving the best or near-best results across all six settings while using fewer parameters than most competitors (e.g., SaProt-650M at 650M, ESM-GearNet at 690M).

## Baselines & Comparisons

ProSST outperforms all 20 baselines on ProteinGYM across all three metrics (ρs, NDCG, Top-recall). The closest competitor is SaProt (the only other sequence-structure model), which achieves ρs = 0.457 vs. ProSST's 0.504 — a 0.047 gap in Spearman correlation — while ProSST also leads on Top-recall (0.239 vs. 0.233). Among sequence-only models, GEMME and Tranception-EVE tie at ρs = 0.457, still well below ProSST. On supervised fine-tuning, ProSST (110M) surpasses SaProt-650M (650M) on DeepLoc (94.32% vs. 93.55%) and Metal Ion Binding (76.37% vs. 75.75%), and matches or slightly trails ESM-GearNet (690M) on Thermostability (0.726 vs. 0.651) and GO-BP/CC, doing so with roughly 6× fewer parameters. The ablation showing ProSST without structure (K=0) drops to ρs = 0.392 confirms that the gains over sequence-only baselines like ESM-2 (0.414) and Tranception (0.434) come specifically from the structure quantization and disentangled attention design rather than backbone capacity.

## Relation to Prior Work

ProSST extends the structure-aware protein language modeling line initiated by SaProt and ProstT5, which both rely on Foldseek to discretize protein structures into tokens. It departs from this lineage in two key ways: (1) replacing Foldseek's 3-residue context with a GVP-based encoder that captures each residue's local 3D neighborhood (up to 40 nearest neighbors), and (2) substituting standard self-attention with a DeBERTa-inspired disentangled attention that explicitly decomposes interactions between residue, structure, and position tokens. The structure encoder itself is a denoising auto-encoder built on the GVP-GNN architecture, trained with RF-Diffusion-style perturbations, while the k-means codebook replaces Foldseek's hand-designed 3-mer vocabulary.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

ProSST demonstrates that a 110M-parameter model can outperform the 650M-parameter SaProt on both zero-shot mutation prediction and supervised downstream tasks, suggesting that explicit sequence-structure interaction modeling is more important than raw model scale for structure-aware PLMs. The practical limitation is that ProSST requires 3D structure inputs, which are less abundant than sequences; the authors address this with an MST variant and AlphaFold/ESMFold structure prediction, though performance degrades noticeably without structures. Open questions include whether the local-structure quantization approach scales to larger structure databases, how to handle intrinsically disordered regions (where AlphaFold pLDDT correlates with performance), and whether the disentangled attention formulation generalizes to other multi-modal biological sequence tasks.
