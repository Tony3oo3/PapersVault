## Problem

Existing safety mechanisms for text-to-image diffusion models—NSFW filters, model unlearning, and data cleaning—can be circumvented by fine-tuning on a small set of harmful images, restoring the model's ability to generate inappropriate content. This paper asks whether catastrophic forgetting, a phenomenon universally treated as a training pathology, can be deliberately induced so that harmful content generation becomes a 'forgotten' task, rendering subsequent malicious fine-tuning ineffective.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/NEURIPS20243657/cropped_page1_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method fine-tunes Stable Diffusion models (v1.4, v2.1, and SDXL) that consume text prompts and produce RGB images via VAE latent-space denoising. Training pairs clean prompts (sourced from LAION-5B, DiffusionDB, COCO) with harmful prompts (generated by Mistral-7B-Instruct-v0.2), using 200 gradient steps at learning rate 1e-5 and batch size 1. The resulting safety-aligned model is then stress-tested by applying 20–200 steps of malicious fine-tuning on harmful data and measuring NSFW scores, inappropriate rates, aesthetic scores, and CLIP scores against held-out clean and harmful prompt sets.

## Method

The core mechanism is a contrastive-learning training policy that maximizes the latent-space distance between clean and harmful data distributions while preserving clean image generation quality. Two instantiations alter the harmful-data prediction objective: Latent Transformation (LT) applies a random spatial transformation (rotation matrix R plus translation b) to the predicted latents of harmful samples, and Noise Guidance (NG) shifts the forward-process noise for harmful data from N(0, I) to N(μ_f, I), where μ_f is fixed at −1 or dynamically adjusted. The composite loss combines standard diffusion denoising on clean data (maintaining quality), negative log-likelihood on harmful data (suppressing harmful generation), and a contrastive term that pushes harmful latent predictions away from the clean-data center. By widening the distributional gap, malicious fine-tuning on harmful data becomes a novel task for the safety model, triggering catastrophic forgetting that prevents recovery of harmful image generation even after extended fine-tuning.

## Results

On SD v2.1 with sexual prompts, the NG method reduces NSFW Score from 0.6034 to 0.4517 and IP from 0.36 to 0.23 before malicious fine-tuning, and critically maintains NSFW at 0.4732 and IP at 0.24 after 20 steps of malicious fine-tuning (vs. 0.5887/0.42 for the original model). On SD v1.4, the LT method achieves even stronger resistance, dropping NSFW from 0.6269 to 0.4421 before and 0.4051 after malicious fine-tuning, with IP falling from 0.44 to 0.20. Clean image quality is preserved, with Aesthetic Score remaining within ~0.5 points of the original model (e.g., 6.7442 vs. 6.6954 for SD v2.1 LT). In the safety reinforcement setting (Table 2), the reinforced ESD-Nudity model's IP actually drops from 0.25 to 0.19 after malicious fine-tuning, demonstrating that the method not only resists but can further harden already-safe models.

## Baselines & Comparisons

Against the original SD v1.4/v2.1 baselines, both LT and NG substantially reduce harmful generation (NSFW drops by 0.15–0.19, IP drops by 0.11–0.21) while keeping Aesthetic and CLIP scores within ~0.1–0.5 of the original. Compared to ESD (Gandikota et al., ICCV 2023), the proposed method achieves a comparable IP of 0.21 vs. ESD-Nudity-u1's 0.16 on the I2P dataset (Table 5), but the critical distinction is robustness: ESD's NSFW/IP scores increase sharply after malicious fine-tuning, whereas the proposed method's scores remain flat or decrease. Against SLD (Schramowski et al., CVPR 2023), SLD-Max achieves a lower IP of 0.06 vs. the proposed 0.21, but SLD is vulnerable to UnlearnDiffAtk attacks (82.39% ASR vs. 38.03% for the proposed method in Table 11). Against Forget-Me-Not (Zhang et al., 2023), the proposed method is dramatically more robust: FMN's UnlearnDiffAtk ASR is 97.89% (nearly fully compromised) while the proposed method's is 38.03%. On SD XL (Podell et al., 2023), the same pattern holds: NG reduces NSFW from 0.5347 to 0.4952 and IP from 0.53 to 0.35 for sexual content, confirming generalizability to the newer architecture.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper builds on the latent diffusion model architecture (Stable Diffusion) and the concept-erasure line of work (ESD, SLD, Forget-Me-Not) that aims to remove harmful content from T2I models. Its key conceptual departure is repurposing catastrophic forgetting—a phenomenon in continual learning universally treated as a negative factor to be mitigated—as a positive safety mechanism. By applying contrastive learning to widen the latent-space distance between clean and harmful data distributions, the authors make malicious fine-tuning effectively a 'new task' that the safety model will struggle to learn, rather than a simple relearning of previously unlearned concepts as addressed by Metaunlearning [10]. The two instantiations (latent transformation and noise guidance) are lightweight modifications to the standard diffusion training loop, making the approach composable with existing unlearning methods like ESD.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

This work addresses a concrete and underexplored vulnerability: existing safety-aligned diffusion models (ESD, SLD, Forget-Me-Not) can be trivially retrained on harmful data to restore harmful generation, undermining their safety guarantees. The proposed method is practical, composable, and requires only modest training (200 steps, ~1 GPU-hour), making it deployable as a post-hoc safety reinforcement layer. However, the method does not fully eliminate harmful generation (some prompts still escape), the 'abrupt change' phenomenon around 80 malicious fine-tuning steps is observed but not mechanistically explained, and a potential adversarial strategy—deliberately pulling clean and harmful distributions closer before standard fine-tuning—is acknowledged but left unaddressed. Extending the approach beyond diffusion models and to more diverse harmful concept categories remains open.
