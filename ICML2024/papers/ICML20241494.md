## Problem

Sensitivity sampling for ℓ_p subspace embeddings via plain ℓ_p leverage scores requires Õ(ε⁻²𝔖d) samples, which is off by a factor of d from the known lower bound of Õ(ε⁻²d). While Woodruff & Yasuda (2023c) improved this to Õ(ε⁻²𝔖^{2/p}) for p∈[1,2], the bound deteriorates near p=1, and it remained open whether sensitivity sampling could achieve the optimal linear Õ(ε⁻²(𝔖+d)) = Õ(ε⁻²d) complexity. This paper resolves the question affirmatively by introducing ℓ_2 augmentation and simultaneously proves a matching lower bound showing pure ℓ_p leverage score sampling cannot achieve optimality.

## Input / Output

The method takes a data matrix A ∈ ℝ^{n×d} along with precomputed ℓ_p and ℓ_2 leverage scores (or close approximations) and produces a weighted subsample S ⊂ [n] of size m = Õ(ε⁻²dμ) with weights w_i = 1/p_i. A critical design choice is that weights are kept as separate auxiliary variables rather than being folded into the data matrix (i.e., w_i|a_i x|^p is not rewritten as |(w_i^{1/p} a_i)x|^p), which is shown to be necessary for simultaneously preserving both ℓ_2 and ℓ_p norms. The sampling probability for row i is p_i ≥ min{1, α(μ l_i^{(p)} + l_i^{(2)} + 1/n)}, where α is a polylogarithmic factor in d, μ, ε⁻¹, and δ⁻¹.

## Method

The core technique, called ℓ_2 augmentation, adds ℓ_2 leverage scores to the ℓ_p sensitivity sampling probabilities, enabling a simultaneous ℓ_2 and ℓ_p subspace embedding with only Õ(ε⁻²d) samples. The analysis bounds the approximation error via a Gaussian process (Dudley's theorem), where the diameter is controlled by the weighted ℓ_∞ norm and the entropy by covering numbers of weighted ℓ_p balls by weighted ℓ_∞ balls. The ℓ_2 leverage scores in the sampling distribution ensure the weighted largest ℓ_2 leverage score τ is bounded by O(1/α), which balances the diameter and entropy terms in the moment bound. A key technical contribution is generalizing the covering number analysis to weighted norms, including a weighted Dual-Sudakov-minoration bound and an interpolation argument for p < 2. As an application, the p-ReLU extension of the main theorem yields the first fully linear Õ(ε⁻²μd) sensitivity sampling bound for logistic regression, improving over the previous Õ(ε⁻²μ²d) Lewis weights bound.

## Results

The paper establishes that ℓ_p sensitivity sampling with ℓ_2 augmentation achieves a sample complexity of Õ(ε^{-2}(S^{(p)} + d)) = Õ(ε^{-2}d) for all p ∈ [1,2], matching the known Ω̃(d/ε²) lower bound. A matching lower bound of Ω̃(d^{2-p/2}) is proven against pure ℓ_p leverage score sampling, confirming that the Chen & Derezinski (2021) bound is tight. As an application, the paper obtains the first fully linear Õ(ε^{-2}μd) sensitivity sampling bound for logistic regression, improving over the previous Õ(ε^{-2}μ²d) Lewis weights bound of Mai et al. (2021).

## Baselines & Comparisons

Compared to the standard sensitivity framework (Feldman et al., 2020), which requires Õ(ε^{-2}d²) samples in the worst case, this paper's ℓ_2 augmentation reduces the dimension dependence to linear d for all p ∈ [1,2]. Against Woodruff & Yasuda (2023c), which achieves Õ(ε^{-2}d^{2/p}), the improvement is most dramatic near p = 1 (from d² to d) and vanishes at p = 2 where both achieve linear d. Against Chen & Derezinski (2021), which gives Õ(ε^{-2}d^{2-p/2}), the paper proves their bound is tight via a matching Ω̃(d^{2-p/2}) lower bound against pure ℓ_p sampling, then improves it to linear d by augmenting with ℓ_2 leverage scores. For logistic regression, the paper improves over Mai et al. (2021) from Õ(ε^{-2}μ²d) to Õ(ε^{-2}μd), a factor of μ improvement, by directly approximating the ReLU component rather than bounding it additively.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the sensitivity sampling framework (Feldman et al., 2020) for ℓ_p subspace embeddings by introducing 'ℓ_2 augmentation'—mixing ℓ_p and ℓ_2 leverage scores into the sampling probabilities. It builds directly on the Gaussian process and Dudley-theorem analysis of Woodruff & Yasuda (2023c), generalizing their unweighted analysis to handle auxiliary weights, and proves that the implicit bound of Chen & Derezinski (2021) (obtained via Lewis weight relations) is tight for pure ℓ_p sensitivity sampling. The key departure from prior work is the observation that the standard trick of folding weights into the data prevents simultaneous ℓ_2 and ℓ_p embedding, necessitating a weighted-norm analysis that the authors develop from scratch.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The result resolves an open question of Woodruff & Yasuda (2023c) by showing that sensitivity sampling achieves the same Õ(ε^{-2}d) complexity as Lewis weights for all p∈[1,2], making the computationally simpler leverage-score approach theoretically competitive. The application to logistic regression improves the sample complexity from Õ(ε^{-2}μ²d) to Õ(ε^{-2}μd), removing a quadratic dependence on the data-dependent complexity parameter μ. The case p>2 remains open, where the paper's techniques do not improve over Woodruff & Yasuda (2023c), and extending the ℓ_2 augmentation idea to that regime or to more general loss functions (e.g., p-generalized probit regression) is identified as future work.
