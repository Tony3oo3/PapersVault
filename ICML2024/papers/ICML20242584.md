## Problem

Vision Transformers achieve strong visual representation learning through self-attention, but their quadratic complexity in sequence length makes them impractical for high-resolution images. While Mamba demonstrated that selective state space models can replace attention for long-sequence language modeling, no pure-SSM-based backbone had been explored for vision, where position-sensitivity and the need for global context pose unique challenges.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ICML20242584/cropped_page0_idx1.webp)

## Input / Output

Vim takes an RGB image, splits it into non-overlapping 16×16 patches, linearly projects them to a hidden dimension D, and prepends a learnable class token with position embeddings to form a 1D token sequence. For classification, the normalized class token is fed to an MLP head to produce a class label; for dense prediction (segmentation, detection, instance segmentation), per-patch features are passed to UperNet or Cascade Mask R-CNN to yield pixel-level masks or bounding boxes with instance masks. Notably, Vim processes the entire image as a flat sequence without any 2D windowing or local inductive bias, enabling direct inference at resolutions up to 1248×1248 (6084 tokens) where DeiT becomes infeasible.

## Method

Vim replaces self-attention with bidirectional selective state space models: each Vim block normalizes the input, projects it into x and z streams, applies a 1D convolution followed by a data-dependent SSM (with input-dependent Δ, B, C parameters) in both forward and backward directions, gates the two SSM outputs with SiLU(z), and adds a residual connection. Position embeddings are added at the input to provide spatial awareness, and a middle class token is used for classification to exploit the recurrent nature of SSMs. The architecture stacks L=24 Vim blocks with hidden dimension D=192 (Tiny) or D=384 (Small) and SSM dimension N=16, achieving linear O(M) complexity in sequence length versus O(M²) for attention. Hardware-aware optimizations (IO-efficient SRAM scheduling and activation recomputation) further reduce memory from O(BMEN) to O(BME+EN), yielding 2.8× speedup and 86.8% GPU memory savings over DeiT at 1248×1248 resolution.

## Results

On ImageNet-1K classification, Vim-Ti (7M params) achieves 76.1% top-1 accuracy, Vim-S (26M) reaches 80.3%, and Vim-B (98M) attains 81.9%, surpassing DeiT at all scales. On ADE20K semantic segmentation, Vim-S + UperNet achieves 44.9 mIoU, matching ResNet-101 + UperNet (44.9 mIoU) with nearly 2× fewer parameters. On COCO object detection, Vim-Ti achieves 45.7 AP^box and 39.2 AP^mask with Cascade Mask R-CNN, outperforming DeiT-Ti by 1.3 and 1.1 points respectively. In terms of efficiency, Vim is 2.8× faster than DeiT and saves 86.8% GPU memory when processing 1248×1248 images (6084 tokens per image).

## Baselines & Comparisons

Against DeiT (the primary Transformer baseline), Vim wins at every scale: Vim-Ti beats DeiT-Ti by 3.9 points (76.1 vs 72.2), Vim-S beats DeiT-S by 0.5 points (80.3 vs 79.8), and Vim-B edges DeiT-B by 0.1 points (81.9 vs 81.8), all with comparable parameter counts. Against ResNet-50 (25M params, 76.2%), Vim-S (26M params) achieves 80.3%, a 4.1-point gain; against ResNeXt50-32×4d (77.6%) and RegNetY-4GF (80.0%), Vim-S also leads by 2.7 and 0.3 points respectively. Compared to the original ViT-B/16 (86M, 77.9%), Vim-B (98M) improves by 4.0 points. Against the SSM-based S4ND-ViT-B (89M, 80.4%), Vim-S (26M) achieves nearly identical accuracy (80.3%) with roughly 3× fewer parameters. In the hierarchical architecture comparison (Appendix C), Hier-Vim outperforms Swin Transformer by 1.3% (tiny), 0.2% (small), and 0.4% (base) on ImageNet. On downstream tasks, Vim-Ti consistently beats DeiT-Ti: +1.8 mIoU on ADE20K segmentation, +1.3 AP^box and +1.1 AP^mask on COCO detection/segmentation.

## Relation to Prior Work

Vim directly adapts Mamba's selective state space model with hardware-aware parallel scan to the vision domain, while borrowing ViT's patch-embedding and class-token formulation to treat images as 1-D token sequences. It departs from prior SSM-for-vision efforts (S4ND, TranS4mer, U-Mamba, VMamba) by being a pure-SSM backbone that introduces no convolutional or attentional hybrid components, and it departs from ViT/DeiT by replacing quadratic self-attention with bidirectional selective SSM plus learned position embeddings. The bidirectional design (forward + backward SSM with a 1-D convolution) is the key architectural addition over vanilla Mamba, motivated by the spatial symmetry of visual data that unidirectional sequence processing cannot naturally capture.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

Vim demonstrates that a pure sequence-modeling backbone without any 2D inductive bias can match or exceed DeiT across classification, detection, and segmentation while scaling linearly in sequence length, opening the door to end-to-end processing of gigapixel images (pathology, remote sensing, aerial) that are infeasible for quadratic-attention Transformers. The 2.8× speedup and 86.8% GPU-memory reduction at 1248×1248 resolution are practically significant for high-resolution downstream applications. Open questions include whether Vim benefits from large-scale self-supervised pretraining (e.g., MAE-style), how it performs in multimodal (CLIP-style) settings, and whether the efficiency advantage persists at much larger model scales where SSM's fixed N dimension may become a bottleneck.
