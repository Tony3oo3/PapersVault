## Problem

Risk-sensitive reinforcement learning with the entropic risk measure has been studied primarily in the online setting, where the agent can interact with the environment. No provably efficient offline RL algorithm exists for this risk measure, even in the tabular MDP case, and the linear MDP setting with function approximation has not been examined from a risk-sensitive standpoint. The paper asks whether one can design a provably efficient risk-sensitive RL algorithm that learns a near-optimal policy from a pre-collected dataset without further environment interaction.

## Input / Output

The algorithms consume a pre-collected offline dataset of K trajectories {(s_h^τ, a_h^τ, r_h(s_h^τ, a_h^τ))} generated by a behavior policy in a linear MDP with feature map φ: S×A → R^d (‖φ‖≤1), rewards in [0,1], and episode length H. The variance-aware variant (VA-RSPVI) additionally requires an independent auxiliary dataset of the same size to estimate conditional variances without correlating them with regression targets. The output is a risk-sensitive policy π̂ maximizing the entropic risk measure V_β = (1/β)log E[e^{βR}], accompanied by a suboptimality bound that scales with the risk-sensitivity factor (e^{|β|H}−1)/|β| and the feature dimension d (or √d under a coverage assumption).

## Method

The core technical contribution is a 'shifting and scaling' operator S_h that rescales regression targets at each step to match the error propagation structure of the exponential Bellman equation, avoiding the e^{|β|H²} amplification present in prior risk-sensitive analyses. Algorithm 1 (RSPVI) performs backward value iteration with two ridge regressions per step—one on rewards and one on the shifted/scaled next-step value—then constructs a pessimistic Q-estimate by subtracting a bonus γ‖φ(s,a)‖_{Λ_h^{-1}} inside the exponential Bellman update, yielding a suboptimality bound of Õ(d)·(e^{|β|H}−1)/|β| without any coverage assumption. Under a data-coverage assumption (Assumption 2.2), reference-advantage decomposition improves the d-dependence to Õ(√d). Algorithm 2 (VA-RSPVI) further sharpens both the d-dependence and the risk-sensitivity factor by replacing unweighted ridge regression with variance-weighted regression (generalized least squares), where the variance estimator is computed from an independent auxiliary dataset to maintain independence from the Bellman residuals.

## Results

The paper provides three main theoretical guarantees for suboptimality. Theorem 5.1 (no coverage assumption) yields SubOpt(π̂) ≤ Õ(d) · (e^{|β|H}−1)/|β| · Σ_h E_{π*}[‖φ(s_h,a_h)‖_{Λ_h^{-1}} | s_1]. Under the data coverage assumption (Assumption 2.2), Theorem 5.2 improves the dimension dependence to Õ(√d) with the same risk-sensitivity factor. The variance-aware algorithm (Theorem 5.3) further sharpens the bound to Õ(√d) · 1/|β| · Σ_h E_{π*}[‖φ(s_h,a_h)‖_{(Σ_h*)^{-1}} | s_1], removing the explicit (e^{|β|H}−1) factor from the leading term. When β→0⁺, the bounds recover the known Õ(dH) rate for risk-neutral offline RL matching Jin et al. (2021).

## Baselines & Comparisons

This is a purely theoretical paper with no empirical baselines; comparisons are made at the level of suboptimality bounds. Compared to Fei et al. (2021b), the only prior work on risk-sensitive RL with linear function approximation (online setting), the shifting-and-scaling technique removes a potential e^{|β|H²} factor from the bound. Under the coverage assumption, the dependence on feature dimension d improves from Õ(d) (as in Fei et al. 2021b) to Õ(√d), matching the tightest known rate for risk-neutral offline RL by Xiong et al. (2022) and Yin et al. (2022). The variance-aware algorithm (Theorem 5.3) additionally eliminates the (e^{|β|H}−1) multiplicative factor present in Theorems 5.1–5.2, yielding a bound that depends on 1/|β| rather than (e^{|β|H}−1)/|β|. In the extreme case where the variance floor σ̲² = (e^{|β|H}−1)², Algorithm 2 reduces to Algorithm 1 and Theorem 5.3 recovers the Theorem 5.2 bound, confirming the variance-aware method is never worse.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper sits at the intersection of risk-sensitive RL (primarily studied in online settings via the exponential Bellman equation by Fei et al. 2021a,b) and offline RL with linear function approximation (Jin et al. 2021; Xiong et al. 2022; Yin et al. 2022), but no prior work had combined these threads. The pessimistic value iteration structure is directly inherited from Jin et al. (2021), while the variance-aware weighted regression and reference-advantage decomposition are non-trivial generalizations of techniques from Xiong et al. (2022) and Yin et al. (2022) to the fundamentally different exponential Bellman equation. The key analytical departure is the 'shifting and scaling' operator, which unifies the shifted exponential V-functions from Fei et al. (2021b) and the decaying bonus from Fei et al. (2021a) into a single framework that eliminates the e^{|β|H²} factor present in prior online risk-sensitive bounds.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

This is the first provably efficient risk-sensitive offline RL algorithm, opening a previously unexplored problem space with practical relevance to finance and safety-critical control where online interaction is infeasible. The risk-sensitivity factor (e^{|β|H}−1)/|β| appears in all bounds, but whether it is tight remains an open question since no lower bound exists for the offline setting. The covering number analysis for risk-sensitive value function estimates in linear MDPs is a new technical tool of independent interest. Future work could extend the analysis to other risk measures (CVaR, general utility functions), low-rank or kernel MDPs, and establish matching lower bounds.
