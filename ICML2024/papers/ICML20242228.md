## Problem

AI weather forecasting models like FengWu produce accurate and fast forecasts but depend on initial states generated by traditional physics-based data assimilation (4DVar), which is computationally expensive (~50 min on 256 processors) and inconsistent with the AI model's dynamics. This paper addresses the gap by coupling an AI forecasting model directly with 4DVar to create a fully self-contained data-driven forecasting framework that eliminates the need for a separate physics-based assimilation system.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ICML20242228/cropped_page1_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The framework consumes a background field (prior state estimate from the previous 6-hour FengWu forecast), sparse observations at 5–15% of the 128×256 grid points with Gaussian noise (σ = 0.001–0.01 × variable std), and the pre-trained FengWu model (a 1-hour model for flow dependency and a 6-hour model for forecasting), spanning 69 predictands (5 atmospheric variables at 13 pressure levels plus 4 surface variables). It produces an analysis field (the optimized initial state) and a 6-hour forecast field that becomes the background for the next assimilation cycle, operating cyclically over a full year at 6-hour intervals.

## Method

The core contribution is an AI-embedded 4DVar algorithm with three key components. First, the 4DVar objective function replaces the physics-based model with the differentiable FengWu model for flow dependency and augments the observation covariance with a model error term (HQ_τH^T) to down-weight future observations, compensating for the faster error growth of AI models. Second, the background error covariance B is decomposed as B=UU^T where U encodes horizontal correlations via spherical convolution, implemented through the spherical harmonic transform (SHT) using NVIDIA's torch-harmonics package, avoiding the information loss of diagonal approximations. Third, auto-differentiation replaces the manually coded adjoint model: the control variable u_0 is treated as a learnable parameter of a computational graph, and L-BFGS optimization (rather than batch-based SGD/Adam) solves the single-sample minimization. The entire assimilation step runs in ~29 seconds on one A100 GPU, roughly 100× faster than traditional 4DVar.

## Results

FengWu-4DVar achieves stable self-contained global weather forecasts for a full year (first demonstrated in the literature), with analysis field RMSE on z500 of approximately 20 m²/s² at 15% observation proportion, comparable to or better than the IFS 6-hour forecast. The framework remains robust at 5% observation proportion (RMSE ~40 m²/s², equivalent to IFS 18-hour forecast) and under 10× increased observation noise (RMSE rises to >70 m²/s² but does not diverge). Computationally, assimilating ~300,000 observations in a 6-hour window takes 29.3 seconds on a single NVIDIA A100 GPU, approximately 100× faster than traditional 4DVar on 256 PI-SUGON processors.

## Baselines & Comparisons

Against the traditional 4DVar system at CMA (Zhang et al., 2019), FengWu-4DVar achieves a ~100× speedup: 29.3 seconds on a single A100 GPU versus ~50 minutes on 256 PI-SUGON processors, while producing analysis fields with accuracy comparable to the IFS 6-hour forecast. Against the 3DVar baseline (which assimilates only same-time observations without flow dependency), AI-embedded 4DVar consistently outperforms in assimilation accuracy, and the advantage grows as observation noise increases, because 4DVar can leverage future observations to compensate for quality defects. The analysis field RMSE of FengWu-4DVar at 15% observation proportion is smaller than the average RMSE of IFS 6-hour forecast fields on most of the 69 predictands, and the bias is also reduced relative to the background field across all variables. The paper does not perform a direct end-to-end comparison against an operational 4DVar system using real observations, which the authors acknowledge as a limitation.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the classical 4DVar data assimilation framework (Rabier et al., 1998; 2000) by replacing the physics-based forecasting model in the flow dependency with a differentiable AI model (FengWu), following the conceptual suggestion of Geer (2021) and the preliminary implementation on low-dimensional systems by Dong et al. (2022). The key departures from prior AI data assimilation work are threefold: (1) introducing a model error covariance term to account for the faster error growth of AI forecasters, (2) using spherical harmonic transforms to encode horizontal background error correlations (rather than the diagonal approximation common in prior AI assimilation), and (3) leveraging auto-differentiation to eliminate the need for manually coded adjoint models. The framework is positioned as the first to achieve a fully self-contained, AI-only global weather forecasting cycle without any physics-based model in the assimilation loop.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

This work demonstrates that a data-driven weather forecasting system can operate in a fully self-contained manner for an entire year, removing the dependency on expensive physics-based models for data assimilation — a significant step toward practical AI weather operations. The ~100× speedup over traditional 4DVar (29 s on one A100 GPU vs. 50 min on 256 CPU cores) makes frequent assimilation cycles computationally feasible. However, the evaluation relies entirely on simulated observations derived from ERA5, and no end-to-end comparison against an operational 4DVar system is provided, leaving open whether the framework performs comparably under real-world observation sparsity, heterogeneity, and quality issues. Future work will need to validate the approach on actual observational networks and explore whether the SHT-based correlation and model error formulations generalize across different AI architectures and resolutions.
