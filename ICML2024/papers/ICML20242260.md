## Problem

Text-to-image diffusion models produce deformed human poses and fail to render interaction boundary regions correctly when prompted with human-object interaction (HOI) descriptions, with a 60.9% semantic misalignment rate observed in human studies. This paper is the first to address HOI image generation from pure text prompts, targeting both the structural complexity of human poses and the semantic fidelity of interaction boundaries simultaneously.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ICML20242260/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method takes a templated text prompt (e.g., 'A photo of a person hugging a cow') describing an HOI category from 150 predefined categories spanning human-animal, human-object, and human-human interactions, and produces a 512×512 RGB image. It operates entirely training-free on top of pre-trained Stable Diffusion v1.5 using a DDIM scheduler with 50 denoising steps, requiring no additional model weights or fine-tuning. The iterative refinement pipeline can run multiple rounds, each re-inverting the previous output via null-text inversion before re-applying guided denoising.

## Method

SA-HOI introduces two complementary mechanisms. First, Pose and Interaction Boundary Guidance (PIBG) identifies problematic regions by combining pose detector confidence scores (low scores flag deformed body parts) with proximity measurements between human joints and object contours (close distances flag interaction boundaries). Gaussian blurring is applied selectively to these masked regions, and the high-frequency difference between the original and blurred latents serves as a guidance signal within the generalized diffusion guidance framework, steering the denoising process to refine only the flagged areas while preserving the rest of the image. Second, an Iterative Inversion and Refinement (IIR) pipeline uses null-text inversion to recover the noise and text embedding from each generated image, re-runs the guided denoising, and evaluates quality via Pose Confidence Score, iterating until improvement falls below a threshold. The final guided noise combines pose-guided, interaction-boundary-guided, and classifier-free guidance terms with separate scale factors.

## Results

On the HOI Generation Benchmark (150 categories across H-A, H-O, H-H scenarios), SA-HOI achieves the best FID of 54.55 (H-A), 74.70 (H-O), and 134.72 (H-H), outperforming Stable Diffusion by 4.29, 3.58, and 2.94 points respectively. On HOI-specific metrics, SA-HOI attains the highest HOIF (34.38% H-A, 32.77% H-O, 29.32% H-H) and R-Acc@1 (42.48% H-A, 94.69% H-O, 52.10% H-H) across all scenarios. In subjective evaluation, SA-HOI scores 3.54/5 overall quality versus 2.98 (SD), 3.04 (SAG), and 2.15 (Diffusion_HPC), approaching ground-truth level (4.25).

## Baselines & Comparisons

SA-HOI consistently outperforms Stable Diffusion on all metrics across all three scenarios: FID improves by 4.29 (H-A), 3.58 (H-O), and 2.94 (H-H), while HOIF gains 4.32, 7.56, and 2.24 percentage points respectively. Against SAG (self-attention guidance), SA-HOI achieves lower FID (54.55 vs 56.91 on H-A) and substantially higher HOIF (34.38% vs 32.14%) and R-Acc@1 (42.48% vs 38.92%), confirming that pose and interaction boundary guidance provides more targeted refinement than generic self-attention maps. Compared to Diffusion_HPC (which uses SMPL for pose prior), SA-HOI achieves lower FID by 4.87%/3.22%/4.71% and higher HOIF by 2.83/5.27/3.31 points, though Diffusion_HPC edges out on body PCS in H-A (74.58% vs 73.88%) and H-H (76.99% vs 75.31%), suggesting SMPL provides slightly stronger pose plausibility at the cost of overall image quality and semantic fidelity. In subjective evaluation, SA-HOI leads all baselines on every criterion (Human, Object, Interaction, Overall), with the largest margin on Interaction Semantics (4.03 vs 3.67 for SD), directly validating the interaction boundary guidance component.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the generalized diffusion guidance framework introduced by SAG (Hong et al., 2023), which uses self-attention maps to steer the denoising process, by replacing those maps with explicitly constructed pose-quality and interaction-boundary attention maps derived from external detectors (RTMPose, Mask R-CNN). It builds on Stable Diffusion v1.5 as the base generator and incorporates null-text inversion (Mokady et al., 2022) to enable iterative refinement without retraining. The key conceptual departure from prior pose-guided human image generation (ControlNet, Diffusion_HPC) is that SA-HOI targets not just pose plausibility but also the semantic fidelity of the interaction boundary region, and unlike InteractGAN or ReVersion, it operates purely from text prompts without requiring reference human/object images or per-relation training.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The introduction of the first dedicated HOI generation benchmark (150 fine-grained categories across human-object, human-animal, and human-human interactions) with tailored metrics (PDD, HODD, PCS, HOIF, R-Acc) gives the community a standardized way to evaluate compositional interaction generation, filling a gap left by general compositional benchmarks like T2I-CompBench. The training-free nature of the method makes it immediately deployable on top of existing Stable Diffusion checkpoints. Open questions include whether the interaction-boundary guidance generalizes to more complex multi-object or multi-person interactions, whether the iterative refinement pipeline scales efficiently to higher resolutions, and whether the benchmark's reliance on HICO-DET categories captures the full diversity of real-world HOI semantics.
