## Problem

Consistency Models (CMs) enable few-step high-quality image generation, but no effective method existed for adding new conditional controls (e.g., sketch, depth, pose) to pre-trained CMs. Directly transferring a diffusion-model ControlNet to a CM preserves high-level semantic control but degrades low-level detail and realism due to the intrinsic gap between the two model families.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ICML20242235/cropped_page1_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method accepts a text prompt together with one of seven spatial control signals (sketch, Canny edges, HED edges, depth map, human pose skeleton, masked RGB+binary-mask 4-channel image, or a 64×64 bicubic-downsampled image for 16× super-resolution) and produces a single 1024×1024 image in 1–4 NFEs (~0.2–0.9 s on one A100). Control signals are extracted by dedicated models (Midas, Canny, HED, OpenPose, etc.) and fed through a condition extractor h(·) into the ControlNet branch, while the text prompt is processed by the frozen CM's text encoder.

## Method

The paper proposes three complementary strategies for controllable generation with CMs. (1) Direct transfer: a ControlNet trained on a diffusion model is plugged into the frozen CM, which works for semantic-level control but produces unrealistic details. (2) Consistency training for ControlNet (the core contribution): the frozen CM and a trainable ControlNet are treated as a single conditional consistency model f_{θ,ψ}(x_t, t; c_txt, c_ctrl), and only ψ is optimized with the consistency training loss L_CT^N(ψ) that enforces self-consistency along PF-ODE trajectories using a stopgrad teacher (no EMA), L1 distance, and N=50 — eliminating the need for a diffusion model as an intermediary. (3) Unified adapter: a shared Δψ is trained with the same consistency loss across multiple DM-based ControlNets {ψ_1,…,ψ_K} to bridge the CM–DM gap, improving both in-context and zero-shot condition transfer. Ablations confirm L1 distance and N=50 as optimal, and cosine-similarity / Fourier analysis shows the DM–CM ControlNet discrepancy is concentrated in deep (low-level) layers.

## Results

The proposed CM's ControlNet+CM achieves an average FID of 7.61 and Fidelity of 0.130 at 4 NFEs (0.9 s per 1024×1024 image), closely matching the diffusion-model baseline (DM's ControlNet+DM: FID 7.31, Fidelity 0.122) that requires 100 NFEs and 23.6 s. At 1 NFE, CM's ControlNet+CM still reaches FID 9.09 / Fidelity 0.116, far outperforming the direct transfer of DM's ControlNet to CM (FID 23.19 / Fidelity 0.231). The unified adapter further narrows the gap for the direct-transfer setting, reducing average FID from 16.16 to 10.27 at 4 NFEs.

## Baselines & Comparisons

The sole external baseline is the original diffusion-model ControlNet (Zhang et al., ICCV 2023) paired with a Stable Diffusion U-Net (DM's ControlNet+DM), which achieves an average FID of 7.31 and Fidelity of 0.122 at 100 NFEs (23.6 s). The proposed CM's ControlNet+CM at 4 NFEs (0.9 s) reaches FID 7.61 / Fidelity 0.130, a near-parity result at roughly 25× fewer function evaluations and 26× less wall-clock time. At 1 NFE the gap widens slightly (FID 9.09 vs 7.31) but the speedup is ~100×. Directly transferring the DM's ControlNet to the CM without retraining degrades FID to 23.19 at 1 NFE, confirming that the intrinsic CM–DM gap is substantial; the consistency-trained ControlNet closes this gap by more than 14 FID points. The unified adapter, trained on five conditions, further reduces the direct-transfer FID from 16.16 to 10.27 at 4 NFEs and generalizes to the untrained depth condition, though it still trails the from-scratch CM's ControlNet (10.27 vs 7.61).

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper sits at the intersection of two previously separate research threads: consistency models (Song et al., 2023; Luo et al., 2023a), which enable few-step image generation, and ControlNet (Zhang et al., 2023), which provides conditional control for diffusion models. The key conceptual move is recognizing that the consistency training objective — originally designed to train CMs from scratch without a diffusion teacher — can be repurposed to train a ControlNet directly for a pre-trained LCM, bypassing the need for a diffusion model as an intermediary. The paper also introduces a unified adapter as a lighter-weight alternative that bridges the gap between DM-trained ControlNets and CMs, positioning itself between full retraining and zero-shot transfer.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

Real-time controllable generation (1–4 NFEs vs. 50×2 for diffusion) is a practically significant capability for interactive applications, and this paper provides a clean, reproducible recipe to achieve it. The depth-wise correlation analysis (cosine similarity dropping from ~0.55 at shallow layers to ~0.16 at deep layers) offers a useful diagnostic for understanding *why* direct DM→CM transfer degrades on fine details, which could inform future adapter or distillation designs. Open questions include whether the approach generalizes beyond SD-based LCMs to other fast generative families (e.g., rectified flow, adversarial distillation), whether the adapter can be made condition-agnostic without per-condition training, and how the method scales to video or 3D generation.
