## Problem

Rectified flow models connect data and noise via straight-line trajectories, offering better theoretical properties and fewer sampling steps than curved diffusion paths, yet they have not been decisively established for high-resolution text-to-image synthesis. Additionally, the standard practice of feeding a fixed text representation into the image model via cross-attention limits text comprehension and typography, and no systematic scaling study has been conducted for text-to-image diffusion transformers.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ICML20240503/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The model takes a natural language text prompt as input, which is encoded by three frozen text encoders (CLIP L/14, OpenCLIP bigG/14, and T5-v1.1-XXL) into a pooled vector conditioning (2048-d) and a sequence conditioning (154×4096). It produces high-resolution RGB images up to 1024×1024 pixels by sampling in the 16-channel latent space of a pretrained autoencoder (8× spatial downsampling) and decoding back to pixel space. The same architecture extends to video generation (16 frames at 256×256) by adding temporal patching and spatio-temporal attention.

## Method

The paper introduces two key technical contributions. First, it proposes a logit-normal timestep sampling distribution (rf/lognorm(0.00, 1.00)) for training rectified flow models, which biases training towards intermediate noise levels where the velocity prediction target is most difficult, outperforming uniform sampling and established diffusion formulations (EDM, LDM-Linear, cosine) in a 61-variant systematic study. Second, it presents the MM-DiT architecture, which concatenates text and image token sequences for joint self-attention while maintaining separate weight sets for each modality, enabling bidirectional information flow that improves text comprehension and typography over cross-attention baselines (DiT, CrossDiT, UViT). The model is trained with the conditional flow matching objective in latent space, uses a 50/50 mix of original and CogVLM synthetic captions, and is scaled up to 8B parameters (depth=38) with QK-normalization for stable high-resolution training, demonstrating predictable scaling trends where validation loss correlates strongly with GenEval, T2I-CompBench, and human preference scores.

## Results

On the GenEval benchmark, the largest model (depth=38, 1024², with DPO) achieves an overall score of 0.74, outperforming DALL-E 3 (0.67), IF-XL (0.61), SDXL (0.55), and PixArt-α (0.48). In the systematic formulation study (Table 1), the proposed rf/lognorm(0.00, 1.00) rectified flow variant achieves the best average rank (1.54) across 24 evaluation settings, surpassing LDM-Linear (2.88), EDM (5.63–15.58), and vanilla RF (5.67). In human preference evaluation on Parti-prompts, the 8B model outperforms DALL-E 3, Ideogram, and Playground v2.5 across all three categories: visual aesthetics, prompt following, and typography generation. Additionally, larger models require fewer sampling steps to reach peak performance (Table 6: depth=38 shows only 2.71% CLIP score decrease at 5/50 steps vs. 4.30% for depth=15).

## Baselines & Comparisons

Against the LDM-Linear formulation (eps/linear, Rombach et al. 2022), the proposed rf/lognorm(0.00, 1.00) rectified flow variant achieves a significantly better average rank (1.54 vs. 2.88) in the 61-variant systematic study, and is the only RF formulation that consistently outperforms LDM-Linear across both 5-step and 50-step sampling settings. Compared to EDM (Karras et al. 2022), all EDM variants rank substantially worse (ranks 5.63–15.58), with the best EDM configuration still trailing the proposed method by over 4 rank positions. In the architecture comparison (Figure 4), MM-DiT significantly outperforms vanilla DiT (Peebles & Xie 2023), CrossDiT, and UViT (Hoogeboom et al. 2023) on validation loss, CLIP score, and FID on CC12M. On the GenEval benchmark, the 8B model (0.74 overall) surpasses DALL-E 3 (0.67) by 0.07, IF-XL (0.61) by 0.13, SDXL and SDXL Turbo (both 0.55) by 0.19, and PixArt-α (0.48) by 0.26, with particularly large gains in Position (0.33 vs. 0.43 for DALL-E 3) and Color Attribution (0.60 vs. 0.45). In human preference evaluation on Parti-prompts, the 8B model wins against DALL-E 3, Ideogram v1.0, and Playground v2.5 in all three assessed categories (visual aesthetics, prompt following, typography), confirming the quantitative GenEval advantage in perceptual quality.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper builds on the Conditional Flow Matching framework (Lipman et al., 2023) and the Rectified Flow formulation (Liu et al., 2022), but departs from prior RF work by introducing tailored timestep samplers (logit-normal, mode, CosMap) that bias training toward perceptually relevant intermediate noise levels. Architecturally, it extends DiT (Peebles & Xie, 2023) into MM-DiT by replacing cross-attention with modality-specific weight sets that enable bidirectional information flow between text and image token streams, while retaining the latent-space training paradigm of LDM (Rombach et al., 2022). The unified weighted-loss formulation from Kingma & Gao (2023) and EDM (Karras et al., 2022) provides the common language for the systematic 61-variant comparison, and the synthetic caption strategy follows DALL-E 3 (Betker et al., 2023).

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

This is the first large-scale demonstration that rectified flow with logit-normal timestep sampling decisively outperforms established diffusion formulations (EDM, LDM-Linear) for high-resolution text-to-image synthesis, resolving a long-standing question about whether RF's theoretical advantages translate to practice. The predictable scaling trends and strong validation-loss-to-quality correlation provide a practical roadmap for future model development, and the 8B model's GenEval score (0.74) surpassing DALL-E 3 (0.67) marks a milestone for open models. Open questions include whether the scaling trend truly avoids saturation at even larger scales, how well the approach transfers to video (only preliminary results are shown), and whether the modality-specific architecture generalizes beyond text-to-image to other multi-modal generation tasks.
