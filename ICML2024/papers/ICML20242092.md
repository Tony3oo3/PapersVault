## Problem

No existing RL algorithm achieves consistently superior sample efficiency across diverse domains simultaneously. DreamerV3, while universal, still lags behind domain-specific SOTAs in Atari, proprioceptive control, and vision control; EfficientZero excels in discrete control but cannot handle high-dimensional continuous action spaces; and TD-MPC2's MPPI-based planning incurs prohibitive computational costs for vision-based tasks. The open question is how to achieve both high performance and sample efficiency across discrete and continuous control, visual and low-dimensional inputs, under limited data budgets.

## Input / Output

The method accepts either raw RGB frames (84×84 for Atari, 84×84 for DMControl vision tasks) or low-dimensional proprioceptive state vectors (1-D to 12-D depending on the task) as input, and produces either a discrete action (Atari) or a continuous action vector (DMControl, up to 12-D for Quadruped Walk). Internally, all planning operates in a learned 128-dimensional latent space: the representation function compresses observations into latent states, and the policy outputs a squashed Gaussian distribution over actions. The search uses only 32 simulated latent states per decision step (vs. 9216 for TD-MPC2), making it suitable for edge deployment.

## Method

EZ-V2 builds on EfficientZero's latent world model (representation, dynamics, policy, and value networks trained jointly with temporal consistency loss) but replaces MCTS with a sampling-based Gumbel search that extends Gumbel search to continuous action spaces. At the root node, K actions are sampled from a mixture of the current Gaussian policy and a flattened prior distribution, then selected via Sequential Halving bandit, guaranteeing policy improvement (q(s,a*_S) ≥ E_{a~p_t}[q(s,a)]). For value estimation, the paper introduces Search-Based Value Estimation (SVE), which averages N bootstrapped return estimates from the search tree's imagined trajectories to produce accurate targets for off-policy transitions, with a provable MSE upper bound that vanishes as model errors decay. A mixed value target switches between multi-step TD targets (early training or fresh data) and SVE (stale data) to handle the transition from model inaccuracy to model accuracy during learning.

## Results

On Atari 100k, EZ-V2 achieves a human-normalized mean score of 2.428 and median of 1.286, surpassing the previous SOTA BBF (2.247/0.917) and EfficientZero (1.943/1.090). On the DeepMind Control Suite, EZ-V2 attains a mean score of 723.2 on Proprio Control (50k/100k) and 726.1 on Vision Control (100k/200k), outperforming DreamerV3 by 40% and 45% respectively. EZ-V2 also requires 1000× fewer FLOPs per decision step than TD-MPC2 (4.7×10⁷ vs 3.6×10¹⁰) while using ~4× fewer parameters (1.3M vs 4.9M).

## Baselines & Comparisons

On Atari 100k, EZ-V2 (normed mean 2.428) decisively outperforms all model-free and model-based baselines: SimPLe (0.443), CURL (0.381), DrQ (0.357), SPR (0.704), and MuZero (0.562), and also surpasses the prior SOTA BBF (2.247) and its direct predecessor EfficientZero (1.943). On the DeepMind Control Suite, EZ-V2 dominates DreamerV3 across all three benchmarks (Proprio: 723.2 vs 517.1; Vision: 726.1 vs 498.5) and SAC (723.2 vs 552.0), as well as CURL (726.1 vs 437.3) and DrQ-v2 (726.1 vs 410.3) on Vision Control. The sole exception is TD-MPC2 on Proprio Control, which edges out EZ-V2 slightly (740.9 vs 723.2 mean), though EZ-V2 achieves this with 1000× fewer FLOPs per decision step (4.7×10⁷ vs 3.6×10¹⁰) and ~4× fewer parameters. EZ-V2 also requires only 32 search simulations versus 50 for Sample MCTS, and its tree search uses 32 imagined latent states compared to TD-MPC2's 9216, making it substantially more suitable for real-time robotic deployment.

## Relation to Prior Work

EZ-V2 extends EfficientZero (Ye et al., 2021) by replacing its MCTS-based planning with a sampling-based Gumbel search that generalizes to continuous action spaces, drawing on the Gumbel-Top-k trick (Kool et al., 2019) and the policy-improvement guarantees of Gumbel search (Danihelka et al., 2021). It also introduces Search-Based Value Estimation (SVE) to address off-policy issues in stale replay data, departing from EfficientZero's adaptive multi-step bootstrapping. Conceptually, the paper positions itself against two competing paradigms: world-model-based imagination (DreamerV3) and model-predictive control (TD-MPC2), arguing that a lightweight tree-search planner over a learned latent model achieves superior sample efficiency with far lower computational cost than either approach.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The paper addresses a persistent gap in sample-efficient RL: no prior algorithm consistently achieves near-SOTA performance across both discrete and continuous control, visual and low-dimensional inputs, under limited data budgets. The practical impact is significant for real-world robotics, where the 1000× reduction in FLOPs per decision step versus TD-MPC2 makes real-time edge deployment feasible. Open questions include whether the approach transfers to truly open-ended real-world manipulation tasks (the authors note this as future work) and whether the theoretical SVE error bounds are tight in practice, particularly during early training when model errors are large.
