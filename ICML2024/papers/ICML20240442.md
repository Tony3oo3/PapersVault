## Problem

Current LLM context windows are limited to ~128k tokens because untrained position indices introduce catastrophic out-of-distribution values, long training texts are scarce and expensive, and attention dispersion degrades short-context performance. Existing positional interpolation methods (PI, NTK, YaRN) apply uniform or heuristically grouped rescaling across RoPE dimensions, failing to exploit the complex non-uniform information entropy in the embedding and thus limiting achievable extension ratios.

## Input / Output

The method takes a pre-trained RoPE-based LLM (LLaMA2-7B or Mistral-7B), a target context window size, and validation text samples exceeding that length. It outputs an extended-context LLM whose RoPE rotation angles are rescaled by per-dimension factors and a token-position threshold, with the model dynamically switching between long-context and short-context rescale factors at inference time depending on input sequence length. The search is guided by perplexity computed on a small number of validation samples (3–5), making it feasible even at 2048k context lengths.

## Method

LongRoPE identifies two non-uniformities in positional interpolation—varying RoPE dimensions and initial token positions—and searches for optimal per-dimension rescale factors λ_i and a starting-token threshold n̂ using an evolutionary algorithm with two key optimizations: seeding the initial population with PI/NTK/YaRN solutions and imposing a monotonically non-decreasing constraint on λ_i to prune the search space. This non-uniform interpolation achieves an 8× context extension without fine-tuning, enabling a progressive strategy: search for 256k factors, fine-tune for only 1k steps at 256k length, then perform a second search on the fine-tuned model to reach 2048k without additional training. A final readjustment search recovers short-context (4k/8k) performance by reducing interpolation at those lengths, and an attention temperature scaling (Eq. 4) mitigates attention dispersion at extreme lengths.

## Results

LongRoPE extends LLaMA2-7B and Mistral-7B context windows to 2048k tokens (512× extension) with only 1k fine-tuning steps at ≤256k training length. On Proof-pile at 256k evaluation length, LongRoPE-2048k(ft=256k) achieves 1.87 perplexity for LLaMA2 and 1.77 for Mistral, compared to 99.64 (YaRN-128k) and >10³ (LongLoRA-100k). On Books3 at 2048k, LongRoPE-LLaMA2-2048k(ft=256k) achieves 7.08 perplexity while all baselines exceed 10⁴. Passkey retrieval maintains ≥90% accuracy from 4k to 2048k for LLaMA2 and 100% up to 1800k for Mistral, while baselines drop to 0% beyond 128k.

## Baselines & Comparisons

LongRoPE decisively outperforms all positional-interpolation baselines (PI/Together-32k, NTK/Code LLaMA, YaRN-64k, YaRN-128k, LongLoRA-100k) at context lengths beyond their trained windows: at 256k on Proof-pile, LongRoPE achieves 1.87–1.88 perplexity while the best baseline (YaRN-128k) reaches 99.64 and others exceed 10³–10⁴. At the extreme 2048k length on Books3, LongRoPE-LLaMA2-2048k(ft=256k) records 7.08 perplexity whereas every baseline (LongLoRA, Code LLaMA, YaRN) exceeds 10⁴. On passkey retrieval, LongRoPE maintains ≥90% accuracy up to 2048k (LLaMA2) and 100% up to 1800k (Mistral), while all baselines collapse to 0% beyond 128k. On standard 4k-context benchmarks (ARC-c, HellaSwag, MMLU, TruthfulQA), LongRoPE-2048k matches or slightly exceeds the original LLaMA2-7B (e.g., 54.1 vs 53.1 on ARC-c) and outperforms MistralLite on TruthfulQA (44.1 vs 38.3), confirming that the 512× extension does not degrade short-context performance. YaRN, the strongest non-uniform interpolation baseline, is consistently beaten by LongRoPE at every evaluation length beyond 64k, with the gap widening dramatically as context length increases.

## Relation to Prior Work

LongRoPE builds directly on the positional interpolation lineage for extending RoPE-based LLMs: PI (linear rescaling), NTK (frequency-dependent non-uniform scaling), and YaRN (three-group frequency-based strategy). It departs from these by replacing their human-designed interpolation formulas with an evolutionary search over two axes of non-uniformity—per-RoPE-dimension rescale factors and a token-position threshold—treating the problem as a combinatorial optimization rather than a closed-form design. The progressive extension strategy (fine-tune to 256k, then re-search for 2048k) and the short-context recovery mechanism are novel procedural contributions not present in prior positional interpolation work.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

LongRoPE provides the first publicly available recipe to push RoPE-based LLMs to 2M tokens with only ~1k fine-tuning steps at 256k, making extreme context extension accessible without prohibitively long training data. The 8× non-fine-tuning extension and the per-dimension search are immediately reusable for any RoPE-based model, and the short-context recovery addresses a practical concern that prior methods largely ignored. Open questions remain: inference cost at 2M tokens is still high (8×80GB A100), the token-position non-uniformity loses effectiveness at extreme lengths, and it is unclear how downstream tasks can effectively exploit such vast context windows.
