## Problem

LLMs trained on short context windows (e.g., 4k tokens) suffer severe perplexity degradation on longer inputs, and existing training-free extrapolation methods such as Position Interpolation and NTK-Aware RoPE typically fail beyond 8k tokens. Finetuning for longer contexts is prohibitively expensive, particularly for 70B-scale models, leaving open-source models far behind proprietary counterparts. The paper asks whether context windows can be extended to 100k+ tokens without any additional training while preserving both local and long-range dependencies.

## Input / Output

The method takes a long text token sequence (up to 192k tokens) and a pretrained RoPE-based LLM with completely unchanged weights, modifying only the inference-time attention computation via a monkey patch to the LlamaAttention module. The chunk size is set to 3/4 of the pretraining context length (3072 for Llama2's 4k window), and the approach integrates seamlessly with Flash Attention 2, maintaining comparable GPU memory and inference speed to standard self-attention. Outputs include generated text, perplexity on PG19, and QA/summarization answers on long-context benchmarks such as NarrativeQA, Qasper, QuALITY, QMSum, and L-Eval.

## Method

DCA segments self-attention into chunks smaller than the pretraining window and applies three distinct position-index schemes to the relative position matrix M: (1) intra-chunk attention resets position indices to 0…s−1 within each chunk via modular arithmetic, preserving exact local relative positions; (2) inter-chunk attention assigns all cross-chunk queries a constant position index of c−1 (the maximum pretraining position), ensuring queries always exceed all key indices while bounding relative distances; (3) successive-chunk attention adjusts the first w positions of the inter-chunk query indices to s, s+1, …, s+w−1 to restore precise locality between adjacent chunks. The key insight is that by redesigning how position indices are assigned rather than scaling them (as PI does) or modifying the RoPE base frequency (as NTK does), all relative positions in M remain within the range encountered during pretraining, eliminating the need for any weight updates. The three attention components are computed as separate Flash Attention 2 calls and combined via a unified softmax normalization, making the approach a drop-in replacement for standard attention in existing inference stacks.

## Results

On PG19 perplexity, CHUNKLLAMA2 70B (training-free, 4k pretraining context) achieves PPL of 5.24 at 4k, 5.21 at 16k, 5.30 at 32k, and 5.59 at 64k — a mere 0.35 increase over 16× the training length, while all prior training-free methods (PI, NTK, YaRN) degrade to PPL >15 or OOM by 16k. On few-shot long-context tasks, the training-free CHUNKLLAMA2 70B achieves an average score of 37.8, surpassing the finetuned Longlora 70B (37.2) and approaching the proprietary Llama2 Long 70B (40.7, trained on 400B tokens). In zero-shot L-Eval, CHUNKLLAMA2-Chat 70B reaches an average of 63.20, attaining 94% of GPT-3.5-16k's performance (67.03) without any additional training.

## Baselines & Comparisons

Against training-free RoPE-scaling baselines, DCA dominates decisively: at 16k context, PI 7B degrades to PPL 15.11 and NTK 7B to 26.12, while CHUNKLLAMA2 7B maintains 7.64; at 32k, PI and NTK exceed PPL 58, whereas DCA 7B stays at 7.89. ReRoPE encounters OOM at 16k due to Flash Attention incompatibility. Among finetuned baselines, DCA 70B (training-free) matches or exceeds Longlora 70B on few-shot tasks (37.8 vs. 37.8 avg) and surpasses it on zero-shot L-Eval (63.20 vs. 59.88), despite Longlora requiring 1000 LoRA training steps. DCA also outperforms LongChat-7B (46.64 vs. 41.85 avg on L-Eval) and Vicuna-13B-16k in the training-free setting. The main gap is against the proprietary Llama2 Long 70B (40.7 vs. 37.8 few-shot avg; 72.52 vs. 63.20 zero-shot), which was trained on 400B additional tokens over 100k steps — a ~3-point gap the authors attribute to the absence of long-context finetuning. DCA is also orthogonal to existing long-context models: integrating it with Together-32k (PI) and CodeLlama (NTK) extends both to 192k context with PPL 7.83 and 9.83 respectively, versus >10² for the unmodified models at 64k.

## Relation to Prior Work

DCA builds directly on RoPE (Su et al., 2022) by reusing the pretrained model's position embeddings unchanged, but departs fundamentally from the dominant line of work (PI, NTK-Aware RoPE, YaRN, CLEX) that modifies position indices or base frequencies to compress the relative position range. Instead, it draws on chunk-based sparse attention patterns (Child et al., 2019; Song et al., 2023; Ratner et al., 2023) to reassign which position index each query uses depending on its chunk relationship to the key, thereby keeping all relative positions within the pretrained range without any scaling. The method is explicitly orthogonal to existing scaled encodings, meaning it can be stacked on top of PI- or NTK-extended models to push context further.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The practical impact is significant: DCA requires only an inference-time code patch (monkey-patching LlamaAttention), is compatible with Flash Attention 2, and needs no additional training data or GPU hours, making it immediately deployable for any RoPE-based LLM. The 70B validation and the orthogonality property (extending already-32k models to 192k) address the most common practitioner pain points. Limitations include PPL degradation at the extreme end (7.05 at 192k for 70B), dependence on RoPE-specific structure, and the ad-hoc nature of the three-way position index assignment; open questions include generalization to non-RoPE architectures (ALiBi, T5-style), behavior during autoregressive generation over very long outputs, and whether the chunk-size hyperparameter can be made adaptive.
