## Problem

Large-scale text-to-image diffusion models struggle to follow complex prompts, often misunderstanding context and ignoring keywords. Existing grounding inputs present a trade-off: bounding boxes are user-friendly but too coarse-grained, while semantic or depth maps provide fine-grained control but are difficult for users to construct and manipulate. The paper seeks a grounding representation that is simultaneously fine-grained, modular, human-interpretable, and easy to generate by LLMs.

## Input / Output

The model takes a global text caption plus a set of blob representations (up to 15 per image), where each blob consists of a 5-element parameter vector [c_x, c_y, a, b, θ] specifying a tilted ellipse and a free-text description sentence. Images are 512×512, encoded into a 64×64×4 latent space via the SD-1.4 VAE. Blob parameters are encoded with Fourier features (with θ mapped to sin/cos), and blob descriptions are encoded with the CLIP text encoder; the two are concatenated per token and projected through an MLP. When augmented with LLMs, the blob representations themselves are generated from a text prompt via in-context learning (GPT-4 for parameters in CSS format, LLaMA-13B for descriptions).

## Method

BlobGEN is built on the frozen SD-1.4 latent diffusion model, adding only new cross-attention layers trained with the standard denoising score matching loss. The core architectural contribution is a masked cross-attention module: for each blob, a binary ellipse mask (downsampled to feature-map resolution) restricts the attention so that the blob's key/value vectors are only visible to query positions within that ellipse, preventing cross-region interference and enabling modular, disentangled generation. Blob embeddings are formed by concatenating the CLIP-encoded description tokens with the Fourier-encoded parameter vector, then projecting through a shared MLP. The module is added in a gated fashion (learnable scalar), and an optional gated self-attention layer from GLIGEN is included. For LLM augmentation, the paper designs two separate in-context learning procedures: blob parameters are generated in a CSS-like format (e.g., 'object {major-radius: ?px; minor-radius: ?px; cx: ?px; cy: ?px; angle: ?}') using GPT-3.5/GPT-4 with retrieved top-k demonstrations, while blob descriptions are generated as 'object {text sentence}' pairs using LLaMA-13B. Training uses 1M Common Crawl image-text pairs with synthetic global captions from LLaVA-1.5, 400K steps at batch size 512 on 64 A100 GPUs, with 50% global-caption dropout to force reliance on blob conditioning.

## Results

On MS-COCO with the consistency decoder, BlobGEN achieves a zero-shot FID of 8.61, improving over the base SD-1.4 model (10.40) and GLIGEN (11.15), while also substantially outperforming both on controllability: mIOU 0.5103 vs. 0.1316 (base) and 0.4238 (GLIGEN), rCLIP_t 0.2794 vs. 0.2381 and 0.2591, and rCLIP_i 0.8288 vs. 0.7520 and 0.7976. On NSR-1K compositional reasoning with GPT-4, BlobGEN achieves 90.23% spatial layout accuracy and 80.16% spatial image accuracy, outperforming LayoutGPT (86.23% and 74.35% respectively) and SDXL (46.59% spatial image accuracy).

## Baselines & Comparisons

BlobGEN outperforms the base SD-1.4 model on all four metrics, with the most dramatic gain in mIOU (0.5103 vs. 0.1316), confirming that blob grounding provides far stronger spatial control than text-only conditioning. Against GLIGEN (bounding-box grounding), BlobGEN wins on FID (8.61 vs. 11.15) and all three controllability metrics, with mIOU improving by ~20% relative (0.5103 vs. 0.4238), demonstrating that ellipse-based blobs encode richer spatial information than axis-aligned boxes. On NSR-1K compositional reasoning with GPT-4, BlobGEN surpasses LayoutGPT by 4.0% in spatial layout accuracy (90.23 vs. 86.23) and 5.8% in spatial image accuracy (80.16 vs. 74.35), while also achieving higher numerical image accuracy (62.96 vs. 60.25). Compared to text-to-image models without layout planning (SDXL: 46.59% spatial image accuracy; Attend-and-Excite: 36.74%), BlobGEN's LLM-augmented pipeline yields substantially more reliable compositional generation. Against LMD (Lian et al., 2023) under the same fixed in-context protocol, BlobGEN achieves higher spatial image accuracy (77.84 vs. 73.50) and numerical image accuracy (60.74 vs. 57.61) while also producing more natural images without the 'copy-and-paste' artifacts observed in LMD.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the layout-guided text-to-image generation line (GLIGEN, RECO, LayoutDiffusion) by replacing coarse bounding boxes with dense blob representations—tilted ellipses paired with object-level text descriptions—as grounding inputs. It builds directly on the Stable Diffusion / LDM architecture and borrows the gated cross-attention design from GLIGEN, but introduces a masked cross-attention module that restricts each blob embedding to attend only to its local spatial region, enabling modular and disentangled generation. For the LLM-augmented pipeline, it follows LayoutGPT's in-context learning paradigm for layout planning but generalizes the output from bounding boxes to the richer blob format, and uses a separate LLM (LLaMA-13B) for generating blob descriptions.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The blob representation offers a practical middle ground between user-friendly bounding boxes and fine-grained but hard-to-construct semantic/depth maps, and the masked cross-attention design naturally enables local editing and object repositioning without relying on attention guidance or sample blending tricks. The LLM-augmented pipeline demonstrates that compositional reasoning (spatial and numerical correctness) can be improved by delegating layout planning to LLMs, though the paper acknowledges failure cases where LLM-generated blobs are suboptimal. Open questions include whether blob representations can scale to very complex scenes with many overlapping objects, whether the approach generalizes beyond 2D to 3D or video generation, and how to better integrate inversion methods for faithful image recovery from blobs alone.
