## Problem

While data distillation from diffusion models into lightweight GANs enables real-time on-device image editing, each new editing concept still requires training a GAN from scratch at significant computational cost (e.g., 110 min on an H100 for Co-Mod-GAN). The paper asks whether the GAN distillation process itself can be made dramatically more efficient in both training time and per-concept storage.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ICML20240639/cropped_page1_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The model takes a 256×256 RGB image and a text prompt specifying the target editing concept, and produces a single edited image in that concept domain. At inference, the 7.1M-parameter generator runs in 15.5 ms on an iPhone 14 Pro GPU (30 FPS real-time). Training pairs are constructed by applying diffusion models (Stable Diffusion, IP2P, NI, ControlNet, InstructDiffusion) to real images from FFHQ and Flickr-Scenery, with the best-quality generated image selected per pair.

## Method

E²GAN introduces a three-stage efficient fine-tuning pipeline. First, a base GAN with 3 ResNet Blocks and 1 Transformer Block (self-attention + cross-attention for text conditioning) is pre-trained on 20 diverse concepts selected via K-means clustering of CLIP embeddings, providing generalized features. Second, only the sampling layers and transformer block are identified as crucial (via a freezing ablation), and LoRA with a simple rank-doubling search (evaluating FID every 10 epochs until saturation) is applied to these layers, reducing trainable parameters to 1.29% of the base model. Third, Similarity Clustering (SC) selects only the data point nearest each K-means cluster center from the training set, cutting training iterations by N/K without incurring extra per-step cost. Together these techniques reduce training FLOPs by 14–25× and per-concept storage by 869× versus baselines while achieving lower FID.

## Results

On FFHQ, E²GAN achieves a Clean FID of 80.28, outperforming pix2pix (86.03) and pix2pix-zero-distilled (87.76). On Flickr-Scenery (Landscape), E²GAN reaches 109.37 FID versus 114.2 and 132.6 for the two baselines. In terms of training efficiency, E²GAN reduces training FLOPs by 14× and 25×, and training time by 4.8× and 33× compared to pix2pix and pix2pix-zero-distilled respectively, while requiring only 0.092M parameters to update per concept (869× fewer than pix2pix-zero-distilled). On-device inference latency is 15.5 ms on iPhone 14 Pro, enabling real-time 30 FPS editing.

## Baselines & Comparisons

E²GAN outperforms pix2pix (Isola et al., 2017) on both FID (80.28 vs. 86.03 on FFHQ; 109.37 vs. 114.2 on Landscape) and efficiency (15.5 ms vs. 21 ms latency; 7.1M vs. 11.4M parameters; 14× fewer training FLOPs). Against pix2pix-zero-distilled / Co-Mod-GAN (Zhao et al., 2021; Parmar et al., 2023), E²GAN achieves substantially better FID (80.28 vs. 87.76 on FFHQ; 109.37 vs. 132.6 on Landscape) while being 33× faster to train and 869× smaller in per-concept storage (0.092M vs. 79.2M parameters); Co-Mod-GAN is not even deployable on mobile devices. Compared to SnapFusion (Li et al., 2023), E²GAN is ~126× faster at inference (15.5 ms vs. 1956 ms) and ~121× smaller (7.1M vs. 861M parameters). On conventional paired benchmarks (Facades, Cityscapes, Edges2Shoes), E²GAN's 3RB+1TB architecture beats GAN Compression (Li et al., 2020) on all three metrics (FID 121.89 vs. not reported, mIoU 43.20 vs. 41.71, FID 24.03 vs. 25.76). On the unpaired Horse2Zebra task, E²GAN achieves FID 44.12, surpassing CycleGAN (74.04), GAN Slimming (86.09), and GAN Compression (64.95), and matching CUT (45.76).

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the data distillation pipeline introduced by pix2pix-zero (Parmar et al., 2023), which uses diffusion models to generate paired training data for GANs, by making the GAN training step itself efficient. It builds on the classic pix2pix ResNet architecture (Isola et al., 2017) but replaces most ResNet Blocks with a single Transformer Block for better global reasoning at lower cost, and adapts LoRA (Hu et al., 2021)—originally designed for large language models—to parameter-efficient fine-tuning of GANs. The key departure from prior work is the introduction of a multi-concept pre-trained base GAN that enables transfer learning, combined with a systematic identification of which layers are truly crucial for concept adaptation, rather than training each concept's GAN from scratch.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

This work provides a practical pathway to democratize diffusion-model-quality image editing on mobile devices, reducing per-concept training time by up to 33× and storage by 869× while achieving better FID than training from scratch. The finding that a base model trained on 20 diverse concepts outperforms one trained on 200 art concepts suggests that concept diversity, not quantity, drives generalization—a useful principle for future base model construction. Key limitations remain: the pipeline still depends on expensive diffusion model inference for data generation, and the approach is validated primarily at 256×256 resolution on face and scenery domains, leaving open whether the same efficiency gains hold at higher resolutions or for more structurally complex edits.
