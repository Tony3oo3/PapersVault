## Problem

Linear attention offers O(nd²) theoretical complexity versus O(n²d) for softmax attention, yet no leading LLM has adopted it due to two persistent issues: (1) a notable performance gap compared to state-of-the-art softmax attention models, and (2) slow training speed caused by cumulative summation (cumsum) operations that are unavoidable in causal settings, negating the theoretical speedup. This paper addresses both gaps simultaneously.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ICML20241688/cropped_page1_idx1.webp)

## Input / Output

The model consumes raw text sequences (tokenized via BPE with expanded Chinese vocabulary) of varying lengths from 1K to 128K tokens and produces next-token predictions for autoregressive generation. A key operational property is that training throughput (tokens per GPU per second) remains constant across all sequence lengths under fixed memory, whereas FlashAttention-2 and vanilla linear attention exhibit quadratic degradation. Inference throughput reaches up to 11× that of equivalent 7B transformer models on A100 GPUs.

## Method

Lightning Attention eliminates the cumsum bottleneck by splitting causal attention into intra-block computations (conventional left product with a B×B causal mask) and inter-block computations (right product using a running d×d KV state matrix), achieving O(nd² + nBd) time complexity. Tiling across both forward and backward passes keeps all intermediate matrices in on-chip SRAM, making the implementation IO-aware in the style of FlashAttention. The TransNormerLLM (TNL) architecture pairs this attention with Gated Linear Attention (Swish-activated) for token mixing, a Simple Gated Linear Unit (SGLU) without activation for channel mixing, LRPE-d positional encoding with per-head/per-layer exponential decay to balance local and global interactions, and SRMSNorm for efficient tensor normalization. The full system is deployed with FSDP, activation checkpointing, BFloat16 mixed precision, and a custom model-parallelism scheme that splits SGLU and GLA blocks independently across GPUs.

## Results

On Wikitext-103 (44M params), TNL achieves the lowest test perplexity of 24.03, outperforming Transformer (24.78), HGRN (24.82), and TNN (24.67). At 15B parameters, TNL reaches 60.06 MMLU (5-shot) and 53.01 C-Eval (5-shot), surpassing LLaMA2-13B (55.70 MMLU, 38.34 C-Eval) and Baichuan2-13B (57.02 MMLU, 59.63 C-Eval). In inference throughput, TNL with Lightning Attention achieves up to 11× higher tokens/sec than transformer-structured 7B models on A100 80G. Training speed remains constant across sequence lengths from 1K to 128K, while LLaMA-FA2, HGRN, and TNN all show rapid degradation.

## Baselines & Comparisons

On Wikitext-103 (44M), TNL (PPL 24.03) beats every baseline including Transformer (24.78), HGRN (24.82), TNN (24.67), and RWKV (25.07). At 6.8B, TNL matches or exceeds LLaMA2-6.7B on PIQA (80.09 vs 78.07) and OBQA (63.40 vs 44.20) and substantially outperforms it on C-Eval (43.18 vs 33.20), though it trails slightly on MMLU (43.10 vs 45.30) and BoolQ (75.87 vs 77.68). At 15B, TNL surpasses LLaMA2-13B on MMLU (60.06 vs 55.70), C-Eval (53.01 vs 38.34), HellaSwag (82.18 vs 79.35), and ARC-c (50.51 vs 48.98), while Baichuan2-13B remains ahead on C-Eval (59.63 vs 53.01). In training efficiency, TNL maintains constant tokens-per-GPU-per-second from 1K to 128K sequence length, whereas LLaMA-FA2, HGRN, and TNN all degrade sharply. In inference, TNL achieves up to 11× higher throughput than transformer-structured 7B models, and Lightning Attention shows linear (vs. quadratic) scaling in both forward and backward pass runtime compared to FlashAttention-2.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the TransNormer linear attention architecture (Qin et al., 2022a) by solving its central practical limitation: the cumsum bottleneck that prevented linear attention from achieving its theoretical O(nd²) complexity in causal settings. The key algorithmic insight—splitting attention into intra-block (conventional quadratic computation) and inter-block (kernel-trick linear computation) via a tiling strategy—borrows the IO-aware tiling philosophy from FlashAttention (Dao et al., 2022a; Dao, 2023) but applies it to a fundamentally different computational structure. The gating mechanism is adapted from Flash (Hua et al., 2022), and the positional encoding extends LRPE (Qin et al., 2023b) with exponential decay to balance local and global interactions.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

This is the first demonstration that linear attention can maintain constant training speed across sequence lengths (1K–128K) under fixed memory, closing the gap between theoretical and practical complexity that has plagued the field since Katharopoulos et al. (2020). The 11× inference throughput advantage over transformer-based LLMs at 7B scale has direct practical implications for deployment cost and latency. Open questions include whether the performance parity with softmax attention holds at 70B+ scale, whether the architecture generalizes to multimodal or non-autoregressive settings, and whether the block-size hyperparameter B introduces a fundamental trade-off between speed and expressiveness at extreme sequence lengths.
