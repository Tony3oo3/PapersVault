## Problem

Prior work on differentially private community detection for stochastic block models established exact recovery conditions only for the symmetric (equal-size community) case, with algorithms running in quasi-polynomial time or with recovery thresholds loose by constants. The conditions for exact recovery under edge-DP in the more realistic asymmetric, censored, and general-structure SBM variants remained entirely open, and no polynomial-time algorithm with tight thresholds existed for any SBM variant under arbitrary small ε.

## Input / Output

The algorithm takes as input an n-vertex graph G generated by one of three SBM variants (Binary Asymmetric, Binary Censored, or General Structure) in the dense regime (p = a log n / n, q = b log n / n), along with privacy parameters ε and δ (set to δ = n^{-c}). It outputs the ground-truth community assignment: a binary vector σ* ∈ {±1}^n for the two-cluster models or a cluster matrix Z* for the multi-cluster General Structure model. The recovery threshold depends jointly on the SBM parameters (a, b, ρ, ξ) and the privacy parameter ε, and the algorithm runs in O(poly(n)) time with high probability.

## Method

The core mechanism composes the Stability mechanism (adding Laplace noise to the distance-to-instability of the SDP estimator) with model-specific SDP relaxations. The key technical contribution is the C-concentration framework: for each SBM variant, a custom set of concentration conditions is designed that simultaneously (1) holds w.h.p. for SBM-generated graphs, (2) persists under log n edge perturbations, and (3) deterministically constructs a dual certificate proving the SDP's unique optimum is the ground-truth cluster matrix. The polynomial-time algorithm (Algorithm 2) avoids the expensive distance-to-instability computation by directly testing C-concentration using estimated parameters (â, b̂) and the SDP solution as a proxy for σ*; when the test passes, a lower bound d(G) ≥ c log n / ε is used instead of the exact value, yielding O(poly(n)) running time w.h.p. while preserving (ε,δ)-DP and exact recovery guarantees.

## Results

The paper establishes the first rigorous exact recovery conditions under edge-DP for three SBM variants (Binary Asymmetric, Binary Censored, and General Structure), all with O(poly(n)) running time. For the Binary Symmetric case (Corollary 1), the recovery threshold is √a − √b ≥ √2·√(1 + 2/ε), which matches the non-private threshold √a − √b ≥ √2 as ε→∞. For the Binary Asymmetric case (Theorem 1), the condition involves √a − √(b(1+log(a/b))) > √(c·log(a/b)/ε) plus a function h̃(c/ε) > 1. All three variants achieve exact recovery with probability at least 1 − n^{−Ω(1)} at δ = n^{−2}, and the polynomial-time algorithm (Algorithm 2) avoids the quasi-polynomial cost of directly computing the distance to instability.

## Baselines & Comparisons

Compared to Mohamed et al. (2022), the prior best result, this paper extends exact recovery from symmetric SBMs only to three additional variants (Binary Asymmetric, Binary Censored, General Structure) and improves the symmetric threshold from √2·√(1 + 3/(2ε)) to √2·√(1 + 2/ε) while reducing running time from quasi-polynomial n^{O(log n)} to O(poly(n)). Against Chen et al. (2023), the only prior polynomial-time method with constant ε, this paper's threshold scales as 1/√ε (matching non-private as ε→∞) rather than 1/ε, and avoids the large constant gap (√a − √b ≥ 16) that Chen et al. require. Compared to Seif et al. (2023), which uses vertex sampling with probability ζ and inherits the exponential/quasi-polynomial complexity of Mohamed et al., this paper achieves polynomial time without any sampling overhead or the 1/√ζ degradation factor. The key differentiator is the C-concentration framework, which enables a deterministic dual certificate construction that persists under log n edge perturbations, yielding the first polynomial-time algorithms for all three SBM variants under arbitrary small ε.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the Stability mechanism framework of Mohamed et al. (2022), which established the first rigorous exact recovery bounds under edge-DP but only for symmetric SBMs, to three more complex variants: Binary Asymmetric, Censored Binary, and General Structure SBMs. The SDP formulations and dual certificate conditions are adapted from Hajek et al. (2016a,b), but the key analytical novelty is the C-concentration framework—a set of model-specific conditions that simultaneously hold w.h.p., persist under log n edge perturbations, and suffice to construct a deterministic dual certificate. The polynomial-time algorithm in Section 5 departs from prior work (Mohamed et al. 2022, Seif et al. 2023) that required exponential or quasi-polynomial time, or from Chen et al. (2023) that achieved polynomial time but with recovery thresholds off by constants from the non-private setting.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

This work establishes the first rigorous recoverability conditions for three important SBM variants under edge-DP, closing a gap left by prior work that was limited to symmetric communities. The results show that the privacy cost (in terms of recovery threshold degradation) scales as 1/√ε and vanishes as ε→∞, matching non-private thresholds, which provides a clean theoretical characterization of the privacy-utility tradeoff. Open questions include whether these thresholds are information-theoretically tight under DP (i.e., whether recovery is impossible below these bounds), whether the C-concentration framework extends to node-DP or degree-corrected SBMs, and whether the polynomial-time algorithm's reliance on knowing (or estimating) the SBM parameters a, b can be made fully adaptive. The purely theoretical nature limits immediate practical impact, but it sets the benchmark for future algorithmic work in private community detection.
