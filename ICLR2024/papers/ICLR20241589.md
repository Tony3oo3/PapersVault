## Problem

Existing disentangled VAEs impose information bottlenecks on latent variables but do not directly optimize the informativeness of individual latent dimensions, limiting their ability to represent complex, realistic datasets. Additionally, no prior work has provided quantitative evidence that disentanglement and generative diversity are statistically correlated, despite intuitive arguments from symmetry-based definitions of disentanglement.

## Input / Output

The model consumes 64×64 RGB images in a fully unsupervised setting and produces a K-dimensional latent code (K=10 for factorized datasets, K=48 for CelebA) where each dimension is encouraged to capture a distinct generative factor. For generation, it accepts either a noise vector sampled from a standard normal or an encoded latent representation with individual dimensions shifted by ±1–10 standard deviations (traversal strategy), decoding to produce 64×64 images. The encoder outputs per-dimension means and standard deviations used both for reparameterization and for constructing the product-of-marginals prior in the CEB term.

## Method

α-TCVAE optimizes a novel lower bound on the joint total correlation TC(z, x) = Σ_k I(z_k, x) − I(z, x), which simultaneously promotes disentanglement (via the joint MI term) and individual variable informativeness (via the per-dimension MI terms). The bound decomposes into a convex combination of the Variational Information Bottleneck (VIB) term, KL(q(z|x)‖r(z)), and the Conditional Entropy Bottleneck (CEB) term, KL(q(z|x)‖r_p(z|x)), where r_p(z|x) is a product-of-marginals Gaussian prior with parameters σ_p and μ_p computed from the individual latent dimensions' encoder outputs. A hyperparameter α ∈ [0,1] trades off the two terms: α=0 recovers β-VAE, while α=1 recovers a pure CEB objective. Unlike β-TCVAE, the bound requires no auxiliary networks, importance sampling, or Monte-Carlo estimation, making it a drop-in replacement for the standard VAE ELBO. The CEB term induces an inductive bias that pushes otherwise noisy latent dimensions to become informative, which the authors hypothesize enables discovery of novel generative factors absent from the training data.

## Results

α-TCVAE consistently achieves higher Vendi (diversity) and lower FID (faithfulness) scores than all VAE baselines across five datasets (Teapots, 3DShapes, MPI3D-Real, Cars3D, CelebA), with its Vendi score comparable to StyleGAN while its FID is only slightly worse. The improvement is most pronounced on MPI3D-Real, the most realistic factorized dataset, where α-TCVAE is the only model to significantly outperform the entangled vanilla VAE on the SNC disentanglement metric. In the downstream RL Ant Maze task, replacing Director's β-VAE Goal VAE with α-TCVAE (Alpha-Director) yields significantly higher mean return, more diverse sub-goals (higher Vendi), and faster exploration. Traversal-based sampling produces substantially more diverse and faithful images than prior-sampling for all VAE models, and all disentangled VAEs outperform the vanilla VAE on diversity.

## Baselines & Comparisons

Against β-VAE (Higgins et al., 2016), α-TCVAE wins on Vendi, FID, DCI, SNC, and unfairness across all five datasets, with the gap widening on more realistic data (MPI3D-Real, Cars3D). Against β-TCVAE (Chen et al., 2018) and Factor-VAE (Kim & Mnih, 2018), α-TCVAE similarly dominates on diversity and disentanglement metrics, and notably does not require the expensive Monte-Carlo/importance-sampling strategies used by β-TCVAE. Against β-VAE+HFS (Roth et al., 2023), α-TCVAE matches or exceeds performance without the Hausdorff distance computation overhead. Against the vanilla VAE (Kingma & Welling, 2013), α-TCVAE wins on every metric, confirming that the TC bound's dual VIB+CEB regularization is strictly beneficial. Against StyleGAN (Karras et al., 2019), α-TCVAE achieves a comparable Vendi score and only a slightly higher FID, while training in ~2 hours versus >30 hours (15× faster) and additionally learning a representational model, not just a generative one. In the RL setting, Alpha-Director (using α-TCVAE) significantly outperforms Director (using β-VAE) on the Ant Maze task in mean return, goal diversity, and exploration coverage.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the disentangled VAE lineage (β-VAE, Factor-VAE, β-TCVAE) by deriving a novel total correlation lower bound that is a convex combination of the Variational Information Bottleneck (VIB) and Conditional Entropy Bottleneck (CEB) terms, rather than relying solely on an information bottleneck or expensive Monte-Carlo TC estimation as in β-TCVAE. The key conceptual departure is that the CEB term explicitly maximizes the informativeness of individual latent variables, addressing the known limitation that bottleneck-only objectives leave (K−M) noisy dimensions uninformative. The paper also connects to the symmetry-based redefinition of disentanglement (Higgins et al., 2019; 2022) and the Vendi score for diversity (Friedman & Dieng, 2022), positioning its empirical correlation study as the first quantitative bridge between these two perspectives.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The first quantitative correlation study linking disentanglement metrics (DCI, SNC) to generative diversity (Vendi score) provides empirical grounding for the symmetry-based hypothesis that smaller latent domains yield better generative capabilities, a claim previously supported only qualitatively. The downstream RL experiment (replacing β-VAE with α-TCVAE in Director on Ant Maze) demonstrates practical utility beyond image generation, suggesting the TC bound could benefit any model-based agent relying on diverse sub-goal sampling. However, the method's inability to disentangle high-dimensional data (CelebA) and the acknowledged scaling limitations of the entire disentangled VAE paradigm remain significant open questions, as does whether diversity can serve as a practical surrogate for disentanglement in complex settings.
