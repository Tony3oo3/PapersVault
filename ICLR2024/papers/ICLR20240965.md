## Problem

Multimodal VAEs remain weak at unconditional generation, and VAE-based clustering methods require the number of clusters to be specified a-priori, making model selection complex and fragile. Moreover, the integration of diffusion models—which dramatically improve generative quality in unimodal VAEs—into the multimodal VAE framework had not been explored. This paper tackles all three gaps simultaneously by proposing a multimodal VAE that enforces a clustering structure in the latent space, automatically recovers the true number of clusters post-hoc, and incorporates DDPMs for high-fidelity generation.

## Input / Output

The model consumes M paired modalities per data point (e.g., five image modalities with different backgrounds in PolyMNIST, or an image plus a text caption in CUBICC), using the cross-modal pairing as weak supervision. It produces cluster assignments over a discrete set of K latent clusters, reconstructed and generated samples for each modality, and—when the D-CMVAE extension is used—sharp images via a per-modality diffusion process. The model is trained with an intentionally over-specified K (e.g., K=40 for 10 true clusters), and a post-hoc pruning procedure on a validation set recovers the minimal set of latent clusters that model the true data structure without any label supervision.

## Method

CMVAE extends the MMVAE+ architecture by placing a Gaussian mixture prior p(z|c) on the shared latent subspace z, where c is a discrete cluster variable drawn from a categorical prior p_π(c); modality-specific latents w_m remain independent. The ELBO combines self-reconstruction, cross-reconstruction estimated via auxiliary distributions r_n(w_n) (following MMVAE+), and a β-weighted KL term, with the cluster posterior q(c|z,X)=p(c|z) computed analytically without an extra encoder. A post-hoc procedure iteratively prunes latent clusters by zeroing their prior probabilities, selecting the configuration that minimizes the normalized entropy of p(c|z) penalized by the normalized joint log-likelihood, thereby recovering the true cluster count from a single over-parameterized training run. D-CMVAE then freezes the CMVAE weights and trains M independent DDPMs whose reverse denoising process is conditioned on both self- and cross-modal CMVAE reconstructions (sampled with equal probability), yielding sharp cluster-specific generations while preserving the clustered latent geometry.

## Results

On PolyMNIST, CMVAE achieves 0.97 NMI, 0.97 ARI, and 0.99 accuracy for clustering, matching CMC (which requires a-priori knowledge of K=10) while operating without any such information. On the more challenging CUBICC dataset, CMVAE substantially outperforms all baselines, reaching 0.67 NMI, 0.59 ARI, and 0.76 accuracy versus 0.37 NMI for the best competing method (CMC). For generative performance on PolyMNIST, CMVAE improves unconditional coherence from 0.095 (MMVAE+) to 0.560 at β=1.0, while also achieving a lower FID (78.52 vs. 86.64). The post-hoc cluster selection procedure consistently recovers the true number of clusters (K̄=10 on PolyMNIST, K̄=8 on CUBICC) from an over-specified K=40 or K=35.

## Baselines & Comparisons

On PolyMNIST, CMVAE ties CMC (0.97 NMI / 0.97 ARI / 0.99 ACC) but without requiring the true number of clusters as input; when CMC is given K=40 (matching CMVAE's over-specified training), its performance drops to 0.78 NMI / 0.45 ARI / 0.38 ACC. CMVAE decisively outperforms the unimodal baselines VaDE (0.43 NMI) and DeepCluster (0.12 NMI) on PolyMNIST, which fail to capture digit content and instead model background features. On CUBICC, the gap widens dramatically: CMVAE (0.67 NMI) more than doubles CMC (0.37 NMI) and is 3.5× better than DeepCluster (0.19 NMI) and 4.5× better than VaDE (0.15 NMI). For generative quality, CMVAE surpasses MMVAE+ (the strongest prior multimodal VAE) in unconditional coherence by a factor of ~6 (0.560 vs. 0.095 at β=1.0) while also achieving a lower FID score. The D-CMVAE extension with DDPMs further improves image sharpness, achieving an average FID of ≈28 on CUBICC, a substantial improvement over prior multimodal VAEs in related settings.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper sits at the intersection of two established research lines: scalable multimodal VAEs (building directly on MMVAE+'s shared/modality-specific latent decomposition and auxiliary distributions) and VAE-based probabilistic clustering (adopting VaDE's parameter-free cluster posterior). The key architectural contribution is imposing a Gaussian mixture prior on the shared latent subspace to enforce a clustering structure, while the D-CMVAE extension adapts DiffuseVAE's reconstruction-conditioned DDPM to the multimodal setting by sampling from both self and cross-modal reconstructions. The post-hoc entropy-based cluster selection procedure departs from prior work that either requires specifying K a-priori or relies on non-parametric priors, offering a simpler and more practical alternative.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The paper addresses two persistent weaknesses in multimodal VAEs: poor unconditional generation (where CMVAE achieves a large coherence gap over prior methods) and the inability to integrate diffusion models for high-fidelity image synthesis. The post-hoc K-selection procedure removes a significant practical barrier for deploying generative clustering in real-world settings where the number of clusters is unknown. However, the computational overhead of maintaining separate diffusion processes per modality limits scalability, and the approach has not been validated on datasets with a large number of modalities in realistic settings, which the authors identify as a key direction for future work.
