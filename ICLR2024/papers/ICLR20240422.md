## Problem

Protein conformation sampling via MC or MD simulations is hampered by high energy barriers that prevent adequate exploration within feasible timescales, while existing learning-based samplers require target-specific simulation data for training, limiting their generalizability to unseen proteins. The paper asks whether one can sample diverse, physically valid conformational ensembles for any protein in a zero-shot manner without any simulation data during training or inference.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ICLR20240422/cropped_page1_idx3.webp)

## Input / Output

The method takes a single initial backbone structure (produced by ESMFold or AlphaFold2 from the protein sequence) parameterized as per-residue SE(3) frames (rotation matrix + translation vector) and produces an ensemble of 1,000 all-atom conformations. The backbone is sampled on the SE(3)^n manifold via the forward-backward process; backbone oxygen positions are determined by predicted ψ torsion angles, and side chains are packed post-hoc using FASPR rotamer libraries conditioned on the sampled backbone. The entire pipeline is roto-translation equivariant, so the output ensemble is not a trivial rotated/translated copy of the input.

## Method

STR2STR formulates conformation sampling as a structure-to-structure translation on the SE(3)^n manifold of residue frames, using a forward-backward process inspired by simulated annealing: a forward perturbation step (isotropic Gaussian on SO(3) for rotations, VP-SDE for translations) injects noise up to a truncated time T_δ < T to enhance exploration, followed by a backward score-based annealing step that denoises the perturbed frames to recover physically plausible conformations. The score function is predicted by DenoisingIPA, a variant of AlphaFold2's structure module with Invariant Point Attention layers, trained via amortized denoising score matching on 26,459 PDB crystal structures (treated as local energy minima) plus auxiliary backbone MSE and distogram losses. Because training uses only general PDB structures rather than target-specific trajectories, a single pretrained model performs zero-shot sampling for any unseen protein. The equivariance of the score network with respect to global roto-translations guarantees that the full forward-backward process preserves this property, preventing degenerate outputs.

## Results

On the 12 fast-folding protein benchmark, STR2STR(SDE) achieves the best generative-model performance across all fidelity and diversity metrics: JS-PwD 0.348, JS-TIC 0.400, JS-Rg 0.365, MAE-TM 0.133, and MAE-RMSD 0.184, while maintaining high validity (Val-Clash 0.977, Val-Bond 0.982). These numbers are comparable to 10 µs MD reference trajectories (JS-PwD 0.236, MAE-TM 0.144) and substantially outperform all zero-shot baselines. In a speed comparison on the WW domain, STR2STR produces a comparable-fidelity ensemble in 510 GPU-seconds versus >160 GPU-days for 100 µs explicit-solvent MD.

## Baselines & Comparisons

STR2STR(SDE) decisively outperforms all three zero-shot baselines on every fidelity and diversity metric. Against MSA subsampling (AF2-based), STR2STR reduces JS-PwD from 0.634 to 0.348 and MAE-RMSD from 0.713 to 0.184, though MSA subsampling retains a slight validity edge (Val-Clash 0.999 vs 0.977). Against EigenFold (sequence-to-structure diffusion), STR2STR wins on all seven metrics, with the largest gaps in Val-Clash (0.977 vs 0.812) and JS-Rg (0.365 vs 0.666), indicating EigenFold fails to capture conformational diversity. Against idpGAN (GAN-based), STR2STR dominates on Val-Bond (0.982 vs 0.032, a near-total failure of idpGAN to maintain backbone bonds) and on all JS/MAE metrics. In absolute terms, STR2STR(SDE) approaches 10 µs MD reference on diversity (MAE-TM 0.133 vs 0.144) but still lags on fidelity (JS-PwD 0.348 vs 0.236), and the authors note it underperforms 100 µs MD on distributional metrics.

## Relation to Prior Work

STR2STR builds directly on the SE(3)^n diffusion framework of Yim et al. (2023) and the Riemannian score-based generative modeling of De Bortoli et al. (2022), adapting them to a structure-to-structure translation problem rather than the sequence-to-structure generation used by EigenFold or idpGAN. The score network architecture (DenoisingIPA) is a variant of AlphaFold2's Invariant Point Attention module, and the translation-component diffusion follows the VP-SDE formulation of Song et al. (2020). The key conceptual departure from prior generative conformation samplers (Boltzmann generators, CGNets, idpGAN) is the reframing of sampling as an amortized denoising task on PDB crystal structures—treating them as local energy minima—thereby eliminating the need for system-specific simulation data during both training and inference, and the simulated-annealing-inspired forward-backward process that limits perturbation to a sub-full noise level to preserve initial-structure information.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The practical impact is significant: STR2STR achieves comparable fidelity to 100 µs MD simulations in ~510 GPU seconds versus >160 GPU days, a roughly five-orders-of-magnitude speedup, while requiring no simulation data at all. This opens the door to rapid conformational ensemble generation for any protein from a single sequence, which is currently intractable via classical simulation for most systems. Key limitations remain: the method still underperforms the longest MD references on distributional metrics (JS-PwD, JS-TIC), struggles to capture rare transient states that require climbing energy barriers, and the isotropic perturbation kernel does not exploit collective variables. Open directions include biased/anisotropic perturbation, integration with physical force fields for enhanced sampling, and few-shot fine-tuning with system-specific trajectories.
