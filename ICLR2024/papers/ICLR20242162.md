## Problem

Single-view novel view synthesis is severely under-constrained: given only an input image and a target camera pose, the unobserved geometry and texture admit many plausible solutions, leading to implausible generations (e.g., a shark with two tails) and no means for explicit user control over concealed parts. Zero123 frames NVS as a pure image-to-image translation problem, which lacks high-level semantic guidance to narrow the solution space. TOSS addresses this by introducing text as an additional semantic constraint that both improves plausibility and grants users controllability over what appears in unobserved regions.

## Input / Output

TOSS takes a single RGB image (encoded by a frozen VAE), an optional text description (encoded by a frozen CLIP text encoder), and a relative camera pose (rotation and translation, passed through frequency encoding) as input, and produces a novel view image at the specified viewpoint. The model operates in the latent space of Stable Diffusion v1.5 at 256×256 (or 512×512 with a higher-resolution variant). Notably, text is optional: without it, TOSS degenerates to a classical image-and-pose-conditioned NVS model. For 3D reconstruction, the generated multi-view images are fed into an instant-ngp NeRF trained with SDS loss, and meshes are extracted via marching cubes.

## Method

TOSS fine-tunes Stable Diffusion for NVS by introducing a dense cross-attention module that lets VAE feature maps of the noisy target x_t attend to VAE feature maps of the condition image x_c at every spatial position, avoiding the spatial-alignment requirement of channel concatenation and the information loss of CLIP token cross-attention. Camera pose is injected as a single frequency-encoded key-value pair appended to the image keys/values in the same cross-attention, implicitly modulating attention strength between views. A composed classifier-free guidance scheme with separate scales α (image) and β (text) handles the non-independence of the two conditions. To improve quality at fixed compute cost, TOSS trains two expert denoisers specialized to high timesteps (1000–800, governing pose correctness) and low timesteps (800–0, governing fine details), initialized from a shared base model. Training uses 800K captioned Objaverse 3D-text pairs (12 views per object) for 120K steps at batch size 2048 on 8 A100 GPUs, with 50% text and 10% image dropout for classifier-free guidance.

## Results

On GSO, TOSS with expert denoisers achieves PSNR 19.70, SSIM 0.8589, LPIPS 0.1131, and KID 0.0027 (160M training images), outperforming Zero123 (PSNR 17.75, SSIM 0.8139, LPIPS 0.1369, KID 0.0046) by ~1.95 dB in PSNR. On RTMV, TOSS reaches PSNR 11.22 and SSIM 0.5823 versus Zero123's 9.58 and 0.4180. For 3D reconstruction, TOSS reduces Chamfer Distance from 0.0757 to 0.0655 on GSO and from 0.1546 to 0.1236 on RTMV while improving Volume IoU in both cases.

## Baselines & Comparisons

TOSS consistently outperforms Zero123 across all four NVS metrics on both GSO and RTMV. On GSO, TOSS with expert denoisers gains +1.95 dB PSNR and +0.045 SSIM over Zero123 at 160M training images, and +1.49 dB PSNR at 250M. On RTMV, the PSNR improvement is even larger: +1.64 dB (160M) and +1.34 dB (250M). For 3D reconstruction (Table 3), TOSS reduces Chamfer Distance by ~13% on GSO (0.0655 vs 0.0757) and ~20% on RTMV (0.1236 vs 0.1546) compared to Zero123, while also improving Volume IoU. Magic123, included only in the 3D reconstruction comparison, performs substantially worse than both Zero123 and TOSS on GSO (CD 0.1508, IoU 0.3220), confirming that the diffusion-based NVS approach is superior to the 3D-diffusion-prior method for single-image reconstruction.

## Relation to Prior Work

TOSS builds directly on Zero123's paradigm of fine-tuning a pre-trained Stable Diffusion model on 3D renderings for zero-shot open-set NVS, but departs from its image-to-image translation framing by introducing text as a first-class semantic conditioning signal. Architecturally, it replaces Zero123's channel concatenation and CLIP cross-attention with a dense cross-attention module operating on VAE feature maps, and adds frequency-encoded camera pose tokens to the same attention mechanism. The expert denoiser strategy draws on eDiff-I's ensemble-of-experts idea, specialized here for pose correctness (high timesteps) and fine details (low timesteps).

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

By adding text as a semantic constraint, TOSS addresses the fundamental under-constrained nature of single-view NVS in a way that grants users explicit controllability over unobserved geometry and texture, which is critical for downstream 3D generation pipelines. The approach is orthogonal to text-to-image model upgrades (demonstrated by swapping SD v1.4 for v1.5), making it a composable component in evolving 3D generation stacks. Key limitations include reliance on synthetic 3D data (distribution shift) and sensitivity to caption quality; open questions include developing NVS-specific captioning models and training on natural images/videos to reduce the synthetic-to-real gap.
