## Problem

Extending the context window of pre-trained LLMs to longer sequences typically requires full-length fine-tuning at the target context size, incurring quadratic computational cost that makes extreme extensions (e.g., 2k→128k) impractical. Existing Position Interpolation methods mitigate out-of-distribution position indices but still mandate training at the full target length, limiting scalability.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ICLR20240760/cropped_page1_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method takes a pre-trained RoPE-based LLM with a fixed context window (e.g., 2,048 tokens) and a target extension length (e.g., 128k). During fine-tuning, the actual input sequence length equals only the original context window, but position indices are manipulated to span the full target range. The model outputs standard next-token predictions, and at inference time it processes sequences up to the target length using the interpolated position encoding.

## Method

PoSE partitions the original context window into N chunks (default N=2) and assigns each chunk a randomly sampled skipping bias term from a discrete uniform distribution, shifting its position indices to cover a wider range of relative positions. Both chunk lengths and bias terms are re-sampled for every training example, ensuring comprehensive coverage of all relative positions within the target context window over training. Within each chunk, position indices remain continuous, closely resembling the pre-training structure to preserve learned language modeling ability. Position interpolation (Linear, NTK, or YaRN) is applied on top of the manipulated indices for stabilized fine-tuning. This decouples the fine-tuning sequence length from the target context length, reducing memory and time overhead to that of the original window size while enabling extension to arbitrarily long contexts.

## Results

On GovReport and Proof-pile, PoSE trained with only a 2k context window achieves perplexity of 4.60 (GovReport) and 2.60 (Proof-pile) at 16k evaluation context, nearly matching Full-length fine-tuning (4.59 and 2.53 respectively). Extending to 32k target, PoSE maintains 4.66 (GovReport) and 2.59 (Proof-pile) perplexity, while the original LLaMA model degrades to >10³ beyond its 2k window. For extreme extension to 128k, PoSE combined with YaRN interpolation achieves 11.33 (PG-19) and 13.81 (Books3) perplexity at 128k evaluation context, demonstrating viable extremely-long context modeling.

## Baselines & Comparisons

PoSE is compared against two baselines: Full-length fine-tuning (Chen et al., 2023a) and RandPos (Ruoss et al., 2023). Against Full-length fine-tuning, PoSE achieves nearly identical perplexity while using only 2k training context instead of 16k/32k: at 16k target, PoSE scores 4.60 vs. 4.59 (GovReport) and 2.60 vs. 2.53 (Proof-pile), a negligible gap. Against RandPos, PoSE dramatically outperforms: at 2k→16k, PoSE achieves 4.60 vs. 15.16 (GovReport) and 2.60 vs. 7.73 (Proof-pile); at 2k→32k, the gap widens to 4.66 vs. 97.57 (GovReport) and 2.59 vs. 66.47 (Proof-pile). The authors attribute PoSE's advantage over RandPos to maintaining continuous position indices within each chunk, which preserves pre-trained language modeling ability. On passkey retrieval, both PoSE-16k and PoSE-32k maintain ≥90% accuracy within their target windows, matching Full-length, while Original, PI-only, and RandPos drop to 0% beyond 2k.

## Relation to Prior Work

PoSE builds directly on RoPE's relative position encoding (Su et al., 2021) and the Position Interpolation (PI) framework introduced by Chen et al. (2023a), which down-scales position indices to stabilize fine-tuning. It departs from the full-length fine-tuning paradigm (Chen et al., 2023a; Peng et al., 2023) by decoupling training length from target length, and from RandPos (Ruoss et al., 2023) by preserving continuity of position indices within each chunk to minimize disruption to pre-trained language modeling. The method is positioned as a training-time technique that is orthogonal to and composable with any PI strategy (Linear, NTK, YaRN) and any RoPE-based architecture.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

PoSE offers a practically transformative efficiency gain: extending LLaMA from 2k to 128k context requires only 2k training sequences on 8 V100 GPUs, compared to the 128 A100 GPUs needed for full-length fine-tuning at 8k. This opens the door to extreme context extension (theoretically unbounded, limited only by inference memory) at a fraction of the cost. Open questions include whether the approach degrades gracefully beyond 128k, how it interacts with other long-context techniques (e.g., sparse attention, memory mechanisms), and whether the 2-chunk design is optimal or whether adaptive chunking could further improve coverage at longer targets.
