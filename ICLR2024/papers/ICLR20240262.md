## Problem

Existing MLLMs that generate images force the language model to produce conditional embeddings aligned to CLIP's intermediate space, which discards modality-specific information and prevents true end-to-end raw image generation. No prior framework simultaneously models both language and image posteriors in a unified autoregressive manner, leaving the potential learning synergy between multimodal comprehension and creation unrealized.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ICLR20240262/cropped_page1_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

DREAMLLM consumes interleaved documents where images are encoded as CLIP-Large patch embeddings projected into the LLM's token space, and text is tokenized as usual. For image generation, the model predicts a special <dream> token at the desired insertion point, then a set of 64 learnable dream queries are causally fed through the MLLM to produce conditional embeddings that drive a frozen Stable Diffusion U-Net decoder to synthesize raw pixel-space images. The synthesized images are fed back into the MLLM as comprehension inputs for subsequent generation, enabling free-form interleaved document output without human-specified image placement.

## Method

DREAMLLM introduces two key mechanisms: (1) dream queries, a set of learnable embeddings that causally query the MLLM to extract holistic semantics for diffusion conditioning, replacing CLIP-alignment objectives with score distillation against a frozen Stable Diffusion model that acts as the score function in pixel space; and (2) Interleaved Generative Pre-Training (I-GPT), which trains the model to generate both text tokens and <dream> tokens over interleaved documents from MMC4, forming all joint, marginal, and conditional multimodal distributions. Training proceeds in three stages: cross-modal alignment of projectors and dream queries on ~30M image-text pairs, I-GPT pretraining on ~4M interleaved documents with the LLM unfrozen, and supervised fine-tuning on ~120K instruction-following samples. The unified MLE objective treats both word tokens and image synthesis as autoregressive predictions, with the image posterior modeled via denoising score matching where the SD parameters remain frozen.

## Results

DREAMLLM-7B achieves 56.6 on VQAv2, 44.3 on OKVQA, 45.8 on VizWiz, and 34.9 on TextVQA for multimodal comprehension, while scoring 49.9 on MMBench and 35.9 on MM-Vet for comprehensive evaluation. For text-to-image generation, DREAMLLM-7B achieves 8.46 FID on MS-COCO and 20.53 FID on LN-COCO, outperforming the Stable Diffusion v2.1 baseline (12.43/34.26) by 3.97 and 13.73 FID respectively. With LLaVA-1.5 SFT data (DREAMLLM-7B*), the model further improves to 72.9 on VQAv2, 58.2 on MMBench, and 36.6 on MM-Vet, setting new state-of-the-art among 7B MLLMs.

## Baselines & Comparisons

DREAMLLM-7B outperforms all comprehension-only MLLMs across every reported benchmark: it surpasses LLaVA-7B by +11.2 on MMBench (49.9 vs 38.7) and +12.1 on MM-Vet (35.9 vs 23.8), and exceeds OpenFlamingo-9B by +45.3 on MMBench (49.9 vs 4.6). Against concurrent MLLMs with image synthesis capability, DREAMLLM-7B achieves +16.6 accuracy over Emu-13B on VQAv2 (56.6 vs 40.0) and +20.5 over CM3Leon-7B on OKVQA (44.3 vs 23.8). For text-to-image generation (Table 2), DREAMLLM-7B achieves 8.46 FID on MS-COCO, beating Emu-13B (11.66) by 3.20 FID, GILL-8B (12.20) by 3.74 FID, and the SDv2.1 baseline (12.43) by 3.97 FID. On LN-COCO, DREAMLLM-7B (20.53) substantially outperforms SDv2.1 (34.26) by 13.73 FID, demonstrating superior long-context processing. The ablation in Table 3 confirms that joint learning (comprehension + creation) yields the best results on both MM-Vet (35.9) and MS-COCO FID (8.46), while adding CLIP alignment loss causes training collapse, validating the paper's design choice to avoid CLIP-space alignment.

## Relation to Prior Work

DREAMLLM builds on the Stable Diffusion latent diffusion model as a frozen score function, CLIP as a visual encoder, and Vicuna/LLaMA as the autoregressive LLM backbone. It departs sharply from concurrent MLLMs (Emu, GILL, CM3Leon) that force the language model to produce CLIP-aligned conditional embeddings for image generation; instead, DREAMLLM introduces learnable 'dream queries' and score distillation to synthesize raw images directly in pixel space, avoiding the modality gap and information loss of CLIP's intermediate representation. The Interleaved Generative Pre-Training (I-GPT) paradigm, which trains on free-form interleaved documents from MMC4, is a novel training objective that unifies all conditional, marginal, and joint multimodal distributions under a single autoregressive MLE objective.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

DREAMLLM is the first MLLM to generate free-form interleaved documents (arbitrary text-image layouts) and demonstrates a measurable learning synergy where image creation improves comprehension and vice versa. The finding that adding CLIP alignment loss causes training collapse (Table 3, line 6) challenges the prevailing assumption that aligning MLLM outputs to CLIP space is always beneficial. Limitations include reliance on a frozen SD decoder (capping image quality), a 7B scale that may not fully realize the synergy at scale, and a human evaluation gap (60.68% vs. 77.24% supportive rate for ground-truth documents) indicating generated content is still clearly distinguishable. Open questions include whether the synergy scales to larger models, whether the approach extends to video or 3D, and whether the dream-query mechanism can replace the need for a separate diffusion decoder entirely.
