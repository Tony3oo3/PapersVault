## Problem

While recent work has achieved horizon-free regret bounds for episodic RL with fixed or stochastic rewards, it remains open whether such results extend to adversarial RL where the reward is chosen by the environment each episode. This paper asks whether near-optimal horizon-free algorithms can be designed for adversarial linear mixture MDPs with unknown transition and function approximation, and answers affirmatively.

## Input / Output

The algorithm operates in a full-information feedback setting where the agent observes the entire adversarial reward function r^k(·,·) at the end of each episode (with per-step rewards bounded by 1/H), along with state transitions from a linear mixture MDP parameterized by a known feature mapping φ: S×A×S→R^d. It outputs a stochastic policy π^k for the next episode, an occupancy measure z^k over state-action-next-state triples, and a weighted least-squares estimate θ̂ of the unknown transition parameter θ*. The 1/H reward scaling is critical: it offsets the H-dependence that would otherwise arise from reward accumulation over the horizon.

## Method

HF-O²PS replaces direct policy optimization with online mirror descent on occupancy measures, which decouples the gradient magnitude from the Q-function (and hence from H) and yields a horizon-free OMD regret term. For transition learning, it employs a variance-uncertainty-aware value-targeted regression (VTR) estimator with a high-order moment estimator (HOME) adapted to stochastic policies, recursively estimating 2^m-th moments of the value function to construct tighter Bernstein-type confidence sets. The occupancy measure update is constrained to a feasible set D_k ensuring the induced transition lies within the confidence set C_k, combining the optimism-in-face-of-uncertainty principle with the occupancy-measure framework of Rosenberg & Mansour (2019). The key technical insight for the analysis is a recursive bounding scheme that treats transition noise and policy noise as a single martingale, allowing the total variance to be controlled by higher-order transition noise without introducing √(KH) terms.

## Results

HF-O²PS achieves a regret bound of Õ((d + log(|S|²|A|))√K + d²) for adversarial linear mixture MDPs with full-information feedback (Theorem 5.1), which is the first horizon-free regret guarantee in this setting. The bound is near-optimal, matching the minimax lower bound of Ω̃(d√K) (Theorem 5.5) up to logarithmic factors. A separate lower bound (Theorem 5.3) shows that without a finite state space assumption, any algorithm incurs at least Ω(√(HK log|A|)) regret, justifying the log|S| term in the upper bound.

## Baselines & Comparisons

HF-O²PS is the first algorithm to achieve horizon-free regret in the adversarial linear mixture MDP setting. Compared to the two prior adversarial methods, OPPO (Cai et al., 2020) with regret Õ(dH²√K) and POWERS (He et al., 2022b) with regret Õ(d√(H³K)), HF-O²PS eliminates all polynomial dependence on H, yielding Õ(d√K + d²) (up to log|S| factors). Against the non-adversarial horizon-free baselines VARLin2 (Kim et al., 2022) and HF-UCRL-VTR+ (Zhou & Gu, 2022), which also achieve Õ(d√K + d²), HF-O²PS matches their regret rate while additionally handling adversarially chosen rewards per episode. The earlier VARLin (Zhang et al., 2021b) has a substantially worse dependence on d (d^4.5√K + d^9 vs. d√K + d²). The minimax lower bound of Ω̃(d√K) confirms that HF-O²PS is near-optimal in its dependence on both d and K.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper sits at the intersection of two research threads: horizon-free RL (Zhang et al. 2021a,b; Zhou & Gu 2022) and adversarial RL with linear function approximation (Cai et al. 2020; He et al. 2022b). It departs from prior adversarial RL methods (OPPO, POWERS) that use direct policy optimization via value-function-based mirror descent, which inherently introduces polynomial H-dependence. Instead, it adopts the occupancy-measure-based OMD framework from Jin et al. (2020a) and Rosenberg & Mansour (2019a), combined with the high-order moment estimator from Zhou & Gu (2022), adapted here to stochastic policies. The key conceptual departure is that occupancy measures allow the gradient signal to be the per-step reward r^k (bounded by 1/H) rather than the Q-value (bounded by 1), which is what eliminates the H-dependence.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

This is the first algorithm to achieve horizon-free regret in adversarial linear mixture MDPs, closing a gap between the fixed-reward horizon-free literature and the adversarial RL literature. The hardness results (Theorem 5.3, 5.5) establish that the log|S| term is unavoidable and that the d√K scaling is minimax-optimal, giving a complete picture of the problem's difficulty. A key limitation is the requirement that individual rewards be bounded by 1/H (Assumption 3.1), which is stronger than the total-reward-bounded-by-1 assumption used in some horizon-free tabular results; extending to the weaker assumption is left as future work. The result also requires full-information feedback, leaving the bandit-feedback adversarial horizon-free setting open.
