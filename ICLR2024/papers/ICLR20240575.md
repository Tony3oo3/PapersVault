## Problem

Existing KG-based LLM reasoning methods treat knowledge graphs merely as factual knowledge bases for retrieval, overlooking the structural information (relation paths) that encodes how entities are connected. This structural blindness, combined with LLMs' inherent hallucinations and stale knowledge, leads to unfaithful and uninterpretable reasoning chains that fail in high-stakes applications.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ICLR20240575/cropped_page1_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method takes a natural language question (with entities pre-linked to KG nodes) and a knowledge graph (Freebase subgraph with ~2.5M entities and 7K relations) as input. It produces three outputs: (1) a set of top-K relation paths (sequences of relation names delimited by special tokens <PATH>/<SEP>/</PATH>) serving as faithful plans, (2) concrete reasoning paths (entity→relation→entity chains retrieved via constrained BFS from the KG), and (3) a final answer with step-by-step interpretable explanations grounded in those reasoning paths. The planning module is designed to be plug-and-play, meaning it can generate plans for any arbitrary LLM at inference time without retraining that LLM.

## Method

RoG introduces a planning-retrieval-reasoning framework where relation paths (sequences of relations, not entities) serve as faithful reasoning plans, motivated by the observation that KG relations are more stable than dynamically updated entities. The planning module is trained via KL-divergence minimization against a uniform posterior over shortest valid relation paths in the KG, effectively distilling structural KG knowledge into the LLM. The retrieval module executes each plan via constrained breadth-first search from question entities, and the reasoning module follows a FiD-style architecture to fuse multiple retrieved reasoning paths and generate answers. Training jointly optimizes both modules through an ELBO objective with two instruction-tuning tasks (planning and retrieval-reasoning) on LLaMA2-Chat-7B, and the resulting planning module can be seamlessly attached to other LLMs (ChatGPT, Alpaca, Flan-T5) at inference to boost their KGQA performance by 8–119% without any retraining.

## Results

RoG achieves state-of-the-art Hits@1 of 85.7 on WebQSP and 62.6 on CWQ, outperforming the previous best methods DECAF (82.1 Hits@1 on WebQSP) and UniKGQA (51.2 Hits@1 on CWQ) by 4.4% and 22.3% respectively. On CWQ, RoG also improves F1 by 14.4% over UniKGQA (56.2 vs 49.1). However, on WebQSP F1, RoG (70.8) trails DECAF (78.8), suggesting RoG's advantage is most pronounced on the more challenging multi-hop CWQ benchmark.

## Baselines & Comparisons

RoG outperforms all 19 baselines on Hits@1 across both datasets. On WebQSP, RoG (85.7 Hits@1) surpasses the previous SOTA DECAF (82.1) by 4.4 points and UniKGQA (77.2) by 8.5 points; however, RoG's F1 (70.8) is lower than DECAF's (78.8) and UniKGQA's (72.2), indicating a precision-over-recall trade-off. On the more challenging CWQ dataset (up to 4-hop), RoG dominates: it beats UniKGQA by 22.3% in Hits@1 (62.6 vs 51.2) and 14.4% in F1 (56.2 vs 49.1), and outperforms KD-CoT by 6.9% in Hits@1 (62.6 vs 55.7). Among pure LLM baselines, RoG exceeds ChatGPT+CoT by 10.1% Hits@1 on WebQSP (85.7 vs 75.6) and 13.7% on CWQ (62.6 vs 48.9). Retrieval-augmented methods (SR+NSM+E2E: 69.5 Hits@1 on WebQSP) and semantic parsing methods (QGG: 73.0 Hits@1) are consistently outperformed, confirming that the planning-retrieval-reasoning framework with KG-grounded relation paths provides a meaningful advantage over both subgraph retrieval and logical query generation approaches.

## Relation to Prior Work

RoG positions itself at the intersection of LLM reasoning (plan-and-solve, chain-of-thought, ReACT) and KGQA, departing from both semantic parsing methods (which generate brittle logical queries) and retrieval-augmented methods (which treat KGs as flat factual stores). Its core conceptual move is using relation paths as structurally grounded 'faithful plans' that are executable on the KG, rather than free-text reasoning steps or full SPARQL queries. The retrieval-reasoning module directly adopts the FiD multi-document fusion architecture (Izacard & Grave, 2021; Singh et al., 2021), and the overall optimization is framed via an ELBO decomposition into a KL-divergence planning loss and an expectation-maximizing reasoning loss, borrowing the variational inference perspective from prior KG reasoning work.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The plug-and-play planning module is the most practically impactful contribution: it can be attached to any off-the-shelf LLM at inference time to substantially improve KGQA performance without retraining, which lowers the barrier to faithful KG reasoning. However, the SOTA claim is primarily on Hits@1; on WebQSP, RoG's F1 (70.8) trails DECAF (78.8), suggesting the method excels at identifying the top answer but struggles with multi-answer coverage. Open questions include scalability to much larger or temporal KGs, handling of questions where no valid relation path exists in the KG, and whether the instruction-tuning data construction (shortest paths between question and answer entities) generalizes beyond the Freebase schema.
