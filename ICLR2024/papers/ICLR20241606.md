## Problem

Existing VL-NLE methods suffer from two underexplored failure modes: implausibility (explanations irrelevant to the question or contradicting established facts) and hallucination (explanations not grounded in the visual input). While these issues have been studied in text-only NLE, they remain largely unaddressed in vision-language settings, where explanations must be simultaneously factually supported and visually faithful.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ICLR20241606/cropped_page1_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method takes an RGB image and a textual question as input and produces a predicted answer concatenated with a natural language explanation as output. A key intermediate is a rationale string generated on-the-fly by a dedicated rationale generator, which is concatenated with the image-question pair as a prompt to the reasoning module. No ground-truth rationales are available during training; only answer-explanation pairs are provided as supervision.

## Method

Rapper is a two-stage reinforced rationale-prompted paradigm. Stage A (Knowledge Distillation) elicits pseudo rationales from LLaMA-65B via few-shot in-context learning, filters them using a UnifiedQA model with a token-level F1 threshold, then trains a rationale generator G via cross-entropy distillation and a reasoning module M via supervised loss conditioned on the filtered rationales. Stage B (RLNF) applies PPO-based reinforcement learning to G, where the reward combines the predicted probability of the ground-truth answer (enforcing visual faithfulness) and CIDEr score of the explanation (maintaining plausibility), regularized by a KL penalty against the post-distillation policy to prevent drift. At inference, G generates a rationale on-the-fly via beam search, which is concatenated with the image-question pair to prompt M for the final answer-explanation sequence.

## Results

On VQA-X (filtered setting), Rapper achieves 124.0 CIDEr, 24.5 SPICE, and 87.25% VQA accuracy, surpassing the previous best S3C (116.7 CIDEr, 23.0 SPICE, 85.6% accuracy) by 7.3 CIDEr and 1.65% accuracy. On e-SNLI-VE, Rapper reaches 128.6 CIDEr, 34.9 SPICE, and 75.73% accuracy, outperforming NLX-GPT (117.4 CIDEr, 33.6 SPICE, 73.91% accuracy) by 11.2 CIDEr. In terms of faithfulness, Rapper attains a RefCLIPScore of 67.05 on VQA-X (vs. 65.09 for S3C and 64.06 for NLX-GPT), and human evaluation confirms higher plausibility (0.845) and faithfulness (0.859) scores.

## Baselines & Comparisons

Rapper outperforms all six baselines across every NLE metric on both VQA-X and e-SNLI-VE. Against the two most recent SOTA methods, Rapper improves CIDEr by 7.3 over S3C (124.0 vs. 116.7) and by 13.4 over NLX-GPT (124.0 vs. 110.6) on VQA-X, while on e-SNLI-VE it gains 11.2 CIDEr over NLX-GPT (128.6 vs. 117.4). In VQA accuracy, Rapper leads S3C by 1.65 points (87.25% vs. 85.6%) on VQA-X and NLX-GPT by 1.82 points (75.73% vs. 73.91%) on e-SNLI-VE. For faithfulness (RefCLIPScore on VQA-X), Rapper scores 67.05, exceeding S3C (65.09) and NLX-GPT (64.06), confirming reduced hallucination. Human evaluation further validates these gains: Rapper achieves 0.845 plausibility and 0.859 faithfulness, compared to 0.797/0.811 for S3C and 0.771/0.795 for NLX-GPT. The older predict-then-explain baselines (PJ-X, FME, RVT, e-UG) trail Rapper by 30–70 CIDEr points, reflecting the advantage of the rationale-prompted paradigm over separate answer/explanation generation.

## Relation to Prior Work

Rapper builds on the VL-NLE line of work (Park et al. 2018, Kayser et al. 2021, NLX-GPT, S3C) by introducing a rationale-prompting intermediary between the visual input and the answer-explanation generation, drawing on the chain-of-thought and generated-knowledge prompting literature (Wei et al. 2022, Liu et al. 2022b). It departs from prior VL-NLE methods that either predict-then-explain or jointly generate answer and explanation without an explicit rationale, by learning a dedicated rationale generator G through two distinct mechanisms: knowledge distillation from LLaMA-65B (for plausibility) and a novel RLNF stage using PPO with answer-probability and CIDEr rewards (for faithfulness). The RLNF component is conceptually related to RAINIER (Liu et al. 2022a) and self-critical sequence training (Rennie et al. 2017), but is the first to use NLE-specific feedback signals to shape rationale generation in a multimodal setting.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

Rapper provides a principled two-stage recipe for addressing the two underexplored failure modes in VL-NLE—implausibility (irrelevant explanations) and hallucination (visually unsupported claims)—that prior joint-generation methods like NLX-GPT and S3C do not explicitly target. The RLNF mechanism, which uses the VQA answer probability as a reward to push the rationale generator toward visually grounded content, is a reusable technique that could transfer to other explanation-generating tasks. Limitations include reliance on a 65B-parameter LLM for distillation (high compute cost), a manually tuned filtering threshold (τ=0.54), and evaluation on only two relatively small benchmarks (VQA-X ~32K, e-SNLI-VE ~43K), leaving open whether the approach scales to larger, more diverse VL tasks or to settings without ground-truth answer-explanation pairs.
