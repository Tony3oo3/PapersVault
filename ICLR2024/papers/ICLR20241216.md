## Problem

Existing 3D-aware image synthesis methods assume a shared canonical coordinate system, requiring posed images or learned camera distributions that are unavailable or ill-defined for in-the-wild datasets. When adapted to view space, GAN-based approaches produce flat geometry and suffer from severe mode collapse, limiting distribution coverage on diverse data.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ICLR20241216/cropped_page1_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The autoencoder takes a single unposed 256×256 RGB image concatenated channel-wise with a monocular depth estimate (from pretrained ZoeDepth) and compresses it into a 32×32×4 latent code. The decoder renders from a fixed input camera pose P₀ and samples novel views uniformly from ±35° azimuth and ±15° polar offsets. The diffusion model optionally accepts a class label via cross-attention with 10% dropout for classifier-free guidance, and outputs a latent code that decodes to a triplane representation from which arbitrary novel views and geometry can be rendered at 256×256 resolution.

## Method

WildFusion is a two-stage 3D-aware latent diffusion model. Stage 1 trains an autoencoder where an FPN encoder maps the image-depth pair to a compressed latent, and a decoder (transformer blocks + CNN) predicts a triplane feature field rendered via volume rendering with disparity sampling and a contraction function for unbounded scenes. The autoencoder is trained with reconstruction losses (pixel, VGG perceptual, 2D/3D depth) on the input view and a dual discriminator for adversarial supervision of novel views, plus a separate depth discriminator—no multiview or explicit 3D supervision is needed. Stage 2 trains a standard 2D U-Net denoiser (LDM architecture) on the 3D-aware latent space using the v-prediction objective with a cosine noise schedule, sampled via DDIM (200 steps). The key architectural insight is that the latent space is a spatial 2D feature grid, so the diffusion model follows the exact LDM training recipe while the decoder's triplane output provides 3D consistency for free.

## Results

On the SDIP datasets, WildFusion achieves FID of 12.2 / 2.9 / 4.3 (Dogs / Elephants / Horses) with non-flatness scores (NFS) of 31.7 / 32.2 / 28.8, outperforming all view-space GAN baselines on image quality, geometry, and diversity. On class-conditional ImageNet (guidance scale s=2), WildFusion reaches FID=35.4, NFS=33.8, Precision=0.59, and Recall=0.20, substantially exceeding GAN-based baselines on geometry and sample diversity. The autoencoder achieves LPIPS of 0.21–0.28 and nvFID of 9.0–17.4 with a reconstruction time of 0.04 s, compared to >100 s for GAN-inversion-based baselines.

## Baselines & Comparisons

Against EG3D* (adapted to view space), WildFusion wins on every metric across all three SDIP datasets: FID improves from 16.3→12.2 (Dogs), 3.0→2.9 (Elephants), 6.7→4.3 (Horses); NFS jumps from 11.8→31.7 (Dogs) and 13.3→32.2 (Elephants); Recall increases from 0.29→0.38 (Dogs) and 0.31→0.34 (Elephants). Adding a depth discriminator to EG3D* narrows the gap but WildFusion still leads on FID, NFS, and Recall. Against StyleNeRF, WildFusion achieves lower FID (12.2 vs 12.3 on Dogs, 2.9 vs 10.0 on Elephants, 4.3 vs 4.5 on Horses) and higher NFS and Recall. Against 3DGP, the comparison is mixed: 3DGP achieves lower FID on Dogs (5.9 vs 12.2) and Elephants (3.7 vs 2.9), but WildFusion consistently outperforms on Recall (0.38 vs 0.38 Dogs, 0.34 vs 0.22 Elephants, 0.37 vs 0.28 Horses) and is competitive on NFS. On ImageNet, WildFusion (s=2) achieves NFS=33.8 vs 18.5 for 3DGP and Recall=0.20 vs 0.02, while 3DGP's lower FID (19.7 vs 35.4) is attributed to severe mode collapse that FID fails to capture.

## Relation to Prior Work

WildFusion builds on the two-stage LDM framework (Rombach et al., 2021) and EG3D's triplane representation with dual discriminator (Chan et al., 2022), but departs fundamentally from the GAN-based paradigm that dominates 3D-aware image synthesis (3DGP, POF3D, StyleNeRF). The key conceptual shift is modeling instances in view space rather than canonical space, which eliminates the need for posed images or learned camera pose distributions that prior methods require. Additionally, the autoencoder leverages monocular depth cues (ZoeDepth) and adversarial supervision for novel views without any multiview or explicit 3D geometry supervision, distinguishing it from prior single-image-to-3D methods that rely on GAN inversion or multiview training.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

By removing the dependency on pose estimation and canonical coordinate systems, WildFusion makes 3D-aware image synthesis applicable to truly in-the-wild, unaligned datasets—a major practical bottleneck for prior methods. The demonstration that diffusion models avoid the severe mode collapse observed in GAN-based 3D-aware generators (e.g., 3DGP on ImageNet) suggests a more robust and scalable path forward. However, the ImageNet results are compute-limited (batch size 256 vs. >1000 typical for 2D LDMs), so the full potential of the approach remains untested. Future work on scaling to text-conditional generation and larger compute budgets could significantly broaden the impact.
