## Problem

Transformer LLMs using Rotary Position Embeddings (RoPE) cannot generalize to sequence lengths beyond their pre-trained context window, and existing interpolation methods (Position Interpolation, NTK-aware) either lose high-frequency positional information or require empirical tuning of interpolation parameters. The paper addresses the need for a principled, compute-efficient method that extends context windows with far fewer tokens and training steps than prior approaches.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ICLR20241257/cropped_page1_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method takes a pre-trained RoPE-based LLM (LLaMA, Llama 2, GPT-NeoX, or PaLM families) and modifies only the RoPE frequency computation, leaving the architecture unchanged. Fine-tuning uses the PG19 dataset chunked into 64k-token segments with BOS/EOS bookends; notably, the s=32 (128k) model is trained on the same 64k data as the s=16 (64k) model yet extrapolates to 128k context. Evaluation uses sliding-window perplexity (S=256) on 128k Proof-pile and GovReport documents, passkey retrieval at various prompt lengths, and standard Hugging Face Open LLM benchmarks (ARC-c, HellaSwag, MMLU, TruthfulQA).

## Method

YaRN combines two mechanisms: (1) NTK-by-parts interpolation, which applies a ramp function γ(r) over the ratio r = L/λ_d between the pre-trained context length L and each RoPE dimension's wavelength λ_d — dimensions with short wavelengths (r > β, encoding relative position) are left uninterpolated, dimensions with long wavelengths (r < α, encoding absolute position) are fully interpolated by the scale factor s, and intermediate dimensions receive a linear blend, with recommended α=1, β=32 for LLaMA; and (2) a pre-softmax attention temperature t, implemented as a zero-overhead scaling of the complex RoPE rotation matrices by √(1/t) where √(1/t) = 0.1·ln(s) + 1, which counteracts the entropy increase from longer contexts. This design requires no architectural changes, is compatible with Flash Attention 2, and converges in ~400 fine-tuning steps (128 A100 GPU-hours for 7B), achieving 10× fewer tokens and 2.5× fewer steps than prior methods while supporting both fine-tuned extension and inference-time Dynamic Scaling for up to 2× extension without any fine-tuning.

## Results

YaRN extends Llama 2 7B and 13B from 4k to 128k context using only 400+200 fine-tuning steps on 64k-token PG19 chunks. On 128k Proof-pile documents, the 7B model achieves 2.37 sliding-window perplexity and the 13B model achieves 2.24, while passkey retrieval accuracy reaches 99.4% at 128k context. On standardized benchmarks (ARC-c, HellaSwag, MMLU, TruthfulQA), YaRN-extended models show less than 1% degradation relative to their Llama 2 baselines. The total training cost is 256 A100 GPU-hours for the 128k 7B model, compared to 64,000 GPU-hours for the NTK-aware approach of Xiong et al. (2023).

## Baselines & Comparisons

Against Position Interpolation (PI, Chen et al. 2023), YaRN dramatically outperforms at scale factors ≥8: at s=8, PI's perplexity exceeds 10¹ while YaRN achieves 3.33 at 16k context; at s=16, PI is completely broken (>10²) while YaRN reaches 3.45 at 32k. Against NTK-aware interpolation (bloc97 2023a), YaRN is superior at all scale factors, with the gap widening as s increases (e.g., at s=16, NTK-aware hits >10¹ at 32k while YaRN achieves 3.45). Against NTK-by-parts (bloc97 2023b), YaRN's additional attention-temperature scaling provides consistent small gains (e.g., 2.77 vs 2.81 at 32k fine-tuned, s=16). Against the published Together.ai 32k model (PI), YaRN s=16 7B achieves 2.42 perplexity at 64k context versus Together's >10², and against Code Llama 100k (NTK-aware), YaRN s=32 7B achieves 2.37 at 128k versus Code Llama's 2.71. In terms of training efficiency, YaRN matches LLongMA-2 7B (PI) perplexity (3.35 vs 3.34 at 8k) using only 400 steps versus PI's 1000 steps, and requires 256 A100 GPU-hours versus 64,000 for Xiong et al.'s NTK-aware 50k extension—a 250× reduction.

## Relation to Prior Work

YaRN sits at the confluence of several community-developed RoPE interpolation methods: Position Interpolation (PI) provided the initial framework of scaling position indices, NTK-aware interpolation (bloc97) introduced the insight that high-frequency dimensions should be scaled less to preserve local positional information, and NTK-by-parts (bloc97) formalized this into a wavelength-based piecewise strategy. YaRN's primary conceptual addition is a temperature scaling on the attention logits (Eq. 14), which is implemented as a zero-overhead rescaling of the rotary embeddings themselves, making it compatible with Flash Attention 2. The paper also formalizes and unifies the previously informal 'Dynamic NTK' inference-time scaling, presenting a complete lineage from PI through NTK-aware, NTK-by-parts, and finally YaRN as a single coherent method.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

YaRN's practical significance lies in its dramatic reduction in fine-tuning cost (10x fewer tokens, 2.5x fewer steps) while achieving the first successful 128k context extension of Llama 2, all with zero additional inference overhead and Flash Attention 2 compatibility. The 'train short, test long' result—where a model fine-tuned on 64k data extrapolates to 128k—suggests that the interpolation geometry generalizes beyond the training distribution, which is important for compute-constrained practitioners. Open questions include whether the temperature formula (Eq. 15) truly generalizes beyond the LLaMA/Llama 2 family, whether the method extends to non-RoPE architectures, and whether the observed extrapolation holds for tasks beyond perplexity and passkey retrieval.
