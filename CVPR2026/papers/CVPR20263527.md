## Problem

Dense 3D shape correspondence must simultaneously generalize across pose, topology, and domain while remaining computationally efficient, yet existing paradigms force a trade-off: deformation-based and functional-map methods require per-pair optimization or post-refinement, 2D-vision-model approaches suffer from occlusion and high inference cost, and hybrid systems still depend on pre-alignment or candidate selection. No prior method achieves both strong inter-category generalization and near real-time inference in a single feed-forward pass without compensatory alignment or optimization steps.

## Input / Output

The method takes a pair of unaligned 3D meshes (potentially with different topology, resolution, or domain) as input and produces dense per-vertex correspondences via nearest-neighbor search in a learned descriptor space. No pre-alignment, pairwise optimization, or post-refinement is required; inference is a single forward pass yielding normalized per-vertex descriptor vectors, from which correspondences are retrieved by cosine similarity. The framework is topology-invariant, operating on remeshed variants without requiring matching vertex counts or connectivity.

## Method

SGSoft constructs a geodesic correspondence field on a canonical SMPL template by lifting hard anchor pairs into continuous Gaussian-kernel fields over geodesic distance, providing topology-invariant and remeshing-robust supervision that replaces brittle hard anchors. A multimodal descriptor is initialized from Uni3D pretrained semantic features and refined through geodesic grouping (FPS-based patches with geodesic-weighted interpolation), global geodesic encoding (pairwise geodesic distance matrix processed by a transformer encoder), and cross-modal fusion of semantic and geometric patch embeddings. Training combines three objectives: a geodesic-correspondence-field-weighted InfoNCE contrastive loss that smooths the embedding space along the intrinsic surface manifold, a part classification loss for coarse semantic alignment, and a symmetry-aware loss that penalizes descriptor similarity across bilaterally mirrored regions. At inference, both meshes are mapped to the shared intrinsic template space in one forward pass, and dense correspondences are retrieved by argmax cosine similarity between normalized per-vertex descriptors, achieving 1.7 s per pair on an RTX A6000.

## Results

SGSoft reports mean geodesic errors of 2.5, 2.9, and 4.0 on FAUST, SCAPE, and SHREC19 respectively, matching the accuracy of the strongest refinement-based methods (DenoisingFM, DiffuMatch) while operating in a purely feed-forward manner. On zero-shot cross-domain benchmarks, SGSoft achieves 8.1 (DT4D-Intra) and 8.3 (DT4D-Inter), substantially outperforming DenoisingFM (5.5/16.8) and NJF (43.4/32.8) on the inter-category setting. Inference time is 1.7 seconds per shape pair, more than an order of magnitude faster than all baselines (4.2–628.9 s), making it the only method capable of near real-time correspondence estimation.

## Baselines & Comparisons

On near-isometric benchmarks (FAUST, SCAPE, SHREC19), SGSoft (2.5/2.9/4.0) is within 0.6 geodesic error of the best hybrid methods DenoisingFM (1.9/2.4/4.2) and DiffuMatch (1.9/4.4/3.9), while being 20–80× faster. On DT4D-Inter (cross-category), SGSoft (8.3) clearly outperforms DenoisingFM (16.8) by 8.5 error and ties with DiffuMatch (8.6), while NJF (32.8) and Diff3f (30.3) degrade severely. On DT4D-Intra, SGSoft (8.1) trails DiffuMatch (1.8) and DenoisingFM (5.5), which benefit from per-pair optimization and diffusion priors respectively. Against the deformation-based NJF baseline, SGSoft wins on every benchmark by a wide margin (e.g., 43.4→8.1 on DT4D-Intra). The 2D-distillation baseline Diff3f is consistently the weakest in accuracy (16.3–30.3) and the slowest (628.9 s), confirming the limitations of multi-view rendering pipelines for dense correspondence.

## Relation to Prior Work

SGSoft sits at the intersection of template-guided supervision (SMPL-based pseudo-ground-truth), 3D foundation model semantic priors (Uni3D), and contrastive descriptor learning (InfoNCE), but departs from all three in a specific way: it replaces discrete hard anchors with a continuous geodesic correspondence field defined on the canonical template, and uses that field to weight the contrastive objective rather than treating it as a separate geometric module. Prior hybrid methods (DenoisingFM, DiffuMatch, Diff3f) combine geometric and semantic signals but still require per-pair optimization, multi-view rendering, or post-refinement; SGSoft instead fuses semantic, geometric, and spatial cues into a single feed-forward descriptor trained under a unified geodesic supervision signal, eliminating all compensatory alignment steps.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The practical impact is significant: a single forward pass at 1.7 s per pair, with no pre-alignment, optimization, or refinement, makes dense correspondence viable for large-scale shape repositories and interactive applications where iterative methods are infeasible. The paper also highlights an open tension in 3D foundation models—semantic generalization versus geometric specificity—that SGSoft partially resolves but does not fully close, leaving room for future work on whether the geodesic field formulation generalizes beyond human-centric templates to fully category-agnostic settings. A key limitation is that the template-based supervision still relies on SMPL parameterization, which constrains applicability to human-like topologies despite the cross-domain evaluation on DT4D.
