## Problem

Existing ViT-based pose estimators like ViTPose process each video frame independently, ignoring temporal coherence and producing unstable predictions under motion blur, occlusion, or defocus. Prior attempts to add temporal modeling to ViTPose rely on heavy auxiliary modules (Transformer or Mamba-based) for multi-frame fusion, complicating the pipeline and deviating from the plain ViT design. This paper asks whether temporal modeling can be embedded directly into the ViTPose framework in a plug-and-play manner while preserving its architectural simplicity and lightweight decoding.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/CVPR20260577/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method consumes a person-specific video clip of 2T+1 frames (default T=2, i.e., 5 frames total), where each frame is a person crop obtained by expanding the Faster R-CNN bounding box by 25% and applying it across the sequence. Only the current frame's pose is predicted: the output is a set of N=15 per-keypoint heatmaps at the ViT feature resolution, upsampled by the original two-layer deconvolution decoder. Auxiliary frames contribute features but do not produce their own final heatmaps.

## Method

TAR-ViTPose appends a temporal modeling module between the ViT encoder and the original lightweight decoder, consisting of two components. Joint-centric Temporal Aggregation (JTA) assigns each of the N keypoints a learnable query token and stacks six layers of masked cross-attention (feature-to-joint) followed by self-attention (joint-to-joint), where spatial masks derived from decoded heatmaps (threshold φ=0.2) restrict each query to attend only to its corresponding keypoint region across all frames, enforcing joint-level temporal alignment. Global Restoring Attention (GRA) then performs a single cross-attention with the current frame's feature tokens as queries and the updated joint query tokens as keys/values, injecting the aggregated temporal cues back into the spatial feature map while preserving global context. The entire pipeline is trained end-to-end with L2 heatmap loss, and the ViT encoder and decoder are initialized from COCO-pretrained ViTPose weights while the temporal modules are trained from scratch.

## Results

On PoseTrack2017 val, TAR-ViTPose with a ViT-H backbone achieves 86.8 mAP using detector-predicted bounding boxes, surpassing the single-frame ViTPose baseline by +2.1 mAP and the previous SOTA DSTA (ViT-H) by +1.2 mAP. With ground-truth bounding boxes, the method reaches 90.3 mAP, outperforming Poseidon (88.9 mAP) by +1.4 mAP. On PoseTrack2018 and PoseTrack21, the method achieves 84.2 and 84.1 mAP respectively (detector boxes), and 89.8 and 91.0 mAP with ground-truth boxes. The ViT-S variant runs at 413 FPS on an A6000 GPU, substantially faster than PoseWarper (52 FPS) and DCPose (128 FPS).

## Baselines & Comparisons

Against the single-frame ViTPose baseline, TAR-ViTPose consistently gains +1.8 to +2.3 mAP across ViT-S/B/L/H backbones, with the largest per-joint improvements on ankles (+3.4 to +3.8 mAP) and wrists (+2.1 to +2.6 mAP). Among detector-box methods, TAR-ViTPose (ViT-H, 86.8 mAP) outperforms DSTA (ViT-H, 85.6 mAP) by +1.2 mAP, FAMI-Pose (84.8 mAP) by +2.0 mAP, and DCPose (82.8 mAP) by +4.0 mAP. In the ground-truth-box setting, TAR-ViTPose (ViT-H, 90.3 mAP) surpasses Poseidon (88.9 mAP) by +1.4 mAP, GLSMamba (88.0 mAP) by +2.3 mAP, and CM-Pose (87.5 mAP) by +2.8 mAP. The ViT-S variant (81.9 mAP) already outperforms the much larger HRNet-W48-based DCPose (82.8 mAP) by a narrow margin while running at 413 FPS versus 128 FPS. Overall, TAR-ViTPose sets new SOTA on all three PoseTrack benchmarks (2017, 2018, 2021) under both detector-box and ground-truth-box protocols.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends ViTPose [41]—a plain ViT-based single-frame pose estimator—by appending a lightweight temporal modeling module (JTA + GRA) after the ViT encoder, preserving the original architecture and decoder. It departs from prior video-based methods (DSTA, CM-Pose, GLSMamba, Poseidon) that treat ViTPose as a frozen feature extractor and bolt on heavy Transformer or Mamba-based temporal fusion pipelines with dedicated decoders. The key conceptual shift is joint-specific temporal alignment via learnable query tokens with mask-aware attention, rather than treating all spatial tokens uniformly during cross-frame aggregation.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The plug-and-play design means any existing ViTPose-based system can gain temporal robustness without architectural redesign, and the 413 FPS (ViT-S) result makes video pose estimation practical for real-time deployment. The ablation showing a 13.7 mAP drop when skipping GRA highlights that global spatial context is irreplaceable for keypoint localization even when temporal cues are available. Open questions include whether the approach generalizes to 3D pose, longer temporal spans, or occlusion-heavy scenarios beyond the PoseTrack benchmarks, and whether the reliance on a separate human detector limits end-to-end applicability.
