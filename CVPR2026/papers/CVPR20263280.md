## Problem

VLM-based long-term action recognition methods rely on statistical correlations rather than causal mechanisms, making them vulnerable to three confounding effects: co-occurrence hallucination (text embeddings aligning with non-causal visual features), codependency illusion (misinterpretation of inter-action dependencies), and persistent visual confounders (background, attire) that weaken textual supervision. Existing approaches either use explicit cross-modal interaction (sensitive to hallucination and illusion) or implicit interaction (sensitive to visual confounders), without explicitly modeling and intervening on the underlying causal structure.

## Input / Output

The method consumes video clips (averaging 2.3–2.36 minutes on Breakfast/COIN, 30 seconds on Charades) alongside action label text, processing them through a frozen CLIP VLM backbone and a TimeSformer video encoder. It produces multi-label atomic action class predictions, evaluated via both mAP (capturing concurrent spatiotemporal dependencies among atomic actions) and single-label accuracy. The model operates without pre-trimmed video supervision, handling complete videos with multiple overlapping actions, and requires no LLM at inference time, keeping FLOPs at 650G and parameters at 211M.

## Method

PCMCI applies three progressive causal interventions grounded in a Structural Causal Model: (1) ICH uses optimal transport to identify disproportionately aligned cross-modal features as a surrogate for the hallucination confounder, then performs back-door adjustment via Normalized Weighted Geometric Mean to obtain deconfounded text embeddings; (2) ICI employs K relational basis functions to model latent action codependencies and applies a second back-door adjustment to eliminate the codependency illusion; (3) IVC uses the fully deconfounded text embeddings as a mediator for cross-modal front-door adjustment, where a conditional transformation operator refines visual features by integrating spatio-temporal dependencies conditioned on the mediator, thereby blocking the visual confounder's back-door path. The progressive ordering is critical: cross-modal spurious correlations and textual codependencies must be removed before the front-door adjustment, since the mediator must be d-separated from visual confounders to validly transmit the causal effect from video to prediction.

## Results

On Breakfast, PCMCI achieves 97.46% accuracy and 90.51% mAP, setting new state-of-the-art on both metrics. On COIN, it reaches 94.53% accuracy and 86.54% mAP, with the mAP representing a 16.4-point improvement over the previous best (HierarQ, 70.10%). On Charades, PCMCI attains 53.3% mAP without pre-trimmed video training, surpassing all non-pre-trimmed baselines and even several methods that rely on pre-trimmed supervision. Notably, PCMCI achieves these results with only 650 GFLOPs and 211M parameters, roughly 58× fewer FLOPs than HierarQ (37,877 GFLOPs).

## Baselines & Comparisons

PCMCI outperforms all VLM-based baselines on mAP across both Breakfast and COIN, with the largest margin over HierarQ (90.51 vs. 76.32 on Breakfast mAP; 86.54 vs. 70.10 on COIN mAP). On accuracy, PCMCI edges out HierarQ on Breakfast (97.46 vs. 97.18) but trails by 0.25 points on COIN (94.53 vs. 94.78), the only metric where it does not lead. Against recognition-oriented VLM methods (BIKE, Text4Vis), PCMCI gains 1.7–2.0 points in accuracy and 25–30 points in mAP, while using fewer FLOPs (650 vs. 835). On Charades, PCMCI (53.3 mAP, no pre-trimmed training) surpasses all non-pre-trimmed VLM methods (MA-LMM 48.7, MSQNet 47.6) and even pre-trimmed BIKE (50.4) and Text4Vis (46.0), though it remains below pre-trimmed TokenLearner (66.3) and AssembleNet (58.6). In terms of efficiency, PCMCI uses ~58× fewer FLOPs and ~37× fewer parameters than HierarQ, making it the most computationally efficient method among all VLM-based approaches tested.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper carves new ground at the intersection of VLM-based long-term action recognition and causal inference, rather than extending a single prior line of work. It departs from explicit VLM-based methods (BIKE, Text4Vis) that rely on statistical cross-modal alignment, and from implicit LLM-based methods (MA-LMM, HierarQ) that treat VLM tokens as text inputs, by arguing both are vulnerable to spurious correlations that causal intervention can address. The progressive three-stage design—optimal-transport back-door adjustment for cross-modal hallucination, relation-aware back-door adjustment for textual codependency, and cross-modal front-door adjustment for visual confounders—repurposes classical Pearl-style causal operations (back-door, front-door, NWGM) in a novel multi-stage pipeline tailored to the specific confounding structure of VLM-based LTAR, a combination not present in prior causal video classification work (e.g., CMCIR, counterfactual VQA).

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The framework offers a principled, interpretable alternative to the increasingly dominant LLM-based paradigm for long-term action recognition, achieving substantially higher mAP with a fraction of the parameters (211M vs. 7.5B for MA-LMM), which is practically significant for deployment. However, the SCM is hand-crafted rather than learned, the optimal-transport hallucination surrogate is an approximation whose fidelity is unverified, and the intervention order, while ablated, may not generalize beyond the three tested datasets. Open questions include whether the progressive causal structure can be automated or discovered from data, whether the approach extends to open-vocabulary or zero-shot settings, and whether the front-door mediator assumption (text embeddings being d-separated from visual confounders) holds under distribution shift.
