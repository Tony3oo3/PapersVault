## Problem

Video re-rendering from a monocular source faces a fundamental trade-off: geometrically conditioned methods (re-rendered point clouds or meshes) are brittle to depth estimation errors, causing warped shapes and inconsistent parallax, while unconditioned diffusion models produce visually realistic but geometrically incoherent outputs under large viewpoint changes. No prior approach provides geometric guidance that is both informative and robust to reconstruction noise.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/CVPR20263196/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method takes a monocular source video (resampled to 33 frames at 480×832), a user-specified target camera trajectory (poses), and a text caption describing the scene. The source video is consumed twice: once through a standard video VAE for appearance latents and once through CUT3R for 4D scene-state tokens. The output is a generated video of the same scene rendered along the target trajectory, with the same temporal length as the source.

## Method

The core idea is to condition a pretrained video diffusion transformer (DiT) on the persistent latent state of CUT3R, a large 4D reconstruction model, rather than on explicit point-cloud renderings. A lightweight adapter converts CUT3R's per-frame token sets (shape T×s×d) into spatial feature maps (T/k×h×w×c) via an MLP embedding followed by a query-based cross-attention transformer, then concatenates them with source video VAE latents and noisy target latents along the temporal axis. Source and target camera poses are injected into each DiT block through two separate small MLP adapters. Training uses a conditional flow-matching objective; only the DiT projector and self-attention layers are finetuned while the CUT3R adapter and pose adapters are trained from scratch with a 3× higher learning rate, all on the synthetic MultiCamVideo dataset.

## Results

LaVR achieves the best performance across all evaluated metrics. On cycle consistency (static scenes from DL3DV), it attains PSNR 20.74, LPIPS 22.47×10⁻², and CLIP 98.07×10⁻², outperforming the closest competitor Gen3C (20.62 / 23.23 / 97.47). On VBench video quality metrics (dynamic scenes from Pexels), LaVR leads in subject consistency (95.22×10⁻²), multi-view consistency (17.11×10⁻²), and background consistency (92.83×10⁻²). For target pose reconstruction accuracy, LaVR achieves the lowest absolute translation error (14.39 mm), relative translation error (7.798 mm), and relative rotation error (0.411°), confirming tight adherence to the specified camera trajectory.

## Baselines & Comparisons

LaVR outperforms all three baselines on every reported metric. Against Gen3C (point-cloud-conditioned, 7B params), LaVR improves cycle-consistency PSNR from 20.62 to 20.74 and LPIPS from 23.23 to 22.47, while also leading on all VBench consistency scores and pose reconstruction errors (Abs(t): 14.39 vs 24.45 mm). Against TrajectoryCrafter (point-cloud-conditioned, 5B params), the margin is substantial: PSNR 20.74 vs 14.84 and LPIPS 22.47 vs 41.59, reflecting that TrajectoryCrafter's re-rendered point clouds introduce severe warping artifacts. Against ReCamMaster (unconditioned, 1.3B params), LaVR gains 3.0 dB in PSNR and 10.16 in LPIPS, showing that the CUT3R latent conditioning provides meaningful geometric structure that the unconditioned model lacks. Notably, LaVR achieves these results with only ~1.3B parameters, matching ReCamMaster's size while surpassing the much larger Gen3C (7B) and TrajectoryCrafter (5B) models.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper positions itself between two existing families of video re-rendering methods: geometrically conditioned approaches (Gen3C, TrajectoryCrafter) that re-render explicit point clouds as conditioning, and unconditioned methods (ReCamMaster) that rely solely on the diffusion prior. The key departure is replacing explicit point-cloud conditioning with the continuous latent state of a pretrained large 4D reconstruction model (CUT3R), which encodes geometry and camera motion in a token-based representation rather than pixel-aligned renders. The cross-attention adapter that converts CUT3R's temporal token sequence into spatial feature maps compatible with a DiT backbone is a new architectural contribution not present in prior work on either side of this spectrum.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The 'soft latent conditioning' paradigm offers a principled middle ground: it provides geometric structure to the generative process while preserving the diffusion model's ability to regularize small inconsistencies, avoiding the brittleness of explicit depth-to-point-cloud pipelines. This could generalize to other tasks where a pretrained reconstruction model's latent space is more informative than its decoded outputs. However, the method currently struggles with transparent or highly reflective objects (a limitation inherited from CUT3R), incurs additional computation from the LRM forward pass, and has only been validated on relatively short (33-frame) clips at moderate resolution, leaving open questions about scalability to longer, more complex dynamic scenes.
