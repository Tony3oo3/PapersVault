## Problem

Existing part-level 3D generation methods either rely on fine-grained segmentation cues that are sensitive to mask inaccuracies and fail to complete occluded geometry, or operate without explicit segmentation but lack controllability and produce parts with ambiguous boundaries. The paper addresses the need for a decomposition framework that simultaneously achieves high geometric fidelity, semantically coherent part boundaries, and user-controllable part structure.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/CVPR20263350/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method consumes a 3D point cloud sampled from a watertight mesh (XYZ + normals + sharp-edge flags), per-part axis-aligned bounding boxes, and high-dimensional per-point semantic features extracted by P3-SAM. Bounding boxes serve as coarse spatial prompts indicating part location and scale rather than pixel-level masks, and the model supports up to 50 parts per object. The output is a set of complete, watertight part geometries decoded from latent tokens via a fine-tuned VAE, enabling downstream tasks like per-part UV unwrapping and interactive editing.

## Method

X-Part builds a synchronized multi-part diffusion process on top of a vecset-based 3D VAE, where all parts' latent tokens are jointly denoised by a DiT backbone using a flow-matching objective. The key conditioning strategy replaces fine-grained segmentation masks with bounding-box prompts (sampled points within each box encoded by a learnable part encoder) concatenated with interpolated P3-SAM semantic features, providing both spatial and semantic guidance while remaining robust to segmentation noise. The DiT blocks alternate between intra-part self-attention (even layers) and inter-part self-attention (odd layers) to balance local part coherence with global structural consistency, and a learnable part-embedding codebook distinguishes individual parts. A Repaint-style training-free editing pipeline enables interactive part split, merge, and scale adjustment by selectively re-denoising tokens within modified bounding boxes while freezing others.

## Results

On the ObjaversePart-Tiny dataset (200 samples), X-Part achieves a Chamfer Distance of 0.11, F-Score of 0.80 at threshold 0.1, and F-Score of 0.71 at threshold 0.05 for 3D shape decomposition, outperforming all baselines. In the image-to-3D holistic shape generation setting, X-Part reaches CD 0.08, F-Score 0.92 (0.1), and F-Score 0.78 (0.05), matching or exceeding the best generative baselines (OmniPart, PartPacker) while producing more semantically refined part decompositions.

## Baselines & Comparisons

X-Part outperforms all seven baselines across both evaluation settings. In the decomposition task (Table 1), it reduces Chamfer Distance by 27% over SAMPart3D (0.11 vs. 0.15) and by 58% over HoloPart (0.11 vs. 0.26), while improving F-Score@0.05 by 12.5 points over SAMPart3D and by 28 points over HoloPart. Segmentation-based methods (SAMPart3D, PartField) produce the best surface-level alignment but cannot recover occluded or internal geometry, whereas generative methods (HoloPart, OmniPart) complete parts but with noticeably lower fidelity. In the image-to-3D setting (Table 2), X-Part matches OmniPart on CD (0.08) and edges it out on F-Score (0.92 vs. 0.91 at 0.1; 0.78 vs. 0.77 at 0.05), while substantially surpassing PartCrafter (CD 0.20) and PartPacker (CD 0.11). Part123, which generates parts directly from a single image without an intermediate mesh, lags far behind (CD 0.42), highlighting the advantage of conditioning on a full 3D shape.

## Relation to Prior Work

X-Part builds directly on the vecset-based 3D latent diffusion paradigm established by 3DShape2VecSet and Hunyuan3D, and adopts the alternating intra/inter-part attention structure from PartCrafter for synchronized multi-part generation. Its key departure from prior part-generation methods (HoloPart, PartCrafter, PartPacker, OmniPart) is the use of coarse bounding boxes as spatial prompts rather than fine-grained segmentation masks, combined with point-wise semantic features from P3-SAM to provide semantic guidance without overfitting to mask boundaries. The interactive editing pipeline follows the Repaint inpainting strategy, extending the framework beyond one-shot decomposition to iterative user-controlled part manipulation.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

Part-level 3D generation is a critical bottleneck in production pipelines for UV unwrapping, retopology, and 3D printing, and X-Part's bounding-box-based controllability makes it more practical than segmentation-dependent alternatives that are sensitive to mask errors. The demonstrated UV unwrapping simplification and interactive editing (split, merge, adjust) point toward a usable workflow for 3D artists. However, the method lacks physical priors for decomposition, and inference time scales linearly with part count, limiting real-time applicability for high-part-count objects. Open questions include whether the approach generalizes to open-vocabulary part semantics beyond P3-SAM's training distribution and whether the 50-part limit is sufficient for complex assets.
