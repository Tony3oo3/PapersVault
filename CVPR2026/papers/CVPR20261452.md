## Problem

State-of-the-art whole-body pose estimators produce anatomically implausible predictions in challenging scenes with occlusion, clutter, or difficult lighting, because they learn spurious correlations between visual context and pose rather than true causal relationships. The authors formalize this failure as a confounding problem in a Structural Causal Model, where visual context creates a non-causal backdoor path that corrupts the model's reasoning, and prior robustness approaches (distillation, larger datasets) do not directly address this confounding mechanism.

## Input / Output

The method takes RGB person-crop images at 256×192 or 384×288 resolution (cropped using RTMPose person detection boxes) and produces per-keypoint 2D coordinate heatmaps as posterior probability distributions P(k,x) and P(k,y) for 133 whole-body keypoints (or 17 for COCO). The confounder score s_c(k) is computed directly from the peak concentration of these heatmaps, making the intervention mechanism tightly coupled to the output representation rather than a separate detection stage.

## Method

CIGPose introduces a Causal Intervention Module (CIM) that approximates the do-operator by (1) computing a confounder score s_c(k) = 1 − ½(max P_k,x + max P_k,y) from the keypoint heatmaps to identify uncertain/confounded embeddings, and (2) replacing the top-n most confounded embeddings with learned canonical embeddings from a context-invariant table Z, thereby severing the backdoor path F←X←C→Y. A two-stage hierarchical GNN then refines the deconfounded embeddings: an EdgeConv layer over the anatomical skeleton graph models local kinematics, followed by a semantic hypergraph with channel-wise attention that captures long-range inter-part dependencies. Training uses a composite objective combining KL-divergence supervision on the counterfactual path with a counterfactual consistency loss that penalizes divergence between observational and counterfactual predictions for stable (non-intervened) keypoints, ensuring interventions are targeted without disrupting reliable representations.

## Results

On COCO-WholeBody, CIGPose-x achieves 67.0% whole-body AP using only COCO-WholeBody training data, surpassing the previous SOTA DWPose-l* (66.5% AP) which relies on two-stage distillation and the additional UBody dataset. With UBody added, CIGPose-x further improves to 67.5% AP. On COCO val2017, CIGPose-l† reaches 78.5% AP (vs. 77.3% for RTMPose-l†), and on CrowdPose, CIGPose-x† achieves 75.8% AP, outperforming HRFormer-B (72.4% AP) by 3.4 points.

## Baselines & Comparisons

CIGPose-x (67.0 AP) outperforms DWPose-l* (66.5 AP) by 0.5 AP on COCO-WholeBody without using UBody data or two-stage distillation, demonstrating superior data efficiency. CIGPose-l at 384×288 (66.3 AP, 10.7 GFLOPs) surpasses the larger RTMPose-x (65.3 AP, 18.1 GFLOPs) with fewer compute resources. On COCO val2017, CIGPose-l† achieves 78.5 AP, a 1.2-point gain over RTMPose-l† (77.3 AP) at nearly identical cost (9.4 vs. 9.3 GFLOPs). On CrowdPose, CIGPose-x† (75.8 AP) leads HRFormer-B (72.4 AP) by 3.4 AP, and even CIGPose-m (71.4 AP) beats RTMPose-m (70.6 AP) and HRNet (71.3 AP). The consistent gains across all three benchmarks and model scales indicate the causal intervention and hierarchical GNN provide broad, architecture-agnostic improvements rather than dataset-specific tuning.

## Relation to Prior Work

CIGPose repurposes Pearl's do-calculus and structural causal models—previously applied to semantic segmentation and VQA—to formalize why pose estimators fail in complex scenes, identifying visual context as a confounder creating a backdoor path. Architecturally, it builds on RTMPose as the encoder backbone and adapts hierarchical GNN reasoning (inspired by HD-GCN) for anatomical consistency, but departs from prior robustness strategies (knowledge distillation in DWPose, massive pretraining in Sapiens) by targeting the root cause of spurious correlations rather than compensating for them with more data or supervision. The Causal Intervention Module, which uses predictive uncertainty to flag confounded keypoints and substitutes learned canonical embeddings, is a novel mechanism not seen in prior pose or causal-inference-for-vision work.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The causal framing offers a principled, interpretable explanation for pose estimation failures and a targeted fix that improves data efficiency—CIGPose-x matches or exceeds DWPose-l without the extra UBody training data. However, the confounder identification relies on predictive uncertainty validated only against occlusion, and the ablation shows the CIM contributes a modest +0.2 AP on top of the GNN components, leaving open whether the causal intervention generalizes to subtler confounders (lighting, texture) beyond heavy occlusion. Extending the framework to 3D pose and out-of-distribution scenarios, and rigorously validating the uncertainty-as-confounding proxy across diverse failure modes, are natural next steps.
