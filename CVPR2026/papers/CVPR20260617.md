## Problem

Existing 3D super-resolution methods rely on dense LR multi-view inputs, per-scene 3DGS optimization, and pseudo-HR supervision from pretrained 2D super-resolution models. This paradigm caps reconstruction fidelity at the quality of 2DSR priors, prevents cross-scene learning, and makes real-time inference infeasible. No prior work learns a generalized, feed-forward mapping from sparse LR views directly to HR 3DGS representations.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/CVPR20260617/cropped_page0_idx1.webp)

## Input / Output

SR3R takes as few as two LR RGB views (downsampled to 64×64) along with their camera intrinsics and produces a full HR 3DGS representation at 256×256 target resolution. The LR 3DGS scaffold from the backbone is densified 6× via Gaussian Shuffle Split before offset prediction, so the network operates on 6M Gaussians (where M is the original count) rather than regressing all parameters from scratch. The framework is plug-and-play: any feed-forward 3DGS backbone (NoPoSplat, DepthSplat) supplies the initial scaffold, and SR3R upscales it without requiring scene-specific optimization at test time.

## Method

SR3R reformulates 3DSR as a direct feed-forward mapping: a ViT encoder processes upsampled LR images, a bidirectional cross-attention module refines 2D features using geometry-aware tokens from the pretrained 3DGS backbone, and a ViT decoder fuses cross-view information. The key architectural choice is Gaussian offset learning: instead of regressing absolute HR Gaussian parameters, a PointTransformerV3-based module predicts residual offsets (Δμ, Δα, Δr, Δs, Δc) from the densified LR scaffold, constraining the learning target to local high-frequency corrections. This residual formulation yields substantially more stable training and sharper reconstructions than direct parameter regression. The entire pipeline is trained end-to-end via differentiable Gaussian rasterization with MSE and LPIPS losses on large-scale multi-scene data (RE10K, ACID), enabling zero-shot generalization to unseen scenes like DTU.

## Results

On RE10K (64×64→256×256), SR3R with DepthSplat backbone achieves 26.25 PSNR, 0.856 SSIM, and 0.165 LPIPS, outperforming the upsampled-input baseline (Up-DepthSplat: 24.71 PSNR) by 1.54 dB. On ACID, the same configuration reaches 27.02 PSNR and 0.797 SSIM, a 1.70 dB gain over Up-DepthSplat. In zero-shot transfer from RE10K to DTU, SR3R (NoPoSplat backbone) achieves 17.24 PSNR and 0.291 LPIPS, surpassing per-scene optimization methods SRGS (12.42 PSNR) and FSGS+SRGS (13.72 PSNR) by 4.8 and 3.5 dB respectively, while running in 1.69 s versus 300–420 s for optimization-based methods.

## Baselines & Comparisons

SR3R consistently outperforms both feed-forward 3DGS backbones (NoPoSplat and DepthSplat) and their upsampled-input variants across all metrics on RE10K and ACID. Against NoPoSplat, SR3R gains +3.46 dB PSNR on RE10K and +4.09 dB on ACID; against DepthSplat, the gains are +3.10 dB and +3.22 dB respectively. Compared to the upsampled-input baselines (which naively upscale LR images before direct HR Gaussian regression), SR3R still leads by 1.4–1.7 dB PSNR while using fewer Gaussians (49,152 vs. 131,072) and less training memory. In the zero-shot RE10K→DTU setting, SR3R (17.24 PSNR, 0.291 LPIPS) surpasses the per-scene optimization methods SRGS (12.42 PSNR) and FSGS+SRGS (13.72 PSNR) by 4.8 and 3.5 dB, demonstrating that the learned feed-forward mapping generalizes better than scene-specific fitting even on completely unseen object-centric scenes.

## Relation to Prior Work

SR3R departs fundamentally from the dominant 3DSR paradigm (SRGS, SuperGaussian, GaussianSR) that injects high-frequency priors via pretrained 2DSR models to supervise per-scene 3DGS optimization. Instead, it builds on the emerging feed-forward 3DGS reconstruction line (NoPoSplat, DepthSplat, MVSplat) and the Gaussian Shuffle Split densification from S2Gaussian, repurposing them as a structural scaffold upon which a learned ViT-based mapping network predicts residual Gaussian offsets. The key conceptual shift is replacing 2DSR pseudo-supervision with data-driven 3D-specific prior learning across large-scale multi-scene data, turning 3DSR from an optimization problem into a single forward pass.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

By eliminating per-scene optimization, SR3R enables real-time 3DSR inference (1.69 s vs. 300–420 s for optimization baselines) and, more importantly, strong zero-shot cross-scene generalization that even surpasses per-scene methods on unseen DTU scenes. The plug-and-play design (compatible with any feed-forward 3DGS backbone) makes the approach broadly applicable and easy to integrate into existing pipelines. Open questions include whether the approach scales to higher SR factors (beyond 4×), more than two input views, or more diverse scene categories, and whether the Gaussian offset formulation generalizes to other 3D representations beyond 3DGS.
