## Problem

Diffusion-generated novel views used to densify sparse-view 3D reconstruction often contain photometric and geometric artifacts that degrade 3DGS training if used unfiltered. Existing cross-reference IQA methods either assess only overlapping regions (MEt3R) or rely on patch-level similarity without geometric awareness (CrossScore, PuzzleSim), leaving unobserved regions unevaluated and failing to capture high-level structural inconsistencies. No prior method produces a complete, dense quality map for a diffusion-generated view using only unaligned reference images from different poses.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/CVPR20260382/cropped_page1_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method takes a diffusion-generated query image, one or more real reference images from different camera poses of the same scene, and camera parameters (used to recover dense 3D point maps via VGGT for feature warping). It outputs a dense pixel-level quality map Q ∈ [0,1]^{H×W} that approximates a full-reference metric (DINOv2-SIM or SSIM) without requiring a pixel-aligned ground truth. In the 3DGS application, the quality map is additionally thresholded at the 50th percentile to yield a binary confidence mask that restricts the reconstruction loss to high-confidence pixels.

## Method

PR-IQA operates in two stages. First, it warps DINOv2 features from the reference view into the query view using VGGT-derived 3D point maps, computing a partial quality map via cosine similarity only in geometrically overlapping regions. Second, a three-stream encoder-decoder (query, reference, partial map) completes the partial map into a full-image quality map; the query and partial-map encoders use reference-conditioned cross-attention (reference features as keys/values) at every scale, while each stage employs a CBAM-derived dual-gated attention block that sequentially applies channel attention, spatial attention, and an FFN to decouple feature relevance from spatial propagation. A ConvFuse operation merges the query and partial streams after each stage, anchoring quality propagation to geometry-validated regions. The network is trained supervised with a combined L1 + Jensen-Shannon Divergence + PLCC loss against FR-IQA target maps, and is deployed in a dual-filtering 3DGS pipeline that selects the best pseudo-GT candidate per view and masks low-confidence pixels during optimization.

## Results

On the IQA evaluation (Table 1), Ours_DINOv2 achieves the best CR-IQA PLCC of 0.555/0.573/0.453 (Mip-NeRF 360 / Tanks and Temples / RealEstate10K) against the DINOv2-SIM target, while Ours_SSIM achieves 0.535/0.625/0.632 against the SSIM target. Notably, Ours_SSIM surpasses the FR metric LPIPS on the SSIM target for Tanks and Temples (0.625 vs. 0.598 PLCC) and RealEstate10K (0.632 vs. 0.452 PLCC), demonstrating FR-level accuracy without ground-truth supervision. In the downstream 3DGS application (Table 3), Ours_DINOv2-guided 3DGS achieves the best reconstruction quality among all CR-IQA methods: PSNR 16.76/16.24/17.72, SSIM 0.493/0.551/0.632, and LPIPS 0.414/0.403/0.327 across the three benchmarks.

## Baselines & Comparisons

Among CR-IQA baselines, Ours_DINOv2 outperforms PuzzleSim (best prior CR method on DINOv2 target) by +0.251 PLCC on Mip-NeRF 360 (0.555 vs. 0.304) and +0.222 on Tanks and Temples (0.573 vs. 0.351). Ours_SSIM dominates CrossScore on the SSIM target, achieving 0.625 vs. 0.444 PLCC on Tanks and Temples and 0.632 vs. 0.442 on RealEstate10K. MEt3R, which only evaluates overlapping regions, trails Ours_partial (the non-learned partial map) by a wide margin on DINOv2 target (e.g., 0.105 vs. 0.437 PLCC on Mip-NeRF 360), confirming that geometric overlap alone is insufficient without completion. NR-IQA methods (PAL4VST, PaQ-2-PiQ, PIQE) are uniformly poor, with PLCC values near or below zero on most settings, confirming their inability to capture cross-view geometric inconsistencies. In the 3DGS application (Table 3), Ours_DINOv2-guided training beats ViewCrafter (no IQA) by +0.58 dB PSNR on Mip-NeRF 360 (16.76 vs. 16.18) and +0.78 dB on Tanks and Temples (16.24 vs. 15.77), while also outperforming the FR-IQA DINOv2† baseline on LPIPS for Mip-NeRF 360 (0.414 vs. 0.399), showing that the CR approach can match or exceed FR-guided reconstruction without requiring aligned ground truth.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper unifies two previously separate strategies in cross-reference IQA: patch-based similarity (CrossScore, PuzzleSim) and multi-view geometric consistency (MEt3R). It departs from MEt3R's limitation of only evaluating overlapping regions by reformulating the non-overlapping portion as a quality completion problem, guided by a reference-conditioned cross-attention network. The geometric warping via VGGT point maps and DINOv2 feature similarity for the partial map directly extends MEt3R's approach, while the three-stream encoder-decoder with dual-gated attention (CBAM-derived) is a novel architectural contribution for propagating quality signals across views.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

PR-IQA provides a practical, GT-free quality assessment tool that plugs directly into diffusion-augmented 3DGS pipelines, addressing a real bottleneck in sparse-view reconstruction where naive use of generated views degrades geometry. The dual-filtering strategy (image selection + pixel masking) is immediately applicable to existing pipelines like ViewCrafter. Open questions include generalizability beyond diffusion-generated views (e.g., GAN-based or video-model outputs), dependence on VGGT's geometric accuracy, and whether the quality completion formulation transfers to other completion-like tasks in 3D vision.
