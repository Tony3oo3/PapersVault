## Problem

Diffusion-based virtual try-on methods achieve high visual fidelity but suffer from architectural complexity (auxiliary reference networks that double parameters) and slow inference, making the fidelity-efficiency trade-off a persistent challenge. Additionally, existing approaches either ignore dressing-style control or rely on closed-source VLMs for style description, and image-level concatenation wastes resolution on low-information spatial conditions like masks and pose.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/CVPR20260291/cropped_page0_idx1.webp)

## Input / Output

The model takes a person image at 1024×768, one or more garment images, and an optional dressing-style text prompt as input. The person image is preprocessed by masking out clothing regions (via body parsing + DW-Pose), extracting pose and body shape with a custom iteratively-trained DensePose model, and downsampling spatial conditions to 25% of their original token count before merging. The system also handles incomplete real-world inputs: a TryOff model extracts garment images from model photos when isolated garment images are unavailable, and a fine-tuned Qwen2.5-VL-7B generates multi-garment style descriptions from catalog images.

## Method

PROMO is built on the FLUX.1-dev Flow Matching DiT backbone, fine-tuned via LoRA (rank 128, 580M trainable parameters) on VITON-HD and DressCode. Three key mechanisms drive efficiency: (1) Spatial Condition Merging downsamples mask and pose conditions in pixel space and pastes pose onto the masked person image, reducing 2N condition tokens to N/4 (87.5% reduction); (2) Temporal Self-Reference caches condition Key-Value pairs at the first denoising timestep and reuses them across subsequent steps with queries containing only the latent and text tokens, eliminating the need for a separate reference network; (3) 3D-RoPE repurposes the temporal dimension of rotary position encodings as group identifiers, enabling single-garment training to generalize to multi-garment inference in one forward pass. A region-aware loss weighting scheme (body weight 1+λ, background 1−λ, λ=0.5) focuses the model on garment details while suppressing background reconstruction, and a fine-tuned Qwen2.5-VL-7B Style Prompt System provides structured multi-garment dressing-style descriptions via a two-stage annotation pipeline.

## Results

On DressCode (paired), PROMO achieves the best KID of 0.4902 (vs. 1.0565 for Any2AnyTryon and 0.8550 for OOTDiffusion) and a competitive FID of 3.3103, while on the unpaired split it attains the best FID (4.7393) and KID (0.4992) among all VTON baselines. On VITON-HD (paired), PROMO records the lowest LPIPS (0.1111) and KID (1.4895), with FID of 6.8903 comparable to LaDI-VTON (6.8500). In the in-the-wild user study, PROMO achieves the highest overall excellence rate of 84.42%, surpassing Huiwa (78.85%), Douyin (61.54%), and Kling (60.19%), with particular strength in body-shape consistency (94.62%) and style consistency (96.92%).

## Baselines & Comparisons

Against dedicated VTON methods, PROMO's clearest advantage is KID: it achieves 0.4902 (DressCode paired) and 0.4992 (DressCode unpaired), roughly 2× better than Any2AnyTryon (1.0565 / 1.5258) and 4–9× better than CatVTON (3.9591 / 4.4897). On FID, Any2AnyTryon retains a slight edge on DressCode paired (3.0828 vs. 3.3103) and SSIM (0.9107 vs. 0.8913), but PROMO dominates on the unpaired split (FID 4.7393 vs. 5.5404). OOTDiffusion shows a dramatic failure on VITON-HD (FID 32.89, KID 20.08), while LaDI-VTON and PromptDresser are competitive on VITON-HD FID but trail PROMO on KID. Against general image-editing models (Table 2), PROMO outperforms Seedream 4.0 and Qwen Image Edit on nearly all metrics; Nanobanana (Gemini 2.5-Flash-Image) is the closest competitor, matching PROMO on VITON-HD unpaired KID (0.4411 vs. 1.5828 in PROMO's favor) but trailing on DressCode KID (0.7160 vs. 0.5763) and FID. In the in-the-wild user study, PROMO leads overall excellence (84.42%) and body-shape consistency (94.62%), though Douyin edges it on texture (96.73 vs. 93.65) and Huiwa on color (99.04 vs. 97.88).

## Relation to Prior Work

PROMO positions virtual try-on as a structured multi-conditional image editing problem and builds directly on the FLUX.1-dev Flow Matching DiT backbone, extending FastFit's temporal self-reference mechanism from UNet to the transformer setting to eliminate the auxiliary reference networks used by OOTDiffusion, IDM-VTON, and FitDiT. It departs from image-level concatenation approaches (CatVTON, IC-LoRA) by injecting conditions in latent space with spatial downsampling and hierarchical merging, and extends OminiControl's 3D-RoPE grouping to distinguish multiple garment conditions without iterative error accumulation. The Style Prompt System generalizes PromptDresser's text-based dressing-style control by replacing GPT-4o with a distilled Qwen2.5-VL-7B model and supporting multi-garment descriptions.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

By demonstrating that a single DiT backbone with self-reference caching and spatial token merging can match or exceed reference-network VTON methods at roughly 40% of the inference time, PROMO offers a practical efficiency-quality recipe likely to be adopted in production e-commerce pipelines. The region-aware parsing loss and 3D-RoPE grouping are modular contributions that transfer to other multi-condition generation tasks. Open questions remain around heavily layered occlusions, the dependency on auxiliary modules (TryOff, Style Prompt System), and whether the flow-matching DiT advantage persists at even higher resolutions or with more than two garment categories simultaneously.
