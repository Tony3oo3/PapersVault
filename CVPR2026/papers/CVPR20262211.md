## Problem

SAM2 excels at single-view segmentation given spatially grounded prompts, but cross-view object correspondence (e.g., ego-exo) renders direct coordinate prompting infeasible due to drastic viewpoint shifts. Existing visual-reference extensions remove spatial prompts entirely, losing SAM2's localization strength and failing under severe appearance changes. The paper asks whether SAM2's coordinate-based prompting can be restored in cross-view settings and whether spatial and visual prompts can be combined for complementary gains.

## Input / Output

The method takes a temporally aligned query-target image (or video frame) pair from distinct viewpoints (ego-centric and exo-centric) along with a binary object mask in the query view, and produces the corresponding object mask in the target view. No camera poses, semantic labels, or explicit 3D geometry are provided; the model relies solely on visual information. At inference, all three experts run in parallel and the PCCS selects a single final mask per object instance.

## Method

V²-SAM adapts SAM2 (encoder, prompt encoder, mask decoder; memory modules removed) with two complementary prompt generators. V²-Anchor extracts DINOv3 patch features, performs foreground-constrained cross-view cosine matching, applies stratified sampling to enforce minimum pairwise distance, and linearly transforms the surviving correspondences into SAM2's canonical coordinate space, yielding a training-free coordinate prompt. V²-Visual mask-pools SAM2 features within the query mask, then passes them through a Visual Prompt Matcher (VPMatcher) comprising a Transformer cross-attention Feature Mapping Branch and a CNN-based Structural Mapping Branch (with FiLM conditioning) that jointly produce an appearance-guided prompt. Three experts (Anchor, Visual, Fusion) share the same decoder architecture but distinct parameters; the Anchor Expert is entirely training-free, while the Visual and Fusion Experts train the VPMatcher and decoder with a combined contrastive, structural, and mask (CE+Dice) loss. At inference, a Post-hoc Cyclic Consistency Selector (PCCS) back-projects each expert's target mask to the query view via V²-Anchor and selects the expert whose back-projected points best match the original query mask, operating at the point level to avoid an extra decoding pass.

## Results

On Ego-Exo4D (Correspondences v2 test split), V²-SAM (Multi-Experts) achieves a total IoU of 48.0, with 46.3 IoU on Ego2Exo and 49.6 IoU on Exo2Ego, surpassing the previous best (O-MaMa, 43.4 total IoU) by 4.6 points. On DAVIS-2017 (20-frame temporal gap), V²-SAM reaches J&Fm of 78.8, outperforming the prior best (PCC, 70.2) by 8.6 points. On HANDAL-X zero-shot transfer, V²-SAM attains 77.2 IoU, more than doubling the best prior method (ObjectRelator, 42.8 IoU).

## Baselines & Comparisons

V²-SAM (Multi-Experts) outperforms all 13 baselines across all three benchmarks. On Ego-Exo4D, it surpasses the closest competitor O-MaMa (43.4 total IoU) by 4.6 points and ObjectRelator (37.8) by 10.2 points, while using only ~1% of ObjectRelator's trainable parameters (15.3M vs. 1587.3M). The single-expert variant already exceeds O-MaMa by 2.5 IoU, confirming the strength of the individual prompt generators. On DAVIS-2017, V²-SAM leads the prior best PCC by 8.6 J&Fm points (78.8 vs. 70.2) and DINO ViTb/8 by 12.4 points. On HANDAL-X zero-shot, V²-SAM achieves 77.2 IoU versus 42.8 for ObjectRelator and 39.9 for PSALM, demonstrating strong cross-domain generalization from Ego-Exo4D training to robotic manipulation scenes. Notably, Ref-SAM* (visual-reference-guided SAM2) trails V²-SAM by 10.2 IoU, highlighting the benefit of combining geometric and visual prompts over appearance-only guidance.

## Relation to Prior Work

V²-SAM builds directly on SAM2 as its segmentation backbone, retaining the encoder, prompt encoder, and mask decoder while discarding memory modules. It departs from prior cross-view correspondence methods (ObjectRelator, O-MaMa, DOMR) by introducing coordinate-based prompting for SAM2 in cross-view settings—a direction the authors identify as largely unexplored—and by combining it with a visual prompt matcher inspired by visual reference-guided segmentation (ViRefSAM, VRP-SAM). The multi-expert design with a post-hoc cyclic consistency selector draws on the Mixture-of-Experts paradigm but applies it to prompt-conditioned mask decoders rather than feed-forward classifiers, representing a novel architectural integration.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The framework demonstrates that SAM2's spatial prompting capability can be repurposed for cross-view correspondence with only 15M trainable parameters (~1% of ObjectRelator's 1.6B), suggesting an efficient path for adapting foundation segmentation models to new task settings. The PCCS mechanism offers a general, non-parametric strategy for selecting among competing prompt-based predictions that could transfer to other multi-hypothesis segmentation scenarios. Open questions include whether the approach extends beyond ego-exo to arbitrary multi-view 3D settings, whether DINOv3's geometry-aware features are truly necessary versus lighter alternatives, and how the method behaves under extreme domain shifts (e.g., synthetic-to-real or cross-embodiment) beyond the HANDAL-X zero-shot evaluation.
