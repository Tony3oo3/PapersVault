## Problem

Existing any-to-any multimodal models require massive amounts of fully paired data across all modalities, which is prohibitively expensive to acquire in ecology where modalities like satellite imagery, species audio, and environmental covariates lack one-to-one correspondence. Moreover, these models assume all modalities are present at inference and cannot infer or reconstruct missing ones, nor do they capture the uncertainty inherent in predicting unavailable modalities.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/CVPR20262432/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The model operates on pre-computed global embeddings from frozen modality-specific encoders (transformers for images/audio/text, a random Fourier feature network for geolocation, and a feedforward network for environmental covariates) rather than raw signals. At inference it accepts any subset of 1–6 modalities as input tokens and outputs a joint Gaussian distribution (mean and diagonal variance) over all modalities, from which embeddings of masked modalities are reconstructed via the reparameterization trick. For downstream use, the hidden encoder representations (including register tokens) serve as the final embedding for linear probing, while a mixed query embedding (original query + reconstructed target, weighted by δ) is used for cross-modal retrieval.

## Method

ProM3E is a two-stage framework: first, TaxaBind-style contrastive alignment (using SupCon loss on massive image-paired data) projects all six modalities into a unified embedding space; second, a lightweight 27M-parameter masked variational autoencoder is trained on only 79K all-paired samples from this aligned space. The MVAE encoder is a single-layer transformer that treats each modality embedding as a token alongside [μ], [σ], and register tokens, learning a joint Gaussian over the input modalities; during training, 1–2 modalities are randomly kept visible while the rest are dropped (MultiMAE-style masking). The decoder samples from the joint distribution via the reparameterization trick and uses modality-specific two-layer feedforward decoders to reconstruct each masked embedding. The training objective replaces the standard VAE MSE reconstruction with an InfoNCE-based contrastive loss computed over Euclidean distances within the batch, preventing representation collapse to centroids, combined with a VIB KL-divergence term to regularize the variance. A key design choice is modality inversion: the model can reconstruct any target modality from any query modality, enabling a retrieval strategy that blends inter-modal and intra-modal similarities through a learned mixing coefficient.

## Results

On zero-shot species image classification (Table 1), ProM3E achieves 86.89% on Birds525, 82.85% on CUB-200-2011, 37.49% on BioCLIP-Rare, 66.96% on Inquire, 75.83% on iNat-2021, and 39.45% on TaxaBench-8k, outperforming the strongest baseline TaxaBind by up to 5 percentage points. In the bimodal setting (Table 2), ProM3E reaches 78.27% on iNat-2021 and 47.00% on TaxaBench-8k, a ~10% improvement over TaxaBind. For cross-modal retrieval (Table 3), ProM3E achieves R@1 of 17.87 (vs. 8.43 for TaxaBind and 8.79 for ImageBind) in the best setting, roughly doubling retrieval accuracy. On bird audio classification (Table 4), ProM3E attains 58.94% top-1 on BirdCLEF'22 unimodal and 71.56% multimodal, surpassing the best audio-specific baseline MGACLAP by ~3% and TaxaBind by ~6%.

## Baselines & Comparisons

ProM3E outperforms all six baselines across every evaluated task. Against TaxaBind [44] (the closest ecological baseline), ProM3E gains 3–5 pp on unimodal zero-shot classification, ~10 pp on bimodal classification (e.g., 47.00 vs. 36.59 on TaxaBench-8k), roughly doubles R@1 on cross-modal retrieval (17.87 vs. 8.43), and improves audio classification by 6–12 pp. Against ImageBind [17], ProM3E wins on bimodal classification (78.27 vs. 71.02 on iNat-2021) and retrieval (17.87 vs. 8.79 R@1), confirming that the masked-VAE fusion stage adds substantial value over simple contrastive alignment. Against the audio-specific baselines CLAP [15] and MGACLAP [32], ProM3E's multimodal audio probing reaches 71.56% on BirdCLEF'22, exceeding MGACLAP's 56.05% by ~16 pp, demonstrating that ecological multimodal context (satellite, location, text) substantially aids fine-grained audio recognition. BioCLIP [50] and BioTroveCLIP [67] are outperformed on all six zero-shot classification datasets, with the largest margins on rare-species (BioCLIP-Rare: 37.49 vs. 34.52) and fine-grained (TaxaBench-8k: 39.45 vs. 32.88) benchmarks.

## Relation to Prior Work

ProM3E builds directly on TaxaBind's ecological multimodal alignment recipe as its first stage, then departs from the any-to-any paradigm (4M, MultiMAE) by training a lightweight masked variational autoencoder on pre-aligned embeddings rather than raw signals. The probabilistic formulation draws on PCME/PCME++'s variational information bottleneck loss, but repurposes it for masked reconstruction rather than contrastive retrieval. The key conceptual departure is the two-stage design: align first (using massive image-paired data), then fuse (using small-scale all-paired data), which sidesteps the data bottleneck that limits prior any-to-any models in domains where certain modalities (hyperspectral, MRI) are hard to acquire or synthesize.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The data-efficiency result—only ~3% average performance drop at 10% of paired data (7,931 samples)—is practically significant for ecology and remote sensing, where acquiring all-paired multimodal observations is expensive and slow. The finding that the model's learned uncertainty (σ) correlates with Shannon Diversity Index (Spearman ρ=0.401) opens a path toward using representation uncertainty as an ecological signal, not just a training artifact. Open questions include whether the probabilistic framework generalizes beyond the six modalities studied, whether the modality-gap reduction observed here holds at larger scale, and whether the 27M-parameter MVAE can be replaced by larger backbones without losing the data-efficiency advantage.
