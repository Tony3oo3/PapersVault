## Problem

Existing style-personalized image generation methods either require per-style fine-tuning (incurring high computational cost and long inference) or rely on diffusion-based architectures with slow iterative denoising. No training-free approach has demonstrated competitive style fidelity and prompt fidelity simultaneously on fast autoregressive models, leaving a gap for real-time, deployment-friendly style personalization.

## Input / Output

The method takes a single style reference image and a text prompt formatted as '<content> in <style>' as input, and produces a 1024×1024 RGB image in approximately 3.58 seconds on a single A6000 GPU. The style reference is encoded by Infinity's multi-scale image encoder into a sequence of quantized feature maps, while the text prompt is shared identically between two parallel generation streams to prevent semantic mismatch. No per-style training or tuning is required at inference time.

## Method

The method operates on the frozen Infinity 2B scale-wise autoregressive model using a dual-stream architecture: an unmodified content path provides structural guidance, while a generation path is modulated by two lightweight modules. The key insight, derived from step-wise prompt-injection and SVD analysis, is that the third-step feature F_3's dominant singular value predominantly encodes style while contributing minimally to content. Principal Feature Blending (PFB) intervenes at step 3 by applying an exponential reweighting function Φ to the singular values of the reference image's F_3^sty, then replacing only the principal component in the generation path while preserving its residual structure. Structural Attention Correction (SAC) is applied from step 3 onward by substituting the generation path's self-attention queries and keys with those from the content path, stabilizing spatial coherence and preventing shape distortion introduced by the style injection.

## Results

On the FineStyle benchmark (190 Parti prompts × 10 styles), the method achieves a harmonic score of 0.437, prompt fidelity (S_txt) of 0.334, and style fidelity (S_img) of 0.630, with an inference time of only 3.58 seconds per 1024×1024 image on a single A6000 GPU. It attains the highest prompt fidelity among all compared methods and a near-best harmonic score (0.437 vs. StyleAligned's 0.438), while being up to 195× faster than the slowest baseline (DreamStyler at 698.98 s). The method is fully training-free, requiring no per-style fine-tuning.

## Baselines & Comparisons

The method achieves the best prompt fidelity (S_txt = 0.334) across all eight baselines, outperforming the next-best DB-LoRA (0.323) and B-LoRA (0.324) by 1.1 and 1.0 points respectively. On harmonic score, it ties closely with StyleAligned (0.437 vs. 0.438) and marginally beats IP-Adapter (0.433) and StyleAR (0.434), while clearly surpassing the training-based methods StyleDrop (0.386), DreamStyler (0.403), DB-LoRA (0.420), B-LoRA (0.410), and CSGO (0.421). On style fidelity (S_img), the method trails IP-Adapter (0.763), StyleAligned (0.716), and StyleAR (0.701), but the paper argues these higher scores reflect content leakage rather than genuine style transfer, as confirmed by qualitative inspection and the lower S_txt of those methods. Inference speed is the most dramatic advantage: at 3.58 s, the method is 2.8× faster than the next-fastest CSGO (15.87 s) and up to 195× faster than DreamStyler (698.98 s), with no per-style tuning overhead. In a 30-participant user study, the method received the highest preference for prompt fidelity (35.3%) and competitive style fidelity (32.0%), compared to the best competing scores of 28.7% (prompt) and 30.7% (style).

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper builds on the Infinity scale-wise autoregressive T2I architecture and the next-scale prediction paradigm, applying a training-free style injection strategy that departs from the dominant diffusion-based fine-tuning approaches (DreamBooth, LoRA, StyleDrop, B-LoRA). Conceptually, it draws on the content-style separation literature (B-LoRA, CSD-VAR) but repurposes SVD-based spectral decomposition as a lightweight, interpretable mechanism for isolating style from content in AR feature spaces. The Structural Attention Correction module adapts the attention-alignment idea from diffusion-based editing (P2P, Delta Denoising) to the autoregressive setting, while the evaluation protocol follows FineStyle.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The training-free, single-reference formulation removes the per-style fine-tuning bottleneck that limits scalability of current personalized generation systems, and the ~3.6 s inference time on a single GPU makes interactive style-personalized generation practical. However, the method is tightly coupled to the Infinity architecture and its specific 12-step scale-wise schedule; whether the SVD-based style encoding at F3 generalizes to other AR or diffusion backbones remains an open question. Additionally, the approach handles only a single style reference and a single text prompt, leaving multi-style composition and fine-grained style intensity control as natural extensions.
