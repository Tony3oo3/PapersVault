## Problem

Existing voxelized 3D representations universally depend on a lossy pipeline: converting raw meshes to watertight proxies, assigning inside/outside labels via global operations (winding numbers, flood-fill), and extracting iso-surfaces through Marching Cubes or variants. Each stage introduces artifacts—surface thickening, loss of internal cavities, and grid-lattice bumping—and the global dependencies cap practical resolution below 1024³. The paper asks whether one can directly obtain a faithful sparse voxel representation from arbitrary raw meshes, eliminating both distance-field conversion and iso-surface extraction entirely.

## Input / Output

The encoder consumes raw triangle meshes (open, non-manifold, multi-component) or point clouds sampled from them, normalized to [-1,1]³ with a 0.025 margin, and produces Faithful Contour Tokens (FCT): a sparse set of per-voxel records containing a fitted anchor position and normal, up to eight dual-anchor entries with binary validity masks, and a six-element semi-axis orientation code in {-1,0,1}⁶. The decoder reconstructs a triangle mesh from these tokens, and a dual-mode VAE further compresses FCTs (or point-cloud features) into an 8×-downsampled sparse latent code. The representation scales to 2048³ resolution—beyond what any prior voxel method achieves—because all operations are fully local and GPU-parallelizable.

## Method

The core mechanism replaces the SDF→Marching Cubes pipeline with a direct anchor-fitting procedure: for each active voxel (detected via Separating Axis Theorem), triangle-voxel intersection polygons are clipped and their area-weighted centroids collected; a Tikhonov-regularized least-squares fit then solves for a single anchor position and normal per voxel that best satisfies all tangent-plane constraints, biasing toward sharp edges. Connectivity is encoded by testing semi-axis crossings with the Möller–Trumbore algorithm, producing a compact orientation code that the decoder uses to orient quadrilaterals formed by four incident dual anchors and select the triangulation diagonal minimizing normal deviation. For reconstruction, a dual-mode VAE with cascaded sparse 3D convolutional residual blocks and lightweight local attention compresses FCTs (or point-cloud features aggregated into voxels) into a compact latent, trained with MSE on anchor offsets, cosine similarity on normals, binary cross-entropy on axis codes/masks/occupancy, and KL divergence for latent regularization.

## Results

On the VAE reconstruction task (Table 2), FaithC at 1024³ resolution achieves a Chamfer Distance of 0.46×10⁻⁴ and F₁₀.₀₀₁ of 34.91 on Toys4K, reducing CD by ~93% and improving F-score by ~35% over the strongest baselines (SparseFlex: CD 1.33, F₁₀.₀₀₁ 25.95; Sparc3D: CD 11.42, F₁₀.₀₀₁ 18.26). On the representation fidelity benchmark (Table 1), FaithC at 1024³ attains the lowest Hausdorff Distance (0.11×10⁻²) and CD_{G→P} (0.01×10⁻⁴), while at 2048³ it pushes CD_{G→P} below 0.01×10⁻⁴ and F₁₀.₀₁ to 99.99 — a resolution unattainable by prior voxel methods. The method is the only one evaluated that scales to 2048³ without global optimization or rendering losses.

## Baselines & Comparisons

FaithC decisively outperforms all seven baselines on both Toys4K and Dora. Against the closest competitor SparseFlex [21] (Vox. 4×, 1024³), FaithC (Vox. 8×, 1024³) reduces CD from 1.33 to 0.46 (−65%) and raises F₁₀.₀₀₁ from 25.95 to 34.91 (+35%) on Toys4K, while also using a higher compression ratio (8× vs 4×). Sparc3D [28] (Vox. 8×) is far behind at CD 11.42 and F₁₀.₀₀₁ 18.26, reflecting the inherent smoothing of SDF-based remeshing. XCube [42] and Trellis [57] (both Vox. 4×) show moderate CD (4.35 and 12.90) but very low F₁₀.₀₀₁ scores (1.61 and 4.05), indicating poor preservation of fine detail. Dora [6] and Craftsman [27] (Vec. compression) achieve CD 11.15 and 13.08 respectively, with F₁₀.₀₁ scores of 81.54 and 56.51, well below FaithC's 97.89. 3PSDF [7] (Vec.) sits in the middle at CD 4.51 but still trails FaithC by 10× in CD. On the representation fidelity benchmark (Table 1), FaithC at 1024³ achieves the lowest HD (0.11) and CD_{G→P} (0.01) versus UDF (0.20/0.42), Flood-fill (0.75/1.16), and SparC (0.71/1.19), and uniquely extends to 2048³ where all baselines are inapplicable.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper departs from the dominant SDF-to-Marching-Cubes pipeline (and its variants like Dual Contouring, FlexiCubes, SparC3D, SparseFlex) by eliminating both the distance-field conversion and the iso-surface extraction step entirely. Instead of global operations (winding numbers, flood-fill) followed by MC remeshing, it performs purely local per-voxel anchor fitting from raw triangle intersections, drawing conceptually on Dual Contouring's idea of placing points on surfaces but without any field function. The VAE architecture inherits the sparse 3D convolution + local attention design from SparseFlex, SparC3D, and Trellis, but operates on the new FCT token format rather than SDF values or occupancy.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

By removing the SDF and Marching Cubes dependencies, Faithful Contouring becomes the first voxel representation that natively handles open surfaces, non-manifold elements, and multi-component assemblies at 2048+ resolution via fully parallel local computation—addressing a scalability bottleneck that has capped prior methods at ~1024. The practical impact extends to 3D generation pipelines (Trellis, SparseFlex, Hunyuan3D) that currently inherit MC artifacts and resolution limits from their underlying representations. Open questions include robustness to severe self-intersections and closely spaced thin layers, whether differentiable contouring can be integrated into gradient-based training, and whether FCT tokens can serve as a structured latent for high-precision generative models beyond the current VAE.
