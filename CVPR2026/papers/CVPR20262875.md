limitations: The paper does not include an explicit limitations section. surprise_factor: The 5.4× speedup over DiffPose at comparable accuracy is the headline result, but it is largely anticipated from the ODE-vs-SDE comparison in the generative-modeling literature; the more interesting (if modest) finding is that RPEA consistently outperforms both uniform averaging and joint-wise best-of-N selection across hypothesis counts, and that joint-wise RPEA preserves anatomical coherence (P-MPJPE) where joint-wise JPMA degrades it—suggesting the weighted aggregation, not just the selection, is what maintains structural consistency. ## Problem

Monocular 3D pose estimation is inherently ambiguous, motivating probabilistic multi-hypothesis methods, but existing diffusion-based approaches require 10–50 iterative denoising steps per hypothesis, making inference too slow for real-time use. Flow Matching, which learns a deterministic ODE velocity field and needs only a few integration steps, has not been applied to 3D pose lifting.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/CVPR20262875/cropped_page2_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method takes a 2D pose (J×2 joint coordinates) as conditioning signal, typically from a CPN keypoint detector (Human3.6M) or ground-truth annotations (MPI-INF-3DHP). It outputs N diverse 3D pose hypotheses (each J×3) generated by integrating the learned ODE with S=3 Euler steps from different Gaussian noise seeds, which are then fused into a single 3D pose via the RPEA aggregation module. Horizontal-flip augmentation produces additional hypotheses (Flipped-Hypothesis Aggregation), so N is always even in practice.

## Method

FMPose3D formulates 3D pose lifting as conditional distribution transport: a neural network v_θ(x_t, t, c) learns a velocity field that deterministically moves samples from a standard Gaussian prior to the conditional 3D pose distribution given a 2D input, trained with a linear-interpolation path and the Conditional Flow Matching loss ‖v_θ − (x₁−x₀)‖². The backbone processes per-joint features through parallel local GCN and global self-attention branches, fused via LayerNorm and an MLP regression head. At inference, sampling different noise seeds yields diverse hypotheses, and the proposed RPEA module approximates the Bayesian posterior expectation by weighting each hypothesis (or per-joint candidate) with exp(−α·L_reproj), where the 2D reprojection error serves as a likelihood proxy, after filtering to the top-K lowest-loss candidates.

## Results

On Human3.6M, FMPose3D with 40 hypotheses (N=40, using Flipped-Hypothesis Aggregation + RPEA) achieves 47.3 mm average MPJPE, outperforming the best prior probabilistic method DiffPose (49.7 mm) by ~4.8% relative improvement and the best deterministic method MLP-JCG (49.7 mm). On MPI-INF-3DHP (cross-dataset, no fine-tuning), FMPose3D (N=20) achieves 86.4% PCK and 54.6 AUC, surpassing ProPose (84.4% PCK, 52.1 AUC). On animal pose estimation, FMPose3D reaches 61.5 mm P-MPJPE on Animal3D (vs. 80.4 for AniMer) and 44.0 mm on CtrlAni3D (vs. 44.1 for AniMer). Inference speed is 145.6 FPS with N=40 hypotheses on an RTX 4090, approximately 5.4× faster than DiffPose (27.15 FPS with DDIM).

## Baselines & Comparisons

FMPose3D (N=40) achieves the best average MPJPE of 47.3 mm on Human3.6M, beating the strongest probabilistic baseline DiffPose (49.7 mm, N=5) by 2.4 mm (~4.8% relative) and the best deterministic method MLP-JCG (49.7 mm) by 2.4 mm. On MPI-INF-3DHP, FMPose3D (N=20) leads all methods with 86.4% PCK and 54.6 AUC, outperforming ProPose (84.4% PCK, 52.1 AUC) and UGRN (84.1% PCK, 53.7 AUC) without any fine-tuning on the target dataset. On Animal3D, FMPose3D reduces P-MPJPE from 80.4 mm (AniMer) to 61.5 mm, a 23.5% improvement, and matches AniMer on CtrlAni3D (44.0 vs. 44.1 mm) while substantially outperforming HMR2.0 (60.9 mm) and WLDO (71.5 mm). In inference speed, FMPose3D with N=40 hypotheses runs at 145.6 FPS, roughly 5.4× faster than DiffPose with DDIM (27.15 FPS) and 43× faster than DiffPose without DDIM (3.36 FPS), making it suitable for real-time applications where diffusion-based methods are impractical.

## Relation to Prior Work

FMPose3D sits at the intersection of two research threads: (1) probabilistic 2D-to-3D pose lifting, which has progressed from Mixture Density Networks and CVAEs through normalizing flows and GANs to diffusion-based models (DiffPose, D3DP, CHAMP), and (2) Flow Matching as a generative framework (Lipman et al., Liu et al., ICLR 2023) that replaces stochastic SDE denoising with deterministic ODE velocity-field transport. The paper's core departure is substituting the iterative SDE denoising of diffusion-based pose estimators with a few-step ODE integration, and it introduces RPEA—a reprojection-error-weighted Bayesian posterior expectation aggregation—as a principled replacement for the uniform averaging or best-of-N selection used in prior multi-hypothesis methods. Architecturally, the parallel GCN + self-attention backbone follows the local/global decomposition common in recent pose networks (GraphSH, GraFormer, UGRN) but applies it to a velocity-field prediction head rather than a direct regression head.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The practical significance is the inference-speed/accuracy trade-off: FMPose3D achieves 145 FPS with 40 hypotheses on a single RTX 4090, roughly 5.4× faster than DiffPose with DDIM, while matching or exceeding its accuracy—making multi-hypothesis 3D pose estimation viable in near-real-time settings where diffusion-based methods are impractical. The extension to animal pose estimation (Animal3D, CtrlAni3D) is notable because this domain has been dominated by shape-fitting (SMAL) approaches and lacks strong keypoint-based baselines, so a general-purpose lifting framework that transfers across species is a useful step. Open questions include whether the reprojection-error-as-likelihood proxy in RPEA remains well-calibrated under heavy occlusion or multi-person scenes, whether the 3-step Euler integration is sufficient for more complex pose distributions (e.g., full-body with hands), and whether the approach extends naturally to temporal (video) settings without additional modeling.
