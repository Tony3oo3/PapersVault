## Problem

Existing radar-camera BEV fusion methods suffer from three interrelated issues: (1) Lift-Splat-Shoot view transformation produces uneven BEV feature density (near-dense, far-sparse) due to lack of depth refinement, (2) 4D radar point clouds are inherently sparse and noisy, making direct feature fusion unreliable, and (3) spatial and semantic misalignment between radar and image BEV features degrades fusion quality. The paper argues that 4D radar's explicit physical measurements (RCS, Doppler velocity, height) have not been fully exploited to guide image-to-BEV transformation and to robustly encode radar features.

## Input / Output

The method consumes 4D radar point clouds (each point carrying x, y, z, Doppler velocity, and RCS) voxelized at 0.05 m × 0.05 m × 0.1 m resolution, together with multi-view camera images processed by a frozen Swin-Tiny backbone (pre-trained on DeepLabV3). BEV feature maps are generated at 0.4 m × 0.4 m spatial resolution over detection regions up to 70 m range (TJ4DRadSet) or 51.2 m (VoD). The output is 3D bounding boxes (Car, Pedestrian, Cyclist, Truck) with IoU thresholds of 0.5 for vehicles and 0.25 for vulnerable road users, evaluated in both 3D and BEV projection.

## Method

RPGFusion introduces three coupled mechanisms. First, radar prior maps (confidence and depth) are constructed directly in BEV space via Gaussian-kernel spreading of RCS-weighted radar points, then used to initialize image BEV queries through cross-modulation and to modulate attention weights during image-to-BEV sampling, where the radar-provided height z=h_g anchors vertical positioning for accurate projection. Second, a hybrid robust encoding pipeline extracts mean/median pillar statistics and neighborhood-weighted RCS confidence to suppress multipath noise, followed by a sparse-to-dense propagation that fills empty BEV cells using spatial-proximity and feature-similarity-weighted aggregation. Third, a two-stage deformable cross-attention module performs spatial alignment (learnable offsets within local neighborhoods) and semantic fusion (fixed-position cross-modal exchange), culminating in a sigmoid-gated unified fusion that adaptively weights image and radar contributions per BEV cell before feeding a 3D detection head.

## Results

On the View-of-Delft (VoD) validation set, RPGFusion achieves 69.31% mAP over the entire annotated area and 86.20% mAP within the driving corridor, surpassing the previous best method CVFusion by 3.90% and 3.78% respectively. On the TJ4DRadSet test set, RPGFusion attains 43.05% mAP for 3D detection and 46.86% mAP for BEV detection, exceeding CVFusion by 3.05% and 2.79%. The method also demonstrates backbone-agnostic superiority, outperforming all compared methods with ResNet-50, ResNet-101, and Swin-Tiny backbones on VoD.

## Baselines & Comparisons

RPGFusion outperforms all 14 compared baselines on both VoD and TJ4DRadSet. On VoD, it surpasses the strongest prior method CVFusion (65.41% EAA mAP) by 3.90% and the next-best RaGS (61.86%) by 7.45%, with the largest gains over early fusion methods like BEVFusion (49.25%) and RCFusion (49.65%). On TJ4DRadSet, RPGFusion leads CVFusion by 3.05% in 3D mAP (43.05% vs. 40.00%) and 2.79% in BEV mAP (46.86% vs. 44.07%), while radar-only methods (SMURF at 32.99% 3D mAP, RPFA-Net at 29.91%) lag by over 10 points. The method also wins across all three backbone configurations (ResNet-50, ResNet-101, Swin-Tiny) in the VoD backbone comparison, with margins of 15.53–19.29% over the best competing method per backbone. Notably, RPGFusion achieves the best per-category AP for Car, Pedestrian, and Cyclist simultaneously on both evaluation regions of VoD, indicating consistent gains rather than category-specific trade-offs.

## Relation to Prior Work

RPGFusion sits at the intersection of two established lines of work: BEV query-based image-to-BEV transformation (BEVFormer, RaCFormer) and 4D radar pillar encoding (SMURF, RPFA-Net, CVFusion). It departs from prior radar-camera fusion methods (RCFusion, RCBEVDet, CVFusion, HGSFusion) that treat radar and image features as parallel streams fused only at the BEV level, by instead injecting radar-derived confidence and depth priors directly into the image BEV query initialization and sampling step. The sparse-to-dense radar encoding and deformable cross-attention alignment modules draw on techniques from point-cloud densification and multi-modal transformer fusion, but their specific combination for 4D radar-camera BEV fusion is new.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The paper demonstrates that 4D radar can serve as a geometric guide for image feature sampling rather than merely a complementary feature source, which reframes how radar information is exploited in multi-sensor perception. This is practically significant as 4D radar is becoming a standard automotive sensor due to its weather robustness and Doppler/height measurements, yet its potential in fusion pipelines has been underutilized. Open questions include whether the radar-guided sampling strategy generalizes to LiDAR-camera or LiDAR-radar-camera tri-modal settings, whether the frozen-backbone protocol limits the method's ceiling, and whether the approach maintains its advantage under real-time deployment constraints on embedded hardware.
