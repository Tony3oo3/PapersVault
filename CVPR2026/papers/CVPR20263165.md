## Problem

Existing multimodal safety guards evaluate only the input question and final answer, leaving intermediate reasoning traces unmonitored, while text-only reasoning safety detectors lack visual grounding to identify threats tied to image content. No prior system provides end-to-end, vision-aware moderation across the full Question-Thinking-Answer (QTA) trajectory of multimodal large reasoning models.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/CVPR20263165/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The model receives a complete QTA triple: an RGB image paired with a text question, the full chain-of-thought reasoning trace produced by an external MLRM, and the final answer. It outputs a structured two-part response — a free-text safety Analysis explaining identified risks and a Judgment assigning one of three discrete labels (0 = Safe, 0.5 = Potentially Harmful, 1 = Harmful). The detector operates as a non-reasoning classifier, consuming the trace as input without generating its own intermediate steps, and for binary evaluation 0.5 and 1 are pooled as the positive (harmful) class.

## Method

GuardTrace-VL is built on Qwen2.5-VL-3B-Instruct and trained with a three-stage progressive pipeline: (1) SFT on ~4.6K unanimous-consensus samples to acquire core safety concepts; (2) DPO on ~4.9K majority-vote (2:1) samples to resolve ambiguous judgments via preference pairs; (3) Oracle-Guided Refined DPO (OGDPO), which constructs a final preference set from 726 hard negatives identified by re-evaluating DPO data with an external oracle (Qwen3-VL-Plus) that adjudicates model-vs-annotation conflicts, plus 287 expert-resolved 1:1:1 ambiguous cases. The supporting GuardTrace dataset (~11.8K QTA examples) is built by expanding text-only S-Eval safety queries into diverse multimodal inputs (irrelevant, semantically aligned, and jailbreak-typographic images), generating full QTA traces with multiple open- and closed-source MLRMs, and annotating via an ensemble of three MLLMs with human expert verification. This curriculum from coarse to fine-grained supervision enables the 3B model to outperform 12B+ guard models and closed-source LLMs by 4–14 F1 points on multimodal QTA safety detection.

## Results

GuardTrace-VL-3B achieves an average F1 of 93.10% across four GuardTrace-Test subsets (S-Eval-VL: 93.33%, HADES-Eval: 95.88%, MM-Eval: 91.31%, MMJ-Eval: 92.39%), representing a 13.5% F1 improvement over the strongest prior multimodal guard (LLaMA-4-Guard-12B at 79.55%). On the text-only ReasoningShield-Test benchmark, GuardTrace-VL attains 88.11% F1, slightly below the text-only specialist ReasoningShield (90.23%) but surpassing all other text baselines. The model's 3B parameter size yields these results despite being substantially smaller than competing guard models (7B–12B) and closed-source LLMs.

## Baselines & Comparisons

GuardTrace-VL-3B outperforms all nine baselines on the multimodal GuardTrace-Test benchmark. Against the strongest closed-source generative model, GPT-5 (avg F1 88.86%), GuardTrace-VL gains +4.24 F1 points, and over Qwen3-VL-Plus (87.54%) it gains +5.56. Among dedicated multimodal guard models, the gap is largest: LLaMA-4-Guard-12B (79.55%) is surpassed by +13.55 F1, GuardReasoner-VL-7B (74.32%) by +18.78, and LLaMA-3-Guard-11B-Vision (73.34%) by +19.76. The OpenAI Moderation API (64.86%) and the untrained Qwen2.5-VL-3B-Instruct (47.74%) are far behind, confirming that neither generic moderation APIs nor raw VLMs provide adequate QTA-level safety detection. On the text-only ReasoningShield-Test, GuardTrace-VL (88.11% F1) trails the text-only specialist ReasoningShield-3B (90.23%) by 2.12 points, but still exceeds WildGuard-7B and Qwen3-Guard-8B, indicating that multimodal training does not sacrifice text-only reasoning safety detection.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the safety-guard line of work (LLaMA-Guard, GuardReasoner-VL, ReasoningShield) by addressing a structural gap: existing multimodal guards evaluate only input-output QA pairs, while text-only reasoning guards (ReasoningShield) lack visual grounding. GuardTrace-VL is positioned as the first detector to jointly audit the full Question-Thinking-Answer trajectory in a vision-language setting, combining multimodal perception with reasoning-trace safety. Methodologically, it builds directly on DPO (Rafailov et al., 2023) and introduces an Oracle-Guided Refined DPO stage that uses an external oracle model to mine hard negatives, extending the standard SFT→DPO recipe with a targeted refinement step.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The GuardTrace benchmark (11.8K QTA examples with fine-grained safety labels) fills a concrete gap in multimodal safety evaluation and is likely to become a standard resource for trajectory-level safety research. The finding that a 3B model with task-specific data and iterative preference training outperforms 12B guard models and GPT-5 by 13.5% F1 suggests that curated supervision and training methodology can compensate for model scale in safety detection. Open questions include whether the approach generalizes to longer or more diverse reasoning formats, whether the reliance on open-source MLRMs for training data generation limits coverage of closed-model failure modes, and how the detector performs under adaptive attacks that specifically target the QTA boundary.
