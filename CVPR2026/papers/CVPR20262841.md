## Problem

Sketch-based caricature synthesis suffers from a fundamental ID-shape conflict: when identity and shape conditions are jointly injected into a diffusion model, they create condition signal contamination that causes the denoising trajectory to collapse toward either bland identity-preserving portraits or unrecognizable shape-following distortions. Prior controllable methods either require expensive per-sample fine-tuning (DemoCaricature, ~70 s per identity) or constrain sketches to fixed Bezier curves (CaricatureBooth), and none explicitly resolve the underlying signal interference.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/CVPR20262841/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method takes an identity reference photo, a free-hand sketch in any format (edge maps, rough drawings, no preprocessing or Bezier fitting required), and a text prompt, producing a 768×768 RGB caricature in under 16 seconds on a single RTX 4090. Unlike CaricatureBooth, there is no constraint on sketch topology or number of curves, and unlike DemoCaricature, no per-identity optimization is performed at inference time.

## Method

CaricHarmony is a training-free framework built on SDXL that maintains three parallel denoising paths during inference: a pure-identity path P^i (conditioned only on the reference photo via PuLID's ID encoder), a pure-shape path P^s (conditioned only on the sketch via T2I-Sketch-Adapter), and a harmonized output path P^{i+s} conditioned on both. At each denoising step, two cross-attention energy functions steer P^{i+s} toward balance: E_shape aligns query features Q with Q^s using stroke-confidence-weighted layout energy plus a semantic attention-map energy, while E_id performs token-level correspondence matching that, for each output token o_j, retrieves the best-matching target token o_k^i via cosine similarity of query tokens and applies a confidence-scaled L2 penalty. The combined energy E_b = E_shape + E_id is injected as a gradient term on top of classifier-free guidance, with timestep-constrained activation (E_shape active at t ∈ [700, 1000], E_id at t ∈ [400, 900]) to respect the coarse-to-fine denoising hierarchy.

## Results

On the WebCaricature dataset (1216 samples), CaricHarmony achieves the highest shape fidelity score (S-CLIP 0.8615 vs. 0.8450 for DemoCaricature) with a competitive identity consistency score (I-CLIP 0.7512 vs. 0.7591). It substantially outperforms all baselines on aesthetic quality: ImageReward 0.8509 (vs. 0.2871 for DemoCaricature) and PickScore 0.2049 (vs. 0.0949). In the user study (16 raters, 200 samples), CaricHarmony achieves the highest overall preference score of 7.81 (vs. 6.06 for DemoCaricature), with the best shape consistency (8.08) and ID consistency (6.83) ratings.

## Baselines & Comparisons

CaricHarmony outperforms DemoCaricature (the closest controllable baseline) on shape fidelity (S-CLIP 0.8615 vs. 0.8450), ImageReward (0.8509 vs. 0.2871), and PickScore (0.2049 vs. 0.0949), while achieving a marginally lower I-CLIP (0.7512 vs. 0.7591) that the authors attribute to CLIP's inability to handle caricature domain gaps. In the user study, CaricHarmony beats DemoCaricature on all three criteria: ID (6.83 vs. 6.03), Shape (8.08 vs. 5.51), and Overall (7.81 vs. 6.06). Against the warping-based baselines (StyleCariGAN, WarpGAN, AutoToon), CaricHarmony dominates on every reported metric, as these methods lack explicit shape controllability and produce lower aesthetic quality. CaricatureBooth is excluded from quantitative comparison because it only accepts fixed Bezier curves and cannot process free-hand sketches or edge maps used in the evaluation protocol.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper builds on PuLID's contrastive alignment mechanism for identity injection via cross-attention and T2I-Adapter's spatial conditioning pipeline, repurposing both as components of a training-free caricature synthesis framework. It departs from DemoCaricature (which requires 70-second per-identity fine-tuning and ignores the ID-shape conflict) and CaricatureBooth (which constrains sketches to fixed Bezier curves and requires large-scale synthetic pre-training) by reframing the core problem as 'condition signal contamination' in the denoising trajectory and resolving it through parallel uncontaminated diffusion paths with cross-attention energy functions. The conceptual lineage traces from classifier-free guidance and energy-based guidance in diffusion models, but the specific application of maintaining three simultaneous denoising paths with token-level correspondence matching for identity alignment under extreme shape distortion is novel.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The training-free, format-agnostic approach (16 seconds, any sketch) makes professional-quality caricature synthesis practically accessible, removing the per-sample optimization bottleneck that limited prior controllable methods. The 'condition signal contamination' framing is a useful diagnostic that could generalize to other multi-condition diffusion tasks where competing signals cause trajectory collapse. However, evaluation is limited to a single dataset (WebCaricature, 1216 samples), the I-CLIP score is slightly below DemoCaricature (0.7512 vs. 0.7591), and the large ImageReward gap (0.8509 vs. 0.4340) is partly attributable to the SDXL base model rather than the guidance mechanism itself. Open questions include whether the contamination-resolution strategy transfers to other multi-condition generation tasks and whether the energy functions remain effective with different base architectures or higher-resolution outputs.
