## Problem

SDS-based 3D generation relies on 2D diffusion priors that lack 3D geometric understanding, causing spatial hallucinations, the Janus problem, and multi-view inconsistency. Complex text prompts exacerbate these issues through gradient competition and guidance collapse, as existing methods apply a single fixed prompt throughout optimization without structured decomposition or iterative correction.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/CVPR20261881/cropped_page1_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

For text-to-3D, the method takes a natural language prompt and produces a 3D asset (Gaussian splatting or NeRF) evaluated over 16 uniformly sampled views at 360° azimuth. For image-to-3D, a single 256×256 RGB image serves as the conditioning input. During optimization, the MLLM consumes multi-view renderings paired with structured prompt templates to generate hierarchical descriptions, quality scores, and negative prompts that feed back into the correction loop.

## Method

Thoughtful3D introduces a dual-phase structural CoT strategy. 3DBlueprint-CoT decomposes the prompt into object, attribute, and abstract-concept components via an MLLM, then plans K=3 progressive optimization stages where sub-prompts increase in complexity with linearly interpolated transition windows. 3DRefine-CoT operates during training: a reflection phase uses the MLLM to score multi-view renderings, flag low-quality views, and generate negative prompts via contrastive analysis against reference views; a correction phase applies DDIM sampling with those negative prompts and selects the best candidate through multi-MLLM majority voting, then trains the 3D model with an MSE loss toward the corrected image. A Cross-view Semantic Appearance Alignment loss enforces consistency by computing cross-attention similarity matrices between image query features and text key features across views, retrieving Top-K matched features per text token, and penalizing L2 divergence of those features between view pairs. The total training objective combines SDS, the refinement MSE, and the alignment loss with learned hyperparameters.

## Results

On text-to-3D generation (100 ChatGPT prompts, 16 views per object), Thoughtful3D consistently improves CLIP scores across all five baselines. The strongest baseline, GaussianDreamer, improves from 21.45/26.71/27.33 (B/32, B/16, L/14) to 24.65/29.15/30.39 with Thoughtful3D, while the weakest, Fantasia3D, jumps from 16.71/20.45/21.28 to 23.33/26.75/27.26. In the 30-participant user study, Thoughtful3D-augmented methods achieve the highest alignment, quality, and consistency scores (e.g., SJC+Thoughtful3D: 8.36/9.10/8.25 vs. SJC alone: 5.54/5.08/5.71). On image-to-3D (GSO + Objaverse-XL), adding Thoughtful3D to Wonder3D reduces Chamfer Distance from 0.1335 to 0.1297 and improves Vol. IoU from 0.4025 to 0.4343.

## Baselines & Comparisons

Thoughtful3D is a plug-and-play enhancement that improves every baseline it is applied to, rather than competing as a standalone method. The largest absolute CLIP gains appear for weaker baselines: Fantasia3D improves by +6.62 (B/32) and DreamFusion-IF by +5.58, while the already-strong GaussianDreamer still gains +3.20 (B/32) and +3.06 (L/14). In user-study ratings, the quality score improvement is most dramatic for SJC (5.08→9.10) and Fantasia3D (5.26→9.03), suggesting the CoT reasoning and cross-view alignment modules are especially effective at correcting structural hallucinations in less robust pipelines. On the image-to-3D task, Thoughtful3D applied to Wonder3D outperforms Zero123+Thoughtful3D on all geometric metrics (CD 0.1297 vs. 0.1398; Vol. IoU 0.4343 vs. 0.3714), indicating the framework's gains are not limited to text-conditioned generation. Across all comparisons, no baseline outperforms its Thoughtful3D-augmented counterpart on any reported metric.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the SDS-based 3D generation pipeline (DreamFusion and its successors like Magic3D, GaussianDreamer, SJC) by introducing MLLM-driven Chain-of-Thought reasoning as an external planning and correction layer, rather than modifying the diffusion model architecture itself. It draws on the CoT paradigm established in NLP (Wei et al.) and its recent transfer to 2D image generation (LayoutGPT, ImageGen-CoT), but is the first to apply structured CoT to the iterative optimization of 3D representations. Unlike multi-view consistency methods that embed 3D priors into the diffusion model (Wonder3D, SyncDreamer, MVDream), Thoughtful3D operates as a plug-and-play wrapper that works atop any existing SDS pipeline, complementing rather than replacing architectural modifications.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The Janus problem and guidance collapse under complex prompts remain persistent failure modes in SDS-based 3D generation, and this paper demonstrates that structured reasoning (prompt decomposition + iterative MLLM reflection) can substantially mitigate both without retraining the base diffusion model. The cross-view semantic alignment loss offers a lightweight, architecture-agnostic mechanism for enforcing geometric consistency that could be adopted independently. Open questions include the computational overhead of multiple MLLM calls per optimization round, scalability to multi-object scenes, and whether the approach transfers to emerging feed-forward 3D generation paradigms that bypass SDS entirely.
