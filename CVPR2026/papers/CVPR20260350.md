## Problem

Current spatial VLMs lack an implicit grounding mechanism that automatically associates free-form textual mentions with visual regions during generation, and monocular 3D grounding from a single view remains ill-posed due to entangled depth, scale, and camera intrinsics. Existing approaches either bypass the intermediate 2D localization step, rely on multi-view supervision, or are constrained by the scarcity of 3D box annotations, leaving a gap between grounded 2D perception and reliable 3D inference.

## Input / Output

The model consumes a single RGB image and a natural-language instruction, and produces 2D bounding boxes and 3D bounding boxes (center, dimensions, normalized Euler angles) in a unified HTML-style textual format via the language head, without any separate detection branch. During chain-of-thought generation, the model dynamically inserts region tokens into the text stream, so the output is an interleaved sequence of text and visual evidence. 3D predictions are in camera-relative coordinates, and the model applies intrinsic-aware normalization (rescaling image dimensions by focal length) to handle varying camera parameters across datasets.

## Method

GR3D builds on NVILA-8B-Lite with SR-3D's region-prompt design and introduces three complementary grounding capabilities in a unified architecture. The key mechanism is streaming region insertion for implicit 2D grounding: during CoT generation, when an entity is mentioned, the model predicts its 2D bounding box, encodes the corresponding image region into a token via a region encoder, and injects that token back into the ongoing text stream, enabling reasoning to proceed over grounded visual evidence without a separate detection phase. For monocular 3D grounding, each grounded 2D region serves as a spatial query for 3D box prediction, supported by intrinsic-aware normalization and dense geometric supervision from depth-derived pointmap reconstruction (e.g., 100 sampled surface points per image). Training proceeds in two stages: spatial pretraining (freezing the visual encoder while training the projector, positional encoding, and LLM on a mixture of grounding and 3D detection data) followed by detection CoT finetuning (fine-tuning only the LLM on CoT-formatted detection data), using 97K grounded CoT samples, 780K 3D detection samples, and 272K pointmap reconstruction samples.

## Results

On the Omni3D benchmark, GR3D-8B achieves an overall AP3D of 25.40, outperforming the best vision-specialist baseline DetAny3D (24.92) and all VLM baselines. The gains are most pronounced on indoor datasets: GR3D reaches 31.64 mAP on SUN-RGBD (vs. 18.96 for DetAny3D) and 52.52 mAP on ARKITSCENES (vs. 46.13). On spatial VQA, GR3D-8B after Stage 1 pretraining improves CVBench average from 86.31 to 87.23 and ERQA from 36.25 to 40.25 over the NVILA-Lite-8B base. On the MM-GCoT grounded CoT benchmark, GR3D achieves 78.3% answer accuracy, 74.2% grounding accuracy, and 67.7% consistency, surpassing LLaVA-GCoT-7B (74.5/63.3/58.1) and Qwen2.5-VL-7B (73.1/64.3/56.8).

## Baselines & Comparisons

GR3D outperforms all vision-specialist baselines on overall AP3D: 25.40 vs. DetAny3D (24.92), Cube R-CNN (23.26), OVMono3D (22.98), SMOKE (10.4), and ImVoxelNet (9.4). Against the strongest specialist DetAny3D, GR3D wins decisively on indoor domains (SUN-RGBD mAP 31.64 vs. 18.96; ARKITSCENES mAP 52.52 vs. 46.13; HYPERSIM mAP 10.87 vs. 7.17) but trails on outdoor driving datasets (KITTI mAP 14.75 vs. 31.61; NUSCENES mAP 16.59 vs. 30.97), which the authors attribute to the imbalanced indoor-heavy training distribution. Among VLMs, GR3D surpasses Qwen3-VL-8B on every reported per-dataset metric (e.g., SUN-RGBD mAP 31.64 vs. 17.77; ARKITSCENES mAP 52.52 vs. 45.23) and is the only method to report a competitive AP3D score. On the BLINK-Depth spatial reasoning benchmark, GR3D reaches 94.4% accuracy, beating SR3D-8B (90.3%), SpatialRGPT-8B (87.9%), and all LLaVA/CogVLM variants (50.8–52.4%). On MM-GCoT, GR3D leads all baselines in answer accuracy (78.3%), grounding accuracy (74.2%), and consistency (67.7%), with the largest margin over LLaVA-7B (70.0/9.8/10.1) and a clear gap over Qwen2.5-VL-7B (73.1/64.3/56.8).

## Relation to Prior Work

GR3D extends the spatial VLM lineage from SpatialRGPT and SR-3D by adding two capabilities those systems lack: implicit 2D grounding via streaming region insertion during chain-of-thought generation, and monocular 3D grounding conditioned on region prompts. Architecturally it builds on NVILA-8B-Lite as the base encoder-projector-LLM stack and preserves SR-3D's region-prompt design, but departs from prior VLMs (e.g., Qwen3-VL) that attempt 3D detection in a single undifferentiated step by explicitly decomposing the problem into 2D grounding followed by 3D box regression. The streaming region insertion mechanism also distinguishes GR3D from 'Thinking with Images' approaches that rely on external tools or explicit visual thought steps, instead embedding grounding natively within the generative language stream.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The paper demonstrates that grounding functions as an effective inductive bias for spatial understanding more broadly, not merely for the grounding task itself—implicit grounding improves performance on non-grounded spatial benchmarks (CVBench, ERQA, SAT) and the 2D→3D decomposition yields a ~12 AP gain over direct 3D prediction. This suggests a general principle for spatial VLMs: forcing the model to localize before reasoning provides a stable geometric anchor that transfers across tasks. Open questions include whether the approach scales to multi-view and embodied settings (the paper notes multi-view results are in supplementary), how sensitive performance is to the noisy Florence-2-generated training annotations, and whether the gap to specialized detectors on outdoor domains (KITTI, NuScenes) can be closed with more 3D data or better intrinsic handling.
