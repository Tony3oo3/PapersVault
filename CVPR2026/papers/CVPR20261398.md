## Problem

Existing in-context image generation methods, whether inpainting-based (global-canvas encoding) or T2I-based (per-panel encoding), are panel-agnostic at the attention level: tokens from different panels receive no explicit signal distinguishing intra-panel from inter-panel interactions. This forces standard PEFT adapters like LoRA to simultaneously learn cross-panel relation transfer and preserve the backbone's pre-trained intra-panel synthesis geometry, creating a dual burden that degrades adaptation quality.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/CVPR20261398/cropped_page1_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method operates on a tiled-panel layout where a source reference image (resized to 512×512) and a target query panel are arranged side-by-side on a 512×1024 canvas, conditioned by a text instruction. OPRO is inserted as a lightweight module (+0.93M parameters at ρ=32) into the attention layers of a frozen diffusion transformer backbone (e.g., FluxFill or FLUX.1), producing an edited image in the target panel. The same architecture generalizes to multi-panel configurations (up to 4×4 grids) for compositional reasoning tasks.

## Method

OPRO applies learnable, panel-specific orthogonal operators U_p ∈ SO(d_h) to the backbone's frozen position-aware queries and keys, so that the attention score between tokens i and j becomes q̃_i^T (U_{p(i)}^T U_{p(j)}) k̃_j / √d_h. Two exact guarantees follow: isometry (orthogonality preserves vector norms, preventing unintended rescaling of attention logits) and same-panel invariance (when p(i)=p(j), the relative operator collapses to identity, leaving intra-panel attention identical to the pre-trained backbone). The operators are parameterized efficiently via low-rank skew-symmetric generators A_p = L_p R_p^T − R_p L_p^T in the Lie algebra so(d_h), exponentiated to yield dense orthogonal transforms, with zero-interference initialization (L_p = 0) ensuring the adapter starts as an exact identity mapping. Combined with LoRA (r=8), OPRO adds only 0.111M parameters on ViT-B and consistently outperforms LoRA alone across APE, RoPE, LieRE, and ComRoPE positional encoding regimes.

## Results

On the MagicBrush test set, OPRO (ρ=32, +0.93M parameters) consistently improves all four baselines across L1↓, CLIP-I↑, and DINO↑. The largest gain is on ICEdit, where L1 drops by 34.31% (0.1189→0.0781), CLIP-I rises from 0.8703 to 0.9002, and DINO jumps from 0.7706 to 0.8531. On the controlled two-stage compositional reasoning benchmark, LoRA+OPRO outperforms LoRA alone by up to +18.0% top-1 accuracy (ComRoPE, 4×4 grid), with gains increasing as task difficulty grows.

## Baselines & Comparisons

OPRO is evaluated as a plug-in module on four state-of-the-art ICG baselines spanning both inpainting-based (global-canvas) and T2I-based (per-panel) paradigms. Against ICEdit (NeurIPS'25), OPRO delivers the most dramatic improvement: L1 drops from 0.1189 to 0.0781 (−34.31%), CLIP-I rises to 0.9002, and DINO surges to 0.8531, indicating substantially better context preservation and edit fidelity. Against UNO (ICCV'25), the strongest absolute baseline, OPRO still reduces L1 from 0.0575 to 0.0387 (−32.7%) and nudges CLIP-I and DINO upward. Against ACE++ and InsertAnything (both arXiv'25), OPRO wins on all three metrics with moderate gains (e.g., ACE++ DINO: 0.7394→0.7767; InsertAnything DINO: 0.7917→0.8009). In every comparison, OPRO adds only 0.93M trainable parameters (4.16% of ICEdit's 22.4M, 0.20% of UNO's 478.2M), confirming that the orthogonal panel-relative modulation is both effective and parameter-efficient across model scales.

## Relation to Prior Work

OPRO sits at the intersection of tiled-panel in-context image generation (IC-LoRA, Diptych, ICEdit, UNO, ACE++, InsertAnything) and orthogonal relative positional encodings (RoPE, LieRE, ComRoPE). Its core departure is that all existing ICG methods are panel-agnostic at the attention level—tokens from different panels receive no explicit structural signal—forcing adapters to simultaneously learn inter-panel transfer and preserve intra-panel synthesis. OPRO resolves this dual burden by applying panel-specific orthogonal operators (parameterized via the Lie algebra exponential map from Lezcano-Casado & Martinez-Rubio, 2019) to frozen position-aware queries and keys, with a zero-interference initialization strategy borrowed from ControlNet. Unlike standard LoRA, which updates shared projections without panel awareness, OPRO's relative operator U_{p(i)}^T U_{p(j)} collapses to identity for same-panel pairs, providing an exact invariance guarantee that no prior PEFT method for ICG offers.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The two exact structural guarantees (isometry and same-panel invariance) give OPRO a clean theoretical foundation that standard PEFT lacks, and the consistent gains across four positional-encoding families (APE, RoPE, LieRE, ComRoPE) and two ICG paradigms (inpainting-based and T2I-based) suggest the technique is broadly applicable to any tiled-panel diffusion pipeline. The extreme parameter efficiency (0.93M params, ~0.2% of UNO's 478M) makes it a practical drop-in module. Key limitations include the fixed panel layout assumption (new layouts require new operators), modest increases in inference latency from the extra orthogonal transforms, and the fact that the largest gains appear on weaker baselines (ICEdit) rather than the strongest (UNO), leaving open whether OPRO's benefits saturate as backbone capacity grows.
