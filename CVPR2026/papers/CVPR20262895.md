## Problem

Unified HSI models like HSI-GPT still lag behind specialist models on both understanding and generation due to three core limitations: single-granularity VQ codebooks that overemphasize low-level motion details while neglecting text-aligned semantics, limited decoding capacity of direct VQVAE detokenizers that cannot satisfy physical constraints like stable contacts and collision avoidance, and reliance on supervised fine-tuning alone that restricts the model to passive imitation rather than compositional and long-range reasoning.

## Input / Output

The model consumes text prompts, 3D scene geometry (xyz + color, N_s×6) with computed affordance maps, and optionally partial human motion sequences (SMPL-X). It adopts a continuous-input, discrete-output scheme: pre-quantized continuous motion features are projected into the LLM's textual space via motion projectors, while the LLM outputs discrete semantic and detail tokens. The generated output is structured with CoT reasoning enclosed in think tags followed by motion tokens in answer tags, which are then decoded by a latent diffusion model into full 3D human motions grounded in the scene.

## Method

HSI-GPT2 introduces three coupled components. First, a Dual-Granularity Motion Tokenizer (DMoTok) pretrains a CLIP-style contrastive encoder to extract text-aligned semantic features, quantizes them into a semantic codebook, and in parallel encodes fine-grained kinematic details into a separate detail codebook, decoupling semantic fidelity from physical articulation. Second, an LLM+Diffusion hybrid architecture uses the LLM as a semantic planner that generates interleaved language, semantic, and detail tokens autoregressively, while a latent diffusion decoder (MLD) conditioned on scene queries and LLM tokens iteratively denoises motion latents to produce physically grounded motions with higher fidelity and diversity than direct VQVAE decoding. Third, a Motion Chain-of-Thought (MoCoT) data engine distills Qwen3-VL-235B into structured reasoning trajectories grounded in rendered SMPL-X video, and a GRPO-based RL stage optimizes the policy with verifiable multi-aspect rewards (format compliance, motion fidelity via detail-encoder cosine similarity, and semantic alignment via CLIP-style encoder) to encourage generalized reasoning beyond SFT imitation.

## Results

On HumanML3D, HSI-GPT2 achieves R-Precision Top-1/Top-3 of 0.545/0.835, FID of 0.139, MM-Dist of 2.708, Diversity of 10.489, and MModality of 2.891, surpassing all LLM-based unified baselines (e.g., HSI-GPT: 0.495/0.788, FID 0.187) and matching or exceeding specialized generators on semantic alignment and diversity. On HUMANISE, HSI-GPT2 attains a Goal Distance of 0.143 (+21.4% over HSI-GPT), APD of 4.876 (+39.7%), and Contact Rate of 97.98% (+5.67%). On the Novel Evaluation Set (unseen scenes), HSI-GPT2 improves R-P@3 by +6.3%, reduces FID by −24.2%, and raises Contact Rate by +4.0% over HSI-GPT, demonstrating strong out-of-distribution generalization.

## Baselines & Comparisons

HSI-GPT2 outperforms its direct predecessor HSI-GPT [53] across all metrics on HumanML3D (R-P@3: 0.835 vs 0.788, FID: 0.139 vs 0.187, Diversity: 10.489 vs 9.845) and on HUMANISE (Goal Dist: 0.143 vs 0.182, Contact: 97.98% vs 92.31%). Among LLM-based unified models, it beats MotionGPT-3 [76] (FID 0.139 vs 0.217, MModality 2.891 vs 1.366), Motion-R1 [38] (R-P@3 0.835 vs 0.818, FID 0.139 vs 0.201), and MotionAgent [57] (FID 0.139 vs 0.491). Against specialized non-LLM generators, HSI-GPT2 leads on semantic alignment (R-P@3) and diversity but trails MotionAnything [72] on FID (0.139 vs 0.028), indicating that the diffusion decoder still has a gap in raw motion realism compared to purpose-built diffusion baselines. On the unseen-scene Novel Evaluation Set, HSI-GPT2 consistently surpasses HSI-GPT and Afford-Motion [55] in R-Precision, FID, MM-Dist, and Contact Rate, confirming that the MoCoT + GRPO pipeline generalizes beyond in-distribution training scenes.

## Relation to Prior Work

HSI-GPT2 is the direct sequel to HSI-GPT (CVPR 2025), inheriting its unified scene-motion-language architecture and pre-trained scene encoder/Q-Former aggregator, but replacing the single-granularity VQ-VAE tokenizer with a dual-granularity DMoTok (CLIP-aligned semantic codebook + detail codebook) and swapping direct VQVAE decoding for an MLD-based latent diffusion decoder. The RL training stage adapts GRPO from DeepSeekMath to the HSI domain for the first time, while the MoCoT data engine extends the Motion-R1 CoT paradigm by grounding reasoning in rendered SMPL-X video rather than unimodal text. Conceptually, the paper sits at the intersection of the unified-MLLM-for-motion line (MotionGPT series, M3GPT) and the emerging LLM+Diffusion hybrid paradigm (BLIP3-o, Illume+), applying both to the physically-constrained HSI setting.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

By decoupling semantic planning (LLM) from physical synthesis (diffusion) and adding verifiable-reward RL, HSI-GPT2 addresses the long-standing gap where unified motion-language models underperform specialist generators on both fidelity and diversity. The MoCoT + GRPO pipeline is the first demonstration that chain-of-thought reasoning with multi-aspect motion-aware rewards can improve HSI generation beyond SFT imitation, opening a path toward interpretable, goal-directed motion synthesis. Open questions include whether the RL reward design (cosine-similarity-based fidelity and semantic scores) scales to longer-horizon or multi-agent interactions, and whether the dual-granularity tokenization generalizes to other articulated-body or object-interaction domains beyond human-scene settings.
