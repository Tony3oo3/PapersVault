## Problem

Generating multi-view consistent (MVC) 3D Gaussian head avatars in a minimal-resource setting—without multi-view captures, 3D ground truth, or intermediate view synthesis—remains unsolved. Existing feedforward 3D generators lack explicit MVC enforcement, while multi-view diffusion pipelines are computationally prohibitive at scale and suffer from identity drift across synthesized views. The paper tackles this gap by asking whether MVC can be induced purely through architectural design and a learned consistency critic, using only 2D face images as supervision.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/CVPR20260363/cropped_page0_idx1.webp)

## Input / Output

For unconditional generation, the model takes a latent code z ~ N(0, I) and produces a set of 240K anisotropic 3D Gaussians organized across L hierarchical levels, renderable from arbitrary SE(3) camera poses at 512×512 resolution. For conditional (personalized) generation, a single face image is inverted via optimization to recover a latent code while preserving identity through an ArcFace loss. Training supervision comes exclusively from 2D face images (FFHQ, FFHQ-C); no 3D labels, multi-view pairs, or intermediate synthesized views are used at any point.

## Method

MVCHead is a single-shot state space model built on GS-GAN's hierarchical Gaussian framework, replacing the transformer backbone with Hierarchical State Space (HiSS) blocks that progressively refine Gaussians from coarse anchors to fine details. Within each HiSS block, a Dual-Mixer combines self-attention (for global identity cues) with a proposed Hierarchical Bi-directional State Scan (HiBiSS) that runs four 2D recurrences (→, ←, ↓, ↑) aligned to the image axes where multi-view drift is strongest—horizontal for yaw, vertical for pitch—implementing anisotropic, pose-aware smoothing. A key training innovation is the SE(3) Multi-view Critic, a ViT-style encoder augmented with Geometric Transform Attention that learns to score whether a set of self-renders arises from a single 3D configuration, providing a differentiable MVC reward without ever observing real multi-view pairs. The total loss combines this critic, a camera-conditioned adversarial texture term, and spatial regularizers (KNN spacing and center-drift penalties), all trained jointly for 10M steps on 4×H100 GPUs.

## Results

On FFHQ, MVCHead achieves a FID of 4.39 and FID3D of 4.39, outperforming the prior SOTA CGS-GAN (4.94/4.94) and GGHead (5.15/7.90). On FFHQ-C, MVCHead reaches FID 3.94 and FID3D 3.94, again leading CGS-GAN (4.53/4.53). For multi-view consistency (Table 3), MVCHead surpasses CGS-GAN on all texture metrics (cPSNR 22.08 vs 21.85, cSSIM 0.764 vs 0.743, cLPIPS 0.053 vs 0.062) and geometric consistency (MEt3R 0.262 vs 0.281), while shape consistency (CD 0.665 vs 0.672) is comparable.

## Baselines & Comparisons

MVCHead outperforms the closest competitor CGS-GAN [6] on every reported metric: FID improves from 4.94→4.39 (FFHQ) and 4.53→3.94 (FFHQ-C), FID3D from 4.94→4.39 and 4.53→3.94, and MEt3R (geometric consistency) from 0.281→0.262. Against GGHead [44], MVCHead gains 0.76 FID on FFHQ and 1.43 FID on FFHQ-C, with a much larger FID3D gap (7.90→4.39 on FFHQ). GS-GAN [38], the architectural base, is surpassed by 1.21 FID on FFHQ and 1.23 on FFHQ-C, and by 6.11 FID3D on FFHQ. On the MVC-specific metrics (Table 3), MVCHead beats CGS-GAN on cPSNR (+0.23), cSSIM (+0.020), cLPIPS (−0.009), and MEt3R (−0.019), while depth error is essentially tied (6.665 vs 6.662). Older 3D-aware GANs (EG3D, StyleNeRF, Mimic3D) and 3DGS-based methods (GSM) are clearly outperformed, with FID gaps of 0.3–24 points, confirming that the minimal-resource, single-shot SSM approach is competitive with or superior to methods that rely on multi-view data or intermediate view synthesis.

## Relation to Prior Work

MVCHead builds directly on the hierarchical 3D Gaussian generation framework of GS-GAN, replacing its transformer backbone with a state space model (Mamba/SS2D) and introducing the HiBiSS bidirectional scan aligned to the principal axes of multi-view drift. It departs from the dominant multi-view diffusion paradigm (Cap4D, FaceLift, SpinMeRound) by eliminating intermediate view synthesis entirely, and from prior feedforward generators (GGHead, CGS-GAN) by adding a learned SE(3) Multi-view Critic that enforces cross-view consistency without any multi-view supervision. The use of Geometric Transform Attention in the critic borrows from multi-view transformer literature, while the AdaIN-based appearance conditioning follows CGS-GAN's disentanglement strategy.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The paper provides the first comprehensive quantitative MVC evaluation protocol for 3D head avatars (adapting MVG-Bench and MEt3R) and releases FaceGS-10K, the first large-scale dataset of ready-to-use 3D Gaussian head assets, both of which fill notable gaps in the field. The core insight that self-renders from a fixed 3D model are geometrically consistent 'by construction'—enabling a critic to be trained without any real multi-view pairs—is an elegant reframing that could generalize to other 3D generation tasks. Limitations include coverage of only front/side views (no 360°), geometric priors learned purely from 2D supervision, and the critic's reliance on relatively easy negatives (different identities rather than geometrically perturbed views of the same identity), leaving room for stronger consistency enforcement.
