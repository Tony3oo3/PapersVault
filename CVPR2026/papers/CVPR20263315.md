## Problem

Existing temporal camera-based SSC methods simply stack multi-frame features without explicit spatio-temporal correspondence modeling, leading to geometric-semantic inconsistencies and ambiguous predictions in occluded regions. The paper asks how temporal 2D visibility cues and 3D voxel-level similarity can be jointly leveraged to refine occupancy and produce spatially and temporally consistent semantic completion.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/CVPR20263315/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method consumes T=4 sequential RGB frames (1226×370 on SemanticKITTI, 1408×376 on KITTI-360) along with depth maps estimated by a frozen MobileStereoNet and per-frame segmentation masks from a frozen MobileSAM. It outputs a dense 256×256×32 voxel grid (0.2 m resolution, covering 51.2 m × 51.2 m × 6.4 m) with per-voxel semantic class probabilities over 20 (SemanticKITTI) or 19 (KITTI-360) categories including free space.

## Method

ConSSC introduces two complementary modules. The Hierarchical Voxel Refinement (HVR) module first generates a coarse occupancy by fusing current and historical depth maps weighted by a Gaussian depth-consistency score, then applies a Unified Spatiotemporal Refinement unit that computes a learnable feature-weighted squared difference between current and historical voxel features, converts it to a similarity score, and produces a sigmoid soft mask to selectively inject relevant historical voxel information; remaining hallucinated regions are filled via Deformable Cross-Attention. The Temporal Semantic Aggregation (TSA) module extracts MobileSAM segmentation masks per frame, computes pixelwise inner-product correspondences across frames, derives softmax-normalized temporal stability weights, and aggregates SAM features using block attention with a learnable time-aware positional bias; the aggregated 2D features are then back-projected into 3D via depth probability distributions and fused with the HVR-refined voxels. The combined voxel features pass through a voxel encoder and a scene-adaptive decoder before a task head predicts the final semantic occupancy. Training uses a ResNet-50 backbone, AdamW optimizer, and a Hungarian-matched loss combining mask, Dice, and classification terms.

## Results

On the SemanticKITTI hidden test set, ConSSC achieves an IoU of 48.17% and mIoU of 19.20%, setting a new state-of-the-art among camera-based SSC methods. On SSCBench-KITTI-360, it reaches IoU 48.79% and mIoU 20.85%, again outperforming all prior camera-based approaches. The method uses only 60.9M parameters and 891.47 GFLOPs, substantially fewer parameters than HTCL-S (181.4M) and OccFormer (202.7M) while delivering the highest accuracy.

## Baselines & Comparisons

ConSSC outperforms the strongest prior temporal method SOAP (IoU 47.54, mIoU 18.72) by 0.63% IoU and 0.48% mIoU on SemanticKITTI, and by 0.31% IoU and 0.68% mIoU on SSCBench-KITTI-360. Against the best single-frame methods, ConSSC exceeds ScanSSC by 3.63% IoU and 1.80% mIoU, and CGFormer by 3.76% IoU and 2.57% mIoU on SemanticKITTI, demonstrating the substantial benefit of explicit temporal consistency modeling. Among temporal baselines, ConSSC surpasses HTCL-S by 3.94% IoU and 2.11% mIoU, and VoxFormer-T by 4.96% IoU and 5.79% mIoU, indicating that simply stacking multi-frame features (VoxFormer-T) or using cross-frame affinity (HTCL-S) is insufficient compared to the proposed hierarchical refinement and visibility-aware semantic aggregation. On SSCBench-KITTI-360, ConSSC leads all camera-based methods, beating the next-best SOAP by 0.31% IoU and 0.68% mIoU, while LiDAR-based SSCNet (IoU 53.58) remains higher, confirming the inherent difficulty of camera-only perception. In terms of efficiency, ConSSC (60.9M params, 891 GFLOPs) is comparable to Symphonies (59.3M, 612 GFLOPs) yet achieves 7% higher IoU, and is far lighter than HTCL-S (181.4M, 2622 GFLOPs) while delivering 3.6% higher IoU.

## Relation to Prior Work

ConSSC extends the camera-based 3D Semantic Scene Completion line initiated by MonoScene and advanced by sparse voxel Transformers (VoxFormer, Symphonies, BRGScene) and geometry-aware methods (CGFormer, ScanSSC). It departs from prior temporal approaches (VoxFormer-T, HTCL-S, SOAP) that simply stack or cross-attend multi-frame voxel features by introducing an explicit coarse-to-fine hierarchy: depth-guided occupancy initialization followed by similarity-filtered voxel refinement, plus a SAM-driven multi-view semantic aggregation stage. The 2D-to-3D lifting follows Lift-Splat-Shoot, and the final decoding reuses SOAP's scene-adaptive decoder, positioning ConSSC as a targeted architectural refinement rather than a new paradigm.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The paper demonstrates that explicit spatial-temporal consistency—enforced through depth-anchored occupancy and similarity-gated historical voxel fusion—can close the gap between camera-only SSC and LiDAR-based methods (48.79 IoU vs. 53.58 for SSCNet on KITTI-360) while using only 60.9 M parameters, roughly one-third of HTCL-S. A practical open question is the reliance on a frozen MobileSAM for per-frame segmentation masks, which adds inference latency and may not transfer cleanly to unseen object categories or degraded weather conditions. Whether the hierarchical refinement strategy generalizes to higher-resolution grids or longer temporal windows beyond four frames remains untested.
