## Problem

Existing embodied QA datasets and benchmarks remain fragmented, each targeting a narrow subset of reasoning skills such as spatial grounding or procedural planning, while neglecting the decision-making process that unfolds as an agent interacts with a dynamic environment. Without explicit modeling of how perception, reasoning, and action evolve over time, current benchmarks cannot capture the instant decisions crucial for real embodied intelligence.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/CVPR20260718/cropped_page1_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The model consumes RGB images or video frames (from simulated 3D scenes, egocentric recordings, or robot trajectories) paired with a text question, and produces heterogeneous outputs depending on the task: free-form text answers, 2D point or bounding-box predictions in pixel space for grounding/referring, scalar depth values, or structured next-action decisions. The EQA-Decision dataset contains over 4 million QA pairs across nine sub-tasks, while the evaluation benchmark comprises 2,118 curated samples strictly disjoint from training data, scored via LLM-Match (GPT-5 on a 1–5 scale) for open-ended answers and pixel-overlap accuracy for spatial predictions.

## Method

RoboDecision is built on Qwen3-VL-8B-Instruct (a ViT encoder with DeepStack multi-level feature injection into a decoder-only transformer using MRoPE) and trained in three successive stages. Stage 1 applies LoRA-based SFT on the full EQA-Decision dataset with the vision encoder frozen; Stage 2 performs CoT-SFT on a ~10% subset annotated with Gemini-2.5-pro-generated chain-of-thought rationales to instill multi-step causal reasoning; Stage 3 introduces GRPO reinforcement learning with a hybrid reward R_total = α·R_reason + β·R_answer + γ·R_visual, where the reasoning reward measures E5-large cosine similarity between predicted and reference rationales, the answer reward combines semantic similarity for free-form text with rule-based scoring for structured spatial outputs, and the visual consistency reward aligns OpenCLIP embeddings of the input image with the generated rationale to prevent text-prior drift. This perception-to-decision optimization shifts the model from pattern-matching QA responses to image-grounded, temporally aware decision-making.

## Results

On the EQA-Decision Benchmark (2,118 samples across six task modules), RoboDecision-8B achieves an overall score of 68.06, substantially outperforming the strongest general-purpose VLM (GPT-5 at 51.03) and the embodied baseline RoboBrain-7B-2.0 (37.32). The largest gains appear in Temporal Reasoning (52.95 vs. 17.42 for Gemini-2.5-Pro) and Grounding-Referring (68.12 vs. 47.56). On external benchmarks, RoboDecision-8B also leads: 86.97 BLEU-1 on RoboVQA, 54.50 on ERQA, and 67.08 on Where2Place, confirming transferability beyond the proposed dataset.

## Baselines & Comparisons

RoboDecision-8B outperforms Gemini-2.5-Pro by a wide margin across all six EQA-Decision modules, with the overall score rising from 48.68 to 68.06 (+19.4 points). The gap is most pronounced in Temporal Reasoning (52.95 vs. 17.42, a 35.5-point lead) and Grounding-Referring (68.12 vs. 47.56), indicating that the multi-stage CoT-SFT and GRPO training specifically addresses weaknesses in temporal and spatial grounding that general-purpose VLMs struggle with. Against the embodied baseline RoboBrain-7B-2.0, RoboDecision-8B leads by 30.7 points overall (68.06 vs. 37.32), with particularly large advantages in Spatial-Depth (70.82 vs. 25.62) and Planning Reasoning (65.02 vs. 33.70), while RoboBrain retains a slight edge only in Grounding-Referring (61.93 vs. 68.12 — actually RoboDecision still wins here). On external benchmarks, RoboDecision-8B surpasses both baselines on RoboVQA (86.97 vs. 34.76 BLEU-1 for Gemini, 49.56 for RoboBrain), ERQA (54.50 vs. 48.70 and 39.44), and Where2Place (67.08 vs. 18.11 and 63.59), demonstrating that the gains are not dataset-specific but reflect genuine improvements in embodied perception-to-decision reasoning.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the embodied question answering (EQA) paradigm, originally framed around navigation and scene description in 3D environments [18], by explicitly incorporating temporal dynamics and instant decision-making as first-class QA tasks. Prior embodied QA datasets (RoboVQA, Robo2VLM, ShareRobot) each cover a subset of reasoning dimensions—spatial grounding, affordance, or sub-task planning—but none jointly address progress estimation and context-aware instant decisions. Methodologically, the training pipeline builds on GRPO [45] for the reinforcement stage and on the Qwen3-VL-8B architecture (DeepStack fusion, MRoPE), but the key departure is the hybrid reward that explicitly penalizes text-prior drift by scoring visual consistency via OpenCLIP embeddings, a design not present in prior VLA or embodied QA training recipes.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

With over 4 million QA pairs spanning four reasoning modules and nine sub-tasks, EQA-Decision is one of the largest unified embodied QA resources to date, and the six-dimension benchmark offers a reproducible protocol for comparing open and closed-source models on perception-to-decision reasoning. A notable open question is the reliability of the LLM-Match evaluation (GPT-5 scoring on a 1–5 scale) for free-form answers, which may introduce systematic bias favoring verbose or stylistically similar responses. The large gap between the 8B specialized model and frontier proprietary models on a self-constructed benchmark raises questions about whether the advantage reflects genuine capability or benchmark-specific optimization; broader validation on independently constructed decision-centric evaluations would strengthen the claims.
