## Problem

Long-video temporal grounding requires simultaneously capturing broad temporal context and pinpointing fine-grained moments, but existing methods compromise temporal fidelity through naive downsampling, fixed-length pooling, or sliding-window heuristics that fragment structure. The quadratic cost of self-attention further forces these compromises, making it difficult to model dependencies across hour-scale videos while preserving the resolution needed for precise localization.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/CVPR20260031/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The model consumes precomputed clip-level video features (extracted by frozen backbones such as EgoVLP at 32-frame/16-stride, CLIP ViT-B/32 or ViT-L/14, or C3D at 16-frame/16-stride) and a natural language query embedded by a frozen text encoder (CLIP or GloVe). It outputs a single pair of start and end timestamps (t_s, t_e) identifying the contiguous segment that answers the query. Videos span 3.5–20 minutes (Ego4D-NLQ), up to ~110 minutes (MAD), or ~5 minutes (TACoS), with ground-truth moments as short as ~2% of video duration.

## Method

HieraMamba stacks L Anchor-MambaPooling (AMP) blocks, each of which pools local frame windows into compact anchor tokens, interleaves them with the original frame sequence, and applies a bidirectional Mamba scan (via Hydra) for global context alongside a narrow-window local Transformer for fine-grained attention, with learnable sigmoid gates controlling feature fusion between stages. Stacking AMP blocks produces a multi-scale feature pyramid where each level captures temporal context at increasing coarseness, all in linear time. Training combines a supervised boundary-regression loss with two contrastive objectives: an anchor-conditioned contrastive (ACC) loss that pulls each anchor toward its constituent frame tokens while pushing from distant anchors (self-supervised, applied at every layer), and a segment-pooled contrastive (SPC) loss that aligns ground-truth segment prototypes against surrounding context (supervised). This hierarchical, content-aware compression replaces the indiscriminate downsampling and fixed-window heuristics of prior methods, enabling precise grounding in hour-long videos at roughly 2.5× fewer FLOPs than quadratic-attention baselines.

## Results

On Ego4D-NLQ, HieraMamba achieves 25.66 average recall (R@1@0.3: 18.81, R@1@0.5: 13.04, R@5@0.3: 40.82, R@5@0.5: 29.96), setting a new state-of-the-art. On MAD-v2 (hour-scale movies), it reaches 18.05 average recall, and on TACoS it achieves 66.65 average recall, both also state-of-the-art. In the efficiency analysis on MAD-v2, HieraMamba improves average recall by +2.52% over SnAG (Global) while requiring roughly 2.5× fewer FLOPs, demonstrating a superior accuracy-compute trade-off.

## Baselines & Comparisons

HieraMamba outperforms all 23 baselines across all three benchmarks. On Ego4D-NLQ, it beats the strongest prior methods—SnAG (23.08) by +2.58%, DeCafNet (24.44) by +1.22%, and RGNet (21.81) by +3.85% in average recall. On MAD-v2 (hour-long movies), it surpasses SnAG (15.25) by +2.80% and RGNet (14.87) by +3.18%. On TACoS, it leads OSGNet (64.96) by +1.69%, DeCafNet (64.11) by +2.54%, and SnAG (63.28) by +3.37%. The efficiency analysis further shows that HieraMamba achieves +2.52% over a full-attention SnAG (Global) variant while using ~2.5× fewer FLOPs, confirming that the gains come from the hierarchical AMP design rather than simply replacing attention with Mamba (naive SnAG-Mamba yields no improvement over SnAG).

## Relation to Prior Work

HieraMamba builds directly on Mamba's selective state-space scanning and Hydra's bidirectional SSM as its sequence-modeling backbone, but departs fundamentally from prior video temporal grounding methods (SnAG, DeCafNet, OSGNet, RGNet) that rely on quadratic self-attention and compensate with naive downsampling, fixed-length pooling, or sliding windows. The key architectural departure is the Anchor-MambaPooling (AMP) block, which replaces indiscriminate strided pooling with content-aware, learned token compression to construct a multi-scale temporal hierarchy. The two contrastive objectives (ACC and SPC) are novel additions that explicitly shape the hierarchical embeddings for compactness and discriminability, going beyond the purely supervised boundary-regression losses used by prior grounding models.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

By achieving state-of-the-art grounding accuracy with roughly 2.5× fewer FLOPs than the strongest quadratic-attention baseline, HieraMamba makes hour-long video temporal grounding practically feasible without the temporal-fidelity compromises of prior downsampling strategies. The AMP block is positioned as a general-purpose hierarchical compression module extendable to other long-video understanding tasks, and the authors identify adaptive, content-driven anchor generation as a natural next step. A notable open question is whether the linear-time advantage persists when the grounding decoder itself (currently a lightweight transformer) is scaled up, and whether the hierarchical SSM design transfers to tasks requiring dense per-frame predictions rather than sparse moment localization.
