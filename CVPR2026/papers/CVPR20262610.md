## Problem

Existing 3D part segmentation methods face a fundamental trade-off: native 3D models suffer from poor generalization due to scarce annotated data, while 2D-pretrained transfer methods (e.g., multi-view SAM lifting) produce cross-view inconsistencies on occluded or slender structures. Additionally, prior approaches lack a continuous, real-time mechanism to control segmentation granularity, relying instead on discrete post-processing clustering or fixed point prompts.

## Input / Output

The method consumes only XYZ coordinates of a 10,000-point cloud normalized to the unit sphere, a single point prompt index, and an optional continuous scale prompt s ∈ [0,1] that encodes the desired part size as a fraction of total points. It outputs a per-point probability mask in [0,1]^N in a single forward pass, with the scale prompt enabling smooth transitions from fine-grained to coarse-grained segmentation without re-inference. Unlike baselines such as Point-SAM (which uses color) or P³-SAM (which uses normals), S²AM3D requires no auxiliary geometric or photometric channels.

## Method

S²AM3D employs a decoupled two-stage architecture. The point-consistent part encoder uses a PVCNN voxel encoder to produce a tri-plane representation, aggregated by Transformer blocks and supervised jointly by multi-view 2D SAM distillation and an intra-instance 3D contrastive loss (cosine similarity with temperature τ=0.07 over same-object point pairs), which compacts intra-part clusters and sharpens inter-part boundaries. The scale-aware prompt decoder maps the continuous scale s through a learnable sinusoidal embedding into FiLM parameters (γ, β with a learnable gate α) that modulate the global feature map across interleaved Transformer layers, then performs bi-directional cross-attention between the prompted query vector and the scale-conditioned global features to fuse multi-granularity context in one pass. Training is staged: the encoder is first stabilized with contrastive + distillation objectives, then the decoder is fine-tuned with a frozen encoder using a hybrid loss of dynamically reweighted BCE (adaptive positive-ratio weighting β) and Dice to handle the extreme class imbalance from single-part-per-sample supervision. The authors also curate a 100k+ instance, 1.2M part-label dataset from Objaverse with automated quality filtering (PointNet validator) and DBSCAN-based connectivity refinement.

## Results

On full segmentation, S²AM3D achieves 63.29 mIoU on PartObjaverse-Tiny and 77.98 mIoU on PartNet-E (average 70.64), outperforming the strongest baseline P³-SAM (58.10 / 65.39, avg 61.75) by 5.19 and 12.59 points respectively. On interactive (point-prompted) segmentation, the method reaches 46.47 / 62.52 mIoU without scale input and further improves to 61.19 / 77.51 (avg 69.35) with the scale prompt, surpassing Point-SAM (avg 40.85) and P³-SAM (avg 37.52) by a wide margin. The scale-aware variant gains approximately 15 points over the non-scale version, demonstrating the effectiveness of continuous scale conditioning.

## Baselines & Comparisons

S²AM3D outperforms all six baselines on both evaluation datasets. Against the closest competitor P³-SAM [23], it gains +5.19 mIoU on PartObjaverse-Tiny and +12.59 on PartNet-E in full segmentation, and +11.42 / +22.54 in interactive segmentation (non-scale setting). PartField [19] and SAMPart3D [35], both relying heavily on 2D priors, trail by 11.75 and 14.50 mIoU respectively on PartNet-E, with the paper attributing their gap to cross-view inconsistencies on thin and occluded structures. SAMesh [31] (26.66 on PartNet-E) and Find3D [24] (21.69) are substantially weaker, reflecting the limitations of purely native-3D or zero-shot approaches without large-scale part supervision. In the interactive setting, Point-SAM [40] (avg 40.85) is surpassed by 13.65 mIoU in the non-scale configuration, confirming that the point-consistent encoder and bi-directional cross-attention decoder together yield more accurate single-part localization than prior point-prompt methods.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper builds directly on PartField's tri-plane encoder (PVCNN + SAM distillation) but departs from the 2D-to-3D transfer paradigm by adding native 3D contrastive supervision to enforce cross-view consistency. It extends the promptable 3D segmentation line (Point-SAM, P3-SAM) by introducing an explicit continuous scale signal via FiLM modulation and bi-directional cross-attention, moving beyond the binary point-prompt interaction. Conceptually, the key positioning is a hybrid 2D-3D training recipe that treats 2D priors as initialization rather than the primary supervision source, addressing the cross-view inconsistency that plagues purely 2D-lifting methods.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The 100k+ instance, 400-category dataset with 1.2M part labels is one of the largest publicly available 3D part segmentation resources, directly addressing the data scarcity bottleneck that limits generalization in this area. Scale-controllable segmentation via a single continuous parameter is a practical capability for 3D content editing workflows (part replacement, assembly, parametric editing) that prior methods could only approximate through post-hoc clustering. Open questions include whether the approach extends to text-prompted or open-vocabulary settings (acknowledged as future work), whether the small evaluation benchmarks (200 samples for PartObjaverse-Tiny) adequately validate the claimed generalization, and whether the decoupled encoder-decoder training transfers to other 3D representation backbones.
