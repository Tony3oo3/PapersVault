## Problem

3D-native generators produce geometrically plausible but visually synthetic 3D models because their training data is dominated by synthetic assets, while real-world 3D scans are scarce, low-detail, and limited in category coverage. The core challenge is how to introduce photorealistic appearance details into 3D generation without breaking multi-view consistency or 3D structural fidelity, since naively using image-generated multi-views introduces view-dependent detail variations that cause texture misalignment under strict pixel-level supervision.

## Input / Output

At inference, the method accepts a text prompt (processed by LLaMA-3-8B into an object-centric description) and/or a single-view image, and produces a fully textured 3D asset in the form of structured 3D latents, a 3D mesh, or a 3D Gaussian Splatting model depending on the underlying generator. During training, the Photo3D-MV dataset supplies four orthogonal rendered views per object at 512×512 resolution paired with the corresponding 3D geometry; the DINOv3-based structure matching operates at an upscaled 1024×1024 resolution to capture finer patch-level correspondences. The framework is paradigm-agnostic, plugging into both geometry-texture coupled (Trellis) and decoupled (Step1X-3D, TexGaussian) pipelines with tailored training objectives.

## Method

Photo3D constructs a structure-aligned multi-view dataset (Photo3D-MV, 10K objects across 373 LVIS categories) by first generating 3D assets with Trellis, rendering four orthogonal views, and then refining them with GPT-4o-Image using a cross-view-aligned editing prompt that enriches photorealistic detail while preserving 3D structure. Because GPT-4o-Image introduces view-dependent detail variations, the paper proposes a relaxed realism loss L_real = L_adapt + L_match that avoids strict pixel-wise supervision: L_adapt uses CLIP embeddings with random cropping to capture fine-grained perceptual details at multiple scales, while L_match uses DINOv3 patch-level features to establish semantic structural correspondences between synthesized and GT views via nearest-neighbor matching. For training, the paper designs paradigm-specific strategies: for the coupled Trellis pipeline, it injects realistic priors by conditioning the 3D diffusion on a randomly selected view and supervising the decoded 3DGS rendering with L_real; for decoupled pipelines, it trains TexGaussian as a single feed-forward texturing model conditioned on mesh and text, and fine-tunes Step1X-3D's multi-view diffusion by encoding realistic multi-view latents and conditioning on rendered geometry images.

## Results

On ImageNet, Photo3D(Trellis) achieves the best overall performance with CLIP 0.679, KID 0.044, MANIQA 0.470, MUSIQ 72.39, NIMA 5.548, and Aesthetic Score 4.927, along with a 95.0% Gemini winning rate and 4.4/5 human realism score. On the Real 3D datasets (GSO, Omni3D, DTC), Photo3D(Trellis) scores CLIP 0.864, KID 0.002, MANIQA 0.459, MUSIQ 65.72, NIMA 4.856, Aesthetic 4.689, with a 93.8% Gemini winning rate and 4.8/5 human score. Across all three fine-tuned paradigms (Trellis, Step1X-3D, TexGaussian), Photo3D consistently improves fidelity, realism, and aesthetic quality over the respective baselines.

## Baselines & Comparisons

Photo3D(Trellis) dominates all baselines on both test sets: on ImageNet it achieves a 95.0% Gemini winning rate vs. 68.1% for Trellis, 55.0% for Hunyuan3D, 42.5% for 3DTopia-XL, and 0% for Real3D, with human scores of 4.4 vs. 3.4, 2.5, 2.3, and 1.0 respectively. On the Real 3D datasets, Photo3D(Trellis) reaches 93.8% Gemini winning rate and 4.8/5 human score, outperforming Trellis (70.6%, 3.9), Hunyuan3D (50.6%, 2.5), 3DTopia-XL (46.9%, 2.1), and Real3D (0%, 1.0). Within the decoupled paradigm, Photo3D(Step1X-3D) improves the Gemini winning rate from 66.9% to 80.0% (ImageNet) and 65.0% to 83.1% (Real 3D), while Photo3D(TexGaussian) lifts it from 13.8% to 28.8% (ImageNet) and 14.4% to 25.6% (Real 3D), though TexGaussian remains the weakest variant due to its single feed-forward architecture. Real3D consistently performs worst across all metrics, confirming that single-view supervision without stereo constraints is insufficient for stable, realistic 3D generation.

## Relation to Prior Work

Photo3D positions itself at the intersection of 3D-native generation and photorealistic appearance enhancement, building directly on three representative generators (Trellis for coupled generation, Step1X-3D for multi-view texturing, and TexGaussian for feed-forward 3D-native texturing) by designing paradigm-specific training strategies for each. It departs from Real3D's single-view cycle-consistent supervision and from prior multi-view generation approaches that suffer from structural drift, by introducing a structure-aligned multi-view synthesis pipeline that uses GPT-4o-Image to refine 3D renderings while preserving geometric consistency. The relaxed supervision scheme—combining CLIP-based perceptual feature adaptation with DINOv3 semantic structure matching—represents a conceptual departure from strict pixel-level losses, motivated by the inherent view-dependent variability of generative image models.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The paper addresses a persistent and practically important gap: 3D-native generators produce geometrically plausible but visually synthetic-looking models because training data is dominated by synthetic assets. By demonstrating that a general detail-enhancement framework can be plugged into multiple 3D-native paradigms with consistent gains, Photo3D offers a reusable recipe for improving appearance realism without re-architecting the underlying generator. However, the reliance on a closed-source image model (GPT-4o-Image) for data construction introduces a dependency and potential bias that the authors acknowledge as a limitation, and the 10K-object dataset, while diverse across 373 categories, remains modest in scale. Open questions include whether the approach transfers to scene-level or articulated-object generation, and whether the GPT-4o-Image aesthetic preferences can be disentangled from genuine photorealism.
