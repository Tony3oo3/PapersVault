## Problem

Multi-view diffusion models generate geometrically consistent novel views, but the internal mechanism responsible for this consistency remains unclear, and consistency degrades under large viewpoint changes or complex geometry. The paper identifies that 3D self-attention maps in deep layers implicitly encode geometric correspondence, yet this signal is incomplete—its precision lags behind dedicated geometry models (e.g., VGGT) and collapses under large viewpoint rotations—motivating explicit supervision of this signal.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/CVPR20261340/cropped_page0_idx1.webp)

## Input / Output

The model takes N reference RGB images (512×512) with known camera poses and M target camera poses, and outputs M novel-view RGB images at the same resolution. During training, F=4 views are sampled per batch with 1–3 randomly designated as targets; geometric correspondence maps are precomputed from VGGT pointmaps and bilinearly interpolated to the token-level spatial resolution (h×w). Evaluation uses 1-to-3 and 2-to-2 view configurations on scene-level (RealEstate10K) and object-centric (CO3D, DTU) benchmarks.

## Method

CAMEO adds a cross-entropy alignment loss between the cross-view attention map A_{i,j}^l of a single deep layer (l=10 for U-Net, l=32 for DiT) and a one-hot geometric correspondence map P_{i,j} derived from VGGT pointmaps via DUSt3R-style 3D nearest-neighbor matching. A visibility mask M_{i,j} computed through cycle-consistency checking (threshold τ=1.5) restricts the loss to reliably matched tokens. An MLP projection head is applied to the attention logits before softmax to preserve multi-head representational diversity, and the total objective is L_denoise + 0.02·L_CAMEO. The key insight is that supervising just one deep attention layer—where emergent correspondence is strongest—suffices to accelerate convergence by 2× and improve final NVS quality across U-Net and DiT architectures.

## Results

On RealEstate10K, CAMEO reaches PSNR 19.40 at 80k iterations, whereas the CAT3D baseline requires 160k+ iterations to match this level—a 2× training acceleration. At 320k iterations, CAMEO achieves PSNR 20.16 / SSIM 0.716 / LPIPS 0.279 on RealEstate10K and PSNR 18.20 / SSIM 0.608 / LPIPS 0.425 on CO3D, surpassing the baseline (19.88 / 17.55 PSNR) and both feature-alignment baselines. On the out-of-domain DTU dataset, CAMEO also consistently outperforms the baseline, confirming generalization beyond the training distribution.

## Baselines & Comparisons

CAMEO consistently outperforms the CAT3D baseline across all datasets and iteration counts, with the largest gains at intermediate training (e.g., +0.41 dB PSNR on RealEstate10K at 80k, +0.65 dB on CO3D at 320k). Against REPA (feature alignment with DINOv2), CAMEO wins on PSNR at every iteration on RealEstate10K (e.g., 19.40 vs 18.70 at 80k) and on CO3D (16.76 vs 16.10 at 80k), though REPA occasionally edges out CAMEO on SSIM at 160k. Against Geometry Forcing (feature alignment with VGGT), CAMEO achieves higher PSNR at all tested iterations on both datasets (e.g., 19.40 vs 18.92 on RealEstate10K at 80k; 17.37 vs 16.91 on CO3D at 160k). CAMEO is also applied to two additional architectures—MVGenMaster (U-Net with geometric conditioning) and Hunyuan-DiT (DiT-based)—where it improves PSNR by 0.96 dB at 20k and 1.75 dB at 120k respectively, confirming model-agnostic applicability.

## Relation to Prior Work

CAMEO builds on multi-view diffusion models (CAT3D, MVGenMaster, Hunyuan-DiT) that use 3D self-attention to synthesize geometrically consistent novel views, and on the broader trend of supervising diffusion model internals with external signals (REPA, Geometry Forcing, NVComposer, Track4Gen). However, it departs from these feature-alignment methods by targeting a specific, identified mechanism—cross-view geometric correspondence in deep attention layers—rather than aligning generic intermediate features. The geometric correspondence maps are computed via DUSt3R-style pointmap nearest-neighbor matching, and the supervision is a simple cross-entropy loss on attention logits, making it orthogonal to and composable with prior feature-alignment approaches.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The finding that geometric correspondence emerges specifically in deep attention layers and that a single-layer supervision yields a 2× training speedup provides both an interpretability insight and a practical, model-agnostic recipe applicable to any multi-view diffusion architecture. Limitations include dependence on an external geometry model (VGGT) for ground-truth correspondences, and the correspondence signal still degrades under very large viewpoint rotations even after CAMEO supervision. Open questions include whether the approach extends to video diffusion (temporal correspondence), whether the optimal supervised layer shifts with architecture depth or training regime, and whether self-supervised correspondence could replace the external geometry model.
