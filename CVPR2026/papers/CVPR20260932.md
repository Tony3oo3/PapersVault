## Problem

Reconstruction-based scene video generation methods suffer from cumulative errors that grow with each iteration, caused by non-differentiable 3D reconstruction and rendering operators and by the decoupling of geometry estimation from novel-view inpainting into separate, non-end-to-end models. These structural limitations make it impossible to apply standard autoregressive forcing or joint optimization techniques to mitigate the error accumulation, degrading scene consistency over long camera trajectories.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/CVPR20260932/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The model takes a single reference image (640×352 per view, encoded via FLUX-VAE) and a user-specified camera trajectory of T novel views as input. It produces a scene-consistent video sequence where each frame corresponds to the requested camera pose; optionally, depth maps for intermediate views can be elicited via a <Geometry> text prompt. During training, 8-frame sequences are used with 1–4 context frames and the remainder as targets, and four consecutive views are combined into a single grid-pattern frame to enhance cross-view consistency.

## Method

GaC flattens the iterative reconstruction pipeline (geometry estimation → unprojection → rendering → inpainting) into a single autoregressive DiT (Bagel-7B) that processes an interleaved text-image-geometry sequence, replacing all non-differentiable 3D operators with differentiable generation. A Camera Gated Attention (CGA) module encodes camera poses as patchified Plücker rays that additively modulate the attention query and produce a sigmoid gating matrix over self-attention outputs, enabling the model to distinguish camera's role in geometry prediction versus novel-view synthesis. A geometry dropout strategy randomly removes geometry frames during training (ratio r), halving sequence length and allowing purely RGB-to-RGB inference while preserving learned 3D consistency. The model is trained end-to-end on 66K RealEstate10K clips using FSDP with batch size 1 on 8×H100 GPUs for 40K iterations (~2 days), with text tokens (<Geometry>, <Image>) scheduling which modality to generate at each step.

## Results

On RealEstate10K, GaC achieves the best performance across all metrics: PSNR 19.01, SSIM 0.656, LPIPS 0.354, FID 55.76, rotation error 0.024, and translation error 0.270. On Tanks-and-Temples (larger camera motions), GaC leads in PSNR (15.77) and SSIM (0.532) but trails SEVA slightly on FID (93.29 vs. 88.76). The forth-and-back trajectory (Table 2) shows a marked performance drop for all methods, with GaC still leading in PSNR (16.34) and FID (64.31) on RealEstate10K.

## Baselines & Comparisons

GaC outperforms all five baselines on RealEstate10K across every metric, with the largest margins over CameraCtrl (+3.08 PSNR, −22.17 FID) and ViewCrafter (+2.29 PSNR, −24.71 FID). Against the strongest reconstruction-based competitor Voyager, GaC gains +0.31 PSNR, +0.040 SSIM, and a 9.36-point FID reduction, while also halving the translation error (0.270 vs. 0.596). On Tanks-and-Temples, GaC leads in PSNR and SSIM but is slightly behind SEVA on FID (93.29 vs. 88.76) and LPIPS (0.507 vs. 0.490), and ViewCrafter retains the best translation error (0.399 vs. 0.442). GEN3C and SEVA are the closest competitors on Tanks-and-Temples, with GaC's advantage narrowing to ~0.1–0.2 dB in PSNR. Overall, GaC's unified differentiable pipeline yields the most consistent gains in perceptual quality (FID, LPIPS) and camera-pose accuracy (Rerr, Terr) on the indoor RealEstate10K benchmark, while the outdoor Tanks-and-Temples setting with larger camera motions remains a harder regime where all methods degrade substantially.

## Relation to Prior Work

GaC departs from the dominant reconstruction-based scene video generation pipeline (ViewCrafter, Voyager, GEN3C, Wonderworld) that chains separate geometry estimators, non-differentiable unprojection/rendering, and inpainting models. Rather than treating these as distinct modules, GaC internalizes the entire iterative loop into a single autoregressive DiT (Bagel-7B) that processes interleaved RGB-geometry sequences, drawing on the camera-conditioned generation paradigm from CameraCtrl and ReCamMaster. The Camera Gated Attention mechanism extends prior Plücker-ray camera conditioning by adding a learned gating pathway, specifically to disambiguate camera's role in geometry prediction versus novel-view synthesis within one model.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

By eliminating non-differentiable reconstruction operators and enabling end-to-end training, GaC addresses a structural limitation that has prevented prior reconstruction-based methods from benefiting from forcing-style training strategies. The geometry dropout strategy offers a practical efficiency gain (halving training and inference time) with minimal quality loss, making the approach more accessible. However, the forth-and-back trajectory experiments reveal that all methods—including GaC—suffer significant degradation over long camera paths, indicating that context memory and long-range consistency remain open challenges that this work does not fully resolve.
