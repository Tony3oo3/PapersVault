## Problem

Foundation-model tokenizers (e.g., DINOv2) reveal multimodal, union-of-manifold structures in latent spaces, yet standard flow matching still uses a target-blind isotropic Gaussian source with independent coupling, forcing probability mass to cross between distant modes and producing curved, hard-to-learn velocity fields. The paper asks: can the source distribution and data coupling be co-designed to exploit this multimodality, yielding straighter, shorter flows and substantially faster convergence?

## Input / Output

At inference, a sample is drawn from a fitted 8192-component GMM (diagonal covariance) in the DINOv2-B patch-token latent space (768×16×16), integrated along the learned ODE for 50 Euler steps (as few as 5 suffice vs. 25 for the Gaussian-source baseline), and decoded to a 256×256 RGB image. The GMM is estimated once from training latents using the 768-dim [CLS] token for mode assignment and the full patch-token space for per-mode Gaussian parameters, adding negligible overhead to training speed.

## Method

MM-FM replaces the isotropic Gaussian source with a data-adaptive GMM fitted to the DINOv2-B latents and introduces mode-dependent coupling: each target latent is softly assigned to GMM modes via posterior responsibilities, and the paired source sample is drawn from the corresponding mode's Gaussian, ensuring probability mass moves locally rather than crossing distant modes. The GMM is made operational in the high-dimensional patch-token space by performing mode selection in the lower-dimensional [CLS] token space and estimating per-mode means/covariances in the patch-token space. Training uses the standard conditional flow matching loss with linear paths on the DiT^DH-XL backbone (839M params) operating directly in DINOv2-B latents. Theoretically, the paper proves that the multimodal source plus mode coupling bounds the flow straightness, total trajectory length, and velocity-field Lipschitz constant by those of the unimodal generation problem, while a unimodal source admits arbitrarily large Lipschitz constants—explaining the observed 30× faster convergence and 5× fewer ODE steps.

## Results

On ImageNet-256 unconditional generation, MM-FM (DiT^DH-XL with GMM source + mode coupling, 80 epochs) achieves FID 3.18 without guidance and FID 2.74 with autoguidance, establishing new SOTA. This represents a 3× improvement over the Gaussian-source baseline (FID 9.33) at the same 80-epoch budget, and outperforms RCG (FID 4.89, 400 epochs) and DLC (FID 5.75, 80 epochs) without requiring additional generative models for representation conditioning. In the few-step regime, MM-FM needs only 5 ODE steps to match the FID that classic FM requires 25 steps to reach. With only 10% of ImageNet data, MM-FM achieves FID 7.48 vs. 24.33 for the baseline at 800 epochs.

## Baselines & Comparisons

Against the direct baseline DiT^DH-XL [89] (same DINOv2-B tokenizer, Gaussian source, independent coupling), MM-FM reduces FID from 9.33 to 3.18 at 80 epochs (2.9× improvement) and from 16.51 to 4.74 at 20 epochs (3.5× improvement). MM-FM outperforms RCG [51] (FID 4.89, 400 epochs) by 35% in FID while using only 80 epochs and no additional generative model for representation conditioning. It also beats DLC [47] (FID 5.75, 80 epochs) by 45% in FID under the same training budget. Notably, MM-FM's unconditional FID of 3.18 (without guidance) matches or surpasses several class-conditioned methods including SiT-XL [55] (FID 17.20), LightningDiT-XL with VA-VAE [85] (FID 5.14), and SVG-XL [70] (FID 6.57), demonstrating that the multimodal source and mode coupling can compensate for the absence of class labels. The paper also shows that MM-FM is compatible with BatchOT [64] for further trajectory straightening, and that the GMM source alone (without mode coupling) already provides substantial gains (FID 3.82 vs. 9.33), confirming that both components contribute independently.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the conditional flow matching framework (Lipman et al., 2024) by co-designing the source distribution and data coupling, departing from the standard isotropic Gaussian source with independent pairing. It builds directly on the DiT^DH-XL architecture and DINOv2-B latent space from Zheng et al. (2025), which reveals union-of-manifold structures in the target distribution. The key conceptual departure from prior GMM-source works (MixSGM, CondPrior) is operating in high-dimensional foundation-model latents with an operational GMM subroutine (using [CLS]-token clustering to guide patch-token covariance estimation), rather than in pixel space or class-conditioned VAE latents. The mode-dependent coupling generalizes the independent coupling assumption in standard FM, ensuring probability mass transports locally within each mode.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The 30× faster convergence and 5× fewer ODE steps (5 vs. 25) represent a substantial practical win for flow-based generation, and the 3× FID gap at 10% data suggests the method fundamentally reduces learning complexity rather than merely improving optimization. The theoretical results (Theorem 3.1 bounding straightness/length/Lipschitz, Theorem 3.2 showing unimodal sources can yield arbitrarily curved velocity fields) provide generalizable justification beyond the specific DINOv2-B setting. Open questions include whether the approach transfers to other foundation models (SigLIP2, DINOv3), conditional generation, video, and whether the GMM mode count (8192) is near-optimal or if adaptive selection could further improve efficiency. The reliance on a high-quality foundation model encoder as a prerequisite limits applicability to domains where such models exist.
