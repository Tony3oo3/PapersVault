## Problem

Single-view RGB 6D pose estimation is fundamentally limited by depth ambiguity, occlusions, and appearance ambiguities in cluttered scenes. Existing multi-view methods either discard valid candidate poses through restrictive assumptions or require object-specific training that takes hours to days, preventing generalization to unseen objects. AlignPose addresses both gaps by providing a training-free, symmetry-agnostic multi-view pose estimation pipeline that generalizes to novel objects given only their 3D mesh.

## Input / Output

The method consumes multiple extrinsically calibrated RGB images (4 views for BOP-Classic datasets, predefined views for industrial datasets), camera intrinsics and extrinsics from a one-time offline calibration, and the object's 3D mesh. It also requires per-view pose candidates from any off-the-shelf single-view estimator (e.g., FoundPose, MegaPose, Co-Op). The output is a single consistent 6D pose T_WO ∈ SE(3) in the world frame plus a confidence score in [0,1] computed as 1 minus the average per-view feature-metric loss, which can be used for downstream tasks like grasping.

## Method

AlignPose is a three-stage training-free pipeline: (1) per-view pose candidates are generated by any existing single-view estimator, (2) candidates are transformed into a common world frame and consolidated via 3D Non-Maximum Suppression using 3D IoU on bounding boxes, and (3) each surviving candidate is refined by a novel multi-view feature-metric alignment. The refinement jointly optimizes a single world-frame pose by minimizing the sum of per-view feature-metric losses, where each loss compares 3D registered features (obtained by on-the-fly rendering of color and depth from the current pose, extracting DINOv2-L layer-18 features, and lifting them to 3D via the rendered depth) against 2D query features sampled at the projected 3D point locations using bilinear interpolation and Barron's robust cost. Optimization uses Levenberg-Marquardt for up to 30 iterations. Unlike prior single-view feature-metric refinement (FoundPose), the multi-view formulation enforces a single consistent pose across all cameras simultaneously, and unlike CosyPose, it requires no symmetry annotation and does not jointly refine camera poses.

## Results

On the unseen-object multi-view pose estimation task, AlignPose achieves AR of 83.8 and AP of 83.3 on YCB-V, and AR of 89.6 and AP of 92.4 on T-LESS (using Co-Op single-view candidates), substantially outperforming the CosyPose multi-view baseline (81.0/79.2 and 78.7/78.9 respectively). On the challenging BOP-Industrial datasets, the gains are even more dramatic: IPD AP jumps from 31.4 (FoundPose single-view) to 79.8, XYZ-IBD AP from 32.5 to 66.5, and ITODD-MV AP from 41.2 to 76.8, surpassing CosyPose MV by more than 14 points on all three. In the seen-object setting on T-LESS, AlignPose (AR 86.8, AP 91.0) outperforms all trained multi-view baselines including CosyPose MV (AR 81.5, AP 81.7) and DPODv2 MV (AR 72.0).

## Baselines & Comparisons

AlignPose is compared against four single-view pose estimators (FoundPose, GigaPose, MegaPose, Co-Op) used as candidate generators, and against CosyPose MV as the primary multi-view refinement baseline. Across all four single-view backbones on both YCB-V and T-LESS, AlignPose consistently outperforms CosyPose MV by 2–11 AR points and 3–14 AP points, with the largest gap on T-LESS using Co-Op (89.6 vs. 78.7 AR). On the BOP-Industrial datasets (IPD, XYZ-IBD, ITODD-MV), the advantage over CosyPose MV is even more pronounced: +43.1 AP on IPD, +14.1 on XYZ-IBD, and +22.3 on ITODD-MV. In the seen-object setting on T-LESS, AlignPose (AR 86.8, AP 91.0) beats all trained multi-view baselines—CosyPose MV (AR 81.5, AP 81.7), DPODv2 MV (AR 72.0), and CenDerNet (AR 71.3)—despite requiring no object-specific training. The method also outperforms the single-view FoundPose baseline by 14–27 AR points on T-LESS depending on the input estimator, confirming that multi-view aggregation and feature-metric refinement are the primary sources of improvement.

## Relation to Prior Work

AlignPose extends FoundPose's single-view feature-metric refinement (which uses frozen DINOv2 features to align rendered templates with query images) into a multi-view setting, jointly optimizing a single world-frame pose across all cameras via Levenberg-Marquardt. Conceptually, it adapts the feature-metric refinement paradigm from camera localization and SfM bundle adjustment (Sarlin et al., Lindenberger et al.) to the object-centric problem, replacing photometric losses with foundation-model feature discrepancies. The key departure from the closest multi-view baseline, CosyPose, is the use of a simple 3D NMS aggregation followed by per-candidate multi-view refinement, rather than RANSAC-based pose-level aggregation and joint camera-object bundle adjustment, and the elimination of any object-specific training or symmetry annotation requirement.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

This work provides the first training-free, symmetry-agnostic multi-view pose estimation pipeline that generalizes to unseen objects, filling a gap where prior multi-view methods either required object-specific training or made restrictive assumptions that discarded valid candidates. The >14% AP gains on industrial datasets (IPD, XYZ-IBD, ITODD-MV) are practically significant for factory settings where multi-camera rigs are standard and objects are textureless or reflective. Open questions include how the method scales to very large view counts, whether the reliance on a calibrated multi-camera setup limits deployment flexibility, and whether the quality of the single-view candidate generator remains the dominant bottleneck in cluttered scenes.
