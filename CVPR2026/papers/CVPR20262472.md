## Problem

Existing multimodal reasoning models, when applied to remote sensing imagery, exhibit widespread pseudo reasoning: they produce explicit reasoning chains that merely narrate a process rather than genuinely reason from visual evidence, and their performance often degrades below the non-reasoning base model. The paper attributes this to the 'Glance Effect,' where a single coarse perception pass over large-scale RS imagery (with wide spatial extents, large scale variations, and sparse visual clues) yields incomplete understanding, causing the model to drift toward linguistically self-consistent narratives instead of evidence-grounded logic.

## Input / Output

The model accepts a remote sensing image in any of three modalities (RGB, infrared, or SAR) paired with a free-form text question, and produces a structured self-Q&A reasoning trace followed by a final answer. For VQA tasks the answer is a short text response; for grounding tasks it is a bounding box in [x1,y1,x2,y2] format. The reasoning trace itself is the distinctive output: it consists of alternating language-driven reasoning segments and targeted perceptual sub-questions (e.g., 'How many airplanes are visible in the left half?'), each answered by re-examining the image, forming an iterative evidence-seeking loop rather than a single linear chain.

## Method

The core contribution is RS-EoT (Remote Sensing Evidence-of-Thought), a paradigm that frames reasoning as a language-driven loop where the model iteratively poses perceptual questions to itself and integrates the returned visual evidence to refine its hypothesis. To instill this pattern, the authors first cold-start via SFT on RS-EoT-4K, a 4.3K-sample dataset synthesized by SocraticAgent—a self-play multi-agent system in which a text-only Reasoner (GPT-5-mini) and an image-only Perceiver (Gemini-2.5-flash) engage in up to 6 rounds of dialogue, with a self-play prompting mechanism that tells each agent the other is 'weak' to force incremental, concise questioning. A Verifier (Doubao-Seed-1.6-thinking) then validates the final answer against ground truth before the dialogue is formatted into a training trace. Building on the SFT model, a two-stage progressive RL pipeline follows: Stage 1 performs GRPO on fine-grained grounding tasks (DIOR-RSVG, VRSBench) with an IoU-based reward to sharpen evidence-seeking, and Stage 2 performs GRPO on general RS VQA using a novel multiple-choice data reconstruction strategy (converting simple Yes/No QA pairs into multi-option verification problems) with a symmetric graded reward that mitigates reward hacking on inherently simple datasets.

## Results

RS-EoT-7B achieves state-of-the-art performance across all evaluated remote sensing VQA and grounding benchmarks. On VQA, it reaches 67.85 Avg@5 on RSFG-VQA, 75.16 Avg@5 on RSVQA, and 63.09 Avg@5 on VRSBench, consistently outperforming all baselines. On fine-grained grounding, it achieves 45.29 mIoU on DIOR-RSVG and 48.04 mIoU on VRSBench-Ref, substantially surpassing the next-best model (VHM-RL at 40.32 and WeThink at 34.07 respectively). The only metric where RS-EoT-7B does not lead is RSFG-SC Scene@acc, where VHM-RL narrowly edges it (64.90 vs. 64.05).

## Baselines & Comparisons

RS-EoT-7B wins on 16 out of 17 reported metrics, losing only on RSFG-SC Scene@acc where VHM-RL narrowly leads (64.90 vs. 64.05). On VQA benchmarks, the advantage is most pronounced on RSVQA (75.16 Avg@5 vs. 67.20 for the base Qwen2.5-VL and 67.29 for VHM-RL) and RSFG-VQA (67.85 vs. 62.45 base), while on VRSBench the margin over the base model is smaller (63.09 vs. 62.45). The grounding results show the largest gaps: on VRSBench-Ref, RS-EoT-7B achieves 48.04 mIoU versus 34.07 for WeThink and 18.74 for VHM-RL, and on DIOR-RSVG it reaches 45.29 mIoU versus 40.32 for VHM-RL and 35.64 for the base model. Notably, several general-purpose reasoning models (Vis-R1, MM-E, R1-OV) suffer severe grounding degradation (mIoU < 10 on DIOR-RSVG), confirming the paper's claim that single-pass reasoning paradigms fail on fine-grained spatial tasks. Among the RS-specific baselines, VHM-RL is the closest competitor on grounding but still trails by 5+ mIoU points, while Geo-R1 lags significantly on both VQA and grounding.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the SFT-RL paradigm popularized by DeepSeek-R1 to the multimodal remote sensing domain, but departs from prior multimodal reasoning models (Vision-R1, WeThink, R1-OneVision) by identifying a fundamental limitation: single-pass global perception is insufficient for large-scale RS imagery, leading to 'pseudo reasoning' where models narrate rather than genuinely reason. The key conceptual departure is RS-EoT, an iterative evidence-seeking loop where language-driven reasoning dynamically guides targeted visual inspection, replacing the static perception assumption inherited from language-oriented CoT frameworks. For data synthesis, the paper introduces SocraticAgent—a self-play multi-agent system inspired by the Socratic method—as an alternative to direct distillation from frontier models, and proposes a two-stage progressive RL pipeline (Grounding → VQA) with a novel multiple-choice reconstruction strategy to stabilize training on simple RS VQA datasets.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The identification of the 'Glance Effect'—where explicit reasoning chains actually degrade RS performance below the base model—challenges the assumption that adding structured reasoning universally helps multimodal tasks, and highlights a domain-specific gap that generic reasoning models fail to address. The iterative evidence-seeking paradigm and the SocraticAgent synthesis method offer a reusable framework for domains where visual evidence is spatially distributed and requires targeted inspection. Open questions include whether the RS-EoT pattern generalizes beyond remote sensing to other large-scale or multi-scale visual domains, whether the multi-agent synthesis can be replaced by cheaper single-model approaches, and whether the reliance on GPT-5-mini and Gemini-2.5-flash for trace generation introduces a ceiling on the quality of the learned reasoning.
