## Problem

Existing streaming vision-language models for fitness coaching operate on pixel-level features without explicit biomechanical constraints, producing generic, poorly timed, and non-personalized feedback. They lack the ability to reason over 3D joint angles, ranges of motion, and exercise phases, and they cannot ground advice in individual body morphology, making it difficult to deliver the precise, anatomy-specific, and phase-aware cues that expert human coaches provide.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/CVPR20261085/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The system consumes streaming fitness video at 224×160 resolution in a true causal (no-future-frame) setting, processing a 3-second sliding window (τ=12 frames) through two parallel backbones: a pre-trained 3D CNN for visual tokens and HSMR/SKEL for 46-dimensional Euler-angle joint poses plus 10-dimensional SMPL shape coefficients. Output is temporally triggered coaching text that includes anatomy-specific quantitative cues (e.g., 'increase elbow flexion to 90° at the bottom'), phase-aligned to detected repetition cycles, with special <next>/<feedback> action tokens controlling when feedback is issued.

## Method

BioCoach organizes a three-stage pipeline over dual modalities (visual + 3D kinematic). First, a lightweight 3-layer MLP attention head scores all joints from visual features and selects the top-K=12 exercise-salient joints, automatically including all their degrees of freedom. Second, a structured biomechanical context module has two submodules: an individual morphometric context that converts SMPL shape parameters into interpretable anthropometric measurements (height, mass, chest, waist, hips) via Virtual Measurements, and a motion-quality context that detects repetition cycles via prominence-based peak detection, time-normalizes each cycle against a curated reference trajectory, and evaluates exercise-specific constraints (variance for static joints, deviation at key frames for dynamic joints). Third, a vision-biomechanics conditioned feedback module fuses visual tokens with morphometric tokens via cross-attention and prepends the motion-quality context as structured instruction to LLaMA-2-7B. Training is parameter-efficient: both the 3D CNN and LLaMA-2-7B are frozen, updating only the cross-attention fusion layers and the DoF-selection network, with a binary cross-entropy loss for joint selection and autoregressive cross-entropy with action-token down-weighting (α=0.1) to encourage timely feedback.

## Results

On the new QEVD-bio-fit-coach benchmark, BioCoach achieves METEOR 0.312, ROUGE-L 0.302, BERTScore 0.877, LLM-Acc 3.12, LLM-Bio-Acc 3.26, and T-F-Score 0.544, outperforming Stream-VLM by +262.8% (METEOR), +179.6% (ROUGE-L), +67.7% (LLM-Acc), and +89.5% (LLM-Bio-Acc) while maintaining near-parity timing. On the original QEVD-fit-coach, BioCoach scores METEOR 0.129, ROUGE-L 0.122, BERTScore 0.864, LLM-Acc 2.56, and T-F-Score 0.544, surpassing the strongest fine-tuned baseline (Stream-VLM) on all text-quality and correctness metrics with a slight 2.9% drop in temporal F-score.

## Baselines & Comparisons

Against the strongest fine-tuned streaming baseline, Stream-VLM (NeurIPS'24), BioCoach wins on METEOR (0.129 vs. 0.127), ROUGE-L (0.122 vs. 0.112, +8.9%), BERTScore (0.864 vs. 0.863), and LLM-Acc (2.56 vs. 2.45, +4.5%), while trailing slightly on T-F-Score (0.544 vs. 0.56, −2.9%). All six zero-shot VLMs (InstructBLIP, Video-LLaVA, Video-ChatGPT, Video-LLaMA, LLaMA-VID, LLaVA-NeXT) are substantially outperformed by BioCoach on every reported metric; for example, LLaVA-NeXT scores METEOR 0.104 and LLM-Acc 2.27, both well below BioCoach's 0.129 and 2.56. The fine-tuned turn-based baselines (Video-ChatGPT, LLaMA-VID) achieve T-F-Score 0.50, indicating weaker temporal triggering compared to BioCoach's 0.544. On the new QEVD-bio-fit-coach benchmark, the gap widens dramatically: BioCoach's LLM-Bio-Acc of 3.26 exceeds Stream-VLM's 1.72 by 89.5%, confirming that explicit biomechanical grounding yields substantially more anatomy-specific and phase-aware feedback than pixel-only VLMs.

## Relation to Prior Work

BioCoach extends the streaming fitness-coaching framework of Stream-VLM (Panchal et al., NeurIPS 2024), which introduced action tokens for timely feedback but operated purely on pixel-level 3D CNN features. The key departure is the explicit integration of biomechanically-grounded 3D skeletal kinematics via HSMR/SKEL (46-dimensional Euler angles with joint-specific constraints) and SMPL shape parameters, coupled with a structured pipeline (DoF selection → morphometric/motion-quality context → cross-attention fusion) that replaces implicit visual pattern matching with verifiable, rule-level evidence. The paper also introduces a re-annotated dataset (QEVD-bio-fit-coach) and a biomechanics-specific LLM judge metric, extending the evaluation paradigm beyond generic lexical and holistic scoring.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

By exposing explicit kinematic and morphometric intermediates to the language model, BioCoach makes coaching feedback auditable and personalized in a way that black-box VLMs cannot, which is critical for safety-sensitive applications like injury prevention and rehabilitation. The ablation revealing that the Motion Quality Context module is the dominant driver (~57% METEOR drop when removed) suggests that structured biomechanical reasoning, not visual appearance, is what grounds correct form feedback. Open questions include robustness to occlusions and clothing, coverage of exercise variants beyond curated references, and whether the approach extends to kinetic (force-level) reasoning for detecting compensatory strategies invisible to angle-only analysis.
