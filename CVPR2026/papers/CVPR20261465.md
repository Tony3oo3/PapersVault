## Problem

Existing perspective-to-360° video generation methods are constrained by the quadratic attention cost of vanilla video diffusion models, limiting native generation to at most 1K resolution (≈1024×512). To reach the 4K (3840×1920) resolution required for immersive VR, prior work relies on post-hoc super-resolution modules that lack intrinsic generative reasoning and introduce error cascades, yielding outputs that are high-resolution but detail-deficient compared to native high-resolution synthesis.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/CVPR20261465/cropped_page0_idx5.webp)

## Input / Output

The method takes a perspective video (N frames) captured by a conventional camera with arbitrary rotation trajectory, along with an optional global or per-face text prompt. The perspective frames are projected to equirectangular and converted to a six-face cubemap (F, R, B, L, U, D) with binary masks marking observed pixels, serving as the conditional input. The output is a complete 360° panoramic video in equirectangular format at up to 4K (3840×1920), generated natively without any external super-resolution step.

## Method

CubeComposer is a spatio-temporal autoregressive diffusion model fine-tuned from the Wan 2.2 5B DiT backbone that generates 360° video by decomposing it into cubemap faces and synthesizing one face per temporal window in a coverage-guided spatial order (descending mask coverage within each window). A three-part context mechanism—history tokens from prior windows, current-window tokens from already-generated faces and perspective conditions, and dynamically selected future fragment tokens from adjacent faces—maintains cross-face and temporal coherence. A sparse context attention design restricts context self-attention to a diagonal-banded local mask (bandwidth = one face's spatial token length), reducing attention complexity from quadratic to linear in context length. Continuity across face boundaries is ensured via cube-aware positional encodings that remap positions to a flattened cubemap topology, plus topology-aligned padding and pixel-space blending of overlapping strips. Training uses a flow-matching objective with simulated autoregressive context construction on ground-truth 360° videos, supporting both global and face-wise text conditioning.

## Results

On the 4K360Vid dataset, CubeComposer at native 4K resolution achieves the best FVD (2.22), imaging quality (0.5618), and aesthetic quality (0.4051), while at 2K it achieves the best LPIPS (0.3696), CLIP similarity (0.9234), FID (119.10), and overall consistency (0.1773). On ODV360, the 4K model similarly leads in FVD (3.51), I.Q. (0.5543), and A.Q. (0.4168). Notably, applying VEnhancer super-resolution to baselines consistently degrades their FVD and FID scores (e.g., Argus FVD worsens from 4.08 to 6.13), confirming that post-hoc upscaling introduces error cascades that native high-resolution generation avoids.

## Baselines & Comparisons

CubeComposer outperforms all three baselines across every metric on both datasets. Against Argus (the strongest baseline at 1K), CubeComposer at 4K reduces FVD from 4.08 to 2.22 (−45%) and FID from 141.15 to 130.92 on 4K360Vid, while improving imaging quality from 0.4266 to 0.5618. Against ViewPoint, the gap is even larger: FID drops from 196.53 to 130.92 and CLIP similarity rises from 0.8532 to 0.9111. Against Imagine360, CubeComposer achieves substantially lower LPIPS (0.3831 vs. 0.7367) and FID (130.92 vs. 254.29). A critical finding is that VEnhancer super-resolution consistently degrades baseline quality—Argus's FVD worsens from 4.08 to 6.13 and Imagine360's from 5.10 to 10.21—demonstrating that post-hoc upscaling introduces temporal inconsistencies that native 4K generation avoids.

## Relation to Prior Work

CubeComposer builds on the Wan 2.2 5B DiT-based video foundation model, fine-tuning it with a spatio-temporal autoregressive generation strategy over cubemap faces. It departs from prior perspective-to-360° video methods (Argus, Imagine360, ViewPoint) that rely on vanilla full-attention diffusion and are thus capped at ≤1K native resolution, requiring external super-resolution (e.g., VEnhancer) to reach higher resolutions. The autoregressive decomposition is conceptually related to temporal-only autoregressive video generation for long-form or streaming synthesis, but CubeComposer extends this to a joint spatial (across cube faces) and temporal (across windows) schedule with coverage-guided ordering, a setting not addressed by prior autoregressive video work.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

This is the first method to natively generate 4K 360° panoramic videos without post-hoc super-resolution, which is practically important for VR applications where resolution directly affects immersion and where error cascades from external upscaling degrade detail fidelity. The 4K360Vid dataset (11,832 clips) and the cubemap-based autoregressive framework provide reusable infrastructure for the 360° video generation community. Open questions include reducing the total number of diffusion passes (one per face per window) for faster inference, and extending toward streaming 360° generation to amortize computation and lower latency, as the authors note.
