## Problem

Existing 3D dense captioning methods operate in Euclidean embedding spaces, creating a fundamental geometric conflict: fine-grained local object localization demands flat, isotropic metrics, while global scene-level semantic hierarchies (object relationships, compositional structure) inherently require hyperbolic curvature to represent exponentially growing distances. This conflict causes either inaccurate bounding-box regression or shallow, disjointed scene descriptions that fail to capture multi-scale contextual relationships.

## Input / Output

The method consumes raw 3D point clouds of 40,000 points with xyz coordinates and F-dimensional features (R^{40,000×(3+F)}), tokenized via PointNet++ set-abstraction into 2,048 patches and downsampled to 1,024 scene tokens. It produces decoupled outputs: 3D bounding boxes for object localization (via iterative vote-query refinement with 256 sampled points) and natural-language captions generated from concatenated instance, object-aware-context, and context-aware-object features. The dual-decoder architecture separates instance features from context features, with the caption head consuming the first dimension of instance features while bidirectional attention operates on the second dimension.

## Method

CAC extends Vote2Cap-DETR++ with stage-specific non-Euclidean manifold projections that resolve the localization-contextualization conflict. In the encoder, Geodesic Oblique Self Attention projects Q and K matrices onto the Oblique Manifold (column-wise unit-norm constraint), replacing dot-product similarity with pairwise geodesic distances computed via arccos, which enforces isotropic attention distribution and stabilizes bounding-box regression. In the decoder, Bidirectional Lorentz Geodesic Attention maps features into the Lorentz hyperboloid via exponential mapping from the origin, then computes hyperbolic geodesic distances (arcosh-based) for both Object-aware Context and Context-aware Object cross-attention modules, capturing hierarchical semantic relationships across scene instances. The curvature complementarity between the two spaces—Oblique for optimization stability and Lorentz for hierarchical semantics—is theoretically grounded, and the three-stage training pipeline (ScanNet pretraining → MLE joint training → SCST reinforcement fine-tuning) with Hungarian-matched detection loss and CIDEr-reward caption loss achieves SOTA CIDEr@0.5 on both ScanRefer and Nr3D.

## Results

On ScanRefer (IoU=0.50, SCST), CAC(O&H) achieves 80.35 CIDEr@0.5, 39.95 BLEU-4@0.5, 26.94 METEOR@0.5, and 55.66 ROUGE-L@0.5, outperforming Vote2Cap-DETR++ (78.16 CIDEr@0.5) by +2.19 points. On Nr3D (IoU=0.50, SCST), CAC(O&H) reaches 52.78 CIDEr@0.5, 29.78 BLEU-4@0.5, 26.13 METEOR@0.5, and 55.94 ROUGE-L@0.5, surpassing Vote2Cap-DETR++ (47.62 CIDEr@0.5) by +5.16 points. The Oblique-only variant CAC(O) already gains +1.46 CIDEr@0.5 on ScanRefer and +3.37 on Nr3D over Vote2Cap-DETR++, confirming that the Lorentz decoder provides additional hierarchical-semantic benefits.

## Baselines & Comparisons

CAC(O&H) outperforms all 11 baselines on the primary CIDEr@0.5 metric across both benchmarks. On ScanRefer (SCST), it surpasses Vote2Cap-DETR++ by +2.19 CIDEr@0.5 (80.35 vs. 78.16) and BiCA^R by +3.93 (80.35 vs. 76.42), while also leading on BLEU-4@0.5 (39.95 vs. 39.72). On Nr3D (SCST), the margin widens substantially: CAC(O&H) gains +5.16 CIDEr@0.5 over Vote2Cap-DETR++ (52.78 vs. 47.62) and +6.36 over BiCA^R (52.78 vs. 46.42), with consistent improvements on BLEU-4 (+1.37) and ROUGE-L (+1.17). The Oblique-only variant CAC(O) already beats Vote2Cap-DETR++ on CIDEr@0.5 for both datasets, confirming that the encoder's geodesic self-attention alone improves localization. Notably, CAC(O&H) achieves the highest BLEU-4@0.5 on ScanRefer (39.95) and Nr3D (29.78) simultaneously, indicating the Lorentz decoder contributes to both fluency and hierarchical coherence. The paper also reports that BiCA's independently generated contextual/instance queries underperform when reproduced under identical conditions (Table 5), with CAC(O)^BiCA scoring 67.07 vs. CAC(O)'s 68.07 on ScanRefer CIDEr@0.5.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends Vote2Cap-DETR++ by replacing its standard self-attention and cross-attention modules with geodesic attention computed on two distinct non-Euclidean manifolds: the Oblique Manifold (column-wise unit-norm constraint) for encoder self-attention, and the Lorentz hyperboloid for decoder bidirectional cross-attention. The conceptual departure from prior work is the stage-specific manifold assignment—flat/oblique geometry for local spatial localization and hyperbolic geometry for global hierarchical semantics—drawing on independent lines of research in oblique-manifold optimization (ICP, few-shot classification) and hyperbolic embeddings (hierarchical representation, point cloud processing) that had not previously been combined in a single vision-language pipeline. The bidirectional attention structure is inherited from BiCA, though the authors note their reproduction of BiCA's independent query generation underperformed, motivating their retained decoupled architecture.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The stage-specific curvature assignment offers a principled geometric rationale for why different parts of a 3D captioning pipeline benefit from different metric structures, potentially guiding future work on non-Euclidean attention in other multi-stage vision-language tasks. However, the practical gains are modest (2.7% CIDEr on ScanRefer, 4.6% on Nr3D), and the inconsistency where Oblique-only outperforms Oblique+Lorentz under MLE training on Nr3D (50.99 vs. 49.90) raises questions about whether the hyperbolic component's benefit is robust or training-protocol-dependent. Open questions include whether the geometric motivation genuinely explains the gains versus acting as a beneficial regularization, whether the specific manifold choices are optimal, and how the approach scales to larger or more complex scenes beyond ScanNet's indoor settings.
