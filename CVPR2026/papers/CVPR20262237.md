## Problem

Weakly-supervised HOI detection relies on an external detector to generate human-object candidate pairs, but prior classification modules either crop union regions for each pair (incurring N_h × N_o forward passes and including irrelevant areas that cause false positives) or tightly couple the classifier to a specific detector, preventing transfer. The core gap is the lack of an efficient, detector-agnostic interaction module that can learn localized spatial cues under image-level supervision and transfer directly to instance-level reasoning without retraining.

## Input / Output

During training, the model consumes only an RGB image and image-level HOI triplet labels (e.g., 'human ride bicycle') with no bounding boxes; all vision and text encoders (CLIP, DINOv2) remain frozen. At inference, an off-the-shelf detector (Faster R-CNN, DETR, or H-DETR) supplies human and object instances as bounding boxes with class labels and confidence scores, which are used to construct region-aware masks that constrain query aggregation and interactiveness scoring to each specific instance pair. The output is a set of localized interaction triplets, each with a predicted action class and a confidence score computed as the product of the interaction score, pairwise interactiveness, and detector confidences.

## Method

RegFormer adopts a sequential HO→I decoding strategy: a pairwise instance encoder first constructs spatially grounded HO queries by computing patch-level cosine similarities between image features and text embeddings of the human and object classes, applying softmax to obtain patch importance scores, and aggregating weighted patch features into a single HO representation; an interaction decoder then performs cross-attention with these queries against the full image feature map and scores interactions via cosine similarity to action text embeddings. A key innovation is interactiveness-aware learning, which computes a pairwise interactiveness score (geometric mean of human and object scores) that gates the interaction prediction, combining local interactiveness (aggregated within the instance region) with masked global interactiveness (scene-level saliency restricted to the instance) to suppress non-interactive pairs. At inference, a simple region-aware mask (log of the indicator function over the bounding box) is injected into both the patch importance computation and the interactiveness aggregation, enabling direct transfer from image-level classification to instance-level detection with only a single backbone forward pass regardless of the number of candidate pairs.

## Results

On HICO-DET, RegFormer with H-DETR + DINO-B + CLIP-B achieves 38.14 mAP (Full), 40.31 (Rare), and 37.49 (Non-rare), surpassing the best fully supervised method (HOICLIP, 34.69 Full) by 3.45 mAP. With DETR + DINO-B, RegFormer reaches 32.90 Full / 35.18 Rare, outperforming the previous weakly supervised SOTA (Weakly HOI-CLIP, 22.89 Full) by over 10 mAP. On V-COCO, RegFormer with DETR achieves 57.5 AP_role2, improving over Weakly HOI-CLIP (48.1) by 9.4 points. In zero-shot HOI detection (RF-UC), RegFormer attains 31.53 mAP on unseen compositions, exceeding OpenCat (21.46) by 10.07 mAP despite OpenCat's 750k-image pretraining.

## Baselines & Comparisons

RegFormer consistently outperforms all prior weakly supervised methods across every detector and backbone configuration. Against the previous weakly supervised SOTA (Weakly HOI-CLIP, 22.89 Full), RegFormer with the same CLIP-RN50 backbone gains +2.19 mAP, and with DINO-B the gap widens to +10.44 mAP. On rare classes, RegFormer with H-DETR (40.31) exceeds the best fully supervised method (CMMP, 33.53) by 6.78 mAP, a setting where supervised approaches typically struggle most. On V-COCO, RegFormer (57.5 AP_role2 with DETR) surpasses Weakly HOI-CLIP (48.1) by 9.4 points and OpenCat with large-scale pretraining (36.1) by 21.4 points. In zero-shot evaluation (RF-UC), RegFormer achieves 31.53 mAP on unseen compositions, beating OpenCat (21.46) by 10.07 mAP and even surpassing several fully supervised zero-shot methods (e.g., CLIP4HOI at 28.47, CMMP at 29.45) despite using only image-level supervision and no large-scale pretraining. The ablation (Table 1) confirms that the spatially grounded query contributes +4.59 mAP (Full) and the interactiveness score adds a further +7.93 mAP over the spatially grounded variant alone, with the combined module requiring only 1 backbone forward pass versus N_h × N_o for ML-Decoder.

## Relation to Prior Work

RegFormer builds directly on the ML-Decoder architecture (Ridnik et al., WACV 2023) for its cross-attention-based multi-label classification backbone, and adopts the sequential HO→I decoding paradigm from prior two-stage HOI detectors. It departs from earlier weakly-supervised HOI methods (e.g., Weakly HOI-CLIP, OpenCat) that either crop union regions per instance pair (incurring N_h × N_o forward passes) or tightly couple the classifier to a specific detector. The key conceptual departure is the spatially grounded pairwise query construction combined with an interactiveness-aware gating signal, which together enable the module to transfer from image-level classification to instance-level detection without any retraining—a property absent in prior weakly-supervised HOI pipelines.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The practical significance lies in the efficiency gain (single backbone forward pass versus per-pair cropping) and the detector-agnostic transferability, which reduces the engineering burden of swapping detectors and mitigates error propagation from imperfect detections. The near-parity with fully supervised methods (32.90 vs. 33.80 Full mAP on HICO-DET) suggests that image-level supervision, when paired with spatially grounded reasoning, may be sufficient for most practical HOI detection needs. Open questions include whether the interactiveness gating generalizes to other relation detection tasks (e.g., visual relationship detection), whether the approach degrades gracefully when the off-the-shelf detector is weak, and whether the spatial grounding mechanism can be extended to video or 3D interaction understanding.
