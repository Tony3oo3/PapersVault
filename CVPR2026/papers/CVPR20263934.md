## Problem

Vision Transformers remain highly vulnerable to adversarial perturbations because their globally coupled self-attention propagates local corruptions across the entire image, while the spatially uniform feed-forward network enforces no intra-region consistency. Existing robust ViT methods target global representation stability or architectural regularization but overlook the intrinsic local vulnerability arising from the absence of explicit region-level semantic modeling, allowing perturbations on a few critical patches to rapidly compromise regional semantics and trigger global performance degradation.

## Input / Output

The method takes standard RGB images (32×32 for CIFAR-10/100, 224×224 for ImageNet/Imagenette) that are split into patch tokens (patch size 4 or 16) and produces a class label via the standard ViT classification head. ReMoE is a plug-and-play module inserted into specific Transformer layers (e.g., layers 6 and 10) that operates on the patch token sequence X ∈ R^{N×D} internally, requiring no changes to the input pipeline or output structure of the host ViT.

## Method

ReMoE replaces the standard FFN in ViT blocks with a region-aware Mixture-of-Experts layer comprising three multi-granularity experts (global, center, and regional) that process tokens at complementary spatial scales. An Attention Expert Activation (AEA) module derives region-level activation weights from self-attention outputs, and a Patch-to-Region (P2R) transformation aggregates patch-level attentions into region descriptors that drive a top-k routing mechanism, ensuring spatially coherent expert assignment rather than independent per-token gating. A Region-to-Patch (R2P) transformation scatters expert outputs back to their original spatial positions, and a Unified Region Integration module fuses the three branches with attention-driven region weights. On the optimization side, a Region-adaptive Adversarial Loss dynamically reweights clean and adversarial losses based on the discrepancy between their region activation vectors, while a Region Alignment Loss enforces angular consistency of region-level attention patterns between clean and adversarial inputs, jointly regularizing the model at the region level.

## Results

On CIFAR-10, ReMoE (DeiT-S + SAT) achieves 86.58% clean accuracy and 50.16% AutoAttack (AA) robust accuracy, outperforming all compared methods by 2.9–5.3 points under AA. On CIFAR-100, ReMoE reaches 59.78% clean accuracy and 26.82% AA robust accuracy, again leading all baselines. The method also generalizes to larger datasets: on ImageNet (ViT-S + SAT), ReMoE improves AA accuracy from 21.24% to 23.67%, and on Imagenette (DeiT-T + SAT) from 55.2% to 58.8%. Computational overhead is minimal, adding only ~0.01G FLOPs and ~2.4M parameters to ViT-S.

## Baselines & Comparisons

ReMoE consistently outperforms all six baselines on both CIFAR-10 and CIFAR-100 across every attack type. Against the strongest prior method ReiT (CVPR'24), ReMoE gains +2.85 points on CIFAR-10 AA (50.16 vs 47.31) and +1.93 points on CIFAR-100 AA (26.82 vs 24.89), while also matching or exceeding ReiT's clean accuracy. Compared to the training-strategy baselines SAT and TRADES, ReMoE improves CIFAR-10 AA by +5.26 and +3.91 points respectively, and CIFAR-100 AA by +5.29 and +3.44 points. Against the most recent 2025 methods FullLoRA (TIP'25) and PIAT (TIFS'25), ReMoE leads by +4.87 and +4.18 points on CIFAR-10 AA, and by +3.35 and +2.38 points on CIFAR-100 AA. Notably, ReMoE also achieves the highest clean accuracy (86.58% on CIFAR-10), indicating that the region-aware expert design does not sacrifice natural performance for robustness.

## Relation to Prior Work

ReMoE sits at the intersection of two research threads: adversarially robust ViT design (TORA-ViTs, ReiT, SpecFormer, PAT) and Mixture-of-Experts architectures (Switch Transformer, V-MoE, DyViT). Prior robust ViT work has focused on global regularization (spectral constraints, position-aware modulation) or training-strategy refinements, while prior MoE-in-ViT work routes experts at the individual token level. ReMoE departs from both by introducing a region-centric routing granularity: it replaces token-level top-k gating with attention-guided patch-to-region and region-to-patch transformations that activate multi-granularity experts (global, center, regional) in a spatially coherent manner, and pairs this with a region-aware adversarial loss that reweights losses by regional vulnerability.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The plug-and-play design (inserted at layers 6 and 10 with <1% FLOPs overhead) makes ReMoE immediately applicable to existing ViT pipelines without retraining from scratch, which is practically valuable for deploying robust vision models. The region-centric framing—arguing that ViTs' vulnerability stems from the absence of intra-region consistency rather than from global attention instability alone—offers a useful diagnostic lens for future robustness research. Open questions include whether the fixed spatial partitioning into K regions generalizes to irregular or object-centric regions, how the method interacts with other architectural robustness techniques (e.g., SpecFormer's spectral penalties), and whether the gains persist at the scale of ViT-L or ViT-H on full ImageNet-1K with stronger attacks.
