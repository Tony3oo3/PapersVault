## Problem

Event-based continuous-time optical flow estimation is severely limited by the absence of temporally dense ground-truth annotations, forcing reliance on self-supervised objectives. Existing contrast maximization (CM) frameworks sharpen the Image of Warped Events but neglect temporal continuity and structural coherence of motion trajectories, producing physically inconsistent results under complex or nonlinear dynamics.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/CVPR20260929/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method consumes asynchronous event streams converted into voxel grids with B temporal bins over a 100 ms window (B=15 for DSEC-Flow, B=5 or 15 for MVSEC depending on temporal granularity). It outputs a dense continuous-time flow field parameterized by quadratic Bezier control points at two spatial scales (1/4 and 1/8 resolution), enabling inference at arbitrary temporal resolution—trained at 10 Hz but theoretically unbounded at test time.

## Method

The core insight is Spatio-temporal Structural Consistency (STSC): events from the same physical surface form a temporally stable manifold, so correct motion compensation should yield stable local structure and coherent trajectories across time. Instead of warping all events to a single time slice, the method constructs Volumetric Warped Events (VWE) that preserve within-bin relative-time structure, then enforces two self-supervised losses—Local Structural Consistency (penalizing deviation of each relative-time phase from the volume's mean structure) and Trajectory Consistency (minimizing variance of spatio-temporal gradient fields across source bins). The architecture combines a bidirectional multi-scale encoder (Bi²ME) with correlation volumes and motion-aware difference features, a Scale Alternating Unit (SAU) for cross-scale fusion via a Weaving Gate, and a Bidirectional Refinement Update (BRU) that traverses the sequence in both directions for temporally unbiased estimation. Training uses a curriculum schedule that linearly decays the supervised endpoint loss while ramping up the STSC self-supervision, anchoring early learning to sparse ground truth before transitioning to dense manifold regularization.

## Results

On DSEC-Flow, the proposed STSC-Flow achieves an EPE of 0.663, outperforming the best LTR method IDNet (0.719) by 7.8% and the best HTR method BFlow (0.750) by 11.6%. It also attains the highest FWL score of 2.18 (vs. ResFlow's 2.14) and the lowest 3PE (1.60) and 2PE (2.67), representing 21.6% and 23.7% reductions over IDNet respectively. On MVSEC, the method achieves EPE 0.22 / %Out 0.00 at dt=1 and EPE 0.62 / %Out 0.78 at dt=4, surpassing the prior best EDCFlow (0.23/0.00 and 0.67/0.85) with relative improvements of 7.5% and 8.2% in the sparse-event setting.

## Baselines & Comparisons

The method outperforms all 13 compared baselines on DSEC-Flow across every reported metric. Against the strongest LTR competitor IDNet [31] (EPE 0.719), STSC-Flow reduces EPE by 7.8%, 3PE by 21.6%, and 2PE by 23.7%, while also achieving a lower AE (2.53 vs. 2.72). Among HTR-specific methods, it surpasses BFlow [8] (EPE 0.750, FWL 1.98) by 11.6% in EPE and 10.1% in FWL, and ResFlow [35] (EPE 0.754, FWL 2.14) by 12.1% in EPE and 1.9% in FWL. Self-supervised and contrast-maximization baselines (MultiCM, TamingCM, Motion-priorCM, E2FAI) are far behind, with EPEs ranging from 1.78 to 3.47, confirming the advantage of the hybrid supervised + STSC paradigm over purely self-supervised training. On MVSEC, the method beats EDCFlow [16] (the prior best) by 7.5% EPE and 8.2% %Out in the sparse-event setting (dt=4), and matches the best %Out (0.00) while achieving the lowest EPE (0.22) in the dense setting (dt=1), also outperforming TMA [17], IDNet [31], and E-RAFT [7].

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper departs from the dominant contrast maximization (CM) paradigm for self-supervised event-based flow, which sharpens the Image of Warped Events (IWE) but neglects temporal continuity. Instead, it introduces Spatio-temporal Structural Consistency (STSC), which warps events into a volumetric representation (VWE) and enforces both local structural stability and trajectory coherence across relative-time phases. Architecturally, it builds on the IDNet backbone but replaces its forward-only single-scale design with a bidirectional multi-scale encoder (Bi²ME) and a bidirectional refinement update (BRU) using cross-scale recurrent units. The curriculum-guided hybrid training strategy bridges the gap between sparse supervised endpoints and dense self-supervised manifold regularization, addressing a supervision gap that prior CM-based and implicit-supervision methods (BFlow, ResFlow, EVA-Flow) handle less explicitly.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The STSC principle offers a more physically grounded self-supervision signal than contrast maximization, and the finding that trajectory quality (FWL) *improves* with temporal resolution—while BFlow's degrades—suggests that structure-consistency constraints are better suited to continuous-time modeling than endpoint-alignment objectives. This opens a path toward event-based motion estimation that scales gracefully to arbitrarily high inference resolutions without additional supervision. Open questions include whether STSC generalizes to other event-based tasks (depth, 3D motion), whether the curriculum schedule is near-optimal, and whether the approach remains effective under extreme sparsity or multi-object occlusion beyond the driving-scenario benchmarks tested.
