## Problem

Existing deep unfolding networks for spectral compressive imaging operate directly on the full 3D hyperspectral data cube, forcing the solver to infer H×W×B unknowns from a single 2D coded measurement. This creates a severe dimensionality gap that makes each unfolding stage highly ill-posed and incurs heavy computational cost, limiting both reconstruction fidelity and efficiency.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/CVPR20260987/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The network takes a 2D CASSI compressed measurement (H×W′, where W′=W+d(B−1)) and the corresponding system sensing matrix Φ as input. Rather than outputting the full HSI directly, it predicts a compact spectral basis E (B×k) and subspace images A (H×W×k), from which the reconstructed HSI is recovered via the mode-3 product X = A ×₃ E. Experiments use 256×256 spatial patches with 31 spectral bands, physical rank k=11, and feature dimension C=16.

## Method

LRDUN reformulates the CASSI sensing model by substituting the low-rank factorization X = A ×₃ E, yielding two coupled sub-models (spectral basis and subspace image) that reduce the unknowns from HWB to Bk + HWk. An alternating proximal gradient descent is then unrolled into N stages, where each stage solves an E-problem (1D convolutions with QR-decomposition-enforced orthonormality) and an A-problem (U-Net with Spatial Conv Attention Blocks) via learnable ProxyNets. A Generalized Feature Unfolding Mechanism (GFUM) decouples the physical rank k from the network's feature dimension C≥k, splitting each variable into a k-dimensional physical component (driving data fidelity) and a (C−k)-dimensional auxiliary component (carrying spatially-varying information) that is preserved through the data-fidelity step and refined by the ProxyNet. The network is trained end-to-end with a multi-stage RMSE loss using Adam and cosine annealing over 300 epochs.

## Results

On the KAIST benchmark (10 scenes, 256×256), LRDUN-9stg achieves the highest average PSNR of 40.96 dB and SSIM of 0.982, surpassing the previous SOTA DUN MiJUN-9stg (40.86 dB, 0.982) while using only 30.58G FLOPs versus MiJUN's 73.67G. The parameter-sharing variant LRDUN-9stg* retains 40.75 dB PSNR with just 0.25M parameters. Even the lightweight LRDUN-3stg (0.69M params, 10.26G FLOPs) reaches 39.44 dB, outperforming all model-based, E2E, and PnP baselines by a wide margin.

## Baselines & Comparisons

LRDUN-9stg (40.96 dB) outperforms all 19 baselines in average PSNR on KAIST. Among DUNs, it surpasses MiJUN-9stg (40.86 dB) by 0.10 dB while using less than half the FLOPs (30.58G vs. 73.67G), and beats DPU-9stg (40.52 dB) and SSR-L (40.69 dB) by 0.44 and 0.27 dB respectively with substantially lower computational cost. Against E2E methods, LRDUN-9stg leads S2Transformer (36.48 dB) by 4.48 dB and BIRNAT (37.58 dB) by 3.38 dB, while using far fewer parameters (2.04M vs. 4.40M for BIRNAT). The parameter-sharing variant LRDUN-9stg* (0.25M params) matches RDLUF-9stg's parameter budget but exceeds its PSNR by 1.18 dB (40.75 vs. 39.57) with 73% fewer FLOPs. Model-based (TwIST, GAP-TV, DeSCI) and PnP (DIP-HSI) methods trail LRDUN by more than 14 dB, confirming the superiority of the low-rank unfolding paradigm over handcrafted priors and fixed denoisers.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper departs from the dominant DUN paradigm for CASSI reconstruction (DGSMP, GAP-Net, DAUHST, PADUT, RDLUF, DPU, SSR, LADE-DUN, MiJUN), where every stage operates on the full 3D HSI cube and back-projects 2D residuals into 3D space. Rather than treating low-rank structure as an auxiliary regularizer (as in TLPLN's tensor CP decomposition or the SP post-processing prior), LRDUN explicitly reformulates the sensing model itself via LR decomposition, yielding two new low-dimensional imaging models (spectral basis and subspace image) that the network solves directly. The unfolded PGD framework and the GFUM mechanism for decoupling physical rank from feature dimensionality are novel architectural contributions layered on this reformulated model.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The 3–4× FLOPs reduction (30.58G vs. 73–115G for comparable 9-stage DUNs) while matching or exceeding SOTA PSNR makes LRDUN a practically attractive option for real-time or resource-constrained CASSI deployment. The conceptual shift—solving for compact low-rank components instead of the full data cube—offers a template that could be extended to other compressive imaging geometries or to problems where the target has known low-dimensional structure. Open questions include whether the fixed physical rank (k=11) generalizes across diverse scenes and materials, whether the approach extends to dual-disperser or multi-frame CASSI systems, and whether the GFUM's auxiliary-component mechanism transfers to other unfolding architectures beyond CASSI.
