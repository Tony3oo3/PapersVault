## Problem

Existing Spatio-Temporal Video Grounding (STVG) methods suffer from error propagation between temporal and spatial stages: temporal models trained on video temporal grounding paradigms fail to capture the fine-grained spatial semantics embedded in STVG queries, so even minor temporal misalignment cascades into irreversible spatial grounding failures. Prior MLLM-based approaches rely on extensive supervised fine-tuning that degrades open-world generalization, while no prior work has applied reinforcement learning to jointly refine both temporal and spatial predictions in STVG.

## Input / Output

The framework takes a complete video clip (sampled at 2 FPS) and a natural language query, composed into a prompt template, as input. The MLLM first outputs a temporal interval (start/end timestamps), which is used to crop the video into sub-clips (up to 64 frames) fed to the spatial module. The spatial module then produces N object predictions per frame, each consisting of a binary classification probability and a bounding box, with the highest-scoring sequence selected at inference via Hungarian matching.

## Method

SARL-STG couples a pretrained MLLM (Qwen2.5-VL-7B) for temporal grounding with an open-set detector (GroundingDINO-B) for spatial localization, augmented by a Global-Local Feature Fusion Module (cross-attention with FPN) and a Temporal Enhancement Module (dual self-attention over time and query dimensions). Training proceeds in two stages: (1) large-scale SFT on 220K STVG-Wild samples with a joint cross-entropy + L1/GIoU + Focal Loss objective using LoRA, and (2) GRPO-based RL on a 10K high-quality subset where the spatial module acts as a discriminator, converting bounding-box accuracy into an implicit reward that injects spatial knowledge into the MLLM's temporal reasoning. The key mechanism is a spatio-temporal dynamic reward R_ST = R_tIoU + (tIoU)^α · R_sIoU, which forces the agent to first converge coarse temporal boundaries before shifting optimization toward fine-grained spatial alignment, with α controlling the transition steepness.

## Results

On the primary STVG benchmark HCSTVG V2, SARL-STG achieves 64.2 m_tIoU and 42.5 m_vIoU, surpassing the previous SOTA (TA-STVG) by 3.8 and 2.3 points respectively. On VidSTG, it reaches 52.3 m_tIoU / 35.5 m_vIoU (declarative) and 50.4 m_tIoU / 29.9 m_vIoU (interrogative). In zero-shot evaluation on ST-Align, SARL-STG attains 49.5 m_tIoU and 40.4 m_sIoU, outperforming all prior methods by a wide margin (e.g., +5.7 m_tIoU over CG-STVG). On temporal grounding (Charades-STA), it achieves 61.3 m_tIoU and 73.6 R@0.5, setting a new SOTA.

## Baselines & Comparisons

SARL-STG outperforms the previous task-specific SOTA, TA-STVG [10], on every metric across all three STVG benchmarks (e.g., +3.8 m_tIoU, +2.3 m_vIoU on HCSTVG V2; +0.6 m_tIoU, +1.1 m_vIoU on VidSTG declarative). Among MLLM-based methods, it surpasses the strongest competitor SpaceVLLM [42] by a large margin (+6.2 m_tIoU, +8.5 m_vIoU on HCSTVG) and LLaVA-ST [21] by an even wider gap (+43.0 m_tIoU on HCSTVG). On zero-shot ST-Align, SARL-STG leads CG-STVG [9] by +3.7 m_tIoU and LLaVA-ST [21] by +5.7 m_tIoU, while also reporting the only m_sIoU value (40.4) among zero-shot methods. For temporal grounding on Charades-STA, SARL-STG edges out VideoChat-R1 [6] by +0.5 m_tIoU and +1.9 R@0.5, though VideoChat-R1 retains a slight advantage on R@0.7 (50.2 vs. 49.1); on the out-of-domain ActivityNet set, the two are nearly tied (36.0 vs. 36.6 m_tIoU). In zero-shot Referring Expression Comprehension, SARL-STG substantially outperforms GroundingDINO [26] (+12.7 on RefCOCO Val) but remains slightly below DQ-DETR [17] on RefCOCO Val (86.7 vs. 88.6) while matching or exceeding it on test splits.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper departs from two established STVG paradigms: task-specific single/two-stage architectures (TubeDETR, CG-STVG, TA-STVG) that sacrifice generalization, and MLLM-based approaches (LLaVA-ST, SpaceVLLM) that rely on SFT and degrade the model's intrinsic semantic understanding. It repurposes the RLVR paradigm—recently successful in LLM post-training (DeepSeek-R1) and video reasoning (VideoChat-R1, Time-R1)—for the first time in STVG, using a pretrained MLLM (Qwen2.5-VL) for temporal grounding and an open-set detector (GroundingDINO) for spatial grounding, coupled through cross-attention and dual self-attention modules. The key conceptual departure is treating the spatial module as an implicit RL discriminator whose box-accuracy signal injects fine-grained spatial knowledge into the MLLM's temporal reasoning, rather than optimizing temporal and spatial losses independently.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

By introducing RL as a training paradigm for STVG, this work opens a path toward iterative spatio-temporal refinement that pure SFT cannot achieve, potentially reducing the error-propagation bottleneck that has plagued two-stage pipelines. The STVG-Wild dataset (220K samples, 50+ categories) addresses a persistent data-scarcity limitation in the field. However, the approach remains tied to specific backbone choices (Qwen2.5-VL + GroundingDINO), the RL stage only updates the LLM component, and the spatial module's demonstrated sensitivity to temporal misalignment (sIoU drops from 72.1 to 66.4 with 40% shift) means the cascading-error problem is mitigated but not eliminated. Open questions include whether the spatial-discriminator reward generalizes to other detector architectures or longer video sequences, and whether the GRPO-based refinement can be extended to multi-object or compositional STVG queries.
