## Problem

Existing virtual cell models capture only direct associations—Perturbation → RNA or Perturbation → Morphology—while overlooking the mechanistic RNA → Morphology link that connects transcriptional changes to phenotypic outcomes. This gap prevents simulating the cell as an integrated system where molecular events causally drive cellular form, limiting the path toward a predictive AI Virtual Cell.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/CVPR20262275/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The model takes a pre-perturbation gene expression vector (L1000 profile, averaged per compound into a single representative vector of dimension N_genes) and a drug identifier (SMILES string) as input, and produces a 512×512 RGB Cell Painting image (DAPI/tubulin/actin channels merged) as output. The gene expression is consumed as a continuous high-dimensional vector through a dedicated RNA encoder, while the drug is processed through a molecular encoder; the two are fused into a single latent condition vector that guides the diffusion process.

## Method

TRIDENT is a two-stage cascade: (1) a Transcription-Drug Condition Module, a VAE that encodes (G_pre, D) into a latent vector z regularized to reconstruct the post-perturbation transcriptome G_post, thereby forcing z to capture the perturbation's molecular effect; and (2) a Morphology Generation Module, a latent diffusion model with a Diffusion Transformer (DiT) backbone that denoises image latents via cross-attention against z concatenated with a time embedding. The VAE is trained with an ELBO objective (reconstruction of G_post + KL divergence), while the DiT is trained with a standard ε-prediction L2 loss in the compressed image latent space. The two modules are optimized jointly (L_TRIDENT = L_VAE + L_LDM), so the condition vector is simultaneously shaped to be predictive of molecular outcomes and informative for morphology synthesis. This explicit (Perturbation + RNA) → Morphology pathway, rather than a direct Perturbation → Morphology shortcut, is the key architectural distinction that yields substantially higher fidelity and better out-of-distribution generalization.

## Results

On the in-distribution test set, TRIDENT achieves a FID of 49.770, representing a 5× improvement over MorphoDiff (250.290) and a 7× improvement over Stable Diffusion (354.576), with KID of 0.013 versus 0.248 and 0.378 respectively. On the out-of-distribution test set (unseen compounds), TRIDENT's FID of 126.150 is more than 3× better than both baselines (387.135 and 393.129). The KID gap on OOD is particularly striking: 0.222 for TRIDENT versus 0.436 (MorphoDiff) and 0.543 (Stable Diffusion).

## Baselines & Comparisons

TRIDENT is compared against two baselines: MorphoDiff (a transcriptome-guided diffusion model for cellular morphology) and a fine-tuned unconditional Stable Diffusion. TRIDENT decisively outperforms both on every metric in both in-distribution and out-of-distribution settings. On the ID set, TRIDENT's FID of 49.770 is 5.0× lower than MorphoDiff (250.290) and 7.1× lower than Stable Diffusion (354.576); its KID of 0.013 is roughly 19× and 29× lower respectively. On the OOD set, the FID advantage narrows to ~3× (126.150 vs. 387.135 and 393.129), but TRIDENT still dominates by a wide margin. Qualitatively, both baselines collapse to a generic high-density monolayer and fail to capture drug-specific phenotypes (e.g., the low cell density induced by cytochalasin B), whereas TRIDENT generates morphologies virtually indistinguishable from ground truth.

## Relation to Prior Work

TRIDENT builds on the DiT architecture [34] and DDPM diffusion framework [20] to create a two-stage cascade: a VAE-based Transcription-Drug Condition Module that encodes pre-perturbation gene expression and drug identity into a latent vector, followed by a DiT that synthesizes morphology conditioned on that vector. Conceptually, it departs from the dominant Perturbation→Morphology line (MorphoDiff, MorphoDiff, IMPA, Mol2Image) and the Perturbation→RNA line (GEARS, STATE, scGen) by explicitly inserting the intermediate transcriptomic state as a conditioning signal, thereby modeling the full tripartite Perturbation+RNA→Morphology pathway. The closest prior work, RNA-CDM [8], generated histopathology tiles from RNA-seq but did not jointly condition on a perturbation or target single-cell morphology.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

By mechanistically linking transcriptome to phenome, TRIDENT provides a concrete architectural template for the AI Virtual Cell vision where molecular events causally drive observable cellular form. The MorphoGene dataset (98 compounds, paired L1000 + Cell Painting) is a useful resource, though its scope is limited to a single cell line (MCF7) and bulk profiles, constraining cross-cell-type generalization and cell-to-cell heterogeneity modeling. Key open questions include whether the RNA→Morphology mapping learned here transfers to single-cell transcriptomics, whether it generalizes across cell lines without retraining, and whether the cascade can be extended to multi-step temporal dynamics of perturbation response.
