## Problem

Diffusion-based virtual try-on methods depend on large-scale high-quality garment-person pairs, but open-source datasets contain only tens of thousands of examples and collecting more is prohibitively expensive, limiting generalization to real-world scenarios. Additionally, existing inpainting-based approaches face a persistent trade-off: masks small enough to preserve body parts (hands, feet) leak original garment textures, while masks large enough to suppress leakage overwrite and distort those same body parts.

## Input / Output

At inference, the method takes a person image and a garment image, horizontally concatenated into a single tensor of shape H×2W×3, and produces a synthesized person image wearing the target garment at 1024×768 resolution. Inference is two-stage: Stage 1 (10 denoising steps) generates a coarse result with a dilated cloth-bbox mask, and Stage 2 (25 denoising steps) refines it with a tighter garment-aware mask derived from the Stage 1 output and DensePose body-part segmentation. The try-off branch also outputs a disentangled garment image, which is used internally during training but not exposed as a user-facing output.

## Method

CCVTON builds on the Flux.1 backbone and introduces a Unified Diffusion Transformer (UDiT) that performs both try-on and try-off within a single DiT architecture by applying task-specific binary masks to the concatenated [garment‖person] input, enabling full parameter sharing. The key training innovation is Cycle-Consistent Learning (CCL): the try-off branch extracts a pseudo-garment from an in-the-wild person image, and the try-on branch transfers it back to a masked version of the same person, forming a reconstruction cycle supervised only by the original person image—eliminating the need for paired data. A Multi-Criteria Filtering Operation (CondViT cosine similarity + Qwen-VL binary judgment) pre-filters pseudo-garments from 1M wild images, and the retained garments serve as soft VGG perceptual regularization on the try-off branch to prevent collapse to trivial copying, rather than as direct pixel-level supervision. At inference, a Garment-Aware Mask Generation (GAMG) strategy uses a two-stage refinement: a dilated cloth-bbox mask in Stage 1 allows free garment deformation, then a tighter mask parsed from the coarse result combined with DensePose body-part masks in Stage 2 suppresses leakage while preserving hands and other non-target regions.

## Results

On VITON-HD (paired), CCVTON achieves FID 4.814, KID 0.171, SSIM 0.895, and LPIPS 0.056, outperforming the next-best method Leffa (FID 5.189, KID 0.488) by a clear margin. On DressCode (paired), CCVTON reaches FID 2.930 and KID 0.543, substantially ahead of Leffa (FID 4.806, KID 1.218) and CatVTON (FID 5.119, KID 1.020). In unpaired settings, CCVTON also leads on both datasets (VITON-HD: FID 8.521, KID 0.561; DressCode: FID 5.820, KID 1.727), confirming robustness beyond the training distribution.

## Baselines & Comparisons

CCVTON outperforms all eight baselines on every metric across both VITON-HD and DressCode in both paired and unpaired settings. The closest competitor is Leffa [46], which achieves FID 5.189 / KID 0.488 on VITON-HD (paired); CCVTON improves FID by 0.375 and KID by 0.317 over Leffa. On DressCode (paired), the gap widens considerably: CCVTON's FID of 2.930 beats Leffa (4.806) by nearly 2.0 points and CatVTON [7] (5.119) by over 2.2 points. Among the earlier diffusion-based methods, OOTDiffusion [38] and IDM-VTON [5] trail by 1.3–3.4 FID points on VITON-HD. In unpaired evaluation, CCVTON maintains its lead (e.g., KID 0.561 vs. Leffa's 0.609 on VITON-HD), indicating that the cycle-consistent training on in-the-wild data generalizes well beyond the paired training distribution. The ablation (Ours w/o GAMG) shows that the two-stage mask contributes an additional ~0.3 FID improvement, confirming that mask design is a meaningful factor on top of the CCL training strategy.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the cycle-consistent VTON line (DCTON, CycleVTON, and the diffusion-based variant of [45]) by replacing paired-data supervision with in-the-wild person images as the sole reconstruction target, while retaining a unified try-on/try-off architecture inspired by Voost and Two-Way Garment Transfer. It departs from prior inpainting-based VTON methods (IDM-VTON, CatVTON, PromptDresser) that rely on fixed or heuristically dilated masks by introducing a two-stage garment-aware mask that adapts to both garment geometry and body pose. The MCFO filtering step, combining CondViT similarity and Qwen-VL semantic judgment, is a novel data-quality gate not seen in prior VTON pipelines, and the use of VGG perceptual regularization (rather than pixel-wise loss) to anchor the try-off branch to pseudo-garments is a deliberate design choice to tolerate noise in the filtered extractions.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

By demonstrating that a diffusion VTON model can be effectively fine-tuned on ~700K in-the-wild person images without any additional paired annotations, CCVTON addresses the most practical bottleneck in deploying VTON systems at scale. The ablation revealing that MCFO-filtered pseudo-paired data (Exp.3) actually degrades performance relative to the base UDiT (Exp.2) highlights a non-trivial tension: more data is not automatically better when the pseudo-labels carry residual artifacts. Open questions include whether the cycle-consistent formulation extends to multi-garment or accessory try-on, how the two-stage inference cost (35 total denoising steps) compares to single-pass methods in latency-sensitive settings, and whether the approach generalizes to 3D or video try-on.
