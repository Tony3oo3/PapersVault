## Problem

Existing region proposal networks and open-vocabulary detectors rely on external prompts—text descriptions, exemplar images, or predefined category names—to localize potential objects, which limits their applicability in real-world scenarios where such prompts are unavailable (e.g., industrial defect detection, underwater object detection). Prompt-free alternatives that use vision-language models to generate textual descriptions introduce significant memory and latency costs. The paper addresses the gap of producing high-quality object proposals from purely visual features without any external prompt, while maintaining strong zero-shot cross-domain generalization.

## Input / Output

The method takes a single RGB image as input and requires no text prompts, exemplar images, or category names at inference time. It outputs a set of region proposals (bounding boxes) ranked by a combined classification-and-centerness score, evaluated at 100, 300, and 900 candidate box thresholds. The model is trained on only 5% of COCO (80 classes) plus 5% of ImageNet (1000 classes) with pseudo bounding boxes, and can be directly deployed to unseen domains—underwater, remote sensing, industrial, clipart, insect, and marine imagery—without any domain-specific fine-tuning.

## Method

PF-RPN builds on Grounding DINO (Swin-B backbone) and replaces its text-guided query mechanism with three visual-only modules. First, the Sparse Image-Aware Adapter (SIA) uses a Mixture-of-Experts router to select the top-k most informative feature levels from the multi-scale encoder output, then fuses them with a learnable embedding via cross-attention, providing a strong initial localization prior. Second, the Cascade Self-Prompt (CSP) module iteratively refines this embedding from deep to shallow layers using cosine-similarity masks and masked average pooling, progressively expanding object-consistent activations while suppressing background noise over three iterations. Third, the Centerness-Guided Query Selection (CG-QS) module scores each query by its predicted proximity to the object center and combines this with classification scores to select the highest-quality proposals. Training jointly optimizes contrastive classification, L1/GIoU regression, centerness, and MoE load-balancing losses on limited data, enabling zero-shot transfer to 19 cross-domain datasets.

## Results

On the CD-FSOD benchmark (six cross-domain datasets), PF-RPN achieves AR100/AR300/AR900 of 60.7/65.3/68.2, surpassing the strongest prompt-free baseline Cascade RPN (45.8/52.0/56.9) by 14.9/13.3/11.3 AR. On the ODinW13 benchmark (13 diverse domains), PF-RPN reaches AR100/AR300/AR900 of 76.5/78.6/79.8, improving over Cascade RPN by 15.6/13.1/9.6 AR. The model is trained on only 5% of COCO and 5% of ImageNet, yet generalizes zero-shot to all 19 evaluation datasets without fine-tuning. When integrated into well-trained detectors (DE-ViT, CD-ViTO), PF-RPN yields +3.7 AP on COCO and +5.5 AP on CD-FSOD.

## Baselines & Comparisons

PF-RPN outperforms all 11 baselines on both CD-FSOD and ODinW13 across every AR metric. Against the strongest prompt-free RPN baseline (Cascade RPN), PF-RPN gains +14.9/+13.3/+11.3 AR on CD-FSOD and +15.6/+13.1/+9.6 AR on ODinW13. Compared to the prompt-free OVD model GenerateU, PF-RPN improves by +13.0/+11.2/+12.5 AR on CD-FSOD and +9.2/+7.1/+7.6 AR on ODinW13. Against the MLLM Qwen2.5-VL-7B, PF-RPN achieves a massive +40.6/+45.2/+48.1 AR advantage on CD-FSOD, highlighting the latency and accuracy gap of VLM-based approaches. Even against Grounding DINO with full class-name prompts (GDINO†), PF-RPN surpasses it by +7.8/+11.8/+13.5 AR on CD-FSOD while requiring no text input at all. When plugged into existing detectors, PF-RPN adds +3.7 AP to DE-ViT on COCO and +5.5 AP to CD-ViTO on CD-FSOD, confirming its plug-and-play utility.

## Relation to Prior Work

PF-RPN builds directly on Grounding DINO's DETR-like architecture but replaces its text-embedding-based query mechanism with a learnable visual embedding, fundamentally shifting from language-guided to vision-only proposal generation. It departs from prompt-free OVD methods (GenerateU, CapDet, DetCLIPv3) that rely on computationally expensive VLM captioners to generate object descriptions, instead using a lightweight MoE-based adapter and iterative self-prompting to aggregate visual features. The centerness-guided query selection extends the standard DETR query selection paradigm by incorporating spatial centrality as an explicit scoring criterion, drawing on observations from anchor-free detection literature.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

PF-RPN addresses a practical gap in open-world deployment: in scenarios like industrial defect detection or underwater object detection, category names and exemplar images are often unavailable, yet existing OVD and prompt-free methods still require some form of external input. The demonstration that a model trained on only 5% of COCO can achieve zero-shot cross-domain proposal quality surpassing both text-prompted OVD models and VLM-based prompt-free methods suggests that visual self-supervision via learnable embeddings is a viable alternative to language grounding. Open questions include whether the approach generalizes to truly novel object categories beyond the 80 COCO classes seen during training, and whether the fixed threshold (δ=0.3) in the CSP module can be made adaptive for diverse domains.
