## Problem

Egocentric action anticipation is hampered by the inherent stochasticity of future actions and the compositional, high-dimensional verb-noun action space. Prevailing end-to-end methods lack explicit semantic reasoning, while holistic action prototypes suffer from feature entanglement (e.g., 'pick-up-bottle' vs. 'open-bottle') and unreliable initialization under long-tailed distributions. The paper asks whether disentangled, vision-language-aligned verb and noun prototypes can serve as robust semantic anchors to transform unconstrained temporal prediction into a structured, conditional forecasting task.

## Input / Output

The model consumes egocentric RGB video frames resized to 224×224, sampled at 2 fps over a 15-second observation window (EPIC-KITCHENS-100), 10 seconds (EGTEA), or 0.25 fps (50-Salads), with optional optical flow as an additional modality. A frozen LaViLa (TSF-B) backbone pre-trained on Ego4D extracts spatiotemporal features. The output is a triple of predictions—verb class, noun class, and the joint verb-noun action pair—produced at the end of the observation period for the action commencing after a 1-second lead time (τ_a = 1 s).

## Method

PAR-VLA operates in three sequential stages. First, Disentangled Prototypes Acquisition fine-tunes only verb and noun classification heads on the frozen visual backbone with cross-entropy plus cosine-similarity alignment to pre-extracted text embeddings, then clusters the resulting features into k sub-prototypes per class to capture intra-class diversity. Second, Prototypical Context Reasoning-Guided Verb-Noun Encoding runs two asymmetric branches (temporal-focused for verbs, spatiotemporal for nouns), each containing stacked layers of self-attention, cross-branch interaction attention, and a novel Grouped ProtoAttention module that computes cosine similarity to the full prototype set, samples the most relevant prototypes via a multinomial distribution, and performs prototype-guided cross-attention to inject semantic context. Third, Dual-Stream Symbiotic Predictive Decoders use interleaved self- and cross-attention layers over fused verb-noun feature sequences to model fine-grained interdependencies for joint anticipation. Training combines a self-supervised feature-prediction MSE loss, cross-entropy classification loss, and a vision-language alignment cosine loss that matches visual-prototype similarity vectors to text-label similarity vectors, with prototypes kept trainable throughout both training stages.

## Results

On EPIC-KITCHENS-100, PAR-VLA (TSF-B, RGB) achieves Top-5 recall of 40.3% (verb), 43.2% (noun), and 22.5% (action), surpassing the previous best RGB-only method RAFTformer (18.0% action) by 4.5 points and SGEAR-2B (19.5%) by 3.0 points. With RGB+Flow input, performance further improves to 44.9%/47.6%/24.1%. On EGTEA, PAR-VLA reaches 70.2% Top-5 recall (RGB) and 72.7% (RGB+Flow), outperforming the prior best VS-TransGRU (67.4%) by 2.8 points. On 50-Salads, PAR-VLA achieves 65.4% Top-1 accuracy, exceeding SGEAR-2B (63.9%) by 1.5 points.

## Baselines & Comparisons

PAR-VLA consistently outperforms all 12 baselines across all three datasets. On EPIC-KITCHENS-100, it beats the strongest prior methods—RAFTformer-2B (19.1% action) and SGEAR-2B (19.5%)—by 3.4 and 3.0 points respectively, while also surpassing multi-modal approaches like AVT+ (15.9%) and RULSTM (14.0%) that rely on optical flow or object detection. On EGTEA, PAR-VLA (70.2% RGB) exceeds VS-TransGRU (67.4%) by 2.8 points and RAFTformer (63.5%) by 6.7 points, notably without requiring optical flow. On 50-Salads, the 10.5-point margin over RAFTformer (54.9%) and 1.5-point gain over SGEAR-2B (63.9%) confirm the advantage of the prototypical reasoning mechanism. The ablation studies further show that PAR and VLA components are complementary: removing either degrades performance, and their joint use yields the best results on both EPIC-KITCHENS-100 (22.5% vs. 21.7% or 20.3% individually) and EGTEA (70.2% vs. 68.4% or 68.1%).

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper synthesizes three research threads—egocentric action anticipation (AVT, RAFTformer, UADT), prototypical learning (IPL, S-GEAR), and vision-language model alignment—into a unified framework. It departs from prior prototypical approaches (S-GEAR, IPL) that used holistic verb-noun action prototypes or verb-only prototypes by learning *disentangled* verb and noun prototype groups via Ego-VLM cross-modal alignment, and introduces a Grouped ProtoAttention mechanism that retrieves multiple sub-prototypes per class to capture intra-class diversity. The dual-stream symbiotic decoder for joint verb-noun prediction extends the decoupled prediction paradigm of UADT and Symbiotic Attention with explicit prototype-guided semantic reasoning at the encoding stage.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The paper demonstrates that transforming an unconstrained temporal prediction problem into a semantically-anchored conditional forecasting task via disentangled VLM-aligned prototypes is a principled way to reduce prediction uncertainty in egocentric action anticipation, with consistent SOTA gains across three datasets. A notable practical finding is that RGB-only PAR-VLA outperforms several multimodal (RGB+Flow+Obj) baselines, suggesting that semantic structure can substitute for additional sensor modalities. Open questions include whether the approach scales to much larger action taxonomies (e.g., Ego4D's 2,000+ categories), whether the VLM alignment stage is truly necessary versus simpler text-embedding initialization, and how the framework generalizes to non-egocentric or multi-person anticipation settings.
