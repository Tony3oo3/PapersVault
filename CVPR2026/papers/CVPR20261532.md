## Problem

In-Context Learning in Large Multimodal Models is computationally expensive due to visual token overhead and highly sensitive to demonstration selection and ordering. Prior approximation methods (e.g., MimIC, LIVE) model the ICL effect as a simple additive linear shift to hidden states, but the paper shows via a rigorous attention decomposition that the true ICL mechanism is a query-dependent, non-linear dynamic mixture of standard self-attention and context values — a structure that linear shift vectors fundamentally cannot capture.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/CVPR20261532/cropped_page1_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method operates on a frozen LMM backbone (LLaVA-Interleave-7B or Idefics2-8B-base) and takes an RGB image paired with a text query (VQA) or an image alone (captioning) as input. Instead of prepending explicit demonstration examples to the context, HiFICL injects per-head virtual key-value matrices of sequence length n=8 and low rank r (typically 8) directly into each attention head, so the model processes only the single query image and prompt at inference time. Outputs are free-form text answers or captions, evaluated with VQA accuracy and CIDEr-D respectively.

## Method

HiFICL derives the exact attention output under ICL as α·SA(q,K,V) + β·V_D, where α and β are query-dependent coefficients, and then directly parameterizes the source (K_D, V_D) with learnable 'virtual key-value pairs' per attention head rather than approximating the downstream shift. To keep the parameter count small and training stable, each virtual matrix is factorized into a dual low-rank product (K_A·K_B, V_A·V_B with r≪d_h), and V_B is zero-initialized so the contextual term starts at zero. Training is teacher-free and end-to-end: only the final cross-entropy task loss is backpropagated through the frozen backbone and the injected virtual parameters, eliminating the costly teacher-student alignment used by MimIC. This design makes HiFICL a form of context-aware PEFT — dynamic and content-dependent through the native softmax interaction — in contrast to LoRA's static, input-agnostic weight-space updates, while using roughly 8× fewer parameters than LoRA or MimIC on LLaVA.

## Results

On LLaVA-Interleave-7B, HiFICL achieves 74.66% VQAv2 accuracy, 54.19% OK-VQA accuracy, and 1.3315 CIDEr-D on COCO Captioning, using only 2.2M trainable parameters. On Idefics2-8B, it reaches 72.08% VQAv2, 59.56% OK-VQA, and 1.2951 CIDEr-D. HiFICL consistently outperforms all trainable baselines (LoRA, LIVE, MimIC) and 8-shot ICL across both models and all three benchmarks, while requiring roughly 8× fewer parameters than LoRA and MimIC on LLaVA.

## Baselines & Comparisons

HiFICL outperforms the prior state-of-the-art MimIC [19] on all three benchmarks for both models: on LLaVA it gains +1.9% on OK-VQA (54.19 vs. 52.29) and +0.015 CIDEr-D on COCO, while on Idefics2 the VQAv2 gap widens to +2.79% (72.08 vs. 69.29). Against LoRA [17], HiFICL achieves substantially larger margins—e.g., +6.0% OK-VQA on LLaVA (54.19 vs. 48.19) and +4.51% on Idefics2 (59.56 vs. 55.05)—while using roughly 8× fewer parameters (2.2M vs. 19.7M/17.6M). Compared to LIVE [36], HiFICL leads by +2.42% OK-VQA on LLaVA and +4.70% on Idefics2, with a modest parameter increase (2.2M vs. 0.13M). Notably, HiFICL also surpasses 8-shot ICL by a wide margin (e.g., +6.46% VQAv2 on Idefics2) while being ~2× faster at inference, and it avoids the performance degradation that 32-shot ICL suffers on Idefics2 (50.55% VQAv2). In hallucination analysis (CHAIR), HiFICL achieves the lowest instance-level score (CHAIR_i = 2.2) and highest Recall (45.7) on COCO, outperforming 8-shot ICL (CHAIR_i = 3.9) and MimIC (CHAIR_i = 2.9).

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper departs from the dominant 'linear shift approximation' paradigm for ICL (LIVE, MimIC, Task/Function Vectors) by providing a rigorous decomposition of the attention formula that shows the ICL effect is an exact, analytical consequence of the softmax mechanism rather than an externally added offset. It repurposes LoRA's low-rank factorization concept but applies it to virtual key-value pairs in the activation space rather than static weight-space updates, creating a dynamic, context-aware PEFT. The teacher-free end-to-end training objective also breaks from MimIC's costly teacher-student distillation paradigm, and the paper explicitly positions HiFICL as the first concrete training-time instantiation of the 'inference-time finetuning' hypothesis from mechanistic interpretability literature.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The clean mathematical reframing—from approximating an indirect effect to directly parameterizing its source—provides a more principled foundation for the ICL approximation literature and clarifies why linear shift methods are structurally limited. The dynamic PEFT perspective (context-aware vs. input-agnostic) opens a new design axis for parameter-efficient adaptation that could influence future PEFT methods beyond ICL. However, the evaluation is limited to two 7–8B models and three VQA/captioning benchmarks, leaving open whether the virtual KV approach scales to larger models, more diverse task types (e.g., reasoning, grounding), or longer contexts. The modest parameter budget (~2.2M) and significant training efficiency gains over MimIC make it practically attractive, but the 1–3% margins over the prior SOTA suggest the ceiling of this paradigm may be approaching.
