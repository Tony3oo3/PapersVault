## Problem

Existing 3D human-object reconstruction methods rely heavily on physical contact cues and local geometric proximity, making them unable to handle non-contact interactions (e.g., gazing at or pointing toward an object) and producing globally implausible reconstructions that ignore holistic appearance context. No prior framework jointly reconstructs textured 3D humans and objects while reasoning about the full semantic spectrum of human-object interactions.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/CVPR20262115/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method takes a single RGB image and produces textured 3D human and object representations as 3D Gaussians (convertible to meshes), along with a 2D background. GPT-4 generates two text prompts from the input image—a holistic interaction description and a contact-part specification—which condition the optimization. The 3D human is parameterized by SMPL-X pose/shape plus Gaussian attributes in canonical space, while the object uses a canonical Gaussian set with rotation, translation, and scale; the entire pipeline is optimization-based (200 steps) rather than feed-forward.

## Method

TeHOR is a two-stage optimization framework. The reconstruction stage initializes 3D human Gaussians via LHM, 3D object Gaussians via InstantMesh (converted through 3DGS), and a 2D background via SmartEraser. The HOI optimization stage jointly refines both Gaussian sets over 200 steps using three losses: a reconstruction loss (MSE on front-view RGB and silhouettes), a contact loss (proximity between text-specified contacting body-part Gaussians and the nearest object surface within 10 cm), and a novel appearance loss that applies score distillation sampling with StableDiffusion conditioned on the GPT-4-generated holistic text prompt, sampling random novel viewpoints and compositing renders onto the 2D background. The key insight is that the diffusion network's text-conditioned score gradients provide dense, pixel-level semantic supervision that captures holistic interaction context—object orientation, gaze direction, body posture—beyond what contact geometry alone can express, enabling accurate reconstruction even in non-contact scenarios.

## Results

On Open3DHOI, TeHOR achieves the best scores across all four metrics: CD_human = 4.941 cm, CD_object = 16.701 cm, Contact F1 = 0.412, and Collision = 0.047. On BEHAVE, TeHOR similarly leads with CD_human = 5.615 cm, CD_object = 17.339 cm, Contact F1 = 0.412, and Collision = 0.016. In the non-contact subset of Open3DHOI (Table 5), TeHOR's advantage widens dramatically, reducing CD_object from 25.4–65.5 cm (baselines) to 17.5 cm, while also achieving the lowest collision rate (0.005).

## Baselines & Comparisons

TeHOR outperforms all five baselines on CD_human, CD_object, and Contact F1 on both Open3DHOI and BEHAVE. The largest margin is on object reconstruction: TeHOR's CD_object of 16.701 cm on Open3DHOI is roughly 3× better than PHOSA (49.180 cm) and ~30% better than the next-best HOI-Gaussian (19.363 cm). On BEHAVE, TeHOR's Contact F1 of 0.412 substantially exceeds InteractVLM (0.379) and HOI-Gaussian (0.371), while LEMON+PICO collapses to 0.082, indicating severe contact-prediction failure. The only metric where TeHOR is not best is Collision on BEHAVE, where PHOSA achieves 0.010 vs. TeHOR's 0.016, though the gap is small. In the non-contact subset (Table 5), where contact cues are entirely absent, TeHOR's CD_object of 17.546 cm is 45–73% lower than all baselines, confirming that text-guided semantic reasoning is the key differentiator in the absence of physical contact.

## Relation to Prior Work

TeHOR departs from the dominant contact-driven paradigm in 3D human-object reconstruction (PHOSA, CONTHO, InteractVLM, HOI-Gaussian) by replacing local geometric proximity fitting with a text-guided semantic alignment objective. The 3D representation builds on 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS, Mip-Splatting) with LHM for human and InstantMesh for object initialization, while the core optimization leverages score distillation sampling from DreamFusion, repurposing a pre-trained StableDiffusion network as a text-conditioned appearance prior. The key conceptual shift is treating a vision-language model (GPT-4) as a semantic reasoner that produces interaction descriptions, which then drive holistic multi-view appearance alignment rather than point-to-point contact enforcement.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

By grounding reconstruction in textual semantics rather than physical contact alone, TeHOR addresses a clear gap: non-contact interactions (gazing, pointing, approaching) where contact-based methods have no signal. Being the first to jointly produce textured 3D human and object assets also opens the door to AR/VR and digital content pipelines that require appearance, not just geometry. Open questions include the computational cost of 200 diffusion-based optimization steps per image, the dependence on GPT-4 captioning quality, and whether the approach generalizes to multi-person or dynamic (video) settings.
