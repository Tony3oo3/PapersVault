## Problem

Unified multimodal models can accept multi-modal inputs but typically produce only single-modality outputs, making natural multi-turn interleaved text-image generation difficult. The core obstacle is the scarcity of large-scale, high-quality interleaved training data, which leads to sparse supervision, unstable joint optimization, and an inability to learn long-range cross-modal dependencies and modality transitions.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/CVPR20263219/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The model accepts text prompts, single reference images, or multi-image references as input, with images encoded via a frozen ViT and VAE. It autoregressively produces interleaved text-image sequences where each generated element is appended to the conditioning history; images are synthesized at native aspect ratios spanning ~196² to 1440² pixels. The planner emits a dense textual prompt (enclosed in <imagine> tags) at each image-generation step, which serves as the visualizer's conditioning signal alongside VAE-encoded reference tokens.

## Method

Wan-Weaver adopts a Mixture-of-Transformers architecture with a planner expert (initialized from Qwen2.5-VL 32B) and a visualizer expert (a DiT trained from scratch with flow-matching loss). The key insight is decomposing interleaved generation into textual planning and visual coherence, enabling decoupled training: the planner is fine-tuned on large-scale synthetic textual-proxy data where images are replaced by dense captions, while the visualizer is trained independently on reference-guided generation data (T2I, single-image-to-image, multi-image-to-image) with the planner frozen. A Dense Prompt Context Window (DPCW) mechanism defines a self-attention window centered on the dense-prompt position, allowing the visualizer to access precise long-range contextual features without excessive noise. This design eliminates the need for any real interleaved training data while achieving long-range textual coherence and visual consistency across generated sequences.

## Results

On the OpenING interleaved generation benchmark, Wan-Weaver achieves an overall score of 8.67, surpassing all open-source baselines (best: SEED-X at 5.84) and integrated pipelines (GPT-4o+DALL-E3 at 8.20, Gemini+Flux at 7.23), while trailing only the commercial Nano Banana (8.85) by 0.18. On the authors' new WeaverBench, Wan-Weaver scores 8.43 overall, edging out Nano Banana (8.38) and substantially outperforming Emu3.5 (7.53). On single-modality tasks, Wan-Weaver achieves 74.9 on MMMU, 84.3 on MathVista, 0.89 on GenEval, 87.21 on DPG, 4.31 on ImgEdit, and 7.39 on GEdit-EN, outperforming both unified models (BAGEL, UniWorld-V1) and specialized generators.

## Baselines & Comparisons

Wan-Weaver (8.67 overall) decisively outperforms all open-source unified models on OpenING, with the largest gap over SEED-X (5.84, +2.83) and Emu3 (5.76, +2.91), and also beats the GPT-4o+DALL-E3 pipeline (8.20, +0.47) and Gemini+Flux pipeline (7.23, +1.44). Against the top commercial system Nano Banana (8.85), Wan-Weaver trails by only 0.18 overall but wins on Completeness (9.41 vs. 9.34) and Richness (8.03 vs. 8.00), while losing on IT Coherency (8.78 vs. 9.27) and Multi-step Consistency (8.56 vs. 8.70). On the authors' WeaverBench, Wan-Weaver (8.43) narrowly beats Nano Banana (8.38) and substantially outperforms Emu3.5 (7.53, +0.90), Anole (3.74, +4.69), and Orthus (1.89, +6.54). For single-modality tasks, Wan-Weaver surpasses BAGEL on all six benchmarks (e.g., MMMU 74.9 vs. 55.3, GenEval 0.89 vs. 0.88, ImgEdit 4.31 vs. 3.20) and UniWorld-V1 on all reported metrics, while also exceeding specialized models like FLUX.1-dev on DPG (87.21 vs. 84.0) and Step1X-Edit on both editing benchmarks (4.31 vs. 3.06 on ImgEdit, 7.39 vs. 6.70 on GEdit-EN).

## Relation to Prior Work

Wan-Weaver builds directly on the Mixture-of-Transformers (MoT) architecture introduced by Liang et al. [34] and instantiated in BAGEL [16], adopting its separated-expert design for a planner and visualizer. However, it departs fundamentally from prior unified multimodal models (BAGEL, Mogao, Show-o, Emu3) that rely on joint training over scarce interleaved data: here, the planner is trained on synthetic textual-proxy sequences while the visualizer is trained independently on reference-guided generation data, with the two experts never jointly optimized. The Dense Prompt Context Window mechanism, inspired by LLM context-window concepts, is a novel conditioning strategy that has no direct analogue in prior unified generation work.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The core practical contribution is a training recipe that sidesteps the well-known data-scarcity bottleneck for interleaved generation, making the task accessible to groups without access to large-scale real interleaved corpora. The competitive performance with Nano Banana (8.67 vs. 8.85 overall on OpenING) without any real interleaved data suggests the decomposition into planning and visual coherence is a viable general principle. Open questions include whether the textual-proxy approach extends to video or audio interleaving, whether the planner-visualizer split remains optimal at larger scales, and whether the small gap to commercial systems reflects a fundamental ceiling of the decoupled paradigm or simply a data-quality limitation.
