## Problem

Existing vision-language models for panoramic dental radiograph analysis operate under a static single-pass paradigm that cannot revisit ambiguous regions or perform the bilateral symmetry comparisons dentists routinely use to identify subtle lesions. Traditional detectors provide only bounding boxes without diagnostic rationale, and single-pass VLMs lack the iterative thought-action-observation loop needed for clinically coherent, multi-step reasoning on high-resolution panoramic images.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/CVPR20260563/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The model consumes a single panoramic dental radiograph image and a free-form text query, then produces an iterative trajectory of thoughts, tool invocations (Zoom-In for localized high-resolution crops, Mirror-In for horizontally mirrored contralateral views), and observations, culminating in a structured diagnostic report. The Mirror-In tool is distinctive: given a selected region [x₁,x₂]×[y₁,y₂], it retrieves the mirrored counterpart I(W−x, y) with 1.5× padding to absorb minor anatomical asymmetry, forming a dual-view pair for comparative reasoning. Evaluation uses GPT-5-mini as a continuous 0.0–1.0 rubric judge on open-ended answers, with Avg@5 reporting on MMOral-X.

## Method

OralGPT-Plus is built on Qwen2.5-VL-3B/7B-Instruct and trained in two stages. First, Dentist-like Instruction Tuning performs full-parameter SFT (vision encoder frozen) on 4k expert-curated trajectories from the DentalProbe dataset, teaching the model a stable tool-use schema for global inspection, localized examination, and symmetry-based verification. Second, Reinspection-Driven RL optimizes under GRPO with a hybrid reward combining three components: a rubric-based continuous score from GPT-5-mini (replacing sparse binary rewards), a conditioned diagnostic-driven reward that gates a curiosity bonus on sufficient rubric confidence to prevent reward hacking, and a format reward for structural correctness. The key architectural insight is the Mirror-In tool, which converts dental bilateral symmetry from an implicit prior into an explicit model action, enabling contralateral comparison that the ablations show is critical for detecting low-contrast and subtle abnormalities.

## Results

On MMOral-X, OralGPT-Plus-7B achieves 43.16 (Simple), 20.60 (Moderate), and 24.96 (Complex), surpassing the strongest proprietary baseline GPT-5 (32.90 / 8.30 / 9.30) by 10.3, 12.3, and 15.7 points respectively. On MMOral-OPG open-ended VQA, OralGPT-Plus-7B attains an overall score of 45.35, outperforming GPT-5 (42.34) and the best medical-specific model HuatuoGPT-V (36.02). The 7B model leads in key diagnostic sub-tasks including Teeth (45.34), Patho (33.56), and Jaw (75.38), while the 3B variant (24.48 overall) shows that sufficient model capacity is critical for tool-driven RL gains.

## Baselines & Comparisons

OralGPT-Plus-7B outperforms all 16 baselines across every difficulty level on MMOral-X and the overall score on MMOral-OPG. Against the strongest proprietary model GPT-5, it gains +10.3 on Simple, +12.3 on Moderate, and +15.7 on Complex (MMOral-X), and +3.0 on MMOral-OPG overall (45.35 vs. 42.34). Among medical-specific VLMs, the gap is even larger: HuatuoGPT-V (36B) scores only 6.40/2.62/4.02 on MMOral-X and 36.02 overall on MMOral-OPG, trailing OralGPT-Plus-7B by over 36 points on Simple and 9.3 points overall. The agentic baseline Mini-o3 (7B) performs poorly (3.14/2.18/2.04 on MMOral-X, 14.72 OPG overall), indicating that generic visual-search tool use does not transfer to dental diagnosis without domain-specific trajectory supervision. Notably, the 235B-parameter Qwen3-VL-thinking model scores only 10.24/3.88/3.42 on MMOral-X, confirming that raw model scale alone cannot compensate for the absence of structured diagnostic tool interaction.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the agentic VLM paradigm (as explored by Mini-o3 for visual search) to dental panoramic radiograph analysis, departing from the single-pass VLM paradigm used by prior dental models like OralGPT and MMOral-OPG. It builds on Qwen2.5-VL as the base architecture and adapts GRPO (from DeepSeekMath) with a novel hybrid reward system that incorporates a curiosity bonus (from Pixel Reasoner) conditioned on rubric-based diagnostic confidence. The key conceptual departure is the introduction of the 'Mirror-In' tool, which formalizes the clinical practice of bilateral symmetry comparison into an explicit model action—something absent from prior agentic VLMs that rely solely on Zoom-In operations.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

This work demonstrates that a small (7B) agentic model can substantially outperform frontier proprietary models (GPT-5, Gemini) on holistic panoramic diagnosis, suggesting that structured tool-augmented reasoning can compensate for raw model scale in domain-specific medical tasks. The Mirror-In tool and conditioned diagnostic-driven reward offer transferable design principles for other imaging modalities with inherent symmetry (e.g., chest X-rays, brain MRI). However, absolute scores remain low (best ~43/100 on Simple), the 3B variant fails to benefit from RL, and evaluation depends on GPT-5-mini as judge—open questions include whether the paradigm generalizes beyond dental imaging and whether larger base models would close the remaining gap.
