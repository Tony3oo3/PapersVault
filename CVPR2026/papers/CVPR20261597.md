## Problem

Existing panoramic semantic segmentation methods that use depth information rely on HHA representation, which misses the second degree of freedom of surface normal direction and depends on camera intrinsics and posture. Additionally, standard multi-modal fusion applies a single strategy uniformly across all image regions, ignoring the heterogeneous distortion patterns introduced by equirectangular projection (ERP) at different latitudes.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/CVPR20261597/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method takes an RGB panoramic image (2048×4096 pixels) and a corresponding depth map as input. The depth map is converted into a 3-channel REL representation (Rectified Depth, EGVIA, LOA) computed purely from the depth image without requiring camera intrinsics or extrinsics. The output is a pixel-wise semantic segmentation mask over the ERP-projected panoramic image, evaluated on 13 semantic classes. During inference, SMMF processes overlapping 1080×1080 regions sampled on the cylinder side surface (3 rows × 7 columns, stride 720 px), allowing regions to wrap across the left-right image boundary.

## Method

REL-SF4PASS introduces two key components on top of the CMX transformer backbone. First, the REL representation encodes each pixel's 3D location and surface normal using cylindrical coordinates (ρθz): Rectified Depth captures planar distance, EGVIA adaptively fuses height and the normal-gravity angle (weighting by surface orientation), and LOA encodes the angle between the surface normal and the local tangent line to the latitude circle. Second, SMMF (Spherical-dynamic Multi-Modal Fusion) partitions the cylinder side surface into overlapping m×n regions and uses a Mixture-of-Experts gate network to independently select among B fusion operations (from no-fusion to full RGB-D fusion) per region, with a two-stage soft-to-hard training schedule and early-stop constraint that propagates 'no fusion' decisions forward. This region-level adaptivity exploits the spherical prior that pixels at the same latitude share similar distortion and semantic content, while the cylinder-surface sampling reduces structural breakage from ERP expansion.

## Results

On Stanford2D3D Panoramic datasets, REL-SF4PASS (RGB-REL) achieves 63.06% average mIoU across all 3 official folds and 67.37% mIoU on fold 1, outperforming the strongest RGB-D baseline CMX* (RGB-HHA) by 2.35% and 3.39% respectively. Under SGA validation (3D rotation disturbance up to 5°/5°/360°), REL-SF4PASS achieves a mean mIoU of 65.38 with a variance of 1.607, reducing variance by ~70% compared to HHA (variance 5.316). Against the RGB-only SGAT4PASS under the same SGA protocol, REL-SF4PASS-Reproj improves mean mIoU by 9.06 points (65.04 vs. 55.98) while cutting variance from 0.066 to 0.021.

## Baselines & Comparisons

REL-SF4PASS outperforms all 13 compared baselines on both average mIoU and fold-1 mIoU. Among RGB-only methods, the closest competitor is Twin (55.85% Avg mIoU), which REL-SF4PASS surpasses by 7.21 points; SGAT4PASS (55.3%) and Trans4PASS+ (53.7%) trail by 7.76 and 9.36 points respectively. Among RGB-D methods, the strongest prior work is CMX* with HHA (60.71% Avg mIoU, 63.98% F1 mIoU), which REL-SF4PASS beats by 2.35% and 3.39% respectively; SFSS with full HHA (60.60%) is the next closest at a 2.46-point gap. The gap over earlier RGB-D baselines is larger: PanoFormer RGB-D (57.03%), HoHoNet RGB-D (56.73%), and CBFC RGB-D (56.70%) all fall 6+ points behind. Under SGA validation, REL-SF4PASS also dominates SGAT4PASS (the best RGB-only 3D-robustness method), improving mean mIoU by 9.06 points and reducing variance by ~68%, confirming that the cylindrical-coordinate REL representation and spherical region-adaptive fusion provide genuine 3D geometric robustness rather than ERP-pattern overfitting.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper builds on CMX (a transformer-based cross-modal fusion architecture for RGB-X segmentation) as its backbone and adapts DyMM's Mixture-of-Experts dynamic fusion mechanism to the panoramic setting. Its key departure from prior RGB-D PASS work is the replacement of the widely-used HHA depth representation with a cylindrical-coordinate-based REL representation, motivated by the observation that HHA omits the second degree of freedom of surface normal direction and depends on camera intrinsics. The SMMF module further extends DyMM's sample-level adaptive fusion to a region-level granularity aligned with ERP's spherical geometry, sampling overlapping regions on the cylinder side surface rather than the flat image.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The REL representation offers a cleaner, intrinsics-free way to encode 3D geometry for panoramic images, and the SGA validation showing ~70% variance reduction under 3D rotation disturbances suggests the method is less overfit to the ERP pattern than HHA-based alternatives. However, evaluation is limited to a single dataset (Stanford2D3D Panoramic), and the method still requires depth input, restricting its applicability to RGB-only panoramic segmentation. Open questions include whether the cylindrical-coordinate formulation generalizes to other projection types (e.g., cubemap, tangent) and whether the SMMF region-adaptive fusion transfers to larger-scale or outdoor panoramic benchmarks.
