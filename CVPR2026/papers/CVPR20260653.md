## Problem

Classifier-Free Guidance (CFG) in conditional diffusion models relies on a fixed or heuristically chosen guidance weight that treats all timesteps uniformly, ignoring the fact that the score discrepancy between conditional and unconditional distributions evolves non-trivially during the diffusion process. Existing dynamic guidance strategies (interval guidance, frequency-dependent scaling, manifold-constrained variants) are motivated by empirical observations rather than rigorous theory, leaving a gap in principled, theoretically-grounded time-dependent guidance design.

## Input / Output

The method is a training-free, plug-in guidance schedule applied at inference time to pre-trained conditional diffusion models. It consumes the same conditioning signals as standard CFG—class labels for ImageNet (64×64, 256×256, 512×512) or text prompts for MS-COCO—and produces a single generated image per sample. No additional network parameters, classifiers, or reward models are introduced; the only change is replacing the scalar guidance weight ω with a time-varying function ω(t) evaluated at each denoising step.

## Method

The paper first establishes rigorous upper bounds on the mean-square error between conditional and unconditional score functions for both VP-SDE and VE-SDE formulations (Theorems 1–2), showing the discrepancy decays as O(e^{-t}) in reparameterized time. Complementary Harnack-type inequalities (Theorems 3–4) characterize how the probability density itself becomes harder to bound as t→0, confirming that the conditional–unconditional gap is largest near the data manifold. Guided by these results, C²FG replaces the fixed CFG weight with an exponential control function ω(t) = ω₀ exp(λ(1 − t/t_max)), which is high near t=0 (where the score discrepancy is maximal) and decays toward ω₀ at t=t_max (where the distributions have nearly converged). The method requires only two hyperparameters (ω₀ and λ), is continuously differentiable, and is orthogonal to existing strategies such as interval guidance and autoguidance, allowing it to be stacked on top of them for further gains.

## Results

On ImageNet-256 class-conditional generation, C²FG reduces FID from 2.29 to 2.07 on DiT-XL/2 (ODE, 250 steps) and from 1.80 to 1.51 on SiT-XL/2 (REPA, SDE), while also improving IS from 276.8 to 291.5 and 284.0 to 315.0 respectively. On the stronger SiT-XL/2 (REPA, Interval) baseline, C²FG further reduces FID from 1.42 to 1.41 (SDE) and from 1.56 to 1.54 (ODE). On ImageNet-512, FID improves from 6.81 to 6.54 with IS jumping from 229.5 to 280.9. On MS-COCO text-to-image, U-ViT FID drops from 5.37 to 5.28, and on ImageNet-64 pixel-space EDM2-S with autoguidance, FID improves from 1.04 to 1.03.

## Baselines & Comparisons

C²FG consistently outperforms all baselines on FID across every configuration tested. Against DiT-XL/2 with fixed ω=1.5 (FID 2.29), C²FG achieves 2.07, also beating Rectified Diffusion (2.13) on the same architecture. On SiT-XL/2 (REPA), C²FG reduces FID from 1.80 to 1.51 (SDE) and from 3.64 to 3.40 (ODE), while also improving IS by 31 and 0.2 points respectively. Against the interval-guidance baseline [20] (FID 1.42 SDE / 1.56 ODE), C²FG provides further gains to 1.41 and 1.54, confirming orthogonality. On MS-COCO, C²FG improves U-ViT FID from 5.37 to 5.28 and SD1.5 CLIP-Score from 31.8 to 31.9. On the near-saturated EDM2-S + autoguidance baseline (FID 1.04 on ImageNet-64), C²FG still yields a marginal improvement to 1.03, demonstrating plug-and-play applicability even at the performance ceiling. The β-CFG baseline in the 2D toy example produces more outliers than C²FG, which better matches the target conditional distribution.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper builds directly on Classifier-Free Guidance (CFG) [Ho, 2022] by replacing its fixed guidance weight with a time-dependent exponential decay function, and provides a theoretical interpretation of the interval-guidance strategy [Kynkäänniemi et al., NeurIPS 2024] as a special case of the proposed framework. It departs from prior dynamic-CFG approaches (FDG, CFG++, TFG, β-CFG, RAAG) that the authors characterize as heuristic and empirically motivated, instead grounding the guidance schedule in rigorous score-MSE bounds derived from VP-SDE and VE-SDE dynamics and Harnack-type PDF inequalities. The method is positioned as training-free, plug-in, and orthogonal to existing strategies such as autoguidance, distinguishing it from task-specific or reward-model-dependent approaches.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The paper provides the first principled theoretical justification for time-dependent CFG, showing that the conditional–unconditional score discrepancy decays exponentially during the forward diffusion process, which explains why fixed-weight guidance is suboptimal. Practically, the two-hyperparameter exponential schedule (ω₀, λ) is broadly applicable across architectures (DiT, SiT, U-ViT, SD, EDM2) and tasks (class-conditional, text-to-image), yielding consistent FID improvements of 0.1–0.3 and even a marginal gain on the near-saturated EDM2-S+autoguidance baseline (1.04→1.03). Open questions include whether the exponential form is truly optimal versus other decaying schedules, how the bounds behave in the singular t→0 regime, and whether the approach extends naturally to non-Gaussian forward processes or multi-step rectified-flow formulations.
