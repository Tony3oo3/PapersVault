## Problem

Existing virtual try-on methods restrict users to a single static perspective, and modular pipelines that chain try-on with reposing suffer from cumulative error propagation that degrades both identity and garment fidelity. Additionally, current VTON benchmarks use paired tuples that enforce input-dependency bias and prevent proper unpaired evaluation, while ignoring the interdependence between how a person is clothed and how they pose.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/CVPR20262485/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The model takes a single user reference image, spatially concatenated front-and-back views of both top and bottom garments, and a target pose rendered as a ViTPose skeleton image. The pose condition is channel-wise concatenated with the noisy latent (being pixel-aligned), while garment and user-image latents are processed as separate parallel streams without any text conditioning. The model supports arbitrary subsets of garment inputs being absent at inference time, enabling it to function as a pure reposer or a pure try-on system depending on which conditions are provided.

## Method

Built on SDXL's latent diffusion backbone, the method replaces the standard UNet's single-stream processing with a multi-stream architecture where each non-pixel-aligned condition (user image, top garment, bottom garment) is processed through its own set of learnable weights in parallel with the noisy latent, then all streams interact via a joint self-attention module that concatenates Q, K, V along sequence length after adding position and stream embeddings with QK-normalization. Text cross-attention is entirely removed, eliminating the ~0.5B CLIP interface parameters. Training follows a two-stage strategy: Stage 1 pre-trains on large-scale pose transfer data with partial garment dropout to enforce reliance on explicit garment conditions for identity preservation, and Stage 2 fine-tunes on a 60/40 mixture of multi-pose try-on and pose transfer data to learn full outfit transfer without catastrophic forgetting. Classifier-free guidance is enabled via 0.15 probability null-condition dropout during both stages.

## Results

On the Clothe and Pose task (2,400 evaluation pairs across four pose configurations), the proposed method achieves LPIPS of 0.151–0.166, PSNR of 18.60–19.03 dB, and SSIM of 84.4–86.2, outperforming all baselines by a wide margin. The largest gains appear in the extreme Front→Back transformation (LPIPS 0.166 vs. 0.340 for the next-best Qwen-Image-Edit) and lateral poses (Front→Left/Right, LPIPS 0.151–0.153 vs. 0.186–0.207 for Qwen-Image-Edit). On the Reposing task, the full model achieves LPIPS 0.108–0.125 and PSNR 20.80–22.44 dB, surpassing Qwen-Image (LPIPS 0.117–0.279) and all pipeline methods, with the garment-conditioned variant consistently outperforming the no-garment ablation.

## Baselines & Comparisons

The proposed method decisively outperforms all seven baselines across every pose configuration and metric. Among pipeline baselines, CatVTON+Leffa is the strongest (LPIPS 0.262–0.277), yet the proposed method still achieves roughly 40% lower LPIPS (0.151–0.166) and ~2 dB higher PSNR, with the gap widening to 0.166 vs. 0.277 on Front→Back. The text-guided editing baselines (CatVTON+Kontext, Leffa+Kontext) perform worse than the Leffa-based pipelines on Front→Back (LPIPS 0.317–0.324), indicating that text conditioning is insufficient for extreme pose changes. Qwen-Image-Edit (20B parameters) is the closest single-model competitor, achieving LPIPS 0.186–0.187 on Front→Front and Front→Right, but degrades sharply to 0.340 on Front→Back, where the proposed 5B model reaches 0.166. On the Reposing task (Table 2), the method also leads all baselines; notably, the no-garment ablation (LPIPS 0.112–0.138) already beats Qwen-Image (0.117–0.279), and adding garment conditioning further improves results to LPIPS 0.108–0.125, confirming that explicit garment information aids pose transfer.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper unifies two previously separate tasks—virtual try-on (VTON) and pose transfer/reposing—into a single joint generation problem, departing from the modular pipeline paradigm (e.g., CatVTON + Leffa) that suffers from error propagation. Architecturally, it builds on SDXL's latent diffusion backbone but replaces the standard text cross-attention and spatial-concatenation conditioning with a multi-stream joint-attention design inspired by recent text-to-image models (Flux, SD3), processing garment and user-reference conditions in parallel streams with learnable stream embeddings. The evaluation framework also departs from the standard VTON benchmark setup (G_A, P_A G_A) pairs by introducing triplets (P_A G_A, G_B, P_A G_B) that enable paired ground-truth comparison and expose input-dependency bias in existing methods.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The evaluation framework is arguably the most broadly useful contribution: by revealing that current VTON benchmarks enforce input-dependency bias (the original garment leaks into the output) and by providing paired ground truth across pose changes, it gives the field a more honest yardstick for measuring garment fidelity and identity preservation. The empirical finding that explicit garment conditioning measurably improves reposing quality (Table 2 ablation) supports the paper's central hypothesis that clothing and pose are interdependent, suggesting future reposing methods should incorporate garment information. Limitations include the controlled evaluation setting (tied hair, minimal accessories, simple backgrounds), a 5B-parameter model that still trails Qwen-Image on Front→Front, and the reliance on a proprietary multi-pose capture dataset for training, which limits immediate reproducibility.
