## Problem

Existing audio generation methods cannot produce film-quality stereo Foley with frame-accurate spatio-temporal alignment: monaural models lack spatial dimensions, stereo models lack precise localization control and temporal synchronization with visual events, and mono-to-stereo converters require pre-existing sources. Additionally, no high-quality stereo audio dataset with spatial metadata and precise temporal annotations exists for training spatially grounded Foley generation models.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/CVPR20261513/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The system takes a silent video clip (sampled into N keyframes) and an optional film script as text input. A VLM localizes sound-source bounding boxes per keyframe, Depth Anything V2 produces depth maps, and event detection yields binary activation vectors at frame-level granularity. The output is a multi-layer stereo Foley track (foreground and background events) that can be upmixed to 5.1-channel surround format compliant with ITU-R BS.775, with each sound event carrying explicit depth and azimuth trajectories synchronized to visual motion.

## Method

FoleyDesigner is a three-stage pipeline: (1) a Tree-of-Thought multi-agent module decomposes the silent clip into a hierarchical Foley script with layered sound events, iteratively refined by generator and validator agents; (2) a DiT-based latent diffusion model (built on Stable Audio Open) generates each event as stereo audio, conditioned on text prompts and spatio-temporal cues extracted from visual tracking—depth and azimuth per keyframe are encoded via Fourier feature transformation, modulated by binary activation masks, processed through a convolutional encoder matching the audio latent compression ratio, and injected via cross-attention blocks inserted after every four DiT layers; (3) a multi-agent refinement stage applies reverberation, equalization, and dynamics processing determined by specialist agents, then upmixes to 5.1 surround. The key architectural novelty is the position-aware injection mechanism that provides explicit grounding in continuous spatial trajectories and frame-accurate timing, rather than relying on coarse text descriptions or static image conditioning.

## Results

On the FilmStereo dataset, FoleyDesigner achieves the best spatio-temporal alignment across all metrics: GCC-MAE of 48.79, CRW-MAE of 34.23, FSAD of 0.138, and IoU of 32.2, outperforming the strongest baseline SpatialSonic by 0.8%, 7.2%, 15.3%, and 15.8% respectively. For audio quality, it attains the highest CLAP score (0.679) and lowest FAD (1.88). On film-specific metrics, FoleyDesigner achieves an ImageBind Score of 0.402 and AV-Sync of 0.726, surpassing SpatialSonic by 60.2% and 33.2%, along with the highest Sonic Richness Score (8.27) and Cinematic Clarity Score (6.2).

## Baselines & Comparisons

FoleyDesigner outperforms all three baselines on every spatio-temporal alignment metric. Against SpatialSonic (the closest competitor), it reduces GCC-MAE from 49.20 to 48.79, CRW-MAE from 36.87 to 34.23, FSAD from 0.163 to 0.138, and improves IoU from 27.8 to 32.2. Against Stable Audio, the gains are substantially larger: GCC drops from 61.17 to 48.79 (20.2% reduction) and IoU rises from 24.5 to 32.2 (31.4% improvement). See2Sound is weakest overall, with IoU of only 21.3 and FSAD of 0.291. On film-specific metrics (Table 3), FoleyDesigner leads SpatialSonic by 60.2% on ImageBind Score (0.402 vs 0.251) and 33.2% on AV-Sync (0.726 vs 0.545), while Stable Audio and See2Sound trail significantly. The only metric where FoleyDesigner does not lead is Inception Score (12.36 vs SpatialSonic's 13.79), which the authors attribute to IS measuring sample diversity rather than quality or semantic accuracy.

## Relation to Prior Work

FoleyDesigner builds on Stable Audio Open's DiT-based latent diffusion backbone and extends it with a position-aware cross-attention injection mechanism that conditions generation on Fourier-encoded spatio-temporal trajectories extracted via Depth Anything V2 and VLM-based bounding boxes. Conceptually, it departs from prior spatial audio generators (SpatialSonic, See2Sound, OmniAudio) that condition on text or image but lack frame-level temporal grounding, and from monaural Foley systems (FoleyCrafter, DiffFoley, VideoFoley) that ignore spatial dimensions entirely. The multi-agent Tree-of-Thought decomposition and professional mixing pipeline draw on LLM orchestration patterns but apply them to a domain-specific audio production workflow rather than general reasoning.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The introduction of FilmStereo—the first stereo Foley dataset with spatial metadata, precise timestamps, and semantic annotations across eight categories—addresses a genuine data gap that has constrained spatial audio research. The framework's end-to-end integration from silent clip to 5.1-channel ITU-R BS.775 output offers a practical path toward automating a labor-intensive post-production step, though the authors acknowledge degradation under densely overlapping concurrent events. Open questions include whether the spatio-temporal injection mechanism generalizes beyond the eight Foley categories, whether the multi-agent refinement stage contributes meaningfully beyond simpler rule-based mixing, and whether the self-constructed evaluation dataset introduces circularity that inflates reported gains.
