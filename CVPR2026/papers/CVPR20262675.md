## Problem

While recent methods have shown that neural networks can synthesize novel views without explicit 3D reconstruction, it remains unclear whether 3D inductive biases still matter in such reconstruction-free settings, and the design space of feed-forward NVS architectures (decoder-only vs. bottleneck vs. highway encoder-decoder) is largely unexplored. This paper asks whether initializing a reconstruction-free NVS network from a 3D reconstruction backbone pre-trained with explicit geometric supervision can substantially improve rendering quality.

## Input / Output

The model accepts 1–10 RGB source images (up to 512×512) with optionally known camera poses, plus a target camera pose parameterized as an 11-dimensional vector (unit quaternion, translation, horizontal/vertical FOV, and scene scale). The target camera is encoded densely as a 6-channel Plücker ray map (ray direction and moment per pixel), which is downsampled to HW/64 tokens via a strided convolution. The output is a single 512×512 RGB image rendered from the target viewpoint, produced at 30+ FPS on a single H100 GPU.

## Method

LagerNVS is a highway encoder-decoder where the encoder is the VGGT transformer backbone initialized from its pre-trained 3D reconstruction weights, extracting per-image feature tokens (concatenated local and global attention outputs) that carry implicit 3D structure without any explicit 3D representation. A 2-layer MLP projects optional source camera parameters into VGGT's camera-token slots, enabling inference with or without known poses. The decoder is a lightweight ViT-B transformer that takes Plücker ray-map tokens of the target camera and attends to all source-image tokens via cross-attention (O(V) complexity) or full attention (O(V²)), then projects back to pixel space. The key architectural choice is the 'highway' design—no fixed-size bottleneck constrains information flow between encoder and decoder—combined with end-to-end fine-tuning of the entire network (not just the decoder) using L2 + perceptual losses on a 13-dataset mixture. Ablations show that 3D-aware pre-training contributes +2.9 dB PSNR over random initialization, and that freezing the VGGT backbone degrades quality because geometry-only pre-training discards appearance cues needed for rendering.

## Results

On RealEstate10k (2-view, 256×256), LagerNVS with the highway full-attention decoder at batch size 512 achieves 31.39 PSNR, 0.928 SSIM, and 0.078 LPIPS, outperforming the prior SoTA LVSM decoder-only (29.67 PSNR) by +1.7 dB. Against explicit 3D reconstruction baselines, LagerNVS achieves 27.56 PSNR on DL3DV 4-view (vs. DepthSplat's 22.30) and 22.37 PSNR on CO3D 9-view (vs. AnySplat's 15.87). The model renders at 30+ FPS at 512×512 with up to 9 source views on a single H100 GPU.

## Baselines & Comparisons

LagerNVS outperforms all five baselines across every evaluated setting. Against LVSM (the prior SoTA for reconstruction-free NVS), LagerNVS gains +1.7 dB PSNR on RealEstate10k, with the advantage attributable to 3D-aware pre-training from VGGT and the absence of an encoding bottleneck. Against DepthSplat (explicit 3DGS with monocular depth priors), LagerNVS achieves 27.56 vs. 22.30 PSNR on DL3DV 4-view, a +5.3 dB margin. In the camera-free setting on RealEstate10k, LagerNVS (26.07 PSNR) beats NoPoSplat (24.06), Flare (23.77), and AnySplat (17.05) by +2.0, +2.3, and +9.0 dB respectively. On the more challenging CO3D 360° captures (9-view, 512×512), LagerNVS achieves 22.37 PSNR versus AnySplat's 15.87, a +6.5 dB gap, with the paper attributing the difference to AnySplat's pixel-aligned Gaussian constraint failing in occluded regions.

## Relation to Prior Work

LagerNVS sits at the intersection of two NVS paradigms: reconstruction-free feed-forward methods (SRT, LVSM, RayZer) that render views directly without explicit 3D geometry, and 3D reconstruction networks (VGGT, AnySplat, DepthSplat) that extract explicit scene representations. Its key departure is repurposing VGGT's pre-trained transformer backbone as a feature encoder that produces '3D-aware' latent tokens—trained with explicit 3D supervision but never outputting explicit 3D geometry—thereby injecting strong geometric inductive bias into a purely neural renderer. Architecturally, it introduces the 'highway encoder-decoder' design, which avoids the fixed-size bottleneck of LVSM-style encoders while still amortizing encoding cost across multiple target views, and pairs it with a lightweight cross-attention decoder for real-time rendering.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The paper demonstrates that 3D pre-training of encoder features (via VGGT) is substantially more beneficial for NVS than generic 2D pre-training (DINOv2), and that end-to-end fine-tuning of the entire backbone is essential—insights that should inform how future reconstruction models are designed (e.g., including rendering heads during pre-training). The practical impact is significant: a single model handles unknown cameras, single views, 360° scenes, and in-the-wild images at 30+ FPS on one GPU without custom kernels, making it a strong baseline for both research and deployment. Open questions include whether the preliminary diffusion decoder can match dedicated generative NVS methods at scale, and how the highway architecture behaves at much larger source-view counts or resolutions.
