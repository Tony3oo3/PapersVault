## Problem

Existing autoregressive video diffusion models suffer from a fundamental training-inference discrepancy: conditioning frames are differentiable during training but fixed during inference, causing cumulative temporal drift and quality degradation over long sequences. Additionally, most long-video generation methods lack explicit 3D geometric consistency, leading to unstable geometry, flickering textures, and inconsistent scene layouts that become pronounced in extended or infinite-horizon generation.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/CVPR20263672/cropped_page0_idx1.webp)

## Input / Output

The model takes a text prompt and an initial video frame (or short clip) as input, generating a continuous 3D-consistent video at 832×480 resolution and 16 FPS. It operates in two alternating conditioning modes—long-context (18 latent frames → 3 new latents/12 frames) and short-context (3 latent frames → 18 new latents/72 frames)—to balance coherence with throughput, achieving 17 FPS real-time inference on a single H100 GPU. The 3D fusion module is optional, allowing the model to run with or without geometric guidance without switching architectures.

## Method

Endless World builds on the Wan2.1-T2V-1.3B backbone converted to a few-step causal-attention framework via distribution matching distillation (DMD). The key training reformulation detaches gradients from conditioning frames, so only newly generated frames receive parameter updates, aligning the optimization with the fixed-conditioning inference process and preventing error accumulation. For 3D consistency, VGGT extracts scene geometry from the video, which is projected through a convolution layer and added to text embeddings via a zero-convolution fusion module, enabling global 3D-aware attention without retraining the generation backbone. An optional cosine-similarity 3D loss further enforces geometric coherence between predicted and reference 3D features. At inference, an attention sink retains the initial frame's tokens as persistent context, and rotational positional embeddings are applied after the KV cache to encode temporal phase continuity across generation windows.

## Results

On the 30-second VBench benchmark, Endless World achieves a total score of 84.54 (Quality 85.52, Semantic 80.60) at 17.0 FPS on a single H100 GPU, outperforming all 10 baselines. The most significant margin is in semantic consistency, where Endless World scores 80.60 versus 75.82 for LongLive and 72.70 for Self-Forcing. At 60 seconds, Endless World maintains a quality score of 84.73, exceeding Self-Forcing's 30-second quality (83.82) and LongLive's 60-second quality (84.38), demonstrating minimal degradation over extended durations.

## Baselines & Comparisons

Endless World outperforms all 10 baselines on every VBench metric at 30 seconds. Against the closest long-video competitor LongLive (Total 83.52, Semantic 75.82), Endless World gains +1.02 total and +4.78 semantic points, with the semantic gap driven by stronger object consistency (90.55 vs. lower) and spatial relationship scores. Compared to Self-Forcing (Total 81.59, Semantic 72.70), the improvement is +2.95 total and +7.90 semantic, reflecting the benefit of gradient-detached conditioning and 3D fusion. Notably, Endless World's 30-second quality score (85.52) matches or exceeds the 5-second Wan2.1 diffusion model (85.30) while generating 6× longer videos at 17 FPS versus 0.78 FPS. At 60 seconds, Endless World (Quality 84.73) still surpasses Self-Forcing at 30 seconds (83.82) and LongLive at 60 seconds (84.38), whereas Self-Forcing degrades from 85.07 (5s) to 83.82 (30s) in quality. The only baseline with a higher FPS is LongLive (20.7 vs. 17.0), but it trails Endless World on all quality and semantic dimensions.

## Relation to Prior Work

Endless World is a direct methodological refinement of the Self-Forcing autoregressive video diffusion paradigm [12], which itself builds on the distribution-matching distillation (DMD) training objective from CausVid [44] and DMD [43]. The key departure is a clean reformulation of the autoregressive conditioning: rather than allowing gradients to flow through previously generated frames (as Self-Forcing does), Endless World detaches them, aligning the training objective with the fixed-conditioning reality of inference. On top of this fix, the paper introduces a 3D structural guidance channel by fusing VGGT-extracted features into text embeddings, a design that differs from prior 3D-aware video methods (e.g., Gen3C) that condition on point clouds or rendered views.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

Real-time, infinite-length video generation with geometric consistency is a practically important capability for interactive content, VR, and streaming applications, and this paper demonstrates that a 1.3B model can sustain 17 FPS on a single H100 while maintaining 3D coherence over minutes of video. The gradient-detachment fix is simple and generalizable, likely to be adopted by other autoregressive video pipelines. However, the 3D similarity loss reveals a fundamental tension between geometric fidelity and perceptual naturalness (aesthetic quality drops from 66.33 to 61.60), and quality still degrades modestly at 60 seconds (84.54 → 82.31), leaving open whether truly lossless infinite generation is achievable with current architectures.
