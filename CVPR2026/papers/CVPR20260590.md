## Problem

Vision Foundation Models produce coarse voxel grids with surface noise, floaters, and missing undersides that are unsuitable as inputs for high-fidelity 3D generative models, yet existing voxel completion methods rely exclusively on geometry, use poorly scalable convolutional architectures, and are trained on small category-specific datasets without multi-view image supervision. This paper formalizes Conditioned Voxel Refinement as a large-scale, category-agnostic task that repairs both synthetic corruptions and realistic VFM artifacts at 64³ resolution under multi-view image guidance.

## Input / Output

The method takes an incomplete noisy 64³ voxel grid (from a VFM like Pi3 or synthetic corruption pipeline), 4 multi-view RGB images selected via K-Means clustering over 150 rendered views, and estimated camera poses as conditioning. The voxel grid is encoded into a compressed latent via a pretrained sparse-structure VAE (from Trellis), and images are encoded into patch tokens by DINOv2; the model operates entirely in this latent token space (16³ voxel tokens + 4×37² image tokens ≈ 9,572 tokens per forward pass) and outputs a refined 64³ voxel grid decoded by the same VAE.

## Method

VIAFormer combines three synergistic components. First, a Correctional Flow objective (a specialized Flow Matching formulation) defines a linear interpolation path between the noisy latent z_V and clean latent z_gt, training the network to predict the constant correction velocity (z_gt − z_V) rather than generating from random noise. Second, an Image Index assigns explicit 3D coordinates to each 2D image patch token by rendering the noisy voxel grid with coordinates encoded as face colors, producing per-view index maps that are patch-averaged to yield a 3D position for every image token. Third, a Hybrid Stream Transformer (24 blocks: 8 dual-stream + 16 single-stream) fuses the two modalities within a Voxel-Image Union-Space where both voxel and image tokens carry 3D sinusoidal/RoPE positional encodings, enabling spatially-aware bidirectional attention that prevents the 'Attention Collapse' observed with standard cross-attention. The dual-stream stage lets both streams query a shared key-value space for co-evolution, after which the concatenated sequence undergoes global self-attention in the single-stream stage.

## Results

On VFM-derived artifacts (Table 1), VIAFormer achieves IoU of 0.4460 on Toys4k and 0.4585 on Dora, improving over the Pi3 VFM baseline by 5.0% and 3.4% respectively, while also reducing Chamfer Distance (0.0163 vs. 0.0193 on Toys4k). On synthetically corrupted inputs (Table 2), the gains are far more dramatic: VIAFormer reaches IoU 0.8580 on Toys4k, a 39.1% absolute improvement over the input reference (0.4666), with Chamfer Distance dropping from 0.0315 to 0.0027. The model uses 0.61B parameters, processes 9,572 tokens per forward pass, and requires 10 ODE steps (14.5 s on a single V100) for inference.

## Baselines & Comparisons

VIAFormer outperforms all geometry-only baselines (DiffComplete, PatchComplete, WSSC) on both datasets, with the largest margin over WSSC (IoU 0.4460 vs. 0.3580 on Toys4k). Against the single-image SDFusion baseline, VIAFormer gains 3.9% IoU on Toys4k (0.4460 vs. 0.4071) and 5.2% on Dora (0.4585 vs. 0.4070), demonstrating the benefit of multi-view conditioning. The Trellis cross-attention variant, which generates from pure noise rather than refining a VFM prior, is substantially worse (IoU 0.3332 on Toys4k), confirming the value of the Correctional Flow formulation. On the synthetic corruption benchmark (Table 2), the gap widens dramatically: VIAFormer achieves IoU 0.8580 versus 0.2620 for SDFusion and 0.2127 for DiffComplete, while WSSC and PatchComplete essentially fail (IoU 0.0036 and 0.0414). The paper also shows that simply adding cross-attention or stronger conditioning (VGGT features with MLP or self-attention adapters) to a 24-layer transformer yields negligible gains over geometry-only self-attention, validating the necessity of the explicit Image Index spatial grounding mechanism.

## Relation to Prior Work

VIAFormer extends the voxel completion line (DiffComplete, PatchComplete, WSSC) by introducing multi-view image conditioning and operating in a compressed latent space at 64³ resolution, departing from prior geometry-only methods confined to explicit 32³ space. Its Correctional Flow objective is a specialized reparameterization of Flow Matching that learns a direct noisy-to-clean trajectory rather than generation from Gaussian noise, and its Hybrid Stream Transformer borrows the dual-to-single-stream block organization from OmniControl. The most significant conceptual departure is the diagnosis of 'Attention Collapse' in standard cross-attention for voxel-image fusion, motivating the Voxel-Image Union-Space with explicit 3D coordinate grounding via the Image Index and RoPE.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

VIAFormer provides a practical, cost-effective 'where' stage that bridges noisy VFM reconstructions (e.g., Pi3) to high-fidelity generative pipelines like Trellis, eliminating the need for expensive per-scene optimization. The Attention Collapse finding offers a useful diagnostic for anyone designing cross-modal attention: without a shared spatial basis, even powerful conditioning features and complex adapters cannot recover meaningful token-level correspondence. Open questions include whether the approach scales beyond 64³ resolution, how sensitive the Image Index is to camera pose estimation errors, and whether the Union-Space paradigm generalizes to other 3D representations (mesh, SDF, point cloud).
