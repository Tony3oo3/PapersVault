## Problem

Existing self-evolving large multimodal models optimize answer agreement or self-consistency as their training signal, which implicitly assumes that consistent outputs reflect genuine visual grounding. In practice, the decoder can exploit statistical language priors to produce self-consistent answers without attending to the image, a failure mode the authors term visual under-conditioning. This manifests as insufficient attention to visual tokens during decoding, causing hallucination, modality bypass, and poor performance on captioning and region-level description tasks despite gains on structured reasoning benchmarks.

## Input / Output

The method consumes raw unlabeled RGB images with no captions, bounding boxes, category labels, or metadata. During training, the model operates in a self-questioning regime: it first generates a natural-language localization query about a prominent object in the scene, then predicts a bounding box in normalized [0, 1000]⁴ coordinate space. The training output is a completion containing the predicted box coordinates, while evaluation outputs include image captions, VQA answers, and binary object-presence judgments across 18 benchmarks.

## Method

VISE is a single-model, fully unsupervised self-evolving framework that regularizes the decoder's visual conditioning policy through two complementary invariance rewards instead of answer-agreement signals. The geometric invariance reward applies a known spatial transformation (affine, crop, or flip) and computes GIoU between the model's predicted box on the transformed view and the analytically projected box from the original, penalizing spatial inconsistency. The semantic invariance reward (ghosting) Gaussian-blurs the predicted region and assigns reward only if the model judges the queried object as visible before perturbation but absent afterward, directly penalizing evidence-agnostic generation. Training uses KL-regularized REINFORCE with an adaptive KL coefficient against a frozen reference policy, LoRA fine-tuning of the multimodal projector and decoder (vision encoder frozen), and an exponential moving average baseline for variance reduction. The key distinction from prior self-evolving methods is the elimination of multi-role proposer-solver formulations and the replacement of consistency rewards with invariance-based signals that force the decoder to maintain attention to visual tokens throughout generation.

## Results

Using Qwen3-VL-2B as the base model, VISE achieves +16.85 CIDEr on COCO (21.54→38.39), +19.66 CIDEr on TextCaps (22.20→41.86), +14.73 CIDEr on NoCaps, and +16.55 CIDEr on Flickr30k, with gains 4×–7× larger than the strongest baseline on each benchmark. On hallucination, VISE reduces Chair-I from 13.21 to 8.21 (−5.00) and Chair-S from 45.96 to 40.51 (−5.45) at 2B while simultaneously improving POPE accuracy by +1.02. Across 12 VQA and reasoning benchmarks, VISE improves all twelve at 2B (e.g., +4.19 ScienceQA, +2.41 InfoVQA, +1.75 MMMU) with no regressions at any scale. Gains attenuate with model size, shrinking from +16.85 CIDEr at 2B to +8.72 at 32B on COCO.

## Baselines & Comparisons

VISE outperforms all four baseline families across every captioning benchmark and scale, with the largest margins at 2B where gains are 4×–7× those of the strongest baseline (VisionZero-RW, +4.04 COCO CIDEr). EvoLMM and iReasoner, which optimize answer-consistency rewards on math/science-centric images, actually regress on captioning at 2B (−0.70 and −0.61 COCO CIDEr respectively), confirming the visual under-conditioning failure mode; VISE reverses this with +16.85 CIDEr. On VQA and reasoning, VISE is the only method that improves all 12 benchmarks simultaneously at 2B with zero regressions, whereas EvoLMM and iReasoner gain +3.59/+3.70 on ScienceQA but drop −2.73/−2.63 on OK-VQA, and VisionZero variants show domain-specific tradeoffs (e.g., Chart variant gains ChartQA but drops OK-VQA). On hallucination, VISE reduces Chair-I by −5.00 at 2B, surpassing the best baseline (VisionZero-RW, −2.99) by 2.01 points, while also improving POPE accuracy (+1.02) where EvoLMM and iReasoner simultaneously degrade (−1.42, −1.31). VisPlay, which uses diversity/difficulty rewards, shows the weakest captioning gains (+2.31 COCO at 2B) and even increases hallucination (Chair-I +0.003, Chair-S +0.23), indicating that question-complexity rewards do not improve visual faithfulness.

## Relation to Prior Work

VISE departs from the dominant self-evolving LMM paradigm (EvoLMM, iReasoner, VisPlay, VisionZero) that frames multimodal self-improvement as a multi-role Proposer-Solver game optimized by answer-agreement or self-consistency rewards. Rather than extending these formulations, VISE eliminates specialist roles entirely and replaces the reward signal with two invariance-based objectives—geometric consistency under known spatial transformations (measured via GIoU) and semantic sensitivity under regional perturbation (ghosting)—that directly regularize the decoder's visual conditioning policy. The optimization machinery (KL-regularized REINFORCE with adaptive β, LoRA fine-tuning) is standard, so the contribution is squarely in the reward design and the single-model formulation, not in the training algorithm or architecture.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The paper provides the first systematic evidence that answer-consistency rewards in self-evolving LMMs actively degrade visual grounding (EvoLMM and iReasoner regress on captioning while improving on structured reasoning), challenging the field's implicit assumption that self-consistent outputs reflect improved visual understanding. The practical impact is substantial: a simple two-reward self-supervised objective yields large, tradeoff-free gains across 18 benchmarks, four model scales, and four backbone families, with no annotations or external reward models required. Open questions include whether the gains persist at even larger scales (they attenuate from +16.85 CIDEr at 2B to +8.72 at 32B), whether the binary semantic reward can be made continuous for smoother gradients, and whether the invariance framework extends to video, multi-image, or embodied settings where spatial and temporal consistency interact.
