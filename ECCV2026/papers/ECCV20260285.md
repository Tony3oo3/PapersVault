## Problem

Generating complete 3D scenes from sparse, unconstrained views requires reasoning beyond observed content, but existing feed-forward reconstruction methods are inherently limited to visible geometry, and 3D generative models are hindered by the cubic scaling of dense volumetric representations and the scarcity of large-scale 3D supervision. SPAR3S addresses this by formulating scene completion in a sparse, voxel-aligned 3D latent space that only materializes occupied voxels, enabling tractable generative modeling without ground-truth 3D data.

## Input / Output

The method takes as input a sparse set of unconstrained RGB views (as few as 2, at 224×224 resolution) along with estimated camera poses and pointmaps (from MASt3R-SfM or backprojected depth). It produces a complete 3D scene as a set of voxel-aligned 3D Gaussian splats, mediated by a compact 32-dimensional sparse latent representation where only occupied voxels carry tokens. Unlike methods requiring known novel camera poses, SPAR3S generates content directly in 3D, and the autoregressive model jointly outputs both voxel occupancy scores and continuous latent vectors for each unobserved position.

## Method

SPAR3S comprises two components: (1) a VAE-style encoder-decoder that maps multi-view images to a sparse 3D latent space via pointmap-initialized voxel tokens updated by bidirectional cross-attention with geometry-guided bincount logits, then decodes to 3D Gaussians with hierarchical upsampling and false-positive noise injection for robustness; and (2) a masked autoregressive transformer that performs scene completion by jointly predicting voxel occupancy (BCE head) and latent token values (token-wise DDPM diffusion head) over unobserved positions. The autoregressive ordering uses a BFS-based region-growing strategy over a sparse k-NN graph of observed voxels to promote spatial consistency. Training is fully self-supervised: the encoder-decoder is optimized with photometric reconstruction loss via differentiable Gaussian splatting (no 3D ground truth), and the generative model is trained with masked auto-regression where targets come from encoding dense views, with unobserved positions always masked from the loss. A hierarchical coarse-to-fine occupancy prediction further improves efficiency by first predicting a dense coarse occupancy grid before refining to fine-grained voxels.

## Results

On the challenging two-view novel-view synthesis setting at 224×224, SPAR3S achieves FID 59, PSNR 15.18, SSIM 0.62, and LPIPS 0.50 on 3DFront, and FID 41, PSNR 16.72, SSIM 0.57, and LPIPS 0.36 on RealEstate10K. These results represent substantial improvements over the strongest generative baseline (LatentSplat) by 121 FID points and 1.26 dB PSNR on 3DFront, and by 12 FID points and 0.63 dB PSNR on RealEstate10K. SPAR3S also outperforms the best deterministic reconstruction method (DepthSplat) by 51 FID points and 1.42 dB PSNR on 3DFront.

## Baselines & Comparisons

SPAR3S outperforms all six baselines on every reported metric across both datasets. Against the strongest generative baseline LatentSplat [53], SPAR3S improves FID from 180→59 and PSNR from 13.92→15.18 on 3DFront, and FID from 53→41 and PSNR from 16.09→16.72 on RealEstate10K; the paper attributes LatentSplat's relative weakness on 3DFront to its epipolar-based encoder struggling with wide viewpoint variations. MVSplat360 [10] achieves FID 111/PSNR 9.72 on 3DFront but degrades on unconstrained camera poses, while SPAR3S reaches FID 59/PSNR 15.18. Among deterministic methods, DepthSplat [56] (FID 110, PSNR 13.76 on 3DFront) is the strongest but still cannot reconstruct unobserved regions, whereas SPAR3S gains 51 FID points and 1.42 dB PSNR. PixelSplat [6] and vanilla 3DGS [20] fail to produce meaningful reconstructions under the two-view protocol due to their inability to extrapolate beyond conditioning frustums. DiffusioNeRF [54] (FID 229, PSNR 12.80 on 3DFront) is the weakest generative baseline, confirming that 2D diffusion priors alone are insufficient for geometrically consistent 3D scene completion.

## Relation to Prior Work

SPAR3S sits at the intersection of three research threads: feed-forward 3DGS reconstruction (PixelSplat, MVSplat, DepthSplat), 3D generative modeling (LatentSplat, MVSplat360, DiffusioNeRF), and masked auto-regressive generation (MaskGIT, MAR). It departs from prior 3D generative methods that apply diffusion to fixed-size Gaussian sets by instead using a sparse voxel-aligned latent space where an autoregressive transformer jointly predicts occupancy and latent token values, borrowing the masked auto-regression paradigm from 2D image generation and adapting it to spatially sparse 3D token sequences. The encoder-decoder is trained purely with photometric supervision via differentiable 3DGS rendering, avoiding the need for ground-truth 3D data that limits scalability of prior 3D generative approaches.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The paper demonstrates that conditional 3D scene completion from truly unconstrained sparse views (as few as 2, with no overlap) is tractable without any 3D supervision, addressing a chicken-and-egg problem that plagues 2D-diffusion-based approaches requiring known novel camera poses. A notable open question is the occupancy prediction bottleneck: the oracle-occupancy variant achieves PSNR 17.90 versus 15.18 for the full model, suggesting that geometry prediction remains the primary limitation. The evaluation is limited to 224×224 resolution and primarily synthetic indoor scenes, leaving open whether the approach scales to higher resolutions, outdoor environments, or larger scene extents. The sparse voxel + masked AR formulation could become a useful template for other 3D generative tasks beyond scene completion.
