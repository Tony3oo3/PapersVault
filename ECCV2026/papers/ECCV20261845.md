## Problem

Most text-to-3D methods operate on monolithic latents that entangle geometry, appearance, and semantics without explicit part identities or inter-part relations. Even recent part-aware approaches remain geometry-focused, treating parts as isolated units and leaving language as a one-shot condition rather than a persistent relational signal, so generated objects frequently violate plausible spatial or functional assembly constraints.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ECCV20261845/cropped_page0_idx1.webp)

## Input / Output

The method takes a natural-language text prompt (and optionally explicit relational triplets of the form (part_i, part_j, predicate)) as input, processed by a frozen Gemma 2 text encoder followed by a learned projection. Output is a set of individual 3D part meshes decoded from a 3D VAE (trained on surface points with normals), assembled into a complete object, with per-part appearance rendered from paired 2D latents produced by a pretrained PixArt-α image VAE on multi-view renderings. Each part carries a learnable identity embedding that persists across denoising steps, enabling downstream part-level editing and scene composition.

## Method

DreamPartGen formulates part-based 3D generation as a collaborative diffusion process between two complementary latent streams: Duplex Part Latents (DPLs), which pair 3D geometry tokens and 2D appearance tokens per part with a learnable slot-identity embedding, and Relational Semantic Latents (RSLs), which comprise persistent global relational tokens (S^glb) derived from canonicalized (i, j, ρ) triplets and co-denoised local semantic tokens (S^loc) encoding fine-grained part attributes. At each denoising step, intra-part synchronization aligns geometry and appearance within each part under local semantic guidance, while inter-part synchronization propagates context across all parts via cross-part attention and injects global relational constraints, with a bottom-up grounding update that refines S^glb from the current part latents. Training proceeds in two phases—first optimizing separate 3D and 2D diffusion objectives under RSL conditioning, then jointly fine-tuning all denoisers and synchronization modules with an SNR-based weighting schedule—on the newly curated PartRel3D dataset of ~11K objects, 90K parts, and 300K canonicalized functional/spatial triplets across 175 categories.

## Results

DreamPartGen achieves the lowest Chamfer Distance and Earth Mover's Distance across all four geometric benchmarks (Objaverse, ShapeNet, ABO, PartRel3D), with average improvements of ↓60% CD and ↓41% EMD over the strongest baseline. On PartRel3D, it reaches CD 0.081 and EMD 0.412, compared to 0.355/1.474 for the best prior method. For text-shape alignment on PartVerse, DreamPartGen improves CLIP and ULIP-T by ≥20% at the object level (e.g., ULIP-T 0.197 vs. 0.164) and ≥35% at the part level (e.g., ULIP-T 0.153 vs. 0.113). It also attains the lowest pairwise IoU (↓27.2% on average), indicating better part disentanglement.

## Baselines & Comparisons

DreamPartGen outperforms all four main baselines (Trellis, CLAY, HoloPart, PartCrafter) on every geometric and text-alignment metric across all benchmarks. Against the strongest part-aware baseline PartCrafter, DreamPartGen reduces CD by 49–79% (e.g., 0.141 vs. 0.278 on Objaverse, 0.081 vs. 0.371 on PartRel3D) and EMD by 27–72%, while also achieving lower pairwise IoU (0.359 vs. 0.453 on Objaverse). Against HoloPart, the gains are similar in magnitude (CD 0.141 vs. 0.334 on Objaverse; 0.081 vs. 0.355 on PartRel3D). On text-shape alignment (PartVerse), DreamPartGen surpasses the best baseline by 21% on object-level ULIP-T (0.197 vs. 0.164 Trellis) and by 35% on part-level ULIP-T (0.153 vs. 0.113 HoloPart). For 3D scene generation inference, DreamPartGen runs in 52 s per sample versus 102 s for MIDI, while for part-level generation it takes 109 s compared to 112 s for PartCrafter and 21 min for HoloPart.

## Relation to Prior Work

DreamPartGen extends the part-aware 3D generation line (CoPart, PartCrafter, HoloPart) by introducing persistent, language-derived Relational Semantic Latents that encode inter-part predicates (support, attach, symmetry, articulation) and co-denoise alongside Duplex Part Latents, rather than treating parts as geometrically isolated units conditioned on one-shot text embeddings. It departs from prior part-based methods that rely on bounding boxes or segmentations as the primary structural signal, instead grounding part assembly in canonicalized relational triplets extracted from natural language. The PartRel3D dataset (300K triplets, 175 categories) provides the large-scale relational supervision that prior part-aware datasets (PartNet, PartVerse) lack, enabling the model to learn assembly-level semantics directly from language.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The framework addresses a concrete gap in part-aware 3D generation: without explicit relational semantics, locally faithful parts can assemble into globally incoherent objects (floating wheels, detached wings, torn joints). The dramatic ablation results (438–647% EMD degradation when removing co-denoised local semantic tokens) suggest that one-shot text conditioning is fundamentally insufficient for part-level synthesis, a finding that could reshape how the community designs text-to-3D pipelines. Open questions include scalability to densely entangled structures (bushes, hair), the sensitivity to triplet quality when no VLM is available, and whether the part-annotation requirement limits applicability to novel or unsegmented categories.
