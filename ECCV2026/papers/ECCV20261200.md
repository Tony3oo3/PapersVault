## Problem

Existing diffusion-based novel view synthesis methods face a fundamental trade-off: reconstruction-based pipelines enforce spatial consistency through explicit 3D priors but degrade under large viewpoint changes due to sparse reconstruction limits, while purely implicit generative models offer flexibility but suffer from scale drifting and imprecise camera control because their internal geometry is scale-ambiguous. MetaView addresses this by combining implicit geometry modeling with minimal yet essential explicit 3D cues to achieve both geometric consistency and precise controllability from a single image.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ECCV20261200/cropped_page1_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

Given a single RGB source image, its camera intrinsics matrix, and a target camera pose (6-DoF SE(3) extrinsics), MetaView synthesizes the corresponding novel view image. Metric depth is estimated off-the-shelf via DepthAnything3-Metric from the input image and downsampled to match latent token resolution before injection. The text prompt is fixed to 'Turn to the target view,' and inference uses 40 flow-matching sampling steps with classifier-free guidance scale of 4.

## Method

MetaView is built on a frozen Qwen-Image-Edit MM-DiT backbone and introduces two non-invasive geometric signals via parallel attention layers. First, intermediate features from DepthAnything3 are projected into 'geometry tokens' that attend to image tokens through a dedicated image-geometry attention stream, providing implicit relative geometry priors without explicit reconstruction. Second, camera intrinsics and extrinsics are encoded into a modified RoPE (following PRoPE), with an extra z-axis subspace assigned the metric depth to anchor spatial scale and eliminate the scale drifting issue. The generated image tokens are bound to the target pose with zero z-axis to avoid over-constraining the output, while source and geometry tokens share the origin pose with the downsampled depth. Only the newly introduced projection parameters and parallel attention layers are trained under a flow-matching objective, preserving the pretrained model's semantic knowledge.

## Results

On the DL3DV dataset (aggregated), MetaView achieves PSNR 15.18, SSIM 0.4456, LPIPS 0.2137, and DMD 10.29, outperforming the best reconstruction-based baseline Gen3C (PSNR 13.78, DMD 20.01) by ~1.4 dB in PSNR and ~9.7 in DMD. On RealEstate10K, MetaView reaches PSNR 15.27 and DMD 6.44, surpassing the strongest baseline Voyager (PSNR 14.55, DMD 12.95). On Sekai-Real-Walking-HQ, MetaView attains PSNR 17.72 and DMD 6.69, beating Gen3C (PSNR 16.56, DMD 8.72). The advantage grows with viewpoint difficulty: on DL3DV-Hard, MetaView's DMD of 20.74 is more than 2× better than the next-best method (Gen3C at 41.07).

## Baselines & Comparisons

MetaView outperforms all six baselines on every metric across all three datasets. Among reconstruction-based methods, Gen3C [38] is the strongest competitor (PSNR 13.78 on DL3DV, 16.56 on Sekai), yet MetaView still leads by 1.4 dB and 1.2 dB respectively, with DMD gaps of ~10 and ~2 points. PE-Field [1] and Voyager [18] are competitive on RealEstate10K (PSNR 14.51 and 14.55) but fall 0.7–0.8 dB short of MetaView and show DMD values 2–8× higher. ViewCrafter [66] degrades most severely under large viewpoint changes, with DMD reaching 27.34 on DL3DV and 30.12 on Voyager's own metric. The purely implicit methods HY-World-1.5 [49] and Lingbot-World [52] trail all reconstruction-based baselines on PSNR/SSIM (e.g., 11.44 and 11.65 on DL3DV) and exhibit the worst DMD scores (22.51 and 35.32), confirming the scale-drifting and imprecise camera-control issues the paper identifies. The gap widens substantially on the DL3DV-Hard subset, where MetaView's DMD of 20.74 is roughly half that of Gen3C (41.07) and Lingbot-World (55.39), underscoring the advantage of combining implicit geometry priors with metric-scale anchoring under extreme viewpoint changes.

## Relation to Prior Work

MetaView positions itself as a middle ground between two established lines of diffusion-based NVS: reconstruction-based pipelines (ViewCrafter, Gen3C, PE-Field, Voyager) that warp explicit 3D geometry into the diffusion model, and purely implicit world models (HY-World-1.5, Lingbot-World) that condition on camera poses without geometric grounding. Rather than performing full 3D reconstruction, it repurposes intermediate features from a feed-forward geometry network (DepthAnything3) as soft implicit priors injected via parallel attention, while borrowing PRoPE's camera-as-RoPE formulation and extending it with a metric-depth z-axis to resolve scale ambiguity. The architecture builds on Qwen-Image-Edit's MM-DiT backbone and the flow-matching training paradigm, with GTA-style geometry-aware attention providing the integration mechanism.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The paper addresses a practical bottleneck in monocular NVS: the inability of current methods to simultaneously maintain geometric consistency and generalize under large viewpoint changes, a tension that limits real-world deployment of single-image 3D content creation. The identification and mitigation of the scale-drifting issue in implicit generators is a concrete, reusable insight for the broader world-model community. Open questions include whether the approach extends to dynamic/4D scenes (acknowledged as future work), how sensitive performance is to the quality of the geometry prior network, and whether the DMD metric generalizes beyond the UFM-based correspondence it relies on. The method's dependence on a frozen large pretrained backbone also raises questions about compute cost and adaptability to new domains without retraining.
