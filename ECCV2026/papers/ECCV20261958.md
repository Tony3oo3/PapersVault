## Problem

Current MLLMs excel at single-view semantic recognition but lack the ability to bridge geometric gaps between disparate ego-centric and exo-centric viewpoints. Existing spatial benchmarks probe isolated single-view frames, while existing ego-exo benchmarks focus on semantic tasks like action recognition, leaving the critical intersection of multi-view geometric spatial deduction entirely unexplored.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ECCV20261958/cropped_page1_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The benchmark consumes synchronized ego-exo image tuples (1 ego + up to 5 exo views, max 6 images per question) alongside multiple-choice questions averaging 105.9 words with 3–4 options in single- or multi-choice format. SEER-Map additionally accepts 2D pixel coordinates of target entities to ground them into 3D space, and produces a minimalist 2D Bird's-Eye View topology map (camera frustums + orthogonally projected target positions on the ground plane) that is interleaved with the original images as an extra visual input to the MLLM at inference time.

## Method

SEER-Map is a training-free, tool-augmented baseline that decouples 3D geometric reconstruction from spatial reasoning. It first employs DUSt3R to recover dense 3D point clouds and relative camera extrinsics from the synchronized ego-exo image set without requiring camera intrinsics. Second, 2D target pixel coordinates are indexed into the point cloud to obtain absolute 3D physical coordinates. Third, the height dimension is discarded, camera frustums are computed from extrinsics, and all 3D elements are orthogonally projected onto the ground plane and rendered as a 2D BEV topology sketch. This explicit top-down spatial prior is fed to the MLLM alongside the original multi-view images, transforming the cross-view alignment problem into a simplified topological matching task and yielding consistent accuracy gains (e.g., +13.1 for GPT-5.2, +10.6 for Gemini-3-Flash) across all evaluated model families.

## Results

On SEERBench (1,198 QA pairs, 10 tasks, 3 cognitive levels), human experts achieve 91.5% average accuracy, while the best-performing MLLM, Gemini-3-Pro, reaches only 44.7%, leaving a 46.8-point absolute gap. Among open-source models, Qwen3-VL-32B-Thinking leads at 36.6%, and spatial-oriented models (SenseNova-SI-8B: 27.5%, Spatial-SSRL-4B: 22.2%) trail general-purpose MLLMs. The SEER-Map tool-augmented baseline yields consistent gains, e.g., boosting GPT-5.2 from 38.2% to 51.1% (+13.1) and Gemini-3-Flash from 42.2% to 52.8% (+10.6), with the largest improvements on Camera Layout Inference (+38 to +48 points).

## Baselines & Comparisons

All 19 evaluated MLLMs fall substantially short of the 91.5% human baseline. Among closed-source models, Gemini-3-Pro (44.7%) edges out GPT-5.2-Pro (43.3%) and Gemini-3-Flash (42.2%), with GPT-5.2 (38.2%) and Qwen3-VL-Plus (35.8%) trailing further; GPT-4o (32.9%) and Claude-Sonnet-4.5 (32.4%) are the weakest proprietary entries. In the open-source tier, Qwen3-VL-32B-Thinking (36.6%) and Qwen3-VL-32B-Instruct (36.0%) lead, while GLM-4.6V (35.4%), Qwen3-VL-8B-Instruct (32.5%), and the InternVL3 variants (26.1–27.3%) form a mid-to-lower cluster; LLaVA-NeXT-7B (23.2%) and LLaVA-OneVision-Qwen2-7B (25.0%) are the weakest overall. Notably, spatial-oriented models underperform general MLLMs: SenseNova-SI-8B (27.5%) and Spatial-SSRL-4B (22.2%) score below most open-source generalists, indicating that current spatial specialization does not transfer to ego-exo reasoning. The SEER-Map augmentation consistently narrows the gap across all model families tested (GPT-5.2: +13.1, Gemini-3-Flash: +10.6, Qwen3-VL-Plus: +8.1, Qwen3-VL-8B-Instruct: +7.6, SenseNova-SI-8B: +8.6), with the largest single-task gains on Camera Layout Inference (+38 to +48 points), confirming that explicit BEV topology is the key missing geometric prior.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper sits at the intersection of two previously separate research threads: ego-exo video understanding (Ego-Exo4D, EgoExoBench, E3VQA) which has focused on semantic-level tasks like action recognition and temporal consistency, and spatial reasoning benchmarks (ScanQA, SpatialMQA, MMSI-Bench) which have been confined to single-view or symmetric multi-camera setups. SEERBench deliberately carves out the unexplored middle ground—cross-view *geometric* deduction across asymmetric ego-exo viewpoints—while SEER-Map extends the tool-augmented spatial reasoning line by repurposing DUSt3R's 3D reconstruction to generate explicit 2D BEV topology maps as spatial priors, rather than relying on the MLLM to implicitly infer cross-view correspondences.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The 46.8% absolute gap between the best MLLM (Gemini-3-Pro at 44.7%) and human experts (91.5%) exposes a fundamental geometric deficiency in current multimodal foundations that prior benchmarks have not surfaced. The finding that spatial-specialized models (SenseNova-SI-8B, Spatial-SSRL-4B) actually underperform general-purpose MLLMs challenges the assumption that explicit spatial pre-training transfers to cross-view ego-exo reasoning. Open questions include whether the BEV topology approach generalizes to more complex or dynamic scenes beyond 35 curated environments, whether training on ego-exo spatial data can close the gap, and whether the information-equivalence gap between visual and textual spatial priors reflects a fundamental cross-modal bottleneck or a fixable prompting issue.
