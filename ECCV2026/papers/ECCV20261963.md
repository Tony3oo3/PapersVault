## Problem

Diffusion-based 3D generation methods like Trellis and Hunyuan3D excel at single-object synthesis but lack controllable compositionality across multiple heterogeneous 2D assets. The two natural strategies—uplifting each asset independently then composing, or synthesizing a compositional 2D image then generating 3D—suffer from spatial misalignment and geometric overlap, or from modality-conversion distortions and multi-view inconsistency, respectively. No training-free, multi-asset, geometrically consistent solution for customized 3D composition existed prior to this work.

## Input / Output

The method takes a source text prompt c, N text-image pairs {(o_i, I_i)} that bind specific terms in c to reference 2D asset images, and a designated anchor (main) asset (o_B, I_B) to reduce layout ambiguity. It produces a unified 3D asset represented as a 64³ voxelized occupancy grid for coarse geometry and a Structured Latent (SLAT) for fine-grained appearance, where each component's geometry and texture faithfully follow its corresponding 2D asset. Layout hints are extracted at per-component granularity via text-based segmentation (LangSAM) and 3D back-projection (modified FlashSplat) on rendered multi-view images.

## Method

CAST3D is a training-free two-stage pipeline built on Trellis's Rectified Flow Transformers. The core innovation is Stochastic Trajectory Manipulation (STM), which converts the deterministic ODE denoising of Rectified Flow into a reverse-time SDE (with diffusion coefficient g(t)=√(2λ_diff)·t) and then manipulates the denoising trajectory by extracting per-step noise from a source trajectory and re-injecting it into a target trajectory via a generic noise mapper and trajectory generator. In the 3D Layout Hinting stage, STM is applied to the anchor geometry with the full text prompt to produce a voxelized layout proposal that preserves the anchor's structure while incorporating compositional semantics; per-component layout hints are then extracted via segmentation. In the Compositional Generation stage, each component is generated conditioned on its 2D asset and layout hint using STM, composed via a connectivity-based pruning strategy that removes disconnected artifacts while preserving floating and thin structures, and refined with a final STM pass. Appearance is composed by assigning optimal velocities per component region and interpolating with Gaussian-kernel soft boundaries to ensure smooth transitions.

## Results

CAST3D achieves the highest average VLM-based score of 8.84/10, surpassing Uplift-then-Compose (6.96) and Customize-then-Generate (6.12–6.23) across Faithfulness (9.19 vs. 8.33/5.73–6.77), Aesthetic (8.68 vs. 7.25/5.75–5.89), and Structural Rationality (8.65 vs. 5.29/6.03–6.87). On CLIP metrics, CAST3D attains the best component-level appearance alignment (CLIP-I = 0.789) and competitive textual alignment (CLIP-T = 22.84). A user study confirms these findings, with CAST3D scoring 8.62 in faithfulness and 8.49 in rationality, well ahead of all baselines.

## Baselines & Comparisons

CAST3D outperforms both constructed baselines on every metric. Against Uplift-then-Compose (which independently lifts each 2D asset to 3D via Trellis then arranges them), CAST3D gains +0.86 in Faithfulness and +3.36 in Structural Rationality, as the baseline suffers from severe clipping and geometric overlap due to inconsistent scales and coordinate frames. Against Customize-then-Generate (which synthesizes a compositional 2D image via Qwen-Image-Edit then generates 3D from it), CAST3D leads by +2.42 to +3.46 in Faithfulness, since the 2D-to-3D conversion introduces occlusion artifacts, multi-view inconsistencies, and content drift. In qualitative comparisons with adapted Tailor3D and VoxHammer (both augmented with Nano Banana Pro for 2D fusion), CAST3D maintains sharper geometry and better faithfulness to the source assets, whereas Tailor3D exhibits significant blurriness and VoxHammer shows clipping and inconsistencies. The user study further confirms CAST3D's advantage, with human raters scoring it 8.62/10 in faithfulness versus 7.41 for UtC. and 6.49–6.85 for CtG.

## Relation to Prior Work

CAST3D builds on Trellis's structured 3D latent (SLAT) representation and Rectified Flow generative framework, extending them with a stochastic SDE formulation that unifies and generalizes prior trajectory-manipulation methods (FlowEdit, FlowAlign) into a single algorithmic template. Conceptually, it departs from both the 'uplift-then-compose' paradigm (independent image-to-3D lifting followed by spatial arrangement) and the 'customize-then-generate' paradigm (multi-concept 2D synthesis followed by whole-object 3D generation), instead performing layout reasoning and composition entirely in 3D latent space. The multi-concept image composition literature (CustomDiffusion, MS-Diffusion, LoRA-based methods) and personalized 3D generation line (DreamBooth3D, Consist3D, TIP-Editor) provide the problem context, but none address the joint challenge of multi-asset spatial reasoning and geometric consistency in 3D.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

This is the first training-free framework to compose multiple heterogeneous 2D assets into a geometrically coherent 3D object under textual guidance, addressing a gap between the abundance of editable 2D content and the difficulty of 3D scene construction. The SDE-based trajectory manipulation offers a principled, general mechanism for structure-preserving edits in flow models that could transfer beyond 3D generation. However, evaluation is limited to 27 test objects with VLM-based scoring and self-constructed baselines, leaving open questions about scalability to larger scenes, robustness across different 3D backbones, and whether the approach generalizes to dynamic or articulated compositions.
