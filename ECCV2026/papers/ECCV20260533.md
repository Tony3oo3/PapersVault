## Problem

Single-view 3D generative models must hallucinate unseen geometry, introducing ambiguity that limits practical utility. Existing multi-view conditioned generation methods either require fixed canonical viewpoints or depend on external reconstruction modules whose domain gap imposes heavy training costs and caps generation fidelity. The paper identifies that pretrained single-view models already encode useful 2D-to-3D correspondences, but naively combining multiple views destabilizes generation because the conditioning mechanism entangles orientation control with geometric structure transfer.

## Input / Output

The method accepts an arbitrary number (1 to 12+) of unposed RGB images from arbitrary viewpoints—no camera intrinsics, extrinsics, or fixed pose configuration is required. Input images are encoded by a frozen DINOv2 ViT-L/14 (ViT-G/14 in the refinement stage) into 256 patch tokens per view, projected to match the DiT hidden dimension. The output is a complete watertight 3D mesh, produced in two stages: a coarse geometry stage operating on 4096 3DShape2VecSet latent tokens, followed by a LATTICE-based refinement stage on 6144 tokens guided by voxel-grid positional encoding from the coarse output.

## Method

ROAR-3D upgrades a pretrained single-view flow-matching DiT (Hunyuan3D 2.1) with three lightweight components that decouple orientation control from geometry transfer. A token-wise view router, applied at each transformer block, computes routing logits between each 3D latent token (as query) and mean-pooled DINOv2 patch features of each view (as key), using Gumbel-Softmax to assign each token to its single most informative view; the router's Q/K projections are initialized from the pretrained cross-attention weights. A dual-stream cross-attention design routes tokens assigned to the user-designated primary view through the original pretrained attention (preserving orientation and base geometry) while tokens assigned to auxiliary views pass through a copied auxiliary stream restricted to geometric enrichment. An orientation perturbation strategy, applied with probability 0.2 during training, randomly rotates the 3D latent, removes the primary-view designation, and updates only the auxiliary stream, forcing it to learn orientation-independent 2D-to-3D correspondence. The per-token cross-attention cost remains identical to the single-view baseline, and a second-stage LATTICE refinement model reuses the same multi-view conditioning architecture at higher resolution.

## Results

On the held-out Objaverse/ObjaverseXL test set of 100 objects, ROAR-3D (stage 2) achieves a symmetric Chamfer Distance of 21.039×10⁻³, F1(0.05) of 81.6%, ULIP-I of 0.173, and Uni-I of 0.349, outperforming all baselines across every metric. Compared to the strongest multi-view conditioned baseline ReconViaGen (CD 32.055, F1(0.05) 72.7%), ROAR-3D reduces CD by 34% and improves F1(0.05) by 12%. The method also supports test-time view scaling from 1 to 12+ views with consistent quality improvements, and the ANYVIEW-200 qualitative set (200 objects with real-world challenges) confirms robustness to inconsistent and unposed inputs.

## Baselines & Comparisons

ROAR-3D (stage 2) wins on all five metrics against every baseline. Against the strongest multi-view conditioned generation baseline ReconViaGen, it reduces CD from 32.055 to 21.039 (−34%) and raises F1(0.05) from 72.7% to 81.6% (+12%), while also marginally improving ULIP-I (0.173 vs. 0.165) and Uni-I (0.349 vs. 0.345). Against single-view generative baselines (Hunyuan3D 2.1, Trellis2), the CD gap is even larger (21.039 vs. 33.7–34.0), reflecting the elimination of hallucinated unseen geometry. Multi-view reconstruction methods fare worst: LGM produces heavily over-smoothed surfaces (CD 58.009, F1(0.05) 52.3%), and VGGT, while geometrically competitive (CD 34.686), scores poorly on semantic alignment (ULIP-I 0.103, Uni-I 0.211) due to the absence of a generative prior. The multi-view variant of Hunyuan3D 2.0-MV shows only marginal improvement over its single-view counterpart, confirming that simply adding views without proper routing is insufficient.

## Relation to Prior Work

ROAR-3D builds directly on the Hunyuan3D 2.1 single-view image-to-3D diffusion backbone and the LATTICE refinement model, repurposing their pretrained cross-attention weights as the foundation for a multi-view routing mechanism. It departs from prior multi-view conditioned generation in two key ways: rather than requiring fixed canonical viewpoints (as in LGM or commercial systems) or injecting external reconstruction features (as in ReconViaGen with VGGT), it introduces a lightweight token-wise view router and dual-stream attention that operate entirely within the pretrained backbone. The central conceptual contribution is the diagnosis that single-view attention entangles orientation control with geometry transfer, and the architectural solution of separating these into primary and auxiliary streams with orientation perturbation training.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

ROAR-3D makes multi-view 3D generation practical for unconstrained real-world inputs (concept art, sketches, video frames) without requiring camera poses or external reconstruction pipelines, with only ~3–4 GPU days of additional training and low inference overhead. The test-time view scaling from 1 to 12+ views with consistent improvements opens a practical knob for quality-vs-cost tradeoffs. Limitations include the qualitative-only evaluation on ANYVIEW-200 (no ground-truth meshes), dependence on a designated primary view for orientation anchoring, and the method's coupling to specific backbone architectures (Hunyuan3D 2.1, LATTICE). Open questions include whether the routing mechanism generalizes to texture/material generation, how it behaves with very large view counts beyond 12, and whether the orientation perturbation strategy can be replaced by learned pose-agnostic representations.
