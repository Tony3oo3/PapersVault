## Problem

Existing diffusion-based 3D scene generation methods operate in 2D image or video latent spaces, where cross-view geometric and appearance consistency is not explicitly enforced, leading to inconsistent multi-view reconstructions. Concurrent approaches that move toward 3D representation spaces (e.g., Gen3R) compress 3D features to align with VAE video latents, limiting representational capacity and forcing separate geometry and appearance generation. Additionally, train-inference exposure bias causes diffusion samples to drift off the learned 3D manifold, a problem amplified by coupled cross-view constraints in 3D generation.

## Input / Output

The method takes a single RGB image (resized to 224×448), its camera intrinsics and extrinsics in the dataset convention, and an optional text prompt (dropped with 50% probability during training to support non-text-conditioned generation). It outputs a full 3D Gaussian Splatting scene (per-pixel GS parameters) plus depth maps, from which arbitrary novel views can be rendered. The conditioning view is encoded independently by 3D-URAE (not jointly with the target), ensuring consistent inference when only one view is available at test time.

## Method

OneWorld builds a 3D Unified Representation Autoencoder (3D-URAE) by fine-tuning the π³ feed-forward 3D reconstructor with two added branches: an appearance-injection branch that concatenates lightweight convolutional appearance tokens with DINOv2 semantic tokens before the geometry encoder, and a semantic-distillation branch that aligns geometry tokens to VFM tokens via marginal cosine and distance-matrix similarity losses, producing a unified latent encoding geometry, appearance, and semantics. A DiT diffusion model (initialized from Wan-2.1-T2V-1.3B) then generates target-view tokens directly in this unified 3D space using x₀-prediction with v-loss, augmented by a token-level Cross-View-Correspondence (CVC) loss that enforces the predicted target tokens to preserve the nearest-neighbor matching pattern to the conditioning view. To counteract train-inference exposure bias, Manifold-Drift Forcing (MDF) trains the 3DGS prediction heads on interpolated mixtures of diffusion-sampled (off-manifold) and ground-truth (on-manifold) latents across intermediate timesteps, making the decoder robust to the drift that accumulates during iterative denoising.

## Results

On the 1-view novel view synthesis benchmark, OneWorld achieves the best PSNR of 21.57 and SSIM of 0.735 on RealEstate10K, and 17.19 PSNR and 0.589 SSIM on DL3DV-10K, with the lowest LPIPS (0.231 and 0.418 respectively) among all compared methods. On the reference-free WorldScore protocol, OneWorld attains the highest 3D Consistency (84.98 indoor, 78.21 outdoor) and Photometric Consistency (81.67 indoor, 74.09 outdoor), confirming superior cross-view geometric and appearance coherence. The method also leads on VBench Image Quality (0.604 indoor, 0.556 outdoor) while maintaining near-saturated subject and background identity scores.

## Baselines & Comparisons

OneWorld outperforms all six baselines on every reconstruction metric (PSNR, SSIM, LPIPS) across both RealEstate10K and DL3DV-10K. The closest competitors are FlashWorld (20.18 PSNR on Re10K) and Gen3R (20.09 PSNR), which OneWorld surpasses by 1.39 and 1.48 PSNR respectively on Re10K, and by 1.17 and 1.25 PSNR on DL3DV-10K. On the reference-free WorldScore benchmark, OneWorld leads in 3D Consistency and Photometric Consistency but trails FlashWorld on Content Alignment (46.88 vs. 49.27) and Gen3R on Style Consistency (76.74 vs. 79.10) for indoor scenes, while remaining competitive on Object Control and Subjective Quality. The 2D-latent-based methods (LVSM, Gen3C, GF, Aether) consistently underperform the 3D-representation-based methods (FlashWorld, Gen3R, OneWorld) by 3–6 PSNR, confirming the advantage of operating in a unified 3D space. Among the 3D-native baselines, OneWorld's gains over FlashWorld and Gen3R are most pronounced on DL3DV-10K (outdoor, larger viewpoint changes), suggesting the 3D unified representation better handles geometrically challenging scenes.

## Relation to Prior Work

OneWorld departs from the dominant paradigm of diffusing in compressed 2D image/video VAE latents (as in FlashWorld, SplatFlow, Gen3R) by performing diffusion directly in the representation space of a pretrained 3D foundation model (π³), inspired by the image-generation trend of replacing VAEs with vision foundation model tokenizers (RAEs). It builds on π³'s feed-forward geometry encoder, augmenting it with an appearance-injection branch and a semantic-distillation branch (following VA-VAE's marginal similarity losses) to produce a unified 3D latent that jointly encodes geometry, appearance, and semantics. The diffusion training further draws on JiT's x₀-prediction parameterization and introduces two novel regularizers—cross-view correspondence preservation and manifold-drift forcing—that have no direct counterpart in prior 2D-latent 3D generation pipelines.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

By grounding generation in a native 3D representation space rather than per-view 2D latents, OneWorld offers a principled path toward inherently cross-view-consistent 3D scene synthesis, and the CVC and MDF components provide reusable techniques for any diffusion model operating in structured multi-view spaces. Limitations include reliance on a single 3DGS output format, conditioning on only one view, and training on a relatively modest ~70K-scene corpus, leaving open whether the approach scales to larger, more diverse scene collections or to text-driven generation without the current 50% classifier-free text dropping. A key open question is whether the 3D-URAE formulation generalizes beyond the π³ architecture and whether the manifold-drift forcing strategy transfers to other 3D representations (NeRF, mesh, point cloud).
