## Problem

Diffusion-based HOI generation methods suffer from error accumulation across iterative denoising steps, producing unstable trajectories and physically implausible interactions due to the tight kinematic coupling between human and object motions. No prior work has applied generative masked modeling in a discrete VQ space to full-body HOI motion, and the challenge of performing differentiable guidance over non-differentiable discrete tokens remains unaddressed.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ECCV20261184/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method takes a free-form text description (encoded by a frozen CLIP text encoder) and object geometry (encoded by an MLP) as conditioning signals. It outputs synchronized full-body human motion (SMPL joint positions and rotations over T frames) and object motion (centroid rotation and global 3D position), where the human decoder is conditioned on the predicted object trajectory to enforce spatial alignment. The 2D token maps jointly encode motion dynamics and per-joint/per-surface-point contact states, so the output inherently carries contact-aware structure rather than requiring a separate contact prediction stage.

## Method

HOIMask first encodes human and object motions (along with their contact signals) into 2D discrete token maps via a dual-branch HOI VQ-VAE that uses 2D convolutions and learned codebooks, preserving spatial-temporal interaction structure that 1D quantization discards. A HOI-Mask Transformer then generates these tokens conditioned on text and object geometry, employing Spatial-Temporal Attention (STA) for within-entity motion dependencies and Human/Object-Centric Attention (HOCA) for cross-entity interaction, with a dual masking strategy combining random masking and human-object masking to strengthen inter-entity coupling. During inference, a novel Contact-aware Reconstruction Guidance backpropagates contact and penetration loss gradients through a Gumbel-Softmax differentiable sampling and a Soft-VQ module (probabilistic mixture of codebook entries) to iteratively update the predicted logits, enabling diffusion-like differentiable optimization in the discrete token space without requiring continuous representations.

## Results

On FullBodyManipulation, HOIMask achieves FID 0.109, R-Precision (Top-3) 0.826, Contact Precision 0.74, and Contact Percentage 52.96%, outperforming the strongest diffusion baseline LIGHT (FID 0.105, C% 49.57%) on all interaction metrics. On BEHAVE, HOIMask attains FID 0.424 and C% 66.90%, surpassing LIGHT (FID 0.467, C% 65.89%) on both realism and contact coverage. The method also achieves the lowest Penetration Score (0.065) among all methods on FullBodyManipulation, confirming superior physical plausibility.

## Baselines & Comparisons

HOIMask outperforms all seven diffusion-based baselines on the majority of interaction metrics. Against the closest competitor LIGHT [54], HOIMask wins on R-Precision (0.826 vs 0.779), Contact Precision (0.74 vs 0.71), and Contact Percentage (52.96% vs 49.57%) on FullBodyManipulation, while LIGHT retains a marginal FID advantage (0.105 vs 0.109). On BEHAVE, HOIMask leads LIGHT on FID (0.424 vs 0.467) and C% (66.90% vs 65.89%), though LIGHT achieves a slightly lower Penetration Score (0.135 vs 0.141). Compared to ROG [58], which achieves the second-best FID on FullBodyManipulation (0.199), HOIMask improves FID by 45% and more than doubles the Contact Percentage (52.96% vs 27.56%). Against CHOIS [27], HOIMask reduces FID from 0.810 to 0.109 (an 87% improvement) while increasing C% from 45.46% to 52.96%. The earlier baselines (MDM, InterDiff, HOI-Diff, SemGeoMo) are substantially outperformed across all metrics, with FID gaps exceeding 1.0 on FullBodyManipulation.

## Relation to Prior Work

HOIMask departs from the dominant diffusion-based paradigm for HOI generation (HOI-Diff, CHOIS, ROG, LIGHT) by adopting a generative masked modeling framework in discrete token space, a paradigm that has proven effective for single-human motion (MoMask, MMM, InterMask) but had not been extended to full-body human-object interaction. It builds on VQ-based motion tokenization (HOIGPT, T2M-GPT) but replaces 1D sequence quantization with 2D spatio-temporal token maps to preserve joint-level interaction structure. The contact-aware reconstruction guidance draws conceptual inspiration from contact-guided denoising in diffusion HOI methods (CG-HOI, CHOIS) but requires a novel differentiable mechanism (Gumbel-Softmax + Soft-VQ) to operate in the non-differentiable discrete token space.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

This work demonstrates that generative masked modeling—a paradigm previously validated only for single-human motion—can be successfully extended to the substantially more complex full-body HOI setting, challenging the assumption that iterative denoising is necessary for capturing tight human-object coupling. The contact-aware reconstruction guidance in discrete space is a transferable technique that could benefit other VQ-based generative models requiring physical constraints at inference. However, evaluation is limited to two datasets (FullBodyManipulation, BEHAVE), the FID advantage over the strongest diffusion baseline (LIGHT) is marginal on FullBodyManipulation (0.109 vs. 0.105), and the added inference cost from iterative logits optimization and Soft-VQ decoding has not been quantified. Open questions include scalability to multi-person interactions, longer sequences, and whether the 2D token map approach generalizes to more complex object geometries.
