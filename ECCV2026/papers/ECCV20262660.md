## Problem

While 2D foundation image models generate photorealistic images, it remains unclear whether they implicitly encode 3D spatial understanding sufficient for coherent world synthesis. Existing 3D generation methods are bottlenecked by scarce 3D training data or the computational cost of maintaining multi-view consistency through Score Distillation Sampling, leaving open the question of whether 2D models can be directly leveraged as 3D world generators without additional 3D-specific training.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ECCV20262660/cropped_page0_idx1.webp)

## Input / Output

The method takes a single free-form text prompt as input and produces a set of N=14 posed RGB images with absolute camera poses in a global coordinate frame, which are then lifted into a 3D Gaussian Splatting representation via AnySplat for interactive novel-view rendering. The generation process is budgeted with R̂=28 total tries (allowing up to 2 re-samples per viewpoint if verification fails), and the entire pipeline operates zero-shot with no 3D-aware weight updates to the underlying 2D foundation model.

## Method

WorldAgents orchestrates three specialized agents in an iterative loop: a VLM Director that dynamically formulates view-specific text prompts conditioned on the verified scene history, a Generator that synthesizes new views via 3D-aware sequential inpainting (reconstructing a 3DGS from verified frames, rendering from the next camera pose, and using a 2D diffusion model only to fill disoccluded regions), and a two-stage VLM Verifier that gates each candidate by checking 2D semantic coherence and 3D geometric consistency (rendering all historical views from a provisional AnySplat reconstruction and evaluating PSNR/SSIM/LPIPS degradation). The camera trajectory alternates between rightward and leftward exploration with fixed rotation plus random perturbation for diverse coverage. The key insight is that grounding generation in explicit 3D reprojection before applying the 2D generative prior constrains the model to fill only structurally logical regions, while the dual-space verification prevents geometrically inconsistent frames from corrupting the global reconstruction.

## Results

Using the best configuration (Flux.2[Pro] + GPT-4.1), WorldAgents achieves a CLIP Score of 26.79, Inception Score of 2.26, and CLIP-IQA of 0.89, outperforming all four baselines on prompt alignment (CS) and image quality (IQA). On 3D consistency, the method attains the lowest reprojection error (0.36) among all compared methods, beating WorldExplorer (0.39), Text2Room (0.45), WonderWorld (0.46), and SEVA (0.61). The Inception Score is slightly lower than Text2Room (2.79) and WonderWorld (2.48), suggesting a trade-off between diversity and per-image fidelity.

## Baselines & Comparisons

WorldAgents (Flux.2[Pro]+GPT-4.1) outperforms all four baselines on CLIP Score and CLIP-IQA, with the largest margin over Text2Room (CS +4.52, IQA +0.62) and SEVA (CS +1.77, IQA +0.28). On 3D reprojection error, it achieves the best score (0.36), improving over the closest competitor WorldExplorer (0.39) by 7.7% and over SEVA (0.61) by 41%. The only metric where a baseline leads is Inception Score: Text2Room (2.79) and WonderWorld (2.48) both exceed WorldAgents' 2.26, though the gap is modest. Qualitatively, the paper reports that baselines produce sparser scenes with structural artifacts and blurring around object boundaries, whereas WorldAgents generates denser, more geometrically detailed environments with fewer hallucinated duplicates.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper carves new ground by repurposing off-the-shelf 2D image diffusion models (Flux.2, Nano Banana) and VLMs (GPT-4.1, Qwen3-VL) as agents in a multi-agent orchestration loop, rather than fine-tuning camera-controlled video models (SEVA, WorldExplorer) or relying on handcrafted prompts (WonderWorld, Text2Room). It departs from panorama-based generation (LayerPano360, DreamScene360) by using sequential 3D-reprojection-guided inpainting, and from code-based agent approaches (VIGA) by directly generating image frames. The key conceptual departure is framing 3D scene generation as an iterative, VLM-directed process where 3D reconstruction information is used only for verification and re-projection, never back-propagated into the generative model.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The paper provides empirical evidence that 2D foundation models encode sufficient implicit 3D spatial structure to produce multi-view-consistent scenes without any 3D-specific training, which reframes how the community thinks about the relationship between 2D and 3D generative priors. However, the current implementation is limited to 14-frame left/right exploration, takes ~25 minutes per scene, and the quantitative gains over strong baselines (e.g., CS 26.79 vs. 26.67 for WonderWorld) are modest. Open questions include whether the agentic verification loop scales to much larger scenes, whether extending to video diffusion models (as the authors suggest) can overcome the per-frame latency, and whether the implicit 3D understanding generalizes to dynamic or physically interactive environments.
