## Problem

Multimodal LLMs struggle with multi-step visual reasoning because textual Chain-of-Thought suffers from a modality gap that loses fine-grained spatial information, explicit visual generation is computationally prohibitive, and existing latent approaches either depend on external vision experts (introducing domain gaps) or attempt monolithic single-step prediction that overloads representational capacity and causes latent collapse.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ECCV20260199/cropped_page1_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The model takes a single RGB image and a free-form textual query as input. It produces a final text answer preceded by K=4 progressive latent thought steps, each encoded as 4 learnable special tokens (16 tokens total) in the LLM's embedding space. For spatial tasks the latent chain follows a Coarse-to-Fine Causal Chain (Grid View → Bounding Box → Crop → Segmentation), while for logical tasks it follows a Dialectical Reasoning Chain (Edge Enhancement → Directional Guidance → Counterfactual Simulation → Solution Verification). Supervision signals come from programmatically synthesized auxiliary images (e.g., geometric crops, segmentations) rather than real intermediate frames.

## Method

ProLaViT decomposes visual reasoning into a structured chain of K=4 latent thought steps represented by learnable tokens embedded in the LLM input sequence. Supervision is provided via an Endogenous Self-Distillation mechanism: a frozen copy of the model's own vision encoder extracts features from programmatically synthesized auxiliary images, and a single cross-attention layer aligns the LLM's latent token representations to these teacher features via an MSE loss. To prevent latent collapse (where successive steps become indistinguishable), a Distance-Weighted Diversity Loss penalizes cosine similarity between step centroids with a margin, weighted by the causal distance between steps so that distant steps are forced more distinct while adjacent steps retain natural feature sharing. Training follows a four-phase curriculum: (1) warm-up of latent token embeddings, (2) distillation-only alignment, (3) introduction of the diversity constraint, and (4) joint fine-tuning on general VQA to prevent catastrophic forgetting. The entire pipeline runs on a single ViT pass at inference (1.21× base latency), avoiding the 3.4× overhead of iterative tool-use or pixel-level generation.

## Results

ProLaViT (Full) achieves the highest average accuracy of 75.11% across seven visual reasoning benchmarks, outperforming the base Qwen2.5-VL-Instruct (66.00%) by +9.11 points. The largest gains appear on perception-intensive and logical tasks: BLINK-Jigsaw improves from 59.33% to 76.00% (+16.67%), ChartQA from 78.45% to 78.99%, and VisPuzzle from 34.75% to 74.25% (+39.50%). Inference efficiency is preserved, with only 1.21× latency and +12% TFLOPs over the base model, compared to 3.4× latency for explicit tool-use approaches.

## Baselines & Comparisons

Against the base Qwen2.5-VL-Instruct, ProLaViT (Full) improves average accuracy by +9.11 points (75.11% vs. 66.00%), with the most dramatic gain on VisPuzzle (+39.50%) and BLINK-Jigsaw (+16.67%), confirming that the base model's single-step latent prediction is insufficient for multi-step visual reasoning. Compared to CoVT [23], which distills from external vision experts (SAM, DepthAnything), ProLaViT achieves +13.66% average accuracy; the gap is most stark on ChartQA (78.99% vs. 24.99%), where CoVT's external experts suffer a domain gap between natural images and abstract charts. Against LVR [16], which performs unstructured single-step latent reasoning, ProLaViT gains +10.48% on average, with particularly large improvements on VisPuzzle (+39.25%) and BLINK-Jigsaw (+18.67%), validating the benefit of structured progressive decomposition over monolithic latent prediction. ProLaViT also outperforms the One-step Latent Prediction ablation by +4.26% on average, with a +10.00% gain on BLINK-Jigsaw, demonstrating that the Locate→Focus→Isolate causal chain is essential for complex spatial and logical tasks.

## Relation to Prior Work

ProLaViT carves new ground at the intersection of latent visual reasoning and structured multi-step derivation. It departs from textual CoT methods (Qwen-VL, LLaVA-CoT) by arguing the modality gap makes text insufficient for spatial grounding, and from explicit visual generation (Anole, ThinkMorph, Zebra-CoT) by eliminating pixel-level synthesis cost. Against prior latent approaches (CoVT, LVR, Mirage), it introduces two key departures: (1) an endogenous self-distillation mechanism using the model's own frozen ViT as teacher rather than external experts like SAM or DINOv2, and (2) a progressive multi-step causal chain (Locate→Focus→Isolate) with a Distance-Weighted Diversity Loss to prevent the latent collapse that plagues single-step or unstructured latent methods. The programmatic synthesis pipeline for generating algorithmically precise auxiliary image sequences is a novel training-data strategy that internalizes geometric operations without inference-time tools.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The endogenous self-distillation strategy is practically significant: it eliminates the pipeline complexity and domain-shift fragility of external expert supervision (demonstrated by CoVT's catastrophic 24.99% collapse on ChartQA), while the 1.21× latency overhead versus 3.4× for explicit tool-use makes structured latent reasoning viable for real-time deployment. The Distance-Weighted Diversity Loss offers a general-purpose regularization for any sequential latent reasoning setup, addressing the latent collapse pathology that likely affects other multi-step latent methods. Limitations include evaluation on a single 7B architecture, static images only, and a relatively narrow set of perception-intensive benchmarks; the authors note extension to video reasoning and complex geometric transformations as open directions. Whether the programmatic synthesis pipeline generalizes beyond the four canonical operations (grid, bbox, crop, segmentation) to more diverse visual tasks remains untested.
