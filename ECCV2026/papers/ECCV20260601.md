## Problem

Existing HOI video generation methods either operate in static exocentric views or condition on privileged future object states (ground-truth trajectories, waypoints), effectively reducing the task to conditional video generation rather than true world modeling. A reliable egocentric HOI world model that infers contact-driven interaction dynamics strictly from user-specified actions—without future-state shortcuts—remains largely absent, despite being critical for embodied AI and robotics.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ECCV20260601/cropped_page1_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The model takes a single first-frame RGB image (480×480) plus a per-step action sequence of 3D hand mesh renders and metric head-pose trajectories (intrinsics + extrinsics relative to frame 1) over an 81-frame horizon, and produces a photorealistic egocentric video rollout at 480×480 resolution. Notably, no future object trajectories, waypoints, or ground-truth object states are provided; the model must infer object dynamics purely from hand kinematics and ego-motion. At inference the system runs at 16 FPS after 8000 training steps on 16 H100 GPUs.

## Method

EgoHOI builds on a frozen Wan 2.1 14B DiT backbone (fine-tuned via LoRA, rank 128) and injects three physics-informed embeddings through lightweight adapters that preserve the pretrained generative prior. Hand Kinematic Embeddings (HKE) encode a temporal stack of 3D hand mesh renders via a 3D convolutional network with a reference-hand branch, fused into the main token stream via a gated residual update. Ego-Motion Embeddings (EME) represent per-pixel Plücker ray fields from the head-pose sequence, processed by causal 3D convolutions and hybrid spatiotemporal blocks, then injected through zero-initialized linear adapters in the first D DiT blocks. Object Entity Embeddings (OEE) anchor object identity by encoding first-frame segmentation through the frozen VAE and appending the resulting tokens with shifted RoPE as KV-only context in self-attention. A first-frame CLIP embedding further stabilizes global appearance via fixed cross-attention keys/values, and the entire pipeline operates in the VAE latent space with a standard diffusion denoising objective.

## Results

On the HOT3D benchmark (100 held-out clips, 81-frame rollouts), EgoHOI achieves PSNR 21.05, SSIM 0.65, and LPIPS 0.27 for frame prediction, substantially outperforming the strongest baseline Cosmos 14B (PSNR 15.89, SSIM 0.59, LPIPS 0.38). For ego-motion consistency, EgoHOI reaches ATE 0.084, RRE 5.192, and RPE 0.021, compared to Cosmos 14B's 0.112/9.046/0.022. For kinematic fidelity, EgoHOI reports MR 5.84%, MPJPE 0.014, and RMSE 0.044, versus Cosmos 14B's 14.61%/0.041/0.074, indicating roughly 60% reduction in hand-joint errors.

## Baselines & Comparisons

EgoHOI outperforms all three baselines on every reported metric. Against the Wan 2.1 14B backbone, EgoHOI gains +6.27 dB PSNR, reduces ATE from 0.124 to 0.084 (−32%), and cuts MR from 19.67% to 5.84% (−70%), showing that physics-informed embeddings add far more than the pretrained video prior alone. Against Cosmos 14B (the strongest general-purpose world model baseline), EgoHOI improves PSNR by 5.16 dB, reduces RRE from 9.046 to 5.192 (−43%), and lowers MPJPE from 0.041 to 0.014 (−66%), confirming that scaling a general world model does not recover contact-driven interaction dynamics. Uni3C, which offers explicit camera and human-motion control, actually degrades under rollout evaluation (ATE 0.168, MR 20.50%), suggesting that motion control without physics-aware regularization fails to maintain contact consistency. The paper also reports VBench scores where EgoHOI leads in Subject Consistency (95.51%), Background Consistency (94.91%), Aesthetic Quality (52.03%), and Imaging Quality (64.48%), though its Dynamic Degree (53.29%) is lower than Cosmos 14B (89.50%), reflecting a deliberate trade-off favoring stable interaction over high camera dynamism.

## Relation to Prior Work

EgoHOI builds on the Wan 2.1 DiT video diffusion backbone and the broader world-modeling line (Cosmos, GEM, PlayerOne), but departs sharply from prior HOI video generation methods (RE-HOLD, IDiT-HOI, ByteLoom) that condition on privileged future object trajectories or waypoints, which the authors argue reduces the task to conditional video generation rather than true action-driven simulation. The key methodological lineage is the use of 3D foundation models (HaMeR for hand pose, Plücker ray fields for camera geometry, segmentation for object identity) to distill physics-informed embeddings, injected via lightweight LoRA adapters and gated residual tokens into a frozen DiT. This positions the work at the intersection of egocentric world modeling and contact-driven HOI synthesis, where neither thread alone had addressed the combined challenge of first-person, high-DoF, occlusion-heavy interaction without future-state shortcuts.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

Egocentric HOI rollouts are a critical data source for VLA pre-training, dexterous manipulation, and physical-intelligence research, yet no reliable world model for this setting existed before this work. The paper's central claim—that explicit 3D geometric and kinematic structure, rather than pure RGB scaling, is what enables physically plausible interaction dynamics—could reshape how future world models incorporate physical priors. Limitations include evaluation on a single dataset (HOT3D, 100 clips), 480×480 resolution, 81-frame rollouts, and 16 FPS inference, leaving open whether the physics-informed embedding strategy scales to longer horizons, higher resolutions, and more diverse interaction categories. A key open question is whether the approach generalizes beyond hand-object to broader embodied interactions (tool use, multi-object manipulation) without re-deriving task-specific 3D priors.
