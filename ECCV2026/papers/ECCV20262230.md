## Problem

Existing visual prompting methods are restricted to the input pixel space, where prompt signals attenuate severely through deep layers and fail to align intermediate representations with task-relevant semantics. Extending prompts to deep activations triggers a Capacity-Integrity Paradox: sufficient prompting capacity for task adaptation causes parameter explosion in high-channel layers and overwrites pre-trained representations, while insufficient capacity leaves the model unable to adapt.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ECCV20262230/cropped_page1_idx2.webp)

## Input / Output

The method takes standard RGB images (resized to 224×224) and produces class labels via a frozen backbone plus a trainable classification head. The key distinction is that learnable prompt tensors are injected element-wise into intermediate feature maps at multiple depths (not just the input), with the backbone weights remaining frozen throughout training. Only the low-rank tensor parameters (~168K for ViT-B/16) are optimized, making the approach a parameter-efficient fine-tuning method.

## Method

LoTeP formulates deep visual prompts as low-rank tensors decomposed across both channel and spatial dimensions, offering two variants: LoTeP-TK (Tucker decomposition with a core tensor and three factor matrices) and LoTeP-CP (sum of rank-one outer products). This tensor factorization decouples prompt complexity from the raw channel count C, reducing parameter cost from O(C·r·(H+W)) to O(r·(C+H+W)). A cosine rank-decaying schedule progressively reduces the tensor rank from r₀ at shallow layers to zero at a depth d=⌊L·ρ⌋, preserving pre-trained semantics in deeper layers while granting task-adaptation capacity in earlier ones. Channel-dimension parameters are zero-initialized so the prompt starts as a zero tensor, and a theoretical proposition bounds the intrinsic rank of learned prompts as decreasing with depth, corroborating the decaying design.

## Results

On ViT-B/32, LoTeP-TK achieves 91.44% average accuracy across 10 downstream datasets, surpassing full fine-tuning (91.11%) and LoRA (91.22%) while using only ~169K trainable parameters. On ResNet-18, LoTeP-TK reaches 82.19% average, outperforming LoR-VP (78.15%) by 4.04%. For out-of-distribution robustness, LoTeP-TK achieves 44.41% on ImageNet-A, a +5.05% gain over LoR-VP (39.36%), while maintaining comparable in-distribution accuracy (84.83% vs 84.66% on ImageNet-1K). Overall, LoTeP adds fewer than 0.1% of backbone parameters relative to LoR-VP while delivering a consistent >2.5% average improvement.

## Baselines & Comparisons

Against the primary SOTA VP baseline LoR-VP [18], LoTeP wins on 9 of 10 datasets on both ViT-B/32 (+2.50% avg) and ResNet-18 (+4.04% avg), with the sole exception being OxfordPets where LoR-VP edges ahead by ~0.3%. Compared to LoRA [16], LoTeP-TK surpasses it on ViT-B/32 (91.44% vs 91.22%) with ~1/66th the parameters (169K vs 11.05M), but loses on ResNet-18 (82.19% vs 82.79%) where the small backbone's limited capacity constrains VP's upper bound. Against VPT-Deep [17], LoTeP dominates by a wide margin (91.44% vs 87.66% on ViT-B/32) while also training faster (1.59h vs 3.48h) and using fewer parameters (169K vs 538K). ILM-VP [7] and AutoVP [38] are substantially outperformed across all backbones and datasets, with ILM-VP particularly struggling on fine-grained tasks (e.g., 14.13% on Tiny-ImageNet with ResNet-18). On OOD generalization, LoTeP-TK outperforms LoR-VP on all four OOD benchmarks, most notably by +5.05% on ImageNet-A (44.41% vs 39.36%), suggesting deep tensor prompting reduces overfitting to source-specific artifacts.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the Visual Prompting (VP) paradigm—previously confined to input pixel space (CLIP-VP, AutoVP, ILM-VP, LoR-VP)—into deep network activations, a direction that prior work deemed impractical due to parameter explosion and semantic disruption. It draws on low-rank tensor decomposition (Tucker and CP) from the model compression literature to formulate deep prompts as structured tensors, and on the channel-redundancy observations from feature-pruning work to motivate the design. The preliminary channel-grouping study serves as an empirical bridge, revealing the Capacity-Integrity Paradox before the full tensor formulation is introduced.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

LoTeP provides a principled, theoretically grounded mechanism (rank-decaying low-rank tensors) for the first time to apply visual prompts to deep activations without destroying pre-trained semantics, closing a gap that has kept VP methods restricted to input space. The OOD robustness gains (notably +5% on ImageNet-A) suggest deep tensor modulation may reduce overfitting to source-specific artifacts, a finding with broader implications for transfer learning. Limitations include the theoretical analysis being restricted to CNNs, the continued disadvantage on small-capacity backbones (ResNet-18) where FT/LoRA dominate, and evaluation limited to image classification—extending the framework to dense prediction tasks or non-CNN architectures theoretically remains open.
