## Problem

Monocular depth foundation models systematically hallucinate spurious 3D structures from planar or low-curvature surfaces bearing perceptually ambiguous textures (e.g., 3D street art), a failure the authors term the 3D Mirage. This vulnerability is systemic across all SOTA architectures, is exacerbated when surrounding context is removed (restricted FOV), and is invisible to standard pixel-wise metrics like MAE and RMSE. No prior benchmark, reference-free scoring protocol, or targeted mitigation strategy existed for this specific failure mode.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ECCV20262832/cropped_page1_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method consumes a single RGB image in two paired views—a full-context frame and a context-restricted crop (retaining ≥40% of the ROI diagonal)—and outputs a dense per-pixel depth map. The 3D-Mirage benchmark provides 1,872 such paired instances with manually annotated polygonal ROI masks (including nested exclusions for real objects) that delineate the low-curvature carrier regions. Evaluation uses reference-free second-order magnitude metrics (DCS for hallucination intensity, CCS for contextual instability) computed on ROI pixels without requiring ground-truth depth, complementing standard metrics on NYUv2, KITTI, and DA-2K for preservation checks.

## Method

Grounded Self-Distillation (GSD) injects LoRA adapters (rank 16, ~4M trainable params, ~1.2% of DAv2-L) into the DINOv2 encoder of a frozen Depth-Anything-V2 teacher, trained in a dual-view (full + crop) setup with shared student weights. The composite loss has two axes: Hallucination Knowledge Re-editing (HKR) penalizes second-order curvature inside ROIs and re-targets each ROI instance toward a teacher-guided mixture of up to K simple surface hypotheses selected by a learned gating network (avoiding forcing a single flat plane), while Non-hallucination Knowledge Preservation (NKP) distills the frozen teacher's depth and second-order structure on background, seam, and edge-ring regions to prevent catastrophic forgetting. Training interleaves illusion samples with non-illusion regularizer images (Penn-Fudan, CamVid) at a 4:1 ratio, and the entire adaptation converges in one epoch on a single A100, yielding a 94% reduction in DCS and 87% reduction in CCS over the teacher.

## Results

On the 3D-Mirage benchmark, Grounded Self-Distillation (GSD) achieves a Deviation Composite Score (DCS) of 58.64 and a Confusion Composite Score (CCS) of 1.907×10⁻⁴, representing a 94.1% reduction in geometric hallucination and an 87.0% reduction in contextual instability relative to its DAv2-L teacher (DCS 994.6, CCS 1.466×10⁻³). On the 3D-Visual-Illusion (3DVI) cross-architecture test set, the adapted model attains EPE 1.75, bad₂ 26.67%, AbsRel 0.03, and δ₁ 99.50%, matching or exceeding the VLM-driven 3DVI baseline (EPE 1.77, δ₁ 99.60%) while using only a single monocular forward pass. Knowledge preservation is confirmed on NYUv2 (AbsRel 0.1597, δ₁ 79.58%) and DA-2K (pairwise accuracy 96.08%), showing no catastrophic forgetting.

## Baselines & Comparisons

All 16 SOTA monocular depth baselines exhibit severe 3D mirage hallucinations, with DCS ranging from 459.8 (DA-S, the best baseline) to 2083.0 (DepthFM, the worst), confirming the failure is systemic across transformer, diffusion, and generative architectures. GSD outperforms every baseline by a wide margin: it reduces DCS by 87–97% and CCS by 75–98% relative to the best and worst baselines respectively. On the 3DVI cross-architecture benchmark, GSD (EPE 1.75, δ₁ 99.50%) matches the VLM-fusion 3DVI method (EPE 1.77, δ₁ 99.60%) while being a single-view monocular model, and substantially outperforms DAv2 (EPE 5.81, δ₁ 92.86%), Metric3D (EPE 12.46, δ₁ 48.97%), and Marigold (EPE 21.16, δ₁ 63.65%). Transfer to ZoeDepth yields a 43% DCS reduction (589→335), and LoRA adaptation of Marigold cuts DCS/CCS by 44%/40%, demonstrating architecture-agnostic applicability. Among simpler adaptation strategies, full encoder fine-tuning achieves the lowest DCS (27.85) but catastrophically degrades general depth (DA-2K pairwise drops to 58.51%), whereas GSD maintains 96.08% pairwise accuracy while achieving DCS 58.64—confirming the surgical precision of the LoRA + self-distillation approach.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper builds directly on Depth Anything V2 (DAv2) as its base architecture and on LoRA as the parameter-efficient adaptation mechanism, but departs from prior MDE work by reframing the evaluation question from pixel-wise geometric accuracy to structural and contextual robustness. It extends the line of 3D visual illusion research (Booster, MonoTrap, 3DVI) by introducing controlled context variation (paired full-context vs. restricted-FOV views) and by targeting low-curvature outdoor carriers rather than indoor or specular surfaces. The Grounded Self-Distillation strategy is novel in applying PEFT specifically to suppress second-order curvature on annotated illusion ROIs while using a frozen teacher for background preservation, a formulation not previously explored for depth hallucination.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The 3D-Mirage benchmark and reference-free DCS/CCS metrics give practitioners a practical, GT-free audit tool for detecting spurious 3D structure in deployed depth models—a gap that standard MAE/RMSE metrics cannot fill. The parameter-efficient GSD approach (4M trainable params, ~1.2% of backbone) makes mitigation deployable without retraining, which is important for safety-critical systems like autonomous driving where a hallucinated bump on a flat road can trigger unsafe maneuvers. Key limitations remain: the benchmark covers only low-curvature illusion carriers (not reflections, glass, shadows, or adverse weather), and the method has not been validated on real road hazards like potholes or curbs. Whether the DCS/CCS framework generalizes to other perceptual ambiguity classes beyond texture-based illusions is an open question.
