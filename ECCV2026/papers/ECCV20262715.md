## Problem

Existing camera-controlled video diffusion models rely on implicit spatiotemporal representations without explicit 3D geometric grounding, leading to geometrically implausible structures, distorted object shapes, and cross-view spatial inconsistencies under large viewpoint changes. The paper asks whether explicit structural priors from 3D foundation models can be integrated into video diffusion to achieve geometry-consistent world modeling.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ECCV20262715/cropped_page1_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

Given a single scene image, a user-defined camera trajectory specifying L arbitrary poses, and a text prompt, the method synthesizes an L-frame novel view video at 832×480 resolution (81-frame clips). The output must simultaneously satisfy trajectory adherence (each frame follows its prescribed camera pose), text alignment, and scene coherence with the initial observation. During inference, the initial image is first projected to target viewpoints via point-cloud warping to produce incomplete warped frames that serve as conditioning for the geometry model.

## Method

DreamWorld decouples generation into a geometry-then-appearance two-stage pipeline, both initialized from Wan 2.1 and trained with rectified flow matching. In the geometry stage, a video diffusion model is trained via feature-level flow matching to predict complete geometry features (extracted from a 3D foundation model like DA3) for target novel views, conditioned on incomplete warped geometry features and the initial image; a lightweight MLP projection module maps high-dimensional 3D foundation model features into a diffusion-friendly latent representation. In the appearance stage, a second video diffusion model synthesizes photorealistic VAE latents conditioned on the predicted geometry features (concatenated along the channel dimension with noisy latents) plus the initial image and text prompt, with a KL regularization enforcing Gaussian structure on the projected geometry features. Training is progressive: the appearance model and projection module are jointly optimized first (with perturbations to geometry features for robustness), then the projection module is frozen while the geometry model learns structural completion via distillation from the 3D foundation model.

## Results

On RealEstate10K (Hard Set), DreamWorld achieves PSNR 20.94, SSIM 0.739, LPIPS 0.267, and FID 31.16, outperforming the best baseline Gen3C (PSNR 18.04, FID 46.67) by a substantial margin. On Tanks-and-Temples (Hard Set), DreamWorld reaches PSNR 19.59 and FID 36.95, again leading all competitors. On the WorldScore benchmark, DreamWorld attains the highest average score of 75.04, with particularly strong 3D Consistency (84.96) and Photo Consistency (92.70), surpassing the next-best WonderWorld (70.65 avg).

## Baselines & Comparisons

DreamWorld outperforms all nine baselines across every metric on both RealEstate10K and Tanks-and-Temples. Against the strongest warping-based competitor Gen3C, DreamWorld improves Hard Set PSNR by +2.90 dB (20.94 vs. 18.04) on RealEstate10K and +1.64 dB (19.59 vs. 17.95) on Tanks-and-Temples, while reducing FID by ~33% (31.16 vs. 46.67) and halving rotation error (Rdist 0.521 vs. 0.838). Against implicit-embedding methods (CameraCtrl, MotionCtrl), the gains are even larger: DreamWorld achieves 3.79 dB higher PSNR and 2.4× lower FID than CameraCtrl on RealEstate10K Hard. On the WorldScore benchmark, DreamWorld leads by 4.39 points in average score over WonderWorld (75.04 vs. 70.65), with the largest gap in Photo Consistency (92.70 vs. 67.86) and Camera Control (81.97 vs. 92.32, where WonderWorld actually leads). The paper attributes its advantage to the decoupled geometry-then-appearance pipeline, which avoids the task entanglement seen in unified models like FantasyWorld and Voyager.

## Relation to Prior Work

DreamWorld sits at the intersection of two active research threads: camera-controlled video diffusion (CameraCtrl, CamCo, ViewCrafter, Gen3C) and 3D foundation models (Depth Anything 3, VGGT). It departs from prior camera-controlled VDMs in two key ways: rather than injecting low-dimensional camera parameters as implicit conditioning tokens or relying on raw point-cloud warping as pixel-level guidance, it distills high-level geometric features from a pretrained 3D foundation model into a dedicated geometry diffusion branch. It also departs from unified world models (FantasyWorld, Voyager) that jointly optimize geometry and appearance in a single objective, instead proposing a strict geometry-then-appearance decoupling where each stage operates in its own latent space.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The paper addresses a recognized failure mode of current world models—cross-view geometric inconsistency under large viewpoint changes—by introducing an explicit structural scaffold that is generatively completed before appearance synthesis. The decoupled paradigm is a reusable recipe: any future system that needs to separate structural reasoning from photorealistic rendering could adopt this two-stage distillation-from-3D-foundation-models pattern. Key limitations remain: the method targets static scenes, the geometry branch inherits the quality of the underlying 3D foundation model, and extending the approach to dynamic or heavily occluded scenes is left as future work. Whether the distillation paradigm generalizes beyond the specific DA3/VGGT features used here, and whether the decoupling overhead is justified at scale, are open questions.
