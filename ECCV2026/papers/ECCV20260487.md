## Problem

Self-play trajectory fine-tuning, which has proven effective for diffusion models, faces two fundamental incompatibilities with Normalizing Flows: (1) directly penalizing self-generated data contradicts the NF's maximum-likelihood training objective, causing training collapse, and (2) NFs lack the pre-defined noise-to-data trajectories that diffusion dynamics provide, making it unclear which trajectories are 'winning' versus 'losing' for preference alignment. The paper seeks a self-contained mechanism to improve NF generative fidelity without external vision encoders or manually annotated preference sets.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ECCV20260487/cropped_page1_idx1.webp)

## Input / Output

The model operates in latent space: 256×256 RGB images are encoded to 16×16 latents via a MAR-VAE encoder, and generation starts from standard Gaussian noise conditioned on a class label. The NF architecture comprises 51 causal-transformer blocks (5 shallow layers of 2 blocks plus 1 deep layer of 46 blocks) with 1.45B parameters. During self-play, the frozen competitor model performs on-the-fly inference to synthesize candidate images, which are then jointly evaluated with real data by frozen jigsaw heads before the main model is updated.

## Method

JSON introduces a jigsaw puzzle reassembly head (stacked conv layers) at each NF block that predicts the original spatial coordinates of shuffled feature patches, serving as an intrinsic spatial-coherence evaluator. A bounded self-play loss modifies the Bradley-Terry preference to constrain the model's likelihood on synthesized data between the competitor's real-data and synthesized-data likelihoods, preventing the training collapse caused by unconstrained forgetting. The training follows a nested-loop two-stage pipeline: 155 pre-training epochs with joint NF + VAE + jigsaw losses, followed by 5 self-play epochs where the inner loop alternates between bounded self-play (when real data has better jigsaw coherence) and no fine-tuning (when synthesized data is superior), while the outer loop updates the competitor snapshot after each epoch to track the evolving distribution.

## Results

On ImageNet-1K at 256×256, JSON achieves FID 2.10 and IS 306.2 (with CFG=1.1), setting a new state-of-the-art among Normalizing Flow models. Without guidance, it reaches FID 11.68 and IS 118.4. The model uses 1.45B parameters trained for 160 epochs (155 pre-training + 5 self-play), matching SimFlow's training budget while adding only ~50M parameters for jigsaw heads.

## Baselines & Comparisons

Among NF models, JSON clearly outperforms all prior work: it beats SimFlow (FID 2.15, IS 276.8) by 0.05 FID and 29.4 IS, StarFlow (FID 2.40) by 0.30 FID, BiFlow (FID 2.39, IS 303.0) by 0.29 FID, and FARMER (FID 3.60) by 1.50 FID. Against diffusion models, JSON outperforms DiT (FID 2.27) by 0.17 FID but trails REPA-E (FID 1.12) and RAE (FID 1.13) by roughly 1.0 FID, as those methods rely on external pretrained vision encoders for feature alignment. Against autoregressive models, JSON beats MAR (FID 1.55) on IS (306.2 vs. 303.7) but loses to VAR (FID 1.73, IS 350.2) and xAR (FID 1.24) on both FID and IS. In the ablation, the direct self-play baseline (B-NSP) causes training collapse (NaN losses), while bounded self-play alone (without jigsaw) yields FID 2.46/IS 311.64, confirming that the jigsaw-guided trajectory selection is essential for the final FID improvement to 2.10.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the trajectory fine-tuning (TFT) paradigm—well established for diffusion models via feature alignment (REPA) or reinforcement learning (DPO, PPO)—to normalizing flows, where it has been largely unexplored due to NFs' lack of predefined data-noise dynamics. It builds directly on the StarFlow/SimFlow latent NF architecture (causal transformer blocks with MAR-VAE) and adapts the self-play fine-tuning concept from LLMs (SPIN) and diffusion models, but departs from standard self-play by introducing a bounded preference loss to resolve the conflict between MLE and synthesized-data forgetting, and by repurposing jigsaw puzzle reassembly (from unsupervised representation learning) as an intrinsic spatial-coherence evaluator to define winning/losing trajectories.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

JSON provides a self-contained, supervision-free mechanism to improve NF generative fidelity without pretrained vision encoders or human-annotated preference sets, addressing a practical bottleneck in NF research. The bounded self-play formulation and the insight that likelihood alone is insufficient for trajectory-level quality assessment (motivating a complementary spatial signal) are transferable ideas for other invertible generative models. However, the gains are modest (FID 2.15→2.10 over SimFlow), self-play is restricted to only 5 epochs due to computational overhead, and the approach has been validated solely on ImageNet-1K at 256×256, leaving open whether the jigsaw-based trajectory evaluation generalizes to higher resolutions, video generation, or other NF architectures.
