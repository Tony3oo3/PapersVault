## Problem

Medical vision-language models hallucinate spurious findings because language priors override subtle visual cues during decoding, and existing training-free contrastive decoders (e.g., VCD) suppress these priors via globally perturbed views that inadvertently erase the very diagnostic evidence they aim to protect—standard VCD blur flips nearly one in five CheXpert labels on held-out chest radiographs. No prior training-free decoder simultaneously preserves localised lesion evidence, addresses the distinct drivers of existence versus attribute errors, and explicitly constrains counterfactual views to prevent silent representational drift.

## Input / Output

PCCD operates entirely at decoding time on frozen VLM weights, consuming a medical image (CXR or GI endoscopy frame) and a text prompt/question, and producing token-by-token decoded text (yes/no answers, radiology reports, or GI descriptions). Per image, it caches a Grad-CAM attribution mask, the pooled vision-encoder embedding, and a VQGAN latent code with a style/content channel partition; per token, it performs the standard VLM forward plus one auxiliary forward on the admissible counterfactual view, matching VCD's asymptotic cost (~2× greedy wall-clock). The method is composable with training-time alignment and post-hoc detectors without any weight updates.

## Method

PCCD gates contrastive decoding through a dual proximity constraint: a counterfactual view v′ is admitted only if both a global cosine similarity s(v,v′) ≥ τ and an object-masked similarity s_obj(v,v′) ≥ τ_obj (computed on the Grad-CAM-masked region) are satisfied in the VLM's representation space, defining a trust region that preserves diagnostic content. Two complementary branches target heterogeneous error drivers—OA-VCD preserves the attribution-indicated lesion region while applying semantics-preserving context edits (blur, jitter, illumination shift) to the surrounding area to suppress co-occurrence priors, while LD-VCD edits photometric style channels in a frozen VQGAN latent space (ranked by photometric sensitivity × (1 − object necessity)) with structural content channels held fixed to suppress attribute/style biases. An adaptive per-token selector routes each token to the branch with the higher admissible contrastive margin, modulating the contrast weight α_t by measured context reliance ρ_t; when neither branch passes the proximity gate, PCCD reverts to greedy decoding, guaranteeing non-degradation. Under a first-order logit decomposition with stated assumptions (local logit linearity, bounded object drift, stronger context attenuation for spurious tokens), admissible views provably increase the contrastive margin for faithful over spurious tokens to first order.

## Results

On MIMIC-Diff-VQA with CheXagent-3B, PCCD achieves 89.59% GREEN-based AUC, outperforming the strongest prior decoder VASparse (87.82%) by +1.77 pp; on LLaVA-Med-7B the gap widens to +1.55 pp (77.70 vs. 76.15). On the Med-POPE existence probe, PCCD improves F1 by +2.0/+2.0/+2.3 pp over VASparse under Random/Popular/Adversarial regimes, with the largest gain under the most adversarial negatives. On MIMIC-CXR report factuality, PCCD raises RadGraph F1 to 23.1 (+2.2 pp over greedy, +1.3 pp over VASparse) and GREEN to 30.8 (+2.3 pp over greedy). On Gut-VLM GI endoscopy, PCCD reaches 92.70% QAAS (+1.35 pp over VASparse) and 4.19 R-Sim (+0.10 over VASparse).

## Baselines & Comparisons

PCCD outperforms all ten baselines across every benchmark and backbone combination. Against the strongest prior decoder VASparse [27], PCCD gains +1.77 pp AUC on MIMIC-Diff-VQA (CheXagent), +1.80/+1.90 pp on VQA-RAD, +2.3 pp F1 on Med-POPE (Adversarial), +1.35 pp QAAS on Gut-VLM, and +1.3 pp RadGraph F1 on MIMIC-CXR. The original VCD [16] and its variant HALC [4] trail VASparse by 0.8–1.0 pp AUC, confirming that unguarded global perturbation is insufficient for medical images. Attention-guided decoders AGLA [1] and Yang et al. [24] consistently underperform VASparse (by 0.4–1.0 pp AUC), indicating that heuristic attention reweighting without semantic proximity constraints cannot protect subtle diagnostic cues. Post-hoc detectors (VASE [18], RadFlag [26], Semantic Entropy [14], Cross-Checking [6]) score fixed greedy outputs and lag PCCD by 1.8–21 pp AUC, as they cannot alter token selection. Gains are largest on LLaVA-Med-7B versus CheXagent-3B across all benchmarks, consistent with stronger generic language priors in broader-domain backbones being more susceptible to PCCD's contrastive suppression.

## Relation to Prior Work

PCCD extends Visual Contrastive Decoding (VCD) by replacing its single globally-perturbed counterfactual view with a dual-branch, proximity-gated framework. The core contrastive logit combination is inherited directly from VCD, but the paper departs in three structural ways: (1) a dual similarity bound (global + object-masked) that rejects counterfactual views causing representational drift, (2) a split between an object-aware branch (Grad-CAM-guided context perturbation) and a latent-disentangled branch (VQGAN style editing) targeting existence vs. attribute hallucinations separately, and (3) an unconditional greedy fallback when neither branch passes the gate. This positions PCCD as a safety-augmented refinement of the VCD/HALC/VASparse decoder family, explicitly contrasting with attention-reweighting methods (AGLA, Yang et al.) that lack any rejection mechanism.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

Because medical VLMs are routinely deployed frozen for regulatory and privacy reasons, a training-free decoding-time method with a guaranteed non-degradation property (greedy fallback) addresses a real deployment constraint that post-hoc detectors cannot. The 2–3 pp gains are modest but consistent across four backbones, two modalities, and three task types, and the Med-POPE existence probe fills a gap in balanced yes/no hallucination evaluation. Open questions include whether the VQGAN-based style disentanglement generalises beyond CXR and GI to 3D CT or histopathology, whether the first-order margin analysis holds under stronger (non-local) logit nonlinearity, and whether the ~2× per-token overhead remains acceptable in high-throughput clinical pipelines. The proximity thresholds (τ, τ_obj) are tuned on a 50-image subset, raising questions about cross-institutional transferability.
