## Problem

Sparse voxel-based 3D generation pipelines rely on two-stage diffusion with full attention over 20K–60K tokens, making high-resolution (1024) generation prohibitively expensive. Existing efficient alternatives like fixed window attention misalign with 3D object semantics, causing style inconsistencies and degraded geometry. The paper addresses how to accelerate sparse voxel latent generation without sacrificing fidelity by exploiting the geometric structure of objects.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ECCV20260987/cropped_page1_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method takes a single RGB image as input, encoded via DINOv2 embeddings, and produces a 3D mesh at 1024 resolution. Internally, it first generates a coarse 512-resolution mesh through VecSet, voxelizes it into sparse voxels at 128 resolution, then refines per-voxel latent features. Camera parameters are required for Part Cross Attention: ground-truth cameras during training and estimated cameras from the VecSet decoder at inference.

## Method

ULTRA3D replaces the expensive coordinate-prediction stage with a compact VecSet diffusion model that generates a coarse mesh in seconds, which is then voxelized into sparse voxel coordinates. The core contribution is Part Attention, a geometry-aware localized attention mechanism that restricts self-attention to tokens within the same semantic part group (8 parts) and constrains cross-attention so each 3D voxel only interacts with 2D image pixels projected onto its part. The DiT architecture stacks one full-attention block (operating at downsampled resolution for cross-part style alignment) followed by three Part Attention blocks repeatedly, achieving up to 6.7× speedup in latent generation. A scalable part annotation pipeline using PartField converts raw meshes into part-labeled sparse voxels in ~2 seconds per sample, with filtering based on part-ratio imbalance and neighborhood inconsistency to ensure annotation quality.

## Results

ULTRA3D achieves a 6.7× speedup in Part Self Attention and 4.1× in Part Cross Attention over full attention, translating to a 3.1× training speedup and 3.3× inference speedup for the full DiT pipeline. The end-to-end generation time drops from over 15 minutes (full attention baseline) to approximately 4 minutes per sample at 1024 mesh resolution with 128-resolution sparse voxels. The framework supports high-resolution 3D generation at 1024 resolution using a 1.8B-parameter part-aware DiT trained on 1M 3D objects, and the authors report state-of-the-art visual fidelity and user preference in qualitative comparisons.

## Baselines & Comparisons

The paper does not provide a named multi-method benchmark comparison table; instead, its primary quantitative comparison is between full attention and Part Attention (Table 1), showing 6.7× and 4.1× speedups for self- and cross-attention respectively. Qualitatively (Fig. 5), ULTRA3D is compared against unnamed prior sparse-voxel-based methods and consistently produces higher-fidelity geometry with richer surface details that align more closely with the input image. In the attention-mechanism ablation (Fig. 2), Part Attention matches full global attention in quality while 3D window attention (fixed 8-region spatial partitioning) produces visibly degraded geometry and style inconsistencies due to misalignment with semantic boundaries. The VecSet-based first stage reduces coarse layout generation from several minutes to a few seconds compared to the Trellis-style dense-grid DiT approach. Overall, ULTRA3D positions itself as an efficiency improvement over the Trellis [59] pipeline without sacrificing output quality, though direct numerical quality metrics (e.g., FID, user-study scores) are deferred to the supplementary material.

## Relation to Prior Work

ULTRA3D builds directly on the two-stage sparse voxel generation pipeline introduced by Trellis [59] (coordinate prediction followed by per-voxel latent generation via DiT) and the compact VecSet representation from 3DShape2VecSet [68], while adopting the SparConv-VAE from SparC3D [26] for the sparse VAE. The key departure is architectural: rather than using an expensive DiT to predict sparse voxel coordinates in the first stage, the paper substitutes a VecSet-based mesh generator followed by voxelization, and replaces full attention in the second stage with Part Attention, a geometry-aware localized mechanism that restricts computation within semantically coherent part groups. This positions the work as an efficiency-oriented re-architecture of the established sparse voxel generation paradigm rather than a new representation or generation objective.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The 3.3× end-to-end speedup and 6.7× attention speedup make 1024-resolution 3D generation practically feasible, removing a major bottleneck that has constrained prior sparse voxel methods to lower resolutions. The scalable part annotation pipeline (2 seconds per mesh on an A800) addresses a data infrastructure gap that limits part-aware 3D generation more broadly. However, inference latency remains a concern, the sparse voxel token count still grows with resolution, and the evaluation relies on a single private 1M-object dataset, leaving open questions about generalization to diverse domains and whether sparsity-friendly attention can further reduce the near-dense cost that Part Attention still incurs at high resolutions.
