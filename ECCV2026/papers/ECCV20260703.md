## Problem

Existing computational pathology pipelines produce opaque, unjustifiable predictions because they lack the iterative, evidence-driven reasoning trajectory that human pathologists use when dynamically zooming, refocusing, and self-correcting. While CLIP-style models, VLMs, and patch-aggregated LLMs each address part of the WSI analysis challenge, none simultaneously handles sparse diagnostic clue identification, gigapixel-scale navigation, and reasoning across a shifting field of view with traceable intermediate evidence.

## Input / Output

The method takes a gigapixel whole-slide pathology image (decomposed into non-overlapping 512×512 patches at 5× magnification) and a free-form text question as input. It produces a diagnostic prediction with an evidence-grounded rationale, along with a full traceable trajectory of analytic states, actions, and visual findings across iterations. The WSI is examined at adaptively chosen magnification levels, and the output trajectory records which patches were selected, at what magnification, and what morphological descriptions were generated at each step.

## Method

PathAgent is a training-free agent framework coordinating three off-the-shelf components: a CLIP-based Navigator (PLIP) for relevance-scored RoI retrieval, a pathology VLM Perceptor (Patho-R1) for morphological description generation, and an LLM Executor (Qwen3) that orchestrates the analysis through Multi-Step Reasoning. At each iteration, the Executor first estimates an answer from accumulated evidence, then self-reflects on whether the evidence is sufficient, and finally selects one of three actions: exploring new unexamined regions via text-guided Navigator queries, zooming into finer magnification for the current patches, or concluding with a final prediction. The Navigator uses a simplified query strategy where the Executor generates a natural-language instruction describing the missing information, which is then matched against visual embeddings of unexamined patches to retrieve the top-k most relevant regions. The entire process is zero-shot with a maximum iteration budget of 5, and the actual number of iterations is determined dynamically by the Executor's self-reflection, yielding a structured evidence trajectory that makes every diagnostic decision traceable.

## Results

On SlideBench-VQA (BCNB), PathAgent-online achieves 59.31% overall accuracy, surpassing the best baseline SlideChat (54.14%) by 5.17 points. On WSI-VQA, PathAgent-online reaches 60.74% accuracy versus WSI-LLaVA's 54.63%, and on PathVQA it attains 62.23% versus SlideChat's 55.03%. On the patch-level PathMMU benchmark, PathAgent-online achieves 72.54% overall accuracy, exceeding the reported expert performance of 71.82% and the best baseline SlideChat (50.82%) by over 21 points.

## Baselines & Comparisons

PathAgent-online outperforms all nine baselines across every evaluated dataset. On SlideBench-VQA (BCNB), it leads SlideChat (54.14%) and WSI-LLaVA (52.68%) by 5.17 and 6.63 points respectively, with the largest per-task gain on Histological Grading (+9.93 over offline). On WSI-VQA, PathAgent-online (60.74%) surpasses WSI-LLaVA (54.63%) by 6.11 points and WSI-VQA (46.90%) by 13.84 points. On PathVQA, it beats SlideChat (55.03%) by 7.20 points. On the patch-level PathMMU benchmark, PathAgent-online (72.54%) exceeds the expert-level performance (71.82%) and the strongest baseline SlideChat (50.82%) by 21.72 points, with particularly large gains on PubMed (+23.34) and PathCLS (+38.99) subsets. General-purpose VLMs (GPT-4o, Qwen3-VL) and medical VLMs (LLaVA-Med, MedDr, Quilt-LLaVA) trail significantly, confirming that slide-level context and iterative evidence retrieval are critical for WSI analysis.

## Relation to Prior Work

PathAgent carves new ground by orchestrating three off-the-shelf pathology components (PLIP as Navigator, Patho-R1 as Perceptor, Qwen3 as Executor) into a training-free agentic loop, rather than training a unified end-to-end model. It departs from CLIP-style retrieval models (PLIP, CPath-CLIP) that are bound to static templates, from VLMs (Quilt-LLaVA, Patho-R1) constrained by limited receptive fields, and from patch-aggregated LLMs (SlideChat, WSI-LLaVA) that lack traceable intermediate evidence. Among agentic CPath systems (Slideseek, PathFinder, CPathAgent), PathAgent is distinguished by requiring no task-specific training data and by producing a structured state-action-finding trajectory that mirrors the iterative zoom-refocus-self-correct workflow of human pathologists.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

PathAgent demonstrates that a training-free orchestration of existing pathology models can reach or exceed expert-level accuracy (72.54% vs. 71.82% on PathMMU) while producing fully traceable diagnostic reasoning, addressing a key trust barrier in clinical AI deployment. The human collaborative experiment showing that pathologist interventions improve both accuracy and efficiency suggests a practical human-in-the-loop pathway for real-world use. Open questions remain around tasks requiring immunohistochemical evidence (HER2, grading), the Navigator's bottleneck on classification subtasks, and whether the agentic paradigm scales to multi-modal inputs (genomics, clinical notes) or real-time interactive diagnosis.
