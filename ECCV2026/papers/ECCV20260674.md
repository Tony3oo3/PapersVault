## Problem

Standard flow-matching and diffusion objectives regress velocity or score fields without any constraint on the information geometry of the transport trajectory, permitting low-entropy bottlenecks where mass transiently concentrates and depletes semantic modes mid-trajectory even when endpoints match well. The paper asks whether a constraint-level mechanism can certifiably prevent such collapse channels in both deterministic (ODE) and stochastic (SDE) transport models.

## Input / Output

The method takes a base distribution μ_0 (Gaussian noise), a target distribution μ_T, and a reference drift field u* (from a teacher model or closed-form interpolation), and produces a velocity field v together with an induced path (μ_t)_{t∈[0,T]} satisfying the continuity equation and the entropy-rate budget d/dt H(μ_t) ≥ −λ a.e. In practice, the learned velocity field generates samples by integrating the ODE from μ_0 to μ_T, while the entropy-rate diagnostic on a discrete time grid serves as a certificate of mode coverage and a training-time enforcement signal via dual multipliers.

## Method

ECFM formulates generative modeling as a constrained optimization in Wasserstein space: minimize the flow-matching mismatch ½∫‖v − u*‖² dμ_t dt subject to the continuity equation and the entropy-rate budget d/dt H(μ_t) ≥ −λ. The key insight is that the entropy-rate identity d/dt H(μ_t) = E_{μ_t}[∇·v] makes this constraint a direct bound on average compressibility, ruling out the divergence spikes responsible for mode depletion. The KKT system introduces a nonnegative multiplier η(t) that acts as an adaptive anti-collapse pressure, injecting a score-like correction ∇log ρ_t only when the trajectory attempts to dissipate entropy faster than λ. The problem is convex in flux variables, admits a Schrödinger-bridge-equivalent representation with strict convexity in the path law, and in the pure-transport specialization (u*≡0) recovers entropic OT geodesics with Γ-convergence to classical OT as λ→0. Training uses a projected primal-dual augmented-Lagrangian update where entropy-rate violations on a time grid increase nonnegative multipliers while feasible bins incur no dual pressure.

## Results

This is primarily a theoretical paper; the only quantitative empirical result is a controlled toy 8-Gaussian experiment (Fig. 1). ECFM with λ=1.0 preserves all 8/8 target modes and satisfies 96% of entropy-feasible time bins while achieving better final MMD than unconstrained flow matching, whereas smaller λ values are more conservative. The main contributions are ten theorems establishing KKT/Pontryagin optimality, equivalence to Schrödinger bridges, Γ-convergence to classical OT as λ→0, and quantitative mode-coverage/density-floor guarantees with Lipschitz perturbation stability.

## Baselines & Comparisons

The paper does not compare against standard generative-modeling baselines (e.g., DDPM, Flow Matching, Rectified Flow) on benchmark datasets; it is a theoretical contribution with a single illustrative toy experiment. In that 8-Gaussian toy setting, ECFM (λ=1.0) is compared directly against unconstrained flow matching: ECFM preserves all 8/8 modes and 96% of entropy-feasible bins with better final MMD, while unconstrained FM routes mass through a transient low-entropy bottleneck that starves several target modes despite matching endpoints. Theorem 10 further constructs explicit near-optimal unconstrained FM sequences that exhibit singular entropy collapse and mode depletion, demonstrating that the entropy-rate constraint is structurally necessary rather than merely beneficial. No head-to-head comparison with diffusion-based or rectified-flow baselines on image benchmarks is provided.

## Relation to Prior Work

ECFM extends the flow-matching framework (Lipman et al., ICLR 2023) by adding a trajectory-level entropy-rate constraint (d/dt H(μ_t) ≥ −λ) to the standard velocity-regression objective, building on the Benamou–Brenier continuity-equation/flux formulation as its mathematical backbone. The paper draws a sharp distinction from Schrödinger bridges and entropic OT: while those are posed as KL/entropy-penalized interpolations with a fixed stochastic reference and a pre-chosen regularization ε, ECFM retains an arbitrary FM reference drift u* and lets the KKT multiplier adaptively induce an endogenous entropic level ε(λ), activating only when the unconstrained FM trajectory violates the budget. In the pure-transport specialization (u*≡0), ECFM recovers entropic OT geodesics and Γ-converges to classical OT as λ→0, unifying the Benamou–Brenier, Schrödinger-bridge, and entropic-OT literatures under a single constrained variational principle.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The principal contribution is a structural necessity result (Theorem 10): unconstrained flow matching admits near-optimal trajectories with singular entropy bottlenecks and intermediate-time mode depletion, meaning the standard FM objective cannot support certificate-level non-collapse guarantees without additional constraints. ECFM's entropy-rate budget converts this into a verifiable, Lipschitz-stable mode-coverage and density-floor guarantee, offering a principled anti-collapse mechanism compatible with both ODE and SDE generators. However, all empirical validation is confined to a controlled 8-Gaussian toy; whether the entropy-rate constraint meaningfully improves mode coverage or sample diversity on real vision tasks (ImageNet-scale, latent diffusion, rectified flows) remains entirely open, as does the practical cost of enforcing the constraint via primal-dual augmented-Lagrangian updates at scale.
