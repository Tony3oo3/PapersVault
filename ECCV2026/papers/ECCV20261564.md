## Problem

Diffusion-based virtual try-on methods require 30+ sampling steps for high-fidelity results and depend on garment masks plus multiple auxiliary encoders (DensePose, parsing maps), making inference slow and sensitive to preprocessing errors. Mask-free alternatives are further limited by the absence of large-scale paired datasets without mask annotations, leaving a gap in both efficiency and practicality for real-time try-on systems.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ECCV20261564/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

In mask-free mode the method takes only a garment image and a person image (standardized to 1024x768) and produces a photorealistic try-on image in as few as 6 diffusion steps. In mask-based mode a segmentation mask is additionally supplied. Textual try-on instructions (encoded by a T5 encoder) provide compositional guidance, and the OANO sub-module internally consumes the garment-person pair to generate an optimized noise tensor that replaces the standard random initialization.

## Method

FDM-MFVT couples two modules on a frozen Flux1.Fill DiT backbone. The OANO module performs SVD on the random noise x_T, encodes garment-person features, and injects them via learnable cross-attention to produce an outfit-aware optimized noise, trained with MSE on noise pairs constructed by image inversion and quality filtering; this shifts the starting point of the reverse diffusion so that 6 steps suffice where 30 are normally needed. The IDT module encodes garment, person, and mask latents through a VAE and a textual prompt through T5, concatenates all tokens, and processes them through parallel MM-Attention and MLP branches with LoRA adapters that fine-tune only a small parameter subset of the frozen DiT blocks. The authors also construct MFVT, a 30,000-pair mask-free dataset generated by a pretrained try-on model, to enable supervised training without segmentation annotations.

## Results

On the mask-based VTON-HD benchmark at 6 inference steps, FDM-MFVT achieves FID 8.687 and KID 1.201, outperforming the best baseline (ITA-MDT, FID 11.054) by 2.367 FID. On DressCode Upper-body at 6 steps, FDM-MFVT reaches FID 11.231 and KID 1.401, surpassing the second-best (FastFit, FID 21.316) by over 10 FID. In the mask-free setting on MFVT, FDM-MFVT at 6 steps achieves FID 8.316–8.431 and SSIM 0.872–0.887 across garment categories, matching or exceeding Any2AnyTryon's 30-step results (e.g., 0.872 SSIM vs. 0.829 for dresses). The OANO module enables the full pipeline to run in approximately 0.28 s per image.

## Baselines & Comparisons

FDM-MFVT outperforms all eight mask-based baselines on both VTON-HD and DressCode Upper-body across FID and KID at both 6 and 30 inference steps. The largest margins appear on DressCode Upper-body at 6 steps, where FDM-MFVT (FID 11.231) beats ITA-MDT (20.437) by 9.2 FID and OOTDiffusion (37.106) by over 25 FID. On VTON-HD at 6 steps, the gap over the closest competitor ITA-MDT (11.054) is 2.367 FID, and over FastFit (12.316) is 3.629 FID. Notably, FDM-MFVT's 6-step FID on DressCode (11.231) already exceeds FastFit's 30-step FID (11.542), demonstrating that fewer steps with optimized noise initialization can surpass more steps with standard random noise. In the mask-free setting, FDM-MFVT reduces LPIPS from 0.324–0.475 (Any2AnyTryon, 6 steps) to 0.083–0.102 and improves SSIM from 0.769–0.774 to 0.872–0.887, while matching Any2AnyTryon's 30-step FID with only 6 steps. The method also generalizes to in-the-wild StreetVTON images without any in-the-wild training data, maintaining garment fidelity and body consistency.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper sits at the intersection of two active research threads: noise-optimization-based few-step diffusion (building directly on Golden Noise [47]'s observation that SVD components of optimized and random noise share strong similarity) and LoRA-adapted Diffusion Transformers for conditional generation (following the in-context LoRA paradigm of [22]). It departs from the dominant diffusion-based VTON pipeline—which typically chains garment masks, DensePose, and human-parsing encoders—by replacing all auxiliary conditions with a unified visual-textual prompt and a learned noise initializer. The mask-free VTON direction is acknowledged as underdeveloped due to dataset scarcity, and the paper addresses this gap by constructing MFVT from a pretrained mask-based generator, a data-synthesis strategy that has not been systematically applied to VTON at this scale.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The practical impact is significant: reducing inference from 30 to 6 steps (with comparable or better FID) and eliminating mask/auxiliary-encoder dependencies makes diffusion-based try-on viable for near-real-time e-commerce deployment. The 30K-pair MFVT dataset fills a concrete gap that has constrained mask-free VTON research. However, the mask-free evaluation rests on a single retrained baseline (Any2AnyTryon) on the authors' own dataset, and the mask-based comparison reports only FID/KID rather than the full metric suite, leaving open questions about robustness across diverse backbones, garment categories beyond the three tested, and whether the OANO noise-optimization strategy transfers to other diffusion tasks beyond VTON.
