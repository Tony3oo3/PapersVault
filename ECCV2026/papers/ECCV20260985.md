## Problem

MLLMs have demonstrated strong performance on conventional images but their perception of 360° omnidirectional images remains largely unexplored, with the best model achieving only 46.5% accuracy versus 86.3% for humans. The challenges include geometric distortion from planar projections, complex spatial relations on a continuous spherical surface, and the extensive field of view requiring fine-grained feature capture. No comprehensive high-resolution benchmark or training-free method existed to systematically evaluate and address these limitations.

## Input / Output

The method takes a 7K-resolution 360° image (7296×3648 ERP or 7296×5472 CMP) paired with a text question and four answer options. It produces a serialized scene graph in structured text listing entity nodes, view nodes (six cube faces), attribute relations, and spatial relations, followed by a selected answer and reasoning analysis. The framework uses a hybrid projection strategy: CMP for entity detection (reducing distortion) and ERP for spatial reasoning (preserving angular continuity), with a fallback to the raw image if the graph lacks sufficient information.

## Method

Free360 is a training-free scene-graph-based framework that decomposes 360° VQA into four modular steps: (1) Entity Identification on the CMP image to detect question-relevant entities with bounding boxes, (2) Attribute Extraction by cropping each entity region and prompting the MLLM for fine-grained descriptions, (3) Inter-Entity Relation Detection via spherical rotation that centers each entity pair in a rotated ERP image to facilitate spatial reasoning, and (4) Entity-View Relation Detection that maps each entity to one of six cube-face view nodes. The resulting scene graph is serialized into structured text (nodes, attribute relations, spatial relations) and fed to the MLLM with the question for final answer generation. The key insight is that 360°-specific operations—entity-centered spherical rotation and view mapping—provide an explicit reasoning scaffold that compensates for the MLLM's inability to natively process spherical geometry, yielding up to 22.9% improvement on spatial reasoning subtasks without any parameter updates.

## Results

On 360Bench, Free360 achieves the best overall accuracy of 45.3% among all evaluated models, outperforming its base model Qwen2.5-VL-7B (CMP: 38.1%) by +7.3% and the strongest open-source MLLM by a wide margin. The best proprietary model, Gemini Pro 2.5 (CMP), reaches 46.5% overall, still far below the human performance of 86.3%. Free360's largest per-task gain is +22.9% on SR-OV (object-to-viewer spatial reasoning) and +12.5% on PP-IR (projection-distorted instance recognition) relative to Qwen2.5-VL-7B with ERP. Inference time is 22.5 seconds per sample, within the human response range of 28.9 seconds and substantially faster than DC² (617–762 s) and DeepEyes (67–78 s).

## Baselines & Comparisons

Free360 (45.3% overall) outperforms all open-source MLLMs and enhancement methods: it beats Qwen2.5-VL-7B (38.1%) by +7.3%, Omni-CoT (39.5%) by +5.8%, GPT-4o (41.3%) by +4.0%, and ZoomEye (35.3%) by +10.0%. The only model that surpasses Free360 is Gemini Pro 2.5 (46.5%), a proprietary black-box model, by a narrow 1.2% margin. The largest per-task improvements are on spatial reasoning: +22.9% on SR-OV and +12.6% on SR-Os over the base model, confirming that scene-graph-based spherical rotation is most beneficial for viewer-centric spatial tasks. Among enhancement methods, DC² (28.1%) and SEAL (25.7%) are the weakest, with Free360 exceeding them by 17.2% and 19.6% respectively, while also being 27–34× faster in inference. Free360's inference time of 22.5 s is comparable to ZoomEye (19.5–23.9 s) and Omni-CoT (16.6 s), but far below DeepEyes (66.9–78.4 s) and DC² (617.9–761.6 s), making it a practical training-free solution.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the 360° VQA line of work (VQA360, OmniVQA, ODI-Bench) by introducing the first high-resolution (7K) benchmark with manually curated annotations across diverse indoor, outdoor, and aerial scenes, and seven subtasks targeting fine-grained perception, spatial reasoning, and projection robustness. Methodologically, Free360 repurposes the scene-graph generation paradigm from conventional image understanding (GPT4SGG, PRISM-0) into a question-relevant, training-free framework that incorporates 360°-specific operations—entity-centered spherical rotation and CMP view mapping—absent from prior MLLM enhancement methods like ZoomEye, DC², or Omni-CoT. The hybrid use of CMP for detection and ERP for spatial reasoning departs from prior work that evaluates a single projection format in isolation.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The quantified gap between the best MLLM (46.5%) and human performance (86.3%) on 360° perception underscores a fundamental limitation in current multimodal models for holistic spatial understanding, with implications for autonomous driving, assistive robotics, and surveillance. The finding that ERP and CMP have complementary strengths (ERP for spatial reasoning, CMP for projection-distorted perception) provides actionable guidance for practitioners choosing input representations. Open questions include whether the scene-graph decomposition generalizes to 360° video, whether finer perception modules (e.g., patch-wise retrieval) can close the remaining gap, and whether the approach scales to even higher resolutions or more complex multi-step reasoning.
