## Problem

Existing dyadic conversational facial animation methods either rely on direct audio-to-motion mapping (visually reactive but semantically hollow) or use LLMs to reason over sentence-level text (losing prosodic cues and producing coarse, temporally misaligned reactions). No prior framework simultaneously achieves fine-grained semantic understanding of evolving dialogue emotion and precise, audio-synchronized motor generation in a streaming, causal setting.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ECCV20260653/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The system consumes continuous raw audio streams from both interlocutors in a strictly causal, streaming manner: the Ventral module processes 1.5-second mixed audio chunks, while the Dorsal module operates frame-by-frame at 25 FPS conditioned on historical motion and the most recent emotion state. Output is 3D facial motion encoded as 51-dimensional ARKit blendshape coefficients (expressions) plus 3D Euler angles (head pose), generated autoregressively to support unbounded-length inference without the ~10-second fixed-length constraint of prior diffusion-based methods.

## Method

MindFlow decouples generation into a Ventral module (cognitive semantic reasoning) and a Dorsal module (reflexive motion synthesis), mirroring the neuroscience Ventral-Dorsal dual-pathway model. The Ventral module replaces conventional Sentence-Action reasoning with a Chunk-State approach: an MLLM analyzes 1.5s raw audio chunks and maintains a streaming Chain-of-State that appends each predicted emotion state to the expanding context, ensuring temporally consistent, fine-grained emotional tracking without prosodic loss. The Dorsal module is an autoregressive Transformer backbone (6 alternating blocks) with a flow matching head, featuring two key injectors: a Stylistic Temporal Modulator that embeds the Ventral emotion state via masked causal attention, and a Selective Acoustic Injector that temporally interleaves unmixed audio from both speakers and lets the motion query attend adaptively—producing an emergent, unsupervised gating that locks onto self-audio for lip-sync during speech and shifts to partner-audio for reactive expressions during listening. Training uses a two-stage supervised strategy (pre-train on HDTF+VICOX, fine-tune on MEAD+VICO) with a flow matching objective, and inference runs in real time with a 5-step Euler solver.

## Results

On the HDTF talking-state benchmark, MindFlow achieves the best lip-sync quality (SyncD 0.333, SyncC 0.520) and the lowest expression Fréchet Distance (15.76) and pose FD (0.01) among all compared methods. On the VICO listening-state benchmark, it attains the best expression FD (13.86), expression MSE (0.30), and ties for best pose FD (0.03) and pose MSE (0.01). The system operates in real time at 25 FPS with 59 GB VRAM, sustaining over 2-minute sequences without memory growth.

## Baselines & Comparisons

MindFlow outperforms all six baselines across both talking and listening evaluations. Against the closest competitor A2P (Ng et al., CVPR 2024), it improves SyncD from 0.341 to 0.333, FD-Exp from 17.64 to 15.76, and listening MSE-Exp from 0.34 to 0.30, while matching SyncC (0.520 vs 0.519) and tying on pose metrics. Against DualTalk (Peng et al., CVPR 2025), gains are substantial: SyncD drops from 0.467 to 0.333, FD-Exp from 26.06 to 15.76, and listening FD-Exp from 22.27 to 13.86. The talking-head-only baselines EmoTalk (ICCV 2023) and UniTalker (ECCV 2024) lag further behind, with SyncD of 0.429 and 0.480 respectively, and FD-Exp of 23.52 and 29.88. In the listening setting, MindFlow also surpasses RLHG (ECCV 2022) by a wide margin (FD-Exp 13.86 vs 39.02). Qualitatively, the Chunk-State approach produces better-timed reactions than CustomListener's (CVPR 2024) sentence-level Sentence-Action strategy, as confirmed by a 24-evaluator perception study where MindFlow was consistently preferred for both naturalness and semantic fitness.

## Relation to Prior Work

MindFlow builds on the autoregressive flow-matching backbone of StreamingTalker [43] and the generative flow-matching formulation of Lipman et al. [24], but repositions them within a neuroscience-inspired Ventral-Dorsal dual-pathway architecture. It departs from prior Dorsal-only audio-driven methods (A2P, DualTalk) that map acoustic signals directly to motion without semantic grounding, and from Sentence-Action approaches (CustomListener, Social Agent) that rely on LLMs over text transcripts, by introducing a Chunk-State mechanism that reasons over raw audio chunks with a streaming Chain-of-State for temporal consistency. The Selective Acoustic Injector replaces the standard concatenation-based audio fusion used in prior dyadic methods with an interleaved, query-driven attention that learns speaker-selective gating without explicit supervision.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The paper addresses a persistent practical gap in conversational avatar systems: generated listening and talking behaviors that are visually reactive but semantically hollow. The Chunk-State approach and unsupervised speaker-selective attention offer a cleaner conceptual and architectural recipe for combining cognitive-level emotion tracking with reflexive motion generation in real time. However, the framework is limited to audio-only input, ignoring visual cues (gaze, body language) that are critical in face-to-face interaction, and the MLLM-based Ventral module introduces a 1.38 s per-chunk latency that constrains the granularity of semantic guidance. Open questions include whether the dual-pathway decomposition generalizes to richer multimodal settings and whether the flow-matching head's 5-step Euler solver can be further compressed for lower-latency deployment.
