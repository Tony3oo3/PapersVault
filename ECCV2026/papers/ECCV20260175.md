## Problem

Existing affordance prediction methods are confined to pinhole camera models with narrow fields of view, fragmenting the environment and forcing embodied agents to repeatedly reorient to gather sufficient context. No prior work has explored affordance prediction in 360° panoramic imagery, leaving the potential of holistic omnidirectional perception for actionable intelligence entirely unexplored.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ECCV20260175/cropped_page0_idx1.webp)

## Input / Output

The method takes a natively captured 360° equirectangular-projection (ERP) panoramic image at 11904×5952 resolution (from an Insta360-X5 camera) together with a free-form text task description requiring reasoning (e.g., 'spilled juice, what can I use to wipe it up?'). It produces a pixel-level instance segmentation mask of the relevant affordance object, expressed in the original ERP coordinate space. The input images are aggressively downsampled during the routing stage to manage token constraints, and the final mask is re-projected from a local perspective crop back into the full panoramic frame.

## Method

PAP is a training-free, coarse-to-fine pipeline inspired by human foveal vision, operating in three stages. First, Recursive Visual Routing overlays a 4×3 numerical grid on a downsampled panorama and prompts a VLM (Qwen3-VL-32B) to select the grid cell containing the target and generate an explicit object description; for sub-scale targets, the process recursively crops and re-grids with progressively higher relative resolution. Second, Adaptive Gaze projects the selected spherical region onto a tangent plane via a spherical-to-perspective transformation, eliminating ERP geometric distortion, magnifying tiny objects, and reconstructing boundary-split objects as whole. Third, Cascaded Affordance Grounding deploys Rex-Omni (open-vocabulary detector) to localize the named object in the rectified perspective crop, followed by SAM-2-Large for precise instance-level segmentation, with the resulting mask inverse-projected back to ERP space.

## Results

On PAP-12K, PAP achieves a gIoU of 71.56%, cIoU of 62.30%, P@50 of 75.49%, and P@50:95 of 64.97%, outperforming all six baselines across every metric. The margin over the strongest baseline (A4-Agent) is +9.01 gIoU, +12.33 cIoU, +8.40 P@50, and +10.69 P@50:95. On the Hard subset (extreme scale or boundary-discontinuity cases), the advantage widens to +17.60 gIoU and +16.17 cIoU over A4-Agent, confirming that the foveal pipeline specifically addresses panoramic-specific challenges. Inference time is approximately 10 s per sample, comparable to A4-Agent (~11.8 s) and Affordance-R1 (~10.4 s).

## Baselines & Comparisons

PAP decisively outperforms all six baselines on every metric. Against the strongest competitor A4-Agent [49] (gIoU 62.55, cIoU 49.97), PAP gains +9.01 gIoU and +12.33 cIoU, with the gap widening to +17.60 gIoU on the Hard subset where extreme-scale and boundary-split objects dominate. Affordance-R1 [36] (gIoU 51.80) and VisionReasoner [25] (gIoU 49.33) are mid-tier, both roughly 20 points below PAP in gIoU. The perspective-image affordance methods AffordanceVLM [38] (gIoU 9.66) and LISA [19] (gIoU 15.21) collapse on panoramic inputs, confirming the severe domain gap between pinhole and ERP imagery. OV-Seg [22] (gIoU 29.48), a general open-vocabulary segmentation model, also degrades substantially, underscoring that no off-the-shelf 2D foundation model can handle 12K-resolution ERP panoramas without the proposed rectification and routing pipeline.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends affordance prediction from the well-studied pinhole-camera setting to 360° panoramic imagery, a domain where the task had not previously been formulated. Methodologically, it builds directly on the A4-Agent framework [49] for decoupling reasoning from grounding, repurposing its cascaded OVD + SAM pipeline within a new panoramic context. The conceptual departure is the foveal-vision-inspired pipeline (Recursive Visual Routing, Adaptive Gaze) that addresses ERP-specific challenges—geometric distortion, extreme scale variation, and boundary discontinuity—without any panoramic-specific training, relying instead on geometric rectification and progressive zooming over existing foundation models (Qwen3-VL-32B, Rex-Omni, SAM-2).

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

PAP-12K is the first large-scale benchmark explicitly designed for panoramic affordance prediction, and it provides a valuable resource for the embodied-AI community studying holistic scene understanding. The demonstration that perspective-trained affordance methods degrade catastrophically on 360° imagery (e.g., AffordanceVLM dropping to ~5 gIoU on hard cases) highlights a real gap that current embodied systems face when deploying omnidirectional sensors. Key limitations include the dataset's focus on indoor scenes, the single-camera-height variation, and the absence of true panoramic-specific baselines (all comparisons are perspective methods applied out-of-domain). Open questions include whether the training-free geometric-rectification strategy generalizes to multi-step affordance chains, outdoor/industrial environments, and real-time robotic deployment.
