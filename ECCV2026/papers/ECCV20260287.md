## Problem

Existing Video Virtual Try-on methods are locked to the fixed camera trajectory of the input monocular video, preventing users from freely rotating to inspect garments from novel viewpoints. This paper defines Camera-controllable Video Virtual Try-on (CaM-VVT), which requires viewpoint-agnostic texture hallucination and strict structural synchronization between nonrigid human dynamics and background under arbitrary, unconstrained camera movements—a task no prior method addresses end-to-end.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ECCV20260287/cropped_page0_idx1.webp)

## Input / Output

The method consumes a monocular source video (from which dense depth, camera intrinsics/extrinsics, and point clouds are estimated), a single target garment image (used for 2D try-on to produce a reference frame), and a user-specified camera trajectory (e.g., orbit, zoom, tilt). It produces a photorealistic try-on video rendered along the prescribed trajectory, along with an editable 4D Try-on Proxy (a clothed 3DGS avatar in a metric-aligned world space with a background point cloud) that supports downstream edits like human relocalization and bullet-time effects.

## Method

TryOnCrafter comprises two synergistic components. First, a Renderable 4D Try-on Proxy is constructed by: (a) reconstructing the scene via feed-forward MVS and partitioning it into human/background with SAM 2; (b) estimating a temporally consistent SMPL-X sequence via GVHMR and aligning it to world space through a confidence-aware point-to-surface similarity transformation; (c) distilling a 2D try-on result into a canonical 3DGS-based avatar and animating it via Linear Blend Skinning. Second, a Proxy-Anchored Video DiT (built on Wan2.1-I2V-14B) synthesizes the final video using a three-tiered conditioning hierarchy: pixel-aligned rendered priors from the 4D proxy provide structural grounding, a Cross-view Reference Adapter (CRA) with shared K-V and independent Q-O projections recovers fine-grained identity and background details, and CLIP/UmT5 multi-modal semantic tokens ensure global consistency in occluded regions. This explicit 4D geometric anchor decouples the human subject from the environment, enabling physically plausible garment deformations and texture persistence across radical viewpoint transitions that two-stage cascaded pipelines cannot achieve.

## Results

On the ViViD-S test set, TryOnCrafter achieves the best VFID_I^p (9.61) and VFID_R^p (0.18) in paired settings, and VFID_I^u (10.76) and VFID_R^u (0.21) in unpaired settings, outperforming the closest competitor DreamVVT by ~13% on VFID_I^u. On the newly introduced CaM-VVTBench, TryOnCrafter attains an Overall Score of 75.47 with Subject Consistency of 92.71 and Imaging Quality of 74.28, surpassing the best two-stage baseline (TryOnCrafter†+ReCaMaster, 74.32) and the Magic-Tryon+TrajectoryCrafter pipeline (71.64). The method also achieves the lowest LPIPS (0.0567) and highest Aesthetic Quality (51.44) among all compared methods.

## Baselines & Comparisons

TryOnCrafter outperforms all seven VVT baselines on ViViD-S across every VFID metric, with the largest margins over GAN-based methods (StableVITON, OOTDiffusion) and the closest competitor being DreamVVT (VFID_I^u: 10.76 vs. 16.95, a 36% relative improvement). On SSIM, Magic-Tryon (0.884) and CatV2TON (0.873) slightly edge out TryOnCrafter (0.868), but TryOnCrafter achieves the best LPIPS (0.0567 vs. 0.0619 for DreamVVT), indicating superior perceptual quality. On the CaM-VVTBench, the two-stage baselines (Magic-Tryon+TrajectoryCrafter at 71.64, Magic-Tryon+ReCaMaster at 61.92) are clearly outperformed by TryOnCrafter (75.47), with the gap widening on Subject Consistency (92.71 vs. 87.76/87.93) and Imaging Quality (74.28 vs. 68.27/66.69). Even when TryOnCrafter's own VVT output is fed into TrajectoryCrafter or ReCaMaster as a two-stage pipeline (71.95 and 74.32 respectively), the unified end-to-end model still wins, confirming that cascaded error accumulation degrades performance. The ablation in Table 2 further shows that removing the rendered video prior (w/o V_render) drops VFID_I^p from 9.61 to 16.46, validating the 4D proxy as the critical structural anchor.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper sits at the intersection of two established lines: video virtual try-on (ViViD, CatV2TON, Magic-Tryon, DreamVVT) and camera-controllable video generation (TrajectoryCrafter, ReCamMaster, CAT4D). It departs from both by rejecting the natural two-stage cascade (try-on then camera redirection) and instead unifying them in a single DiT conditioned on an explicit Renderable 4D Try-on Proxy—a 3DGS-based SMPL-X avatar metrically aligned to a background point cloud. The key architectural departure from prior camera-control work is the use of a decoupled, parametric human representation (3DGS + LBS) rather than a monolithic dynamic point cloud, providing a continuous geometric anchor that survives radical viewpoint changes.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

By formalizing CaM-VVT and providing CaM-VVTBench, the paper opens a practical path toward interactive, 3D-aware virtual fitting (360° orbital viewing, bullet time, human relocalization) that was previously impossible with trajectory-locked VVT. The 4D-proxy-as-anchor paradigm for grounding video diffusion in explicit geometry is a reusable design pattern beyond try-on. However, the framework's reliance on SMPL-X estimation and iterative DiT denoising limits it to offline use, and extreme viewpoint transitions still expose parallax and pose-estimation errors—real-time interaction and non-parametric human shapes remain open challenges.
