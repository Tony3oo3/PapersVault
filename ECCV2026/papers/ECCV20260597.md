## Problem

Existing 3D semantic feature methods (e.g., PartField, SAMesh, SAMPart3D) produce embeddings that are inconsistent across diverse geometries and highly sensitive to non-canonicalized poses, so the same functional part can occupy drastically different regions of feature space on different objects. This makes cross-instance correspondence transfer unreliable for both 3D understanding tasks like cosegmentation and physical manipulation tasks requiring precise contact geometry.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ECCV20260597/cropped_page1_idx2.webp)

## Input / Output

The method takes two 3D meshes from the same semantic category, extracts PartField triplane features at sampled surface meta-points, and produces a sparse soft correspondence matrix W_AB between meta-points plus aligned dense feature fields at full mesh resolution. For manipulation, a single reference demonstration trajectory is additionally consumed and remapped to novel instances via contact-aware SE(3) alignment. Notably, the cross-attention module never receives absolute geometric coordinates—only semantic tokens—so the output correspondences are topology-agnostic soft assignments rather than rigid point-to-point mappings.

## Method

XSemanticFlow is a hierarchical transformer that explicitly decouples geometric coordinates from correspondence prediction by restricting all attention operations (local aggregation, intra-object self-attention, and inter-object cross-attention) to semantic tokens only, with no positional encoding in the self-attention block. The inter-object cross-attention weights directly yield a sparse meta-to-meta correspondence matrix, and dense features are propagated back to mesh vertices via a final semantic-only attention step. Training follows a three-stage recipe: (1) SE(3) finetuning of the PartField backbone, (2) self-supervised cross-pose alignment on Objaverse using random SE(3) transforms with a spatial correspondence loss and feature loss, and (3) mixed finetuning on Objaverse plus PARTNET-COSEG where cross-instance pairs use canonical-space spatial targets and a Triplet Relative Consistency Loss (symmetric KL divergence on softmax-normalized pairwise feature distances) to align semantic distributions without requiring exact point-to-point ground truth.

## Results

On PARTNET-SE3 (self-segmentation under SE(3) augmentations), XSemanticFlow achieves 76.18% accuracy and 62.47% mIoU, improving over the strongest baseline PartField by +20.63 Acc and +19.06 mIoU. On PARTNET-COSEG (cross-instance cosegmentation), XSemanticFlow reaches 73.75% accuracy and 63.32% mIoU, a +12.52 Acc and +10.89 mIoU gain over PartField. For zero-shot manipulation, XSemanticFlow achieves 80% success on Mug Hanging and 70% on Block Pouring, compared to 10–20% and 40% respectively for the video-based baselines NovaFlow and NovaPlan.

## Baselines & Comparisons

XSemanticFlow decisively outperforms all three 3D segmentation baselines on both evaluation protocols. Against PartField [16] (the backbone it builds upon), it gains +20.63 Acc / +19.06 mIoU on self-segmentation and +12.52 Acc / +10.89 mIoU on cross-instance cosegmentation, confirming that semantic-only cross-attention produces substantially more transform-consistent and cross-instance-aligned features. SAMesh [26] and SAMPart3D [36] trail PartField on both metrics, with XSemanticFlow exceeding them by 20–30 points in accuracy. For zero-shot manipulation, XSemanticFlow ties NovaPlan [7] at 90% on the lenient Water Plant task but pulls ahead sharply on contact-critical tasks: it reaches 70% on Block Pouring (vs. 40% for both NovaFlow [13] and NovaPlan) and 80% on Mug Hanging (vs. 10% for NovaFlow and 20% for NovaPlan), where video-generation quality becomes the bottleneck for the baselines.

## Relation to Prior Work

XSemanticFlow builds directly on PartField's triplane semantic feature fields as its backbone but departs fundamentally from prior 3D part-feature methods (PartField, SAMesh, SAMPart3D) by explicitly decoupling geometric coordinates from the correspondence-prediction mechanism. Where prior approaches learn per-instance embeddings that drift under pose changes and across geometries, this paper restricts cross-attention to purely semantic tokens, forcing correspondences to follow functional semantics rather than spatial patterns. On the manipulation side, it positions itself as an alternative to video-generation-based zero-shot planners (NovaFlow, NovaPlan), replacing brittle synthesized visual plans with direct correspondence-based trajectory remapping from a single reference demonstration.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The paper demonstrates that dense, semantically grounded correspondences can serve as a general-purpose primitive unifying category-level 3D perception (cosegmentation) and physical manipulation (demonstration remapping), reducing both to correspondence lookups in an aligned feature space. Key limitations include restriction to rigid objects (dense correspondences are collapsed into a single SE(3) transform), failure on mismatched label sets and geometric symmetries, and absence of obstacle-aware trajectory optimization. Open questions include whether the semantic-only cross-attention principle extends to non-rigid manipulation, whether the three-stage recipe scales to open-vocabulary or cross-category settings, and whether the manipulation gains hold under more diverse task configurations and object categories beyond PartNet's 24 classes.
