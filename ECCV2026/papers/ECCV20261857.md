## Problem

Text-driven hand-object interaction (HOI) generation remains challenging because contact is spatiotemporally structured—it varies across the object surface and evolves continuously as hands approach, grasp, and release. Prior methods either rely on multi-stage pipelines with static binary contact cues or implicit latent priors, failing to model time-varying, distance-based contact evolution in a unified framework, which leads to conspicuous artifacts like floating and interpenetration.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ECCV20261857/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method takes a text prompt (encoded via CLIP) and a canonical object point cloud (encoded via PointNet into a 1088-dim feature vector, with 1024 surface anchors sampled via farthest-point sampling) as input. It produces a time-aligned sequence of up to 196 frames (GRAB) or 64 frames (ARCTIC) at 30 fps containing: per-frame bimanual MANO parameters (99-dim per hand: 3D translation + 16×6D joint rotations), object state (10-dim: translation, 6D rotation, articulation scalar), and per-hand dynamic contact maps (1024-dim each, storing minimum Euclidean distance from each anchor to the nearest hand mesh vertex).

## Method

JOINTHOI is a single-stage transformer diffusion model that jointly generates HOI motion and dynamic contact maps by concatenating per-frame kinematic and contact dimensions into a unified token (d_y = 99+99+10+2×1024), so self-attention learns contact-motion coupling within a shared latent space. Contact is parameterized as a continuous distance-to-surface field over fixed object-centric anchors, separated per hand to capture asymmetric bimanual dynamics. Training uses clean-sample L2 reconstruction on the joint sequence conditioned on prefix tokens (CLIP text, PointNet object, timestep). At inference, Contact Inner Guidance (CIG) enforces consistency between the explicitly generated contact maps and those analytically derived from the synthesized geometry via a log-ratio L1 energy, backpropagated through differentiable MANO and distance operators to steer the denoising trajectory—reducing penetration and floating without any post-processing or secondary network.

## Results

On ARCTIC, JOINTHOI achieves the best Top-1 accuracy (0.948), Top-3 accuracy (0.983), FID (0.033), interpenetration volume (4.406 cm³), and penetration depth (0.426 cm) among all synthesized methods, approaching ground-truth levels. On GRAB, JOINTHOI delivers the best Top-3 accuracy (0.847), FID (0.031), IV (3.419 cm³), and ID (0.525 cm), while halving the penetration volume compared to Text2HOI (3.419 vs. 7.790 cm³). Inference time is 10.98 s for a 196-frame sequence (17.85 FPS), comparable to the single-stage MDM baseline (10.61 s) and substantially faster than multi-stage methods like DiffH2O (124.44 s).

## Baselines & Comparisons

JOINTHOI outperforms all four baselines on FID and interpenetration metrics across both datasets, with the most dramatic gains over Text2HOI (FID 0.033 vs. 0.148 on ARCTIC; IV 4.406 vs. 8.281 cm³) and MDM (FID 0.033 vs. 0.365 on ARCTIC). On ARCTIC, JOINTHOI achieves the highest Top-1 (0.948) and Top-3 (0.983) accuracy, surpassing Text2HOI (0.816/0.927) by a wide margin and even exceeding the oracle-assisted DiffH2O* (0.636/0.862). On GRAB, Text2HOI retains a slight Top-1 edge (0.711 vs. 0.663), but JOINTHOI leads on Top-3 (0.847 vs. 0.831), FID (0.031 vs. 0.116), and IV (3.419 vs. 7.790 cm³), indicating more reliable semantic capture and far fewer physical violations. DiffH2O and LatentHOI, both multi-stage or latent-prior methods, trail JOINTHOI on nearly every metric, with DiffH2O also being ~11× slower at inference (124.44 s vs. 10.98 s). Contact Ratio values for JOINTHOI (93.71% ARCTIC, 93.00% GRAB) remain close to ground truth (95.56%, 95.02%) while keeping penetration low, whereas Text2HOI inflates CR (97.33%, 98.24%) at the cost of higher interpenetration.

## Relation to Prior Work

JOINTHOI departs from the dominant multi-stage paradigm in text-to-HOI generation (Text2HOI, DiffH2O) by unifying motion and contact synthesis into a single diffusion process, and from latent-prior methods (LatentHOI, HOI-GPT) by making contact an explicit, distance-based, temporally dense signal rather than an implicit code. Conceptually, it repurposes the 'inner modality' idea from video generation (VideoJAM co-generates optical flow; Redi co-generates DINOv2 features) to the HOI domain, treating dynamic contact maps as a co-generated auxiliary target that the transformer learns to couple with kinematics through self-attention. The inference-time Contact Inner Guidance (CIG) draws on classifier-based guidance from diffusion literature but applies it to a differentiable geometry pipeline (MANO + anchor distances) rather than a learned classifier, keeping the mechanism training-free.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

By eliminating multi-stage pipelines and post-refinement, JOINTHOI offers a simpler, faster (≈11 s for 196 frames, comparable to MDM) route to physically plausible bimanual HOI, which is directly relevant to robotics manipulation and AR/VR where contact fidelity is critical. The CIG mechanism provides a reusable, training-free recipe for enforcing geometric consistency at inference time in any joint motion-auxiliary-signal diffusion model. Open questions include generalization beyond the two evaluated datasets (GRAB, ARCTIC) to open-world objects and longer horizons, whether the fixed 1024-anchor discretization scales to highly articulated or deformable objects, and whether the approach extends to single-hand or whole-body interactions without the bimanual L/R separation that drives much of the FID gain.
