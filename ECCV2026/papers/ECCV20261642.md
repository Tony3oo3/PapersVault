## Problem

Single-view 3D clothed human-object interaction (CHOI) generation remains unsolved because existing methods either depend on predefined object templates with poor generalization to in-the-wild scenes, or rely on slow, unreliable layout optimization that fails to capture fine-grained interaction details. Two specific gaps persist: (1) no reliable mechanism to resolve complex human-object spatial relationships from a single image, and (2) no explicit physical contact modeling to prevent object penetration through human clothing.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ECCV20261642/cropped_page1_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method accepts a single RGB image (or a text prompt first rendered to an image via a VLM) and produces a decomposed 3D scene: a clothed human mesh parameterized by SMPL-X (pose θ, shape β, expression ψ) plus N separate object meshes each parameterized by rotation, scale, and translation. Unlike prior methods that output a single coupled mesh, PartCHOI separates entities within the scene, enabling downstream editing such as object replacement or addition. Evaluation renders the scene from four viewpoints (0°, 90°, 180°, 270°) and measures penetration via a signed distance field sampled over object volumes.

## Method

PartCHOI's core insight is to reformulate CHOI as a human-centered scene where each interacting object is treated as a 'part' of the body, enabling a part-aware mutual guidance mechanism that is entirely training-free. The Human-Guided Pose Estimator (H-GPE) uses the SMPL-X mesh surface as a geometric boundary within TRELLIS-generated voxels, applies occlusion-aware ray casting to extract object-specific voxel subsets, and recovers each object's pose via a single closed-form Kabsch SVD alignment—avoiding iterative optimization. The Object-Guided Refinement Module (O-GRM) then deforms the human mesh using a 3D Jacobian deformation field, solving a Poisson equation with a part-aware interaction loss that constrains each triangle to remain in the gap between the SMPL-X surface and the object surface, preventing penetration while encouraging natural clothing deformation. A Pose-Aligned Reconstruction (PAR) stage progressively aligns SMPL-X parameters with TRELLIS voxel structure, transitioning supervision from TRELLIS-rendered references to PSHuman multi-view outputs to maintain appearance-pose consistency.

## Results

PartCHOI achieves the best scores across all metrics in the main comparison (Table 1). In the user study, it receives 69% preference for appearance fidelity, 46.25% for layout consistency, and 61.875% for interaction realism, substantially outperforming the next-best method PSHuman (23.125%, 4.625%, 9.625%). On reconstruction quality, PartCHOI attains PSNR 20.04, SSIM 0.8532, LPIPS 0.0967, and CLIP score 0.2962, all leading the baselines. The GPT-5 automated evaluator selects PartCHOI as best in 92.5% of cases, compared to 3.75% for PSHuman and 2.5% for TRELLIS.

## Baselines & Comparisons

PartCHOI wins on every reported metric against all nine baselines. The largest margins appear in perceptual/interaction metrics: in the user study, PartCHOI's interaction realism score (61.875%) is more than 3× that of TRELLIS (19.375%) and 6× that of PSHuman (9.625%), and its GPT-5 selection rate (92.5%) dwarfs the next-best PSHuman (3.75%). Among human-focused baselines, PSHuman is the closest competitor on reconstruction quality (PSNR 19.47 vs. 20.04; SSIM 0.8420 vs. 0.8532; LPIPS 0.1104 vs. 0.0967), but it lacks interaction modeling, resulting in far lower layout and interaction scores. General-purpose 3D methods (Wonder3D, Unique3D, Ouroboros3D) trail significantly on all metrics, with PSNR values 3–4.5 dB below PartCHOI, reflecting their inability to model human-object spatial relationships. TRELLIS, while capable of coarse interaction modeling, produces tightly coupled structures with high penetration and lacks fine-grained contact, yielding a G-S score of only 2.5%. InterFusion (text-driven HOI) is qualitatively compared in Fig. 6 and is noted to capture spatial relationships but lack fine-grained interaction details relative to PartCHOI.

## Relation to Prior Work

PartCHOI sits at the intersection of three research threads: single-view clothed human generation (PSHuman, MagicMan), part-level 3D reconstruction (PartGen, Part123, OmniPart), and compositional scene generation (ComboVerse, Flash Sculptor, CAST). Its key conceptual departure is reframing human-object interaction as a human-centered part-assembly problem: objects are treated as 'parts' of the body, enabling the use of part-localization strategies (voxel filtering + Kabsch alignment) instead of the iterative layout optimization that plagues compositional methods. The pipeline composes TRELLIS as a spatial/pose backbone, PSHuman for photorealistic human texture, and Neural Jacobian Fields for contact-aware surface deformation, all in a training-free framework that requires no task-specific data.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The training-free, template-free design makes PartCHOI broadly applicable to in-the-wild CHOI scenes without the data scarcity and generalization limits of learned HOI reconstruction. The 3-minute runtime (vs. 65 min for LayoutDreamer) and the part-aware reframing offer a practical path toward editable, multi-entity 3D human scenes for VR, gaming, and embodied AI. However, reliance on SMPL-X as the interaction boundary means clothing geometry is not explicitly modeled, and the method may sacrifice input-image fidelity to enforce non-penetration. Open questions include scalability to many simultaneous objects, dynamic/temporal interactions, and whether the part-aware paradigm extends to non-human-centric scenes.
