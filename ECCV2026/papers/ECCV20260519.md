## Problem

In real-world image restoration, pseudo-labels generated by teacher networks inevitably contain residual artifacts, forcing existing mean-teacher methods into a binary dilemma: unconditionally trusting all PLs causes the student to replicate degradation artifacts, while aggressive NR-IQA filtering discards most data and limits generalization. This paper argues the dilemma stems from treating PL quality as a filtering criterion rather than a learnable signal, and asks whether quality information can be repurposed as a conditional input that lets the student both discount low-quality labels and extrapolate beyond the teacher's quality ceiling.

## Input / Output

The student network receives a degraded RGB image and a continuous quality score (capped at 7, with all scores above 7 merged into a single high-quality bin) as a conditioning signal. The score is encoded via sinusoidal positional encoding (L frequency bands) followed by an MLP to produce a channel-dimension embedding that is additively injected at the encoder-decoder bottleneck of the restoration backbone. At inference, the maximum score of 7 is injected to elicit the highest-quality restoration, while lower scores produce correspondingly lower-quality outputs, enabling continuous quality control. The output is a single restored RGB image at the same spatial resolution as the input.

## Method

QualiTeacher is a plug-and-play framework built on any mean-teacher restoration backbone with three synergistic components. First, a quality-based pseudo-labeling module applies three geometric augmentations (horizontal flip, vertical flip, 90° rotation) to generate a diverse PL quality spectrum, scores each PL with an ensemble of complementary NR-IQA models (MUSIQ-KonIQ, BRISQUE, CLIP-IQA), applies a dual-drop consistency gate, and maintains a memory bank of the top-3 historical PLs. Second, the ensemble IQA score is embedded via sinusoidal encoding + MLP and additively injected into intermediate student features, while a 2×2 block-wise IQA weighting map modulates the pixel-level reconstruction loss to account for spatially non-uniform PL quality. Third, a score-based preference optimization objective (inspired by DPO) enforces monotonic quality separation between high- and low-score outputs through three loss terms—inter-space margin, upward anchoring against the teacher's best PL, and weight regularization—combined with a cropped consistency loss that compares restore-then-crop versus crop-then-restore IQA scores to prevent adversarial over-optimization of the IQA metrics.

## Results

QualiTeacher achieves state-of-the-art NR-IQA scores across all five restoration tasks (desnowing, deraining, dehazing, LLIE, UIE). On desnowing (RealSnow 10K-Test, NAFNet backbone), it reaches 0.675 CNNIQA, 0.559 HyperIQA, and 4.030 Q-Align, surpassing the strongest baseline SnowMaster (0.670/0.501/3.836) and Colabator (0.628/0.470/3.936). On dehazing (RTTS, CORUN backbone), the FADE residual-haze metric drops from 0.824 (Colabator) to 0.590, a 28% reduction. On LLIE (DICM, SemiLL backbone), QualiTeacher leads all eight metrics including 0.626 CNNIQA and 59.372 MFRQA, and on UIE (Sea-thru, AIM-Net) it attains 0.515 CNNIQA and 2.467 URANKER, both new SOTA.

## Baselines & Comparisons

The most direct and consistent comparison is against Colabator [10], the prior SOTA pseudo-labeling framework that uses the same mean-teacher + label-pool architecture but applies binary IQA filtering. QualiTeacher outperforms Colabator on every metric across all five tasks: on desnowing the gap is largest (e.g., +0.089 HyperIQA, +0.094 Q-Align), while on deraining the margin is narrower (+0.004 CNNIQA, +0.002 HyperIQA) because HistoFormer already produces high-quality PLs. Against end-to-end semi-supervised baselines, QualiTeacher (SemiLL backbone) surpasses SemiDDM-Weather [38] by +0.006 CNNIQA and +0.047 Q-Align on desnowing, and by +0.012 CNNIQA and +0.039 ManIQA on LLIE. On dehazing, CORUN+QualiTeacher reduces residual haze (FADE) from 0.824 to 0.590 versus Colabator(CORUN), a 28% improvement, while also leading on all NR-IQA metrics. On underwater enhancement, AIM-Net+QualiTeacher beats both Semi-UIR [24] and SyreaNet [47] on all eight metrics, with the largest gain over SyreaNet being +0.066 CNNIQA and +0.876 URANKER. The only metric where QualiTeacher does not lead is Topiq on deraining (0.508 vs. 0.520 for HistoFormer+Colabator) and LIQE on deraining (2.539 vs. 2.680), suggesting a slight trade-off on that specific backbone–task combination.

## Relation to Prior Work

QualiTeacher extends the mean-teacher (MT) pseudo-labeling paradigm by repurposing the quality of pseudo-labels from a filtering criterion into a continuous conditioning signal injected into the student network. It builds directly on Colabator's label-pool/memory-bank structure but replaces binary keep-or-discard decisions with score-conditioned generation, and adapts DPO's sigmoid preference loss to deterministic regression models by using NR-IQA scores as automated preference signals in place of log-probabilities. The multi-IQA ensemble (MUSIQ, BRISQUE, CLIP-IQA) draws on the NR-IQA literature but is the first to integrate quality scores at every pipeline stage—from curation through conditioning to preference optimization—rather than using them solely as post-hoc evaluation metrics.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The framework establishes that a student model can extrapolate beyond its teacher's quality ceiling by learning a quality-graded restoration manifold, which challenges the implicit assumption in MT frameworks that the student is bounded by the teacher's output range. Its plug-and-play applicability across five restoration tasks (desnowing, deraining, dehazing, LLIE, UIE) and multiple backbones (NAFNet, HistoFormer, CORUN, SemiLL, CIDNet, AIM-Net) suggests broad practical utility for the RWIR community. Open questions include whether the quality manifold remains well-separated and monotonic for highly diverse content, whether the approach transfers to generative (diffusion-based) restoration pipelines, and how sensitive performance is to the specific NR-IQA ensemble chosen, given the identified MUSIQ grid-artifact failure mode.
