## Problem

Existing event-based sign language datasets are small-scale, low-resolution (≤640×480), and limited to indoor scenes, creating a data scarcity bottleneck for event-camera SLT research. Additionally, most prior SLT methods focus almost exclusively on temporal modeling while neglecting the spatial detail features that are critical for distinguishing fine-grained gestures, and RGB-based approaches remain vulnerable to illumination changes, motion blur, and privacy concerns.

## Input / Output

The method consumes raw asynchronous event streams (quadruples of x, y, t, p) captured at 1280×720 by a Prophesee EVK4-HD camera, which are first stacked into fixed-resolution event frames via non-overlapping temporal windows and then resized to 224×224 for network input. Each video is segmented into clips of 8 event frames. The output is a free-form Chinese sentence generated directly from the event video without any intermediate gloss annotation (gloss-free), decoded by a 3-layer mBART Transformer.

## Method

EvSLT extracts per-frame features with ResNet-18, then splits them into fixed-length clips (8 frames) and applies a Mamba-based memory aggregation module: each clip's spatial features are globally pooled into a compressed vector, concatenated with the full spatial tokens, and processed by two bidirectional Vision Mamba layers (hidden dim 512); the compressed vector from clip i is carried forward into clip i+1, enabling progressive inter-clip memory accumulation analogous to sequential human recall. A graph-guided spatiotemporal fusion module then builds a K-NN hypergraph (K=10) over the compressed clip vectors, propagates information through two HGNN layers, and fuses the resulting graph features with temporal features (from two 1D conv blocks) via self-attention, while the full spatial features interact with the graph features through cross-attention (graph as query, spatial as key/value) to keep attention cost low. The fused representation is projected through a sign embedding layer and decoded by a 3-encoder/3-decoder mBART initialized from pre-trained multilingual parameters, trained end-to-end with cross-entropy loss over 200 epochs using SGD with cosine annealing.

## Results

On the newly proposed Event-CSL dataset, EvSLT achieves ROUGE-L 69.80 and BLEU-4 50.76, outperforming all gloss-free baselines including MMSLT (ROUGE-L 68.95, BLEU-4 49.95) and Sign2GPT (ROUGE-L 68.74, BLEU-4 49.23). On the public EvSign dataset, the method achieves ROUGE-L 53.43 and BLEU-4 20.20, surpassing the previous best event-based method MMSLT by +1.29 ROUGE-L and +1.08 BLEU-4. The model also demonstrates strong efficiency, processing each video in approximately 86 ms with 138.2M parameters and 252.0G FLOPs, compared to 1382 ms for MMSLT.

## Baselines & Comparisons

On Event-CSL, EvSLT outperforms all gloss-free baselines: it beats MMSLT [20] by +0.85 ROUGE-L and +0.81 BLEU-4, Sign2GPT [49] by +1.06 ROUGE-L and +1.53 BLEU-4, and SignCL [52] by +2.04 ROUGE-L and +2.24 BLEU-4. However, it still trails the gloss-based method Chen et al. [5] by −5.80 ROUGE-L and −4.92 BLEU-4, indicating that gloss annotations remain beneficial. On EvSign, EvSLT achieves the best overall performance, surpassing MMSLT [20] by +1.29 ROUGE-L and +1.08 BLEU-4, and GFSLT [55] by +1.45 ROUGE-L and +2.32 BLEU-4. Among older event-based baselines (Joint-SLT, VAC+TH, CorrNet+TH, Zhang et al.), the gap is substantially larger, with EvSLT exceeding the best prior event method (Zhang et al. [54]) by +11.00 ROUGE-L and +7.83 BLEU-4. In terms of efficiency, EvSLT is roughly 16× faster per video than MMSLT (86 ms vs. 1382 ms) while using fewer parameters (138.2M vs. 641.9M).

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper sits at the intersection of event-based vision and sign language translation, building directly on Vision Mamba's bidirectional state-space scanning for spatial feature aggregation and on Hypergraph Neural Networks for modeling inter-clip relationships. It departs from the dominant RGB-based SLT literature (Joint-SLT, GFSLT, Sign2GPT, MMSLT) by operating on asynchronous event streams from a Prophesee EVK4-HD camera, and from the sole prior event-based SLT effort (EvSign, ECCV 2024) by providing a substantially larger, higher-resolution dataset and a gloss-free architecture that explicitly models spatial detail rather than relying primarily on temporal convolution. The Mamba-based memory aggregation module repurposes the selective SSM from sequence modeling to compress and propagate clip-level spatial features in a sequential, memory-like fashion, while the hypergraph bridge is borrowed from graph-based relational learning.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

Event-CSL fills a critical data gap: prior event-based SLT datasets were small (≤6,773 videos), low-resolution (≤640×480), and limited to indoor scenes, making robust benchmarking impossible. The dataset's high-definition resolution (1280×720), outdoor coverage, and provision of raw event streams position it as the reference benchmark for event-based SLT going forward. However, the authors acknowledge two significant limitations that constrain the practical impact: resizing to 224×224 negates much of the high-definition advantage, and stacking asynchronous events into fixed temporal windows obscures the very temporal sparsity that makes event cameras attractive. Open questions include whether event-native architectures (operating directly on point clouds or spike streams) can outperform frame-based encoders, and whether the modest Mamba-over-Transformer gains (+1.02% BLEU-4) will hold at larger scale or with longer sequences.
