## Problem

Existing 3D head stylization methods either operate in 2D and fail to maintain multi-view consistency, or rely on GAN-based pipelines that require per-style fine-tuning and produce limited visual fidelity. The core gap is achieving identity-preserving, multi-view consistent stylization from a single style reference without per-style retraining, while also supporting controllable intensity and fine-grained local editing.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ECCV20261378/cropped_page0_idx2.webp)

## Input / Output

The method takes a single content portrait image, one style reference image, and a target camera pose as input, and produces a full 360° set of view-consistent stylized head images. At inference, users can additionally supply binary region masks (generated via SAM 2) for local multi-style editing and a scalar temperature factor τ for continuous stylization intensity control. No per-style optimization or retraining is required; novel styles are applied zero-shot at inference in roughly 3 minutes.

## Method

StyleFusion360 builds on the DiffPortrait360 3D-aware latent diffusion backbone and introduces a Style Fusion Attention mechanism with a two-stage design. In Stage 1 (Content Key Modulation), a cross-attention in the key space discovers soft spatial correspondences between frozen content features and style features, and the resulting signal is applied via AdaIN to modulate the content key, preventing the frozen structural pathway from dominating fusion. In Stage 2 (Shared Cross-Attention), the modulated content key is concatenated with unmodulated style keys and latent keys for a unified attention computation. A separate Style Appearance Module (initialized from the content encoder but trained independently) extracts style-specific representations, while the Content Appearance Module remains frozen to preserve identity. The model is fine-tuned on GAN-generated paired multi-view data (PanoHead stylized for representative domains), yet generalizes to real images at inference because the frozen content pathway was pretrained on high-quality real faces. A temperature scaling factor τ applied to style keys before Softmax provides continuous control over stylization strength.

## Results

On FFHQ, StyleFusion360 achieves the best FID (64.48) and CLIP similarity (0.8237), substantially outperforming the next-best methods (StyleShot: FID 86.23; Identity3DHead: CLIP 0.8209). On RenderMe360, it leads in FID (74.86), CLIP (0.7939), depth consistency ΔD (0.0235), and identity preservation ID (0.45). In the user study on FFHQ, participants preferred StyleFusion360 76.0% of the time versus 14.0% for the next-best method (Identity3DHead). The only metric where a baseline edges ahead is MEt3R multi-view consistency, where DiffusionGAN3D scores slightly lower (9.68 vs. 9.99 on FFHQ; 15.20 vs. 15.55 on RenderMe360).

## Baselines & Comparisons

StyleFusion360 outperforms all ten baselines on FID and CLIP across both datasets, with the largest margins over 2D-then-3D pipelines (IP2P, InstantID, StyleShot) and GAN-based methods (StyleCLIP, StyleGAN-NADA). On identity preservation (ID score, RenderMe360), it leads by a clear margin (0.45 vs. 0.43 for StyleID and 0.42 for InstantID), while on depth consistency (ΔD) it improves over the best GAN baseline (StyleGAN-Fusion, 0.0303) by roughly 22%. The only metric where a competitor is slightly ahead is MEt3R multi-view consistency: DiffusionGAN3D scores 9.68 vs. 9.99 (FFHQ) and 15.20 vs. 15.55 (RenderMe360), a gap the authors attribute to DiffusionGAN3D's explicit triplane 3D representation. In the user study, StyleFusion360 is preferred 76.0% of the time, dwarfing Identity3DHead (14.0%) and IP2P (7.3%), confirming that quantitative gains translate to perceptual quality. Among GAN-based stylization methods, Identity3DHead [3] is the closest competitor on CLIP (0.8209 vs. 0.8237 on FFHQ) but trails significantly on FID (88.26 vs. 64.48) and user preference.

## Relation to Prior Work

StyleFusion360 builds directly on DiffPortrait360, a 3D-aware latent diffusion backbone for 360° portrait synthesis, and repurposes its frozen content pathway as a structural anchor while introducing a dedicated Style Appearance Module and a two-stage Style Fusion Attention (cross-attention + AdaIN) for style injection. It departs from GAN-based 3D head stylization methods (Identity3DHead, StyleGAN-Fusion, DiffusionGAN3D) that require per-style optimization or domain-specific fine-tuning, and from 2D-stylize-then-reconstruct pipelines that corrupt geometry. The paper also explicitly contrasts with StyleAligned, showing that global feature-statistics alignment fails in the 3D-aware multi-view setting, motivating the spatially selective key modulation design.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

By eliminating per-style retraining and offering continuous intensity control plus local multi-style editing, StyleFusion360 makes 3D head stylization practical for interactive creative workflows (avatars, animation, virtual production). A notable architectural insight is that training on GAN-generated paired data with a frozen, real-data-pretrained content encoder still yields photorealistic stylizations at inference, effectively decoupling training-data fidelity from output quality. Open questions include generalization to expression and pose variations (acknowledged as future work), scalability beyond heads to full-body or object stylization, and whether the GAN-data training strategy transfers to other 3D-aware diffusion backbones.
