## Problem

Existing pinhole-camera 3D reconstruction pipelines require thousands of densely captured images to cover multi-room residences, and long incremental alignment chains accumulate severe drift. Panoramic methods that could alleviate this are restricted to single-view depth prediction or at most 2–5 views, leaving house-scale multi-view panoramic reconstruction with sparse captures unsolved.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ECCV20260875/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

CasaMaestro takes 20–50 sparse equirectangular (ERP) indoor panoramas at 448-pixel single-side resolution with no camera extrinsics or calibration parameters as input. It simultaneously outputs per-view metric depth maps and per-view camera poses (3D translation plus unit quaternion rotation), from which a full-house point cloud is obtained by back-projection. Because panoramas have fixed HFOV=360° and VFOV=180°, no FOV regression is needed, simplifying the pose output to a 7-dimensional vector per view.

## Method

The architecture is a single DINOv2 ViT backbone (initialized from Depth Anything 3) that processes an arbitrary number of views by alternating intra-view and cross-view self-attention, implemented purely through token reordering without additional attention layers. A standard DPT head predicts per-view metric depth from multi-scale intermediate features. The key architectural contribution is a lightweight panoramic camera pose decoder that applies a second-stage cross-view attention over the view dimension (with per-view MLP refinement and optional masking for invalid regions) before regressing translation via a linear head and rotation via a normalized quaternion head, explicitly decoupling global geometric alignment from the backbone's dense feature extraction. To address the limited rotational diversity in training data, the paper introduces an ERP data augmentation strategy that samples random yaw-pitch-roll rotations, warps both RGB and depth via equirectangular remapping with horizontal wrap-around, and consistently updates the camera-to-world rotation, generating abundant pose-view pairs while preserving 3D geometry.

## Results

On Realsee-Real, CasaMaestro achieves AUC@30 of 0.903 with a combined pose error of 3.225 (rot. 2.608, trans. 2.132), outperforming the best prior method (PI3-Finetune, AUC@30 = 0.510) by 84%. On Realsee-Synthetic, it reaches AUC@30 of 0.927 with pose error 2.281, a 119% improvement over the best baseline. For zero-shot metric depth on unseen datasets (Replica, PanoSUNCG, HM3D), CasaMaestro achieves an average 21.98% lower AbsRel than the best single-view panoramic depth methods (e.g., 0.058 vs. 0.070 on Replica, 0.042 vs. 0.054 on PanoSUNCG).

## Baselines & Comparisons

CasaMaestro decisively outperforms all ten baselines on both pose and depth metrics. Among panoramic-specific methods, PanoPose [32] and SPR [46] fail catastrophically (AUC@30 of 0.166 and 0.191 on Real), as they were designed for dense video streams with large view overlap and cannot handle sparse house-scale captures. Pinhole 3D foundation models (VGGT [37], PI3 [41], DepthAnything3 [15], InfiniteVGGT [44], StreamVGGT [47]) show moderate pose accuracy when zero-shot but degrade severely on synthetic scenes; even after fine-tuning on Realsee, VGGT and PI3 reach only AUC@30 of 0.411 and 0.510 respectively, far below CasaMaestro's 0.903. For zero-shot metric depth on unseen datasets, CasaMaestro (AbsRel 0.058/0.042/0.105 on Replica/PanoSUNCG/HM3D) surpasses dedicated single-view panoramic methods PanoFormer [29] (0.076/0.054/0.151), DA² [13] (0.070/0.060/0.164), and DAP [16] (0.124/0.130/0.143), despite having no task-specific depth module. The gap is most dramatic on rotation error: CasaMaestro achieves 2.61° mean rotation error versus 21–62° for all baselines, confirming that the cross-view pose decoder is critical for large-displacement panoramic settings.

## Relation to Prior Work

CasaMaestro extends the minimalist feedforward 3D reconstruction paradigm established by Depth Anything 3 (DA3) from pinhole cameras to panoramic inputs, retaining the vanilla DINOv2 backbone and DPT depth head without panoramic-specific architectural modifications. It departs from prior panoramic methods (PanoPose, SPR, PanoSplat3R) that are restricted to single-view depth, 2–5 views, or dense video streams with large overlap, by formulating house-scale multi-view panoramic reconstruction as a single feedforward pass over 20–50 sparse panoramas. The dedicated cross-view pose decoder and ERP rotation augmentation represent new components not present in either the pinhole VFM lineage or the panoramic depth estimation literature.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

By enabling metric house-scale 3D reconstruction from a handful of panoramic captures, CasaMaestro removes the need for LiDAR hardware or thousands of pinhole frames, directly addressing a bottleneck for building sim-to-real environments for embodied AI. The finding that a vanilla DINOv2 backbone without panoramic-specific inductive biases suffices challenges the assumption that wide-FoV inputs require specialized architectures, potentially lowering the barrier for future panoramic/fisheye vision foundation models. Open questions include scalability to multi-floor or outdoor scenes, real-time inference on edge devices, and whether the Realsee3D-centric training limits generalization to architectural styles outside the dataset's distribution.
