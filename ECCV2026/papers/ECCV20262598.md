## Problem

Collaborative 3D semantic occupancy prediction faces a severe perception-communication trade-off: transmitting dense 3D voxel features is impractical under bandwidth constraints, while existing workarounds either project 3D features into 2D (losing geometric detail) or scale the number of transmitted 3D Gaussians (coupling accuracy directly to bandwidth). No prior method achieves both high-fidelity 3D occupancy prediction and minimal communication volume in multi-agent settings.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ECCV20262598/cropped_page1_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

Each agent ingests multi-view RGB images from four surround cameras plus pose metadata, which are lifted into a continuous 3D feature volume over a 100×100×8 voxel grid (0.4 m resolution, 40×40×3.2 m range). The final output is a per-voxel semantic occupancy prediction across C categories, also projected to a BEV 2D semantic map for downstream evaluation. During V2X exchange, agents transmit only discrete code indices (log₂K bits per selected voxel) rather than floating-point feature vectors, reducing per-agent communication to ~0.013 MB.

## Method

VQSOP introduces a Sparse-Aware Vector Quantization (SAVQ) mechanism that first predicts a spatial confidence map and thresholds it to a binary mask, retaining only informative voxels; the surviving features are then quantized via nearest-neighbor lookup into a shared learnable codebook, so only compact integer indices are transmitted. On the receiver side, indices are decoded back to continuous features through the same codebook and spatially fused with the ego agent's local volume. A Dual-Branch Adaptive Spatial Refinement (ASR) module then refines the fused representation: a local 3D-convolution branch recovers fine-grained geometric boundaries, a dilated-convolution context branch captures long-range semantics, and a spatially adaptive softmax weighting dynamically blends the two before a residual connection. The entire pipeline is trained end-to-end with a composite loss combining voxel cross-entropy, scene-class affinity, confidence-map supervision, and codebook reconstruction error, achieving 82× communication reduction while improving mIoU by 4.10% over the strongest collaborative baseline.

## Results

On the Semantic-OPV2V dataset, VQSOP achieves 70.40% IoU and 33.61% mIoU under the single-agent setting, and 73.79% IoU and 41.54% mIoU under the collaborative setting. In the collaborative scenario, VQSOP requires only 0.013 MB of communication volume per agent, which is 60× smaller than CoHFF (0.78 MB) and 82× smaller than GSFusion at full resolution (1.07 MB), while simultaneously delivering the highest accuracy. On BEV segmentation with up to 7 agents, VQSOP reaches 77.48% IoU on Vehicle and 87.04% on Road, outperforming all baselines.

## Baselines & Comparisons

Against CoHFF [26], which uses a tri-perspective view representation and transmits only orthogonal plane features, VQSOP wins decisively: in the collaborative setting, VQSOP improves mIoU from 34.16% to 41.54% (+7.38%) and IoU from 50.46% to 73.79% (+23.33%), while using 60× less bandwidth. Against GSFusion [3], which transmits 3D Gaussians, VQSOP achieves +4.10% mIoU (41.54% vs. 37.44%) and +0.92% IoU in the collaborative setting, while reducing communication volume from 1.07 MB to 0.013 MB (82× reduction); GSFusion's performance degrades when its Gaussian count is reduced (36.02% mIoU at 6,400 Gaussians), whereas VQSOP's accuracy is decoupled from bandwidth. Against GaussianFormer [15] in the single-agent setting, VQSOP gains +4.41% mIoU (33.61% vs. 29.20%) and +2.64% IoU. On BEV segmentation (Table 2), VQSOP outperforms CoBEVT [38] by +17.08% Vehicle IoU and +24.04% Road IoU under the up-to-7-agent setting, and also surpasses GSFusion by +2.18% Vehicle and +2.08% Road IoU in the same setting.

## Relation to Prior Work

VQSOP positions itself at the intersection of collaborative perception and 3D semantic occupancy prediction, a task first formulated by CoHFF [26] using a tri-perspective view representation that inherently loses 3D geometric detail. It departs from both CoHFF's 2D-projection-based compression and GSFusion's 3D Gaussian approach (where accuracy is tightly coupled to the number of transmitted Gaussians) by introducing vector quantization over sparse 3D voxel features, drawing on the VQ-VAE codebook paradigm [13,30] and the spatial-confidence filtering idea from Where2comm [11]. The dual-branch ASR refinement module is architecturally conventional (local conv + dilated conv + adaptive gating) but is applied here specifically to recover fine-grained structure lost during quantization and multi-agent fusion.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The 82× communication reduction while simultaneously achieving SOTA mIoU is practically significant for real-world V2X deployment, where bandwidth is a hard constraint and prior methods forced a direct trade-off between transmitted volume and accuracy. The key conceptual contribution is decoupling perception quality from communication budget: unlike GSFusion, where reducing Gaussians degrades performance, VQSOP's sparsity-aware quantization preserves essential structure at a fixed, minimal code size. However, evaluation is limited to the simulated Semantic-OPV2V benchmark, and open questions remain about robustness on real-world data, sensitivity to codebook size in dynamic scenes, and whether the shared-codebook assumption holds under domain shift between agents.
