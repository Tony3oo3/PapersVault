## Problem

Monocular 3D object detection suffers from severe depth ambiguity, and existing approaches either depend on LiDAR or geometric priors (e.g., inverse height-depth consistency) to compensate, or sacrifice real-time efficiency to achieve competitive accuracy. No prior method simultaneously delivers state-of-the-art 3D detection accuracy and real-time inference using only a single RGB image without auxiliary modalities or hard-coded viewpoint assumptions.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ECCV20260745/cropped_page1_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method takes a single RGB image as input and outputs per-object 3D bounding boxes parameterized by center (x,y,z), dimensions (w,h,l), full SO(3) rotation matrix, and a category label, along with a depth uncertainty estimate. The model family spans five sizes (N through X, 3.8M–~50M params) following the YOLOv10 naming convention, with the largest variant (X) serving as the frozen teacher during distillation. Inference supports TensorRT acceleration, enabling >60 FPS even for the largest model on an NVIDIA RTX 8000.

## Method

LeAD-M3D builds on YOLOv10 extended with 3D detection heads (offset, dimension, orientation, depth, uncertainty) and introduces three components. First, Asymmetric Augmentation Denoising Distillation (A2D2) creates teacher-student asymmetry by feeding the teacher clean images while the student receives MixUp-blended images, then aligns object-specific depth-head features using a loss weighted by both the teacher's relative depth-error quality score and per-channel importance derived from the teacher's regression weights. Second, 3D-aware Consistent Matching (CM3D) augments the standard 2D IoU + classification matching score with Marginalized Generalized IoU (MGIoU) between predicted and ground-truth 3D boxes, yielding more stable supervision especially under MixUp. Third, Confidence-Gated 3D Inference (CGI3D) restricts the expensive 2D/3D regression heads to 3×3 patches around top-k high-confidence locations at inference time, cutting head FLOPs by ~75% with no accuracy loss because the heads' effective receptive field is exactly 3×3.

## Results

On the KITTI test set, LeAD-M3D X achieves 30.76/21.20/18.76 AP_{3D|R40}^{0.7} (Easy/Mod./Hard) for the Car category, surpassing the previous SOTA MonoDiff (30.18/21.02/18.16) while running 3.6× faster. On Waymo validation, LeAD-M3D X reaches 16.46/14.54 AP_{3D}^{0.5} (Level 1/2), exceeding MonoLSS (13.49/13.12) by 2.97 AP on Level 1. On Rope3D (roadside views), LeAD-M3D X attains the best Car AP (16.45) and Rope score (31.34) among ground-plane-free methods. The model family spans 3.8M to 24.9M parameters, with the smallest (N) achieving 14.14 AP_{3D}^{0.7} Hard at 9.7 ms inference.

## Baselines & Comparisons

LeAD-M3D X outperforms all 18 SOTA baselines on the KITTI test set, including LiDAR-assisted methods (MonoTAKD: 27.91/19.43/16.51) and geometry-prior methods (MoGDE: 27.25/17.93/15.80), without any auxiliary modality. Against the closest no-extra-data competitor MonoDiff, LeAD-M3D X gains +0.58/+0.18/+0.60 AP on Easy/Mod./Hard while being 3.6× faster in inference. Among lightweight models (<30M params), LeAD-M3D B (24.9M) surpasses MonoLSS (21.5M) on nearly all KITTI metrics (e.g., 20.17 vs 19.15 AP Mod.) while running 34% faster (13.9 ms vs 20.2 ms). On Rope3D, LeAD-M3D X leads all ground-plane-free methods on Car (16.45 AP vs 12.86 for GroundMix) but trails MonoFlex (13.10 vs 8.71) on the rare Big Vehicle class; however, MonoFlex requires ground-plane input, and in a fair comparison (MonoFlex w/o ground-plane: 10.86 AP), LeAD-M3D X wins by 5.59 AP. On domain generalization (nuScenes→KITTI), LeAD-M3D B (45.50/32.14/28.56) exceeds the specialized DG method MonoGDG (33.48/27.14/26.37) by over 12 AP on Easy, without any DG-specific components.

## Relation to Prior Work

LeAD-M3D builds on YOLOv10 as its real-time backbone and extends it with 3D detection heads following the MonoLSS formulation, placing it in the category of M3D methods that rely solely on 3D box supervision without LiDAR, stereo, or geometric priors. Its key conceptual departure from prior M3D distillation work (MonoDistill, HSRDN, MonoTAKD, etc.) is replacing modality-based teacher-student asymmetry (LiDAR) with augmentation-based asymmetry: the student receives MixUp-noised images while the teacher sees clean ones, framing distillation as a denoising task. The 3D-aware matching (CM3D) extends standard 2D IoU-based assignment with MGIoU, and the confidence-gated inference (CGI3D) is a lightweight sparsification strategy distinct from RoI-Align-based approaches.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

By achieving SOTA monocular 3D detection accuracy on KITTI, Waymo, and Rope3D while running 3.6× faster than the previous best high-accuracy model (MonoDiff), LeAD-M3D makes real-time, LiDAR-free 3D detection practically deployable in autonomous driving and robotics. The model family (N through X) gives practitioners a tunable accuracy-speed tradeoff, and the strong domain generalization (outperforming specialized DG methods without DG-specific components) suggests the learned representations are broadly transferable. Open questions include whether the offline distillation paradigm extends to semi-supervised or self-supervised settings, and whether the MixUp-based asymmetry generalizes to other 3D perception tasks beyond detection.
