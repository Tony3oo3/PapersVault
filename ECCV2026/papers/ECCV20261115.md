## Problem

Camera-controlled video generation faces two fundamental limitations: reprojection-based methods are highly sensitive to errors from inaccurate depth estimation, and trajectory-conditioned methods trained on limited trajectory-video pair datasets inherit distribution-specific biases that degrade generalization. This paper addresses both by eliminating the depth dependency and augmenting training data to cover diverse trajectories and focal lengths.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ECCV20261115/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method takes a source video (F×C×H×W), source and target camera intrinsics (K_s, K_t ∈ R^{3×3}), and a target camera trajectory T ∈ R^{F×3×4} expressed in SE(3) relative to the source video's first-frame pose, along with a text description extracted via LLaVA. It produces a novel-view video V_t at the same resolution (480×832 for 81-frame sequences) that faithfully follows the specified trajectory and target intrinsic configuration. Notably, the source camera poses are assumed unknown at inference time, with only intrinsics estimated (e.g., via UniDepth for real-world videos).

## Method

InfCam builds on the frozen Wan2.1 video diffusion backbone, adding only a camera encoder and homography-guided self-attention layers. The key mechanism is an infinite homography warping module that decomposes reprojection into a noise-free rotation component (H_∞ = K_t R K_s^{-1}) applied to the source latent and a residual parallax term (K_t t/Z) learned end-to-end, thereby removing dependence on external depth estimation. The homography-guided attention layer processes source, target, and warped latents per-frame with camera embeddings, constraining the model's parallax search space between the epipole and the warped point. A data augmentation pipeline (AugMCV) transforms MultiCamVideo by reversing and concatenating trajectories to break initial-frame bias, and by resizing/cropping to simulate focal length changes, enabling the model to learn robust view synthesis across diverse camera motions and intrinsics.

## Results

On the WebVid real-world evaluation, InfCam achieves the lowest rotation error (3.162°) and translation error (0.438) among all methods, substantially outperforming the next-best TrajectoryCrafter (5.007°, 0.735). It also attains the best FID (29.702) and FVD (286.952) for perceptual quality, along with the highest Subject Consistency (0.949). On the AugMCV synthetic test set, InfCam leads in all reconstruction metrics: PSNR 22.677 / SSIM 0.718 / LPIPS 0.246 (shared intrinsics) and PSNR 22.261 / SSIM 0.699 / LPIPS 0.270 (different intrinsics), surpassing the strongest baseline TrajectoryCrafter by 1.45 dB in PSNR and 0.058 in SSIM under shared intrinsics.

## Baselines & Comparisons

InfCam decisively outperforms all three baselines on camera pose accuracy: its rotation error (3.162°) is 37% lower than TrajectoryCrafter (5.007°), 62% lower than ReCamMaster (8.375°), and 67% lower than GEN3C (9.588°); translation error shows a similar pattern (0.438 vs. 0.735, 1.027, 3.012). On perceptual quality (FID/FVD), InfCam edges out TrajectoryCrafter (29.702 vs. 30.877 FID) and substantially beats ReCamMaster and GEN3C. ReCamMaster achieves the highest Motion Smoothness (0.994) but at the cost of the second-worst rotation error, indicating it sacrifices trajectory fidelity for temporal smoothness. GEN3C, despite its 3D cache design, fails to transform the initial frame viewpoint and shows the worst pose errors overall. TrajectoryCrafter is the closest competitor on pose metrics but still lags by ~1.8° in rotation error and exhibits reprojection artifacts from inaccurate depth estimation, which also depresses its Subject Consistency score (0.940 vs. 0.949 for InfCam).

## Relation to Prior Work

InfCam sits at the intersection of two established lines of camera-controlled video generation: depth-reprojection methods (GEN3C, TrajectoryCrafter) that condition on reprojected renders, and trajectory-conditioned methods (ReCamMaster, SynCamMaster) that learn from paired trajectory-video data. Its key conceptual departure is the infinite homography warping module, which decomposes the reprojection equation into a noise-free rotation term (H_∞ = K_t R K_s^{-1}) and a residual parallax term learned end-to-end, thereby eliminating the dependency on external depth estimation that plagues reprojection-based approaches. The method builds on the Wan2.1 video diffusion backbone and the MultiCamVideo dataset, extending them with a trajectory-intrinsic data augmentation pipeline that synthesizes diverse camera paths and focal-length variations from existing multi-view captures.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

By removing the depth-estimation bottleneck, InfCam addresses a practical failure mode that has limited the reliability of reprojection-based camera control in real-world post-production settings, where monocular depth is often inaccurate. The trajectory-intrinsic augmentation strategy is a lightweight, dataset-agnostic recipe that could be applied to other multi-view video datasets to improve generalization. However, the paper acknowledges that the framework still cannot synthesize truly unobserved scene regions (extreme novel views), and the evaluation on real-world WebVid videos relies on estimated intrinsics and proxy metrics (FID/FVD) rather than ground-truth comparisons, leaving open how well the method scales to longer sequences, higher resolutions, and more complex dynamic scenes.
