## Problem

Existing 3D scene generation methods are either constrained to fixed spatial extents, limited to specific data distributions, or rely on hand-crafted procedural rules that sacrifice photorealism. No prior approach simultaneously produces unbounded scenes with high-fidelity geometry, realistic texture, and full controllability across both indoor and outdoor domains, while avoiding the two-stage autoencode-then-generate paradigm that introduces representational bottlenecks.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ECCV20261421/cropped_page1_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method takes Gaussian noise as the initial sample, a vectorized scene layout (polylines for structural boundaries and bounding boxes for object extents) that is voxelized on-the-fly at each resolution level, and discrete scene attributes (e.g., environment type, lighting) encoded as compact embeddings. Output is a raw volumetric tensor of truncated unsigned distance fields (TUDF) with optional sparse color channels, generated at multiple voxel resolutions (e.g., 0.4 m → 0.2 m for outdoor, 0.044 m → 0.022 m for indoor) with a shared truncation threshold τ. For unbounded scenes, the volume is partitioned into overlapping chunks whose local velocity fields are blended via feather-weighted averaging before a global Euler step, so spatial extent is limited only by available compute.

## Method

WorldFlow3D reformulates hierarchical 3D generation as a sequence of independent conditional flow matching transports between progressively richer data distributions (noise → coarse geometry → fine geometry → texture), rather than successive conditional denoising from fresh noise at each level. Each transition is modeled by a 3D UNet with residual blocks, multi-scale self-attention, and FiLM conditioning on timestep and layout summary, trained with a rectified-flow objective directly in raw volumetric TUDF space—no latent autoencoder is required. When finer levels introduce new attributes (e.g., RGB), unknown source dimensions are initialized as Gaussian noise and concatenated, while a spatial upsampling operator with optional additive noise bridges resolution gaps between levels. At inference, overlapping chunks are processed in parallel and their per-chunk velocity fields are combined via a spatially varying feather-weighted average before a single global Euler integration step, eliminating border artifacts and enabling truly unbounded generation. This latent-free, flow-through-distributions design converges in roughly 12 hours per hierarchy level on two H100 GPUs, roughly 2× faster than equivalent latent-space baselines, while producing higher geometric fidelity and distribution coverage.

## Results

On the Waymo outdoor dataset (96 m × 96 m scenes), WorldFlow3D achieves the best coverage (COV 33.00 CD / 32.70 EMD), lowest minimum matching distance (MMD 16.57 CD / 2.81 EMD), and lowest 1-NN accuracy (89.15 CD / 70.15 EMD, where 50% indicates indistinguishable distributions) in unconditional generation, outperforming XCube, LidarDM, and LT3SD. In conditional generation, WorldFlow3D further improves to COV 39.70 and MMD 12.44 (CD). On the 3D-FRONT indoor dataset (2 m × 2 m scenes), WorldFlow3D achieves the best scores across all five metrics, including COV 38.30, MMD 0.039 (CD), JSD 0.164, and FD_C 36.45, surpassing BlockFusion, LT3SD, and WorldGrow.

## Baselines & Comparisons

WorldFlow3D outperforms all five baselines on the majority of metrics across both domains. Against XCube [31] on Waymo, WorldFlow3D improves COV by 5.5 points (33.00 vs 27.50 CD), reduces MMD by 3.18 (16.57 vs 19.75 CD), and lowers FD_C by 65% (74.83 vs 214.08), though XCube retains a marginal JSD advantage (0.480 vs 0.490). Against LidarDM [47], WorldFlow3D dominates in both unconditional and conditional settings, achieving roughly 2× higher COV and 2–3× lower MMD. Against LT3SD [24] (re-trained on both datasets), WorldFlow3D wins on COV, MMD, 1-NNA, and FD_C in both outdoor and indoor evaluations. On 3D-FRONT indoor scenes, WorldFlow3D surpasses BlockFusion [43] and WorldGrow [18] on every metric, with the largest gap in FD_C (36.45 vs 165.46 and 172.58 respectively), indicating substantially better semantic and structural similarity to the training distribution as measured by the Concerto foundation model.

## Relation to Prior Work

WorldFlow3D builds directly on conditional flow matching (Lipman et al., ICLR 2023) and rectified flow (Liu et al., 2022), but departs from the standard noise-to-data formulation by modeling 3D generation as transport through a sequence of intermediate data distributions (coarse geometry → fine geometry → texture) rather than successive conditional denoising. It also breaks from the dominant autoencode-then-generate paradigm used by XCube, LT3SD, BlockFusion, and WorldGrow, operating entirely in raw volumetric TUDF space without a latent autoencoder. The feather-weighted velocity averaging for unbounded chunk-based generation is a new inference-time technique not present in prior outpainting or sequential generation methods.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

Unbounded, controllable 3D world generation is a prerequisite for scalable simulation in autonomous driving and robotics, and this paper demonstrates that a single latent-free flow-matching framework can handle both sparse outdoor driving scenes and dense indoor environments with competitive or superior fidelity. The 2×+ training efficiency gain over latent-based approaches and the elimination of the two-stage autoencoder pipeline lower the barrier to training scene generators on new domains. Open questions include whether the approach scales to animated scenes or radiance fields (mentioned as future work), how it behaves at truly city-scale extents beyond the 96 m × 96 m evaluations, and whether the latent-free advantage persists as voxel resolutions grow beyond what current hardware supports.
