## Problem

Traditional 3D visual grounding methods rely on BERT/CLIP text encoders and are trained from scratch on limited 3D datasets, yielding weak compositional language reasoning and poor generalization to unseen scenes. Recent VLM-based approaches attempt to bridge this gap but use perspective (pinhole) images as the 2D-3D interface, whose narrow field of view fails to capture holistic spatial context needed for relation-heavy queries. The paper asks whether a single image representation can simultaneously preserve scene-wide 3D context and remain directly consumable by pretrained 2D VLMs.

## Input / Output

The method takes a renderable 3D scene (triangle mesh or 3D Gaussian Splatting) and a free-form natural language query, and outputs a single axis-aligned 3D bounding box in metric coordinates. Internally, the scene is rendered as equirectangular panoramic images (RGB, range, and multi-view fused semantic feature maps) at a small set of selected viewpoints; the VLM predicts 2D bounding boxes as sequences of digit tokens normalized to [000, 999], which are then unprojected via depth and fused across views. No manual 2D box annotations are needed—ground-truth 2D boxes are derived automatically from per-pixel instance-ID maps rendered at each camera.

## Method

PanoGrounder is a three-stage pipeline. First, Structure-Aware Camera Placement estimates the floor via RANSAC, scores a regular grid of candidate positions by ray coverage, distance-to-surface, and optional distance-to-trajectory, and greedily selects viewpoints until 90% grid coverage is reached. Second, a multi-modal panoramic VLM (CogVLM-17B with LoRA) processes each panorama: geometric features (range maps encoded by a frozen DINOv2) are injected into mid-level ViT layers and multi-view fused semantic features into later layers, both via ZeroConvolution adapters initialized to zero so the pretrained VLM is undisturbed at initialization. Third, Visibility-Aware 3D Aggregation extracts per-view masks with SAM, unprojects them to 3D point clouds, selects an anchor view by cross-view IoU consistency, and fuses visibility-filtered points into a final axis-aligned box. Training combines token-level cross-entropy with an Earth Mover's Distance loss that penalizes digit predictions by numerical distance (weighted by place value), plus auxiliary geometric QA pairs that encourage the model to exploit the injected depth features.

## Results

On Nr3D (single-dataset training), PanoGrounder achieves 74.6% overall accuracy, outperforming the previous best ViewSRD (69.9%) by +4.7 points; joint training on ScanRefer+ReferIt3D (S+R) further lifts this to 76.1%. On Sr3D, it reaches 79.1% (single) / 79.9% (S+R) overall, and on ScanRefer it attains 61.0% Acc@0.25 (single) / 62.0% (S+R), both state-of-the-art. In a zero-shot setting (no fine-tuning), PanoGrounder with CogVLM-17B scores 53.2% on Nr3D, surpassing VLM-Grounder with GPT-4V (48.0%) and SeeGround with Qwen2-VL-72B (46.1%).

## Baselines & Comparisons

PanoGrounder achieves the best Nr3D overall accuracy (74.6% single, 76.1% S+R), beating the strongest prior single-dataset method ViewSRD (69.9%) by +4.7 points and the mixed-training PQ3D (66.7%) by +9.4 points. On ScanRefer, it leads with 61.0% Acc@0.25 (single) and 62.0% (S+R), edging out VGMamba (60.0%) and LIBA (59.6%) under single-dataset training, with the largest margin on the Multiple subset (55.3% vs. 54.8% VGMamba). On Sr3D, it reaches 79.1% (single) / 79.9% (S+R), surpassing ViewSRD (76.0%) and PQ3D (75.6%/79.7%). In the zero-shot comparison (Table 2), PanoGrounder with CogVLM-17B (53.2%) outperforms VLM-Grounder using GPT-4V (48.0%) and SeeGround using Qwen2-VL-72B (46.1%), demonstrating that the panoramic multi-modal representation compensates for a smaller backbone. On cross-dataset scene generalization (Table 3), PanoGrounder degrades far less than 3D-based baselines: on ARKitScenes it scores 53.5% vs. 3D-VisTA's 32.9% and BUTD-DETR's 36.1%, and on 3RScan it reaches 43.8% vs. 3D-VisTA's 37.7%. For text robustness (Table 4), PanoGrounder matches PQ3D on paraphrased queries but pulls ahead by +15.9 points on the +Aff.-N variant (41.8% vs. 33.3%) where the target noun is removed, highlighting superior relational reasoning.

## Relation to Prior Work

PanoGrounder departs from two established lines in 3D visual grounding. Traditional supervised methods (BUTD-DETR, 3D-VisTA, MCLN) use separate BERT/CLIP text encoders and point-cloud encoders with cross-modal fusion, trained from scratch on limited 3DVG datasets; recent VLM-based methods (SeeGround, VLM-Grounder) inject perspective images into large proprietary VLMs but suffer from narrow field-of-view and computational cost. This paper's key architectural departure is using 360° panoramic renderings—augmented with geometric (range) and semantic (multi-view DINOv2) features injected via ZeroConvolution adapters—into a mid-size open VLM (CogVLM-17B) fine-tuned with LoRA, replacing both the point-cloud encoder and the need for oracle view selection.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The panoramic-as-2D-3D-interface framing offers a practical path to generalizable 3DVG: the method requires only casually captured RGB frames plus an off-the-shelf SfM/NVS pipeline rather than curated point clouds, and demonstrates notably smaller degradation on unseen scenes (ARKitScenes, 3RScan) and text rephrasings compared to fully supervised baselines. Open questions include handling multi-target or absent-target queries, extending to multi-task settings (3D captioning, VQA), and scaling to outdoor or building-level environments where floor-grid camera placement breaks down. The reliance on a 17B VLM and multi-view rendering also makes inference slower than feed-forward 3D methods, a trade-off the authors acknowledge.
