## Problem

Existing real-world super-resolution methods assume generic degradation pipelines and ignore the compound distortions unique to omnidirectional imaging, where fisheye capture, stitching, and Equirectangular Projection introduce projection-specific geometric warping that entangles with standard degradations (blur, noise, compression). Prior ODI-SR methods address latitude-dependent distortion but do not model the full spectrum of real-world degradations encountered across the practical ODI pipeline, limiting restoration fidelity and generalization.

## Input / Output

The method takes a low-quality ERP omnidirectional image (HR reference at 1024×2048; LQ inputs at ×4, ×8, or ×16 downscale) and produces a high-resolution ERP omnidirectional image. During processing, the ERP image is partitioned into patches that are simultaneously fed to an ERP branch and converted into perspective patches via the PPR mapping, so the network operates on both projection domains in parallel. Training uses 256×256 random crops with flip augmentation and an L1 reconstruction loss, with no explicit degradation annotations required.

## Method

D²R²OSR employs a dual-branch architecture where an ERP branch (EAMBs) compensates for latitude-dependent geometric distortions and a PPR branch (PAMBs) handles viewpoint-centric real-world degradations. The key PPR conversion avoids lossy spherical resampling by formulating a continuous ERP→PPR coordinate mapping via first-order Jacobian and second-order Hessian terms, then learning a Fourier-domain implicit neural representation conditioned on these spatial priors to predict PPR pixel values. Each branch embeds a Degradation-Specific Module that generates learnable degradation descriptors via adaptive pooling and injects them as prompts into the value branch of windowed multi-head self-attention, enabling degradation-aware modulation without extra attention heads. A Projection Fusion Attention Module performs adaptive spatial-channel gated fusion between branches at every stage, followed by depth-wise attention across stages to capture inter-stage dependencies. The entire network (6 EAMB-PAMB pairs, 3.5M parameters) is trained end-to-end with supervised L1 loss on a mixed fisheye-ERP degradation model.

## Results

D²R²OSR achieves the best WS-PSNR and WS-SSIM across all three evaluation datasets (Flickr360, ODI-SR, SUN360) at every scaling factor (×4, ×8, ×16). On Flickr360 ×4, it reaches 25.64 dB WS-PSNR and 0.6934 WS-SSIM, surpassing the second-best method AdaIR (25.49/0.6912) by 0.15 dB. The largest margin is 0.28 dB on Flickr360 ×8 (23.24 vs. 22.96 WS-PSNR), and even under the extreme ×16 setting the method maintains clear advantages on all datasets. With only 3.50M parameters and 0.55T FLOPs, it is approximately 4× faster than OSRT while delivering superior restoration quality.

## Baselines & Comparisons

D²R²OSR outperforms all seven baselines on every dataset and scaling factor. The closest competitor is AdaIR [10], which D²R²OSR surpasses by 0.15 dB WS-PSNR on Flickr360 ×4 (25.64 vs. 25.49) and by the largest margin of 0.28 dB on Flickr360 ×8 (23.24 vs. 22.96). Among ODI-specific methods, OSRT [41] and BPOSR [32] are the strongest baselines, but D²R²OSR leads them by 0.23 and 0.30 dB respectively on Flickr360 ×4, indicating that explicit real-world degradation modeling beyond geometric distortion correction yields meaningful gains. General-purpose restoration methods (SwinIR, HAT, PromptIR) trail the ODI-specific approaches by roughly 0.2–0.4 dB, confirming that projection-aware design remains important. Real-ESRGAN [34], despite its strong real-world degradation pipeline, lags by over 1.3 dB on Flickr360 ×4, highlighting the additional challenge of ERP geometric distortion. All compared methods were retrained under a unified real-world ODI degradation protocol to ensure fair architectural comparison.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper sits at the intersection of two established lines of work: real-world image super-resolution (Real-ESRGAN, DASR, AdaIR) that handles blind pixel-level degradations, and omnidirectional image super-resolution (OSRT, BPOSR, FATO) that addresses ERP projection distortions. It departs from both by arguing that neither family is sufficient alone—Real-SR ignores the coupled fisheye-to-ERP imaging pipeline, while ODI-SR assumes idealized bicubic degradation. The geometric warping formulation (Jacobian/Hessian-based coordinate mapping) is directly inherited from LTEW and ResVR, and the core feature backbone reuses HAT's HAB block, but the key architectural novelty is the Perspective Projection Representation (PPR) that creates a viewpoint-centric parallel branch to explicitly disentangle projection geometry from real-world degradation.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The paper addresses a practical gap: real-world ODI pipelines (VR/AR content delivery) suffer from mixed degradations that no existing method handles jointly, and the PPR concept provides a clean, reusable mechanism for decoupling projection-specific distortion from pixel-level degradation in any spherical-to-planar setting. The efficiency profile (3.5M params, 0.34 s/image) makes it deployable on resource-constrained devices, which is critical for immersive applications. Open questions include whether the degradation model generalizes to video ODIs, whether PPR extends naturally to other projection schemes (cubemap, polyhedron), and whether the modest SOTA margins (~0.2 dB) would widen under truly diverse real-world capture conditions beyond the simulated pipeline.
