## Problem

Temporal Forgery Localization (TFL) requires identifying manipulated segments within untrimmed audio-visual sequences, but existing architectures face a fundamental trade-off: CNNs lack global receptive fields, Transformers incur quadratic O(T²) complexity, and linear models like Mamba struggle to simultaneously compress global authentic context and remain sensitive to abrupt, instantaneous forgery boundaries. The paper identifies that TFL uniquely demands both 'global smooth compression' and 'local abrupt perception,' a balance that conventional linear sequence models do not naturally support.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ECCV20261480/cropped_page1_idx1.webp)

## Input / Output

The method consumes pre-extracted visual features (TSN) and audio features (BYOL-A) from untrimmed sequences, fused and projected into a feature sequence X ∈ R^{T×D} with T ≈ 1500 frames and embedding dimension C=256. Following an anchor-free detection paradigm, it produces dense per-frame classification scores P ∈ R^{T×Nc} and boundary offsets O ∈ R^{T×2}, which are post-processed via SoftNMS into top-100 temporal segment proposals (start, end, score). The framework handles both visual-only and visual+audio configurations across multi-modal deepfakes, video inpainting, and audio cloning scenarios.

## Method

MG-RWKV replaces quadratic self-attention with a Bidirectional RWKV-7 architecture that performs independent forward and backward recurrent scans with data-dependent decay and in-context state modulation, achieving O(T) complexity while capturing full temporal context. A Multi-Granularity Mixture of Experts (MG-MoE) module constructs K experts as BiRWKV branches with explicit dilation rates {1,2,4}, each encoding a physically interpretable temporal receptive field; a lightweight router using channel-wise mean and max pooling with Top-K=2 sparse gating adaptively selects granularities per position based on local content. A Cross-Granularity Consistency (CGC) loss enforces cosine similarity between adjacent FPN scale features exclusively in authentic regions, with boundary-aware weighting that relaxes constraints near segment transitions and a progressive warmup schedule to prevent premature multi-scale collapse. Training combines Focal Loss, DIoU Loss, an auxiliary reconstruction objective, and the CGC term under AdamW with cosine annealing over 45 epochs.

## Results

MG-RWKV achieves state-of-the-art performance across all three benchmarks. On Lav-DF (Visual+Audio), it reaches 98.92% AP@0.5, 94.81% AP@0.75, and 38.47% AP@0.95 with 93.41% AR@100. On TVIL (Visual), it attains 91.22% AP@0.5, 87.44% AP@0.75, and 71.31% AP@0.95 with 92.24% AR@100. On Psynd (Audio), it achieves 100.00% AP@0.5, 98.38% AP@0.75, and 90.09% AP@0.95 with 98.61% AR@100. The full model runs at 73.4 ms inference with 274 MB memory on Lav-DF, maintaining O(T) linear complexity.

## Baselines & Comparisons

MG-RWKV outperforms the strongest baseline UMMAFormer [51] on all three datasets and all precision thresholds. On TVIL, the gains are most pronounced: +8.88% AP@0.95 (71.31 vs. 62.43), +2.74% AP@0.75, and +2.54% AP@0.5, with AR@100 also improving from 91.16 to 92.24. On Psynd, MG-RWKV improves AP@0.95 by 10.22% (90.09 vs. 79.87) while matching UMMAFormer's perfect AP@0.5. On Lav-DF (Visual+Audio), MG-RWKV edges out UMMAFormer by 0.86% AP@0.95 (38.47 vs. 37.61) and 0.93% AR@100 (93.41 vs. 92.48). Against other strong baselines, MG-RWKV surpasses MFMS [52] by 10.67% AP@0.95 on Lav-DF and ActionFormer [46] by 43.14% AP@0.95 on TVIL. The only metric where MG-RWKV trails is AP@0.75 on Psynd (98.38 vs. UMMAFormer's 100.00), a negligible gap given the near-saturated performance.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper builds on RWKV-7's data-dependent recurrent state evolution and extends it bidirectionally with dilation-based multi-granularity branches, departing from the Transformer-based TFL paradigm established by UMMAFormer and ActionFormer. It also draws on the anchor-free dense prediction formulation from UMMAFormer and the FPN-based multi-scale fusion common in temporal action detection, but repurposes these for forgery-specific needs: explicit temporal receptive fields as interpretable MoE experts and a cross-granularity consistency loss to suppress false positives in authentic regions. The key conceptual departure from generic linear models (Mamba, S4) is the argument that RWKV's input-dependent decay and in-context state modulation are specifically better suited to detecting locally-concentrated, abrupt forgery boundaries rather than smooth global patterns.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The O(T) complexity is practically important for processing long untrimmed video sequences where Transformer-based TFL methods become prohibitive, and the interpretability of the MoE routing (which granularity is selected at which position) has value for forensic and legal contexts where detection decisions must be explainable. However, the method still relies on frozen pre-trained TSN/BYOL-A features, so the actual video understanding is limited to what those extractors capture, and the evaluation is confined to three relatively small datasets with sequences up to ~1500 frames. Open questions include whether the multi-granularity recurrent design generalizes to other temporal anomaly detection tasks, how it scales to much longer sequences (minutes to hours), and whether the CGC constraint remains effective when forgery boundaries are highly irregular or overlapping.
