## Problem

Existing Path-Specific Counterfactual Fairness (PSCF) methods require estimating marginal potential outcome probabilities, which demands high-dimensional conditional density estimation and becomes infeasible for modalities like medical images due to the curse of dimensionality. This renders principled causal fairness guarantees practically unattainable in the very settings (radiology, genomics) where bias is most consequential.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ECCV20260905/cropped_page3_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method consumes high-dimensional inputs—chest X-ray images (MIMIC-CXR, CheXpert) or gene expression profiles (TCGA-LUAD)—along with a binary sensitive attribute (gender or age) and a ground-truth diagnosis label. The raw input X is first perturbed by a learned generator to produce a masked version X̃ = X ⊕ g(X), which is then embedded by a fixed encoder f_θ; the sensitive attribute S and label Y jointly condition the discriminator. The final output is a standard disease classification label Ŷ produced by a lightweight head f_h on the embedding.

## Method

SPARC's core insight is that PSCF (zero direct causal effect of S on Ŷ) is guaranteed by the causal conditional independence I(S; X | Y) = 0, replacing intractable counterfactual density estimation with a discriminative objective. Training proceeds in two stages: (1) a JSD-MI pretraining phase maximizes I(X̃; E) to preserve task-relevant information along the legitimate indirect path S→Y→X→Ŷ, and (2) an adversarial phase where a generator g learns masks that remove S-information from X̃ given Y, while a conditioned discriminator d_Y attempts to recover S from (X̃, Y). The generator loss combines mutual-information removal, an entropy regularizer to prevent trivial attribute flipping, and a task-accuracy term weighted by β, yielding a min-max objective that is directly monitorable via Equalized Odds without explicit counterfactual computation.

## Results

On MIMIC-CXR, SPARC achieves 84.26% accuracy and 62.10% AUC while reducing Equalized Odds (EOd) to 2.25×10⁻³ (vs. 10.59 for Vanilla) and Equalized Opportunity (EOp) to 2.02×10⁻² (vs. 5.10). On CheXpert, SPARC reaches 85.80% accuracy with EOd of 1.20×10⁻³ and EOp of 0.98×10⁻², the best fairness scores among all methods. On TCGA-LUAD, SPARC attains 97.85% accuracy with EOd of 1.46×10⁻³, roughly 5× lower than Vanilla (7.21×10⁻³), while maintaining the second-highest accuracy overall.

## Baselines & Comparisons

SPARC dominates all five baselines on the primary fairness metric EOd across every dataset: 2.25 vs. 4.40 (Group-DRO) on MIMIC-CXR, 1.20 vs. 3.07 on CheXpert, and 1.46 vs. 3.12 on TCGA-LUAD. Against the closest fairness competitor Group-DRO, SPARC also achieves higher accuracy on all three datasets (e.g., 84.26% vs. 83.20% on MIMIC-CXR; 97.85% vs. 96.65% on TCGA), indicating a superior accuracy–fairness trade-off. Compared to the adversarial baselines AD and FAAP, SPARC improves both accuracy and fairness simultaneously (e.g., on CheXpert: 85.80% ACC / 1.20 EOd vs. AD's 84.45% / 6.31 and FAAP's 83.50% / 4.84). SUBG, a data-balancing method, is outperformed on all fairness metrics while SPARC maintains comparable or better accuracy. The only metric where SPARC does not lead is raw accuracy on MIMIC-CXR, where Vanilla (85.46%) edges out SPARC (84.26%) by ~1.2 points, reflecting the small cost of enforcing the causal independence constraint.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper builds directly on Path-Specific Counterfactual Fairness (PSCF) by Chiappa et al. and the broader counterfactual fairness framework of Kusner et al., but departs from prior PSCF methods that required estimating marginal potential outcome probabilities via high-dimensional conditional density estimation. The key conceptual move is reducing the PSCF objective to a causal conditional independence constraint I(S;X|Y)=0, which is enforced through an adversarial generator-discriminator loop rather than explicit counterfactual computation. The JSD-MI pretraining stage draws on mutual information estimation literature (Hjelm et al., Nowozin et al.) to preserve task-relevant information along the indirect causal path, while the adversarial masking mechanism is conceptually related to adversarial debiasing (Zhang et al.) but targets the input modality rather than model representations.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

By replacing intractable counterfactual density estimation with a discriminative adversarial objective, SPARC makes PSCF practically applicable to high-dimensional modalities like chest X-rays and gene expression data, where prior causal fairness methods were infeasible. The theoretical bridge showing that the conditional independence objective is directly observable through Equalized Odds provides a clean monitoring signal for practitioners. However, the framework assumes a fixed, known causal graph with binary sensitive attributes and a simplified SCM without exogenous variables; extending to richer attribute structures, multi-task settings, and more complex causal topologies remains open. The empirical gains, while consistent across datasets and architectures, are moderate relative to strong baselines like Group-DRO on some metrics, suggesting the method's primary value is theoretical tractability rather than dramatic performance leaps.
