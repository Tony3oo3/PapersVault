## Problem

Existing monocular 3D tracking methods are either restricted to sparse points on the first frame (STV2, DELTA, St4RTrack) or rely on slow optimization-based pipelines that fuse multiple modalities (TrackingWorld), making dense, all-pixel 3D tracking in a world-centric coordinate system intractable. No feedforward architecture can efficiently estimate 3D trajectories for every pixel across all frames of a video in a single forward pass.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ECCV20260864/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method takes a monocular video of T frames and processes the entire sequence simultaneously through a ViT backbone to extract camera-centric point clouds, camera poses, and geometric features. The scene flow decoder operates at 1/8 resolution on sparse anchor points (sparse-to-dense), estimating pairwise 2D-3D flows between arbitrary (not just adjacent) frame pairs, which are then chained to produce continuous 3D trajectories for every pixel in world-centric coordinates. A hybrid unprojection step combines the high-precision 2D flow with the Z-axis displacement from 3D scene flow to produce the final metric 3D flow.

## Method

Track4World builds on a VGGT-style ViT backbone (initialized from DA3 or Pi3) for global geometry, then introduces a novel 2D-to-3D correlation module in the scene flow decoder that replaces expensive 3D k-NN spatial correlation with image-plane correlation. In each iterative update, a RAFT-style GRU first refines the 2D flow using both geometric and semantic feature correlation volumes, then the 2D displacement is lifted to 3D by interpolating target-frame point maps, and a 3D flow head fuses the lifted target samples with source context features and a RAFT-style 3D geometric correlation volume to predict the 3D flow update. This 2D-anchored design enables joint 2D-3D supervision, allowing the model to leverage abundant 2D flow datasets to guide 3D flow learning and circumvent the scarcity of 3D ground truth. The sparse-to-dense formulation (iterating at 1/8 resolution, then convex upsampling) keeps computation at O(N) rather than O(N log N) or O(N²), enabling dense all-pixel tracking without OOM failures that plague 3D-correlation-based methods.

## Results

On 3D tracking (APD, higher is better), Track4World achieves an average of 0.5712 (L-16) and 0.5469 (L-50) across PointOdyssey, ADT, PStudio, and DriveTrack in camera coordinates, outperforming the next-best ZeroMSF (0.4782/0.4390) and STV2 (0.2400/0.2383). For scene flow on Kubric-3D (short-range), it achieves EPE3D of 0.1537 and EPE2D of 1.8685, substantially beating ZeroMSF (EPE3D 0.3528) and RAFT (EPE2D 6.7974). On 2D tracking, the method is competitive with the dedicated dense tracker AllTracker (AJ 59.1 vs 59.2 on Kinetics) while achieving higher occlusion accuracy (OA 90.6 vs 80.4). In efficiency, Track4World runs dense tracking on 16-frame sequences in 3.4 s with 14 GB memory, whereas STV2 and a 3D-correlation variant of Track4World both OOM under the same dense setting.

## Baselines & Comparisons

Track4World consistently outperforms all 3D tracking baselines across every dataset and both coordinate systems. In camera coordinates, it leads ZeroMSF by ~9 APD points on average (0.5712 vs 0.4782, L-16) and STV2 by ~33 points (0.5712 vs 0.2400); the gap is largest on ADT (0.6501 vs 0.5382 for ZeroMSF). In world coordinates, it beats the strongest concurrent methods V-DPM (0.5636 vs 0.5142) and Any4D (0.5636 vs 0.5043) on average. For scene flow, Track4World achieves EPE3D of 0.1537 on Kubric-3D short-range, roughly 2.3× better than ZeroMSF (0.3528) and 4× better than RAFT-3D (0.6170), while its EPE2D of 1.8685 is ~3.6× lower than RAFT's 6.7974. On 2D tracking, the method is essentially tied with AllTracker on Kinetics AJ (59.1 vs 59.2) but surpasses it on occlusion accuracy (OA 90.6 vs 80.4) and RoboTAP OA (93.3 vs 92.9), confirming that joint 3D supervision does not degrade 2D performance. For geometry and camera pose, Track4World is competitive with DA3 (Abs Rel 0.0552 vs 0.0743 average) and achieves the best Sintel ATE (0.119) among all compared methods, slightly edging out DA3 (0.124) and STV2 (0.133).

## Relation to Prior Work

Track4World sits at the intersection of two research threads: feedforward 3D geometry reconstruction (VGGT, Pi3, DA3) and iterative correlation-based motion estimation (RAFT, STV2). It departs from prior 3D trackers (STV2, DELTA, TAPIP3D) that rely on expensive k-nearest-neighbor searches in 3D space—limiting them to sparse points—by replacing explicit 3D spatial correlation with a 2D-to-3D lifting scheme anchored on image-plane correlations. It also extends pairwise scene flow methods (POMATO, ZeroMSF) from implicit regression to a dense, iterative, feedforward formulation, and introduces joint 2D-3D supervision to mitigate the scarcity of 3D ground-truth data.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

This is the first feedforward model that achieves dense 3D tracking of every pixel in a world-centric coordinate system, a capability that was previously restricted to sparse points or expensive optimization pipelines. The 2D-to-3D correlation mechanism is a reusable architectural pattern that could benefit other dense 3D motion tasks, and the joint 2D-3D supervision strategy offers a practical recipe for training 3D models when 3D annotations are scarce. Open questions include scalability to very long video sequences (beyond the 16–50 frame windows evaluated), real-time deployment on edge hardware, and whether the world-centric formulation generalizes to multi-view or egocentric settings with significant camera motion.
