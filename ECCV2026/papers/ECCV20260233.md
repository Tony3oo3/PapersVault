## Problem

Existing block-pruning methods for video diffusion models compute importance scores averaged across all denoising steps, which can obscure stage-critical blocks and bias pruning toward removing blocks essential for certain stages while retaining redundancy in others. Additionally, these methods lack explicit generation-quality feedback during the pruning decision, limiting the recoverability of the resulting pruned models.

## Input / Output

The method takes a text prompt as input and produces a generated video via a 4-step denoising trajectory on the Wan2.1-1.3B foundation model. Each denoising step invokes a different stage-specialized variant (a subset of the original transformer blocks), so the per-step computational footprint is reduced while the total stored parameter count remains comparable to the original model through shared-block reuse across variants.

## Method

SSBP partitions the denoising trajectory into K stages (K=4) and assigns each stage a tailored pruned variant composed of shared blocks and stage-specific blocks. A hierarchical selection mechanism drives iterative pruning: the Inter-Stage Variance Selector (IVS) picks the stage with the highest variance in block-importance scores (indicating greater removable redundancy), and the Intra-Stage Quality Selector (IQS) constructs N candidate pruned variants from the lowest-importance blocks, generates full videos with each, and selects the best via round-robin pairwise GPT-5.2 comparisons on visual fidelity, motion dynamics, and temporal consistency. A two-phase training pipeline then recovers performance: first, each variant is fine-tuned on its assigned timestep range using flow matching; second, DMD2 distillation with stochastic chain truncation trains the variants to operate sequentially for few-step generation, with shared blocks synchronized across variants and stage-specific blocks updated independently.

## Results

On VBench, SSBP-Wan2.1-1.3B (30% acceleration) achieves a total score of 83.52, quality score of 85.02, and semantic score of 77.54, matching or exceeding the unpruned Wan2.1-1.3B (83.31 total) while reducing per-step latency by 30%. Against the DMD2-distilled few-step baseline (84.21 total), SSBP trades a small quality-score gap (85.02 vs. 85.98) for a higher semantic score (77.54 vs. 77.09) and better temporal flickering (99.15 vs. 98.69). In the direct pruning-method comparison at 30% acceleration, SSBP (83.52 total) outperforms TinyFusion (80.46) by 3.06 points and L-OBS (78.53) by 4.99 points.

## Baselines & Comparisons

Against the DMD2-distilled few-step baseline (DMD2-Wan2.1-1.3B*), SSBP achieves a slightly lower total VBench score (83.52 vs. 84.21) and quality score (85.02 vs. 85.98), but surpasses it on semantic alignment (77.54 vs. 77.09), temporal flickering (99.15 vs. 98.69), and dynamic degree (73.88 vs. 72.50), while delivering 30% lower per-step latency. SSBP also edges out the unpruned Wan2.1-1.3B (83.52 vs. 83.31 total) and HunyuanVideo (83.52 vs. 83.43), and clearly outperforms CogVideoX-5B (83.52 vs. 81.61) and VideoCrafter2 (83.52 vs. 80.44). In the head-to-head pruning comparison (Table 2), SSBP dominates both baselines at both 20% and 30% acceleration: at 30%, it leads TinyFusion by 3.06 total points (83.52 vs. 80.46) and L-OBS by 4.99 points (83.52 vs. 78.53), with the gap widening on semantic scores (77.54 vs. 71.43 vs. 69.31). The ablation further confirms that the stage-wise + IVS + IQS pipeline contributes incrementally: one-stage global pruning yields 78.07, stage-wise alone 80.91, adding IVS 82.44, and the full SSBP 83.52.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends importance-based block pruning for diffusion models by replacing the standard global-averaging scheme with a stage-specialized paradigm: the denoising trajectory is partitioned into K stages, each served by a distinct pruned variant that shares a common set of blocks. It builds directly on DMD2 for few-step distillation and on the feature-similarity importance criterion used by prior methods like L-OBS, but departs from both importance-based (which averages scores across all steps) and learnable-mask-based (which optimizes a single global mask) approaches by introducing a hierarchical IVS/IQS selection loop that uses inter-stage variance to decide where to prune and GPT-5.2 quality feedback to decide which block to remove. The two-phase training pipeline (flow-matching fine-tuning followed by DMD2 with stochastic chain truncation) is a practical adaptation of existing distillation techniques to the multi-variant setting.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

SSBP offers a practical 30% per-step latency reduction on Wan2.1-1.3B while maintaining competitive VBench scores, making it relevant for deployment of video diffusion models where step distillation alone is insufficient. The stage-specialized framing is a natural and generalizable principle that could extend to other DiT-based video models, though the current evaluation is limited to a single 1.3B architecture and a fixed 4-stage partition. Key open questions include whether the approach generalizes to larger models (5B+), whether adaptive stage boundaries outperform the manual partition, and whether intra-block pruning within the stage-specialized framework can push acceleration further without the quality cliff that block-level pruning hits at aggressive ratios.
