## Problem

Existing 4D generation methods from monocular video or text suffer from blurry rendering, spatial-temporal inconsistency, and slow optimization, particularly when relying on implicit NeRF representations that produce over-saturated appearances. The core challenge is generating high-fidelity dynamic 3D content that maintains both multi-view spatial coherence and frame-to-frame temporal consistency without requiring pre-training or fine-tuning of diffusion networks.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ECCV20240951/cropped_page1_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method accepts a monocular video (real or synthetic), a text prompt, or a single RGB image as input. For text/image inputs, an off-the-shelf SDXL model generates an image and Stable Video Diffusion converts it to a video before the 4D lifting stage. The output is a deformable 3D Gaussian point cloud parameterized by a hex-plane-based deformation field, renderable in real-time at 150 FPS on an RTX 3090, with training completing in approximately 1 hour.

## Method

STAG4D combines a 4D Gaussian splatting representation (3D Gaussians with a hex-plane deformation field) with a multi-view diffusion pipeline that enforces spatial-temporal consistency via a training-free attention fusion module. Specifically, Zero123++ generates spatially consistent multi-view images, and a temporal attention mechanism mixes the keys and values from the first frame into subsequent frames' self-attention (weighted by γ=0.5) to anchor temporal coherence without any additional training. The 4D Gaussians are optimized via multi-view Score Distillation Sampling that selects the nearest generated view for each rendered camera, combined with reference photometric and mask losses, in a two-stage process (1000 steps for static canonical shape, then 7000 steps for deformation). An adaptive densification strategy replaces the fixed gradient threshold of standard 3DGS by selecting only the top 2.5% of points by accumulated gradient, preventing both under- and over-densification across diverse scenes.

## Results

On the Consistent4D video-to-4D benchmark, STAG4D achieves the best scores across all reported metrics: CLIP 0.909, LPIPS 0.126, FID-VID 52.58, FVD 992.21, and FVD-16 952.41. The method outperforms Consistent4D by 13.7% in FVD (992.21 vs. 1133.93) and 4DGen by 5.3% in FVD-16 (952.41 vs. 1005.72), while also achieving the highest CLIP score (0.909 vs. 0.894 and 0.877) and lowest LPIPS (0.126 vs. 0.130 and 0.134). For text-to-4D generation, qualitative comparisons show STAG4D produces smoother geometry and more consistent textures across views compared to 4Dfy and DreamGaussian4D. The method also achieves 2× faster generation speed than prior video-to-4D approaches and supports real-time rendering at 150 FPS.

## Baselines & Comparisons

STAG4D outperforms all four baselines on every metric where a direct comparison is possible. Against Consistent4D [6], STAG4D improves CLIP by 0.032 (0.909 vs. 0.877), reduces LPIPS by 0.008 (0.126 vs. 0.134), and lowers FVD by 141.72 points (992.21 vs. 1133.93), indicating substantially better semantic alignment and temporal coherence. Against the concurrent 4DGen [36], STAG4D achieves a 19.41-point improvement in FID-VID (52.58 vs. 71.99) and a 53.31-point improvement in FVD-16 (952.41 vs. 1005.72), while also matching or exceeding CLIP and LPIPS. For text-to-4D, qualitative comparisons show 4Dfy [1] produces coarse results that fail to capture complex text semantics, and DreamGaussian4D [23] generates fine front-view details but suffers from severe artifacts and distortions in side and back views, whereas STAG4D maintains smooth, consistent geometry and realistic texture across all views. The paper also notes that Consistent4D's cascade Dynerf representation leads to over-saturated and unrealistic patterns, while 4DGen struggles with reasonable Gaussian deformation and produces over-smoothed textures.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the 3D generation pipeline (multi-view diffusion + SDS + explicit representation) to the 4D setting, replacing the NeRF-based dynamic representations used by Consistent4D and MAV3D with a 4D Gaussian Splatting formulation. It draws on Zero123++ for multi-view spatial consistency and adapts the cross-frame attention idea from Text2Video-Zero into a training-free temporal anchor mechanism, combining both in a single diffusion pass. The adaptive densification strategy departs from the fixed-threshold approach in vanilla 3DGS and 4DGS reconstruction, motivated by the fundamentally different gradient statistics in the generative (single-view-conditioned) setting versus the multi-view reconstruction setting.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The combination of 4D Gaussian Splatting with a training-free spatial-temporal attention fusion offers a practical, no-fine-tuning pipeline for 4D generation that renders in real time (150 FPS) and trains in ~1 hour, making 4D content creation more accessible than prior NeRF-based approaches. However, the method still relies on a fixed set of 6 anchor views and a simple linear mixing coefficient (γ) for temporal consistency, leaving open questions about scalability to longer sequences, more complex multi-object scenes, and whether the heuristic densification threshold generalizes beyond the tested object categories. The 2× speedup over Consistent4D and the elimination of diffusion model fine-tuning are practically significant for downstream applications in gaming, film, and spatial video.
