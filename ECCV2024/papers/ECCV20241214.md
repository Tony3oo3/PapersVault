## Problem

Existing virtual try-on methods suffer from two key limitations: (1) warping stages apply uniform deformation mappings or rely on static, annotation-driven clothing parsers, causing texture distortion in local garment patterns; and (2) diffusion-based synthesis stages simultaneously optimize denoising and inpainting with inherently similar objectives, introducing learning ambiguities that degrade reconstruction quality of both garments and body regions.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ECCV20241214/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method takes a garment image G, a reference human image, and a condition triplet O (human pose, densepose pose, and preserve region mask) at 512×384 resolution. The garment image additionally serves as a CLIP global condition for the diffusion synthesis network. The output is a single photorealistic try-on result image where the garment is warped to fit the person and seamlessly integrated with the body.

## Method

D⁴-VTON is a two-stage framework. The deformation network uses Dynamic Semantics Disentangling Modules (DSDMs) that exploit self-similarity of garment features to unsupervisedly group feature channels into K semantic groups via Gumbel-Softmax, then learn distinct local flows per group through cascaded coarse-to-fine Group Warping Blocks, enabling semantically disentangled garment warping without parsers. The synthesis network builds on the PBE latent diffusion architecture and introduces a Differential Information Tracking Path (DITP): a lightweight differential mapper predicts the latent-space difference between complete and incomplete noisy inputs, which is used to first inpaint the incomplete latent before separate denoising, thereby decoupling the two degradation-restoration tasks and eliminating the optimization conflict present in prior dual-branch diffusion VTON methods.

## Results

On VITON-HD, D⁴-VTON achieves the best scores across all seven metrics: FIDu=8.530, KIDu=0.25, FIDp=4.845, KIDp=0.04, PSNRp=24.71 dB, SSIMp=0.892, and LPIPSp=0.065. In the paired setting it surpasses the second-best method (DCI-VTON) by 0.70 dB in PSNR and 0.004 in SSIM; in the unpaired setting it improves FIDu by 0.224 and KIDu by 0.43 over DCI-VTON. On DressCode, D⁴-VTON attains the best or near-best results across Upper, Lower, and Dresses categories, with the only exceptions being FIDp and KIDp on Dresses where SDAFN and GP-VTON edge it slightly.

## Baselines & Comparisons

D⁴-VTON outperforms all eight baselines on VITON-HD across every metric. The closest competitor is DCI-VTON (the strongest diffusion-based baseline), which D⁴-VTON beats by 0.70 dB in PSNR (24.71 vs. 24.01) and by 0.43 in KIDu (0.25 vs. 0.68) in the unpaired setting. Among GAN-based methods, GP-VTON is the strongest (FIDu=9.365, KIDu=0.79), still trailing D⁴-VTON by 0.835 in FIDu and 0.54 in KIDu. The CNN-based SDAFN and GAN-based PF-AFN fall further behind, with FIDu values of 9.782 and 9.654 respectively. On DressCode, D⁴-VTON dominates Upper and Lower categories on all metrics; on Dresses it wins all unpaired metrics but is marginally outperformed by SDAFN (FIDp=7.915 vs. 8.276) and GP-VTON (KIDp=0.32 vs. 1.07) in the paired setting, which the authors attribute to potential overfitting in those methods. LADI-VTON, the other diffusion baseline, underperforms notably (KIDu=1.66 on VITON-HD), confirming that textual-inversion guidance is insufficient for precise garment pattern reconstruction.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper builds directly on the PBE (Paint by Example) diffusion architecture and the LDM latent-space framework for its synthesis stage, while departing from prior VTON warping methods that rely on annotated clothing parsers (GP-VTON, KGI, COTTON) for semantic grouping. The key conceptual departure is twofold: (1) replacing static, annotation-driven parser segmentation with self-discovered semantic grouping via Gumbel-Softmax over feature-channel self-similarity (DSDM), and (2) replacing the joint denoising+inpainting objective used by DCI-VTON with a Differential Information Tracking Path that explicitly separates the two degradations. The lineage traces from TPS-based warping (VITON, CP-VTON) through appearance-flow methods (ClothFlow, PF-AFN) to diffusion-based VTON (LADI-VTON, DCI-VTON), with this work addressing the specific failure modes of each stage.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The DSDM removes the practical bottleneck of requiring annotated semantic regions for garment deformation, making the warping stage more adaptable to diverse garment types without retraining parsers. The DITP addresses a structural weakness in diffusion-based VTON—simultaneous multi-degradation restoration—that likely affects other inpainting-plus-denoising tasks beyond try-on. However, the method still struggles with partially occluded garments (e.g., missing sleeves), where the self-discovered semantics cannot recover absent semantic components, and the paired-setting advantage on DressCode-Dresses is marginal, suggesting the approach may overfit in some categories. Open questions include whether the self-discovered grouping generalizes to lower-body garments with more complex topology and whether the differential tracking idea transfers to other multi-degradation diffusion tasks.
