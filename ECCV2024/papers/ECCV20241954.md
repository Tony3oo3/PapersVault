## Problem

Current diffusion-based image-to-3D methods produce novel views with blurred textures and inconsistencies relative to the reference image, because optimization relies on inferred text prompts that cannot capture fine visual details, and zero-shot novel view synthesis models (e.g., Zero-1-to-3) degrade in fidelity compared to the input view. This limits the practical usability of generated 3D assets for applications requiring high visual quality.

## Input / Output

The method takes a single RGB reference image as input, estimates its depth map via MiDaS (normalized to [-1,1]), and uses a depth-conditioned Stable Diffusion model throughout. For zero-shot novel view synthesis, it outputs enhanced novel view images at the resolution of the underlying model. For image-to-3D generation, it produces a DMTet mesh with fixed geometry and refined textures, rendered at 1024×1024 resolution. The coarse stage uses Instant-NGP at 64→128 resolution, and the refine stage operates on the exported DMTet with T=20 DDIM steps and attention injection starting at step l=12.

## Method

HiFi-123 introduces two coupled contributions. First, the Reference-Guided Novel View Enhancement (RGNV) pipeline performs depth-based DDIM inversion on both the reference image and a coarse novel view simultaneously, then applies attention injection—replacing the K,V matrices of the denoising U-Net's self-attention in the novel view branch with those from the reference branch—during the final l denoising steps to transfer fine textures. The key enabling insight is that incorporating depth conditioning into DDIM inversion yields near-perfect reconstruction, making the inversion and sampling processes nearly consistent. Second, the Reference-Guided State Distillation (RGSD) loss reformulates SDS by distilling from the RGNV pipeline's intermediate noisy states rather than from text-conditioned diffusion, reducing to an L2 distance between the rendered view's noisy state and its RGNV-enhanced counterpart. The full image-to-3D pipeline uses a coarse-to-fine strategy: Instant-NGP optimized with hybrid 2D/3D SDS plus reconstruction, depth, and normal losses, followed by DMTet texture refinement supervised by RGSD loss with alternating fixed-view and random-view optimization targets.

## Results

On the 3D dataset (Google Scanned Objects, 30 objects), HiFi-123 achieves PSNR 23.68, SSIM 0.875, LPIPS 0.101, Chamfer Distance 0.025, and Volume IoU 0.577, substantially outperforming the strongest baseline Magic123 (PSNR 19.33, IoU 0.453). On the single-view dataset, it attains CLIP similarity 0.916 and contextual distance 1.627, compared to Magic123's 0.883 and 1.882. For zero-shot novel view synthesis, the RGNV pipeline improves SyncDreamer from PSNR 19.98 to 21.08 and LPIPS from 0.142 to 0.123 on the 3D dataset.

## Baselines & Comparisons

HiFi-123 outperforms all five baselines on every reported metric. Against Magic123 (the strongest baseline), it improves PSNR by 4.35 dB (23.68 vs. 19.33), Volume IoU by 0.124 (0.577 vs. 0.453), and reduces Chamfer Distance by 52% (0.025 vs. 0.052) on the 3D dataset. On the single-view dataset, it achieves the best CLIP similarity (0.916 vs. 0.883) and lowest contextual distance (1.627 vs. 1.882) over Magic123. Make-It-3D trails by a wider margin (PSNR 17.08, IoU 0.401), while RealFusion, a one-stage method, lags most significantly (PSNR 15.37, IoU 0.274). For zero-shot novel view synthesis, the RGNV pipeline as a plug-and-play module improves both Zero-1-to-3 (PSNR 18.95→20.45) and SyncDreamer (PSNR 19.98→21.08), confirming its generality across different base architectures.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper builds directly on the Score Distillation Sampling (SDS) paradigm from DreamFusion and the zero-shot novel view synthesis framework of Zero-1-to-3, but departs from both by replacing text-prompt-based guidance with depth-conditioned DDIM inversion of the reference image. The key conceptual shift is that instead of distilling from a text-conditioned T2I diffusion process (which loses fine details due to prompt ambiguity and strong CFG), the method distills from a reference-image-conditioned inversion-sampling pipeline where the reference's textures are explicitly preserved. The attention injection mechanism for cross-branch texture transfer draws from consistent video generation work (MasaCtrl, Tune-A-Video), repurposing it for the 3D generation setting.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The RGNV pipeline's plug-and-play nature means it can immediately improve existing zero-shot novel view synthesis methods without retraining, and the RGSD loss offers a principled drop-in replacement for SDS in any optimization-based image-to-3D pipeline. The ~4 dB PSNR improvement over Magic123 and the qualitative elimination of texture seams at occlusion boundaries represent a meaningful step toward practical single-image 3D asset creation. Open questions include whether the depth-conditioning requirement (MiDaS estimation) degrades on objects with ambiguous depth, whether the approach extends to multi-object or scene-level generation, and whether the 40-minute optimization budget can be further reduced for interactive use.
