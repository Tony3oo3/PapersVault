## Problem

Zero-1-to-3 methods generate novel views from a single image but suffer from geometry and appearance inconsistency across views due to the extreme information sparsity of one input, with quality degrading sharply when camera pose changes exceed ~45°. This is particularly problematic for complex objects such as insects, humans, transparent items, and stacked multi-object scenes where a single view cannot disambiguate occluded or unseen surfaces.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ECCV20241094/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method takes a single RGB image with a known camera pose as input and produces a target-view image at an arbitrary camera pose, plus (optionally) a 3D mesh via SDS loss and NeuS reconstruction. Internally, the pipeline first generates P self-prompted nearby views constrained to small angular offsets (±45° azimuth, 30° elevation) from the source view; these intermediate views are not final outputs but serve as additional conditioning for the second stage. The final output is a single target-view image at the requested pose, with the 3D reconstruction pipeline reusing the same self-prompted views as multi-view inputs to NeuS without re-invoking the first-stage model.

## Method

Cascade-Zero123 stacks two Zero-1-to-3 latent diffusion models in a cascade: Base-0123 generates P nearby views from the source image using fixed small-angle camera offsets, and Refiner-0123 takes the source image plus all P self-prompted views (each with its recomputed relative pose to the target) as multi-condition inputs to synthesize the final target view. In Refiner-0123, all condition images are encoded and concatenated along the token dimension so that a single cross-attention mechanism attends jointly over the source and all prompted views, providing richer 3D context than a single condition. Base-0123 is progressively improved via exponential moving average (EMA) self-distillation from the better-performing Refiner-0123, so the first stage becomes a stronger generator of nearby views over training. Both models are initialized from Zero123-XL pre-trained weights and trained jointly on Objaverse for 200k steps, with the cascade design reducing the effective view-transition difficulty at each stage.

## Results

On the GSO dataset, Cascade-Zero123 (with Zero123-XL backbone) achieves PSNR 20.35, SSIM 0.850, LPIPS 0.113, and CLIP 0.846, outperforming Zero123-XL (PSNR 18.81, LPIPS 0.142) and SyncDreamer (PSNR 20.05, LPIPS 0.146). On Objaverse, the method reaches PSNR 21.42 and LPIPS 0.125, a substantial improvement over Zero123-XL (PSNR 18.68, LPIPS 0.189). For single-image-to-3D reconstruction on GSO, Cascade-Zero123 attains the best Chamfer Distance (0.0207) and volumetric IoU (0.5792), surpassing SyncDreamer (0.0261, 0.5421) and Zero123 (0.0339, 0.5035).

## Baselines & Comparisons

Cascade-Zero123 consistently outperforms Zero123-XL [43] across all four evaluation datasets: on GSO it improves PSNR by 1.54 dB and reduces LPIPS from 0.142 to 0.113; on Objaverse it gains 2.74 dB in PSNR and drops LPIPS from 0.189 to 0.125; on RealFusion15 it leads with PSNR 26.098 vs. 25.220 and CLIP 0.916 vs. 0.897. Against SyncDreamer [46], the cascade variant (Ours(SyncDreamer)) further improves PSNR from 20.05 to 21.10 and LPIPS from 0.146 to 0.095 on GSO, and achieves better 3D reconstruction (C Dist. 0.0207 vs. 0.0261, Vol IoU 0.5792 vs. 0.5421). On RealFusion15, the method beats Consistent-123 [40] by 0.42 dB in PSNR (26.098 vs. 25.682) and 0.072 in CLIP (0.916 vs. 0.844), and surpasses Magic123 [61] by 0.46 dB in PSNR. For 3D reconstruction on GSO, Cascade-Zero123 substantially outperforms One-2-3-45 [42] (C Dist. 0.0207 vs. 0.0629), Point-E [54] (0.0207 vs. 0.0426), and Shap-E [30] (0.0207 vs. 0.0436), with volumetric IoU gains of 0.17–0.29 over these baselines.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper builds directly on Zero-1-to-3 (Liu et al., 2023), a latent diffusion model that generates novel views from a single image conditioned on relative camera pose, and extends it with a two-stage cascade architecture (Base-0123 + Refiner-0123) inspired by cascade designs in image generation (SDXL, DeepFloyd-IF). The key conceptual departure is the self-prompting mechanism: rather than conditioning on only the source image, the first stage generates nearby views that are fed as additional conditions to the second stage, progressively extracting 3D information. Unlike Consistent1-to-3, which introduces auxiliary Transformer modules for consistency, this paper argues that Zero-1-to-3's own generative capacity suffices if used in a progressive, multi-condition manner, preserving its open-set generalization from large-scale pre-training.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The cascade self-prompting approach offers a practical, architecture-light remedy to the view-inconsistency problem that plagues single-image-to-3D methods, with consistent gains across four diverse benchmarks (Objaverse, GSO, RTMV, RealFusion15) and demonstrated compatibility with other backbones like SyncDreamer. However, the method remains bounded by Zero-1-to-3's inherent limitations: it struggles with high-elevation inputs, overlapping objects where depth is ambiguous, and cases where the base model fundamentally fails. Open questions include whether multi-view cross-attention or additional conditioning modalities (depth, normals) can push consistency further, and whether the cascade framework generalizes to video or dynamic 3D generation.
