## Problem

Existing 3D avatar generation methods produce 'toy-like' results that lack fine details such as hair strands and cloth textures. The paper identifies a previously overlooked root cause: catastrophic forgetting in the shared MLP decoder when triplanes are fitted sequentially on many avatars, leaving the decoder under-fitted and unable to render sharp details on novel triplanes. Additionally, prior conditioning (e.g., CLIP global tokens) discards the fine-grained 2D texture cues needed for high-fidelity generation.

## Input / Output

The method takes a single frontal portrait image (or a text prompt first converted to a portrait via a LoRA-finetuned Stable Diffusion model) and produces a 512×512×32 triplane representation, from which 360° multi-view images at 1024×1024 resolution can be rendered via the shared MLP decoder. The triplane is the native 3D output; rendered images are derived. The model also supports unconditional generation by dropping the conditional features with 20% probability during training, enabling classifier-free guidance.

## Method

The framework has two stages. In the fitting stage, a shared MLP decoder and per-avatar triplanes are jointly trained using a task-replay strategy (cycling through avatars in short bursts rather than fitting each to convergence) combined with an Identity-aware Weight Consolidation (IWC) regularizer that penalizes deviation of important weights from their consolidated values via a Fisher-information-based penalty, preventing catastrophic forgetting and reducing high-frequency noise in triplanes. In the diffusion stage, a cascaded U-Net (base model generating a low-resolution triplane, then an upsample model producing the full 512×512×32 triplane) is conditioned on multi-scale VAE encoder features from the portrait image, injected at multiple U-Net resolutions via cross-attention to preserve fine 2D texture cues. The noise schedule is specifically optimized for triplanes: a stronger adjusted cosine schedule for the base model and a much stronger sigmoid schedule for the upsample model, accounting for the high spatial and channel redundancy of triplanes relative to images.

## Results

On conditional avatar generation (from a single portrait), RodinHD achieves FID 26.49, LPIPS 0.299, and SSIM 0.765, outperforming Rodin (FID 33.20, LPIPS 0.323, SSIM 0.758). On unconditional generation, RodinHD achieves FID 32.62, surpassing all 3D-aware GAN baselines (Pi-GAN 78.3, GIRAFFE 64.6, EG3D* 40.5) and Rodin without 2D refinement (45.70), though slightly trailing Rodin with 2D refinement (30.29). For 3D consistency measured via NeuS fitting, RodinHD achieves PSNR 35.46 and LPIPS 0.041, substantially outperforming Rodin* (PSNR 31.73, LPIPS 0.051), confirming that the 2D refiner in Rodin breaks cross-view consistency.

## Baselines & Comparisons

The primary comparison is against Rodin [65], the closest prior work. On conditional generation, RodinHD improves FID from 33.20 to 26.49 and LPIPS from 0.323 to 0.299, while also achieving higher SSIM (0.765 vs 0.758). On unconditional generation, RodinHD (FID 32.62) is slightly worse than Rodin with 2D refinement (30.29) but dramatically better than Rodin without refinement (45.70); however, RodinHD achieves far superior 3D consistency (PSNR 35.46 vs 31.73, LPIPS 0.041 vs 0.051), since the 2D refiner in Rodin introduces cross-view flickering. Against 3D-aware GAN baselines, RodinHD substantially outperforms EG3D* (FID 40.5), GIRAFFE (64.6), and Pi-GAN (78.3) on unconditional generation, confirming that the diffusion-based triplane approach with the proposed fitting and conditioning strategies yields significantly sharper and more diverse avatars than GAN-based methods.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends Rodin [65], which established the triplane-based cascaded diffusion framework for 3D avatar generation, by identifying and solving a previously overlooked training pathology: catastrophic forgetting in the shared MLP decoder when fitting triplanes sequentially. It borrows elastic weight consolidation from continual learning [31] and adapts noise-schedule findings from high-resolution 2D image generation [8, 42] to the 3D triplane setting. The key departure from Rodin is the rejection of its 2D convolution refiner (CodeFormer) in favor of natively generating high-frequency detail through better fitting and multi-scale VAE feature conditioning via cross-attention, preserving cross-view consistency.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The identification of catastrophic forgetting as a silent bottleneck in shared-decoder 3D fitting is broadly applicable to any pipeline that sequentially trains a shared network on many individual representations, and the task-replay + IWC remedy is lightweight and general. The paper demonstrates that high-fidelity 3D avatars with sharp hair and clothing textures can be produced without post-hoc 2D refinement, which is critical for applications requiring geometric consistency (animation, VR). Limitations include validation only on synthetic Blender avatars (46K) and a single avatar domain; whether the techniques transfer to general 3D scene generation or larger-scale real-world data remains open, as does the question of whether the 512×512×32 triplane resolution is sufficient for production-quality results.
