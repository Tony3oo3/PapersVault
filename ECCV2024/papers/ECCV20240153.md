## Problem

Existing text-to-3D scene generation methods either rely on slow score distillation (suffering from multi-view inconsistency) or progressive inpainting (producing distorted, disjointed structures when targeting full 360° coverage). Neither approach handles unconstrained prompts well, and both require extensive manual prompt engineering to achieve desired results. The paper addresses how to generate globally coherent, complete 360° 3D scenes from arbitrary text in minutes without auxiliary inputs.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ECCV20240153/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method takes a single free-form text prompt (e.g., 'A bedroom', 'An anime style Japanese city street') and produces a real-time explorable 360° panoramic 3D Gaussian Splatting scene. Internally, the text is first converted to a 1024×2048 equirectangular panorama, which is then projected onto 20 tangent perspective views (via icosahedron decomposition) for depth estimation and Gaussian initialization. The final 3D scene supports free camera navigation with full 360° coverage and no blind spots, rendered in real time via Gaussian splatting.

## Method

DreamScene360 operates in three stages. First, a 360° panorama is generated using MultiDiffusion with StitchDiffusion (a LoRA-adapted text-to-panorama model), followed by a multi-round self-refinement loop where GPT-4V scores candidate images and revises the prompt iteratively. Second, the panorama is projected to 20 tangent views, and a learnable geometric field (MLP) with per-view scale and per-pixel shift parameters is optimized to resolve the affine ambiguity of monocular DPT depth, yielding a globally consistent dense point cloud that initializes the 3D Gaussians. Third, the Gaussians are optimized end-to-end with a composite loss combining photometric (L1 + D-SSIM) loss, a semantic regularization (cosine similarity of DINOv2 [CLS] tokens between training views and synthetically perturbed virtual cameras), and a geometric regularization (correlation between rendered depth and DPT-predicted depth on virtual views). Virtual cameras are created by progressively perturbing the panoramic viewpoint (3 stages of increasing displacement) to emulate parallax and fill invisible regions, while densification is disabled to avoid floaters.

## Results

On the unconstrained 360° text-to-3D scene generation task, DreamScene360 outperforms LucidDreamer on all four quality metrics: CLIP Distance (0.8732 vs. 0.8900, lower is better), Q-Align (3.1094 vs. 3.0566, higher is better), NIQE (4.9165 vs. 6.2305, lower is better), and BRISQUE (38.3911 vs. 51.9764, lower is better). Runtime is comparable at 7 min 20 sec versus 6 min 15 sec. The method achieves complete 360° coverage without blind spots, whereas LucidDreamer produces hallucinated or repetitive novel views.

## Baselines & Comparisons

The sole quantitative baseline is LucidDreamer [7], which generates 3D Gaussian Splatting scenes via progressive inpainting from a single conditioned image. DreamScene360 wins on every quality metric: it reduces CLIP Distance by ~1.9% (0.8732 vs. 0.8900), improves Q-Align by ~1.7% (3.1094 vs. 3.0566), lowers NIQE by ~21% (4.9165 vs. 6.2305), and reduces BRISQUE by ~26% (38.3911 vs. 51.9764). The runtime cost is modest at roughly 1 minute longer. Visually, LucidDreamer hallucinates inconsistent novel views (e.g., multiple divergent bedrooms for a single-bedroom prompt, or a repeated waterfall for a Yosemite scene) and leaves black blind spots, while DreamScene360 delivers globally consistent semantics, style, and geometry across the full 360° sphere.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper departs from two dominant paradigms in text-to-3D scene generation: score distillation (DreamFusion) and progressive multi-view inpainting (LucidDreamer, Text2Room). Instead, it introduces a panoramic image as a global 2D intermediate representation, generated via MultiDiffusion and StitchDiffusion, which is then lifted into 3D Gaussian Splatting with monocular depth initialization and a learnable geometric field. The use of GPT-4V for iterative prompt self-refinement and the distillation of DINOv2/DPT features from synthesized virtual cameras as unsupervised regularizers represent novel combinations not seen in prior text-to-3D pipelines.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The pipeline offers a practical 'one-click' path to full 360° immersive scenes without manual prompt engineering or auxiliary layout inputs, addressing a real gap for VR/content-creation workflows. However, the evaluation is limited to a single baseline (LucidDreamer) with modest quantitative margins, and the output resolution is capped by the pretrained diffusion model (512×1024). Open questions include whether the virtual-camera parallax trick generalizes to highly dynamic or unbounded outdoor scenes, and whether the approach scales to 4D or higher-resolution generation as the authors themselves note as future work.
