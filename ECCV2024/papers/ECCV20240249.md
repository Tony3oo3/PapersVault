## Problem

Transformer-based RIS methods incur quadratic computation cost, limiting their ability to capture long-range visual-language dependencies in large images with long descriptions. While Mamba offers linear-complexity sequence modeling, directly applying it to multi-modal fusion fails because its scanning operation is nearly independent across channels, making it inadequate for fusing visual and textual features arranged along the channel dimension.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ECCV20240249/cropped_page1_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The model takes an RGB image (480×480 for SOTA comparison, 256×256 for ablations) and a natural-language referring expression as input, producing a pixel-level segmentation mask. The image is processed by a Mamba-B backbone initialized with ImageNet pre-trained weights, while the text is encoded and injected at every Mamba Twister block rather than only at the final layer. A lightweight convolution-based decoder converts the intermediate multi-modal features into the final mask, keeping the decoder task-agnostic.

## Method

ReMamber stacks multiple Mamba Twister blocks, each combining VSS layers (Cross-Scan Module from VMamba for 2D spatial scanning) with a Twisting layer that injects text into the visual stream. The Twisting layer first computes a vision-language interaction map via local matrix multiplication between projected image and text tokens, and a global text representation via CLS pooling; these are concatenated with the visual feature along the channel axis to form a hybrid feature cube. The cube is then 'twisted' by two successive SSM scans: a Channel Scan (1-D selective scan along the channel dimension) that enables cross-modality communication, followed by a Spatial Scan (2-D VSS) that redistributes information across patches within each modality. This two-axis twisting mechanism directly addresses Mamba's inter-channel independence, and the paper further validates its superiority over in-context conditioning, cross-attention, and FiLM-based norm adaptation through controlled ablations.

## Results

ReMamber (Mamba-B backbone) achieves state-of-the-art oIoU across all three RIS benchmarks: 74.54/76.74/70.89 on RefCOCO (val/testA/testB), 65.00/70.78/57.53 on RefCOCO+, and 63.9/64.0 on G-Ref (valU/testU). It outperforms the strongest prior method LAVT (Swin-B) by 1.8–2.4 oIoU on RefCOCO, 2.4–2.9 on RefCOCO+, and 1.9–2.7 on G-Ref. The paper also reports mIoU and Precision@X metrics in ablation tables, where ReMamber's Mamba Twister variant achieves 71.6 mIoU and 82.1 Pr@50 on RefCOCO val, surpassing all other fusion strategies tested.

## Baselines & Comparisons

ReMamber outperforms all 18 baselines across every dataset and split. The closest competitor is LAVT (Swin-B), which ReMamber surpasses by 1.8 oIoU on RefCOCO val (74.54 vs. 72.73), 2.9 on RefCOCO+ val (65.00 vs. 62.14), and 2.7 on G-Ref valU (63.9 vs. 61.24). Among transformer-based methods, ETRIS with ViT-B-16 (70.51 RefCOCO val) and CRIS with CLIP ResNet-101 (70.47) trail ReMamber by roughly 4 oIoU. Older CNN-based methods (PCAN, BUSNet, CGAN, etc.) lag further behind, with the best CNN baseline PCAN reaching only 69.51 on RefCOCO val. The paper also ablates three alternative Mamba-based fusion strategies (In-Context Conditioning, Attention-based Conditioning, Norm Adaptation) and shows the Mamba Twister consistently outperforms them by 1.4–3.5 oIoU, with cross-attention performing worst due to a fundamental mismatch with Mamba's sequential state propagation.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper builds directly on Mamba's selective state space model and VMamba's Cross-Scan Module, repurposing both for the multi-modal setting of referring image segmentation. It departs from the dominant transformer-based RIS paradigm (LAVT, ReSTR, CRIS) by replacing quadratic self-attention with linear-complexity SSM scans, and identifies a fundamental incompatibility: the standard token-splicing fusion strategy used in transformers fails under Mamba because SSMs lack sufficient cross-channel interaction between differently-typed tokens. The Mamba Twister block is introduced specifically to address this gap, making this the first Mamba-based architecture for multi-modal RIS, whereas prior Mamba-in-vision work (Vim, VMamba, U-Mamba, etc.) has been confined to single-modality tasks.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The paper opens a new direction for efficient multi-modal understanding by demonstrating that SSMs can match or exceed transformer-based RIS methods while offering linear scaling, which is particularly relevant for large images with long descriptions. The finding that cross-attention is fundamentally misaligned with Mamba's sequential state dynamics is a useful caution for the broader community exploring SSM-attention hybrids. However, the current decoder is a simple convolutional stack, and the authors themselves note that more sophisticated Mamba-compatible decoders remain an open problem; the practical impact will depend on whether the twisting mechanism generalizes beyond RIS to other multi-modal tasks.
