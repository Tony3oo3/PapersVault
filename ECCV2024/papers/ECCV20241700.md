## Problem

Existing audio-visual MLLMs are limited to coarse-grained tasks like captioning and question-answering, lacking the fine-grained spatio-temporal grounding ability to localize sounding objects in images or pinpoint audio events in time. No unified framework exists that can simultaneously ground spatially in the visual modality and temporally in the audio modality within a single LLM interface, and no large-scale instruction-tuning dataset supports such fine-grained audio-visual learning.

## Input / Output

The model consumes an RGB image, a 30-second audio clip, and a natural-language instruction containing special tokens (<image>, <audio>, <obj>) that are replaced with modality-specific embeddings. Outputs are generated as natural-language text: bounding boxes are encoded as normalized [xLeft, yTop, xRight, yBottom] coordinates, temporal segments as [tStart, tEnd] values, fact-checking as binary True/False, and QA/captioning as free-form text. This unified text-based output format allows a single LLM to handle all five task types without task-specific decoders.

## Method

MEERKAT builds on Llama 2-Chat 7B (fine-tuned via LoRA, r=32) with CLIP-ViT-B/16 for image features and CLAP for audio features, connected through two novel alignment modules. The AVOpT module constructs discrete probability distributions over image and audio patch embeddings and minimizes the Wasserstein distance between them via an optimal transport plan, providing weak patch-level cross-modal alignment. The AVACE module adds an adapter-based cross-attention layer whose attention map is regularized by a mask derived from ground-truth bounding boxes, maximizing attention within the object region and suppressing background attention for strong region-level supervision. Training combines cross-entropy loss on LLM outputs with the OT alignment loss and the attention consistency loss in a single multi-task objective over 3M instruction samples (AVFIT) spanning five tasks, with GPT-3.5/4-assisted instruction generation to ensure fine-grained spatial and temporal references in the dialogues.

## Results

On Audio Referred Image Grounding, MEERKAT achieves 48.51 cIoU on VGG-SS, outperforming the closest generalist baseline BuboGPT (40.31) by +20.34% and the best specialist method (Alignment, 42.64) by +13.8%. On Image Guided Audio Temporal Localization, MEERKAT reaches 54.96 F1 on LLP and 56.85 F1 on AudioSet Strong, surpassing TimeChat by +7.18% and +4.01% respectively. On AVQA, MEERKAT attains 88.24/86.65/86.55 accuracy (Exist/Localis/Temp), and on AV captioning (VALOR-32K) it achieves 16.88 BLEU@4 and 76.84 CIDEr, a +37.12% relative improvement over X-InstructBLIP on BLEU@4.

## Baselines & Comparisons

On Audio Referred Image Grounding, MEERKAT outperforms all eight specialist baselines (SSPL, EZ-VSL, SSL-TIE, SLAVC, MarginNCE, HearTheFlow, FNAC, Alignment) and the generalist BuboGPT by 7.4–20.3% cIoU across VGG-SS, Flickr-SoundNet, PascalSound, and AVSBench. On Image Guided Audio Temporal Localization, MEERKAT surpasses specialist methods AVE (35.47), AVSDN (37.15), and AVVP (48.93) as well as the generalist TimeChat (51.28) on LLP F1, achieving 54.96. On Audio-Visual Fact-checking, MEERKAT leads all generalist baselines (Macaw-LLM, PandaGPT, VideoLlama, BuboGPT, X-InstructBLIP, TimeChat) across all four verification types, with the largest gap over TimeChat at +14.86% on Type 1. On AVQA, MEERKAT exceeds the strongest specialist CAD (83.42/73.97/76.16) and the best generalist X-InstructBLIP (85.53/80.09/83.91) on Exist/Localis/Temp accuracy. On AV captioning (VALOR-32K), MEERKAT achieves 16.88 BLEU@4 and 76.84 CIDEr, surpassing X-InstructBLIP (12.31/65.73) by +37.12% and +16.9% respectively, and the specialist VALOR model (8.97/55.73) by a wide margin. MEERKAT wins on every task and every metric reported in the paper; no baseline is matched or exceeded by MEERKAT.

## Relation to Prior Work

MEERKAT sits at the intersection of three research threads: (1) audio-visual LLMs like Macaw-LLM, PandaGPT, and X-InstructBLIP that incorporate audio but restrict themselves to coarse-grained tasks (captioning, QA); (2) single-modality grounding models such as BuboGPT (visual grounding) and TimeChat (temporal localization) that handle fine-grained spatial or temporal grounding but not both jointly; and (3) optimal-transport-based alignment methods (DeepEMD, PLOT, VLAP) previously applied to image-image or image-text patch matching. The key departure is repurposing OT for audio-visual patch-level alignment (AVOpT) and extending cross-attention consistency enforcement from visual grounding to the audio-visual setting (AVACE), unifying both spatial and temporal grounding within a single LLM interface for the first time.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

By introducing the first unified framework for fine-grained audio-visual spatio-temporal grounding, MEERKAT opens a path toward truly multimodal assistants that can reason about where and when events occur. The AVFIT-3M dataset and MEERKATBENCH benchmark will serve as important resources for the community, and the ablation showing that fine-grained grounding training transfers to coarse-grained tasks (captioning, QA) suggests a productive training recipe. Limitations include the restriction to image-audio pairs rather than video, bounding-box-level (not segmentation-level) grounding, and reliance on GPT-generated instruction data whose quality bounds may be hard to assess. Open questions include whether the approach scales to video temporal grounding, whether the OT alignment generalizes beyond the CLIP/CLAP encoder pair, and how robust the model is to distribution shift in open-domain audio-visual scenes.
