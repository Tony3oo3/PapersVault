## Problem

Conditional GANs are widely criticized for poor sample fidelity and limited diversity compared to diffusion models, which benefit from guided sampling (classifier guidance or classifier-free guidance) to trade off fidelity and diversity. However, it remains unexplored whether GANs can also leverage a guided sampling strategy without requiring a pre-trained classifier or a separately learned unconditional model, despite their well-organized latent spaces.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ECCV20240674/cropped_page1_idx1.webp)

## Input / Output

The method takes a random noise vector z and a target class label c, and produces a single synthesized RGB image (64×64 or 128×128 on ImageNet). In the training-free variant, the noise is additionally fused with a subset of other class labels (the 'opposite condition set') to estimate the joint distribution in W space; in the learning-based variant, a single empty-condition embedding replaces this averaging step. The guidance is applied entirely within the style (W) space of the mapping network before decoding, so the output is a standard one-step GAN sample with no iterative denoising.

## Method

The core insight is that the well-organized W space of style-based GANs allows the joint data distribution to be estimated via simple vector arithmetic, eliminating the need for a classifier or unconditional model. The training-free GANdance computes a conditional latent w_c = G_map(z, c) and an 'opposite' latent w_oppo by averaging G_map(z, c') over a subset of other conditions, then applies guided interpolation w'_c = w_oppo + λ(w_c − w_oppo) before decoding with G_syn. The learning-based variant adds a single empty-condition embedding to both generator and discriminator, so w_oppo = G_map(z, ∅) is obtained in one forward pass with no averaging. Theoretically, Theorem 1 shows that under bijectivity of G_syn, moving along (w_c − w_oppo) increases log p(c|w) in W space, mirroring the classifier-gradient step in diffusion guidance. A layer-wise extension applies decreasing guidance scales to higher-resolution groups in the mapping network, exploiting the fact that low-resolution channels encode high-level semantics (class identity) and tolerate stronger guidance.

## Results

On ImageNet 64×64, the learning-based GANdance applied to Aurora reduces FID from 8.87 to 4.37 (Precision 0.41→0.73, Recall 0.48→0.53) in a single forward pass (NFE=1). The training-free variant alone improves Aurora's FID from 8.87 to 6.06 with negligible inference overhead. On ImageNet 128×128, GANdance improves StyleGAN2 from FID 25.39 to 19.63 and BigGAN from 10.76 to 9.07. The best GANdance result (FID 4.37) is within 0.35 of the state-of-the-art one-step diffusion model iCT (FID 4.02) under comparable model size (~203M vs 269M parameters).

## Baselines & Comparisons

Aurora+GANdance (FID 4.37, NFE=1) outperforms all one-step and few-step diffusion baselines except iCT: it beats CD at NFE=2 (FID 4.70), CD at NFE=1 (FID 6.20), DPM-Solver at NFE=10 (FID 6.61), CT at NFE=2 (FID 11.10), CT at NFE=1 (FID 13.00), and DDIM at NFE=50 (FID 13.70). It is slightly behind iCT (FID 4.02) but uses fewer parameters (203M vs 269M). Against multi-step diffusion models ADM (FID 2.07, 250 NFE) and EDM (FID 2.44, 79 NFE), GANdance is worse in absolute FID but achieves competitive quality in a single forward pass. All GAN baselines show consistent improvement with GANdance: StyleGAN2 drops from FID 21.32→17.80 (64×64) and 25.39→19.63 (128×128), while BigGAN improves from 10.76→9.07 (128×128). The training-free variant alone (no retraining) already reduces Aurora's FID from 8.87 to 6.06, and combining GANdance with the truncation trick further pushes FID to 4.07.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper adapts the guided sampling paradigm from diffusion models (classifier guidance and classifier-free guidance) to conditional GANs, but departs fundamentally from the diffusion formulation by exploiting the well-organized W space of style-based GANs. Rather than requiring a pretrained classifier or a jointly trained unconditional model, GANdance estimates the joint data distribution through simple vector arithmetic over an 'opposite condition set' in latent space, grounded in a Bayesian theorem linking W-space directions to image-space conditional probabilities. The training-based variant mirrors the empty-condition training strategy of classifier-free guidance [15], while the training-free variant is a novel plug-in inference trick with no prior analogue in the GAN literature. The work also builds on the GAN editing literature (e.g., StyleSpace, re-scoring analysis) that established the interpretability and linearity of W space.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

By closing most of the gap between GANs and one-step diffusion models (FID 4.37 vs. 4.02 for iCT) at comparable model size with negligible inference overhead, GANdance offers a practical path to reviving GANs for high-fidelity conditional generation without the multi-step sampling cost of diffusion models. However, the method's efficacy depends sensitively on the guidance strength λ and the probability of resetting conditions during training, and the optimal settings remain unexplored across different model architectures and data domains. The paper also acknowledges that GANs still struggle with broader multimodal distributions and open-vocabulary text-to-image synthesis, limiting the immediate generalizability of the approach beyond class-conditional ImageNet-style tasks.
