## Problem

Existing subject-driven 3D generation methods like DreamBooth3D require 3–6 carefully selected subject images and over 3 hours of optimization, while also suffering from a distribution gap between wild subjects and pre-trained diffusion models. This makes it impractical to produce high-fidelity, subject-specific 3D assets with text-driven modifications (novel colors, poses, attributes) that are unseen in any input image.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ECCV20242257/cropped_page0_idx1.webp)

## Input / Output

The method takes a single casual subject image (no camera pose or additional views required) and a free-form text prompt describing desired modifications (e.g., 'with elf ears', 'wearing a suit'). It produces a textured polygonal mesh via a 3D Gaussian → Instant-NGP NeRF → mesh pipeline, along with the intermediate multi-view color images and 3D Gaussians. The entire pipeline completes in approximately 5 minutes on a single RTX 3090, with ~1 min for identity-aware optimization, ~3 min for subject-prior optimization, and ~1 min for mesh conversion.

## Method

Make-Your-3D introduces a co-evolution framework that harmonizes the distributions of a 2D personalized model (IP-Adapter) and a multi-view diffusion model (Wonder3D) to approximate the target subject distribution. In the identity-aware optimization stage, the frozen multi-view diffusion model lifts the single input image into 3D space to produce multi-views, which are then used (with CLIP-encoded features) to fine-tune only the image cross-attention layers of IP-Adapter, injecting enhanced identity information. In the subject-prior optimization stage, the original (unoptimized) IP-Adapter generates diverse subject images under the text modification, and these—along with inferred normal maps—are used to fine-tune the cross-domain self-attention module of the multi-view diffusion model, infusing subject-specific prior while a parameter-preservation term maintains multi-view consistency. The two optimized models are then cascaded at inference: IP-Adapter produces the front view with text-driven modifications, and the multi-view diffusion model generates the remaining views, which are fed into LGM for 3D Gaussian prediction and subsequently converted to a NeRF and mesh.

## Results

On the DreamBooth3D evaluation set, Make-Your-3D achieves the highest CLIP R-Precision across all three CLIP variants (0.817 on ViT-B/16, 0.764 on ViT-B/32, 0.826 on ViT-L/14), outperforming DreamBooth3D (0.783/0.710/0.797) and MV DreamBooth (0.805/0.735/0.813). In a 30-participant user study, Make-Your-3D scores 9.05/10 on overall quality, 8.91 on subject fidelity, 8.70 on prompt fidelity, and 9.01 on multi-view consistency, all substantially ahead of both baselines. The full pipeline completes in ~5 minutes on a single RTX 3090, a 36× speedup over DreamBooth3D's 3-hour optimization on 4-core TPUv4.

## Baselines & Comparisons

Make-Your-3D outperforms both baselines on every reported metric. Against DreamBooth3D [40], the gains are large: CLIP R-Precision improves by ~0.03–0.05 across variants, and user-study scores jump from 5.33 to 9.05 in overall quality, 6.42 to 8.91 in subject fidelity, and 6.89 to 8.70 in prompt fidelity, while also being 36× faster (5 min vs. 3 h) and requiring only a single input image instead of 3–6. Against MV DreamBooth [47], the advantage is more moderate but consistent: CLIP R-Precision leads by ~0.01–0.03, and user-study scores are higher on all four axes (e.g., 9.05 vs. 7.59 overall quality, 8.91 vs. 7.55 subject fidelity). Qualitatively, the paper notes that MV DreamBooth tends to overfit to data-bias styles and produces less diverse outputs, whereas Make-Your-3D preserves subject identity while respecting text-driven modifications more faithfully. DreamBooth3D also fails on thin-structure objects (e.g., sunglasses) and limited view variation, cases where Make-Your-3D shows clear improvements.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper departs from DreamBooth3D's pipeline (DreamBooth fine-tuning + SDS-based NeRF optimization) by replacing both stages with a co-evolution framework that jointly adapts an adapter-based 2D personalized model (IP-Adapter) and a multi-view diffusion model (Wonder3D) to the subject distribution. Rather than naively cascading these pre-trained models—which the paper shows fails due to distribution variance between the wild subject and training data—the co-evolution loop uses each model's outputs to optimize the other (identity-aware optimization for the 2D model, subject-prior optimization for the multi-view model). The mesh extraction stage repurposes LGM's Gaussian prediction and Instant-NGP NeRF training, building on the broader image-to-3D literature (Magic123, DreamGaussian, Wonder3D) but applying it to the personalization setting.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The 36× speedup (5 minutes vs. 3+ hours) and single-image requirement make subject-driven 3D generation practically accessible for artists and content creators, removing the need for 3–6 carefully curated subject photos. However, quality remains bounded by the Stable Diffusion v1.5 backbone, and the method is limited to object-level personalization; the authors explicitly flag scene-level 3D personalization as a more challenging open problem. Whether the co-evolution framework generalizes to larger diffusion backbones (e.g., SDXL) or to multi-subject and scene-level settings remains to be demonstrated.
