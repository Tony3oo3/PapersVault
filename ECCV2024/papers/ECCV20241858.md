## Problem

Semantic scene completion requires inferring unobserved 3D semantic patterns from partial observations, but the high complexity of 3D spatial relations (26 inter-voxel neighbors vs. 8 in 2D) makes direct 3D pattern reasoning inherently difficult. Existing methods address this by scaling up multi-modality network capacity rather than reducing the fundamental reasoning burden, and the sparsity of depth-guided 2D-3D projected feature volumes further degrades completion quality.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ECCV20241858/cropped_page1_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method consumes a single RGB image (processed by a Swin Transformer backbone) and a TSDF volume, where the TSDF is used solely for computing distance-aware projection weights rather than being fed into a separate geometry sub-network. The output is a dense 3D voxel grid with per-voxel semantic class labels (13 categories) and binary occupied/empty geometry, evaluated at the resolution of the scene bounding box.

## Method

The SidePaint strategy decomposes 3D pattern completion into two stages: (1) a distance-aware 2D-3D projection that initializes occluded voxels with attenuated 2D image features weighted by their TSDF distance to observed surfaces, producing dense feature volumes instead of the sparse ones from standard depth-guided projection; and (2) a side-view context inpainter that slices the 3D volume into 2D maps along three orthogonal axes (top-down, left-right, front-back), applies independent 2D convolutions (3×3 kernels with dilations 1, 2, 3) to inpaint each slice, and fuses the three inpainted volumes via a 1×1×1 convolution. A feature enrichment module with dimensional decomposition residual blocks and 3D transpose convolutions sits between projection and inpainting to provide multi-view consistency priors. Training uses a cross-entropy loss on final predictions plus a pattern-matching loss that aligns intermediate encoder features with a copy module trained on ground-truth semantics, ensuring reliable semantic pattern priors for the 2D inpainting. The entire pipeline is a single-stream model without dual-branch multi-modality fusion.

## Results

On NYUv2, the SidePaint-based model achieves 52.5% SSC mIoU and 72.2% SC IoU, outperforming the previous best (FFNet, 44.4% mIoU) by 8.1 points. On NYUCAD, it reaches 64.6% SSC mIoU and 83.9% SC IoU, surpassing FFNet (57.4%) by 7.2 points. On SUNCG-RGBD, the model attains 89.9% SSC mIoU and 91.0% SC IoU, exceeding 3D-Sketch (70.4% mIoU) by 19.5 points. Notably, the method uses only RGB+TSDF (with TSDF used solely for distance-aware projection, not as a separate feature stream), yet matches or exceeds multi-modality approaches that employ additional geometry sub-networks.

## Baselines & Comparisons

The SidePaint model decisively outperforms all 16 compared baselines on SSC mIoU across all three benchmarks. On NYUv2, it leads FFNet (44.4%) and IMENet (44.2%) by over 8 points in mIoU, despite using a simpler single-stream architecture without a dedicated geometry feature sub-network. On NYUCAD, it surpasses FFNet (57.4%) and MFF (57.2%) by 7.2 and 7.4 points respectively, while on SUNCG-RGBD it exceeds 3D-Sketch (70.4%) by a remarkable 19.5 points. For scene geometry completion (SC IoU), the method is competitive but not always the best: on NYUv2 it trails MFF (73.1%) by 0.9 points, and on NYUCAD it is slightly below FFNet (85.5%) by 1.6 points, though it leads on SUNCG-RGBD (91.0% vs. 87.5%). The consistent large margin in semantic mIoU over geometry IoU suggests the SidePaint strategy's primary benefit lies in semantic pattern reasoning rather than geometric reconstruction.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper departs from the dominant dual-branch multi-modality paradigm in semantic scene completion (e.g., DDRNet, AICNet, FFNet) by proposing a single-stream framework where the core reasoning is performed in 2D rather than 3D. It builds on DDRNet's dimensional decomposition residual blocks for feature enrichment and adopts TSDF-based geometry, but its key conceptual departure is the SidePaint strategy: decomposing 3D pattern completion into 2D side-view context inpainting with dense distance-aware projections. While tri-plane feature learning methods [9,19] also leverage 2D views for 3D tasks, they process 2D clues as 3D spatial context through cross-view networks; SidePaint instead performs the actual pattern reasoning within each 2D slice, reducing the inter-voxel relation complexity from 26 to 8 neighbors.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The clean ablation showing that 2D inpainting consistently outperforms 3D inpainting (by 1.1–2.9% mIoU) and that the distance-aware projection only becomes effective when paired with 2D reasoning provides a principled argument for where to place the 'hard' reasoning in SSC pipelines. The single-stream simplicity (no separate geometry branch, no complex fusion) achieving SOTA suggests that much of the architectural complexity in prior SSC work may be compensating for the difficulty of 3D pattern reasoning rather than being fundamentally necessary. Open questions include whether this 2D-reasoning principle generalizes to outdoor/lidar settings, multi-view inputs, or dynamic scenes, and whether more sophisticated 2D inpainting (attention-based, iterative) could further close the gap.
