## Problem

Existing surgical desmoking methods either train on synthetic smoke (suffering a domain gap to real surgical smoke) or use unpaired single-image learning (ill-posed and unstable for dense smoke). No prior work exploits the temporal structure of real surgical videos, where frames captured before high-energy device activation are naturally clearer and can serve as self-supervision for subsequent smoky frames.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ECCV20241929/cropped_page1_idx1.webp)

## Input / Output

The method takes a sequence of smoky laparoscopic video frames (1080×1920 resolution) plus a pre-smoke (PS) reference frame—the frame captured immediately before high-energy device activation—as an additional input channel. The PS frame is consumed in two roles: as a spatially misaligned supervision target during training and as a reference feature input during both training and inference. The model processes frames sequentially via a unidirectional recurrent architecture for online (not offline) video desmoking, producing temporally consistent clean frames.

## Method

SelfSVD leverages the PS frame in two complementary ways: (1) as unaligned supervision, where a pre-trained PWC-Net estimates optical flow to warp the desmoking output toward the PS frame before computing an L1 reconstruction loss, tolerating spatial misalignment; and (2) as a reference input fed into a masked-ref generator that produces warped reference features, with an SSIM-based patch-level masking strategy and an L1 sparsity regularization term preventing the model from trivially copying reference content. The architecture is a unidirectional RNN with five modules (feature encoder, masked-ref generator, optical-flow alignment, fusion, and reconstruction), trained with a combined loss of deformation-based reconstruction, reference regularization, and adversarial (LSGAN) terms. A two-stage SelfSVD* variant further enhances supervision by running the pre-trained model on the PS frame to produce a cleaner target, then fine-tuning the network against this improved reference.

## Results

On the LSVD dataset, SelfSVD* achieves the best PSNR of 24.58 dB and SSIM of 0.6279, outperforming the strongest baseline BasicVSR++ (23.35 dB) by 1.23 dB. The lightweight variant SelfSVD*-S reaches 24.00 dB PSNR with only 169 GFLOPs and 1.92M parameters, running at 0.03 s per frame on 1080×1920 video. SelfSVD* also leads on all no-reference metrics: FADE 0.4193, NIQE 4.72, and PI 3.86, indicating superior perceptual quality and color fidelity.

## Baselines & Comparisons

SelfSVD* outperforms all 16 baselines on PSNR and SSIM. Among video methods, it beats BasicVSR++ [7] (the strongest competitor) by 1.23 dB PSNR and 0.0083 SSIM, and MAPNet [60] by 1.30 dB PSNR. Among single-image self-supervised methods, the best is Dehamer [19] at 23.13 dB, which SelfSVD* surpasses by 1.45 dB. Unpaired image methods (Desmoke-LAP [41] at 22.52 dB, RefineNet [66] at 22.87 dB) and unsupervised methods (DCP [21] at 17.77 dB, PSD [9] at 14.69 dB) trail by 2–10 dB. On no-reference metrics, SelfSVD* achieves the best FADE (0.4193) and PI (3.86), though DistentGAN [49] edges it slightly on NIQE (3.60 vs 4.72); however, DistentGAN over-processes and produces color distortion. The lightweight SelfSVD*-S (169 GFLOPs, 1.92M params, 0.03 s/frame) still exceeds all image-based baselines on PSNR while being 6–40× faster than video baselines.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper departs from two dominant paradigms in surgical desmoking: supervised methods trained on synthetic smoke (which suffer from domain gap) and unpaired single-image methods (which are ill-posed and unstable for dense smoke). It builds architecturally on the unidirectional recurrent network design from RBSR [58] and the residual block structure of BasicVSR++ [7], but introduces a fundamentally different training paradigm: self-supervised video desmoking that exploits the pre-smoke (PS) frame as both misaligned supervision and reference input. The deformation-based reconstruction loss using PWC-Net optical flow alignment, combined with a masking strategy and L1 regularization to prevent trivial solutions, represents a novel loss design not seen in prior desmoking or dehazing work.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The LSVD dataset is the first real-world laparoscopic surgery video desmoking benchmark, filling a clear gap that limits reproducibility and fair comparison in this domain. The PS-frame observation is a practical, domain-specific insight that makes self-supervised training feasible without any paired data, which is highly relevant for surgical settings where ground truth is unobtainable. However, the method's applicability is constrained to scenarios where a sufficiently clear pre-smoke frame exists and the video content does not change drastically; generalization to other smoke/haze types, longer surgical sequences, or settings without a clean reference frame remains open. The lightweight variant (SelfSVD-S) achieving competitive results at 169 GFLOPs suggests practical real-time deployment is feasible, but validation on diverse surgical procedures and institutions is still needed.
