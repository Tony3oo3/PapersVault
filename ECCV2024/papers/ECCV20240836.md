## Problem

Finetuned ControlNet for semantic image synthesis produces unrealistic sub-structures within large semantic regions and misaligns generated content with the provided label masks. The paper identifies the root cause as a distributional mismatch: the noised latents encountered during training do not follow the standard N(0,I) prior assumed at inference, causing the denoising trajectory to deviate from the learned manifold.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ECCV20240836/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method takes a color-coded semantic segmentation mask as input, which is downsampled via nearest-pixel selection to the latent grid resolution (H'×W') to index the pre-computed prior on a token-by-token basis. It produces a photorealistic RGB image decoded from the VQGAN latent space, at 1024×512 for Cityscapes and 512×512 for ADE20K. The entire inference procedure is training-free: only a set of N=10,000 reference images is needed to pre-compute the Gaussian prior statistics, and no further model weights are modified.

## Method

SCP-Diff introduces a Spatial-Categorical Joint Prior that replaces the standard N(0,I) initialization at inference. From N reference images, it computes per-(x, y, c) Gaussian statistics over VQGAN-encoded latent tokens, where (x, y) is a spatial position on the downsampled grid and c is the semantic class at that position, falling back to class-level statistics when local sample counts are too small. At inference, a latent is sampled from this joint prior, noised for μT steps (μ ∈ [0.8, 0.9] optimal), and then denoised through the finetuned ControlNet for only the remaining μT steps. The spatial component provides global scene-layout coherence and color diversity, while the categorical component ensures class-specific alignment with the mask; their combination yields state-of-the-art FID (10.53 on Cityscapes) without any retraining of the diffusion model.

## Results

SCP-Diff achieves FID 10.53 on Cityscapes, 12.66 on ADE20K, and 11.3 on COCO-Stuff, setting new state-of-the-art on all three benchmarks. On Cityscapes, the joint prior improves mIoU from 65.14 (normal prior) to 67.92 and reduces FID from 23.35 to 10.53, a 12.82-point FID reduction. On ADE20K, mIoU improves from 20.73 to 25.61 and FID drops from 20.58 to 12.66. The method is entirely training-free at inference, requiring only pre-computed Gaussian noise priors from 10,000 reference images.

## Baselines & Comparisons

SCP-Diff outperforms all 22 baselines on every dataset where it is evaluated. On Cityscapes, it achieves FID 10.5, nearly halving the FID of ControlNet (23.4) and more than quadrupling the improvement over the best GAN-based method ECGAN (44.5). On ADE20K, SCP-Diff's FID of 12.7 surpasses ControlNet (20.6) by 8 points and the previous best diffusion method SDM (27.5) by 15 points. On COCO-Stuff, applying the joint prior to FreestyleNet reduces FID from 14.4 to 11.3, beating all prior methods including ECGAN (15.7) and SDM (15.9). The method also improves mask alignment: on Cityscapes, mIoU rises from 65.14 (normal prior) to 67.92, and on ADE20K from 20.73 to 25.61, indicating the spatial-categorical joint prior better respects the input semantic layout. Notably, SCP-Diff is training-free at inference, requiring no retraining of the underlying ControlNet or FreestyleNet models.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper builds directly on ControlNet (Zhang et al., ICCV 2023) for dense conditional control over Stable Diffusion, and on FreestyleNet (Xue et al., CVPR 2023) for the COCO-Stuff experiments. It departs from the standard diffusion inference assumption that initial noise should be drawn from N(0,I) by introducing domain-specific noise priors computed from reference images, connecting to the broader line of work on noise tricks in diffusion models (FreeInit, PYoCo, FreeNoise) but specifically targeting the semantic image synthesis setting. The key conceptual departure is diagnosing the quality gap in finetuned ControlNet not as a training/architecture failure but as a distributional mismatch between the noised training data and the standard normal prior at inference, then bridging it with a training-free spatial-categorical joint prior.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The training-free nature of the fix—simply changing the inference initialization distribution—makes it immediately deployable on any finetuned ControlNet or similar conditional diffusion model without retraining, which is a significant practical advantage. The dramatic FID reduction (23.35 → 10.53 on Cityscapes, more than 2×) suggests that a substantial portion of the quality gap in conditional diffusion generation may be attributable to inference-time distribution mismatch rather than model capacity, an insight with implications beyond SIS. Limitations include a slight diversity reduction (LPIPS 0.56 vs. 0.59), the need to pre-compute priors from ~10K reference images, and the requirement to tune the denoising step fraction μ. Open questions include whether the joint prior generalizes to other dense conditional tasks (depth, edge, pose) and whether the marginal independence assumption across spatial tokens can be relaxed for further gains.
