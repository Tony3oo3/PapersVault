## Problem

Direct text-to-3D methods produce blurry textures and incorrect interactions for human-object scenes due to the scarcity of paired text-interaction training data and the inherent difficulty of jointly generating multiple concepts with complex spatial relationships. Existing HOI synthesis methods rely on ground-truth 3D mocap data, limiting them to narrow, lab-captured scenarios. This paper tackles zero-shot text-driven 3D HOI generation without requiring any 3D interaction supervision.

## Input / Output

The method takes a triplet of free-form text prompts specifying human appearance style, object appearance style, and interaction type (e.g., 'a policewoman', 'a motorcycle', 'riding'). It outputs two separate NeRF representations (H-NeRF for the human, O-NeRF for the object) that are alpha-composited into a unified 3D interaction scene. The human NeRF is additionally constrained by a COAP occupancy field derived from the estimated SMPL pose, and the object NeRF is constrained to avoid occupying the human body volume.

## Method

InterFusion is a two-stage framework. In stage 1, it builds a 55K pseudo-SMPL pose dataset by generating diverse interaction images with Stable Diffusion, estimating 3D poses via PIXIE, encoding multi-view renderings with CLIP, and clustering into a 2048-centroid codebook; at inference, a text query retrieves top-k poses and GPT-4V selects the final anchor pose. In stage 2, H-NeRF and O-NeRF are optimized separately via Score Distillation Sampling with DeepFloyd, using tailored text prompts for each component, while a COAP-based geometric loss forces the human to occupy the anchor pose volume and the object to avoid it. A novel camera tracing module adaptively repositions the camera toward the centroid of occupied voxels during optimization. A global interaction SDS term, computed on the alpha-composited rendering of both NeRFs, jointly refines the scene for spatial and semantic coherence, with gradient truncation toward the human model to prevent the object from dominating optimization.

## Results

On the 61-prompt evaluation set, InterFusion (Ours-HC) achieves a CLIP score of 0.3308 and a GPT-4V select rate of 65.57%, substantially outperforming all baselines. The most dramatic gain is in GPT-4V select, where InterFusion reaches 65.57% versus 11.48% for the best baseline (Magic3D), indicating far superior physical interaction correctness and object completeness. The CLIP score improvement over Magic3D (0.3179) is more modest at +0.013, but the object-centric ablation (Ours-OC, 0.3203 / 13.11%) confirms that pose-based geometric priors are the key driver of the large GPT-4V select gap.

## Baselines & Comparisons

InterFusion decisively outperforms all five baselines on both metrics. Against DreamFusion (CLIP 0.3027, GPT-4V 8.20%), Magic3D (0.3179, 11.48%), and TextMesh (0.2761, 1.64%), the full method gains +0.028, +0.013, and +0.055 in CLIP score respectively, while the GPT-4V select gap is far larger: +57.4, +54.1, and +63.9 percentage points. The object-centric ablation (Ours-OC) narrows the CLIP gap to only +0.010 but still lags by 52.5 points in GPT-4V select, confirming that pose-based geometric constraints are essential for physically plausible interactions. The paper notes that baselines suffer from multi-concept confusion (e.g., generating only the cap but not the person, or failing to produce the object entirely), whereas InterFusion's decomposed local-global optimization avoids these failure modes. Additional comparisons with MVDream and ProlificDreamer are deferred to the supplementary material.

## Relation to Prior Work

InterFusion builds directly on DreamFusion's Score Distillation Sampling as its core optimization mechanism and on COAP's neural occupancy representation for geometric constraints, but departs from both the HOI synthesis literature (Shape2Pose, PiGraphs, PSI, POSA, COINS) and the text-to-3D literature (DreamFusion, Magic3D, TextMesh) in a key way: it is the first to combine zero-shot text-driven generation with explicit 3D pose priors for multi-concept human-object scenes. Prior HOI methods require ground-truth 3D interaction data, while prior text-to-3D methods handle only single subjects; InterFusion bridges this gap by synthesizing a pose codebook from 2D diffusion-generated images (via PIXIE) and then performing a local-global NeRF optimization that separates human and object generation before jointly refining them.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

By eliminating the need for paired 3D interaction datasets, InterFusion opens a path toward generating arbitrary, diverse HOI scenes from natural language alone, which is practically important for VR/AR content creation and embodied AI. However, the method is currently limited to a single human-object pair, depends on the quality of 2D diffusion models (DeepFloyd) for both pose synthesis and SDS guidance, and the evaluation relies on a modest 61-prompt set with a somewhat subjective GPT-4V metric. Open questions include whether the pose-prior strategy generalizes to multi-object or sequential interactions, whether the approach scales to more complex scenes, and whether the reliance on synthesized 2D images for pose estimation introduces systematic biases that limit pose diversity.
