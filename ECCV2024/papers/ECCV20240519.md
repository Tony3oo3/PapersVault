## Problem

Existing multimodal layout generation methods cannot simultaneously condition on all three modalities—arbitrary background images, foreground images, and foreground texts—leaving designers without a unified tool for content-aware layout automation. Handcrafting such designs is skill-demanding and non-scalable to batch production, yet no prior method handles the full multimodal conditioning problem in a single framework.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ECCV20240519/cropped_page1_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method takes a background image of arbitrary resolution (H×W), a set of N foreground elements (each either a text string with a class label from {header, body, disclaimer, button} and a quantized length in [0,255], or an image patch), and a latent noise vector. It outputs N normalized bounding boxes parameterized as (y/H, x/W, h/H, w/W), one per foreground element, which are then used to overlay and render the elements onto the background. Box correspondences to input elements are given by the conditioning order, making evaluation matching trivial.

## Method

LayoutDETR reformulates background-conditioned layout generation as a detection problem, adopting DETR's ViT image encoder and transformer decoder but replacing the learnable object queries with foreground embeddings (concatenation of a noise embedding and either BERT text-string + class + length embeddings or DINO image features). Foreground elements interact via self-attention in the decoder while background features interact via cross-attention, enabling natural multimodal fusion. Training jointly optimizes a GAN objective (with both conditional and unconditional discriminators, each augmented by an auxiliary decoder for position-aware regularization), a VAE reconstruction + KL objective, a supervised gIoU loss, an auxiliary reconstructor loss for controllability, and overlap/misalignment regularity losses. Three variants (GAN, VAE, VAE-GAN) are provided, with the VAE-GAN variant performing best on complex multi-element layouts.

## Results

On the authors' Ad Banner Dataset (7,196 samples), LayoutDETR-GAN achieves the best realism across all four metrics: Layout FID 3.19, Layout KID 5.62×10⁻³, Image FID 27.35, and Image KID 8.31×10⁻⁵, outperforming the next-best baselines (LayoutGAN++ at 4.25/16.62/28.40/58.54). On accuracy, LayoutDETR-VAE reaches IoU 0.216 and DocSim 0.152, while LayoutDETR-GAN achieves IoU 0.208 and DocSim 0.151, both surpassing all baselines. On the CGL Chinese ad banner dataset, LayoutDETR-VAE-GAN leads with Layout FID 2.65 and Image FID 2.66; on CLAY mobile UI, LayoutDETR-GAN achieves Layout FID 1.84 and Image KID 11.19×10⁻⁵, the best in their respective categories.

## Baselines & Comparisons

LayoutDETR-GAN dominates all six baselines on every realism metric on the Ad Banner Dataset, with Layout FID 3.19 vs. the best baseline LayoutGAN++ at 4.25 (25% lower) and Layout KID 5.62 vs. LayoutTransformer at 13.87 (60% lower). On accuracy, LayoutDETR-VAE achieves the highest IoU (0.216) and DocSim (0.152), beating the best baselines READ (0.177/0.141) and Vinci (0.104/0.143) by substantial margins. On regularity, LayoutDETR-GAN is second-best: READ achieves lower overlap (0.093 vs. 0.101) and Vinci achieves lower misalignment (0.271 vs. 0.646), though the paper notes these margins are minor. On the CGL Chinese dataset, LayoutDETR-VAE-GAN is the best overall method, leading in Layout FID (2.65 vs. 5.11 LayoutTransformer) and Image FID (2.66 vs. 4.22 ICVT), while READ and Vinci lead in accuracy (IoU 0.269/0.266) but underperform significantly in realism. On CLAY, LayoutDETR-GAN wins 6 of 8 metrics including Layout FID (1.84 vs. 2.64 LayoutTransformer) and Overlap (0.083 vs. 0.099 READ), while LayoutDETR-VAE takes the accuracy lead (IoU 0.327, DocSim 0.123). In the user study, LayoutDETR-GAN is preferred over every baseline with p-values well below 0.05, with the largest margin against Vinci (62.6% preference, p=3×10⁻¹⁵) and LayoutTransformer (63.5%, p=2×10⁻¹⁷).

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper bridges two previously separate research threads: generative layout design (GANs, VAEs, autoregressive, and diffusion-based models) and object detection (specifically DETR's transformer encoder-decoder with gIoU supervision). It inherits the GAN/VAE/VAE-GAN training paradigms and auxiliary decoder techniques from LayoutGAN++ (Kikuchi et al., 2021) and the VAE-GAN joint optimization from Larsen et al. (2016), but departs from all prior layout generators by replacing freely-learnable object queries with multimodal foreground embeddings (BERT text features, DINO image features, class/length dictionaries) and by jointly optimizing detection-style gIoU loss with adversarial and reconstruction objectives. The key conceptual move is treating 'where to place foreground elements on a background' as a detection problem, which no prior layout method had explicitly done while also conditioning on foreground content.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

This is the first layout generator to simultaneously condition on background images, foreground images, and foreground texts with class/length metadata, filling a gap where prior methods handled only subsets of these modalities. The newly curated 7,196-sample English ad banner dataset with text bounding boxes, categories, and content annotations addresses a practical data scarcity that limited prior multimodal layout work. Limitations include scope restricted to 2D graphic layouts (ad banners, mobile UIs) rather than natural scenes or 3D, and the reliance on OCR + inpainting to separate foreground from background, which introduces pipeline fragility. Open questions include whether the DETR-based formulation scales to layouts with variable element counts or to open-domain backgrounds without curated training data.
