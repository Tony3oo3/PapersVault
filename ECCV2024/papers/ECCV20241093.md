## Problem

Cross-attention-based point cloud registration methods suffer from redundant feature interactions between semantically unrelated regions, leading to erroneous correspondences. Additionally, existing approaches rely solely on 3D geometry or basic color channels, overlooking the rich semantic information available in aligned 2D RGB images that could disambiguate geometrically similar but semantically distinct structures (e.g., a bed vs. a table).
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ECCV20241093/cropped_page1_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method consumes two partially overlapping point clouds (X ∈ R^{m×3}, Y ∈ R^{n×3}) together with their aligned RGB images (I_x, I_y ∈ R^{w×h×3}) and depth channels for 2D-to-3D projection. Point clouds are downsampled to ~1.5K points via KPConv, and 2D semantic features (~2K per cloud) are extracted by a frozen DINOv2 backbone and projected onto the sparse 3D points. The output is a rigid transformation (R* ∈ SO(3), t* ∈ R^3) estimated via 50K RANSAC iterations over coarse correspondences (0.1K–1K pairs), optionally refined by point-to-point ICP.

## Method

SemReg is a 2D-3D cross-modal framework whose core mechanism is a Gaussian Mixture Semantic Prior (GMSP): for each source point, cosine similarity with all target 2D-semantic features selects salient correspondences above threshold η, and a mixture of Gaussians (support radius r=0.1) centered at those salient target points encodes the semantic correlation field. This prior is injected into a Semantics Guided Feature Interaction module with two stages: (1) Semantics Embedded Self-attention fuses projected 2D features into KPConv local descriptors before standard self-attention, and (2) GMSP Guided Cross-attention multiplies the cross-attention score by the GMSP value θ_ij^x, suppressing interactions between semantically unrelated regions. A Semantics Constrained Matching module applies dual-softmax matching with an Iverson bracket that zeros the confidence score whenever both mutual GMSP values fall below τ_m, and a Semantics Aware Focal Loss restricts supervision to point pairs lying within the same semantic region (intersection of ground-truth matches and salient semantic correspondences), using α=0.25 and γ=2.

## Results

On 3DMatch, SemReg achieves a Registration Recall (RR) of 94.7% and Feature Matching Recall (FMR) of 98.6%, outperforming the previous best (SIRA-PCR at 93.6% RR) by 1.1%. On the more challenging 3DLoMatch (low-overlap) subset, SemReg reaches 75.9% RR and 89.8% FMR, surpassing GeoTransformer (75.0% RR) by 0.9%. After ICP refinement, the final registration rate improves to 95.4% (3DMatch) and 77.4% (3DLoMatch), with the best Relative Rotation Error (RRE) of 1.83° and 2.71° respectively.

## Baselines & Comparisons

SemReg achieves the highest RR on both 3DMatch (94.7%) and 3DLoMatch (75.9%), beating the closest competitors SIRA-PCR (93.6%/73.5%) and Lepard (93.5%/69.0%) by 1.1–1.2% and 0.9–6.9% respectively. On FMR, SemReg leads all methods with 98.6% (3DMatch) and 89.8% (3DLoMatch), surpassing RoITr (89.6%) and SIRA-PCR (88.8%) on the low-overlap set. However, SemReg's Inlier Ratio (56.2% on 3DMatch) is notably lower than fine-grained methods like RoITr (82.6%) and RoReg (81.6%), which the authors attribute to its coarse matching strategy producing fewer but more semantically reliable correspondences. After ICP refinement (Table 2), SemReg extends its lead, achieving 95.4% RR on 3DMatch (vs. Lepard 93.9%, GeoTransformer 91.8%) and 77.4% on 3DLoMatch (vs. GeoTransformer 74.5%, Lepard 71.3%), while also recording the best RRE (1.83°/2.71°). The prior cross-modal method PCR-CG [52] trails SemReg by 5.3% RR on 3DMatch and 9.6% on 3DLoMatch, confirming that explicit semantic priors substantially outperform simple color-channel concatenation.

## Relation to Prior Work

SemReg extends the 2D-3D cross-modal registration paradigm initiated by PCR-CG, which projected raw color channels onto 3D features, by instead extracting high-level semantic features from a frozen DINOv2 backbone and using them as a spatial prior. Architecturally, it follows the KPConv + self/cross-attention pipeline established by Predator and Lepard, but introduces a Gaussian Mixture Semantic Prior that modulates cross-attention weights to suppress interactions between semantically unrelated regions. The Semantics Aware Focal Loss and Semantics Constrained Matching modules further integrate this semantic signal into the supervision and matching stages, going beyond the geometric-only matching used in prior attention-based registrars.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The paper addresses a concrete failure mode in attention-based registration—redundant cross-attention between semantically unrelated regions—and shows that a lightweight 2D semantic prior can mitigate it with modest but consistent gains (1–2% RR) over strong baselines. A key limitation is the cross-modal requirement: SemReg needs aligned RGB images, restricting it to RGB-D capture or requiring a rendering step for standalone point clouds, which the authors flag as future work. The broader open question is whether 2D semantic priors can be made self-supervised or rendered from geometry alone, and whether the Gaussian mixture formulation generalizes to larger, more diverse outdoor scenes beyond the 10 m KITTI evaluation window.
