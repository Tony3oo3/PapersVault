## Problem

Multi-modal MAE pre-training for autonomous driving faces a fundamental alignment challenge: projecting LiDAR onto the image plane introduces geometric distortions, while BEV-based fusion discards the height dimension critical for accurate object representation. Existing approaches either align inputs before feature extraction (ignoring modality-specific characteristics) or use camera-to-LiDAR projection that loses camera features due to LiDAR sparsity, resulting in suboptimal cross-modal feature fusion.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ECCV20240580/cropped_page1_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method consumes LiDAR point clouds voxelized at 0.5m×0.5m×4m resolution within a [-50m,50m]×[-50m,50m]×[-5m,3m] perception range, and multi-camera images divided into non-overlapping patches. During pre-training, 70% of LiDAR voxels and 75% of image patches are randomly masked; the decoders reconstruct point counts per voxel (supervised by Chamfer distance + occupancy BCE) and pixel values (MSE). The final output is pre-trained encoder weights (SST for LiDAR, Swin-T for camera) plus the MMIM, which are transferred to downstream detectors (BEVFusion, TransFusion, FocalFormer3D) for 3D object detection and BEV map segmentation.

## Method

UniM²AE introduces two core designs: (1) a unified 3D volume space that extends BEV along the height (z) axis, where LiDAR voxel features are placed via ego-vehicle coordinates (distortion-free) and image features are projected via 2D-3D spatial cross-attention using camera intrinsics/extrinsics; (2) a Multi-modal 3D Interaction Module (MMIM) that concatenates both volume features along the channel dimension and processes them through 3 stacked 3D deformable self-attention blocks (128 input / 256 hidden channels) to model global context at high resolution. After interaction, the fused volume is split back into modality-specific features and projected to original token spaces for reconstruction. This unified representation preserves height information lost in standard BEV, enables direct remapping to original modalities without additional alignment, and the deformable attention mechanism adaptively focuses on salient features across the large 3D scene, accelerating convergence.

## Results

On the nuScenes validation set for 3D object detection, UniM²AE with pre-trained MMIM boosts BEVFusion-SST to 72.7 NDS / 69.7 mAP and FocalFormer3D to 73.8 NDS / 71.1 mAP, surpassing the strongest prior multi-modal baselines (MSMDFusion: 72.1 NDS; FocalFormer3D: 73.1 NDS). In the data-efficient setting (20% labeled data), UniM²AE achieves 52.8 NDS / 55.9 mAP, outperforming PiMAE (52.0 NDS / 52.5 mAP) and the GreenMIM+Voxel-MAE combination (51.2 NDS / 54.3 mAP). For BEV map segmentation, UniM²AE with pre-trained MMIM reaches 67.8 mIoU, exceeding the previous SOTA X-Align (65.7 mIoU) by 2.1 mIoU.

## Baselines & Comparisons

UniM²AE consistently outperforms all compared baselines across both 3D detection and BEV segmentation. In multi-modal 3D detection, it improves BEVFusion-SST by 1.2 NDS / 1.5 mAP (with pre-trained MMIM) and FocalFormer3D by 0.7 NDS / 0.6 mAP, while in the LiDAR-only setting it adds 0.5 NDS / 0.7 mAP over TransFusion-L-SST. In the data-efficient regime (20% labeled data), UniM²AE surpasses PiMAE—the prior SOTA multi-modal MAE—by 0.8 NDS and 3.4 mAP, and beats the independent GreenMIM+Voxel-MAE initialization by 1.6 NDS / 1.6 mAP. For BEV map segmentation, UniM²AE with pre-trained MMIM achieves 67.8 mIoU, exceeding X-Align (65.7 mIoU) by 2.1 mIoU and the camera-only BEVFusion baseline by 16.6 mIoU. The ablation in Table 4 further confirms that the 3D volume interaction space (65.2 NDS) outperforms BEV-space interaction (64.3 NDS) by 0.9 NDS, validating the height-preserving representation as a key differentiator over prior BEV-based fusion methods.

## Relation to Prior Work

UniM²AE extends the masked autoencoder paradigm (He et al., 2022) to the multi-modal autonomous driving setting, building directly on Voxel-MAE's LiDAR reconstruction targets (Chamfer distance + occupancy) and BEVFormer's 2D-3D spatial cross-attention for image-to-volume projection. Its key conceptual departure from PiMAE (Chen et al., 2023), the first multi-modal MAE, is the rejection of pixel-space alignment in favor of a unified 3D volume space that preserves the height dimension lost in BEV representations. The Multi-modal 3D Interaction Module adapts deformable self-attention (Deformable DETR) to operate over 3D voxel grids, enabling efficient cross-modal fusion at high resolution without the geometric distortion inherent in camera-plane projection.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The paper addresses a practical bottleneck in autonomous driving: the high cost of multi-sensor annotation. By showing that a unified 3D volume space outperforms both BEV and pixel-space alignment for multi-modal MAE, it provides a principled recipe for future multi-sensor pre-training that generalizes beyond camera-LiDAR to additional modalities. However, the authors acknowledge that random per-modality masking ignores inter-modal correlations and that temporal continuity in driving sequences is unexploited, both of which likely cap the achievable gains. The 6.5 mIoU jump on BEV segmentation with pre-trained MMIM is a strong practical signal, but the 1.2 NDS detection improvement is modest relative to the architectural complexity introduced.
