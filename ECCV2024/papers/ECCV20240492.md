## Problem

Panoramic segmentation, amodal (occlusion-aware) prediction, and unsupervised domain adaptation have each been addressed in isolation, but no prior work unifies all three challenges simultaneously. Existing panoramic methods cannot reason about occluded objects, while amodal segmentation methods fail to generalize to distorted 360° imagery, and neither handles the domain gap between label-rich pinhole and label-scarce panoramic data in a seamless manner.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ECCV20240492/cropped_page1_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The model takes a single 360° equirectangular panoramic RGB image (2048×400) as input and simultaneously produces five segmentation maps: per-pixel semantic labels, class-agnostic instance masks, amodal instance masks (full object extent including occluded regions), panoptic segmentation, and amodal panoptic segmentation. Training is formulated as UDA from pinhole KITTI360-APS (1408×376, 12,320 labeled images) to unlabeled panoramic BlendPASS (2,000 training + 100 test images), with a top-down Mask R-CNN-style decoder used for both instance and amodal instance branches since contour-based bottom-up decoders are impractical when a single pixel belongs to multiple overlapping objects.

## Method

UnmaskFormer is a four-stage vision transformer backbone with three key designs. First, an Unmasking Attention (UA) block replaces standard self-attention by appending a global-average-pooling-based query that produces a sigmoid-gated occlusion-aware mask, which is element-wise multiplied with the self-attended features to explicitly model occluded regions. Second, Deformable Patch Embedding layers are interleaved into Stages 2 and 4 (rather than only the early stage as in Trans4PASS), providing reinforced distortion-aware modeling across the network. Third, an Amodal-oriented Mix (AoMix) augmentation reconstructs masked source images using randomly scaled and padded amodal instance masks, then pastes selected semantic-class regions onto target images, simultaneously bridging the pinhole-to-panoramic domain gap and teaching the model to reconstruct invisible object parts. Training combines source-domain supervised losses with confidence-weighted self-training pseudo-labels under a Mean-Teacher framework, and a custom OAFusion module merges the three branches into five outputs by disregarding overlapping regions during amodal classification to avoid misassigning occluded objects to the occluder's category.

## Results

On the KITTI360-APS → BlendPASS benchmark, UnmaskFormer achieves state-of-the-art OASS performance with 43.66% mIoU (semantic segmentation) and 26.58% mAPQ (amodal panoptic segmentation), outperforming the best UDA panoptic baseline EDAPS by +3.49% mIoU and +3.44% mAPQ. On the SynPASS panoramic semantic segmentation benchmark, UnmaskFormer reaches 45.34% mIoU, surpassing Trans4PASS (44.80%). On DensePASS, it achieves 48.08% mIoU with only 13.96M parameters, outperforming Trans4PASS (45.89%) and DATR (42.22%).

## Baselines & Comparisons

UnmaskFormer outperforms all baselines on the primary BlendPASS benchmark. Against EDAPS (the strongest UDA panoptic segmentation method), it gains +3.49% mIoU and +3.44% mAPQ, with particularly large improvements on safety-critical classes like pedestrians (+1.85% mIoU), trucks (+14.95% mIoU), and two-wheelers (+2.69% mIoU). Compared to Trans4PASS (the leading distortion-aware panoramic transformer), UnmaskFormer improves mIoU by +3.00% and mAPQ by +6.32%, demonstrating that its interleaved DPE placement in Stages 2 and 4 plus the UA block handles both distortion and occlusion more effectively. On DensePASS, UnmaskFormer (48.08% mIoU, 13.96M params) beats Trans4PASS (45.89%, 13.93M) by +2.19% and DATR (42.22%, 14.72M) by +5.86% while using fewer parameters. On SynPASS, the gain over Trans4PASS is more modest (+0.54% mIoU), suggesting the occlusion-aware designs contribute most in the amodal/panoptic setting rather than pure semantic segmentation. UniDAPS, which uses a contour-based bottom-up decoder incompatible with amodal prediction, is excluded from APS comparisons and trails UnmaskFormer by 5.20% mIoU on SS.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper unifies three previously separate research threads—distortion-aware panoramic segmentation (Trans4PASS/DPE, DATR), amodal instance/panoptic segmentation (KINS, Amodal Panoptic Segmentation), and domain-adaptive panoptic segmentation (EDAPS, UniDAPS)—into a single task called OASS. Architecturally, it builds on the SegFormer four-stage transformer backbone and Mask R-CNN's top-down proposal decoder, but rearranges the Deformable Patch Embedding into Stages 2 and 4 (rather than the early stage used in Trans4PASS) and inserts a novel Unmasking Attention block that couples self-attention with a global-average-pooling query to produce occlusion-aware features. The AoMix data augmentation extends the class-mix strategy from DAFormer/EDAPS by conditioning on amodal instance masks, so that masked-image modeling explicitly targets occluded object regions rather than arbitrary patches.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

By formalizing OASS and releasing BlendPASS (2,000 training + 100 pixel-annotated test panoramas with amodal instance and panoptic labels), the paper provides the first benchmark where FoV expansion, in-scene occlusion reasoning, and cross-domain adaptation must be solved jointly, a setting that is directly relevant to 360° autonomous-driving perception. The ablations confirm that each component (UA, AoMix, OAFusion) contributes meaningfully, and the OAFusion design—disregarding overlapping regions during majority voting—fixes a concrete failure mode of standard panoptic fusion under heavy occlusion. However, the absolute mAPQ of 26.58% and the small 100-image test set leave open whether the unified framework generalizes to more diverse scenes, larger category sets, or video settings, and whether the amodal reconstruction quality is sufficient for downstream 3D reasoning tasks.
