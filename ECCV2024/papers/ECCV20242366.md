## Problem

Encoding 2D image information into 3D representations is a critical but under-studied operation in 3D computer vision. Existing approaches either rely on large pretrained 2D feature extractors (e.g., DinoV2) that are expensive and limited to specific 3D representations like voxel grids, or use memory-heavy cross-attention to inject 2D features into 3D backbones. No systematic, representation-agnostic encoding mechanism exists that works for arbitrary 3D neural representations (MLPs, hash-grids, triplanes, voxel grids) without requiring a pretrained image encoder.

## Input / Output

The method consumes k source RGB images along with their corresponding camera intrinsics/extrinsics (4×4 matrices) and produces a set of 3D Radiance-Field parameters ζ_enc that parameterize the scene function g(c,d) mapping 3D coordinates and viewing directions to density and RGB radiance. The encoding is defined relative to a zero-origin initialization of the chosen representation, making it a residual-like signal rather than an absolute feature map. In the GOEmbedFusion pipeline, these encodings condition a DiT-based denoiser that outputs full 3D scene parameters for generation or reconstruction.

## Method

GOEmbed defines the 3D encoding as the negative gradient of the MSE between renders (from a zero-initialized 3D representation) and ground-truth 2D views, backpropagated through a differentiable Emission-Absorption volume raymarcher with respect to the zero-origin parameters. This makes the mechanism representation-agnostic by design: any 3D parameterization supporting differentiable rendering can be encoded, with only ~17K learnable parameters in the renderer versus ~1B in pretrained 2D feature extractors. GOEmbedFusion wraps this encoding in a diffusion-with-forward-models pipeline using a DiT denoiser in the x_start formulation, where a pseudo-deterministic clean estimate ζ̂₀ is first predicted from noise conditioned on the GOEmbed, then corrupted via standard DDPM forward diffusion before final denoising. Training combines three losses (L_GOEmbed for encoding information, L_PSE-DET for pseudo-deterministic quality, L_GOEmbedFusion for the diffusion objective) with classifier-free guidance (0.5 dropout probability) to support both conditional and unconditional sampling.

## Results

On the OmniObject3D 3D generation benchmark, GOEmbedFusion achieves a new SOTA FID of 22.12 and KID of 0.6, outperforming the prior best DiffTF (FID 25.36, KID 0.8) and the DFM framework (FID 73.51, KID 3.8). In the Plenoptic Encoding experiment, GOEmbed outperforms the DinoV2 cost-volume baseline on Triplanes (PSNR 15.919 vs 14.711 with 4 source views) and uniquely supports MLP representations where cost-volume approaches are inapplicable. For sparse-view 3D reconstruction, the GOEmbed reconstruction model achieves PSNR 27.650 and SSIM 0.900, substantially exceeding the LRM 6-layer (PSNR 23.788) and 16-layer (PSNR 23.247) baselines.

## Baselines & Comparisons

GOEmbedFusion decisively outperforms all seven baselines across the three experimental settings. In 3D generation, it beats the prior SOTA DiffTF by 3.24 FID points (22.12 vs 25.36) and 0.2 KID (0.6 vs 0.8), while achieving a 51.39 FID improvement over DFM (73.51) and a 19.44 FID improvement over EG3D (41.56). The gap over GET3D (FID 49.41) and DiffRF (FID 147.59) is even more pronounced. In Plenoptic Encoding, GOEmbed surpasses the DinoV2 cost-volume approach on Triplanes (PSNR 15.919 vs 14.711 at 4 views) and matches it closely on Voxel-Grids (15.755 vs 15.834), while being the only method that can encode into MLPs (PSNR 11.599). In sparse-view reconstruction, GOEmbed recon (PSNR 27.650, SSIM 0.900) outperforms both LRM variants by over 3.8 PSNR, approaching the Triplane SSO upper bound of 28.165.

## Relation to Prior Work

GOEmbed draws conceptual inspiration from Gradient Origin Networks (GON) but repurposes the gradient-of-loss idea for 3D scene encoding rather than latent compression. It departs from the dominant cost-volume paradigm (DinoV2 features un-projected into voxel grids or triplanes) by eliminating the need for any pretrained 2D feature extractor, instead backpropagating through a differentiable Emission-Absorption renderer from a zero-initialized 3D representation. In the generative modeling context, GOEmbedFusion is a novel realization of the DFM (Diffusion with Forward Models) framework, replacing DFM's voxel-grid-specific feature extractor with the representation-agnostic GOEmbed mechanism and removing the expensive auto-regressive sampling step.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The representation-agnostic encoding mechanism addresses a genuine practical gap: prior 2D-to-3D encoding methods are tied to specific representations (voxel grids, triplanes) and cannot handle MLPs or hash-grids without custom engineering. The finding that a ~17K-parameter renderer can transfer roughly 50% of the information achievable by single-scene overfitting—without any large pretrained 2D network—suggests a simpler, more general path for 3D feature extraction. Open questions include whether the approach extends to non-radiance-field representations (meshes, point clouds, 3DGS), how to eliminate the observed checkerboard artifacts (hypothesized to stem from DiT positional encodings), and whether the method scales to larger, more diverse datasets beyond OmniObject3D.
