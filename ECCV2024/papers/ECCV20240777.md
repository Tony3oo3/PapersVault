## Problem

Real-world super-resolution faces unknown degradations in LR inputs without paired training data, and existing blind generalist models sacrifice performance on specific degradations for broad generalization. This paper addresses the gap of adapting a specialist model (trained on a known synthetic degradation) to a target real-world degradation in an unsupervised manner, without requiring paired data or explicit degradation modeling.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ECCV20240777/cropped_page1_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method takes 48×48 LR patches (×4 SR) with unknown real-world degradation as unlabeled inputs, alongside synthetic LR counterparts generated by bicubic-downsampling HR images from DF2K as labeled inputs. It produces 192×192 HR reconstructions, with a post-hoc color correction step that normalizes per-channel mean and variance to match the input. Evaluation uses full images or 512×512 non-overlapping crops at ×4 scale on RealSR, DRealSR, and NTIRE20.

## Method

The core contribution is Pairwise Distance Distillation (PDD), which adapts a specialist M_S to real-world degradation by enforcing two consistencies in VGG19 feature space: (1) intra-model distance distillation, where the ℓ₁ distance between M_S's synthetic and real-world predictions (d_S) is matched to the corresponding distance in M_G (d_G) via a Cross-Entropy loss, and (2) inter-model distance distillation, where the Gram matrix of VGG feature differences between M_S and M_G predictions is made consistent across synthetic and real-world domains via a Frobenius norm loss. Two configurations are provided: a Static version (M_G frozen, M_S initialized as a bicubic specialist) and an EMA version (both initialized from a generalist, with M_G maintained as an exponential moving average of M_S). Training jointly optimizes supervised losses (Haar wavelet ℓ₁, VGG perceptual, and GAN losses) on synthetic data and the unsupervised PDD losses plus GAN loss on real-world data, effectively pushing the specialist's real-world predictions toward the low-level characteristics of its high-quality synthetic predictions.

## Results

On NTIRE20-Valid, RealESRGAN+Ours achieves 26.42 dB PSNR (+1.34 over RealESRGAN) and 6.2263 NRQM, while BSRGAN+Ours reaches 26.37 dB PSNR (+0.93) and 6.2781 NRQM. Across all five real-world domains (NTIRE20, RealSR-Canon, RealSR-Nikon, DRealSR-Panasonic, DRealSR-Olympus), the method consistently improves PSNR by 0.56–1.34 dB over the base generalist while maintaining or improving LPIPS and NRQM perceptual scores. A user study with 30 evaluators confirms that at least 65% of voters prefer the proposed method over RealESRGAN, BSRGAN, and FeMaSR.

## Baselines & Comparisons

DAN, EDAN, and DASR achieve the highest PSNR/SSIM on most datasets (e.g., DAN reaches 28.99 dB on DRealSR-Panasonic) but produce visibly blurry outputs with NRQM scores of only 2.8–5.9, far below the proposed method's 5.4–6.3. FeMaSR achieves competitive NRQM (6.0–6.3) but lags significantly in fidelity (PSNR 23.5–25.0 vs. 25.9–27.9 for the proposed method) and produces high-frequency artifacts noted in qualitative comparisons. Compared to the base generalists, the proposed method improves PSNR by +0.56 to +1.34 dB (e.g., RealESRGAN 25.08→26.42 on NTIRE20) while maintaining or slightly improving LPIPS and NRQM. Against RealESRGAN+TG, the proposed method wins on PSNR (26.42 vs. 25.56) and NRQM (6.2263 vs. 5.7926) on NTIRE20, confirming that pairwise distance distillation outperforms the multi-task learning alternative. The EMA version consistently outperforms the static version across all metrics, with the largest gains on NRQM (e.g., +0.236 on RealSR-Nikon vs. +0.120 for static).

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper repurposes knowledge distillation—originally developed for model compression [10] and applied to high-level tasks like segmentation domain adaptation [6,38]—for the first time in unsupervised real-world super-resolution. It departs from the two dominant RWSR strategies: (1) training generalist models on complex synthetic degradation pipelines (RealESRGAN, BSRGAN) and (2) image-to-image translation for domain adaptation (CycleGAN-based methods). Instead, it introduces a pairwise distance distillation framework that combines a degradation-specific specialist with a generalist by enforcing consistency of intra-model and inter-model VGG feature distances, bypassing the need to explicitly model or replicate real-world degradations.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The framework offers a practical, modular recipe for improving any off-the-shelf SR model on a specific real-world degradation domain without paired data, which is a common deployment scenario (e.g., a fixed camera pipeline). A notable finding is that naive distillation (directly imitating the generalist's output) degrades perceptual quality substantially while preserving fidelity, highlighting that the *relationship* between predictions carries more useful signal than the predictions themselves. Open questions include whether the approach extends to multiple simultaneous degradation domains, video super-resolution, or settings where no suitable specialist pre-trained model exists for the target degradation.
