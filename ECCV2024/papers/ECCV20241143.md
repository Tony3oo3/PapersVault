## Problem

Existing 3D multimodal LLMs rely on 2D image renderings of point clouds, which suffer from visual hallucination and fail to capture precise 3D geometry needed for embodied interaction tasks like 6-DoF pose estimation. Moreover, no prior work provides a unified 3D LLM that handles both general semantic understanding and physically-grounded embodied interaction (e.g., part-level spatial reasoning, task decomposition) through a single language interface, and no comprehensive benchmark exists to evaluate such capabilities.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ECCV20241143/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

SHAPELLM takes a 3D point cloud (potentially single-view and occluded, as from real-world RGB-D sensors) and a natural-language instruction as input, and produces free-form text outputs including 3D captions, VQA answers, step-by-step embodied task plans, and 6-DoF bounding boxes serialized as eight 3D corner coordinates in canonical object space (e.g., [x1,y1,z1],…,[x8,y8,z8]). During training, multi-view RGB images and depth maps are rendered from the point cloud to provide distillation targets, but at inference only the raw point cloud is required.

## Method

The core encoder, RECon++, extends RECon by introducing multi-view image query tokens that are matched to rendered view features (RGB + depth) via a DETR-style bipartite matching with the Hungarian algorithm, which implicitly learns pose-invariant alignment and enables selective distillation of complementary multi-level geometry. The encoder concatenates local point-cloud encoder features with global decoder features, and the resulting representation is linearly projected into the LLM (LLaMA) alongside absolute position encodings derived from 3D coordinates, with prefix-tuning prompts modulating the two streams. Training proceeds in two stages: self-supervised pretraining of RECon++ on ShapeNet/Objaverse/ABO/3D-FUTURE using contrastive-reconstruction objectives, followed by instruction-tuning of the full SHAPELLM on ~45K GPT-4V-generated Q&A pairs from Objaverse-LVIS and ~30K embodied part-understanding samples from GAPartNet. The model is evaluated on the newly introduced 3D MM-Vet benchmark (59 objects, 232 human-written QA pairs across four capability levels) and its corruption variant 3D MM-Vet-C.

## Results

On the newly proposed 3D MM-Vet benchmark, SHAPELLM-13B achieves 53.1% total accuracy, surpassing the previous best 3D point cloud method PointLLM-13B (46.6%) by +6.5%, with particularly strong embodied interaction performance (68.4% vs. 50.9%). The RECon++ encoder achieves state-of-the-art fine-tuned accuracy of 95.25% on ScanObjectNN PB_T50_RS (+1.85% over PointGPT-L) and 95.0% on ModelNet40 8kP. In zero-shot open-world recognition, RECon++-L reaches 53.7% Top-1 on Objaverse-LVIS and 65.4% on ScanObjectNN, outperforming Uni3D-L by +0.6% and +7.2% respectively.

## Baselines & Comparisons

Among 3D point cloud methods, SHAPELLM-13B decisively outperforms PointLLM-13B by +6.5% total on 3D MM-Vet, with the largest gap in Embodied Interaction (+17.5%) and Language Generation (+15.1%), while PointLLM-13B retains a slight edge in Spatial Awareness (+0.1%). SHAPELLM-7B similarly beats PointLLM-7B by +6.2% total, driven by a +15.8% advantage in Embodied Interaction. Against 2D image-based methods, SHAPELLM-13B (53.1%) still trails GPT-4V with 4-view images (63.4%) by ~10%, but surpasses LLaVA-13B (47.9%) and DreamLLM-7B (50.3%) while using only a single 3D point cloud input. For the RECon++ encoder, fine-tuned results on ScanObjectNN PB_T50_RS show RECon++-L (95.25%) beating PointGPT-L (93.4%) by +1.85% and RECon (91.26%) by +3.99%. In zero-shot recognition, RECon++-L leads Uni3D-L by +0.6% on Objaverse-LVIS and +7.2% on ScanObjectNN, while matching or exceeding MixCon3D and TAMM across all three benchmarks. On robustness (3D MM-Vet-C), SHAPELLM-13B degrades less than PointLLM-13B under single-view occlusion (43.6% vs. 41.3%) and jitter (47.8% vs. 42.3%), confirming stronger real-world applicability.

## Relation to Prior Work

ShapeLLM extends the RECon 3D encoder (Qi et al., ICML 2023) into RECon++ by introducing multi-view image distillation with Hungarian-algorithm-based bipartite matching (borrowed from DETR), moving beyond the single-view distillation paradigm used by prior 3D representation methods like ULIP and OpenShape. On the LLM side, it follows the LLaVA instruction-tuning recipe but replaces 2D image inputs with 3D point clouds, departing from concurrent 3D-LLM efforts (PointLLM, PointBind&LLM) that either use rendered images or lack embodied interaction data. The key conceptual departure is treating 3D geometry as a first-class input for language-grounded physical interaction (6-DoF pose output, task decomposition) rather than merely semantic recognition.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

This is the first 3D MLLM explicitly targeting embodied interaction, and the 3D MM-Vet benchmark (59 objects, 232 Q&As) provides a much-needed evaluation axis for spatial awareness and embodied task planning that prior 3D VQA benchmarks lack. However, the benchmark's small scale limits statistical confidence, and the embodied interaction data is drawn from a narrow set of indoor articulated furniture (GAPartNet), leaving open whether the 6-DoF grounding capability generalizes to open-world manipulation. A key open question is whether the text-based coordinate output format scales to high-precision robotic control or remains a coarse approximation, and whether the multi-view distillation gains persist when pretraining data is further scaled.
