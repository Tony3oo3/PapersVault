## Problem

Existing inpainting-based text-to-texture methods (e.g., TEXTure, Text2Tex) propagate semantics only locally across sequentially generated views, causing accumulated semantic divergence and occlusion artifacts on sparse views. This leads to unsatisfactory long-range consistency across the entire 3D object, particularly for shapes requiring high global coherence such as cylindrical objects.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ECCV20242290/cropped_page1_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method takes a free-form text prompt and a 3D mesh (with UV mapping computed via Xatlas) as input, and produces a UV-mapped texture atlas as output. The texture is internally represented as a coordinate-to-color mapping via Instant-NGP, rendered at 512×512 resolution for the final generation stage. The pipeline is iterative, requiring up to 10 adaptively selected viewpoints, and the generation is conditioned on per-pixel depth maps extracted via differentiable rasterization (nvdiffrast).

## Method

P3G introduces a two-stage pipeline. First, a coarse but globally consistent guidance texture is learned by optimizing an Instant-NGP texture model with Score Distillation Sampling using a depth-conditioned Stable Diffusion model, progressing from latent-space optimization (64×64) to RGB-space optimization (512×512) via a point-wise latent-to-RGB projection. Second, during high-quality multi-view image generation, the denoising process is hijacked at a late time step (τ=500) by replacing the foreground region with the guidance texture rendering, thereby injecting globally consistent semantics while preserving the diffusion model's detail-generation ability in the remaining denoising steps. A complementary inpainting mechanism (alternating between a depth-conditioned denoiser and an inpainting-specific denoiser) maintains local continuity with previously generated regions. A novel view selection strategy dynamically picks the viewpoint that maximizes coverage of ungenerated faces above a distortion threshold, reducing the number of views needed and encouraging large contiguous generation areas.

## Results

On 410 meshes from Objaverse, P3G achieves the best FID (58.0), KID (20.3), CLIP-IQA (46.17), and CLIP Variance (8.13) among all compared methods, while maintaining a competitive CLIP Score of 25.53 (vs. 25.66 for TEXTure). In the user study on ~50 meshes, P3G reduces perceived inconsistency from 23.63 to 15.55 and increases overall quality preference from 36.23 to 42.45 compared to TEXTure. The ablation baseline without guidance (FID 60.1, CLIP Variance 10.20) confirms that the pseudo 3D guidance is the primary driver of the consistency improvement.

## Baselines & Comparisons

P3G outperforms TEXTure (SIGGRAPH'23) on all quality and consistency metrics: FID drops from 63.7 to 58.0, KID from 23.7 to 20.3, CLIP-IQA rises from 40.19 to 46.17, and CLIP Variance decreases from 9.28 to 8.13, while the CLIP Score remains nearly identical (25.53 vs. 25.66), confirming that the guidance does not sacrifice text alignment. Against Text2Tex (ICCV'23), P3G wins decisively on visual quality (CLIP-IQA 46.17 vs. 35.48, FID 58.0 vs. 59.1) and also achieves better consistency (CLIP Variance 8.13 vs. 8.82), though Text2Tex's simpler textures give it a deceptively competitive consistency score. The user study further confirms P3G's advantage over TEXTure, with a 34% reduction in reported inconsistency (15.55 vs. 23.63) and a 17% increase in quality preference (42.45 vs. 36.23). The internal ablation (removing guidance) shows that without the pseudo 3D guidance, consistency degrades substantially (CLIP Variance 10.20 vs. 8.13), isolating the guidance as the key contribution.

## Relation to Prior Work

P3G extends the multi-view inpainting-based texturing paradigm established by TEXTure and Text2Tex, which propagate semantics locally from previously generated views. The key departure is the introduction of a 'pseudo 3D guidance' texture learned via Score Distillation Sampling (from DreamFusion) with a depth-conditioned diffusion model, which provides a globally consistent semantic anchor that constrains the high-quality generation process. The latent-to-RGB optimization strategy is adapted from Latent-NeRF, and the inpainting mechanism follows Repaint's denoising-diffusion approach. Conceptually, the paper shifts the consistency problem from iterative local propagation (which accumulates divergence) to explicit global semantic anchoring via a coarse but coherent 3D representation.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The paper addresses a fundamental and practically important limitation of inpainting-based 3D texturing: accumulated semantic divergence over many views leads to visible long-range inconsistencies, particularly on objects with repetitive or cylindrical geometry. The pseudo 3D guidance concept is a clean, generalizable principle—using a low-fidelity but globally coherent signal to constrain a high-fidelity local generator—that could transfer to other 3D generation or editing tasks. Limitations include the two-stage computational overhead, the reliance on SDS which inherently produces lower-quality guidance, and evaluation limited to single-object meshes from Objaverse. Open questions include whether the approach scales to multi-object scenes, whether the guidance quality ceiling limits final texture fidelity, and whether the method generalizes to open-ended or multi-object text prompts.
