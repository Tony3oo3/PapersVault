## Problem

Existing post-training backdoor defenses require 1–5% clean training data, which is often inaccessible in real-world deployments, and none can simultaneously achieve backdoor robustness and meaningful model compression in a data-free manner. The only prior data-free method (CLP) can prune only a tiny fraction of channels before clean accuracy collapses, precluding any practical compression benefit.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ECCV20241604/cropped_page4_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method consumes only the weights of a suspicious (potentially backdoored) DNN—no training data, no knowledge of the training process, and no external unlabeled data are required. It outputs a purified model in low-rank Tucker-2 factorized form (core tensor + two factor matrices per convolutional layer) at a user-specified compression ratio (2×–4×), with only batch normalization parameters updated during the final fine-tuning stage.

## Method

C&C exploits the observation that rank components of convolutional weight tensors associated with high normalized singular values (obtained via Tucker-2 decomposition) are disproportionately sensitive to backdoor triggers. It first scales down the contribution of those sensitive rank components using a threshold τ_scale on normalized singular values, effectively suppressing the trigger-induced activation gap. It then truncates the remaining rank components below a global singular-value cutoff to achieve low-rank compression. To recover clean accuracy lost during scaling and truncation, the method generates synthetic data via a modified ZeroQ objective (matching batch-norm statistics plus an inception loss) and fine-tunes only the batch-norm layers through knowledge distillation from the original poisoned model, while subtracting a penalty term on adversarially perturbed inputs to prevent backdoor transfer.

## Results

On ResNet-18 / CIFAR-10, C&C (data-free) achieves an average clean accuracy of 91.82% and attack success rate of 2.18% across six all-to-one backdoor attacks, and 92.45% ACC / 1.64% ASR across four all-to-all attacks. When jointly compressing the model, C&C maintains 90–93% ACC at 2×–4× compression ratios (reducing parameters from 11.17M to 2.78M), whereas ANP and CLP collapse to ~10% ACC at just 2× compression. Across GTSRB and CIFAR-100, C&C consistently outperforms the data-free CLP baseline by 2–4% ACC while matching or lowering ASR.

## Baselines & Comparisons

C&C outperforms all four baselines on average ACC while requiring no training data. Against the data-free CLP (the closest competitor), C&C gains ~2.5% ACC (91.82 vs 89.33) and slightly lower ASR (2.18 vs 2.35) on all-to-one attacks, and ~3.2% ACC (92.45 vs 89.26) on all-to-all attacks. Against ANP (1% clean data), C&C improves ACC by ~1.3% (91.82 vs 90.53) and reduces ASR from 2.26% to 2.18% on all-to-one, with a larger gap on all-to-all (92.45 vs 90.05 ACC). NAD achieves comparable ACC to C&C on all-to-one (91.43 vs 91.82) but has a notably higher ASR (4.68 vs 2.18) and requires 1% clean data. I-BAU is the weakest baseline, with average ACC of only 83.20% and ASR of 38.49% on all-to-one attacks, far behind C&C. In the joint purification + compression setting (Table 2), C&C is the only method that maintains >90% ACC at 2×–4× compression, while ANP and CLP drop to ~10% ACC at 2×.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper departs from the dominant neuron/channel-level sensitivity paradigm in post-training backdoor defense (NAD, ANP, I-BAU, CLP) by proposing rank-level singular values from Tucker-2 decomposition as the sensitivity metric. It builds on Tucker-2 tensor factorization for the decomposition machinery and on ZeroQ-style synthetic data generation for the fine-tuning stage, but repurposes both in a novel way: the same singular value information that identifies backdoor-sensitive rank components also directly enables low-rank compression. The key conceptual departure from CLP (the only prior data-free method) is shifting from channel pruning to rank scaling, which avoids the catastrophic accuracy collapse that pruning causes at even modest compression ratios.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

C&C addresses a genuinely practical gap: defenders in deployment scenarios often have neither training data nor the ability to retrain, yet still need both a clean model and a compact one for resource-constrained devices. The 2-in-1 purification-plus-compression capability is unique among existing defenses. However, limitations remain: evaluation is confined to small-scale datasets (CIFAR-10/100, GTSRB) and CNN architectures, the fine-tuning only updates batch-norm layers (limiting recovery capacity), and the method's behavior on transformer-based models or larger-scale benchmarks (ImageNet) is untested. An open question is whether the singular-value sensitivity finding generalizes beyond convolutional weight tensors to attention matrices or other parameter structures.
