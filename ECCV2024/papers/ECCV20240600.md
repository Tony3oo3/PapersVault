## Problem

Short-Term Object Interaction Anticipation (STA) requires simultaneously predicting the next-active object's bounding box, noun and verb categories, and time-to-contact from egocentric video, yet prior approaches yielded only incremental gains over baselines. No architecture was explicitly designed for the image-video input pair that defines the STA task, and predictions lacked grounding in the consistent interaction patterns humans exhibit across similar environments.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ECCV20240600/cropped_page1_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The model consumes a high-resolution last-frame image I_T and a low-resolution video clip of t preceding frames, processed by separate 2D (DINOv2) and 3D (TimeSformer) encoders. The interaction hotspot sub-module additionally ingests hand and object detection coordinates and features. Output is a set of detections, each a tuple of bounding box, noun label, verb label, time-to-contact scalar, and confidence score; the affordance module further produces noun and verb probability distributions from a pre-built zone database that are fused multiplicatively with the STA head's predictions.

## Method

STAformer extracts 2D image tokens via DINOv2 and 3D video tokens via TimeSformer, then applies a frame-guided temporal pooling attention that uses last-frame video tokens as queries to compress the 3D representation into the 2D spatial reference of the final frame. A dual image-video cross-attention module then bidirectionally enriches image tokens with temporal dynamics and video tokens with fine-grained visual details, after which both are mapped to multi-scale feature pyramids that are summed and fed to a modified Faster R-CNN prediction head. Two affordance modules ground predictions in human behavior: an environment affordance module matches the input video to activity-centric zones in a pre-built database (using EgoVLP-v2 visual and text descriptors with KNN retrieval) to produce noun/verb distributions fused with STA predictions via unnormalized joint likelihoods, and an interaction hotspot module forecasts a spatial probability map from hand trajectories and object positions to re-weight detection confidence scores by bounding-box center location.

## Results

On the Ego4D-STA v2 validation set, the full model (STAformer+MH+AFF) achieves 29.39 N, 15.38 N+V, 9.94 N+δ, and 5.67 All Top-5 mAP, yielding relative gains of +43.3%, +47.6%, +36.5%, and +42.1% over the second-best method (GANOv2). On the Ego4D v1 validation set, the method achieves a +45.0% relative gain in All mAP, and on the private Ego4D test set (trained on v2) it reaches 6.75 All mAP with a +30.3% relative gain. On the newly curated EPIC-Kitchens STA benchmark, the method achieves 4.31 All mAP, a +42.9% relative improvement over StillFast.

## Baselines & Comparisons

The proposed STAformer+MH+AFF outperforms all seven compared baselines across every metric on both Ego4D validation splits. Against the strongest prior method GANOv2 [50] (20.52 N, 10.42 N+V, 7.28 N+δ, 3.99 All on v2-val), the full model gains +9.87 N, +4.96 N+V, +2.66 N+δ, and +1.68 All mAP. The original Ego4D baseline FRCNN+SF [17] is surpassed by a wide margin (e.g., 5.67 vs. 2.98 All mAP on v2-val). StillFast [42], the previous end-to-end SOTA, is outperformed by +9.13 N and +1.71 All mAP on v2-val. On the private Ego4D test set, the method beats Language NAO (5.18 All) by +0.57 and GANOv2 (5.16 All) by +1.59. The paper also shows that its KNN-based environment affordance module substantially outperforms the EGO-TOPO [33] neural-network approach: on STAformer, the proposed E.AFF yields 23.55 N mAP versus 17.21 for EGO-TOPO, confirming that the database-matching strategy avoids overfitting to obvious in-scene interactions.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper builds on the STA task formalized by Grauman et al. [17] and the end-to-end StillFast framework [42], whose prediction head is directly adopted. Architecturally, it departs from the convolutional backbones (SlowFast, X3D) used in prior STA work by replacing them with transformer-based extractors (DINOv2 for images, TimeSformer for video) and introducing novel attention-based fusion components (frame-guided temporal pooling, dual image-video cross-attention). The affordance modules adapt two prior ideas—environment affordances from EGO-TOPO [33] and interaction hotspots from Liu et al. [25]—but replace the learned affordance classifier with a KNN-based retrieval over a pre-built zone database, and upgrade the hotspot module with EgoVLP features and a fine-tuned hand detector.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The paper provides the first substantial leap in STA accuracy (relative gains of +42–45% over prior SOTA) and contributes a second large-scale benchmark (EPIC-Kitchens STA annotations), reducing the field's dependence on Ego4D alone. The demonstration that affordance modules are architecture-agnostic (benefiting both StillFast and STAformer) suggests they can be plugged into future STA systems. However, absolute mAP All scores remain low (≤5.7), indicating the task is far from solved; open questions include whether the KNN-based affordance retrieval scales to open-world scenes, whether the independence assumption in the data-fusion step is limiting, and whether the approach generalizes beyond the two egocentric datasets tested.
