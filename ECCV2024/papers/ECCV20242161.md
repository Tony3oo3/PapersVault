## Problem

Previous Transformer-based methods for video Snapshot Compressive Imaging (SCI) reconstruction lack an insight into the information skewness caused by the mixed degradation of spatial masking and temporal aliasing, where informative clues concentrate on spatial rather than temporal dimensions. This leads to ineffective early temporal aggregation that exaggerates artifacts and to attention mechanisms without appropriate inductive bias, limiting both performance and computational efficiency.

## Input / Output

The method takes a single-shot observation image (H×W) and known temporal masks (H×W×T) as input, which are combined via element-wise division to initialize a highly-degraded video (H×W×T) that loses all temporal correlations. The output is a sequence of reconstructed video frames at 8×256×256 (grayscale) or 8×512×512×3 (color) resolution. The color case is inherently coupled with demosaicing because optical masks collide with the Bayer filter, making it a joint reconstruction-and-demosaicing task.

## Method

HiSViT is a multi-branch hierarchical Transformer block with dense connections along the channel dimension, where each branch operates at a different spatial scale (ρ=1, 2, 4). The key attention mechanism, CSS-MSA, performs spatial-then-temporal attention within a single layer: a windowed spatial attention between normal queries and average-pooled keys/values (enabling cross-scale, multi-frequency interactions) followed by a global temporal attention, introducing an inductive bias that favors intra-frame over inter-frame aggregation to match the information skewness. GSM-FFN enhances locality via a gated self-modulation mechanism and factorized spatial-temporal convolutions (STConv). The overall architecture replaces 3D CNN feature extraction with a 2D Residual Swin Transformer Block (RSTB) to avoid early temporal interactions, uses a downsampling-refinement-upsampling pipeline for efficiency, and adds skip connections between shallow and refined features.

## Results

On six grayscale benchmark videos (8×256×256), HiSViT₉ achieves 37.00 dB PSNR / 0.978 SSIM with 8.98M parameters and 1535.92G MACs, while HiSViT₁₃ reaches 37.29 dB / 0.980 SSIM with 12.16M parameters and 1947.30G MACs. On six color benchmark videos (8×512×512×3), HiSViT₉ achieves 38.01 dB / 0.972 SSIM, outperforming the previous best EfficientSCI by 0.52 dB with comparable complexity. Compared to the prior SOTA CTM-SCI (36.52 dB, 81.81M params, 12793.93G MACs), HiSViT₉/₁₃ improves PSNR by 0.48/0.77 dB while using only 12.00%/15.22% of the MACs and 10.98%/14.86% of the parameters.

## Baselines & Comparisons

HiSViT outperforms all 14 baselines across both grayscale and color benchmarks. Against the two optimization algorithms (GAP-TV, DeSCI) and two plug-and-play methods (PnP-FFDNet, PnP-FastDVDnet), HiSViT₉ leads by more than 4 dB in PSNR. Among seven CNN-based methods (E2E-CNN, BIRNAT, GAP-net-Unet-S12, MeteSCI, RevSCI, DUN-3DUnet, ELP-Unfolding), HiSViT₉ surpasses the best (ELP-Unfolding, 35.41 dB) by 1.59 dB while using only 1.6% of its parameters. Against the three Transformer-based competitors, HiSViT₉ beats EfficientSCI (36.48 dB) by 0.52 dB with nearly identical parameter count (8.98M vs 8.82M) and MACs (1535.92G vs 1426.38G), and beats CTM-SCI (36.52 dB) by 0.48 dB while using only 12% of its MACs and 11% of its parameters. On color videos, HiSViT₉ (38.01 dB) outperforms both EfficientSCI and STFormer (37.32 dB) by 0.69 dB, with STFormer requiring 1.35× the MACs. The method also demonstrates strong generalization on real captured data (Duonimo, WaterBallon), where it reconstructs finer details and avoids out-of-scene artifacts compared to EfficientSCI.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the Transformer-based video SCI reconstruction line (STFormer, EfficientSCI, CTM-SCI) by identifying a previously overlooked property of the degradation—'information skewness'—where spatial dimensions carry far more informative clues than temporal ones because input frames lose all motion dynamics. Architecturally, it departs from prior work's use of 3D CNNs for shallow feature extraction by substituting a 2D Residual Swin Transformer Block (RSTB from SwinIR), and introduces CSS-MSA, which fuses spatial windowed attention and temporal global attention within a single layer with cross-scale key/value pooling, drawing on the shifted rectangle-window strategy from Cross Aggregation Transformer [10] and the factorized-attention lineage of ViViT/TimeSformer. The separable design is explicitly motivated by separable convolutions, and the gated FFN borrows from recent image restoration Transformers (e.g., Restormer-style gating).

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The information skewness insight provides a principled rationale for why early temporal aggregation is counterproductive in video SCI, which could guide architecture design for other compressive or heavily degraded video tasks. The practical impact is notable: HiSViT achieves SOTA with only ~12–15% of CTM-SCI's MACs while outperforming it by up to 0.77 dB, and matches EfficientSCI's parameter count with a 0.52 dB gain. Open questions include whether the skewness analysis generalizes to other single-shot multiplexed imaging modalities (e.g., single-pixel imaging, coded aperture) and whether the cross-scale separable attention transfers to longer video sequences or higher resolutions where temporal modeling becomes more critical.
