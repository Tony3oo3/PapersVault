## Problem

The SDS loss used in text-to-3D generation applies heavy classifier-free guidance (CFG) during optimization, yet the underlying diffusion model was trained without CFG, creating a training-inference divergence that produces over-saturated, low-diversity 3D outputs. Simply removing CFG is infeasible because 3D optimization requires high guidance weights to capture fine details. No prior work provides a unified mechanism to reconcile this gap while preserving the high initial CFG needed for 3D fidelity.

## Input / Output

For text-to-3D, the method takes a text prompt and optimizes a randomly initialized NeRF (or 3DGS) via differentiable rendering, using a pretrained Stable Diffusion model as the diffusion prior. For image-to-3D, it follows the Magic123 pipeline, replacing the Stable Diffusion SDS prior with LODS while retaining a Zero-123 shape prior. The embedding-based variant requires only ~20 additional lines of code and is agnostic to internal U-Net structure, making it directly applicable to geometry-aware diffusion models like MVDiffusion.

## Method

LODS decomposes the normalized SDS loss into a reference SDS term (no CFG) plus a CFG-weighted unconditional denoising term, then introduces learnable parameters to minimize the latter, effectively reducing the running CFG over optimization. Two variants are proposed: (1) an embedding-based method that learns a null embedding α to replace the fixed unconditional embedding, and (2) a LoRA-based method that learns low-rank parameters ψ on the diffusion network's unconditional path. The algorithm alternates between optimizing the 3D model θ with the current SDS (learnable params frozen) and optimizing the learnable params to drive the unconditional prediction toward the true noise. This iterative scheme starts from high CFG for detail capture and gradually converges toward the reference SDS, bridging the training-inference gap. The paper further shows that VSD's initial state corresponds to an infinitely large CFG in LODS, explaining VSD's 'floating' artifact, and that LODS's unconditional LoRA design differs fundamentally from VSD's conditional LoRA.

## Results

On the T³ Bench text-to-3D benchmark, LODS(Emb) achieves an average score of 47.3, surpassing the previous best method ProlificDreamer (VSD) at 43.3 by 4.0 points. The advantage is most pronounced on the Multiple Objects category (39.7 vs. 35.8) and Single Object category (52.3 vs. 49.4). On image-to-3D (NeRF4), LODS(Emb) improves PSNR from 24.62 (Magic123) to 24.83 and LPIPS from 0.03 to 0.03, while maintaining CLIP-Similarity at 0.80.

## Baselines & Comparisons

LODS(Emb) outperforms all 11 baselines across every T³ Bench category. Against the closest competitor ProlificDreamer (VSD), LODS(Emb) gains +3.0 on Single Object (52.3 vs. 49.4), +5.0 on Single with Surroundings (49.8 vs. 44.8), and +3.9 on Multiple Objects (39.7 vs. 35.8), with the largest relative improvement in alignment scores for Multiple Objects (33.0 vs. 25.8). The gap over earlier methods is substantial: Magic3D scores 32.7 average, DreamFusion 21.7, and SJC 18.7. On image-to-3D (NeRF4), LODS(Emb) improves PSNR over Magic123 from 24.62 to 24.83 and matches its CLIP-Similarity (0.80), while on RealFusion15 it achieves the best PSNR (19.63 vs. 19.50 for Magic123) and best LPIPS (0.09 vs. 0.10). The LoRA variant (LODS-LoRA) is consistently slightly below the embedding variant but still surpasses ProlificDreamer on all categories (45.4 vs. 43.3 average). Qualitatively, LODS avoids the 'floating object' artifact that plagues VSD/ProlificDreamer while retaining the fine detail generation that SDS lacks.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper builds directly on DreamFusion's Score Distillation Sampling (SDS) loss, identifying a fundamental 'guidance gap' between the diffusion model's training (which learns the conditional score without CFG) and the SDS optimization (which applies heavy CFG, typically w=100). It positions itself as a unified framework that subsumes SDS, VSD (ProlificDreamer), and CSD as special cases, showing that VSD's initial state is equivalent to an infinitely large CFG weight and that VSD's optimization objective implicitly reduces the running CFG. The two proposed variants—a learnable null embedding and a learnable unconditional LoRA—offer a spectrum of complexity, with the embedding variant requiring only ~20 lines of code.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The clean theoretical diagnosis of the CFG gap provides a principled explanation for why SDS produces over-saturated, low-diversity outputs, and the iterative optimization framework offers a practical, drop-in replacement that achieves SOTA on T³ Bench. The embedding-based variant's extreme simplicity makes it highly adoptable across different 3D representations (NeRF, 3DGS) and diffusion backbones. However, the paper acknowledges a key limitation: LODS primarily improves texture fidelity but does not address geometric quality, leaving open the question of how to integrate this prior with geometry-aware diffusion models (e.g., MVDiffusion, Zero123++) for holistic 3D improvement.
