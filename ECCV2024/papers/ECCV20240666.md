## Problem

Existing Transformer-based spectral super-resolution methods suffer from two bottlenecks: they model spatial-wise and spectral-wise correlations in separate modules, disrupting the 3D structure of hyperspectral images and preventing exploitation of unified spatial-spectral correlation; and their self-attention mechanism only captures full-rank pairwise token correlations, making it unable to represent the linear dependence among multiple spectral bands that is prevalent in HSI.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ECCV20240666/cropped_page1_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method takes a standard 3-channel RGB image (482×512 spatial resolution in the NTIRE benchmarks) and produces a 31-band hyperspectral image spanning 400–700 nm at 10 nm intervals. The RGB input is first expanded to 31 channels via a 3×3 convolution before entering the U-shaped network, and training is performed on 128×128 patches with random rotations and flips for augmentation. The network is trained end-to-end with MRAE as the primary loss and AdamW optimizer under a cosine annealing schedule.

## Method

ECT employs a multi-stage U-shaped architecture with two key mechanisms inside each Exhaustive Self-Attention Block. First, the Spectral-wise Discontinuous 3D (SD3D) splitting strategy generates tokens by applying continuous splitting along the spatial dimension and discontinuous splitting along the spectral dimension, enabling unified attention that simultaneously captures spatial non-local and spectral local/non-local correlations without fragmenting spatial structure. Second, the Dynamic Low-Rank Mapping (DLRM) module spatially pools tokens, flattens them into a 2D matrix, and applies a 1D convolution across heads and tokens to produce a low-rank (rank ≤ k) dependence map that captures linear relationships among multiple tokens, complementing the full-rank pairwise attention from the Unified Spatial-Spectral self-Attention (USSA) module. The final token fusion multiplies V by both the full-rank attention map and the low-rank dependence map, and the architecture alternates between cross-token (ESAB_C) and within-token (ESAB_I) attention blocks at different spatial resolutions, achieving SOTA with only 1.19 M parameters and 82 ms inference latency.

## Results

ECT achieves the lowest MRAE on all three benchmark datasets: 0.1564 on NTIRE 2022, 0.0588 on NTIRE 2020, and 0.0635 on ICVL, while also having the fewest parameters (1.19M) and lowest inference latency (82 ms) among all compared methods. On NTIRE 2022, ECT improves MRAE by 0.0035 over the previous SOTA HySAT (0.1599) and by 0.0081 over MST++ (0.1645). On real-world data, ECT further reduces MRAE to 0.2012 (outdoor) and 0.2114 (indoor), outperforming HySAT by 0.0123 and 0.0088 respectively.

## Baselines & Comparisons

ECT outperforms all seven spectral super-resolution baselines on MRAE across all three datasets. The closest competitor is HySAT [39], which ECT surpasses by 0.0035 MRAE on NTIRE 2022 (0.1564 vs. 0.1599), 0.0001 on NTIRE 2020 (0.0588 vs. 0.0589), and 0.0019 on ICVL (0.0635 vs. 0.0654), while also using fewer parameters (1.19M vs. 1.40M) and lower latency (82 ms vs. 126 ms). Against MST++ [9], the first Transformer-based spectral super-resolution method, ECT improves MRAE by 0.0081 on NTIRE 2022 and reduces parameters by 26%. CNN-based baselines (HSCNN+, HR Net, AWAN, DRCR) are substantially outperformed, with MRAE gaps exceeding 0.02 on NTIRE 2022. ECT also beats the CASSI-based reconstruction method PADUT [26] by 0.0286 MRAE on NTIRE 2022, demonstrating that spectral super-resolution from RGB can outperform dedicated compressive sensing pipelines. On real-world data, ECT achieves the best MRAE, RMSE, and SAM in both outdoor and indoor settings, confirming strong generalization beyond simulated data.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the spectral-wise Transformer line initiated by MST++ (Cai et al., CVPRW 2022) and HySAT (Wang et al., ACM MM 2023), which model only spectral-band correlations via self-attention over channel tokens. ECT departs from this lineage in two ways: it unifies spatial and spectral attention through the SD3D splitting strategy (spatially continuous, spectrally discontinuous tokenization), and it augments full-rank pairwise attention with a low-rank dependence map (DLRM) to capture multi-token linear structure. The U-shaped multi-stage architecture and the L2-normalized attention with learnable temperature are inherited directly from MST++ and XCiT, respectively.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

ECT achieves the best MRAE on all three benchmark datasets while using the fewest parameters (1.19 M) and lowest inference latency (82 ms) among all compared methods, making it a practical candidate for lightweight spectral reconstruction pipelines. The real-data experiments on color charts under controlled lighting further validate generalization beyond simulated training distributions. Open questions include whether the low-rank dependence assumption holds for HSIs with very different spectral sampling (e.g., 200+ bands) or for non-natural scenes, and whether the SD3D splitting hyperparameters (c, s) transfer across datasets without re-tuning.
