## Problem

State-of-the-art text-to-3D generation models, across both optimization-based (SDS) and feed-forward paradigms, suffer from two fundamental gaps: coarse semantic alignment that fails to capture fine-grained prompt details, and weak 3D spatial understanding that produces geometrically incoherent outputs with disconnected parts and Janus artifacts. Existing guidance signals rely on CLIP-style encoders or 2D diffusion priors that lack the deep, language-grounded spatial reasoning needed to enforce multi-view 3D consistency and compositional semantics.

## Input / Output

The method takes a natural language text prompt as input and produces a 3D asset (NeRF in the optimization pipeline, or a mesh/3D Gaussian Splatting in the feed-forward pipeline). Internally, the 3D representation is rendered into N multi-view images at 512×512 resolution, which are fed to a Qwen2.5-VL 7B backbone constrained to output a binary Yes/No judgment. A key engineering requirement is that the VLM's image preprocessor must be re-engineered to operate entirely on Torch tensors (rather than NumPy arrays) to preserve end-to-end gradient flow from the VLM logits back to the 3D parameters.

## Method

VLM3D introduces a differentiable dual-query critic that repurposes a pre-trained VLM as a joint semantic and spatial reward model. The VLM is prompted with a structured query containing two explicit criteria—Content Match (semantic fidelity to the prompt) and Geometric Quality (absence of Janus faces, broken surfaces, intersecting geometry, and unrealistic facets across views)—and is constrained to output only Yes or No. The reward is computed as the log-odds difference r_VLM = z_yes − z_no, and the full mapping from 3D parameters θ through multi-view rendering to r_VLM is differentiable, enabling direct backpropagation of the VLM's judgment into the 3D representation. This critic is integrated in two ways: (1) as a reward term in the optimization loss L_total = L_SDS − λ_VLM · r_VLM with a decaying λ_VLM schedule that prioritizes semantic/structural correction early and texture refinement later, and (2) as a test-time guidance signal during the iterative DiT sampling of native 3D feed-forward models (e.g., Hunyuan3D), where ∇_{z_t} r_VLM is computed via a differentiable nvdiffrast rendering pipeline and added to the sampling direction at each denoising step with a linearly decaying guidance weight.

## Results

On the GPTEval3D benchmark (110 prompts), VLM3D achieves the highest Elo score on all six metrics, with an Overall score of 1268.6, surpassing the strongest baseline DreamDPO (1203.1) by 65.5 points. It leads in Text-Asset Alignment (1365.5 vs. 1298.9 for DreamDPO) and 3D Plausibility (1293.7 vs. 1171.9), confirming gains in both semantic fidelity and geometric coherence. In the feed-forward setting (24 prompts), VLM3D reduces CLIP-D from 0.23 (Hunyuan3D) to 0.19 and Geometry score from 0.58 to 0.49, while also improving FID from 338.6 to 274.9.

## Baselines & Comparisons

VLM3D outperforms all seven optimization-based baselines on every GPTEval3D metric. The closest competitor, DreamDPO (1203.1 Overall), is surpassed by 65.5 points overall, with the largest gap in 3D Plausibility (1293.7 vs. 1171.9, +121.8) and Texture-Geometry Coherency (1365.4 vs. 1276.4, +89.0). MVDream, the diffusion backbone used by VLM3D, scores 1097.7 Overall, showing that the VLM reward adds 170.9 points over the base SDS pipeline. DreamReward (1193.3) and ProlificDreamer (1012.5) trail further behind, with ProlificDreamer notably weak on Plausibility (1058.7). In the feed-forward setting, VLM3D beats both Hunyuan3D 2.1 and CLAY on all four metrics: CLIP-D improves from 0.23/0.22 to 0.19, FID from 338.6/310.3 to 274.9, CLIP-FID from 54.01/53.11 to 45.79, and Geometry from 0.58/0.63 to 0.49, demonstrating that the VLM test-time guidance corrects spatial and semantic errors without retraining the base model.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the optimization-based text-to-3D lineage (DreamFusion/SDS, MVDream, ProlificDreamer) and the emerging feed-forward 3D generation paradigm (Hunyuan3D, CLAY) by replacing their weak CLIP-style or 2D-diffusion-based guidance with a differentiable VLM critic. The key departure from prior reward-based methods like DreamReward (which uses a 3D reward model trained on human feedback) is the use of a frozen, pre-trained VLM's Yes/No log-odds as a dual-query signal for both semantic fidelity and geometric coherence, made end-to-end differentiable through re-engineered image preprocessors. The test-time guidance application to native 3D DiT samplers (Hunyuan3D) is novel in that it intervenes during iterative denoising without any model retraining, drawing on the broader test-time guidance literature but applying it to 3D latent spaces.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The framework addresses two persistent failure modes in text-to-3D—coarse semantic alignment and spatial incoherence (Janus artifacts, floating parts)—with a single, general-purpose critic that transfers across paradigms without architectural changes. The practical contribution of making VLM image preprocessors fully differentiable (replacing NumPy conversions with pure Torch operations) is a reusable technical enabler for future VLM-in-the-loop generation. However, the authors acknowledge that the current binary Yes/No formulation underutilizes the VLM's rich visual reasoning (e.g., missing fine details like a lifted leg on the Embracing Peace statue), and disentangling semantic vs. geometric feedback or using hierarchical queries remain open directions that could substantially improve fidelity on complex prompts.
