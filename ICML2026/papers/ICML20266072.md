## Problem

Existing ternarization methods for LLMs are weight-only, retaining activations in full precision and dequantizing ternary weights at inference, which fundamentally limits end-to-end acceleration. The core obstacles are a geometric mismatch between unimodal Gaussian weight distributions and the ternary codebook {-1,0,+1}, and heavy-tailed activation outliers that cause severe accuracy collapse under low-bit quantization. No practical post-training quantization framework has jointly achieved ternary weights and low-bit activations without expensive quantization-aware training.

## Input / Output

The method consumes full-precision pretrained LLM weights and a small calibration set (128 WikiText2 sequences of length 2048) plus a validation set for bit allocation. It produces per-layer ternary weight matrices (1.58-bit, packed 5 values per 8-bit integer), a pair of small Kronecker orthogonal factors (R1, R2) per layer that are folded into the activation pathway as an inverse rotation, and a per-layer activation bitwidth assignment drawn from {2,4,6,8} under a global budget. The rotation is function-preserving: the forward mapping is unchanged, but the weight coordinates are reshaped for ternary projection while activation outliers are statistically dispersed.

## Method

TWLA is a retraining-free PTQ pipeline with three coupled modules. E2M-ATQ performs two-stage ternary quantization: an iterative Euclidean warm-start (coordinate descent on row-wise shift μ, scale α, and discrete pattern T under Frobenius geometry) followed by a manifold relocation that freezes T and solves a per-row 2×2 linear system under the calibration-induced metric S = XᵀX, yielding a closed-form output-aligned update. KOTMS learns a Kronecker-structured orthogonal rotation R = R1⊗R2 via Cayley-parameterized gradient descent on a tri-modal Gaussian-mixture shaping loss, reshaping unimodal weights into a ternary-friendly tri-modal form while the same rotation disperses activation outliers. ILA-AMP allocates activation bits across layers by constructing a chain-structured surrogate objective with per-layer NLL costs and adjacent second-order interaction costs, then solving the budget-constrained assignment exactly via dynamic programming, preventing cascading degradation from a few weak layers.

## Results

Under the headline W1.58A4 setting, TWLA achieves 58.00% average zero-shot accuracy and 8.31 WikiText2 perplexity on LLaMA2-7B, compared to 25.80%/2e4 for GPTQ (2-bit) and 45.23%/11.97 for ResQ (2.3MP/4.2MP). On LLaMA2-70B, TWLA reaches 71.10% average accuracy (exceeding 92% of FP16's 76.71%) with 4.77 WikiText2 PPL, while the strongest baseline QuaRot (2-bit) only reaches 53.64%/6.77. In the weight-only W1.58A16 setting, TWLA attains 62.91% accuracy on LLaMA2-7B (vs. 42.82% for PT2-LLM at the same 1.58-bit weight precision) and 73.60% on LLaMA2-70B (vs. 68.79% for QuaRot at 2-bit). On Qwen3-32B-Instruct with 4-bit activations, TWLA preserves 70.21% MMLU, 37.58% HumanEval, and 48.67% GSM8K, while all other baselines collapse to near-random levels.

## Baselines & Comparisons

TWLA decisively outperforms all six baselines under the W1.58A4 setting. Against GPTQ (2-bit) and PB-LLM (1.7-bit), the gap is extreme: these methods suffer perplexity explosion (PPL in the thousands to millions) and accuracy drops to ~25–27%, while TWLA maintains 55–71% accuracy and single-digit-to-low-double-digit PPL. QuaRot (2-bit) is the strongest non-ternary baseline at A4, reaching 53.64% on LLaMA2-70B, but TWLA still leads by 17.5 points (71.10%) at a lower weight bit-width (1.58 vs. 2). ResQ (2.3MP/4.2MP) is the closest competitor, achieving 56.43% on LLaMA2-70B, yet TWLA surpasses it by 14.7 points while using fewer weight bits. Among ternary methods, PT2-LLM (same 1.58-bit weight precision) drops to 26–29% accuracy at A4, whereas TWLA gains 25–30 points over it, demonstrating that the KOTMS rotation and ILA-AMP mixed-precision allocation are critical for surviving 4-bit activation quantization. On instruction-tuned Qwen3-32B-Instruct, TWLA at W1.58A4 retains 70.21% MMLU and 48.67% GSM8K, while GPTQ, SliM-LLM, and PB-LLM all collapse to near-random (≤24% MMLU, ≤1.3% GSM8K).

## Relation to Prior Work

TWLA sits at the intersection of three prior lines: (1) ternary weight quantization (TWN, PT2-LLM), from which it inherits the asymmetric ternary parameterization and row-wise thresholding; (2) rotation-based outlier suppression (QuaRot, SingleQuant), which it adapts via a Kronecker-structured orthogonal transform optimized with a Cayley parameterization and a tri-modal GMM shaping loss; and (3) mixed-precision bit allocation (SliM-LLM, ResQ), which it extends with an inter-layer second-order interaction term solved via dynamic programming. The key departure is that prior ternarization methods (PT2-LLM, TernaryLLM) are weight-only and keep activations in full precision, while BitNet v2 achieves W1.58A4 but requires >10⁴ GPU-hours of QAT; TWLA is the first to do this jointly under PTQ without retraining.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

TWLA opens the W1.58A4 PTQ regime, which was previously inaccessible without expensive quantization-aware training, enabling 3.64× inference speedup and >80% memory reduction over FP16 on LLaMA2-13B. The inter-layer interaction modeling in ILA-AMP addresses a previously unmodeled failure mode (cascading degradation from a few weak layers) that becomes critical at 4-bit activations. Open questions include whether the Kronecker-2-factor structure is optimal versus deeper Kronecker hierarchies, whether the approach transfers to MoE architectures with heterogeneous expert distributions, and whether the 4-bit activation floor can be pushed to 2-bit without the accuracy collapse observed in all baselines.
