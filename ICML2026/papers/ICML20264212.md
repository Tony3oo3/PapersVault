## Problem

High Update-to-Data (UTD) ratios improve sample efficiency in off-policy RL but induce overfitting. While prior work has addressed value-function bias via ensembles or normalization, representation-level instability in Self-Predictive Learning (SPL) under intensive experience reuse remains underexplored. The paper identifies a specific theoretical conflict: standard zero-centering in redundancy reduction methods mathematically eliminates the dominant eigenmode that SPL captures to encode global transition dynamics.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ICML20264212/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method operates on low-dimensional state vectors (11–348 dimensions) and actions from continuous control environments (Gym MuJoCo and DMC-Hard). The encoder produces a latent representation z that is used both for the SPL prediction objective and as input to the actor-critic networks. In the SimbaV2-SPL variant, the raw state is additionally linearly projected, L2-normalized, and concatenated with z before feeding the actor and critic, preserving high-frequency details that might be lost in the bottleneck encoding.

## Method

R2R2 replaces the standard centered covariance loss in VICReg with a non-centered inner-product form (Eq. 8) that preserves the constant eigenmode corresponding to the dominant spectral component of the transition matrix. The final training objective combines the SPL prediction loss, the non-centered redundancy reduction loss, and a variance-preserving term, all applied directly to the encoder output without an auxiliary projector. The theoretical justification (Proposition 2) shows that the centering matrix H annihilates the projection of the optimal representation onto the constant eigenvector, so removing centering is necessary to retain global dynamics information. To demonstrate orthogonality to architectural advances, the authors also construct SimbaV2-SPL by integrating a tailored encoder-predictor SPL module into the SimbaV2 backbone, which alone establishes a new state-of-the-art (1.34 at UTD=20) and gains further from R2R2 (1.38).

## Results

At UTD=20, R2R2 improves TD7's aggregate normalized score from 1.02 to 1.24 (+22%), with the gain most pronounced on DMC-Hard (1.02→1.32). On the state-of-the-art SimbaV2 backbone, integrating a tailored SPL module alone (SimbaV2-SPL) establishes a new SOTA of 1.34, and adding R2R2 on top yields a further gain to 1.38. R2R2 also prevents the severe degradation seen in TD7+LayerNorm at UTD=20 (0.88→1.10) and protects Minimalist φ from collapse in Gym MuJoCo (0.41→0.57). All results are reported as normalized scores (relative to UTD=1 baseline) averaged over 5 seeds across 11 continuous control environments.

## Baselines & Comparisons

Against TD7 (Fujimoto et al., 2023), R2R2 wins decisively at UTD=20, lifting the total normalized score from 1.02 to 1.24 and DMC-Hard from 1.02 to 1.32, while remaining competitive at UTD=1 (1.00→1.06). Against Minimalist φ (Ni et al., 2024), R2R2 prevents catastrophic collapse in Gym MuJoCo at UTD=20 (0.41→0.57) and improves the total from 5.28 to 6.20, though the large normalized ratios reflect the very low absolute returns of this minimal baseline. Against TD7+LayerNorm (Ba et al., 2016), the comparison is particularly instructive: TD7+LN alone degrades below its UTD=1 performance at UTD=20 (0.88), while adding R2R2 recovers and exceeds it (1.10), demonstrating that R2R2 addresses a distinct form of representational instability that architectural normalization cannot resolve. Against SimbaV2 (Lee et al., 2025b), the paper first constructs SimbaV2-SPL by integrating a tailored SPL module, which alone surpasses the original SimbaV2 (1.20→1.34 at UTD=20), and R2R2 provides an additional +0.04 gain (1.38), confirming orthogonality to the strongest existing architecture.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper sits at the intersection of self-predictive learning (SPL) for RL and redundancy reduction from self-supervised learning. It builds directly on VICReg's variance-invariance-covariance decomposition but departs from it by removing the zero-centering step, motivated by a spectral analysis of SPL (Tang et al., 2023) showing that the dominant eigenmode of a row-stochastic transition matrix is constant and thus annihilated by centering. Architecturally, it extends the state-of-the-art SimbaV2 backbone (Lee et al., 2025b) with a tailored SPL module, and positions its representation-level regularization as orthogonal to value-centric high-UTD methods (REDQ, CrossQ) and architectural normalization (LayerNorm).

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The paper identifies and resolves a concrete theoretical conflict (zero-centering vs. spectral preservation) that had gone unnoticed in the SPL-for-RL literature, providing a principled justification for a simple design choice. Practically, the non-centered redundancy reduction term is lightweight, hyperparameter-free (fixed λ=0.01 across all tasks), and composable with existing high-UTD strategies. However, the method is validated only on low-dimensional state-vector environments; the authors acknowledge that full-scale evaluation on pixel-based visual RL remains open, and the gains on the strongest backbone (SimbaV2-SPL) are modest (+0.04), suggesting a performance ceiling effect that limits broader impact.
