## Problem

Automated proving of unconstrained polynomial inequalities remains intractable at scale: purely symbolic SOS/SDP methods suffer combinatorial explosion beyond 4–5 variables, and LLM-based provers lack the formalized training data needed for complex multivariate algebra. Existing neuro-symbolic systems (e.g., AIPS, LIPS) are confined to low-dimensional cases and restrict LLMs to search guidance rather than direct symbolic conjecture generation, leaving a gap for high-dimensional (up to 10-variable) inequality proving with machine-checked certificates.

## Input / Output

The method takes a nonnegative polynomial f(x) in up to 10 variables (even degree 2d) in expanded form and produces a verified Lean 4 proof script via a callable tactic `llm_ineq`, alongside an exact rational SOS certificate. The LLM generates K=32 candidate SOS structure conjectures ranked by coefficient-matching error; the symbolic correction stage refines the top-ranked candidates into exact rational Gram matrices, and the Lean template discharges two obligations—polynomial-to-SOS equality via `linear_combination` and nonnegativity via the `positivity` tactic—yielding a fully machine-checked proof within a 1-hour budget.

## Method

NSPI is a three-stage neuro-symbolic pipeline: (1) Neural Conjecture, where a Qwen3-8B model is trained as an SOS structure conjecturer via supervised fine-tuning on over 1 million synthetic polynomial-SOS pairs (generated through Gram matrix synthesis using computation-driven spectral shifting/factored/LMI methods and structure-driven diagonally dominant/scaled-diagonally dominant constructions), followed by curriculum-based GRPO reinforcement learning that progresses from 3–5 to 6–8 to 9–10 variables with a composite reward of accuracy, format compliance, and algebraic structure penalty; (2) Symbolic Correction, which applies Cholesky-parameterized Gauss-Newton iteration to drive the backward error below 1e-15, then performs exact rational recovery via orthogonal projection (interior-point case) or truncated LDL^T with LLL-based Diophantine approximation (boundary/rank-deficient case); (3) Formal Verification, which fills a pre-defined Lean proof template with the recovered rational SOS terms and invokes `linear_combination` and `positivity` to produce a machine-checked proof. The key insight is elevating the LLM from a search guide to a primary conjecture generator of approximate SOS structures, with symbolic computation serving as a precision bridge that converts neural approximations into exact, certifiable rational certificates.

## Results

On PolyIneqBench (522 problems, n=3 to 10), NSPI achieves the best pass rate at every dimension from n=4 onward: 40.3% (n=4), 36.7% (n=5), 29.5% (n=6), 26.7% (n=7), 21.7% (n=8), 15.0% (n=9), and 11.7% (n=10), while all other methods drop to 0% by n=8 or n=10. On the synthetic high-dimensional subset (PolyIneq-Synth), NSPI reaches a 25.95% pass rate with the lowest average execution time, a 2.87× improvement over the hybrid LIPS system. NSPI also maintains the fastest inference among all methods, with mean execution times of 14–64 seconds versus 100–1500+ seconds for competing approaches.

## Baselines & Comparisons

NSPI loses to symbolic solvers Maple and Z3 at n=3 (44.1% vs. 97.6%) and to the hybrid LIPS system at n=3 (44.1% vs. 71.4%), but decisively outperforms all baselines from n=4 onward. Against LIPS, NSPI gains +13.0 pp at n=4 (40.3% vs. 27.3%), +23.4 pp at n=5 (36.7% vs. 13.3%), and maintains a 2.87× pass-rate advantage on PolyIneq-Synth overall. All dedicated LLM-based provers (DeepSeek-Prover-V2, Goedel-Prover-V2, Kimina-Prover) collapse to 0% pass rate by n=5, while NSPI sustains 36.7% at n=5 and 11.7% at n=10. Among general-purpose LLMs, Gemini-3-Pro is the strongest competitor (24.7% at n=4, 6.7% at n=10), yet NSPI still leads by 15.6 pp at n=4 and 5.0 pp at n=10. At n=10, NSPI is the only method achieving a non-zero pass rate (11.7%), with a mean execution time of 58.3 s versus 116.7 s for Gemini-3-Pro and 1525.6 s for Maple.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the neuro-symbolic theorem proving line (AlphaGeometry, AIPS, LIPS) by fundamentally repositioning the LLM's role: rather than using LLMs for search guidance or tactic selection, NSPI trains an LLM as a direct generator of approximate SOS structural conjectures, which are then refined via Gauss-Newton iteration (following Kaltofen et al., 2012b) and rational recovery (following Peyrl & Parrilo, 2008a) into exact certificates. This departs from both purely symbolic pipelines (Maple, Z3) that suffer combinatorial explosion, and LLM-based provers (DeepSeek-Prover-V2, Goedel-Prover, Kimina-Prover) that depend on scarce formal-proof training data. The three-stage conjecture-correction-verification architecture is architecturally distinct from prior hybrid systems that interleave LLM and symbolic steps within a search loop.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The paper addresses a genuine scalability gap: existing methods (symbolic, LLM-based, and hybrid) all degrade sharply beyond 5 variables, while NSPI maintains consistent (if modest) pass rates up to 10 variables. The PolyIneqBench benchmark fills a clear void in high-dimensional polynomial inequality evaluation. However, the framework is fundamentally limited to polynomials admitting SOS certificates—27 of 102 real-world instances lack such representations—so the approach cannot cover all nonnegative polynomials. Extending to sums of squares of rational functions and to related certification tasks (Lyapunov stability, global optimization) are natural but open directions.
