## Problem

Long-horizon protein molecular dynamics generation is hampered by error accumulation in time-domain autoregressive models, which causes structural drift, and by fixed step-size constraints that limit temporal resolution. Existing trajectory generators (MDGEN, ProAR, ConfRover) model dynamics purely in frame-wise coordinates, leaving the multi-scale spectral structure of protein motion—high-frequency local vibrations coupled to low-frequency collective modes—entangled and unexploited.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ICML20262753/cropped_page2_idx1.webp)

## Input / Output

The method takes a protein sequence (encoded via ESM-2-650M residue embeddings) and an initial Cα anchor structure as input, and produces a Cα-coordinate trajectory (evaluated at 250 frames, 400 ps spacing) or a 250-sample conformational ensemble. Internally, generation operates on non-overlapping windows of W=2500 frames (Δt=10 ps), each converted to F=1251 frequency bins via per-window Kabsch alignment and rFFT, yielding 6-dimensional real tokens (Re/Im × x/y/z) per residue per bin. Windows are rolled out sequentially with the last generated frame becoming the next anchor.

## Method

BioDynaSpec reformulates protein dynamics as a spatio-spectral autoregressive diffusion process. Independent Windowed Fourier Decomposition (IWFD) partitions trajectories into aligned windows and converts them to normalized rFFT spectral tokens, making local near-equilibrium mode structure explicit in frequency space. A Spatio-Spectral Hybrid Transformer planner alternates inter-residue spatial attention (capturing collective motions) with intra-residue spectral attention using RoPE (modeling phase correlations), generating tokens progressively from low to high frequencies via a two-phase training strategy (progressive masking followed by self-forcing). A diffusion denoising head (50-step DDIM at inference) produces each spectral token conditioned on the planner's features. Inter-Residue Frequency Coupling (IRFC) injects a learnable per-head Gaussian distance penalty (−τ_h·d²) into attention logits, encoding a harmonic-approximation prior where stiff local bonds enforce narrow-kernel attention and soft long-range couplings permit broader receptive fields.

## Results

On the ATLAS test set (82 proteins, 250 frames at 400 ps/frame), BioDynaSpec achieves R₂₅₀ = 1.509 Å mean per-frame Cα-RMSE, reducing error by 60.4% versus MDGEN (3.813 Å) and 57.2% versus ProAR (3.529 Å). For equilibrium conformational sampling, it attains Root Mean W₂ = 1.31, MD PCA W₂ = 0.90, and Joint PCA W₂ = 1.19, improving over the next best method by 50.03%, 35.25%, and 47.58% respectively. It also leads on near-equilibrium local dynamics (PCA-PSD-LogCorr = 0.817) and covariance consistency (CFRE = 0.989), a 21.9% gain and 36.7% reduction over the next best method.

## Baselines & Comparisons

BioDynaSpec outperforms all five baselines across every reported metric. Against MDGEN (Jing et al., 2024b), it reduces R₂₅₀ from 3.813 Å to 1.509 Å (−60.4%) and improves PCA-2D stepwise displacement correlation from 0.024 to 0.456. Against ProAR (Cheng et al., 2026), it reduces R₂₅₀ from 3.529 Å to 1.509 Å (−57.2%) and improves CFRE from 2.030 to 0.989 (−36.7%). For conformational sampling, BioDynaSpec beats AlphaFlow (Jing et al., 2024a) on Root Mean W₂ (1.31 vs. 2.64, −50.4%), ConfDiff (Wang et al., 2024) on all three W₂ metrics (1.31/0.90/1.19 vs. 2.75/1.41/2.27), and ConfRover (Shen et al., 2025) on Root Mean W₂ (1.31 vs. 2.62, −50.0%) and Joint PCA W₂ (1.19 vs. 2.28, −47.8%). The ablation further confirms that removing the IRFC bias causes catastrophic degradation (RMSE jumps from 1.540 Å to 33.923 Å), underscoring the critical role of the harmonic distance prior.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper departs from the dominant time-domain autoregressive paradigm for protein dynamics generation (MDGEN, ProAR, ConfRover) by reformulating trajectory prediction as a window-wise spectral reconstruction problem, motivated by the local harmonic approximation from normal-mode theory. It builds on the Self-Forcing technique (Huang et al., 2025) to bridge the train-test gap in autoregressive generation, and repurposes SE(3)-invariant architectural components (IPA, triangular attention from AlphaFold) within a Structure-Aware Representation Encoder. The key conceptual departure is the Independent Windowed Fourier Decomposition, which converts coordinate time-series into spectral tokens and generates them via low-to-high frequency autoregression with diffusion denoising, rather than predicting frame-wise Cartesian coordinates directly.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The frequency-domain formulation offers a physically grounded alternative to time-domain autoregression that could mitigate the error accumulation problem plaguing long-horizon molecular dynamics generation, with the 60%+ RMSE reduction over strong baselines suggesting the spectral decomposition is not merely a reparameterization but a genuinely more tractable generation space. However, the method relies on a fixed-window local near-equilibrium assumption that may break down for very long-timescale conformational transitions, and velocity/acceleration continuity across window boundaries is not enforced. Open questions include whether the spectral formulation generalizes beyond the ATLAS dataset (100 ns trajectories, ≤1000 residues), how it scales to larger systems or longer timescales, and whether the strong dependence on the IRFC structural prior (22× RMSE degradation without it) limits flexibility across diverse protein classes.
