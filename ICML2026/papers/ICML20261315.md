## Problem

The dominant positional encoding in modern LLMs, RoPE, entangles content ('what') and position ('where') information in the attention score through an interaction term between key and query phases. This confound impairs the model's ability to independently match on content or position, as demonstrated by a diagnostic task where RoPE-based Transformers achieve only 11% accuracy while the proposed method reaches 95%. The paper also notes that RoPE's length extrapolation degrades significantly at test time, requiring costly fine-tuning or frequency interpolation workarounds.

## Input / Output

The method takes d-dimensional real-valued query and key vectors along with their scalar position indices (t and s) and produces a scalar attention score. Unlike RoPE, which pairs elements into d/2 two-dimensional components, PoPE treats each of the d elements independently as a complex number, effectively doubling the number of frequency channels. The magnitudes are derived via softplus activation of the original values (ensuring non-negativity), while phases are set purely by position, and the final score is the real part of a complex conjugate dot product.

## Method

PoPE re-expresses RoPE's attention score in polar coordinates, revealing that the content-dependent phases of keys and queries introduce an interaction term (φ_k − φ_q) that shifts the effective relative position. PoPE eliminates this term by constructing d complex-valued components (one per original element rather than per pair), where each component's magnitude is the softplus of the corresponding real value and its phase is determined solely by position (sθ_c or tθ_c). An optional learnable per-frequency bias δ_c (bounded to [−2π, 0]) replaces the removed interaction term, providing a fixed offset that tunes optimal relative alignment per frequency. The attention score reduces to a sum of magnitude products times cosines of pure relative-position differences, and is implemented in a custom Triton Flash Attention kernel that computes the real part of the complex product in-kernel, adding only one extra multiplication per component over standard RoPE.

## Results

On the diagnostic Indirect Indexing task, PoPE achieves 94.82±2.91% accuracy versus RoPE's 11.16±2.45%, demonstrating near-perfect decoupling of content and position matching. On OpenWebText language modeling, PoPE consistently outperforms RoPE across all three model scales: 21.33 vs 21.55 (124M), 18.55 vs 18.88 (253M), and 15.45 vs 15.85 (774M) perplexity. On downstream tasks, PoPE-based models achieve higher mean accuracy than RoPE at every scale (46.19 vs 45.33 at 124M; 48.78 vs 48.76 at 253M; 52.46 vs 51.80 at 774M). PoPE also exhibits strong zero-shot length extrapolation to 10× training context length on PG-19, outperforming both RoPE and the interpolation-based YaRN method.

## Baselines & Comparisons

PoPE decisively outperforms RoPE on every experiment: the Indirect Indexing diagnostic (94.82% vs 11.16% accuracy), music NLL (0.4889 vs 0.5081 on JSB; 1.486 vs 1.501 on MAESTRO), genomic NLL (4.152 vs 4.217), and language modeling perplexity at all three scales. On length extrapolation (PG-19, up to 10240 tokens), PoPE generalizes zero-shot without any fine-tuning or frequency interpolation, whereas RoPE degrades sharply and YaRN (which requires fine-tuning plus NTK-aware interpolation) also degrades beyond its 4096-token fine-tuning length; PoPE outperforms YaRN in this regime. On the Indirect Indexing task, PoPE also far exceeds ALiBi applied to half the heads (27.35±10.77%) and sinusoidal positional embeddings (1.90±0.04%), confirming that the what-where confound is specific to RoPE's rotation mechanism rather than a general limitation of relative positional encodings. The performance gap in language modeling perplexity holds steady or slightly widens with model scale, suggesting the benefit is not an artifact of small-model noise.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper builds directly on RoPE (Su et al., 2024), the dominant positional encoding in frontier LLMs, by re-expressing its attention score in polar coordinates to reveal that content ('what') and position ('where') information are entangled through an interaction term φ_k − φ_q. PoPE eliminates this entanglement by assigning magnitudes (via softplus) and phases (via position-dependent rotation) to separate, non-interacting channels, drawing on the complex-valued embedding idea from Wang et al. (2020) but applying it per-element rather than per-pair. It positions itself against length-extrapolation methods like YaRN (Peng et al., 2024) and ALiBi (Press et al., 2022), and distinguishes itself from CosFormer (Qin et al., 2022) by applying multi-frequency per-component modulation rather than a single scalar decay.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The zero-shot length extrapolation to 10× training context without fine-tuning or frequency interpolation—outperforming YaRN which requires both—is practically significant for reducing the cost of context-window extension in deployed LLMs. The Indirect Indexing diagnostic (11% vs. 95% accuracy) provides a clean, reproducible benchmark for evaluating what-where disentanglement in positional encodings. Open questions include whether the 2× memory overhead for complex-valued keys/queries is acceptable at 7B+ scale, whether the modest perplexity gains (0.2–0.4 points) grow or shrink at larger model sizes, and whether the softplus activation and learnable bias δ remain necessary or become redundant with more training data.
