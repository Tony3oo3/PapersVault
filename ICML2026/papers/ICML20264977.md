## Problem

LLMs exhibit paradoxical fragility in multi-digit arithmetic, frequently committing off-by-one carry errors as operand counts grow, yet lightweight probes can decode the correct answer from the residual stream of a failing model. Prior work identified this disconnect between internal computation and discrete output but left unexplained the geometric mechanism by which internal activation structures induce specific carry-propagation failures. This paper asks: what is the representational geometry that allows ground truth and hallucination to coexist in a single activation vector?

## Input / Output

The method consumes final-layer residual stream activations h_p^(L) (2560-dim vectors from Qwen3-4B, L=36) extracted at each autoregressive digit generation position during 3-term 10-digit addition, along with the model's current predicted digit and the column-wise raw sum of input digits. It produces a token-level correction: either the original digit is preserved (consistency check passes) or it is overridden by (r̂_p + ⌊Φ̂_p⌋) mod 10 (consistency check fails), effectively projecting the divergent state back onto the correct manifold fiber. All probes are lightweight MLPs (512-128 hidden units) trained on balanced subsets of the 10,000-problem dataset, operating at individual digit positions rather than whole-answer granularity.

## Method

The paper identifies Iso-Raw-Sum Trajectories (IRSTs) in the residual stream: continuous fibers of constant raw sum r_p that thread through adjacent digit basins as the carry state varies, forming a hierarchical geometry of macroscopic digit anchors modulated by microscopic carry-stratified sub-manifolds. Arithmetic errors are characterized as geometric slippages—drifts along an IRST that push representations across decision boundaries between adjacent digit basins. To formalize this, the Noisy Quantization Model posits that the LLM maintains a continuous Carry Potential Φ_p = Σ_j r_{p+j}/10^j (the weighted accumulation of raw sums from lower-order positions), which is corrupted by additive Gaussian noise (σ ≈ 0.05) and discretized via a floor operation; errors occur when noise pushes Φ̂_p across integer quantization thresholds, producing a periodic bathtub error distribution validated with R²=0.80. The inference-time intervention uses two orthogonal probes on the same activation—a raw-sum classifier (local calculation stream) and a carry-potential regressor (global context stream)—and overrides the output only when no plausible carry c in a δ-neighborhood of Φ̂_p can explain the generated digit via the modular identity, achieving 89.56% token accuracy versus 86.26% baseline.

## Results

On Qwen3-4B performing 3-term 10-digit addition, the proposed Dual-Stream Consistency method (δ=0.1) achieves 89.56% token-level accuracy, outperforming the original model (86.26%) by 3.3 points. The method also attains the highest False Positive Preservation (98.13%) among all intervention methods, meaning it preserves 98.13% of originally correct tokens while correcting 30.46% of errors. The Noisy Quantization Model fits the empirical bathtub error distribution with R²=0.80, estimating an internal cognitive noise level of σ≈0.05.

## Baselines & Comparisons

The paper compares its Dual-Stream Consistency method against two primary baselines from prior probing and intervention literature. Against Re-prompting (Sun et al., 2025), which uses a probe to trigger natural-language correction, the proposed method wins decisively: 89.56% vs. 79.90% token accuracy, with 30.46% true positive correction versus a near-zero 0.08%. The authors attribute Re-prompting's failure to KV-cache representation locking, where erroneous internal states persist despite lexical corrections. Against Steering (Bhalla et al., 2024), which injects a probe-derived direction into activations, the proposed method achieves higher token accuracy (89.56% vs. 88.27%) and substantially better false positive preservation (98.13% vs. 96.97%), while maintaining comparable correction rates (30.46% vs. 30.58%). The ablation in Table 3 further isolates the mechanism: replacing the noisy carry probe with the true carry (R+TC) pushes token accuracy to 96.0%, confirming that the carry potential estimation is the primary bottleneck, while the local raw-sum signal remains highly robust.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the geometric-representation line of work on LLM arithmetic (Nanda et al. 2023 on rotational dynamics, Kantamneni & Tegmark 2025 on trigonometric encodings) by identifying a concrete, testable structure—the Iso-Raw-Sum Trajectory (IRST)—and coupling it with a quantitative error model (Noisy Quantization) that predicts the observed bathtub error distribution. It departs from prior probing studies (Sun et al. 2025, Yan 2025) that treat internal states as discrete variables to be classified, instead explaining *why* lightweight probes can simultaneously decode ground truth, model output, and carry states from a single activation vector: the manifold's topological separability along IRST fibers makes these signals geometrically distinct. The dual-stream consistency check builds on inference-time intervention methods (steering, re-prompting) but reframes correction as projecting a divergent state back onto the correct IRST fiber rather than applying an external signal.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The Noisy Quantization Model provides a mechanistic, falsifiable account of arithmetic failures (noise-induced threshold crossings of a continuous Carry Potential) that unifies the off-by-one error pattern, the probe versatility phenomenon, and the periodic bathtub error distribution under one framework. The practical dual-stream consistency check (89.56% vs. 86.26% baseline) is modest but serves as causal validation that correct latent information persists even in erroneous outputs. Key limitations include the restriction to addition with single-digit tokenization (Qwen3, Gemma3), reliance on UMAP visualization plus probe decoding rather than full circuit-level localization, and the assumption that local raw-sum computation is robust (validated at ~89% but not perfect). Open questions include whether the IRST geometry and quantization mechanism generalize to multiplication, to BPE-tokenized models where carry signals may be entangled in multi-digit embeddings, and how the cognitive noise σ scales systematically with model size and training.
