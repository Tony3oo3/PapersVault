## Problem

Self-supervised visual foresight reasoning in model-based RL suffers from multi-step error accumulation, while existing remedies inject domain-specific knowledge that limits generality. The paper asks whether a purely self-supervised, task-agnostic reasoning representation can provide informative visual guidance for long-horizon combat in 3D open-world environments without relying on photorealistic reconstruction fidelity.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ICML20265243/cropped_page2_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method consumes raw RGB frames (80×128 on MineDojo, 64×64 on DMC Vision) plus actions, and internally constructs an enhanced observation tensor of shape (h, w, 3H+6) by concatenating the raw image, the lower-layer residual, and H frames of upper-layer foresight rollouts along the channel axis. The decoder reconstructs only the raw and residual signals (not the foresight), while the actor outputs actions and the world model produces blurry but task-relevant visual foresight representations that modulate the encoder's input at every layer.

## Method

ResDreamer builds a Hierarchical Recurrent State-Space Model (HRSSM) from stacked Predictive Processing Blocks, where each layer k is trained to reconstruct the reconstruction residual of layer k−1 rather than the raw image, enabling progressive abstraction of world dynamics. Upper layers perform open-loop latent rollouts and decode 'imaginary hint observations' (H-step foresight) that are added to lower-layer reconstructions via residual connections, while lower layers pass normalized reconstruction errors upward as self-supervised training signals—no gradients cross layer boundaries. The actor-critic is trained exclusively on imagined trajectories following the DreamerV3 paradigm, with the only new hyperparameter being the foresight horizon H. This design yields linearly growing cross-layer communication cost, requires no language modules or domain priors, and operates at 50–200M parameters while achieving state-of-the-art sample and parameter efficiency on MineDojo combat tasks.

## Results

ResDreamer (100M×2, 192.7M total params) is the only evaluated method to achieve non-near-zero success on the high-difficulty Shulker combat task within 1×10⁶ environment steps. The lighter ResDreamer (50M×2, 92.0M params) surpasses DreamerV3's (109.5M params) average performance across all five MineDojo combat tasks while using only 84% of the parameter budget. On DMC Vision continuous control, ResDreamer consistently outperforms DreamerV3 across foresight horizon settings (H=4, 8, 16), with H=16 achieving the best results on Hopper Hop. The 3-layer ablation variant (50M×3) reaches a mean success rate of 0.776, up from 0.727 for the 2-layer configuration, confirming the scalability of the residual hierarchy.

## Baselines & Comparisons

Against DreamerV3 (Hafner et al., 2025), ResDreamer (50M×2) achieves higher success rates and episode scores with fewer environment steps across nearly all five MineDojo combat tasks, despite having 92M vs. 109.5M parameters; ResDreamer (100M×2) further dominates by being the sole method to solve the Shulker task within 1M steps. Against Steve-1 (Lifshitz et al., 2023), a zero-shot text-instruction-following VPT model, ResDreamer substantially outperforms on all combat tasks, as Steve-1 was not designed for online RL in dynamic combat. Against PTGM (Yuan et al., 2024), a hierarchical goal-generation + goal-conditioned RL method, ResDreamer achieves superior sample efficiency without requiring large-scale pretraining on task-agnostic datasets. Against IRIS (Micheli et al., 2022), a transformer-based world model, the reproduction on MineDojo failed to achieve meaningful success on even the easiest Combat Spider task within 500K steps, highlighting the difficulty of applying transformer MBRL to high-dimensional 3D combat without extensive tuning.

## Relation to Prior Work

ResDreamer extends DreamerV3's single-layer RSSM into a multi-layer hierarchical world model, but departs fundamentally from prior hierarchical MBRL (e.g., THICK, HIRO) by communicating between layers through pixel-space reconstruction residuals rather than latent-state sub-goals or temporal abstractions. The design is conceptually grounded in predictive coding from neuroscience, where higher cortical layers encode prediction error rather than raw sensory input. Unlike LLM-powered embodied agents (JARVIS-1, MC-Planner) or large video-generation world models (Genie, Cosmos) that inject domain-specific or language-grounded priors, ResDreamer adheres to a purely self-supervised, task-agnostic training paradigm, arguing that informative residual signals suffice without photorealistic fidelity.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The paper demonstrates that a lightweight (50–200M parameter) hierarchical world model with residual-modulated visual foresight can match or exceed larger single-layer baselines in sample and parameter efficiency on challenging 3D combat tasks, and is the only evaluated method to solve the high-difficulty Shulker task within 1M steps. The residual hierarchy is shown to be scalable (3-layer > 2-layer), suggesting a path toward deeper world models without the quadratic communication costs of full latent stacking. Key open questions include whether the fixed foresight horizon H can be made adaptive or learnable, whether the residual-modulation mechanism transfers to non-Minecraft domains beyond DMC, and whether the approach generalizes to continuous high-dimensional action spaces beyond MineDojo's MultiDiscrete setting.
