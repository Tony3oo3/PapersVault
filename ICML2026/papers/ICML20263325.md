## Problem

3D spatial reasoning faces a fundamental trade-off: neuro-symbolic 3D (NS3D) methods provide transparent, step-by-step reasoning programs but are constrained to closed-set concept vocabularies and require dense procedural supervision unavailable beyond simple synthetic datasets, while end-to-end 3D multi-modal LLMs handle open-vocabulary natural language but reason as black boxes with no interpretable verification trace. No existing method combines the systematic, compositional reasoning of symbolic programs with the semantic flexibility and open-world knowledge of modern LLMs.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ICML20263325/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method consumes a 3D RGB-D scene segmented into object instances (via Mask3D), where each instance is encoded with 3D geometric features (Uni3D), 2D appearance features (DINOv2), and a lattice-based positional embedding for 6D configuration (x,y,z,h,w,l), yielding a compact ~400-token object-centric representation interleaved with instruction tokens. The output is a structured chain-of-thought trace containing explicit plans (query decomposition) and spatially grounded executions (each referencing objects by unique ID with coordinates and dimensions), followed by a final grounded answer specifying object ID, 3D center, and bounding box dimensions. This contrasts with video-based 3D MLLMs that require 10K–40K scene tokens.

## Method

APEIRIA is a neuro-symbolic 3D MLLM (Qwen3-VL-8B backbone with LoRA) trained via a three-stage curriculum that distills reasoning patterns from NS3D programs into natural-language chain-of-thought. Stage 1 (Perception Alignment) grounds 3D visual-geometric features to the LLM's embedding space via ~193K object-centric perception tasks. Stage 2 (Symbolic Reasoning Injection) performs CoT-SFT on 144K verified execution traces parsed from neuro-symbolic programs (scene, filter, relate primitives), where each trace serializes plans and executions with ground-truth object sets, teaching systematic query decomposition and stepwise spatial verification. Stage 3 (Open-Set Generalization) applies CoT-RL via GRPO with a composite reward—Soft Grounding Reward (exponential decay on location and size similarity for dense spatial feedback) plus a binary Format Reward (enforcing plan/think structure)—to extend learned reasoning patterns to open-vocabulary concepts and deeply nested instructions where procedural supervision is absent. The key insight is transferring the 'syntax' of spatial reasoning (decomposition patterns, verification structure) rather than concept-specific knowledge, preserving NS3D virtues of transparent traces and modular interchangeability of planning and perception components at inference time.

## Results

On ScanRefer, APEIRIA achieves 58.4% Acc@0.25 and 51.2% Acc@0.5, surpassing the strongest 3D MLLM baseline Inst3D-LMM (57.8% / 51.6%) and Video-3D LLM (58.1% / 51.7%). On Multi3DRefer, APEIRIA reaches 59.2% F1@0.25 and 53.8% F1@0.5, outperforming all prior methods. With modular inference enhancement (APEIRIA†), performance further improves to 60.5% Acc@0.25 on ScanRefer and 60.9% F1@0.25 on Multi3DRefer. On the zero-shot Nr3D open-set generalization test, APEIRIA achieves 36.5% accuracy, exceeding the fully supervised NS3D baseline (33.9%) by +2.6%.

## Baselines & Comparisons

Against neuro-symbolic baselines, APEIRIA (58.4% ScanRefer Acc@0.25) substantially outperforms LaSP (49.2%), LARC (32.9%), and NS3D (22.4%), confirming that distilling reasoning patterns into an MLLM overcomes the closed-set vocabulary bottleneck of traditional NS3D methods. Among 3D MLLMs, APEIRIA edges out Inst3D-LMM (57.8%) by +0.6% on ScanRefer Acc@0.25 and Video-3D LLM (58.1%) by +0.3%, while also leading on Multi3DRefer F1@0.25 (59.2% vs. 58.3% and 58.0% respectively). On the zero-shot Nr3D open-set test, APEIRIA (36.5%) surpasses the fully supervised NS3D (33.9%) and R2G zero-shot (25.8%), demonstrating that distilled reasoning patterns generalize to unseen linguistic expressions. On Scan2Cap dense captioning, APEIRIA achieves 90.6 C@0.25 and 84.1 C@0.5, exceeding LEGO (84.7/78.6) and Video-3D LLM (–/83.8); on SQA3D it ties Video-3D LLM at 58.6 EM while surpassing LLaVA-3D (55.6) and LEGO (54.8). The modular enhancement variant (APEIRIA†) further gains +2.1% on ScanRefer and +1.7% on Multi3DRefer by swapping in a stronger perception model (SegDINO3D), approaching the oracle upper bound (61.3/61.3).

## Relation to Prior Work

APEIRIA bridges two previously separate paradigms in 3D spatial reasoning: neuro-symbolic 3D (NS3D) learners (Hsu et al., 2023; Feng et al., 2024), which decompose queries into compositional programs executed by rigid concept-specific networks, and end-to-end 3D MLLMs (Chat-Scene, Inst3D-LMM, Video-3D LLM), which handle open-vocabulary instructions but reason as opaque black boxes. The key conceptual departure is distilling the *reasoning patterns* (query decomposition, stepwise spatial verification) from verified symbolic program execution traces into natural-language chain-of-thought within an MLLM, rather than training separate concept networks. It builds on NS3D's program structure for supervision and Chat-Scene's object-centric representation for efficiency, while introducing a three-stage curriculum (perception alignment → CoT-SFT from program traces → GRPO-based CoT-RL) that progressively transfers systematic reasoning to open-set and complex real-world instructions.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The paper addresses a fundamental trade-off in 3D spatial reasoning: interpretability (NS3D) vs. flexibility (MLLMs), and demonstrates that reasoning *patterns* can be decoupled from concept-specific knowledge and transferred across paradigms. The modular architecture—where planning and perception components can be independently upgraded at inference without retraining—offers a practical path for continuous improvement as 3D perception models advance. However, the system remains bounded by its perception module (oracle labels yield +2.9% over best external perception), deep reasoning chains (≥5 nested operations) are not well validated, and the framework is limited to static indoor scenes without physics or dynamics. Open questions include whether the curriculum generalizes to outdoor/navigation domains, whether emergent logical primitives (intersection, union) remain coherent at greater depth, and whether the approach extends to causal or temporal reasoning.
