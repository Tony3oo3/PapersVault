## Problem

While GP-UCB enjoys well-established high-probability, lenient regret, and improved cumulative regret bounds, GP-TS analyses have been limited to expected regret with only a polynomial 1/δ dependence via Markov's inequality. Whether the recent tighter GP-UCB bounds on lenient regret and the time horizon T can be transferred to GP-TS remained an open problem, and no regret lower bound characterizing the fundamental δ-dependence of GP-TS had been established.

## Input / Output

The analysis takes as input a black-box objective function f assumed to be a sample path from a zero-mean GP with a predefined kernel (squared exponential, Matérn, or linear) over a compact domain X ⊂ [0,r]^d, plus Gaussian observation noise. The outputs are purely theoretical: a regret lower bound (Theorem 3.1), a second-moment and high-probability cumulative regret upper bound (Theorem 3.2), expected lenient regret upper bounds (Theorem 3.3), and an improved Õ(√T) high-probability cumulative regret bound (Theorem 3.5). All bounds are expressed in terms of the maximum information gain γ_T, the time horizon T, and the failure probability δ, with kernel-specific constants for the Matérn smoothness parameter ν and lengthscale ℓ.

## Method

The paper establishes four results through distinct proof techniques. First, a two-armed GP instance with correlation 1/2 is constructed to show GP-TS incurs Ω(1/δ^c) regret with probability δ, proving that O(log(1/δ)) bounds are impossible in general. Second, the second moment E[R_T²] is bounded by O(Tγ_T log T) by decomposing regret via the GP-TS property that x_t and x* are identically distributed given D_{t-1}, then bounding the squared positive-part term (f(x*)−U_t(x*))_+² and the sum of posterior variances via MIG. Third, expected lenient regret is bounded using a novel inequality |T| ≤ Σ_{t∈T}(f(x*)−f(x_t))²/Δ² inspired by the elliptical potential count lemma, combined with a new lemma bounding E[Σ_{t∈T} σ²_{t−1}(x*)] and E[Σ_{t∈T} σ²_{t−1}(x_t)] by γ̃_{E[|T|]} despite x_t and x* not being identically distributed under the conditioning t∈T. Fourth, the Õ(√T) cumulative regret bound is obtained by partitioning the index set into regret-level strata {T_i} with geometrically decreasing thresholds Δ_i, bounding each stratum's regret via the lenient regret machinery and the shrinking MIG over balls B(√(c_quad^{-1}Δ_i); x*), and showing the number of strata is O(1) under the relaxed Matérn condition ν > 2 (previously requiring 2ν+d ≤ ν²).

## Results

The paper establishes four main theoretical results for GP-TS in the Bayesian setting. (i) A regret lower bound (Theorem 3.1) showing GP-TS incurs Ω(1/δ^c) regret with probability δ on a two-armed instance, proving O(log(1/δ)) high-probability bounds are impossible in general. (ii) A second-moment bound E[R_T²] = O(T γ_T log T) (Theorem 3.2), yielding a high-probability bound of Õ(√(T γ_T log T / δ)), improving the previous Õ(√(T γ_T) / δ) by a factor of 1/√δ. (iii) Polylogarithmic expected lenient regret upper bounds (Theorem 3.3), the first such bounds for any BO algorithm. (iv) An improved Õ(√T) high-probability cumulative regret bound for SE and Matérn (ν>2) kernels (Theorem 3.5), matching GP-UCB's improved bound while relaxing the Matérn condition from 2ν+d≤ν² to ν>2.

## Baselines & Comparisons

The paper compares GP-TS against GP-UCB and prior GP-TS analyses on the dimension of high-probability regret bounds. GP-UCB achieves O(√(T γ_T) log(1/δ)) high-probability regret with logarithmic dependence on δ, while the best prior GP-TS bound was Õ(√(T γ_T) / δ) via Markov's inequality on expected regret. Theorem 3.2 tightens GP-TS to Õ(√(T γ_T log T / δ)), a 1/√δ improvement, but Theorem 3.1 proves GP-TS fundamentally cannot match GP-UCB's O(log(1/δ)) dependence. For the time horizon T, Theorem 3.5 achieves Õ(√T) for SE and Matérn kernels, matching GP-UCB's improved bound from Iwazaki (2025b) and relaxing the Matérn smoothness condition from 2ν+d≤ν² to ν>2. For lenient regret, the paper provides the first expected (rather than high-probability) polylogarithmic bounds, matching the order of GP-UCB's existing high-probability results from Cai et al. (2021) and Iwazaki (2025b) but via a different proof technique.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the regret analysis lineage for GP-TS established by Russo & Van Roy (2014) and Takeno et al. (2024), which provided only expected regret bounds, by deriving the first high-probability bounds with improved δ-dependence via a second-moment analysis. It adapts the refined T-dependence analysis of Iwazaki (2025b) for GP-UCB to GP-TS, relaxing the Matérn kernel smoothness condition from 2ν+d≤ν² to ν>2. The lenient regret analysis draws on the elliptical potential count lemma technique of Iwazaki & Takeno (2025) but employs a distinct proof strategy to obtain expected (rather than high-probability) bounds, a gap not previously addressed in the BO literature.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The paper closes a notable asymmetry in the BO theory literature where GP-UCB had well-developed high-probability and lenient regret guarantees while GP-TS was limited to expected regret with worse δ-dependence. The lower bound (Theorem 3.1) revealing that GP-TS fundamentally cannot achieve O(log(1/δ)) regret is a conceptual result that clarifies a structural difference between GP-TS and GP-UCB, though the large constant (c₂=17) limits practical implications. Key open questions remain: whether the δ-dependence in Theorem 3.2 is tight, whether GP-TS with variance inflation can recover logarithmic δ-dependence, and whether the ν>2 condition for Matérn kernels can be further relaxed to cover commonly used values like ν=1/2 or 3/2.
