## Problem

Image-to-3D multi-instance generation requires both accurate global layout and distinct local instances, yet existing approaches either rely on multi-stage compositional pipelines prone to error accumulation or fine-tune pre-trained I23D models at substantial training cost while still producing fused instances and imprecise layouts. The paper observes that pre-trained I23D diffusion models already encode meaningful spatial priors that remain underutilized, as evidenced by persistent instance entanglement, and asks whether these priors can be exploited and disentangled in a training-free manner.

## Input / Output

The method takes a single RGB reference image and a set of instance segmentation masks (extracted via Grounded-SAM) as input, and produces a multi-instance 3D scene as output. It operates entirely within the frozen inference pipeline of a pre-trained I23D diffusion model (Hunyuan3D 2.0), modifying only the cross-attention computations during early denoising steps (t≤15) and early transformer layers (l≤4), without any parameter updates. The 3D geometry is represented as a latent sequence z ∈ R^{L×d} decoded by a 3D VAE, and the guidance is applied to the 3D-to-image cross-attention sub-matrix A_zc that governs how 3D tokens query 2D image features.

## Method

TIMI introduces two complementary inference-time guidance modules applied to a frozen pre-trained I23D diffusion model. The Instance-aware Separation Guidance (ISG) projects the 3D-to-image cross-attention matrix onto discrete semantic instances using binary masks to form an Instance Probability Map, then optimizes a spatially weighted negative log-likelihood separation loss that encourages each 3D latent token to associate consistently with a single instance while preserving internal structural coherence. The Spatial-stabilized Geometry-adaptive Update (SGU) stabilizes the resulting gradients by applying isotropic 3D Gaussian smoothing to suppress high-frequency perturbations, followed by a peak-normalization-based adaptive scaling factor that modulates update magnitude relative to the current latent feature energy, and a momentum-based update to smooth temporal oscillations. Together, ISG promotes local instance disentanglement during early denoising while SGU preserves global layout coherence and geometric structure, achieving state-of-the-art spatial fidelity without any additional training.

## Results

On the 3D-Front synthetic benchmark, TIMI achieves the best global layout consistency (LCD 0.598) and scene-level F-Score (FS-S 0.458), while also attaining the highest Separation Success Rate (SSR 0.809) and object-level F-Score (FS-O 0.353) among all compared methods. Inference time is 59.2 s, comparable to the base Hunyuan3D 2.0 model (54.2 s) and substantially faster than MIDI (90.1 s) and DPA (783 s). On a larger 500-sample evaluation, TIMI maintains its lead over MIDI on all six spatial-fidelity metrics (e.g., LCD 0.596 vs 0.608, SSR 0.705 vs 0.690) within 95% confidence intervals.

## Baselines & Comparisons

Against the training-based MIDI (CVPR 2025), TIMI wins on LCD (0.598 vs 0.634), FS-S (0.458 vs 0.396), SSR (0.809 vs 0.737), and FS-O (0.353 vs 0.312), while MIDI retains a narrow edge on CD-S (0.0409 vs 0.0424) and CD-O (0.0760 vs 0.0855); TIMI is also ~34% faster in inference (59.2 s vs 90.1 s). Compared to the compositional DPA (NeurIPS 2024), TIMI dominates on every metric—LCD (0.598 vs 0.649), FS-S (0.458 vs 0.249), CD-O (0.0855 vs 0.1620), FS-O (0.353 vs 0.124)—and runs 13× faster (59.2 s vs 783 s). Relative to the unmodified Hunyuan3D 2.0 backbone, TIMI improves LCD (0.598 vs 0.627), FS-S (0.458 vs 0.450), SSR (0.809 vs 0.697), and FS-O (0.353 vs 0.339) with only a 5 s overhead, confirming that the training-free guidance adds spatial fidelity without sacrificing speed. Cross-backbone experiments on Trellis (CVPR 2025) further show TIMI improving LCD from 0.692 to 0.635 and FS-S from 0.221 to 0.273, demonstrating generalizability beyond Hunyuan3D.

## Relation to Prior Work

TIMI positions itself against two established paradigms for Image-to-3D multi-instance generation: compositional pipelines (e.g., DPA) that generate instances independently and assemble them, and training-based fine-tuning (e.g., MIDI) that adapts I23D models with multi-instance attention. It departs from both by operating entirely at inference time on a frozen pre-trained DiT-based I23D model (Hunyuan3D 2.0), intervening on early cross-attention layers to enforce instance separation (ISG) and stabilizing the resulting gradients with spatial smoothing and geometry-adaptive scaling (SGU). The conceptual lineage draws on score-distillation-style inference-time guidance in 3D generation, but repurposes it for the specific problem of disentangling entangled instances without any parameter updates.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

By demonstrating that pre-trained I23D models already encode usable spatial priors for multi-instance layout, TIMI lowers the barrier to high-fidelity multi-instance 3D generation by eliminating the fine-tuning cost that prior training-based methods require. The cross-backbone validation on Trellis suggests the guidance mechanism is broadly applicable rather than tied to a single architecture. However, the approach still depends on accurate instance masks (via Grounded-SAM), is evaluated primarily on 2–3 instance scenes, and the absolute spatial fidelity metrics remain moderate, leaving open whether the method scales to denser scenes with many overlapping instances or to more complex real-world layouts.
