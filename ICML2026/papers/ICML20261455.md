## Problem

LLMs express human values through two distinct routes—preference-learned (intrinsic) and prompt-elicited (prompted)—but whether these routes share the same internal mechanisms or rely on separate ones has been largely unexamined at the mechanistic level. Prior work treats the two approaches as interchangeable based on researcher intuition, leaving open the question of when each is appropriate and what each uniquely contributes to model behavior.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ICML20261455/cropped_page1_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method consumes 26,334 real-world first-turn queries (from ShareGPT and LMSYS-Chat-1M) and generates matched response sets under two conditions: an empty system prompt (intrinsic) and a value-targeting system prompt drawn from 500 augmented templates (prompted). It produces per-value, per-layer linear steering vectors in the residual stream, a set of attributed MLP intermediate neurons classified as shared, intrinsic-unique, or prompted-unique, and—when applied at inference time—steered text responses that can be evaluated across five languages and multiple benchmark formats (PVQ, situational dilemmas, Value Portrait).

## Method

The pipeline proceeds in three stages. First, for each of ten Schwartz values, value vectors are extracted as difference-in-means directions over token-averaged residual stream activations, contrasting GPT-4o-mini-labeled value-expressed versus value-unexpressed responses under intrinsic and prompted conditions separately. Second, the paired vectors are disentangled via two complementary decompositions: orthogonalized directions (removing one vector's projection onto the other) for causal ablation, and SVD of the 2D subspace spanned by both vectors to yield a shared axis and mechanism-unique axes for geometric analysis. Third, MLP neurons are identified by projecting their output weight vectors onto the joint value subspace and classifying them by angular alignment (<30°) with the shared or unique axes. Validation through activation steering (adding α·v at a selected middle layer), cross-lingual transfer, and direct neuron scaling reveals that shared components recover Schwartz's theoretical circumplex structure, intrinsic-unique components promote lexical and semantic diversity, and prompted-unique components encode a value-agnostic instruction-compliance mechanism that generalizes to jailbreak and non-value tasks.

## Results

On the PVQ-40 (6-point scale) with Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, prompted value vectors achieve a mean score delta of +2.21 across ten Schwartz values and five languages, versus +1.74 for intrinsic vectors; the prompted-orthogonal component retains +1.62 while the intrinsic-orthogonal drops to +0.47, indicating prompted vectors encode additional non-collinear steering information. On the free-form 10-point PVQ, both vectors achieve comparable gains (+1.04 prompted, +0.98 intrinsic). In jailbreaking experiments, steering along the mean prompted-minus-intrinsic delta direction raises Attack Success Rate from 13.3% to 97.2% on AdvBench and from 23.8% to 90.4% on HarmBench for Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct. Shared value axes recover Schwartz's theoretical circumplex with R²≈0.6–0.7 at the four higher-order domain level, while intrinsic steering consistently produces higher lexical diversity (e.g., Distinct-2 of 0.362 vs. 0.342) than prompted steering.

## Baselines & Comparisons

This is a mechanistic interpretability study rather than a method-vs-method benchmark, so the primary comparisons are between the two value-expression mechanisms (intrinsic vs. prompted) and their decomposed components. Prompted vectors consistently outperform intrinsic vectors on PVQ steering (+2.21 vs. +1.74 on the 6-point scale), and this gap persists after orthogonalization: the prompted-orthogonal component retains +1.62 average gain (removing only 32–73% of its norm) while the intrinsic-orthogonal component drops to +0.47, revealing that prompted vectors carry additional non-collinear information. On the diversity axis, intrinsic steering wins: it produces higher Distinct-2 (0.362 vs. 0.342), higher embedding variation (0.563 vs. 0.549), and broader token-distribution entropy than prompted steering. On instruction compliance, the prompted-unique (mean delta) direction dominates: it raises jailbreak ASR from 13.3% to 97.2% on AdvBench (Llama-3.1-8B), far exceeding what system prompts alone achieve. External validation confirms the vectors capture meaningful semantics: Benevolence vectors show higher cosine similarity with Persona Vectors for altruism/forgiveness than Power vectors do, and Power steering increases MACHIAVELLI power-seeking scores while Benevolence steering decreases them.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper builds on the difference-in-means vector extraction framework (Li et al., 2023; Marks & Tegmark, 2024) and activation-engineering steering (Turner et al., 2024), but applies them to a comparative setting that prior work has not addressed: systematically decomposing intrinsic value vectors (Jin et al., 2025) and prompted value vectors (Su et al., 2025) into shared and mechanism-unique components via orthogonalization and SVD. It further extends the analysis to the neuron level using MLP output-weight projection, going beyond the residual-stream-only view of prior value-steering work. The use of Schwartz's ten-value circumplex as a structured ground truth for validating extracted directions is a departure from prior work that typically treats values as unstructured categories.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The discovery that prompted-unique components encode a value-agnostic instruction-compliance channel—evidenced by the dramatic jailbreak ASR increase (13.3%→97.2% on AdvBench) when steering along the mean delta direction—has direct implications for AI safety monitoring and robustness. For pluralistic alignment, the paper provides a mechanistic basis for when to prefer intrinsic mechanisms (diversity, naturalness) versus prompted mechanisms (steerability, compliance), moving the field beyond heuristic choices. Open questions include whether the compliance channel is truly separable from value semantics at scale, whether the shared-component recovery of Schwartz's circular structure holds in multilingual or multimodal settings, and whether neuron-level interventions can be made more targeted without the broad side effects observed here.
