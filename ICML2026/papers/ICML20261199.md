## Problem

Transformer attention scales quadratically with sequence length and requires external positional encodings that limit context extrapolation. Prior Fourier-based replacements (e.g., FNet) cannot enforce causality for autoregressive generation, while state-space models like Mamba achieve linear complexity but depend on hardware-specific CUDA kernels, hindering portability. No existing architecture simultaneously achieves sub-quadratic global mixing, strict causality, data-dependent filtering, and hardware-agnostic implementation.

## Input / Output

The model consumes a standard text token sequence (GPT-2 tokenizer) shaped as a B×L×D tensor and produces next-token logits over the vocabulary for autoregressive generation. Training uses a context length of 512 tokens (10B tokens on FineWeb-10B) or 4096 tokens (15B tokens) for the broader comparison, with no positional encoding module in the input pipeline. The architecture is a decoder-only stack of 12–24 layers, each producing a residual-updated hidden state of dimension D before the final language modeling head.

## Method

Caracal replaces global attention with a Multi-Head Fourier (MHF) module that performs causal, data-dependent token mixing in O(L log L) time. Each MHF layer applies a causal depthwise 1D convolution for local inductive bias, then splits the normalized input into a content stream (linear projection) and a gate stream (Linear → SiLU → grouped Conv1d for intra-head communication). Both streams are zero-padded to 2L, transformed via FFT, multiplied element-wise in the frequency domain, inverse-transformed, and truncated to L—mathematically equivalent to a causal convolution whose kernel is dynamically generated from the input. The hybrid architecture interleaves MHF layers with Sliding Window Attention layers at a 2:1 ratio (window size 256), and omits all explicit positional encodings since the Fourier basis inherently encodes relative position. This design relies exclusively on standard library FFT operators, avoiding the custom CUDA kernels required by selective SSMs while retaining content-aware mixing comparable to Mamba's selective state updates.

## Results

At the Large scale (724M params, 10B tokens, 512 ctx), Caracal achieves 49.01% average accuracy across 8 benchmarks, matching Mamba (49.00%) and Jamba (49.07%) while using fewer parameters, and outperforming Llama (48.73%) and Mamba-2 (48.75%). On LAMBADA perplexity, Caracal scores 29.39, competitive with Llama (24.92) and Mamba (34.34). In the broader 345M/15B-token/4096-ctx comparison (Table 3), Caracal (Default) achieves 46.62% average accuracy and 37.27 LAMBADA PPL, outperforming all listed baselines including Moneta (46.45%), YAAD (45.94%), and Gated DeltaNet (45.42%). On long-context retrieval (SWDE/FDA), Caracal (5.37% avg) trails Llama (9.55%) but is on par with Mamba (5.46%).

## Baselines & Comparisons

Against Llama (Transformer + RoPE), Caracal matches or slightly exceeds average accuracy at Tiny (41.14 vs 40.87) and Large (49.01 vs 48.73) sizes, but trails at Medium (46.47 vs 47.07) and on LAMBADA PPL across all sizes. Versus Mamba, Caracal is essentially tied at Tiny (41.14 vs 41.12) and Large (49.01 vs 49.00), slightly behind at Small (43.35 vs 43.60), and ahead at Medium (46.47 vs 46.39). Against Mamba-2, Caracal wins at every size class (e.g., 49.01 vs 48.75 at Large). Against Jamba, Caracal is competitive but slightly behind at Large (49.01 vs 49.07) and Medium (46.47 vs 46.54), while matching at Tiny. In the broader 345M/15B-token comparison (Table 3), Caracal (Default) at 46.62% average accuracy outperforms all listed baselines: Moneta (46.45%), YAAD (45.94%), Memora (45.51%), Gated DeltaNet (45.42%), TTT (44.51%), GLA (44.09%), DeltaNet (44.04%), Mamba (43.59%), RetNet (42.54%), and Transformer++ (42.92%). The one clear loss is on long-context retrieval (SWDE/FDA), where Llama's dense attention achieves 9.55% average versus Caracal's 5.37%, though Caracal is on par with Mamba (5.46%) and Mamba-2 (5.18%).

## Relation to Prior Work

Caracal extends the FNet line of work (replacing attention with FFT) by solving the long-standing causality barrier that confined spectral models to encoder-only settings, using a pad-FFT-multiply-iFFT-truncate pipeline equivalent to causal convolution. It departs from SSMs (Mamba, Mamba-2) by achieving data-dependent mixing through input-generated frequency-domain filters rather than selective state updates, thereby preserving the parallel convolutional structure and avoiding custom CUDA kernels. The hybrid MHF + Sliding Window Attention design is structurally analogous to Jamba (Mamba + Attention) but substitutes the SSM component with a Fourier module, while the content-based gating distinguishes it from Hyena's position-based implicit convolutions.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

Caracal offers a practically important middle ground: O(L log L) complexity with hardware-agnostic standard library operators, making it accessible to researchers and practitioners who cannot invest in custom kernel development (as Mamba requires) or accept quadratic attention costs. The acknowledged retrieval gap versus dense attention on fine-grained extraction tasks (SWDE, FDA) remains a significant open question, as does whether the architecture scales to frontier model sizes. The finding that explicit positional encodings are redundant given the Fourier basis is a clean theoretical validation, but the broader question of whether O(L log L) can close the remaining performance gap with O(L) SSMs at scale remains unresolved.
