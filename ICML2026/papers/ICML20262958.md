## Problem

Post-training quantization of diffusion models suffers from progressive error accumulation across iterative denoising steps, where per-step quantization errors compound and fundamentally limit lower-bit viability. Existing PTQ methods minimize per-step errors in isolation but lack any formal model of how these errors propagate and accumulate along the sampling trajectory, leaving a critical gap between single-step accuracy and end-to-end generation fidelity.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ICML20262958/cropped_page2_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method operates on a pre-quantized diffusion model (SDXL, SDXL-Turbo, PixArt-σ, OpenSora, CogVideoX, Flux) built atop SVDQuant or ViDiT-Q, taking a text prompt and producing images at 512×512 or 1024×1024 resolution, or 480P videos. A small offline calibration set (1,024 COCO prompts for images, 128 WebVid samples for video) is used to precompute per-timestep channel-wise scaling matrices K ∈ ℝ^{T×C}, which are then cached and applied during inference. At runtime, the only additional state required is the noise-estimation output from the immediately preceding denoising step, making the correction overhead negligible (<0.5% E2E latency).

## Method

TCEC derives the first closed-form solution for cumulative quantization error in diffusion models by formulating the error propagation equation δ_{t−1} = A_t δ_t + B_t ε_t from the DDIM update rule, then applying two simplifications: (1) neglecting the Jacobian term (empirically <0.5% of the dominant propagation coefficient) to collapse the product of inverse matrices into a telescoping ratio, and (2) a temporal locality approximation that proves m=1 suffices, meaning only the two most recent per-step errors contribute meaningfully to the current cumulative error. The per-step error ε_t is reconstructed online as ε_t = K_t ⊙ μ̃_θ(x̃_t, t), where K_t are channel-wise scaling coefficients solved in closed form from a regularized MSE loss calibrated offline. At each denoising step, the correction term Δ_t = −(1/√α_{t−1}) Σ_{k=t}^{min(t+1,T)} √α_{k−1} B_k K_k μ̃_θ(x̃_k, k) is added to the DDIM update, dynamically aligning the quantized trajectory with the full-precision one. The framework extends to higher-order solvers like DPM++ with solver-specific propagation coefficients, and is fully orthogonal to the underlying PTQ algorithm.

## Results

On SDXL W4A4, SVDQuant+TCEC achieves an sDCI PSNR of 21.9 (vs. 20.7 for SVDQuant) and MJHQ FID of 18.1 (vs. 20.6), surpassing even TensorRT's W8A8 PSNR of 21.7. On PixArt-σ W4A4, TCEC reduces MJHQ FID from 19.2 to 18.1 and improves sDCI PSNR from 16.5 to 16.9. For video generation on OpenSora (W4A8), TCEC achieves 64.97 Imaging Quality and 37.20 Scene Consistency on VBench, outperforming ViDiT-Q by +3.90 and +1.01 respectively. The additional end-to-end latency overhead is less than 0.5% across all tested models.

## Baselines & Comparisons

TCEC consistently outperforms SVDQuant (Li et al., 2024b) across all models and precision levels: on SDXL W4A4 it improves sDCI PSNR by +1.2 (21.9 vs. 20.7) and MJHQ FID by −2.5 (18.1 vs. 20.6); on PixArt-σ W4A4 it reduces MJHQ FID by 1.1 and improves sDCI PSNR by +0.4. Against MixDQ (Zhao et al., 2024b) on SDXL-Turbo W4A4, TCEC avoids complete quantization failure (MixDQ FID=353) and achieves FID 23.9 vs. 24.6. On video generation (OpenSora VBench), TCEC surpasses ViDiT-Q (Zhao et al., 2024a) by +3.90 on W4A8 Imaging Quality and +1.01 on Scene Consistency, and outperforms Q-Diffusion (Li et al., 2023a) and PTQ4DiT (Wu et al., 2024) by large margins (e.g., 64.97 vs. 23.30/37.97 Imaging Quality at W4A8). On CIFAR-10 (Table 9), TCEC outperforms QNCD (Chu et al., 2024) in FID and sFID at both W4A8 and W4A6, confirming its advantage over prior error-correction approaches that target single-step noise rather than cumulative propagation.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper builds directly on SVDQuant and ViDiT-Q as its base quantization backbones, but departs from the entire line of diffusion-model PTQ work (Q-Diffusion, PTQ4DM, MixDQ, SVDQuant, ViDiT-Q) that focuses on minimizing per-step quantization error at individual denoising layers. The key conceptual departure is shifting the optimization target from single-step fidelity to the cumulative error that accumulates across the iterative sampling trajectory, for which the authors derive the first closed-form solution using DDIM as a paradigmatic case. The resulting TCEC mechanism is explicitly orthogonal to existing PTQ algorithms and can be layered on top of them without modification, positioning it as a complementary correction stage rather than a replacement quantization strategy.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The theoretical framework—particularly the closed-form cumulative error solution and the proof that only the immediately preceding step's error matters (m=1)—provides a principled foundation that prior heuristic approaches lacked, and the <0.5% inference overhead makes it immediately deployable alongside existing quantization pipelines. This enables practical W4A4 quantization of large diffusion models (3.5× memory reduction, 3× speedup over NF4) with quality close to FP16, which was previously infeasible due to error accumulation. Open questions include whether the m=1 temporal locality bound holds universally across all solver orders and architectures, whether the channel-wise scaling assumption (Eq. 14) generalizes to non-UNet/DiT backbones, and how the method behaves under extreme step reductions (≤2 steps) where accumulated error is minimal.
