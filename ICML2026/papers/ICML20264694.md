## Problem

Reconstructing accurate 3D surfaces from noisy point clouds remains difficult because standard Noise2Noise (N2N) denoising relies on exact pixel-coordinate correspondences that do not exist in unstructured point clouds. While prior 3D N2N extensions (e.g., N2NM) resort to soft geometric losses like Earth Mover's Distance, no prior work has exploited the structured coordinate-to-value mapping of neural Signed Distance Functions to enable exact coordinate-wise noisy supervision in 3D.

## Input / Output

The method takes two independently corrupted point clouds (2048 points each, normalized to a unit cube) of the same underlying shape, with zero-mean Gaussian noise at σ=0.01 or 0.02 applied online. One point cloud feeds the denoising network while the other is processed by a frozen Point2SDF network to generate noisy SDF targets; both are evaluated at 8192 shared query points (50% near-surface from the noisy clouds, 50% uniform in the cube). The output is a set of denoised SDF values at arbitrary query coordinates, from which a clean watertight surface mesh is extracted via Marching Cubes in a single forward pass at inference.

## Method

The core insight is that a neural SDF maps 3D coordinates to scalar distance values, establishing the same coordinate-wise correspondence that N2N exploits in 2D images, so two noisy SDFs of the same shape can be directly compared with MSE loss. During training, a transformer-based denoising network (built on 3DS2V's cross-attention encoder, self-attention latent refinement, and cross-attention decoder) takes noisy point cloud p₁ and predicts SDF values at query points, while a frozen 3DS2V instance processes the paired noisy point cloud p₂ to produce noisy SDF targets. The MSE between predicted and target SDFs at shared coordinates drives learning, requiring no clean ground truth. A first-order analysis shows that zero-mean Gaussian perturbation of closest surface points yields unbiased SDF estimates, providing theoretical grounding for the noisy-target supervision. The framework is architecture-agnostic, validated with both 3DS2V and 3DILG as denoising backbones and target generators.

## Results

On ShapeNet (σ=0.01), NoiseSDF2NoiseSDF achieves mean IoU 0.929 vs. 3DS2V's 0.884 (+5.1%), NC 0.958 vs. 0.916 (+4.6%), and Chamfer 0.0113 vs. 0.0132. At higher corruption (σ=0.02), gains widen to IoU +16.3% (0.572 vs. 0.492) and NC +18.4% (0.868 vs. 0.733). Across the ABC, Famous, and Real benchmarks (σ=0.01), the method attains the best mean NC (0.847), Mesh NC (0.027), and F-Score (0.945) among all compared data-driven and overfitting-based approaches, while remaining competitive on Chamfer Distance.

## Baselines & Comparisons

Against 3DS2V (the backbone architecture), NoiseSDF2NoiseSDF wins on every metric at both noise levels: at σ=0.01 it gains +0.045 IoU and +0.042 NC, and at σ=0.02 the NC gap widens to +0.135, confirming that noisy supervision alone suffices to surpass clean-supervised training. Among data-driven SOTA methods (P2S, PPSurf, POCO, NKSR), the proposed method achieves the best mean NC (0.847 at σ=0.01) and Mesh NC (0.027), outperforming POCO (0.844 NC) and NKSR (0.785 NC) by clear margins, while POCO retains a slight edge on Chamfer Distance (0.016 vs. 0.015 mean) and F-Score (0.962 vs. 0.945) at σ=0.01. Against overfitting-based methods SAP-O and PGR, the proposed method consistently leads in NC (0.847 vs. 0.713/0.825) and Mesh NC (0.027 vs. 0.076/0.035), though PGR occasionally achieves lower Chamfer Distance due to its adaptive resolution producing artificially smooth meshes. Compared to N2NM (the closest prior N2N-for-point-clouds method), the proposed method with test-time optimization (Ours-TTO) achieves lower Chamfer Distance under medium noise (0.0108 vs. 0.0132) while running ~138× faster (20 s vs. 2760 s per shape). In the appendix comparison against NSH and Poisson reconstruction, the method dominates on all four metrics (e.g., mean NC 0.819 vs. 0.765 for NSH and 0.764 for Poisson), and against the two-stage IterativePFN+3DS2V pipeline it improves IoU from 0.882 to 0.927 and F-Score from 0.969 to 0.986.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the Noise2Noise (N2N) paradigm from 2D image denoising to 3D neural signed distance functions, identifying a structural parallel between pixel-coordinate-to-intensity mappings and SDF-coordinate-to-scalar mappings that enables direct MSE-based supervision. It departs from prior 3D N2N work (N2NM, TotalDenoising) that was forced to use Earth Mover's Distance due to the unstructured nature of point clouds, by reformulating the problem in the continuous SDF domain where coordinate-wise correspondence is naturally established. The denoising backbone is built on 3DS2V's transformer-based encoder-decoder architecture, and the noisy target is generated by a frozen off-the-shelf Point2SDF network, making the approach architecture-agnostic as validated with 3DILG.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The framework demonstrates that clean neural fields can be learned entirely from noisy supervision without any clean ground truth, which is practically significant in domains where clean 3D geometry is unavailable (robotics, AR, cultural heritage). The method's robustness across noise types, target generators, and denoising backbones suggests broad applicability, though performance remains dependent on the quality of the Point2SDF target generator. Open questions include scaling to larger point clouds for complex geometries, handling non-i.i.d. structured sensor noise (partially addressed with LiDAR-style experiments), and whether the approach extends to other neural field types beyond SDFs.
