## Problem

Low-rank KV cache compression for LLMs has been limited to moderate compression rates (30–50%) because prior methods rely on fixed or heuristic rank selection that cannot adapt to the heterogeneous sensitivity of individual attention heads and decoder blocks. Pushing beyond this regime causes severe accuracy degradation, and the reconstruction overhead of low-rank factorization further increases inference latency. The paper tackles the gap of enabling aggressive (up to 75%) KV cache compression with minimal quality loss through learned, fine-grained rank control.

## Input / Output

At inference, the method consumes an input token sequence and pre-computed low-rank projection factors (truncated SVD factors of W_K and W_V, obtained offline after a short calibration fine-tune). It produces compressed KV cache entries as low-rank latent representations of dimension r (adaptive per head for keys, per block for values) rather than full d_h-dimensional tensors, which are further stored in mixed-precision (4-bit for the top 20% of channels, 3-bit for the rest) after a block-wise Hadamard transform. The final output is generated text, with the compressed cache enabling up to 20× overall KV memory reduction and 128K context on a single RTX 4090.

## Method

STAR-KV introduces three coupled mechanisms. First, a differentiable soft-threshold operator (shifted tanh) is applied to the singular values of each key/value projection, with a learnable threshold α per attention head (keys) and per decoder block (values); this is trained via a joint objective combining KL-divergence knowledge distillation from the uncompressed teacher and an adaptive compression loss (sum of e^{-α_i}) that automatically balances compression pressure against accuracy recovery. Second, a hybrid decomposition scheme applies head-wise decomposition to keys (lower reconstruction FLOPs, less spectral sensitivity) and joint decomposition to values (lower approximation error, higher spectral sensitivity), with weight absorption on the value path to offset the joint-decomposition overhead. Third, a low-rank-aware block-wise Hadamard quantization splits the latent channels into outlier (top 20%) and inlier blocks, applies independent Hadamard rotations to each, and quantizes them at different bit widths (4-bit vs. 3-bit), exploiting the ordered singular-value structure to handle outliers without explicit modeling. Custom Triton kernels fuse RoPE, dequantization, reconstruction, and attention computation to realize up to 6.9× attention speedup.

## Results

On LongChat-7B-v1.5, STAR-KV at 60% KV cache compression achieves 61.53% average zero-shot accuracy, exactly matching the uncompressed baseline (61.53%), while Palu at 50% compression drops to 58.49% and ReCalKV at 70% drops to 53.07%. At 75% compression, STAR-KV retains 60.49% average accuracy versus 26.33% degradation for static rank allocation. When combined with 3.2-bit mixed-precision quantization, STAR-KV achieves 20× overall KV cache compression at 59.18% average accuracy, outperforming KVQuant (6.9× at 58.00%) and Palu with 4-bit quantization (8.0× at 56.71%). Custom Triton kernels deliver up to 6.9× attention speedup and 3.1× end-to-end generation throughput improvement.

## Baselines & Comparisons

Against Palu (Chang et al., 2025), the primary low-rank KV compression baseline, STAR-KV at 60% compression achieves 61.53% average zero-shot accuracy on LongChat-7B-v1.5, exceeding Palu at 50% (58.49%) by 3.04% while compressing 10% more aggressively; at 75% compression STAR-KV (60.49%) still outperforms Palu at 50% by 2.0%. Against ReCalKV (Yan et al., 2025), STAR-KV at 75% compression (60.49%) surpasses ReCalKV at 70% (53.07%) by 7.42% on LongChat-7B-v1.5, demonstrating substantially better accuracy retention at higher compression. In the quantization comparison (Table 4), STAR-KV with 3.2-bit mixed-precision quantization achieves 20× overall KV cache compression at 59.18% accuracy, outperforming KVQuant (Hooper et al., 2024) at 6.9×/58.00% and KIVI (Liu et al., 2024) at 5.1×/60.3%, while also beating Palu with 4-bit quantization (8.0×/56.71%) by both compression and accuracy. Against H2O (Zhang et al., 2023), STAR-KV is complementary rather than directly competitive: combining 60% low-rank compression with 60% H2O token pruning reaches 84% total KV cache compression at 59.99% accuracy, slightly exceeding H2O alone at 60% pruning (59.95%). On long-context benchmarks (RULER at 4K), STAR-KV at 60% compression scores 88.26 on LongChat-7B-v1.5 versus Palu at 50% (85.06), and on LLaMA-3.1-8B-Instruct improves from 85.06 to 89.03.

## Relation to Prior Work

STAR-KV extends the low-rank KV cache compression line (Palu, ReCalKV, Eigen Attention) by replacing fixed or heuristic rank allocation with a differentiable soft-thresholding mechanism borrowed from token-pruning literature (Li et al., 2022), applied here to singular value truncation. It adopts weight absorption from DeepSeek-V2's MLA for value reconstruction to offset the overhead of joint decomposition, and introduces a hybrid HD-for-keys / JD-for-values scheme grounded in a sensitivity analysis of singular value spectra. The quantization component departs from prior KV quantization methods (KIVI, KVQuant) that rely on explicit outlier modeling by instead exploiting the ordered singular-value structure to enable block-wise Hadamard with mixed precision, requiring no additional outlier detection.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The 75% low-rank compression with near-lossless accuracy and 20× total compression when combined with 3.2-bit quantization directly addresses the dominant memory bottleneck in long-context LLM inference, making 128K+ context feasible on consumer GPUs. The learned rank profiles' stability across calibration datasets (≤1.3% deviation) and model variants suggests the approach is robust and low-cost to deploy (<6 GPU hours). Open questions include whether the method scales to 70B+ models, how it interacts with architectural changes like MLA or GQA at higher compression, and whether the calibration requirement can be eliminated entirely for truly zero-shot deployment.
