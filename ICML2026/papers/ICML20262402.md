## Problem

Existing geometry-integration strategies for MLLMs (e.g., VG-LLM, VLM-3R) treat 3D geometric features as a uniformly exposed stream and fuse them indiscriminately at the input level, causing semantic-geometry misalignment and injection of redundant noise. The paper identifies that geometric cues are both task-dependent and spatially selective—relevant geometry is often confined to specific regions—yet passive fusion provides no mechanism for the model to selectively retrieve only the geometric evidence it needs for a given reasoning step.

## Input / Output

The model consumes a sequence of RGB video frames (8 or 32 frames per scene during training, extrapolating to 128 frames at inference) alongside a natural-language spatial query. A VGGT geometry encoder processes all frames jointly to produce per-frame 3D geometry feature maps, which are resampled onto the MLLM's spatially-merged token grid (accounting for the 2×2 patch merge in Qwen-VL). The output is a free-form text response answering the spatial reasoning question (e.g., relative distance, object count, route planning, or multiple-choice spatial relations).

## Method

GeoThinker replaces input-level geometry fusion with Spatial-Grounded Fusion (SGF), a mid-layer cross-attention module where semantic visual hidden states serve as queries that actively retrieve task-relevant geometric cues from VGGT features. Two mechanisms enforce selectivity: (1) Frame-wise Constraints reshape tokens back to spatial dimensions so each semantic query attends only to geometry from the same frame, preventing cross-frame interference; (2) Importance Gating predicts a per-token sigmoid score from image hidden states via a lightweight MLP, converted to a log-space additive bias on cross-attention logits to emphasize salient structures (object boundaries, relational links) while suppressing irrelevant regions. A global learnable scalar α (initialized to 0, applied via tanh) controls overall injection intensity. SGF is deployed at strategically selected middle LLM layers (fusion ratio ρ=0.5–0.75), deliberately skipping the first and last layers to preserve early visual processing and late-stage linguistic generation, with the fused features added back to the residual stream.

## Results

GeoThinker Qwen3VL-8B achieves a peak score of 72.6 on VSI-Bench (Scaled regime), setting a new state-of-the-art and outperforming the previous best open-source model SenseNova-SI-1.1-InternVL3-8B (68.8) and proprietary Gemini-3-Pro-Preview (52.5). Across six spatial intelligence benchmarks, GeoThinker reaches an average of 62.23, surpassing GPT-5 (57.50) and Gemini-3-Pro-Preview (62.16). On VSI-Debiased with 128-frame inference, GeoThinker scores 68.1, well above Cambrian-S-7B (59.9) and VG-LLM-8B* (55.1), confirming robustness against language shortcuts. Downstream, GeoThinker improves embodied referring by +1.66% average accuracy on RefSpatial-Bench and boosts autonomous driving PDMS by +2.0 points on NAVSIM.

## Baselines & Comparisons

GeoThinker decisively outperforms the primary geometry-integration baseline VG-LLM-8B* (51.05 avg) by +11.18 points, with the largest gap on VSI-Bench (72.6 vs. 62.2) and MindCube (83.0 vs. 36.1), confirming that mid-layer Spatial-Grounded Fusion is far more effective than input-level patch addition. Against the strongest open-source spatial model SenseNova-SI-1.1-InternVL3-8B, GeoThinker leads on VSI-Bench (72.6 vs. 68.8) and ViewSpatial (45.9 vs. 54.7 is a loss), but trails on MindCube (83.0 vs. 85.7) and MMSI-Bench (30.9 vs. 43.3). GeoThinker surpasses all proprietary models including GPT-5 (57.50) and Gemini-3-Pro-Preview (62.16) on average, though Gemini-3-Pro-Preview retains an edge on MMSI-Bench (45.2 vs. 30.9) and CV-Bench (92.0 vs. 85.1). On VSTI-Bench, GeoThinker achieves 67.4, outperforming GPT-4o (38.2) by 29.2 points and VLM-3R-7B (58.8) by 8.6 points. On PhysGame, GeoThinker (56.9%) edges out PhysVLM-SFT (56.7%) and GPT-4o-0806 (56.1%). The model also beats Cambrian-S-7B (47.28) by +14.95 points on average, with the advantage most pronounced on MindCube (+43.4) and VSI-Bench (+5.1).

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper builds directly on VG-LLM's integration of VGGT geometry features into MLLMs, but departs fundamentally from the passive, input-level fusion paradigm (patch-level addition or global cross-attention) by proposing Spatial-Grounded Fusion: a mid-LLM-layer mechanism where semantic tokens actively query task-relevant geometry via frame-strict cross-attention, further modulated by a learned Importance Gating bias. It positions itself against a broader family of geometry-aware MLLMs (VLM-3R, 3DThinker, G²VLM) that inject geometry through training-time supervision or dedicated experts, arguing that selective, inference-time retrieval conditioned on internal reasoning demands is more effective than uniform exposure. The data-scaling line (Cambrian-S, SenseNova-SI) is treated as complementary rather than competing, and GeoThinker demonstrates that its architecture is more robust to the task interference that plagues pure 2D VLMs when mixed with general video data.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The practical impact is significant: GeoThinker achieves SOTA on VSI-Bench (72.6) with only 1.8M training samples (vs. 8M for SenseNova-SI), and transfers to embodied referring and autonomous driving with consistent gains, suggesting the active-integration principle is broadly applicable. The finding that ρ=1.0 (fusing geometry into every LLM layer) causes catastrophic collapse (score drops to 0.41) reveals an important constraint on where external structural signals can be injected without disrupting linguistic generation. Open questions remain around absolute metric estimation (still well below human on distance tasks), the sequential VGGT bottleneck limiting real-time deployment, and whether the approach generalizes beyond indoor scenes to outdoor or dynamic 3D environments.
