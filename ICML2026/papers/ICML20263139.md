## Problem

LLMs excel at high-level semantic reasoning but remain unreliable at the precise numerical computation that decision-critical quantitative tasks demand, particularly in financial settings where small errors compound under nonlinearity and uncertainty. Existing approaches either entangle planning and computation in a single reasoning loop or rely on fixed tool sets, and evaluations predominantly use historical backtesting with exogenous prices that obscure whether observed performance reflects genuine reasoning or spurious correlation.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ICML20263139/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The agent consumes a sliding window of length D over partial market signals (OHLC prices, volumes, derived technical indicators, and agent-induced order-flow statistics) for N tradable assets, with no access to absolute time or future prices. It produces two distinct output types: (1) executable quantitative tools (indicators, feature transformations, diagnostic procedures) generated as constrained code that must satisfy hard constraints on causality, temporal alignment, and execution safety, and (2) portfolio-level allocation actions mapping to buy/sell quantities per asset. All numerical computation is delegated to the generated tools; free-text arithmetic by the LLM is explicitly disallowed, making the tools verifiable intermediate artifacts rather than transient execution steps.

## Method

DeMAgent implements a three-stage pipeline—Market Analysis, Knowledge & Tool Creation, and Evaluation & Refinement—where the LLM acts solely as a high-level planner that synthesizes task-specific quantitative tools as executable code, rather than performing computation itself. Tool generation is formulated as constrained program synthesis: outputs must be syntactically valid, numerically sound, causally correct (no look-ahead bias), and interface-compliant, with violations causing execution failure rather than soft degradation. The evaluation stage uses prompt-based self-assessment with task-specific financial criteria to diagnose underperforming tool components, feeding structured diagnostic signals back to the tool-creation module for iterative refinement without parameter updates. This loop operates within DECOUPLED MARKET, a digital twin market where prices form endogenously from collective agent interactions via a centralized matching engine with bounded price impact (tanh-scaled excess demand over recent volume), modeled as a partially observable Markov game with induced non-stationarity from adaptive agent policies. The self-play is single-agent and performance-driven: the agent refines tools and strategies across episodes using metrics like total return and Sharpe ratio, with overfitting mitigated by evaluation across multiple markets and time periods.

## Results

On the DECOUPLED MARKET digital twin, DeMAgent w/Gemini-3-Pro achieves the highest total return (TR = 13.95%) and Sharpe ratio (SR = 1.60) with zero maximum drawdown, outperforming the best standalone LLM (Gemini-3-Pro, TR = 10.52%, SR = 0.56) and the best prior agent (ArenaTrader w/GPT-5-mini, TR = 6.18%, SR = 0.97). On real-world markets, DeMAgent w/GPT-5-mini achieves TR = 8.49% (SR = 0.21) on NASDAQ, TR = 41.32% (SR = 0.29) on CSI, and TR = 25.21% (SR = 0.29, WR = 67.86%) on CRYPTOCURRENCY, consistently leading all baselines in both absolute and risk-adjusted metrics. The ablation confirms that Knowledge & Tool Creation (KTC) contributes the largest single-module gain, and the full three-stage pipeline (MA + KTC + ER) yields the most stable cross-market performance.

## Baselines & Comparisons

DeMAgent w/GPT-5-mini outperforms all seven classical technical strategies (Buy&Hold, ATR, RSI, ZMR, Bollinger, SMA, MACD) on TR and SR across all three real markets; for example, on NASDAQ it achieves TR = 8.49% vs. the best technical strategy (ATR, TR = 0.60%) and on CRYPTOCURRENCY TR = 25.21% vs. Buy&Hold (13.76%). Against the four RL baselines, DeMAgent leads on NASDAQ (TR 8.49% vs. TD3's 1.93%) and CRYPTOCURRENCY (TR 25.21% vs. A2C/TD3's 4.78%), and on CSI it surpasses TD3 (TR 41.32% vs. 32.60%) while also achieving a higher SR (0.29 vs. 0.20). Among standalone LLMs, DeMAgent w/GPT-5-mini beats every backbone used in standalone mode: GPT-5-mini standalone (TR 1.20% on NASDAQ), DeepSeek-V3.2 (TR 14.75% on CRYPTO), and Gemini-3-Pro (TR 12.48% on CSI), with the gap widening in volatile CRYPTOCURRENCY (25.21% vs. 14.75%). Against the two LLM-agent frameworks, DeMAgent outperforms StockSim w/GPT-5-mini on all three markets (e.g., CSI TR 41.32% vs. 10.33%) and ArenaTrader w/GPT-5-mini on all three (NASDAQ 8.49% vs. -7.57%; CRYPTO 25.21% vs. 13.85%), indicating that the tool-creation and evaluation-refinement pipeline provides gains beyond multi-agent orchestration alone. In the DECOUPLED MARKET, DeMAgent w/Gemini-3-Pro (TR 13.95%, SR 1.60, MD 0.00) dominates both the best standalone LLM (Gemini-3-Pro, TR 10.52%, SR 0.56) and ArenaTrader w/GPT-5-mini (TR 6.18%, SR 0.97), confirming the advantage of decoupled planning and tool-based computation in a controlled endogenous environment.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper departs from prior LLM-agent trading work (StockSim, ArenaTrader, TradingAgents) in two key ways: it explicitly decouples semantic planning from numerical computation by treating LLM-generated tools as first-class intermediate reasoning artifacts rather than transient execution calls, and it replaces replay-based backtesting with a digital twin market (DECOUPLED MARKET) where prices emerge endogenously from agent interactions. Conceptually, it extends the tool-use paradigm in LLM agents (Wölflein et al., 2025; Rietschel & Steinfeld, 2025) by making tool creation a constrained, iteratively refined process guided by structured diagnostic feedback rather than one-shot code generation. The self-play framing is deliberately lightweight—single-agent iterative refinement against fixed or heuristic opponents—distinguishing it from classical multi-agent RL self-play.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The DECOUPLED MARKET environment offers a useful methodological contribution: a controlled, leakage-free setting where causal attribution between strategy design and observed performance is cleaner than in historical backtesting, addressing a real gap in LLM trading agent evaluation. However, the endogenous price dynamics are highly simplified (a tanh-scaled excess-demand model), and the 'self-play' is really single-agent refinement against static opponents, limiting the generality of the emergent reasoning. Open questions include whether the tool-creation behaviors transfer to genuinely novel market microstructures, whether the bounded refinement loop scales to longer horizons or more complex multi-agent adversarial settings, and whether the moderate real-world gains (e.g., 8.5% TR on NASDAQ vs. 1.2% for standalone GPT-5-mini) persist under out-of-distribution regimes or transaction-cost realism.
