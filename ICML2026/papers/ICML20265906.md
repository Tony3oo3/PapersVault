## Problem

Existing feed-forward methods for joint 3D reconstruction and scene understanding operate in an offline-global paradigm, repeatedly recomputing cross-frame interactions over an ever-growing history, causing runtime and GPU memory to grow rapidly with sequence length (e.g., SIU3R runs out of memory at ~80 frames on a 140 GB GPU). Meanwhile, streaming reconstruction methods that scale well to long sequences lack semantic understanding and instance-level decomposable representations, leaving a gap for online systems that need both reconstruction and understanding without reprocessing past frames.

## Input / Output

S2GS consumes an uncalibrated, unposed RGB image stream (no camera intrinsics or extrinsics required) under a strictly causal constraint: at each time step t, only the current frame I_t and a persistent state accumulated from all prior frames are used, with no access to future frames or re-forwarding of historical observations. Optionally, a free-form text prompt is provided at test time for open-vocabulary segmentation. The output is a persistent 3D Gaussian scene representation supporting novel view synthesis from arbitrary viewpoints, per-frame semantic/instance/panoptic segmentation masks, predicted camera parameters, and depth maps, all updated incrementally as new frames arrive.

## Method

S2GS employs a geometry-semantic decoupled dual-backbone architecture. The geometry stream uses a causal Transformer encoder with a temporal attention mask and KV caching to perform frame-wise causal aggregation, predicting depth, camera parameters, and per-pixel Gaussian attributes that are back-projected into a persistent 3D Gaussian scene; geometric distillation from a pretrained 3D foundation model (Stream3R) provides depth and camera pseudo-labels to stabilize long-horizon reconstruction. The semantic stream independently encodes each frame with a frozen SigLIP2 2D foundation model, passes features through a lightweight adapter into a Mask2Former-style query-based decoder to produce per-frame masks, class scores, and query identity embeddings, preventing geometric update noise from corrupting semantic representations. Instance identity is stabilized via supervised contrastive learning on query embeddings during training and a lightweight instance memory bank with cosine-similarity bipartite matching and exponential moving average updates during inference. A lightweight Query Semantic Projector, trained with distillation against SigLIP2 masked-region embeddings, maps query embeddings into the vision-language space to enable text-conditioned query retrieval for open-vocabulary segmentation. Per-frame 2D semantic predictions are lifted to 3D by assigning them to pixel-aligned Gaussians and fusing through differentiable splatting, yielding a view-consistent 3D semantic field.

## Results

On ScanNet under the 32-view setting, S2GS achieves 19.92 PSNR, 0.665 SSIM, 48.95 mIoU, 30.01 T-mIoU, and 62.39 T-SR, outperforming the strongest offline baseline SIU3R (17.82 PSNR, 39.98 mIoU, 41.24 T-SR) across all metrics. Under the 8-view setting, S2GS reaches 20.83 PSNR and 49.53 mIoU, again surpassing SIU3R (19.74 PSNR, 44.78 mIoU). Critically, S2GS scales to 1,024 frames at 0.62 s/frame and 76.88 GB peak GPU memory, whereas SIU3R runs out of memory beyond ~80 frames on a 140 GB H200 GPU.

## Baselines & Comparisons

Against the primary joint baseline SIU3R, S2GS wins on every metric at 8, 14, and 32 views (e.g., +1.09 PSNR, +9.57 mIoU, +21.15 T-SR at 32 views), while slightly trailing SIU3R on PSNR/SSIM at the extremely sparse 2-view setting (24.90 vs 25.79) where offline global aggregation has an advantage. Against Uni3R, S2GS leads by a wide margin on both reconstruction (19.92 vs 16.74 PSNR at 32 views) and semantics (48.95 vs 32.17 mIoU). Versus 2D-only segmentation baselines Mask2Former and LSeg, S2GS achieves substantially higher mIoU (48.95 vs 41.59 and 30.46 at 32 views) and additionally provides 3D reconstruction and temporal instance consistency. In the long-sequence regime (64 and 256 views), S2GS maintains usable quality (18.71 and 16.37 PSNR) while SIU3R degrades at 64 views and hits OOM at 256 views. Compared to the optimization-based Splat-SLAM, S2GS achieves comparable PSNR at 32 views (19.92 vs 20.88) in ~4 s versus ~345 s, and at 256 views S2GS actually surpasses Splat-SLAM in PSNR (16.37 vs 14.01) while being ~50× faster. On zero-shot cross-dataset transfer (ScanNet++, Replica), S2GS outperforms SIU3R by +2.48/+2.52 PSNR and +8.16/+16.05 mIoU respectively.

## Relation to Prior Work

S2GS sits at the intersection of two previously separate research threads: (1) offline feed-forward joint reconstruction-and-understanding methods (SIU3R, Uni3R, IGGT) that globally reprocess all frames, and (2) streaming 3D reconstruction methods (CUT3R, Stream3R, StreamGS) that achieve causal, reprocessing-free inference but model only geometry and appearance. The paper departs from both by introducing a geometry-semantic decoupled dual-backbone architecture within a strictly causal 3DGS framework: the geometry stream follows the causal Transformer + KV-cache lineage of Stream3R/CUT3R, while the semantic stream independently leverages a frozen 2D foundation model (SigLIP2) and a Mask2Former-style query decoder, bridging the gap that prior streaming methods leave for instance-level semantics. Geometric distillation from a pretrained causal 3D foundation model (Stream3R) stabilizes incremental Gaussian placement, and query-level contrastive learning plus an online instance memory bank address the identity-consistency problem that offline methods solve implicitly through global cross-view attention.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The most practically significant finding is the dramatic scalability gap: offline joint methods OOM at roughly 80 frames on a 140 GB H200, while S2GS processes over 1,000 frames with only mild runtime and memory growth, making long-horizon online 3D scene understanding feasible for the first time in a feed-forward 3DGS setting. Open questions remain around dynamic scenes (the paper acknowledges the need for online Gaussian maintenance and object-level dynamic modeling), outdoor/large-scale environments, and whether the 2D-to-3D semantic lifting pipeline can handle more complex instance interactions. The 2-view reconstruction being weaker than offline baselines is an expected trade-off of the causal constraint, but it highlights that the streaming paradigm still pays a geometric cost under extreme sparsity.
