## Problem

Existing non-asymptotic convergence guarantees for discretized underdamped Langevin dynamics (ULD) scale polynomially with the ambient dimension d, rendering them vacuous in high-dimensional settings where the potential's geometry is effectively low-dimensional. While a dimension-free result exists for the randomized midpoint scheme in Wasserstein-2 distance (Liu et al., 2023), dimension-independent convergence guarantees for ULD discretizations measured in KL divergence had remained open.

## Input / Output

The analysis takes as input a twice-differentiable potential V with a known Hessian upper bound matrix H (satisfying ∇²V ⪯ H ⪯ βI), an initial distribution μ, and a step size h. It produces non-asymptotic KL divergence bounds for N-step ULMC or RMD chains whose complexity depends on tr(H) rather than d, covering both the α-strongly convex (α > 0) and general convex (α = 0) regimes. The key structural requirement is that H be a PSD matrix upper-bounding the Hessian everywhere, which in ridge-separable or power-law eigenvalue settings yields tr(H) ≪ βd.

## Method

The paper refines the KL local error framework of Altschuler et al. (2025) to a dimension-free setting through two technical ingredients. First, the one-step strong and weak discretization errors are bounded using the H-weighted norm ‖p‖_H = √(pᵀHp) for the momentum component and tr(H) for the noise contribution, replacing the standard Euclidean norm that would introduce √d factors. Second, a dimension-free change-of-measure lemma controls state-dependent terms (E[‖∇V(x)‖²] and E[pᵀHp]) via tr(H) + β·KL(μ‖π), using a Taylor expansion of the exponential in the Donsker-Varadhan formula rather than crude Gaussian moment bounds that would reintroduce d. Plugging these refined local errors into the shifted-composition recursion yields the first dimension-free KL convergence rates for both standard ULMC (Õ(κ^{3/2}β^{-1/2}[tr(H)]^{1/2}/ε) in the strongly convex case) and RMD (Õ(κ[β^{-1}tr(H)]^{1/3}ε^{-2/3})), with the RMD rate matching the state-of-the-art O(1/ε³) dependence in the general convex setting.

## Results

The paper establishes the first dimension-free KL convergence bounds for underdamped Langevin Monte Carlo (ULMC) and its randomized midpoint variant (RMD). For standard ULMC in the strongly convex setting, the sample complexity is Õ(κ^{3/2} β^{-1/2} [tr(H)]^{1/2} / ε) in KL divergence, and for RMD it is Õ(κ [β^{-1} tr(H)]^{1/3} ε^{-2/3}). In the general convex setting, ULMC achieves O(β [tr(H)]^{1/2} W³ / ε⁴) while RMD achieves Õ(β [tr(H)]^{1/4} W^{5/2} / ε³), the latter matching the state-of-the-art ε^{-3} rate. All bounds depend on tr(H) (where H ⪰ ∇²V) rather than the ambient dimension d, yielding non-vacuous guarantees when tr(H) ≪ d.

## Baselines & Comparisons

Against the closest prior dimension-free result for ULD (Liu et al., 2023, RMD in W2 with complexity Õ(κ^{5/3}β^{-2/3}[tr(H)]^{1/3}ε^{-2/3})), this paper's RMD KL bound Õ(κ[β^{-1}tr(H)]^{1/3}ε^{-2/3}) implies via Talagrand's T₂ inequality a W2 rate of Õ(κ^{4/3}β^{-2/3}[tr(H)]^{1/3}ε^{-2/3}), strictly improving the condition-number dependence from κ^{5/3} to κ^{4/3}. Compared to the dimension-free overdamped KL bound of Freund et al. (2022), Õ(κ²β^{-1}tr(H)/ε²), the ULMC KL result Õ(κ^{3/2}β^{-1/2}[tr(H)]^{1/2}/ε) improves both the κ and tr(H) dependence. When H = βI (isotropic case), all bounds reduce exactly to those of Altschuler et al. (2025), confirming consistency. All prior ULD KL results (Altschuler et al., 2025) and W2 results (Cheng et al., 2018; Dalalyan & Riou-Durand, 2018; Shen & Lee, 2019) carry explicit d^{1/2} or d^{1/3} factors, making them vacuous in high dimensions; this paper removes that dependence entirely. In the general convex setting, no prior dimension-free guarantee existed for any Langevin discretization; this paper provides the first, with RMD matching the ε^{-3} rate of Altschuler et al. (2025) while replacing d^{1/4} with [tr(H)]^{1/4}.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the KL local error framework of Altschuler et al. (2025) and Altschuler & Chewi (2024b) to a dimension-free setting for underdamped Langevin dynamics, building directly on the shifted composition and cross-regularity machinery they developed. It also refines the randomized midpoint discretization of Shen & Lee (2019) by replacing Euclidean norms with Hessian-weighted norms in the local error analysis. The key departure from prior work is that all existing non-asymptotic guarantees for ULD discretizations in KL divergence (Altschuler et al., 2025) scale with the ambient dimension d, while the only dimension-free ULD result (Liu et al., 2023) was restricted to Wasserstein-2 distance; this paper closes the gap by proving the first dimension-free KL bounds for ULD, with complexity governed by tr(H) rather than d.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The result is practically relevant for high-dimensional sampling where the potential V has effectively low-dimensional curvature (e.g., ridge-separable or power-law Hessian spectra), as the sample complexity can be substantially smaller than d-dependent bounds. Two open questions remain: extending the analysis to the nonconvex setting (where log-Sobolev or Poincaré inequality assumptions would be needed, but dimension-free versions appear substantially harder) and handling stochastic gradients, where bounding additional noise terms in a dimension-independent manner requires new moment or tail assumptions. The paper also notes that the Θ(1/ε³) rate for RMD in the general convex setting matches the state-of-the-art but whether it can be further improved remains open.
