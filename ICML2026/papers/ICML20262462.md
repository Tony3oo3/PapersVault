## Problem

Diffusion models have emerged as strong self-supervised representation learners, yet the connection between their denoising training objective and the geometric properties of good representations (invariance and expansion) studied in SSL remains unexplored. There is also no practical, label-free way to monitor whether a diffusion model is generalizing or memorizing during training, particularly in data-limited regimes where standard metrics like FID are unreliable memorization detectors.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ICML20262462/cropped_page2_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method consumes bottleneck-layer features from a frozen diffusion backbone (EDM U-Net or SiT transformer) extracted at a fixed noise level σ_t, along with multiple augmented views (crops, flips, color jitter) of each training image. It produces a single scalar ICR value in [0,1], the invariant and residual covariance matrices Σ_s and Σ_ξ, and the full spectrum of Fisher-direction eigenvalues. Crucially, ICR is computed entirely from training features using as few as two augmented views per image and a subset of ~4096 samples, requiring no labels, no external evaluation network, and no generated samples.

## Method

The core contribution is an invariance-residual decomposition of diffusion features: for each image, the representation h(a(x_0)) is split into a perturbation-invariant component s(x_0) = E_a[h(a(x_0))|x_0] and a residual ξ(a,x_0) = h(a(x_0)) − s(x_0), yielding the covariance decomposition Σ_h = Σ_s + Σ_ξ. The paper then solves the generalized eigenproblem Σ_s v_i = λ_i Σ_ξ v_i to obtain Fisher directions, where each eigenvalue λ_i measures the invariant signal-to-noise ratio along that direction, and defines ICR = 1/(1 + (1/d)Σλ_i) as a label-free scalar summarizing how much residual variation contaminates stable structure. In practice, the covariances are estimated via a two-view approximation using the sum and difference of paired augmented features. The metric is then tracked across the diffusion noise schedule (revealing a U-shaped semantic window where ICR is minimized and linear-probe accuracy peaks) and across training checkpoints (revealing a monotonic decrease in data-rich regimes and a U-shaped early-learning pattern in data-limited regimes that anticipates memorization onset).

## Results

The paper's central finding is that ICR (Invariant Contamination Ratio) exhibits a U-shaped curve across diffusion noise levels, with its minimum coinciding with the peak of linear-probe classification accuracy on CIFAR-10, CIFAR-100, and ImageNet (Figure 2). In data-rich training (full CIFAR-10, full ImageNet), ICR and FID both decrease monotonically over training, confirming that improving generative quality corresponds to increasing representation invariance (Figure 3). In data-limited training (4,096 CIFAR-10 images, 10K ImageNet-64, 20K ImageNet-256), ICR follows a U-shaped trajectory whose minimum precedes the onset of memorization, providing a label-free early-stopping signal (Figures 4–5). ICR also outperforms alignment loss, class separation, and silhouette score in tracking the generalization-to-memorization transition (Figure 11).

## Baselines & Comparisons

Against the alignment and uniformity metrics of Wang & Isola (2020), ICR is shown to be more informative: while ICR decreases monotonically with training (indicating improving invariance), the alignment loss L_align actually increases because it is an absolute distance that grows with overall feature variance (Figure 10). Compared to the class separation metric R² of Kornblith et al. (2021) and the Silhouette score, ICR exhibits a substantially stronger and more consistent alignment with the memorization ratio in data-limited training; both class separation and silhouette saturate early and fail to capture the late-stage memorization rise (Figure 11). Relative to FID (Heusel et al., 2017), ICR tracks generative quality in the data-rich regime but remains reliable in the data-limited regime where FID is known to be an imperfect memorization detector (Stein et al., 2023). Against the memorization ratio of Pizzi et al. (2022) and Zhang et al. (2024), ICR serves as a leading indicator: the ICR minimum occurs before the memorization ratio begins to rise, and ICR can be computed from training features alone without generating samples or running expensive nearest-neighbor searches.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper synthesizes three research threads—SSL representation evaluation (alignment/uniformity, class separation), Fisher discriminant analysis and neural collapse, and diffusion model representation learning—into a unified geometric framework. Rather than building on a single prior method, it repurposes the generalized eigenvalue structure of Fisher LDA in a self-supervised setting where each image acts as its own 'class' and within-class variation is replaced by augmentation/noise sensitivity. It departs from Wang & Isola's alignment/uniformity by showing these absolute-distance metrics fail to track relative invariance during diffusion training, and from prior label-free SSL metrics (effective rank, covariance spectrum decay) by explicitly decomposing features into invariant and residual components induced by the diffusion noise schedule.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

ICR provides a practical, label-free training-time monitor that complements FID and memorization-ratio tests, which are expensive (requiring generation) or unreliable in data-limited regimes (Stein et al., 2023). The semantic window finding gives a simple rule for selecting the most informative noise level for feature extraction, and the U-shaped ICR trajectory offers an early-stopping signal before overfitting dominates. Limitations include reliance on a simple Gaussian toy model for theoretical grounding, experiments primarily on CIFAR-scale and small ImageNet subsets, and the need for augmented views (adding forward-pass cost). Open questions include whether ICR extends to text-to-image or video diffusion models, and whether the Fisher-direction decomposition can inform architectural or training modifications rather than just evaluation.
