## Problem

Conventional SBDD generative models construct probability paths separately in Euclidean space for continuous atomic coordinates and in probabilistic space for discrete chemical categories, inducing a modality mismatch that fails to capture structural-chemical synergy. While exponential geodesics on a unified statistical manifold offer a principled alternative, directly targeting Dirac distributions causes instantaneous trajectory collapse at the manifold boundary, compressing the effective learning window and preventing the model from capturing intermediate structural correlations.

## Input / Output

The model takes a protein binding site as a set of N_P atoms with Cartesian coordinates (R^{N_P×3}) and one-hot amino-acid/element encodings, and produces a full ligand as a triplet of heavy-atom 3D coordinates, atom types (K_v categories), and explicit bond types (K_b categories over N_M×(N_M−1) edges). The ligand atom count N_M is sampled from an empirical distribution or predicted by a network prior to generation, and hydrogen atoms are added post-hoc from the predicted bond graph. The protein-ligand interaction is encoded as a heterogeneous graph with 4-dimensional edge-type one-hot vectors distinguishing ligand-ligand, protein-protein, and cross-interactions.

## Method

EvoEGF-Mol represents each molecule as a product exponential-family distribution (isotropic Gaussian for coordinates, Dirichlet for atom and bond types) on a unified statistical manifold, and defines the generative path as an e-geodesic—linear interpolation in concatenated natural parameters—so that continuous and discrete variables share a single Fisher-Rao-calibrated schedule. The key innovation is the Evolving Exponential Geodesic Flow: rather than targeting a fixed Dirac endpoint (which causes variance/concentration to diverge and the path to collapse), the target distribution dynamically concentrates over time via σ̃₁(t)=λ(1−t) for Gaussian precision and α̃₁(t)=(1−λ(1−t))e_k+λ(1−t)(1/K)·1_K for Dirichlet concentration, keeping the trajectory in the manifold interior. Training follows a progressive-parameter-refinement paradigm: the network (a UnitTransformer with gated edge attention) predicts terminal natural parameters from noisy intermediate states, and the loss is the KL divergence between the true and model-induced one-step distributions, whose Fisher-Rao quadratic expansion automatically calibrates relative supervision strengths across coordinate, atom-type, and bond-type losses without heuristic weighting. The paper further shows that Straight-Line Diffusion Model is a special case of EGF under static regularized endpoints, and that the dynamic-endpoint path is not reducible to any nonlinear time reparameterization of a fixed-endpoint geodesic.

## Results

On CrossDock, EvoEGF-Mol achieves a PoseBusters passing rate of 93.4%, approaching the reference-level 95.0% and substantially outperforming all baselines (MolCRAFT 84.6%, Pocket2Mol 72.3%, DecompDiff 71.7%, AR 59.0%, TargetDiff 50.5%). Its strain energy 25th percentile is 8.94 kcal/mol, an order of magnitude lower than MolCRAFT (83) and Pocket2Mol (102), indicating far more physically realistic conformations. On MolGenBench scaffold-level evaluation, EvoEGF-Mol attains the highest Pass Rate (31.79% in-distribution, 29.61% out-of-distribution) and recovers the most bioactive scaffolds (72 in-distribution targets, 33 out-of-distribution targets), with a Hit Fraction of 1.08% and 0.93% respectively.

## Baselines & Comparisons

EvoEGF-Mol decisively outperforms all five baselines on geometric validity: its PB-Valid of 93.4% exceeds MolCRAFT (84.6%) by 8.8 points, Pocket2Mol (72.3%) by 21.1 points, and TargetDiff (50.5%) by 42.9 points. On strain energy, EvoEGF-Mol's 25th percentile (8.94) is roughly 9× lower than MolCRAFT (83) and 11× lower than Pocket2Mol (102), while TargetDiff and AR produce severely strained conformations (369 and 259 at the 25th percentile). On binding affinity, MolCRAFT achieves a slightly better Vina Score (-6.55 vs. -6.14) and Vina Min (-7.21 vs. -6.98), but EvoEGF-Mol edges out MolCRAFT on Vina Dock (-7.72 vs. -7.67) and matches the CrossDock reference level. On MolGenBench scaffold discovery, EvoEGF-Mol leads all baselines on Pass Rate (31.79% vs. MolCRAFT 23.07%), Hit Recovery (72 vs. 66 in-distribution), and Hit Fraction (1.08% vs. 0.70%), though absolute hit rates remain below 5% across all methods, highlighting that geometric plausibility alone is insufficient for practical hit discovery.

## Relation to Prior Work

EvoEGF-Mol builds on the progressive-parameter-refinement paradigm of Bayesian Flow Networks (Graves et al., 2023) and Parameter Interpolation Flow (Jin et al., 2025), but replaces their ad-hoc noise schedules with exponential geodesics on a Fisher-Rao statistical manifold. The key conceptual departure from prior SBDD methods (TargetDiff, DecompDiff, MolCRAFT) is the unification of continuous atomic coordinates (Gaussian) and discrete atom/bond types (Dirichlet) into a single product exponential-family manifold, where all modalities share a synchronized linear interpolation in natural-parameter space rather than separately tuned Euclidean and categorical paths. The paper further shows that the recently proposed Straight-Line Diffusion Model (SLDM) is a special case of their EGF framework under regularized static endpoints, positioning EvoEGF as a generalized dynamic solution to the Dirac-boundary singularity that plagues fixed-endpoint geodesic flows.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The evolving-endpoint strategy provides a principled and analytically tractable solution to the trajectory-collapse problem inherent in e-geodesic flows targeting Dirac distributions, yielding an order-of-magnitude improvement in strain energy over baselines and near-reference PoseBusters validity. However, the paper is candid that absolute bioactivity recovery remains extremely low (Scaffold Hit Rate < 5%, Hit Fraction ~1%), indicating that geometric plausibility alone is insufficient for practical drug discovery. The open question is whether training on explicit bioactivity datasets (beyond CrossDock's structural poses) can bridge the gap from 'physically plausible' to 'pharmacologically active' generation, and whether the information-geometric framework scales to larger, more diverse target spaces.
