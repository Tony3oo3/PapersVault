## Problem

Distributionally robust assortment optimization (DRAO) under the MNL choice model has only been studied with KL-divergence ambiguity sets, and only in non-contextual settings. No computationally efficient solvers exist for TV or Chi-square ambiguity sets, and extending DRAO to the linear contextual MNL model—where choice probabilities depend on item features—remains entirely unexplored.

## Input / Output

The method consumes a historical dataset of observed (choice, feature, assortment) tuples, item feature vectors x_i ∈ R^d (bounded by ||x_i||₂ ≤ 1), a revenue vector r with r_i ∈ [0,1], an uncertainty radius δ ≥ 0, and a cardinality cap K. It outputs a robust optimal assortment S_D ⊆ [N] with |S_D| ≤ K that maximizes worst-case revenue over the f-divergence ambiguity set. For KL and Chi-square, the output is ε-optimal via one-dimensional search; for TV, the solution is exact.

## Method

The key insight is that strong duality (from Iyengar 2005, Hu & Hong 2013, Xu et al. 2023) reduces the inner robust minimization to a univariate dual, but the outer problem couples a continuous dual variable with combinatorial assortment search in a divergence-specific way. For TV, the dual objective is concave and piecewise-linear with knots at reward values, yielding an exact O(N² log N) algorithm that enumerates N candidate dual variables and calls StaticMNL with truncated revenues. For KL, a bisection on a threshold variable t combined with an EVAL-G subroutine (exploiting the single-intersection property of pairwise cost curves) gives an O~(N²) ε-optimal solver. For Chi-square, a Golden Section Search over the dual variable η, with each evaluation solved exactly by StaticMNL on transformed revenues r̃_i = 2ηr_i − r_i², achieves O(N log N · log(1/ε)). In the data-driven contextual setting, a λ-regularized MLE constructs lower-confidence-bound (LCB) attractions, which are plugged into the above algorithms; sub-optimality is bounded via a two-term decomposition where the second term is non-positive at the LCB optimizer by a monotonicity property of the robust revenue.

## Results

The paper establishes high-probability sub-optimality bounds for data-driven robust assortment selection under three f-divergences: TV yields O~(Σ p(i|S*_TV)‖x_i‖_{H^{-1}(θ*)})), KL yields O~_δ(Σ p(i|S*_KL)‖x_i‖_{H^{-1}(θ*)})), and χ² yields O~_δ(√(Σ p(i|S*_χ²)‖x_i‖_{H^{-1}(θ*)}})). Algorithmically, the TV solver is exact with O(N² log N) runtime, the χ² solver achieves ε-optimality in O(N log N · log(1/ε)) time via Golden Section Search, and the KL solver runs in O~(N²) time. These are the first polynomial-time robust assortment algorithms for TV and χ² ambiguity sets, and the first finite-sample guarantees for contextual DRAO under any divergence.

## Baselines & Comparisons

Compared to Han et al. (2025a), the closest non-robust contextual baseline, this paper's bounds replace the squared norm ‖x_i‖²_{H^{-1}} with the linear norm ‖x_i‖_{H^{-1}} (for TV and KL) and drop the p(0|S*) no-purchase weight, yielding a tighter, probability-weighted self-normalized rate that is localized to items in the optimal robust assortment. Against Lu et al. (2026), the prior robust work, this paper extends from non-contextual to contextual MNL and from KL-only to TV and χ², while improving the rate from a uniform K/√(min n_i) to a feature-dependent sum over the optimal assortment. Relative to Dong et al. (2023), the contextual non-robust baseline requiring global assortment-wise exploration, this paper's coverage condition only demands that items in S*_D are well-covered, a strictly weaker requirement. The TV bound is independent of δ (linear in estimation error), while KL and χ² carry prefactors min{1/log(3/2), 1/δ} and (1+δ)/(√(1+δ)−1) respectively, reflecting the continuous dual structure of those divergences. No baseline simultaneously achieves contextual + robust + multiple divergences, making this work the first to cover all three dimensions.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the distributionally robust assortment optimization (DRAO) line initiated by Rusmevichientong & Topaloglu (2012) and Jin et al. (2022), which was limited to KL divergence and non-contextual settings. It draws on strong duality results from the DRO literature (Iyengar 2005 for TV/Chi-square, Hu & Hong 2013 for KL) to reduce the inner robust minimization to univariate dual problems, then couples these with the StaticMNL combinatorial solver (Rusmevichientong et al. 2010). The data-driven analysis builds on the LCB-based pessimistic estimation framework for contextual MNL from Han et al. (2025a), but the robust sub-optimality decomposition is new, requiring a monotonicity argument specific to the robust objective that has no analogue in the non-robust literature.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

This is the first work to provide polynomial-time algorithms and finite-sample guarantees for DRAO under all three standard f-divergences (TV, KL, Chi-square) in the contextual MNL setting, filling a clear gap left by prior KL-only, non-contextual results. The probability-weighted self-normalized bounds offer a localized coverage condition that is strictly weaker than the global assortment-wise exploration required by Dong et al. (2023), making the guarantees applicable to more realistic offline data. Open questions include: the Chi-square algorithm's reliance on empirical unimodality (Golden Section Search) rather than a provable convergence guarantee, the small-gap condition required for the KL bound, and whether the distinct δ-prefactor scalings across divergences reflect genuine differences in robustness or are artifacts of the upper-bound technique.
