## Problem

Differentially private SGD injects isotropic Gaussian noise, but deep networks have highly anisotropic loss landscapes where parameter sensitivities vary by orders of magnitude, causing low-sensitivity directions to drown in noise while high-sensitivity directions are over-clipped. Second-order preconditioning (e.g., KFAC) can resolve this geometric mismatch, but estimating curvature typically requires private data (consuming privacy budget) or public proxy data (introducing unquantified distribution shift that fails in specialized domains like medical imaging). The paper asks whether the curvature needed for preconditioning can be recovered without either data source.

## Input / Output

The method consumes private training data only for the per-sample gradient computation within the DP-SGD loop; all curvature estimation is performed on internally generated synthetic probes (pink noise with 1/f^α spectral shaping for images, Zipfian-weighted random token sequences with structural anchors for text) that carry no privacy cost. The output is a set of per-layer KFAC preconditioner matrices (inverse square roots of activation and gradient covariance factors, computed via eigendecomposition with Tikhonov damping), which are frozen and broadcast to the training loop for T_freq steps before being refreshed. The final output is differentially private model parameters with the same (ε,δ)-DP guarantees as standard DP-SGD, since the preconditioner is batch-independent.

## Method

DP-KFC exploits the block-diagonal KFAC factorization of the Fisher Information Matrix and Mean Field Theory to show that the spectral decay of layer-wise covariance factors is an intrinsic architectural property (governed by depth, initialization, and nonlinearity) rather than a data-dependent quantity. The network is probed with modality-specific synthetic noise—pink noise generated by modulating white noise in the frequency domain with a 1/f^α power-law filter for images, and frequency-weighted random token sequences with structural anchors for text—then a forward and backward pass yields per-layer activation covariances Â and gradient covariances Ĝ. Inverse square roots are computed via eigendecomposition with damping terms, producing preconditioner matrices U_A and U_G whose Kronecker product transforms each per-sample gradient into an isotropic coordinate system before the standard DP-SGD clipping and noise injection (scale-then-privatize). Because the preconditioner depends only on synthetic noise and the current model state (not the current private batch), it incurs zero privacy cost and the L2 sensitivity of the clipped gradient sum remains bounded by C, preserving standard Gaussian mechanism guarantees. The approach avoids amplifying privacy noise by the preconditioner's condition number (unlike post-privatization preconditioning) and strictly dominates public-data baselines under domain mismatch, where proxy statistics conflict with the target task.

## Results

On MNIST (CNN) at ε=1, Synthetic DP-KFC achieves 94.2% test accuracy, outperforming DP-SGD (91.7%) by +2.5% and DP-AdamBC (94.0%) by +0.2%. On CIFAR-100 (CrossViT), Synthetic DP-KFC matches Public DP-KFC across all privacy budgets, confirming that architecture alone suffices for attention-based models. On the domain-mismatch transfer task (PathMNIST, ε=1), Synthetic DP-KFC reaches 78.2%, matching the private-data Oracle (78.4%) while Public DP-KFC degrades to 73.4% — a 4.8% gap demonstrating robustness to negative transfer. Combining Synthetic DP-KFC with post-privatization DP-AdamBC yields 95.5% on MNIST at ε=1, beating either method alone.

## Baselines & Comparisons

Against DP-SGD (Abadi et al., 2016), Synthetic DP-KFC wins across all modalities and privacy budgets: +2.5% on MNIST (ε=1), +1.4% on CIFAR-100 (frozen-backbone fine-tuning), +2.3% on StackOverflow (ε=1), and +2.7% on IMDB (ε=2.8). Against AdaDPS (Li et al., 2022), Synthetic DP-KFC dominates in both aligned and mismatched transfer settings, with the gap widening to 4.8% under domain shift (PathMNIST: 78.2% vs. 73.4% for Public DP-KFC, which uses AdaDPS-style public proxies). Against DiSK (Zhang et al., 2025), a post-privatization Kalman denoiser, Synthetic DP-KFC achieves higher accuracy at every ε on MNIST (e.g., 94.2% vs. 93.7% at ε=1) without requiring any public data. Against DP-AdamBC (Tang et al., 2024), Synthetic DP-KFC slightly outperforms it standalone (94.2% vs. 94.0% at ε=1), but the two are complementary: their combination (pre-privatization KFAC + post-privatization bias correction) reaches 95.5%, exceeding either alone by 1.3–1.5%.

## Relation to Prior Work

DP-KFC extends the KFAC preconditioning framework (Martens & Grosse, 2015) into the differential privacy setting by constructing preconditioners from synthetic noise rather than private or public data. It builds on Mean Field Theory (Poole et al., 2016; Schoenholz et al., 2016) to argue that the Fisher Information Matrix's eigenspectrum is an architectural property, and extends the scale-then-privatize principle of Ganesh et al. (2025) from diagonal to block-diagonal KFAC geometry. The method sits between two existing paradigms: private-data preconditioners (which consume privacy budget) and public-data preconditioners (which introduce distribution shift), offering a third option that requires neither.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The method enables second-order preconditioning under DP in domains where public proxy data is unavailable or itself sensitive (e.g., medical imaging), which is a genuine practical gap. The 4.8% domain-mismatch result (PathMNIST) where synthetic noise matches the private oracle while public data degrades is compelling evidence for the architectural-dominance claim. However, practical gains over DP-SGD are modest (2–3% in strong privacy regimes), throughput drops by ~2.2×, directional alignment degrades in deep layers, and on NLP fine-tuning the method still trails public-data preconditioning because random token probes miss the low-dimensional text manifold. Open questions include whether manifold-aware synthetic generators can close the NLP gap and whether the approach extends to other preconditioned optimizers (Muon, Shampoo).
