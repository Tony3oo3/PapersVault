## Problem

Existing panoramic video generation methods treat 360° content as a special 2D modality, fine-tuning perspective diffusion models without exploiting the underlying spherical geometry. This 2D-aware paradigm leads to geometric inconsistencies at boundaries and poles, limited motion range, and imprecise camera control, preventing the synthesis of truly explorable and physically consistent 3D worlds.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ICML20265537/cropped_page1_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The model takes an initial equirectangular panorama image (generated via FLUX with a panorama LoRA) and a 6-DoF 3D trajectory (XYZ translations plus yaw/pitch/roll Euler angles per frame) as conditioning signals. It produces a 49-frame panoramic video at 480×960 resolution (1:2 aspect ratio required by ERP). The backbone is fine-tuned at 480×720 and post-resized to 480×960 to preserve the pretrained model's prior knowledge while accommodating the panoramic aspect ratio.

## Method

PanoWorld-X fine-tunes CogVideoX-5B-I2V with two key architectural additions. First, the Exploration Panoramic Plücker Embedding (PPE) reformulates standard Plücker rays for spherical geometry: each ERP pixel is mapped to a unit direction vector via longitude/latitude on a sphere (so polar pixels converge to [0,±1,0]ᵀ), and translations are normalized by the 75th-percentile scene depth to unify scale across assets; the resulting 6-channel (direction + moment) per-pixel signal is injected through a ControlNet-style Exploration-Aware Attention module. Second, Sphere-Aware Attention computes pairwise token distances using the Haversine formula on the reprojected spherical surface (with temporal rotation compensation via Euler-angle rotation matrices) and applies a binary mask that only activates attention between tokens within a spherical distance threshold τ, operating as a parallel branch with a zero-initialized linear layer to avoid disrupting pretrained weights. The model is trained on the PanoExplorer dataset (116,759 Unreal Engine-rendered sequences with Delaunay-triangulated, Dijkstra-smoothed, collision-checked trajectories) for ~10,000 iterations on 8 A100 GPUs.

## Results

On the PanoExplorer test set (200 panoramic videos), PanoWorld-X achieves PSNR 19.34, SSIM 0.63, LPIPS 0.24, FID 28.01, and FVD 467.18 for panoramic generation, outperforming the best panoramic baseline GenEX (PSNR 16.12, FVD 1113.72) by a wide margin. For camera-controllable evaluation on cropped perspective views, PanoWorld-X Perspective achieves PSNR 16.76, FVD 586.51, rotation error 0.061, and translation error 0.073, surpassing AC3D (PSNR 13.77, FVD 842.29, R_err 0.081, T_err 0.087) on every metric. In a user study on in-the-wild videos, PanoWorld-X is ranked first by participants 51.8% (graphics quality), 47.0% (frame consistency), 56.5% (end continuity), and 62.6% (content distribution) of the time.

## Baselines & Comparisons

PanoWorld-X outperforms all five baselines on every reported metric. Against the three panoramic video generation baselines, the gains are most dramatic versus 360DVD (PSNR 19.34 vs. 10.66; FVD 467 vs. 2049) and Imagine360 (PSNR 19.34 vs. 11.62; FVD 467 vs. 1830), while the closest competitor GenEX still trails by 3.22 dB PSNR and 2.4× FVD. In the camera-controllable setting (evaluated on cropped perspective views), PanoWorld-X Perspective beats CameraCtrl by 5.2 dB PSNR and reduces translation error from 0.245 to 0.073, indicating far more precise trajectory adherence. Against AC3D, the nearest camera-controllable baseline, PanoWorld-X improves PSNR by 2.99 dB, FVD by 30%, and reduces both rotation error (0.061 vs. 0.081) and translation error (0.073 vs. 0.087), confirming superior controllability on complex exploration trajectories.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the video diffusion transformer paradigm (building on CogVideoX-5B-I2V) to the panoramic domain by introducing two geometry-aware components: an Exploration Panoramic Plücker Embedding that adapts ray-based camera control from pinhole to spherical geometry, and a Sphere-Aware Attention mechanism that replaces Euclidean token distances with great-circle distances on the sphere. It departs from prior panoramic video generation work (360DVD, GenEX, Imagine360), which treats panoramas as a 2D data domain and lacks trajectory controllability, and from camera-controllable generation (CameraCtrl, AC3D), which operates on perspective views and cannot handle the 360° topology. The PanoExplorer dataset (116K synthetic panoramic videos with 6-DoF trajectories from Unreal Engine) provides the training substrate that neither prior panoramic datasets nor camera-control datasets offer.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The work establishes a principled 3D-aware formulation for panoramic video generation that could become a standard recipe for immersive content pipelines (VR, embodied AI training, autonomous driving simulation). The sphere-aware attention and spherical Plücker embedding are modular techniques likely reusable in other panoramic or omnidirectional generation settings. Key limitations remain: training is entirely on synthetic Unreal Engine data (raising open questions about real-world generalization), resolution is capped by the backbone (480×960), and long-segment generation still accumulates temporal drift. Whether these geometry-aware inductive biases transfer to higher-resolution or longer-duration backbones as the field scales is an important open question.
