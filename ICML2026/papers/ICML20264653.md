## Problem

Video foundation models produce visually compelling clips but lack intrinsic 3D geometric understanding, causing objects to morph, vanish, or distort during complex camera movements. Existing remedies either inject 3D priors through architectural modifications (adding decoders or control modules) or enforce rigid constraints at inference time, both of which incur high computational costs and limit the scalability and generative diversity of the base model.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ICML20264653/cropped_page0_idx1.webp)

## Input / Output

The method takes a pure text prompt (optionally containing camera-motion keywords like 'orbit left' or 'push in') and generates a 3D-consistent video at 832×480 resolution. The camera trajectory is parsed from the prompt via keyword detection and embedded into the latent noise initialization, so no auxiliary camera-pose input or reference image is required. The output is a standard video clip whose frames are geometrically coherent under the specified camera motion, verifiable via 3DGS reconstruction and re-rendering.

## Method

World-R1 aligns a pre-trained flow-matching video model (Wan 2.1) with 3D geometry through online reinforcement learning (Flow-GRPO-Fast) without any architectural change. Camera control is achieved implicitly by warping the initial Gaussian noise along a prompt-derived trajectory using discrete mass-transport (Go-with-the-Flow), injecting motion priors into the latent space. The reward is a composite of three 3D-aware terms—meta-view plausibility scored by Qwen3-VL on a novel-view 3DGS render, reconstruction fidelity measured as 1−LPIPS between the video and its 3DGS re-render, and trajectory alignment between the commanded and reconstructed camera paths—plus a general HPSv3 aesthetic score. A periodic decoupled training strategy alternates every 100 steps between full 3D-reward optimization and a dynamic-data phase using only the general reward, preventing the model from overfitting to static rigidity and suppressing non-rigid motion. Training uses a ~3,000-prompt pure-text dataset synthesized by Gemini, covering diverse scenes and multi-level camera-control complexity.

## Results

On the 3D consistency benchmark (reconstruction-based PSNR/SSIM/LPIPS), World-R1-Small achieves 27.63 dB PSNR and World-R1-Large achieves 27.67 dB, representing improvements of 10.23 dB over Wan2.1-T2V-1.3B (17.40 dB) and 7.91 dB over Wan2.1-T2V-14B (19.76 dB). On VBench general video quality, World-R1-Small scores 65.74 Aesthetic Quality and 67.53 Imaging Quality, surpassing its Wan2.1-T2V-1.3B backbone (62.43 / 66.51) while all camera-control baselines score below 43 on Aesthetic Quality. The user study confirms a 92% win rate for geometric consistency and 86% overall preference over the base model.

## Baselines & Comparisons

World-R1 decisively outperforms all foundation-model baselines on 3D consistency: it beats CogVideoX-1.5-5B (24.44 dB) by 3.19 dB, Wan2.2-T2V-14B (23.47 dB) by 4.16 dB, and its own Wan2.1-T2V-1.3B backbone (17.40 dB) by 10.23 dB in PSNR. Against camera-control methods (GCD, Trajectory-Attention, DAS, ReCamMaster), World-R1-Small achieves 27.63 dB PSNR versus their range of 18.26–20.58 dB, while also scoring 65.74 on VBench Aesthetic Quality compared to their 38–43 range, showing that explicit control modules sacrifice visual quality for trajectory adherence. Among 3D-conditioned I2V methods (ViewCrafter 23.15, Voyager 21.38, FlashWorld 22.46, VerseCrafter 23.82), World-R1-Small leads by 3.8–6.3 dB in PSNR without requiring an input image or architectural modification. On VBench, World-R1-Small also surpasses CogVideoX-1.5-5B (62.07) and Wan2.1-T2V-1.3B (62.43) on Aesthetic Quality, confirming that RL-based 3D alignment does not degrade general generation quality. In the user study, World-R1 wins 92% of geometric-consistency comparisons and 76% of camera-control comparisons against Wan 2.1, with 86% overall preference.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper builds on Flow-GRPO (Liu et al., 2025) for RL-based optimization of flow-matching models and Go-with-the-Flow (Burgert et al., 2025) for parameter-free camera conditioning via noise warping. It departs from the dominant 3D-aware video generation paradigm (Voyager, FlashWorld, ViewCrafter, VerseCrafter) that injects 3D priors through architectural modifications or inference-time constraints, instead arguing that pre-trained video models already encode latent 3D geometry that can be elicited through reinforcement learning with 3D foundation model feedback. The use of a pure text dataset (synthesized via Gemini) rather than 3D-annotated video data represents a further departure from prior camera-control and 3D-consistency training pipelines that rely on paired video-geometry supervision.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The framework offers a practical, architecture-agnostic path to 3D-consistent video generation that can be applied as post-training on any existing flow-matching video model, potentially making it widely adoptable as base models improve. Key limitations include the high computational cost of online RL rollouts for video, the circularity of the primary 3D-consistency metric (reconstruction via 3DGS then comparison to source), and the fact that results are demonstrated only on the Wan 2.1 family at 832×480 resolution. Open questions include whether the approach scales to longer horizons and more complex multi-object scenes, whether the pure text dataset can be further expanded, and whether the RL training cost can be reduced to make the pipeline accessible beyond large compute clusters.
