## Problem

Policy optimization methods are the dominant practical approach in large-scale RL, yet they remain theoretically underexplored for stochastic Contextual MDPs with general offline function approximation. The prior state-of-the-art (LOLIPOP) achieves rate-optimal regret but relies on a complex layer-by-layer exploration strategy with high polynomial dependence on |S| and |A|, while earlier methods require additional structural assumptions such as minimum reachability or finite Eluder dimension. This paper asks whether a simple, practical policy optimization algorithm can achieve near-optimal regret in this setting without such assumptions.

## Input / Output

At each episode the agent observes a context c drawn i.i.d. from an unknown distribution, then interacts with the context-specific MDP for H steps, receiving state-action pairs and stochastic losses in [0,1]. The algorithm accesses two offline regression oracles over finite function classes F (losses) and P (dynamics): a least-squares oracle for loss estimation and a log-loss oracle for transition dynamics. Policies are generated on demand only for contexts actually encountered, and the output is a context-dependent policy π_c updated via exponential weights, with the guarantee being a high-probability regret bound of Õ(H⁴√(T|S||A|log(|F||P|))).

## Method

OPO-CMDP extends the optimistic policy optimization framework of Shani et al. (2020) to CMDPs by constructing an optimistic Q-function from function-approximation-based loss and dynamics estimates augmented with exploration bonuses. The key design choice is the bonus: for each context c, round t, and state-action pair (s,a), the bonus is inversely proportional to the cumulative occupancy of all past policies evaluated under the single most recent dynamics estimate P̂_c^t, rather than under contemporaneous or true dynamics. This avoids the need for Eluder dimension or minimum reachability assumptions. The policy is then updated via an exponential-weights (OMD with KL divergence) step on the optimistic Q-values. The main technical contribution is a bonus bound that analyzes four event combinations (dynamics approximation error × expected visitations) per fixed context before taking expectation over contexts, enabling the use of the dynamics generalization guarantee and yielding the near-optimal regret bound with linear dependence on |S| and |A|.

## Results

OPO-CMDP achieves a high-probability regret bound of Õ(H⁴√(T|S||A|log(|F||P|))) for stochastic CMDPs under general offline function approximation, with no additional structural assumptions. This is the first regret bound with optimal (linear) dependence on both |S| and |A|, matching the known lower bound of Ω̃(√(HT|S||A|log|G|)) up to a factor of H^3.5. The algorithm requires only standard offline regression oracles (least-squares for losses, log-loss for dynamics) and uses a simple exponential-weights policy update with occupancy-based exploration bonuses.

## Baselines & Comparisons

Compared to the prior state-of-the-art LOLIPOP (Qian et al., 2024), OPO-CMDP dramatically improves the dependence on state and action spaces from |S|⁴|A|³ to |S||A|, while also requiring no structural assumptions and operating in a loop-free setting (LOLIPOP handles layered but non-loop-free MDPs). Against RM-UCDD (Levy & Mansour, 2023), OPO-CMDP eliminates the minimum reachability assumption (removing the p_min⁻² factor) and reduces the state-space dependence from |S|³ to |S|. Against E-UC³RL (Levy et al., 2024), OPO-CMDP removes the Eluder dimension assumption entirely, trading a slightly worse horizon dependence (H⁸ vs. H⁶) for a fully assumption-free guarantee. In all comparisons, OPO-CMDP is the only method that simultaneously achieves loop-free structure, no structural assumptions, and linear dependence on both |S| and |A|.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the optimistic policy optimization (OPO) framework of Shani et al. (2020) from tabular MDPs to stochastic Contextual MDPs with general offline function approximation, and generalizes the policy optimization approach for contextual bandits (Levy and Mansour, 2025) to the full multi-step MDP setting. It departs from prior CMDP algorithms (RM-UCDD, E-UC³RL, LOLIPOP) by eliminating the need for additional structural assumptions such as minimum reachability or finite Eluder dimension, and by replacing LOLIPOP's complex layer-by-layer density estimation with a single evolving policy updated via exponential weights. The key technical departure is a novel exploration bonus that evaluates all past policies' occupancy measures under the most recent dynamics estimate, rather than under contemporaneous or true dynamics, which is what enables the assumption-free analysis.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

This is the first policy optimization algorithm for CMDPs with general offline function approximation, bridging a gap between the practical dominance of PO methods (PPO, TRPO, SAC) in large-scale RL and their theoretical underexploration in contextual settings. The near-optimal regret bound with optimal |S| and |A| dependence (matching the known lower bound up to H^{3.5}) demonstrates that simple exponential-weighting updates suffice without structural assumptions, offering a computationally lighter alternative to LOLIPOP's multi-layer epoch-based procedure. Open questions include closing the H^{3.5} gap to full optimality, eliminating the need for counterfactual occupancy computation, and extending the framework to latent-state settings like block MDPs or MDPs with rich observations.
