## Problem

In Actor-Critic LLM training (e.g., PPO), the value model must be synchronously retrained as the policy evolves, incurring massive compute and memory overhead; GRPO eliminates this coupling but shifts the cost to extensive Monte Carlo sampling that wastes budget on trivially solved or unsolvable prompts. No existing method can estimate the expected performance of an arbitrary, unseen policy on a given prompt without either maintaining a coupled critic or resorting to large-scale rollout sampling.

## Input / Output

The method consumes a target prompt x and a context set C_π of N=256 historical (query, binary reward) pairs drawn from the policy's rollout history, optionally augmented with normalized cost scores for routing. It produces a calibrated success probability V₀(x, C_π) ∈ [0,1] in a single forward pass with no gradient updates, which directly drives per-prompt rollout budget allocation during training or model-fleet routing decisions at inference. The context is ingested as in-context learning input to a frozen TabPFN head, so adapting to a new policy or pricing scheme requires only swapping the context, not retraining.

## Method

V₀ reframes value estimation from a parameterized function V^π(s₀) to a conditional prediction V(C_π, s₀), treating the policy's capability as an explicit in-context input rather than a latent variable in model weights. The architecture has three stages: a frozen Qwen3-Embedding-0.6B backbone maps queries to 1024-d semantic vectors; a trainable Residual Query Adapter (168 static queries plus dynamic offsets generated from the embedding, projected via 3-head MHA into 168×6 structured features) acts as a 'semantic prism' aligning entangled embeddings to fixed-coordinate channels; and a frozen TabPFN-v2.5 head performs Bayesian in-context inference over the structured context in one forward pass. A mutual information decomposition I(Y;X,C) = I(Y;C) + I(Y;X|C) proves that naive cross-entropy training degenerates into a context-shortcut heuristic, so the authors introduce a composite objective combining a Bradley-Terry pairwise ranking loss (provably invariant to any additive context bias b(C)) with soft cross-entropy for probability calibration, balancing discrimination within a policy against absolute calibration across policies.

## Results

On the primary value estimation benchmark (Table 1), V₀ achieves Intra-Context AUC of 0.887–0.913, Pairwise Accuracy of 0.884–0.956, and Calibration MSE of 0.072–0.099 across three policy architectures (1.5B, 4B, 7B), consistently outperforming all baselines. Under strict zero-shot generalization (Table 2), V₀ retains 0.689–0.710 Intra AUC on unseen queries while the Vanilla Value Model collapses to 0.512–0.560. For budget allocation (Table 11), V₀-guided allocation improves AIME 2024 accuracy from 41.04% (GRPO) to 50.21%, and for inference routing, V₀ establishes a Pareto frontier across 12 benchmarks that dominates EmbedLLM and Model-SAT.

## Baselines & Comparisons

Against the Reward Model baseline (Qwen2.5-Math-RM-72B), V₀ achieves substantially higher Intra AUC (e.g., 0.913 vs. 0.539 on 1.5B) and lower Calibration MSE (0.072 vs. 0.624), confirming that policy-agnostic difficulty scoring cannot capture policy-specific capability. Against the GRPO baseline (Shao et al., 2024), V₀-guided budget allocation improves AIME 2024 accuracy from 41.04% to 50.21% and OlympiadBench from 54.53% to 56.34%, demonstrating that real-time capability-aware allocation outperforms uniform sampling. For inference routing, V₀ dominates the Pareto frontier over both EmbedLLM (Zhuang et al., 2025) and Model-SAT (Zhang et al., 2025), achieving higher accuracy at equivalent cost or lower cost at equivalent accuracy across 12 benchmarks. V₀ also matches or exceeds the computationally expensive Step-wise Retrain baseline (which retrains a fresh value model from scratch at every timestep) on Pairwise Accuracy (0.940 vs. 0.876 on 1.5B) while requiring zero gradient updates, validating the in-context paradigm as a practical alternative to synchronous retraining.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper departs from the standard Actor-Critic paradigm (PPO) where a value model must be synchronously retrained to track an evolving policy, and from GRPO which eliminates the value model at the cost of expensive Monte Carlo sampling. It reframes value estimation as an in-context learning problem by treating the policy's behavioral history as explicit context C_π rather than an implicit variable in model parameters, drawing on the learnware philosophy and TabPFN's pre-trained Bayesian in-context inference. The Residual Query Adapter bridges high-dimensional semantic embeddings to TabPFN's structured tabular interface, and the composite loss (Bradley-Terry ranking + soft CE) is motivated by a mutual information decomposition showing that naive cross-entropy training degenerates into a context-shortcut heuristic.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The decoupling of value estimation from policy parameters enables zero-gradient adaptation to any policy, which is practically valuable for GRPO training (adaptive budget allocation improving AIME 2024 accuracy by ~9%) and LLM inference routing (Pareto-optimal cost-performance trade-offs). The mutual information analysis providing a formal characterization of why coupled value models fail under distribution shift, combined with the shift-invariant ranking loss as a principled fix, offers a reusable theoretical lens for other context-conditional prediction tasks. Key limitations include the restriction to state-zero (coarse-grained) estimation, the focus on binary-reward math/reasoning tasks, and the dependence on sufficient context length (N≥256) for reliable capability characterization. Extending to token-level process supervision and non-binary reward settings remains open.
