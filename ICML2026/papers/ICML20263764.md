## Problem

Spiking Neural Networks offer energy-efficient 3D perception but lag behind ANNs in generalization and multimodal reasoning, lacking both open-world understanding (zero-shot classification, 3D QA) and scalable pretraining. Existing SNN pretraining methods either degrade with dataset complexity, demand heavy computation incompatible with neuromorphic hardware, or lack language-guided multimodal integration. Additionally, vision-language models rely on large text encoders at inference, undermining the hardware efficiency that makes SNNs attractive for edge deployment.

## Input / Output

The method consumes 3D point clouds (voxelized at 0.01–0.1 m resolution), event streams (converted to spatio-temporal event clouds via a sliding window that normalizes timestamps into a z-axis), and text prompts, alongside RGB images for alignment. During training, inputs are encoded by I-LIF neurons that emit integer-valued spikes; at inference these collapse to binary spikes. Outputs span class labels (via a spike-count argmax decision rule that replaces softmax), segmentation masks, detection boxes, action labels, and free-form text captions/QA responses generated through a LLaVA-style pipeline coupling the spike encoder to a Vicuna LLM.

## Method

SVL comprises two core modules: (i) Multi-scale Triple Alignment (MTA), a label-free triplet contrastive objective that jointly aligns spike firing rates with CLIP text embeddings (semantic spike-text InfoNCE) and with image features (fine-grained spike-image InfoNCE plus an MSE term for tighter coupling), all while keeping CLIP frozen; and (ii) Rep-VLI, which pre-computes text-encoder outputs into a compact K×C weight matrix so that inference uses only a spike-count argmax over accumulated firing rates, eliminating the text encoder entirely. The framework is instantiated with three spike-driven backbones, including the first fully spike-driven point Transformer (Spike-driven PointFormer), whose 3D spike-driven self-attention (3D-SDSA) replaces standard QK^T V matmuls with addition-only operations on spike tensors via address-event accumulation, enabling 4.3× faster training and 4.1× lower training memory than prior spike Transformers while matching ANN-level accuracy on ModelNet40 and ScanObjectNN.

## Results

On zero-shot 3D classification, SVL with E-3DSNN-H achieves 85.4% top-1 accuracy on ModelNet40 and 47.0% on Objaverse-LVIS, outperforming all prior SNN baselines and matching or exceeding strong ANN methods (e.g., ULIP-2: 84.7%/50.6%) while consuming only 0.79 mJ vs. 161.8 mJ for OpenShape-based Point-Bert. On downstream fine-tuned tasks, SVL consistently improves SNN backbones: E-3DSNN-L+SVL reaches 93.7% on ModelNet40 and 83.0% on ScanObjectNN (+2.5%/+2.8% over the unpretrained backbone), and Spike PointNet+SVL gains +2.1% on Semantic KITTI mIoU and +2.1% on DVS Action accuracy. For generative 3D captioning, SVL-13B with SpikeLLM achieves 51.21 S-BERT and 50.18 SimCSE, surpassing LLaVA-13B (46.37/45.90) and PointLLM-13B* (50.15/50.83) on semantic alignment metrics.

## Baselines & Comparisons

SVL decisively outperforms the only prior SNN zero-shot method, SpikeCLIP (Lv et al., 2025), by a massive margin: 85.4% vs. 5.1% on ModelNet40 and 47.0% vs. 0.5% on Objaverse-LVIS, demonstrating that contrastive vision-language pretraining is essential for spike-based open-world understanding. Against the strongest ANN baselines, SVL's E-3DSNN-H matches ULIP-2 (Xue et al., 2024) on ModelNet40 (85.4% vs. 84.7%) and surpasses OpenShape-based Point-Bert (Liu et al., 2023b) by 2.0%, while consuming only 0.79 mJ versus 161.8 mJ—a 204× energy advantage. On downstream fine-tuned classification, SVL-pretrained Spike PointNet (Ren et al., 2024) jumps from 70.0% to 76.1% on ScanObjectNN (+6.1%), and E-3DSNN-L (Qiu et al., 2025a) improves from 91.2% to 93.7% on ModelNet40, surpassing the ANN Pointformer (Zhao et al., 2021) at 92.8%. For 3D captioning, SVL-13B with SpikeLLM (Xing et al., 2025) achieves 51.21 S-BERT, exceeding LLaVA-13B (Liu et al., 2023a) by 4.84 points and PointLLM-13B* (Xu et al., 2024) by 1.06 points, establishing the first SNN-based method competitive with ANN VLMs on this task. The proposed Spike-driven PointFormer-S (7.69M params) matches ANN Pointformer on ModelNet40 (92.6% vs. 92.8%) while surpassing it on ScanObjectNN (82.1% vs. 81.3%) at 5.1 mJ versus 30.1 mJ, and trains 4.3× faster with 4.1× less memory than Spike Point Transformer (Wu et al., 2025b).

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the CLIP-style contrastive pretraining paradigm (Radford et al., 2021) to the spiking domain, adapting the triplet alignment objective to spike firing rates rather than continuous embeddings, and introduces Rep-VLI to eliminate the text encoder at inference—a critical departure from prior 3D VLMs (ULIP, ULIP-2, OpenShape) that retain large text encoders and are incompatible with neuromorphic hardware. Architecturally, it departs from prior spike-based 3D encoders (Spike PointNet, E-3DSNN) by proposing the first fully spike-driven point Transformer whose 3D-SDSA replaces matrix multiplications with sparse additions, and from prior SNN pretraining (STDP, knowledge distillation, masked modeling) by using a label-free, multimodal contrastive objective that scales to open-world settings. The generative pipeline (3D captioning, QA) directly adapts the LLaVA instruction-tuning framework (Liu et al., 2023a) to a spiking visual encoder.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

SVL provides the first practical pathway to deploy open-world 3D understanding (zero-shot classification, QA, captioning) on neuromorphic hardware, with a 204× energy advantage over the closest ANN baseline (ULIP-2) while matching accuracy—a result that could accelerate adoption of SNNs in edge 3D perception. The I-LIF + 3D-SDSA design principle (shallow time, deep layers, addition-only interactions) offers a general recipe for building efficient spiking Transformers beyond this specific task. Key open questions include scalability to larger foundation-model scales (the authors acknowledge this limitation), whether the Rep-VLI reparameterization degrades gracefully with very large prompt sets, and whether the T-vs-D tradeoff findings generalize to other spiking architectures and modalities.
