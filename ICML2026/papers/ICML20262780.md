## Problem

Standard scaled dot-product attention implicitly regularizes toward a uniform prior over positions (via Shannon entropy), which forces positional structure to be injected heuristically through content-vector manipulations like RoPE or ALiBi. This entangles structural biases with semantic representations, causes attention sinks to emerge as high-norm content keys, and yields poor length extrapolation. The paper asks: what happens if we make the prior explicit, learnable, and derived from first principles?

## Input / Output

GOAT consumes the same inputs as standard Transformers (token sequences up to L_train, image patch grids at 224×224, DNA nucleotide strings, or interleaved image+text tokens) and produces standard outputs (next-token logits, ImageNet class scores, passkey strings, VQA answers). The key structural constraint is that the head dimension d_h is partitioned into a content subspace d_c and a prior subspace d_p = 2R+2 (Fourier features plus one sink lane), so the total dimension is unchanged and value vectors remain purely content-based at full d_h. The method is implemented as a single unmodified SDPA call with pre-scaled query/key vectors, requiring no L×L bias materialization.

## Method

GOAT reinterprets attention as a one-sided Entropic Optimal Transport problem where the entropy regularizer is equivalently a KL divergence from a uniform prior; replacing this with an arbitrary learnable prior π yields the closed-form solution p* = softmax(s/τ + log π). The log-prior K_ij is parameterized as a sum of (1) a truncated Fourier series κ(i−j) with R learnable spectral weights (α_r for symmetric, β_r for antisymmetric interactions) providing translation-equivariant relative position, and (2) a key-only bias u(j) = λj + g(φ_abs(j)) + b·1{j=0} providing an explicit, query-independent attention sink. The critical implementation trick pre-scales the composite query vector (content by √(d_h/d_c), positional by √d_h) so that the standard SDPA kernel's 1/√d_h normalization cancels exactly, leaving the prior term unscaled and the content term at 1/√d_c — preserving FlashAttention compatibility with zero kernel modification. A classification theorem (Appendix G) proves that under SDPA-compatibility, translation equivariance, and boundedness, the admissible relative priors are exactly finite trigonometric polynomials, making the Fourier parameterization the most general form in this class.

## Results

On the 1.23B FineWeb benchmark, GOAT matches RoPE in-distribution (20.75 vs. 20.82 perplexity at 2k) but dramatically outperforms it under length extrapolation: at 8× training length, GOAT achieves 22.69 perplexity while RoPE degrades to 89.45, and at 16×, GOAT reaches 24.77 versus RoPE's 417.43. On the 125M C4 setting, GOAT lowers in-distribution perplexity by 1.55 points over ALiBi while maintaining robust extrapolation to 16× length. On long-context retrieval (Passkey and NIAH), GOAT maintains near-perfect span-token accuracy at context lengths far exceeding training, whereas RoPE, position-interpolated RoPE, and sinusoidal baselines degrade sharply. On DNA modeling, GOAT reduces peak CUDA memory by 36% (2.86→1.83 GB) while achieving lower validation NLL than RoPE.

## Baselines & Comparisons

Against RoPE (Su et al., 2021), GOAT matches in-distribution perplexity at 1.23B scale (20.75 vs. 20.82) but achieves a 4× perplexity advantage at 8k (22.69 vs. 89.45) and a 17× advantage at 16k (24.77 vs. 417.43), demonstrating that RoPE's multiplicative position injection causes catastrophic extrapolation failure. Against ALiBi (Press et al., 2022), GOAT improves in-distribution C4 perplexity by 1.55 points while matching ALiBi's flat extrapolation profile, showing that the learnable prior captures structure that ALiBi's fixed monotone slope cannot. On Passkey Retrieval and NIAH, GOAT substantially outperforms position-interpolated RoPE (Chen et al., 2023) and sinusoidal absolute encodings (Vaswani et al., 2017), maintaining near-perfect accuracy at 8× training length where all three baselines degrade sharply. On ImageNet-1k resolution extrapolation, GOAT outperforms the standard ViT with absolute positional embeddings (Dosovitskiy et al., 2021), which degrades as input resolution increases beyond the 224×224 training size. On the Spatial VQA benchmark, GOAT achieves 20.3% exact answer accuracy versus 16.5% for RoPE and 17.2% for no positional encoding, with the lowest answer NLL (0.350 vs. 0.367 and 0.359).

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the one-sided Entropic Optimal Transport (EOT) formulation of scaled dot-product attention (Litman, 2025) by replacing the implicit uniform prior (Shannon entropy regularizer) with a learnable KL-divergence prior, yielding a closed-form softmax over content scores shifted by a log-prior. The spectral parameterization draws on random Fourier features (Rahimi & Recht, 2007) and the Sinkhorn/EOT machinery (Cuturi, 2013; Sinkhorn & Knopp, 1967), while the key-only sink term and ALiBi-equivalent recency slope are justified via maximum-entropy and minimal-rank arguments. Conceptually, the paper positions RoPE and ALiBi as heuristic approximations of the 'true' EOT-derived prior, departing from RoPE's multiplicative (norm-entangled) position injection and ALiBi's rigid monotone structure in favor of an additive, disentangled, and fully learnable log-prior realized within a single unmodified SDPA call.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The EOT framing provides a first-principles explanation for attention sinks as optimal transport defaults under a peaked prior, converting an empirically observed pathology into a controllable modeling primitive that can be decoupled from semantic content. The theoretical classification (Theorem 2) showing that SDPA-compatibility, translation equivariance, and boundedness uniquely restrict admissible relative priors to finite trigonometric polynomials gives practitioners a principled design space rather than ad-hoc choices. Open questions include whether the learned prior transfers across tasks and modalities without retraining, whether the specific geometric frequency grid is truly optimal versus learned frequencies (ablations suggest insensitivity), and whether the approach scales to the 10B+ parameter regime where RoPE's degradation may be less catastrophic due to other architectural factors.
