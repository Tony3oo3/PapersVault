## Problem

Despite rapid progress in text-based reasoning data synthesis post-DeepSeek R1, vision-centric reasoning datasets remain limited in scale, problem complexity, and cognitive richness of reasoning traces. Existing approaches like LongPerceptualThoughts (LPT) rely solely on dense captions for problem generation, causing diversity saturation at ~30K examples, and produce traces that lack higher-order cognitive structures such as subgoal setting and backtracking. This paper addresses all three gaps simultaneously: scaling to 1M+ problems, increasing problem difficulty, and enriching CoT traces with complex reasoning patterns.

## Input / Output

The synthesis pipeline consumes RGB images from the DOCCI dataset along with human-annotated dense captions and open-vocabulary bounding boxes (from Grounded SAM-2, median 10.7 objects per image at confidence 0.9). It produces three data types: (1) SFT triplets of image + MCQ + expanded reasoning trace + answer, (2) preference pairs for DPO based on correctness and compactness of CoTs, and (3) verifiable MCQ problems with ground-truth answers for online RL. The dense caption is used only during synthesis by the reasoning LLM and is never exposed to the trained model at inference time. Training images are processed at 512×512 resolution with 1024-token input cutoff for Stage 1.

## Method

The framework operates in two stages. Stage 1 generates diverse MCQs at scale by conditioning an LLM on both dense captions and grounded object metadata (bounding-box coordinates + tags), which prevents the problem-synthesis saturation observed in caption-only methods and yields a 3.2× wider semantic spread. Stage 2 applies a composition hardening algorithm that merges up to 5 Stage-1 MCQs from the same image into a single multi-hop problem via an LLM, reducing the trivially-solvable rate from 36.7% to 3.3% (~10×). For each stage, reasoning traces are synthesized via a two-step distillation: a VLM (Qwen2.5-VL-7B for Stage 1, 72B for Stage 2) produces an in-distribution 'Simple CoT', which is then expanded by a frontier reasoning LLM (DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-32B or R1-671B/Qwen3-235B-Thinking) to inject subgoal setting, backtracking, and verification, increasing cognitive behavior frequency by +206%. Training follows a curriculum: SFT on Stage 1 then Stage 2 data, optionally followed by offline DPO (preference pairs from correct/incorrect CoTs) or online GRPO (verifiable MCQ rewards with LLM-judge scoring), with the key finding that SFT on high-quality data with cognitive behaviors is a prerequisite for online RL to scale effectively.

## Results

Fine-tuning Qwen2.5-VL-7B-Instruct on Long Grounded Thoughts with SFT+GRPO achieves 81.68 on V*Bench, 83.80 on CV-Bench, 72.00 on MMVP, 69.02 on RealWorldQA, and 68.40 on MMStar-V. The multistage SFT+DPO configuration reaches 83.25 on V*Bench and 82.28 on CV-Bench, the highest V*Bench score among all compared models. On out-of-domain tasks, the SFT+DPO model gains +8.8 points on NiEH (embodied QA, 56.34 vs. 47.55 base) and +4.07 on MMLU-Pro (51.07 vs. 47.00 base), while the Omni-7B variant improves MMAU-Music by +3.02 points.

## Baselines & Comparisons

Against the strongest open-data baseline LPT (30K problems), the SFT+GRPO configuration wins on 4 of 5 benchmarks: V*Bench (81.68 vs. 80.60), CV-Bench (83.80 vs. 75.30, +8.5 pts), RealWorldQA (69.02 vs. 67.45), and MMStar-V (68.40 vs. 64.13, +4.3 pts), while losing on MMVP (72.00 vs. 77.00). Against the closed-data MiMo-VL-7B-RL, the best configuration matches on V*Bench (81.68 vs. 81.70) and surpasses on CV-Bench (83.80 vs. 82.30) and MMStar-V (68.40 vs. 67.07), but trails on MMVP (72.00 vs. 77.67) and RealWorldQA (69.02 vs. 72.68). The weaker open-data baselines VLAA-Thinker and Revisual-R1-final underperform even the unmodified base model on most benchmarks, underscoring the scarcity of high-quality open vision-centric reasoning data. On cross-modal transfer, LGT data yields positive gains on MMAU-Music (+3.02) and MMLU-Pro (+4.07) for the Omni-7B model, whereas Virgo (visual math) causes substantial degradation (MMAU avg drops from 71.00 to 62.60).

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper directly extends LongPerceptualThoughts (LPT; Liao et al., 2025a), which synthesized 30K vision-centric problems with structured CoTs by using dense captions as a language proxy for images. The key departures are: (1) replacing caption-only synthesis with grounded object metadata (bounding boxes from Grounded SAM) to overcome problem-saturation at scale, and (2) introducing a composition-hardening stage that merges multiple MCQs into multi-hop problems, pushing complexity and cognitive-behavior richness well beyond LPT's linear traces. The paper also positions itself against other open multimodal reasoning datasets (Virgo, VLAA-Thinking, DriveLMM-o1) that are either small-scale, math-focused, or lack the full SFT + offline/online RL data coverage this work provides.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

This is one of the first open, vision-centric reasoning datasets at 1M+ scale that covers the full VLM post-training spectrum (SFT, DPO, GRPO), filling a clear gap where prior open data was either tiny or math-centric. The finding that SFT on high-quality data with explicit cognitive behaviors is a prerequisite for online RL to scale (base-model GRPO underperforms SFT alone) is practically important for the field's RL-for-VLMs direction. Cross-modal transfer from purely visual MCQ data to audio (MMAU) and embodied QA (NiEH) suggests reasoning-structure data may be modality-agnostic, though evidence is limited to single-model/single-benchmark settings. Open questions include how to push GRPO past its ~70K plateau, whether the pipeline generalizes beyond DOCCI's human-annotated captions, and whether the frontier-model dependency for Stage 2 can be reduced.
