## Problem

2D text-to-image generators remain brittle under compositional structural constraints such as generative numeracy, attribute binding, and part-level relations, often hallucinating counts or omitting repeated structures. Text-only Chain-of-Thought can expand reasoning traces but lacks visual grounding, making structural drift along the generation trajectory unverifiable and uncorrectable. The paper asks whether a single autoregressive model can perform process-supervised progressive shape assembly entirely in the rendered 2D domain, without external 3D engines at inference time.

## Input / Output

The method takes a single natural-language goal prompt (e.g., 'Build a sturdy wooden chair with four legs, cushioned seat, and curved backrest') and produces an interleaved multimodal trace of N steps, where each step consists of a concise textual rationale (10–20 words describing the incremental structural action) followed by a 512×512 rendered image of the cumulative assembly state under a fixed canonical front camera. The trace is serialized into a unified token stream where each image is represented by a block of noised VAE latents, clean VAE latents, and ViT understanding tokens; the final rendered image is decoded from the last VAE latent block. No 3D geometry, meshes, or part poses are ever exposed to the model.

## Method

SoT fine-tunes Bagel-7B (MoT variant), a unified early-fusion multimodal Transformer, to generate interleaved text-image assembly traces via a 'decide-then-ground' decoding order: at each step the model autoregressively predicts a textual plan (which part to add and how it attaches), then samples the corresponding rendered observation through 50-step rectified-flow in a frozen VAE latent space, and deterministically encodes the result with a ViT for subsequent-step conditioning. Training uses teacher forcing on serialized interleaved streams with a joint objective of next-token cross-entropy for text positions and MSE velocity regression for noised VAE latent positions (λ_CE:λ_MSE = 1:1), with the VAE frozen and the Transformer backbone plus ViT encoder fine-tuned end-to-end. The supervision comes from SoT-26K, a 26K-trace dataset constructed from PartNet CAD hierarchies through a four-stage pipeline (data curation, fine-grained hierarchy decomposition into ≤15–25 leaf parts per step, headless Blender rendering at 512×512, and GPT-4o-generated step rationales following a structured slot schema). A key design choice is that each step introduces a uniquely defined structural delta ΔP_n forming one semantic component, and the model only observes states through a fixed 2D rendering function—never explicit 3D geometry—so structural assembly logic is learned purely from rendered visual evidence.

## Results

On T2S-CompBench, SoT (Bagel-7B-SoT) achieves 88.44 on Component Numeracy (CN), 84.76 on Visual Topology (VT), 86.25 on Connectivity Plausibility (CP), and 81.51 on Attribute Fidelity (AF), outperforming direct Bagel-7B generation by +24.2 points on CN and +19.3 points on VT. On the process metrics, SoT reaches 91.30 Trace Stability (TS) and 79.19 Rationale Alignment (RA), compared to 32.71 and 45.49 for text-only CoT. Human evaluation scores 4.08/5, the highest among all compared methods. A budget-controlled comparison (Appendix F) confirms the gains are not explained by extra decoding: SoT reaches 85.09 Avg. at 259s versus 73.67 Avg. for Refine-K at 249s.

## Baselines & Comparisons

Against direct Bagel-7B generation, SoT wins on every T2S-Structure metric, with the largest margins on CN (+24.2) and VT (+19.3), indicating that interleaved visual grounding specifically addresses counting and spatial-topology failures. Compared to text-only CoT (Bagel-7B-CoT), SoT improves CN by +12.6, VT by +13.4, and dramatically boosts process metrics (TS: 91.30 vs. 32.71; RA: 79.19 vs. 45.49), showing that rendered intermediate states provide visual working memory that text rationales alone cannot. Among rendered 3D baselines, SoT outperforms Shap-E, LGM, and L3GO on CN, AF, CP, and VT, but Meshy 6 (a commercial closed-source service) retains an advantage on Shape Fidelity (95.43 vs. 83.62) due to its superior global appearance quality. The budget-controlled ablation (CoT-K, Refine-K) confirms that SoT's structural gains stem from the decide-then-ground interleaving mechanism rather than simply spending more compute on additional decoding steps.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper builds directly on the Bagel-7B unified multimodal autoregressive Transformer (Deng et al., 2025), extending its early-fusion text-image interleaving paradigm from general-purpose generation to process-supervised progressive shape assembly. Conceptually, it sits at the intersection of visual Chain-of-Thought (Chen et al., 2024; Cheng et al., 2025; Gu et al., 2025) and compositional text-to-image generation (T2I-CompBench++, Huang et al., 2025), but departs from both: unlike text-only CoT, each reasoning step is grounded in a rendered 2D state; unlike tool-augmented 3D systems (L3GO, 3D-Premise) or 3D-injection approaches (3D-LLM, LRM), SoT uses part-based CAD hierarchies (PartNet) solely for data synthesis and operates entirely in the rendered 2D domain at inference time without external engines.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The paper introduces a concrete, reproducible recipe for process-supervised generation in the rendered domain, paired with a benchmark (T2S-CompBench) that evaluates not just final-image quality but trace stability and rationale faithfulness—axes largely absent from prior T2I compositional benchmarks. Key open questions include whether the single-view, canonical-camera setting generalizes to multi-view or textured 3D output, whether the learned assembly procedure encodes genuine physical constraints (contact, support, symmetry) or merely visual plausibility, and whether the approach scales beyond the 24 PartNet categories to open-vocabulary objects. The computational cost (32×H100, ~258s per sample) and the reliance on GPT-4o for both annotation and evaluation are practical limitations that may constrain adoption.
