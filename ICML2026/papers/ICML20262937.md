## Problem

Existing self-rewarding vision-language models rely on purely text-based self-evaluation, which cannot verify complex multi-step computations, spatial reasoning, or precise geometric calculations, and is prone to evaluation hallucination where linguistically plausible but visually incorrect answers are rewarded. This limits the upper bound of autonomous self-improvement in multimodal reasoning tasks.

## Input / Output

The agent receives a multimodal task instance (x = (I, q)) consisting of an RGB image and a text question, and produces a multi-turn reasoning trajectory τ = {(s_t, a_t, o_t)} where each step is either a textual reasoning action or a structured tool invocation (e.g., Python code execution, image cropping/zooming). The Verifier outputs a per-step feedback tuple V_t = (score_t ∈ [-1,1], conf_t ∈ [0,1], critique_t) in structured JSON, and the final output includes a confidence score and a FINAL_ANSWER. The entire process is modeled as a POMDP with partial observability over the multimodal environment.

## Method

Agent0-VL unifies a Solver and a Verifier within a single LVLM under a shared policy π_θ, where the Solver performs multi-turn tool-integrated reasoning and the Verifier generates tool-grounded process-level rewards by re-invoking external tools to cross-check intermediate steps. A confidence-gated repair mechanism (sigmoid gate on conf_t vs. threshold τ_c) triggers selective local correction when verification confidence is low, with a penalty for unnecessary repairs. The Self-Evolving Reasoning Cycle (SERC) couples an inner loop (trajectory generation + step-wise verification + repair) with an outer loop that updates the shared policy via GRPO using self-generated rewards, requiring zero external reward signals. Training proceeds in two stages: SFT on ~200k tool-augmented trajectories for cold-start, followed by RL on ~40k tasks where the model's own Verifier provides all reward signals, achieving stable monotonic improvement across iterations.

## Results

Agent0-VL-7B achieves an average score of 65.6 across seven visual reasoning benchmarks, a 12.5% improvement over its Qwen2.5-VL-7B base model (58.3) and a 4.29% gain over the strongest open-source 7B reasoning baseline ThinkLite-VL-7B (62.9). Agent0-VL-8B reaches 74.6 average, surpassing the Qwen3-VL-8B base by 6.1% and outperforming closed-source GPT-4o (60.5) on MathVista (83.7 vs. 63.8), HallusionBench (74.3 vs. 55.0), and ChartQA (89.7 vs. 85.7). Iterative self-evolution yields monotonic gains: +5.2% (Iter 1), +4.0% (Iter 2), and +2.8% (Iter 3) over the base model.

## Baselines & Comparisons

Agent0-VL-7B outperforms all open-source 7B reasoning baselines: it beats ThinkLite-VL-7B (62.9) by 4.29%, Vision-R1-7B (61.3) by 7.3%, and MM-Eureka-Qwen-7B (60.2) by 11.4% in average score. Against its own base model Qwen2.5-VL-7B (58.3), the 12.5% gain is driven primarily by MathVision (+12.2 pts) and HallusionBench (+7.9 pts), indicating strong improvements in both geometric reasoning and hallucination resistance. Agent0-VL-8B surpasses the stronger Qwen3-VL-8B base (70.3) by 6.1% and its tool-integrated variant Qwen3-VL-8B-TIR (71.3) by 4.6%, confirming the framework's compatibility across model scales. Notably, Agent0-VL-8B exceeds closed-source GPT-4o (60.5) by 14.1 points on average and leads on MathVista, HallusionBench, and ChartQA individually. The ablation confirms that removing the Self-Evolving Reasoning Cycle (SERC) causes the largest drop (−8.7% avg), followed by removing tool use (−6.5%) and self-repair (−2.5%), validating that all three components contribute synergistically.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the self-rewarding vision-language model paradigm (Zhou et al., NeurIPS 2024; Vision-Zero; ViPER) by addressing its core weakness—purely text-based self-evaluation that suffers from hallucination on visual reasoning—through tool-grounded verification. It also extends Tool-Integrated Reasoning (TIR) for VLMs (GRIT, DeepEyes, OpenThinking) by moving tool use beyond the reasoning loop into the self-evaluation and self-repair stages, creating a unified dual-role (Solver + Verifier) architecture within a single LVLM. The training mechanism (GRPO-based Self-Evolving Reasoning Cycle) follows the RL-for-VLM-reasoning line (R1-VL, Vision-R1, MM-Eureka) but replaces external outcome rewards with internally generated, tool-verified process rewards.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The paper demonstrates that a single 7B/8B model can serve simultaneously as reasoner, verifier, and repair agent, achieving monotonic multi-iteration gains without human annotation—a practical path toward reducing reliance on expensive preference data for VLMs. The Verifier's utility as a standalone process reward model (7.3% average gain across external models) suggests the tool-grounded critique signal generalizes beyond the generating policy. Open questions include whether the approach scales to larger models or more complex multi-step visual tasks, whether the SFT cold-start dependency on GPT-5-generated trajectories limits true autonomy, and whether the 'zero external reward' claim (which still requires an external-reward RL warm-up phase) holds under stricter definitions of self-sufficiency.
