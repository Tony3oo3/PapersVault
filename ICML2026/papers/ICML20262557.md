## Problem

Existing restoration methods for degraded sensory streams face a fundamental trade-off: approaches that bridge long-term occlusions (e.g., Transformers) suffer quadratic complexity limiting temporal context, while generative denoisers (e.g., diffusion models) introduce stochastic artifacts and hallucinations. No unified module simultaneously achieves efficient long-horizon data retrieval and strict noise-suppressed reconstruction, leaving safety-critical perception pipelines vulnerable under heterogeneous sensor degradation.

## Input / Output

NeuroMamba consumes a temporal sequence of T degraded BEV feature maps at 180×180 resolution with 256 channels, produced by a multi-modal (LiDAR + camera) fusion backbone under physically grounded corruptions (fog, rain streaks, sector dropout, occlusions) applied via a non-i.i.d. temporal burst protocol. It outputs a corrected BEV feature map that is injected into the raw observation via a residual connection (x̂_t = x_t + S_t ⊙ ΔX_t), where the binary spike mask S_t determines which spatial patches receive the inertial correction, preserving raw observations in low-confidence regions.

## Method

NeuroMamba is a plug-and-play module with two synergistic components. The Hybrid Spatiotemporal Rectification (HSR) module projects sparse observations into compact latent tokens via cross-attention, then applies a single Mamba selective SSM layer (hidden dim 16) with 3-level hierarchical embeddings (time, spatial, region) to model motion inertia with linear complexity; window-based multi-head self-attention (M=5) restores local geometric coherence, and a coarse-to-fine transposed-convolution decoder reconstructs 6×6 patches. The Spiking Confidence Gate (SCG) repurposes a Leaky Integrate-and-Fire neuron as a temporal evidence accumulator: a lightweight MLP transduces latent tokens into scalar synaptic currents, the membrane potential integrates over frames with a learnable decay factor τ, and a Heaviside step function produces a binary spike that gates the residual correction. Training uses an occupancy-guided weighted binary cross-entropy loss on the continuous membrane potential (not the discrete spike) to enable gradient flow, jointly optimized with the downstream detection objective.

## Results

On the nuScenes-D benchmark, NeuroMamba (as a plug-in on BEVFusion) achieves 52.5 mAP and 52.7 NDS under medium degradation, a +48.3% mAP and +22.3% NDS improvement over the vanilla BEVFusion baseline (35.4 mAP, 43.1 NDS). Under extreme degradation, it retains 9.9 mAP and 27.0 NDS versus the baseline's 7.8 mAP and 17.3 NDS (+26.9% mAP, +56.1% NDS). On clean data, NeuroMamba reaches 62.7 mAP (highest among all plug-in variants) with a marginal NDS drop to 58.2 (vs. 59.7 baseline). Inference cost is 33× fewer FLOPs (42.8G vs. 1413.2G) and 10× lower latency (8.27 ms vs. 84.19 ms) compared to the BEVDiffuser plug-in.

## Baselines & Comparisons

Against the BEVFusion backbone baseline, NeuroMamba consistently improves mAP at every degradation level (e.g., +48.3% at Medium, +26.9% at Extreme) and NDS at all levels except Clean (where it drops 1.5 points from 59.7 to 58.2). Compared to the BEVDiffuser plug-in, NeuroMamba wins on mAP across all conditions (e.g., 52.5 vs. 38.1 at Medium) and on NDS at Light through Extreme, while BEVDiffuser retains a slight NDS edge on Clean data (60.2 vs. 58.2). Against dedicated robust perception architectures, NeuroMamba outperforms MoME (52.5 vs. 49.3 mAP; 52.7 vs. 52.1 NDS) and UniBEV (52.5 vs. 46.6 mAP; 52.7 vs. 51.0 NDS) under medium degradation, despite operating as a lightweight post-fusion plug-in rather than an end-to-end modality-routing system. NeuroMamba also achieves 33× fewer FLOPs and 10× lower latency than BEVDiffuser while using only 8.0M parameters versus 60.0M.

## Relation to Prior Work

NeuroMamba sits at the intersection of selective state-space models (Mamba) and spiking neural network dynamics, repurposing the LIF neuron not for energy-efficient neuromorphic hardware but as an algorithmic hard-threshold gate that standard Mamba's continuous soft gating cannot provide. It departs from prior robust-perception work (UniBEV, MoME, MetaBEV) by operating post-fusion on the unified BEV feature stream rather than at the modality-routing level, and from diffusion-based restoration (BEVDiffuser) by replacing stochastic iterative denoising with deterministic inertial extrapolation plus binary spike gating. The paper also introduces a temporally coherent degradation benchmark (nuScenes-D) that contrasts with the per-frame i.i.d. corruption protocol of nuScenes-C.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The plug-and-play design and ~33× FLOPs reduction over BEVDiffuser make this a practically attractive option for real-time autonomous driving stacks where sensor degradation is inevitable. However, the method's reliance on short-horizon motion inertia means it cannot handle sudden maneuvers or near-total information loss, and the authors explicitly recommend deployment within a redundant perception stack. Open questions include whether ego-pose alignment can extend the effective temporal window beyond T=5, and whether probabilistic generative priors can be integrated for scenarios where inertial extrapolation has no valid history to draw from.
