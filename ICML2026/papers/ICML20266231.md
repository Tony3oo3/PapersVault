## Problem

Computational research on attributional cognition is hampered by the absence of large-scale datasets and reliable automatic evaluation for attributional style assessment and reframing. Existing psychological instruments (ASQ, CAVE) require intensive manual scoring or fixed-choice formats, scale poorly, and provide limited insight into how attributional styles respond to intervention. No prior benchmark unifies attribution-style classification, reframing generation, strategy rating, and preference-aligned optimization under a single theoretically grounded framework.

## Input / Output

For assessment, each input is a JSON-formatted Event-Attribution unit pairing a real-world trigger event (mental, social, or physical) with the subject's first-person causal explanation; the model outputs one of seven style labels (IAS, EAS, SAS, UAS, GAS, SPAS, NEU) or three parallel binary dimension predictions. For reframing, the input is a maladaptive attribution text and the output is an open-ended adaptive reframe with no single gold answer; quality is scored along four CBT-aligned dimensions (attributional shift, event catastrophizing, coherence, constructive coping), each rated 1–5 by an evaluator LLM. The 42,000-sample dataset is split 4:1 for supervised training, and reframing evaluation uses 1,000-sample agreement sets with three human raters.

## Method

The paper introduces a Prevent-Filter-Validate (PFV) pipeline that grounds LLM generation in retrieved real-world events (reducing hallucination), applies heterogeneous LLM critics and rule-based checks to prune inconsistencies, and routes low-confidence samples (top-two probability margin < 0.2) to three domain experts for majority-vote adjudication, yielding 42,000 labeled event-attribution pairs. For assessment, the authors benchmark supervised BERT/RoBERTa classifiers and nine LLMs (Gemma 3, Llama 3.2/3.3, DeepSeek-R1, GPT-4o, Claude Sonnet 4, Gemini 2.5-Flash) across zero- and 3-shot prompting, finding that ASTD-trained discriminative models reach ~97% average accuracy while prompt-only LLMs show clear scaling and few-shot gains but remain ~4 points below. For reframing, they propose a four-dimensional metric suite validated against human raters (mean Spearman ρ = 0.946), then derive preference pairs from metric scores and fine-tune LLMs via Direct Preference Optimization, achieving consistent gains (mean Δ = 0.565 on attributional shift) without any human annotation, with DPO's KL anchor preserving coherence where SFT-on-preferred causes catastrophic forgetting.

## Results

On the ASTD benchmark, supervised BERT-base achieves the highest average classification accuracy at 97.33% across the three attributional dimensions (IAS-EAS, SAS-UAS, GAS-SPAS), with RoBERTa-base close behind at 96.97%. LoRA-tuned Llama 3.2-3B matches BERT-base at 97.32%, while the best prompt-only LLMs (GPT-4o 3-shot: 93.48%, Claude Sonnet 4 3-shot: 93.40%) trail the supervised tier by ~4 points. For attributional reframing, DPO fine-tuning yields a mean Attributional Shift improvement of +0.565 and a Constructive Coping gain of up to +1.113 (SAS→UAS), with Coherence preserved near ceiling (+0.03).

## Baselines & Comparisons

BERT-base (97.33% avg) edges out RoBERTa-base (96.97%) by 0.36 points, with the gap driven primarily by the IAS-EAS dimension (96.89 vs. 95.56). LoRA-tuned Llama 3.2-3B (97.32%) essentially ties BERT-base, demonstrating that a small modern LLM with supervised fine-tuning reaches parity with a dedicated discriminative encoder. All prompt-only LLMs, including frontier APIs (GPT-4o, Claude Sonnet 4, Gemini 2.5-flash at 92.5–93.5% avg), consistently underperform the supervised tier by approximately 4 points, and no prompt-only model surpasses even the LoRA-tuned 3B model. Within the prompt-only tier, scaling and few-shot examples yield clear gains: Llama 3.3-70B improves by ~17 points from 0-shot to 3-shot on the hardest GAS-SPAS dimension, and reasoning-optimized models (DeepSeek-R1-8B: 80.26% 3-shot) outperform larger non-thinking variants (Gemma 3-12B: 79.90% 3-shot). For the reframing task, DPO outperforms SFT-on-y⁺ by 3–5× on Attributional Shift and Coping while preserving Coherence (+0.08 vs. SFT's −0.19 catastrophic forgetting), confirming that preference optimization is necessary rather than merely beneficial.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the line of work on cognitive reframing and emotional support datasets (Cognitive Reframing, ESConv, PPF, PatternReframe) by grounding the task specifically in the reformulated learned helplessness theory's three attributional dimensions (locus, stability, generality) rather than general CBT or positive-psychology heuristics. For data construction, it borrows the retrieval-generation coupling idea from RAG to anchor LLM-generated attributions to real-world events, and for model alignment it adopts DPO as the preference-optimization backbone. It departs from prior CBT-prompting work (Diagnosis of Thought, HealMe) by focusing on a narrower, theoretically specified component—dimension-specific attributional shifts—rather than full therapeutic reasoning pipelines, positioning ASTD as a modular element within larger cognitive systems.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

ASTD provides the first large-scale (42k) benchmark that unifies attribution-style classification, dimension-specific reframing generation, automatic four-dimensional quality scoring, and preference-aligned fine-tuning under a single psychologically grounded framework, addressing a gap where prior tools (ASQ, CAVE) required manual scoring and scaled poorly. The finding that compact supervised models and LoRA-tuned small LLMs outperform frontier prompt-only APIs by ~4–60 points underscores that domain-specific supervision, not raw model scale, drives accurate psychological language understanding. Open questions include cross-cultural generalizability (the dataset encodes norms from its source data and LLMs), whether the LLM-based evaluator metrics fully capture edge-case psychological nuances, and how these components integrate into clinician-in-the-loop therapeutic systems with proper safety guardrails.
