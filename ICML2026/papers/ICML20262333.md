## Problem

Masked diffusion language models generate text by iteratively unmasking tokens, but conventional greedy unmasking strategies (confidence, margin, entropy) are short-sighted, optimizing only local token-level certainty while ignoring sequence-level dependencies. A single incorrect unmasking decision cascades irreversibly through subsequent steps, and no existing mechanism can detect or recover from such local errors at inference time.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ICML20262333/cropped_page1_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method operates at inference time on pretrained masked diffusion language models (LLaDA-8B, LLaDA-1.5) without any fine-tuning. It takes a text prompt and a fully masked token sequence of length 128 or 256, unmasking 2 tokens per denoising step, and produces a complete generated text sequence. The method requires only 2–3 candidate paths per step (a 2–3× computational overhead), and all candidate evaluations are parallelized, yielding modest wall-clock overhead compared to sequential baselines like ReMDM.

## Method

LookUM reformulates unmasking as a reward-guided path selection problem with two components: a path generator that proposes k candidate unmasking sets by sampling from a high-certainty pool (N-best ranking or threshold-based filtering), and an uncertainty-based verifier that scores each candidate by evaluating the sequence-level predictive distribution one step ahead (using average negative entropy or average confidence). The key insight is that local errors inflate sequence-level uncertainty, so paths with lower verifier scores are more reliable. Candidates are selected via Nested Importance Sampling or Sequential Monte Carlo, treating the verifier as a surrogate reward model within a KL-regularized objective. The entire framework is unsupervised and model-free, requiring no external reward model, and achieves near-optimal performance with just 2–3 paths.

## Results

On LLaDA-8B (sequence length 128), LookUM achieves 72.7% on GSM8K, 28.8% on MATH500, 27.4% on HumanEval, 30.5% on MBPP, 25.4% on Countdown, and 25.0% on Sudoku, outperforming all five baselines on most benchmarks. On the RL-tuned LLaDA-1.5, LookUM further improves to 74.5% GSM8K, 29.2% MATH500, 30.7% HumanEval, 45.0% MBPP, 27.3% Countdown, and 26.8% Sudoku. The method requires only 2–3 candidate paths (≈2–3× compute) to reach peak performance, with wall-clock time of 7.34 s vs. 4.30 s for single-path baselines. Notably, LLaDA-8B + LookUM matches or exceeds vanilla LLaDA-1.5 on several benchmarks (e.g., GSM8K 72.7 vs. 69.5, MBPP 30.5 vs. 40.3 at 128), suggesting inference-time optimization can partially substitute for RL training.

## Baselines & Comparisons

LookUM consistently outperforms all five baselines on the majority of benchmarks for both LLaDA-8B and LLaDA-1.5. Against Confidence unmasking (Chang et al., 2022), LookUM gains +4.4 pts on GSM8K (72.7 vs. 68.3) and +7.9 pts on HumanEval (27.4 vs. 19.5) for LLaDA-8B, with similar margins on LLaDA-1.5. Margin-based unmasking (Kim et al., 2025b) is the closest competitor on MATH500 (28.4 vs. 28.8 for LLaDA-8B) and actually edges out LookUM on LLaDA-1.5 HumanEval (31.1 vs. 30.7), but LookUM dominates on all other benchmarks. Entropy-based unmasking (Koh et al., 2024) shows the largest gaps, particularly on MBPP (27.2 vs. 30.5) and Sudoku (0.0 vs. 25.0) for LLaDA-8B. PC-Sampler (Huang et al., 2025) is competitive on Countdown (26.5 vs. 25.4 for LLaDA-8B, the one benchmark where it beats LookUM) but trails by 2–5 pts elsewhere. ReMDM (Wang et al., 2025), which remasks low-confidence tokens, achieves the strongest GSM8K baseline (69.1/70.4) but degrades sharply on Sudoku (0.4) and HumanEval, while LookUM maintains consistent gains across all six tasks without disrupting established structure.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper builds on the reward-guided sampling framework for diffusion models established by Li et al. (2024, SVDD) and Wu et al. (2023), adapting their NIS and SMC machinery to the discrete masked-diffusion setting. It departs from the dominant line of token-level greedy unmasking strategies (confidence, margin, entropy) by reformulating unmasking as a sequence-level path selection problem, and from post-hoc correction methods like ReMDM by preventing errors proactively rather than remasking them. A key conceptual departure is the use of intrinsic model uncertainty (negative entropy of the predictive distribution) as a model-free verifier, in contrast to reward-guided approaches that require externally trained process reward models.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

LookUM offers a practical, training-free inference-time improvement for masked diffusion language models that is complementary to RL-based fine-tuning, as demonstrated by consistent gains on both LLaDA and LLaDA 1.5. The finding that a simple uncertainty signal outperforms a dedicated 7B process reward model (Qwen2.5-Math-PRM-7B) challenges the assumption that task-specific reward models are necessary for guided generation in DLMs, and that naive multipath decoding without guidance actually degrades performance. Open questions include whether internal model representations (attention patterns, intermediate activations) could yield stronger verifiers than output-distribution uncertainty alone, and whether the approach generalizes to continuous diffusion or other discrete generative frameworks beyond masked diffusion.
