## Problem

Existing text-to-3D methods face a fundamental trade-off: 2D-based approaches (SDS, multi-view diffusion) produce rich textures but suffer from geometric anomalies like the multi-face Janus problem, while 3D-only diffusion models ensure geometric consistency but yield low-quality textures due to limited 3D dataset diversity. Simply combining a 2D and 3D diffusion model fails because the two processes, trained independently, produce inconsistent generative directions that deviate from each other during denoising.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/CVPR20241132/cropped_page0_idx1.webp)

## Input / Output

The method takes a single text prompt as input and produces a textured 3D object represented as a radiance field (SDF + color field on a 128³ grid via SparseNeuS), from which a mesh can be extracted, along with M=8 multi-view images at 64×64 resolution used internally for guidance. An optional post-processing step distills the radiance field into a higher-resolution InstantNGP representation (supporting 512×512 rendering) and refines it via SDS optimization, reducing the full pipeline from ~3.4 hours to ~20 minutes.

## Method

BiDiff jointly trains a 3D diffusion model (denoising in SDF space via sparse 3D convolutions) and a 2D multi-view diffusion model (built on frozen DeepFloyd IF-XL with ControlNet-style residual injection) through bidirectional guidance: at each denoising step, the 3D model's intermediate radiance field is volumetrically rendered into multi-view images that condition the 2D model, while the 2D model's denoised multi-view images are back-projected into a 3D feature volume (aggregating mean and variance across views) that conditions the 3D model. The 3D model incorporates Shap-E latent codes as geometry priors via a NeRF MLP feature volume, with Gaussian noise added at a fixed timestep to prevent shortcut memorization and enable generation beyond Shap-E's distribution. Classifier-free guidance with separate scales γ_3d and γ_2d allows independent control of geometry and texture strength, enabling users to fix shape while changing texture or vice versa.

## Results

On 50 test prompts from untrained Objaverse, BiDiff in feed-forward sampling mode achieves 0.79 CLIP R-Precision in 40 seconds, while the post-optimization variant (BiDiff + ProlificDreamer) reaches 0.85 in 20 minutes. This surpasses ProlificDreamer (0.83, 3.4 h) and DreamFusion (0.67, 1.1 h) in both quality and speed. The sampling-only mode is approximately 300× faster than ProlificDreamer while remaining within 0.04 R-Precision.

**Table 1: CLIP R-Precision and inference time on 50 Objaverse test prompts.**

| Method | CLIP R-Precision | Inference Time |
|---|---|---|
| DreamFusion | 0.67 | 1.1 h |
| ProlificDreamer | 0.83 | 3.4 h |
| BiDiff (sampling) | 0.79 | 40 s |
| BiDiff (post-optimization) | **0.85** | 20 min |

## Baselines & Comparisons

BiDiff-post outperforms ProlificDreamer by 0.02 CLIP R-Precision (0.85 vs. 0.83) while reducing inference time from 3.4 hours to 20 minutes (~10× speedup). BiDiff-sampling (0.79) trails ProlificDreamer (0.83) by 0.04 but runs in 40 seconds versus 3.4 hours, making it ~300× faster. Against DreamFusion, BiDiff-sampling improves R-Precision by 0.12 (0.79 vs. 0.67) while being roughly 165× faster. In visual comparisons (Fig. 5), Zero-1-to-3 produces multi-view inconsistencies and cannot directly output 3D meshes, whereas BiDiff generates geometrically consistent results with accurate 3D structure. Compared to Shap-E (Fig. 4), BiDiff produces more diverse geometries that better adhere to text prompts, demonstrating the benefit of incorporating 2D priors alongside 3D priors.

## Relation to Prior Work

BiDiff unifies two previously separate lines of text-to-3D generation: 2D-to-3D up-projection methods (DreamFusion, ProlificDreamer) that leverage large-scale 2D diffusion priors for rich textures but suffer from geometric anomalies, and 3D-native diffusion models (Shap-E, Point-E) that ensure structural consistency but produce low-quality textures due to limited 3D dataset diversity. The key architectural departure is the bidirectional guidance mechanism, which renders intermediate 3D outputs into 2D images to steer the 2D denoiser and back-projects 2D outputs into 3D to steer the 3D denoiser, keeping both processes coherent at every diffusion step. The 3D-to-2D guidance reuses the ControlNet residual injection pattern, while the independent prior-strength control adapts classifier-free guidance to the 3D domain for the first time in a joint 2D-3D setting.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

BiDiff offers the first diffusion-based 3D generation pipeline with independent, user-controllable geometry and texture, a practical capability absent from both SDS-based and 3D-only diffusion methods. The 40-second feed-forward generation (vs. hours for optimization) and its use as a strong initialization that cuts ProlificDreamer's runtime from 3.4 hours to 20 minutes make it a practical tool for iterative design workflows. However, evaluation is limited to CLIP R-Precision on 50 prompts and two datasets (ShapeNet-Chair, Objaverse 40K), and the method still depends on a 3D prior (Shap-E) that constrains geometric novelty; scaling to open-ended, high-resolution, or multi-object scenes remains an open question.
