## Problem

Pretrained text-to-image diffusion models (e.g., Stable Diffusion) produce rich 2D semantic features suitable for perception, but they lack 3D spatial awareness and exhibit a domain gap for 3D tasks. Existing 3D detection methods either require dense multi-view annotations (NeRF-Det, ImVoxelNet) or train from scratch, forfeiting the benefits of large-scale pretrained representations. The paper asks whether 3D awareness can be efficiently injected into frozen diffusion features without degrading their 2D semantic quality.

## Input / Output

The method takes a single RGB image (256×256 or 512×512) and camera intrinsics as input; no text captions or camera extrinsics are required at test time. Features are extracted via a single forward denoising step of the Stable Diffusion UNet (no iterative sampling). The output is a set of 3D bounding boxes (class, center, dimensions, orientation) predicted by a CubeRCNN-style detection head. At inference, predictions from virtual camera rotations (±15°) are merged via non-maximum suppression to produce the final box set.

## Method

3DiffTection repurposes the frozen Stable Diffusion UNet decoder as a feature extractor and injects 3D awareness through two trainable ControlNets that preserve the original model's capabilities. The geometric ControlNet is trained on ~40K posed image pairs from ARKitScenes to perform novel view synthesis; it incorporates an epipolar warp operator that warps source-view features along epipolar lines into the target view, with warping applied only to the final two UNet decoder stages to balance geometric alignment with semantic consistency. The semantic ControlNet is then trained jointly with a 3D detection head on the target dataset to adapt features for detection while keeping both the base SD and geometric ControlNet frozen. At inference, features from all three branches (base SD, geometric ControlNet with identity pose, semantic ControlNet) are summed and fed to the detection head, and a virtual-view ensemble (±15° rotations) further refines predictions via NMS.

## Results

On Omni3D-ARKitScenes, 3DiffTection achieves 39.22 AP3D at 256×256 and 43.75 AP3D at 512×512, surpassing the prior best single-view method CubeRCNN-DLA by 7.4% and 9.43% respectively. At 512×512, it also outperforms CubeRCNN-DLA-Aug (trained on 6× more data) by 2.03% AP3D. In cross-domain zero-shot transfer to Omni3D-SUNRGBD, 3DiffTection with semantic ControlNet reaches 22.64 AP3D without ground-truth 2D boxes, beating DIFT-SD (16.74) and CubeRCNN (16.81) by ~5.9%.

## Baselines & Comparisons

3DiffTection wins across all five baselines on the primary Omni3D-ARKitScenes benchmark. Against CubeRCNN-DLA [5], it gains 7.4% AP3D at 256×256 and 9.43% at 512×512, and even surpasses the augmented variant CubeRCNN-DLA-Aug (trained on 234K images) by 2.03% while using ~6× less data. Against DreamTeacher-Res50 [24], which distills Stable Diffusion features into a ResNet-50 backbone, 3DiffTection leads by 6.02% (256) and 7.61% (512) AP3D. Against multi-view methods NeRF-Det-R50 [54] and ImVoxelNet [37], which require ≥10 views during training, 3DiffTection (using only 2 views for geometric training and 1 for detection) outperforms them by 6.09% and 7.13% AP3D respectively. In cross-domain zero-shot transfer to Omni3D-SUNRGBD, 3DiffTection with semantic ControlNet (22.64 AP3D) beats DIFT-SD [46] (16.74) and CubeRCNN (16.81) by approximately 5.9% and 5.8% without any target-domain tuning.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper sits at the intersection of two active research threads: using pretrained diffusion model features for dense perception (DIFT, DreamTeacher) and novel view synthesis with diffusion (SparseFusion, Zero-123, MVDream). It departs from prior NVS-with-diffusion work by treating view synthesis not as the primary objective but as an auxiliary task to inject 3D awareness into frozen 2D diffusion features, using ControlNet to preserve the original semantic quality. The epipolar warp operator is adapted from SparseFusion but embedded within a ControlNet architecture, and the overall detection pipeline builds on the CubeRCNN/Omni3D framework, replacing its image backbone with 3D-enhanced diffusion features.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The paper demonstrates that 3D spatial reasoning can be effectively injected into pretrained 2D diffusion features through a lightweight auxiliary task (view synthesis on unannotated video pairs), yielding strong label efficiency (50% labels outperforming 100%-label baselines) and zero-shot cross-domain transfer. This suggests a general recipe for adapting powerful 2D foundation models to 3D tasks without expensive 3D annotation. Key limitations include the ~7.5 fps inference speed (unsuitable for real-time applications), dependence on accurately posed image pairs for the geometric stage, and evaluation primarily on indoor scenes (ARKitScenes, SUNRGBD), leaving open whether the approach transfers to outdoor or dynamic settings.
