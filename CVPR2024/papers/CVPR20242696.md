## Problem

Existing 3D human motion prediction methods largely ignore the external 3D scene, producing physically implausible predictions where the human mesh intersects or distorts with the environment. Even when scene information is incorporated, prior approaches encode the entire point cloud into a single global embedding, failing to distinguish salient interactive points from irrelevant background geometry, and underutilize human gaze as an intention signal for trajectory planning.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/CVPR20242696/cropped_page0_idx8.webp)

## Input / Output

The method takes a 3-second past motion sequence (2 fps, each frame comprising global translation, SO(3) orientation, and 32-dim body pose embedding), a 3D scene point cloud downsampled to 4096 points, and a gaze point sequence (3D coordinates of the eye-ray/scene intersection). The motion input is padded by repeating the last observed pose for the 5-second prediction horizon before encoding. Output is a 5-second future motion sequence: global translation, orientation, body pose embedding, and 23-joint positions reconstructed via SMPL-X and VPoser.

## Method

SIF3D encodes the three modalities separately (6-layer transformer MotionEncoder, PointNet++ for scene, and a GazeEncoder for gaze-indexed scene features), then fuses them through two novel cross-modal attention mechanisms. Ternary Intention-Aware Attention (TIA) computes a global salience score over all scene points relative to a condensed motion representation, multiplies it with scene features to isolate globally salient points, and concatenates the result with motion and gaze features for trajectory planning. Semantic Coherence-Aware Attention (SCA) computes per-frame local salience between each pose embedding and scene points, augmented with a spatial salience bias derived from relative point positions to the predicted trajectory, to capture fine-grained local scene details for pose prediction. A GCN-based MotionDecoder reconstructs joint positions from predicted trajectory and pose via frozen SMPL-X/VPoser, and a 3-layer transformer geometric discriminator provides adversarial supervision for physical plausibility. The full model is trained end-to-end with AdamW (lr=0.0004, exponential decay 0.98) for 100 epochs at batch size 8 on a single RTX 3090.

## Results

On GIMO, SIF3D with Scene+Gaze achieves a trajectory destination deviation of 666 mm and a destination MPJPE of 195.7 mm, outperforming all baselines under the same multimodal setting. On GTA-1M, SIF3D reaches 836 mm Traj-dest and 227.7 mm MPJPE-dest, again the best among all compared methods. The method shows the largest gains in long-term (5-second) predictions, where GCN-based baselines (LTD, SPGSN) degrade substantially (Traj-dest > 900 mm on GIMO) while SIF3D maintains tighter accuracy.

## Baselines & Comparisons

SIF3D outperforms all four baselines on the primary metrics (Traj-dest and MPJPE-dest) across both datasets when Scene+Gaze are provided. On GIMO, SIF3D reduces Traj-dest by 61 mm over the closest competitor BiFu (666 vs. 727) and by 135 mm over AuxFormer (666 vs. 746); on GTA-1M the margin is 67 mm over BiFu (836 vs. 903) and 182 mm over SPGSN (836 vs. 1018). For MPJPE-dest, SIF3D leads by 9.3 mm over BiFu on GIMO (195.7 vs. 205.0) and by 6.5 mm over BiFu on GTA-1M (227.7 vs. 234.2). The only metrics where SIF3D is not the best are MPJPE-path: LTD achieves 155.6 mm on GIMO (vs. SIF3D's 156.6) and SPGSN achieves 162.7 mm on GTA-1M (vs. SIF3D's 164.9), indicating that GCN-based methods retain a slight edge in average per-frame pose accuracy, while SIF3D excels in endpoint and trajectory fidelity.

## Relation to Prior Work

SIF3D extends the scene-aware motion prediction line initiated by BiFu (GIMO), which first combined 3D point clouds and human gaze for motion forecasting. However, BiFu encodes the entire scene into a single global embedding, treating all points uniformly. SIF3D departs from this by introducing two complementary cross-modal attention mechanisms—Ternary Intention-Aware Attention (TIA) for global trajectory planning and Semantic Coherence-Aware Attention (SCA) for per-frame pose prediction—that explicitly score and weight scene point salience rather than relying on a monolithic scene representation. The architectural backbone (transformer encoder, PointNet++ scene encoder, GCN decoder, geometric discriminator) is assembled from established components, with the novelty concentrated in the attention design and the ternary (motion + scene + gaze) fusion.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The paper addresses a practical failure mode—predicted human meshes intersecting or distorting with the 3D environment—that prior scene-aware methods still exhibit, and the dual global/local attention design offers a principled way to separate trajectory-level scene reasoning from pose-level scene reasoning. Limitations include evaluation on only two datasets (one synthetic, GTA-1M, where gaze is approximated from face orientation), a fixed 5-second prediction horizon, and reliance on a frozen SMPL-X model that constrains expressiveness. Open questions include whether the saliency-aware attention generalizes to outdoor or multi-agent scenes, whether the geometric discriminator can be replaced by differentiable physics constraints, and how the approach scales to real-time robotic deployment where point clouds arrive at higher rates.
