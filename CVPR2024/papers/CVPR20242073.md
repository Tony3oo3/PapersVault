## Problem

Jaccard distance, the standard metric for pseudo-label generation and re-ranking in unsupervised person re-ID, is unreliable under camera variation because intra-camera samples dominate k-nearest neighbors, injecting high-weight negative samples and excluding informative inter-camera positive samples from the relevant-neighbor overlap computation. This paper identifies and addresses this camera-induced degradation of neighbor reliability.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/CVPR20242073/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method consumes feature vectors extracted by any pre-trained re-ID model from unlabeled RGB person images, together with their camera labels, which are used to partition each sample's ranking list into intra-camera and inter-camera subsets. It outputs a CA-Jaccard distance matrix that directly replaces the standard Jaccard distance in either a DBSCAN-based clustering pipeline (producing pseudo labels) or a re-ranking pipeline (producing re-ranked retrieval lists), requiring only a drop-in swap with no architectural changes to the base model.

## Method

CA-Jaccard modifies two components of Jaccard distance in a camera-aware manner. First, Camera-aware K-reciprocal Nearest Neighbors (CKRNNs) split each sample's ranking list into intra-camera and inter-camera lists, apply the k-reciprocal constraint separately with a small k for intra-camera (k₁^intra=5) and a large k for inter-camera (k₁^inter=20), then union the results—this excludes intra-camera negatives while guaranteeing a large proportion of inter-camera positives in the neighbor set. Second, Camera-aware Local Query Expansion (CLQE) averages the weighted CKRNNs vectors from both intra-camera and inter-camera k-nearest neighbors (k₂^intra=2, k₂^inter=4), exploiting camera variation as a constraint so that samples appearing frequently across multiple cameras receive higher weights, thereby amplifying reliable positives. The combination yields a distance matrix with higher neighbor accuracy and lower computational cost than standard Jaccard, since the expensive recall operation of robust KRNNs is eliminated.

## Results

Applying CA-Jaccard distance to PPLR achieves 86.1% mAP / 94.4% Rank-1 on Market1501, 44.3% mAP / 75.1% Rank-1 on MSMT17, and 45.3% mAP / 90.4% Rank-1 on VeRi-776, surpassing all prior unsupervised re-ID methods by a large margin. The improvement is most pronounced on datasets with greater camera variation: CC* gains +11.7% mAP on MSMT17 (31.1→42.8) and +5.0% mAP on VeRi-776 (38.1→43.1), while the gain on Market1501 is +3.8% mAP. In the re-ranking scene, CA-Jaccard applied to BoT yields 74.1% mAP / 86.2% Rank-1 on MSMT17, outperforming KR re-ranking (66.9/79.4) and ECN (69.0/80.5) by 7.2 and 5.1 mAP points respectively.

## Baselines & Comparisons

CA-Jaccard consistently improves every base method it is applied to, with the largest gains on datasets with greater camera variation. On MSMT17, CC*+CAJ gains +11.7% mAP over CC* (31.1→42.8), and ICE+CAJ gains +4.1% mAP over ICE (38.9→43.0), both substantially outperforming prior SOTA like ISE (35.0 mAP) and PPLR (42.2 mAP). On Market1501, PPLR+CAJ (86.1 mAP) surpasses the previous best unsupervised method ISE (84.7 mAP) by 1.4 points and PPLR itself by 1.7 points. On VeRi-776, ICE+CAJ (44.5 mAP) and PPLR+CAJ (45.3 mAP) both exceed the prior best CC (42.5 mAP) by 2.0 and 2.8 points respectively. In the re-ranking scene, CA-Jaccard outperforms both KR re-ranking and ECN across all three datasets, with the margin widening on MSMT17 (+7.2 mAP over KR, +5.1 over ECN), confirming that camera-aware distance is most beneficial under large viewpoint/illumination variation.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper directly extends the Jaccard distance from K-reciprocal re-ranking (Zhong et al., CVPR 2017), which has become the de facto distance metric in clustering-based unsupervised person re-ID and re-ranking pipelines. The key departure is identifying camera variation as a systematic source of unreliability in the robust KRNNs and local query expansion components, and modifying both in a camera-aware manner (CKRNNs and CLQE). Unlike RIDES [7], which implicitly addresses camera variation by encouraging smaller distances for reliable inter-camera pairs, this work modifies the distance metric itself, and unlike ECN [28] or other re-ranking methods, it explicitly partitions neighbor search into intra- and inter-camera ranking lists.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

CA-Jaccard is a drop-in replacement for Jaccard distance that consistently improves multiple state-of-the-art unsupervised re-ID methods (CAP, CC, ICE, PPLR) in both clustering and re-ranking scenarios, with gains scaling with camera variation (larger on MSMT17 and VeRi-776 than Market1501). Its practical value lies in being a general, low-complexity distance metric that requires only camera labels and no architectural changes. An open question is whether the camera-aware partitioning strategy generalizes to settings where camera labels are unavailable or to other re-ID domains with different sources of domain shift (e.g., temporal variation, occlusion patterns).
