## Problem

Existing multi-view synthesis methods that enforce 3D consistency, such as SyncDreamer, rely on heavy global 3D feature volumes that slow generation to ~60 seconds for 16 views and tend to overfit to specific viewpoints, producing blurry and distorted images. The paper asks whether multi-view consistency can be achieved with a lightweight, localized module that preserves the generative quality of the base diffusion model while enabling faster and more diverse viewpoint synthesis.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/CVPR20241400/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method takes a single 256×256 RGB image and a set of N target camera extrinsics (rotation R ∈ ℝ³ˣ³ and translation T ∈ ℝ³) as input, and produces N multi-view images in the same latent space as the base diffusion model. In experiments, N=16 views are generated at either a fixed 30° elevation with uniform azimuths or at varying elevations from −10° to 40°, and the resulting images can be fed into an instant-NGP-based SDF reconstruction pipeline to recover a 3D mesh in ~2 minutes per object.

## Method

EpiDiff inserts a lightweight Epipolar-constrained Attention Block (ECA Block) into the middle and upsample stages of a frozen Zero123 UNet, training only the ECA parameters while preserving the base model's feature distribution. The ECA Block operates in two stages: (1) Near-Views Cross-Attention emits rays from target-view patches, samples S=16 points along each ray, projects them onto F=4 neighboring views, and uses cross-attention to aggregate epipolar-line features from those views; (2) Ray Self-Attention applies self-attention along each ray to model depth weighting and fuse the 3D point features back into a 2D pixel feature map. Positional encoding uses Plücker coordinates (inspired by Light Field Networks) combined with a ray-relative canonicalizing transform (from GPNR) to mitigate absolute-position bias. This localized design—interacting only with 4 nearby views rather than all 16—reduces generation time to 12 seconds for 16 views while improving PSNR, SSIM, and LPIPS over both Zero123 and SyncDreamer.

## Results

On the Google Scanned Objects dataset under the fixed elevation 30° setting, EpiDiff achieves PSNR 20.49, SSIM 0.855, and LPIPS 0.128 while generating 16 multiview images in only 12 seconds, outperforming SyncDreamer (PSNR 20.11, SSIM 0.829, LPIPS 0.159, 60s) and Zero123 (PSNR 17.79, SSIM 0.796, LPIPS 0.201, 7s). For single-view 3D reconstruction, EpiDiff attains the best Chamfer Distance (0.0429) and Volume IoU (0.4518), surpassing SyncDreamer (0.0496, 0.4149) and all other baselines. Under the more challenging uniform elevation setting, EpiDiff further widens the gap, reaching PSNR 18.83 versus 15.90–15.91 for both Zero123 and SyncDreamer.

## Baselines & Comparisons

EpiDiff outperforms all five baselines on every reported metric. Against Zero123 [29], EpiDiff improves PSNR by 2.7 dB (20.49 vs. 17.79) and LPIPS by 0.073 (0.128 vs. 0.201) at only 5× the runtime (12s vs. 7s), reflecting the cost of cross-view interaction. Against SyncDreamer [31], EpiDiff achieves higher quality (PSNR +0.38, SSIM +0.026, LPIPS −0.031) while being 5× faster (12s vs. 60s), demonstrating that localized epipolar attention is both more effective and more efficient than global 3D feature volume modeling. For 3D reconstruction, EpiDiff beats SyncDreamer by 0.0067 Chamfer Distance and 0.037 Volume IoU, and substantially outperforms One-2-3-45 [28] (Chamfer 0.0768), Point-E [38] (0.0570), and Shap-E [24] (0.0689), confirming that higher multiview consistency translates directly into better geometric recovery.

## Relation to Prior Work

EpiDiff sits in the lineage of view-conditioned diffusion models for novel view synthesis, building directly on Zero123's camera-conditioned UNet and the Latent Diffusion Model framework. Its key conceptual departure is from SyncDreamer, which introduced a heavy global 3D feature volume to enforce multiview consistency; EpiDiff replaces this with a lightweight localized epipolar attention block that only interacts features among a small set of neighboring views (F=4 out of N=16). The positional encoding design draws from Light Field Networks (Plücker coordinate ray representation) and GPNR (ray-relative canonicalization), adapting these geometric encoding strategies to the diffusion latent space.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The 5× speedup over SyncDreamer (12s vs 60s for 16 views) while simultaneously improving quality metrics (PSNR 20.49 vs 20.11, LPIPS 0.128 vs 0.159) makes EpiDiff a practically attractive option for feed-forward 3D generation pipelines where latency matters. The ability to generate views at diverse elevations (not just a fixed ring) addresses a real limitation of prior methods and improves downstream reconstruction quality. However, the two-stage pipeline (multiview synthesis followed by separate 3D reconstruction) remains a bottleneck, and the authors acknowledge that performance degrades for viewpoints far from the input view, pointing toward the need for more robust base NVS models or a unified end-to-end framework.
