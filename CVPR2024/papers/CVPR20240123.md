## Problem

Existing text-driven 3D human generation methods face a fundamental efficiency-quality trade-off: mesh-based approaches cannot capture fine details like accessories and wrinkles, while NeRF-based methods are prohibitively slow for high-resolution rendering. Adapting 3D Gaussian Splatting to this domain is non-trivial because sparse tile-based gradients hinder geometry optimization, large classifier-free guidance scales cause over-saturation, and stochastic SDS loss destabilizes the native density control, producing blurry results with floating artifacts.

## Input / Output

The method takes a single text prompt as input and produces a 3D human represented by 100k anisotropic Gaussians (position, covariance, SH color, opacity) initialized on an SMPL-X mesh surface. Training runs at 1024 resolution with batch size 8 for 3600 iterations on a single A100 GPU (~1 hour), with the camera zooming into the head region at 25% probability during later iterations to enhance facial detail. The output supports real-time novel-view rendering via tile-based rasterization of the Gaussian splats.

## Method

HumanGaussian introduces three key mechanisms to tame 3DGS for text-driven human generation. First, Structure-Aware SDS anchors 100k Gaussians on the SMPL-X mesh and extends Stable Diffusion with a dual-branch UNet (replicating conv_in, first DownBlock, last UpBlock, conv_out) that simultaneously denoises RGB and depth conditioned on a 2D pose skeleton, then applies a weighted dual-branch SDS loss to jointly optimize appearance and geometry. Second, Annealed Negative Prompt Guidance decomposes the SDS gradient into a noisy generative score and a cleaner classifier score, discards the former, and augments the latter with negative prompts whose weight τ₂ is annealed from 1.0 to 0.0 at timestep 200, enabling realistic generation at a nominal CFG scale of 7.5. Third, a size-conditioned prune-only phase (iterations 2400–3300) removes Gaussians whose scaling factor exceeds 0.008, eliminating floating artifacts that arise from the high-variance SDS-driven densification.

## Results

In the user study (MOS 1–5 scale, 17 raters, 30 prompts), HumanGaussian achieves the highest scores across all three criteria: 4.24 Texture Quality, 3.88 Geometry Quality, and 4.71 Text Alignment, outperforming the closest competitor TADA (3.76 / 3.53 / 4.35). On objective metrics evaluated on frontal views, HumanGaussian scores 30.82 CLIP, 6.436 Aesthetic, and 0.262 HPSv2, leading TADA (30.13 / 5.709 / 0.253) and DreamHuman (29.98 / 5.170 / 0.248). The two general-purpose 3DGS baselines (DreamGaussian, GaussianDreamer) trail by a wide margin, e.g., CLIP 24.77 and 25.12 respectively versus 30.82 for HumanGaussian.

## Baselines & Comparisons

HumanGaussian outperforms all four baselines on every reported metric. Against the closest specialized human-generation method TADA, it leads by +0.48 in Texture Quality, +0.35 in Geometry Quality, and +0.36 in Text Alignment (user study), and by +0.69 CLIP, +0.727 Aesthetic, and +0.009 HPSv2 (quantitative). DreamHuman is second-closest on Geometry Quality (3.65 vs 3.88) but lags on texture and text alignment. The two general-purpose 3DGS baselines—DreamGaussian and GaussianDreamer—are substantially weaker, scoring 2.18 and 2.94 in Texture Quality respectively versus 4.24 for HumanGaussian, and 24.77 / 25.12 in CLIP versus 30.82, indicating that domain-specific structural priors (SMPL-X initialization, dual-branch RGB+depth SDS) are critical for the human category. The gap is largest on text alignment, where HumanGaussian (4.71) exceeds even TADA (4.35) by a notable margin, suggesting the annealed negative prompt guidance at a nominal CFG of 7.5 improves prompt fidelity without the over-saturation seen at higher scales.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper sits at the intersection of three active research threads: 3D Gaussian Splatting for real-time rendering, Score Distillation Sampling for text-to-3D optimization (DreamFusion), and SMPL-X-based human body priors for avatar generation (DreamHuman, TADA). It departs from prior 3DGS-based text-to-3D work (DreamGaussian, GaussianDreamer) by anchoring Gaussian initialization on the SMPL-X mesh rather than SfM points or Point-E, and by extending Stable Diffusion into a dual-branch model that jointly denoises RGB and depth. The annealed negative prompt guidance builds directly on the SDS decomposition into generative and classifier scores from concurrent noise-free score distillation work, but applies it specifically to the high-variance human generation setting where over-saturation is most damaging.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

As one of the earliest demonstrations that 3DGS can produce high-quality text-driven 3D humans with fine-grained geometry (wrinkles, accessories) in roughly one hour on a single A100, this paper establishes a practical efficiency-quality baseline that mesh- and NeRF-based methods struggle to match. The SMPL-X initialization strategy and size-conditioned pruning are simple but effective ideas likely to be adopted in subsequent human-centric 3DGS work. However, the authors acknowledge that hand and foot generation remains unreliable due to limitations in the underlying T2I model, and the method is locked to a fixed pose (no animation), leaving the broader question of animatable, multi-pose 3D human generation from text still open.
