## Problem

Existing single-image 3D clothed human reconstruction methods suffer from two critical gaps: (1) insufficient prior guidance during the 2D-to-3D feature translation step, where current approaches merely project features onto 3D points or use fixed learnable embeddings without effectively leveraging body priors, and (2) lack of texture priors for predicting realistic textures in unseen views, resulting in blurry or inconsistent appearances. These limitations cause failures on complex poses, loose clothing, and hidden body regions in in-the-wild images.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/CVPR20242320/cropped_page0_idx1.webp)

## Input / Output

The method takes a single RGB image and SMPL-X parameters (estimated via PIXIE for in-the-wild inputs) as input, and produces a textured 3D clothed human mesh with a UV texture map. The mesh is extracted via Marching Cubes at 256 resolution, with hands replaced by SMPL-X models for visual quality. The texture refinement stage additionally consumes a text description of the input image (generated by a vision-to-text model) as a conditioning signal for the diffusion-based inpainting of unseen views.

## Method

SIFU introduces a Side-view Decoupling Transformer that uses rendered SMPL-X side-view normals (left, back, right) as queries in a cross-attention mechanism with ViT-encoded image features, effectively separating side-view features from the visible front view during 2D-to-3D mapping. A Hybrid Prior Fusion Strategy then combines spatial query features (projected onto four feature planes) with body-prior features (barycentric interpolation on the SMPL-X mesh), along with SDF and pixel-aligned normal features, feeding an MLP that predicts occupancy and color. For texture, a 3D Consistent Texture Refinement pipeline renders the coarse mesh from multiple views, applies a pretrained text-to-image diffusion model with a consistent editing technique (DDIM inversion plus nearest-neighbor feature propagation across key views) to refine unseen regions, and optimizes the UV texture map using combined MSE, VGG perceptual, Chamfer distance, and input-view fidelity losses.

## Results

On THuman2.0, SIFU achieves an unprecedented Chamfer distance of 0.596 cm and P2S distance of 0.606 cm, outperforming the previous best (GTA) by ~18% in Chamfer and ~17% in P2S. On CAPE-NFP, SIFU records 0.773 Chamfer and 0.735 P2S, again leading all baselines. For texture quality, SIFU surpasses PIFu by 22.2% in PSNR on THuman2.0 rendered views. Under noisy SMPL-X inputs (robustness test on THuman2.0), SIFU maintains 0.994 Chamfer vs. GTA's 1.047, confirming its advantage persists with imperfect body priors.

## Baselines & Comparisons

SIFU outperforms all nine baselines across every geometry metric on all three test splits. The closest competitor is GTA (NeurIPS 2023), which SIFU beats by 0.078 Chamfer on CAPE-NFP, 0.023 on CAPE-FP, and 0.137 on THuman2.0; the gap widens substantially on THuman2.0 where SIFU reaches 0.596 vs. GTA's 0.733 Chamfer. Among CNN-based methods, D-IF (ICCV 2023) is the strongest, yet SIFU still leads by 0.051 Chamfer on CAPE-NFP and 0.574 on THuman2.0. ICON and ECON, both normal-map-driven approaches, trail SIFU by 0.11–0.66 Chamfer depending on dataset. PIFu and PIFuHD, the earliest pixel-aligned implicit methods, are far behind (1.6–3.8 Chamfer), confirming the benefit of SMPL-X priors and transformer-based feature decoupling. For texture, SIFU's diffusion-based refinement surpasses PIFu by 22.2% PSNR and visually outperforms TEXTure and DreamGaussian in both detail and multi-view consistency (Fig. 6). Under perturbed SMPL-X inputs, SIFU retains its lead over GTA (0.994 vs. 1.047 Chamfer), while CNN-based baselines degrade more sharply (ECON jumps to 2.12 Chamfer).

## Relation to Prior Work

SIFU extends the implicit-function-based clothed human reconstruction line (PIFu → PaMIR → ICON → ECON → GTA) by introducing a Side-view Decoupling Transformer that uses SMPL-X normals as cross-attention queries to separate side-view features during the 2D-to-3D mapping step—a transition that prior methods largely treated as a simple projection or fixed embedding. The Hybrid Prior Fusion Strategy and occupancy-field formulation are inherited directly from GTA [92] and PIFu [69], while the 3D Consistent Texture Refinement pipeline borrows the consistent-editing mechanism from TokenFlow [19] and applies text-to-image diffusion priors (a trend established by DreamFusion, Magic3D, and Zero123) to a per-subject texture optimization problem. The paper's core departure is the observation that geometric priors have been underexploited specifically in the intermediate 2D-to-3D feature translation step, and that cross-attention with rendered side-view normals is an effective mechanism to close this gap.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

SIFU's 0.6 cm Chamfer/P2S on THuman2.0 sets a new benchmark for single-image clothed human reconstruction and demonstrates that the method generalizes to in-the-wild images with complex poses and loose clothing—scenarios where prior methods produce missing body parts or disrupted garments. The diffusion-based texture refinement enables practical downstream tasks (3D printing, scene assembly, text-guided texture editing) that were previously infeasible with the coarse vertex colors produced by implicit-function methods. However, the texture refinement remains a per-subject optimization (requiring iterative rendering and diffusion inference), limiting throughput for large-scale applications, and the geometry pipeline still depends on an external SMPL-X estimator (PIXIE) whose errors, while mitigated, are not fully eliminated. Open questions include whether the side-view decoupling strategy can be extended to multi-image or video inputs, and whether the diffusion-based texture step can be made feed-forward to match the speed of the geometry reconstruction.
