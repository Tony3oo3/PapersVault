## Problem

Existing 3D Semantic Scene Completion methods rely on voxel-wise feature aggregation via pixel-voxel projection, neglecting higher-level instance semantics and scene context. This makes them vulnerable to geometric ambiguities from occlusion and perspective errors, as isolated voxels lack the holistic understanding that humans naturally derive from perceiving coherent instances within a scene.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/CVPR20242424/cropped_page1_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method takes a single RGB image (1226×370 on SemanticKITTI, 1408×376 on KITTI-360) as the sole input modality, supplemented by a pre-trained MobileStereoNet depth estimate used internally to compute the implicit surface and 3D reference points. It outputs per-voxel occupancy and semantic class logits on a 256×256×32 grid (0.2 m voxel size, covering a 51.2 m × 51.2 m × 64 m scene), with 20 or 19 semantic classes depending on the dataset.

## Method

Symphonies introduces contextual instance queries as intermediaries that orchestrate 2D-to-3D reconstruction and 3D scene modeling through Serial Instance-Propagated Attentions. The pipeline comprises three stages: (1) an Instance-Aware Image Encoder (Deformable Transformer initialized with MaskDINO panoptic-segmentation weights) extracts multi-scale features enriched with instance semantics; (2) a Depth-Rectified Voxel Proposal Layer uses stereo depth to project image features onto the implicit surface, initializing sparse voxel proposals with accurate geometry; (3) iterative Symphonies Decoder Layers perform five attention operations in sequence—Deformable Instance-Image Cross-Attention, Scene-Instance Cross-Attention (restricted to FOV), Deformable Scene Self-Attention, Deformable Instance-Scene Cross-Attention, and Instance Self-Attention—propagating instance-centric semantics bidirectionally between image and voxel domains while fusing global context across queries. The model is trained end-to-end for 30 epochs with a Scene-Class Affinity Loss plus frequency-weighted cross-entropy, achieving 15.04 mIoU on SemanticKITTI and 18.58 mIoU on SSCBench-KITTI-360.

## Results

On SemanticKITTI, Symphonies achieves 42.19 IoU and 15.04 mIoU, outperforming the previous best camera-based method (OccFormer, 12.32 mIoU) by 2.72 mIoU. On SSCBench-KITTI-360, Symphonies reaches 44.12 IoU and 18.58 mIoU, surpassing OccFormer (13.81 mIoU) by 4.77 mIoU and even exceeding the LiDAR-based SSCNet (16.95 mIoU). The method shows particularly strong gains on instance-centric classes such as buildings (+8.9 mIoU over OccFormer), cars (+2.0), and trunks (+6.1) on SemanticKITTI.

## Baselines & Comparisons

Symphonies outperforms all camera-based baselines on both benchmarks in mIoU. On SemanticKITTI, it surpasses OccFormer (12.32) by 2.72 mIoU, VoxFormer (12.20) by 2.84 mIoU, TPVFormer (11.26) by 3.78 mIoU, and MonoScene (11.08) by 3.96 mIoU. On SSCBench-KITTI-360, the margins widen substantially: +4.77 over OccFormer, +6.67 over VoxFormer, +4.94 over TPVFormer, and +6.27 over MonoScene. The only metric where Symphonies trails a baseline is IoU on SemanticKITTI, where VoxFormer edges ahead (42.95 vs. 42.19), attributed to VoxFormer's two-stage training with an extra occupancy network that introduces geometric ambiguities. Against LiDAR-based methods (LMSCNet, AICNet, JS3C-Net, SSCNet), Symphonies achieves dramatically higher mIoU (15.04 vs. 7.07–8.97 on SemanticKITTI; 18.58 vs. 16.95 on KITTI-360), demonstrating that instance-query-driven camera-based modeling can rival or exceed LiDAR-based approaches in semantic understanding.

## Relation to Prior Work

Symphonies departs from the prevailing voxel-wise paradigm in camera-based SSC (MonoScene, TPVFormer, OccFormer) that relies on pixel-voxel projection and local feature aggregation. It draws the deformable attention machinery from Deformable DETR and the Scene-Class Affinity Loss from MonoScene, while explicitly repositioning VoxFormer's sparse voxel proposal as a starting point that it refines with stereo depth rectification. The core conceptual departure is introducing learnable instance queries as intermediaries that propagate semantics bidirectionally between 2D image features and 3D voxel features, rather than treating voxels as the atomic unit of scene reasoning.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The instance-query formulation addresses a recognized weakness of voxel-centric methods—geometric ambiguity under occlusion and perspective distortion—by leveraging global contextual reasoning across instances, yielding substantial mIoU gains (2.72 on SemanticKITTI, 4.77 on SSCBench-KITTI-360) over prior camera-based SOTA. The end-to-end training (no auxiliary occupancy network, unlike VoxFormer) simplifies the pipeline and improves robustness across datasets. Open questions include whether the fixed number of instance queries scales to denser or more diverse scenes, whether the approach transfers to multi-camera or video settings, and whether the reliance on a pre-trained stereo depth estimator limits deployment in monocular-only scenarios.
