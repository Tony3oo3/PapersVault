## Problem

Diffusion models produce higher-quality virtual try-on images than GANs but suffer from poor controllability over garment patterns and textures, and require dozens of sampling steps that preclude real-time use. GAN-based methods, while fast, struggle with complex poses and generate blurry or artifact-laden results. The paper addresses both gaps simultaneously: making diffusion-based try-on controllable at the pixel level and drastically reducing the number of denoising steps needed.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/CVPR20240424/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method takes a person image and a target garment image at 512×384 resolution, along with auxiliary control signals (densepose, a garment mask, and a masked person image). It outputs a single virtual try-on image in which the person wears the target garment. The final image is post-processed with Poisson blending so that regions outside the garment mask are pixel-identical to the original person image, avoiding the seams and face distortions that arise from naive concatenation or latent-space round-tripping.

## Method

CAT-DM combines two components. First, GC-DM builds a ControlNet on top of a frozen PBE (Paint-by-Example) U-Net, replacing CLIP with DINO-V2 as the garment feature extractor so that patch-level tokens preserve pixel-level pattern and texture information; these features are injected via cross-attention, and additional garment-agnostic conditions (densepose, mask) are fed through the ControlNet skip connections. Second, a truncation-based acceleration strategy uses a pre-trained GAN (GP-VTON) to synthesize a coarse try-on image, adds Gaussian noise to obtain x_{T_trunc}, and then runs only 2 DDIM denoising steps from that point rather than the full 50-step schedule. Training updates only the ControlNet parameters (learning rate 2×10⁻⁵, AdamW, two RTX 4090s), keeping the PBE backbone frozen, and the diffusion loss is computed over t ∈ {1,…,T_trunc} instead of the full range.

## Results

On VITON-HD, CAT-DM achieves the best scores across all six metrics: FIDu=8.93, KIDu=1.37, FIDp=5.60, KIDp=0.83, SSIMp=0.877, and LPIPSp=0.0803, while requiring only 2 DDIM sampling steps (T_trunc=100) compared to 50 steps for DCI-VTON—a 25× acceleration. GC-DM (without the GAN initialization) also outperforms all baselines on FID and KID in both paired and unpaired settings (e.g., FIDp=7.11 vs. 8.19 for DCI-VTON). On DressCode, GC-DM similarly leads all diffusion baselines, achieving FIDu=12.62 and KIDu=1.89 on the Upper category versus 17.30/5.11 for MGD.

## Baselines & Comparisons

CAT-DM outperforms every baseline on all six VITON-HD metrics. Against the strongest diffusion baseline DCI-VTON, CAT-DM reduces FIDp from 8.19 to 5.60 (−31%) and KIDp from 2.93 to 0.83 (−72%), while also improving SSIMp (0.877 vs. 0.875) and LPIPSp (0.0803 vs. 0.0816). Against the best GAN baseline GP-VTON, CAT-DM lowers FIDu from 10.49 to 8.93 and FIDp from 7.71 to 5.60, and improves LPIPSp from 0.0897 to 0.0803, while also fixing artifacts (e.g., missing limbs) that GP-VTON produces. GC-DM alone (16 DDIM steps) already beats DCI-VTON on FID and KID in both settings, confirming that the ControlNet + DINO-V2 design improves controllability over prior diffusion try-on methods. The largest gap is against PBE (the locked backbone), where CAT-DM reduces FIDp from 14.32 to 5.60, demonstrating that the garment-conditioned ControlNet and Poisson blending are critical for pixel-level garment fidelity. On DressCode, GC-DM leads MGD and LaDI-VTON by 4–5 FID points and 3–4 KID points across all three garment categories, though CAT-DM is not evaluated there due to the absence of a pre-trained GAN on that dataset.

## Relation to Prior Work

CAT-DM builds on the PBE exemplar-based diffusion model as a frozen backbone and wraps it with a trainable ControlNet to inject garment-agnostic person representations (densepose, mask, masked image) as additional control conditions, following the ControlNet paradigm for conditional generation. The acceleration strategy is conceptually derived from TDPM's idea of starting the reverse diffusion chain from a learned implicit distribution at an intermediate step, but departs from TDPM by replacing the learned distribution with a pre-trained GAN (GP-VTON) that generates a coarse try-on image which is then noised to the truncation step. For garment feature extraction, the paper replaces PBE's CLIP encoder with DINO-V2 to obtain patch-level pixel information, and adds Poisson blending to resolve the seam artifacts inherent in latent-space diffusion reconstruction.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The 25-fold reduction in sampling steps (2 vs. 50 for DCI-VTON) makes diffusion-based virtual try-on viable for near-real-time applications, which was previously a major barrier. The hybrid GAN-then-diffusion formulation is a practical recipe that practitioners can adapt to other conditional generation tasks, though the paper acknowledges that CAT-DM's ceiling is partially bounded by the quality of the underlying GAN. Open questions include whether the truncation strategy generalizes to other diffusion backbones beyond PBE, how sensitive performance is to the choice of GAN backbone (the paper shows dependence on GP-VTON quality), and whether the approach extends to video or 3D try-on settings.
