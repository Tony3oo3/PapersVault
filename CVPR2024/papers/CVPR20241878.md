## Problem

Existing event-based camera pose relocalization (CPR) networks convert asynchronous events into frame-based representations (event images, time surfaces), discarding the fine-grained temporal information that is central to event data. Additionally, these methods rely on computationally heavy architectures that contradict the low-power, low-latency design philosophy of event cameras, making them impractical for edge deployment.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/CVPR20241878/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The network consumes raw event streams (x, y, t, p) directly without any format conversion, partitioned into sliding windows of fixed duration R=1 ms and uniformly sampled to N=1024 points. Spatial coordinates are normalized by camera resolution (w, h) and temporal coordinates by the window duration, yielding a pseudo-point cloud with (x/w, y/h, Δt/R) as 3D coordinates. The output is a 6-DOF pose: a 3D translation vector and 3D rotation expressed as Euler angles, regressed end-to-end from a single forward pass.

## Method

PEPNet treats each event as a point in a pseudo-3D space and processes it through a three-stage hierarchical structure adapted from PointNet++ but with two critical modifications: (1) temporal ordering of points is strictly preserved (no permutation invariance), and (2) MaxPooling aggregation is replaced by an attention-based temporal aggregation that computes softmax weights over the K nearest neighbors along the time axis. After hierarchical spatial feature extraction, an Attentive Bidirectional LSTM (A-Bi-LSTM) captures explicit temporal dependencies across the sequence of aggregated features, with an attention mechanism selecting the most informative timesteps for the final regressor. The entire network is deliberately lightweight (0.774M parameters, 0.459G FLOPs), trained with a weighted L2 loss on translation and rotation plus L2 regularization, achieving 38% and 33% error reduction over SOTA on IJRR and M3ED respectively while running in 6.7 ms.

## Results

On the IJRR dataset (random split), PEPNet achieves an average combined translation and rotation error of 0.013 m / 0.904°, outperforming the previous SOTA event-based method CNN-LSTM (0.019 m / 1.591°) by 38%. The lightweight variant PEPNet_tiny (0.064M parameters, 0.033G FLOPs) achieves 0.019 m / 1.306° while using only 0.5% of CNN-LSTM's parameters. On the outdoor M3ED dataset, PEPNet delivers a 33% improvement over prior methods. Inference time is 6.7 ms on a server, compared to ~30 ms for DSAC* and AECRN.

## Baselines & Comparisons

PEPNet outperforms all eight baselines on the IJRR random split. Against the closest event-based competitor CNN-LSTM [37], PEPNet reduces average translation error from 0.019 m to 0.013 m and rotation error from 1.591° to 0.904° (a 38% combined improvement), while using only 6% of its parameters (0.774M vs. 12.63M). Against the frame-based baselines (PoseNet, Bayesian PoseNet, Pairwise-CNN, LSTM-Pose), PEPNet achieves 10–18× lower translation error. On the novel split (Table 2), PEPNet (0.029 m / 2.13°) beats AECRN (0.035 m / 2.23°) and DSAC* (0.040 m / 2.53°) while running in 6.7 ms versus their ~30 ms. The tiny variant PEPNet_tiny matches CNN-LSTM's average translation (0.019 m) with 99.5% fewer parameters, confirming that the point-based representation itself—not model scale—drives the performance gain.

## Relation to Prior Work

PEPNet adapts the hierarchical Set Abstraction architecture of PointNet++ to event data, but makes two deliberate departures: it abandons permutation invariance to preserve the strict temporal ordering of events, and it replaces MaxPooling with an attention-based temporal aggregation that explicitly weights each point's contribution along the time axis. The pseudo-point-cloud formulation (treating (x, y, t) as 3D coordinates) follows Wang et al.'s space-time event clouds, but PEPNet is the first to apply this representation end-to-end for 6-DOF pose relocalization rather than gesture recognition. The A-Bi-LSTM module complements the implicit temporal features extracted hierarchically with explicit recurrent temporal modeling, a combination not previously explored in event-based CPR, which had relied on frame-based representations (event images, time surfaces, entropy frames) that discard fine-grained temporal resolution.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The practical significance lies in the extreme efficiency: PEPNet_tiny matches SOTA accuracy with 0.064M parameters (0.5% of CNN-LSTM) and 6.7 ms inference, making event-camera CPR viable on battery-powered edge devices where frame-based pipelines are infeasible. However, the evaluation is limited to two scene-specific datasets (IJRR indoor, M3ED outdoor) with small sequence counts, and the novel-split results show notable overfitting, raising questions about generalization to unseen scenes or larger-scale deployments. Open questions include whether the point-based formulation scales to multi-scene or open-world relocalization, whether the attention-based aggregation generalizes beyond the K=24 neighborhood size tested, and whether the 6.7 ms latency holds on actual FPGA/ASIC hardware rather than a desktop GPU.
