## Problem

Blind text image super-resolution must simultaneously preserve correct character structures (text fidelity) and produce visually realistic styles (fonts, glyphs, colors, poses), which is especially difficult for Chinese text with complex strokes under severe real-world degradation. Existing methods either rely on fixed text priors that fail under heavy distortion, or use predefined font codebooks that cannot generalize to unseen styles, making it hard to achieve both fidelity and realness at once.

## Input / Output

The method takes a single low-resolution Chinese text image (degraded via BSRGAN/Real-ESRGAN pipelines from 128×512 HR ground truth) and produces a high-resolution text image at ×2 or ×4 scale. During inference, a text recognition model (TransOCR) provides an initial text sequence estimate from the LR image, which seeds the Text Diffusion Model; the final output is the reconstructed HR image decoded from the latent space, with the refined text sequence available as a byproduct for evaluation via ACC and NED metrics.

## Method

DiffTSR jointly optimizes an Image Diffusion Model (IDM, a latent-space Stable Diffusion UNet conditioned on the LR image latent and text prior) and a Text Diffusion Model (TDM, a discrete multinomial diffusion Transformer decoder that refines the text sequence) through a Mixture of Multi-modality (MoM) module. At every reverse diffusion step, MoM fuses the current latent features Z_t, the LR latent Z_LR, and the current text state c_t to produce an image condition for TDM and a text condition for IDM, enabling mutual correction: TDM uses progressively clearer image information to fix misrecognized characters, while IDM uses the corrected text to generate more faithful stroke structures. TDM starts its reverse process from the initial OCR prediction rather than pure noise, and the two models iterate over T collaborative steps before the VAE decoder reconstructs the final HR image.

## Results

On the synthetic CTR-TSR-Test dataset, DiffTSR achieves 25.08 PSNR, 0.156 LPIPS, 5.906 FID, 0.8594 ACC, and 0.8718 NED at ×2, and 21.85 PSNR, 0.231 LPIPS, 8.482 FID, 0.8350 ACC, and 0.8471 NED at ×4, outperforming all baselines on every metric. On the real-world RealCE dataset (without fine-tuning), DiffTSR reaches 18.88 PSNR, 0.211 LPIPS, 25.08 FID, 0.9085 ACC, and 0.9247 NED at ×2, and 17.49 PSNR, 0.336 LPIPS, 70.59 FID, 0.8475 ACC, and 0.8747 NED at ×4. The largest margins over the strongest baselines appear in text-fidelity metrics (ACC, NED), where DiffTSR leads by 5–15 percentage points over the next-best method.

## Baselines & Comparisons

DiffTSR outperforms all seven baselines on every metric (PSNR, LPIPS, FID, ACC, NED) at both ×2 and ×4 on both the synthetic CTR-TSR-Test and real-world RealCE datasets. Among natural-image SR methods, ESRGAN is the closest competitor on perceptual quality (LPIPS 0.191 vs. 0.156 at ×2 synthetic) but still trails DiffTSR by a wide margin on text fidelity (ACC 0.8112 vs. 0.8594). Among text-specific SR methods, TATT and NAFNet are the strongest on PSNR (24.87 and 25.04 vs. 25.08 at ×2), yet DiffTSR leads them by 5–6 percentage points on ACC and NED, confirming that the joint image–text diffusion strategy yields substantially better character-level accuracy. MARCONet, the most recent text-SR method using a codebook and StyleGAN, performs worst among text-specific baselines on RealCE (ACC 0.6638 at ×2), highlighting its limited generalization to unseen styles. On the real-world RealCE benchmark (no fine-tuning), DiffTSR's advantage is even more pronounced: it achieves 0.9085 ACC at ×2 versus 0.7953 for the next-best TATT, a gap of over 11 percentage points, demonstrating strong generalization to unknown degradations and text styles.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper builds on two distinct diffusion model lineages: latent diffusion (Stable Diffusion) for continuous image generation and multinomial/categorical diffusion for discrete sequence modeling. It departs from prior text image SR methods (TSRN, TBSRN, TATT, MARCONet) that rely on fixed text recognition priors or codebook-based structure priors by replacing the static text prior with a jointly optimized text diffusion model. The key architectural departure is the Mixture of Multi-modality (MoM) module, which creates a bidirectional information channel between the image and text diffusion processes at every denoising step, rather than the one-way conditioning used in the baseline.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The joint image-text diffusion framework offers a principled way to simultaneously improve text fidelity and visual realism, addressing a long-standing tension in text SR where structure accuracy and style naturalness are often at odds. The strong generalization to the unseen RealCE dataset without fine-tuning suggests the approach captures broader text image distributions rather than overfitting to training styles. Limitations include restriction to Chinese text at a fixed resolution (128×512), the computational cost of iterative diffusion sampling, and the lack of real-time inference capability. Open questions include whether this joint diffusion paradigm extends to other structured restoration tasks (e.g., document images, mathematical notation) and whether the MoM coupling strategy generalizes to other discrete-continuous modality pairs.
