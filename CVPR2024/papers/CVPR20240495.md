## Problem

Score Distillation Sampling (SDS) and its variant VSD, the dominant paradigms for 3D generation from 2D diffusion priors, suffer from over-saturation, over-smoothing, Janus-like artifacts, and limited diversity due to their inherent mode-seeking behavior. VSD partially alleviates these issues by treating the 3D scene as a distribution, but its discrete particle-based representation (fewer than 10 NeRFs) remains unexpressive and still produces over-saturated colors. The paper addresses the gap of how to faithfully model the full continuous conditional distribution of a pre-trained diffusion model for 3D synthesis without the quality degradation of score-based optimization.

## Input / Output

The method takes a single RGB image (256×256) and a text prompt as conditioning signals, producing a 3D object represented as a triplane feature volume (3×N×N×C) that can be volumetrically rendered from arbitrary camera poses. Unlike SDS-based methods that require 10+ hours of per-object optimization, the trained generator samples diverse novel 3D objects in under a second. The triplane representation also supports continuous 3D interpolation in the W+ latent space and single-view reconstruction via inversion.

## Method

CAD replaces score distillation with adversarial distillation: a StyleGAN2-based 3D generator (with triplane features, following EG3D) is trained with a non-saturating GAN loss against a discriminator, where 'real' samples are drawn from a view-conditioned diffusion model (Zero-1-to-3) rather than optimized via denoising score gradients. To ensure 3D consistency at high resolution, the 2D image-space upsampler is 'baked' into a 3D triplane upsampler by minimizing LPIPS between the two branches' renderings at patch level, so that all views from the same triplane are inherently consistent. A multi-view sampling pipeline addresses the frontal-view bias of diffusion models through camera pose pruning (filtering geometrically inconsistent samples via overlapping masks and CLIP similarity) and distribution refinement (re-denoising with DeepFloyd and depth-conditioned ControlNet to increase appearance diversity while preserving pose). The generator's W+ latent space enables downstream tasks including diversified sampling, single-view reconstruction by inversion, and continuous 3D interpolation.

## Results

On a 360° camera trajectory (120 frames) evaluated with CLIP similarity, CAD achieves the highest Image Score of 82.56 (ViT-L/14) and 89.16 (ViT-B/32), substantially outperforming all baselines. For Text Score, CAD leads with 25.32 (ViT-L/14) and is nearly tied with Magic123 on ViT-B/32 (29.81 vs. 29.89). The evaluation covers 1,200 images from real-world, synthetic (Synthetic-NeRF, Synthetic-NSVF), and diffusion-generated sources, with a single input image and text prompt as conditioning.

## Baselines & Comparisons

CAD outperforms all four baselines on the CLIP Image Score, with the largest margin over DreamFusion (+16.85 on ViT-L/14) and ProlificDreamer (+9.84), reflecting the elimination of over-saturation and over-smoothing artifacts inherent to SDS/VSD. Against Magic123, the closest single-view competitor, CAD gains +6.95 (ViT-L/14) and +5.14 (ViT-B/32) on Image Score, indicating noticeably sharper and more photorealistic renderings. On Text Score (ViT-L/14), CAD leads all baselines (25.32), while on ViT-B/32 it is essentially tied with Magic123 (29.81 vs. 29.89). Qualitatively, CAD produces fewer multi-face (Janus) artifacts than ProlificDreamer and avoids the blurry, color-shifted outputs of Zero-1-to-3 and Magic123. A key practical advantage is that CAD generates diverse novel 3D objects in under a second from the learned generator, whereas DreamFusion and ProlificDreamer require over 10 hours of per-sample re-optimization.

## Relation to Prior Work

CAD departs from the dominant Score Distillation Sampling (SDS) paradigm (DreamFusion) and its variational extension (ProlificDreamer/VSD), which optimize a single or small set of NeRF instances to match diffusion model scores. Instead, it reframes 3D generation as a distribution-matching problem solved via adversarial training, building on the EG3D triplane generator architecture and the view-conditioned diffusion model of Zero-1-to-3 for multi-view sampling. The 3D-consistent upsampling strategy follows Mimic3D's 3D-to-2D imitation, and the overall pipeline repurposes 2D diffusion models (DeepFloyd, ControlNet) as data engines rather than optimization targets.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

CAD provides a practical and conceptually clean alternative to SDS-based pipelines, resolving over-saturation, over-smoothing, and Janus artifacts while enabling diverse generation in seconds rather than hours of per-object optimization. Key limitations include slow volumetric rendering (~0.1 FPS), reliance on a single conditioning input, and object-level scope only. Open questions include whether the approach extends to scene-level synthesis, whether multi-condition training can unlock greater geometric diversity, and whether faster renderers like Gaussian Splatting can close the efficiency gap without sacrificing quality.
