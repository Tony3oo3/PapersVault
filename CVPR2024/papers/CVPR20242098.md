## Problem

Self-supervised anomaly detection methods face two key limitations: existing anomaly synthesis strategies (CutPaste, DTD blending, Poisson editing) produce synthetic anomalies that diverge significantly from real-world anomaly distributions, and large-scale pre-trained features suffer from redundancy and classification-task bias that no prior method addresses with a unified, category-agnostic selection strategy.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/CVPR20242098/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

RealNet consumes 256×256 industrial RGB images and produces a per-pixel anomaly score map (from which the image-level score is the maximum value) plus a binary anomaly mask via thresholding. During training, only normal images are used; anomalous training samples are synthesized on-the-fly by blending diffusion-generated perturbations with Perlin-noise-shaped masks. The method runs at 31.93 FPS on a single RTX 3090 with 4 GB GPU memory.

## Method

RealNet combines three modules around a WideResNet50 backbone. (1) SDAS modifies the DDPM reverse diffusion step by inflating the variance to (1+s)Σ, sampling from low-density regions to generate anomalies with controllable strength s; these are blended into normal images via Perlin-noise masks. (2) AFS scores each pre-trained feature map by how well its squared difference between anomalous and normal images aligns with the ground-truth mask, then retains only the top-m_k maps per layer, reducing dimensionality and pre-training bias while broadening the effective receptive field. (3) RRS applies both GlobalMaxPooling and GlobalAveragePooling to the concatenated multi-scale reconstruction residuals, selecting the top-r/2 channels by each criterion to discard residuals lacking anomalous information before feeding the selected residuals into a discriminator that outputs the final anomaly score map. The overall loss is the sum of L2 reconstruction loss and cross-entropy segmentation loss.

## Results

On MVTec-AD, RealNet achieves 99.6% Image AUROC and 99.0% Pixel AUROC, outperforming all compared methods. On MPDD, RealNet reaches 96.3% Image AUROC and 98.2% Pixel AUROC, surpassing the previous best (CFlow at 86.1% Image AUROC) by 10.2 percentage points. On VisA and BTAD, RealNet achieves 97.8%/98.8% and 96.1%/97.9% (Image/Pixel AUROC) respectively, all without dataset-specific tuning. The method also achieves 31.93 FPS inference speed on a single RTX 3090 with only 4 GB GPU memory.

## Baselines & Comparisons

On MVTec-AD, RealNet (99.6/99.0) ties SimpleNet on Image AUROC (99.6) but leads by 0.9 points on Pixel AUROC, and outperforms RD++ (99.4/98.3), PatchCore (99.1/98.1), and FastFlow (99.3/98.1) on both metrics. The gap widens substantially against reconstruction-based methods: DRAEM+SSPCAB (98.9/97.2), DeSTSeg (98.6/97.9), DiffAD (98.7/98.3), DSR (98.2/–), and UniAD (96.6/96.6) all trail by 0.7–3.0 points on Image AUROC. On MPDD, RealNet dominates every baseline by a wide margin, beating CFlow (86.1/97.7) by 10.2 points on Image AUROC and PatchCore (82.1/95.7) by 14.2 points, while the remaining methods (PaDiM, SPADE, DAGAN, Skip-GANomaly) fall 19–31 points behind on Image AUROC. For anomaly synthesis quality (Table 1), SIA outperforms CutPaste and NSA across object categories and matches or slightly trails DTD on texture categories, with an overall average of 99.65/99.03/93.07 (Image/Pixel AUROC/PRO) versus DTD's 99.52/98.92/91.40.

## Relation to Prior Work

RealNet sits at the intersection of two active research threads in industrial anomaly detection: diffusion-based image generation (building on DDPM) and pre-trained feature reconstruction (building on the DRAEM/DSR/RD++ lineage). It departs from prior self-supervised synthesis methods (CutPaste, NSA, DRAEM) by operating in the probability space of a trained diffusion model rather than relying on data augmentation rules or external texture datasets, and it departs from prior feature reconstruction methods by replacing manual layer selection or small backbones with an adaptive, category-specific feature map selection (AFS) over a large WideResNet50 backbone. The RRS module further distinguishes the work by adaptively discarding uninformative reconstruction residuals rather than aggregating all scales uniformly, a design choice motivated by the observation that different spatial resolutions capture different anomaly types.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The release of the 360K-image SIA dataset provides a practical, reusable resource that can substitute for the DTD texture dataset in self-supervised anomaly detection pipelines, potentially reducing the need for external data. The finding that a single set of hyperparameters (AFS dimensions, RRS retention ratio) transfers across four diverse benchmarks without per-category tuning is practically valuable for deployment. However, the approach still requires training a separate diffusion model per category for anomaly synthesis, and the reliance on synthetic anomalies for AFS selection means performance may degrade when real anomaly distributions diverge significantly from what SDAS produces. Open questions include whether the diffusion-based synthesis generalizes to 3D or multi-modal industrial inspection and whether the feature selection strategy can be made fully end-to-end differentiable.
