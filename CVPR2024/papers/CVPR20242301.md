## Problem

Existing egocentric 3D pose estimation methods either rely on monocular cameras (suffering from depth ambiguity and self-occlusion) or use stereo setups without fully exploiting scene geometry or temporal video context. In compact eyeglasses-based configurations, severe self-occlusion further degrades 3D reconstruction accuracy, and no prior method integrates explicit scene depth or sequential temporal information into the pose estimation pipeline.

## Input / Output

The method consumes T=5 egocentric stereo fisheye RGB frames (170° FOV, 12 cm baseline, resized to 256×256) and produces a device-relative 3D pose of 16 body joints at the final time step. Internally, it also generates 2D joint heatmaps (64×64, 15 joints), human body masks via SAM, and SfM-reconstructed depth maps (H/4 × W/4) that may contain invalid regions due to self-occlusion. The output is a single 16×3 pose vector, with auxiliary supervision applied to intermediate frames during training.

## Method

The framework is a four-stage pipeline: (1) a ResNet18-based 2D heatmap estimator, (2) SAM-guided human body segmentation to isolate the background, (3) SfM-based 3D scene reconstruction over uniformly sampled 4-second windows (using Metashape) to produce depth maps and depth region masks, and (4) a DETR-based transformer decoder that regresses 3D poses. Two key mechanisms distinguish the 3D module: depth padding masks (set to −∞ in attention logits) that suppress invalid depth values caused by self-occlusion, and video-dependent joint query augmentation that fuses temporally pooled stereo intermediate features (via adaptive average pooling over T frames) into each frame's joint queries through a learned FC offset. The transformer decoder uses cross-attention over concatenated depth and heatmap features as memory, with self-attention among augmented queries, and is trained with a combined MPJPE + bone-angle cosine loss applied to both the target frame and all past frames in the sequence.

## Results

On UnrealEgo (device-relative), the proposed method achieves 46.20 mm MPJPE and 40.19 mm PA-MPJPE, outperforming the temporal Baseline (63.44/50.97), Akada et al. (84.53/63.92), and Zhao et al. (88.12/65.36). On the larger synthetic dataset UnrealEgo2, the method reaches 30.53 mm MPJPE and 26.72 mm PA-MPJPE, a ≥40% improvement over the Baseline (52.23/39.78). On the real-world UnrealEgo-RW dataset, the method achieves 104.14 mm MPJPE and 82.18 mm PA-MPJPE, surpassing the Baseline (115.95/85.00) by over 10%. The method also leads on 3D PCK and AUC across all three datasets.

## Baselines & Comparisons

The paper compares against three external baselines and a modified temporal Baseline (Akada et al.'s autoencoder 3D module extended to T=5 frames). On UnrealEgo (device-relative), the proposed method reduces MPJPE from 84.53 mm (Akada et al.) and 88.12 mm (Zhao et al.) to 46.20 mm, a >45% improvement, and also beats the temporal Baseline (63.44 mm) by 27%. On UnrealEgo2, the gap widens dramatically: 30.53 mm vs. 52.23 mm (Baseline), 72.80 mm (Akada), and 79.64 mm (Zhao), confirming the ≥40% headline claim. On the real-world UnrealEgo-RW dataset, the method achieves 104.14 mm MPJPE vs. 115.95 mm (Baseline), 122.64 mm (Akada), and 117.57 mm (Zhao), a >10% gain. Kang et al. [12] is only reported for pelvis-relative on UnrealEgo (60.82 mm MPJPE) and is outperformed by the proposed method (50.55 mm). The consistent wins across all three datasets and both pelvis-relative and device-relative formulations indicate the transformer-based 3D module with scene depth and query augmentation is the primary source of the improvement over the autoencoder-based 3D modules used by prior work.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the egocentric stereo 3D pose estimation line initiated by Akada et al. (UnrealEgo, ECCV 2022) by replacing their autoencoder-based 3D lifting module with a DETR-style transformer decoder that incorporates scene depth from SfM-based 3D reconstruction and temporal context via video-dependent joint query augmentation (adapted from the multi-view query augmentation of Wang et al., NeurIPS 2021). It departs from prior stereo egocentric methods (Zhao et al., Rhodin et al., Kang et al.) that either ignore scene geometry or rely on optimization-based pipelines, instead treating scene depth as a learned feature channel with explicit padding masks to handle the frequent depth failures caused by self-occlusion in compact eyeglasses setups. The use of SAM for human body segmentation to isolate background for SfM is a practical integration that prior egocentric capture systems did not employ.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The introduction of UnrealEgo2 (2.5M images, 2.8× larger than UnrealEgo) and especially UnrealEgo-RW (260k real-world images with ground-truth 3D poses) fills a critical gap: prior publicly available real-world stereo egocentric datasets were tiny (EgoCap) or not released (Zhao et al.), making rigorous real-world evaluation nearly impossible. The finding that a single-frame transformer (T=1) already outperforms the best prior autoencoder method with temporal inputs suggests the architectural shift from autoencoder to transformer decoder is the dominant factor, which may redirect future work toward attention-based lifting rather than deeper autoencoder variants. Open questions include whether the SfM-based scene reconstruction generalizes to dynamic or textureless environments, whether the approach scales to multi-person scenes, and whether real-time inference is feasible on the compact hardware the device targets.
