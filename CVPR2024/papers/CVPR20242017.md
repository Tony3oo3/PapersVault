## Problem

Existing 3D human generative methods either depend on scarce 3D scan datasets or limited 2D image collections with imbalanced viewing angles and imprecise SMPL-based pose priors, resulting in generated avatars that lack photorealism, geometric accuracy, and content diversity. The core challenge is to produce high-quality, animatable 3D humans in a zero-shot manner without directly relying on any pre-existing 3D or 2D assets, while ensuring accurate physical modeling between 3D shapes and their 2D projections.

## Input / Output

The method accepts random latent noise, free-form text prompts, or single guided images as input and produces a textured 3D human mesh aligned to an SMPL-X skeleton, an explicit UV texture map (split into 5 body parts via cylinder unwrapping), and view-consistent 360° renderings at 512² resolution. Training uses 1.4 million synthetically generated images across 7 horizontal viewpoints (0°–180°, camera radius 2.7), with the full 360° coverage achieved by horizontal flipping. The output mesh supports direct animation via the underlying skeleton and texture editing via the disentangled UV map.

## Method

En3D is a three-stage pipeline: (1) a 3D generative module (3DGM) that synthesizes view-balanced, diverse human images by projecting 3D skeletons to 2D pose conditions for a ControlNet-guided diffusion model, then trains a triplane-based GAN with patch-composed volume rendering and dual discriminators (RGB + silhouette) using the known camera parameters from synthesis; (2) a geometric sculpting module (GS) that adapts a DMTET representation to the coarse Marching Cubes mesh and refines it in ~15 s using multi-view normal constraints extracted by a pre-trained normal estimator; (3) an explicit texturing module (ET) that optimizes a UV texture map via a differentiable rasterizer with multi-view reconstruction and total-variation losses over 5 semantically partitioned body parts. The key insight is that accurate physical parameters obtained through synthetic data instantiation—rather than estimated from real images—enable realistic multi-view appearance learning, while the inference-time optimization modules bridge the gap between the coarse GAN output and high-fidelity geometry and texture.

## Results

En3D achieves a FID of 2.73 on 360° views, substantially outperforming the next-best baseline (EVA3D-syn at 6.27) and the original EVA3D (15.91) and AG3D (10.93). On geometry accuracy (Normal FID), En3D scores 5.62 versus 8.64 for AG3D-syn and 30.81 for EVA3D. The method also attains the highest IS-360 (3.43) and identity consistency (ID 0.74) among all compared methods, demonstrating superior multi-view realism and facial identity preservation.

## Baselines & Comparisons

En3D outperforms all three baselines on every reported metric. Against EVA3D [15], the improvement is most dramatic on geometry (Normal FID drops from 30.81 to 5.62) and image quality (FID from 15.91 to 2.73), while EVA3D also fails to produce reasonable 360° back-view renderings. Against AG3D [9], En3D reduces FID from 10.93 to 2.73 and Normal FID from 20.83 to 5.62, with a modest IS-360 gain (3.28 → 3.43). Even when EVA3D and AG3D are retrained on En3D's synthetic data (the '-syn' variants), En3D still leads on FID (2.73 vs. 6.27) and Normal (5.62 vs. 8.64), confirming that the advantage comes from the architecture and training pipeline rather than data alone. Against EG3D [5] (the triplane backbone), En3D improves FID from 7.48 to 2.73 and Normal from 12.74 to 5.62, showing the benefit of the human-specific synthesis flow, patch-composed rendering, and the GS/ET optimization modules.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper builds directly on EG3D's triplane-based 3D GAN architecture as its generative backbone, but departs fundamentally from prior 3D human generation methods (EVA3D, AG3D) that learn from pre-existing 2D image collections with estimated (and often inaccurate) SMPL pose and camera parameters. Instead, it introduces a synthesis-based training pipeline where a 2D diffusion model (ControlNet + Stable Diffusion) generates view-balanced, diverse human images with known physical parameters, eliminating the data scarcity and pose-estimation errors that limit prior work. Two additional optimization modules—geometric sculpting via DMTET with multi-view normal constraints and explicit UV texturing via a differentiable rasterizer—are integrated at inference time to refine shape and texture beyond what the GAN alone can produce.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The zero-shot formulation (no pre-existing 3D or 2D collections required) addresses a core bottleneck in 3D human generation: the scarcity, cost, and bias of real 3D scans and 2D photo collections. The finding that synthetic data with accurate physical parameters outperforms real data with estimated parameters (FID 2.73 vs. 4.28 in ablation) challenges the implicit assumption that real images are always superior for 3D-aware training. Limitations include dependence on the SMPL-X template topology, 512² resolution, and the quality of the underlying diffusion model; open questions include scaling to higher resolutions, more diverse body morphologies, and whether the optimization modules can be amortized into the generator for real-time generation.
