## Problem

Prior multimodal systems either bolt modality-specific encoders/decoders onto a pre-trained LLM (limiting generation to text or a single visual modality) or train from scratch but support at most two modalities. No single autoregressive model had demonstrated the ability to both understand and generate across image, text, audio, and action simultaneously, and the training instability that arises when integrating many modalities into one transformer remained unsolved.

## Input / Output

All modalities are tokenized into a shared discrete sequence: images are encoded by a ViT (concatenating 2nd and 2nd-to-last layer patch features) and generated via a dense VQ-GAN (8×8 patches, 256×256 → 1024 tokens, codebook 16512); audio is encoded by an Audio Spectrogram Transformer on up to 4.08 s spectrograms and generated via a custom ViT-VQGAN (256×128 → 512 tokens, codebook 8196); sparse structures (boxes, keypoints, 3D cuboids) are discretized into 1000 special tokens; and up to 4 image/audio history items are compressed to 32/16 tokens each via a perceiver resampler. The model outputs a single token stream that is decoded into whichever modality the task requires—free-form text, an image, an audio clip, robot actions, or dense per-pixel maps (depth, normals, segmentation).

## Method

UNIFIED-IO 2 is a 6.8 B-parameter encoder-decoder transformer trained from scratch on 600+ TB of multimodal data using a Multimodal Mixture of Denoisers objective that generalizes UL2's span-corruption/causal-LM paradigms to image and audio targets (masked denoising [R], conditional generation [S], and extreme corruption [X]). To stabilize training across heterogeneous modalities, the architecture introduces 2D rotary position embeddings, QK LayerNorm on attention logits, and scaled cosine attention within the perceiver resampler, while freezing the ViT and AST encoders during pre-training. A dynamic packing strategy—applying modality-specific encoders before packing and heuristically matching long and short examples—yields a ~4× throughput increase under Jax's fixed-tensor constraint. The model is then instruction-tuned on a mixture of 120+ datasets covering 220 tasks (60% prompted supervised data, 30% pre-training carryover, 6% synthetic task augmentation, 4% free-form chat) to acquire broad instruction-following ability across all supported modalities.

## Results

On the GRIT benchmark, UIO-2_XXL (6.8B) achieves 67.0 overall, setting a new state-of-the-art and surpassing the previous best (Unified-IO, 64.3) by 2.7 points. On vision-language tasks, it scores 79.4 on VQAv2, 90.7 on RefCOCO, 88.7 on ScienceQA, 75.9 on TallyQA, 87.7 on POPE (object hallucination), 61.8 on SEED-Bench, and 71.5 on MMBench — the highest scores among all 7B-class generalist models. On text-to-image generation (TIFA), it reaches 81.3, outperforming CoDi (71.6) and Emu (65.5) and approaching minDALL-E (79.4) and Stable Diffusion 1.5 (78.4). On text-to-audio (AudioCaps FAD), it achieves 2.64, competitive with specialist models like AudioLDM (1.96) and AudioGen (3.13).

## Baselines & Comparisons

UIO-2_XXL outperforms all generalist baselines on RefCOCO (90.7 vs. Qwen-VL 89.4, Ferret 87.5, Shikra 87.0), MMBench (71.5 vs. LLaVa-1.5 13B 67.7, mPLUG-Owl2 64.5), POPE (87.7 vs. mPLUG-Owl2 86.2), ScienceQA (88.7 vs. InstructBLIP 79.5), and TallyQA (75.9 vs. InstructBLIP 68.2). It also edges out LLaVa-1.5 13B on SEED-Bench (61.8 vs. 61.6) despite being roughly half the size. On TIFA text-to-image faithfulness, it leads CoDi by ~10 points (81.3 vs. 71.6) and Emu by ~16 points (81.3 vs. 65.5), though it trails the diffusion-based Stable Diffusion 1.5 on FID (13.39 vs. 11.26 for CoDi). It loses to LLaVa-1.5 13B on VQAv2 (79.4 vs. 80.0), to Qwen-VL and mPLUG-Owl2 on OK-VQA (55.5 vs. 58.6/57.7) and COCO Captioning (125.4 vs. 131.9/137.3), which the authors attribute to less extensive text pre-training. On audio generation, it is competitive with specialist models (FAD 2.64) but trails the latent diffusion model AudioLDM (1.96). On robotic manipulation (VIMA-Bench), it achieves 56.3% success, below VIMA's 72.6% (which uses object-centric inputs) but well above VIMA-IMG (42.5%) which uses image inputs like UIO-2.

## Relation to Prior Work

UNIFIED-IO 2 directly extends UNIFIED-IO (Lu et al., ICLR 2023), which unified vision and language in a single encoder-decoder transformer with VQ-GAN image generation, by adding audio and action modalities and scaling to 6.8B parameters. The training objective generalizes UL2's Mixture of Denoisers (Tay et al., ICLR 2023) from text-only span corruption to a multimodal setting with modality-specific denoising paradigms. Unlike the dominant paradigm of bolting modality encoders onto pre-trained LLMs (Flamingo, BLIP-2, LLaVA), this work trains a unified model from scratch, and unlike prior from-scratch multimodal models that handled at most two modalities (text+image), it covers four modalities simultaneously with a single architecture.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

This is the first autoregressive model to jointly understand and generate across image, text, audio, and action, demonstrating that a single 7B model can achieve SOTA on GRIT while also producing competitive image and audio generations — suggesting that breadth of capabilities need not come at the cost of individual task performance. Key limitations include weaker language modeling than dedicated LLMs (due to fewer pre-training tokens), FID scores trailing diffusion-based generators, and weak depth estimation. Open questions include whether the encoder-decoder architecture can be replaced by a decoder-only design (as the authors plan), whether the architectural stabilization tricks (2D RoPE, QK norm, scaled cosine attention) generalize to even larger scales, and whether the multimodal MoD objective can be further refined to better exploit cross-modal supervision.
