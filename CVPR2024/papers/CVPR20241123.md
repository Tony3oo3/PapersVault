## Problem

Existing 3D generative models are constrained to small, clean, and well-aligned datasets like ShapeNet (51K objects), limiting them to single-class generation with limited diversity. Large-scale 'in-the-wild' datasets such as Objaverse (10M+ objects) suffer from heterogeneous quality and unaligned poses, making them unusable for direct training. No prior method could train a 3D generative model end-to-end on such noisy, unaligned data at scale.

## Input / Output

The model takes a free-form text prompt (encoded by a pre-trained CLIP text encoder) and produces a 3D NeRF in a single forward pass (~12 seconds on one V100). The NeRF is parameterized by two disentangled tri-planes (geometry f_g and color f_c) at 128² resolution by default, or 512² with an optional super-resolution plug-in. Training supervision consists solely of multi-view 2D rendered images; no explicit 3D labels or point clouds are required. The geometry tri-plane can also be extracted independently and plugged into Score Distillation Sampling pipelines as a 3D geometry prior.

## Method

DIRECT-3D uses a hierarchical tri-plane diffusion architecture where a geometry denoising network generates f_g first, and a color denoising network generates f_c conditioned on both the text prompt and the generated geometry, with a NeRF auto-decoder converting the tri-planes into volume density and RGB. The key training innovation is an iterative pose-estimation loop embedded within the diffusion step: for each object, the model samples multiple candidate 3D rotations, evaluates the diffusion loss at a fixed timestep with a frozen denoiser, and updates a per-object rotation parameter toward the lowest-loss sample, thereby automatically aligning objects and filtering out noisy ones that fail to converge within 10 iterations. This 'Automatic Alignment and Cleaning' (AAC) mechanism eliminates the need for manual data curation and enables direct training on 800K+ Objaverse objects after a short warm-up on a small aligned set. A coarse-to-fine caption pipeline (DeiT classification → BLIP-2/LLaVA multi-stage QA → CLIP-based filtering) generates four granularity levels of prompts per object to improve text-3D alignment during training.

## Results

On single-class generation, DIRECT-3D achieves FID 6.90 / KID 1.84 on SRN Cars, FID 7.01 / KID 2.12 on PS Chairs, and FID 7.26 / KID 1.89 on ABO Tables, outperforming all prior methods by a large margin (e.g., reducing Table FID from 14.27 to 7.26 vs. SSDNeRF). In the text-to-3D user study on 475 prompts, 73.9% of raters prefer DIRECT-3D over Shap-E overall, with 77.1% preferring its detail and 71.6% preferring its realism. When used as a 3D geometry prior for DreamFusion, the generation success rate on animal prompts jumps from 12% to 84%, and geometry consistency improves from 16% to 84%.

## Baselines & Comparisons

DIRECT-3D dominates all four single-class baselines across every category. Against the strongest prior method SSDNeRF [10], it nearly halves FID on Cars (6.90 vs. 11.08) and Tables (7.26 vs. 14.27) while also improving KID by roughly 2×. Compared to EG3D [8] and DiffRF [43], the gains are even more dramatic (e.g., Table FID 7.26 vs. 31.18 and 27.06 respectively), and π-GAN [7] is far behind on all metrics. For large-scale text-to-3D, DIRECT-3D is preferred over Shap-E [29] by 73.9% of users in a 1425-vote MTurk study, excelling in realism (71.6%), detail (77.1%), and overall quality. Finally, when plugged into DreamFusion [47] as a 3D geometry prior, DIRECT-3D raises the animal generation success rate from 12% to 84% and geometry consistency from 16% to 84%, while also improving texture consistency from 30% to 98%.

## Relation to Prior Work

DIRECT-3D builds on the tri-plane representation (EG3D, SSDNeRF) and NeRF auto-decoder architecture, combined with a 2D latent diffusion model for conditional generation. It departs from prior 3D diffusion methods (DiffRF, SSDNeRF, triplane diffusion) that require clean, well-aligned ShapeNet-scale data, and from 2D-lifting approaches (DreamFusion, Magic3D) that avoid the data bottleneck but suffer from the Janus problem and slow per-object optimization. The key conceptual departure is introducing an iterative pose-estimation-and-filtering loop within the diffusion training step, enabling direct end-to-end training on 800K+ noisy, unaligned Objaverse assets without manual curation — a capability no prior 3D generative model possessed. The disentangled geometry/color tri-plane design also distinguishes it from prior single-tri-plane approaches, enabling the geometry branch to serve as a plug-and-play 3D prior for existing SDS-based pipelines.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The paper addresses the fundamental data bottleneck in 3D generation by showing that large-scale 'in-the-wild' assets can be used directly, removing the need for expensive manual alignment and quality filtering. The 3D geometry prior that dramatically improves DreamFusion's success rate (12%→84%) offers a practical, low-cost fix for the Janus problem that has plagued 2D-lifting methods. Open questions include whether the iterative alignment approach scales to Objaverse-XL (10M+ objects), whether the NeRF output can be converted to meshes without quality loss, and whether the disentanglement strategy generalizes to more complex scenes beyond single objects. The reliance on multi-view rendered images as supervision also limits applicability to settings where such renders are unavailable.
