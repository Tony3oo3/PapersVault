## Problem

Existing text-to-image customization methods follow a pseudo-word paradigm where subject representations and the given text share the same entire influence scope, creating a dual-optimum paradox: increasing subject similarity necessarily degrades text controllability and vice versa. Moreover, the required correspondence between pseudo-words and subjects confines methods to lengthy per-subject optimization or training on limited object datasets, precluding real-time open-domain customization.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/CVPR20242344/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method takes a single RGB image of an arbitrary subject (any category: toys, portraits, paintings, etc.) and a free-form text prompt containing a target real word (e.g., 'toy') that semantically describes the subject. It outputs a new high-quality image where the subject appears in a novel scene governed by the text, with the subject potentially varying in location, pose, and style while maintaining identity. Inference is real-time (zero test-time optimization steps) and operates in the open domain without category-specific training.

## Method

RealCustom introduces a train-inference decoupled framework built on Stable Diffusion. During training, an adaptive scoring module (two lightweight MLPs) selects a subset of key CLIP image features as visual conditions by fusing textual and visual correlation scores via a timestep-aware schedule, then injects them through a new visual cross-attention branch added alongside the existing textual cross-attention; only the scoring module and projection layers are trained while the base model stays frozen. During inference, an adaptive mask guidance strategy runs two parallel branches: a T2I branch (visual condition = 0) whose aggregated cross-attention maps of the target real word define the influence scope, and a TI2I branch (visual condition = given subject) that multiplies this scope by current generated features to compute influence quantity and by the visual cross-attention output to prevent leakage into irrelevant regions. The scope and quantity are iteratively updated across diffusion steps, progressively narrowing the real word from its general connotation to the specific subject while keeping all other regions purely text-controlled.

## Results

RealCustom achieves the best score on all four evaluation metrics while operating in real-time (0 test-time optimization steps): CLIP-T 0.3204, ImageReward 0.8703, CLIP-I 0.8552, and DINO-I 0.7865. Compared to the strongest real-time baselines (ELITE, BLIP-Diffusion), RealCustom improves CLIP-T by 8.1% and ImageReward by 223.5%, while also surpassing optimization-based methods (DreamBooth, Custom Diffusion) on similarity metrics (CLIP-I, DINO-I) without requiring any per-subject optimization. The paper highlights that existing methods are trapped in a dual-optimum paradox where improving similarity degrades controllability, whereas RealCustom breaks this trade-off.

## Baselines & Comparisons

RealCustom outperforms all five baselines on every metric. Against the optimization-based methods, it surpasses DreamBooth (the strongest on similarity with CLIP-I 0.8466, DINO-I 0.7851) by 1.0% on CLIP-I and 0.2% on DINO-I, while also achieving far better controllability (CLIP-T 0.3204 vs. 0.2783) and quality (ImageReward 0.8703 vs. 0.2393) without the 800-step per-subject optimization. Against the real-time encoder-based baselines ELITE and BLIP-Diffusion, RealCustom leads by a large margin on controllability (CLIP-T 0.3204 vs. 0.2920/0.2967) and quality (ImageReward 0.8703 vs. 0.2690/0.2172), and also wins on similarity (CLIP-I 0.8552 vs. 0.8022/0.8145; DINO-I 0.7865 vs. 0.6489/0.6486). The most striking gap is ImageReward, where RealCustom's 0.8703 exceeds the best baseline (ELITE at 0.2690) by over 220%, indicating substantially higher perceived image quality. Textual Inversion, the weakest baseline, lags far behind on all metrics and requires 5000 optimization steps.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper departs fundamentally from the dominant pseudo-word paradigm in text-to-image customization (Textual Inversion, DreamBooth, Custom Diffusion, ELITE, BLIP-Diffusion), which represents subjects as learned tokens entangled with the text prompt. Instead, RealCustom repurposes the cross-attention mechanism of Stable Diffusion to progressively narrow a real text word (e.g., 'toy') from its general connotation to a specific subject, using a train-inference decoupled framework where training learns general visual-text alignment on Laion-5B and inference applies an adaptive mask guidance strategy. Conceptually, it draws on cross-attention manipulation techniques (Prompt-to-Prompt, Attend-and-Excite) but redirects them from editing or semantic alignment toward subject-specific generation, and on classifier-free guidance by extending it to a three-branch formulation (unconditional, T2I, TI2I).

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The paper identifies and resolves what it calls the 'dual-optimum paradox'—the inherent trade-off between subject similarity and text controllability in pseudo-word methods—by precisely limiting subject influence to cross-attention-relevant regions only. This enables real-time, open-domain customization without per-subject optimization or limited-category training, which is practically significant for AIGC applications. Limitations include restriction to single-subject scenarios, dependence on the Stable Diffusion architecture, and evaluation on a relatively narrow set of metrics; open questions include whether the narrowing paradigm extends to multi-subject composition, video generation, or alternative diffusion backbones, and whether the iterative mask refinement can be accelerated beyond 50 DDIM steps.
