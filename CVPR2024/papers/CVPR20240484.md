## Problem

Existing PAN-guided DCCHI reconstruction methods rely on handcrafted prior constraints and model inter-modality correlations from a single perspective (e.g., edge alignment or content enrichment), failing to fully exploit the comprehensive spatial-semantic guidance available in the PAN image. This limits reconstruction quality on complex scenes and requires manual parameter tuning. The paper addresses this by proposing a unified framework that simultaneously leverages intra-similarity (within the PAN as a proxy for HSI) and inter-similarity (cross-modal alignment) to guide hyperspectral reconstruction.

## Input / Output

The method takes two inputs from a dual-camera system: a single CASSI compressive measurement (a 2D image formed by spatial modulation via a coded aperture and spectral modulation via a dispersion prism, with dispersion step d=2 in simulation) and a full-resolution PAN panchromatic image. The PAN image is processed once through a convolutional feature pyramid extractor to produce multi-scale guidance features [G₁, G₂, G₃] that are shared across all unrolling stages. The output is a reconstructed 3D hyperspectral cube of shape H×W×C (e.g., 256×256×28 in simulation), produced by summing the initial estimate with the final denoiser output.

## Method

In2SET is a U-shaped Transformer denoiser embedded in a PAN-guided deep unrolling (PGDU) framework that solves the MAP formulation via half-quadratic splitting: the data-fidelity subproblem is handled by conjugate gradient iterations (5 iterations chosen for speed-quality trade-off), while the prior subproblem is solved by the In2SET denoiser at each stage. Each In2AB block contains two attention mechanisms: (1) intra-similarity attention, where MHA-C captures spectral self-similarity from the HSI features and MHA-S captures spatial self-similarity using the PAN image's spatial attention as a reliable proxy (avoiding unreliable intermediate HSI estimates); and (2) inter-similarity attention via cosine similarity reweighting (CRW), which scores feature alignment between HSI and PAN to upweight regions of high cross-modal consistency. The PAN feature pyramid provides hierarchical multi-scale spatial guidance, and the entire network is trained end-to-end with L1 loss on 256×256×28 patches from the CAVE dataset.

## Results

On 10 simulated KAIST scenes, In2SET-9stg achieves the best average PSNR of 43.69 dB and SSIM of 0.990, outperforming the strongest baseline DAUHST-DC-9stg (42.62 dB / 0.987) by +1.07 dB while using fewer FLOPs (59.40 vs. 72.11 GFLOPs). The lighter In2SET-3stg variant (20.79 GFLOPs) matches DAUHST-DC-5stg (41.27 dB) at only 51.3% of the computational cost. In2SET-2stg (14.35 GFLOPs) already surpasses all end-to-end Transformer baselines (MST-DC, MST++-DC, CST-DC, HDNet-DC) in both PSNR and SSIM.

## Baselines & Comparisons

In2SET-9stg wins on every one of the 10 test scenes against all 9 baseline families, achieving an average PSNR of 43.69 dB versus 42.62 dB for the strongest competitor DAUHST-DC-9stg (+1.07 dB) and 39.03 dB for MST++-DC (+4.66 dB). At comparable computational budgets, In2SET-3stg (20.79 GFLOPs) slightly edges out DAUHST-DC-3stg (24.70 GFLOPs) by +0.40 dB while using ~16% fewer FLOPs, and matches DAUHST-DC-5stg (41.27 dB) at only 51.3% of its cost. The lightweight In2SET-2stg (14.35 GFLOPs) already surpasses HDNet-DC (144.31 GFLOPs) by +2.50 dB, demonstrating a 10× efficiency advantage. Against model-based methods (TV-DC, PIDS-DC, PFusion-RGB), In2SET gains 3–10 dB in PSNR, confirming the superiority of learned PAN-guided priors over handcrafted constraints. On real-world Ninja data, In2SET visually outperforms all compared methods in spatial-spectral fidelity across four spectral bands.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the deep-unrolling paradigm for spectral compressive imaging (DNU, DAUHST, RDLUF) by introducing a PAN-guided unrolling framework (PGDU) that incorporates a multi-scale feature pyramid from the panchromatic camera as structural guidance at every unrolling stage. It departs from prior PAN-guided methods (ANSR, PFusion, PIDS) that model inter-modality correlation from a single perspective (edges, content) by proposing a dual-similarity mechanism: intra-similarity extracted from the PAN image serves as a proxy for HSI spatial self-similarity, while inter-similarity via cosine similarity reweighting (CRW) aligns HSI and PAN features semantically. The attention architecture builds directly on the spectral-wise multi-head attention (MHA-C) from MST, repurposing it within a U-shaped denoiser that also introduces a spatial cross-attention branch (MHA-S) conditioned on PAN features.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The efficiency story is the most practically relevant contribution: In2SET-3stg matches DAUHST-5stg in reconstruction quality while using only ~51% of the FLOPs, suggesting that the dual-similarity mechanism provides a stronger per-stage prior than simply adding more unrolling iterations. The validation that PAN intra-spatial correlation closely approximates HSI intra-spatial correlation (Table 2) provides empirical grounding for the proxy assumption, though it is tested on only three datasets. Open questions include whether the PAN-as-proxy assumption degrades for scenes with strong spectral-spatial decorrelation (e.g., fluorescence or multi-material scenes), and whether the CRW mechanism generalizes to other dual-modality imaging tasks beyond DCCHI.
