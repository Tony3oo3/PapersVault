## Problem

Capsule Networks suffer from persistent capsule redundancy that previous pruning methods only address at the primary layer, while dynamic routing's fully connected structure and non-orthogonal weight matrices reintroduce redundancy in deeper layers. Additionally, dynamic routing requires multiple iterations to converge, further inflating computational cost. The paper asks whether redundancy can be systematically eliminated across all layers while simultaneously removing the iterative routing bottleneck.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/CVPR20242297/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The model accepts RGB images at dataset-native resolutions (28×28 for MNIST/Fashion-MNIST, 32×32 for CIFAR-10/100/SVHN, 48×48 for smallNORB after resizing from 96×96 and cropping) and outputs a single class label via a flat capsule layer. SmallNORB is the only dataset requiring preprocessing; all others retain original sizes. Training uses margin loss without reconstruction loss, and the model is evaluated on clean accuracy, FLOPS, parameter count, and PGD adversarial robustness.

## Method

OrthCaps introduces three coupled mechanisms: (1) a pruned capsule layer that orders capsules by L2-norm importance and removes those exceeding a cosine-similarity threshold (θ=0.7) relative to more active capsules; (2) a non-iterative orthogonal sparse attention routing that replaces dynamic routing, using α-Entmax (α=1.5) over QK^T/√d to produce sparse coupling coefficients and eliminating the iterative convergence loop; (3) Householder orthogonal decomposition to parameterize W_Q, W_K, W_V as products of Householder reflections (W = ∏(I − 2b_i b_i^T/‖b_i‖²)), guaranteeing strict orthogonality during training without penalty terms. Two variants are provided: OrthCaps-S (shallow, ~105K params, simplified single-matrix attention routing) and OrthCaps-D (deep, 7 residual capsule blocks with depthwise convolutions, ~574K params). The orthogonality preserves inter-capsule angles through routing, preventing the similarity drift that non-orthogonal matrices would otherwise introduce in deeper layers.

## Results

OrthCaps-S achieves 99.68% on MNIST, 96.26% on SVHN, 98.30% on smallNORB, and 86.84% on CIFAR-10 using only 105.5K parameters (1.25% of standard CapsNet's 8.4M) and 673.1M FLOPS. OrthCaps-D reaches 90.56% on CIFAR-10, 70.56% on CIFAR-100, 99.59% on MNIST, and 94.60% on Fashion-MNIST with 574K parameters and 3,345M FLOPS. Under PGD adversarial attack on CIFAR-10, OrthCaps achieves 23.52% post-attack accuracy versus 14.62% for CapsNet and 0.35% for a baseline CNN.

## Baselines & Comparisons

OrthCaps-S outperforms all eight shallow baselines on every dataset. The most striking gap is on CIFAR-10, where OrthCaps-S (86.84%) surpasses CapsNet (68.72%) by 18.1 points while using 80× fewer parameters, and beats the closest competitor DA-CapsNet (85.47%) by 1.37 points with 66× fewer parameters. On SVHN, OrthCaps-S leads DA-CapsNet (94.82%) by 1.44 points and Efficient-Caps (93.12%) by 3.14 points. For the deep variant, OrthCaps-D (574K params) beats AR-CapsNet-7 (6.3M) by 1.62 points on CIFAR-10 and 14.03 points on CIFAR-100, and outperforms Inverted Dot-Product (1.4M) on all four datasets. OrthCaps-D trails DeepCaps (13.5M) by 0.45 points on CIFAR-10 (90.56 vs. 91.01) but exceeds it on CIFAR-100 (70.56 vs. 69.72) with 23.4× fewer parameters. Against standard CNNs, OrthCaps-D uses only 1.41% of ResNet-18's parameters and 0.11% of VGG-16's while remaining competitive on CIFAR-10/100.

## Relation to Prior Work

OrthCaps extends the CapsNet lineage (Sabour et al., 2017) by replacing its iterative dynamic routing with a non-iterative orthogonal sparse attention routing mechanism, drawing on the feed-forward attention routing concept from Choi et al. (2019) and the α-Entmax sparsification from sparse Transformer work (Correia et al., 2019). It departs from prior capsule pruning efforts (Jeong et al., Sharifi et al., Renzulli et al.), which addressed only primary-layer redundancy, by identifying that non-orthogonal routing weight matrices reintroduce redundancy in deeper layers and proposing Householder orthogonal decomposition (Uhlig, 2001) as a penalty-free fix. The orthogonality idea is borrowed from orthogonal CNNs (Wang et al., 2020) and orthogonal Transformers (Huang et al., 2022), but its application to capsule routing weight matrices via Householder products is new to the capsule literature.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The 1.25% parameter footprint of OrthCaps-S while matching or exceeding CapsNet accuracy on four datasets is a striking efficiency result for the capsule network community, and the Householder orthogonalization offers a drop-in, penalty-free technique potentially transferable to other architectures. However, all evaluations are confined to small-to-medium benchmarks (MNIST, CIFAR-10/100, SVHN, smallNORB, FashionMNIST); the deep variant still trails ResNet-18 by ~5 points on CIFAR-10, and no ImageNet-scale or video/3D evaluation is provided. Open questions include whether the pruning-plus-orthogonality strategy scales to high-resolution or multi-class (thousands of classes) settings, and whether the fixed α-Entmax sparsity level (α=1.5) remains optimal as network depth or input complexity grows.
