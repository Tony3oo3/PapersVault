## Problem

Most 3D diffusion models target single objects or synthetic scenes, leaving real-world outdoor scene generation largely unaddressed. Real-outdoor datasets (e.g., SemanticKITTI) contain substantially more empty space than synthetic counterparts due to sensor limitations such as limited range, occlusions, and restricted field-of-view, which causes voxel-based diffusion approaches to struggle with learning meaningful scene distributions.

## Input / Output

The method consumes voxelized 3D semantic scenes (256×256×32 for SemanticKITTI with 20 classes, 128×128×8 for CarlaSC with 11 classes) and produces categorical voxel grids decoded from a generated triplane via an implicit MLP, enabling resolution-independent output (e.g., 1024×1024×128 from a 256×256×32 training resolution). For downstream tasks, a binary spatial trimask over triplane space controls inpainting/outpainting regions, and an SSC model's prediction is encoded into triplane form to condition the refinement process. Outpainting extends a 256×256×32 scene to 1792×3328×32 without additional training.

## Method

A triplane autoencoder compresses a voxelized scene into three orthogonal 2D feature planes (xy, xz, yz) via 3D convolutions and axis-wise average pooling, then reconstructs semantic labels through an implicit MLP decoder with sinusoidal positional encoding, trained with weighted cross-entropy and Lovász-softmax losses. A DDPM diffusion model (x0-parameterization, 100 steps) is trained on the triplane space to generate novel triplanes, which are decoded into 3D semantic scenes. The key insight is that triplane factorization reduces the representation of empty air volumes prevalent in real-outdoor data, making the diffusion target more informative. Triplane manipulation during denoising—overriding masked regions with known triplane samples (RePaint-inspired) for inpainting/outpainting, or concatenating an SSC-encoded triplane for refinement—enables practical downstream tasks without retraining the diffusion model.

## Results

On SemanticKITTI, SemCity achieves a FID of 56.55, KID of 0.04, IS of 3.25, Precision of 0.39, and Recall of 0.32, substantially outperforming the voxel-based SSD baseline (FID 112.82, KID 0.12, IS 2.23, Precision 0.01, Recall 0.08). On CarlaSC, SemCity reaches FID 40.63, KID 0.02, IS 3.51, Precision 0.31, and Recall 0.09 versus SSD's FID 87.39, KID 0.09, IS 2.44, Precision 0.14, and Recall 0.07. For SSC refinement on SemanticKITTI, SemCity boosts IoU by 13.3 points over MonoScene (37.12→50.44) and 9.0 points over SCPNet (50.24→59.25), with consistent mIoU gains across all four SSC baselines.

## Baselines & Comparisons

For scene generation, SemCity dominates the sole baseline SSD [22] on every metric across both datasets: FID is halved (56.55 vs 112.82 on SemanticKITTI; 40.63 vs 87.39 on CarlaSC), Precision improves by ~39× on SemanticKITTI (0.39 vs 0.01) and ~2.2× on CarlaSC (0.31 vs 0.14), and Recall rises from 0.08 to 0.32 on SemanticKITTI. The paper attributes SSD's weakness to its voxel-based representation struggling with the large empty spaces inherent in real-outdoor data. For SSC refinement, SemCity consistently improves all four baselines: MonoScene [9] gains +13.3 IoU and +5.58 mIoU; OccDepth [29] gains +8.6 IoU and +3.95 mIoU; SSA-SC [51] gains +2.46 IoU and +1.04 mIoU; and SCPNet [49] gains +9.01 IoU and +0.64 mIoU. The largest absolute gains appear for RGB-based methods (MonoScene, OccDepth), which suffer most from geometric-semantic discrepancies when projecting 2D features into 3D space.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the authors' own prior voxel-based scene diffusion work (SSD [22]) by replacing the 3D voxel representation with a triplane factorization [10], motivated by the prevalence of empty space in real-outdoor LiDAR data. The diffusion backbone follows standard DDPM [16] with x0-parameterization, and the inpainting/outpainting strategy adapts RePaint's resampling logic [27] to the triplane domain. The SSC refinement application borrows the DDPS conditional sampling idea [21], concatenating the SSC prediction's triplane as a condition. Conceptually, the key departure from prior scene diffusion is the shift from explicit volumetric representations to a compact 2D-factorized proxy, which the authors argue is better suited to the sparse, sensor-limited nature of real outdoor captures.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The paper addresses a practical gap: real-outdoor 3D scene generation has been largely neglected in favor of synthetic or single-object settings, and the triplane manipulation framework provides a unified mechanism for inpainting, outpainting, and SSC refinement without retraining. The consistent IoU/mIoU gains across four diverse SSC backbones (MonoScene, OccDepth, SSA-SC, SCPNet) suggest the learned 3D prior is broadly applicable for autonomous driving pipelines. However, limitations remain: occluded building rears, truncated vertical structures, and motion traces from frame-merged ground truth all constrain fidelity, and the city-scale outpainting, while visually impressive, lacks quantitative evaluation. Open questions include whether incorporating explicit city layout priors or multi-sensor fusion could resolve the occlusion and height issues.
