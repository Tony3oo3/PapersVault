## Problem

Existing single-image 360-degree novel view synthesis methods are designed for isolated objects with masked backgrounds, using 3DoF camera representations that cannot express arbitrary scene poses. For in-the-wild scenes with complex multi-object backgrounds, both the camera conditioning and the SDS distillation procedure fail: camera representations are either ambiguous or inexpressive, and SDS mode collapse produces monotone, low-diversity backgrounds in unobserved regions.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/CVPR20240283/cropped_page1_idx1.webp)

## Input / Output

The method takes a single RGB image (256×256 for general scenes, 400×300 for DTU) and produces a 3D NeRF (combining Mip-NeRF 360 features with Instant-NGP hash encoding) that can be rendered from arbitrary viewpoints up to 360 degrees. During training, depth maps from COLMAP or ORB-SLAM and camera extrinsics are used to construct the conditioning signal, but at inference only the input image is required; the scale parameter q_i is chosen heuristically. The diffusion model is initialized from pretrained Zero-1-to-3-XL with the conditioning module swapped out.

## Method

ZeroNVS introduces a 6DoF+1 camera conditioning representation (relative pose plus field of view) with a viewer-centric normalization scheme that scales camera translations by the 20th quantile of the input view's depth map, resolving scale ambiguity across heterogeneous datasets (CO3D, RealEstate10K, ACID) without aggregating over all views. The diffusion model is trained on this mixed dataset to predict novel views conditioned on the input image and the camera embedding. For 3D distillation, the paper proposes SDS anchoring: k novel views are first sampled via DDIM at evenly spaced azimuths, and during SDS optimization the diffusion model is conditioned on the nearest anchor view (input or sampled) rather than always the input, which preserves background diversity while maintaining 3D consistency.

## Results

On the DTU benchmark, ZeroNVS achieves a state-of-the-art LPIPS of 0.380 in the zero-shot setting, outperforming all baselines that were directly trained on DTU (NeRDi: 0.421, DietNeRF: 0.487, SinNeRF: 0.525). On the newly proposed Mip-NeRF 360 single-image NVS benchmark, ZeroNVS achieves the best LPIPS of 0.625, surpassing Zero-1-to-3 (0.667) and PixelNeRF (0.718). A user study with 21 participants shows SDS anchoring is preferred over standard SDS on Realism (78%), Creativity (82%), and Overall Preference (80%).

## Baselines & Comparisons

ZeroNVS achieves the best LPIPS across all comparisons, which the authors emphasize as the most perceptually meaningful metric for view synthesis. On DTU, ZeroNVS (LPIPS 0.380) beats NeRDi (0.421), DietNeRF (0.487), SinNeRF (0.525), PixelNeRF (0.535), and DS-NeRF (0.649), despite being the only method not fine-tuned on DTU. However, ZeroNVS trails on PSNR and SSIM: SinNeRF achieves the highest PSNR (16.52 vs. 13.55) and SSIM (0.560 vs. 0.469), and PixelNeRF also leads on both (PSNR 15.55, SSIM 0.537). Against Zero-1-to-3 (retrained on the same mixture data), ZeroNVS wins on all three metrics on both DTU (LPIPS 0.380 vs. 0.472, PSNR 13.55 vs. 10.70, SSIM 0.469 vs. 0.383) and Mip-NeRF 360 (LPIPS 0.625 vs. 0.667). The authors note that PSNR/SSIM can be misleading for NVS due to misalignment issues (Figure 7), and that the LPIPS gap reflects better semantic and perceptual quality of synthesized views.

## Relation to Prior Work

ZeroNVS extends the two-stage pipeline of Zero-1-to-3 (train a 2D conditional diffusion model, then distill a NeRF via SDS) from object-centric to scene-centric novel view synthesis. The key departures are: (1) replacing Zero-1-to-3's 3DoF camera parameterization (elevation, azimuth, radius) with a 6DoF+1 relative-pose representation and a viewer-centric depth-based normalization scheme, enabling training on heterogeneous in-the-wild scene data (CO3D, RealEstate10K, ACID) with arbitrary COLMAP/ORB-SLAM poses; and (2) introducing 'SDS anchoring,' which uses DDIM-sampled pseudo-ground-truth views as diverse conditioning signals during SDS optimization to mitigate the mode-collapse that standard SDS exhibits on complex backgrounds. The method is initialized from the pretrained Zero-1-to-3-XL diffusion backbone and uses a ThreeStudio-based NeRF with Instant-NGP for the 3D representation.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

ZeroNVS is the first method to demonstrate that SDS-based distillation can lift full in-the-wild scenes (with complex, multi-object backgrounds) to 3D from a single image, and it sets a new zero-shot LPIPS SOTA on DTU while outperforming methods fine-tuned on that dataset. The introduction of Mip-NeRF 360 as a single-image NVS benchmark addresses a real gap, since DTU's forward-facing camera rig cannot evaluate the large viewpoint changes that are the core challenge of scene-level NVS. Open questions include whether SDS anchoring can be formalized more rigorously (it is currently a heuristic), whether the approach scales to higher resolutions beyond 256×256, and whether the diversity gains generalize to quantitative metrics beyond user studies. The reliance on SDS also inherits its slow optimization cost, limiting practical deployment.
