## Problem

Existing StyleGAN compression methods (CAGAN, StyleKD) that combine channel pruning with distillation consistently degrade sample diversity (recall) in the compressed student relative to the teacher, even when the mapping network is preserved. The root cause is that prior pruning criteria ignore how individual channels respond to latent vector variations, which is the mechanism underlying generative diversity.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/CVPR20242649/cropped_page1_idx1.webp)

## Input / Output

The method operates on a pre-trained StyleGAN2 (or StyleGAN3) teacher generator and produces a compressed student generator with a 70% channel pruning ratio applied to the synthesis network (e.g., 30.0M→5.6M params at 256×256, 49.1M→9.2M at 1024×1024). At inference, the student takes a 512-dim latent vector z ~ N(0,1) through the retained mapping network and pruned synthesis network to produce an RGB image at the same resolution as the teacher. The pruning itself requires no additional training data or supervision—only forward and backward passes through the frozen teacher.

## Method

The core contribution is a diversity-sensitive channel importance score computed via latent perturbation-induced gradients. For each latent vector w, the method samples N directional vectors d (from GANSpace PCA or random N(0,1)), computes the L1 image difference between g(w) and g(w+αd), and backpropagates this difference to obtain per-weight gradient magnitudes. The channel score S^σ(c) is defined as the L1 norm of the variance of these gradient magnitudes over perturbations (i.e., subtracting the per-latent-vector mean gradient offset before squaring), which suppresses channels that are uniformly active regardless of perturbation and highlights channels selectively sensitive to specific semantic directions. Channels with the lowest scores are pruned to initialize the student, which is then fine-tuned using StyleKD's four-objective distillation loss (adversarial, RGB, LPIPS, and latent-direction relation losses). This variance-based scoring outperforms a simple average-based score S^μ by 0.36 FID points on FFHQ-256 and enables the compressed model to reach prior state-of-the-art FID with only half the training iterations.

## Results

On FFHQ-256, the proposed method achieves FID 6.35 with Recall 0.339, outperforming CAGAN* (FID 7.41, R 0.309) and StyleKD* (FID 7.47, R 0.306). On FFHQ-1024, it reaches FID 5.80 and Recall 0.378, improving 1.8 and 1.39 FID points over CAGAN and StyleKD respectively. On LSUN Church and LSUN Horse, the method achieves FID 4.87 (R 0.372) and FID 5.11 (R 0.356), again leading all baselines on both FID and Recall. The method also converges 2× faster, reaching prior SOTA FID with half the training iterations.

## Baselines & Comparisons

The paper compares against three baselines: CAGAN (content-aware pruning + distillation), StyleKD (random synthesis init + latent-direction distillation), and GAN Slimming (GS, unified optimization). The proposed method consistently wins on FID across all four datasets (FFHQ-256, FFHQ-1024, LSUN Church, LSUN Horse), with the largest margin over CAGAN on FFHQ-1024 (5.80 vs 7.60). On Recall (diversity), the advantage is even more pronounced: 0.378 vs 0.277 on FFHQ-1024 and 0.372 vs 0.302 on LSUN Church, confirming the diversity-preserving motivation. On Precision, the method is slightly lower than baselines without the truncation trick (e.g., 0.706 vs 0.713 on FFHQ-256), but applying ψ=0.95 recovers or exceeds baseline precision (0.732) while maintaining competitive recall. Against GAN Slimming, the improvement is substantial (FID 6.35 vs 12.4 on FFHQ-256). The method also converges 2× faster than StyleKD and 2.5× faster than CAGAN to reach equivalent FID, attributed to the improved initialization from diversity-aware pruning.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper sits within the two-stage StyleGAN compression pipeline (channel pruning followed by distillation) established by CAGAN and StyleKD, and adopts StyleKD's distillation objectives (adversarial, RGB, LPIPS, and latent-direction losses) as its training backbone. Its key departure is in the pruning criterion: rather than pruning based on content-aware activation (CAGAN) or randomly reinitializing the synthesis network (StyleKD), it scores channels by their sensitivity to latent vector perturbations, directly targeting the diversity-preserving capacity of the generator. The perturbation directions are drawn from GANSpace-style PCA, repurposing an interpretability tool as a pruning signal.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The paper identifies and addresses a concrete, previously under-diagnosed failure mode in GAN compression: the synthesis network's diversity (recall) degrades substantially even when the mapping network is preserved, and the root cause is the pruning/initialization strategy rather than the distillation stage. The practical payoff is a 2× reduction in training iterations to reach prior SOTA FID, which is meaningful for practitioners with limited compute. Open questions include whether the latent-perturbation sensitivity criterion generalizes to conditional GANs, diffusion-based generators, or architectures where the latent-to-image mapping is not a single synthesis network, and whether the fixed 0.7 pruning ratio studied here is optimal across different compression budgets.
