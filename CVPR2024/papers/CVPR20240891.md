## Problem

Existing co-speech motion generation methods face a fundamental trade-off: deterministic approaches (e.g., Habibie et al.) produce coordinated but repetitive motions, while probabilistic methods that model face and body separately (e.g., TalkShow) gain diversity but lose whole-body coordination. No prior work simultaneously addresses both variability and coordination for holistic (face + hands + body) co-speech motion generation.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/CVPR20240891/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method takes speech audio encoded by wav2vec 2.0 into 768-dimensional feature sequences, optionally augmented with motion context frames (for completion/editing) and a speaker identity embedding (via AdaIN for style consistency). The output is a sequence of N frames, each a 376-dimensional vector of 6D SMPL-X parameters covering jaw pose, body pose, hand pose, and facial expression, generated with a window size of 8 frames.

## Method

ProbTalk is a three-stage pipeline built on a PQ-VAE backbone. First, a product-quantized VAE partitions the holistic motion latent space into G=4 subspaces, each quantized by a dedicated codebook of K=128 codes, yielding K^G ≈ 268M combinations versus only K×G for standard VQ-VAE, dramatically reducing quantization error. Second, a MaskGIT-style non-autoregressive Transformer predictor generates all PQ codes in parallel and iteratively refines them over T=8 steps, with a novel 2D positional encoding (sum of temporal sine/cosine encoding and subspace encoding) that preserves the two-dimensional structure of time × subspace in the linearized code sequence. Third, a deterministic sequence-to-sequence Refiner sharpens high-frequency details, particularly fast facial transitions, by conditioning on the preliminary motion, audio, mask, and identity. The three components are trained sequentially under supervised learning with L1 reconstruction, velocity, and commitment losses for the PQ-VAE, and cross-entropy for the predictor.

## Results

On the SHOW dataset, ProbTalk achieves the best FGD (Holistic) of 3.98, substantially outperforming TalkShow (6.60) and Habibie et al. (44.60). It also attains the lowest FGD (Face) of 5.59 and FGD (Body) of 5.21, while maintaining a competitive inference speed of 1067 FPS (vs. 205 FPS for TalkShow). In terms of diversity, ProbTalk achieves Var (Face) of 0.17 and Var (Body) of 0.26, demonstrating both realistic and varied motion generation.

## Baselines & Comparisons

ProbTalk decisively outperforms both external baselines on all FGD metrics. Against Habibie et al. [21] (deterministic regression), ProbTalk reduces FGD (Holistic) from 44.60 to 3.98 (an ~11× improvement) and FGD (Face) from 43.66 to 5.59, while also introducing non-zero motion diversity (Var Face 0.17 vs. 0). Against TalkShow [59] (separate probabilistic body + deterministic face), ProbTalk lowers FGD (Holistic) from 6.60 to 3.98 and FGD (Face) from 7.69 to 5.59, while achieving 5× higher inference speed (1067 vs. 205 FPS) and non-zero facial diversity (Var Face 0.17 vs. 0). Internally, ProbTalk also beats the 'Separate' baseline (4.47 FGD Holistic) and the 'Simultaneous-P' baseline (4.76 FGD Holistic, 15.49 FGD Face), confirming that the joint probabilistic modeling plus the Refiner stage are both critical for holistic realism.

## Relation to Prior Work

ProbTalk extends the VQ-VAE line of discrete representation learning by replacing single-codebook vector quantization with product quantization (PQ), drawing on classical PQ theory (Gray, 1984) and its VAE adaptation (Wu & Flierl, 2019) to handle the higher-dimensional holistic motion space. For generation, it adapts the MaskGIT non-autoregressive paradigm (Chang et al., CVPR 2022) from image tokens to structured motion codes, adding a 2D positional encoding to preserve the temporal × subspace structure that PQ introduces. Conceptually, it departs from TalkShow (Yi et al., CVPR 2023), which models face deterministically and body probabilistically in separate branches, by unifying all body parts under a single probabilistic framework, and from Habibie et al. (IVA 2021), which generates holistic motion deterministically, by introducing explicit variability through the VAE latent space.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

This is the first framework to jointly optimize for both variability (diverse motions for the same speech) and coordination (harmonious alignment across face, hands, and body) in co-speech motion generation, addressing a gap where prior methods sacrificed one for the other. The PQ-VAE + MaskGIT + 2D-PE recipe offers a practical template for efficient non-autoregressive generation of complex structured data beyond co-speech motion. However, evaluation is confined to the SHOW dataset (4 speakers, 26.9 hours), and only two external baselines are compared, leaving open questions about generalization to more diverse speaker populations, longer sequences, and real-time deployment. The Refiner's reliance on a deterministic sequence-to-sequence model also raises questions about whether the high-frequency details it adds could alternatively be captured within the probabilistic stage.
