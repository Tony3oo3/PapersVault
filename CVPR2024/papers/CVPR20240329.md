## Problem

Existing avatar reconstruction methods focus on recovering 3D geometry but do not produce the complete set of simulation-ready assets—2D garment patterns, cloth material parameters, and accurate body shape—required for physics-based cloth simulation. Manually creating and calibrating these assets is prohibitively expensive for individualized avatars, and no prior method jointly optimizes garment design, body shape, and material properties in a physically grounded manner from a single noisy scan.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/CVPR20240329/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The pipeline consumes 18 multi-view images from a 3dMD capture system, reconstructs a noisy 3D scan (containing holes and missing regions), segments the target garment via per-view semantic segmentation with majority-vote consistency, and selects a garment template from a minimal library based on the identified garment type. The output is a simulation-ready 2D garment pattern (developable, with matched seam lengths), SMPL body shape and pose coefficients, cloth material parameters (bending and stretching compliance), and a 3D draped garment mesh with valid topology suitable for downstream dynamic simulation.

## Method

DiffAvatar performs a unified gradient-based co-optimization of garment 2D pattern shape, body shape/pose, and cloth material parameters by running differentiable XPBD cloth simulation inside the optimization loop. The key architectural contribution is a differentiable control cage representation: a small set of 2D boundary handles (selected via convex hull and curvature criteria) deforms the full pattern through Mean Value Coordinates, regularizing the high-dimensional pattern space and preventing non-physical inverted rest shapes. Gradients flow through the XPBD solver via the adjoint method (DiffXPBD), with the body influencing the garment solely through differentiable collision constraints, enabling joint recovery of body shape and garment drape. The loss combines boundary L2 matching, interior Chamfer distance, seam-length consistency, and boundary-curvature regularization to produce manufacturable, simulation-ready patterns that match the scan's overall fit and style without requiring exact wrinkle reproduction.

## Results

On the dress evaluation, DiffAvatar achieves the best Chamfer Distance against both the 3D scan (CD = 1.311) and the artist-made ground truth (CD = 1.688), outperforming all baselines. Against the artist ground truth, DiffAvatar also attains the best perceptual metrics: LPIPS = 0.085 and SSIM = 0.893. Critically, DiffAvatar is the only method producing simulation-ready mesh quality (min conditioning = 0.143), while all three baselines (PiFU-HD, Diffusion+NeuS, PoP) yield a minimum mesh quality of 0.000, indicating degenerate triangles unsuitable for physics simulation.

## Baselines & Comparisons

DiffAvatar outperforms all four baselines across every reported metric. Against PiFU-HD [47], DiffAvatar reduces CD by ~32% (1.311 vs. 1.930 vs. scan; 1.688 vs. 2.009 vs. artist) and improves SSIM by 6–10 points. Against Diffusion+NeuS [57], the gap is even larger, with DiffAvatar halving the CD (1.311 vs. 3.410 vs. scan). Against PoP [37], the closest 3D-scan-based competitor, DiffAvatar still leads on CD (1.311 vs. 1.695 vs. scan; 1.688 vs. 1.866 vs. artist) and SSIM (0.842 vs. 0.831 vs. scan; 0.893 vs. 0.842 vs. artist). The most decisive advantage is mesh quality: all three 3D baselines produce a minimum triangle conditioning of 0.000 (degenerate/zero-area triangles), making their outputs unusable for cloth simulation, whereas DiffAvatar maintains a minimum of 0.143, comparable to the artist-made reference (0.171). For 2D pattern quality, DiffAvatar's optimized patterns closely match artist-designed patterns, while NeuralTailor [25] misses key structural features such as shirt sleeves.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the differentiable simulation lineage (DiffXPBD, DiffCloth) from material and shape estimation to full garment pattern optimization in 2D rest-shape space, a step not previously attempted. It departs from geometry-reconstruction methods (PiFUHD, PoP, NeuralTailor) that produce meshes without simulation-ready topology or physical parameters, and from prior pattern-estimation work that relies on flattening or learned networks without physical grounding. The control cage formulation using Mean Value Coordinates to regularize 2D pattern optimization is a novel contribution that bridges the gap between unconstrained vertex optimization and physically meaningful garment design.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

DiffAvatar addresses a practical bottleneck in telepresence and avatar applications: the labor-intensive manual creation of simulation-ready garment assets. By recovering 2D patterns, material parameters, and body shape from a single noisy scan in a unified differentiable simulation loop, it makes personalized avatar clothing scalable. Key limitations include reliance on quasi-equilibrium states (limiting dynamic garment recovery), inability to handle discrete topology changes or multi-layered occluded clothing, and computational cost (20–200 minutes per garment). Open questions include extending to dynamic sequence matching, handling complex multi-layer outfits, and reducing optimization time through GPU acceleration.
