## Problem

Existing diffusion model-based style transfer methods either require time-consuming per-style optimization (fine-tuning or textual inversion) or, in the training-free case (e.g., DiffStyle), fail to function correctly on large-scale latent diffusion models like Stable Diffusion. This prevents users from leveraging the strong generative priors of large-scale pre-trained diffusion models for high-quality, optimization-free style transfer.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/CVPR20242294/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method takes a content image and a style image (both center-cropped to 512×512 RGB) and produces a single stylized image that preserves the content's spatial layout while adopting the style's texture and color tone. No text prompt is required (an empty string is used as the condition). The entire pipeline runs in approximately 12.4 seconds per pair on a single TITAN RTX GPU (8.2 s for DDIM inversion + 4.2 s for 50-step DDIM sampling), making it roughly 30× faster than optimization-based diffusion baselines.

## Method

The core mechanism is attention-based style injection: during the reverse DDIM process of the stylized latent, the key and value vectors of the self-attention (SA) layers are replaced with those collected from the style image at the same timestep, while the query is blended between the content and stylized latents (query preservation, controlled by γ) to retain spatial structure. This substitution is applied only to the latter 7 of 12 decoder layers, which govern local texture. Two auxiliary components address failure modes: (1) attention temperature scaling multiplies the pre-softmax attention scores by τ > 1 to counteract the blurring caused by cross-image key substitution, and (2) initial latent AdaIN constructs the starting noise by modulating the channel-wise mean and standard deviation of the content latent to match those of the style latent, thereby transferring color tone without altering spatial layout. The entire procedure is training-free and requires no text supervision.

## Results

On the MSCOCO–WikiArt style transfer benchmark (800 stylized images), the proposed method achieves the best scores across all four metrics: ArtFID 28.80, FID 18.13, LPIPS 0.506, and CFSD 0.228. The closest conventional baseline (AdaAttn) trails by 1.55 in ArtFID and 0.038 in LPIPS, while the strongest diffusion-based baseline (DiffStyle) is outperformed by 12.66 in ArtFID and 0.388 in LPIPS. Inference time is 12.4 s per content–style pair on a single TITAN RTX, roughly 29–66× faster than other diffusion-based methods (355.9–816.9 s).

## Baselines & Comparisons

The proposed method outperforms all 13 baselines on every reported metric (ArtFID, FID, LPIPS, CFSD). Among conventional style transfer methods, the closest competitor is AdaAttn (ArtFID 30.35 vs. 28.80; LPIPS 0.544 vs. 0.506), while MAST achieves a slightly higher FID (18.20 vs. 18.13) but is substantially worse on content fidelity (LPIPS 0.629). Against diffusion-based baselines, the margins are much larger: DiffStyle (ArtFID 41.46, LPIPS 0.893) and InST (ArtFID 40.63, LPIPS 0.800) both suffer severe content disruption, and DiffuseIT (ArtFID 40.72, FID 23.07) fails to transfer style effectively. In terms of speed, the proposed method requires only 12.4 s per pair (8.2 s DDIM inversion + 4.2 s sampling), compared to 355.9 s for DiffStyle, 792.8 s for DiffuseIT, and 816.9 s for InST, because it leverages features collected during a short DDIM inversion rather than requiring full optimization or long sampling schedules.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper repurposes the key-value substitution paradigm from Prompt-to-Prompt's cross-attention editing [14] and Plug-and-play's self-attention layout control [41], but applies it to self-attention layers with style image features as the 'condition' rather than text prompts. It departs from prior diffusion-based style transfer (InST [53], StyleDiffusion [45]) by eliminating all gradient-based optimization, and from DiffStyle [18] by operating within the latent space of large-scale text-to-image models like Stable Diffusion where DiffStyle's h-space manipulation breaks down. The three auxiliary components (query preservation, attention temperature scaling, initial latent AdaIN) address specific failure modes of the naive K/V swap, drawing on AdaIN [17] for color correction and attention sharpening for structural fidelity.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The training-free, 12.4-second inference (vs. 355–817 s for other diffusion-based methods) makes high-quality artistic style transfer on large-scale pre-trained DMs practically deployable, removing the per-style optimization bottleneck that has limited adoption of diffusion-based style transfer. The interpretable γ parameter for content–style trade-off offers user control absent in most prior methods. However, the approach is validated only on Stable Diffusion 1.4 at 512×512 with a specific MSCOCO/WikiArt pairing, leaving open whether the self-attention manipulation generalizes to video diffusion, higher resolutions, or non-artistic style domains. The finding that self-attention features have negligible effect on color tone (necessitating the AdaIN workaround) also hints at a deeper limitation in how attention-based editing captures global appearance statistics.
