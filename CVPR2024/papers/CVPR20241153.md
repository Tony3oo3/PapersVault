## Problem

Multi-view diffusion models for text-to-3D generation, trained via supervised finetuning (SFT) on limited 3D datasets, produce inconsistent views that cause NeRF reconstruction artifacts (floaters, broken geometry, blurring). Prolonged SFT worsens this by shifting the output distribution toward the small training set, degrading diversity and realism. The field also lacks a reliable quantitative metric for multi-view consistency, forcing reliance on qualitative inspection.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/CVPR20241153/cropped_page1_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The model takes a single text prompt and produces a 2×2 grid of four multi-view images (one denoising pass over a 2.6B-parameter UNet from SDXL). These four views are then fed to a sparse-view Large Reconstruction Model (LRM) for feed-forward NeRF reconstruction. During RLFT training, the model is conditioned on a curated set of 30 creative text prompts with random initial noise, and the MRC reward is computed by comparing the generated views against LRM-rendered views at identical camera poses, with LPIPS normalized to the foreground bounding box.

## Method

Carve3D introduces two coupled contributions: (1) the Multi-view Reconstruction Consistency (MRC) metric, which measures consistency as the LPIPS distance between generated multi-view images and their NeRF re-renderings at the same viewpoints, normalized by the foreground bounding box to prevent reward hacking via object shrinkage; and (2) an RLFT algorithm that uses negative MRC as the reward signal. The RLFT algorithm diverges from standard DDPO by adopting pure on-policy REINFORCE (DDPO_SF) over partially on-policy PPO for training stability, adding a normalized KL-divergence penalty to prevent distribution shift, and identifying scaling laws showing that compute (batch size) and data size must be scaled in tandem to maximize reward. Applied to Instant3D-10K, the resulting Carve3DM achieves the best MRC score (0.0606) among all compared models while preserving prompt alignment and texture detail.

## Results

On the DreamFusion testing set (414 prompts, 4 outputs each), Carve3DM achieves an average MRC of 0.0606 (lower is better), outperforming all baselines. This represents a 32% improvement over the Instant3D-10K base model (0.0892) and a 12% improvement over the most SFT-heavy variant, Instant3D-100K (0.0685). In a user study with 15 participants, 68.33% preferred Carve3DM's 3D consistency over the base model, while prompt alignment was rated as comparable.

## Baselines & Comparisons

Carve3DM (0.0606) outperforms all four compared methods on the MRC metric. Against the SFT-only variants of Instant3D, Carve3DM beats the 10K base model by 32% (0.0606 vs. 0.0892), the 20K model by 24% (vs. 0.0795), and the 100K model by 12% (vs. 0.0685), demonstrating that RLFT with an explicit consistency objective is more efficient than simply extending SFT. Against MVDream (0.1222), a text-conditioned multi-view diffusion model, Carve3DM achieves roughly a 50% reduction in MRC. Against the image-conditioned models Zero123++ (0.0700) and SyncDreamer (0.1018), Carve3DM improves by 13% and 40% respectively. Notably, Carve3DM achieves these gains while preserving the prompt alignment, texture detail, and generation diversity of the Instant3D-10K base model, whereas longer SFT (20K, 100K) degrades these qualities.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper positions multi-view diffusion model training as analogous to LLM alignment, where SFT on limited 3D datasets (Objaverse) plays the role of instruction tuning and RLFT addresses the resulting distribution shift. It builds directly on DDPO (Black et al., 2023) for RL-based diffusion model optimization but departs from both DDPO and the LLM RLHF literature by adopting pure on-policy REINFORCE over PPO for training stability. The MRC metric extends the 3D consistency scoring idea (Watson et al., 2022) by using fast sparse-view LRM reconstruction and LPIPS-based comparison across all input views, providing a practical quantitative tool where prior work relied on qualitative inspection.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

As the first application of RLFT to a 2.6B-parameter text-to-3D diffusion model, this work establishes a viable SFT+RLFT pipeline for 3D generation and provides the MRC metric as a reusable benchmark for evaluating multi-view consistency across methods. The finding that only 30 training prompts suffice for generalization to 415 test prompts suggests RLFT is data-efficient relative to SFT, which is critical given the scarcity of high-quality 3D data. Open questions include whether the REINFORCE-over-PPO stability advantage generalizes beyond this task and model scale, whether the identified scaling laws hold for other diffusion architectures, and whether the approach extends to multi-object or scene-level generation where bounding-box normalization may not apply.
