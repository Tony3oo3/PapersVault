## Problem

Existing post-training quantization (PTQ) methods for Video Diffusion Transformers suffer from two critical limitations: they rely on computation-heavy calibration procedures that cannot adapt to timestep-dependent activation scale variations, and they experience severe quality degradation (27.5%–61.3% VBench drops) under aggressive W4A4 quantization. No prior method enables W4A4 PTQ for video DiTs while preserving visual fidelity.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ICLR20261214/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

DVD-Quant is a data-free post-training quantization framework applied to pre-trained Video DiT models (HunyuanVideo, Wan2.1). It takes a text prompt and produces a generated video via a 50-step flow matching scheduler (CFG scale 6.0, flow shift 7.0), with no calibration dataset required at any stage. The quantized model directly uses standard W4A4 and W4A8 GEMM kernels on Tensor Cores, and the δ-GBS mechanism dynamically switches activation precision between 4-bit and 8-bit across denoising steps at negligible inference overhead.

## Method

DVD-Quant integrates three data-free components. (1) Bounded-init Grid Refinement (BGR) addresses Gaussian-like weight distributions by iteratively tightening quantization bounds and solving the least-squares quantization objective in closed form for step-size and zero-point, achieving 86% error reduction over MinMax with zero inference overhead. (2) Auto-scaling Rotated Quantization (ARQ) eliminates calibration by applying Hadamard rotation (via fast Hadamard transform) to both activations and weights, then computing per-channel scaling factors online at each timestep, combining outlier suppression from rotation with dynamic range adaptation. (3) δ-Guided Bit Switching (δ-GBS) monitors the normalized L1 distance between successive denoising outputs; when cumulative feature change stays below threshold δ, activations use 4-bit precision, and once it exceeds δ, precision switches to 8-bit with a counter reset, yielding a content-aware mixed-precision schedule (average ~6-bit) that outperforms static temporal partitioning, alternating, and stochastic strategies.

## Results

On VBench with HunyuanVideo, DVD-Quant W4A6 achieves 62.27 Aesthetic Quality and 64.22 Imaging Quality, nearly matching the BF16 baseline (62.53 / 64.78) while using 25% fewer activation bits than all W4A8 baselines. Under the extreme W4A4 setting, DVD-Quant scores 61.96 Aesthetic Quality and 61.82 Imaging Quality, outperforming the best W4A4 baseline (SmoothQuant at 48.41 / 59.46) by +13.55 in Aesthetic Quality. The method achieves 3.68× memory reduction and up to 2.12× latency speedup over BF16, and when combined with TeaCache reaches 4.85× total speedup at W4A4.

## Baselines & Comparisons

DVD-Quant decisively outperforms all four quantization baselines (MinMax, SmoothQuant, QuaRot, ViDiT-Q) at both W4A6 and W4A4 settings. At W4A6, DVD-Quant's 62.27 Aesthetic Quality exceeds the best W4A8 baseline (SmoothQuant, 60.50) by +1.77 while using 2 fewer activation bits, and its 58.33 Dynamic Degree surpasses all W4A8 methods by 2.8–9.7 points. At W4A4, the gap widens dramatically: DVD-Quant (61.96 Aesthetic) beats SmoothQuant (48.41) by +13.55, QuaRot (44.85) by +17.11, ViDiT-Q (45.36) by +16.60, and MinMax (24.20) by +37.76. Notably, MinMax and ViDiT-Q produce near-zero Dynamic Degree at W4A4 (0.00), indicating complete failure to generate coherent motion, while DVD-Quant maintains 56.94. Against the BF16 HunyuanVideo baseline, DVD-Quant W4A6 loses only 0.26 in Aesthetic and 0.56 in Imaging, confirming near-lossless compression at 3.68× memory reduction.

## Relation to Prior Work

DVD-Quant sits at the intersection of three PTQ research threads: rotation-based outlier suppression (QuaRot, SpinQuant, QuIP#), channel-wise scaling (SmoothQuant), and search-bound initialization (2DQuant, OmniQuant). It departs from prior video-specific PTQ methods (ViDiT-Q, Q-DiT, SVDQuant) by eliminating the calibration dataset entirely—replacing offline pre-scaling with online Hadamard rotation plus per-channel scaling (ARQ) and replacing fixed-range weight quantization with iterative grid refinement (BGR). The δ-GBS component extends the observation of timestep redundancy in diffusion models (TeaCache, DeepCache) from caching to adaptive bit-width allocation, making precision a continuous function of feature evolution rather than a static schedule.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

Enabling W4A4 PTQ for video DiTs without calibration removes two major deployment barriers simultaneously: the need for representative calibration data (which is difficult to construct for 50-step diffusion processes) and the quality cliff that has kept prior methods at W4A8. The 2× speedup with near-BF16 VBench scores makes low-bit video generation practically viable on consumer hardware. Open questions include whether the approach generalizes to image DiTs at comparable bit widths, whether even lower bits (W2A2) remain feasible, and how the method interacts with emerging architectural changes (e.g., sparse attention, MoE DiTs) that alter activation distributions.
