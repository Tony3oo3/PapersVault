## Problem

Existing Maps of Dynamics (MoDs) for modeling human motion patterns rely on spatial discretization into grid cells, introducing information loss, discontinuities at cell boundaries, and the need for costly per-cell model fitting. Temporal MoDs like STeF-map further discretize orientation into fixed bins and assume periodicity, limiting expressiveness. The paper asks whether a continuous, differentiable representation can capture spatio-temporal motion statistics without any spatial or angular discretization.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ICLR20264967/cropped_page1_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method takes a single spatio-temporal query (x, y, t) where x ∈ R² is a 2D position (normalized to [-1,1]) and t is a continuous time-of-day scalar. Spatially, x is bilinearly interpolated against a learnable grid G_s ∈ R^{H×W×C_s} (default 16×16 to 64×64) to produce local context features; temporally, t is encoded by a two-layer SIREN network. The output is a full SWGMM with J=3 components (18 raw values per query): softmax-normalized weights, non-negative speed means, wrapped orientation means, clamped-exponential variances, and tanh-bounded correlations, yielding a continuous joint distribution over speed ρ ∈ R⁺ and orientation θ ∈ [0, 2π).

## Method

NeMo-map replaces the discrete grid of per-cell SWGMMs with a single neural implicit function Φ_θ: (x, t) → SWGMM parameters, trained end-to-end by minimizing the negative log-likelihood of observed velocities. The architecture fuses three input streams — raw coordinates, bilinearly-interpolated spatial grid features, and SIREN-encoded temporal features — via FiLM conditioning before passing through an MLP (hidden sizes [128, 64], ReLU) that regresses the 6J SWGMM parameters. The key design choice is the SIREN temporal encoder (sinusoidal activations with ω₀=30 in the first layer), which outperforms both a 24-bin temporal grid and 4-frequency Fourier features in capturing continuous daily periodicity. Because the representation is continuous in both space and time, inference at arbitrary coordinates is a single forward pass (~1.4 µs), and the model implicitly learns environment topology (corridors, exits, resting areas) without any explicit geometric map input.

## Results

On the ATC dataset, NeMo-map achieves an average NLL of 0.775 ± 2.052, substantially outperforming Online CLiFF-map (1.527), CLiFF-map (1.964), and STeF-map (5.576) in motion representation accuracy. For the downstream long-term human motion prediction task (60 s horizon on ATC), NeMo-map achieves ADE 5.08 m and FDE 10.97 m, beating all MoD-based baselines and deep-learning predictors (TUTR: ADE 12.10, FDE 27.26; MID: ADE 21.20, FDE 44.47). Training a full day of ATC data takes only 19.26 minutes with per-query inference at ~1.4 µs, compared to 1831 minutes for static CLiFF-map.

## Baselines & Comparisons

NeMo-map outperforms all six baselines on the primary MoD quality metric (NLL). Against CLiFF-map, it reduces NLL from 1.964 to 0.775 (−60%) while cutting training time from 1831 min to 19.26 min and inference from 1.9 ms to 1.4 µs per query. Online CLiFF-map is the closest MoD baseline (NLL 1.527) but still trails NeMo-map by 0.752 NLL and is slower in both training (23.9 vs 19.3 min) and inference (1.9 ms vs 1.4 µs). STeF-map is the weakest MoD baseline (NLL 5.576) due to its 8-bin orientation discretization and lack of speed modeling, though it trains fastest (0.8 s). In the downstream LHMP task, NeMo-map beats CLiFF-LHMP (ADE 5.08 vs 5.45; FDE 10.97 vs 11.45) and STeF-LHMP (ADE 5.88; FDE 12.24), and substantially outperforms the deep-learning predictors TUTR (ADE 12.10; FDE 27.26) and MID (ADE 21.20; FDE 44.47), which produce physically infeasible trajectories that cross walls.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the Maps of Dynamics (MoD) line of work, most directly the CLiFF-map (Kucner et al., 2017), which fits a Semi-Wrapped Gaussian Mixture Model (SWGMM) at each cell of a discrete spatial grid. The key departure is replacing this per-cell, grid-based construction with a single implicit neural network that maps arbitrary spatio-temporal coordinates to SWGMM parameters, drawing on the implicit neural representation paradigm (SIREN, FiLM conditioning) to achieve continuity in both space and time. It also subsumes the temporal modeling of STeF-map (Molina et al., 2022) by encoding time as a continuous input rather than a set of discrete hourly bins, and generalizes the SWGMM output representation from Roy et al. (2016) from a fixed per-location fit to a learned continuous field.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The practical impact is significant for robotics: a single 19-minute neural network training replaces the ~30-hour per-cell EM fitting of CLiFF-map while yielding lower NLL and enabling queries at arbitrary spatio-temporal coordinates without interpolation artifacts. The finding that the learned field implicitly captures environment topology (corridors, benches, exits) without any explicit geometric map input suggests MoDs can serve as a lightweight, differentiable prior for downstream planning and prediction. Open questions remain around handling sharp, event-driven discontinuities (temporary barriers, sudden crowd surges) and extending the framework to online adaptation, which the authors acknowledge as future work.
