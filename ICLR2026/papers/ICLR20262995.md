## Problem

Visual Autoregressive (VAR) models suffer from exponential token growth across resolution scales, making later generation stages computationally expensive. Existing acceleration methods like FastVAR and SkipVAR rely on single-dimensional heuristics (frequency-based token pruning or scale skipping), which cause semantic loss, structural distortion, and detail collapse because they cannot capture the multi-dimensional sparsity structure of the model.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ICLR20262995/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

ToProVAR is an inference-time optimization applied to pre-trained VAR models (Infinity-2B and Infinity-8B). It takes a standard text prompt as conditioning input and produces a high-resolution RGB image, but modifies the internal generation process by adaptively pruning tokens, compressing layers, and adjusting generation depth based on attention entropy analysis. The method operates on the multi-scale token maps (K scales with progressively increasing spatial resolution) that the VAR model produces during coarse-to-fine autoregressive decoding.

## Method

ToProVAR introduces a tri-dimensional attention entropy framework that analyzes sparsity across token, layer, and scale dimensions simultaneously. At the token level, attention entropy identifies semantic salience to prune low-salience tokens; at the layer level, SVD on entropy maps distinguishes Global Layers (structure-critical, pruning-sensitive) from Detail Layers (locally focused, aggressively prunable); at the scale level, a low-entropy ratio metric determines when semantic stability is reached to set the pruning start depth. A unified gating function integrates all three dimensions into a per-token retention probability. To make entropy computation practical, the authors design Flash Attention Entropy (FAE), an online streaming algorithm that computes exact attention entropy within the FlashAttention kernel using an incremental x·log(x) accumulator, adding only ~0.17 ms overhead over baseline FlashAttention.

## Results

On Infinity-2B, ToProVAR achieves a 3.4× speedup (0.61 s vs. 2.10 s) while matching the baseline GenEval Overall score of 0.69 and attaining the highest DPG Overall (83.07) among all accelerated variants. On Infinity-8B, it delivers a 2.7× speedup (1.78 s vs. 4.86 s) with GenEval Overall of 0.83 (identical to baseline) and DPG Overall of 86.70, the best among all methods. On HPSv2.1, ToProVAR preserves the baseline ImageReward score (1.04 on Infinity-8B) while reducing latency by 67%, and on MJHQ30K it even improves FID in the People category (58.84 vs. 58.91) at 3.5× acceleration.

## Baselines & Comparisons

ToProVAR outperforms both FastVAR and SkipVAR on every evaluated metric across both model sizes. Against FastVAR, ToProVAR achieves a higher speedup (3.4× vs. 2.6× on Infinity-2B; 2.7× vs. 2.4× on Infinity-8B) while also matching or exceeding quality: GenEval Overall is 0.69 vs. 0.68 (2B) and 0.83 vs. 0.81 (8B), and DPG Overall is 83.07 vs. 83.39 (2B) and 86.70 vs. 86.50 (8B). Against SkipVAR, the speedup advantage is even more pronounced (3.4× vs. 2.0× on 2B; 2.7× vs. 2.3× on 8B), and quality is consistently better: GenEval Overall 0.69 vs. 0.67 (2B) and 0.83 vs. 0.82 (8B), DPG Overall 83.07 vs. 82.94 (2B) and 86.70 vs. 86.44 (8B). On HPSv2.1, ToProVAR also leads both baselines in overall score on Infinity-2B (30.27 vs. 29.95 FastVAR, 30.39 SkipVAR) and achieves the best ImageReward (1.04, tied with baseline) on Infinity-8B. The key qualitative advantage is that ToProVAR avoids the semantic loss, structural distortion, and detail collapse artifacts visible in FastVAR and SkipVAR outputs, particularly on fine-grained objects and complex compositions.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper builds directly on the VAR (Visual Autoregressive Modeling) paradigm introduced by Tian et al. (NeurIPS 2024), which reformulated image generation as coarse-to-fine next-scale prediction. It departs from prior VAR acceleration methods—FastVAR (frequency-based token pruning) and SkipVAR (scale-level skipping via a trained decision model)—by replacing single-dimensional heuristics with a unified attention-entropy framework that jointly analyzes sparsity across token, layer, and scale dimensions. The conceptual shift is from data-space metrics (pixel frequency, learned skip decisions) to model-space semantic projection analysis, where attention entropy serves as a principled proxy for token salience, layer scope, and scale fineness. The Flash Attention Entropy extension further grounds the method in practical inference constraints by making entropy computation compatible with memory-efficient attention kernels.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

VAR models are emerging as a competitive alternative to diffusion models for high-quality image synthesis, but their exponential token growth at later scales makes them impractical without acceleration. ToProVAR's tri-dimensional approach addresses specific, well-documented failure modes of prior methods (semantic loss, structural distortion, detail collapse) and achieves 3.4× speedup with near-zero quality degradation, making VAR-based generation more deployable. Open questions include whether the entropy-based layer classification (SVD principal component ratio) generalizes to substantially different architectures or training regimes, whether the framework extends cleanly to video or multimodal autoregressive generation as the authors suggest, and whether the threshold τ for scale-depth discrimination can be made fully adaptive rather than calibrated via pre-sampling.
