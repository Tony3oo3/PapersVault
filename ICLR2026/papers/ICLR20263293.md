## Problem

Vector-quantized latents have a dual discrete-continuous nature that existing generative models handle by choosing one side: continuous flow matching preserves embedding geometry but ignores categorical structure (no uncertainty over codes, no temperature control), while discrete flow matching respects index discreteness but collapses geometric relationships into unrelated token jumps. No prior method simultaneously exploits both the smooth geometry of codebook embeddings and the categorical supervision over discrete indices.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ICLR20263293/cropped_page1_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The model takes a class label (for conditional ImageNet-1k generation) and random Gaussian noise as input, and produces 256×256 RGB images via a frozen VQ decoder. Generation occurs in a 16×16 grid of VQ latent positions, where each position is drawn from a codebook of 8192 (vq-f8) or 16384 (vq-ds8-c2i) embeddings of dimension 4 or 8. At inference, a softmax temperature parameter (τ) controls the sharpness-detail trade-off without retraining, a capability absent from both purely continuous and purely discrete baselines.

## Method

Purrception adapts Variational Flow Matching to VQ latents by learning a categorical variational posterior q_θ(c | z_t) over codebook indices given the linear interpolant z_t = t·z_1 + (1−t)·z_0, rather than predicting a velocity vector or a hard index. The velocity field is computed as the expectation of the conditional OT velocity under this categorical posterior, yielding v_θ(z_t) = (μ_θ(z_t) − z_t)/(1−t) where μ_θ is the barycenter of the predicted code distribution—this converts categorical uncertainty into smooth, geometry-aware transport. A DiT backbone outputs logits over the codebook at each spatial position, and training minimizes cross-entropy between the predicted categorical and the ground-truth quantized index. A softmax temperature τ applied to these logits provides an inference-time knob: low τ sharpens predictions for global fidelity, high τ spreads probability across neighboring codes for finer detail. Empirically, this hybrid objective converges 1.6–3.5× faster than CFM and DFM baselines on ImageNet-1k 256×256 while achieving competitive FID (3.88 with LlamaGen tokenizer).

## Results

On class-conditional ImageNet-1k 256×256 generation, Purrception (750M params, DiT-XL/2 backbone, vq-ds8-c2i tokenizer) achieves an FID of 3.88 with τ=0.9 and CFG=1.3, using Euler with 250 ODE steps. In convergence-speed experiments (vq-f8 tokenizer), Purrception reaches the final FID of CFM and CFM-endpoint 1.65×–2.3× faster and the final FID of DFM 3.0×–3.5× faster, depending on backbone size. A U-shaped FID curve over inference-time softmax temperature τ reveals an optimum around τ≈0.8–0.9, yielding a training-free quality improvement over the default τ=1.0.

## Baselines & Comparisons

Purrception (FID 3.88) clearly outperforms all discrete-diffusion and masked-generative baselines: VQ-Diffusion (5.84), Implicit Timestep Model (5.30), and MaskGIT (6.18), with margins of 1.4–2.3 FID points. Against autoregressive VQ methods, Purrception beats VQGAN (5.20) and is competitive with RQTransformer (3.80) while using far fewer parameters (750M vs. 3.8B), but trails LlamaGen-XL (3.39) and ViT-VQGAN (3.04). Against continuous-diffusion baselines, Purrception falls short of DiT-XL/2 (2.27) and SiT-XL/2 (2.06), which the authors attribute to their use of high-quality VAE autoencoders (lower rFID) and roughly twice the training iterations. In convergence-speed comparisons on the same DiT backbones, Purrception reaches CFM/CFM-endpoint's final FID 1.65×–2.3× faster and DFM's final FID 3.0×–3.5× faster, demonstrating that the hybrid categorical-plus-continuous objective accelerates optimization relative to both purely continuous (MSE velocity regression) and purely discrete (categorical) flow-matching objectives.

## Relation to Prior Work

Purrception directly extends Variational Flow Matching (VFM) from Eijkelboom et al. (2024), which reframed flow matching as variational inference and enabled categorical posteriors (CatFlow) for discrete data, by adapting this framework to the dual discrete-continuous structure of vector-quantized image latents. It departs from both continuous flow matching (which ignores codebook discreteness) and discrete flow matching (which discards embedding geometry) by simultaneously learning a categorical posterior over codebook indices while computing velocity fields in the continuous embedding space. The DiT backbone and VQ tokenizer infrastructure follow standard practice from Peebles & Xie (2023) and Esser et al. (2021), respectively, so the architectural contribution is in the training objective and inference mechanism rather than the network design.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The hybrid categorical-continuous formulation offers two practical advantages absent in either pure CFM or DFM: faster convergence (1.65×–3.5× fewer iterations to match baseline FID) and a temperature knob for inference-time quality-diversity control. However, Purrception's FID of 3.88 remains behind top continuous diffusion models (DiT-XL/2 at 2.27, SiT-XL/2 at 2.06), and performance is tightly coupled to the quality of the frozen VQ tokenizer, limiting its ceiling. Open questions include whether jointly training the autoencoder with the flow model closes the gap, whether the approach extends to video, audio, or 3D, and whether principled temperature schedules can further improve sample quality without additional training.
