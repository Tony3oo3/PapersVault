## Problem

Training diffusion policies with RL is challenging: direct reward backpropagation through multi-step denoising is unstable and costly, while Gaussian-likelihood policy gradient methods require impractically small denoising steps. The natural alternative—KL-regularized weighted regression—suffers from an unbounded exponential weighting term that causes loss explosion at high greediness and makes training dominated by a few high-reward samples, creating a fundamental optimality-stability trade-off.

## Input / Output

For RL benchmarks, the method takes environment state vectors (e.g., 24-dim for Walker, 78-dim for Quadruped, 55-dim for Jaco) and produces continuous action vectors (6–12 dimensions) via multi-step diffusion/flow denoising. For the autonomous driving VLA model, input consists of three camera images (front, front-left, front-right) plus a language instruction (e.g., 'turn left'), processed by a Florence-2 encoder feeding a 475M-parameter Diffusion Transformer that outputs a driving trajectory. At inference, the final action is produced by linearly combining the noise predictions of two separate policy networks (positive and negative) with a greediness coefficient ω, followed by optional rejection sampling over N candidate actions scored by a learned Q-function.

## Method

DIPOLE reformulates the KL-regularized RL objective by replacing the standard reference policy μ with a greedified, value-aware reference μ·σ(βG(s,a))/Z(s), where σ is a bounded sigmoid. The resulting closed-form optimal policy π* ∝ μ·σ(βG)·exp(ωβG) can be algebraically decomposed into a ratio of two dichotomous policies: π⁺ ∝ μ·σ(βG) (maximizing return) and π⁻ ∝ μ·(1−σ(βG)) (minimizing return), both trained with bounded sigmoid-weighted diffusion losses that preclude loss explosion. At inference, the optimal score is recovered as (1+ω)ε⁺ − ωε⁻, a linear combination structurally identical to classifier-free guidance, enabling continuous control over greediness via ω. For the 1B-parameter VLA driving model, two LoRA adapters (rank 16, ~6.7M params each) are applied to the diffusion decoder to instantiate the positive and negative policies, fine-tuned offline-to-online using PDMS-score-based advantage estimation with group-relative normalization.

## Results

On ExORL (9 tasks, 4 domains), DIPOLE achieves the best average return across nearly all tasks, e.g., 953±4 on Walker-stand vs. 873±6 (IFQL) and 801±4 (FQL). On OGBench (30 tasks, 6 categories), DIPOLE attains the highest aggregate success rate in 4 of 6 categories, including 68±3 on humanoidmaze-medium-navigate vs. 60±14 (IFQL) and 44±7 on cube-double-play vs. 29±2 (FQL). On the NAVSIM autonomous driving benchmark, DIPOLE fine-tuning on navtest yields a 94.8 PDMS score, a 6.5-point improvement over the pre-trained DP-VLA (88.3) and 5.8 points above DPPO (89.0), while also surpassing all prior end-to-end driving baselines (best prior: Hydra-MDP at 86.5).

## Baselines & Comparisons

On ExORL, DIPOLE fully surpasses IQL (Gaussian weighted regression) across all 9 tasks, with the largest gap on Walker-stand (953 vs. 603). Among diffusion/flow policy baselines, DIPOLE outperforms CFGRL on every task, and even DIPOLE without rejection sampling (w/o rs) already exceeds CFGRL, confirming the value of the dichotomous sigmoid weighting over CFGRL's binary indicator weighting. DIPOLE also beats IFQL on 7 of 9 ExORL tasks and FQL on 6 of 9, though FQL remains competitive on some locomotion tasks. On OGBench, DIPOLE leads in 4 of 6 categories (humanoidmaze-medium, cube-single, cube-double, scene-play) but is slightly behind FQL on antsoccer-arena (57 vs. 60) and humanoidmaze-large (6 vs. 4, within noise). On NAVSIM, DIPOLE's 94.8 PDMS decisively beats all prior end-to-end driving methods (Hydra-MDP 86.5, Transfuser 84.0, UniAD 83.4) and the DPPO baseline (89.0), with the largest gains in ego progress (94.2 vs. 83.5 for DPPO) and no-collision rate (99.2 vs. 97.9). In the offline-to-online setting on OGBench, DIPOLE reaches 97±2 on humanoidmaze-medium after 1M online steps, outperforming IFQL (82±20) and FQL (22±12), while matching FQL on scene-play (both reach 100).

## Relation to Prior Work

DIPOLE builds directly on the KL-regularized RL framework (AWR, AWAC), whose closed-form optimal policy involves an exponential reward weighting of a reference policy. The key departure is replacing this unbounded exp(βG) term with a bounded sigmoid weighting, which the authors show can be algebraically decomposed into a ratio of two 'dichotomous' policies (positive and negative), each trained with a stable weighted diffusion loss. At inference, the optimal policy's score is recovered as a linear combination of the two dichotomous scores — a formulation the paper explicitly connects to classifier-free guidance (CFG), revealing an inherent structural link between greedified KL-regularized RL and the widely-used CFG mechanism. This positions DIPOLE as a principled middle ground between unstable direct reward maximization (ReFL, DRaFT), biased Gaussian-likelihood policy gradients (DDPO, DPPO), and conservative rejection-sampling approaches (IDQL, IFQL, CFGRL).

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The paper addresses a core practical bottleneck in diffusion policy RL — the stability-greediness trade-off that has prevented simple weighted regression from being widely adopted — and resolves it with a clean theoretical decomposition that also yields a natural inference-time controllability knob (ω). The 6.5-point PDMS gain on NAVSIM (88.3→94.8) for a 1B-parameter VLA model demonstrates that the approach scales to complex real-world settings, not just toy RL benchmarks. However, the authors acknowledge that performance remains highly dependent on value function quality, and the method still operates within a behavior-regularized framework that inherently constrains the policy relative to a reference, limiting how far it can push beyond the data distribution. Open questions include whether the dichotomous decomposition generalizes to non-diffusion policy classes and whether the value estimation bottleneck can be addressed without sacrificing the stability guarantees.
