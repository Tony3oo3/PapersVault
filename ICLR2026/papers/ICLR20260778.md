## Problem

Existing KL divergence convergence guarantees for diffusion models under minimal assumptions (only L²-accurate score estimation) achieve linear dependence on data dimension d but require Õ(d/ε²) steps to reach O(ε²) KL error, with the quadratic ε-dependence being potentially suboptimal. This paper asks whether the ε-dependence can be improved to linear while preserving the linear d-dependence, thereby establishing a stronger convergence guarantee than the best prior results.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ICLR20260778/cropped_page5_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The algorithm takes a learned score function ŝ(t,·) (obtained via denoising score matching), a discretization sequence {t_k} with exponentially decaying step sizes h_k = c·min{t_k, 1}, and initializes from a standard normal N(0, I_d) at time T. Each interval update consists of two sub-steps: an Exponential Integrator step along the empirical probability flow ODE, followed by a smaller forward noising step that adds Gaussian noise with variance (1−e^{−2h_{k−1}}). The output is a generated sample x̂′_1 at time t_1 > δ > 0 (early stopping to avoid smoothness assumptions on the data), along with a provable KL divergence bound against the forward process distribution p_{t_1}.

## Method

The key insight is to decompose each generation interval into a reverse ODE step (Exponential Integrator discretization of the probability flow ODE) followed by a partial forward noising step, which converts the Wasserstein-type discretization error into KL with a cubic (rather than quadratic) dependence on step size. The main technical challenge is bounding the ODE discretization error without smoothness assumptions, which requires controlling second-order spatial derivatives of the score (Laplacian and score-Jacobian products) that do not appear in prior SDE-based analyses. The authors introduce a novel ODE-based counterpart of the stochastic localization argument by establishing new identities relating d/dt E[‖s_r‖²], d/dt E[‖∇s_r‖²_F], and d/dt E[‖s_r‖⁴] to the Laplacian-of-score and score-Jacobian terms via the Fokker-Planck equation and integration by parts. These identities allow the d² contribution from the Jacobian term to be reduced to d, achieving linear dimension dependence for the ODE path. The final guarantee shows Õ(d·log^{3/2}(1/δ)/ε) steps suffice for O(ε²) KL divergence, improving the prior best of Õ(d·log²(1/δ)/ε²).

## Results

The paper proves that Õ(d log^{3/2}(1/δ) / ε) steps of the Exponential Integrator discretization (ODE step + forward noising) suffice to achieve O(ε²) KL divergence from the Gaussian-perturbed data distribution, under only L²-accurate score estimation and finite second moment assumptions. This improves the ε-dependence from quadratic (previous best) to linear while maintaining the linear d-dependence. The main bound (Theorem 3.1) is KL(p_{t₁}‖p̂_{t₁}) ≲ (d+m₂)e^{-T} + d²c³K + Tε_score², where the discretization error term d²c³K yields the improved step-size dependence via the ODE path.

**Comparison of convergence guarantees for diffusion model sampling under minimal assumptions (score estimation + finite second moment only).**

| Method | Metric | Steps for target error | d-dependence | ε-dependence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Benton et al. (2024) [SDE] | KL ≤ ε² | Õ(d log²(1/δ) / ε²) | O(d) | O(1/ε²) |
| Li & Yan (2024) [SDE] | TV ≤ ε | O(d / ε) | O(d) | O(1/ε) |
| **This work [ODE+noise]** | **KL ≤ ε²** | **Õ(d log^{3/2}(1/δ) / ε)** | **O(d)** | **O(1/ε)** |

Note: Since TV ≤ √KL (Pinsker), this work's KL guarantee also implies a TV bound of O(ε), matching Li & Yan (2024) but with a stronger (KL) metric.

## Baselines & Comparisons

The primary comparison is with Benton et al. (2024), which achieves Õ(d log²(1/δ) / ε²) steps for ε²-KL via the reverse SDE and stochastic localization. This paper improves the ε-dependence from quadratic to linear (reducing steps by a factor of ~1/ε) while preserving the linear d-dependence, by switching to a probability flow ODE step followed by a smaller forward noising step. Compared to Li & Yan (2024), which achieves O(d/ε) steps for ε-TV, this paper provides a strictly stronger guarantee (KL instead of TV) at comparable iteration complexity. The ODE-based approaches of Li et al. (2024a) and Li et al. (2024b) achieve similar TV rates but require additional assumptions on the Jacobian or divergence of the estimated score, which this paper avoids. The key technical contribution enabling the improvement is a novel ODE-based counterpart of the stochastic localization argument that bounds second-order spatial derivatives (Laplacian of score) without smoothness assumptions, which do not arise in the SDE analysis of Benton et al.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the ODE-step-plus-noising framework introduced by Li & Cai (2024), Li & Jiao (2024), and Jain et al. (2025) to the standard DDPM sampler under minimal assumptions, where prior analyses either required smoothness or yielded suboptimal d^{3/2} dependence. The key conceptual departure is from Benton et al. (2024), which achieves linear d-dependence via the reverse SDE and a direct stochastic localization equivalence; here the probability flow ODE path introduces Laplacian-of-score terms absent in the SDE analysis, requiring a novel ODE-based counterpart of stochastic localization and new identities relating the score to its first- and second-order spatial derivatives. The Exponential Integrator discretization and the Gaussian-perturbation trick for the rescaled process are inherited from Chen et al. (2023a).

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The result improves the state-of-the-art KL convergence guarantee for diffusion models under minimal assumptions from Õ(d/ε²) to Õ(d/ε) steps, and since KL upper-bounds TV² via Pinsker's inequality, it also surpasses the best existing TV guarantee of Li & Yan (2024). The analysis is purely theoretical with no empirical validation, and the step-size rule h_k = c·min{t_k, 1} is inherited from prior work, so the practical sampling schedule is unchanged. An open direction noted by the authors is whether the ODE-plus-noising framework can yield further improvements in the ε-dependence beyond the current linear rate, potentially through higher-order discretizations or adaptive step-size schemes.
