## Problem

Reconstructing high-fidelity 3D clothed humans from truly unconstrained, in-the-wild photo collections—where subjects appear in varying poses, viewpoints, croppings, and occlusions—remains largely unsolved. Existing approaches either demand 'clean' calibrated multi-view captures or rely on slow DreamBooth fine-tuning plus SDS optimization (hours per person), and none efficiently aggregate information from multiple unstructured references while preserving identity and 3D consistency.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ICLR20262080/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method accepts 1 to dozens of raw, unstructured photos of a person in the same outfit (no camera calibration, no pose annotation, arbitrary cropping or occlusion), all resized to 768×768. It produces a textured SMPL-X-based 3D mesh in A-pose (animatable via Mixamo or skin-weight transfer), along with intermediate outputs: predicted SMPL-X shape parameters β, six orthogonal multi-view RGB images, and corresponding multi-view normal maps. The full pipeline completes in approximately 1.5 minutes on a single GPU.

## Method

UP2You introduces a 'data rectifier' paradigm that converts unconstrained photos into clean orthogonal multi-view images in a single forward pass, then applies standard mesh carving and texture baking. The core module, Pose-Correlated Feature Aggregation (PCFA), predicts per-pixel correlation maps between each reference image and a target SMPL-X normal-map pose condition using DINOv2 features, a lightweight ResNet pose encoder, and a cross-attention transformer; a topk selection strategy then retains only the most informative reference features per target view, keeping GPU memory nearly constant regardless of input count. A perceiver-based shape predictor with learnable query tokens regresses SMPL-X shape parameters directly from multiple unconstrained photos, eliminating the need for ground-truth body templates. The multi-view generation backbone is MV-Adapter (row-wise self-attention diffusion with ReferenceNet features), conditioned on SMPL-X normal renderings, trained end-to-end with denoising losses on synthetic multi-view renderings from 6,921 scans across 2,091 identities.

## Results

On PuzzleIOI, UP2You (Mesh) achieves PSNR 24.54, SSIM 0.940, LPIPS 0.0474, Chamfer 2.724, P2S 2.605, and Normal L2 0.0115, outperforming PuzzleAvatar by 15% on Chamfer and 18% on P2S. On 4D-Dress, UP2You (Image) reaches PSNR 25.85 and LPIPS 0.0576, improving PSNR by 21% and reducing LPIPS by 46% over PuzzleAvatar. On the in-the-wild dataset (no 3D GT), UP2You attains CLIP-I 0.972 and DINO 0.932, substantially exceeding PuzzleAvatar (0.907 / 0.742) and AvatarBooth (0.878 / 0.619). The full pipeline completes in ~1.5 minutes per person on a single GPU.

## Baselines & Comparisons

UP2You outperforms all nine baselines across every reported metric. Against the two primary album-to-human methods, PuzzleAvatar [94] and AvatarBooth [102], UP2You (Mesh) reduces Chamfer distance by 15% (2.724 vs. 3.204) and P2S by 18% (2.605 vs. 3.165) on PuzzleIOI, while improving PSNR by 21% and reducing LPIPS by 46% on 4D-Dress; AvatarBooth trails even more significantly (Chamfer 6.635 on PuzzleIOI). For single-image reconstruction, UP2You surpasses PSHuman [48] on all metrics (e.g., Chamfer 0.927 vs. 2.759, PSNR 26.65 vs. 24.13 on 4D-Dress front view), benefiting from its multi-view training. In the unconstrained-vs-single-front-view comparison (Table 8), UP2You also beats Human-3Diffusion [96], ICON [92], ECON [93], and PIFuHD [72] on geometry (Chamfer 1.140 vs. 2.603–4.352) and back-view rendering quality. For shape prediction, the perceiver-based multi-reference predictor achieves V2V error of 7.40 mm at 12 references, outperforming single-image Semantify [17] (11.10 mm) and PromptHMR [89] (9.29 mm) with substantially lower variance (7.40 vs. 6.60 and 19.42 mm respectively).

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper departs from the dominant 'data compressor' paradigm (PuzzleAvatar, AvatarBooth) that distills identity into learned tokens via DreamBooth fine-tuning and then optimizes 3D geometry through SDS-based text-to-3D generation over hours. Instead, UP2You introduces a 'data rectifier' strategy that converts unconstrained photos into clean orthogonal multi-view images in a single forward pass, building on MV-Adapter's multi-view diffusion backbone, ReferenceNet for feature extraction, and PSHuman's mesh carving/texture baking pipeline. The Perceiver-based shape predictor and the Pose-Correlated Feature Aggregation (PCFA) module with topk selection are novel architectural contributions that enable multi-reference processing with near-constant memory, extending single-image methods (PSHuman, ICON, ECON) to the multi-photo unconstrained setting.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

UP2You makes 3D clothed human reconstruction from personal photo albums practical for the first time (1.5 minutes vs. 4+ hours), removing the need for calibrated captures, ground-truth body shapes, or per-subject fine-tuning. The 'data rectifier' framing offers a clean conceptual alternative to SDS-based optimization, suggesting that converting messy inputs into structured views is more effective than compressing them into implicit tokens for identity preservation. Open questions include temporal consistency for animation (each frame is reconstructed independently), handling of missing body parts across all inputs (relying on diffusion hallucination), and whether the approach generalizes to multi-person or multi-outfit scenarios.
