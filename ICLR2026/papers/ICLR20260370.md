## Problem

RL post-training of diffusion and flow-based generative models is prohibitively expensive because each policy update requires sampling complete denoising trajectories, and existing GRPO-based methods assign a single terminal reward uniformly across all denoising steps, obscuring which actions actually contributed to the outcome. TreeGRPO addresses both the sample-efficiency bottleneck and the coarse credit-assignment problem simultaneously.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ICLR20260370/cropped_page1_idx2.webp)

## Input / Output

The method takes a text prompt and an initial Gaussian noise sample as input, and produces one or more generated images (decoded from latent states) as output. During training, a single prompt yields a tree of candidate images sharing deterministic ODE prefixes but diverging at stochastic SDE windows; all leaves are scored by reward models (HPS-v2.1, ImageReward, Aesthetic, ClipScore) and the resulting advantages drive the policy update. Experiments use SD3.5-medium with a fixed NFE budget of 10 denoising steps and the HPDv2 prompt set (103,700 training / 3,200 evaluation prompts).

## Method

TreeGRPO recasts the fixed-horizon denoising process as a sparse search tree: deterministic ODE steps advance all frontier nodes along a shared prefix, while a single contiguous SDE window (selected per epoch via a truncated geometric distribution biased toward early timesteps) creates branching points where each node spawns k stochastic children. Terminal rewards at the leaves are group-normalized per prompt, then propagated bottom-up through the tree using log-probability-weighted averages (a softmax over stored edge probabilities) to yield dense, step-specific per-edge advantages. The policy is updated with a standard PPO-clipped GRPO surrogate applied to these per-edge advantages, with the behavior policy refreshed periodically. Theoretically, the weighted aggregation acts as Rao-Blackwellization (reducing advantage variance by the effective sample size) and implicitly regularizes against sharp reward peaks, explaining the observed Pareto improvements in both training speed (2.4×) and final reward.

## Results

On the HPDv2 evaluation set (3,200 held-out prompts), TreeGRPO achieves the best HPS-v2.1 score (0.3735) and Aesthetic score (6.5094) among all methods, while running at 72.0 s/iteration—2.4× faster than DanceGRPO (173.5 s) and 2.0× faster than MixGRPO (145.4 s). On ImageReward, DanceGRPO leads (1.3668) with TreeGRPO close behind (1.3294). The base SD3.5-medium model scores 0.2725 on HPS-v2.1, so TreeGRPO yields a +37% relative improvement in the primary training reward.

## Baselines & Comparisons

TreeGRPO dominates MixGRPO on all four reward metrics (HPS 0.3735 vs 0.3649, ImageReward 1.3294 vs 1.2263, Aesthetic 6.5094 vs 6.4295, ClipScore 0.3703 vs 0.3612) while being roughly 2× faster (72.0 s vs 145.4 s per iteration). Against DanceGRPO, TreeGRPO wins on HPS-v2.1 (0.3735 vs 0.3556) and Aesthetic (6.5094 vs 6.3080) and is 2.4× faster, but trails on ImageReward (1.3294 vs 1.3668) and ClipScore (0.3703 vs 0.3769). Compared to DDPO, TreeGRPO substantially outperforms on HPS-v2.1 (0.3735 vs 0.2758), ImageReward (1.3294 vs 1.0067), and Aesthetic (6.5094 vs 5.9458), though DDPO retains a slight edge on ClipScore (0.3900 vs 0.3703). Overall, TreeGRPO establishes the best efficiency–reward Pareto frontier: it matches or exceeds the strongest baseline on the primary training reward and aesthetic quality while cutting per-iteration wall-clock time by more than half.

## Relation to Prior Work

TreeGRPO extends the GRPO framework (originally from DeepSeekMath for LLMs) to visual generative models by replacing independent trajectory sampling with a tree-structured search over the denoising process. It builds on the ODE-to-SDE conversion (Song et al., Albergo et al.) that enables policy-gradient RL over flow/diffusion samplers, and on the stochastic sampling strategies in DanceGRPO and FlowGRPO. The key conceptual departure from prior trajectory-based methods (DDPO, DanceGRPO, MixGRPO) is borrowing the prefix-sharing and branching paradigm from tree search in game playing (AlphaGo), applied here to the fixed-horizon denoising MDP where shared initial noise makes prefix reuse natural.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The 2.4× training speedup while simultaneously improving reward scores addresses the central practical barrier to RL post-training of diffusion/flow models, making preference alignment more accessible. The tree-backpropagation credit assignment mechanism is a principled solution to the coarse uniform-credit problem that plagues all trajectory-level GRPO variants. However, the evaluation is limited to a single base model (SD3.5-medium) and a single dataset (HPDv2), and the method introduces additional hyperparameters (branching factor, depth, window position) whose optimal settings may not transfer. Open questions include whether the approach scales to video or 3D generation, whether adaptive tree scheduling can eliminate the manual hyperparameter burden, and whether learned value functions could enable early pruning for further efficiency gains.
