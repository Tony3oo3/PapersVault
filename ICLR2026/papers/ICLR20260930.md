## Problem

Pose-specific MLLMs trained via supervised fine-tuning suffer from a fundamental alignment gap: the one-to-many nature of 3D pose generation (a single image or text prompt maps to a distribution of valid poses) is incompatible with SFT's deterministic regression to a single ground truth, yielding averaged and suboptimal outputs. Moreover, existing reinforcement fine-tuning algorithms are designed exclusively for discrete token spaces and cannot handle the fine-grained continuous parameters of 3D human poses, leaving no principled way to apply reward-driven optimization to this hybrid discrete-continuous generation task.

## Input / Output

The model accepts either an RGB image (for image-to-pose) or a natural language prompt (for text-to-pose) as multimodal input, processed through a frozen CLIP encoder and a frozen pose-aware ViT (HMR2.0) whose features are fused via separate linear projections into the LLaVA-1.5V-7B embedding space. The output is a hybrid response: a discrete textual answer (e.g., a templated sentence) followed by continuous SMPL pose parameters, where the pose is sampled from a learned multivariate Gaussian distribution (mean and covariance predicted by a dedicated pose head) rather than a single deterministic vector.

## Method

Pose-RFT formulates 3D pose generation as a hybrid action space RL problem, factorizing the policy into a discrete sub-policy over text tokens and a continuous sub-policy over SMPL parameters modeled as a multivariate Gaussian. The core algorithm, HyGRPO, extends GRPO to this hybrid space by decomposing the advantage into discrete and continuous components, each normalized independently over G sampled candidates per input, and applying PPO-style clipped importance sampling separately to each branch. Four task-specific rewards guide optimization: inverse joint-position error for spatial accuracy in image-to-pose, cosine similarity from a pretrained text-pose retrieval model for semantic alignment in text-to-pose, a binary format-compliance reward, and a BGE-M3 embedding similarity reward to preserve general QA ability. The framework is built on LLaVA-1.5V-7B with LoRA fine-tuning, a pose-aware ViT encoder for fine-grained visual features, and a distributional pose head whose stochastic sampling is essential for enabling effective reward-driven exploration during RFT.

## Results

On image-to-pose generation, Pose-RFT achieves 85.9 MPJPE / 51.6 PA-MPJPE on 3DPW and 63.0 / 44.5 on Human3.6M, outperforming the best MLLM baseline UniPose (94.7 / 59.1 on 3DPW) by ~9 MPJPE. On the Reasoning Pose Estimation (RPE) task, which requires visual-language reasoning, Pose-RFT sets a new state-of-the-art with 198.6 MPJPE / 87.0 PA-MPJPE, surpassing UniPose (213.4 / 94.1) and ChatPose (275.0 / 101.8). On text-to-pose generation (PoseScript-H2), Pose-RFT achieves the best mean Recall@K across both Full Retrieval (R^T2P: 42.2/53.0/65.5) and Random Sampling (R^P2T: 74.6/86.5/91.5) protocols.

## Baselines & Comparisons

Against specialist pose estimation models (HMR, SPIN, PyMAF, HMR2.0, MEGA, TokenHMR), Pose-RFT still trails on standard reconstruction benchmarks—HMR2.0 achieves 70.0 MPJPE on 3DPW versus Pose-RFT's 85.9—but Pose-RFT is the only method that also handles the RPE reasoning task, where it achieves 198.6 MPJPE, beating HMR2.0 (225.2) and SPIN (244.9). Among MLLM-based pose models, Pose-RFT clearly dominates: it reduces 3DPW MPJPE from 94.7 (UniPose) and 163.6 (ChatPose) to 85.9, and cuts RPE MPJPE from 213.4 (UniPose) and 275.0 (ChatPose) to 198.6. On text-to-pose (PoseScript-H2), Pose-RFT outperforms ChatPose by a large margin (e.g., R^T2P@5: 42.2 vs. 17.6 under Full Retrieval) and edges out UniPose on Random Sampling R^P2T (91.5 vs. 89.6 at K=20) and PoseScript's own retrieval baseline on R^P2T (70.4 vs. 65.9 under Full Retrieval). The ablation in Table 3 confirms that the gains come from the RFT stage itself: adding distributional modeling alone yields marginal changes, but combining it with HyGRPO drops 3DPW MPJPE from 91.4 to 85.9 and lifts mean Recall^T2P from 37.4 to 53.6.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the GRPO algorithm (originally designed for discrete token spaces in language models) to a hybrid discrete-continuous action space, producing HyGRPO, which jointly optimizes language tokens and continuous SMPL pose parameters. It departs from prior pose-specific MLLMs (ChatPose, UniPose) that rely solely on supervised fine-tuning, arguing that SFT's deterministic regression is fundamentally misaligned with the one-to-many ambiguity inherent in pose generation. The framework also builds on the LLaVA-1.5 multimodal architecture and HMR2.0's pose-aware ViT, but the core contribution is the RL training paradigm shift rather than architectural novelty.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

Pose-RFT establishes that reward-driven reinforcement fine-tuning can close the alignment gap that SFT leaves open in ambiguous generation tasks, and the hybrid-action RL formulation (HyGRPO) is a reusable technique for any MLLM task with mixed discrete-continuous outputs. However, the approach is constrained by reward function quality—designing reliable, semantically meaningful rewards for pose plausibility and naturalness remains an open problem—and the multi-candidate sampling introduces non-trivial computational overhead that may limit scalability. The persistent gap versus specialist models (e.g., 85.9 vs. 70.0 MPJPE on 3DPW) suggests that RFT narrows but does not eliminate the efficiency advantage of dedicated architectures, raising the question of whether learned reward models or larger-scale RL could further close this gap.
