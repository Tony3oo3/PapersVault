## Problem

Classifier-Free Guidance (CFG) in autoregressive image generation suffers from two fundamental issues: guidance diminishing, where the conditional-unconditional gap vanishes as decoding progresses because conditioning tokens drift away from local context, and over-guidance, where large guidance scales enforce semantics too aggressively and produce structural artifacts. Unlike diffusion models where guidance is injected at every denoising step, AR models rely on a single conditioning token at the sequence start, making the guidance signal decay rapidly even within short sequences.

## Input / Output

The method operates as a training-free, inference-time modification on existing AR image generators. For class-conditional generation, it takes an ImageNet class label and produces a 256×256 RGB image; for text-to-image, it takes a natural language prompt and produces a generated image (e.g., 756×756 with LuminaGPT2). The input is consumed as a sequence of discrete visual tokens from a VQ autoencoder, and the method only perturbs the unconditional value cache during decoding without altering the model architecture, tokenizer, or any training procedure.

## Method

SoftCFG redefines the CFG guidance offset by incorporating already-generated visual content as an additional guidance signal. For each previously generated token, it computes a confidence score p_max (the maximum conditional probability) and scales the corresponding unconditional value-cache entry by w_i = 1 − p_max, so high-confidence tokens receive stronger perturbations that amplify their downstream influence on future predictions. This produces a context-aware correction term Δ_t^context that is added to standard CFG, effectively letting reliable generated tokens guide subsequent decoding. To prevent cumulative perturbation explosion over long sequences, Step Normalization renormalizes the perturbation weights at every step so that their total budget equals one, yielding a provably bounded deviation from vanilla CFG (Proposition 1). The entire procedure requires no retraining, no architectural change, and adds negligible computational overhead since it only performs in-place scalar rescaling of cached value vectors.

## Results

On ImageNet-1K 256×256 class-conditional generation, Alitok-XL + SoftCFG achieves a FID of 1.27 (IS 302.4, sFID 6.76), improving over the Alitok-XL baseline (FID 1.35) and setting a new state-of-the-art among autoregressive models. This narrows the gap to the best diffusion model (REPA-E, FID 1.26) to just 0.01. On text-to-image generation (DPG-Bench), SoftCFG applied to SimpleAR slightly lowers the overall score (0.617→0.613) but improves two-object composition (89.4%→91.4%) and color attribute correctness (36.0%→40.5%).

## Baselines & Comparisons

SoftCFG consistently improves FID over the corresponding Alitok baselines: Alitok-XL drops from 1.35 to 1.27, Alitok-L from 1.42 to 1.39, and Alitok-B from 1.50 to 1.40. Among AR models, Alitok-XL+SoftCFG (FID 1.27) outperforms RAR-XL (1.50), RandAR-XXL (2.15), and LlamaGen-3B (2.18) by substantial margins, and is within 0.01 FID of the best diffusion model REPA-E (1.26). Against other diffusion baselines, SoftCFG beats REPA (1.42), MDTV2-XL/2 (1.58), and DiMR-XL/2R (1.70). On text-to-image (DPG-Bench), SoftCFG slightly lowers the overall SimpleAR score (0.617→0.613) due to regressions in position (30.3%→26.0%) and counting (33.4%→27.8%), but improves two-object prompts (89.4%→91.4%), color consistency (81.4%→82.2%), and color attribute correctness (36.0%→40.5%), indicating better visual coherence at the cost of strict spatial alignment.

## Relation to Prior Work

SoftCFG extends Classifier-Free Guidance (CFG) from diffusion models (Ho & Salimans, 2022) and its AR adaptation (Sun et al., 2024) by redefining the guidance signal: rather than relying solely on the external class/text condition, it lets previously generated tokens contribute certainty-weighted perturbations to the unconditional value cache. This is conceptually related to perturbed-attention guidance (Ahn et al., 2024) and self-attention guidance (Hong et al., 2023) in diffusion, but those operate on attention maps during denoising, whereas SoftCFG acts on the KV cache during sequential AR decoding. The Step Normalization mechanism, which bounds cumulative perturbation to a unit budget per step, is a novel stabilization device not present in prior CFG variants.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

SoftCFG is training-free, model-agnostic, and adds negligible inference overhead, making it immediately applicable to any existing AR image generator. Achieving FID 1.27 on ImageNet-256 narrows the gap to the best diffusion model (REPA-E, 1.26) to within 0.01, suggesting AR models may soon be competitive alternatives. However, the method's reliance on token confidence as the weighting signal is fragile: the paper itself documents failure cases where confidence concentrates on the wrong object, causing semantic drift. Open questions include whether perceptual scorers (e.g., DINOv3) can provide more robust confidence signals, whether Step Normalization's fixed unit budget is optimal for very long sequences (high-res images, video), and whether the approach transfers cleanly to multi-modal or RLHF settings.
