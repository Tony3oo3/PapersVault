## Problem

Deep Predictive Coding Networks (PCNs) suffer from severe training instabilities that intensify with network depth, preventing them from scaling beyond shallow architectures despite their biologically plausible local learning rules. Through dynamical mean-field analysis, the paper identifies two root causes: prediction-error (PE) imbalance that starves intermediate layers of learning signals, and exploding/vanishing prediction errors (EVPE) driven by weight variance. Prior work offered only symptomatic remedies without a unified theoretical diagnosis or a principled fix.

## Input / Output

The method consumes standard RGB images (32×32 for CIFAR-10/100, 64×64 for TinyImageNet) and outputs class labels via a VGG or ResNet backbone. A key operational detail is that during training, each layer's latent state is refined through T iterative inference steps (fixed-point updates) with feed-forward predictions frozen, whereas at evaluation time the network performs a single feed-forward pass—creating a train-test mismatch that the meta-PE objective is designed to mitigate.

## Method

Meta-PCN combines two synergistic components. First, a meta-prediction-error (meta-PE) loss replaces the standard free-energy objective: instead of minimizing PEs directly (which causes gradient starvation), it minimizes the residual between each layer's realized error and a top-down prediction of that error, effectively linearizing the nonlinear equilibrium system around the feed-forward initialization. Second, a variance-based weight normalization rescales each weight matrix by (√m + √n)σ_w to maintain spectral norms near unity, suppressing the exponential scaling factors that drive EVPE. Parameter updates still follow the conventional PC gradient, while the meta-PE objective governs inference dynamics, preserving fully local learning rules throughout.

## Results

On CIFAR-10, Meta-PCN maintains 80–90% top-1 accuracy across all tested depths (VGG-5 through VGG-13, ResNet-18), while conventional PCNs collapse to 10–20%. On the representative VGG-13/CIFAR-10 configuration, Meta-PCN reaches 89.53% top-1 accuracy versus 87.85% for backpropagation. Across all 30 dataset–architecture–metric configurations (CIFAR-10/100, TinyImageNet; Top-1 and Top-5), Meta-PCN outperforms BP in 29 cases with an average margin of 2.15% (22 statistically significant). Meta-PCN also achieves 12–79% relative accuracy gains over conventional PCNs across all architectures.

## Baselines & Comparisons

Against conventional PCN (Whittington & Bogacz, 2017), Meta-PCN achieves dramatic improvements of 12–79% in accuracy across all architectures, with conventional PCNs plateauing at ~12% on VGG-13/CIFAR-10 while Meta-PCN reaches 89.53%. Against backpropagation under identical hyperparameters, Meta-PCN wins 29 of 30 configurations by an average of 2.15% (22 statistically significant), with the only loss being a negligible 0.02% on CIFAR-100 ResNet-18 Top-1 (p=0.84). Compared to literature-reported PC variants shown as scattered points in Figure 7—positive/negative/centered nudging (Pinchetti et al., 2024), incremental PC (Salvatori et al., 2023), and Deep PCN with/without centered nudging (Qi et al., 2025)—Meta-PCN consistently outperforms all of them, particularly at greater depths where these methods still exhibit significant accuracy degradation. The paper emphasizes that its controlled comparison protocol (identical optimizer, learning rate, batch size, and architecture across BP/PCN/Meta-PCN) may yield somewhat subdued BP numbers relative to literature reports that employ method-specific tuning, making the Meta-PCN advantage over BP potentially conservative.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the predictive coding network (PCN) framework of Whittington & Bogacz (2017) and Millidge et al. (2022a) by providing the first unified dynamical mean-field theory (DMFT) diagnosis of why deep PCNs fail, identifying two specific pathologies: prediction-error imbalance and exploding/vanishing prediction errors. Prior work (Pinchetti et al. 2024, Qi et al. 2025, Goemaere et al. 2025, Innocenti et al. 2025) addressed symptoms through nudging heuristics, error-space reparameterization, or depth-aware scaling, but did not isolate the root spectral mechanisms. Meta-PCN departs from these by replacing the free-energy objective with a meta-prediction-error loss that linearizes the equilibrium system, combined with variance-based weight normalization grounded in random matrix theory, rather than applying separate engineering patches.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The DMFT-based diagnosis provides a principled, architecture-agnostic explanation for deep PCN instability that goes beyond empirical tuning, and the resulting Meta-PCN framework demonstrates that local learning rules can match or exceed backpropagation on standard vision benchmarks without architecture-specific tuning—strengthening the case for biologically plausible, neuromorphic-compatible learning. Key limitations include evaluation only on CIFAR-10/100 and TinyImageNet (no ImageNet), the conservative comparison protocol where BP is not optimally tuned, and the open question of whether variance-based normalization generalizes across all activation functions and very deep architectures where inference steps are fewer than network depth. Future work on streaming/continuous-time formulations and validation in NLP or RL would substantially broaden the impact.
