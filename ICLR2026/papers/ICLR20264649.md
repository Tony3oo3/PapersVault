## Problem

Training-free latent reasoning (e.g., Soft Thinking) preserves richer distributional information than explicit chain-of-thought but suffers from two critical issues: (1) purely latent trajectories diffuse probability mass across many implicit paths, introducing noise and impeding convergence to a single high-confidence solution, and (2) overthinking persists even without explicit text, wasting tokens and degrading efficiency. No existing training-free method dynamically balances exploration in latent space with convergence in explicit space.

## Input / Output

The method takes a standard text question as input and produces a reasoning trace followed by a final answer, operating as a drop-in replacement for the HuggingFace transformers model.generate() interface with no architecture or weight modifications. During the reasoning phase, the model alternates between generating discrete tokens (explicit mode) and feeding probability-weighted soft embeddings back as inputs (latent mode), with the final answer always decoded in explicit mode. The framework is parameterized by a user-specified maximum switch count C_max that controls the token budget, and a dwell window W_{E→L} that governs how long explicit reasoning must persist before re-entering latent exploration.

## Method

SWIREASONING is a training-free inference framework that dynamically alternates between explicit and latent thinking based on block-wise confidence estimated from entropy trends in next-token distributions. Within each thinking block, a reference entropy H̄ is tracked; when current entropy H_t drops below H̄ (confidence rises), the model immediately switches from latent to explicit mode to consolidate progress, while when H_t exceeds H̄ for at least W_{E→L} steps (sustained uncertainty), it switches from explicit to latent mode to re-explore in continuous space. The latent mode uses the Soft Thinking mechanism, forming a probability-weighted mixture of token embeddings as the next input representation rather than committing to a single token. A switch count controller caps the total number of Latent→Explicit transitions, inserting a convergence trigger at ½C_max switches (encouraging early answer production) and a termination trigger at C_max switches (forcing immediate answer generation with a bounded token budget), thereby suppressing overthinking. At each mode transition, thinking-related signal tokens ( and ) are blended into the embedding to align switches with the model's learned reasoning patterns.

## Results

Under unlimited token budgets, SWIREASONING achieves average Pass@1 accuracy gains of +2.03% (Qwen3-8B), +2.68% (Qwen3-1.7B), and +1.80% (DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Llama-8B) over standard CoT with sampling across GSM8K, MATH500, GPQA Diamond, AIME 2024, and AIME 2025. Gains are most pronounced on harder benchmarks: on AIME 2024/2025, SwiR yields +3.34%/+2.50% on Qwen3-8B and +5.00%/+5.00% on Qwen3-1.7B. Under constrained token budgets, SWIREASONING improves average token efficiency by 57%–79% over CoT, with peak efficiency gains of 4.6×–6.8× depending on model size. On broader domains (coding, multi-hop QA, commonsense), average gains reach +2.70% over CoT.

## Baselines & Comparisons

SWIREASONING is compared against three single-mode baselines: standard CoT with sampling, CoT with greedy decoding, and Soft Thinking (training-free latent reasoning). SwiR consistently outperforms all three baselines across every model family and benchmark tested. Against CoT (sampling), SwiR gains +1.80% to +2.68% in average accuracy, with the largest per-benchmark margins on AIME 2024/2025 (up to +5.00% on Qwen3-1.7B). Against CoT (greedy), the advantage is substantially larger (+5.47% to +8.77%), as greedy decoding degrades sharply on harder problems. Against Soft Thinking, SwiR improves by +3.49% to +9.74%, confirming that the dynamic switching mechanism addresses the accuracy loss that purely latent reasoning incurs from probability-mass diffusion. On the larger Qwen3-32B model, SwiR still achieves +1.92% over CoT, with the biggest gain on GPQA Diamond (+4.04%), indicating the method scales with model size.

## Relation to Prior Work

SWIREASONING sits at the intersection of two established lines of work: explicit chain-of-thought reasoning (Wei et al., 2022; Yao et al., 2023a) and training-free latent reasoning, most directly Soft Thinking (Zhang et al., 2025), which replaces hard token selection with probability-weighted embedding mixtures. The key departure is that rather than committing to a single mode throughout inference, the framework dynamically alternates between explicit and latent thinking guided by block-wise entropy trends, and adds a switch-count controller to suppress overthinking. This resonates with the concurrent observation by Wu et al. (2025b) that stochasticity benefits latent reasoning, but achieves diversity through mode switching rather than injecting randomness into distributions.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

As a training-free, plug-and-play inference-time method, SWIREASONING can be layered onto any existing reasoning LLM without retraining, making the reported Pareto improvements (both accuracy and token efficiency) immediately deployable. The 57–79% token efficiency gains under constrained budgets are practically significant for cost-sensitive deployment, and the overthinking suppression mechanism offers a principled way to trade off reasoning depth against token cost. Limitations include struggles with rigid geometric/topological constraints (e.g., 3D surface shortest path), where latent smoothing blurs precise spatial constraints, and the hyperparameters (window size, α₀, β₀) still require per-task tuning. Open directions include making the dwell window and signal-mixing coefficients adaptive to real-time reasoning density and problem difficulty, and integrating the framework with RL-based training.
