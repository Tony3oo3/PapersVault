## Problem

LLMs tend to converge on homogenized, single-perspective answers, yet no scalable benchmark exists to evaluate their ability to represent, distinguish, and reason over multiple viewpoints. Existing pluralism datasets depend on costly human annotation or curated surveys, while debate platforms offer either clean structure at small scale (Kialo) or noisy diversity at large scale (Reddit) but not both simultaneously.

## Input / Output

The three tasks consume different compositions of the dataset's ~100-word expanded arguments: opinion counting takes a concatenated paragraph of m expansions (some sharing the same underlying opinion, interleaved non-adjacently) and outputs an integer; opinion matching takes a single expansion alongside all candidate opinions for its topic and outputs the source opinion index; polarity check takes a paragraph of n randomly sampled mixed expansions and outputs a binary pro/con label. All ground truths are derived deterministically from Kialo's curated opinion identifiers and stance labels, requiring no additional annotation, and evaluation sets of 500 examples per task are generated programmatically with fixed seeds.

## Method

The construction pipeline integrates Kialo's structured pro/con debate graphs with Reddit's linguistic diversity through a retrieval-and-expansion process. For each of 100 topics, the top 20 relevant Reddit threads are retrieved (up to 100 comments each, filtered for quality), then Qwen3-Embedding-8B embeddings are used to match each Kialo opinion to its top-5 semantically related comments via cosine similarity. GPT-4o is prompted with a fixed template on each (topic, opinion, best-match comment) triple to generate a ~100-word naturalistic expansion that preserves the original stance while adopting Reddit's informal register, yielding 5 variants per opinion (3,810 total across 762 opinions). The three benchmark tasks are then derived deterministically from the opinion-to-expansion mapping and stance labels, making the resource fully configurable and extensible beyond the demo tasks.

## Results

Across all three sub-tasks, no single model achieves consistently strong performance. On opinion counting (T1), QwQ-32B leads with 36.2% accuracy and 0.97 MAE, while GPT-4o-mini achieves the lowest MAE (0.94) and 34.0% accuracy; human performance reaches 44.0% accuracy. On opinion matching (T2), DS-R1-Distill-Qwen-32B attains the highest accuracy at 85.4% (95.0% stance accuracy), surpassing GPT-4o (74.8% / 81.2%). On polarity check (T3), GPT-4o leads among models at 76.4% accuracy, while humans achieve 85.6%. All models fall substantially below human performance on every sub-task, with the largest gaps on opinion counting (up to ~8 points) and polarity check (up to ~9 points).

## Baselines & Comparisons

As a benchmark paper, PERSPECTRA evaluates 12 LLMs plus a human baseline rather than proposing a single method. On opinion counting, open-source reasoning models (QwQ-32B at 36.2%, Qwen2.5-32B at 35.2%) outperform proprietary GPT-4o (29.2%), while GPT-4o-mini achieves the best MAE (0.94) among all models. On opinion matching, distillation-based Qwen models dominate: DS-R1-Distill-Qwen-32B reaches 85.4% accuracy, exceeding GPT-4o (74.8%) by over 10 points, and Qwen3-8B (83.6%) also surpasses the GPT-4o family. On polarity check, the pattern reverses—GPT-4o (76.4%) and GPT-4o-mini (72.8%) lead all open-source models, with the best open-source result (Qwen3-32B at 67.6%) trailing by nearly 9 points. Across all three tasks, human performance (44.0% / 90.6% / 85.6%) exceeds every model by a clear margin, confirming that pluralism-sensitive reasoning remains an open challenge for current LLMs.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper carves new ground by synthesizing two previously separate lines of work: structured debate corpora (Kialo, KialoPrime, PERSPECTRUM) that provide clean argumentative scaffolding but limited linguistic diversity, and large-scale social media discourse (Reddit, DEBAGREEMENT, CMV) that offers naturalistic variety but lacks explicit argument structure. Rather than building on a single prior pipeline, it introduces a retrieval-and-expansion bridge (Qwen3-Embedding-8B for semantic matching, GPT-4o for controlled paraphrase generation) that grafts Reddit's stylistic richness onto Kialo's opinion backbone. It departs from survey-based pluralism benchmarks (OpinionQA, GlobalOpinionQA, DICES) by avoiding costly human annotation at scale, and from formal debate corpora (DebateSum, OpenDebateEvidence) by preserving multi-opinion granularity rather than collapsing into binary pro/con summaries.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

PERSPECTRA fills a clear gap: no prior resource simultaneously offers scalable, structured, and linguistically diverse multi-perspective data for evaluating LLM pluralism. The systematic failure modes identified—71.5% oversplitting in opinion counting, 59% of polarity errors from concession-rebuttal structures, and the 8–24 point gap between stance and exact-match accuracy—provide concrete diagnostic signals for model developers. However, the benchmark is limited to three sub-tasks, the retrieval quality is uneven (mean 3.31/5 on best-match relevance), and human validation covered only 100 samples, leaving open whether the failure patterns generalize beyond the 100-topic scope. A key open question is whether the oversplitting tendency reflects a fundamental limitation in how LLMs cluster semantically equivalent but lexically varied expressions, or whether it is an artifact of the expansion pipeline's deliberate stylistic variation.
