## Problem

Existing multi-view generation models require large-scale training data and do not support personalization to novel reference concepts, while customization methods (DreamBooth, CustomDiffusion) lack explicit camera pose control and produce inconsistent surroundings across viewpoints. No prior method simultaneously achieves camera-pose-accurate multi-view synthesis, subject identity preservation from a few reference images, and coherent text-driven generation of both the customized subject and its surrounding scene.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ICLR20264155/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method takes a small set of reference images (approximately 50 frames from CO3Dv2) each paired with its camera pose, a free-form text prompt describing a novel scene, and a sequence of target camera poses (sampled from CO3Dv2 test trajectories). It outputs 8 multi-view images at 512×512 resolution generated via a video diffusion backbone, where the subject retains identity from the references while both subject and surroundings reflect the text prompt. Inference runs 50 DDIM steps with feature replacement active during the first 35 steps, requiring ~131 seconds and 19.3 GB GPU memory per sample.

## Method

MVCustom repurposes AnimateDiff's video diffusion backbone for multi-view generation, replacing its 1D temporal attention with progressively expanded dense 3D spatio-temporal attention to model viewpoint-induced spatial displacements. During training, pose-conditioned transformer blocks (from CustomDiffusion360) with FeatureNeRF learn the subject's identity and geometry from reference images, while textual inversion embeddings capture the concept. At inference, two novel techniques address geometric consistency under limited data: (1) depth-aware feature rendering constructs an anchor mesh from an off-the-shelf depth estimator, renders it to other camera poses, and replaces corresponding feature regions during early denoising steps to explicitly enforce 3D alignment; (2) consistent-aware latent completion stochastically perturbs intermediate latents to synthesize disoccluded regions revealed by viewpoint shifts, leveraging the video backbone's temporal coherence for spatially consistent fill-in. This train/inference separation—learning identity in training and imposing geometric constraints at inference—circumvents the data scarcity that prevents standard multi-view models from generalizing to novel prompts.

## Results

On CO3Dv2 (14 concepts, 16 prompts each, 8-frame multi-view outputs), MVCustom achieves the best Camera Pose Accuracy (0.735±0.10) and near-best Multi-view Consistency (0.121±0.10, DreamSim) and Identity Preservation (0.448±0.11, DreamSim), while maintaining strong Text Alignment (0.744±0.10, CLIP). No single baseline matches MVCustom across all four metrics simultaneously: the closest competitor, Txt-MV gen with DB, achieves slightly better multi-view consistency (0.116) but fails on pose accuracy (0.283) and identity (0.557). MVCustom incurs the highest inference cost (130.92 s, 19.29 GB GPU) due to the external depth estimator and feature-replacement steps.

## Baselines & Comparisons

MVCustom clearly outperforms all three baselines on Camera Pose Accuracy (0.735 vs. 0.675 for SEVA, 0.283 for CameraCtrl+DB, and 0.000 for CustomDiffusion360, which fails COLMAP reconstruction entirely due to inconsistent surroundings). On Multi-view Consistency, MVCustom (0.121) is nearly tied with Txt-MV gen with DB (0.116) and substantially better than CustomDiffusion360 (0.190) and SEVA (0.214). On Identity Preservation, CustomDiffusion360 leads (0.417) with MVCustom close behind (0.448), while both SEVA (0.504) and CameraCtrl+DB (0.557) degrade significantly. On Text Alignment, CustomDiffusion360 achieves the highest score (0.806) versus MVCustom's 0.744, but CustomDiffusion360 does so at the cost of zero pose accuracy and poor holistic consistency. The paper's central claim is that MVCustom is the only method achieving competitive scores across all four axes simultaneously, whereas each baseline excels in one or two metrics while failing in others.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper unifies two previously separate research threads—subject customization (DreamBooth, CustomDiffusion, textual inversion) and multi-view generation with camera control (CameraCtrl, SEVA, ViewDiff)—into a single framework. It builds most directly on CustomDiffusion360's FeatureNeRF for pose-conditioned subject geometry, but departs from it by replacing the image diffusion backbone with a video diffusion model (AnimateDiff) enhanced with dense spatio-temporal attention, repurposing temporal coherence as a proxy for spatial multi-view consistency. The key conceptual departure is moving geometric enforcement from training time (where large datasets are required) to inference time via depth-aware feature rendering and stochastic latent completion, explicitly addressing the data scarcity inherent to customization.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

By defining multi-view customization as a first-class task and providing the first method to simultaneously satisfy camera pose accuracy, subject identity, text alignment, and holistic multi-view consistency, this work opens a practical path toward personalized 3D-consistent content generation for applications like virtual prototyping and e-commerce. Key limitations remain: the framework cannot alter intrinsic object pose from text (FeatureNeRF learns a fixed canonical pose), depends on external depth estimator quality for reflective/textureless surfaces, and incurs substantially higher inference cost (~130s, 19.3 GB GPU) than baselines. Future directions include dynamic neural fields conditioned on text for pose variation and integration with improved depth estimators.
