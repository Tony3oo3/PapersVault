## Problem

Embodied AI requires high-fidelity, editable simulation environments, but existing platforms either rely on uneditable real-world scans (Habitat) or suffer from a stark visual reality gap (vanilla Minecraft). Real-to-sim transfer via NeRF or 3D Gaussian Splatting yields photorealistic views but lacks physical properties and editability, while CAD-based methods require precise instance segmentation. Additionally, the 3D semantic occupancy prediction models that could bridge this gap are limited by data scarcity and poor generalization to diverse real-world scenes.

## Input / Output

The pipeline consumes a sequence of first-person RGB images (1920×1129 in MinecraftOcc) along with their camera intrinsic matrices (derived from virtual FOV) and extrinsic matrices (position + yaw/pitch Euler angles). The output is a discrete voxel grid with per-voxel semantic labels (up to 1,452 fine-grained classes in MinecraftOcc, mapped to 13 standard categories for cross-dataset evaluation), which is further processed into executable Minecraft building commands specifying block types at 3D coordinates. The voxel-based representation naturally aligns with Minecraft's 1×1×1 unit block grid, enabling direct translation without mesh-to-block conversion.

## Method

World2Minecraft operates in three stages: (1) a monocular predictor (EmbodiedOcc) generates per-view semantic occupancy grids from individual RGB images; (2) multi-view predictions are fused into a unified 3D semantic occupancy field using camera extrinsics, then a 3D convolution computes a local density map whose peaks above a threshold yield candidate object centers, refined by per-class DBSCAN clustering; (3) each clustered center's occupancy patch is matched against a furniture template library via IoU maximization over discrete rotation angles, and the optimal template placements are emitted as Minecraft building commands. For the MinecraftOcc dataset, an automated pipeline uses the Screen with Coordinates mod to capture paired images and camera poses during room tours, categorizes viewpoints into axis-aligned and diagonal cases to define the 3D sampling volume, applies a viewpoint-aware fallback offset for diagonal views to reduce peripheral voxel loss, and queries WorldEdit's map data to assign per-voxel semantic labels—producing 100,165 images across 156 scenes with PBR rendering via the TMEO mod to narrow the sim-to-real gap.

## Results

On the layout-based reconstruction comparison (Table 6), World2Minecraft achieves 0.913 Semantic Integrity, 6.145 Visual Realism, 5.186 Scene Completeness, and 6.022 Aesthetic Atmosphere, while maintaining a near-zero OOB rate (0.024) and only 0.2 collisions. On the MinecraftOcc occupancy prediction benchmark (Table 4), the best-performing method (Symphonies at 8k scale) reaches only 39.11 IoU / 21.56 mIoU, and performance degrades substantially at 100k scale (27.60 IoU / 13.08 mIoU), exposing clear generalization limits. On NYUv2 (Table 5), joint training Symphonies with MinecraftOcc 8k yields 50.34 IoU / 29.91 mIoU, a modest but consistent improvement over Symphonies alone (49.91 / 29.70). For VLN, SFT on the Base set pushes Qwen2.5-VL-7B to 0.8000 accuracy on Next-Action, and RFT on the Combined set reaches 0.6753 on Next-View.

## Baselines & Comparisons

Against layout-based scene generators, World2Minecraft wins on all six metrics: it surpasses I-Design (the strongest baseline) in Semantic Integrity (0.913 vs. 0.884), Visual Realism (6.145 vs. 6.001), Completeness (5.186 vs. 4.734), and Aesthetic (6.022 vs. 5.352), while reducing OOB from 0.423 to 0.024 and collisions from 0 to 0.2. LayoutGPT and LayoutVLM are clearly outperformed, with LayoutVLM scoring only 0.348 Semantic Integrity and 2.270 Completeness. On the MinecraftOcc occupancy benchmark, all four baselines (MonoScene, NDC-Scene, ISO, Symphonies) achieve low IoU (23–41) and mIoU (8–22), with Symphonies leading at 8k (39.11 IoU) but degrading to 27.60 at 100k; MonoScene is notably more stable across scales. On NYUv2, Symphonies remains the strongest baseline (49.91 IoU / 29.70 mIoU), and adding MinecraftOcc 8k as auxiliary data (Symphonies*) yields a small gain to 50.34 / 29.91, confirming MinecraftOcc's value as a data augmentation resource rather than a standalone replacement for real-world data.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper positions itself at the intersection of 3D semantic occupancy prediction (building on MonoScene, NDC-Scene, ISO, Symphonies, and EmbodiedOcc) and real-to-sim transfer for embodied AI. Rather than proposing a new occupancy predictor, it repurposes existing monocular and multi-view occupancy models as the geometric backbone for a novel pipeline that converts real-world scenes into editable Minecraft environments, sidestepping the uneditability of NeRF/3DGS and the segmentation fragility of CAD-based methods. The Minecraft platform lineage (MineStudio, ROCKET-1, JARVIS-VLA) is extended by integrating high-fidelity PBR mods (TMEO) to narrow the visual domain gap, and the VLN experiments draw on the Habitat-style instruction-following paradigm but instantiate it in a voxel-native, fully editable world.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The MinecraftOcc dataset (100k images, 156 scenes, 1,452 fine-grained classes) is practically valuable as both a stress-test benchmark exposing the generalization limits of current occupancy predictors and as a low-cost augmentation source that yields modest but consistent gains on NYUv2 when mixed into training. The World2Minecraft pipeline offers a 7× efficiency improvement over manual scene construction, making real-to-Minecraft transfer accessible for VLN and embodied agent research. However, reconstruction fidelity remains bottlenecked by the underlying occupancy model's accuracy, and the VLN evaluation is limited to 20 scenes with small sample sizes (≤2,120 samples), leaving open whether the approach scales to large, open-world navigation without extensive manual refinement.
