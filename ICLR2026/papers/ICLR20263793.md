## Problem

Most diffusion-based TTS conditioning stacks rely on transformer attention or recurrent modules, introducing quadratic complexity, unbounded memory growth, and long-range drift. Prior Mamba-TTS systems remain hybrid at inference, retaining attention-based duration or style predictors that limit streaming robustness. MVC asks whether the entire conditioning path—text, rhythm, and prosody—can be made fully SSM-only at inference while preserving or improving quality under a strictly matched decoder protocol.

## Input / Output

MVC takes phonemized text (via phonemizer with language-specific espeak-ng tags for cross-lingual evaluation) and a reference audio clip (24 kHz waveform, typically 6 s) as input. The reference audio is converted to an 80-bin log-mel spectrogram (FFT 1024, hop 256) from which a global style embedding is extracted via a shared shallow conv/GRU module. The output is a synthesized waveform produced by a fixed 5-step StyleTTS2 diffusion decoder followed by a corpus-matched vocoder (iSTFTNet for LJSpeech, HiFi-GAN for LibriTTS); for streaming, the bidirectional text encoder is swapped for a causal Uni-Mamba with a configurable look-ahead of 0.5–2.0 s.

## Method

MVC replaces all inference-time attention and recurrence with three selective SSM modules: a gated bidirectional Mamba text encoder that fuses forward and backward scans via a learned gate (rather than simple concatenation) and applies AdaLN modulation with the style embedding; a Temporal Bi-Mamba that models phoneme-duration alignment using a lightweight monotonic attention teacher discarded after training; and an Expressive Mamba that injects speaker-specific prosody into mel features in linear time. All Mamba blocks share a state dimension of 96, depthwise convolution kernel of 5, and gating temperature of 1.0, yielding a 21M-parameter encoder with O(T) scans and bounded activations. The diffusion decoder and vocoder are held fixed to StyleTTS2's configuration, so all measured differences isolate the conditioning architecture. Training uses AdamW with cosine decay, EMA, and mixed precision under a combined mel-reconstruction, adversarial (MPD+MRSD), and monotonic-alignment loss, with the aligner contributing supervision only during training.

## Results

On LJSpeech, MVC achieves the best MCD (4.91), highest PESQ (3.85), and lowest RTF (0.0169) among all compared systems, with F0 RMSE of 0.653 Hz and WER of 6.52%. On unseen LibriTTS speakers, MVC reaches 4.22 MOS-N and 4.07 MOS-S, slightly surpassing StyleTTS2 (4.15/4.03) with p < 0.01. For long-form (2–6 min) passages, MVC maintains 4.16 MOS-long versus 3.91 for StyleTTS2, while reducing RTF from 0.0200 to 0.0170. The encoder stack uses only 21M parameters (vs. 42M for StyleTTS2) and delivers a 1.6× encoder throughput improvement.

## Baselines & Comparisons

Against StyleTTS2, MVC achieves modest but statistically significant gains: +0.07 MOS-N, +0.04 MOS-S on LibriTTS zero-shot speakers, 0.02 lower MCD, 0.06 higher PESQ, and 0.0005 lower RTF on LJSpeech, while F0 RMSE (0.653 vs. 0.651) and WER (6.52% vs. 6.50%) are essentially tied. Against VITS, MVC wins on every metric—MOS-N by +0.53, F0 RMSE by 0.014 Hz, MCD by 0.06, WER by 0.71 points, PESQ by 0.21, and RTF by 0.0042—reflecting the larger architectural gap between a VAE-based and a diffusion-based pipeline. Against JETS, MVC leads by +0.30 MOS on in-distribution text and +0.67 MOS on OOD Gutenberg passages, where JETS degrades to 3.21. Protocol-matched Mamba baselines (Hybrid-Mamba Concat, Bi-Mamba Concat-only) confirm that MVC's gated fusion + AdaLN contributes an additional 0.02–0.04 MOS and 0.0004–0.0012 RTF improvement over simpler SSM-only concatenation, isolating the architectural contribution from mere attention removal.

## Relation to Prior Work

MVC builds on two pillars: the StyleTTS2 mel-diffusion-vocoder backbone (kept fixed) and the Mamba selective state-space model for sequence encoding. It departs from prior Mamba-TTS systems (Jiang et al. 2024, Zhang et al. 2024) that remain hybrid at inference by retaining attention in duration or style modules; MVC removes all attention and recurrence across the entire conditioning path (text, rhythm, prosody) at inference, using a training-time-only attention aligner that is discarded. The key architectural novelty is the gated bidirectional Mamba fusion with AdaLN modulation, replacing the simple concatenation used in earlier bi-Mamba TTS encoders.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The paper demonstrates that a fully SSM-only conditioning stack can match or slightly surpass attention-based TTS conditioning under a strictly matched decoder, offering linear-time scans, bounded activation memory, and practical finite-look-ahead streaming. However, the diffusion decoder remains the dominant latency source (~54% of runtime), so end-to-end speedups are modest, and the model is English-only with no fine-grained emotion control. Open questions include whether SSM-only conditioning scales to multilingual or industrial-scale settings, and whether replacing the diffusion decoder with a lighter generative backbone would unlock the full efficiency potential of the SSM conditioning path.
