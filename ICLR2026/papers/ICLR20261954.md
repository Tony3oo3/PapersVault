## Problem

LLM internal activations are distributed and entangled (superposition), lacking the discrete propositional structure needed for systematic reasoning, compositional generalization, and rule enforcement. While sparse autoencoders (SAEs) surface monosemantic latent features, they remain passive: they offer no mechanism for composing features into higher-order concepts, performing multi-step inference, or directly steering model behavior. This paper addresses the gap between interpretable feature extraction and structured logical reasoning in latent activation spaces.

## Input / Output

The method consumes text prompts processed by a backbone LLM (Llama-3.1-8B or Gemma-2-9B), extracts SAE sparse codes from hidden states at a fixed layer, and applies user-defined propositional logic rules (implication, conjunction, disjunction, negation) over the resulting activation scores. It produces an enriched concept activation matrix A′ containing both directly detected and logically inferred propositions at both token-level (A_local) and sequence-level (A_global) granularity, which can then drive downstream analysis or steer generation via a mean-shift update on hidden states using SAE decoder weights scaled by a steering factor α.

## Method

ACTIVATIONREASONING (AR) is a three-stage pipeline that embeds propositional logic into the SAE latent space. Stage 1 grounds concepts in SAE features using three representation types: single-feature (one SAE dimension), multi-feature (weighted convex combination of k features), and relational-feature (shallow decision tree encoding structured interactions like conjunctions and context-dependent exclusions); concepts are extracted automatically via differential activation analysis (argmax of expected sparse codes conditioned on positive vs. negative labels). Stage 2 computes token-level activation scores, applies concept-specific soft thresholds, and aggregates into a weighted activation matrix A. Stage 3 applies forward chaining over user-defined logical rules to A, iteratively deriving new higher-order propositions until a fixed point is reached, yielding the enriched matrix A′. For control, A′ drives activation steering by adding a scaled SAE decoder weight vector to hidden states, biasing generation toward or away from specific concepts without retraining.

## Results

On PrOntoQA, AR achieves 95.0%/95.6%/95.3% (Llama) and 93.5%/93.5%/93.5% (Gemma) exact-match accuracy at 1/3/5 hops, maintaining near-perfect performance as reasoning complexity scales, while all baselines degrade sharply. On Rail2Country, AR (Gemma) reaches 93.7% (Mono) and 86.0% (Meta), surpassing GPT-4o (82.7%/69.0%) and DeepSeek-R1-8B (83.7%/60.0%). On ProverQA, AR (Gemma) scores 94.0%/91.4%/69.6% (Easy/Medium/Hard), the only method to stay above 69% on the hard tier where all other models fall below 50%. On BeaverTails safety classification, AR with relational representations achieves 83.0% balanced accuracy overall, a +25.2 point improvement over base SAE (57.9%).

## Baselines & Comparisons

AR (Llama) outperforms its base Llama-3.1-8B by +44 to +45 points on PrOntoQA across all hop lengths, and AR (Gemma) improves over Gemma-2-9B by +45 to +46 points. Against the much larger instruction-tuned models (Llama-3.1-70B, Gemma-2-27B), AR wins decisively at 3 and 5 hops (e.g., 95.6% vs. 66.7% for Llama-70B at 3 hops) and on all ProverQA tiers (92.8/91.0/70.8 vs. 74.8/58.8/41.0). AR (Gemma) surpasses GPT-4o on R2C-Meta (86.0% vs. 69.0%) and ProverQA Hard (69.6% vs. 46.4%), and AR (Llama) beats GPT-4o on ProverQA Easy and Medium (92.8/91.0 vs. 81.0/65.4). Against the reasoning model DeepSeek-R1-8B, AR (Gemma) leads on R2C-Meta (86.0% vs. 60.0%) and all ProverQA tiers (94.0/91.4/69.6 vs. 65.6/58.6/44.2). The only setting where AR does not lead is PrOntoQA 1-hop, where GPT-4o (97.3%) and Llama-70B (96.2%) slightly exceed AR (Llama) at 95.0%, though the gap vanishes at higher hop counts where AR's advantage becomes decisive.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the SAE-based interpretability line (Bricken et al., 2023; Templeton et al., 2024) by treating extracted monosemantic features as propositional units for symbolic forward chaining, and builds on decoder-based activation steering (Härle et al., 2025) to close the loop from reasoning to model control. Unlike prior neuro-symbolic approaches (DeepProbLog, SLASH, αILP, NEUMANN) that embed logical constraints into differentiable training objectives, AR operates entirely post-hoc on frozen SAE features, requiring no retraining of either the LLM or the SAE. It also departs from concept-bottleneck and CAV methods that treat concepts in isolation by introducing explicit compositional rules that derive higher-order propositions from co-activated base features.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

AR demonstrates that a lightweight, model-agnostic logical layer over SAE activations can outperform GPT-4o and DeepSeek-R1 on structured multi-hop reasoning while being an order of magnitude faster, suggesting a practical path to auditable and steerable LLM behavior without architectural changes. Key limitations include reliance on SAE feature quality (polysemy, context sensitivity), manually or semi-automatically defined rules, and purely deductive (non-probabilistic) inference. Open questions include automatic rule induction, integration with probabilistic or inductive solvers, scaling to open-ended long-context reasoning, and extension to multimodal settings where SAEs are emerging.
