## Problem

Foundational 2D VLMs lack mechanisms to capture complex 3D structural relationships, while existing 3D VLMs operate in fundamentally different representation spaces that prevent leveraging strong 2D priors and suffer from limited 3D training data. Additionally, extending region prompting to multi-view settings remains challenging because objects appear across different views with varying visibility, making comprehensive multi-frame or 3D bounding box annotation tedious and text-based queries imprecise in cluttered scenes.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ICLR20264186/cropped_page0_idx1.webp)

## Input / Output

The model accepts single-view RGB images or uniformly sampled 32-frame multi-view video sequences, with optional region prompts (bounding boxes or segmentation masks) that can be specified on any single frame rather than requiring per-frame annotation. Depth information is derived from off-the-shelf estimators (DepthAnythingV2 for single-view; MAST3R or CUT3R for in-the-wild video) or ground-truth depth with camera poses for training, and is back-projected into a normalized canonical 3D coordinate space. Outputs are free-form text answers covering quantitative tasks (distance in meters/feet, object dimensions, room area) and qualitative spatial queries (relative direction, closest object, taller/wider comparisons).

## Method

SR-3D directly integrates 3D positional embeddings into the foundational VLM's visual tokens (rather than at a separate fine-tuning stage or parallel pathway), encoding depth-derived 3D positions via sinusoidal functions and a learnable MLP, then canonicalizing them into a shared normalized world coordinate space across all views. A novel dynamic tiling-based region extractor (tile-then-stitch) processes images and masks at 448×448 tile resolution, stitching tiled features back together before mask-pooling to produce high-fidelity region embeddings without post-hoc deconvolution refinement. Training proceeds in two stages: single-view pretraining on ~7M samples (blending general VQA with region-prompted data from RegionGPT and SpatialRGPT) with the vision encoder frozen, followed by multi-view fine-tuning on ScanQA, SQA3D, Scan2Cap, and EmbodiedScan with mask augmentations (box conversion, random frame dropping). The unified canonical space and aligned embedding from single-view pretraining enable region representations trained purely on 2D images to generalize zero-shot to multi-view 3D spatial reasoning without ever seeing multi-view data.

## Results

On the primary 3D benchmarks, SR-3D-8B achieves CIDEr of 97.9 on Scan2Cap, 109.3 on ScanQA, and 62.2 EM on SQA3D, setting new state-of-the-art across all metrics. On the proposed SR-3D-Bench for region-level spatial reasoning, SR-3D reaches 83.3% average accuracy, more than doubling the best baseline (GPT-4o + Set-of-Marks at 46.7%). On VSI-Bench for global spatial understanding, SR-3D scores 62.9 average, outperforming GPT-4o (34.0) and Gemini-1.5 Pro (45.3) by a wide margin. On 2D region-level tasks, SR-3D attains 78.0 mAP / 88.6% accuracy on COCO and 90.3% on BLINK-Depth, surpassing SpatialRGPT (72.9/82.9 and 87.9%) while preserving general VQA performance.

## Baselines & Comparisons

SR-3D decisively outperforms the two closest competitors, Video-3D LLM (Zheng et al., 2025) and LLaVA-3D (Zhu et al., 2025), on all three 3D benchmarks: CIDEr improves from 102.1→109.3 (ScanQA) and 83.8→97.9 (Scan2Cap), and SQA3D EM rises from 58.6→62.2. Against proprietary models on VSI-Bench, SR-3D (62.9 avg) surpasses GPT-4o (34.0) and Gemini-1.5 Pro (45.3) by 18–29 points, with the largest gap on relative-direction tasks (81.8 vs. 41.3/46.3). On the region-level SR-3D-Bench, SR-3D (83.3 avg) more than doubles GPT-4o+SoM (46.7) and NVILA-Video+SoM (40.0), indicating that overlaying marks on frames is fundamentally insufficient for multi-frame spatial grounding. On 2D spatial benchmarks (Table 1), SR-3D improves BLINK_Spatial from 79.7→83.9 and SAT from 62.6→64.0 over the NVILA-Lite base, while incurring only minor drops on general tasks (GQA 65.3→64.2, MMMU 25.1→24.6). On BLINK-Depth, SR-3D (90.3%) edges out SpatialRGPT (87.9%) and substantially exceeds GPT-4V-Turbo (66.9%) and Claude 3 Opus (57.3%). The one area where SR-3D does not lead is OCR: it shows a consistent ~0.7–0.9 point drop on TextVQA, ChartQA, and DocVQA relative to the base model, which the authors attribute to the training data mix.

## Relation to Prior Work

SR-3D builds directly on NVILA-Lite's dynamic tiling architecture and extends it with 3D positional embeddings and a tile-and-stitch region extractor. It departs from the most closely related 3D VLMs (LLaVA-3D, Video-3D LLM) by unifying single-view and multi-view processing in a single shared canonical 3D coordinate space, rather than using separate pathways or task-specific fine-tuning stages. The region prompting mechanism generalizes RegionGPT's mask-pooling approach to high-resolution tiled features and, crucially, to multi-frame video inputs where a single-frame annotation suffices for cross-view spatial reasoning.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The demonstration that a 2D-only pretrained model achieves strong zero-shot 3D spatial reasoning (e.g., 79.7% on Big/Small) without ever seeing multi-view data challenges the assumption that 3D spatial understanding requires explicit 3D supervision, suggesting that canonical positional embeddings are a sufficient bridge. Practical impact is significant: the model handles in-the-wild videos without 3D scans or dense annotations, and the flexible region prompting (single-frame mask propagating across views) removes a major annotation bottleneck. Open questions include whether the modest gains from 3D PE at current scale will grow with larger pretraining, how to handle dynamic (non-static) scenes, and how to unify the single-view and multi-view checkpoints into one model.
