## Problem

Existing Hamilton-Jacobi-Bellman (HJ) formulations in reinforcement learning are limited to three primitive operations—Reach, Avoid, and Reach-Avoid—yet many practical tasks require dual-objective satisfaction such as reaching a goal while perpetually avoiding hazards (Reach-Always-Avoid) or visiting two distinct targets (Reach-Reach). Lagrangian methods handle such constraints but demand intricate reward engineering and hyperparameter tuning, while temporal-logic approaches require constructing and representing automata. This paper derives explicit, tractable Bellman equations for these dual-objective problems via value decomposition, eliminating the need for automaton-based state augmentation or Lagrange multipliers.

## Input / Output

The method operates on a finite-state MDP with a state vector s, a reward function r(s) (or two rewards r₁(s), r₂(s) for the RR problem), and a penalty function q(s) (for RAA). Because the HJ-style objectives depend on extremal values over the entire trajectory rather than discounted sums, the MDP is augmented with auxiliary variables y and z that track the running maximum reward and minimum penalty (or maximum of the second reward) seen so far, making the problem Markovian for the augmented policy. The output is an augmented policy π̄(s, y, z) and a value function whose sign directly indicates whether the dual-objective task is accomplished.

## Method

The key theoretical insight is that the RAA value decomposes into a composition of an Avoid value V_A* and a Reach-Avoid value with a modified reward r̃_RAA(s) = min{r(s), V_A*(s)} (Theorem 1), while the RR value decomposes into three Reach values with a modified reward r̃_RR(s) = max{min{r₁(s), V_R₂*(s)}, min{r₂(s), V_R₁*(s)}} (Theorem 2). From these decompositions, explicit Bellman equations are derived (Corollaries 1–2) that propagate extremal min/max values rather than discounted sums. DOHJ-PPO implements these equations as a PPO variant with three modifications: additional actor-critic networks for each decomposed sub-objective, concurrent bootstrapping of composed and decomposed values during on-policy rollouts, and 'coupled resets' that initialize decomposed trajectories from states sampled along composed trajectories to maintain alignment. Stochastic relaxations (SRBE and SRABE) replace the deterministic extrema with expectations over the policy, enabling robustness to stochastic dynamics while preserving the extremal-value structure.

## Results

Across four continuous-control environments (Hopper, F16, SafetyGych, HalfCheetah) and two dual-objective task types (RAA and RR), DOHJ-PPO achieves first or second place in success rate among 10 baselines over 1,000 evaluation trajectories per setting. DOHJ-PPO is the only algorithm that consistently achieves full dual-objective success (both reaching and always-avoiding, or reaching both targets) in both RAA and RR tasks, while most baselines plateau at partial success (achieving only one of the two objectives). In terms of speed, DOHJ-PPO achieves the fewest steps to success across all tasks. Under stochastic dynamics (affine Gaussian noise on HalfCheetah), DOHJ-PPO maintains an 8–22% peak-performance gap over the best baseline in RAA at moderate noise, and a >30% improvement in peak success in RR even at high noise (σ=2.0).

## Baselines & Comparisons

DOHJ-PPO outperforms all 10 baselines in full dual-objective success across every environment-task combination. The four Lagrangian-based methods (CPPO, PPO-LAG, P2BPO, LOGBAR) consistently achieve high partial success (one objective) but fail to balance both objectives simultaneously, with success rates dropping sharply as environment complexity increases (e.g., HalfCheetah). The three HJ-RL baselines (RESPO, RCPPO, RA) are designed for single-objective R or RA problems and cannot natively handle the dual-satisfaction structure of RAA or RR, resulting in poor full-success rates. Among the STL/MORL baselines, D-STL PPO is the closest competitor in RR tasks under stochastic dynamics, but DOHJ-PPO still achieves >30% higher peak success in high-noise regimes and >15% in moderate noise. In terms of speed (steps to success, Figure 6), DOHJ-PPO consistently requires the fewest steps to achieve both objectives, with the gap widening as task difficulty increases. The paper notes that DOHJ-PPO requires little to no hyperparameter tuning compared to the Lagrangian baselines, which require careful tuning of constraint multipliers.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the Hamilton-Jacobi-Bellman (HJ-RL) framework established by Fisac et al. (2019) and Hsu et al. (2021), which provided Bellman equations for three atomic operations (Reach, Avoid, Reach-Avoid), to two new compositional problems: Reach-Always-Avoid (RAA) and Reach-Reach (RR). The key theoretical departure is proving that these compositional value functions decompose into combinations of the existing SBE and RABE, enabling reuse of prior HJ-RL solvers. Algorithmically, DOHJ-PPO builds on the Stochastic Reachability Bellman Equation (SRBE) and PPO variant from So et al. (2024), generalizing the SRBE to a Stochastic Reach-Avoid Bellman Equation (SRABE) and adding concurrent learning of decomposed sub-objectives with coupled resets. Unlike LTL-RL approaches that require automaton-based state augmentation, this paper derives explicit tractable Bellman forms via value decomposition, and unlike Lagrangian methods, it requires no multiplier tuning.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The decomposition theorems provide a principled, hyperparameter-free route to dual-objective safe RL (e.g., reach a target without crashing afterward, or visit two targets in either order), addressing a gap where Lagrangian methods require intricate reward engineering and tuning. A key open question is the validity of the SRABE approximation under stochastic dynamics, since extrema and expectation operators do not commute; the paper acknowledges this limitation and provides only empirical validation via a noise-injection ablation. Future work on extending the decomposition to arbitrary temporal logic compositions (beyond RAA and RR) and on convergence guarantees for the coupled multi-critic training would significantly broaden applicability. The code is not yet public (pending security clearance), limiting immediate reproducibility.
