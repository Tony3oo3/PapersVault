## Problem

Existing multi-view 3D reconstruction methods either rely on expensive global optimization over all views (DUSt3R, MASt3R) or use fixed-size memory buffers and RNN-style state updates (Spann3R, CUT3R) that accumulate drift and scale poorly with sequence length. There is no architecture that handles arbitrarily long streaming inputs with the efficiency and long-range dependency modeling of modern causal Transformers.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ICLR20261454/cropped_page0_idx1.webp)

## Input / Output

The method consumes a stream of uncalibrated RGB images (video frames or unstructured collections) sequentially, with each new frame processed incrementally using cached features from all prior observations. Training uses diverse resolutions from 224×224 to 512×384; inference runs at 512 max dimension (518 for the VGG-T-initialized variant). Outputs per frame include a local point map in camera coordinates, a global point map in the first frame's coordinate system, corresponding confidence maps, and a full camera pose (quaternion rotation, translation, and focal length).

## Method

STREAM3R reformulates dense 3D reconstruction as a decoder-only Transformer task with causal attention, directly borrowing the KV-cache paradigm from LLMs. A shared-weight CroCo ViT encoder tokenizes each incoming frame, and a single (non-symmetric) decoder processes frames sequentially: each block performs frame-wise self-attention followed by causal cross-attention to the cached features of all previously observed frames at the same layer. A learnable register token added to the first frame establishes the canonical world coordinate, eliminating the need for N separate decoders. Two DPT heads predict local and global point maps with confidence, and a separate head regresses camera pose; training uses a confidence-aware scale-invariant regression loss plus L2 pose loss, end-to-end on 29 diverse 3D datasets. The design natively supports sliding-window attention for constant-memory inference and is compatible with FlashAttention, QK-Norm, and BFloat16 mixed-precision training.

## Results

On video depth estimation (per-sequence scale), STREAM3R^β achieves Abs Rel of 0.264 (Sintel), 0.069 (Bonn), and 0.080 (KITTI), outperforming CUT3R (0.421/0.078/0.118) and VGG-T (0.297/0.055/0.073) while running at 12.95 FPS. The sliding-window variant STREAM3R^β-W[5] reaches 32.93 FPS—the fastest among all streaming methods—while maintaining competitive accuracy (0.279/0.064/0.083). On 7-Scenes 3D reconstruction, STREAM3R^β achieves Accuracy 0.122, Completeness 0.101, and Normal Consistency 0.746, surpassing CUT3R (0.126/0.154/0.727) and matching VGG-T (0.087/0.091/0.787) at 20.12 FPS. On ETH3D, STREAM3R^β achieves the best Completeness score (0.245) among all methods, including full-attention VGG-T (0.305).

## Baselines & Comparisons

Against the most relevant concurrent streaming method CUT3R (RNN-based), STREAM3R^β wins on all three video depth benchmarks (Sintel AbsRel 0.264 vs 0.421, Bonn 0.069 vs 0.078, KITTI 0.080 vs 0.118) while running 40% faster (12.95 vs 16.58 FPS); STREAM3R^α is even faster at 23.48 FPS. Against the full-attention flagship VGG-T, STREAM3R^β achieves better Sintel (0.264 vs 0.297) and KITTI (0.080 vs 0.073) depth but slightly worse Bonn (0.069 vs 0.055), and on 7-Scenes reconstruction STREAM3R^β (Acc 0.122, Comp 0.101) is competitive with VGG-T (Acc 0.087, Comp 0.091) at 20.12 FPS vs 12.00 FPS. STREAM3R decisively outperforms optimization-based baselines (DUSt3R-GA, MASt3R-GA, MonST3R-GA) on video depth by large margins (e.g., Sintel AbsRel 0.264 vs 0.656/0.641/0.378) while being 10–40× faster. Against Fast3R and Spann3R, STREAM3R wins on all depth and reconstruction metrics. On dynamic scenes (Sintel, TUM-dynamics), STREAM3R^β substantially outperforms VGGT-SLAM in camera pose estimation (RPE_rot 0.868 vs 4.140 on Sintel), demonstrating robustness to motion that static SLAM methods lack. On ETH3D long sequences, STREAM3R^β achieves the best Completeness (0.245) among all methods including VGG-T (0.305), while GA-based methods (DUSt3R-GA, MASt3R-GA) degrade severely (Acc > 2.5).

## Relation to Prior Work

STREAM3R extends the DUSt3R/MASt3R line of pointmap-regression 3D reconstruction by replacing the fixed two-view or full-attention multi-view decoder with a decoder-only causal transformer, directly borrowing the KV-cache and sequential-generation paradigm from modern LLMs. It departs from the concurrent RNN-based CUT3R (which compresses history into a fixed-size state) and the memory-buffer approach of Spann3R, instead caching per-layer features of all prior frames and attending to them causally. The architecture is initialized from either DUSt3R (STREAM3R^α) or VGG-T (STREAM3R^β), making it a targeted architectural modification rather than a from-scratch design.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

By aligning 3D reconstruction with the LLM training/inference stack (KV cache, FlashAttention, windowed attention, QK-Norm), STREAM3R makes streaming 3D perception compatible with the same infrastructure that has driven LLM scaling, potentially lowering the barrier to very long-sequence and real-time 3D understanding. The sliding-window variant achieving near-full-attention quality with constant memory is practically significant for deployment on resource-constrained hardware. Open questions include the still-weak metric-scale prediction (especially on KITTI), sensitivity to the anchor-view choice, and whether the causal formulation generalizes to truly unbounded sequences without periodic re-anchoring.
