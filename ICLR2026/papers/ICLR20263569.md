## Problem

Video polyp segmentation (VPS) suffers from two critical failures in clinical colonoscopy: shape collapse, where low contrast between polyps and mucosa causes structural loss in segmentation, and episodic amnesia, where pixel-level temporal modeling breaks down under blur, occlusion, and large appearance changes across frames. Existing methods either ignore temporal context (image-based) or rely on implicit dense pixel-level feature propagation that lacks high-level semantic abstraction, making them unable to handle consecutive low-quality frames or drastic inter-frame deformations.

## Input / Output

The network consumes 2-frame colonoscopy video clips (352×352 patches, RGB) and produces multi-scale polyp segmentation masks at four decoder stages with progressively refined spatial resolutions. A PVTv2-B2 backbone extracts four-level features, and the model is trained with a hybrid weighted BCE + IoU loss applied at all four decoder levels with exponentially decaying weights. Inference runs at 31.27 FPS on a single NVIDIA 3090 GPU, supporting real-time clinical deployment.

## Method

HFSTI-Net jointly models frequency, spatial, and temporal domains through two key modules. The Hierarchical Frequency-spatial Interaction (HFSI) module uses a dual-path design: a Frequency Filter Block applies FFT-based channel-wise self-attention to extract global spectral patterns, a Spatial Refinement Block uses multi-scale depthwise separable convolutions (3×3 and 5×5) for edge-aware local detail, and an Interwoven Fusion Block performs gated multiplicative cross-domain attention to adaptively entangle frequency and spatial features, preventing shape collapse under low contrast. The Recurrent Mask-guided Propagation (RMP) module maintains a memory bank of historical high-level features and predicted masks, using cross-attention (temporal alignment) and a mask affinity mechanism to retrieve relevant spatiotemporal context, thereby mitigating episodic amnesia and ensuring long-term segmentation stability across challenging video sequences.

## Results

HFSTI-Net achieves state-of-the-art Dice scores of 88.03 on SUN-SEG-Easy, 86.27 on SUN-SEG-Hard, and 94.31 on CVC-612, outperforming all compared methods across every metric (S_α, E_φ^mn, F_β^w, Dice) on all three benchmarks. The method also maintains real-time inference at 31.27 FPS with 28.53M parameters and 46.77 GFLOPs, surpassing the accuracy of SLTNet (83.36 Dice, 12.36 FPS) and SALI (83.87 Dice, 18.07 FPS) while being significantly faster than ZoomNext (83.51 Dice, 9.69 FPS).

## Baselines & Comparisons

HFSTI-Net outperforms all eight baselines on every metric across all three benchmarks. Among VPS methods, it surpasses the closest competitor SALI by +1.86 Dice on SUN-SEG-Easy (88.03 vs. 86.17), +2.40 on SUN-SEG-Hard (86.27 vs. 83.87), and +5.54 on CVC-612 (94.31 vs. 88.77). VPSAM is the strongest VPS baseline on SUN-SEG-Hard (85.28 Dice) but still trails HFSTI-Net by 0.99. Among NVS methods, SLTNet (83.36 Dice on Hard) and ZoomNext (83.51) are competitive but lack temporal modeling, resulting in a 2.7–2.9 Dice gap. IPS methods (AutoSAM, WeakPolyp) lag further behind, with AutoSAM achieving only 77.37 Dice on SUN-SEG-Hard, confirming that static image-based approaches struggle with temporal variations in colonoscopy video.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper sits at the intersection of two research threads: video polyp segmentation (VPS) methods that model temporal coherence (PNS+, MAST, VPSAM, SALI) and frequency-domain feature learning in segmentation (FcaNet, FAGF-Net, frequency-spatial entanglement). It departs from prior VPS approaches that rely on implicit pixel-level temporal propagation by introducing an explicit memory bank with cross-attention (RMP), and from prior frequency-domain methods that treat spectral and spatial cues independently by proposing an interwoven dual-path fusion (HFSI). The architectural backbone (PVTv2-B2) and overall encoder-decoder structure follow standard practice, so the novelty is concentrated in the two proposed modules rather than in a new paradigm.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The practical significance lies in achieving real-time inference (31.27 FPS) while setting new SOTA on both SUN-SEG and CVC-612, which is directly relevant to clinical deployment where endoscopists need immediate feedback. The frequency-spatial interaction idea could generalize to other 'camouflaged' medical video tasks beyond colonoscopy. However, the paper acknowledges limitations with spot interference, very small low-contrast polyps, and dramatic shape changes, and validation is confined to two colonoscopy datasets, leaving open whether the frequency-spatial mechanism transfers to other modalities or more diverse clinical scenarios.
