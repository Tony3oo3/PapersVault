## Problem

Existing character animation methods rely on 2D-rendered pose images (skeletons, SMPL mesh renderings, depth maps) as motion guidance, which discards rich 4D spatial-temporal information and causes the model to blindly copy fixed-shaped poses pixel-by-pixel, producing distortions when the driving pose deviates from the reference character in shape or position. The paper asks whether raw 4D motion (3D joint coordinates over time) can be directly tokenized and used as conditioning, bypassing intermediate 2D rendering entirely.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ICLR20260347/cropped_page1_idx3.webp)

## Input / Output

The method takes a single reference character image (any visual style: photorealistic, anime, pixel art, ink drawing, or even non-human subjects) and a driving video from which NLF-Pose estimates per-frame SMPL joint rotations and root translations, converted via forward kinematics into 24×3 3D joint coordinates. The 18B variant additionally accepts a free-form text prompt for joint text-motion control. The output is a temporally coherent animated video of the reference character performing the driving motion, supporting both full-body and half-body framing at variable resolutions and aspect ratios.

## Method

MTVCraft introduces two key components. First, 4DMoT is a VQ-VAE that encodes differential 3D joint coordinates (relative to the first frame) into discrete 4D motion tokens via 2D convolutions along temporal and joint axes, a codebook of 8192×3072 entries with EMA updates, and a reconstruction-plus-commitment loss. Second, MV-DiT inserts 4D motion attention modules into DiT blocks where vision latents serve as queries and motion tokens as keys/values, with a novel 4D RoPE (t, x, y, z) applied to both token types to encode structured spatiotemporal geometry; identity is preserved by repeating and concatenating the reference image latent across all frames. A motion-aware classifier-free guidance scheme with learnable unconditional motion tokens further improves controllability. The architecture scales from a 6B CogVideoX-5B backbone to an 18B Wan-2.1-14B backbone with only zero-padding for dimension alignment and an added text cross-attention branch.

## Results

On the TikTok benchmark, MTVCraft-18B achieves FVD 276.65, FID 20.70, and FID-VID 7.31, substantially outperforming the strongest baseline Unianimate-DiT (FVD 402.14, FID 28.47, FID-VID 9.12). On the Fashion benchmark, MTVCraft-18B reaches FVD 64.88, FID 8.74, and FID-VID 4.41, again leading all competitors. The 6B variant also surpasses every baseline on both benchmarks, confirming the framework's effectiveness at smaller scale.

## Baselines & Comparisons

MTVCraft-18B outperforms all six baselines on every metric across both TikTok and Fashion benchmarks. The closest competitor on TikTok is Unianimate-DiT, which MTVCraft-18B surpasses by 125.49 FVD points (276.65 vs. 402.14), 7.77 FID points (20.70 vs. 28.47), and 1.81 FID-VID points (7.31 vs. 9.12). On Fashion, the gap narrows slightly: MTVCraft-18B leads Animate-X by 5.59 FVD (64.88 vs. 70.47) and 1.37 FID (8.74 vs. 10.11), while MimicMotion edges ahead on PSNR (23.80 vs. 23.90) but trails on FVD (80.89 vs. 64.88) and FID-VID (8.17 vs. 4.41). The 6B variant also beats all baselines, with the largest margins over ControlNeXt and Animate-X, confirming that the 4D motion tokenization design is effective even at smaller model scale. Notably, MTVCraft-18B achieves the best PSNR, SSIM, LPIPS, FID, FVD, and FID-VID simultaneously on both benchmarks, indicating consistent gains in both spatial fidelity and temporal coherence.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper departs from the dominant paradigm in character image animation, where 3D poses are rendered into 2D images (skeletons, SMPL meshes, depth maps) to condition a diffusion model. It draws inspiration from motion generation literature that tokenizes SMPL parameters via VQ-VAE (Hosseyni et al., 2025; Guo et al., 2024; Zhang et al., 2023a), but repurposes this idea by tokenizing raw 3D joint coordinates over time rather than rotation parameters, and by feeding the resulting discrete tokens into a video DiT via a novel cross-attention mechanism rather than using them for autoregressive motion prediction. The video generation backbone follows the DiT lineage (CogVideoX, Wan-2.1), and the identity-preservation strategy (repeat-and-concatenate) is simpler than the separate reference networks used by AnimateAnyone, MagicAnimate, and Unianimate-DiT.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

By replacing 2D pose renderings with compact 4D motion tokens, MTVCraft eliminates the pixel-level alignment requirement between driving poses and the reference character, enabling robust animation of arbitrary subjects (full-body, half-body, non-human, AI-generated) without retraining. The zero-shot generalization to animals and inanimate objects—despite training exclusively on human motion—suggests that differential joint-coordinate tokenization captures motion dynamics in a shape- and appearance-agnostic way, opening a path toward truly universal motion transfer. Limitations include imprecise hand articulation (underrepresented in SMPL), inability to handle multi-person scenes, and dependence on the base DiT's generation quality. Open questions include whether the 4D tokenization approach extends to non-rigid or deformable objects beyond the SMPL skeleton, and whether the discrete codebook can be made more expressive for highly complex motions.
