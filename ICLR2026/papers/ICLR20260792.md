## Problem

Point cloud analysis models face a fundamental tension between accuracy and energy efficiency: Transformers capture global dependencies but incur quadratic complexity, Mamba offers linear complexity yet lacks natural handling of unordered point clouds, and existing SNN-based methods still trail ANN baselines by significant margins. No prior SNN architecture effectively unifies local attention-based feature extraction with global state-space modeling within a single energy-efficient spiking framework for point clouds.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ICLR20260792/cropped_page3_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The model takes a raw 3D point cloud P ∈ R^{N×3} (typically N=1024 for ModelNet40, N=2048 for ScanObjectNN) and processes it over T=3 discrete timesteps using Integrate-and-Fire neurons with a fixed threshold of 1.0. Farthest Point Sampling selects L=128 center points, each expanded into a k=4 KNN local patch, forming the token sequence fed into the network. Outputs are task-specific: class logits for shape/few-shot classification, per-point part probabilities for ShapeNetPart segmentation, and per-point semantic class scores for S3DIS and Semantic KITTI scene segmentation.

## Method

3DSMT stacks 12 Spiking Hybrid Blocks, each pairing a Spiking Local Offset Attention (SLOA) module with a Spiking Mamba Block (SMB). SLOA performs local feature propagation via K-Norm and aggregation via K-Pool, then computes spiking Q/K/V where the attention matrix is obtained through logical AND and accumulation operations instead of softmax-weighted MAC, exploiting the binary sparsity of spikes to eliminate floating-point multiply-accumulate cost. SMB encodes the spiking sequence through a bidirectional SSM (original Mamba L+SSM combined with Mamba3D's channel-flipped C-SSM) and applies a spiking gating matrix via Hadamard product for feature selection, all without imposing an explicit point ordering. The entire network is trained end-to-end with surrogate gradients on IF neurons, and a Spiking Position Encoding layer (alternating SN and MLP) injects spatial coordinates into each block to preserve geometric awareness.

## Results

On ModelNet40, 3DSMT achieves 95.2% OA (with voting) and 94.7% OA (without voting), with only 4.3 mJ energy consumption and 1.3G FLOPs. On the three ScanObjectNN variants, 3DSMT reaches 92.0%, 92.1%, and 90.6% OA (with voting), setting new SNN records. For part segmentation on ShapeNetPart, 3DSMT achieves 85.1% Ins.mIoU, and for semantic segmentation on S3DIS it reaches 70.2% mIoU at 11.4 mJ—both the best among SNN-based methods.

## Baselines & Comparisons

Against the strongest SNN baseline SPM (ICCV 2025), 3DSMT wins on all four classification benchmarks by 7.8% (PB_T50_RS), 1.9% (OBJ_BG), 1.1% (OBJ_ONLY), and 2.9% (ModelNet40), while also reducing energy from 5.4 to 4.3 mJ. Against the top ANN method SIM (CVPR 2025), 3DSMT achieves a large 4.7% gain on the hardest variant PB_T50_RS (92.0 vs 87.3) and a 2.5% gain on ModelNet40 (95.2 vs 92.7), though SIM retains a slight edge on OBJ_BG (92.3 vs 92.1) and OBJ_ONLY (91.4 vs 90.6). Compared to the Transformer-based PTv2 (NeurIPS 2022), 3DSMT improves ModelNet40 OA by 1.5% while cutting energy by ~18× (4.3 vs 78.7 mJ). Against the Mamba-based PCM (AAAI 2025), 3DSMT gains 1.8% on ModelNet40 with 48× lower energy (4.3 vs 207.0 mJ). On few-shot classification (Table 2), 3DSMT matches or slightly trails Mamba3D (92.8 vs 92.6 in 5-way 10-shot) but far outperforms the SNN baseline SpikePointNet (52.3%). On S3DIS semantic segmentation, 3DSMT (70.2% mIoU) trails the best ANN method PTv3 (73.6%) by 3.4 points but uses only 11.4 mJ versus PTv3's 687.7 mJ, and beats the prior SNN method E-3DSNN by 2.8 points.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper sits at the intersection of three active research threads: (1) Mamba-based point cloud processing (Mamba3D, PointMamba), which introduced linear-complexity SSM scanning with K-Norm/K-Pool local feature propagation and channel-flipped C-SSM for unordered data; (2) bidirectional SSM design from Vision Mamba, adapted here as L-SSM + C-SSM scanning; and (3) the emerging line of SNN-based point cloud methods (Spike PointNet, P2SResLNet, SPT, SPM), which demonstrated that spiking architectures can approach ANN accuracy at a fraction of the energy. 3DSMT departs from prior SNN point cloud work by being the first to jointly integrate a spiking Transformer-style local attention (SLOA) and a spiking Mamba block (SMB) within a single hybrid architecture, rather than relying on a single backbone. It also departs from ANN-based Mamba3D by replacing all continuous activations with IF spiking neurons and using logical AND/accumulation operations in place of softmax-weighted MACs.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The practical significance lies in closing the long-standing accuracy gap between SNN and ANN point cloud models while achieving 4–50× energy reduction, which is critical for deploying 3D perception on power-constrained edge devices (drones, mobile robots, AR/VR headsets). The paper demonstrates that the hybrid local-attention + global-SSM design transfers well across classification, few-shot, and segmentation tasks, suggesting a general recipe for energy-efficient 3D architectures. Open questions include whether the approach scales to object detection and large-scale scene understanding, whether the remaining MAC operations (first Conv1D, final FC) can be eliminated for full neuromorphic deployment, and whether the 3-timestep IF design is optimal or whether longer temporal windows could further improve accuracy without proportional energy cost.
