## Problem

Existing single-cell foundation models are optimized for predictive tasks (annotation, classification) rather than open-ended mechanistic reasoning, and the RLVR paradigm that has proven effective for math and code cannot be directly applied to biology because most biological outcomes are inherently non-verifiable—there is no single correct gene sequence or perturbation response to check against. This paper asks whether reliable, intrinsic reward signals can be generated from the internal logical structure of biological problems themselves, enabling reinforcement learning alignment without ground-truth labels.

## Input / Output

Cells are encoded as 'Cell Sentences'—descending-ranked sequences of the top-K expressed gene names rendered as text—making them directly consumable by an off-the-shelf LLM (Llama-3.2-3B) without numerical embedding layers. Perturbations are structured tuples (operation ∈ {knockdown, overexpression}, target gene), and the model produces a chain-of-thought reasoning trace followed by a final answer (a categorical label or a full ranked gene sequence). The four tasks form a 2×2 matrix over Cell Identity (annotation, conditional generation) and Cell Dynamics (sensitivity prediction, perturbation response generation).

## Method

CellDuality introduces the principle of Complementary Task Duality: for each primal task (e.g., predict post-perturbation cell state), a complementary dual task reconstructs the unknown input component (e.g., the original perturbation) from the primal output plus known conditions, and the reconstruction fidelity serves as an intrinsic RL reward. Training proceeds in two stages: (1) SFT cold-start on teacher-generated CoT traces filtered by rejection sampling (classification) or Rank-Weighted Jaccard Similarity (generation), teaching both forward and inverse reasoning; (2) GRPO-based self-supervised RL on large unlabeled data, where categorical rewards (exact label reconstruction) are used for generative tasks and continuous RWJS-based rewards (conditional gene inpainting quality) are used for classification tasks. The key insight is that a biologically coherent forward prediction must be logically reversible—rewarding this bidirectional consistency eliminates the need for any ground-truth verification during the RL phase while still driving the model toward mechanistically sound reasoning.

## Results

On the sci-Plex3 OOD perturbation response benchmark, CellDuality (self-supervised RL) achieves scFID 0.038, MMD 0.019, and Wasserstein distance 0.245, closing 28–50% of the performance gap to the fully supervised C2S-Scale (GRPO w/GT) oracle (scFID 0.02, MMD 0.01, Wasserstein 0.21) while requiring no ground-truth labels during RL. On cell type annotation, CellDuality reaches 96.34% accuracy on He-2020-Liver (ID) and 88.45% on Bastidas-Ponce-2019 (OOD), and on drug sensitivity it attains 99.12% accuracy on GSE149383 (ID) and 96.12% on the cross-species GSE110894 (OOD). The self-supervised RL stage consistently improves over the SFT-only baseline across all four tasks, with the largest gains on generative tasks (e.g., scFID drops from 0.045 to 0.038 on sci-Plex3).

## Baselines & Comparisons

Against the supervised RLVR oracle C2S-Scale (GRPO w/GT), CellDuality narrows the gap substantially on sci-Plex3 (scFID 0.038 vs 0.02; MMD 0.019 vs 0.01) but does not fully match it, confirming the cost of forgoing ground-truth rewards. On cell type annotation OOD, CellDuality (88.45% Acc on Bastidas-Ponce-2019) trails InstructCell (91.10%) and scGPT (91.43%) by 2–3 points, though it outperforms scBERT (86.67%) and Cell2Sentence (80.59%). On drug sensitivity, CellDuality (99.12% Acc on GSE149383) edges out Geneformer (98.23%) and InstructCell (97.35%) but falls slightly short of scBERT (99.56%); on the cross-species OOD set GSE110894, it is competitive with scBERT (96.12% vs 95.80%) but below InstructCell (97.20%). For conditional cell generation, CellDuality achieves the best kNN accuracy (26.34% at k=3) among all baselines, surpassing Cell2Sentence-160M (25.88%) and scDiffusion (23.35%), though it lags Cell2Sentence on Gromov-Wasserstein distance (61.45 vs 54.30). Overall, CellDuality's self-supervised RL stage delivers the largest relative gains on generative tasks where SFT alone is weakest, while classification tasks see more modest improvements since SFT already reaches near-saturation accuracy.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper adapts the dual preference optimization principle from DuPO (She et al., 2025), which enabled self-verification in mathematical reasoning, to the fundamentally different setting of single-cell biology where outputs are stochastic and high-dimensional. It extends the RLVR paradigm—previously limited to domains with deterministic verifiers (math, code)—by replacing ground-truth rewards with intrinsic consistency signals from a bidirectional primal/dual reasoning loop. The cell representation follows Cell2Sentence (LeVine et al., 2024), and the policy optimization uses GRPO, but the core departure from prior work is the task-duality reward mechanism that makes RL feasible without any external verification labels.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The framework addresses a genuine bottleneck in biological foundation models: the inability to apply RLVR when ground-truth verification is infeasible (e.g., no single correct gene sequence for a cell type). By closing 35–56% of the performance gap to a fully supervised oracle on OOD perturbation prediction without any ground-truth labels during RL, it offers a scalable path for data-scarce biological domains. However, the duality reward enforces logical consistency rather than biological correctness, the gains on classification tasks are modest, and the 3B model scale leaves open whether the approach scales. Extending beyond transcriptomics to other omics modalities and validating pathway-level interpretability remain important open questions.
