## Problem

Prior human-centric foundation models like Sapiens relied solely on masked image modeling (MAE) pretraining, which preserves low-level spatial detail but yields features that require substantial task supervision to express semantics reliably. Conversely, contrastive learning injects semantic invariance but erodes photometric cues (e.g., color) critical for dense human tasks like albedo and normal estimation. Sapiens2 addresses this by unifying both objectives and scaling to 4K resolution with a hierarchical architecture, while also curating a 1-billion-image human dataset to improve generalization.

## Input / Output

The model accepts RGB images at a native 1024×768 (1K) resolution, with a hierarchical 4K variant (4096×3072) that decodes task outputs at 2K. Outputs are task-specific: 308-keypoint heatmaps (243 face, 40 hands, remainder body), 29-class body-part segmentation masks, focal-length-normalized per-pixel 3D pointmaps, unit surface-normal maps, and diffuse albedo maps. The model family spans 0.4B to 5B parameters, with the 5B variant reaching 15.7 TFLOPs at 1K.

## Method

Sapiens2 pretrains with a joint objective combining MAE pixel reconstruction (anchoring features in pixel space for dense fidelity) and a DINOv3-style student-teacher contrastive loss on [CLS] tokens (organizing features semantically across views), weighted as L = L_MAE + 0.4·L_CL. For 4K inputs, the backbone uses a hierarchical design: initial windowed self-attention layers capture local texture, then [CLS]-guided pooling reduces the token grid before global self-attention fuses long-range context; masking is applied after the local stage to prevent information leakage across masked regions. Architectural upgrades include grouped-query attention in mid-depth blocks, SwiGLU feed-forward layers, QK-Norm, and RMSNorm for stable long-schedule training at scale. Post-training fine-tunes lightweight task heads on ~1M high-quality labels per task (pose, segmentation, pointmap, normals, albedo), with in-the-wild annotations added for pose and segmentation to improve generalization beyond capture-studio data.

## Results

On dense probing (frozen backbone, lightweight decoder), Sapiens2-5B achieves 74.7 mAP on 308-keypoint pose, 69.6% mIoU on 29-class body-part segmentation, 0.358 L2 on pointmap, 13.5° mean angular error on normals, and 3.12×10⁻² MAE on albedo — surpassing DINOv3-7B (6.7B params) on every task. After full fine-tuning, Sapiens2-5B reaches 82.3 mAP on pose (+4.0 over Sapiens-2B), 82.5% mIoU on segmentation (+24.3 over Sapiens-2B), 0.167 L2 on pointmap (beating MoGe's 0.202), 6.73° mean angular error on normals (45.6% lower than DAViD-L's 10.73°), and 0.0119 MAE / 32.6 dB PSNR on albedo.

## Baselines & Comparisons

Sapiens2-5B outperforms DINOv3-7B (the strongest general-purpose baseline) on all five dense-probing tasks: +6.5 mAP on pose, +2.0 mIoU on segmentation, −0.040 L2 on pointmap, −0.7° on normals, and −0.36×10⁻² on albedo. Against its direct predecessor Sapiens-2B, Sapiens2-5B gains +11.3 mAP (pose), +4.5 mIoU (segmentation), −0.157 L2 (pointmap), −1.1° (normals), and −0.60×10⁻² (albedo) in dense probing; after fine-tuning the gaps widen to +4.0 mAP (pose) and +24.3 mIoU (segmentation). On pointmap, Sapiens2-5B (0.167 L2) beats MoGe (0.202), VGGT (0.217), DUSt3R (0.349), and UniDepth (0.368) at every model size. On normals, even the smallest Sapiens2-0.4B (8.63° mean) surpasses DAViD-L (10.73°) and Sapiens-2B (12.38°), with Sapiens2-5B reaching 6.73° and 96.13% of pixels within 30°. On albedo, Sapiens2-5B achieves 0.0119 MAE and 32.6 dB PSNR, outperforming all prior methods while remaining feedforward (vs. diffusion-based alternatives). The Perception Encoder (PE-L/PE-H) baselines are substantially weaker across all tasks, confirming that human-centric pretraining on 1B images yields markedly better transfer than general-purpose encoders of similar scale.

## Relation to Prior Work

SAPIENS2 is the direct successor to Sapiens (Khirodkar et al., 2024), extending it along three axes: pretraining objective, data scale, and architecture. The key methodological departure is replacing pure MAE pretraining with a joint MAE + DINOv3-style contrastive objective, building on the complementary-roles hypothesis explored in CMAE (Huang et al., 2023) but validated at billion-parameter scale across dense human-centric tasks. Architecturally, it imports stability and efficiency techniques from frontier LLMs (GQA, QK-Norm, RMSNorm, SwiGLU) and adopts a hierarchical windowed-attention design (inspired by Hiera and MViTv2) to make 4K-resolution dense prediction tractable, while the 1B curated human image dataset scales the data axis 3× over Sapiens' 300M corpus.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

SAPIENS2 establishes a new reference point for human-centric vision: a 5B-parameter, 4K-capable foundation model with SOTA results on pose, segmentation, normals, pointmap, and albedo, all from a single pretrained backbone with lightweight task heads. The 1B high-quality human image dataset and the 308-keypoint in-the-wild test set are substantial community resources. Open questions include whether the MAE+CL combination generalizes beyond human-centric domains, whether the 4K hierarchical advantage persists for tasks less sensitive to spatial detail, and how the 15-TFLOP inference cost of the 5B model constrains real-time deployment in AR/VR pipelines where it is most needed.
