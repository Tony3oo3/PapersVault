## Problem

LVSM's monolithic full self-attention over all input and target tokens suffers from quadratic complexity in the number of input views and forces rigid parameter sharing between heterogeneous tokens (content-rich input views and pose-only target queries), limiting both efficiency and the model's ability to learn specialized representations for scene understanding versus view rendering.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ICLR20261369/cropped_page1_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The model takes N posed RGB images (patchified at 8×8 with Plücker ray embeddings concatenated in the hidden dimension) and M target camera poses (encoded solely as Plücker rays) at 256 or 512 resolution. It outputs M rendered novel view images of the same resolution. A key operational detail is that input views are processed independently per-view, enabling KV-cache-based incremental inference where adding a new input or target view costs near-constant time rather than reprocessing the entire sequence.

## Method

Efficient-LVSM replaces LVSM's single monolithic transformer with a dual-stream co-refinement architecture: an Input Encoder applies intra-view self-attention (each view processed separately, reducing complexity from O(N²M) to O(NM+N)), while a Target Decoder alternates self-attention among target tokens with cross-attention querying the corresponding encoder layer at every depth rather than only the final layer. This per-layer co-refinement lets the decoder access fine-grained details from early encoder layers and high-level semantics from later ones. The model further employs REPA-style distillation from DINOv3 (layer 8 features aligned via a 3-layer MLP with Smooth L1 loss) to inject pretrained visual semantics into both input and target token streams. The decoupled structure naturally supports KV-caching of input features, enabling incremental inference for interactive applications.

## Results

On RealEstate10K (scene-level, 2 input views, 512 resolution), Efficient-LVSM achieves 29.86 dB PSNR, 0.905 SSIM, and 0.147 LPIPS, surpassing LVSM Decoder-Only (29.53 dB) by 0.33 dB and GS-LRM (28.10 dB) by 1.76 dB. On object-level benchmarks, it reaches 32.65 dB PSNR on ABO and 32.92 dB on GSO at 512 resolution, outperforming LVSM Decoder-Only by 0.55 and 0.56 dB respectively. In terms of efficiency, Efficient-LVSM runs at 24.78 ms inference latency (vs. 109.37 ms for LVSM Decoder-Only), requires only 50% of LVSM's training time, and achieves up to 14.9× speedup over LVSM Decoder-Only with 16 input views.

## Baselines & Comparisons

Efficient-LVSM outperforms all 10 baselines on the primary RealEstate10K scene-level benchmark. Against the closest competitor LVSM Decoder-Only, it gains 0.33 dB PSNR (29.86 vs. 29.53) at 512 resolution while being 4.4× faster in inference (24.78 ms vs. 109.37 ms) and requiring 50% less training time. Over LVSM Encoder-Decoder, the improvement is 1.31 dB PSNR. Against GS-LRM, the gap widens to 1.76 dB PSNR with only 26% of its GFLOPS. On object-level benchmarks (ABO and GSO at 512 resolution), Efficient-LVSM leads LVSM Decoder-Only by 0.55 dB (ABO) and 0.56 dB (GSO), and surpasses GS-LRM by 3.56 dB (ABO) and 2.40 dB (GSO). All earlier methods—pixelNeRF, ViewCrafter, GPNR, SEVA, Du et al., pixelSplat, DepthSplat, and MVSplat—are outperformed by 2.4–9.4 dB PSNR, with the largest margins over optimization-based and diffusion-based approaches. The only metric where a baseline slightly edges out Efficient-LVSM is LPIPS on RealEstate10K (0.147 vs. 0.141 for LVSM Dec-Only), though the PSNR and SSIM advantages are consistent.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper directly extends LVSM (Jin et al., 2025), which introduced a monolithic full self-attention transformer for geometry-free novel view synthesis, by arguing that its unified attention over heterogeneous input and target tokens is both computationally wasteful (quadratic in input views) and representationally suboptimal (shared parameters for content-rich inputs vs. pose-only queries). The proposed dual-stream co-refinement architecture draws conceptual parallels to MM-DiT's separate projections for modalities but goes further with a fully decoupled encoder-decoder where each encoder layer feeds its corresponding decoder layer. REPA (Yu et al., 2025) is incorporated as a distillation mechanism from DINOv3, though the paper shows it is far more effective on the decoupled architecture than on LVSM's monolithic design.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The practical impact is significant: 50% training time, 4.4× faster inference, and KV-cache-enabled incremental inference make feedforward NVS substantially more viable for interactive and multi-view applications. The zero-shot generalization to unseen input view counts broadens the model's applicability beyond its training configuration. However, the paper acknowledges that the 199M-parameter model remains far from industrial deployment, and the path to real-time, memory-constrained settings requires further compression. An open question is whether the co-refinement depth (12+12 layers) is truly necessary or whether the linear-complexity design could be pushed to even smaller models without quality loss.
