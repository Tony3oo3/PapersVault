## Problem

KV-cache memory grows linearly with sequence length, creating severe bottlenecks on resource-constrained GPUs during long-sequence LLM inference. Existing offloading-based methods construct memory pages in original token order, scattering semantically related tokens across pages and causing low retrieval hit rates, while also lacking dynamic update mechanisms that degrade performance in long-generation tasks such as chain-of-thought reasoning.

## Input / Output

During prefill, IceCache consumes the full prompt sequence and builds a per-attention-head DCI-tree index over transformed key embeddings, offloading KV pages to CPU. During decoding, each step takes the current query embedding and retrieves only the top-k most relevant KV pages (budget as small as 64 tokens) from CPU back to GPU, producing the next generated token. The KV-cache is organized as semantically-clustered fixed-size pages rather than sequential token blocks, with sink and window pages always kept resident on GPU.

## Method

IceCache replaces PagedAttention's sequential page layout with a hierarchical DCI-tree that clusters semantically similar key embeddings into the same memory pages, so that tokens relevant to a given query are co-located. During decoding, a head-specific Multi-level DCI (M-DCI) approximate nearest-neighbor search traverses the tree to identify the top-k most relevant pages per attention head, improving retrieval precision over order-based baselines. A bulk-loading scheme aggregates selected non-contiguous pages into contiguous CPU and GPU buffers for a single high-throughput PCIe transfer, and a pipelining strategy overlaps CPU-side DCI indexing with GPU prefilling to hide indexing latency. The DCI-tree supports incremental insertion of new tokens during long-generation, maintaining semantic locality as the cache grows.

## Results

On LongBench with Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct, IceCache achieves an average accuracy of 49.0 with a 256-token KV-cache budget, closing the gap to Full KV (49.5) to just 0.5 points and surpassing the best baseline PQCache (47.3) by 1.7 points. Remarkably, with only a 64-token budget (25% of PQCache's), IceCache still scores 47.8, exceeding PQCache's 47.3. On GSM8K CoT reasoning (10% budget, Mistral-7B), IceCache reaches 47.4% accuracy vs. 46.2% for PQCache and 48.2% for Full KV. On RULER at 250k context length, IceCache matches or exceeds Full KV on all three subtasks (Single-NIAH 100%, Multi-keys NIAH 93.0%, QA 91.7%).

## Baselines & Comparisons

IceCache outperforms all six baselines on LongBench (Llama-3.1-8B) at the 256-token budget: it beats PQCache (47.3) by 1.7 points, OmniKV (46.3) by 2.7, MagicPig (44.6) by 4.4, ArkVale (42.8) by 6.2, SnapKV (40.8) by 8.2, and StreamingLLM (33.5) by 15.5. At the 64-token budget, IceCache (47.8) still exceeds PQCache (47.3) despite using only 25% of the cache, and surpasses MagicPig (44.6) by 3.2 points. On Mistral-7B-Instruct, IceCache (41.7 at budget 256) leads MagicPig (39.1) by 2.6 points, and at budget 64 it ties MagicPig (39.0 vs. 39.1) while using 4× less memory. On GSM8K CoT, IceCache (47.4%) edges out PQCache (46.2%) by 1.2 points and nearly matches Full KV (48.2%), while ArkVale drops sharply to 30.9%. On RULER at 250k tokens, IceCache matches Full KV on Single-NIAH (100%) and slightly exceeds it on Multi-keys NIAH (93.0 vs. 91.0), demonstrating that semantic clustering provides a robustness advantage at extreme context lengths.

## Relation to Prior Work

IceCache builds on two foundational ideas: PagedAttention's fixed-size page abstraction for KV-cache storage, and Prioritized Dynamic Continuous Indexing (P-DCI) for approximate nearest-neighbor search. The key departure is from Quest and ArkVale, which also leverage PagedAttention but construct pages according to original token order, causing semantically relevant tokens to scatter across many pages. IceCache instead introduces a hierarchical DCI-tree that clusters tokens by key-embedding similarity, so each page contains a semantic cluster rather than a positional window. This reframes page selection from a positional problem to a retrieval problem, enabling head-specific, query-aware page loading with higher hit rates.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

KV-cache memory is the primary bottleneck for long-context LLM inference, and this paper demonstrates that a 256-token budget (or even 64) can retain ~99% of full-cache accuracy across diverse tasks and model architectures, making long-context inference feasible on consumer-grade hardware. The dynamic DCI-tree update mechanism specifically addresses the performance degradation that plagues prior offloading methods in long-generation tasks (CoT reasoning, summarization), a gap the paper identifies but that remains underexplored in the literature. Open questions include whether the semantic-clustering assumption degrades at extreme context lengths beyond 250k tokens, how the method interacts with speculative decoding, and whether the per-head DCI-tree overhead becomes prohibitive for models with very large head counts.
