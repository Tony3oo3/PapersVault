## Problem

Vision-Language Models for remote sensing learn an implicit, end-to-end mapping from pixels to output, collapsing the entire reasoning process into a monolithic transformation that is opaque and prone to hallucination. Existing chain-of-thought approaches in Earth Observation either rely on parametric world knowledge for high-level semantic deductions rather than visual investigation, or present visual evidence as non-localizable text with no verifiable link to specific pixel regions. The critical gap is the absence of a perceptually-grounded reasoning paradigm where every analytical step is explicitly tied to falsifiable spatial evidence.

## Input / Output

The model consumes a remote sensing image at variable resolution and aspect ratio (large-scale imagery such as DOTAv2 is tiled into 800×800 patches) paired with a free-form text query. It produces a two-part output: a structured reasoning trace enclosed in <think>... tags that follows a Planning-Grounding-Synthesize protocol with explicit spatial references (bounding boxes, normalized coordinates, relative positions), followed by a task-specific final answer in <answer>...</answer> tags (e.g., oriented bounding boxes for detection, integer counts, class labels, natural-language captions, or VQA responses). The spatial references in the trace are the key differentiator: they make each intermediate claim independently falsifiable against the image.

## Method

RSThinker is initialized from GLM-4.1V-9B-Base, whose Aimv2-Huge vision encoder handles variable resolutions via a dynamic positional encoding scheme that normalizes patch coordinates to [-1,1] and samples the pre-trained position table via bicubic interpolation, complemented by a 3D-RoPE language decoder for spatial awareness. Training proceeds in two stages: first, supervised fine-tuning on Geo-CoT380k (384,591 structured rationales generated by GPT-4V under strict conditioning on verified bounding boxes, image captions, and in-context exemplars to minimize hallucinated reasoning) instills the Planning-Grounding-Synthesize cognitive architecture; second, Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) refines the generative policy toward factual correctness using task-specific outcome rewards (IoU for grounding, MAE-normalized for counting, mAP@0.5 for detection, accuracy for VQA/classification, and weighted BLEU-4/METEOR/CIDEr/ROUGE-L for captioning), with a KL-divergence penalty anchoring the policy to the SFT checkpoint. The ablation reveals a symbiotic dependency: GRPO applied without the CoT-structured SFT scaffold fails to elicit faithful reasoning, while SFT alone leaves sequence-level faithfulness unrefined, confirming that structural instillation and policy refinement must be decoupled into separate stages.

## Results

RSThinker achieves state-of-the-art performance across all evaluated remote sensing tasks. On Visual Grounding, it reaches 80.79 mIoU on VRSBench-VG and 89.02 mIoU on DIOR-RSVG, surpassing the next-best model (GLM-4.1V-Thinking) by 20+ points. On Object Counting, it reduces MAE to 2.728 on DOTAv2 (vs. 8.072 for GLM-4.1V-Thinking) and 0.242 on HRRSD (vs. 0.903). On Scene Classification, it attains 96.89% on RESISC45 and 98.17% on AID, and on VQA it scores 82.84% on the Category subtask of VRSBench-VQA, far exceeding all baselines.

## Baselines & Comparisons

RSThinker outperforms all 11 baselines across nearly every task and metric. Against the strongest general-purpose reasoning model, GLM-4.1V-Thinking (its own base architecture), RSThinker gains +20.1 mIoU on VRSBench-VG grounding, reduces DOTAv2 counting MAE from 8.072 to 2.728, and lifts VQA Category accuracy from 42.54% to 82.84%. Among remote sensing specialists, it surpasses VHM (the closest RS competitor on classification at 91.33% RESISC45) by 5.56 points, and beats EarthDial on all VQA subtasks despite EarthDial's higher CIDEr on NWPU-Captions (123.6 vs. 94.81). Commercial models (ChatGPT-5, Gemini-2.0-flash, Claude-sonnet-4) are consistently outperformed by 10–40+ points on grounding and counting, with ChatGPT-5 being the strongest commercial baseline on classification (82.22% RESISC45) yet still trailing RSThinker by 14.67 points. The ablation (Table 8) confirms that the SFT-with-CoT + GRPO combination is essential: removing CoT rationales from SFT drops VQA accuracy from 77.24% to 74.09%, and removing GRPO drops it to 74.20%.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the GLM-4.1V-9B-Base architecture (Aimv2-Huge vision encoder with dynamic positional encoding and 3D-RoPE decoder) by applying a two-stage alignment strategy—SFT followed by GRPO—borrowed directly from the DeepSeek-R1 paradigm for LLM reasoning. It departs from prior remote sensing VLMs (GeoChat, VHM, SkySenseGPT, EarthDial) that treat reasoning as an implicit end-to-end pixel-to-text mapping, and from generalist grounded CoT frameworks (Visual CoT, VoCoT, Argus) that target salient objects in natural or medical imagery rather than the dense, tiny-object, scale-varied scenes characteristic of Earth Observation. The key conceptual departure is mandating perceptual grounding: every analytical step must carry an explicit, falsifiable spatial reference (bounding box, coordinate, direction), which prior remote sensing reasoning work (SegEarth-R1, RemoteReasoner, SkySense-O) lacks.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The Geo-CoT380k dataset and RSThinker model provide the first concrete pathway from opaque VLM outputs to verifiable, auditable reasoning traces in remote sensing—a critical requirement for high-stakes applications like disaster response and environmental monitoring where a wrong answer must be detectable, not just incorrect. The ablation revealing that GRPO without the CoT cognitive scaffold actually degrades counting performance (MAE 4.51 vs 2.93) is a practical warning that RL cannot substitute for structured supervision of the reasoning process itself. Open questions include whether GPT-4V-generated rationales introduce systematic stylistic biases that limit generalization, whether the framework extends to multi-sensor or multi-temporal data, and whether the very large margins over baselines (partly attributable to domain-specific SFT on 384k samples) would persist under more compute-matched comparisons.
