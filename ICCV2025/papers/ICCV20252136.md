## Problem

Vision-Language Models are increasingly deployed in safety-critical, dynamic real-world settings, yet their robustness to the 3D variations (rotation, translation, scaling) that naturally occur in such environments remains largely unexplored. Prior robustness evaluations focus on 2D perturbations (style shifts, corruptions, pixel-level adversarial noise) or rely on explicit 3D assets and dense multi-view observations, leaving a critical gap: can VLMs withstand worst-case 3D transformations captured from a single natural image and physically reproducible in the real world?

## Input / Output

The method takes a single natural RGB image as input—no 3D model, multi-view captures, or explicit scene geometry are required—and produces physically reproducible adversarial 3D transformation (Adv-3DT) images along with an optimized 6-dimensional transformation parameter distribution p*(Θ) = {yaw, pitch, roll, Δx, Δy, scale}. The output images are constrained to bounded transformation ranges to preserve human recognizability, and the distributional (rather than point) output enables sampling multiple adversarial viewpoints. The framework also yields MM3DTBench, a 215-sample VQA benchmark with candidate answers and orientation annotations for standardized evaluation.

## Method

AdvDreamer integrates three components into a black-box adversarial optimization loop. First, a zero-shot Monocular Pose Manipulation (MPM) pipeline performs the 3D transformation: Grounded-SAM segments the foreground, diffusion inpainting fills the background, a Large Reconstruction Model (TripoSR) reconstructs and re-renders the object under sampled 3D parameters, and a diffusion-based composition model (AnyDoor) re-composites foreground and background with consistent shadows and occlusions. Second, a Naturalness Reward Model (NRM) built on DINOv2 with dual-stream MLP heads predicts visual fidelity and physical plausibility scores, providing continuous regularization that prevents the optimizer from converging to hallucinated or physically implausible artifacts. Third, an Inverse Semantic Probability (ISP) loss operates solely in the visual-text alignment space via cosine similarity between the visual encoder output and text encoder embeddings of candidate labels, making the adversarial objective architecture- and task-agnostic. The full optimization over the 6D parameter space is solved with CMA-ES, a query-based evolution strategy that samples K candidates per iteration, ranks them by ISP loss then NRM score, and updates the Gaussian mean and covariance—converging in approximately 15 iterations at ~0.28 GPU-hours per sample on a single RTX 3090.

## Results

On ImageNet zero-shot classification, AdvDreamer's worst-case Adv-3DT samples (Θ*) reduce accuracy by 65–80% across all tested VLMs: OpenCLIP ViT-B/16 drops from 98.0% to 18.0% (↓80%), BLIP-2 ViT-G/14 from 81.0% to 15.7% (↓65%), and OpenCLIP ViT-G/14 from 96.4% to 18.7% (↓78%). On open-ended tasks, GPT-Score for image captioning decreases by ~50% and GPT-Acc for VQA drops by 25% for GPT-4o (75.4% → 50.3%). Physical-world reproduction of Adv-3DT samples still causes 49% classification accuracy drop (100% → 51.3%) and 50–55% VQA degradation, confirming the attacks are physically realizable.

## Baselines & Comparisons

The paper does not benchmark against prior adversarial 3D methods (e.g., ViewFool, GMVFool) in a head-to-head comparison, but establishes internal baselines: Clean (no transformation) and Random (10 random 3D transformations). AdvDreamer's optimized distribution p*(Θ) consistently outperforms Random by 10–17 percentage points in accuracy degradation across all models (e.g., 44% vs 35% for OpenCLIP ViT-B/16), while the single worst-case point Θ* achieves 2–3× the degradation of Random (80% vs 35%). For transfer attacks, using OpenCLIP or BLIP as surrogate models yields degradation comparable to direct attacks (e.g., 56% drop on OpenAI CLIP ViT-B/16 via OpenCLIP surrogate vs 80% direct), with p*(Θ) showing smaller transfer gaps than Θ*, indicating the distribution captures more generalizable vulnerability regions. In physical-world experiments, physically reproduced Adv-3DT samples retain 49% classification degradation and 50–55% VQA degradation, though with a noticeable gap versus digital attacks (80% digital vs 49% physical), attributed to camera parameter fluctuations and data distribution differences.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the adversarial viewpoint evaluation line (ViewFool, GMVFool, Strike-with-a-Pose) from image classifiers to VLMs, but departs fundamentally in methodology: rather than relying on explicit 3D assets, dense multi-view captures, or NeRF reconstructions, AdvDreamer uses a generative pipeline (Large Reconstruction Models + diffusion-based composition) to synthesize adversarial 3D transformations from a single image. The Inverse Semantic Probability loss is a novel task-agnostic objective that operates solely in the visual-textual alignment space, contrasting with prior task-specific end-to-end optimization. The CMA-ES-based distributional optimization over 3D parameters follows the ViewFool/GMFool paradigm of learning a distribution rather than a single worst-case pose, but applies it in a black-box setting where gradient propagation through generative components is infeasible.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The introduction of MM3DTBench as the first dedicated benchmark for VLM 3D-variation robustness, combined with physical-world validation, provides the community with a concrete evaluation tool for safety-critical deployment scenarios (autonomous driving, robotics). The finding that cross-architecture transferability is strong (OpenCLIP-optimized attacks degrade GPT-4o by ~25% on VQA) suggests a fundamental limitation in current image-text pretraining data rather than a model-specific flaw, pointing toward data-level solutions. Open questions include whether the ISP loss generalizes to non-rigid deformations, whether the 6D parameterization captures all relevant real-world 3D variations, and how to actually train VLMs to be robust to these adversarial 3D regions.
