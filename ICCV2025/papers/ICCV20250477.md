## Problem

Diffusion-based image super-resolution produces rich but hallucinated high-frequency details that are inconsistent across viewpoints, causing geometric distortions and blurring when used to train 3D scene representations. Video super-resolution enforces temporal coherence implicitly but does not guarantee explicit 3D view consistency and often sacrifices fine-grained detail. The paper addresses how to harness the detail-generation power of diffusion models while enforcing multi-view geometric consistency at the 3D scene level.

## Input / Output

The method takes a set of low-resolution RGB images (downsampled ×8 or ×16, then upsampled ×2 or ×4) along with their camera poses as input. A frozen StableSR-Turbo diffusion model (4 denoising steps) and its VAE encoder/decoder are used as a fixed prior; only the 3D Gaussian Splatting parameters are optimized. The output is an optimized Mip-Splatting scene that renders high-resolution novel views with cross-view structural coherence, evaluated at resolutions such as 504×378 (LLFF) and native MipNeRF360 scales.

## Method

3DSR embeds a 3D Gaussian Splatting representation (Mip-Splatting) directly inside the diffusion sampling loop as a spatial consistency filter. At each denoising step, the frozen diffusion model predicts a clean latent x̂₀ from the noisy latent x_t; this is decoded to HR images, which are used to train the 3DGS. The 3DGS then re-renders those same viewpoints, and the rendered images are re-encoded into a 3D-consistent latent ẍ₀ that replaces x̂₀ in the posterior update to compute x_{t−1}. This x₀-guided mechanism ensures that hallucinated details are spatially regularized by the 3D geometry before the next denoising step. The 3DGS is optimized with a combined loss: an HR term (L1 + D-SSIM between the diffusion-decoded images and the 3DGS renderings) plus a subsampled LR term (between the original LR inputs and downsampled renderings) to anchor fidelity to the observations. The entire pipeline requires no fine-tuning of the diffusion or video models, and the 3D representation is swappable (NeRF, GS, etc.).

## Results

On LLFF (504×378, ×4 upsampling), 3DSR achieves 24.21 PSNR, 0.754 SSIM, 0.181 LPIPS, and 20.73 FID, outperforming all baselines on these metrics. On MipNeRF360 (×8 downsampled, ×4 upsampling), 3DSR reaches 26.10 PSNR, 0.746 SSIM, 0.222 LPIPS, and 22.44 FID. The 3D consistency metric MEt3R improves from 0.531 (StableSR) to 0.516 (3DSR) on LLFF and from 0.644 to 0.625 on MipNeRF360, confirming enhanced geometric coherence. FID shows the largest relative gain, roughly halving the gap to ground-truth distribution compared to StableSR and SuperGaussian.

## Baselines & Comparisons

3DSR outperforms all three baselines on PSNR, SSIM, LPIPS, MEt3R, and FID across both datasets. Against StableSR (independent per-image diffusion SR), 3DSR gains +1.46 dB PSNR and reduces FID from 41.13 to 20.73 on LLFF, demonstrating that the 3D-consistent noise sampling eliminates the cross-view hallucination artifacts that plague single-image SR. Against SuperGaussian (VSR-based 3D SR), 3DSR achieves +1.16 dB PSNR and a 2.4× lower FID on LLFF, while also improving MEt3R from 0.541 to 0.516, showing that explicit 3D Gaussian-splatting consistency surpasses implicit temporal coherence from video models. Against DiSR-NeRF (diffusion-guided NeRF SR), 3DSR leads by +1.71 dB PSNR and reduces FID from 54.14 to 20.73, attributed to the x0-guided 3DGS strategy versus DiSR-NeRF's score distillation approach. The only metric where 3DSR does not rank first is NIQE (no-reference perceptual quality), where SuperGaussian edges it out slightly (4.553 vs. 4.632 on LLFF), suggesting the VSR pipeline produces marginally more 'natural-looking' single images despite worse 3D coherence.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper sits at the intersection of diffusion-based super-resolution and 3D scene reconstruction, building directly on Mip-Splatting for the 3D representation, StableSR-Turbo for the diffusion SR prior, and the subsampling regularization from SRGS/NeRF-SR for the training loss. It departs from prior 3D SR work (SuperGaussian, DiSR-NeRF) by making 3D consistency explicit: rather than relying on temporal coherence (VSR) or score distillation (DiSR-NeRF), it re-encodes 3D-rendered images back into the diffusion latent space at each denoising step, using the 3D representation itself as a geometric consistency filter. This is conceptually distinct from 2D multi-view diffusion consistency methods (MultiDiffusion, Generative Powers of Ten), which enforce alignment purely in image space without explicit geometry.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The paper addresses a practical and well-motivated gap: diffusion-based SR produces photorealistic but hallucinated details that are structurally inconsistent across views, degrading 3D reconstructions. The modular design (any frozen diffusion SR model + any 3D representation) makes it easy to adopt as better diffusion SR models emerge. Limitations include evaluation on only two datasets with a single 4-step diffusion model, no ablation isolating the contribution of the 3D re-encoding step versus simply training 3DGS on SR images, and the per-step 3DGS retraining adds significant compute. Open questions include scalability to large unbounded scenes, dynamic content, and whether the approach generalizes to more complex diffusion backbones beyond StableSR-Turbo.
