## Problem

Existing category-level object pose estimation (COPE) methods focus on learning robust representations from data but overlook inherent dataset biases—repeated training samples, limited pose variety, and similar environments—that cause models to overfit to spurious correlations and degrade on novel instances. No prior work has formulated COPE through a causal lens to explicitly identify and block confounding paths between input and output.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ICCV20251265/cropped_page0_idx1.webp)

## Input / Output

The method consumes cropped RGB images (resized to 224×224) and point clouds (1024 points obtained by back-projecting depth with camera intrinsics and downsampling) for each segmented object instance. It outputs the full 9DoF pose: a 3D rotation in SO(3), a 3D translation in R³, and a 3D metric size in R³, recovered via keypoint-level correspondences. Segmentation masks and category labels are provided by an off-the-shelf MaskRCNN detector.

## Method

CleanPose introduces a causal inference module grounded in front-door adjustment, where object keypoint features serve as the mediator M on the causal path X→M→Y, and a MoCo-inspired dynamic queue (organized per category with FIFO updates) supplies cross-sampled features x′ to approximate the expectation over the full representation space. The front-door adjustment is realized through multi-head self-attention over current keypoint features and cross-attention over queue-sampled features, followed by an adaptive sigmoid-gated fusion with the original keypoint features. A second component is a residual-based knowledge distillation module that aligns the student's average-pooled point features with the [CLS] token of a frozen ULIP-2 (PointBert) 3D encoder via a two-layer MLP with zero-initialized second layer, transferring debiased category semantics without introducing additional confounders. The overall loss combines L1 pose supervision with an L2 distillation loss (weighted at 0.01), trained for 120k iterations on a single GPU.

## Results

On the challenging REAL275 dataset, CleanPose achieves 61.7% on the rigorous 5°2cm metric, 67.6% on 5°5cm, and 78.3% on 10°2cm, surpassing the prior-free SOTA AG-Pose by 4.7%, 3.0%, and 3.2% respectively. On CAMERA25, CleanPose reaches 80.3% (5°2cm) and 87.7% (10°2cm), and on HouseCat6D it attains 79.8% IoU50 and 56.5% (10°5cm), setting new state-of-the-art across all three benchmarks. The method also outperforms the best prior-based method GCE-Pose by 4.7% on 5°2cm of REAL275.

## Baselines & Comparisons

CleanPose outperforms the strongest prior-free baseline AG-Pose [22] on all metrics of REAL275, with the largest margin of 4.7% on 5°2cm (61.7% vs. 57.0%) and 3.2% on 10°2cm (78.3% vs. 75.1%). It also beats the best prior-based method GCE-Pose [17] by 4.7% on 5°2cm and 2.7% on 10°2cm, despite not using any shape prior. On CAMERA25, CleanPose edges out AG-Pose on 5°2cm (80.3% vs. 79.5%) and 10°2cm (87.7% vs. 87.1%) but trails slightly on IoU₇₅ (80.7% vs. 81.2%). On HouseCat6D, CleanPose surpasses AG-Pose by 2.9% on IoU50 (79.8% vs. 76.9%) and 2.2% on 10°5cm (56.5% vs. 54.3%). Against earlier prior-free methods like SecondPose [3] and VI-Net [20], the gains are even more pronounced, with 5.5% and 11.7% improvements on 5°2cm of REAL275 respectively.

## Relation to Prior Work

CleanPose builds directly on the AG-Pose keypoint-based framework (CVPR 2024) for feature extraction and pose recovery, but departs from the prevailing COPE paradigm of improving model architecture to capture robust category features. Instead, it reframes the data-bias problem through a causal lens, introducing front-door adjustment (from Pearl's causal inference) as the first application of causal learning to category-level pose estimation. The dynamic queue for cross-sample feature sampling draws on MoCo's momentum-based memory, while the residual distillation module adapts feature-alignment strategies from vision-language knowledge transfer (e.g., Froster) to the 3D point-cloud setting using ULIP-2 as a frozen teacher.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

By identifying dataset biases (repeated samples, limited pose variety) as hidden confounders and explicitly intervening on them, CleanPose offers a principled alternative to the field's default strategy of simply scaling data or refining architectures. The 4.7% gain on the rigorous 5°2cm metric over AG-Pose on REAL275 is a meaningful margin, and the ablations confirm both the causal module and the distillation contribute independently. Open questions include whether the front-door causal structure generalizes to other 3D perception tasks, whether the dynamic queue scales to very large category counts, and whether the reliance on a specific 3D foundation model (ULIP-2) limits transferability as the foundation-model landscape evolves.
