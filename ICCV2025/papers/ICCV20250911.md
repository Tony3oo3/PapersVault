## Problem

Existing multi-modal UAV datasets are biased toward SLAM and 3D reconstruction, or only provide map-level semantic labels without frame-wise annotations for both camera images and LiDAR point clouds. This gap prevents benchmarking high-level perception tasks such as semantic segmentation, depth estimation, and 6-DoF localization on real aerial data with precise pose alignment.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ICCV20250911/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The dataset provides 120k synchronized frames of RGB camera images and Livox-Avia LiDAR point clouds, each annotated with 19 semantic classes (16 static, 2 dynamic, 1 background). Unlike the original MARS-LVIG's 4-DoF RTK poses, UAVScenes supplies full 6-DoF poses reconstructed via DJI Terra SfM, along with per-split 3D point cloud maps. LiDAR annotations are derived by projecting image masks onto point clouds using hardware-synchronized camera-LiDAR calibration, ensuring cross-modal consistency at the frame level.

## Method

UAVScenes extends the MARS-LVIG SLAM dataset through a multi-stage annotation pipeline. First, 6-DoF poses and 3D maps are reconstructed per split using DJI Terra SfM (after LVI-SLAM methods like FAST-LIVO and R3LIVE fail due to LiDAR degeneration from ground-facing flight). Second, 16 static scene classes are manually annotated on the reconstructed 3D maps, then rendered onto camera views to produce sequentially consistent 2D semantic masks. Third, 2 dynamic classes (sedan, truck) are annotated as instance polygons in each image using tracking-assisted labeling followed by human verification, yielding over 280k dynamic instances. Finally, image annotations are projected onto LiDAR point clouds via calibration and manually corrected for fidelity. The resulting dataset is benchmarked across six tasks—image segmentation, LiDAR segmentation, place recognition, depth estimation, 6-DoF localization, and novel view synthesis—using SOTA methods from each domain.

## Results

On image semantic segmentation, DeiT3-m achieves the best mIoU of 68.3%, outperforming all CNN backbones (ResNet-50: 61.3%, ConvNext-t: 55.3%) and MambaOut-s (51.8%). For LiDAR segmentation, SPUNet leads with 34.4% mIoU, closely followed by PTv2 (33.2%) and MinkUNet (32.7%), with the 'Pool' class yielding 0% IoU across all methods. In place recognition, SALAD with DINOv2 backbone achieves the highest Recall@1 of 67.1% (camera-only), while fusion-based MinkLoc++ reaches 47.1% R@1. For 6-DoF localization, RobustLoc achieves the lowest median position error of 6.1 m (0.1° rotation), substantially outperforming AtLoc (8.6 m) and all SCR-based methods (85.5–132.9 m).

## Baselines & Comparisons

Across image segmentation, Transformer backbones (DeiT3, ViT) consistently outperform CNNs (ResNet, ConvNext) and Mamba-based models by 5–17 mIoU points, with DeiT3-m (68.3%) beating ResNet-50 (61.3%) by 7 points. In LiDAR segmentation, the three baselines (MinkUNet, SPUNet, PTv2) are closely matched (32.7–34.4% mIoU), but PTv2 achieves comparable accuracy with only 11M parameters versus 38–39M for the others. For place recognition, camera-only SALAD with DINOv2 (67.1% R@1) substantially outperforms all LiDAR-only methods (best: MinkLoc3D V2 at 42.8%) and all fusion methods (best: MinkLoc++ at 47.1%), suggesting that strong visual foundation models currently dominate over LiDAR or fusion approaches in aerial settings. In 6-DoF localization, APR-based RobustLoc (6.1 m median error) dramatically outperforms SCR-based methods (ACE: 132.9 m, GLACE: 85.5 m), indicating that ground-urban pretraining limits SCR generalization to UAV views. For depth estimation, Metric3D V2 (ViT-l) achieves the best AbsRel (0.540) and SqRel (31.96), while diffusion-based models (Marigold, GeoWizard) perform worst with AbsRel >0.99, confirming poor zero-shot generalization to aerial perspectives.

## Relation to Prior Work

UAVScenes extends the MARS-LVIG dataset [56], originally designed for LiDAR-visual-inertial SLAM with only 4-DoF RTK poses and no semantic labels, by adding frame-wise semantic annotations for both camera images and LiDAR point clouds along with 6-DoF poses reconstructed via DJI Terra SfM. It departs from prior multi-modal UAV datasets (H3D, UrbanScene3D, NTU VIRAL, GauU-Scene, MUM-FRL) that either annotate only reconstructed 3D maps or lack frame-wise labels entirely, and from camera-only UAV datasets that omit the LiDAR modality. The benchmarking methodology draws directly on standard tools from autonomous driving and robotics (UperNet, MinkUNet, 3DGS, RobustLoc, Depth Anything), repurposing them for the aerial perspective without architectural modifications.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

UAVScenes is the first dataset to simultaneously provide frame-wise semantic annotations for both RGB images and LiDAR point clouds with accurate 6-DoF poses in real UAV scenes, enabling unified benchmarking of at least six perception tasks that were previously untestable together in the aerial domain. The notably poor zero-shot depth estimation results (most models achieving δ₁ ≈ 0) and low LiDAR segmentation mIoU (~33%) underscore that ground-vehicle and indoor methods do not transfer well to UAV perspectives, highlighting a significant gap for future research. Key limitations include the restricted 19-class taxonomy, absence of complex downtown scenes with pedestrians and high-rise buildings, and reliance on a single open-source LiDAR (Livox-Avia), leaving open whether findings generalize to denser urban environments and higher-resolution sensors.
