## Problem

Generating large-scale, house-scale 3D indoor scenes with accurate geometry and coherent object layouts remains challenging. Existing text-guided methods struggle with global spatial consistency, object-centric novel view synthesis approaches do not scale to full rooms or houses, and 3D layout proxies are cumbersome for users. The paper addresses how to lift a simple 2D floorplan into a complete, globally consistent 3D scene with explicit mesh geometry.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ICCV20251838/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The input is a vectorized 2D floorplan consisting of N components, each specified by a semantic category and geometry (2D bounding boxes for furniture, line segments for walls/doors/windows). The method samples camera poses within the free space defined by the floorplan, then outputs multi-view RGB-D images (with metric depth clipped to 0–3 m) that are fused via TSDF into a 3D vertex-colored mesh. Depth is generated jointly with RGB in the latent space rather than estimated post-hoc, avoiding the scale ambiguity of monocular depth models.

## Method

HouseCrafter fine-tunes the UNet of Stable Diffusion v1.5 (VAE frozen) to denoise concatenated RGB and depth latents jointly, ensuring per-view semantic-geometric consistency. Three architectural modifications enable scene-level generation: (1) layout-attention blocks at the start of each UNet block perform pixel-wise cross-attention between image latents and a floorplan encoding obtained by ray-casting from each pixel to find intersections with floorplan components; (2) DeCaPE augments the CaPE cross-attention mechanism with 3D positional encodings derived from reference depth images, providing explicit geometric cues for novel view synthesis; (3) generation proceeds autoregressively in batches along a graph traversal of sampled camera poses, where each batch conditions on previously generated RGB-D images at nearby visited nodes. The entire pipeline is trained on ~2 million rendered images from 3D-FRONT and evaluated on 300 held-out houses.

## Results

On the 3D-FRONT dataset, HouseCrafter achieves the best floorplan consistency (mAP@25 = 45.77) among all compared methods, approaching the ground-truth reference (54.51). Its Inception Score is 4.37, slightly below Text2Room (5.35) and BlockFusion (5.01), which the authors attribute to those methods' use of real-world-trained models matching the IS evaluation distribution. In terms of 3D geometry quality, HouseCrafter produces 178.0 connected components (dangling artifacts), better than Text2Room (302.5) but worse than BlockFusion (118.64). A user study with 12 participants (228 data points) confirms that humans significantly prefer HouseCrafter's output over all baselines in both overall quality and floorplan coherence.

## Baselines & Comparisons

Against CC3D, HouseCrafter clearly wins on both floorplan consistency (mAP@25: 45.77 vs. 25.60) and visual quality (IS: 4.37 vs. 4.02), while CC3D does not report artifact counts. Against Text2Room, HouseCrafter produces fewer 3D artifacts (178.0 vs. 302.5) but a lower IS (4.37 vs. 5.35); however, Text2Room does not accept floorplan input, so no mAP comparison is made. Against BlockFusion, HouseCrafter dominates on floorplan consistency (mAP@25: 45.77 vs. 0.81) but trails on IS (4.37 vs. 5.01) and artifact count (178.0 vs. 118.64). Despite the mixed automated metrics, the user study shows participants significantly prefer HouseCrafter over all three baselines in both perceptual quality and floorplan faithfulness, with the high preference for ground-truth meshes validating the study's reliability.

## Relation to Prior Work

HouseCrafter extends the line of layout-guided 3D scene generation (CC3D, BlockFusion, SceneCraft) by replacing 3D proxy boxes with a 2D floorplan and scaling from single rooms to full houses. Methodologically, it builds on Stable Diffusion v1.5's UNet (fine-tuned with frozen VAE) and on the Camera Positional Encoding (CaPE) from ESCHERNet, extending it to DeCaPE by injecting explicit 3D positions from reference depth maps into cross-attention. The key conceptual departure from prior novel-view-synthesis work (which is object-centric and RGB-only) is the autoregressive, batch-wise generation of multi-view RGB-D images at scene scale, where depth is jointly denoised with color in latent space to provide metric geometry for downstream TSDF fusion.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

This work demonstrates that a 2D diffusion model, without any 3D training data, can produce globally coherent house-scale 3D scenes when guided by a floorplan and generated autoregressively in batches — a practical path to interactive 3D environments that avoids the data scarcity problem of 3D generative models. Limitations include evaluation primarily on the synthetic 3D-FRONT dataset, an imperfect floorplan-consistency metric (mAP@25 via ODIN segmentation), and an IS score lower than text-based baselines (attributed to distribution mismatch). Open questions include generalization to real-world floorplans, scalability beyond single-story houses, and whether the autoregressive error accumulation degrades quality in very large or multi-story scenes.
