## Problem

Conditional flow matching models only enforce flow uniqueness within the same class condition, not across classes, causing flows from different conditions to overlap and produce 'averaged' outputs that lack condition-specific detail. This ambiguity is particularly problematic when conditional distributions overlap, as the model has no mechanism to distinguish between them during generation.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ICCV20250586/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The model takes a class label (ImageNet-1k) or text prompt (CC3M) along with Gaussian noise as input and produces a generated image at 256×256 or 512×512 resolution. Images are encoded via the Stable Diffusion VAE into 32×32×4 latent tensors before flow matching operates. The contrastive objective consumes an additional randomly-sampled negative pair from the same training batch, requiring no extra data or forward passes beyond the standard flow matching step.

## Method

Contrastive Flow Matching (ΔFM) augments the standard flow matching loss with a contrastive regularization term that maximizes the squared distance between the predicted velocity field v_θ(x_t, t, y) and the ideal transport vector toward a randomly sampled negative sample (x̃, ε̃) from the batch. The combined objective is L(ΔFM) = E[||v_θ(x_t,t,y) − (α̇_t x̂ + σ̇_t ε)||² − λ||v_θ(x_t,t,y) − (α̇_t x̃ + σ̇_t ε̃)||²], where λ=0.05 controls contrastive strength and the negative is drawn from the marginal p(x) rather than p(x|y), training the model to produce unconditionally unique flows. This is a plug-and-play modification requiring no additional architecture, data, or forward passes, and it composes with REPA representation alignment and classifier-free guidance at inference time.

## Results

On ImageNet-1k (256×256), ΔFM reduces FID from 42.28 to 33.39 (−8.89) for SiT-B/2 and from 20.01 to 16.32 (−3.69) for SiT-XL/2, while sFID drops by more than half in both cases (11.35→5.67 and 8.45→5.08). At 512×512, the FID gains are 8.67 (SiT-B/2) and 3.31 (SiT-XL/2). The method also achieves 9× fewer training iterations to reach the same FID and requires only 50 denoising steps versus 250 for the baseline (5× fewer NFE).

## Baselines & Comparisons

Against the SiT flow-matching baseline (Ma et al., 2024), ΔFM wins on FID, IS, sFID, and Precision at every model scale and resolution, while matching Recall exactly (0.63 at 256×256, 0.60 at 512×512). The largest FID gain is 8.89 points on SiT-B/2 at 256×256, and sFID improvements exceed 50% in all settings. Against the REPA-enhanced baseline (Yu et al., 2024), adding ΔFM further reduces FID by up to 6.81 points (27.33→20.52 for SiT-B/2 at 256×256; 11.14→7.29 for SiT-XL/2), confirming the two objectives are complementary. On the CC3M text-to-image benchmark using the MMDiT architecture (Esser et al., 2024), ΔFM achieves FID 19 versus 24 for the flow-matching baseline, a 5-point improvement. When combined with classifier-free guidance, ΔFM also outperforms the FM+CFG baseline on all metrics (FID 1.97 vs 2.09, sFID 4.49 vs 5.55 for REPA SiT-XL/2 at 256×256).

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends conditional flow matching (Lipman et al., ICLR 2023) by adding a contrastive regularization term that penalizes similarity between flow vectors of different conditioning inputs within a batch. It positions itself as a training-time alternative to classifier-free guidance (CFG), achieving similar condition-separation benefits without the inference-time cost of a second unconditional forward pass. The method is complementary to REPA (Yu et al., 2024), which aligns intermediate representations with pretrained vision encoders, and the two objectives stack additively. The contrastive learning idea itself originates from face recognition and GAN training but had not previously been applied to flow matching or diffusion objectives.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The practical impact is substantial: a single contrastive loss term yields up to 9× faster training convergence and 5× fewer denoising steps while simultaneously improving FID, with negligible per-iteration overhead and no architectural changes. This makes it a highly attractive plug-and-play addition for any flow matching pipeline. Open questions include whether the fixed λ=0.05 remains optimal at larger scales (e.g., video or 3D generation), whether the contrastive signal degrades with very large batch sizes where negative pairs become less informative, and whether the approach transfers to autoregressive or non-diffusion generative paradigms. The paper also raises the broader question of whether deliberately deviating from exact distribution matching in the training objective is a general principle for improving generative quality.
