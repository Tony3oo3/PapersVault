## Problem

World models require three tightly coupled capabilities—4D geometric reconstruction, action-conditioned prediction, and goal-conditioned planning—yet prior work treats these as separate problems. No unified framework has jointly optimized all three objectives, and integrating geometric reasoning into generative video models while maintaining zero-shot synthetic-to-real generalization remains an open challenge.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ICCV20250423/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The model consumes RGB video (full sequence for reconstruction, first frame for prediction, first+last frames for planning) and optionally a 6-channel raymap video encoding camera trajectories as action conditions. Depth inputs are converted to scale-invariant normalized disparity (single channel replicated to three for VAE compatibility), and camera poses are transformed into raymap sequences with log-scaled origins, spatially downsampled 8× and temporally grouped by 4 to match latent dimensions. Outputs are jointly generated in a shared latent space: color video, depth video, and raymap action latents are channel-wise concatenated, so a single denoising pass produces all modalities simultaneously.

## Method

AETHER post-trains CogVideoX-5b-I2V (a diffusion transformer) by extending its input/output channels to accommodate depth and raymap latents alongside color video, then applies a task-interleaved training strategy where conditional inputs are randomly masked (30% observation+goal, 40% observation-only, 28% full video, 2% none) to unify reconstruction, prediction, and planning in one model. Camera trajectories are encoded as 6-channel raymap videos (3 direction + 3 origin channels) with signed log-transformed translations, providing a geometry-informed action space compatible with the DiT's spatiotemporal structure. Training proceeds in two stages: latent-space MSE denoising followed by a shorter image-space refinement using MS-SSIM, scale/shift-invariant depth loss, and pointmap loss. The entire pipeline is trained exclusively on synthetic RGB-D data annotated by a four-stage automatic camera calibration pipeline (dynamic masking via Grounded SAM 2, SIFT/RAFT-based video slicing, DroidCalib coarse estimation, and CoTracker3 + bundle adjustment refinement), enabling zero-shot transfer to real-world scenes.

## Results

On KITTI, AETHER achieves a new SOTA in zero-shot video depth estimation with Abs Rel of 0.056 and δ<1.25 of 97.8%, nearly doubling the δ<1.25 of the previous best reconstruction method CUT3R (88.1%). On Sintel, AETHER attains the lowest Abs Rel (0.324) among reconstruction-based methods, surpassing MonST3R-GA (0.378). For camera pose estimation, AETHER achieves the best ATE (0.189) and RPE Trans (0.054) among feed-forward methods on Sintel, and the best RPE Trans (0.012) on TUM Dynamics. In video prediction, AETHER outperforms the CogVideoX baseline on VBench weighted average (80.04 vs. 78.51 action-free; 80.71 vs. 79.92 action-conditioned) across both in-domain and out-domain validation sets.

## Baselines & Comparisons

AETHER clearly outperforms CUT3R on KITTI depth estimation (Abs Rel 0.056 vs. 0.118; δ<1.25 97.8% vs. 88.1%) and on Sintel camera pose ATE (0.189 vs. 0.213), establishing new SOTA in both settings. Against MonST3R-GA, AETHER wins on Sintel depth (Abs Rel 0.324 vs. 0.378) but loses substantially on BONN (0.273 vs. 0.067), indicating weaker performance on indoor scenes. Among diffusion-based depth estimators, AETHER dominates on Sintel (Abs Rel 0.314 vs. 0.429 for ChronoDepth) and KITTI (0.054 vs. 0.094 for DA-V), while DA-V remains competitive on KITTI δ<1.25 (93.0 vs. 97.7). For camera pose, AETHER beats CUT3R on Sintel RPE Trans (0.054 vs. 0.066) but trails on RPE Rot (0.694 vs. 0.621), and on TUM Dynamics it achieves the best RPE Trans (0.012) but higher ATE (0.092 vs. 0.038 for MASt3R-GA). In video prediction, AETHER consistently surpasses CogVideoX on VBench weighted average in both action-free (80.04 vs. 78.51) and action-conditioned (80.71 vs. 79.92) settings, with the largest gains on out-domain data and dynamic degree metrics.

## Relation to Prior Work

AETHER post-trains the CogVideoX-5b-I2V video diffusion transformer with a multi-task objective that unifies three previously separate capabilities: 4D reconstruction (depth + camera pose), action-conditioned video prediction, and goal-conditioned visual planning. The camera trajectory encoding adopts the raymap representation from Chen et al. [7], and the depth-to-disparity conversion follows DepthMaster [57], but the key departure is the task-interleaved training strategy that randomly combines input/output modalities to enable synergistic knowledge sharing across heterogeneous objectives. Unlike prior reconstruction methods (DUSt3R, MASt3R, MonST3R, CUT3R) that are specialized for geometry estimation, or video generation models that lack geometric grounding, AETHER treats all three tasks as a single generative problem over a shared latent space, trained entirely on synthetic 4D data produced by a robust automatic annotation pipeline.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The demonstration that a model trained exclusively on synthetic 4D data can achieve zero-shot reconstruction performance comparable to or exceeding domain-specific models (e.g., 2× lower Abs Rel than CUT3R on KITTI) suggests that geometric reasoning can be acquired without real-world supervision, opening a path to scalable world-model training. The ablation showing that the reconstruction objective substantially improves planning quality (AETHER vs. AETHER-no-depth) provides evidence that geometric grounding is not merely auxiliary but causally beneficial for downstream decision-making. However, limitations remain: camera pose estimation is less accurate than specialist models (especially on TUM Dynamics), indoor reconstruction lags behind outdoor performance, and predictions without language prompts struggle in highly dynamic scenes—suggesting that co-training with real data and richer action representations are necessary next steps.
