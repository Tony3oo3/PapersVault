## Problem

Generating photorealistic, multi-view-consistent head portraits from novel viewpoints remains difficult: existing diffusion-based methods are restricted to near-frontal angles, produce low-quality or distorted outputs at large rotations, or rely on computationally expensive video diffusion backbones. Moreover, large-scale multi-view head datasets are unavailable due to privacy constraints, limiting training of models that can handle full 360° synthesis while preserving identity.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ICCV20251246/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method accepts 1 or 3 sparse facial images (cropped and aligned), their ArcFace identity embeddings (512-d vectors), and camera poses, and produces up to 8 pairs of (RGB image, shape normal) per sampling step at 64×64 latent resolution. A three-step sampling strategy first generates 7 anchor views spanning ±45° to 180°, then synthesizes intermediate views conditioned on the input plus the two nearest anchors, yielding 48 or more consistent views covering the full head. Shape normals are generated simultaneously with images via channel-wise concatenation in latent space, and can also be retrieved from a single input image through a channel-wise in-painting procedure.

## Method

SpinMeRound is a multi-view latent diffusion UNet initialized from the pre-trained Arc2Face model, augmented with Cat3D-style 3D attention layers inserted between 2D self-attention blocks to enforce cross-view consistency. Identity is injected by replacing the <id> token in a CLIP-encoded text prompt with the ArcFace embedding (padded to 768 dimensions), while camera poses are encoded as 149-channel ray coordinate maps concatenated with the image and normal latents. The model is trained with the EDM framework on a synthetic Panohead dataset (~7k subjects × 125 views), using classifier-free guidance (P_uncond=0.15) and a shifted log-SNR schedule. A key design choice is the joint generation of RGB images and shape normals, which the ablation shows improves both reconstruction fidelity and identity preservation. The sampling strategy leverages anchor views at fixed angular positions to bootstrap full-head coverage from a single in-the-wild input, avoiding the need for per-identity optimization.

## Results

On the NeRSemble dataset (222 identities, 16 angles), SpinMeRound achieves state-of-the-art results among all methods on LPIPS (0.30), SSIM (0.73), ArcFace ID Similarity (0.61), and VGGFace ID Similarity (0.911). Its L2 distance of 0.033 is the best among all diffusion-based methods, though slightly behind the NeRF-based Panohead (0.012). Among diffusion-based baselines, SpinMeRound outperforms the closest competitor SV3D by 27% on LPIPS (0.30 vs 0.41) and 10% on SSIM (0.73 vs 0.66).

## Baselines & Comparisons

Against the two NeRF-based baselines, SpinMeRound trades a small L2 penalty (0.033 vs Panohead's 0.012 and Eg3D's 0.025) for substantially better perceptual and identity metrics: LPIPS improves from 0.32/0.40 to 0.30, SSIM from 0.65/0.55 to 0.73, and ArcFace ID Sim from 0.27/0.31 to 0.61. Among diffusion-based methods, SpinMeRound dominates across all five metrics. The closest diffusion competitor, SV3D, is outperformed by 27% on LPIPS (0.30 vs 0.41), 10% on SSIM (0.73 vs 0.66), and 69% on ArcFace ID Sim (0.61 vs 0.36). DiffPortrait3D achieves a competitive VGGFace ID Sim (0.887) but suffers from poor geometric fidelity (SSIM 0.35, LPIPS 0.50), while Zero123 and Zero123-XL are far behind on every metric (LPIPS ≈ 0.51, ID Sim ≈ 0.12–0.17). The paper notes that NeRF-based methods require per-identity fitting that can fail, whereas SpinMeRound relies solely on a single forward sampling pass.

## Relation to Prior Work

SpinMeRound sits at the intersection of two recent lines of work: identity-conditioned face diffusion (Arc2Face) and general-purpose multi-view diffusion (Cat3D). It adopts Cat3D's latent multi-view UNet with 3D attention layers and ray-coordinate camera conditioning, then specializes the framework for human heads by injecting ArcFace identity embeddings via the Arc2Face token-replacement mechanism and jointly generating shape normals using Relightify's channel-wise in-painting. The key departure from prior face-specific diffusion methods (Zero123, DiffPortrait3D) is the shift from limited near-frontal angular ranges to full 360° coverage, enabled by a structured anchor-and-intermediate sampling strategy rather than per-view fine-tuning.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

By training exclusively on synthetic Panohead renders, SpinMeRound sidesteps the data-permission and licensing barriers that plague real multi-view head datasets, making the pipeline reproducible and accessible. The demonstrated ability to reconstruct consistent 3D Gaussians from generated views validates multi-view coherence beyond single-image metrics. However, the synthetic-only training raises open questions about generalization to diverse real-world conditions (extreme lighting, occlusions, accessories), and the multi-pass sampling (7 anchors + intermediate views) introduces cumulative error that could limit scalability to denser view sets or full-body generation.
