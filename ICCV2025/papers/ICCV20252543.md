## Problem

Existing unsupervised domain adaptation methods for panoramic scene understanding require access to labeled source pinhole data, which is often unavailable due to privacy or commercial restrictions. Moreover, the combination of 360° field-of-view expansion, occlusion-aware amodal reasoning, and source-free adaptation had not been jointly addressed. This paper introduces the Source-Free Occlusion-Aware Seamless Segmentation (SFOASS) task and its first solution, UNLOCK, to enable panoramic seamless segmentation without any source data or target labels.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ICCV20252543/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method takes a pre-trained source model (trained on pinhole images of 1408×376 or 1920×1080) and unlabeled target panoramic images (2048×400 pixels) as input, with no access to source images or labels during adaptation. It produces five segmentation maps simultaneously from a single forward pass: semantic, instance, amodal instance, panoptic, and amodal panoptic, via a shared encoder with three detection branches (semantic, instance, amodal instance) whose predictions are fused at inference. The adaptation is performed via self-training on the unlabeled panoramic data for 10k iterations with crop sizes of 376×376 (K2B) or 400×400 (A2B).

## Method

UNLOCK comprises two synergistic modules. Omni Pseudo-Labeling Learning (OPLL) generates high-quality omni pseudo-labels by first using the semantic branch's global predictions to constrain instance-level masks, then applying Classwise Self-tuning (CS) thresholds that adapt per-class confidence cutoffs to the source model's uneven class performance. Predictions are partitioned into certain and uncertain regions, and an uncertainty-guided BCE loss masks out uncertain areas during training to prevent error propagation. Amodal-Driven Contextual Learning (ADCL) builds a buffer pool of high-quality amodal object samples and performs spatial-aware mixing that respects panoramic layout distortions; for occluded/overlapping regions, it assigns zero pixels in the mixed image while retaining the full amodal mask as the label, thereby preserving complete object shapes without introducing incorrect contextual cues from other objects. Together, OPLL extracts domain-invariant knowledge (the 'key') and ADCL learns intra-domain contextual knowledge (the 'unlocking action'), enabling source-free adaptation to the panoramic domain.

## Results

On the K2B (KITTI360-APS → BlendPASS) Real-to-Real SFOASS benchmark, UNLOCK achieves 26.4 mAPQ, 25.1 mPQ, 41.6 mIoU, 10.9 mAAP, and 11.6 mAP, improving over the Source-only baseline by +4.3, +2.8, +2.9, +0.7, and +1.1 respectively. Notably, UNLOCK surpasses all UDA methods that have full access to 12K source images in instance-level segmentation (mAAP 10.9, mAP 11.6), while matching UnmaskFormer's mAPQ (26.4 vs. 26.6) without any source data. On the A2B (AmodalSynthDrive → BlendPASS) Synthetic-to-Real benchmark, UNLOCK achieves 20.4 mAPQ, 19.0 mPQ, and 34.3 mIoU, outperforming 360SFUDA++ by +1.1, +0.0, and +3.1 respectively.

## Baselines & Comparisons

UNLOCK outperforms the Source-only baseline (no adaptation) by +4.3 mAPQ, +2.8 mPQ, +2.9 mIoU, +0.7 mAAP, and +1.1 mAP, demonstrating that source-free adaptation is effective for panoramic seamless segmentation. Against the strongest SFDA baseline 360SFUDA++ [95], UNLOCK wins on every metric: +1.9 mAPQ, +1.4 mPQ, +2.2 mIoU, +0.6 mAAP, and +1.1 mAP. In instance-level segmentation, UNLOCK surpasses all UDA methods that use 12K source images—achieving the highest mAAP (10.9 vs. 10.7 for EDAPS) and mAP (11.6 vs. 11.1 for UnmaskFormer)—despite having no access to source data. In scene-level metrics, UNLOCK is competitive with UnmaskFormer [7] (the full UDA method with source access), reaching 26.4 vs. 26.6 mAPQ and 41.6 vs. 43.7 mIoU, a gap of only 0.2 and 2.1 points respectively. On the A2B Synthetic-to-Real benchmark, UNLOCK leads 360SFUDA++ by +3.3 mIoU in semantic segmentation and +0.3 mAAP in amodal instance segmentation, confirming generalization across domain gaps.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the Occlusion-Aware Seamless Segmentation (OASS) task introduced by Cao et al. (ECCV 2024) by adding a source-free constraint, creating the SFOASS problem where the source pinhole domain is entirely inaccessible during adaptation. Methodologically, it departs from standard self-training pseudo-labeling by proposing Omni Pseudo-Labeling Learning (OPLL) with class-wise self-tuning thresholds and an uncertainty-guided loss, and introduces Amodal-Driven Contextual Learning (ADCL) as a spatially-aware mixing strategy that specifically handles the conflict between amodal object shapes and scene context. The work also sits within the broader source-free domain adaptation (SFDA) literature, building on 360SFUDA++ for panoramic segmentation but extending it to the much harder multi-branch, occlusion-aware setting.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The practical motivation is strong: in autonomous driving and robotics, source datasets are often locked behind privacy or commercial restrictions, making source-free adaptation the realistic deployment scenario. The paper also pioneers the Synthetic-to-Real (A2B) benchmark for OASS/SFOASS, opening a path toward leveraging cheap synthetic panoramic data for real-world deployment. Open questions include whether the approach generalizes beyond driving scenes to indoor or aerial panoramic settings, whether the class-wise threshold tuning (OPLL) scales gracefully to open-vocabulary or very long-tail class distributions, and whether the amodal mixing strategy (ADCL) can be extended to video or 3D-consistent panoramic understanding.
