## Problem

3D Gaussian Splatting depends on high-quality SfM point clouds for initialization, but SfM fails in textureless or symmetric regions, producing missing or erroneous points that cause detail loss and excessive floaters in the final rendering. Existing SfM-free alternatives (RAIN-GS, Colmap-Free 3DGS) still underperform SfM-initialized 3DGS due to lack of geometric priors or scale inconsistencies in monocular depth.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ICCV20250815/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method takes a posed RGB image sequence with known camera intrinsics (no point cloud, LiDAR, or ToF data required) and produces a 3D Gaussian Splatting model for real-time novel view rendering. It consumes off-the-shelf monocular depth estimates and 2D segmentation masks as auxiliary priors during initialization, and operates on dense sequences of hundreds of images at resolutions up to 1237×658. The refinement stage uses the standard 3DGS training loop with photometric loss, so the output is a conventional 3DGS scene representation.

## Method

The pipeline has three key stages. First, an unbiased depth rendering replaces the Gaussian center depth with the point of maximum opacity along each ray (solving a quadratic), yielding more accurate depth from an under-optimized coarse Gaussian model trained with sparse-large-variance initialization. Second, monocular depth is aligned to this rendered depth via per-object affine transformations (independent scale and shift per segmentation mask), avoiding the unreliable scale-consistency assumption that requires SfM points; the two depths are then ensembled per-object with an edge-aware erosion step to suppress floaters. Third, a progressive segmented initialization selects keyframes based on 2D correspondence overlap, back-projects keyframes fully and local frames only in under-reconstructed regions, and applies importance resampling (training a 1000-step coarse model on 10% of points and retaining the highest-contribution Gaussian per ray) to reduce redundancy before standard 3DGS refinement.

## Results

On Mip-NeRF360, Liberated-GS achieves PSNR 27.59, SSIM 0.822, and LPIPS 0.187, surpassing SfM-initialized 3DGS (27.21 / 0.815 / 0.214) on all three metrics. On Tanks and Temples, it reaches PSNR 23.59, SSIM 0.848, and LPIPS 0.140, improving LPIPS by 23.5% over SfM-based 3DGS (0.183). On the in-the-wild OMMO dataset, it attains PSNR 28.01, SSIM 0.891, and LPIPS 0.157, again outperforming the SfM-initialized baseline. The paper reports a 14.4% LPIPS improvement over SfM-based 3DGS on Mip-NeRF360 and a 30.7% improvement on Tanks and Temples.

## Baselines & Comparisons

Liberated-GS outperforms SfM-initialized 3DGS on all three metrics across all three datasets, with the largest gains in LPIPS (e.g., 0.187 vs. 0.214 on Mip-NeRF360, 0.140 vs. 0.183 on Tanks and Temples), indicating substantially better perceptual quality without any SfM point cloud. Against RAIN-GS, which uses sparse large-variance random initialization, Liberated-GS improves PSNR by 0.36 dB and LPIPS by 0.042 on Mip-NeRF360, and by 0.46 dB and 0.067 on Tanks and Temples, confirming that geometric priors from depth alignment are critical for detail recovery. Colmap-Free 3DGS (with ground-truth poses and rescaled depth) performs the worst among non-random baselines (PSNR 21.46 on Mip-NeRF360), highlighting the scale-ambiguity problem that Liberated-GS's per-object affine alignment resolves. Random-initialized 3DGS is far behind on all metrics (PSNR 22.19 on Mip-NeRF360), confirming that unstructured initialization is insufficient. Finally, Table 2 shows that plugging Liberated-GS's initialization into Mini-Splatting and 3DGS-MCMC also yields improvements over both random and SfM initialization (e.g., PSNR 28.50 vs. 28.34 for 3DGS-MCMC on OMMO), demonstrating the method's compatibility with advanced Gaussian optimization frameworks.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) framework by replacing its standard SfM-based point cloud initialization with a depth-prior-driven pipeline. It builds on RAIN-GS's sparse-large-variance initialization for the pre-training stage and on Colmap-Free 3DGS's use of monocular depth, but departs from both by introducing an unbiased depth rendering mechanism (replacing center-depth with ray-intersection depth) and a per-object affine alignment that resolves scale ambiguity without relying on SfM ground-truth points. The progressive segmented initialization with local Gaussians and importance resampling is a new contribution not present in prior colmap-free methods.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

Removing the SfM dependency addresses a practical bottleneck that limits 3DGS in textureless, symmetric, or large-scale outdoor scenes where COLMAP produces erroneous or sparse point clouds. The demonstrated compatibility with Mini-Splatting and 3DGS-MCMC suggests the initialization pipeline is a drop-in replacement broadly applicable across Gaussian splatting variants. Open questions include scalability to very long image sequences (hundreds to thousands of frames), performance without ground-truth camera poses, and whether the depth alignment strategy generalizes to dynamic or partially observed scenes.
