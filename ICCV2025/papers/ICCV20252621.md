## Problem

Existing open-vocabulary 3D instance segmentation methods rely on CLIP to match isolated 2D object crops to text, capturing only local instance-level features while ignoring scene-level contextual relationships. This limits recognition of rare or ambiguous objects whose identity depends on spatial context (e.g., a 'computer tower' near monitors vs. a generic 'container'), and prevents fine-grained disambiguation in real-world scenes.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ICCV20252621/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method consumes a posed RGB-D image sequence (every 20th frame is processed) with known camera intrinsics and extrinsics, along with the corresponding 3D point cloud. It produces class-agnostic 3D instance masks (binary point assignments) paired with semantic category labels, where labels are matched to a predefined vocabulary via the CLIP text encoder. Visibility of each instance from a candidate viewpoint is determined by comparing projected depth against the depth image with a tolerance threshold δ=0.2.

## Method

OV3D-CG comprises two modules. The Class-agnostic Proposal Module fuses masks from Mask3D (a sparse-convolution + Transformer 3D segmenter for common objects) and SAI3D (a SAM-guided superpoint-merging segmenter for rare/small objects), deduplicating via NMS at IoU 0.5. The Semantic Reasoning Module selects the best 2D viewpoint per instance (maximizing visible pixel count), then constructs three context-aware 2D representations—bounding box, dilated landmarks, and a RANSAC-refined SAM mask—each overlaid on the full scene image to preserve environmental context. These representations are fed into an MLLM (Gemini 1.5 Flash or LLaVA-OneVision) with a two-stage Chain-of-Thought prompt: the first stage generates a structured scene description, and the second stage refines it into a final category label, replacing CLIP's single-shot object-to-text matching with explicit multi-step contextual reasoning.

## Results

On ScanNet200, OV3D-CG (3D+SAI3D mask) achieves 25.4% AP, 32.9% AP50, and 37.0% AP25, outperforming the previous SOTA Open3DIS (23.7% AP) by +1.7% AP and surpassing the fully supervised Mask3D (26.9% AP) in APcomm (24.8 vs. 21.7) and APtail (22.9 vs. 17.9). On Replica, the 3D-only variant reaches 20.0% AP and 32.0% AP25, exceeding Open3DIS (14.9% AP) and OpenMask3D (13.1% AP) by +5.1% and +6.9% AP respectively. With oracle masks on ScanNet200, the Semantic Reasoning Module alone achieves 44.9% AP, surpassing the fully supervised Mask3D (35.5% AP) by +9.4% AP.

## Baselines & Comparisons

OV3D-CG outperforms all eight baselines on ScanNet200. Against the closest competitor Open3DIS (2D+3D with Grounding-DINO+SAM and Mask3D), OV3D-CG gains +1.7% AP (25.4 vs. 23.7), +3.7% AP50, and +3.9% AP25, with particularly strong improvements on rare categories (APtail: 22.9 vs. 21.9). Compared to the 3D-only OpenMask3D, the improvement is +10.0% AP (25.4 vs. 15.4), demonstrating the value of the dual-branch proposal and MLLM-based semantic reasoning over CLIP. Against the 2D-based SAI3D, OV3D-CG achieves +12.7% AP (25.4 vs. 12.7), confirming that 3D geometric priors combined with contextual MLLM reasoning substantially outperform 2D-only pipelines. On Replica, OV3D-CG (3D) leads Open3DIS (3D) by +5.1% AP (20.0 vs. 14.9) and OpenMask3D by +6.9% AP (20.0 vs. 13.1). Notably, with oracle masks, the Semantic Reasoning Module alone reaches 44.9% AP, exceeding the fully supervised Mask3D (35.5% AP) by +9.4%, indicating that contextual MLLM reasoning can surpass even supervised classification when given perfect segmentation.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the OV-3DIS pipeline established by OpenMask3D, Open3DIS, and SAI3D, which pair a pre-trained 3D segmenter (typically Mask3D) with CLIP for zero-shot instance recognition. The key departure is replacing CLIP's isolated object-text matching with MLLM-based (Gemini, LLaVA) Chain-of-Thought reasoning over three context-aware 2D representations (bounding box, landmarks, SAM mask), explicitly leveraging scene-level spatial context that CLIP cannot capture. The Class-agnostic Proposal Module also departs from prior single-branch designs by fusing Mask3D (common objects) with SAI3D's superpoint-based SAM-guided segmentation (rare/small objects) via NMS, addressing a known weakness of 3D-only proposals.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The oracle-mask experiment is the most consequential finding: a zero-shot MLLM recognition module outperforms fully supervised Mask3D by +9.4% AP, suggesting that the current bottleneck in OV-3DIS is mask quality rather than semantic recognition. This reframes where future effort should be directed. However, the method's practical deployment is constrained by the computational cost of large MLLMs per instance and the sensitivity of results to mask quality (the gap between oracle and predicted masks is substantial). Open questions include whether smaller or distilled models can retain the contextual reasoning advantage, and whether the CoT prompting strategy generalizes to dynamic or outdoor scenes beyond the indoor benchmarks tested.
