## Problem

Expanding the receptive field in point transformers via larger group attention causes attention dilution, degrading fine-grained feature extraction. Existing proxy-based alternatives face three critical limitations: unreliable proxy sampling on geometrically diverse point clouds, inefficient proxy-point interaction computation, and imbalanced fusion of local and global information, making them unsuitable for precise 3D understanding tasks.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ICCV20252699/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The model takes a raw 3D point cloud P_point ∈ R^{N×3} as input, where N varies per scene, and produces per-point features for downstream tasks. For semantic segmentation it outputs per-point class labels; for object detection it produces 3D bounding boxes with class and orientation; for instance segmentation it outputs per-point instance assignments. The proxy count M is fixed (e.g., 160) regardless of scene geometry, and the model achieves ~20 FPS on nuScenes, indicating practical real-time viability for outdoor driving scenarios.

## Method

SP²T is a dual-stream U-Net-style transformer where a point stream (using PTv3 serialization attention for local fusion) runs in parallel with a proxy stream (using self-attention with Table-Based Relative Bias for global fusion). Proxies are sampled via a spatial-wise binary search that finds the optimal uniform grid spacing to hit a target proxy count, avoiding the aspect-ratio distortion of fixed-count grids and the FLOPs uncertainty of fixed-size grids. Each point is associated with the 8 corner proxies of its grid cell (vertex-based association, equivalent to L∞-norm KNN with k=8), enabling O(knd) sparse proxy attention via map-reduce over association tuples instead of O(nmd) dense attention. A Table-Based Relative Bias uses trilinear interpolation on a learned 3D lookup table to inject spatial positional information into the sparse attention scores, and empty proxies (grid cells with no points) are retained to encode spatial voids during global fusion.

## Results

On indoor semantic segmentation, SP²T achieves 78.7 mIoU on ScanNet Val, 37.0 mIoU on ScanNet200 Val, and 74.6/79.7 mIoU on S3DIS Area5/6-fold, surpassing PTv3 by 1.2–2.0% across all settings. On outdoor benchmarks, SP²T reaches 81.2 mIoU on nuScenes Val, 71.7 mIoU on Semantic KITTI Val, and 71.9 mIoU on Waymo Val. For Waymo 3D object detection, SP²T attains 72.1 mAPH (single-frame) and 74.0 mAPH (multi-frame), outperforming PTv3 by 1.6% and 1.8% respectively. On indoor instance segmentation, SP²T achieves 42.2 mAP on ScanNet and 24.7 mAP on ScanNet200.

## Baselines & Comparisons

SP²T consistently outperforms the strongest transformer baseline PTv3 [52] across all indoor and outdoor benchmarks, with gains of 1.2% mIoU on ScanNet, 1.8% on ScanNet200, and 2.0% on S3DIS 6-fold. Against proxy-based methods, the improvements are substantially larger: SP²T surpasses PointASNL [53] by 5.9% mIoU on S3DIS (74.6 vs. 68.7) and 15.7% on ScanNet (78.7 vs. 63.0), SPoTr [35] by 3.8% on S3DIS, and Fast PT [34] by 4.5% on S3DIS. On outdoor semantic segmentation, SP²T leads PTv3 by 0.8% on nuScenes, 0.9% on Semantic KITTI, and 0.6% on Waymo, and outperforms SphereFormer [22] by 2.8–3.9% across datasets. For Waymo object detection, SP²T beats PTv3 by 1.6% mAPH (single-frame) and 1.8% (multi-frame), and surpasses CenterPoint [56] and PillarNet [23] by 1.6–3.8% mAPH. On instance segmentation, SP²T improves over PTv3 by 1.3% mAP on ScanNet and 1.6% on ScanNet200, while the transfer study confirms that replacing PTv3's local fusion with MinkUNet [8] still yields a 3.5% gain over MinkUNet alone, validating the plug-and-play nature of the proxy stream.

## Relation to Prior Work

SP2T extends the local proxy-based point transformer lineage (PointASNL, SPoTr, Fast PT) by replacing their FPS-based or learned sampling with a spatial-wise binary-search grid sampling and a vertex-based L-infinity association, and by introducing Sparse Proxy Attention (SPA) with Table-Based Relative Bias to replace costly dense cross-attention between proxies and points. For local feature extraction, it directly adopts PTv3's serialization attention as the point-stream backbone, positioning itself as a hybrid that combines PTv3's local strength with a proxy-stream global receptive field. The dual-stream design draws conceptual inspiration from MobileFormer and dual-scale point cloud networks, but the specific combination of spatial-wise sampling, vertex association, map-reduce sparse attention, and TRB is novel within the proxy-based point cloud literature.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

SP2T demonstrates that a well-designed local proxy mechanism can close the gap between proxy-based and full-attention point transformers on both indoor (ScanNet, S3DIS) and outdoor (nuScenes, Sem.KITTI, Waymo) benchmarks, suggesting that the proxy paradigm is viable for high-precision segmentation when sampling and interaction are handled carefully. The finding that empty proxies (grid cells with no associated points) contribute positively to accuracy is a mildly counterintuitive observation about spatial voids carrying structural information. However, the paper acknowledges that inference speed (74 ms vs. PTv3's 61 ms) and the dual-stream dimensional balance remain open optimization targets, and the modest gains over PTv3 (1.2% mIoU on ScanNet) indicate that the proxy approach has not yet surpassed the strongest non-proxy baseline by a large margin.
