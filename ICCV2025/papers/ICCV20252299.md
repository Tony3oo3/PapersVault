## Problem

Generating standard flat-lay garment images from in-the-wild photos (worn, hung, or casually placed) is a high-specification task where existing diffusion-based methods exhibit structural hallucinations (inaccurate length, fitness, contours) and commercially unacceptable texture/logo distortion. The root cause is the limited spatial reasoning of Stable Diffusion architectures combined with the narrow, highly standardized manifold of garment distributions, which no prior method addresses simultaneously for both structure and fine detail.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ICCV20252299/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method takes a single in-the-wild clothing image (RGB, 768×768) showing a garment in any real-world context and produces a forward-facing flat-lay garment image on a clear background. Beyond the raw image, the pipeline consumes 10 discrete clothing attributes extracted by a frozen Qwen2-VL-7B LLM and retrieves K=4 nearest-neighbor embeddings and silhouette landmarks from a curated memory database of 4,000 standard flat-lay garments, all of which condition the generation process.

## Method

RAGDiffusion is a Retrieval-Augmented Generation framework built on SDXL with two coupled processes. First, a dual-tower StructureNet (CLIP-ViT-L/14 backbone) is trained via InforNCE contrastive learning to produce latent structure embeddings that fuse image features with LLM-derived attribute embeddings; a Structure Locally Linear Embedding (SLLE) then projects the in-the-wild embedding onto the standard-garment manifold using K=4 nearest neighbors, yielding a remapped embedding (soft guidance via an Embedding Prompt Adapter cross-attention branch) and fused silhouette landmarks (hard guidance via a 4-layer Landmark Guider). Second, an isomorphic ReferenceNet UNet preserves fine-grained patterns through concatenated self-attention with the main denoising UNet, while a three-stage Parameter Gradual Encoding Adaptation (PGEA) procedure re-trains the SDXL UNet's conv_in/conv_out layers (4→16 channels) to interface with the higher-fidelity FLUX VAE, eliminating the logo and text distortion that renders prior outputs commercially unusable.

## Results

On the STGarment dataset, RAGDiffusion achieves the best scores across all five metrics: SSIM 0.6963, LPIPS 0.3684, FID 9.99, KID 1.092, and DISTS 0.192. The closest competitor, ReferenceNet, scores FID 10.70 and KID 1.399, while the strongest garment-specific baseline TryOffAnyone reaches FID 18.86 and KID 7.919. RAGDiffusion also demonstrates strong cross-dataset generalization on Viton-HD (FID 12.3, KID 1.45) and DressCode lower-body/dress categories (FID 40.5/23.1, KID 19.4/6.22), substantially outperforming baselines that were not trained on those domains.

## Baselines & Comparisons

RAGDiffusion outperforms all six baselines on every reported metric. Against the closest competitor ReferenceNet (FID 10.70, KID 1.399), RAGDiffusion improves FID by 0.71 and KID by 0.31, while also leading in SSIM (0.6963 vs. 0.6546) and LPIPS (0.3684 vs. 0.3979). The garment-specific baselines TryOffDiff (FID 34.34) and TryOffAnyone (FID 18.86) are substantially outperformed, with RAGDiffusion reducing FID by 24.35 and 8.87 points respectively. Among the general conditional-generation baselines, IP-Adapter (FID 14.50) is the strongest, yet RAGDiffusion still achieves a 4.51-point FID advantage. ControlNet (FID 23.02) and Paint-by-Example (FID 26.55) trail significantly, confirming that naive spatial conditioning without retrieval-based structure aggregation is insufficient for this high-specification task. On cross-dataset evaluation (Viton-HD, DressCode), RAGDiffusion also leads all baselines, with particularly large margins on DressCode lower-body (FID 40.5 vs. 114.2 for TryOffAnyone) and dress (FID 23.1 vs. 38.4 for TryOffAnyone), validating the zero-shot generalizability benefit of the retrieval database.

## Relation to Prior Work

RAGDiffusion extends the garment restoration line (TileGAN → TryOffDiff/TryOffAnyone/IGR) by introducing a Retrieval-Augmented Generation paradigm to diffusion-based image synthesis, a direction previously explored only for content reference (e.g., KNN-Diffusion, RetrieveGAN) rather than structural constraint. It repurposes IP-Adapter's cross-attention mechanism as an Embedding Prompt Adapter for structure conditioning, borrows ReferenceNet's isomorphic UNet for pattern preservation, and adapts Locally Linear Embedding (LLE) into a Structure LLE that projects in-the-wild embeddings onto a standard-garment manifold. The PGEA module further departs from prior work by adapting the SDXL UNet to FLUX's higher-channel VAE, addressing a detail-fidelity gap that no prior garment restoration method has tackled.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The paper demonstrates that retrieval-based external knowledge can serve as both soft (embedding) and hard (landmark) structural constraints in diffusion generation, offering a principled route to mitigate hallucinations in high-specification tasks without retraining the backbone. The zero-shot generalization to unseen garment categories via retrieval database expansion—without paired data or fine-tuning—suggests a scalable, low-cost adaptation strategy for industrial pipelines. However, the approach is currently limited to upper-body clothing, requires a curated retrieval database, and PGEA's 4-day/8-GPU training cost limits immediate adoption. Open questions include whether the RAG-for-structure paradigm generalizes to other high-fidelity generation domains (e.g., product photography, architectural rendering) and whether the SLLE manifold assumption holds for more diverse or open-vocabulary categories.
