## Problem

Existing VLM-based person Re-ID methods still rely on the traditional image-side dominated paradigm of extracting visual features and computing similarity distances, underutilizing the reasoning and instruction-following capabilities of vision-language models. This paper asks whether a VLM's text decoder can be repurposed to perform text-side dominated Re-ID—interpreting task requirements from natural language and performing similarity inference directly—rather than merely serving as an auxiliary semantic branch.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ICCV20251494/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The model accepts a query (person image, text description, or attribute phrase) alongside a gallery of candidate images, and outputs the matched image index as a textual response in a VQA-style multi-turn best-choice format. To handle gallery sizes that exceed VLM token limits, a ResNet-50 baseline first filters the gallery by feature similarity above a threshold τ, and the remaining candidates are presented in non-overlapping pairs for sequential selection. Input images are resized to 256×128 (Stage 1) or 384×192 (Stage 2) to balance resolution with the number of gallery images that fit in context.

## Method

ChatReID builds on Qwen2-VL 2B (ViT encoder + LLM decoder) and introduces a Hierarchical Progressive Tuning (HPT) strategy across three stages trained on 8M+ instruction prompts. Stage 1 fine-tunes both encoder and decoder on 5M image-captioning pairs to ground fine-grained pedestrian attributes (gender, clothing, posture, accessories). Stage 2 replaces the decoder with a freshly pre-trained one and trains on 3M samples across seven retrieval tasks (image-to-image, text-to-image, image-to-attribute at one-to-one, one-to-many, and many-to-many complexities) to instill identity-level matching. Stage 3 freezes the encoder and fine-tunes the decoder on 500K samples from ten benchmarks across four Re-ID settings (standard, cloth-changing, visible-infrared, text-to-image) to sharpen instruction-following and multi-modal reasoning. The key architectural insight is that the LLM decoder itself performs the similarity judgment—selecting the best-matching gallery image through sequential best-choice reasoning—rather than computing a feature distance, making the retrieval process interpretable and adaptable to open-ended, free-form queries.

## Results

ChatReID achieves state-of-the-art mAP and Rank-1 across all ten benchmarks spanning four Re-ID settings. On standard Re-ID, it reaches 96.4% mAP / 97.2% Rank-1 on Market1501, 87.5% / 90.1% on MSMT17, and 91.3% / 92.7% on CUHK03. On cloth-changing Re-ID, it attains 76.3% mAP on LTCC and 75.6% on PRCC; on visible-infrared Re-ID, 83.6% mAP on SYSU-MM01 and 95.8% on RegDB; and on text-to-image Re-ID, 80.1% mAP on CUHK-PEDES, 70.5% on ICFG-PEDES, and 73.1% on RSTPReid.

## Baselines & Comparisons

ChatReID outperforms all 22 baselines across every benchmark and task setting. The most dramatic gains appear in cloth-changing Re-ID, where ChatReID surpasses the strongest prior method IRM [9] by +24.3% mAP on LTCC (76.3 vs. 52.0) and +23.3% on PRCC (75.6 vs. 52.3), and in text-to-image Re-ID, where it leads WoRA [28] by +25.6% mAP on ICFG-PEDES (70.5 vs. 44.9) and MARS [4] by +20.2% on RSTPReid (73.1 vs. 52.9). On standard Re-ID, the margin over IRM is +15.1% mAP on MSMT17 (87.5 vs. 72.4) and +5.9% on CUHK03 (91.3 vs. 85.4). For visible-infrared Re-ID, ChatReID exceeds DEEN [42] by +11.8% mAP on SYSU-MM01 (83.6 vs. 71.8) and MMN [43] by +11.7% on RegDB (95.8 vs. 84.1). Earlier CNN/Transformer baselines such as TransReID [8], SAN [16], and PASS [47] trail by 10–30+ mAP points, confirming that the VLM-based text-side-dominated paradigm with hierarchical progressive tuning substantially narrows the gap to task-specific architectures while unifying all four Re-ID settings in a single model.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper departs from the dominant image-side Re-ID paradigm (feature extraction followed by similarity sorting) by making the LLM decoder the primary reasoning engine for identity matching, a shift from prior VLM-based Re-ID methods like Instruct-ReID that still rely on image encoders for the core matching. It builds directly on Qwen2-VL's Naive Dynamic Resolution architecture as the base model, extending the VLM-driven Re-ID line (Tan et al., Yang et al.) by replacing rigid template-driven prompts with a hierarchical progressive tuning strategy across three stages. The 8M+ instruction dataset and the unification of four Re-ID settings (standard, cloth-changing, visible-infrared, text-to-image) into a single interactive framework represent a significant departure from prior work that treats each setting in isolation.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The text-side dominated paradigm opens a path toward truly interactive, open-ended person retrieval where users can provide free-form, partial, or multi-modal queries in a conversational loop—something fixed-pipeline Re-ID systems cannot support. However, the evaluation still requires a ResNet-50 pre-filter and a multi-turn best-choice reformulation to handle large galleries, limiting the claim of a fully end-to-end VLM solution. The finding that a 2B model matches 7B performance suggests the task may not stress-test VLM reasoning to its limits, raising questions about whether the approach scales to more complex or open-world identification scenarios. Open questions include real-time deployment feasibility, generalization to unseen identities, and whether the paradigm extends beyond person Re-ID to other fine-grained recognition tasks.
