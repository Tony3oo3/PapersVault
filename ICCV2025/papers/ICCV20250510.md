## Problem

State Space Duality (SSD) from Mamba2 inherits the causal property of SSMs, where each token can only access preceding tokens, which is fundamentally mismatched with non-causal image data. Additionally, flattening 2D feature maps into 1D sequences disrupts spatial adjacency, causing nearby patches to become distant in the sequence. Existing multi-scan strategies only partially alleviate these issues without resolving the underlying causal dependency or the 1D distance distortion.

## Input / Output

The model consumes standard RGB images (224×224 for ImageNet classification, 800×1333 for COCO detection/segmentation, 512×512 for ADE20K semantic segmentation) and produces class labels, bounding boxes with class scores, or pixel-level segmentation masks. Internally, 2D feature maps are flattened into 1D token sequences, but the NC-SSD mechanism renders the output independent of the scanning route, and 2D Rotary Position Embedding is applied to the C and B projections to encode spatial relationships without relying on sequential distance.

## Method

The key insight is redefining the role of the scalar A in SSD: rather than A controlling how much of the previous hidden state to retain (causal), it dictates the magnitude of the current token's own contribution to the hidden state (self-referential). This makes each token's contribution independent of all others, so the causal mask can be removed and forward/reverse scanning results integrate into a single global hidden state H = Σ(1/A_j)·Z_j, eliminating the per-token sequence dimension. The computation reduces to Y = C((B·m)ᵀX), a linear-attention-like form where m = 1/A acts as a learned per-token importance weight. The full VSSD architecture wraps NC-SSD with depthwise convolutions, a Local Perception Unit, FFN, 2D RoPE, overlapped downsampling, and replaces the final stage's NC-SSD with standard multi-head self-attention, organized in four hierarchical stages.

## Results

On ImageNet-1K, VSSD-T (28M params, 5.0G FLOPs) achieves 83.8% top-1 accuracy, VSSD-S (50M, 8.1G) reaches 84.6%, and VSSD-B (89M, 16.1G) attains 85.4%, consistently outperforming the SSM-based VMamba by 1.0–1.5% at comparable compute. On MS COCO detection (Mask R-CNN 1×), VSSD-B achieves 49.9 box AP and 44.8 mask AP, surpassing VMamba-B by +0.7 and +0.9 respectively. On ADE20K semantic segmentation (UperNet), VSSD-B reaches 52.2 mIoU (multi-scale), exceeding VMamba-B by +0.6. In terms of efficiency, VSSD uses substantially less memory than VMamba (e.g., 3481 MB vs. 7826 MB at base scale) while maintaining competitive throughput.

## Baselines & Comparisons

VSSD consistently outperforms the closest SSM competitor VMamba across all three model scales on ImageNet-1K: +1.2% (Tiny), +1.0% (Small), and +1.5% (Base) in top-1 accuracy at similar FLOPs. Against attention-based baselines, VSSD-T (83.8%) surpasses Swin-T (81.3%) by 2.5% and NAT-T (83.2%) by 0.6%, while VSSD-B (85.4%) exceeds NAT-B (84.3%) by 1.1% and Swin-B (83.5%) by 1.9%. On COCO detection (1× schedule), VSSD-B leads VMamba-B by +0.7 box AP and +0.9 mask AP, and VSSD-T matches VMamba-T on box AP (47.0 vs. 47.3) while edging ahead on mask AP (42.8 vs. 42.7). On ADE20K segmentation, VSSD-T and VSSD-B beat VMamba by +0.7/+0.9 and +0.5/+0.6 mIoU (SS/MS) respectively, though VSSD-S trails VMamba-S slightly (50.3 vs. 50.6 SS). In efficiency, VSSD achieves markedly lower memory usage than VMamba (e.g., 1946 MB vs. 3204 MB at Tiny; 3481 MB vs. 7826 MB at Base) while maintaining comparable or higher throughput at the Small scale (826 vs. 743 imgs/s).

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper directly extends Mamba2's State Space Duality (SSD) by redefining the role of the scalar transition parameter A: rather than controlling how much of the hidden state is retained (causal), it controls the current token's contribution to a shared hidden state (non-causal). This departs from the dominant multi-scan paradigm used by prior vision SSM models (ViM, VMamba, LocalMamba), which work around causality by running forward and reverse scans and merging results. The theoretical observation that bidirectional scanning of the modified recurrence collapses to a single global sum — eliminating the per-token hidden state dimension — is the paper's core conceptual contribution, building on the SSD formulation from Dao & Gu (ICML 2024) and the hierarchical vision backbone design from VMamba (NeurIPS 2024).

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The NC-SSD formulation offers a principled, single-pass alternative to multi-scan strategies for making SSMs non-causal in vision, with the practical benefit of a global (rather than per-token) hidden state that reduces memory and improves training throughput by over 50% compared to bidirectional scanning. The reduction to a linear-attention-like form (Eq. 10) also clarifies the relationship between SSMs and linear attention in the vision setting. Open questions include whether the single global hidden state remains sufficient for very long sequences or video, whether the approach generalizes to non-scalar A matrices, and whether the modest 1–2% accuracy gains over VMamba persist at larger scales or in downstream generative tasks.
