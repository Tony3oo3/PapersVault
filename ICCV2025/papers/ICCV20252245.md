## Problem

Text-to-3D generation produces detailed static geometry but cannot synthesize motion, while video diffusion generates motion with temporal and multi-view geometric inconsistencies. Existing 4D generation methods either suffer from generalization limits (trained on limited 4D data) or only produce a single animation rather than a fully animatable model. This paper addresses the gap by creating animatable blendshape models for diverse virtual characters (including non-human species) from text prompts alone.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ICCV20252245/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The pipeline takes a text prompt describing a virtual character and a pool of expression prompts (e.g., 'blinking eyes,' 'frowning') as input, with the 3D asset rendered at 512×512 resolution. It produces a set of 100 PCA-derived orthogonal blendshapes, per-frame 3D meshes, and deformable 3D Gaussian splats that together form a fully animatable morphable model. For retargeting, the method additionally consumes human facial landmarks (6 per eye, 8 per mouth) and uses a VLM to identify corresponding regions on non-human characters via shadow mapping to establish 3D landmark correspondences.

## Method

The core contribution is View-Conditioned Deformable Gaussian Splatting (VCDGS), which reconstructs deformable 3D Gaussian splats from 4D multi-view video by decomposing per-control-point transformations into a global SE(3) motion and a view-dependent DeformMLP that takes (position, view index, time) as input to absorb multi-view geometric inconsistencies. A separate RefineMLP predicts per-Gaussian color, scale, and orientation offsets to handle appearance inconsistencies across frames. Both MLPs are used only during training; at inference, the canonical view (v=0) yields clean deformations. The pipeline first generates a static mesh via text-to-3D, animates it with monocular video diffusion, then produces synchronized multi-view videos via 4Real-Video, and finally applies PCA across all captured deformed shapes to extract orthogonal blendshapes without manual selection.

## Results

On GPTEval3D (GPT-4o Elo ratings, higher is better), T2Bs achieves 1301.5 on Text-Asset Alignment, 1306.2 on 3D Plausibility, and 1436.4 on Texture Details, outperforming all four 4D generation baselines on every criterion. In a user study with 4,220 ratings across 422 samples, T2Bs is preferred 86.8% of the time for Appearance, 84.2% for Geometry, and 84.6% Overall, compared to 13.0%, 15.8%, and 15.3% respectively for the closest competitor (4Real-Video). The method also demonstrates superior expression retargeting over LivePortrait, particularly for non-human features such as exaggerated eyes and wide mouth openings.

## Baselines & Comparisons

T2Bs is compared against four 4D generation methods (DreamGaussian4D, SV4D, 4Real-Video, L4GM) and one 2D retargeting method (LivePortrait). On GPTEval3D, T2Bs leads all baselines by a wide margin: it surpasses DreamGaussian4D (the strongest 4D baseline) by ~165 Elo points on 3D Plausibility and ~279 on Texture Details, and outperforms 4Real-Video (the video source it refines) by ~226 on Texture Details. In the user study, T2Bs dominates with 84–87% preference rates across all dimensions, while 4Real-Video (the next-best) receives only 13–41%, and DreamGaussian4D and SV4D are essentially never preferred (<0.5%). For expression retargeting, T2Bs qualitatively outperforms LivePortrait on non-human character features (e.g., closing large eyes, opening wide mouths) where the 2D-only method fails. The ablation studies further confirm that the view-conditioned deformation and LBS components are critical: removing view conditioning causes geometric artifacts, and removing LBS introduces significant noise in reconstructions.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper sits at the intersection of text-to-3D generation, 4D video diffusion, and deformable 3D Gaussian splatting. It builds directly on 4Real-Video for multi-view video generation and on 3D Gaussian Splatting with Linear Blend Skinning as the deformation backbone, but departs from prior 4D generation work (DreamGaussian4D, SV4D, L4GM) by treating the output not as a single animation but as training data for a reusable blendshape model. The key conceptual departure from human head avatar methods (GaussianAvatars, FlashAvatar, GaussianHead) is that T2Bs learns a morphable model entirely from generated data rather than relying on FLAME or real motion capture, enabling non-human and stylized characters.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

T2Bs is the first framework to produce animatable blendshape models for arbitrary virtual characters (including animals and stylized figures) from text alone, removing the need for motion capture or human-specific priors. This opens a path toward scalable, artist-free character animation pipelines for social media and gaming. Key limitations remain: the method is restricted to head animations (blendshapes cannot handle articulated full-body motion), depends on the quality of 4D video generators that still produce occasional temporal inconsistencies, and is sensitive to rapid topological changes like tongue protrusion. An open question is whether the PCA-based blendshape construction generalizes well beyond the ~20 expression prompts used, and whether improvements in 4D video generation will further reduce the need for the view-conditioned correction.
