## Problem

Existing interleaved image-text datasets for VLM pretraining (e.g., MMC4, OBELICS) are crawled from webpages and suffer from loose text-image associations, low knowledge density, and incoherent image sequences, limiting their effectiveness for knowledge-intensive and reasoning tasks. Meanwhile, the vast corpus of online instructional videos—where humans naturally learn foundational subjects through tightly coupled visual demonstrations and verbal explanations—remains largely unexploited for VLM training.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ICCV20250699/cropped_page1_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The pipeline ingests 75,000 English instructional videos (22,697 class hours, ~18 min average) spanning 6 subjects (mathematics, physics, chemistry, earth science, engineering, computer science) collected via an LLM-generated taxonomy of 3,915 knowledge points. It produces 610K interleaved samples averaging 10.7 keyframes and 1,297 text tokens each, where each sample is a chronologically ordered sequence of keyframes, OCR text, and refined ASR paragraphs. The final corpus contains 6.5M images and 0.75B text tokens, with images constrained to 2–45 per sample and text to 11–34,174 tokens.

## Method

The core contribution is a multi-level, coarse-to-fine video-to-textbook pipeline that transforms raw instructional videos into a coherent interleaved corpus. At the video level, whisper-large-v3 transcribes audio, Qwen2-72B rewrites colloquial ASR into fluent text, and an LLM filters videos by relevance, knowledge density, and transcription quality (retaining 75K from 159K). At the clip level, ASR timestamps segment videos into 10–20 s clips, and VideoLlama2-generated captions are matched against ASR via gte-Qwen2-7B-instruct embeddings to discard visually uninformative segments. At the keyframe level, SSIM-based iterative frame comparison detects significant visual changes to extract keyframes, InternVL2-40B performs OCR for on-screen text and formulas, and redundant OCR across consecutive frames is filtered. The key insight is that instructional videos inherently provide temporally coherent, knowledge-dense image-text sequences with tight visual-linguistic alignment—properties absent in webpage-crawled interleaved data—enabling VLMs to learn structured reasoning and in-context attention to interleaved context.

## Results

Pretraining LLaVA-1.5-7B on Textbook-6.5M yields average accuracy gains of +3.2% (0-shot), +8.3% (1-shot), +4.0% (2-shot), and +4.6% (4-shot) over the best baseline across seven benchmarks. On the knowledge-intensive ScienceQA benchmark, Textbook-6.5M reaches 37.3% (4-shot) versus 16.4% for OBELICS and 11.6% for MMC4. On MathVista, it achieves 29.2% (4-shot) compared to 27.6% (OBELICS) and 26.0% (MMC4). Even for the more advanced Idefics2-8B model, pretraining on Textbook-6.5M provides an additional +1.4% average improvement over OBELICS.

## Baselines & Comparisons

Against MMC4 and MMC4-Core-ff, Textbook-6.5M dominates across all seven benchmarks, with the largest margin on ScienceQA (37.3% vs. 11.6%/10.2% at 4-shot) and MathVista (29.2% vs. 26.0%/27.8%). Against OBELICS, the advantage is most pronounced on knowledge- and reasoning-intensive tasks: ScienceQA (37.3% vs. 16.4%), MathVista (29.2% vs. 27.6%), and MathVision (18.1% vs. 14.9% at 4-shot). On general-domain benchmarks like OKVQA, Textbook-6.5M initially lags OBELICS at 0-shot (10.2% vs. 13.0%) but surpasses it at 2-shot (36.8% vs. 35.7%) and 4-shot (39.9% vs. 37.5%), suggesting stronger in-context learning from the coherent video-derived interleaving. OmniCorpus is compared only in dataset statistics (Table 1), where Textbook-6.5M achieves a substantially higher InSI-SIM (0.686 vs. 0.321), confirming tighter intra-sample image coherence. COYO-700M and LAION-5B serve solely as image-text paired references in Table 1 and are not used for pretraining comparison.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the line of work on interleaved image-text corpora for VLM pretraining (MMC4, OBELICS, OmniCorpus), which have been sourced from webpages and documents. It departs from this webpage-centric paradigm by repurposing instructional videos as the primary data source, motivated by the observation that videos offer tighter image-text alignment and more coherent sequential logic than crawled webpages. The construction pipeline draws on the Phi-series insight that textbook-level data quality matters for LLM training, and leverages LLMs (Qwen2-72B for ASR rewriting, InternVL2 for OCR, VideoLlama2 for clip filtering) as components in a multi-level extraction and filtering pipeline. The LLM-proposed knowledge taxonomy for systematic video collection is a novel curation strategy not seen in prior interleaved dataset work.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

This dataset addresses a well-identified gap in VLM pretraining: existing interleaved corpora suffer from loose image-text relations, incoherent image sequences, and low knowledge density, which limits in-context learning and reasoning capabilities. The thorough empirical validation—including the 'cheat test' showing superior interleaved context awareness and the shuffling experiment confirming sequential dependency—provides strong evidence that video-centric coherence is a genuine quality axis, not just a data-source preference. Limitations include English-only content, six subjects, and YouTube-specific sourcing, leaving open whether the approach generalizes to other languages, domains, or video platforms. A key open question is whether the benefits persist at larger scale or when combined with other data sources in mixed pretraining recipes.
