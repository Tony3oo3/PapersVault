## Problem

Existing view-guided point cloud completion (VG-PCC) models suffer severe performance drops when generalizing to unseen categories. The paper identifies two root causes: encoders fail to bridge the image–point-cloud modality gap and thus over-rely on class-specific RGB patterns, while decoders refine global structure holistically and neglect class-agnostic local geometric primitives (edges, surfaces, corners) that transfer across categories.

## Input / Output

The method takes a partial point cloud of 2048 points and a paired 224×224 single-view RGB image as input, and outputs a complete point cloud of 2048 points. The image is first converted to a depth map and camera intrinsics by an off-the-shelf estimator (e.g., UniDepth), then unprojected into 3D space to form a lifted point set that is normalized to unit scale and zero mean before encoding. The final dense prediction (N×M×N_G = 8×8×32 = 2048 points) is concatenated with the original partial points and downsampled back to 2048 for evaluation.

## Method

GAPM comprises a Geometry Aligned Encoder (GAE) and a Prior Modulated Decoder (PMD). The GAE lifts the image depth into 3D so that both the partial point cloud and the lifted image geometry are tokenized (Point-BERT-style FPS+KNN patches) and encoded by a shared 4-layer 3D Transformer backbone; cross-source fusion then alternates cross-attention (image features as queries, point features as keys/values) with an MLP-Mixer to produce a unified representation. The PMD first partitions the encoded features into N=8 spatial regions via a slot-attention-style iterative grouping (GRU-updated region queries with softmax over regions), then learns M=8 class-agnostic local priors parameterized by folding MLPs that deform a 2D grid. Each region's features produce affine modulation parameters (α, β) that adapt the shared priors in a style-based-folding manner, and the sparse prediction per region supplies a centroid offset so that all regions are assembled in a consistent coordinate system. Training is end-to-end supervised with Chamfer Distance on both sparse and dense predictions plus an expansion penalty to prevent region collapse.

## Results

On the ShapeNet ViPC benchmark, GAPM achieves an average CD of 1.107×10⁻³ and F-Score of 0.860 on seen categories, and 1.684×10⁻³ CD and 0.802 F-Score on unseen categories. The headline improvement is on unseen categories, where GAPM reduces CD by 0.670×10⁻³ (28.5% relative) over the previous SOTA (EGIInet, 2.354×10⁻³) and by 2.917×10⁻³ over ViPC (4.601×10⁻³). On the real-world ScanNet-ViPC dataset, GAPM achieves 8.162 CD vs. 11.553 for EGIInet on unseen categories, confirming the generalization advantage in real-world settings.

## Baselines & Comparisons

GAPM outperforms all four baselines on both seen and unseen categories across both CD and F-Score. On unseen categories, the gap is most pronounced: GAPM reduces average CD from 2.354 (EGIInet) to 1.684, a 28.5% relative improvement, and from 4.601 (ViPC) to 1.684, a 63.4% reduction. On seen categories, the improvement is more modest (1.107 vs. 1.211 CD over EGIInet), consistent with the paper's argument that existing methods already perform well in-distribution. Per-category, GAPM leads on Bench (0.878 vs. 1.047 CD), Monitor (1.631 vs. 2.513), Speaker (3.095 vs. 4.282), and Cellphone (1.131 vs. 1.575) over EGIInet. On the real-world ScanNet-ViPC benchmark, GAPM achieves 8.162 CD on unseen categories versus 11.553 for EGIInet, a 29.3% reduction, further validating the generalization claim beyond synthetic data.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the VG-PCC line initiated by ViPC and advanced by XMFnet, CSDN, and EGIInet, all of which fuse image and point-cloud features via attention or style transfer in a holistic decoder. GAPM departs from this lineage in two conceptual ways: it replaces direct cross-modal attention with depth-based 3D lifting (borrowing from monocular depth estimation and unprojection), and it replaces holistic decoding with region-wise prior modulation (drawing on Slot Attention for grouping and style-based FoldingNet for modulation). The encoder tokenizer is adapted from Point-BERT, and the local priors are parameterized as FoldingNet-style MLPs, but their use as shared, class-agnostic building blocks modulated per-region is a novel repurposing of these components.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

By demonstrating that a substantial portion of VG-PCC's unseen-category failure stems from modality misalignment and holistic decoding rather than insufficient data, the paper redirects attention toward architectural choices that promote transferable geometric reasoning. The depth-lifting strategy is a practical, plug-in-friendly way to reduce the image-point modality gap that could benefit other cross-modal 3D tasks. Limitations include reliance on an external depth estimator (albeit shown to be robust across three), evaluation primarily on synthetic ShapeNet with a small 800-object real-world benchmark, and a fixed number of regions/priors that may not scale to highly complex or multi-object scenes. Open questions include whether the approach extends to multi-view or video-guided completion and whether the learned priors can be further disentangled into interpretable geometric primitives.
