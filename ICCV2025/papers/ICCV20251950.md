## Problem

SAM 2's greedy memory selection—always picking the single highest-IoU mask—suffers from error accumulation in long videos: once an incorrect mask enters memory during occlusion or ambiguous frames, it irreversibly corrupts all subsequent predictions. This limits SAM 2's robustness on complex long-term sequences with frequent occlusions and object reappearances.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ICCV20251950/cropped_page1_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method operates in a semi-supervised setting, consuming a video sequence and a first-frame object mask prompt. It outputs per-frame segmentation masks; for VOT benchmarks, masks are converted to bounding boxes via min/max coordinates of non-zero indices. The image encoder runs once per frame (identical to SAM 2), while the lightweight mask decoder and memory module are invoked 3P times per frame (P pathways × 3 candidates), adding only ~14% FPS overhead with P=3.

## Method

SAM2Long replaces SAM 2's single greedy memory pathway with a constrained tree of P (default 3) parallel memory pathways, each maintaining its own memory bank and a cumulative score (sum of log-IoU across the path). At every frame, each pathway's decoder produces 3 mask candidates (3P total), which are pruned back to the top-P by cumulative score, forming a beam-search over segmentation hypotheses. An uncertainty-handling mechanism enforces mask diversity when occlusion scores fall below a threshold, preventing premature convergence on wrong predictions. Additionally, the memory bank is constructed by selecting only frames with IoU above a threshold and positive occlusion score (rather than the nearest N frames), and memory cross-attention keys are reweighted by occlusion confidence to emphasize reliable entries. The entire approach is training-free, introducing no new parameters beyond SAM 2's existing architecture.

## Results

SAM2Long achieves an average J&F improvement of 3.7 points over SAM 2 across 12 direct comparisons (all model sizes on SA-V val/test and LVOS v2 val). The largest gain is 5.3 J&F points for SAM2Long-L on SA-V test (80.8 vs. 75.5), and 4.7 points for SAM2Long-S on SA-V val (77.7 vs. 73.0). On LVOS v2 val, SAM2Long-L reaches 85.4 J&F (+2.4 over SAM2-L), and on LVOS v1, SAM2Long achieves 81.3 J&F (+3.4 over SAM2). On VOT benchmarks, SAM2Long improves over SAM2 by 3.9 AUC on LaSOT (73.9 vs. 70.0) and 4.0 AUC on LaSOText (60.9 vs. 56.9).

## Baselines & Comparisons

SAM2Long consistently outperforms SAM 2 (and SAM 2.1) across all four model sizes (T, S, B+, L) and all three long-term VOS benchmarks, with J&F gains ranging from +0.8 to +5.3 points; the largest improvements appear on SA-V test where longer propagation amplifies the benefit of tree search. Against prior memory-based VOS methods, SAM2Long-L surpasses XMem (60.1 J&F on SA-V val) and STCN (61.0) by over 20 points, and outperforms Cutie-base+ (61.3) and DEVA (55.4) by similar margins, demonstrating that mask-level tree search is far more effective than feature-level memory propagation. On LVOS v1, SAM2Long achieves 81.3 J&F, exceeding DDMemory (60.7) by 20.6 points and XMem (52.9) by 28.4 points. On VOT benchmarks, SAM2Long (73.9 AUC on LaSOT, 60.9 on LaSOText, 81.1 AO on GOT10k) beats SAM2 by 3.9, 4.0, and 0.4 respectively, and is competitive with dedicated trackers like LORAT (75.1 on LaSOT) and ODTrack (74.0 on LaSOT, 78.2 on GOT10k). Performance gains positively correlate with video duration: on short datasets like DAVIS (1.8 s) and YTVOS (4.5 s), improvements are negligible (+0.1), while on long-duration LVOS v1 (95.4 s) the gain reaches +3.2 J&F.

## Relation to Prior Work

SAM2Long builds directly on SAM 2's memory module, which conditions current-frame segmentation on stored object-aware memories from prior frames. The key conceptual departure is replacing SAM 2's greedy single-mask selection with a constrained tree search inspired by Multiple Hypothesis Tracking (MHT) from the visual tracking literature, maintaining P parallel segmentation pathways and pruning to the top-scoring branches at each step. Unlike prior memory-based VOS methods (XMem, STCN, Cutie) that learn feature-level memory representations, SAM2Long operates at the mask level, selecting among SAM 2's already-generated multi-mask hypotheses without modifying any learned parameters. The object-aware memory bank construction and occlusion-score-weighted attention modulation further refine how stored frames contribute to cross-attention, departing from SAM 2's simple FIFO nearest-frame strategy.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

As SAM 2 has become the de facto foundation model for video segmentation, a training-free improvement that consistently adds 3–5 J&F points across all model sizes and 12 benchmarks has immediate practical value for practitioners deploying SAM 2 in long-video or occlusion-heavy settings. The clean correlation between video duration and performance gain validates the error-accumulation diagnosis and suggests the approach will benefit most in applications involving extended tracking. However, the method remains bounded by SAM 2's inherent representational capacity (no learnable parameters are modified), is primarily designed for single-object tracking, and still fails on scenes with dynamic backgrounds and strong visual distractors—pointing to the need for semantic-level understanding beyond pixel-level mask propagation.
