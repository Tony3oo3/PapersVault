## Problem

Existing point cloud oversegmentation methods rely on expensive spatial queries (FPS, KNN) and complex clustering or graph-partitioning algorithms to balance spatial proximity with semantic consistency, resulting in high computational cost and irregular superpoint boundaries. No prior work has exploited serialization strategies to replace these costly 3D queries with efficient 1D sequence operations for the oversegmentation task.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ICCV20250660/cropped_page0_idx2.webp)

## Input / Output

The method takes a raw point cloud P ∈ R^{N×(C+3)} with per-point features (e.g., normals, RGB) and 3D coordinates, and produces a two-level hierarchical set of superpoints (e.g., 1024 fine-grained and 256 coarse-grained segments) with updated feature vectors. When integrated with a segmentation head, it additionally outputs per-point semantic class labels. The Hilbert serialization converts the 3D partitioning problem into iterative 1D sequence splitting, so the spatial structure is implicitly encoded in sequence order rather than queried explicitly.

## Method

SPCNet serializes the point cloud onto a Hilbert curve and recursively applies 1D quaternary splitting to build a hierarchical segment structure, replacing FPS+KNN-based initialization with O(n) sequence partitioning. An adaptive superpoint update algorithm then refines boundaries by computing cosine similarity between each raw point and 12 candidate neighboring segment features (its own four sub-segments plus eight from adjacent parent segments), hard-assigning points to the most similar segment, and updating superpoint features via Cross-Attention where the superpoint feature serves as Query and member point features as Key/Value. The backbone is a sparse 3D U-Net with Mamba state-space modules at the bottleneck to capture long-range semantics while preserving local geometric detail in shallow convolutional layers. A superpoint interaction module (Graph Transformer + GCN over k-NN feature-space graphs) models cross-superpoint context, and a three-component aggregation loss (compactness, distinction, and purity) trains the network end-to-end.

## Results

SPCNet achieves state-of-the-art oversegmentation performance across all three evaluation datasets. On S3DIS Area 5, it reaches F1=40.50 (vs. 22.65 for SPNet and 22.42 for SSP), with BR=89.50 and BP=26.78. On nuScenes, SPCNet attains F1=47.52, surpassing SuperLiDAR (37.59) and SPNet (28.97). On SemanticKITTI, it achieves F1=42.57, outperforming SuperLiDAR (31.25) and SPNet (23.43). Inference time is 12 ms on RTX 4090, yielding a 100× speedup over SPG/SSP/SPNet and 5× over SuperLiDAR.

## Baselines & Comparisons

SPCNet dominates all oversegmentation baselines on every dataset. On S3DIS Area 5, it improves F1 by 17.85 points over SPNet (40.50 vs. 22.65) and 18.08 over SSP (40.50 vs. 22.42), with BP more than doubling (26.78 vs. 13.14/13.02). On nuScenes, SPCNet outperforms the strongest prior method SuperLiDAR by 9.93 F1 points (47.52 vs. 37.59) and 14.05 BR points (88.77 vs. 74.72). On SemanticKITTI, it leads SuperLiDAR by 11.32 F1 points (42.57 vs. 31.25). For the secondary semantic segmentation task, SPCNet (77.8% mIoU on ScanNet Val) slightly edges out PTv3 (77.5%) and beats SPT by 3.2% on S3DIS Area 5 (72.1% vs. 68.9%), though it trails PTv3 on S3DIS (72.1% vs. 73.4%), nuScenes Test (80.2% vs. 82.7%), and SemanticKITTI Test (71.9% vs. 74.2%), indicating the superpoint-based approach is competitive but not uniformly superior to the latest point-based transformers on segmentation.

## Relation to Prior Work

SPCNet departs from the dominant point-based oversegmentation paradigm (SPNet, SuperLiDAR, SSP) that relies on expensive 3D spatial queries like FPS and KNN to establish point-to-superpoint associations. Instead, it repurposes the Hilbert curve serialization strategy popularized by PTv3 for attention efficiency, applying it here as the core mechanism for spatial partitioning and neighbor discovery in oversegmentation. The backbone hybridizes sparse 3D convolutions (for local geometry) with a Mamba state-space module at the U-Net bottleneck (for global semantics), while superpoint refinement uses Cross-Attention and a cascaded Graph Transformer–GCN for inter-superpoint context, drawing on SPT's hierarchical superpoint graph design.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

By reducing oversegmentation to 1D sequence operations, SPCNet achieves O(n) complexity and a 100× speedup over optimization-based baselines while simultaneously improving boundary quality (F1 nearly doubles on S3DIS), suggesting that the spatial queries in prior methods were both a bottleneck and a source of boundary noise. The hierarchical Hilbert partitioning also provides a natural multi-scale representation that transfers to semantic segmentation with minimal extra parameters. Open questions include whether the Hilbert curve's locality guarantee degrades on highly non-uniform or extremely large outdoor scenes, and whether alternative space-filling curves or learned serializations could further improve the spatial-semantic trade-off.
