## Problem

Existing 3D object detection systems are restricted to predefined label spaces and specific camera configurations, failing to generalize to novel objects or unseen sensors. The fundamental barrier is extreme data scarcity: 3D-annotated data spans only millions of samples, roughly 3–4 orders of magnitude less than the billions of 2D images used to train foundation models, making it infeasible to learn a generalizable 3D detector from scratch.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ICCV20250350/cropped_page0_idx1.webp)

## Input / Output

The model consumes a single monocular RGB image together with user prompts (2D box, point, or text) and optionally camera intrinsics. Text prompts are first converted to 2D box prompts via Grounding DINO before entering the network. When intrinsics are not supplied, the model jointly predicts them alongside the 3D boxes; when provided, they serve as geometric constraints that refine depth and box estimation. Outputs include per-object 3D bounding boxes (center, dimensions, rotation matrix, and predicted 3D IoU score), a camera-aware depth map, and the predicted intrinsic parameters.

## Method

DetAny3D fuses two frozen 2D foundation models—ViT-H SAM (promptable spatial backbone) and depth-pretrained ViT-L DINOv2 (geometric priors via UniDepth)—through a 2D Aggregator that applies four cascaded cross-attention alignment units with learnable gating weights to dynamically balance each model's contribution at different hierarchy levels. The fused features feed a Depth/Camera Module (following UniDepth) that produces a geometric embedding of camera-aware depth and intrinsics. A 3D Interpreter then processes 3D box tokens and prompt tokens through a Two-Way Transformer (initialized from the SAM decoder) and a Geometric Transformer, where a Zero-Embedding Mapping (ZEM) mechanism—zero-initialized 1×1 convolutions—gradually injects 3D geometric features without disrupting the pretrained 2D representations, enabling stable training across 16 heterogeneous datasets (DA3D) with diverse camera parameters, scenes, and depth distributions. Training uses SILog depth loss, SILog camera-intrinsic loss, and smooth-L1 + Chamfer detection loss over 80 epochs on 64 A100 GPUs.

## Results

On zero-shot novel categories (target-aware metric with Grounding DINO text prompts), DetAny3D achieves 25.73 AP₃D on KITTI, 21.07 on SUNRGBD, and 24.56 on ARKitScenes, surpassing OVMono3D by +21.02, +4.29, and +11.35 AP₃D respectively. On zero-shot novel camera configurations, DetAny3D gains +4.73, +5.68, and +1.10 AP₃D over OVMono3D on Cityscapes3D, Waymo, and 3RScan. With ground-truth 2D box prompts, the advantage widens dramatically: +20.52, +21.93, and +43.60 AP₃D on the three novel-category datasets. On in-domain Omni3D (unified dataset, GT prompts), DetAny3D reaches 34.38 AP₃D overall, outperforming OVMono3D (25.32) by 9.06 AP₃D.

## Baselines & Comparisons

DetAny3D consistently outperforms all baselines across every evaluation setting. Against OVMono3D [74] (the closest open-vocabulary 3D detector), DetAny3D gains +21.02 AP₃D on KITTI novel categories and +43.60 AP₃D on ARKitScenes with GT box prompts, and +4.73 to +5.68 AP₃D on novel-camera datasets with text prompts. Against Cube R-CNN [8] (the Omni3D unified detector), DetAny3D surpasses it by +5.36 and +4.28 AP₃D on Cityscapes3D and Waymo respectively with aligned Cube R-CNN prompts, and by +1.66 AP₃D on the full Omni3D benchmark. On in-domain Omni3D with GT prompts, DetAny3D (34.38 AP₃D) exceeds OVMono3D (25.32) by 9.06 AP₃D, suggesting that the 2D detector bottleneck in OVMono3D limits its ceiling. DetAny3D also outperforms single-dataset methods (ImVoxelNet, SMOKE) and OV-Uni3DETR on Omni3D_OUT, confirming that multi-dataset training with 2D foundation model priors yields superior cross-domain robustness.

## Relation to Prior Work

DetAny3D extends the multi-dataset 3D detection line (Omni3D/Cube R-CNN, OVMono3D) by reframing the problem as promptable open-world detection and compensating for 3D data scarcity through two pre-trained 2D foundation models—SAM as a promptable backbone and depth-pretrained DINOv2 for geometric priors. The key architectural departure is the 2D Aggregator, a hierarchical cross-attention fusion with learnable gating that aligns heterogeneous SAM and DINO features, and the Zero-Embedding Mapping (ZEM) mechanism in the 3D Interpreter, which uses zero-initialized 1×1 convolutions to stably inject 3D geometry without disrupting 2D priors. Unlike OVMono3D, which lifts Grounding DINO's 2D boxes with a 3D head, DetAny3D integrates 2D foundation model features end-to-end and supports arbitrary camera configurations via predicted intrinsics following UniDepth.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

This is the first promptable 3D detection model demonstrating strong zero-shot generalization to both novel object categories and novel camera configurations simultaneously, which is critical for real-world deployment where sensor parameters and object vocabularies are unpredictable. The DA3D benchmark (16 datasets, 20 camera configurations, 0.4M frames) provides a much richer training and evaluation resource than Omni3D's 6 datasets. Open questions remain: text prompts are routed through Grounding DINO rather than being natively handled, in-domain performance gains over Cube R-CNN are modest (+1.6 AP3D), and it is unclear whether the 2D-to-3D gap can be further closed as 3D annotation scales. The demonstrated application to 3D-box-guided video generation (Sora) hints at broader utility as a geometric grounding tool for generative systems.
