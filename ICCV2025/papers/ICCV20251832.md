## Problem

Video polyp segmentation must handle ambiguous boundaries, dramatic inter-frame variations in polyp scale/contrast/position, and real-time inference constraints simultaneously. Existing approaches either ignore temporal correlations (image-based methods), rely on computationally expensive attention or memory mechanisms that hinder real-time speed, or model temporal dependencies without capturing fine-grained frame-wise dynamic details needed for boundary refinement.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ICCV20251832/cropped_page0_idx2.webp)

## Input / Output

The method takes a short colonoscopy video clip (6 consecutive frames for SUN-SEG, 2 frames for CVC-ClinicDB), each resized to 352×352 RGB, and produces a per-frame binary polyp segmentation mask. A pretrained image backbone (Res2Net-50 or PVTv2-B2) extracts three-stage multi-level features, which are channel-compressed before being fed into the temporal and dynamic modules. The final prediction is generated by a second decoder that fuses spatiotemporal and dynamic features, supervised by a combined Dice + weighted IoU + weighted BCE loss applied at three prediction stages.

## Method

STDDNet harnesses Vision Mamba in two complementary roles. The STDP module horizontally concatenates T-frame high-level features into a single wide tensor and applies a bidirectional scanning Mamba block, so that the SSM scans along the spatial dimension within each frame while propagating temporal dependencies across frames at aligned spatial positions; random intermediate-frame masking during training and a mask-guided MLP filter further improve robustness. The DDFE module operates on channel-concatenated low-level features, using a Mamba block (with position compensation from inverted coarse prediction masks) to extract a global structural representation, then a Gated Dynamic Activation Unit (GDAU) inspired by GRU computes static value, varying value, and a dynamic map from the structural and per-frame features, with an activation ratio derived from current and previous frame masks to selectively amplify dynamic regions. This dual use of Mamba—temporally for spatial-aligned consistency and spatially for structural context—enables both stable polyp localization across frames and fine-grained boundary delineation within each frame at 36–45 FPS.

## Results

On SUN-SEG-Easy, STDDNet with PVTv2-B2 achieves Sα=89.84%, Eφ=93.43%, Fβw=85.08%, and Dice=85.86%, outperforming the best VPS baseline SALI (Sα=89.12%, Dice=84.36%) by 0.72% and 1.50% respectively. On the more challenging SUN-SEG-Hard, STDDNet reaches Sα=88.75% and Dice=84.59%, surpassing SALI (Sα=87.88%, Dice=82.89%) by 0.87% and 1.70%. On CVC-ClinicDB, STDDNet attains Sα=96.74%, Eφ=98.86%, Fβw=96.39%, and Dice=96.52%, leading all competitors by at least 0.4% in Dice. STDDNet also maintains real-time inference at 36–45 FPS depending on backbone, compared to 10 FPS for SALI.

## Baselines & Comparisons

STDDNet outperforms all seven baselines across every metric on both SUN-SEG and CVC-ClinicDB. Against the strongest VPS competitor SALI (PVTv2-B5), STDDNet (PVTv2-B2) gains +0.72% Sα and +1.50% Dice on SUN-SEG-Easy, and +0.87% Sα and +1.70% Dice on SUN-SEG-Hard; on CVC-ClinicDB the gap widens to +1.80% Sα and +2.82% Dice, where SALI's fixed-length memory degrades under frame discontinuity. Compared to the image-based G-CASCADE (PVTv2-B2), STDDNet improves Dice by +2.30% on SUN-SEG-Easy and +3.47% on SUN-SEG-Hard, confirming the benefit of temporal modeling. Against VideoMamba (NVS), STDDNet leads by +1.52% Dice on SUN-SEG-Easy and +3.37% on SUN-SEG-Hard, while also using fewer parameters (29.67M vs. 75.66M) and running at 36 FPS vs. 18 FPS. The NIS baselines Vim and VMamba trail STDDNet by 6–8% Dice on SUN-SEG, underscoring that task-agnostic Mamba architectures are insufficient for the fine-grained boundary delineation required in polyp segmentation.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper positions itself at the intersection of Mamba-based visual modeling and video polyp segmentation (VPS), departing from the attention-based temporal modeling used by prior VPS methods like PNS+ (normalized self-attention) and SALI (memory-augmented short-term/long-term interaction). It builds directly on Vision Mamba's bidirectional SSM architecture but introduces a key conceptual departure: applying Mamba both temporally (via horizontally concatenated multi-frame features for spatial-aligned consistency) and spatially (via channel-concatenated features for structural/dynamic decomposition), a dual-use strategy not present in prior Mamba-for-vision work. The GDAU module draws inspiration from GRU gating but is restructured around divergence perception between structural and frame-wise features rather than standard forget/update dynamics.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The practical significance lies in achieving state-of-the-art VPS accuracy at 36–45 FPS, making intraoperative polyp tracking feasible in clinical settings where real-time inference is non-negotiable. The efficiency gain over SALI (3.6× faster while more accurate) demonstrates that SSM-based temporal modeling can replace costly memory readout mechanisms without sacrificing performance. However, the paper acknowledges limitations on extreme cases (very low contrast, facula-obscured fields, incomplete colon structures), and future directions include adversarial training and hierarchical recurrent tracking mechanisms that remain open.
