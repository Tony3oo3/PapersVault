## Problem

Existing mask-free virtual try-on methods are constrained by rigid one-to-one pseudo-sample pairings that cause the generator to overfit to specific garments and generalize poorly to unseen clothing. Architectural refinements such as coarse-to-fine structures and ReferenceNet-based injection remain limited by the diversity and quality of training inputs, while the randomness of Gaussian-noise sampling during inference further degrades identity preservation and fine garment detail—issues largely overlooked by prior work.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ICCV20251856/cropped_page2_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

At inference the method requires only a clothing image and a person image (no masks, human parsing, or DensePose maps), operating on Stable Diffusion V1.5 latents at resolutions matching the target dataset (256×192 for VITON, 512×384 for VITON-HD and DressCode). During training, the generator consumes triplets {C, I_best, P} where I_best is a text-driven pseudo-sample selected from N candidates, and the output is a reconstructed person image P_rec that approximates the ground-truth reference P. The clothing image is warped to align with the person's pose before latent-space fusion.

## Method

The framework has three stages. First, a text-driven pseudo-input preparation pipeline uses a Stable Diffusion inpainting model conditioned on DeepSeek-generated clothing prompts to synthesize N diverse pseudo-samples per identity, from which the best candidate is selected via a joint appearance-rationality discriminator and HPS v2 scoring, thereby decoupling the pseudo-input from the target garment and enriching clothing-region variability. Second, during mask-free generation learning, a gated manipulation mechanism blends the VAE-encoded clothing and pseudo-input latents through a learnable gate κ (Hadamard product) in latent space, preventing catastrophic forgetting of pre-trained weights without adding heavy reference networks; a texture-aware injection module then applies four-orientation Gabor filters to the clothing, encodes the resulting texture map with CLIP, and feeds it as additional key/value projections into the U-Net cross-attention layers. Third, a refining conditional inference strategy decomposes the initial noisy latent via SVD and uses an MLP with multi-head self-attention to predict refined singular values, aligning the starting point of the denoising trajectory with the distribution of real person samples to suppress Gaussian-noise randomness and preserve identity and garment detail.

## Results

On the VITON dataset, the proposed method achieves the best FID of 9.52 and a top SSIM of 0.91 (tied with USC-PFN and LDE-VTON). On VITON-HD, it attains the best SSIM (0.901) and FID (9.384), with LPIPS of 0.079 and KID of 1.12. On DressCode Upper, it leads in SSIM (0.939), LPIPS (0.048), and FID (10.95), while ranking second in KID (1.78 vs. StableVITON's 0.72). Notably, the method operates in a fully mask-free setting at inference, unlike most competing approaches that require a clothing mask.

## Baselines & Comparisons

The method outperforms all 28 baselines on VITON (FID 9.52 vs. next-best TPD at 9.58) and achieves the best SSIM and FID on VITON-HD (0.901 / 9.384), edging out DCI-VTON (0.880 / 9.410) and LDE-VTON (0.898 / 9.640). On DressCode Upper, it leads in SSIM (0.939 vs. DCI-VTON's 0.937), LPIPS (0.048 vs. DCI-VTON's 0.042), and FID (10.95 vs. IDM-VTON's 9.54), though IDM-VTON achieves a lower FID (9.54) at the cost of a much higher KID (4.32 vs. 1.78). The closest mask-free competitor, LDE-VTON, trails by 0.003 SSIM and 0.256 FID on VITON-HD. Among GAN-based methods, the gap is substantial: GP-VTON (FID 9.701 on VITON-HD) and USC-PFN (FID 10.47 on VITON) are the strongest, yet both require masks or produce visibly smoother, less detailed results. The authors note that occasional second-place finishes on VITON-HD/DressCode stem from baselines overfitting to fixed training garments, whereas the proposed text-driven pseudo-sample strategy intentionally introduces out-of-distribution diversity, slightly penalizing in-distribution test cases.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the mask-free pseudo-sample paradigm established by PF-AFN [13] and USC-PFN [8], which train a generator on synthetic try-on pairs to avoid mask dependency. It departs from prior work in three key ways: (1) replacing the single fixed pseudo-sample per identity with a text-driven, LLM-augmented generation pipeline that produces multiple diverse clothing variants, (2) fusing clothing and person latents via a learned gate in latent space rather than adding a separate ReferenceNet branch (following the latent-space optimization idea of GLV [9]), and (3) refining the initial Gaussian noise at inference via SVD decomposition and an MLP-predicted singular-value correction, inspired by the golden-noise framework [36, 49]. The base generator is an off-the-shelf Stable Diffusion inpainting model [37], consistent with the broader trend of leveraging pre-trained diffusion priors for VTON.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The paper addresses a practical bottleneck in mask-free VTON—overfitting to a narrow set of pseudo-sample transformations—by injecting clothing-region diversity through LLM-generated prompts, a strategy that could transfer to other conditional generation tasks. The SVD-based noise refinement is a lightweight alternative to more complex inference-time optimization, though the authors acknowledge that the noise model is trained on a limited sample set, which may introduce instability. Open questions include whether the text-driven pseudo-input strategy scales to multi-garment or full-body try-on, and whether the gated manipulation generalizes beyond the specific Stable Diffusion V1.5 backbone used here. For practitioners, the framework's reliance on off-the-shelf components (SD inpainting, HPS v2, Gabor filters) makes it relatively accessible to reproduce.
