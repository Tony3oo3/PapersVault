## Problem

Redirecting camera trajectories in monocular videos remains challenging: reconstruction-based methods produce artifacts in occluded regions or require synchronized multi-view captures, while existing generative approaches (e.g., GCD) rely on implicit pose embeddings that limit precise trajectory control and suffer from domain gaps when trained on synthetic data. The paper tackles three coupled challenges—precise camera trajectory control, 4D content consistency with the source video, and robust generalization from diverse, large-scale training data.

## Input / Output

The method takes a monocular source video (casually captured or AI-generated) and a user-specified camera trajectory expressed as a sequence of relative 4×4 transformation matrices from the source camera. The source video is first lifted into a dynamic point cloud via monocular depth estimation (DepthCrafter), then rendered at the target trajectory to produce point cloud renders with occlusion holes and a corresponding mask video. The output is a 49-frame video at 384×672 resolution that follows the specified trajectory while remaining 4D-consistent with the source content.

## Method

TrajectoryCrafter decouples deterministic view transformation from stochastic content generation by using point cloud renders as geometric conditions and the source video as appearance conditions within a dual-stream conditional video diffusion model built on CogVideoX-Fun-5B. The point cloud render stream is VAE-encoded, channel-concatenated with noise, and projected into view tokens that provide strong spatial alignment for camera control. The source video stream is VAE-encoded into reference tokens and injected via novel Ref-DiT blocks, where 3D attention processes text and view tokens followed by a cross-attention layer (view tokens as queries, reference tokens as keys/values) to transfer misaligned appearance details. Training follows a two-stage scheme: stage 1 trains DiT and 3D attention layers on all data while freezing cross-attention and skipping reference tokens; stage 2 unfreezes only cross-attention and patch-embedding layers and trains exclusively on static multi-view triplets to prevent overfitting. A double-reprojection data strategy generates 60K training pairs from web-scale monocular videos (OpenVid-1M) by rendering a random novel view and reprojecting back to the original pose, simulating point cloud render artifacts at scale.

## Results

On the iPhone multi-view benchmark, TrajectoryCrafter achieves a mean PSNR of 14.24 dB, SSIM of 0.417, and LPIPS of 0.519, outperforming the best baseline (Shape-of-motion: 11.28 dB PSNR) by over 3 dB. On the in-the-wild VBench evaluation (160 videos), the method scores 0.9236 on Subject Consistency, 0.9815 on Motion Smoothness, and 0.6479 on Imaging Quality, surpassing ViewCrafter (0.7305, 0.9031, 0.5548) and GCD (0.7677, 0.8950, 0.5203) across all seven VBench dimensions.

## Baselines & Comparisons

TrajectoryCrafter outperforms all three baselines on every metric in both evaluation settings. Against the reconstruction-based Shape-of-motion [78], it gains +2.96 dB PSNR and reduces LPIPS from 0.635 to 0.519, while also producing far fewer occlusion holes. Against the generative baselines GCD [75] and ViewCrafter [95], the improvement is even more pronounced: +3.47 dB PSNR over GCD and +3.49 dB over ViewCrafter on the iPhone benchmark. On the in-the-wild VBench, TrajectoryCrafter leads by a wide margin on Subject Consistency (0.9236 vs. 0.7677 for GCD and 0.7305 for ViewCrafter) and Aesthetic Quality (0.5920 vs. 0.4154 and 0.4950), confirming that the dual-stream conditioning and hybrid training data yield both geometric accuracy and visual fidelity that the baselines cannot match.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper builds directly on CogVideoX, a text-to-video diffusion model, repurposing its 3D VAE, DiT blocks, and text encoders for camera-trajectory-conditional video generation. It departs from prior generative novel view synthesis methods (GCD, ViewCrafter) by explicitly decoupling deterministic view transformation (via dynamic point cloud rendering) from stochastic content generation, rather than relying on implicit pose embeddings or static-scene assumptions. The dual-stream conditioning—combining geometrically-aligned point cloud renders with appearance-rich source video through a novel Ref-DiT cross-attention block—addresses the spatial misalignment problem that plagues naive concatenation approaches. The double-reprojection data curation strategy is a conceptual departure from prior work that relies on scarce synchronized multi-view video datasets.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

This work makes camera trajectory redirection practical for casual monocular videos without per-scene optimization, a capability with clear consumer and creative-tool applications. The double-reprojection trick for generating training pairs from web-scale monocular video is a reusable data strategy that could benefit other video generation tasks. Key limitations remain: depth estimation errors propagate into geometrically implausible outputs, very large viewpoint changes (e.g., 360°) are out of reach, and multi-step diffusion inference is computationally expensive. Open questions include whether the approach can be extended to longer video sequences, whether learned depth models can be jointly optimized with the diffusion model, and whether the dual-stream paradigm generalizes to other conditional video generation tasks.
