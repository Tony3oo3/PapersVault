## Problem

Diffusion-based novel view synthesis models (e.g., ZeroNVS, MegaScenes) generate each viewpoint independently, producing images that lack spatial and appearance consistency across views. While integrating 3D reconstruction or language models can partially address this, such multi-step pipelines incur high computational costs and still struggle with image quality. The inherent factors within diffusion models—per-view independent generation and noise randomness—that directly undermine view consistency have remained unaddressed by prior work.

## Input / Output

The method takes a single RGB scene image, its corresponding depth map, and a set of continuous camera poses (e.g., 19 viewpoints along an orbital trajectory) as input. It outputs a batch of novel view scene images generated simultaneously in a single forward pass through the pre-trained diffusion model, with no additional modules or training required. The entire pipeline runs in roughly 67 seconds for 19 images, comparable to the base diffusion models it augments.

## Method

WAVE is a training-free plug-in that modifies the inference of existing scene-level diffusion models through two mechanisms built on 3D warping. First, warp-guided adaptive attention (WGAA) computes warped region masks via 3D warping, uses their pairwise IoU to adaptively select a relevant reference range of viewpoints, and then applies masked batch self-attention so that each view only attends to spatially overlapping regions of neighboring views rather than all views indiscriminately. Second, pose-aware noise initialization (PANI) encodes warped images through the VAE, adds DDPM noise to obtain z_T, extracts and normalizes the low-frequency spectrum (after filling missing regions with random noise to avoid black-hole artifacts), and mixes it with the high-frequency components of the original Gaussian noise to reinitialize the diffusion starting point. Together, these two components inject geometric view-transformation cues into both the attention pathway and the stochastic noise, directly targeting the independent-generation and noise-randomness issues without any parameter updates.

## Results

On the MegaScenes dataset, MegaScenes+WAVE achieves LPIPS-next of 0.397 and CLIPSIM-next of 0.920, substantially outperforming the base MegaScenes model (0.607 / 0.861) and ZeroNVS (0.561 / 0.887). The most dramatic improvement appears on DTU, where MegaScenes+WAVE reduces LPIPS-next from 0.379 to 0.130 and Angular Consistency from 13.04° to 6.32°. On RE10K (a video sequence with ground truth), MegaScenes+WAVE achieves PSNR 13.27, LPIPS 0.450, FID 15.78, and KID 0.004, surpassing both MegaScenes and VistaDream across all image-quality metrics.

## Baselines & Comparisons

WAVE consistently outperforms both diffusion baselines (MegaScenes and ZeroNVS) across all three datasets and all consistency/camera-accuracy metrics. The largest gains are on DTU, where MegaScenes+WAVE cuts LPIPS-next by 66% (0.379→0.130) and Angular Consistency by 51% (13.04°→6.32°) relative to base MegaScenes. Against VistaDream (a multi-step 3D+diffusion pipeline), MegaScenes+WAVE achieves comparable or better consistency while running roughly 10× faster (~67 s vs. ~600 s for 19 images); on RE10K it also beats VistaDream on every image-quality metric (PSNR 13.27 vs. 10.88, FID 15.78 vs. 29.48, KID 0.004 vs. 0.011). In the 3D Gaussian Splatting downstream task, MegaScenes+WAVE improves PSNR by 2.58 dB over base MegaScenes on the MegaScenes dataset (24.69 vs. 22.11) and by 6.52 dB on DTU (26.95 vs. 20.43), confirming that the consistency gains translate to better 3D reconstruction.

## Relation to Prior Work

WAVE builds on diffusion-based novel view synthesis models (ZeroNVS, MegaScenes) by addressing two inherent limitations—per-image independent generation and noise randomness—through training-free modifications rather than the multi-step 3D pipeline approaches (VistaDream, RealmDreamer) that dominate prior consistency work. It repurposes 3D warping not as a rendering tool but as a geometric prior: warped region masks guide adaptive batch self-attention (extending ConsiStory/StoryDiffusion-style cross-view attention with viewpoint-aware masking), and warped low-frequency content conditions the initial diffusion noise (extending FreeInit/ConsistI2V with pose-specific geometric information). The key conceptual departure is that view consistency can be achieved by modifying the diffusion process itself, eliminating the need for auxiliary 3D reconstruction or language-model modules.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The training-free, plug-and-play nature of WAVE makes it immediately applicable to any camera-conditioned diffusion model without retraining, and the ~67-second inference for 19 views at quality comparable to VistaDream (which takes ~10× longer) demonstrates that explicit 3D representations may not be necessary for view consistency. The comprehensive metric framework (combining video-style LPIPS/CLIPSIM with camera-pose accuracy metrics) addresses a real gap in the field where reconstruction-based metrics are unreliable for consistency evaluation. A key limitation is sensitivity to large viewpoint changes due to missing regions in warped images; the authors suggest an autoregressive generation strategy as a promising direction, but this remains unexplored. Whether the approach generalizes to non-continuous or highly occluded scene transitions is an open question.
