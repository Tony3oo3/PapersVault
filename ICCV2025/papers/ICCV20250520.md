## Problem

High-fidelity 4D view synthesis of humans from sparse-view videos remains challenging because traditional reconstruction methods require dense synchronized camera arrays, while existing 4D diffusion-based generation methods produce videos that lack spatio-temporal consistency due to GPU memory constraints forcing independent multi-pass denoising. This inconsistency is especially severe for humans with complex topology, cloth deformations, and self-occlusions caused by motion.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ICCV20250520/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method takes M sparse-view videos (e.g., 4 or 8 views × T frames at 1024p) along with camera calibration parameters and a 3D human skeleton sequence (extracted via Sapiens and triangulated from 2D poses). These are encoded into a latent sample grid of size (N+M)×T, where input views provide conditioning latents and target views start as noise latents. The output is N dense target-view videos at 1024p resolution, which are then used to reconstruct a 4D Gaussian Splatting representation enabling real-time novel-view rendering.

## Method

The core contribution is a sliding iterative denoising mechanism: a context window of length W slides with stride S over the sample grid, performing only P partial denoising steps per iteration (rather than full denoising as in CAT4D), so each sample accumulates D=2×P×W/S total steps through bidirectional sliding. Denoising alternates between spatial (across views) and temporal (across frames) dimensions for D/2 steps each, allowing each target sample to aggregate both neighboring-view and neighboring-frame context. For conditioning, the method combines projected 3D skeleton RGB maps (encoded via VAE for pixel-aligned pose control) with Plücker coordinates (for explicit camera pose), addressing the front-back ambiguity of skeletons alone. The model uses 3D self-attention layers in a latent diffusion architecture (following CAT3D/MVDream), is trained on DNA-Rendering with M+N=16 samples per sequence, and uses classifier-free guidance with 10% condition dropout.

## Results

On DNA-Rendering with 4 input views, Diffuman4D achieves PSNR 25.39, SSIM 0.864, and LPIPS 0.161, outperforming the strongest generation-based baseline CAT4D† (PSNR 21.45, SSIM 0.806, LPIPS 0.234) by ~4 dB in PSNR. With 8 input views, the gap narrows slightly (PSNR 26.32 vs. 22.53 for CAT4D†). On the zero-shot ActorsHQ dataset, Diffuman4D reaches PSNR 27.88 (4-view) and 28.75 (8-view), again leading all baselines by a wide margin, with LPIPS as low as 0.110 in the 8-view setting.

## Baselines & Comparisons

Diffuman4D outperforms all four baselines across every metric and dataset setting. Against the closest competitor CAT4D† (generation-based), it gains ~3.9 dB PSNR on DNA-Rendering 4-view and ~6.3 dB on ActorsHQ 4-view, with LPIPS reduced by 0.07–0.11. The optimization-based LongVolcap (4DGS) is competitive on ActorsHQ 8-view (PSNR 28.12 vs. 28.75) but degrades sharply on DNA-Rendering 4-view (PSNR 20.06) due to the ill-posed sparse-view problem. The SMPL-prior method GauHuman and the feed-forward GPS-Gaussian both fail under sparse-view conditions, with GPS-Gaussian's depth estimator breaking down entirely (PSNR 11.25 on DNA-Rendering 4-view). The paper notes that even with only 4 input views, Diffuman4D achieves visual quality comparable to dense 48-view reconstruction via LongVolcap.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the multi-view video diffusion paradigm established by CAT4D [60] and the latent diffusion architecture from CAT3D [16] and MVDream [50], but departs in two key ways: (1) replacing CAT4D's full-denoise-per-window-plus-median-filtering strategy with a sliding iterative denoising mechanism that performs only partial denoising steps per window slide, enabling richer cross-window information flow at constant compute cost; and (2) introducing human-specific skeleton conditioning (via Sapiens 2D pose estimation and 3D triangulation) alongside Plücker coordinates, addressing the soft-body deformation and self-occlusion challenges that general-purpose 4D generation methods like CAT4D struggle with. The reconstruction stage leverages LongVolcap [65] as an off-the-shelf 4DGS pipeline, keeping the generative and reconstruction components modular.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The ~4 dB PSNR gap over the closest generation-based baseline (CAT4D) across both DNA-Rendering and ActorsHQ, combined with the claim that 4 input views match 48-view dense reconstruction quality, makes sparse-view 4D human capture practically viable for AR, film, and sports applications without expensive camera arrays. The sliding iterative denoising mechanism is a general-purpose technique that could transfer to other long-sequence or multi-view generation tasks beyond humans. Open questions include whether the approach scales to 4K resolution, handles complex human-object interactions, or enables novel-pose rendering without input-view constraints — all acknowledged limitations that remain unresolved.
