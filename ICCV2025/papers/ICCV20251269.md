## Problem

Diffusion-based virtual try-on methods consistently fail to preserve fine-grained details such as text, patterns, faces, and material textures. Existing refinement modules (e.g., Outfit Anyone) follow the same noise-to-image diffusion paradigm, introducing unnecessary stochastic uncertainty and requiring 30–50 denoising steps, even though the refinement task is inherently deterministic and should start from the already-available coarse result.

## Input / Output

The method takes a coarse try-on result (1024×768) and a reference garment image (512×512) as input, and produces a refined try-on image with restored high-frequency details in as few as 1–10 inference steps. It is designed as a plug-and-play module that can be appended to any existing try-on pipeline (OOTD, StableVITON, SDXL-TryOn) without modifying the base model. No mask or text prompt is required at inference time; only the coarse image and garment image are needed.

## Method

TryOn-Refiner replaces the traditional noise-to-image diffusion refinement with a conditional rectified-flow mapping that directly learns a velocity field v_θ(X_t, C_g, t) transporting the coarse distribution π₀ to the refined distribution π₁ along straight-line paths, conditioned solely on the garment image. The architecture uses an SDXL UNet with two branches: a main branch consuming the interpolated latent X_t = tX₁ + (1−t)X₀ and a reference branch injecting garment features via cross-attention, with all parameters fine-tuned under a flow-matching least-squares loss. Because the starting point is the deterministic coarse image rather than random noise, the model focuses on restoring details while preserving background consistency, and converges in 1–10 steps. A dedicated data construction pipeline (downsampling → VAE encode/decode → super-resolution) generates spatially-aligned coarse–target pairs, and a data smoothing strategy (light Gaussian low-pass filtering plus VAE decoder fine-tuning at lr 1e-6 for 2000 steps) mitigates over-sharpened blocking artifacts that arise from excessive high-frequency contrast between coarse and target images.

## Results

On VITON-HD, OOTD + TryOn-Refiner achieves the best LPIPS (0.069) and FID (8.60), while TryOn-Adapter retains the best SSIM (0.894 vs. 0.886). On the more diverse Dresscode benchmark, OOTD + TryOn-Refiner leads on all four metrics (LPIPS 0.044, SSIM 0.935, FID 4.11, KID 0.35). The refiner requires only 1–10 inference steps (vs. 30–50 for diffusion-based refinement), with performance peaking at 10 steps and showing no further gain at 30 steps.

## Baselines & Comparisons

As a plug-and-play module, TryOn-Refiner consistently improves the base try-on methods it is applied to: OOTD gains LPIPS 0.071→0.069, SSIM 0.878→0.886, FID 8.81→8.60, KID 0.82→0.79 on VITON-HD, and LPIPS 0.045→0.044, SSIM 0.927→0.935, FID 4.20→4.11, KID 0.37→0.35 on Dresscode. StableVITON shows even larger gains (LPIPS 0.084→0.070, SSIM 0.862→0.892, FID 9.13→8.85, KID 1.20→1.15). Against the strongest standalone baseline TryOn-Adapter, OOTD+TryOn-Refiner wins on LPIPS (0.069 vs. 0.071) and FID (8.60 vs. 8.63) but trails on SSIM (0.886 vs. 0.894). In a direct comparison with a replicated diffusion-based refiner (Diff-Refiner, 30 steps) on StableVITON, TryOn-Refiner (10 steps) substantially outperforms it on every metric (LPIPS 0.070 vs. 0.092, SSIM 0.892 vs. 0.828, FID 8.85 vs. 9.02, KID 1.15 vs. 1.22), demonstrating that the deterministic flow-mapping paradigm avoids the background degradation caused by noise initialization.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper builds on the diffusion-based virtual try-on lineage (OOTD, StableVITON, TryOn-Adapter, LaDI-VTON) and the refinement sub-task introduced by Outfit Anyone, which uses a standard noise-to-image diffusion module to correct coarse try-on outputs. The key conceptual departure is replacing the stochastic noise-to-image paradigm with a conditional rectified-flow mapping from the coarse image directly to the refined image, drawing on the rectified flow framework of Liu et al. (2022) and related flow-based restoration work (FlowIE, OSEDiff). Unlike those prior flow-based methods that still start from noise, TryOn-Refiner conditions on the garment image and uses the coarse try-on result as the fixed starting point, making the refinement deterministic. The data construction pipeline (downsampling + VAE encode-decode + super-resolution) is a novel contribution specific to generating paired training data for this flow-matching formulation.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

As a plug-and-play module requiring only 1–10 inference steps, TryOn-Refiner offers a practical, low-cost way to improve detail fidelity (text, patterns, faces, textures) across any existing try-on pipeline without retraining the base model. However, the quantitative gains over already-strong SOTA baselines are modest (e.g., LPIPS 0.071→0.069), and the authors acknowledge that evaluation at 512×384 resolution obscures the true detail-level improvements. Open questions include whether the data construction pipeline and smoothing strategy generalize to other refinement or restoration tasks beyond try-on, and whether the approach scales to video try-on or multi-garment scenarios. The finding that a replicated diffusion-based refiner actually degrades quality (Table 6) raises the question of whether noise-based refinement is fundamentally ill-suited to deterministic correction tasks.
