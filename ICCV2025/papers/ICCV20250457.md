## Problem

Existing virtual try-on methods are confined to single specific tasks, require auxiliary inputs like segmentation masks, poses, or parsing maps, and suffer from limited generalization due to scarcity of paired garment-model data. No prior framework can simultaneously handle virtual try-on, garment reconstruction, model-free try-on, and layered try-on under a single mask-free architecture guided by flexible textual instructions.

## Input / Output

The method accepts a variable number of model and garment images at arbitrary resolutions (trained at 512×384 and 768×576) along with a free-form textual instruction, and produces either an outfitted model image, a flattened garment image, or a garment-driven generated model image depending on the instruction. Unusually, all image conditions are fed as clean (noise-free) latents concatenated at the pixel level with the noisy target latent, rather than being injected through separate adapter branches or ControlNet-style side networks.

## Method

Any2AnyTryon builds on FLUX.1 (a DiT with flow-matching objective) and trains LoRA adapters, concatenating flattened condition image tokens with the noised target latent along the sequence dimension so that all conditions share the same representation space. The key architectural innovation is Adaptive Position Embedding: a three-channel RoPE scheme where the first channel encodes a condition-ID (separating different input images from the target), and the second/third channels encode spatial (y, x) coordinates, with a pixel-alignment rule that assigns identical spatial positions to condition tokens that should correspond to target tokens (preserving non-edited regions) and offset positions otherwise. This enables the model to maintain pixel-level consistency in unchanged areas while freely generating new content in target regions. The training data, LAION-Garment, is the largest open-source garment try-on dataset, constructed by integrating VITON-HD, DressCode, DeepFashion2, and LRVS-Fashion with synthetic augmentation via FLUX-ControlNet-Inpainting and GPT-4o quality filtering, plus Florence-2-generated task-specific text instructions.

## Results

On the VITON-HD virtual try-on benchmark (Table 4), Any2AnyTryon achieves FID 6.934 and KID 0.739 (paired), and FID 8.965 and KID 0.981 (unpaired), outperforming all baselines on the unpaired generation metrics. For model-free virtual try-on (Table 3), the method attains CLIP-I 0.789 and DiffSim 0.202, surpassing StableGarment (0.650, 0.153) and Magic Clothing (0.642, 0.143). In garment reconstruction (Table 2), Any2AnyTryon achieves SSIM 0.805, LPIPS 0.328, FID 13.367, and KID 3.5, improving over TryOffDiff on all four metrics.

## Baselines & Comparisons

For the primary virtual try-on task (Table 4), Any2AnyTryon achieves the best unpaired FID (8.965) and unpaired KID (0.981), beating CatVTON (9.083, 1.130), FitDiT (10.340, 1.648), IDM-VTON (10.745, 2.229), GP-VTON (11.708, 3.990), and OOTD (15.136, 5.774). However, on paired metrics, CatVTON retains the edge with lower LPIPS (0.0582 vs 0.0877), higher SSIM (0.8653 vs 0.8387), lower FID (5.482 vs 6.934), and lower KID (0.384 vs 0.739). In model-free virtual try-on (Table 3), Any2AnyTryon dominates both StableGarment and Magic Clothing across all four metrics (MP-LPIPS 0.141, CLIP-I 0.789, DiffSim 0.202, FFA 0.549). For garment reconstruction (Table 2), the method outperforms TryOffDiff on every metric (SSIM 0.805 vs 0.793, LPIPS 0.328 vs 0.334, FID 13.367 vs 20.346, KID 3.5 vs 6.8). The try-on-in-layers comparison against DiOr is qualitative only, where Any2AnyTryon better preserves model appearance and garment fidelity.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper builds directly on the FLUX.1 DiT architecture and its flow-matching training objective, extending the standard RoPE positional encoding into a three-channel Adaptive Position Embedding that separates condition identity from spatial coordinates. It departs from prior VTON methods (CatVTON, GP-VTON, MMTryon) that require segmentation masks, pose estimates, or parsing maps by formulating try-on as a purely text-and-image-conditioned generation task, and from IC-LoRA by feeding clean (noise-free) condition latents rather than noisy ones. The LAION-Garment dataset extends the synthetic-data augmentation strategy of StableGarment and MMTryon but adds GPT-4o-based quality filtering and Florence-2-generated task instructions to support a unified multi-task training objective.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The unification of virtual try-on, model-free try-on, garment reconstruction, and layered try-on into a single mask-free framework reduces the engineering pipeline complexity for production deployment and removes dependence on fragile segmentation/pose pre-processing. The LAION-Garment dataset, as the largest open-source garment try-on collection, provides a reusable resource for the community. However, quantitative evaluation is confined to VITON-HD with limited baselines per sub-task (e.g., only one competitor for garment reconstruction), and the 'in-the-wild' generalization claims rest primarily on qualitative examples rather than systematic benchmarks, leaving open whether the unified approach scales to truly diverse real-world scenes and higher resolutions.
