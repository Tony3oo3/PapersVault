## Problem

Novel view synthesis from casually captured long videos remains unsolved because existing pipelines either depend on COLMAP (which fails on irregular trajectories), suffer from out-of-memory errors on long sequences (CF-3DGS), or accumulate pose drift over hundreds of frames (MASt3R, LocalRF). No prior method simultaneously handles unknown poses, complex free-moving trajectories, and large-scale scenes within practical memory budgets.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ICCV20250608/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method takes a casually captured long video (hundreds to thousands of frames) with no camera poses or intrinsics as input, using MASt3R to extract per-frame dense point clouds, 2D-3D correspondences, and depth priors as soft initialization. It produces a compact octree-anchored 3D Gaussian Splatting scene (~101 MB) with jointly optimized camera poses, enabling real-time novel view rendering at 281 FPS. Evaluation is conducted at full, 1/2, or 1/4 resolution depending on scene scale, with every 8th or 10th frame held out for testing.

## Method

LongSplat builds on Scaffold-GS's anchor-based 3DGS representation but replaces its fixed-resolution SfM-derived voxels with an adaptive Octree Anchor Formation that splits or prunes voxels based on local point-cloud density, achieving 7.92× compression over per-pixel initialization while preserving detail. Camera poses are estimated per frame via correspondence-guided PnP from MASt3R matches, then refined by photometric alignment against the current 3DGS rendering, with a depth-scale correction to counter MASt3R's drift. The training follows an incremental joint optimization loop: new frames trigger local optimization within a visibility-adapted window (selected by anchor IoU threshold) alternating with periodic global optimization over all processed frames, and a final global refinement pass ensures long-range consistency. The unified loss combines photometric, monocular depth (MASt3R-aligned), and keypoint reprojection terms, applied at both local and global stages to supervise geometry in newly revealed regions.

## Results

On the Free dataset (7 scenes), LongSplat achieves an average PSNR of 27.88 dB, SSIM of 0.85, and LPIPS of 0.17, outperforming all unposed baselines (CF-3DGS: 13.98 PSNR, NoPe-NeRF: 17.63, LocalRF: 20.17) and approaching COLMAP-based Scaffold-GS (29.19 PSNR) without requiring SfM. On Tanks and Temples, LongSplat reaches 32.83 PSNR / 0.94 SSIM / 0.08 LPIPS, surpassing even COLMAP+3DGS (30.21 PSNR) and CF-3DGS (31.28 PSNR). On the challenging Hike dataset (long outdoor videos), LongSplat achieves 25.39 PSNR / 0.81 SSIM, beating LocalRF (23.56 PSNR) by ~1.8 dB. Pose accuracy is also superior: ATE of 0.004 on Free vs. 0.008–0.576 for baselines, and 0.003 on Tanks vs. 0.004–0.006.

## Baselines & Comparisons

LongSplat outperforms all unposed baselines across all three datasets. Against CF-3DGS [14], LongSplat gains +13.9 dB PSNR on Free (27.88 vs. 13.98) and +1.55 dB on Tanks (32.83 vs. 31.28); CF-3DGS also OOMs on most Free scenes and all Hike scenes. Against NoPe-NeRF [5], LongSplat leads by +10.25 dB on Free and +6.49 dB on Tanks, while NoPe-NeRF's pose errors are an order of magnitude larger (ATE 0.576 vs. 0.004 on Free). Against LocalRF [39], LongSplat achieves +7.71 dB on Free and +1.83 dB on Hike, with substantially lower RPE (0.028 vs. 0.754 translation on Free). Against MASt3R+Scaffold-GS (fixed poses) [27,36], LongSplat gains +4.83 dB on Free and +4.16 dB on Hike; even the jointly-optimized variant (MASt3R+Scaffold-GS*) is beaten by +3.46 dB on Free and +1.83 dB on Hike. The only method LongSplat does not surpass is COLMAP+Scaffold-GS on Free (29.19 vs. 27.88 PSNR), but LongSplat eliminates the COLMAP dependency entirely and still wins on Tanks (32.83 vs. 30.21 for COLMAP+3DGS) and achieves the best pose accuracy (ATE 0.003 vs. 0.004 for CF-3DGS).

## Relation to Prior Work

LongSplat builds directly on Scaffold-GS's anchor-based 3D Gaussian Splatting representation, replacing its fixed-resolution voxel grid (which requires SfM initialization) with a density-adaptive octree that operates without any SfM dependency. It treats MASt3R's 3D foundation model outputs as soft priors rather than ground truth, departing from the COLMAP-based pipeline that underpins most 3DGS and NeRF methods. The incremental joint optimization strategy extends the unposed NVS line (BARF, CF-3DGS, NoPe-NeRF) from short or constrained sequences to long casual videos, while the visibility-adaptive local window generalizes LocalRF's progressive local field construction with a covisibility-based selection criterion.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

Casual long videos are the dominant source of real-world 3D content, yet existing unposed methods either OOM (CF-3DGS), fragment under complex trajectories (LocalRF), or accumulate pose drift (MASt3R-based pipelines); LongSplat addresses all three simultaneously, making robust unposed reconstruction practical for hundreds-to-thousands-of-frame sequences. The 7.92× memory compression that *improves* quality over dense per-pixel initialization is a useful design principle for structured 3D representations. Key limitations remain: the method assumes static scenes and fixed intrinsics, and handling dynamic objects or varying focal lengths is left as future work. Open questions include whether the incremental strategy scales to multi-day sequences and whether the octree density thresholds can be learned rather than hand-tuned.
