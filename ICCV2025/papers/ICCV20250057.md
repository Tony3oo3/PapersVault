## Problem

Generating dynamic 4D scenes from a single image remains an open challenge: existing methods either focus on object-level generation (making scene-level infeasible) or require expensive fine-tuning on large-scale multi-view video datasets with limited generalization. The key gap is achieving scene-level 4D generation with strong spatial-temporal consistency in a tuning-free, data-efficient manner that avoids the oversaturation and instability inherent to score distillation sampling.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ICCV20250057/cropped_page0_idx1.webp)

## Input / Output

The method takes a single RGB scene image (or a text prompt for text-to-4D mode) and produces a 4D Gaussian Splatting representation that supports real-time, controllable rendering from arbitrary camera poses at any timestamp. As an intermediate artifact, it generates spatial-temporal consistent multi-view videos along a user-defined camera trajectory (K poses × T frames). The final 4D-GS is optimized on a single A100 GPU over ~10k iterations, with the coarse stage using L1 loss on views sharing the input's timestamp or viewpoint, and the fine stage adding LPIPS loss on modulation-refined renderings.

## Method

Free4D is a three-stage tuning-free pipeline. First, it animates the input image via an off-the-shelf image-to-video model (Kling) and initializes 4D geometry using MonST3R with a progressive static point cloud aggregation strategy that avoids redundant points while preserving cross-frame consistency. Second, it generates multi-view videos using ViewCrafter (a point-conditioned diffusion model) guided by the point clouds, introducing three consistency mechanisms: Adaptive CFG (disabling guidance in point-cloud-visible regions to prevent color shifts while enabling it in occluded regions for quality completion), Point Guided Denoising (fusing noisy latents from coarse point-cloud renders into early denoising steps to suppress unintended motion), and Reference Latent Replacement (substituting latents in co-occluded regions with those from the first timestamp to enforce temporal coherence). Third, it optimizes a 4D-GS representation via a coarse-to-fine strategy: the coarse stage trains only on views constrained by the input image, then the fine stage incorporates remaining generated views through a Modulation-Based Refinement that uses generated images as modulation signals to adjust the denoising direction (z_{0←i}) rather than as pixel-level supervision, effectively integrating new information while suppressing inconsistencies.

## Results

On VBench for text-to-4D, Free4D achieves 96.0% Consistency, 47.4% Dynamic Degree, and 64.7% Aesthetic Score against 4Real, and 96.9% Consistency, 54.1% Dynamic, and 61.9% Aesthetic against 4Dfy. For image-to-4D, Free4D reaches 96.8% Consistency, 100.0% Dynamic, and 57.9% Aesthetic against GenXD, and 96.9% Consistency and 60.5% Aesthetic against Animate124. In user studies with 78 evaluators, Free4D wins 86–96% of pairwise comparisons across all ablation components, confirming consistent superiority.

## Baselines & Comparisons

Against 4Real (the closest text-to-4D scene method), Free4D improves Dynamic Degree by 15.1 points (47.4% vs 32.3%) and Aesthetic Score by 13.8 points (64.7% vs 50.9%) while matching Text Alignment and slightly exceeding Consistency. Against object-centric methods 4Dfy and Dream-in-4D, Free4D dominates on Consistency (96.9% vs 91.6% and 95.2% vs 91.0%) and Aesthetic (61.9% vs 54.5% and 65.3% vs 55.1%), with Dynamic Degree roughly on par (54.1% vs 53.3% and 53.2% vs 53.5%). In image-to-4D, Free4D substantially outperforms GenXD on Consistency (96.8% vs 89.8%) and Aesthetic (57.9% vs 38.0%) while matching Dynamic (100.0% vs 98.3%), and beats Animate124 on Consistency (96.9% vs 90.7%) and Aesthetic (60.5% vs 42.3%) though slightly trailing on Dynamic (40.1% vs 45.4%). Against DimensionX, Free4D is competitive: slightly lower Consistency (95.5% vs 97.2%) but higher Aesthetic (57.3% vs 56.0%) and matched Dynamic (22.1% vs 21.9%), with user-study evaluators consistently preferring Free4D for diversity and realism.

## Relation to Prior Work

Free4D positions itself at the intersection of three established threads: point-conditioned multi-view video generation (ViewCrafter), dynamic scene reconstruction (MonST3R), and 4D Gaussian Splatting as a real-time representation. It departs from the dominant paradigm of fine-tuning video diffusion models on large-scale 4D scene data (DimensionX, GenXD, CAT4D) and from SDS-based optimization (4Real, Animate124) by composing off-the-shelf foundation models—Kling for video animation, MonST3R for geometry, ViewCrafter for multi-view synthesis—without any parameter updates. The novel contributions are entirely in the consistency-enforcement mechanisms (Adaptive CFG, Point Guided Denoising, Reference Latent Replacement) and the modulation-based refinement for 4D-GS optimization, rather than in new generative architectures.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

By eliminating the need for large-scale 4D training data or expensive fine-tuning of video diffusion models, Free4D makes 4D scene generation accessible on a single A100 GPU, which is a significant practical barrier reduction for the field. The tuning-free recipe—distilling spatial-temporal consistency from pre-trained models via targeted denoising interventions—offers a template that could transfer to other 3D/4D generation settings. Open questions include scalability to longer temporal sequences, more complex multi-object interactions, and whether the reliance on a single reference video (from Kling) limits the diversity and physical plausibility of generated dynamics. The method also remains constrained to predefined camera trajectories rather than fully free viewpoint control.
