## Problem

Existing test-time adaptation (TTA) methods for 3D point cloud classification either rely on iterative backpropagation (making them slow and sensitive to update count), require auxiliary training objectives that limit applicability to arbitrarily pretrained models, or depend on class-level source prototypes unavailable in fully source-free settings. No prior method addresses distribution shift at the token level in a single forward pass without gradient updates.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ICCV20251632/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method consumes an unordered 3D point cloud of N_p points, tokenized via furthest-point sampling into L_t tokens each consisting of a center point and its KNN neighborhood. At test time, unlabeled target-domain point clouds with unknown distribution shift are processed; the method outputs a class label after removing L_pg of the most divergent tokens before the attention layers. A parallel entropy-based selection over a small set of candidate purge sizes {0, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32} determines the optimal L_pg per sample without backpropagation.

## Method

Purge-Gate (PG) formalizes how domain-shift noise corrupts transformer attention: after LayerNorm, noise-dominant tokens become near-orthogonal in high dimensions, driving attention scores toward zero and producing uniform, uninformative attention weights. PG computes a per-token divergence score against a source prototype G and removes the top L_pg most divergent tokens before they enter the attention layers. Two variants are proposed: PG-SP uses source-domain mean and variance statistics (via Welford's online algorithm) with Mahalanobis distance, while PG-SF is fully source-free, using the CLS token's query-space projection as the prototype with cosine distance. BatchNorm statistics are reset before each forward pass to avoid stale train-time statistics, and the purge size is selected at test time by choosing the candidate that minimizes output logit entropy across parallel forward passes.

## Results

On ModelNet40-C, PG-SP achieves 68.93% mean top-1 accuracy (+14.77% over Source Only at 54.16%), while PG-SF reaches 68.68% (+14.52%). On ScanObjectNN-C, PG-SP attains 49.13% (+12.17%) and PG-SF 47.54% (+10.58%). On the more challenging ShapeNet-C, both variants are the only methods to improve over Source Only: PG-SF 64.40% (+3.05%) and PG-SP 64.17% (+2.82%). PG-SP is 12.4× faster (14.07 ms vs 173.94 ms per batch) and 5.5× more memory-efficient (1300 MiB vs 7400 MiB) than BFTT3D.

## Baselines & Comparisons

PG-SP outperforms all four baselines across all three datasets. Against BFTT3D (the closest backpropagation-free competitor), PG-SP gains +11.77% on ModelNet40-C (68.93% vs 57.16%), +16.13% on ScanObjectNN-C (49.13% vs 33.00%), and +3.44% on ShapeNet-C (64.17% vs 60.73%). Against TENT (the strongest backpropagation-based TTA method), PG-SP leads by +5.40% on ModelNet40-C and +5.68% on ScanObjectNN-C, while also being 12.4× faster since it requires no gradient updates. Against MATE (TTT), PG-SP improves by +10.03% on ModelNet40-C and +12.23% on ScanObjectNN-C, and unlike MATE it does not require auxiliary reconstruction objectives during pretraining. Against SMART-PC (TTT), PG-SP gains +5.83% on ModelNet40-C and +9.53% on ScanObjectNN-C. PG-SF, the fully source-free variant, also surpasses all baselines on ModelNet40-C and ScanObjectNN-C, and is the only method (alongside PG-SP) to beat Source Only on ShapeNet-C.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper repurposes the token-pruning paradigm from transformer efficiency research (DToP, PPT, Learned Token Pruning) into a test-time adaptation mechanism, shifting the objective from removing uninformative tokens to removing tokens most corrupted by domain shift. It departs from prior point-cloud TTA methods (MATE, BFTT3D, TENT, T3A) by eliminating backpropagation entirely and operating at the token level rather than updating model parameters or matching class-level prototypes. The theoretical grounding—showing that noise-dominant tokens collapse attention to near-uniform weights via high-dimensional orthogonality—is a new analytical contribution that motivates the purging design.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

A backpropagation-free TTA method that is 12.4× faster and 5.5× more memory-efficient than the prior best backprop-free baseline (BFTT3D) is practically significant for real-time 3D perception deployment where iterative optimization is infeasible. The source-free variant (PG-SF) removes the need for stored source statistics, broadening applicability to settings where retraining or source-data access is unavailable. However, gains on ShapeNet-C are modest (+3%), and the method has only been validated on classification with two backbone families; whether token purging transfers to 3D segmentation, detection, or more severe/structured domain shifts remains open.
