## Problem

Diffusion-based virtual try-on faces a fundamental guidance dilemma: learning-based methods treat the garment as a passive control condition without direct warping guidance, losing fine-grained textures, while warping-based methods explicitly deform the garment image, making results highly sensitive to warp estimation errors and prone to geometric artifacts. No prior approach provides a guidance mechanism that is both direct enough to preserve texture detail and flexible enough to tolerate imperfect flow estimation.

## Input / Output

The method takes a person image and a garment image (both at 512×384), along with auxiliary conditions: OpenPose skeleton, DensePose map, and a human parsing mask used to construct the masked person latent. The Denoising UNet receives a 12-channel tensor (4-channel latent noise, 4-channel masked person latent, 1-channel mask, 3-channel skeleton), while the garment image is separately processed by a Garment Net and FashionCLIP to produce local and high-level features. The output is a single 512×384 RGB try-on image preserving the person's identity and pose while rendering the target garment.

## Method

FIA-VTON replaces all cross-attention layers in an inpainting Stable Diffusion v2.1 UNet with a proposed Flow Infused Attention (FIA) module that fuses three guidance signals: a dense warp flow (from DSDM, frozen), a local garment feature (from a Garment Net initialized with the same weights as the denoising UNet), and a high-level spatial embedding (from FashionCLIP). The flow is projected through learnable MLP refinement modules into the model and garment feature spaces, then added element-wise before a joint cross-attention produces a flow-modulated model feature; a second decoupled cross-attention integrates the FashionCLIP spatial embedding. This design treats the flow as an implicit offset on intermediate UNet features rather than an explicit pixel-level warp, allowing the diffusion model to correct flow inaccuracies using pose and other cues. Training uses the standard diffusion noise-prediction loss with the Garment Net and denoising UNet jointly optimized for 100k steps at 512×384 resolution.

## Results

On VITON-HD, FIA-VTON achieves SSIM 0.913, PSNR 27.827, LPIPS 0.047, FIDp 4.686, and FIDu 7.914, setting new records on SSIM, PSNR, and FIDp among all compared methods. On DressCode, FIA-VTON attains the best scores across all three sub-categories (Dresses, Lower, Upper), e.g., SSIM 0.952 and FIDp 6.02 on Lower. The method outperforms the strongest warp-based baseline D4-VTON by 0.661 on FIDu (7.914 vs 8.530) and the strongest learning-based baseline GarDiff by 1.335 on FIDp (4.686 vs 6.021) on VITON-HD.

## Baselines & Comparisons

Against warp-based methods, FIA-VTON dominates: it beats D4-VTON (the strongest warp baseline) by 0.212 on SSIM (0.913 vs 0.892), 3.117 on PSNR (27.827 vs 24.710), and 0.661 on FIDu (7.914 vs 8.530), while also improving FIDp from 4.845 to 4.686. Against learning-based methods, FIA-VTON achieves the best SSIM (0.913 vs GarDiff's 0.912) and a substantially lower FIDp (4.686 vs GarDiff's 6.021), though GarDiff retains a slight edge on LPIPS (0.036 vs 0.047) and FIDu (7.890 vs 7.914). On DressCode, FIA-VTON achieves the best scores on all four metrics (SSIM, LPIPS, FIDp, FIDu) across all three garment sub-categories, with the largest margins over D4-VTON on FIDp (e.g., 6.02 vs 6.67 on Lower) and over GarDiff on FIDu (e.g., 10.58 vs 12.29 on Lower). The ablation confirms that removing the Flow Guider degrades FIDp from 4.686 to 5.438, and removing the Spatial Guider degrades SSIM from 0.913 to 0.888, validating both components' contributions.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper positions itself at the intersection of two established virtual try-on paradigms: learning-based diffusion methods (TryOnDiffusion, StableVITON, GarDiff) that treat the garment as a control condition without explicit warping guidance, and warping-based methods (GP-VTON, DCI-VTON, D4-VTON) that explicitly deform garments before feeding them to a diffusion model. The key departure is replacing the explicitly warped garment with the underlying dense flow map as *implicit* guidance, injected through a novel Flow Infused Attention module that fuses flow, local garment features (via a Garment Net), and high-level spatial features (via FashionCLIP) into the denoising UNet's cross-attention layers. The flow estimation itself is borrowed from D4-VTON's DSDM module and kept frozen, while the backbone is an inpainting variant of Stable Diffusion v2.1.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The implicit flow guidance strategy directly addresses the hard-mapping problem that plagues explicit warping (geometric mismatches, artifacts under large deformations) while simultaneously improving texture preservation over guidance-free learning-based methods. However, the approach is evaluated only at 512×384 resolution on two datasets, and the paper acknowledges limitations in handling more diverse clothing types and real-time efficiency. An open question is whether the implicit flow formulation generalizes to video try-on or to settings where flow estimation is unavailable, and whether the specific choice of FashionCLIP as the spatial guider is critical or interchangeable with other vision-language encoders.
