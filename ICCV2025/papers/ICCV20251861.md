## Problem

Existing single-view 3D scene reconstruction methods enforce consistency between the input image and individually generated novel views, but fail to maintain consistency *among* the generated views themselves, producing noisy and geometrically distorted 3D reconstructions. This multiview inconsistency is particularly problematic for scene-level reconstruction where long trajectories of generated views accumulate drift, and prior approaches like Score Distillation Sampling tend to average content across iterations, yielding blurry results.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ICCV20251861/cropped_page0_idx1.webp)

## Input / Output

The method takes a single RGB image of an indoor or outdoor scene as input and produces a 3D Gaussian Splatting representation that supports real-time rendering from arbitrary novel viewpoints. The pipeline is entirely training-free, relying on pre-trained components: a Latent Consistency Model for diffusion-based refinement, Fooocus for inpainting, LLaVA for generating scene descriptions that guide inpainting, and a monocular depth estimator (Depth-Pro/Metric3D) for geometry. No per-scene fine-tuning of any diffusion model is required.

## Method

VistaDream uses a two-stage pipeline. Stage 1 builds a coarse 3D scaffold by zooming out the camera (widening FoV) and inpainting the exposed borders with Fooocus guided by LLaVA-generated captions, then estimating a depth map to anchor global geometry; a warp-and-inpaint loop then fills remaining gaps to produce an initial 3DGS. Stage 2 introduces Multiview Consistency Sampling (MCS), the core contribution: during the reverse diffusion process, at each denoising step the predicted x̂₀ for all N rendered views is used to train a temporary 3DGS, which is re-rendered to produce multiview-consistent images; these rendered images are blended with the diffusion model's raw predictions (via a timestep-dependent weight w_t) to rectify the denoising direction, explicitly enforcing consistency across all views simultaneously rather than sequentially. This avoids the averaging artifact of SDS (which processes one view per step) and the drift of sequential warp-and-inpaint, yielding sharper and more coherent novel views.

## Results

On 32 single-view images (indoor and outdoor), VistaDream achieves the best geometric accuracy (TSED 0.846), the best LLaVA-IQA score (0.718), and the highest fidelity to the input (PSNR 26.6), outperforming all baselines that require task-specific training. It also achieves the best Qwen2.5-VL score (0.874) and is competitive with CAT3D on InternVL (0.798 vs. 0.806). In terms of efficiency, VistaDream runs in ~7 minutes on a single 4090 (24 GB), achieving a 20× speedup over RealmDreamer (~150 min) while using far less VRAM than CAT3D (which requires 1/16 of an A100 80 GB).

## Baselines & Comparisons

VistaDream outperforms all five baselines on TSED (geometry), LLaVA-IQA, and PSNR (fidelity) without any task-specific training. Against RealmDreamer [51], the gains are the most dramatic: +0.140 TSED, +0.205 LLaVA-IQA, and +11.4 PSNR, while being 20× faster. Against CAT3D [15], VistaDream leads on TSED (0.846 vs. 0.822) and LLaVA-IQA (0.718 vs. 0.684) but trails slightly on InternVL (0.798 vs. 0.806); however, VistaDream requires no training and far less VRAM. Against MVGenMaster [6] and SEVA [75], VistaDream wins on every reported metric (e.g., TSED 0.846 vs. 0.801/0.789; PSNR 26.6 vs. 24.5/23.9). The paper also shows that MCS can be plugged into other pipelines: applying MCS to InstantSplat [13] yields a 17% average LLaVA-IQA improvement, and to RealmDreamer a 5% improvement, confirming MCS as a general-purpose refinement module.

## Relation to Prior Work

VistaDream sits at the intersection of the warp-and-inpaint scene expansion paradigm (RealmDreamer, GenWarp) and diffusion-model-based 3D optimization (DreamFusion's SDS). It departs from prior scaffold construction by replacing the moving-based strategy (small camera translations with large rotations) with a simpler zoom-out (FoV change) that produces a single coherent inpainting region, and from SDS by introducing Multiview Consistency Sampling (MCS), which enforces consistency across all rendered views simultaneously during the reverse diffusion process rather than optimizing one view at a time. The method is built on off-the-shelf components—3DGS for representation, LCM/Fooocus for generation, LLaVA for prompting, and Depth-Pro for geometry—without any task-specific training.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The training-free nature of VistaDream is practically significant: it achieves competitive or superior results to methods requiring large-scale multi-view training (CAT3D) or per-scene optimization (RealmDreamer) while running ~20× faster on a single 24 GB GPU. The MCS algorithm demonstrates generality by improving unrelated baselines (InstantSplat by 17%, Realm-DP by 5%), suggesting it could serve as a plug-in refinement step for other 3D reconstruction pipelines. Open questions include scalability to very large or dynamic scenes, the sensitivity of the w_t consistency-weight schedule, and whether the approach extends to video or 4D reconstruction where temporal consistency adds another dimension.
