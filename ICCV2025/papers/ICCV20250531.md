## Problem

Existing CAD generation methods face a fundamental trade-off: MLLM-based approaches using discrete command tokens cannot represent complex geometry (chamfers, fillets, freeform surfaces), while diffusion-based B-Rep generators lack the ability to accept multimodal user conditioning (text, images, point clouds). No prior framework simultaneously supports multimodal inputs and produces topologically accurate B-Rep models.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ICCV20250531/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method accepts multimodal conditions—text descriptions, point clouds with surface normals, multi-view rendered images (14 fixed angles), or a single transparent image—and produces a complete B-Rep model comprising continuous edge tokens (VAE features + bounding box + endpoint coordinates), surface tokens (VAE features + bounding box), and a binary edge-surface adjacency matrix. Sequence lengths are capped at 128 edges and 64 surfaces for ABC/mmABC (covering >95% of data), with the topology matrix R ∈ ℝ^{N_e × N_s} thresholded at 0.5 to determine adjacency.

## Method

CMT employs a cascade masked autoregressive (MAR) network that generates edges first, then surfaces conditioned on the generated edges, embedding the B-Rep topology prior that 'edges contour surfaces.' A unified multimodal condition encoder (modality-specific tokenizers → frozen CLIP-ViT → learnable projector) produces a fixed-length embedding prepended to the autoregressive sequence. A lightweight cross-attention topology predictor directly estimates the edge-surface adjacency matrix from the generated tokens, operating over 4200× faster than post-processing algorithms. Training uses diffusion-based denoising losses for both edge and surface token reconstruction plus an MSE loss on the topology matrix, with the cascade structure yielding +9.91% Coverage and +22.96% Valid over a single-network baseline in ablation.

## Results

On unconditional CAD generation, CMT achieves 68.60% Coverage and 58.5% Valid ratio on ABC, improving over BrepGen by +10.68% and +10.3% respectively. On DeepCAD, CMT reaches 75.71% Coverage and 70.1% Valid, surpassing BrepGen by +4.45% and +7.2%. For conditional generation, CMT achieves 0.64 Chamfer distance on point-cloud-conditioned tasks (outperforming the SOTA reconstruction method NVDNet at 0.77) and 2.17 Chamfer on image-conditioned tasks (vs. InstantMesh's 6.18).

## Baselines & Comparisons

CMT consistently outperforms BrepGen [48] (the prior SOTA diffusion-based B-Rep generator) on both unconditional generation benchmarks: +10.68% Coverage and +10.3% Valid on ABC, and +4.45% Coverage and +7.2% Valid on DeepCAD. On point-cloud-conditioned generation, CMT (0.64 Chamfer) surpasses not only the generation baseline DeepCAD [42] (5.11 Chamfer) by a large margin but also the SOTA reconstruction methods NVDNet [28] (0.77), Point2CAD [29] (1.55), and ComplexGen [11] (2.04). For image-conditioned generation, CMT (2.17 Chamfer, 92.93 F-score) substantially outperforms InstantMesh [44] (6.18 Chamfer, 84.71 F-score) by +4.01 Chamfer. On text-conditioned generation, CMT achieves a 75% win rate over Michelangelo [51] (25%) according to GPT-4o and 80% (vs. 20%) in human evaluation. SolidGen [17] is only compared on DeepCAD unconditional generation, where CMT leads by +4.68% Coverage and +9.8% Valid.

## Relation to Prior Work

CMT directly adapts the Masked Autoregressive (MAR) framework of Li et al. [25] to B-Rep CAD generation, replacing the single-stage token generation with a cascade 'edge-then-surface' architecture that encodes the topological prior of B-Reps. It also borrows the bounding-box-based surface tokenization from BrepGen [48] and positions itself against two existing paradigms: MLLM-based command-sequence generation (DeepCAD, CAD-MLLM, SolidGen), which cannot handle freeform geometry, and single-stage diffusion-based B-Rep generation (BrepGen), which lacks multimodal conditioning. The key conceptual departure is the topology predictor, a lightweight cross-attention module that replaces the expensive graph-based post-processing used in prior B-Rep pipelines, and the unified multimodal encoder that lets a single generation backbone accept text, point clouds, and images interchangeably.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The introduction of mmABC (1.35M B-Rep models with paired multi-view images, point clouds, and text captions) fills a critical gap: no prior dataset combined B-Rep representation with full multimodal annotations at this scale, making it a likely standard benchmark for future multimodal CAD generation work. The cascade architecture's ablation (+22.96% Valid over a single-network baseline) provides concrete evidence that respecting B-Rep topology during generation is essential rather than merely beneficial. Open questions remain around multi-body models, model completion, and whether the fixed token ordering (bounding-box coordinate sort) generalizes to highly irregular topologies; the authors explicitly flag multi-body generation and completion as future directions.
