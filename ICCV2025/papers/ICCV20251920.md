## Problem

Current 3D scene generation methods face a fundamental tension: LLMs provide open-vocabulary semantic reasoning but produce high-variance layouts with poor spatial constraint adherence, while specialist Graph Diffusion Models enforce geometric relationships faithfully but lack generalization to out-of-domain objects. Additionally, existing pipelines underutilize explicit layout and geometry information during the visual refinement stage, treating layout as a one-shot placement step rather than a continuous prior for multi-view-consistent scene optimization.

## Input / Output

The method takes a free-form text prompt describing a scene (e.g., 'a bedroom with a queen-sized bed, two night stands...') and produces a fully navigable 3D Gaussian Splatting scene comprising individual object splats and a room backdrop, assembled according to a generated layout of 8-parameter bounding boxes (dimensions, center position, azimuthal orientation). The text prompt is consumed through a four-step chain-of-thought procedure that extracts scene graph nodes, edges, bounding boxes, and hierarchical groupings, with M repeated samplings to capture LLM stochasticity. The intermediate layout output is a set of N+1 bounding boxes (objects plus room) that drive both asset placement and adaptive camera trajectory generation.

## Method

MiDSummer is a two-stage planning-assembly pipeline. In planning, a dual-guidance layout diffusion process blends LLM layout proposals with EchoLayout (a specialist GDM) denoising predictions at each DDPM timestep, where the per-object, per-parameter blend weight is computed as the geometric mean of three self-consistency measures (bounding box parameter variance, edge co-occurrence, and clique co-occurrence) across M LLM samples—high consistency favors LLM's semantic knowledge, low consistency falls back to GDM's geometric priors. In assembly, the layout drives an adaptive camera trajectory that interpolates between the convex hull of object splats and scene boundaries, then a multi-view-consistent Stable Diffusion refinement jointly optimizes scene splats and camera positions by steering the denoising direction toward a geometric prior rendering, with a quality-aware trajectory update that re-targets views toward artifact-prone regions.

## Results

On the main text-to-scene comparison (Table 3), MiDSummer achieves the best KID on SUN RGB-D (0.0647) and the highest Variance of Laplacian (633.8), indicating superior visual realism and geometric sharpness over all five baselines. On KID against Places365, MiDSummer scores 0.1097, slightly behind ProlificDreamer (0.0822) but ahead of GALA3D (0.1127) and all other methods. For layout planning on SUN RGB-D (Table 1), the dual-guidance approach achieves 88.8% L/R, 87.6% F/B, and 87.4% U/D spatial accuracy with 50.8 m³ volume error, substantially outperforming LLM-only (47.5% L/R, 64.5 m³) and GDM-only (255.5 m³) configurations.

## Baselines & Comparisons

MiDSummer outperforms all five baselines on KID_SUN (0.0647 vs. best baseline GALA3D at 0.0973) and VarLap (633.8 vs. GALA3D at 593.9), confirming gains in both photorealism and geometric sharpness. The closest competitor is GALA3D, which MiDSummer beats by 33% on KID_SUN and by 40 VarLap points; the gap widens substantially against Set-the-Scene (KID_SUN 0.1635, VarLap 92.28) and ProlificDreamer (KID_SUN 0.1146, VarLap 142.4). The one metric where MiDSummer does not lead is KID_Places, where ProlificDreamer achieves 0.0822 versus MiDSummer's 0.1097, suggesting ProlificDreamer's single-object generation transfers better to the broader Places365 indoor distribution. Ablations show that removing dual-guidance layout degrades KID_SUN from 0.0647 to 0.1198 (nearly doubling), while removing layout-aware refinement lowers VarLap from 633.8 to 545.9, confirming both stages contribute meaningfully to the final quality.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper builds directly on EchoLayout (the layout branch of EchoScene [3]) as its specialist graph diffusion model and on VistaDream [81] for multi-view-consistent scene refinement, but departs from both by introducing a dual-guidance mechanism that fuses LLM layout proposals with GDM denoising predictions, weighted by multi-level self-consistency scores. It extends the GALA3D [5] line of LLM-driven scene generation by (a) adding geometric precision through GDM co-guidance and (b) propagating layout priors into the refinement stage rather than treating layout as a one-shot planning output. The self-consistency weighting draws on prompt-ensembling and self-consistency literature [86–92] but applies it to the novel setting of heterogeneous 3D layout signals.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The paper addresses a practical gap in controllable scene generation: designers need both open-vocabulary semantic flexibility and reliable geometric constraint adherence, and the self-consistency-weighted dual guidance offers a principled mechanism for balancing these. However, the approach is complex (two-stage pipeline, multiple foundation models, iterative refinement) and has acknowledged limitations—regular backdrop geometry is required, azimuthal orientation lacks a canonical interpretation, and the three consistency terms exhibit diminishing returns due to correlation. Open questions include whether the self-consistency framework generalizes beyond indoor scenes, whether the layout-aware refinement trade-off (realism vs. sharpness) can be resolved, and whether the multi-model pipeline can be simplified without sacrificing controllability.
