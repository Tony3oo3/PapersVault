## Problem

Existing image-to-3D avatar generation methods produce avatars locked to the input pose, causing severe self-occlusion that hinders rigging and animation, and the resulting avatars lack fine-grained appearance details, appearing blurry and unrealistic. No prior approach simultaneously enables pose-conditioned multi-view synthesis for arbitrary target poses (including a standard A-pose) and high-fidelity local detail recovery from a single in-the-wild image.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ICCV20250689/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method takes a single full-body RGB image as input, optionally augmented with an estimated input pose and an arbitrary target 3D pose (encoded as 2D semantic pose maps rendered from SMPL) for the reposing task. It outputs a 3D Gaussian Splat avatar in the input pose, a reposed 3DGS avatar in the target pose, or a canonical A-pose avatar ready for SMPL-based skinning and animation. Novel view renderings are produced at 1024×1024 resolution, and the full pipeline runs in approximately 70 seconds on an A100 GPU.

## Method

AdaHuman combines two modules. First, a pose-conditioned 3D joint diffusion model replaces 2D self-attention in a multi-view latent diffusion U-Net with 3D attention, concatenates 2D semantic pose maps and sinusoidal camera ray embeddings as conditions, and interleaves 3DGS reconstruction (via an LGM-based generator) at every denoising step so that rendered 3D-consistent images guide the next diffusion step, ensuring multi-view coherence. Second, a compositional 3DGS refinement module renders zoomed-in canonical views of head, upper body, and lower body from the coarse 3DGS, refines each via SDEdit-style image-to-image editing with the same multi-view LDM, and composes the local and global 3DGS using a crop-aware camera ray map (which back-projects local pixel coordinates into the global view to compute consistent ray embeddings) and a visibility-aware composition scheme that filters splats by view coverage count and alpha-channel gradient salience to eliminate floating artifacts and redundant geometry. The system is trained on MVHumanNet multi-view video and CustomHumans renderings, initialized from SDXL and LGM pretrained weights, with a two-stage schedule (30k reconstruction steps followed by 10k reposing fine-tuning steps).

## Results

On avatar reconstruction, AdaHuman achieves the best rendering quality across all metrics on both CustomHumans and Sizer: PSNR 21.46/21.42, SSIM 0.8925/0.9258, LPIPS 0.1087/0.0856, and FID 27.3/19.15, outperforming the strongest baseline Human3Diffusion (PSNR 21.08/19.50, FID 35.3/20.69). On avatar reposing, AdaHuman's pose-conditioned generation (G_Pt) reaches PSNR 24.64, SSIM 0.9046, and LPIPS 0.0863, substantially exceeding SiTH (PSNR 21.21) and SIFU (PSNR 21.27). In a user study on 53 in-the-wild images, AdaHuman is preferred over SiTH (88.3%), SIFU (99.2%), and Human3Diffusion (79.7%).

## Baselines & Comparisons

AdaHuman outperforms all four baselines on every rendering quality metric (PSNR, SSIM, LPIPS, FID) on both CustomHumans and Sizer. Against Human3Diffusion [51] (the closest competitor), AdaHuman gains +0.38 PSNR and reduces FID from 35.3→27.3 on CustomHumans, and +1.92 PSNR with FID 19.15 vs 20.69 on Sizer; it also achieves the best Chamfer Distance (0.962 vs 1.230 cm on CH). Against SiTH [11], AdaHuman improves PSNR by +0.69 (CH) and LPIPS by 0.019, but SiTH retains a slight edge on Normal Consistency (0.7978 vs 0.7597 on CH), which the authors attribute to sharing the same normal estimation method. Against SIFU [57], AdaHuman leads by +0.87 PSNR and reduces FID from 92.6→27.3 on CustomHumans, while SIFU's template-based mesh approach struggles with loose clothing. On the reposing task (Table 3), AdaHuman's direct pose-conditioned generation (PSNR 24.64) substantially beats both SiTH (21.21) and SIFU (21.27), and also outperforms its own LBS-deformation variant (23.01), demonstrating superior handling of non-rigid cloth deformation.

## Relation to Prior Work

AdaHuman extends the joint diffusion-and-reconstruction paradigm established by Human3Diffusion [51] and CAT3D [8], but introduces two key departures: (1) pose conditioning via 2D semantic pose maps that enables arbitrary-pose generation and reposing without requiring standard-pose training data, and (2) a compositional 3DGS refinement module that leverages SDEdit-style image-to-image editing on local body crops, combined with a novel crop-aware camera ray map to map local-view pixels back into the global 3DGS generator's coordinate space (extending LGM [42]). The visibility-aware composition scheme for merging partial 3DGS reconstructions is entirely new, addressing a practical gap that prior feed-forward 3DGS methods leave unaddressed.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

AdaHuman addresses a critical practical bottleneck in 3D avatar pipelines: generating animation-ready, high-detail avatars from a single in-the-wild image without requiring standard-pose references or per-subject optimization. The compositional refinement strategy (local SDEdit + visibility-aware composition) is a reusable technique that could transfer to other 3DGS reconstruction tasks beyond humans. Limitations include the ~70s inference time (not real-time), the two-mode animation tradeoff (direct reposing vs. LBS skinning), and reliance on multi-view video training data (MVHumanNet) that may not be universally available. Open questions include whether the compositional approach scales to more body parts or non-human subjects, and whether the pose-conditioned diffusion can handle extreme or unseen poses beyond the training distribution.
