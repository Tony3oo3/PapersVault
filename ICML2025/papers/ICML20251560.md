## Problem

Existing video-to-audio generation methods produce non-spatial (mono/stereo) audio and operate on limited-field-of-view perspective videos, missing both the directional cues essential for immersive experiences and the full 3D visual context needed to accurately localize sound sources. No prior work generates spatial audio directly from 360-degree panoramic video, which captures a complete 360° field of view and inherently encodes the spatial relationships between sound-emitting objects and the listener.

## Input / Output

The method takes a 10-second 360-degree panoramic video in equirectangular projection (1:2 height-to-width ratio, sampled at 8 fps) and produces 44.1 kHz First-order Ambisonics (FOA) audio with four channels (W, X, Y, Z) encoding omnidirectional sound pressure, front-back, left-right, and vertical components respectively. The panoramic video is consumed in two complementary forms: the full equirectangular frame (padded to 1:1 square) for global scene context, and a cropped perspective field-of-view for fine-grained local details. Audio is generated in a compressed latent space via a custom spatial VAE before being decoded back to the four-channel FOA waveform.

## Method

OmniAudio uses a 1.2B-parameter Diffusion Transformer (DiT) with latent flow matching as its generative backbone, trained in two stages. First, a self-supervised coarse-to-fine pre-training phase applies masked token prediction: non-spatial audio from ~2M samples is converted to FOA format and pre-trained in a 'coarse' stage, followed by a 'fine' stage on curated FOA audio, allowing the model to learn general audio patterns before specializing to spatial characteristics. Second, supervised fine-tuning introduces a dual-branch video conditioning strategy where a frozen MetaCLIP-Huge encoder processes both the full equirectangular panorama (global branch, passed through max-pooling as a global DiT condition) and a perspective FoV crop (local branch, upsampled and added element-wise to audio latents). The spatial VAE, adapted from the Stable Audio framework by initializing from stereo weights and replacing the MidSide STFT loss with per-channel FOA losses, compresses the four-channel audio into a latent representation that the flow matching model learns to denoise conditioned on the dual video features.

## Results

On the in-distribution Sphere360-Bench, OmniAudio achieves FD of 88.30, KL divergence of 1.58, and spatial angle error (ΔAngular) of 1.28, with subjective scores of MOS-SQ 84.67 and MOS-AF 87.23. On the out-of-distribution YT360 test set, it maintains strong performance with FD 92.57, KL 1.64, and ΔAngular 1.27. Notably, OmniAudio runs at 0.92s inference time, roughly 24× faster than ViSAGe (22.37s) while outperforming it on every metric.

## Baselines & Comparisons

OmniAudio dominates all three baseline families on every reported metric across both test sets. Against the cascaded Diff-Foley+AS system, OmniAudio reduces FD from 331.05 to 88.30 (Sphere360) and 361.65 to 92.57 (YT360), while improving MOS-SQ by ~15 points (84.67 vs. 69.87). Against MMAudio+AS, the FD gap is narrower on YT360 (92.57 vs. 190.40) but still substantial, with MOS-SQ improving from 75.34 to 84.67. Against the spatial-audio-specific ViSAGe, OmniAudio achieves lower ΔAngular (1.28 vs. 1.49 for FoV, 1.28 vs. 1.51 for 360) and lower Δabsφ (0.52 vs. 0.71/0.74), confirming that direct FOA generation from panoramic video captures spatial cues more accurately than perspective-video-based approaches. Critically, OmniAudio's 0.92s inference time is ~24× faster than ViSAGe's 22.37s, making it the only method competitive for near-real-time applications.

## Relation to Prior Work

OmniAudio sits at the intersection of two prior research threads: video-to-audio generation (Diff-Foley, MMAudio, FoleyGen) and spatial audio generation (ViSAGe, DiffSAGe, 2.5D Visual Sound). It builds directly on conditional flow matching (Lipman et al., 2022) as its generative backbone and adapts the Stable Audio VAE (Evans et al., 2024) into a four-channel FOA spatial autoencoder. The key departure is that all prior spatial audio generation methods condition on fixed perspective video, text, or explicit source locations, while OmniAudio is the first to consume full 360-degree panoramic video as input, eliminating the need for external spatial control signals. The self-supervised masked-token pre-training strategy draws from Audiobox and Voicebox but is novel in its coarse-to-fine application across non-spatial and spatial audio domains.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

By introducing 360V2SA as a formal task and releasing Sphere360 (103K clips, 288 hours) with a standardized benchmark, this paper provides the foundational infrastructure for a research direction that was previously infeasible due to data scarcity. The practical impact is significant for VR/AR content creation, where generating spatially-aware audio from panoramic video removes a major production bottleneck. Key open questions remain: the model struggles with scenes containing multiple simultaneous sound sources (e.g., misattributing instrument sound to audience applause), the 103K-clip dataset is acknowledged as insufficient for robust real-world deployment, and whether the dual-branch design generalizes to higher-order Ambisonics or longer-form content. The 24× inference speedup over ViSAGe (0.92s vs. 22.37s) also raises the question of whether flow matching can enable near-real-time spatial audio generation for interactive applications.
