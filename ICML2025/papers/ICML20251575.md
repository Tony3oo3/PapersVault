## Problem

Existing multi-region communication models cannot simultaneously disentangle inter-regional communication, inputs from unobserved brain regions, and local population dynamics. Manually specifying external inputs to each region causes models to forgo learned communication entirely, while overly flexible latent-variable-based messaging can mislocalize computations. No prior method addresses all four challenges of communication identification: accounting for unobserved inputs, capturing nonlinear temporal dynamics, faithfully reconstructing activity, and disambiguating among multiple models that explain the data equally well.

## Input / Output

The model consumes single-trial, multi-region neural population recordings (spike counts or calcium traces) binned into fixed time steps, with each region represented by a set of recorded neurons. Beyond per-region reconstructed firing rates, it outputs time-varying communication messages for each directed region pair, time-varying inferred inputs representing activity from unobserved regions, and a static effectome matrix whose elements quantify the volume of directed communication flow across the connectome. The model is trained exclusively on unperturbed control trials yet can predict brain-wide effects of causal circuit perturbations (e.g., photoinhibition) that were held out during training.

## Method

MR-LFADS is a sequential variational autoencoder in which each recorded brain region is modeled by a gated recurrent unit (GRU) dynamical system driven by both inter-regional communication messages and inferred inputs from unobserved regions. Three design choices distinguish it from prior approaches: (1) a unidirectional encoder-controller automatically infers region-specific external inputs in an unsupervised manner, avoiding the failure mode where manually specified inputs suppress learned communication; (2) communication messages are computed as affine functions of the source region's predicted firing rates rather than its latent dynamical state, anchoring messages to observed data and reducing identification ambiguity; (3) a structured KL bottleneck with β_u = 10β_m penalizes inferred inputs more heavily than communication, encouraging the model to explain signals via observed-region communication before resorting to unobserved inputs. Training maximizes the ELBO with separately scheduled KL penalties for initial conditions, inputs, and messages, and the causal formulation ensures all inferred quantities depend only on neural activity up to the current time step.

## Results

Across 35 randomly generated multi-region DGNs (Experiment 3), MR-LFADS(R) achieved the best data reconstruction R² (indistinguishable from other MR-LFADS variants) and the most accurate effectome cosine similarity (S_cos), with statistically significant advantages over RRR (p=0.00016), MR-SDS (p=0.006), and mp-srSLDS (p=0.0) while being indistinguishable from MR-LFADS(G) (p=0.12). For message content, MR-LFADS(R) was the only model to perform best on both directions of linear decoding (ground-truth-from-inferred and inferred-from-ground-truth R²), significantly outperforming RRR (p=0.0) and mp-srSLDS (p=0.0) in the forward direction. On real multi-region electrophysiology data, MR-LFADS(R) predicted the brain-wide effects of held-out ALM photoinhibition, correctly ranking Thal(A) as most affected and MRN as least affected, and showed more consistent inferred communication across 5 random seeds than MR-LFADS(G).

## Baselines & Comparisons

MR-LFADS(R) outperforms all three baselines on the primary communication identification metrics. Against RRR (reduced-rank regression), MR-LFADS(R) achieves significantly higher effectome S_cos (p=0.00016) and message content R² (p=0.0 in the forward direction), while RRR reconstructs data poorly due to its inability to capture integration timescales. Against mp-srSLDS, MR-LFADS(R) achieves significantly higher effectome S_cos (p=0.0) and message R² (p=0.0), though mp-srSLDS correctly encodes the stimulus in messages on the pass-decision task; the key advantage is that MR-LFADS(R) additionally infers unobserved inputs and avoids spurious bidirectional communication. Against MR-SDS, MR-LFADS(R) achieves significantly higher effectome S_cos (p=0.006) and message R² (p=0.001), with MR-SDS identifying spurious A^D→A^P communication and its effective inputs carrying markedly less stimulus information. Ablated variants confirm that the data-constrained (rate-based) communication design is critical: MR-LFADS(F) and MR-LFADS(G), which infer messages from unconstrained latent variables, identify spurious bidirectional connections and encode the decision variable d in messages where only the stimulus s should appear, demonstrating mislocalization of computation.

## Relation to Prior Work

MR-LFADS extends the single-region LFADS framework (Pandarinath et al., 2018) to multi-region settings by coupling per-region GRU generators through explicit communication channels and adding a structured KL bottleneck that prioritizes communication over inferred unobserved inputs. It departs from existing multi-region models (mp-srSLDS, MR-SDS) in two key ways: (1) it automatically infers region-specific inputs from unobserved sources rather than requiring manual specification, and (2) it constrains communication to originate from reconstructed firing rates rather than unconstrained latent states, reducing identification ambiguity. The paper positions itself as the first method to simultaneously address all four challenges of multi-region communication modeling (unobserved communication signals, unobserved inputs, faithful reconstruction, and disambiguation among equally-fitting models).

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

MR-LFADS provides a principled, end-to-end framework for multi-region communication modeling that is validated by predicting brain-wide effects of causal circuit perturbations (photoinhibition) never seen during training—a strong functional test of whether inferred communication is mechanistically accurate. Key limitations include hyperparameter sensitivity (particularly KL coefficients), the irreducible correlation-causation confound when an unrecorded region drives multiple recorded regions, and the need for further work to interpret the representations of inferred inputs. The structured bottleneck (β_u = 10β_m) and data-constrained communication are design choices that may need re-tuning for different connectivity topologies, and the paper acknowledges that its greatest value may lie in generating hypotheses for targeted causal experiments rather than providing definitive causal maps.
