## Problem

Existing diffusion-native watermarking methods either modify diffusion modules (UNet, VAE decoder) to embed watermarks—causing visible artifacts and quality degradation—or generate context-less watermark embeddings that ignore image content. Additionally, methods like Stable Signature require per-watermark VAE fine-tuning, making them impractical for large-scale commercial deployment with millions of unique keys. The paper asks: can watermarks be embedded during diffusion generation without altering any pretrained diffusion components, while maintaining sharp, artifact-free image quality?
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ICML20250420/cropped_page1_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method takes a standard text-to-image diffusion prompt and a 48-bit watermark sequence as input, producing a watermarked RGB image at 512×512 resolution. The watermark is imprinted on-the-fly during VAE decoding via a 1.3M-parameter adapter that adds feature residuals to intermediate VAE decoder layers, completing in ~30ms. Watermark retrieval is performed by a separate pretrained HiDDeN decoder, decoupling embedding from extraction and enabling plug-and-play deployment across different diffusion backbones.

## Method

WMAdapter introduces a Contextual Adapter consisting of six lightweight Fuser modules (each with an Embedding MLP and a 1×1-conv Fusing block) attached before the Conv, Middle, and four Up blocks of the kl-f8 VAE decoder. Each Fuser conditions on both the watermark bits and the local VAE feature map, producing content-aware feature residuals that adaptively place watermark information in texture-rich regions. Training uses a two-stage procedure: (1) large-scale training with frozen VAE and watermark decoder, optimizing only the adapter with MAE + LPIPS + Watson-VGG + BCE losses for 2 epochs; (2) a Hybrid Finetuning stage that jointly fine-tunes the adapter and VAE decoder on a small dataset with an added total-variation loss, but uses the *original* (unmodified) VAE decoder at inference. This hybrid strategy suppresses grid-like artifacts and lens-flare distortions while preserving the plug-and-play property, yielding the sharpest images (34.8 dB PSNR, 2.5 FID) with competitive bit accuracy (0.90 under combined attacks) and near-perfect tracing accuracy across 10⁴–10⁶ user pools.

## Results

On COCO 2017 val (512×512), WMAdapter-I achieves the best image quality among all compared methods with PSNR of 34.8 dB and FID of 2.5, improving over the closest diffusion-native baseline Stable Signature by ~17% in PSNR and ~22% in FID. It attains perfect TPR@FPR 10⁻⁶ (1.00) and competitive bit accuracy under combined attacks (0.90), trailing only Stable Signature (0.93) by 0.03. For tracing accuracy at 10⁶ users, WMAdapter-F achieves 1.000 and WMAdapter-I achieves 0.999, outperforming Stable Signature (0.998) and WADIFF (0.934).

## Baselines & Comparisons

WMAdapter-I dominates on image quality: it outperforms Stable Signature (PSNR 34.8 vs 29.7, FID 2.5 vs 3.2), WOUAF (PSNR 34.8 vs 25.3, FID 2.5 vs 13.5), RoSteALS (PSNR 34.8 vs 30.4, FID 2.5 vs 5.5), and SSL (FID 2.5 vs 14.8) by large margins, while matching or exceeding HiDDeN (PSNR 34.8 vs 34.1, FID 2.5 vs 3.1). On bit accuracy under combined attacks, WMAdapter-I (0.90) and WMAdapter-F (0.92) trail Stable Signature (0.93) by 0.03 and 0.01 respectively, but surpass SSL (0.88), HiDDeN (0.85), StegaStamp (0.49), and RoSteALS (0.50). For tracing accuracy at 10⁶ users, WMAdapter-F (1.000) and WMAdapter-I (0.999) edge out Stable Signature (0.998) and substantially outperform WADIFF (0.934); Tree-Ring achieves 0.000 due to design flaws. Against regeneration attacks, WMAdapter requires a 4–6 dB PSNR drop to remove the watermark versus only 2 dB for Stable Signature. The key trade-off is that WMAdapter sacrifices ~0.03 combined-attack bit accuracy relative to Stable Signature in exchange for significantly sharper, artifact-free images and full scalability (no per-key VAE finetuning).

## Relation to Prior Work

WMAdapter positions itself against two lines of diffusion watermarking: (1) methods that modify diffusion components (Stable Signature fine-tunes the VAE decoder per-key; WOUAF and AquaLoRA alter the VAE or UNet backbone), and (2) context-less watermark plugins (RoSteALS, WOUAF) that generate watermark embeddings without conditioning on image content. It builds on the HiDDeN pretrained watermark decoder for efficient knowledge transfer and borrows the plug-and-play adapter philosophy from ControlNet-style modules, but departs by attaching a lightweight (1.3M parameter) contextual adapter to the VAE decoder that conditions on intermediate VAE features, and by introducing a Hybrid Finetuning strategy that trains jointly with the VAE decoder yet uses the original VAE at inference.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

WMAdapter addresses a practical bottleneck for commercial AI image generation: scalable, per-user watermarking without degrading image quality or requiring per-key model fine-tuning. The Hybrid Finetuning trick (co-train with VAE, infer with original VAE) is a simple but effective recipe for suppressing grid-like artifacts that plagues prior diffusion-native methods. Limitations remain: the method inherits HiDDeN decoder's vulnerability to rotation and strong Gaussian noise, white-box adversarial attacks can remove the watermark with minimal quality loss, and the approach is currently validated only at 512×512 resolution. Open directions include extending to video generation (the authors note compatibility with AnimateDiff/SVD), building on more robust decoders, and investigating whether the contextual design generalizes to higher resolutions like SDXL's native 1024×1024.
