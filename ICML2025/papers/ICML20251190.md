## Problem

Off-policy RL methods like GFlowNets for biological sequence design generate sequences from scratch, producing out-of-distribution inputs that undertrained proxy models score unreliably. This proxy misspecification causes GFlowNets to perform poorly in large-scale settings (e.g., protein design), as the policy is trained on noisy reward signals from regions where the proxy has no data support. The paper asks how to restrict off-policy exploration to reliable regions while preserving the diversity and novelty advantages of GFlowNets.

## Input / Output

The method takes an offline dataset of scored biological sequences (DNA L=8, RNA L=14, protein L=238/90, peptide L≤50), a trained proxy model with uncertainty estimates, and a conservativeness parameter δ (typically 0.5 for short sequences, 0.05 for long ones). It produces denoised sequence candidates that serve dual purposes: as off-policy training trajectories for the GFlowNet policy and as oracle query batches (size B=128) for active learning. The adaptive variant δ(x;σ) modulates masking probability per data point based on proxy standard deviation, so uncertain regions receive fewer masked tokens (more conservative search).

## Method

δ-Conservative Search (δ-CS) is a three-step off-policy sampling procedure integrated into the GFlowNet active learning loop: (1) sample a reference sequence from the offline dataset using a rank-based reweighted prior that upweights high-scoring sequences; (2) inject noise by independently masking each token with probability δ via a Bernoulli process; (3) sequentially denoise the masked tokens left-to-right using the GFlowNet forward policy, which predicts each masked position conditioned on previously reconstructed tokens. The resulting denoised sequences form off-policy trajectories for trajectory balance (TB) loss training, restricting the policy's exploration to neighborhoods of observed data where the proxy is reliable. The key differentiator from prior off-policy GFlowNet methods is the controllable conservativeness: δ=1 recovers standard GFN-AL (full from-scratch generation), while smaller δ values progressively constrain search to smaller edit distances. An adaptive schedule δ(x;σ) = δ_const − λσ(x) further reduces masking for data points where the proxy is uncertain, aligning exploration aggressiveness with model confidence.

## Results

On the AAV protein design task (L=90), δ-CS achieves a maximum score of 0.708±0.010 and median of 0.663±0.007, substantially outperforming GFN-AL (0.560/0.509) and all other baselines which are capped at 0.500 max. On GFP (L=238), δ-CS reaches 3.592 max and 3.567 median versus 3.578/3.511 for GFN-AL, while most non-GFN baselines plateau at 3.572 max. On RNA-C (L=14), the improvement is most pronounced: δ-CS achieves 1.094 max and 0.972 median compared to GFN-AL's 0.951/0.774. Across all eight tasks, δ-CS consistently outperforms every baseline in at least one of max, median, or mean score.

## Baselines & Comparisons

δ-CS outperforms all 12 baselines across every task. Against the primary baseline GFN-AL (Jain et al., 2022), δ-CS improves max score by 26% on AAV (0.708 vs 0.560), 15% on RNA-C (1.094 vs 0.951), and 4% on GFP (3.592 vs 3.578), while also achieving higher median and mean scores on all tasks. Non-GFN baselines (AdaLead, BO, TuRBO, CMA-ES, CbAS, DbAS, DyNA PPO) are uniformly outperformed; on AAV and GFP, six of these seven methods are completely stuck at the initial dataset maximum (0.500 and 3.572 respectively), never discovering a single sequence above the offline data. Against GFNSeqEditor (Ghari et al., 2023), δ-CS achieves a Pareto improvement: higher scores, diversity, and novelty simultaneously across all six tasks evaluated. Against LS-GFN (Kim et al., 2024d), δ-CS produces more balanced trade-offs among score, diversity, and novelty due to its ability to mask any token position rather than only the last K tokens. On the harder GFP benchmark from Kirjner et al. (2024), δ-CS with δ=0.01 achieves 1.06 on GFP-Medium and 0.86 on GFP-Hard, outperforming LatProtRL (0.93/0.85) and GFN-AL without conservative search (0.21/0.09). On the AMP task, δ-CS with UCB improves mean score from 0.919 to 0.938 and novelty from 19.2 to 23.6 over GFN-AL, and with EI improves mean from 0.940 to 0.958.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the GFlowNet active learning framework for biological sequence design (GFN-AL, Jain et al. 2022) by introducing a conservative off-policy search mechanism (δ-CS) that masks tokens in high-scoring sequences and denoises them with the GFlowNet forward policy. Conceptually, it bridges two previously separate lines of work: off-policy RL with GFlowNets (which generates novel sequences from scratch but is vulnerable to proxy misspecification) and evolutionary/constrained search (which stays near known-good sequences but limits novelty). The adaptive δ control based on proxy uncertainty (via MC dropout or ensembles) departs from prior GFlowNet exploration strategies that assume an accurate reward model, and the plug-and-play compatibility with multiple off-policy RL objectives (TB, SQL, VarGrad, Soft PCL) distinguishes it from method-specific improvements.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The paper addresses a practical bottleneck in lab-in-the-loop biological sequence design: proxy models trained on limited data produce unreliable rewards for out-of-distribution sequences, causing GFlowNets to plateau in large-scale tasks like GFP and AAV design. The δ parameter offers a simple, interpretable knob for the exploration-robustness tradeoff, and the adaptive variant ties conservativeness directly to model confidence. However, the method does not fundamentally resolve proxy misspecification—it mitigates its effects during search—and its effectiveness depends on the quality of uncertainty estimates. Open questions include whether the approach extends to multi-objective settings, whether improved credit assignment (e.g., Jang et al. 2024) further amplifies gains, and whether the principle of uncertainty-aligned exploration generalizes beyond biological sequences to other active learning domains with unreliable surrogates.
