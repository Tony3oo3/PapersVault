## Problem

Source-free unlearning—removing the influence of forget data from a model without access to the original training data—remains underexplored. The few existing methods (GKT, ISPF) rely on data-free knowledge distillation that requires training a new model from scratch, incurring substantial computational costs that limit practicality for large-scale or real-time unlearning.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ICML20252474/cropped_page3_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method takes a pre-trained classifier (ResNet-18 for CIFAR-10/100, ViT for PinsFaceRecognition) and the set of forget class labels as its only inputs, requiring no access to any training samples. It produces a fine-tuned unlearned model that has degraded performance on forget classes while preserving accuracy on retain classes. The synthetic data generated during the process is class-wise and visually indistinguishable, posing no privacy risk.

## Method

DSDA is a two-stage framework. Stage 1 (AEGDS) derives an energy function from the classifier's logits and samples synthetic data via Stochastic Gradient Langevin Dynamics, accelerated by a second-order Runge-Kutta (Heun's) integrator and Nesterov momentum to skip redundant time steps while preserving distributional fidelity. Stage 2 (DAMO) fine-tunes the original model using three objectives—retain loss, forget loss, and a novel discriminative feature alignment loss that enforces intra-class compactness and inter-class separability of retain classes in feature space. To resolve gradient conflicts among these three objectives, DAMO employs a CAGrad-style multitask optimization that finds an update direction maximizing the minimum per-objective progress while constraining deviation from the weighted combined gradient, solved in closed form via a Lagrangian dual.

## Results

On single-class unlearning, DSDA achieves A_r of 77.91% on CIFAR-10, 49.96% on CIFAR-100, and 88.36% on PinsFaceRecognition, matching or exceeding the retrained oracle (77.90%, 53.98%, 90.89%) while reducing execution time by 8.3×, 14.5×, and 3.0× respectively. DSDA achieves 0% A_f across all datasets and the lowest MIA among all source-free methods (11.80%, 13.80%, 18.39%), closely matching the retrained baseline. On multi-class unlearning (CIFAR-100), DSDA maintains A_r of 49.55–50.28% across k=2,4,8 forget classes, outperforming all baselines while completing unlearning in 147–163 seconds versus 447–974 seconds for the next-best source-free method (ISPF).

## Baselines & Comparisons

DSDA dominates all source-free baselines (GKT, ISPF) by a wide margin: on CIFAR-10, DSDA achieves 77.91% A_r versus ISPF's 66.46% and GKT's 44.23%, while also reducing MIA from 31.70% (ISPF) and 24.98% (GKT) to 11.80% and cutting execution time from 304s/394s to 133s. Against non-source-free methods, DSDA matches or exceeds LAU on MIA (11.80% vs 33.78%) and SCAR on A_r (77.91% vs 76.13%), though LAU is slightly faster (113s vs 133s) and marginally higher on A_r (78.37% vs 77.91%). DSDA substantially outperforms UNSIR on MIA (11.80% vs 70.48%) and SSD on A_r (77.91% vs 54.44%), confirming that energy-guided synthesis plus discrimination-aware optimization yields both stronger forgetting and better privacy guarantees. On multi-class unlearning (k=8, CIFAR-100), DSDA maintains 49.56% A_r while ISPF drops to 44.00% and GKT to 25.39%, demonstrating superior scalability as the number of forget classes grows.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper builds on three distinct threads: (1) the energy-based interpretation of classifiers (Grathwohl et al., 2019) to derive an energy function from logits, (2) Stochastic Gradient Langevin Dynamics (Welling & Teh, 2011) as the sampling mechanism for data synthesis, and (3) CAGrad-style gradient conflict resolution (Yu et al., 2020) adapted to the unlearning setting. It departs from prior source-free unlearning methods (GKT, ISPF) that rely on Data-Free Knowledge Distillation requiring full model retraining, instead proposing energy-guided data synthesis (AEGDS) combined with fine-tuning via discrimination-aware multitask optimization (DAMO). The AEGDS component also connects to model inversion literature but replaces GAN-based or gradient-optimization approaches with implicit distribution modeling via Langevin dynamics accelerated by Runge-Kutta and Nesterov momentum.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

Source-free unlearning addresses a practical gap where training data is inaccessible (cloud deletion policies, streaming environments), and DSDA's 68.5% average efficiency improvement over prior source-free methods makes unlearning more deployable in real-world pipelines. The empirical observation that standard unlearning losses disrupt retain-class feature compactness, motivating the discriminative alignment objective, offers a useful diagnostic for practitioners. However, the framework is limited to class-level unlearning; instance-wise or attribute-wise source-free unlearning remains open, as does validation on larger-scale models and datasets beyond CIFAR and a single face-recognition benchmark.
