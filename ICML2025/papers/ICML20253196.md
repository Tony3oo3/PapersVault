## Problem

Existing text-to-3D generation methods rely on pointwise reward scores to guide optimization, which demands highly accurate absolute quality evaluation and offers limited controllability beyond a single quality dimension. This paper addresses the gap by shifting from score-guided to preference-guided optimization, where only distinguishable relative rankings are needed rather than precise pointwise scores, and by integrating large multimodal models to enable instruction-level fine-grained control over generated 3D content.

## Input / Output

The method takes a single text prompt as input and produces an optimized 3D asset parameterized as NeRF or 3D Gaussian Splatting, using MVDream as the backbone diffusion model. During optimization, a rendered RGB image is noised at a sampled timestep to construct pairwise examples, and the reward or LMM model scores the predicted clean images. The full optimization takes approximately 2 hours on a single NVIDIA RTX A6000 GPU, and evaluation renders 120 multi-view images per asset for scoring.

## Method

DreamDPO performs a three-step iterative optimization: (1) pairwise example construction by adding two different Gaussian noises to the same rendered image at the same diffusion timestep, (2) pairwise comparison via a reward model (HPSv2, ImageReward) or LMM (QwenVL) that ranks the two predicted clean images against the prompt, and (3) preference-guided optimization using a reformulated SimPO loss that pulls the win example toward the text-conditioned distribution and pushes the lose example away. The key technical insight is a piecewise gradient: when the preference score gap s_gap falls below a threshold τ, the method falls back to standard SDS on the win example only, preventing chaotic gradients from overly similar pairs. This design reduces the requirement from precise pointwise scoring to merely distinguishable relative rankings, and the LMM variant supports explicit yes/no instruction questions (e.g., correcting object count or motion) for fine-grained controllability.

## Results

On the GPTEval3D benchmark (110 prompts), DreamDPO achieves the best ImageReward score (−0.35) and the highest GPTEval3D Overall Elo rating (1203.1), outperforming the second-best method Latent-NeRF (1178.7) by +24.4 points. DreamDPO also leads on every individual GPTEval3D sub-metric: Alignment (1298.9), Plausibility (1171.9), T-G Coherency (1276.4), Geo Details (1373.2), and Tex Details (1296.9). The largest margin over the runner-up is in Texture Details (+48.4 over MVDream's 1255.5) and Geometry Details (+41.4 over MVDream's 1324.9).

## Baselines & Comparisons

DreamDPO outperforms all 13 baselines on every GPTEval3D sub-metric and the ImageReward score. The closest quantitative competitor is Latent-NeRF (Overall 1178.7, IR −0.42), which DreamDPO surpasses by +24.4 Overall and +0.07 IR. Among text-guided methods, MVDream (Overall 1097.7) and ProlificDreamer (Overall 1012.5) are the strongest, with DreamDPO leading by +105.4 and +190.6 Overall respectively. In the image-guided category, SyncDreamer (963.5) and Wonder3D (970.9) trail DreamDPO by over 230 points. Qualitatively, DreamDPO also improves over DreamReward (Ye et al., 2025) in both text alignment (e.g., correctly rendering 'a trail of flowers' under a bicycle) and geometric/texture detail (e.g., a more luxuriant oak tree), confirming that preference-guided pairwise optimization yields gains beyond pointwise reward maximization.

## Relation to Prior Work

DreamDPO builds on the score distillation sampling (SDS) paradigm established by DreamFusion and extended by MVDream, ProlificDreamer, and others for text-to-3D generation. Its key conceptual departure is from DreamReward (Ye et al., 2025), which regularizes 3D generation using pointwise reward model scores; DreamDPO instead reformulates the optimization around pairwise preference comparisons, drawing directly from Direct Preference Optimization (DPO) and SimPO in the LLM literature. This shift lowers the requirement on reward models from precise absolute scoring to merely distinguishable relative ranking, and opens the door to using large multimodal models (e.g., QwenVL) as ranking oracles for instruction-following control.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

By replacing pointwise score maximization with pairwise preference optimization, DreamDPO offers a more flexible and less brittle alignment mechanism for 3D generation, particularly when reward models are imperfect or when fine-grained controllability (e.g., correcting object count or motion) is needed. The LMM-based ranking pathway is a practical step toward instruction-following 3D generation, though it remains limited by the underlying generative model's capability and the instability of open-API-based annotation. Open questions include whether this preference-guided framework transfers to feed-forward 3D generation methods, whether 3D-native reward models can fully replace 2D proxies, and how to reduce the ~2× training overhead of pairwise construction.
