## Problem

Dynamic systems in logistics, scheduling, and production planning require rapid reoptimization of MILPs when parameters shift slightly between instances. Existing reoptimization techniques (warm-starting, branch-and-bound tree reuse, parameter tuning) either produce invalid solutions under perturbation or fail to reduce problem complexity, while end-to-end ML methods (Neural Diving, Predict-and-Search) focus on binary variables and rely on slow large-neighborhood search. No prior method efficiently finds high-quality feasible solutions for general MILPs with mixed binary, integer, and continuous variables under strict time limits (e.g., 10 seconds).

## Input / Output

The method takes a solved historical MILP instance with full branch-and-bound records (leaf-node LP relaxation, basic variables, dual solutions) and a modified MILP instance encoded as a weighted bipartite graph (variable-constraint edges with coefficient features). The GNN outputs marginal probabilities for each binary variable and predicted lower/upper bounds for integer and continuous variables (encoded via logarithmic transformation into 8-bit binary representations). The Thompson Sampling module then iteratively produces a feasible solution for the modified instance, with the final output being the best objective value found within the time budget.

## Method

VP-OR is a two-stage framework. Stage 1 (Variable Prediction) trains a GNN on the bipartite graph of the modified instance, augmented with historical solving features (leaf-node bounds, depth, estimate, basic-variable indicators, dual information) to predict binary variable probabilities and feasible bounds for integer/continuous variables; a confidence threshold filters uncertain predictions into variable intervals. Stage 2 (Online Refinement) formulates variable selection as a contextual multi-armed bandit: Beta distributions are initialized from GNN predictions (Beta(p_i+ε, 1-p_i+ε) for binary, Beta(1,1) for others), and each iteration samples μ values, fixes the top a% of variables to their predicted values/bounds, solves the reduced subproblem via SCIP, and updates the Beta parameters based on whether the solution improves. A relaxation mechanism handles infeasibility by partitioning fixed variables into 10 groups and solving without each group. The key differentiator is that fixing variables is 3–10× faster than LNS, and Thompson Sampling adaptively identifies which predictions are reliable rather than uniformly applying uncertainty neighborhoods.

## Results

Under a strict 10-second time limit, VP-OR is the only method that finds feasible solutions on all nine reoptimization datasets (Table 3). On the hardest datasets (bnd_2, bnd_3), VP-OR achieves average relative gaps of 0.11 and 0.06 respectively, while SCIP, ND, and PS fail to find any feasible solution. On rhs_1, VP-OR reduces the relative gap from 0.50 (SCIP) and 0.02 (Re_Tuning) to 0.00, and on obj_2 it reduces the absolute gap from 626.52 (SCIP) to 0.64. Across all datasets, VP-OR achieves the best gap_rel and the most 'wins' (closest-to-optimal solutions) in every comparison.

## Baselines & Comparisons

Against SCIP (the default solver), VP-OR dominates on every dataset: it reduces the relative gap from 0.16 to 0.02 on bnd_1, from 0.50 to 0.00 on rhs_1, and from 0.39 to 0.00 on obj_2, while being the only method to find any feasible solution on bnd_2 and bnd_3. Against Re_Tuning (the MIP Workshop 2023 winner), VP-OR wins on all nine datasets; the most dramatic gap is on bnd_2 and bnd_3 where Re_Tuning finds solutions on only 3/5 instances versus VP-OR's 5/5, and on rhs_1 where VP-OR achieves gap_rel 0.00 versus Re_Tuning's 0.02. Against the end-to-end ML baselines ND and PS, VP-OR is strictly superior: ND fails to find feasible solutions on 7 of 9 datasets, and PS produces a catastrophic gap_rel of 193.04 on rhs_1 (vs. VP-OR's 0.00) while also failing on bnd_2 and bnd_3. On the large-scale IS and CA benchmarks (Appendix E.10), VP-OR+Gurobi achieves gap_rel 0.00 with 88/100 wins on CA, outperforming ConPaS+Gurobi (gap_rel 0.02, 0/100 wins) and PS+Gurobi (gap_rel 0.03, 0/100 wins). Light-MILPopt, when its variable-fixing module is disabled, still fails on bnd_2 and bnd_3, whereas VP-OR solves all instances.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper sits at the intersection of two research threads: MILP reoptimization (Ralphs & Guzelsoy, Gamrath, Patel) and GNN-based end-to-end MILP solving (Nair/ND, Han/PS, Khalil/MIP-GNN). It departs from prior reoptimization work (notably Patel 2024) by moving beyond solver-level adjustments (reusing B&B trees, tuning parameters) to explicitly predict how variable values shift under parameter perturbations, leveraging historical branch-and-bound process information (leaf-node LP relaxations, dual solutions) as GNN features. It extends the GNN prediction line by handling integer and continuous variables (via logarithmic binary encoding) rather than only binary variables, and replaces the LNS or fixed-percentage fixing strategies of ND/PS with an iterative Thompson Sampling loop that adaptively selects which variables to constrain based on solution feedback.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The paper addresses a genuinely practical gap: time-critical reoptimization in logistics, scheduling, and production planning where solutions must be updated within seconds. The finding that VP-OR is the only method to find feasible solutions across all nine datasets within 10 seconds, with near-zero relative gaps on several, has direct operational relevance. However, the authors acknowledge the method can stall at suboptimal solutions over longer horizons, and the evaluation uses only 5 test instances per dataset, limiting statistical confidence. Open questions include scalability to ultra-large-scale instances (beyond ~13K variables tested), whether the Thompson Sampling component provides meaningful gains over simpler greedy fixing, and how the approach performs when perturbations are large rather than small.
