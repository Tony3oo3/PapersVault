## Problem

Existing pose-free view synthesis methods either rely on implicit NeRF representations with slow rendering or 3DGS-based approaches that still require accurate camera poses. The core difficulty with pixel-aligned 3D Gaussians in unposed settings is that misaligned Gaussian centers produce noisy or sparse gradients that destabilize training, making the problem nearly intractable without iterative scene-specific optimization or ground-truth supervision.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ICML20250926/cropped_page2_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method takes N sparse, unposed RGB images (N≥2) along with their camera intrinsics as input, with no ground-truth depth or pose required at either training or inference. It outputs photorealistic novel view renderings, per-pixel depth maps, refined camera poses (rotation + translation), and full pixel-aligned 3D Gaussian parameters (centers, opacities, covariances, and spherical harmonic colors). The method naturally extends beyond two views to N-view settings without retraining, and the target view is randomly sampled within a configurable frame distance range during training.

## Method

PF3plat employs a two-stage coarse-to-fine pipeline: first, pretrained monocular depth (UniDepth) and visual correspondence (LightGlue) networks provide initial depth and relative pose estimates via RANSAC; second, lightweight learnable modules refine depth (pixel-wise offsets via self-attention on depth network features) and pose (Plücker-coordinate-conditioned self/cross-attention producing 6D rotation and translation offsets). A geometry-aware confidence score is computed by aggregating a multi-view stereo cost volume with a monocular-guidance cost volume through cross-attention, then taking the max of the softmax matching distribution; this score conditions the prediction of Gaussian opacity, covariance, and color, creating a feedback loop that propagates supervision back to depth and pose. The training objective combines photometric losses (L2, SSIM, LPIPS) with two novel geometric consistency losses: a 2D-3D loss that projects Gaussian centers across views and penalizes reprojection error, and a symmetric 3D-3D loss that directly minimizes distances between corresponding Gaussian centers, both weighted by correspondence confidence.

## Results

On RealEstate-10K, PF3plat achieves 23.59 dB PSNR / 0.782 SSIM / 0.181 LPIPS (avg), outperforming the strongest pose-free baseline CoPoNeRF (19.54 dB) by 4.05 dB. On ACID, it reaches 25.64 dB PSNR / 0.784 SSIM, a 3.2 dB gain over CoPoNeRF (22.44 dB). On DL3DV, the improvement over CoPoNeRF widens to 4.3 dB (small overlap) and 5.1 dB (large overlap). For camera pose estimation, PF3plat achieves 1.76° average rotation error on RealEstate-10K, roughly half that of CoPoNeRF (3.61°).

## Baselines & Comparisons

PF3plat decisively outperforms all three pose-free baselines (DBARF, FlowCAM, CoPoNeRF) across every dataset and metric. On RealEstate-10K, it gains 4.05 dB PSNR over CoPoNeRF (23.59 vs 19.54) and 5.35 dB over FlowCAM; on ACID the margin is 3.2 dB over CoPoNeRF (25.64 vs 22.44). On DL3DV, the gap widens further: 4.3 dB (small) and 5.1 dB (large) over CoPoNeRF, confirming robustness to diverse real-world scenes. For pose estimation, PF3plat achieves 1.76°/9.47° (rot/trans) on RealEstate-10K, halving CoPoNeRF's rotation error (3.61°) and reducing translation error by 26%. Against scene-specific optimization, PF3plat surpasses InstantSplat in PSNR (23.59 vs 23.08) while being ~136× faster (0.39 s vs 53 s), and with test-time optimization reaches 24.69 dB in 24 s. The only comparison where PF3plat trails is pose estimation on ACID, where MASt3R (a dedicated SfM method with GT depth supervision) achieves 2.32° rotation error vs PF3plat's 2.69°, likely due to ACID's large-scale coastal and dynamic scenes challenging monocular depth estimation.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper bridges two previously separate lines of work: pose-free generalizable view synthesis (DBARF, FlowCAM, CoPoNeRF), which relied on implicit NeRF-based representations, and pixel-aligned 3D Gaussian Splatting (PixelSplat, MVSplat), which achieved fast rendering but required ground-truth camera poses. The key departure is making pixel-aligned 3DGS viable in a pose-free, single feed-forward pass by introducing a coarse-to-fine alignment pipeline (leveraging UniDepth and LightGlue) and a geometry-aware confidence mechanism that conditions Gaussian parameter prediction on localization reliability. Unlike scene-specific 3DGS methods (InstantSplat, CF-3DGS) that iteratively refine at inference time, PF3plat achieves comparable or better quality in a single forward pass.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

PF3plat demonstrates that explicit 3D Gaussian representations can match or exceed implicit NeRF-based methods in pose-free settings while offering ~40× faster rendering (0.39s vs 17.29s for CoPoNeRF), making pose-free view synthesis practically deployable. The geometry-aware confidence mechanism—using aggregated multi-view and monocular depth to gate Gaussian parameter prediction—is a reusable design pattern for any explicit-representation method facing localization uncertainty. Key limitations include the requirement for camera intrinsics, inability to handle dynamic scenes, and sensitivity to coarse alignment quality; open directions include joint intrinsic-extrinsic estimation, 4D scene modeling, and scaling to more diverse training data.
