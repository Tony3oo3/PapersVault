## Problem

Existing GUI agents rely on platform-specific textual representations (HTML, accessibility trees) that grow with interface complexity, use heterogeneous action spaces that prevent cross-environment learning, and depend on closed-source LLMs for reasoning. No prior work provides a fully open-source, pure-vision GUI agent that unifies observation and action spaces across web, mobile, and desktop platforms while incorporating structured reasoning for complex multi-step tasks.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ICML20252788/cropped_page1_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The agent takes a raw GUI screenshot (720p, yielding a constant 1,196 visual tokens regardless of interface complexity, versus 4k–6k tokens for HTML-based methods) and a natural-language task instruction. At each step it produces a two-part output: an inner monologue consisting of a thought (high-level reasoning about the current state relative to the goal) and a low-level instruction (a natural-language description of the next atomic action), followed by concrete pyautogui action commands (e.g., pyautogui.click, pyautogui.write) plus pluggable platform-specific functions (e.g., mobile.swipe, mobile.open_app).

## Method

AGUVIS builds on Qwen2-VL (NaViT dynamic-resolution encoder with 2D-RoPE position embeddings) and introduces a unified action space of standard pyautogui commands extended by a plugin system for platform-specific interactions. Training is split into two stages: Stage 1 (grounding) fine-tunes on 1.036M single-step instruction-action pairs across web, mobile, and desktop using a grounding-packing strategy that bundles multiple pairs per screenshot to reduce GPU hours from 6 to 1; Stage 2 (planning & reasoning) fine-tunes on 35K multi-step trajectories whose inner monologue (thoughts + low-level instructions) is augmented by GPT-4o prompted to generate predictive rather than post-hoc reasoning, using a reasoning-mixture schedule that varies the depth of cognitive complexity exposed to the model. A special <|recipient|> token with 'os' or 'all' tags controls whether the model emits only an action or a full reasoning-then-action sequence, enabling both self-planning and enforced-planning modes at inference.

## Results

On ScreenSpot, AGUVIS-72B achieves a state-of-the-art average grounding score of 89.2%, surpassing the previous best (UGround at 73.3%) by 15.9 points, while AGUVIS-7B reaches 84.4%. On Multimodal-Mind2Web, AGUVIS-72B attains step success rates of 64.0/56.5/58.2% (Cross-Task/Cross-Website/Cross-Domain), a +51.9% average improvement over prior vision-based methods. On the online Mind2Web-Live benchmark, AGUVIS-72B achieves 27.1% task success rate at a cost of $0.012 per successful step—93% cheaper than GPT-4o-based approaches. On OSWorld, AGUVIS-72B paired with GPT-4o reaches 17.04% task success, outperforming SoM-based approaches (4.59%) and Claude Computer-Use (14.9%).

## Baselines & Comparisons

AGUVIS decisively outperforms all prior grounding methods on ScreenSpot: AGUVIS-7B (84.4 avg) surpasses UGround (73.3), SeeClick (53.4), CogAgent (47.4), and Qwen2-VL (55.3) by 11.1, 31.0, 37.0, and 29.1 points respectively, and exceeds GPT-4o (18.3) by 66.1 points. On Multimodal-Mind2Web, AGUVIS-7B achieves 60.4% step success rate (Cross-Task), beating GPT-4V+OmniParser (39.4%) and GPT-4o+UGround (not fully reported but element accuracy 47.7%) by over 20 points. On AndroidControl, AGUVIS-7B (61.5/80.5 high/low-level) outperforms GPT-4o+UGround (48.4/62.4) by 13.1/18.1 points. On the online Mind2Web-Live benchmark, AGUVIS-72B (27.1% SR) beats GPT-4o+UGround (19.2%) and all HTML-based baselines (≤24.0%), while on OSWorld, AGUVIS-72B+GPT-4o (17.04%) surpasses SoM (4.59%) and Claude Computer-Use (14.9%), though it trails OpenAI Operator (19.7%). The only setting where AGUVIS does not lead is AndroidWorld, where AGUVIS-72B (26.1%) falls below GPT-4o+UGround (32.8%), suggesting room for improvement in mobile online planning.

## Relation to Prior Work

AGUVIS builds on Qwen2-VL's NaViT-style dynamic-resolution encoder and 2D-RoPE positional embeddings as its architectural foundation, and adapts the inner monologue concept from embodied reasoning (Huang et al., 2022) to the GUI domain. It departs from the dominant line of GUI agent work (Mind2Web, CogAgent, SeeClick, UGround) in two key ways: it replaces platform-specific textual observations (HTML, accessibility trees) with pure visual input, and it eliminates reliance on closed-source LLMs for planning by training open-source VLMs with native structured reasoning via a two-stage pipeline (grounding → planning). The standardized pyautogui action space with a plugin system is a unifying design choice that prior cross-platform agents (e.g., OmniACT, CRAB) have not fully exploited for end-to-end training.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

AGUVIS is the first fully autonomous, open-source, vision-only GUI agent that achieves state-of-the-art results across web, mobile, and desktop benchmarks without any closed-source model in the loop, and it does so at roughly 93% lower inference cost than GPT-4o-based pipelines. The open-sourcing of the 1.036M-sample grounding dataset, 35K planning trajectories, models, and training recipes provides a concrete foundation for the community to iterate on GUI agent capabilities. Key open questions remain: the model cannot express uncertainty or refuse ambiguous instructions (40% of self-plan errors), grounding errors still account for 60% of failures, and generalization to truly novel desktop applications (OSWorld: 17% SR) is far from reliable. The paper itself flags adversarial safety training and dynamic planning-depth selection as necessary next steps before real-world deployment.
