## Problem

Standard primal-dual methods for risk-neutral CMDPs rely on strong duality and a linear value function in the occupancy measure, both of which fail when the constraint involves a nonlinear entropic risk measure. Moreover, the exponential Bellman equation for the composite state-action value function is invalid, and Markovian policies may be suboptimal. This paper is the first to establish sublinear regret and constraint violation bounds for online learning in a CMDP with an entropic risk-sensitive constraint.

## Input / Output

The algorithm operates in a model-free, episodic setting over a tabular MDP with finite state space S, action space A, and horizon H, where the agent only observes rewards and utilities at visited state-action pairs. The continuous budget variable c_h ∈ [-H, H] is discretized at resolution ε₀ = K^{-1/2}, yielding |C| = ⌈2H/ε₀⌉ budget levels that the policy conditions on. The output is a sequence of policies π^k that are Markovian over the discretized augmented state (s, ĉ) but history-dependent in the original state space, along with high-probability regret and violation bounds scaling as Õ(K^{3/4}).

## Method

The key reformulation uses the Optimized Certainty Equivalent (OCE) representation to express the entropic risk as max_τ (τ + E[u(G(π) − τ)]), then augments the state space with a budget variable c_h = τ − Σ_{h'<h} g_{h'} that tracks remaining utility. This shifts the nonlinear entropic risk to a terminal cost u(−c_{H+1}), making the augmented value function additive and amenable to standard Bellman-based value iteration. A primal-dual algorithm then alternates between: (i) solving the inner maximization via optimistic value iteration over the augmented state space for a fixed λ and τ, (ii) selecting the optimal initial budget τ̂_k by maximizing the composite value, and (iii) updating the dual variable via a gradient step truncated at threshold ξ = K^{1/4}. The truncation is essential because the value function is non-convex in the augmented occupancy measure, so strong duality may not hold and unbounded λ updates would not guarantee bounded violation.

## Results

The paper establishes the first sublinear regret and violation bounds for risk-sensitive CMDPs: Regret(K) ≤ Õ(V_{g,max} K^{3/4} + √(H⁴S²A log(1/δ)) K^{3/4}) and Violation(K) ≤ Õ(V_{g,max} √(H³S²A log(1/δ)) K^{3/4}), where V_{g,max} = (exp(|α|H)−1)/|α|. Empirically, on a 5×5 Grid-World with K=15,000 episodes, the algorithm achieves reward values of 2.80 (α=−10⁻⁴, B=2.2) down to 1.88 (α=−10⁻⁴, B=2.9), while maintaining the entropic risk measure at or above the threshold B. The K^{3/4} scaling (vs. K^{1/2} in risk-neutral CMDPs) reflects the cost of the discretized augmented state space and the truncated dual update needed to handle the absence of strong duality.

## Baselines & Comparisons

This paper does not compare against direct empirical baselines, as it is the first work to address the risk-sensitive CMDP problem with entropic risk constraints. Instead, the authors contextualize their theoretical bounds relative to existing results in adjacent settings. Compared to the risk-neutral CMDP setting (Ghosh et al., 2022; Efroni et al., 2020), which achieves Õ(√K) regret and violation, the present work incurs an additional K^{1/4} factor due to the discretized augmented state space and the truncated dual update. Relative to the unconstrained risk-sensitive MDP (Fei et al., 2021), which has a tight O(V_{g,max}√(H²S²AK log(1/δ))) regret bound, the constrained setting introduces an additional √H·K^{1/4} factor in the violation bound. The empirical Grid-World experiments (Figure 1, Table 1) demonstrate that the algorithm converges to feasible policies satisfying the risk constraint, with the dual variable λ increasing rapidly early on and stabilizing once feasibility is achieved. The authors note that for larger |α| and B, the algorithm may fail to find feasible policies, and the growing V_{g,max} necessitates more episodes for reliable evaluation.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the augmented-state-space technique (originally developed for CVaR and spectral risk measures by Bauerle & Ott, 2011, and recently generalized to OCE-based risk measures by Wang et al., 2024) to the constrained MDP setting, where it had not previously been applied. It adapts the primal-dual online learning framework for risk-neutral CMDPs (Ghosh et al., 2022; Efroni et al., 2020; Ding et al., 2021) but must fundamentally depart from it: the exponential Bellman equation for the composite value function is invalid, strong duality may fail, and Markov policies can be suboptimal. The OCE representation from Ben-Tal & Teboulle (2007) provides the key reformulation that makes the augmented formulation possible, and the truncated dual update is a new mechanism introduced specifically to handle the absence of strong duality.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

This is the first work to establish sublinear regret and constraint violation bounds for online learning in CMDPs with entropic risk-sensitive constraints, opening a previously unexplored intersection of risk-sensitive RL and constrained RL. The K^{3/4} rate (versus K^{1/2} for risk-neutral CMDPs) reflects the genuine additional difficulty, and the demonstration that Markov policies can be suboptimal under entropic risk constraints challenges a standard assumption in the CMDP literature. Key open questions include whether tighter violation bounds are achievable, whether the analysis extends to infinite state spaces or unknown reward/utility distributions, and whether no-cancellation violation guarantees (as in Ghosh et al., 2024) can be established. The practical relevance to safety-critical domains (healthcare, autonomous driving, finance) is clear, but the current empirical validation is limited to a small 5×5 grid-world.
