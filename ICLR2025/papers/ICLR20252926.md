## Problem

Generating high-quality 360° panoramas from text or image prompts is challenging because panoramic data is scarce, equirectangular representations introduce severe polar distortions incompatible with standard image models, and autoregressive outpainting suffers from content drift and the Janus effect. Prior methods require complex architectural additions (correspondence-aware attention, circular blending, state-space models) to enforce wrap-around consistency and semantic coherence, limiting generalization and increasing brittleness.

## Input / Output

The method accepts a text prompt (either a single global description or per-face captions for fine-grained control) and optionally one conditional perspective image (a single cubemap face, e.g., the front view). It outputs a full 360°×180° panorama as a cubemap of six 90°-FOV perspective images, each generated in a 128×128×8 latent space. The conditional image is provided as clean VAE latents concatenated with the noisy target latents, with a binary mask channel distinguishing conditioned from unconditioned faces.

## Method

CubeDiff represents the panorama as a cubemap and fine-tunes a pretrained Stable Diffusion LDM by inflating all self-attention and cross-attention layers from b×(hw)×l to b×(6hw)×l, enabling cross-face token interaction while retaining the original pretrained weights. A synchronized GroupNorm in the VAE jointly normalizes features across spatial and inter-view dimensions to prevent color shifts between faces, and 3D-derived UV positional encodings are concatenated to the latents for explicit spatial awareness. Each face is generated with a 95° effective FoV (2.5° overlap on each side) to produce seamless boundaries without explicit blending. Training uses v-prediction with classifier-free guidance on both text and image conditions, and only the inflated attention layers are updated, preserving the internet-scale image prior of the base model.

## Results

On Laval Indoor, CubeDiff (image+text) achieves a FID of 9.5, KID of 0.32×10⁻², CLIP-FID of 3.2, and CLIP Score of 27.02, outperforming the second-best method MVDiffusion (FID 25.7) by a 270% relative margin. On SUN360, the best CubeDiff variant (image+multi-text) reaches FID 24.1, KID 1.33×10⁻², CLIP-FID 7.0, FAED 5.7, and CLIP Score 28.14, again leading all competitors. The method also achieves the lowest FAED (18.4 on Laval, 5.7 on SUN360), indicating superior geometric consistency with the conditioning image.

## Baselines & Comparisons

CubeDiff outperforms all six baselines on every reported metric across both Laval Indoor and SUN360. Against the closest competitor MVDiffusion (FID 25.7 on Laval), CubeDiff (img+txt) achieves FID 9.5, a 270% relative improvement, and also leads on KID (0.32 vs. 1.11) and CLIP-FID (3.2 vs. 13.5). The autoregressive outpainting methods OmniDreamer and PanoDiffusion show the weakest perceptual quality (FID 71.0 and 58.6 on Laval), while the equirectangular-based Diffusion360 and text-only Text2Light/PanFusion also trail by large margins (FID 33.1, 28.3, 41.7 respectively). In the user study, CubeDiff's no-text variant was preferred by 19.5% of participants—nearly matching the ground-truth preference rate of 19.9%—compared to 12.3% for Diffusion360, 7.0% for MVDiffusion, 5.3% for PanoDiffusion, and 1.7% for OmniDreamer. Notably, CubeDiff is the only method that supports per-face fine-grained text conditioning, a capability none of the baselines offer.

## Relation to Prior Work

CubeDiff repurposes the multi-view diffusion paradigm (CAT3D, MVDream) by turning it 'inside-out': instead of generating multiple views of a 3D object, it generates the six fixed-geometry faces of a cubemap panorama. It departs from prior panorama generators (Diffusion360, PanoDiffusion, OmniDreamer) that operate in equirectangular space or use autoregressive outpainting, and from MVDiffusion's correspondence-aware cross-attention, by showing that a simple one-dimensional inflation of all attention layers suffices for cross-face consistency when the viewing geometry is fixed. The base architecture is a standard latent diffusion model (Stable Diffusion-style), fine-tuned with synchronized GroupNorm in the VAE and 3D-derived positional encodings.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The practical recipe—inflate attention, add positional encodings, fine-tune on cubemaps—makes high-quality panorama generation accessible by leveraging the internet-scale prior of existing T2I models, reducing the data-hunger and architectural complexity that plagued prior methods. The per-face text control is a genuinely new capability not offered by any competitor. Open questions include whether the approach scales to higher resolutions without additional tiling strategies, how it handles scenes with strong perspective distortion at the 90° face boundaries, and whether the fixed cubemap geometry limits applicability to non-cubic or non-360°×180° viewing frusta.
