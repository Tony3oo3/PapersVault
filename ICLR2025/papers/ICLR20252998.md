## Problem

Vision Transformers achieve strong performance but suffer from quadratic attention complexity, making high-resolution image processing and long-sequence analysis prohibitively expensive. While linear-attention mechanisms like RWKV and Mamba have shown promise in NLP, adapting them to vision faces unresolved challenges: causal attention is ill-suited to 2D spatial data, prior vision linear-attention models (e.g., Vision Mamba) fail to scale beyond ~30M parameters due to training instability, and local spatial priors are absent.

## Input / Output

The model consumes RGB images of arbitrary resolution (evaluated from 224×224 up to 2048×2048), converts them into HW/p² patches via linear projection plus positional embedding, and processes them through L encoder layers that maintain the input spatial resolution throughout. Outputs are dense feature maps consumed by task-specific heads (classification, Mask R-CNN for detection, UperNet for segmentation). A key design property is that the architecture handles sparse inputs (e.g., masked images for MAE pre-training) by switching Q-Shift to a single-dimension variant, and requires no window partitioning at any resolution.

## Method

VRWKV replaces ViT's quadratic self-attention with Bi-WKV, a bidirectional linear-complexity attention derived from RWKV: the causal mask is removed so all tokens attend globally, the positional bias becomes relative (|t−i|−1)/T to be resolution-agnostic, and the decay vector is allowed to take negative values so attention can focus on distant tokens. The RNN-form recurrence maintains four hidden states (a, b, c, d) updated in O(C) per step, yielding O(TC) total complexity. A quad-directional token shift (Q-Shift) interpolates each token with its four spatial neighbors using learnable per-channel weights, injecting local spatial priors without additional FLOPs. For stable scaling to 335M parameters, the authors introduce bounded exponentials (dividing the decay by T), extra layer normalizations after attention and activation, and layer scale, enabling reliable training on ImageNet-22K at 384×384 resolution.

## Results

On ImageNet-1K, VRWKV-T (6.2M params) achieves 75.1% top-1 accuracy, surpassing DeiT-T (72.2%) by 2.9 points with lower FLOPs (1.2G vs 1.3G). At the large scale, VRWKV-L (335M) reaches 86.0% top-1, outperforming ViT-L (85.2%) by 0.8 points. On COCO detection, VRWKV-L achieves 50.6 box mAP (vs 48.7 for ViT-L with window attention), and on ADE20K segmentation, VRWKV-L attains 53.5 mIoU (vs 53.4 for ViT-L), all with 5–30% lower backbone FLOPs.

## Baselines & Comparisons

VRWKV consistently outperforms DeiT (Touvron et al., 2021a) across all sizes: +2.9 points at Tiny, +0.2 at Small, +0.2 at Base, and +0.8 at Large on ImageNet-1K top-1 accuracy, while using equal or fewer FLOPs. Against ViT-L (Dosovitskiy et al., 2020), VRWKV-L achieves 86.0% vs 85.2% top-1 and 50.6 vs 48.7 box mAP on COCO, with ~3% lower FLOPs. On COCO detection, VRWKV-T/S/B match or exceed ViT with global attention (e.g., VRWKV-S: 44.8 AP^b vs ViT-S global: 44.9) while using 30–45% fewer FLOPs than window-based ViT. On ADE20K segmentation, VRWKV beats global-attention ViT at every scale (e.g., VRWKV-S: 47.2 vs 46.2 mIoU). Against hierarchical baselines (ResNet, Swin, PVT), VRWKV-S/B/L surpass Swin-T/S/B in top-1 accuracy with comparable or fewer parameters. Compared to Vision Mamba (Zhu et al., 2024), VRWKV is slightly behind at Tiny/Small (75.1 vs 76.1, 80.1 vs 80.5) but matches at Base (82.0 vs 81.9) and uniquely scales to 335M parameters where Vim is unavailable. VRWKV also outperforms XCiT at comparable parameter counts (e.g., VRWKV-B: 82.0% vs XCiT-S12: 82.0% at fewer params, and VRWKV-L: 86.0% vs XCiT-L24: 82.9% at similar FLOPs).

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper adapts the RWKV linear attention mechanism from NLP (Peng et al., 2023) to vision by replacing its causal, single-directional attention with a bidirectional global attention (Bi-WKV) and introducing a quad-directional token shift (Q-Shift) tailored to 2D spatial structure. It retains the ViT (Dosovitskiy et al., 2020) block-stacked encoder design with patch embedding and spatial/channel-mix modules, but swaps the quadratic self-attention for the linear-complexity Bi-WKV. The work departs from prior linear-attention vision models (Vision Mamba, VMamba) that were limited to small parameter counts (<30M–100M) by introducing bounded exponentials, extra layer normalizations, and layer scale to achieve stable training at 335M parameters.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

VRWKV demonstrates that linear-complexity attention can now scale to ViT-Large size and match or slightly exceed ViT accuracy while delivering 10× faster inference and 80% lower GPU memory at 2048×2048 resolution, making high-resolution dense prediction (detection, segmentation) practically feasible without window operations. The robustness to test-time resolution changes (VRWKV-B retains 67.2% at 1024×1024 vs. DeiT-B's 57.5%) suggests a fundamentally different inductive bias for spatial generalization. Open questions include whether the Q-Shift operation can be further optimized in CUDA (current PyTorch implementation is a bottleneck), whether the approach extends to video or 3D data, and whether the modest accuracy gains over ViT persist at even larger scales or with more aggressive data regimes.
