## Problem

Tight characterizations of the hardness of privately answering linear queries under differential privacy were previously known only for the ℓ₂² error metric (Edmonds et al., 2020) and for the ℓ_p² metric restricted to unbiased mechanisms in the substitution model (Nikolov & Tang, 2024). This paper introduces the more natural ℓ_p^p error metric (for p ≥ 2), which interpolates between squared error and absolute error, and establishes that the matrix mechanism is optimal among all (ε,δ)-DP mechanisms under this metric in the standard add/remove privacy model, up to logarithmic factors.

## Input / Output

The method takes a workload matrix A ∈ R^{m×n} encoding m linear queries, a dataset x ∈ R^n (with neighboring datasets defined by ‖x−x′‖₁ ≤ 1), and privacy parameters (ε, δ). It produces a differentially private mechanism M: R^n → R^m answering all m queries simultaneously. The key quantitative output is the ℓ_p^p error, defined as max_x (E[‖M(x) − Ax‖_p^p])^{1/p}, which the paper characterizes from below by the factorization norm γ_(p)(A) and from above by the matrix mechanism's error, yielding tight bounds for prefix sum and parity query workloads.

## Method

The lower bound is established in two stages: first, for additive noise mechanisms M(x) = Ax + z with oblivious z, the paper proves that the ℓ_p^p error is Ω(γ_(p)(A)/ε) by connecting the p-trace of the covariance matrix to the width of the sensitivity polytope AB₁^n via the Hammersley-Chapman-Robins bound and χ²-divergence control. A key technical departure from prior work is handling biased mechanisms: the paper shows that large bias already implies large error (via Hölder's inequality), then handles the small-bias case where the minimum width κ(A) of the sensitivity polytope appears only in the applicable range of δ rather than in the bound itself. The full-rank assumption is removed in the high-privacy regime (ε < 1/2) using a variance lower bound from Kasiviswanathan et al. (2010), and the Bhaskara et al. (2012) reduction extends the result from additive noise to arbitrary (ε,δ)-DP mechanisms. The matching upper bound instantiates the matrix mechanism M(x) = L(Rx + z) with z ~ N(0, ‖R‖_{1→2}² I_k), where LR = A is chosen to minimize γ_(p)(A), achieving error O(γ_(p)(A) · √(log(1/δ)·min{p, log(2m)})/ε).

## Results

The paper establishes that the matrix mechanism is near-optimal (up to polylog factors) for answering linear queries under (ε,δ)-DP with the ℓ_p^p error metric for all p ≥ 2. For prefix sum queries, the ℓ_p^p error is Θ_{ε,δ}(n^{1/p} log n) for all constant p, generalizing the p=2 result of Henzinger et al. (2023). For parity queries with m = C(d,w) queries, the lower bound is Ω_{ε,δ}(m^{1/2+1/p}), which is tight when min{p, log m} = O(1). The general lower bound (Theorem 1.4) states err_{ℓ_p^p}(M, A) ≥ (1-δ̃)γ_(p)(A)/(C'ε), where γ_(p)(A) is a generalized factorization norm, and the matching upper bound for the matrix mechanism is O(γ_(p)(A) · √(log(1/δ)·min{p, log(2m)}/ε²)).

## Baselines & Comparisons

Compared to Edmonds et al. (2020), which characterized the ℓ_2² error under the add/remove model, this paper extends the characterization to all p ≥ 2 under the more natural ℓ_p^p metric, and improves the lower bound by a √(log m) factor over discrepancy-based bounds (Muthukrishnan & Nikolov, 2012). Compared to Nikolov & Tang (2024), which proved instance-optimality of the matrix mechanism for unbiased mean estimation under the substitution model with an ℓ_p² metric, this paper works under the stronger add/remove model, does not require unbiasedness, and uses the more natural ℓ_p^p metric that captures the p-th moment of the error vector. For prefix sum queries, the paper recovers and generalizes the tight Θ(n log n) bound of Henzinger et al. (2023) from p=2 to all constant p, and extends the lower bound to larger δ values beyond the packing argument of Dwork et al. (2010). The key technical departure is that the lower bound does not depend on the minimum width κ(A) of the sensitivity polytope in the bound itself (only in the applicable range of δ), making it non-vacuous even when κ(A) = O(1/n), whereas Nikolov & Tang (2024) would give only an inverse-dimension bound in that regime.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the line of work on optimality of the matrix mechanism for privately answering linear queries, building directly on Edmonds et al. (2020) who established tight bounds under the ℓ₂² metric, and Nikolov & Tang (2024) who proved instance optimality for unbiased mean estimation under the ℓ_p² metric in the substitution model. The key departures are threefold: (1) a new ℓ_p^p error metric that more naturally captures the p-th moment of per-query errors, (2) the stronger add/remove privacy model rather than the substitution model, and (3) no unbiasedness assumption on the mechanism. The Bhaskara et al. (2012) reduction from general mechanisms to additive noise mechanisms and the χ²-divergence/Hammersley-Chapman-Robbins toolkit from Kasiviswanathan et al. (2010) and Nikolov & Tang (2024) provide the technical backbone for the lower bound proofs.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

This work completes a natural gap in the DP theory landscape by showing the matrix mechanism is near-optimal (up to polylog factors) under a more natural error metric for all p ≥ 2, in the standard add/remove model, without restricting to unbiased mechanisms. The tight characterization of prefix sum and parity query errors for all constant p has direct implications for private learning and hardness results. A key open question the paper identifies is whether an instance-optimal (asymmetric) lower bound exists for general mechanisms, analogous to what Nikolov & Tang proved for unbiased ones; the current result is worst-case over inputs. The gap between upper and lower bounds for p = ω(1) (a √log(n) factor for prefix sum) also remains open.
