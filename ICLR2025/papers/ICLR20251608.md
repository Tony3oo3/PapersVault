## Problem

Generating view-consistent 3D sketches from flexible inputs such as a single image or text prompt remains largely unexplored. Existing 3D sketch creation tools require professional artistic skills in immersive environments, while the closest reconstruction methods (3Doodle, NEF) depend on multi-view supervision and approximate perspective projection as orthographic, ignoring depth ordering between curves. This limits their ability to function as a true generation pipeline and causes color conflicts in more complex tasks like colored sketch generation.

## Input / Output

The method accepts either a free-form text prompt or a single reference RGB image as input and produces a view-consistent 3D sketch represented as a set of 3D cubic rational Bezier curves (default 56 curves, with control point positions as the optimized parameters). The output is a parametric curve representation saved in STEP format that can be rendered from arbitrary viewpoints, with camera parameters sampled at radius 1.8–2.0, azimuth ±180°, elevation 0–30°, and 60° FOV. Training takes approximately 1 hour (text-to-3D) or 2 hours (image-to-3D) on a single NVIDIA A10 GPU with 4000 optimization steps.

## Method

Diff3DS represents the 3D sketch as 3D rational Bezier curves and performs exact perspective projection (exploiting projective invariance of rational curves) to obtain 2D rational Bezier curves, unlike 3Doodle which approximates this as orthographic. A customized differentiable rasterizer built on DiffVG then renders the projected curves using per-point depth ordering (computed by unprojecting 2D points back to 3D) rather than a uniform per-curve order, enabling accurate occlusion and alpha blending for colored curves. Gradients are computed via Monte Carlo sampling and the Reynolds transport theorem, back-propagated through the projection to update 3D control points. Optimization is driven by Score Distillation Sampling from pre-trained diffusion models (Stable Diffusion 2.1 + MVDream for text; Zero-1-to-3 + Stable-Zero123 for image), augmented with a Time Annealing Schedule that progressively narrows the noise timestep range and a Dynamic Noise Deletion strategy that removes degenerate curves whose discretized length falls below a threshold.

## Results

On the text-to-3D sketch task, Diff3DS achieves CLIP-ScoreT of 0.3046 (ViT B/16) and 0.3034 (ViT B/32), and BLIP-Score of 0.5038, outperforming DreamGaussian (0.2561/0.2453/0.4990) and MVDream (0.2763/0.2653/0.4949). On the image-to-3D sketch task, Diff3DS achieves CLIP-ScoreI of 0.6768 (ViT B/16) and 0.6846 (ViT B/32), surpassing both 3Doodle (0.6734/0.6746) and NEF (0.6612/0.6605), while its LPIPS of 0.2647 is comparable to 3Doodle's 0.2567 and significantly better than NEF's 0.3997. A user study with 40 participants (1000 valid scores) further confirms Diff3DS's superiority in both image fidelity and 3D consistency.

## Baselines & Comparisons

For text-to-3D sketch, Diff3DS outperforms both DreamGaussian and MVDream across all metrics (CLIP-ScoreT and BLIP-Score), with the largest margin over DreamGaussian (~0.05 on CLIP-ScoreT ViT B/16). Notably, the baselines fail to produce sketch-style outputs even when prompted with 'sketch in black and white, line drawing' suffixes. For image-to-3D sketch, Diff3DS surpasses 3Doodle on CLIP-ScoreI (0.6768 vs. 0.6734 for ViT B/16; 0.6846 vs. 0.6746 for ViT B/32) while achieving a comparable LPIPS (0.2647 vs. 0.2567), despite 3Doodle having access to additional losses (LPIPS and CLIP) on the reference view. Diff3DS significantly outperforms NEF on LPIPS (0.2647 vs. 0.3997), reflecting NEF's inability to generalize to novel views from a single input image. The key advantage of Diff3DS over 3Doodle is that it operates as a generation pipeline from a single image or text, whereas 3Doodle requires multi-view supervision and is more akin to a reconstruction method.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the differentiable vector graphics rasterization framework of DiffVG (Li et al., 2020) to the 3D domain by performing true perspective projection of 3D rational Bézier curves and designing a depth-aware rasterizer that preserves occlusion ordering between curve primitives. It departs from the closest prior work, 3Doodle (Choi et al., 2024), which approximates perspective projection as orthographic and assigns a uniform per-curve depth order, by instead computing per-point depth from the 3D control points. For the generation objective, the paper integrates Score Distillation Sampling (DreamFusion, Poole et al., 2023) and image-conditioned SDS (Zero-1-to-3, Liu et al., 2023) to optimize 3D curve parameters from text or single-image inputs, rather than requiring multi-view supervision as in 3Doodle or NEF.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

This is the first framework to generate view-consistent 3D sketches from flexible inputs (text or a single image), opening a path toward user-friendly 3D sketch creation without professional artistic skill. The depth-aware rasterizer also enables colored 3D sketches, which prior differentiable curve renderers could not handle due to color conflicts. Key limitations include inheriting DiffVG's sparse gradient issue, requiring manual specification of initial curve count, and not distinguishing view-independent (feature lines) from view-dependent (silhouette) curves, which constrains expressive capacity. Future directions include scene-level sketch generation and incorporating diverse curve representations from 3Doodle.
