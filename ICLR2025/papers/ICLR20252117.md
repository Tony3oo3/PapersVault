## Problem

Existing driving-scene novel view synthesis methods (NeRF, 3D Gaussian Splatting) render high-quality views only along the recorded ego-vehicle trajectory; when the camera moves to novel viewpoints outside that trajectory, rendering quality degrades drastically because the corresponding camera rays are untrained. This limits their utility for closed-loop embodied simulation where the agent must perceive from arbitrary viewpoints. The paper also notes that no standard benchmark exists for evaluating NVS on truly novel trajectories where ground-truth images are unavailable.

## Input / Output

At inference, FreeVS takes a colored LiDAR point cloud (accumulated across ±2 adjacent frames, with moving-object points merged along their 3D bounding-box trajectories) and a sequence of target camera poses (intrinsics + extrinsics for up to 5 surrounding cameras). The point cloud is projected into pseudo-images at the target viewpoints, which serve as the sole 3D prior condition. The model outputs 8-frame video sequences at 1248×832 resolution (sky regions clipped). No per-scene reconstruction is required; the method is applied directly to any validation sequence using only the LiDAR observations and target poses.

## Method

FreeVS is a conditional video diffusion model (initialized from Stable Video Diffusion) that synthesizes target views from pseudo-images — 2D projections of colored LiDAR point clouds into the target camera viewpoint. The pseudo-image representation unifies color, 3D geometry, and camera pose into a single image modality, reducing the generative task to sparse-pixel image completion rather than explicit 3D-aware view transformation. A ConvNeXt-T encoder maps pseudo-images to latent space, which is concatenated with noised target-view latents (encoded by a frozen VAE) as input to the denoising network; CLIP text embeddings of reference views provide additional conditioning. A key training technique, viewpoint transformation simulation, samples LiDAR observations from temporally mismatched frames (±4) to simulate lateral/longitudinal camera movement, bridging the train-test gap for novel trajectories. At inference, classifier-free guidance scale is increased to 2.0 to strengthen 3D prior control over generated views.

## Results

On the novel trajectory synthesis benchmark (the paper's key contribution), FreeVS achieves a FID of 11.18 at the original trajectory and 22.08 at a 4.0 m lateral offset, substantially outperforming 3D-GS (34.79 / 86.21), EmerNerf (53.88 / 84.81), and StreetGaussian (21.62 / 80.44). In terms of 3D geometry consistency measured by LET-mAP on the vehicle class, FreeVS reaches 0.612 at 4.0 m offset versus 0.452 (3D-GS), 0.464 (EmerNerf), and 0.544 (StreetGaussian). On the novel camera synthesis benchmark, FreeVS attains SSIM 0.628, PSNR 20.70, and LPIPS 0.283, leading all baselines by a clear margin. On the traditional multi-view novel frame synthesis setting, FreeVS also wins with SSIM 0.730 and LPIPS 0.179, while requiring no per-sequence reconstruction (0 s recon time vs. 1–3 h for reconstruction-based methods).

## Baselines & Comparisons

FreeVS outperforms all six baselines on the novel trajectory synthesis benchmark across every lateral offset (1 m, 2 m, 4 m) in both FID and LET-mAP. Against 3D-GS (Kerbl et al., 2023), FreeVS reduces FID from 86.21 to 22.08 at 4 m offset and improves mAP^LET from 0.452 to 0.612; against EmerNerf (Yang et al., 2023a), the FID gap is even wider (84.81 → 22.08). StreetGaussian (Yan et al., 2024) is the closest competitor on FID at 0 m offset (21.62 vs. 11.18) and on mAP at 0 m (0.826 vs. 0.816), but degrades sharply at larger offsets. On the novel camera synthesis benchmark, FreeVS leads all three main baselines (3D-GS, EmerNerf, StreetGaussian) on SSIM, PSNR, and LPIPS. In the appendix, FreeVS also beats UC-NeRF (Cheng et al., 2023) on novel trajectories (FID 15.17 vs. 42.08 at 1 m; mAP 0.781 vs. 0.592) and outperforms PVG (Chen et al., 2023) on nuScenes (FID 35.32 vs. 65.08 at 0 m). On the XLD dataset, FreeVS surpasses MARS (Wu et al., 2023c) and all other baselines on PSNR/SSIM/LPIPS at 2 m and 4 m offsets, though reconstruction-based methods retain a slight PSNR/SSIM edge at 0 m offset due to overfitting on training views.

## Relation to Prior Work

FreeVS departs from the dominant reconstruction-based paradigm for driving NVS (NeRF, 3DGS, EmerNeRF, Street Gaussians) by adopting a fully generative approach built on Stable Video Diffusion, eliminating the per-scene reconstruction step entirely. It also differentiates from prior generative NVS methods (Zero-1-to-3, GeNVS, SGD) through its pseudo-image representation, which unifies color, 3D geometry, and camera pose into a single 2D image modality rather than feeding them as separate conditions. The viewpoint transformation simulation—a data augmentation that mismatches LiDAR observations with target frames to simulate lateral camera movement—addresses a training-inference gap that prior generative NVS works for driving scenes (e.g., SGD) do not tackle, since they remain confined to the recorded trajectory.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

This work addresses a practically critical gap: existing driving NVS methods degrade severely outside the recorded ego trajectory, which is precisely where closed-loop simulation and safety-critical testing require view synthesis. The two proposed benchmarks (novel camera synthesis and novel trajectory synthesis with perceptual robustness evaluation via 3D detectors) provide more meaningful evaluation protocols than the conventional novel-frame-sampling approach, and are likely to be adopted by the community. Key limitations include the 0.9 FPS rendering speed (constrained by video diffusion), dependence on LiDAR inputs (though pseudo-LiDAR from depth estimation partially mitigates this), and difficulty with non-rigid dynamic objects like pedestrians. Open questions include whether the pseudo-image formulation generalizes to scenes without LiDAR, whether the approach scales to longer sequences, and whether the low inference speed can be improved without sacrificing 3D consistency.
