## Problem

Estimating individual counterfactual outcomes is intractable when outcomes are high-dimensional (gene expression profiles, facial images) and covariates are limited, because the rich individual-specific information embedded in the observed outcome cannot be recovered from a handful of covariates. Prior variational and adversarial approaches for counterfactual generation are built on the conditional VAE formulation, which optimizes the marginal-level interventional likelihood p(Y|X,T) rather than the individual-level counterfactual likelihood p(Y'|Y,X,T,T'), creating a fundamental disconnect between training and counterfactual inference that causes models to either ignore treatment effects or fail to preserve individuality.

## Input / Output

The model consumes a factual triplet (observed outcome y, factual treatment t, covariates x) together with a counterfactual treatment t' sampled from the empirical distribution p_data(T|x) during training, since no counterfactual observations exist. It produces a counterfactual outcome y' under t' and a latent representation z, with the counterfactual construction supervised against the covariate-specific outcome distribution of individuals who actually received t'. The framework additionally yields a robust AIPW-style marginal treatment effect estimator that exploits the individual-level latent predictions, enabling covariate-specific effect estimation that varies across individuals within the same covariate group.

## Method

VCI fundamentally reformulates the variational objective from the conditional VAE's marginal likelihood to the individual-level counterfactual likelihood p(Y'|Y,X,T,T'), deriving a new ELBO with three terms: a factual reconstruction term, a counterfactual supervision term estimated end-to-end via a GAN discriminator that scores the counterfactual construction against the covariate-specific outcome distribution, and a latent divergence term penalizing mismatch between the latent distributions encoded from the factual and counterfactual outcomes. This latent divergence term is the key mechanism enforcing disentangled exogenous noise abduction, ensuring the latent captures individual-specific features independent of the factual treatment without requiring paired data. The paper proves that maximizing this ELBO under mild uniqueness assumptions guarantees disentanglement of the latent from the treatment, and provides a robust asymptotically efficient marginal estimator for average treatment effects that leverages the individual-level counterfactual predictions.

## Results

On Morpho-MNIST, VCI achieves the lowest image MSE across all intervention types: 0.42 (do(th)), 0.13 (do(in)), and 0.36 (mix) ×10⁻², substantially outperforming the next-best SAE baseline (0.67, 0.20, 0.47) and the prior state-of-the-art MED (2.20, 0.61, 1.39). On single-cell perturbation datasets, VCI achieves the best R² on differentially-expressed (DE) genes (0.496 on Sciplex, 0.658 on Marson) and on all genes for Marson (0.891), with the largest fractional improvement on DE genes where causal effects are most pronounced. VCI also achieves the best thickness and intensity MAE on Morpho-MNIST (0.20/0.14/0.22 for do(th)/do(in)/mix), confirming superior preservation of both treatment characteristics and individuality.

## Baselines & Comparisons

On Morpho-MNIST, VCI dominates all seven baselines by wide margins: it reduces image MSE by 38% over SAE (0.36 vs 0.47 mix), 74% over MED (0.36 vs 1.39 mix), 93% over CHVAE (0.36 vs 4.81 mix), and 94% over Diff-SCM (0.36 vs 6.30 mix). On single-cell perturbation datasets, VCI achieves the best R² on DE genes for both Sciplex (0.496 vs CPA's 0.474) and Marson (0.658 vs CPA's 0.549, a 20% relative improvement), and the best all-gene R² on Marson (0.891 vs CPA's 0.876), though CPA edges out VCI slightly on Sciplex all genes (0.836 vs 0.832). VCI clearly outperforms CEVAE and GANITE across all single-cell metrics, with the gap most pronounced on DE genes. The ablation SAE baseline (VCI without the latent divergence term) is competitive but less stable in long-run training, confirming that the full VCI objective with both counterfactual supervision and latent divergence is necessary for consistent performance.

## Relation to Prior Work

VCI departs fundamentally from the conditional VAE formulation used by CEVAE, GANITE, CHVAE, MED, and Diff-SCM, arguing that optimizing the interventional likelihood p(Y|X,T) or joint likelihood p(Y,X,T) is the wrong objective for counterfactual generative modeling. Instead, it derives an ELBO for the individual-level counterfactual likelihood p(Y'|Y,X,T,T') using twin-network SCM structure (Balke & Pearl, 1994; Pearl, 1995), with adversarial training (Goodfellow et al., 2014) providing end-to-end counterfactual supervision. The latent divergence term in the ELBO serves a causal disentanglement purpose rather than the standard VAE prior-bounding role, and the disentanglement guarantees build on oracle consistency/restrictiveness definitions from Shu et al. (2019) generalized to stochastic models. The robust marginal estimator extends AIPW-style doubly-robust estimation (Van Der Laan & Rubin, 2006) to the individual-level counterfactual setting.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The paper provides the first theoretically grounded variational framework specifically derived for the counterfactual (rung-3) objective rather than the interventional (rung-2) objective, with formal disentanglement guarantees that prior HVAE/diffusion-based counterfactual methods lack. This reframing has practical implications: it explains why prior methods exhibit lingering factual-treatment artifacts in counterfactual generations and offers a principled fix. Limitations include the reliance on ignorability (no unobserved confounders) and the lack of explicit modeling of causal relations among multiple treatments. Open questions include whether the framework extends to settings with unmeasured confounding, how it scales to very high-dimensional outcomes (e.g., full proteomics), and whether the disentanglement guarantees hold under distribution shift.
