## Problem

Consistency training (CT) achieves strong results in pixel space but fails catastrophically in latent space (FID > 30), blocking its use for large-scale text-to-image and video generation. The paper identifies the root cause: latent data contains extreme impulsive outliers that produce very large temporal-difference (TD) values, which the Pseudo-Huber loss used in iCT cannot adequately suppress, leading to unstable training.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ICLR20252149/cropped_page4_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The model consumes a 4-channel latent tensor (32×32×4) produced by a pretrained KL-8 VAE encoder and outputs a pixel-space image at 256×256 resolution via the VAE decoder. Sampling requires only 1 or 2 function evaluations (NFE), compared to 200–250 NFE for standard latent diffusion models. Training uses a variance-exploding (VE) forward process with a UNet architecture, and all experiments are conducted on CelebA-HQ, FFHQ, and LSUN Church at 256×256.

## Method

The paper replaces the Pseudo-Huber loss with a Cauchy loss, which grows only logarithmically for large residuals, effectively dampening extreme TD outliers without completely ignoring them (unlike Geman-McClure, which zeroes the gradient). A diffusion L2 loss is added at small timesteps (first 25% of discretization steps) to regularize the consistency objective where the ground truth is well-approximated by x₀. Minibatch optimal transport (OT) coupling reassigns noise-data pairs within each batch to reduce training variance at negligible overhead (~0.93% extra time). An adaptive scaling-c scheduler decreases the robustness parameter proportionally to the reduction in TD variance as the exponential curriculum increases discretization steps. Finally, Non-scaling LayerNorm (NsLN) removes the learnable scaling term from LayerNorm to prevent outliers from being amplified across all features through a shared parameter.

## Results

On CelebA-HQ 256×256, the proposed method achieves FID 7.27 and Recall 0.50 with just 1 NFE, and FID 6.93 and Recall 0.52 with 2 NFE, dramatically outperforming the iLCT baseline (FID 37.15 / 16.84). On LSUN Church, 1-NFE FID is 8.72 (vs. 48.82 for iLCT), and on FFHQ it is 8.29. Notably, the 1-NFE result on CelebA-HQ (FID 7.27) surpasses the authors' own LDM-8† diffusion baseline (FID 8.85 at 250 NFE) using the same architecture and noise schedule, while requiring only 1/250th the function evaluations.

## Baselines & Comparisons

The most critical comparison is against iLCT (Song & Dhariwal, 2023), the SoTA pixel-space consistency training method applied directly to latent space: the proposed method reduces FID from 37.15 to 7.27 (1-NFE) and from 16.84 to 6.93 (2-NFE) on CelebA-HQ, a ~5× improvement, confirming that Pseudo-Huber loss is insufficient for latent-space impulsive outliers. Against the authors' own LDM-8† diffusion baseline (same UNet architecture, same VE noise schedule, 250 NFE), the proposed 1-NFE consistency model achieves a lower FID (7.27 vs. 8.85), demonstrating that 1-step consistency sampling can match or beat 250-step diffusion with identical architecture. However, against stronger latent diffusion baselines like LDM-4 (FID 5.11, 200 NFE) and DIMSUM (FID 3.76, 73 NFE), the proposed method still trails in absolute FID, though it requires 1–2 NFE versus 73–200 NFE. On LSUN Church, the same pattern holds: Ours (FID 8.72, 1-NFE) beats iLCT (48.82) and LDM-8† (10.23, 250 NFE) but lags behind LDM-4 (4.98, 200 NFE). The paper notes that the gap to LDM-4 and LFM-8 is partly attributable to using a VE forward process versus VP or flow-matching, so the comparison is not entirely apples-to-apples.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the consistency training (CT) line of work from pixel space to latent space, building directly on iCT (Song & Dhariwal, 2023) as its baseline framework. While prior CT improvements (CTM, ACT, MCM, ECM) all operated in bounded pixel space, this paper identifies that latent data contains impulsive outliers that break the Pseudo-Huber loss assumption, and replaces it with Cauchy loss. It also borrows optimal transport coupling from flow matching literature (Pooladian et al., 2023) for variance reduction and adapts Non-scaling LayerNorm from LLM quantization (Wei et al., 2022) to mitigate outlier sensitivity in the architecture.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

This work provides the first systematic diagnosis of why consistency training fails in latent space (impulsive outliers in unbounded VAE latents) and a practical recipe to fix it, narrowing the FID gap from ~37 to ~7 at 1-NFE. However, a meaningful gap to latent diffusion models (FID 5.11 for LDM-4) remains, and all experiments use a single VAE (KL-8), single architecture (UNet), and 256×256 resolution, leaving open whether these techniques transfer to text-to-image, video, or other latent spaces. The adaptive c scheduler and NsLN are lightweight modifications that could be readily adopted by other latent generative model training pipelines.
