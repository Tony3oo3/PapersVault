## Problem

Existing benchmarks for interleaved text-and-image generation are predominantly language-dominant, lack golden reference answers, and rely on coarse-grained MLLM-as-a-Judge scoring that cannot verify fine-grained text-image consistency, structural adherence, or image-specific semantic accuracy. There is no evaluation framework that decomposes an interleaved response into interpretable, multi-level checks across holistic, structural, block, and image granularities.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ICLR20252692/cropped_page1_idx1.webp)

## Input / Output

ISG takes a multimodal user query (text instruction plus optional input image) and a model's interleaved text-and-image response as input, and produces four scalar scores (structural accuracy, holistic 1–10 score, block-level VQA accuracy, image-level VQA accuracy) along with the full set of generated question-answer pairs for interpretability. The structural level performs exact matching of the predicted block sequence (e.g., 'image, text, image, text') against the generated output; block and image levels each yield per-tuple Yes/No or 1–10 VQA judgments; the holistic level uses an MLLM with a golden answer and Analyze-then-Judge CoT to produce a single 1–10 score.

## Method

ISG parses each query into a scene-graph-like structure where text blocks and image blocks are nodes and atomic requirements (e.g., Describe, Depict, on-the-right-of) are edges, then converts every graph element into TIFA-style VQA questions evaluated by GPT-4o. At the structural level, an LLM predicts the required interleaved layout and the system performs direct sequence matching; at the block level, subject-object-relation tuples are turned into questions scored via VQA; at the image level, dependency-aware entity-attribute-relation tuples tied to specific generated images are verified. The accompanying ISG-BENCH contains 1,150 manually curated samples across 8 categories and 21 subcategories, each paired with a golden answer and designed to be vision-language dependent. ISG-AGENT, the compositional baseline, follows a Plan-Execute-Refine pipeline: GPT-4o plans tool invocations in JSON, a tool library (UltraEdit, DynamiCrafter, SV3D, DreamMover, SD3) executes them, and a refinement loop fixes errors and improves text-image alignment, achieving 122% improvement over the best unified model at the holistic level.

## Results

On ISG-BENCH (1,150 samples, 8 categories), ISG-AGENT achieves a Structural accuracy of 0.871 and a Holistic score of 6.262/10, substantially outperforming the best unified model (CoMM-MiniGPT-5: 0.000 structural, 2.961 holistic) and the best compositional baseline (Gemini & SD3: 0.385 structural, 5.827 holistic). At the fine-grained levels, ISG-AGENT scores 5.515/10 on Block and 0.574 on Image, compared to 3.081 and 0.150 for the strongest compositional baseline (Gemini & SD3). Compositional frameworks overall show a 111% improvement over unified models at the holistic level, yet all methods remain far below the human golden answer (9.265 holistic, 7.611 block, 0.813 image).

## Baselines & Comparisons

Among unified models, Anole achieves the highest holistic score (2.810) but scores 0.000 on structural accuracy, indicating it cannot follow interleaved output format instructions; Show-o is the only unified model with non-zero structural accuracy (0.295) but suffers from hallucinations generating images from system prompts. All other unified models (MiniGPT-5, CoMM-MiniGPT-5, SEED-LLaMA) score 0.000 structurally and cannot be evaluated at block or image levels. Among compositional baselines, Claude & SD3 leads on holistic (6.254) while Gemini & SD3 leads on structural (0.385) and block (3.081), but both remain far below ISG-AGENT on every metric. ISG-AGENT outperforms the best compositional baseline by 7.3% on holistic (6.262 vs. 6.254), 79% on structural (0.871 vs. 0.385), 79% on block (5.515 vs. 3.081), and 283% on image (0.574 vs. 0.150), demonstrating that the plan-execute-refine pipeline with advanced tools (UltraEdit, DynamiCrafter, SV3D, DreamMover) is critical for accurate fine-grained interleaved generation.

## Relation to Prior Work

ISG extends the scene graph representation (Krishna et al., 2017) from image understanding to interleaved text-and-image generation evaluation, and adapts the TIFA-style VQA evaluation paradigm (Hu et al., 2023; Cho et al., 2023) from single-image text-to-image faithfulness checking to a four-level hierarchical protocol (holistic, structural, block, image). The ISG-AGENT baseline repurposes the Plan-Execute-Refine pipeline from GenArtist (Wang et al., 2024) for interleaved generation with specialized tools. Unlike prior interleaved benchmarks (MMC4, CoMM, InterleavedBench, MMIE, GATE Opening) that rely on zero-shot MLLM-as-a-Judge or lack ground-truth answers and fine-grained multi-level scoring, ISG-BENCH introduces structured QA pairs at each granularity with human-verified golden references, filling a gap the authors identify in Table 1.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The benchmark reveals that unified autoregressive models (Anole, Show-o, MiniGPT-5, CoMM-MiniGPT-5, SEED-LLaMA) achieve near-zero structural accuracy on interleaved generation, meaning they fundamentally cannot follow output-format instructions despite being designed for this task. The discovery of an 'image-quality bias' in MLLM-as-a-Judge—where high-quality images receive higher holistic scores even when they violate explicit instructions—challenges the reliability of the dominant evaluation paradigm in the field. Open questions include whether unified models can be salvaged with vision-dominant instruction tuning data, how to evaluate trustworthiness and adversarial robustness of interleaved outputs, and whether compositional agent pipelines like ISG-AGENT can serve as synthetic data generators to bootstrap better unified models.
