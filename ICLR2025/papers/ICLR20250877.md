## Problem

Existing contrastive decoding methods for LVLMs (e.g., VCD, ICD) alleviate hallucinations by holistically disturbing raw vision or instruction inputs, but these vision-and-text-agnostic disturbances do not reliably amplify the desired hallucinations and instead introduce uncertainty noise that compromises normal decoding. Additionally, constructing a second forward pass on disturbed inputs doubles inference cost. The paper identifies that CD methods underperform normal greedy decoding in random POPE settings and depend heavily on an adaptive plausibility constraint to mask the induced noise.

## Input / Output

The method operates on an RGB image (encoded into 576 vision tokens for LLaVA-1.5, 256 for Shikra, or 32 for InstructBLIP via Q-former) and a text instruction, both fed into a pre-trained LVLM. During autoregressive decoding, it intervenes at the token level within early decoder layers (layer 3 for linear-projection models, layer 5 for Q-former models), preserving only the top 10% least-important vision tokens for a contrastive forward pass. The output is the contrastively adjusted next-token logit distribution, which is then decoded via standard greedy or sampling strategies without any architectural modification or auxiliary network.

## Method

SID's key insight is that pre-trained LVLMs can introspectively assess vision token importance via self-attention scores conditioned on all preceding vision, instruction, and generated tokens. The CT²S strategy computes per-vision-token importance scores (Eq. 5: mean attention received by each vision token across heads at layer i) and preserves only the k least-important tokens (default k=10%) for a second forward pass, creating a context- and text-aware hallucination distribution. The contrastive decoding step (Eq. 2) then subtracts the amplified hallucination logits from the original logits, with an adaptive plausibility constraint (Eq. 3) to filter implausible tokens. Unlike VCD/ICD which disturb raw inputs before the encoder, SID operates inside the decoder after multimodal information has been partially integrated, so the induced hallucinations are grounded in visual context and text rather than being aimless. This token-level, layer-specific approach reduces inference time by ~30% compared to full-input contrastive decoding while producing more targeted, vision-and-text-association hallucinations that are more effectively subtracted.

## Results

On the POPE adversarial setting (hardest, prioritizing co-occurring objects), SID achieves 83.24% accuracy / 82.21% F1 with LLaVA-1.5 7B under greedy decoding, outperforming VCD (78.12/80.16), ICD (80.21/80.97), Dola (80.35/81.21), and OPERA (79.16/80.93). On CHAIR (open-end captioning), SID reduces sentence-level hallucination to 44.2% and instance-level to 12.2% (LLaVA-1.5 greedy), the lowest among all baselines. SID also maintains or improves general multimodal ability (MME: 1520.4, MMBench: 65.0) while reducing inference time by ~30% compared to VCD/ICD (668s vs. 904s/974s on NVIDIA V100).

## Baselines & Comparisons

SID consistently outperforms all seven baselines on the POPE adversarial setting, the most challenging configuration: it gains +5.12% accuracy over VCD (83.24 vs. 78.12), +3.03% over ICD (83.24 vs. 80.21), +2.89% over Dola (83.24 vs. 80.35), and +4.08% over OPERA (83.24 vs. 79.16) with LLaVA-1.5 7B greedy decoding. On CHAIR open-end captioning, SID achieves the lowest hallucination rates (C_S=44.2, C_I=12.2) compared to VCD (46.8/13.2), ICD (47.4/13.9), Dola (47.1/13.8), and OPERA (45.2/12.7). Against tuning-based LURE and HA-DPO (Table 10), SID still leads on POPE (82.5 vs. 78.7 for LURE; 86.2 vs. 85.4 for HA-DPO) and CHAIR, and can be stacked on top of both for further gains. Compared to AVISC (attention-based vision calibration), SID achieves higher POPE adversarial accuracy (83.3 vs. 79.8) and lower CHAIR (44.2 vs. 45.3), as AVISC's 'blind token' subtraction introduces residual noise. On general ability benchmarks (MME, MMBench), SID matches or slightly exceeds greedy decoding (1520.4 vs. 1510.8 MME; 65.0 vs. 64.4 MMBench), whereas VCD and ICD degrade performance (1488.5/1473.2 MME), confirming that SID's token-level disturbance avoids the uncertainty noise of holistic input perturbation.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the contrastive decoding (CD) paradigm for LVLMs, building directly on Vision CD (VCD, Leng et al. 2024) and Instruction CD (ICD, Wang et al. 2024a), which holistically disturb raw vision or instruction inputs to amplify hallucinations before subtracting them. The key departure is replacing holistic input-level disturbances with a token-level, context-aware strategy (CT²S) that leverages the model's own self-attention scores to selectively preserve only the least important vision tokens in early decoder layers. This draws on the token pruning/merging literature (Rao et al. 2021; Bolya et al. 2023) but inverts the logic: instead of removing unimportant tokens for efficiency, SID retains them to induce fine-grained, vision-and-text-associated hallucinations for contrastive subtraction. The paper also positions itself against layer-contrastive methods (DoLa, Chuang et al. 2024) and beam-search approaches (OPERA, Huang et al. 2024), showing that its token-selection mechanism avoids the uncertainty noise that plagues holistic CD.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

SID offers a practical, training-free, plug-and-play decoding strategy that reduces hallucinations while cutting inference cost by ~30% relative to CD methods that double forward passes, making it attractive for deployment in latency-sensitive applications. The conceptual finding that pre-trained LVLMs can introspectively assess vision token importance conditioned on preceding text is a useful diagnostic tool that could inform future token-reduction and interpretability work. However, the pruning ratio and layer index remain manually tuned hyperparameters, and the method still inherits the vision encoder's perceptual limitations (e.g., fine-grained attribute errors). Open questions include whether an external network can automatically select optimal CT²S parameters, and whether the strategy generalizes to video or higher-resolution settings where token counts grow substantially.
