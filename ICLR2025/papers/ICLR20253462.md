## Problem

Prior 4D novel view synthesis methods were restricted to narrow domains (object-centric or indoor scenes), relied on noisy SfM poses without metric scale, and could not jointly control camera pose and time in natural scenes. The lack of large-scale, metric-calibrated, posed training data for general scenes made it difficult to achieve both accurate pose alignment and strong generalization to unseen images and free-form camera trajectories.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ICLR20253462/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

4DiM accepts one or more conditioning images at 256×256, per-pixel ray origins and directions (encoding camera extrinsics and intrinsics), and relative scalar timestamps, and produces a sequence of 32 novel view frames. The base model generates frames at 64×64, which a separate 4× super-resolution model upsamples to 256×256. A key design constraint is that any conditioning signal may be absent (e.g., unposed video or static scenes), so the model must gracefully handle incomplete inputs during both training and inference.

## Method

4DiM is a cascaded diffusion model with a UViT backbone that uses temporal attention blocks for cross-frame mixing and per-frame self-attention only at the 16×16 bottleneck to limit quadratic cost. The key architectural innovation is Masked FiLM conditioning: FiLM layers for noise level, per-pixel rays, and timestamps reduce to the identity function when a signal is missing, enabling joint training on a mixture of posed 3D data (ScanNet++, Matterport3D, calibrated RE10K), 4D data (Street View panoramas), and 30M unposed videos. At inference, multi-guidance generalizes classifier-free guidance to assign independent weights to each conditioning variable (images, poses, timestamps), allowing separate tuning of fidelity, pose alignment, and temporal dynamics. The authors also create a scale-calibrated version of RealEstate10K (cRE10K) by regressing SfM points to monocular metric depth, resolving the scale ambiguity that causes pose overshoot/undershoot in single-image conditioning.

## Results

On the in-distribution cRE10K test, 4DiM achieves FID 31.96 and FDD 314.9, substantially outperforming PNVS (FID 51.41, FDD 1007), MotionCtrl (FID 43.07, FDD 370.6), and ZeroNVS (FID 37.35, FDD 522.3). 4DiM also achieves near-perfect 3D consistency with TSED of 0.9935 (ground truth: 1.000) and the best pose alignment (SfMDpos 1.008, SfMDrot 0.3326). On the zero-shot cLLFF evaluation, 4DiM achieves FID 63.78 and FDD 356.8 with TSED 0.9167, dramatically outperforming MotionCtrl (TSED 0.1515) and ZeroNVS (TSED 0.2205) in 3D consistency while maintaining the best image fidelity.

## Baselines & Comparisons

4DiM decisively outperforms all three main baselines on image fidelity: on cRE10K it achieves FID 31.96 vs. PNVS (51.41), MotionCtrl (43.07), and ZeroNVS (37.35), and on cLLFF zero-shot it reaches FID 63.78 vs. PNVS (185.4), MotionCtrl (106.0), and ZeroNVS (75.95). The most dramatic gap is in 3D consistency (TSED): on cLLFF, 4DiM scores 0.9167 while MotionCtrl collapses to 0.1515 and ZeroNVS to 0.2205, indicating severe geometric inconsistency in those methods' zero-shot outputs. On pose alignment (SfMDpos), 4DiM achieves 1.008 on cRE10K (closest to ground truth 1.034) and 0.9131 on cLLFF (closest to ground truth 0.8960), beating all baselines. The one area where 4DiM does not lead is reconstruction-based metrics on cLLFF: ZeroNVS achieves lower LPIPS (0.5008 vs. 0.5415) and higher PSNR (12.58 vs. 11.58), which the authors attribute to ZeroNVS producing blurrier but more content-aligned samples. For frame interpolation on DAVIS, 4DiM is compared against LDMVFI, FILM, and RIFE, with 4DiM generating 4× more frames while maintaining competitive FID on the middle frame.

## Relation to Prior Work

4DiM extends the line of diffusion-based NVS work (Watson et al. 2022; Liu et al. 2023b) from object-centric, relative-pose settings to natural scenes with free-form, metric-scale camera trajectories and simultaneous time control. Architecturally, it builds on UViT (Hoogeboom et al. 2023) and cascaded diffusion (Ho et al. 2022a,b), but introduces Masked FiLM layers to handle incomplete conditioning (missing pose or time) and multi-guidance to generalize classifier-free guidance to per-variable weights. The data strategy departs from prior 3D NVS models that relied solely on SfM-posed datasets by co-training on 30M unposed videos, and introduces scale-calibrated versions of RealEstate10K to resolve the metric ambiguity that plagues COLMAP-estimated poses.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

4DiM is the first NVS method to offer intuitive metric-scale camera pose control and simultaneous space-time generation from as few as one image, opening practical applications like pose-conditioned video-to-video translation, panorama stitching, and 360° rotation from a single photo. Key limitations remain: single-frame conditioning still struggles to produce rich temporal dynamics, 360° rotation quality is imperfect, and the model is limited to 32 frames at 256×256. Open questions include whether adding text conditioning and scaling to larger models will resolve the dynamics gap, and whether the unposed-video co-training strategy generalizes to other 3D/4D generation tasks.
