## Problem

Current egocentric video-language models (EgoVLMs) pretrained with standard contrastive objectives (InfoNCE, EgoNCE) fail to distinguish between simple single-word substitutions in hand-object interaction descriptions, such as 'shakes the frying pan' versus 'hangs the frying pan.' The paper attributes this to a lack of fine-grained negative text supervision during pretraining and an emergent object-centric feature space that favors noun recognition over verb recognition, raising the question of whether EgoVLMs truly understand HOI semantics.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ICLR20253625/cropped_page1_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method consumes ~1-second egocentric video clips (4 frames sampled) paired with HOI captions from a 2.5M-entry subset of EgoClip-3.8M, and produces video-text similarity scores for retrieval, action classification, and multi-choice HOI discrimination. The introduced EgoHOIBench evaluates models on 29,651 video clips, each requiring selection of the correct caption from 10 verb-substituted and 10 noun-substituted hard negatives generated via LLM in-context learning, with a vocabulary spanning ~800 verbs and ~4,000 nouns.

## Method

EgoNCE++ is an asymmetric contrastive pretraining objective with two complementary losses. The video-to-text (V2T) loss introduces fine-grained hard negatives by generating HOI-related negative captions through LLM-based word substitution (LLaMA-3-8B) or vocabulary-based random replacement of verbs/nouns, forcing the model to learn word-level HOI semantics beyond easy batch negatives. The text-to-video (T2V) loss preserves the object-centric feature space by clustering video representations based solely on shared nouns in their captions (unlike EgoNCE, which also groups by verbs), maintaining the model's strong noun-recognition advantage. Training uses LoRA (rank 16) on the visual encoder with the text encoder frozen (except word embeddings for LaViLa), requiring only 3–43M additional parameters and ~12 hours on 8×A800 GPUs for 10 epochs at batch size 576.

## Results

On EgoHOIBench, LaViLa++ achieves 63.17% action accuracy (verb: 80.63%, noun: 75.30%), a +26.32% improvement over LaViLa (36.85%). On ActionBench (temporal understanding), LaViLa++ reaches 91.18% action accuracy, surpassing the previous best fine-tuned models (InternVideo* 90.1%, Clip-Vip* 89.3%) and approaching human performance (92.0%). On zero-shot EK-100-MIR, LaViLa++ achieves 32.0% average mAP and 32.3% average nDCG, and on EGTEA it reaches 34.0% mean accuracy, outperforming HelpingHands (29.4%) and HENASY (29.6%) by over 4 points.

## Baselines & Comparisons

Against HelpingHands (Zhang et al., 2023, +37M params) and HENASY (Phan et al., 2024, +112M params), LaViLa++ (+27M params) achieves higher EGTEA mean accuracy (34.0% vs. 29.4% and 29.6%) and competitive EK-100-MIR mAP (32.0% vs. 31.2% and 31.3%), while using fewer additional parameters than both. On ActionBench, LaViLa++ (91.18%) surpasses all previously reported fine-tuned models including InternVideo* (90.1%), Clip-Vip* (89.3%), and Singularity* (83.8%), approaching human-level performance (92.0%). EgoNCE++ consistently improves all three base EgoVLMs: EgoVLP gains +11.47% on EgoHOIBench action accuracy, EgoVLPv2 gains +6.0%, and LaViLa gains +26.32%, demonstrating the method's architecture-agnostic applicability. On EK-100-OV (open-vocabulary recognition), LaViLa++ improves open-set top-1 action accuracy from 7.57% to 8.48% over LaViLa, though it remains below methods that use an external HOI detector at inference time. The paper notes that EgoNCE++ is orthogonal to approaches like HelpingHands and could be combined with them for further gains.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the egocentric video-language pretraining line initiated by EgoNCE (Lin et al., 2022) and InfoNCE (Zhao et al., 2023), identifying that both objectives lack fine-grained text supervision for HOI word-level distinctions. The proposed EgoNCE++ asymmetric objective draws on hard negative mining from the broader VLM literature (Yuksekgonul et al., 2023; Momeni et al., 2023) but adapts it specifically to egocentric HOI via LLM- or vocabulary-based single-word substitution, while the T2V loss leverages the observed object-centric clustering property documented in prior work (Zhang et al., 2023; Escorcia et al., 2022). The paper also introduces EgoHOIBench as a diagnostic benchmark, positioning it as a more targeted evaluation than existing EgoHOI testbeds (EK-100, ActionBench, EgoMCQ) that focus on broader action recognition or temporal understanding rather than fine-grained word-level HOI discrimination.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The finding that EgoVLMs trained on millions of HOI videos cannot reliably distinguish single-word verb or noun substitutions challenges the assumption that large-scale contrastive pretraining yields robust compositional understanding, and the object-centric feature space diagnosis provides a concrete explanation for the verb-noun performance gap. The practical EgoNCE++ recipe (LoRA on visual encoder, frozen text encoder, LLM-generated hard negatives) is lightweight and model-agnostic, making it broadly applicable. However, the approach is limited to HOI-centric egocentric activities and relies solely on text supervision, which the authors acknowledge is insufficient for broader object recognition; incorporating visual signals (e.g., bounding boxes) or extending to non-HOI egocentric tasks (sports, observation) remains open.
