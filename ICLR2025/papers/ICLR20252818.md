## Problem

Existing self-expressive deep subspace clustering (SEDSC) methods suffer from catastrophic feature collapse, where learned representations are restricted to subspaces with dimensions far below the ambient space, and no theoretical guarantee exists that the representations form a union-of-subspaces structure. This paper addresses the lack of a principled framework that provably prevents collapse while yielding structured UoS representations.

## Input / Output

The method takes pretrained CLIP image features (768-dim, extracted from 224×224 center-cropped images) or raw high-dimensional data points as input. It produces two outputs: (1) structured normalized representations Z_Θ ∈ R^{d×N} from a learnable mapping f(·;Θ), and (2) a self-expressive coefficient matrix C_Ψ generated by applying Sinkhorn projection to Y^T Y where Y comes from a separate mapping h(·;Ψ). Final cluster assignments are obtained by spectral clustering on the induced affinity A = ½(|C| + |C|^T). The reparameterization via Sinkhorn projection makes the method transductive-free, allowing generalization to unseen test data.

## Method

PRO-DSC augments the standard self-expressive model with a log-det regularizer R(Z;α) = log det(I + αZ^T Z), which is concave, differentiable, and encourages maximal intrinsic dimensionality of the representation space. The paper proves three key theorems: (1) eigenspace alignment between G = Z^T Z and M = (I−C)(I−C)^T at the optimum, (2) non-collapse guarantee (rank(Z*) = min{d,N}) under the condition γ < α²/(λ_max(M)(α + min{d/N,1})), and (3) block-diagonal structure of both G and C under a 'compatibly structured coherence' condition, implying the representations lie on a union of orthogonal subspaces. For scalable implementation, the N×N self-expressive matrix is reparameterized via a neural network h(·;Ψ) followed by Sinkhorn projection, and a block-diagonal regularizer ‖A‖_[k] (sum of k smallest Laplacian eigenvalues) is applied to C_Ψ. The full loss L = −½ log det(I + αZ_Θ^T Z_Θ) + γ/2‖Z_Θ − Z_Θ C_Ψ‖_F² + β·r(C_Ψ) is optimized via SGD in mini-batches, with a warm-up phase that diversifies features using only the log-det term before joint training.

## Results

PRO-DSC achieves the best clustering accuracy (ACC) and normalized mutual information (NMI) across all six benchmark datasets using CLIP features. On CIFAR-10, it reaches 97.2% ACC and 92.8% NMI; on CIFAR-100, 77.3% ACC and 82.4% NMI; and on ImageNet-1k, 65.0% ACC and 83.4% NMI. The method also significantly reduces computational cost compared to SEDSC (which OOMs) and CPP, with a time complexity of O(kn_b² + min{n_b³, d³}) versus O((n_b+1)min{n_b³, d³}) for CPP.

## Baselines & Comparisons

PRO-DSC outperforms all 10 baselines on every dataset for both ACC and NMI. Against the strongest deep clustering baselines, it beats TEMI by 0.3% ACC on CIFAR-10 (97.2 vs. 96.9) and by 1.0% ACC on CIFAR-100 (77.3 vs. 73.7), and surpasses CPP by 3.9% ACC on CIFAR-20 (71.6 vs. 67.7) and 6.4% ACC on TinyImageNet-200 (69.8 vs. 63.4). Among subspace clustering methods, PRO-DSC leads EnSC by 1.8% ACC on CIFAR-10 (97.2 vs. 95.4) and by 5.3% ACC on CIFAR-20 (71.6 vs. 61.0). The deep subspace clustering baselines DSCNet and EDESC are substantially outperformed, with DSCNet also running out of memory on ImageNet-1k. Notably, PRO-DSC's advantage is most pronounced on datasets with more classes (CIFAR-20, TinyImageNet-200), where the structured UoS representation provides the greatest benefit over generic deep clustering approaches.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the Self-Expressive Deep Subspace Clustering (SEDSC) framework by incorporating a log-det regularizer (originally from the MCR² principle in Yu et al., 2020) into the self-expressive model, directly addressing the catastrophic feature collapse identified in Haeffele et al. (2021). Unlike prior SEDSC methods (DSCNet, EDESC) that jointly learn representations and coefficients without theoretical guarantees, PRO-DSC provides the first provable conditions under which the learned representations maintain full rank and form a union of orthogonal subspaces. The block-diagonal regularizer from Lu et al. (2018) is used for the coefficient matrix, and the scalable implementation borrows the Sinkhorn-based reparameterization from SENet (Zhang et al., 2021) and the warm-up strategy from CPP (Chu et al., 2024).

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

PRO-DSC is the first deep subspace clustering framework with a rigorous theoretical guarantee against feature collapse, filling a gap left by Haeffele et al. (2021) who diagnosed the problem but offered no solution. The ablation revealing that the specific coefficient regularizer (block-diagonal, L1, nuclear, Frobenius) is largely irrelevant once the log-det term is present is a practical insight suggesting the structured representation learning does most of the work. However, the CSC condition in Theorem 3 (which ensures block-diagonal structure) is not fully characterized—the authors acknowledge they cannot prove when it holds in general, limiting the completeness of the theoretical story. Open questions include whether the framework extends beyond CLIP features to raw pixel inputs, and whether the CSC condition can be enforced or guaranteed through architectural design.
