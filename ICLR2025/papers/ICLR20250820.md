## Problem

Existing GUI datasets and benchmarks are limited to static content in narrow domains (web or mobile), overlooking dynamic sequential operations and diverse scenarios such as desktop software, multi-window interactions, and XR environments. Consequently, current MLLMs cannot reliably handle temporal GUI changes (e.g., pop-ups, state transitions), and video LLMs perform poorly on GUI tasks due to the absence of dedicated GUI video training data.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ICLR20250820/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The benchmark consumes human-annotated GUI video keyframes (averaging 6.7 frames per clip, each tagged with mouse/keyboard actions and sub-goals) alongside a text query, and produces three output formats: free-form descriptions (scored 1–5 by LLM-as-a-Judge), multiple-choice answers (accuracy), and multi-round conversational responses. The dataset spans 12,379 clips across six scenarios (Software, Website, iOS, Multi-window, XR, Android) with 83,176 total frames and 7.463 annotated actions per clip on average.

## Method

GUI-WORLD is built via a Human-MLLM collaboration pipeline: student workers record screen-operation videos (or YouTube tutorials are segmented), humans manually extract and annotate keyframes with operation metadata, and GPT-4V generates diverse QA pairs (captions, static/dynamic retrieval, prediction, sequential reasoning, multi-round conversation) that humans then verify and augment. For the model contribution, GUI-VID fine-tunes VideoChat2 in two progressive stages—Stage 1 aligns the UMT-L visual encoder and QFormer with GUI captions/descriptions, Stage 2 tackles complex tasks (prediction, retrieval, reasoning, conversation)—using LoRA on the LLM and a Vision-grounded Text Generation loss. A key empirical finding is that embodied-AI keyframe selectors (UVD+VIP/R3M) match or exceed human-selected keyframes for GUI tasks, and that higher input resolution and more keyframes yield substantial performance gains, underscoring the critical role of fine-grained visual perception in GUI understanding.

## Results

On the GUI-WORLD benchmark (12,379 GUI videos across six scenarios), GPT-4o achieves the best overall performance with 84.8% MCQA accuracy and 3.573/5 free-form score. The paper's proposed GUI-VID (fine-tuned VideoChat2) achieves 54.6% MCQA and 2.796/5 free-form, representing a ~30% average improvement over the VideoChat2 baseline (42.9% MC / 2.147 Free) and surpassing Qwen-VL-Max (74.0% MC / 2.568 Free) in free-form scoring. Among video LLMs, GUI-VID outperforms all baselines, with the largest gap over Minigpt4Video (16.8% MC / 1.443 Free) and ChatUnivi (22.4% MC / 2.317 Free).

## Baselines & Comparisons

GUI-VID substantially outperforms its base model VideoChat2 across all six scenarios, improving MCQA from 42.9% to 54.6% and free-form from 2.147 to 2.796 (≈30% relative gain). Among video LLMs, GUI-VID dominates: it beats Video-LLaVA (49.4% MC / 2.311 Free) by ~5 MC points and ~0.48 free-form, and far exceeds ChatUnivi (22.4% MC) and Minigpt4Video (16.8% MC). Against image LLMs, GUI-VID still trails GPT-4o (84.8% MC / 3.573 Free), GPT-4V (81.2% / 3.469), and Gemini-Pro-1.5 (81.3% / 3.298) by a wide margin, but its free-form score (2.796) surpasses Qwen-VL-Max (2.568) and LLaVA-OV-7B (2.606). On the downstream Mind2Web-Multimodal operational benchmark, fine-tuning on GUI-WORLD improves both VideoChat2 and GUI-VID, confirming that GUI understanding transfers to GUI operation tasks. In a human preference study (180 videos, 6 scenarios), GUI-VID is preferred over VideoChat2 in 86.0% of cases, with the highest preference in iOS (92.0%) and XR (88.0%) scenarios.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the GUI understanding task from static, single-platform settings (Web, mobile) to a video-based, multi-scenario formulation covering desktop software, websites, iOS, Android, multi-window interactions, and XR environments. Methodologically, GUI-VID builds on the VideoChat2 architecture (UMT-L encoder + QFormer + LLM) with progressive two-stage fine-tuning and LoRA, while the data curation pipeline draws on Human-MLLM collaboration inspired by Dekoninck et al. (2024). A notable conceptual departure is the borrowing of embodied-AI keyframe selection methods (UVD with VIP/R3M representations) for GUI video, which had not been explored in the GUI literature. The paper positions itself against 14 prior GUI datasets/benchmarks (Table 1), all of which lack video modality, dynamic content, or multi-scenario coverage.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

GUI-WORLD fills a clear gap as the first video-based, multi-scenario GUI instruction-tuning dataset, and the benchmark reveals that current MLLMs—especially video LLMs—struggle substantially with dynamic and sequential GUI content, a capability essential for real-world GUI agents. The finding that embodied-AI keyframe selectors (UVD+VIP/R3M) match or exceed human-selected keyframes for GUI tasks offers a practical path toward automated keyframe extraction. However, GUI-VID's performance (~55% MCQA) remains far below commercial image LLMs (~85%), and the authors acknowledge that base LLM limitations make video LLMs as GUI agents a significant open challenge. Future work must address the understanding-to-action gap and whether video LLMs can close the performance gap with stronger backbones.
