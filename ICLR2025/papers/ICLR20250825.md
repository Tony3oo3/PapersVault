## Problem

Deploying vision transformers on mobile devices is hindered by the quadratic complexity of self-attention on large feature maps and the memory-intensive reshaping operations inherent in multi-head attention, which severely inflate inference latency on resource-constrained hardware. While hybrid CNN-Transformer networks have been proposed, existing approaches either sacrifice global modeling capacity (via reduced queries/keys or limited attention spans) or fail to account for actual on-device latency, relying instead on proxy metrics like FLOPs and parameter counts that poorly correlate with real mobile inference speed.

## Input / Output

The model consumes standard RGB images at 224×224 for classification and 512×512 crops for dense prediction tasks (detection, instance segmentation, semantic segmentation), operating at batch size 1 as is typical for on-device inference. Outputs are task-dependent: class labels for ImageNet-1K, bounding boxes and instance masks for COCO via Mask R-CNN, and pixel-level segmentation masks for ADE20K via Semantic FPN. All latency measurements are taken on an iPhone 13 compiled with Core ML Tools, making the evaluation hardware-specific rather than abstract.

## Method

iFormer adopts a four-stage hierarchical architecture where early high-resolution stages use a progressively 'lightened' ConvNeXt (overlapping convolutional stem, Batch Normalization replacing Layer Normalization, deeper stage-3 blocks, and 7×7 depthwise kernels) for fast local feature extraction, while later low-resolution stages employ a novel Single-Head Modulation Attention (SHMA) block. SHMA eliminates the memory-heavy reshaping operations of multi-head attention by using a single attention head—justified by empirical evidence that heads exhibit high cosine similarity—and pairs it with a parallel feature-projection branch whose outputs are fused via element-wise sigmoid-gated multiplication, providing dynamic modulation that compensates for the reduced expressivity of single-head attention. Conditional positional encodings are added to the attention tokens, and the head dimension in SHMA is reduced to a fraction of the feature width to further cut latency. The entire design is optimized end-to-end for on-device latency on iPhone 13 rather than FLOPs, yielding a Pareto-optimal accuracy-latency frontier across classification, detection, and segmentation.

## Results

On ImageNet-1K, iFormer-M achieves 80.4% Top-1 accuracy at 1.10 ms latency on iPhone 13 (224×224, 300 epochs, no distillation or reparameterization), outperforming MNV4-Conv-M (79.9%) and FastViT-T12 (79.1%) at comparable or lower latency. iFormer-L reaches 81.9% Top-1 at 1.60 ms, surpassing SwiftFormer-L1 (80.9%) and RepViT-M1.5 (81.2%). On COCO, iFormer-L achieves 42.2 AP^box / 39.1 AP^mask at 6.60 ms backbone latency, and on ADE20K it attains 44.5 mIoU, exceeding PVTv2-B1 by +2.0% mIoU while running 4× faster.

## Baselines & Comparisons

iFormer-M (80.4%) beats MNV4-Conv-M (79.9%) by +0.5% at nearly identical latency (1.10 vs 1.08 ms), despite MNV4 using a 256×256 input and 500-epoch training. Against FastViT-T12 (79.1% at 1.12 ms, 256 input), iFormer-M gains +1.3% while being slightly faster. iFormer-L (81.9%) outperforms SwiftFormer-L1 (80.9%) by +1.0% at the same 1.60 ms latency, and exceeds RepViT-M1.5 (81.2%) by +0.7% at comparable speed. On COCO detection, iFormer-M surpasses FastViT-SA12 by +1.9% AP^box / +2.0% AP^mask while running 1.32× faster (4.00 vs 5.27 ms). iFormer-L matches Swin-T's AP^box (42.2) but with only 6.60 ms vs 'Failed' latency on iPhone 13, and beats ConvNeXt-T by +1.2% AP^box at 50% the latency. On ADE20K, iFormer-L achieves 44.5 mIoU, exceeding PVTv2-B1 (42.5) by +2.0% and Swin-T (41.5) by +3.0% while being 4× and 10×+ faster respectively.

## Relation to Prior Work

iFormer is built by progressively lightening ConvNeXt (Liu et al., 2022) into a mobile-optimized convolutional backbone, then injecting a novel Single-Head Modulation Attention (SHMA) in later stages. The SHMA mechanism adapts the modulation framework from EfficientMod (Ma et al., 2024) by replacing its convolutional context branch with single-head self-attention, thereby capturing global interactions without the memory-intensive reshaping operations of standard MHA. This positions iFormer at the intersection of the lightweight CNN line (RepViT, GhostNetV3, MobileNetV4) and the hybrid CNN-Transformer line (FastViT, EfficientFormer, MobileViT), but departs from both by using on-device iPhone 13 latency as the primary optimization target rather than FLOPs or parameter counts, and by eliminating multi-head reshaping as the key mobile bottleneck.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

iFormer provides a practical, well-validated architecture for mobile vision that achieves a Pareto-optimal accuracy-latency tradeoff, with the SHMA mechanism offering a reusable design pattern for reducing MHA overhead on resource-constrained hardware. The validation across classification, detection, instance segmentation, and semantic segmentation at high-resolution inputs (512×512) demonstrates that the efficiency gains are not task-specific. Open questions include whether the approach extends to other hardware platforms (Android, Jetson), how it handles very high-resolution inputs where attention still becomes costly, and whether the single-head assumption holds under different training regimes or larger model scales beyond the 99M-parameter iFormer-H.
