## Problem

While continuous-state diffusion models have well-established convergence guarantees, the theoretical analysis of score-based discrete diffusion models under the CTMC framework remains largely absent. In particular, no prior work provides convergence bounds for a discrete-time sampling algorithm in the general high-dimensional state space [S]^d that accounts for both discretization error and score estimation error simultaneously.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ICLR20251241/cropped_page8_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The algorithm initializes from the uniform distribution π^d over [S]^d and consumes learned score estimators ŝ at K predefined discretization points {kh+δ}_{k∈[K]}, where h is the step size and δ is an early-stopping offset. After K iterations of a time-homogeneous CTMC with frozen rate matrices, it outputs a single discrete sample z_K ∈ [S]^d whose law p_{T−δ} approximates the data distribution. Unlike continuous SDE samplers that solve linear ODEs within each step, here each step requires deriving a categorical distribution from the Kolmogorov equation and sampling from it, typically via Poisson uniformization of the CTMC.

## Method

The paper proposes a discrete-time sampling algorithm that approximates the true reverse CTMC by freezing the rate matrix at each interval [kh, (k+1)h] and substituting the true discrete score s_t with a learned estimator ŝ_{(k+1)h+δ}, yielding a sequence of time-homogeneous CTMCs. Convergence is analyzed via a Girsanov-based method that expresses the path-measure KL divergence between the true reverse process and the sampling process as a sum of Bregman divergences D_I(s_t ‖ ŝ), which is then decomposed into a score-movement term (capturing temporal variation of the true score within each step) and a score-error term (capturing estimation quality). Key technical lemmas establish a uniform score bound s_t ≤ 1 + S/(e^t − 1), a score-movement bound scaling as O(dS²h²), and exponential convergence of the forward process to π^d via a modified log-Sobolev inequality with constant 2. The resulting KL divergence bound is nearly linear in dimension d, matching state-of-the-art continuous diffusion results, and consists of three interpretable components: initialization error (de^{−T}log S), discretization error (δ^{−3}C₁S²h³d + C₁S²h²dT), and score estimation error (C₁²ε_score).

## Results

The paper derives KL divergence convergence bounds for a discrete-time sampling algorithm in the general state space [S]^d. With early stopping (Theorem 1), the bound is D_KL(q_δ‖p_{T−δ}) ≲ de^{−T}log S + δ^{−3}C_1 S²h³d + C_1 S²h²dT + C_1²ε_score. Without early stopping under a bounded-score assumption (Theorem 2), the bound improves to D_KL(p_data‖p_T) ≲ de^{−T}log S + C_2 κ²S²h²T + C_2²ε_score, eliminating the δ^{−3} term. Both bounds are nearly linear in dimension d, matching the best known results for continuous diffusion models (Benton et al., 2024a) and discrete diffusion models (Chen & Ying, 2024).

## Baselines & Comparisons

The paper's sole quantitative comparison is with Chen & Ying (2024), the first theoretical analysis of score-based discrete diffusion models. Chen & Ying (2024) achieves a cleaner bound (de^{−T} + ε) by using the CTMC uniformization technique to exactly simulate the reverse process without time discretization, but is restricted to the binary hypercube {0,1}^d. This paper extends the analysis to the general state space [S]^d and introduces a discrete-time sampling algorithm that calls score estimators at fixed points {kh+δ}, which introduces an additional discretization error term (C_1 S²h²dT or C_2 S²h²κ²T) absent in Chen & Ying's bound. Both methods achieve nearly linear dependence on d, but this paper's bound includes a log S factor in the initialization term (de^{−T}log S vs. de^{−T}) due to the larger state space. The trade-off is that this paper's framework is more broadly applicable (general S, discrete-time algorithm) at the cost of an extra discretization error that vanishes as h→0, at which point the bound degrades to the Chen & Ying form.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the CTMC framework for discrete diffusion (Campbell et al., 2022) and the first convergence analysis of score-based discrete diffusion (Chen & Ying, 2024) by introducing a discrete-time sampling algorithm analogous to the exponential integrator in continuous SDE settings. The Girsanov-based proof technique is adapted from Benton et al. (2024b), who unified continuous and discrete diffusion, while the score entropy training objective follows Lou et al. (2024). The key departure from Chen & Ying (2024) is the move from the {0,1}^d hypercube to the general [S]^d state space and the addition of time discretization, which introduces novel discretization error terms absent from prior discrete diffusion convergence results.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

This work provides the first convergence guarantees for a time-discretized sampling algorithm in the general [S]^d discrete diffusion setting, filling a notable gap between the well-developed continuous diffusion theory and the nascent discrete diffusion theory. The nearly linear-in-d KL bounds match the best continuous diffusion results, suggesting the CTMC framework can support efficient sampling at scale. However, the discretization error becomes significant for small δ or extreme data distributions, and the paper acknowledges that refined techniques for handling this error, accelerated algorithms with guarantees, and extensions to more general rate matrices remain open.
