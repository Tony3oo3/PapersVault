## Problem

Text-to-3D methods produce 3D objects with unpredictable appearances because textual prompts lack explicit visual information, while existing single-image-to-3D methods are limited to simple images with a single clear subject and fail on complex, multi-element images. This paper addresses the gap of generating high-fidelity, appearance-controllable 3D objects from complex image prompts that may contain multiple subjects, rich compositions, or no clearly dominant element.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ICLR20251245/cropped_page1_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method accepts one or more complex reference images (which need not have a single clear subject), a text prompt, and an initial coarse 3D model (NeRF or mesh) as input, and outputs a textured 3D mesh with optimized geometry. Rendering operates at 512×512 resolution with batch size 1 (~9 GB GPU memory), and the method additionally consumes normal maps derived from the image prompts for geometry guidance. When multiple diverse image prompts are provided, GPT-4V generates localization text prompts and SAM segments partial images, which are then super-resolved before feature extraction.

## Method

IPDreamer introduces Image Prompt Score Distillation Sampling (IPSDS), which extends SDS by injecting image prompt features (extracted via IP-Adapter's image encoder) into the denoising model through a decoupled cross-attention mechanism, enabling the diffusion prior to condition on rich visual appearance rather than text alone. Geometry optimization uses a normal-map image prompt to guide mesh deformation and SDF values, while texture optimization uses an RGB image prompt augmented with a geometry prompt difference (δ_geo = y_{n,ran} − y_{n,def}) that compensates for the morphological gap between the default and random viewpoints. For multiple complex images, a Mask-guided Compositional Alignment strategy employs GPT-4V to produce localization prompts, computes binarized cross-attention masks from those prompts, and applies them to gate per-image cross-attention so that each partial image's features are projected onto the correct spatial region of the 3D object. A final global optimization stage concatenates all partial-image features with the full-image feature to refine the texture coherently across the entire mesh.

## Results

On the text-to-3D generation benchmark (30 prompts), IPDreamer achieves the best FID of 253.32 and the highest CLIP-Score of 0.2716, outperforming the strongest baseline SV3D (FID 266.21, CLIP-Score 0.2681) by 12.89 FID points and 0.0035 CLIP-Score. In the user study (80 volunteers, 1280 pairwise comparisons), IPDreamer is preferred over all baselines, with the closest competitor SV3D still losing 43.75% of the time. The ablation on δ_geo shows a CLIP-Score improvement from 0.8228 to 0.8389 when the geometry prompt difference is included.

## Baselines & Comparisons

IPDreamer outperforms all eight baselines on both FID and CLIP-Score in the text-to-3D setting. The largest margins are over the earliest SDS-based methods: DreamFusion (FID 320.16) and Magic3D (FID 320.56), where IPDreamer reduces FID by over 66 points. Among the more recent baselines, SV3D (FID 266.21, CLIP 0.2681) and Zero123++ (FID 269.58, CLIP 0.2561) are the closest competitors, but IPDreamer still leads by 12.89 FID and 0.0035 CLIP-Score respectively. In the user study, IPDreamer is preferred 93.55% of the time over DreamFusion and 89.11% over Magic3D, but the advantage narrows against SV3D (56.25% preference) and ProlificDreamer (58.35%), suggesting these methods produce visually competitive results on standard prompts. The paper also highlights qualitative advantages on ambiguous prompts (e.g., 'The shining sun', 'Leaves flying in the wind') where all baselines fail to produce coherent subjects while IPDreamer succeeds, a gap not captured by the aggregate FID/CLIP metrics.

## Relation to Prior Work

IPDreamer extends the Score Distillation Sampling (SDS) framework introduced by DreamFusion by replacing text-only conditioning with image-prompt cross-attention (borrowed from IP-Adapter), enabling appearance-controllable 3D mesh optimization. It departs from single-image-to-3D methods (Zero123++, LGM, SV3D) that require a clear, singular subject by handling complex, multi-subject images through a Mask-guided Compositional Alignment strategy that leverages GPT-4V for localization and SAM for segmentation. The geometry prompt difference (δ_geo) is a novel compensation mechanism that aligns texture optimization across viewpoints by accounting for morphological disparity between rendered normal maps, addressing a gap not addressed by prior SDS variants.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

IPDreamer bridges a practical gap between the uncontrollability of text-to-3D and the subject-simplicity constraint of single-image-to-3D, offering a pipeline directly applicable to gaming, film, and AR/VR content creation where artists need precise appearance control from reference imagery. Key limitations include dependence on a coarse 3D initialization (NeRF or mesh), reliance on GPT-4V for localization (introducing cost and potential error), and acknowledged failure cases where image prompts substantially diverge from the initial geometry. Open questions include whether finer-grained detail transfer is achievable, how the method generalizes to scenes with many objects, and whether the GPT-4V dependency can be replaced by a lighter, more robust localization model.
