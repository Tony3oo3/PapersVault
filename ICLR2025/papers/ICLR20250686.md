## Problem

Existing machine unlearning (MU) methods for diffusion models require access to real training data, rely on multi-step sampling, and often degrade generation quality for retained classes. No prior approach simultaneously achieves effective forgetting of target classes or concepts and accelerates diffusion sampling to one-step generation in a completely data-free manner.

## Input / Output

The method takes a frozen pretrained diffusion score network (DDPM, EDM, or Stable Diffusion) as the sole supervision source, along with a class label or text prompt to forget (c_f) and a class label or text prompt to override with (c_o). At inference, the resulting one-step generator g_θ consumes a single random noise vector n~N(0,I) and a condition (class label or text prompt) to produce a 32×32 or 64×64 RGB image (or 512×512 for Stable Diffusion) in exactly one forward pass, requiring no real data or multi-step denoising at any point during training or inference.

## Method

SFD formulates MU as a bi-level optimization where a one-step generator g_θ and a fake score network s_ψ are trained alternately, guided by a frozen pretrained score network s_φ. The key loss is a cross-class score distillation term: for remaining classes it enforces s_φ(z_t, c_r, t) ≈ s_ψ(z_t, c_r, t) (distillation), while for the forget class it enforces s_φ(z_t, c_o, t) ≈ s_ψ(z_t, c_f, t) (forgetting), effectively overriding the forget class's score with the override class's score. In practice, the loss is expressed via Tweedie's formula as a combination of a squared denoising-matching term and a correction term (Eq. 9–10) that avoids detrimental biased gradients from the SGD approximation of the optimal score. The entire training loop is data-free: all supervision comes from the pretrained score network evaluated on generator-produced samples corrupted by the forward diffusion process, and the alternating update strategy eliminates the need to solve the inner optimization to convergence at each step.

## Results

On CIFAR-10 class forgetting, SFD achieves 99.64% Unlearning Accuracy (UA) with a FID of 5.35 using only 1 NFE (one-step generation), outperforming the retraining baseline (FID 7.94, 1000 NFEs) and all approximate MU methods. On STL-10, SFD reaches 99.02% UA with FID 18.82 (1 NFE), and SFD-CFG further improves to 99.64% UA and FID 15.32. For concept forgetting on Stable Diffusion, SFD-Two Stage reduces the celebrity detection rate (GCD) to 2.5% for Brad Pitt and 1.06% for Angelina Jolie, and lowers the inappropriate content probability to 11.03% on the I2P nudity benchmark, all while remaining fully data-free.

## Baselines & Comparisons

SFD dominates all baselines on generation quality and speed: on CIFAR-10 it achieves FID 5.35 vs. 12.68 (ESD), 9.08 (SA), and 11.25 (SalUn), while requiring only 1 NFE versus 2000 for all MU baselines. On UA, SFD (99.64%) is competitive with SalUn (99.96%) and far exceeds ESD (91.21%) and SA (85.80%), and slightly surpasses the retraining oracle (98.5%). For celebrity forgetting on Stable Diffusion, SFD-Two Stage reduces the GCD score to 2.5% (Brad Pitt) and 1.06% (Angelina Jolie), beating SA (7.52%/7.74%), ESD-x (2.01%/3.35%), and SLD Medium (0.47%/3.29%) on the proportion-without-faces metric (1.76%/1.92% vs. 5.8%/4.4% for SA). On the I2P nudity benchmark, SFD-Two Stage achieves the lowest inappropriate probability (11.03%) and maximum expected inappropriateness (66.90%), outperforming SLD Medium (14.10%/71.73%) and ESD-u (16.94%/69.68%), though SLD retains a slight edge on CLIP score (30.77 vs. 30.25). The key differentiator is that SFD is the only method that is simultaneously data-free, one-step, and competitive on forgetting accuracy, whereas SalUn and Retrain require real data and ESD/SA require 2000 NFEs.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends Score Identity Distillation (SiD, Zhou et al. 2024) and Diff-Instruct (Luo et al. 2023) by embedding a machine-unlearning objective directly into the score distillation loss, creating a unified framework that simultaneously forgets target classes/concepts and distills a multi-step diffusion model into a one-step generator. Prior MU methods for diffusion (ESD, SA, SalUn, SLD) all operate on standard multi-step samplers and require access to real training data; SFD departs from this by being entirely data-free and producing a one-step generator as a byproduct. The reweighting function and denoising-score-matching surrogate follow Yin et al. (2024) and Zhou et al. (2024), while the cross-class score-alignment idea for forgetting is novel in the distillation context.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

SFD addresses two practical pain points simultaneously: the privacy risk of fine-tuning on real data during MU, and the 1000× sampling cost of multi-step diffusion models, making data-free unlearning and rapid synthetic-data generation feasible in a single training run. The method generalizes across DDPM, EDM, and Stable Diffusion architectures, and the SFD-Two Stage variant offers a practical ~1.5k-step recipe for urgent forgetting requests. Open questions include whether the forgetting is truly irreversible under adversarial prompting (the paper shows SFD is vulnerable to UnlearnDiffAtk without the AdvUnlearn-TE augmentation), how the approach scales to larger models (e.g., SDXL, video diffusion), and whether the one-step generator's reduced representational capacity limits the fidelity of the 'remembered' classes at higher resolutions.
