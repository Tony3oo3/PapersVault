## Problem

Existing long-context VLMs employ simplified strategies—LongVA trains on short-context data with a long-context LLM, and LongVLM uses token compression to avoid context extension—sidestepping the harder problems of building a robust long-context multi-modal training framework and designing appropriate long video datasets. No prior work provides a full-stack solution that co-designs both the training pipeline and the distributed system needed to actually train and serve VLMs at million-token video context lengths.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/ICLR20250658/cropped_page1_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The model consumes video sampled at up to 2048 frames (each frame encoded into ~256 visual tokens by the vision encoder) alongside a text question or captioning prompt, producing a free-form text response. A single 1400-frame video yields roughly 274K tokens, and the needle-in-a-haystack evaluation pushes to 6,000 frames exceeding 1M tokens. The training system supports context lengths up to 2M tokens on 256 GPUs without gradient checkpointing, and the inference system distributes decoding across multiple GPUs to handle sequences 2.9× longer than HuggingFace pipeline parallelism.

## Method

LongVILA introduces a five-stage training curriculum (multi-modal alignment → large-scale pre-training → short SFT → LLM context extension via progressive RoPE-augmented continued pre-training with LongLoRA up to 262K → long video SFT) built on VILA-1.5 with Qwen2 backbones. The key system contribution is Multi-Modal Sequence Parallelism (MM-SP), which adopts a 2D-attention mechanism combining intra-node All-to-All (Ulysses-style head parallelism) with inter-node P2P (Ring-style sequence parallelism) to exploit the 18× bandwidth gap between NVLink and InfiniBand. A two-stage sharding strategy first distributes video frames evenly across the SP group to balance image-encoder workload, then aggregates and re-shards at the token level with a balanced from-both-ends partition for the LLM backbone. This design scales beyond the number of attention heads (unlike Ulysses) while maintaining Ulysses-level throughput (unlike Ring-style SP), achieving 2.1×–5.7× speedup over ring-style SP and 1.1×–1.4× over Megatron's hybrid CP+TP.

## Results

LongVILA-7B achieves 65.1% on VideoMME with subtitle (60.1% without), 58.0% on EgoSchema, 67.1% on MVBench, 80.7% on NExT-QA, and 63.0% on VNBench, outperforming all open-source 7B/8B video LLMs on most benchmarks. In the needle-in-a-haystack experiment, LongVILA trained on 2048 frames achieves 99.8% accuracy at 6,000 frames (>1M tokens), the first VLM to handle 1M+ context length. The MM-SP training system scales to 2M context on 256 GPUs without gradient checkpointing, achieving 2.1×–5.7× speedup over ring-style sequence parallelism and 1.1×–1.4× over Megatron's hybrid context+tensor parallelism.

## Baselines & Comparisons

LongVILA-7B outperforms all open-source 7B/8B video LLMs (Video-LLaVA, ShareGPT4Video, VideoLLaMA2.1, Kangaroo, LLaVA-OV) across nearly all benchmarks, with the largest margins on EgoSchema (58.0 vs. 5.9 for Video-LLaVA) and MVBench (67.1 vs. 61.1 for Kangaroo). Against GPT-4V, LongVILA wins on EgoSchema (58.0 vs. 32.6), MVBench (67.1 vs. 43.5), and VideoMME with subtitle (65.1 vs. 63.3), while trailing on EventBench (57.1 vs. 61.3). However, LongVILA falls short of GPT-4o and Gemini-1.5-Pro on VideoMME with subtitle (65.1 vs. 77.2 and 81.3) and VNBench (63.0 vs. 64.4 and 66.7), indicating that proprietary models still lead on the most demanding long-video comprehension tasks. On the system side, MM-SP achieves 2.1×–5.7× throughput speedup over ZigZag-RingAttention and 1.1×–1.4× over Megatron-LM's hybrid CP+TP strategy on 32 H100 GPUs, while supporting ~8× longer context lengths than DeepSpeed-Ulysses at 256 GPUs due to Ulysses' head-count limitation.

## Relation to Prior Work

LongVILA extends the VILA (Lin et al., 2023b) encoder-decoder VLM architecture by appending two training stages—LLM context extension via RoPE augmentation and long-video supervised fine-tuning—to VILA's original three-stage pipeline. On the systems side, it adapts 2D-Attention (USP/LoongTrain) from the text-only LLM sequence-parallelism literature to the multi-modal setting, addressing modality heterogeneity (placeholder tokens expanding to ~256 real tokens per frame) and network heterogeneity (NVLink vs. InfiniBand) that prior ring-style and Ulysses systems ignore. Unlike LongVA (which trains on short-context data) and LongVLM (which compresses tokens to avoid context extension), LongVILA provides a full-stack solution with purpose-built long-video data and a dedicated distributed training/inference system.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

This is the first open-source VLM to demonstrate reliable retrieval at 1M+ token context (99.8% needle-in-a-haystack at 6,000 frames), establishing a practical reference point for extreme long-video understanding. The MM-SP system's seamless HuggingFace Transformers integration and 2.1–5.7× speedup over ring-style SP make it a likely default for future long-context VLM training, lowering the barrier for the community. Open questions include whether the five-stage pipeline is strictly necessary (ablations in Table 1 show modest sensitivity), whether the approach generalizes beyond the Qwen2 backbone, and how performance degrades when the needle is placed in visually complex or semantically ambiguous regions rather than isolated frames.
