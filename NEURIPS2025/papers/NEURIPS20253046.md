## Problem

Current 3D scene understanding benchmarks treat dynamic scenarios (object changes) and dynamic situations (egocentric/allocentric context) in isolation, leaving no dataset that captures both simultaneously in real-world settings. Additionally, no existing 3D MLLM architecture can effectively compare pairs of highly similar point clouds to detect and describe minor environmental changes, as standard token-prepending approaches drown meaningful differences in redundant shared tokens.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/NEURIPS20253046/cropped_page1_idx2.webp)

## Input / Output

The model receives two point clouds (previous and current scene from the same 3RScan scan pair) plus a natural-language text query, and produces one of three output types: concise QA answers averaging ~2 words across 9 question types (egocentric distance/direction/warning, allocentric displacement/relationship, and general affordance/attribute/existence/counting), long-form change descriptions averaging ~29 words with clockwise-ordered spatial references in meters, or rearrangement instructions averaging ~30 words using step-based navigation language. For 2D MLLM baselines, two rendered panoramas (one from the world origin, one from the current situation) substitute for point clouds.

## Method

Sitat3DChange is constructed from 11K human annotations by seven co-authors experienced in assisting blind users, who labeled every changed object in 3RScan with reason, warning, description, and rearrangement instruction; these are enriched with computed egocentric/allocentric spatial relations and object attributes, then expanded via GPT-4 into 174K paired data instances. SCReasoner, the proposed 3D MLLM, is built on the LEO framework but replaces the standard token-prepending with a selective comparison projector: a Mamba-based module selects informative tokens from the previous scene's point cloud embedding, and a parameter-free star operation (element-wise multiplication) fuses them with current-scene tokens before the language decoder. This design injects only half the scene tokens into the LLM while focusing on differences, introducing minimal additional parameters and no extra tokens for the decoder. The model is fine-tuned with AdamW (lr 3e-5, 5 epochs, 4×A100 40GB) on the combined three-task dataset.

## Results

On the Situat3DChange QA task, SCReasoner (mamba*) achieves 53.8% average GPT-score, outperforming the LEO baseline (51.9%) by +1.9% and the best fine-tuned 2D MLLM InternVL2-7B (54.0%) by a narrow margin. On long-form tasks, SCReasoner (mamba*) scores 13.9 GPT on change description and 30.7 on rearrangement instruction, surpassing LEO (12.7 / 30.1) and dramatically exceeding fine-tuned InternVL2-7B (8.2 / 16.3). All zero-shot and one-shot 2D MLLMs remain far below fine-tuned 3D models, with the best one-shot result (DeepSeek-VL-7B) reaching only 36.8% average QA.

## Baselines & Comparisons

SCReasoner (mamba*) outperforms the LEO baseline on long-form tasks by +1.2% GPT-score on change description (13.9 vs 12.7) and +0.6% on rearrangement instruction (30.7 vs 30.1), while also improving QA average from 51.9% to 53.8%. Against the fine-tuned 2D MLLM InternVL2-7B, SCReasoner is competitive on QA (53.8% vs 54.0%) but dominates on long-form generation (13.9 vs 8.2 GPT on description; 30.7 vs 16.3 on rearrangement), confirming that point-cloud input provides superior allocentric context that panoramas lack. All zero-shot and one-shot 2D MLLMs (DeepSeek-VL, Qwen2.5-VL, LLaVA-Next, Janus, InternVL2) score between 26.3% and 36.8% average QA, far below any fine-tuned 3D model. Among SCReasoner variants, the mamba* configuration (Mamba for token selection + star operation for fusion) consistently edges out linear* on both QA (+0.4%) and long-form GPT scores, validating the selective state-space mechanism for focusing on scene differences. On cross-domain transfer (Table 4), fine-tuning on S3C alone causes catastrophic forgetting on ScanNet benchmarks, but combining S3C with ScanNet data (S3C+SN) recovers and slightly improves all ScanNet metrics while retaining S3C performance.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper builds on the LEO 3D MLLM framework (Huang et al., ICML 2024) and the Mamba selective state-space model (Gu & Dao, COLM 2024) to propose SCReasoner, a token-efficient architecture for comparing paired point clouds. It departs from prior 3D change understanding datasets (Dy2Change, ChangeSim, EmbSCU, PanoSCU), which rely on synthetic data without situational context, and from situated 3D QA datasets (SQA3D, MSQA, Spartun3D), which operate on static scenes. The key conceptual departure is the integration of human-annotated egocentric/allocentric spatial reasoning (inspired by accessibility research for blind users) with 3D change detection, grounded in the 3RScan scan pairs and 3DSSG scene-graph annotations, to create a unified perception-action benchmark.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

Situat3DChange is the first large-scale dataset (174K instances) that simultaneously addresses dynamic 3D scene changes and human-centered situational awareness, filling a gap between robotic (Cartesian) and human (cylindrical) spatial perception that limits current embodied AI. The practical motivation for accessibility (assisting visually impaired individuals with environmental changes) gives the work immediate societal relevance. Key limitations include single-annotator coverage per change, dependence on 3RScan's unavailable test set (restricting evaluation to hold-out splits), and the modest architectural gains of SCReasoner over LEO (~1% on GPT scores), leaving open whether the Mamba+star comparison module is truly necessary or whether simpler fusion suffices at scale.
