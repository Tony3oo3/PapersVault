## Problem

Video diffusion transformers (DiTs) are bottlenecked by quadratic 3D attention, where a 5-second 720p clip expands to over 100K tokens. Although attention matrices are naturally sparse, prior methods treat sparsity as a post-hoc inference patch on full-attention-trained models, leaving training cost untouched and introducing a train-test mismatch. The central challenge is identifying critical tokens without paying the quadratic cost of full attention, while respecting the block-sparse layouts required by modern GPU kernels.

## Input / Output

VSA operates on video latents of shape (T, H, W) flattened into 1D sequences (e.g., 16×32×32 = 16K tokens for ablations, up to 61K for scaling experiments), with dimensions required to be divisible by the cube size (4,4,4). Text prompts are injected via cross-attention (using UMT5-XXL), while sparse attention is applied only to self-attention layers. The output is a generated video; when retrofitting Wan2.1-1.3B, the model produces 480P 61-frame clips, and the 14B variant handles 720P 77-frame sequences.

## Method

VSA is a two-stage hierarchical sparse attention that is end-to-end trainable. The coarse stage mean-pools tokens into (4,4,4) cubes (tile size B=64), computes lightweight cube-level dense attention, and selects the Top-K=32 highest-scoring cubes per query row; the fine stage then performs token-level attention only within those selected cubes using a block-sparse ThunderKittens kernel. The two stage outputs are combined through a differentiable gating mechanism, and the entire pipeline is trained jointly with the DiT via flow-matching loss. Each cube maps directly to a GPU SM tile, ensuring hardware-aligned block-sparse computation that sustains 85% of FlashAttention-3 MFU. For retrofitting pretrained full-attention models, an annealing schedule gradually increases sparsity from zero to the target level while learning the gating weights, avoiding training instability.

## Results

VSA achieves a 2.53× reduction in total training FLOPS (8× in attention FLOPS) with no drop in diffusion loss compared to full attention, validated across model scales from 60M to 1.4B parameters. When retrofitted into Wan2.1-1.3B, VSA reduces end-to-end inference latency from 31s to 18s (1.7×) on H100 while maintaining VBench total score of 82.77% (vs. 83.63% for full-attention fine-tuning and 82.56% for the original model). The block-sparse kernel sustains 85% of FlashAttention-3 MFU, and the Sparse-Distill variant achieves a 50.9× speedup for Wan-1.3B with no quality degradation.

## Baselines & Comparisons

Against SVG (Sparse VideoGen) [37], a training-free spatial-temporal sparsification method at 82.5% sparsity, VSA is preferred in human evaluation despite operating at higher sparsity, demonstrating the advantage of trainable sparse attention over post-hoc profiling. In kernel benchmarks, VSA's block-sparse kernel achieves nearly 7× speedup over FlashAttention-3 at long sequence lengths (85% MFU), while FlexAttention [8] with an identical 64×64 block-sparse mask achieves only 2× speedup, highlighting the importance of the custom ThunderKittens kernel. In scaling experiments (60M–1.4B parameters, up to 4×10²¹ FLOPS), VSA consistently produces a better Pareto frontier than full attention, maintaining the 2.53× FLOPS reduction across all scales. For the Wan-14B model at 720P, human preference studies on 200 MovieGen prompts confirm VSA preserves generation quality relative to the official full-attention model, reducing end-to-end generation time from 1274s to 576s.

## Relation to Prior Work

VSA draws its two-stage (coarse/fine) hierarchical attention design from recent LLM sparse attention work (NSA, MoBA) and the tile-to-tile attention of BiFormer, but departs critically by making the mechanism fully end-to-end trainable for video DiTs rather than a post-hoc inference-time substitution. Unlike prior video DiT sparsity methods (STA, Sparge Attention, SVG) that profile or fix sparse masks after full-attention training, VSA learns critical-token selection from data during training, eliminating the train-test mismatch. The hardware-aligned block-sparse kernel design builds on FlashAttention's tile-based computation model, and the sparse distillation setup extends DMD2 by replacing the student's attention with VSA while keeping the teacher unchanged.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

VSA establishes that trainable sparse attention can match or exceed full attention in scaling behavior for video DiTs, cutting training FLOPS by 2.53× with no quality degradation across 60M–1.4B parameters — a result backed by ~90k H200 hours of experiments. This opens a practical path to scaling video DiTs beyond the quadratic attention bottleneck that currently limits resolution and duration. Key open questions remain: how to determine optimal sparsity (Top-K) as a function of model size, sequence length, and compute budget (potentially as a new axis in scaling laws), whether the fixed (4,4,4) cube size generalizes to arbitrary resolutions, and how VSA behaves at even longer sequences (>100K tokens) where the coarse-stage overhead becomes more pronounced.
