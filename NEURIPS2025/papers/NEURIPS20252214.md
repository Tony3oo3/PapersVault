## Problem

Existing UAV tracking methods predominantly optimize for speed and efficiency while neglecting semantic awareness in the correlation modeling between template and search regions. This oversight causes the search region to fail at extracting accurate localization information from the template, leading to suboptimal performance under typical UAV challenges such as camera motion, fast motion, and low resolution.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/NEURIPS20252214/cropped_page1_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method takes a Template & Patches input (a fixed 128×128 template plus dynamically updated representative patches, following the RFGM scheme) and a 256×256 Search Region frame. Both are projected into tokens via patch embedding with 16× downsampling. The output is a bounding box (center offset and normalized size) predicted from a three-branch head that produces a classification score map, an offset map, and a size map over the search region feature grid.

## Method

DSATrack builds on the one-stream OSTrack Transformer framework and introduces a Dynamic Semantic Relevance Generator that operates on the correlation map between search queries and template keys. The generator reshapes the correlation map into a graph where nodes are template positions, computes pairwise semantic similarity via average-pooled node features, and uses a small MLP with Gumbel-Softmax to differentiably select which semantic relevance edges to retain. The resulting optimized correlation map is injected into a Hybrid Attention module (template self-attention, search self-attention, and cross-attention augmented by the semantic-aware map), while less significant template tokens are pruned via a Gumbel-Softmax mask to reduce computation. For deployment flexibility, a Hierarchical Contribution Ranking Pruning strategy quantifies each Transformer block's importance by the cosine dissimilarity between consecutive layer hidden states, then selectively removes the lowest-contributing blocks from Dynamic Semantic-Aware and Standard layers independently, yielding variants (D8/D7/D6/D4) that trade accuracy for speed.

## Results

DSATrack-ViT-B achieves the best precision and success across all four UAV tracking benchmarks: 91.2%/70.6% on DTB70, 88.1%/66.3% on UAVDT, 87.1%/67.0% on VisDrone2018, and 90.2%/69.4% on UAV123. The pruned variant DSATrack-D8 (8 Transformer layers) retains strong performance (e.g., 90.8%/69.1% on VisDrone2018) while running at 162.4 FPS on RTX 3090, and the lightweight DSATrack-ViT-T reaches 27.6 FPS on Jetson AGX Xavier with 86.6%/67.4% on DTB70. On general-purpose benchmarks (Table 9), DSATrack-ViT-B attains 75.0 AO / 85.6 SR₀.₅ / 73.7 SR₀.₇₅ on GOT-10k and 84.1 AUC on TrackingNet, surpassing OSTrack and LoRAT-B-224.

## Baselines & Comparisons

DSATrack-ViT-B outperforms all 17 compared baselines on every UAV benchmark. The largest margins are on DTB70, where it leads the strongest competitor AVTrack-DeiT (84.3/65.0) by +6.9 precision and +5.6 success, and on UAV123, where it surpasses AVTrack-DeiT (84.8/66.8) by +5.4 precision and +2.6 success. On VisDrone2018, DSATrack-D8 actually edges out DSATrack-ViT-B in success (69.1 vs 67.0), and both exceed AVTrack-ViT (86.4/65.9) by a clear margin. Among lightweight methods, DSATrack-ViT-T (86.6/67.4 on DTB70) beats LightFC-ViT (82.8/64.0) and LiteTrack (82.5/63.9) by 3–4% in precision while using fewer parameters (8.21 M vs 49.59 M for LiteTrack). On general-purpose benchmarks (Table 9), DSATrack-ViT-B surpasses OSTrack (71.0 AO on GOT-10k) by +4.0 AO and LoRAT-B-224 (72.1 AO) by +2.9 AO, confirming that the dynamic semantic-aware modeling generalizes beyond UAV-specific data.

## Relation to Prior Work

DSATrack extends the one-stream Transformer tracking framework established by OSTrack (Ye et al., ECCV 2022) by inserting a Dynamic Semantic Relevance Generator that explicitly models semantic correlations between template and search-region tokens via a Gumbel-Softmax–differentiable graph. The patch-based template representation and training loss are inherited from RFGM (Zhou et al., NeurIPS 2023), while the hierarchical contribution ranking pruning departs from prior sequential or uniform layer-removal strategies by scoring each block's feature-transformation impact before selection. Conceptually, the paper positions itself against the efficiency-driven trend in UAV tracking (AVTrack, LiteTrack, HiT) by arguing that semantic-aware correlation modeling, rather than architectural slimming alone, is the missing ingredient for robustness under camera motion, fast motion, and low resolution.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The framework provides a practical menu of model variants (ViT-B through DeiT-T, 12-layer down to 4-layer) with measured FPS on both RTX 3090 and Jetson AGX Xavier, making it directly deployable for onboard UAV perception where compute is constrained. The ~7-point precision gap over prior SOTA on DTB70 and consistent gains across four UAV benchmarks suggest that explicit dynamic semantic modeling is a meaningful lever beyond what global-attention Transformers capture implicitly. A key limitation acknowledged by the authors is the absence of re-detection: once the target is lost, the tracker cannot recover, leaving global tracking and re-acquisition as an open direction. Whether the dynamic relevance graph generalizes beyond single-object tracking to multi-object or long-horizon aerial surveillance remains untested.
