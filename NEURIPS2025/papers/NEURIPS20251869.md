## Problem

Attention's quadratic complexity is the primary bottleneck for long-context LLM inference, and while attention maps are inherently sparse, existing sparsity-based methods rely on predefined patterns or handcrafted heuristics that fail to adapt dynamically across different models, contexts, and attention heads. No prior approach learns the block-level sparsity structure directly from the model itself in a lightweight, post-training manner.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/NEURIPS20251869/cropped_page2_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The AttnGate consumes pre-RoPE Q and K tensors from each attention head of a frozen pre-trained LLM. It pools them along the sequence dimension with block size 64 (AvgPooling on Q; Max+Min+AvgPooling concatenated on K), producing a compact [seq/64, seq/64] gating score matrix. At inference, this score matrix is binarized via TopK or thresholding into a binary block mask that drives a custom block-sparse FlashAttention kernel, yielding the final attention output computed only over selected blocks.

## Method

SeerAttention augments standard attention with a learnable AttnGate module inspired by MoE gating: pooled Q and K pass through separate linear projections, are re-encoded with block-level RoPE (reduced θ′=θ/B) to preserve positional information across lengths, and their product with softmax produces per-block importance scores. The gate is trained via self-distillation using KL divergence against a 2D-MaxPooled attention map extracted from the original dense model, with only the ~101M gate parameters updated (all other weights frozen) for ~500 steps on 64k-token chunks. A customized Triton-based FlashAttention kernel efficiently produces the max-pooled ground truth without materializing the full quadratic attention matrix, keeping training memory comparable to standard FlashAttention-2. At inference, the learned gate automatically adapts sparsity to context length (e.g., ~10% at 4k to ~85% at 128k) and learns diverse patterns (A-shape, Vertical-Slash, diagonal, random) without any predefined structure, achieving 7.3× kernel-level speedup at 90% sparsity on 128k sequences.

## Results

On LongBench with Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct, SeerAttention achieves 54.20 average accuracy at 50% average sparsity, surpassing the dense full-attention baseline (54.07) and all sparse baselines. On RULER, SeerAttention attains 87.60 average accuracy (only 0.41% below dense) while delivering a 1.41× average end-to-end prefilling speedup, peaking at 2.43× on 128k sequences. At the kernel level, the block-sparse FlashAttention kernel achieves a 7.3× speedup over FlashAttention-2 at 90% sparsity on 128k sequences. The method generalizes across model families (Llama-3.1-70B, Qwen2.5-7B/14B/32B) with >50% sparsity and near-lossless accuracy.

## Baselines & Comparisons

SeerAttention outperforms all three main sparse-attention baselines on both accuracy and efficiency. Against MInference [28], SeerAttention achieves +0.47 avg accuracy on LongBench (54.20 vs 53.73) and +1.68 on RULER (87.60 vs 85.92), while delivering 1.41× vs 0.83× end-to-end speedup (MInference actually slows down below 64k due to runtime index-search overhead). Against MoA [18], SeerAttention gains +3.38 avg accuracy on LongBench (54.20 vs 50.82); MoA was excluded from RULER entirely due to OOM issues. Against DuoAttention [54], SeerAttention leads by +1.80 on LongBench (54.20 vs 52.40) and +0.64 on RULER (87.60 vs 86.96), with a 1.41× vs 1.09× speedup advantage. In the decoding-stage comparison (Appendix A.3), SeerAttention-decoding substantially outperforms Quest [48] under tight token budgets: on AIME24 at 2k tokens, SeerAttention scores 55.78 vs Quest's 25.83, and on MATH-500 at 2k tokens, 87.65 vs 57.40, reflecting the learned gate's superior block-selection coherence.

## Relation to Prior Work

SeerAttention departs from prior sparse-attention methods (MInference, MoA, DuoAttention) that rely on predefined sparsity patterns (Vertical-Slash, A-shape) or hand-crafted heuristics to approximate attention sparsity. Instead, it draws inspiration from the Mixture-of-Experts gating mechanism, introducing a lightweight learnable gate (AttnGate) that predicts block-level sparsity directly from the model's own Q and K tensors. The training paradigm is self-distillation: the gate is distilled against the 2D-MaxPooled attention map of the frozen pre-trained model, requiring only ~101M new parameters and ~40 A100 hours. The block-sparse FlashAttention kernel and the block-level RoPE design for length extrapolation are practical contributions that integrate with existing FlashAttention tiling infrastructure.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

SeerAttention offers a practical path to near-lossless long-context prefilling speedup (7.3× at 90% sparsity on 128k) without requiring per-head manual calibration, which is a significant operational advantage over pattern-based methods. The ability to learn diverse, context-adaptive sparsity patterns (including A-shape, Vertical-Slash, and random) from a single unified gate simplifies deployment across models. Key limitations include: the method currently applies only to the prefill stage (not decoding), requires per-model distillation training, and the accuracy gap at 128k (73.37 vs. 76.26 on RULER) suggests diminishing returns at extreme lengths. Open questions include whether the gate can be extended to the decoding stage for long-CoT reasoning, whether joint fine-tuning with the base model (as shown preliminarily with YaRN) can push sparsity higher without accuracy loss, and how the approach scales to models with >100B parameters.
