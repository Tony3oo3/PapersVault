## Problem

Existing 3D vector graphics generation methods (e.g., 3Doodle, Diff3DS) rely on 2D generative priors and Score Distillation Sampling in projection space, which inherently produces cross-view inconsistency, messy strokes, and missing structural details. These approaches also suffer from lengthy optimization times (2–6 hours per object) because they must iteratively reconcile conflicting per-view gradients without a strong 3D geometric anchor.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/NEURIPS20253271/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method takes a single RGB image as input and produces a set of fewer than 100 cubic 3D Bezier curves (each defined by four 3D control points), which can be perspective-projected to any camera viewpoint and rendered as an SVG file via DiffVG. The intermediate 3D mesh from TripoSG serves as the geometric substrate, and the final vector graphic is evaluated across 12 rendered views using CLIPScore and an aesthetic predictor.

## Method

VC3D operates entirely in 3D space to guarantee view consistency by construction. Stage I (Primary Structure Fitting) reconstructs a mesh via TripoSG, extracts a salient point cloud using Sharp Edge Sampling plus silhouette-edge detection, clusters points via iterative expansion guided by PCA-estimated orientation vectors and spatial proximity thresholds, then fits cubic Bezier curves optimized with a differentiable Chamfer Distance loss. Stage II (Detail Refinement) freezes the Stage I curves, randomly initializes additional curves in mesh regions poorly covered by the initial point cloud, encodes the combined point cloud into TripoSG's VAE latent space, and optimizes only the new curve parameters via SDS loss conditioned on the input image. This two-stage design separates structural accuracy (geometry-driven) from visual quality (diffusion-guided), reducing total runtime to ~30 minutes while outperforming 2D-prior baselines on both CLIPScore and aesthetic metrics.

## Results

VC3D achieves a CLIPScore of 0.799 and an Aesthetic Score of 4.352, outperforming 3Doodle (0.729 / 4.122) and Diff3DS (0.673 / 3.769) on both metrics. In terms of generation speed, VC3D completes in approximately 0.5 hours, compared to ~6 hours for 3Doodle and ~2 hours for Diff3DS. A user study with 30 participants further confirms VC3D's superiority, receiving 71.17% of preference votes versus 13.83% for 3Doodle and 15% for Diff3DS.

## Baselines & Comparisons

VC3D outperforms all three baselines on both reported metrics. Against 3Doodle [Choi et al., TOG 2024], VC3D gains +0.070 in CLIPScore (0.799 vs. 0.729) and +0.230 in Aesthetic Score (4.352 vs. 4.122), while reducing generation time from ~6 hours to ~0.5 hours (12× speedup). Against Diff3DS [Zhang et al., arXiv 2024], the margins are larger: +0.126 in CLIPScore (0.799 vs. 0.673) and +0.583 in Aesthetic Score (4.352 vs. 3.769), with a 4× speedup. Against the concurrent work Dream3DVG [Li et al., 2025] (compared in supplementary material using text prompts derived from the same input images), VC3D achieves a higher Aesthetic Score (4.352 vs. 4.1499). Qualitatively, VC3D produces cleaner strokes with fewer spurious lines and better view consistency across 12 rendered viewpoints, whereas 3Doodle and Diff3DS exhibit messy strokes and cross-view inconsistencies.

## Relation to Prior Work

VC3D departs from the dominant paradigm in 3D vector graphics generation (3Doodle, Diff3DS) that relies on 2D diffusion/CLIP priors with Score Distillation Sampling in 2D projection space, which inherently suffers from cross-view inconsistency. Instead, it grounds the entire pipeline in 3D geometric attributes: mesh reconstruction via TripoSG, Sharp Edge Sampling for salient point extraction, PCA-based clustering inspired by EdgeGaussians, and Bezier curve fitting with Chamfer Distance loss. The 3D SDS refinement stage repurposes TripoSG's VAE encoder as a 3D prior, shifting the optimization from 2D pixel space to 3D latent space. This positions VC3D at the intersection of 3D shape reconstruction, geometric curve fitting, and diffusion-based refinement, drawing on techniques from Dora (edge sampling), EdgeGaussians (orientation-based clustering), and DiffVG (differentiable rendering).

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

By operating directly in 3D space, VC3D addresses the fundamental cross-view inconsistency that plagues 2D-SDS-based methods, achieving view consistency by construction rather than through multi-view regularization. The 4–12× speedup over baselines (0.5h vs. 2–6h) makes 3D vector graphics generation more practical for interactive applications like VR sketching and shape retrieval. However, the evaluation is limited to 40 images with only two main baselines, and the paper acknowledges that individual curves may cross semantic boundaries—a limitation that 3D segmentation could address. Open questions include whether the approach generalizes to highly complex or organic shapes, whether the TripoSG dependency limits flexibility, and whether the method can scale to generating large 3D vector graphics datasets for downstream learning tasks.
