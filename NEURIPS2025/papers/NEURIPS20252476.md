## Problem

Vision Transformers lack explicit spatial adjacency modeling in their positional encodings, while Mamba-based models disrupt 2D spatial structure through 1D scanning and lose global dependency precision by omitting softmax. No prior work has integrated Mamba2's input-dependent structured mask mechanism into ViTs to simultaneously capture spatial adjacency priors and strong global context.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/NEURIPS20252476/cropped_page1_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method consumes standard RGB images (224×224 for ImageNet classification, 1280×800 for COCO detection, 512×512 for ADE20K segmentation) and produces class labels, bounding boxes with instance masks, or pixel-wise segmentation maps. Internally, the input is patchified into 2D token grids (H×W) at four hierarchical resolutions, and the polyline path mask operates on these 2D grids rather than flattened 1D sequences, with separate learned decay factors for horizontal and vertical directions.

## Method

The paper proposes a 2D polyline path scanning strategy that connects each token pair via L-shaped paths (vertical-then-horizontal and horizontal-then-vertical), preserving Manhattan-distance adjacency. From this scanning, a polyline path mask is derived using input-dependent horizontal (α) and vertical (β) decay factors learned by MLPs, combined as a sum of V2H and H2V path weights. A key theoretical contribution proves the 2D mask decomposes into a product of horizontal and vertical 1D structured masks, enabling an efficient algorithm that reduces mask-multiplication complexity from O(N²) to O(N^{3/2}) (or O(N) with Mamba2's chunkwise algorithm). The mask is integrated into ViT self-attention via Hadamard product with the attention map, yielding Polyline Path Masked Vanilla Attention and Polyline Path Masked Criss-Cross Attention. The final architecture is a four-stage hybrid Mamba2-Transformer backbone using criss-cross attention in stages 1–3 and vanilla attention in stage 4, with RoPE, CPE, and LCE positional encodings.

## Results

On ImageNet-1K, PPMA-T/S/B achieve 82.6%/84.2%/85.0% top-1 accuracy, surpassing the closest Transformer baseline RMT-T/S/B (82.4%/84.0%/84.9%) by 0.2%/0.2%/0.1%. On COCO with Mask R-CNN (1× schedule), PPMA-B reaches 51.1% box AP and 45.5% mask AP, outperforming the best SSM baseline MLLA-S (49.2%/44.2%) by 1.9%/1.3%. On ADE20K semantic segmentation (single-scale), PPMA-T/S/B achieve 48.7%/51.1%/52.3% mIoU, exceeding RMT-T/S/B by 0.7%/1.3%/0.3% and the best SSM methods by at least 3.6%/2.5%/1.6%.

## Baselines & Comparisons

Against the closest Transformer competitor RMT, PPMA consistently wins across all three scales and all three tasks: +0.2% top-1 on ImageNet (tiny/small), +0.1% (base), +0.4% box AP on COCO (tiny), and +0.7%/+1.3%/+0.3% mIoU on ADE20K (T/S/B). Against SSM-based backbones, PPMA-S (84.2%) outperforms Spatial-Mamba-T (83.5%) and MLLA-T (83.5%) at comparable FLOPs (~4.5–4.9G), while PPMA-B (85.0%) surpasses Spatial-Mamba-S (84.6%) and MLLA-S (84.4%) in the ~7–10G FLOPs range. On COCO detection, PPMA-B (51.1% box AP) leads the best SSM method MLLA-S (49.2%) by 1.9% and the best Transformer RMT-B (50.7%) by 0.4%. On ADE20K, the gap over SSM methods is largest at the tiny scale (PPMA-T 48.7% vs. 2DMamba-T 48.6% and GrootVL-T 48.5%), while at the small scale PPMA-S (51.1%) leads VMamba-S (50.6%) and Spatial-Mamba-S (50.6%) by 0.5%. The ablation (Table 4) further confirms that the 2D polyline path mask contributes +0.32% top-1 and +0.95% mIoU over the unmasked baseline, and that using separate horizontal/vertical decay factors (α≠β) is critical, as sharing them drops performance by 0.23% top-1.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper bridges two previously separate architectural lines: Mamba2's structured masked attention (derived from the SSD framework) and ViT's softmax self-attention. It departs from Mamba's 1D scanning paradigm by proposing a 2D polyline path scanning strategy that preserves Manhattan-distance adjacency, and from RMT's input-independent RetNet decay mask by using an input-dependent selective mask that captures semantic continuity. The key theoretical departure is proving that the resulting 2D polyline path mask decomposes into two 1D structured masks (horizontal and vertical), enabling an efficient O(N^{3/2}) computation algorithm. This is the first work to integrate Mamba2's structured mask mechanism into ViTs in a plug-and-play manner.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The paper addresses a well-documented gap where SOTA Mamba-based backbones still underperform SOTA Transformer backbones on high-level vision tasks, and provides a principled explanation (1D scanning disruption + weak global dependency modeling) along with a concrete fix. The plug-and-play nature of the polyline path mask across attention variants (vanilla, criss-cross, linear) makes it broadly applicable. However, the paper acknowledges that the efficient algorithm incurs additional GPU memory and lower throughput compared to the baseline, and future CUDA/Triton optimizations are needed. Open questions include whether the 3D extension (sketched in the appendix) scales to video or point cloud tasks, and whether the approach generalizes to other structured mask designs beyond polyline paths.
