## Problem

Previous benchmarks for detecting framing and greenwashing in oil & gas advertising relied exclusively on text, ignoring the visual modality that carries much of the strategic messaging in video ads. No benchmark exists for evaluating vision-language models on identifying framing techniques in O&G video content, limiting both model development and large-scale social science analysis of corporate climate communication.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/NEURIPS20252956/cropped_page1_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

Each video is decomposed into up to N_Frame sampled frames (3–10 depending on model) paired with corresponding Whisper-1 transcript segments, where frames are dynamically selected at the midpoint timestamp of each transcript segment; videos without transcripts (~37% of FACEBOOK) fall back to uniform frame sampling. The output is a JSON list of framing labels drawn from a domain-specific taxonomy: seven fine-grained climate-obstruction labels (CA, CB, GA, GC, PA, PB, SA) for FACEBOOK and six impressionistic labels (Community and Life, Economy and Business, Work, Environment, Green Innovation, Patriotism) for YOUTUBE, making this a multi-label classification task.

## Method

The paper introduces a dual-domain benchmark (320 FACEBOOK videos with distant text-derived labels from prior work, 386 YOUTUBE videos with human-annotated impressionistic labels at Fleiss' Kappa 0.61) spanning 50+ entities across 20 countries and 2010–2025. For evaluation, they construct prompts pairing sampled frames with transcript segments and benchmark six VLMs (GPT-4.1, GPT-4o-mini, Qwen2.5-VL 7B/32B, InternVL2, DeepSeek-VL2) in zero-shot and 1-shot settings. The key technical contribution is an entity-aware 1-shot prompting mechanism that restricts the candidate pool to same-entity training videos (Entity Restriction) and retrieves the most similar example via CLIP embeddings of both frames and transcripts combined with equal weighting (λ=0.5 each), selected by cosine similarity. Ablations show transcript input and entity-aware retrieval each contribute meaningfully, with GPT-4.1 achieving 79% F1 on 'Environment' but only 46% on 'Green Innovation', revealing persistent challenges with implicit and culturally embedded framings.

## Results

On the YOUTUBE domain (1-shot, entity-aware prompting), GPT-4.1 achieves the highest per-label F-scores, reaching 79.4% on Green Innovation, 78.3% on Environment, and 75.0% on Work. Among open-weight models, Qwen2.5-VL 32B leads with a micro-averaged F-score of 66.2% on YOUTUBE and 70.5% on FACEBOOK. On FACEBOOK (1-shot), GPT-4.1 reaches 81.9% F1 on SA and 81.2% on PA, while Qwen2.5-VL 32B achieves 90.3% on GC. The entity-aware 1-shot approach (ES+ER) improves Qwen2.5-VL 32B from 64.0% to 66.2% on YOUTUBE and from 59.1% to 70.5% on FACEBOOK over the no-ES baseline.

## Baselines & Comparisons

This benchmark paper evaluates six VLMs (DeepSeek-VL2 4.5B, InternVL2 8B, Qwen2.5-VL 7B/32B, GPT-4o-mini, GPT-4.1) under zero-shot and 1-shot settings. GPT-4.1 dominates on most individual labels in both domains, achieving 79.4% F1 on Green Innovation (YOUTUBE) and 81.9% on SA (FACEBOOK), though its micro-averaged 'All' score is not reported. Among open-weight models, Qwen2.5-VL 32B is the clear leader, outperforming Qwen2.5-VL 7B by 7.0 points on YOUTUBE (66.2% vs. 59.2%) and 12.3 points on FACEBOOK (70.5% vs. 58.2%) in the 1-shot setting. The entity-aware 1-shot prompting (ES+ER) provides consistent gains over the no-ES baseline for Qwen2.5-VL 32B (+2.2 on YOUTUBE, +11.4 on FACEBOOK), confirming that retrieving entity-matched examples is critical. Notably, GPT-4.1 is the only model where zero-shot outperforms 1-shot on YOUTUBE, suggesting its strong intrinsic framing understanding. Smaller models like DeepSeek-VL2 4.5B show large zero-to-1-shot jumps on FACEBOOK (23.2%→62.3%), indicating that in-context examples are essential for fine-grained climate obstruction labels.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the text-only framing detection benchmark for O&G social media ads (Rowlands et al., 2024; Holder et al., 2023) into the multimodal video domain, which prior work explicitly did not address. It introduces a new YOUTUBE domain with impressionistic framing labels (e.g., 'Green Innovation', 'Community and Life') that capture implicit visual messaging, going beyond the explicit climate-obstruction categories of the FACEBOOK subset. The entity-aware 1-shot prompting mechanism adapts CLIP-based retrieval-augmented prompting (Huang et al., 2024) from image classification to video, combining frame and transcript embeddings for sample selection. The paper is the first to systematically benchmark VLMs (GPT-4.1, Qwen2.5-VL, DeepSeek-VL2, etc.) on video-based framing detection in the energy sector.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

This benchmark provides the first evaluation setting for assessing VLMs on video-based strategic communication analysis, enabling systematic greenwashing detection at scale—a task previously limited to qualitative case studies. The findings reveal that VLMs handle explicit, visually-cued framings well (79% F1 for 'Environment' with GPT-4.1) but struggle substantially with implicit or culturally embedded messages (46% F1 for 'Green Innovation'), highlighting a concrete gap for model development. Key open questions include whether performance improves with longer context windows or native video understanding (vs. frame sampling), how to handle non-English and short-form video content, and whether the entity-aware retrieval approach generalizes beyond 1-shot settings. The dataset's modest size (706 videos) and English-only focus limit generalizability, but it fills a clear gap in multimodal climate communication research.
