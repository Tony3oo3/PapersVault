## Problem

Generating an interactive, explorable, and temporally extendable 360-degree panoramic world from a single image remains unsolved: prior panoramic video methods either require multi-view or full-panoramic inputs, produce one-shot results without temporal continuity, or lack interactive viewpoint control. This paper is the first to unify spatial completeness, user-driven viewpoint rotation, and open-ended temporal extension into a single pipeline from a single-view image.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/NEURIPS20255744/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The pipeline takes a single reference image (padded to 2:1 aspect ratio for equirectangular format), user-specified pitch/yaw rotations, and the previous video chunk as conditioning for continuation. It produces 360-degree equirectangular panoramic video at 2048×1024 resolution, 49 frames per chunk at 16 fps. The process is inherently recursive: each generated chunk can be rotated to a new viewpoint and fed back into the continuation model, enabling arbitrarily long world expansion. A fine-tuned VLM (IaaW-VLM) also consumes the input image to produce a global video prompt plus six view-specific prompts paired with spatial masks.

## Method

The visual world model is a diffusion-based video backbone (CogVideoX with MM-DiT blocks) augmented with two key components: (1) 3D Spherical RoPE, which maps each pixel to latitude/longitude angles and encodes position via a factorized trigonometric basis over (θ, φ, t) at multiple frequencies, preserving rotation-equivariance on the sphere; and (2) multi-view composition, which adds a parallel attention path where view-specific text features from IaaW-VLM are concatenated with visual tokens and modulated by binary view masks for localized 3D full attention, enabling fine-grained spatial control during initialization. Two fine-tuned variants share this architecture: IaaW-IM conditions on a single padded image plus multi-view prompts/masks to synthesize the initial panoramic video, while IaaW-CM conditions on the previous rotated video chunk to autoregressively extend the scene. The three-stage pipeline (initialization → exploration via spherical-coordinate rotation → continuation) is trained on 120K curated equirectangular videos from 360-1M at 2048×1024, fine-tuned on 4×A100 GPUs over three weeks including progressive fine-tuning on an 8K-quality subset.

## Results

On world initialization, IaaW-IM outperforms the FETA baseline across all metrics, achieving SC-View 91.8 vs. 89.4, AQ-View 55.9 vs. 49.9, and VTC-View 3.72 vs. 3.19. On world continuation, IaaW-CM leads 360I2V with SC-View 95.8 vs. 92.5 and MS-View 99.3 vs. 98.9, and surpasses 4K4DGen on SC-View (95.8 vs. 94.1) and AQ-View (49.7 vs. 46.0). For the full pipeline, IaaW-IM+CM achieves SC-View 91.0 vs. 81.0 for FETA+360I2V, demonstrating a 10-point advantage in subject consistency on the concatenated world video.

## Baselines & Comparisons

The paper compares against four baselines across three evaluation settings. Against 4K4DGen (the only external published method with released results), IaaW-CM wins on SC-View (95.8 vs. 94.1), AQ-View (49.7 vs. 46.0), and VTC-View (3.26 vs. N/A, since 4K4DGen is text-free), while 4K4DGen edges ahead on MS-View (99.2 vs. 99.3 is a near-tie) and SC-All (95.1 vs. 97.2 — actually IaaW-CM wins here too). Against the self-implemented FETA baseline (Diffusion360 expansion + 360I2V animation), IaaW-IM wins decisively on initialization: SC-View +2.4, AQ-View +6.0, VTC-View +0.53. Against 360I2V on continuation, IaaW-CM gains +3.3 on SC-View and +0.4 on MS-View. On the full pipeline, IaaW-IM+CM dominates FETA+360I2V by +10.0 on SC-View and +0.11 on VTC-View, with the gap driven primarily by the decoupled spatial-then-temporal generation in FETA causing fragmentation between stages.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper builds on CogVideoX's MM-DiT diffusion backbone and extends rotary positional encoding (RoPE) from 1D/2D to a unified 3D spherical formulation, enabling geometry-aware positional signals for equirectangular video. It departs from prior panoramic video generation work (360DVD, 4K4DGen, DynamicScaler, VideoPanda) that either requires panoramic/multi-view input, generates one-shot clips without temporal continuity, or lacks interactive viewpoint control. The three-stage pipeline (initialization → exploration → continuation) and the multi-view composition conditioning mechanism represent a deliberate architectural departure from decoupled spatial-then-temporal approaches like the FETA baseline, unifying spatial completeness and temporal dynamics in a single generative process.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

This is the first framework to produce a user-controllable, temporally expandable 360-degree panoramic world from a single image, opening a practical path toward prompt-driven VR/AR and interactive gaming content creation without expensive multi-view capture. The 3D Spherical RoPE and multi-view composition are reusable components for any spherical video generation task. Key open questions remain: long-term temporal coherence degrades beyond ~1 minute of continuation, complex multi-object scenes (urban streets, crowds) still fail, and the ~17-minute-per-chunk inference cost on a single A100 limits real-time interactivity. Whether autoregressive continuation can be made stable over truly unbounded horizons is a core challenge shared across the video generation field.
