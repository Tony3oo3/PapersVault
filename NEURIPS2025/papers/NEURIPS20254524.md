## Problem

Existing diffusion-based video generators rely on bidirectional attention, which violates the causal structure needed for autoregressive long-range generation and causes severe quality degradation when extrapolating beyond training sequence lengths. Meanwhile, discrete-token autoregressive approaches introduce lossy compression that degrades generation fidelity. The paper addresses how to unify autoregressive causality with continuous-space diffusion for temporally coherent long video synthesis while keeping training and inference computationally tractable.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/NEURIPS20254524/cropped_page1_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

For video generation, the model takes a short sequence of initial frames (e.g., 5 or 13 frames) encoded into continuous latents via WanVAE (4 pixel frames compressed to 1 latent frame) and autoregressively produces subsequent denoised latent frames (e.g., 16 frames), decoded back to pixel space at 256×256 or 192×320 resolution. For few-shot tasks, the input is a source image concatenated with two (source, target) demonstration pairs along the sequence dimension, and the output is the transformed image (colorized, depth-estimated, style-transferred, etc.) generated in a single forward pass without any task-specific adapter module.

## Method

GPDiT performs frame-wise autoregressive diffusion in continuous latent space: each noisy frame is denoised conditioned on all preceding clean frames via a causal attention mask, while full bidirectional attention is preserved within each frame. Two architectural innovations improve efficiency: (1) a lightweight causal attention variant (OF2) that eliminates attention score computation between clean frames, reducing training FLOPs by roughly half and removing the O(2F) KV-cache memory overhead at inference; and (2) a parameter-free rotation-based time conditioning that reinterprets the variance-preserving forward diffusion step as an orthogonal rotation in the (data, noise) 2D plane, replacing adaLN-Zero's per-block MLP with a simple inverse rotation applied to each block's output. The model is trained with a standard noise-prediction diffusion loss under the causal mask, and at inference it generates frames sequentially using classifier-free guidance, with the causal structure enabling arbitrary-length extrapolation beyond the training horizon.

## Results

On MSR-VTT (zero-shot, 12-frame generation conditioned on 5 input frames), GPDiT-H achieves FID 7.4 and FVD 68, while GPDiT-H-LONG further improves FVD to 64. On UCF-101 (zero-shot), GPDiT-H-LONG attains FID 7.9, FVD 218, and IS 66.6, substantially outperforming all prior methods. In the UCF-101 fitting setting (Table 3), the 80M-parameter GPDiT-B-OF2 achieves FVD 214, competitive with the 130M FAR model (FVD 194) while using fewer parameters.

## Baselines & Comparisons

On MSR-VTT, GPDiT-H (FID 7.4, FVD 68) outperforms the closest competitor SnapVideo (FID 8.5, FVD 110) by 38% in FVD, and dramatically surpasses DreamVideo (FVD 149), PixelDance (FVD 381), and MagicVideo (FVD 998). On UCF-101, GPDiT-H-LONG (FID 7.9, FVD 218, IS 66.6) beats all baselines; the nearest competitor PixelDance achieves FVD 243 and IS 42.1, while SnapVideo reaches FVD 260. In the UCF-101 fitting setting (Table 3), GPDiT-B-OF2 (80M params, FVD 214) outperforms ExtDM-K2 (394), ACDiT (376), OmniTokenizer (314), and MAGI (297), but trails FAR (130M params, FVD 194) by 20 FVD points. GPDiT-H also achieves the highest IS (66.5–66.6) on UCF-101, exceeding CogVideo (50.5) and PixelDance (42.1) by a wide margin, indicating superior frame-level quality and diversity.

## Relation to Prior Work

GPDiT sits at the intersection of two active research threads: causal-attention video diffusion (Vid-GPT, ACDiT, FAR, Taming Teacher Forcing) and diffusion-forcing-style asynchronous noise scheduling. It builds directly on the DiT architecture (Peebles & Xie, ICCV 2023) but makes two deliberate departures: replacing the parameter-heavy adaLN-Zero conditioning with a parameter-free rotation in the data-noise plane, and introducing a lightweight causal attention variant that eliminates clean-frame-to-clean-frame attention to halve training compute. Unlike discrete-token AR video models (VideoPoet, OmniTokenizer) that suffer from lossy tokenization, GPDiT operates entirely in continuous latent space, and unlike diffusion-forcing methods that inject independent per-frame noise levels, it uses synchronized frame-wise noise with strict causal masking.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The parameter-free rotation-based time conditioning is a clean architectural simplification that removes roughly 28% of conditioning parameters while matching DiT-level performance, making it a drop-in replacement for any DiT-based pipeline. The demonstrated positive correlation between generative quality (FVD) and linear-probing accuracy suggests that autoregressive diffusion pre-training can serve as a unified foundation for both generation and representation, potentially reducing the need for separate discriminative pre-training. However, the paper is limited to video-only modality, the few-shot learning results are qualitative with only 20 samples per task, and the authors acknowledge resource constraints prevented scaling beyond 2B parameters—leaving open whether the in-context learning potential they speculate about actually emerges at larger scale.
