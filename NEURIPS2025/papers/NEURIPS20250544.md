## Problem

Existing 3D morphing methods are largely limited to untextured geometric transformations, relying on manual correspondence matching and deformation trajectory estimation that generalize poorly to unseen data. Textured 3D morphing—producing smooth transitions that simultaneously deform shape and blend high-frequency appearance details—remains largely under-explored, and the absence of large-scale paired 3D datasets with continuous shape-and-texture transitions makes supervised approaches infeasible.

## Input / Output

The method accepts a pair of source and target prompts, each of which can be either an RGB image (encoded via DINOv2) or a text description (encoded via CLIP). It outputs a discrete sequence of J+1 textured 3D meshes (J=6 by default) forming a smooth morphing trajectory, where the first and last frames correspond to the source and target respectively. The underlying Trellis backbone also supports 3D Gaussian Splatting and NeRF output formats, and a single morphed frame is generated in approximately 30 seconds on an A100 GPU.

## Method

WUKONG is a training-free framework built on the pretrained Trellis rectified-flow transformer, exploiting the deterministic (ODE-based) nature of flow models to guarantee continuous intermediate states. For shape interpolation, it formulates the problem as a free-support 2-Wasserstein barycenter over the condition token distributions, solved via linear programming, and introduces a sequential initialization strategy that seeds each step's barycenter optimization with the previous step's solution to prevent geometric collapse. For texture, a similarity-guided selective interpolation mechanism compares cosine similarity between barycenter tokens and source/target tokens, retaining source high-frequency details only where semantic alignment exceeds a user-tunable threshold τ, thereby enabling identity-preserving texture morphing without forcing static tokens onto deforming geometry. The entire pipeline requires no paired training data and operates entirely in the latent condition space before the flow model's cross-attention layers.

## Results

On the primary textured 3D morphing benchmark, WUKONG achieves FID 4.01, STP-GPT 1.00, SEP-GPT 1.00, PPL 2.91, and V-CLIP 0.90, outperforming all baselines across every metric. The closest competitor, 3DRM, scores FID 6.36 and V-CLIP 0.84, while 2D image morphing methods (DiffMorpher, AID, MV-Adapter, Luma) show substantially worse fidelity (FID 95–218). On the SHREC07 shape-morphing benchmark, WUKONG achieves Dirichlet energy 4.52 and Coverage 0.851, beating the best shape-only method SRIF (6.47 / 0.642) by a wide margin.

## Baselines & Comparisons

WUKONG outperforms all 11 baselines on every reported metric. Against the two 3D textured morphing methods, it beats 3DRM (Yang et al., 2025) by 37% in FID (4.01 vs 6.36) and by 0.06 in V-CLIP (0.90 vs 0.84), while also achieving perfect STP-GPT and SEP-GPT scores of 1.00 versus 0.93/0.88. MorphFlow (Tsai et al., 2022) is far behind with FID 147.70 and V-CLIP 0.78. The four 2D image morphing baselines (DiffMorpher, AID, MV-Adapter, Luma) show the largest gaps, with FID values ranging from 95 to 218, confirming that 2D-only approaches lack 3D structural consistency. On the shape-only SHREC07 benchmark, WUKONG achieves Dirichlet energy 4.52 and Coverage 0.851, surpassing SRIF (6.47 / 0.642), SmoothShells (14.02 / 0.628), BIM (12.47 / 0.467), MapTree (17.73 / 0.397), and NeuroMorph (22.05 / 0.110). The paper further validates that gains are algorithmic rather than backbone-dependent by re-implementing its method on the GaussianAnything backbone (used by 3DRM), where it still outperforms 3DRM (FID 5.15 vs 6.36).

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper builds on the Trellis flow-based 3D generator (Xiang et al., 2024) as its backbone and repurposes the free-support Wasserstein barycenter solver (Lindheim, 2023) as the core interpolation mechanism, replacing the linear latent-space interpolation used by 2D morphing methods like DiffMorpher and AID. It departs from classical 3D morphing pipelines that require explicit correspondence matching and deformation trajectory estimation, and from recent learning-based 3D morphing (3DRM, MorphFlow) that rely on parameter fusion or in-domain training. The key conceptual departure is formulating shape interpolation as an optimal transport problem in the condition-token space of a deterministic flow model, augmented with a sequential initialization strategy and a similarity-guided selective mechanism for texture control.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

Textured 3D morphing was largely under-explored relative to shape-only morphing, and this paper provides the first training-free framework that handles both geometry and texture simultaneously without requiring paired 3D training data. The OT barycenter formulation offers a principled alternative to ad-hoc linear interpolation, and the selective texture interpolation gives users fine-grained control over identity preservation. Open questions include whether the approach can handle extreme topological changes (splitting/merging parts), how it scales to scene-level morphing, and whether the barycenter formulation transfers to other generative paradigms beyond rectified flow transformers.
