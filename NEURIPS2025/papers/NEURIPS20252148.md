## Problem

Existing algorithms for the Stochastically Extended Adversarial (SEA) model, which bridges adversarial and stochastic online convex optimization, require prior knowledge of the domain diameter D and the Lipschitz constant G to set their step-sizes. This paper develops the first parameter-free algorithms for the SEA model that achieve regret bounds scaling with the cumulative stochastic variance σ²_{1:T} and cumulative adversarial variation Σ²_{1:T} without requiring either parameter, even when the decision set is unbounded.

## Input / Output

At each round t, the algorithm receives a stochastic gradient g_t = ∇f_t(x_t) sampled from a distribution D_t, an optimistic prediction m_t (typically the previous gradient), and a range hint z_t bounding ‖g_t − m_t‖₂. It outputs a decision point x_t in a potentially unbounded convex set X, with performance measured by expected regret against any fixed comparator u. The regret bounds are expressed in terms of problem-dependent quantities (σ²_{1:T}, Σ²_{1:T}, ‖u‖₂) rather than the time horizon T, and the algorithms handle the case where D = ∞.

## Method

The paper introduces OONS (Optimistic Online Newton Step) with adaptive step-sizes as the base algorithm, whose second-order Hessian-based structure limits D-dependence to only the term D(z_T − z_1), unlike OMD which incurs D·√(Σ‖g_t−m_t‖²). For the comparator-adaptive case (unknown D, known G), CA-OONS runs N = ⌈log T⌉ base OONS learners on nested bounded domains X_j = {x: ‖x‖₂ ≤ 2^j} and combines them via a three-layer MsMwC meta-algorithm, yielding regret Õ(‖u‖₂² + ‖u‖₂(√σ²_{1:T} + √Σ²_{1:T})). For the fully parameter-free case (both D and G unknown), CLA-OONS adds a gradient-clipping reduction with a doubling schedule on the domain diameter estimate D_t, resetting the OONS matrix A_t upon each doubling, achieving regret Õ(‖u‖₂²(√σ²_{1:T} + √Σ²_{1:T}) + ‖u‖₂⁴ + √(σ_{1:T} + 𝔊_{1:T})).

## Results

The paper establishes three main regret bounds for the SEA model. With known D and G, OONS achieves Õ(√σ²_{1:T} + √Σ²_{1:T}), matching the state-of-the-art up to logarithmic factors. The comparator-adaptive CA-OONS (unknown D, known G) achieves Õ(||u||²₂ + ||u||₂(√σ²_{1:T} + √Σ²_{1:T})), the first parameter-free bound for the SEA model. The fully parameter-free CLA-OONS (both D and G unknown) achieves Õ(||u||²₂(√σ²_{1:T} + √Σ²_{1:T}) + ||u||⁴₂ + √(σ_{1:T} + 𝔊_{1:T})), retaining the same dependence on the cumulative stochastic variance and adversarial variation.

## Baselines & Comparisons

The paper compares against two main baselines: OFTRL/OMD from Sachs et al. [8] and Chen et al. [12], which achieve O(√σ²_{1:T} + √Σ²_{1:T}) but require prior knowledge of both the domain diameter D and Lipschitz constant G. The proposed OONS matches this bound up to logarithmic factors (Õ vs. O) when D and G are known, while the key contribution is that CA-OONS and CLA-OONS remove the need for D and G entirely, at the cost of introducing ||u||₂-dependent terms. Compared to the parameter-free mirror descent of Jacobsen & Cutkosky [27], which yields a bound in terms of the weaker σ̃²_{1:T} (supremum outside expectation), the paper's OONS-based approach achieves the strictly better σ²_{1:T} scaling (supremum inside expectation), which can be a factor of Θ(T) tighter as shown in Appendix A. The CLA-OONS bound, however, carries a larger ||u||₂² multiplier on the leading variance terms compared to the ideal Õ(||u||₂(√σ² + √Σ²)) target, which the authors identify as an open challenge.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper builds on two main threads: the SEA model introduced by Sachs et al. [8] (which bridges adversarial and stochastic OCO via cumulative stochastic variance σ²_{1:T} and adversarial variation Σ²_{1:T}), and the parameter-free online learning framework using multi-scale base-learner ensembles with MsMwC meta-algorithms from Chen et al. [10]. The key methodological departure is replacing the standard Optimistic Mirror Descent (OMD) base algorithm with Optimistic Online Newton Step (OONS) using adaptive step-sizes, whose second-order structure naturally eliminates explicit dependence on the domain diameter D (only a D(z_T − z_1) term remains), making it amenable to unbounded domains. Prior parameter-free OCO results (e.g., [25, 27, 28]) achieve bounds scaling with √T, but the SEA model requires the finer σ²_{1:T} and Σ²_{1:T} dependence, which the paper shows is achievable without knowing D or G.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

This is the first work to provide parameter-free regret guarantees for the SEA model, removing the practical barrier of requiring oracle knowledge of domain diameter and Lipschitz constants. The comparator-adaptive bound Õ(‖u‖² + ‖u‖(√σ²_{1:T} + √Σ²_{1:T})) is a natural extension of the known-parameter result, but the fully parameter-free (CLA-OONS) bound carries a ‖u‖² multiplier on the leading term, which the authors argue may be unavoidable for gradient-variation regret in unconstrained settings (paralleling the d₀² term in accelerated offline optimization). Open questions include whether a single algorithm can simultaneously achieve both the gradient-variation bound and the worst-case Õ(‖u‖G√T) bound, reducing gradient queries from O(log T) to O(1), and extending to high-probability guarantees.
