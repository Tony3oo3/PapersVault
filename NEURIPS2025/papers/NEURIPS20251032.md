## Problem

Existing 3D molecule generation methods maintain separate latent spaces for invariant (atom types, bonds) and equivariant (3D coordinates) modalities, which complicates model architecture, reduces training and sampling efficiency, and risks compromising inter-modality consistency. Additionally, prior latent diffusion approaches largely overlook reconstruction fidelity, allowing small VAE errors to propagate into the diffusion model and produce invalid or unstable 3D structures.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/NEURIPS20251032/cropped_page1_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

For de novo generation, the model takes random Gaussian noise as input; for conditional generation, it additionally takes target quantum property values (e.g., HOMO/LUMO energy, dipole moment) as conditioning vectors. The molecule size (number of atoms) is sampled from the training distribution before generation. The output is a complete 3D molecular structure comprising atom types, bond types, and 3D Cartesian coordinates, decoded from a unified latent token sequence (one token per atom, 16-dimensional) via the VAE decoder.

## Method

UAE-3D is a multi-modal VAE whose encoder is a Relational Transformer (R-Trans) that jointly processes atom-wise embeddings (concatenating 3D coordinates and atomic features) and edge-wise embeddings (Gaussian basis functions of inter-atomic distances plus bond features) into unified latent tokens. The decoder is a standard Transformer with three MLP heads predicting atom types, bond types, and coordinates, trained with a reconstruction loss that includes a bonded-distance term weighting bonded atom pairs more heavily. Crucially, rather than using equivariant architectures, UAE-3D learns SE(3) equivariance through random rotation and translation augmentations applied to input coordinates during training, inspired by the 'bitter lesson' of 3D molecules. UDM-3D then applies a Diffusion Transformer (DiT) with adaptive LayerNorm conditioned on diffusion timestep (and optionally property embeddings via classifier-free guidance) to perform generative modeling directly on the unified latent space, requiring no molecular or geometric inductive bias in the diffusion backbone.

## Results

On GEOM-Drugs, UDM-3D achieves an FCD of 0.692, a 72.6% reduction over the previous best (JODO, 2.523), while also achieving the best V&U&N (0.907), SNN (0.525), and scaffold similarity (0.540). In 3D geometric metrics, UDM-3D reduces bond length MMD by 88.4% (9.89E-03 vs. 8.49E-02 for JODO), bond angle MMD by 55.6%, and dihedral angle MMD by 74.0% over the prior best. On QM9, UDM-3D achieves FCD 0.130 (5.8% better than JODO's 0.138) and V&U&N of 0.950. For conditional generation on QM9, UDM-3D attains the lowest MAE in 5 out of 6 quantum properties, reducing average MAE by 42.2% over GeoLDM.

## Baselines & Comparisons

UDM-3D outperforms all 11 baselines on the primary 2D metric FCD across both datasets: on GEOM-Drugs it achieves 0.692 vs. JODO (2.523), EQGAT-diff (5.898), and MiDi (7.054); on QM9 it reaches 0.130 vs. JODO (0.138) and SemlaFlow (0.863). In 3D geometric fidelity, UDM-3D dramatically surpasses GeoLDM (bond length MMD 9.89E-03 vs. 3.91E-01, a 25× improvement) and EDM (9.89E-03 vs. 4.29E-01) on GEOM-Drugs, and beats JODO (7.04E-02 vs. 1.48E-01) and MDM (7.04E-02 vs. 2.74E-01) on QM9. For conditional generation, UDM-3D achieves the lowest MAE in 5/6 properties, beating JODO (the prior best) by 3.98% on dipole moment and 6.57% on HOMO-LUMO gap, while slightly trailing JODO on polarizability α (+8.4%). ADiT, a concurrent unified-latent approach, shows substantially weaker 3D stability (AtomStable 0.211 vs. UDM-3D's 0.993) and higher reconstruction RMSD (0.3598 Å vs. 0.0002 Å), confirming that near-lossless compression is critical. UDM-3D also trains 2.7× faster than JODO and samples 7.3× faster than EDM/GeoLDM, making it the most efficient method among all compared baselines.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the latent diffusion model (LDM) paradigm from image generation to 3D molecular generation, but departs fundamentally from prior 3D molecular LDMs (GeoLDM, PepGLAD) that maintain separate latent spaces for equivariant (3D coordinates) and invariant (atom/bond types) modalities. UAE-3D unifies all modalities into a single latent space using a Relational Transformer encoder, and—drawing on the 'bitter lesson' philosophy—replaces baked-in SE(3) equivariant architectures with SE(3) data augmentations that teach the network equivariance empirically. The diffusion backbone is a general-purpose DiT with no molecular inductive bias, in contrast to prior methods that require equivariant diffusion processes or interleaved multi-modality generation.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The near-lossless reconstruction (100% atom/bond accuracy, 2×10⁻⁴ Å RMSD) addresses a largely overlooked failure mode in prior molecular VAEs, and the 72.6% FCD reduction on GEOM-Drugs plus 7.3× sampling speedup make this a practically significant advance for drug discovery pipelines. The conceptual finding that SE(3) data augmentation suffices in place of equivariant architectures simplifies the design space for future 3D molecular models. Open questions include scaling to larger molecules (current size is sampled from a fixed distribution), extending to structure-based drug design, and whether the unified latent space can be jointly modeled with text for language-guided generation.
