## Problem

Condition transfer—transporting a sample from one conditional distribution to another—remains unsolved for continuous conditions where each condition value may correspond to only a single observation (non-grouped data). Existing all-to-all methods such as Multimarginal SI and Extended Flow Matching require grouped data with many samples per condition, making them inapplicable to scientific settings like molecular property optimization where conditions are continuous and sparsely observed.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/NEURIPS20253863/cropped_page1_idx1.webp)

## Input / Output

The method takes a source sample x drawn from P_{c_src}, a source condition c_src, and a target condition c_tgt (both conditions are vectors, e.g., 32-dim molecular property embeddings), and produces a transported sample in P_{c_tgt} by integrating a learned ODE from t=0 to t=1. Unlike prior methods that require pre-partitioned condition bins, A2A-FM operates directly on raw (x, c) pairs from any i.i.d. dataset, handling both grouped and non-grouped data without discretization of the condition space.

## Method

A2A-FM learns a (c1, c2)-parameterized vector field v(x, t | c1, c2) via flow matching, where the supervisory paths are constructed from a novel minibatch coupling. The key innovation is the coupling cost (Eq. 11), which minimizes the sum of data distances plus a β-weighted condition distance for both the source and target conditions simultaneously—distinguishing it from conditional OT, which only penalizes one condition. Two independent minibatches are drawn from the dataset, and the optimal permutation π* over the joint (x, c1, c2) space defines linear interpolation paths for training. A theoretical guarantee (Theorem A.2) proves that as β→∞ and N→∞, the induced coupling converges to the pairwise optimal transport between P_{c1} and P_{c2} for almost every (c1, c2) pair. In practice, β = N^{1/(2d_c)} provides a stable heuristic balancing condition alignment against data transport quality.

## Results

On the QED nearby-sampling task (500K ZINC22 subset), A2A-FM achieves a 97.5% success rate, surpassing the previous SOTA COATI-LDM (95.6%) and MolMIM (94.6%). On the LogP-TPSA all-to-all condition transfer benchmark, A2A-FM attains an AUC of 0.990, substantially outperforming OT-CFM (0.819), Multimarginal SI (0.583), and Partial Diffusion with CFG (0.450 at T=300). On synthetic non-grouped data, A2A-FM achieves the lowest MSE from ground-truth pairwise OT at (1.51±0.17)×10⁻², compared to (4.90±0.28)×10⁻² for Multimarginal SI and (6.77±0.14)×10⁻² for Partial Diffusion.

## Baselines & Comparisons

A2A-FM outperforms all seven baselines across every evaluated task. On the QED nearby-sampling task, it beats COATI-LDM (95.6%) by 1.9 points, MolMIM (94.6%) by 2.9 points, QMO (92.8%) by 4.7 points, and DESMILES (76.9%) by 20.6 points. On the LogP-TPSA AUC benchmark, A2A-FM (0.990) dominates OT-CFM (0.819) by a wide margin, and far exceeds Multimarginal SI (0.583) and all Partial Diffusion variants (0.027–0.450). On synthetic grouped data, A2A-FM's coupling (MSE 5.81×10⁻²) is closer to true pairwise OT than the Generalized Geodesic baseline (1.03×10⁰), though the latter has lower variance. On non-grouped synthetic data, A2A-FM (1.51×10⁻²) roughly halves the error of Multimarginal SI (4.90×10⁻²) and reduces Partial Diffusion's error (6.77×10⁻²) by over 4×. The key differentiator is that A2A-FM is the only method that handles non-grouped data natively without discretizing the condition space, which is essential for continuous-valued conditions in molecular property optimization.

## Relation to Prior Work

A2A-FM extends the Flow Matching + minibatch OT framework (OT-CFM) to the all-to-all condition transfer setting, drawing its coupling cost design from Conditional Optimal Transport (COT) theory. However, it explicitly departs from COT's objective: while COT learns a per-condition transport from a fixed source to a target, A2A-FM's cost (Eq. 11) simultaneously couples all pairs of conditions within two minibatches, enabling arbitrary (c₁,c₂) transfers. Prior all-to-all methods (Multimarginal SI, Extended Flow Matching, Meta Flow Matching) all require grouped data where each condition has many samples; A2A-FM's key conceptual departure is handling non-grouped data with continuous conditions, where each condition may correspond to a single observation.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The paper closes a practical gap for scientific applications (e.g., molecular property optimization) where continuous conditions naturally yield non-grouped data that prior all-to-all methods cannot handle. The theoretical guarantee (convergence to pairwise OT in the infinite-sample limit) provides a clean foundation, and the state-of-the-art results on QED and LogP-TPSA nearby sampling demonstrate practical value. The main open limitation is the β hyperparameter trade-off: large β enforces condition-matching but may degrade OT accuracy, while small β shares information across conditions but reduces terminal precision; the heuristic β = N^{1/(2d_c)} works empirically but lacks a full theoretical characterization. Cycle consistency (T_{c₂→c₁}∘T_{c₁→c₂}=id) is not guaranteed by the method, though antisymmetry regularization empirically helps.
