## Problem

Reference-driven image completion struggles when the target view differs significantly from reference images in viewpoint, occlusion, or lighting. Existing generative methods like RealFill rely solely on diffusion priors without explicit geometric cues such as camera pose or depth, often producing hallucinated structures or spatially misaligned completions. The key gap is the absence of 3D geometric guidance within the diffusion process itself.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/NEURIPS20251184/cropped_page1_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method takes 1–5 reference images of the same scene, a target image with a binary mask indicating missing regions, and a text prompt describing dynamic objects (user-provided or LLM-generated via ChatGPT-4o). All images are resized to 512×512 for the diffusion model and 518×518 for VGGT geometry estimation. The output is a completed target image where missing regions are synthesized while preserving geometric consistency with the scene; the method uses per-scene LoRA fine-tuning (2,000 iterations, batch size 16) rather than a single forward pass.

## Method

GeoComplete introduces a dual-branch diffusion architecture built on Stable Diffusion 2 Inpainting (fine-tuned with rank-8 LoRA per scene). The target branch encodes the masked image, while the cloud branch encodes a projected 3D point cloud generated by VGGT (joint camera/depth estimation) after LangSAM filters dynamic objects. A structured joint self-attention mechanism concatenates both branches' token sequences and applies a mask that links each target token to its spatially corresponding cloud token, ensuring geometric cues reach masked regions even when visual information is absent. A target-aware masking strategy projects the target view into each reference to identify 'informative regions' (visible in references but absent in the target), then selectively masks only those regions during training so the model learns from complementary rather than redundant cues. A conditional cloud masking mechanism randomly pads the point cloud with white fill values to prevent over-reliance on potentially inaccurate geometry.

## Results

On RealBench, GeoComplete achieves a PSNR of 17.32 dB, SSIM of 0.578, and LPIPS of 0.197, outperforming the strongest baseline RealFill (14.78 dB PSNR, 0.424 SSIM, 0.431 LPIPS) by 17.1% in PSNR. On the high-level metrics, GeoComplete reaches DINO 0.986 and CLIP 0.987, and scores 4.61/5 in the QualBench user study, the highest among all methods. The method also dominates on DreamSim (0.036 vs. 0.077 for RealFill), indicating substantially better perceptual fidelity.

## Baselines & Comparisons

GeoComplete outperforms all eight baselines on every reported metric. Against the closest competitor RealFill [40], it gains +2.54 dB PSNR (17.32 vs. 14.78), +0.154 SSIM, and reduces LPIPS by 0.234, while also leading on DreamSim (0.036 vs. 0.077), DINO (0.986 vs. 0.948), CLIP (0.987 vs. 0.962), and user study (4.61 vs. 3.98). Against the geometry-aware TransFill [49], the PSNR gap is +4.04 dB, reflecting the benefit of VGGT-based point clouds over TransFill's warping pipeline. Prompt-based methods (SD Inpaint [37], Generative Fill [2]) and the single-reference Paint-by-Example [44] trail by 5–7 dB in PSNR, confirming that text prompts and single-image CLIP embeddings cannot match multi-view geometric conditioning. In the appendix comparison (Table 4) against reference-guided editing models Step1X-Edit [23], OmniGen [43], and Bagel [11], GeoComplete leads by 7–9 dB PSNR across all three scene subsets, further validating the advantage of explicit 3D geometric guidance over purely generative editing approaches.

## Relation to Prior Work

GeoComplete extends the per-scene LoRA fine-tuning paradigm of RealFill by injecting explicit 3D geometric conditioning into the diffusion process. While RealFill and Paint-by-Example rely solely on masked reference images and CLIP embeddings for guidance, and traditional geometry-based methods (TransFill, Photo Uncrop, GeoFill) use fragile sequential pipelines of pose estimation, depth reconstruction, and warping, this paper unifies geometry and generation by conditioning a dual-branch diffusion model on projected point clouds from VGGT. The target-aware masking strategy further departs from RealFill's random masking by selectively occluding only informative (non-redundant) reference regions identified via 3D projection, directing the model toward complementary cues.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The 17.1% PSNR gain over RealFill demonstrates that explicit geometric priors are a critical missing ingredient in reference-driven completion, particularly under large viewpoint changes where image-only diffusion hallucinates misaligned structures. However, the method remains a per-scene optimization (72 minutes for 2000 steps on 4 GPUs), limiting real-time applicability, and its quality is bounded by upstream VGGT/LangSAM accuracy in dynamic or occluded scenes. Open questions include whether pre-training the LoRA on a large-scale task-specific dataset could reduce per-scene optimization to a few hundred steps, and whether the dual-branch geometry conditioning generalizes to video or 3D scene completion settings.
