## Problem

Offline safe RL requires learning reward-maximizing policies from static data while satisfying cumulative cost constraints, but existing Lagrangian-based approaches are unstable in practice—causing oscillation, divergence, or overly conservative near-zero-reward policies. The setting with stringent (small) cost thresholds, critical for safety-critical applications, is severely under-studied. Prior frameworks like Le et al. (2019) require repeated off-policy evaluation calls per iteration, leading to exponential error propagation and high computational cost.

## Input / Output

The method consumes a fixed offline dataset of (state, action, reward, cost, next-state) tuples collected from an unknown behavior policy, along with a user-specified cost threshold κ. At each iteration, the reward signal is reshaped as r′ = r − λ(c − (1−γ)κ) using the current Lagrange variable, and the base offline RL algorithm (e.g., TD3+BC or IQL) is run for only M gradient steps from the previous iteration's parameters. The output is a single last-iterate policy (state-to-action mapping) that satisfies the cost constraint, rather than the theoretically-motivated averaged policy distribution, to avoid prohibitive memory costs.

## Method

O3SRL formulates OSRL as a minimax optimization over a policy distribution and a Lagrange multiplier, then solves it iteratively by alternating between (1) an offline RL oracle that trains on a modified reward combining r and c weighted by the current λ, and (2) a no-regret update that adapts λ. The key practical innovation discretizes λ into K values and replaces the continuous no-regret update with the EXP3 multi-armed bandit algorithm, which eliminates the need for off-policy evaluation entirely. To avoid running the offline RL algorithm to convergence each round, the method performs only M stochastic gradient updates from the previous round's parameters, making the approach plug-and-play with any SGD-based offline RL algorithm. Theoretical analysis (Theorem 2) guarantees convergence to an ε-approximate minimax equilibrium with error O(ε_offline-RL(n) + √(K/T) + 1/K), and empirically even K=2 arms with M=10 gradient steps achieves state-of-the-art safe performance on the DSRL benchmark.

## Results

Under the stringent cost budget κ=5, O3SRL is the only method that satisfies the safety constraint (normalized cost ≤ 1) across all 8 DSRL Bullet tasks. Among safe methods, O3SRL achieves the highest reward in 2 out of 8 tasks and consistently ranks second in the remaining six. For example, on BallRun, O3SRL attains 0.25±0.03 reward with 0.00 cost—over 3× higher than CAPS (0.07±0.05) and FISOR (0.09±0.07). On BallCircle, O3SRL reaches 0.62±0.01 reward while staying safe, more than double CAPS (0.33±0.02) and FISOR (0.32±0.05).

## Baselines & Comparisons

O3SRL wins on the critical safety dimension: it is the only method that remains safe (cost ≤ 1) on all 8 tasks, whereas CDT violates the constraint in 6/8 tasks, COptiDICE in 5/8, CCAC in 4/8, and BEAR-Lag in 5/8. Among the safe methods, O3SRL substantially outperforms CAPS and FISOR on reward: on BallRun, O3SRL scores 0.25 vs. CAPS 0.07 and FISOR 0.09; on BallCircle, O3SRL scores 0.62 vs. CAPS 0.33 and FISOR 0.32. CPQ is the closest safe competitor, matching O3SRL on CarCircle (0.71 vs. 0.66) and CarRun (0.93 vs. 0.96), but it is unsafe on 3 tasks and achieves near-zero reward on AntCircle (0.00). CDT and COptiDICE achieve the highest raw rewards on several tasks (e.g., CDT 0.99 on CarRun, COptiDICE 0.68 on DroneRun) but do so by severely violating the cost budget, making them unreliable for safety-critical deployment. CCAC achieves the single highest reward on BallRun (1.82) and AntCircle (0.49) but is unsafe in 4 of 8 tasks, confirming that high reward without constraint enforcement is not a viable safe-RL solution.

## Relation to Prior Work

O3SRL builds on the Lagrangian relaxation framework for constrained RL and the EXP3 multi-armed bandit algorithm, but departs sharply from prior OSRL methods (Le et al. 2019, Polosky et al. 2022, Xu et al. 2022) that solve intertwined primal-dual optimization problems and require repeated off-policy evaluation (OPE) calls. The key conceptual departure is reframing the Lagrange multiplier selection as a discrete multi-armed bandit problem, which eliminates OPE entirely and provides no-regret convergence guarantees to the minimax solution. The practical algorithm further relaxes the need for a full offline RL oracle by performing only M truncated gradient steps per iteration, making it a plug-and-play wrapper around any existing offline RL algorithm (demonstrated with both TD3+BC and IQL).

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

OSRL is critical for safety-critical domains (healthcare, energy systems, industrial control) where the cost budget is tight and online interaction is infeasible, yet prior methods either violate constraints or collapse to near-zero reward under stringent budgets. O3SRL's plug-and-play design and avoidance of OPE make it a practical drop-in improvement over existing offline RL pipelines, and its consistent constraint satisfaction across all eight DSRL tasks (where no other method achieves this) addresses a real reliability gap. Open questions include whether the approach extends to offline-to-online safe RL, whether the slightly suboptimal convergence rate (T^{-1/3} vs T^{-1/2}) matters at scale, and whether the method generalizes beyond the continuous-control benchmarks tested here to higher-dimensional or partially-observed settings.
