## Problem

Latent diffusion models produce high-quality images but their internal features lack the semantic richness of dedicated self-supervised encoders like DINOv2. While REPA showed that distilling pretrained representations into diffusion features improves generation, this requires an auxiliary alignment objective. The paper asks whether a more direct approach—jointly modeling images and their semantic features within the same diffusion process—can outperform distillation-based alignment while simplifying training.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/NEURIPS20250563/cropped_page0_idx1.webp)

## Input / Output

The model takes Gaussian noise (and optionally a class label for conditional generation) and jointly produces a 256×256 RGB image (decoded from 32×32×4 VAE latents via SD-VAE-FT-EMA) and a corresponding DINOv2 semantic feature map. The DINOv2-B features (768-dim) are first reduced to 8 dimensions via PCA, then bilinearly resized and 2×2-patchified to yield 256 tokens of 32 channels, matching the spatial token count of the VAE latents. At inference, Representation Guidance uses the model's own semantic predictions to iteratively refine the generated image, effectively decoupling the semantic signal from the class label.

## Method

ReDi extends the standard DDPM/flow-matching forward process to simultaneously corrupt both VAE latents x₀ and DINOv2 features z₀ with independent Gaussian noise under the same schedule, then trains a single DiT or SiT transformer to denoise both modalities jointly. The two modalities are fused into one token sequence via either channel-wise merging (preserving the original 256-token length) or sequence concatenation (512 tokens), with modality-specific embedding and prediction heads being the only architectural additions. A PCA projection of DINOv2 features (768→8 dims) is critical to prevent the higher-dimensional semantic branch from dominating model capacity. At inference, Representation Guidance modifies the denoising step as ε̂ = ε(x,t) + w_r·(ε(x,z,t) − ε(x,t)), analogous to Classifier-Free Guidance but using the learned semantic features as the conditioning signal, enabling quality improvements even in unconditional generation where CFG is unavailable.

## Results

On ImageNet 256×256 with Classifier-Free Guidance (SiT-XL/2 base), ReDi achieves FID 1.61 at 800 epochs and FID 1.72 at just 350 epochs, outperforming the fully converged SiT-XL/2 baseline (FID 2.06 at 1400 epochs) and REPA (FID 1.80 at 800 epochs). Without CFG, ReDi accelerates convergence by ~23×: DiT-XL/2 w/ ReDi reaches FID 8.7 at 400K iterations (vs. 9.6 for the 7M-step baseline), and SiT-XL/2 w/ ReDi reaches FID 3.3 at 4M iterations (vs. 5.9 for REPA at the same budget). Representation Guidance further reduces FID from 8.7 to 5.9 for DiT-XL/2 w/ ReDi in the conditional setting.

## Baselines & Comparisons

ReDi outperforms all latent diffusion baselines on FID: it beats SiT-XL/2 (2.06 at 1400 epochs) by 0.45 FID at only 350 epochs (1.72), DiT-XL/2 (2.27 at 1400 epochs) by 0.55 FID, FasterDiT (2.03 at 400 epochs) by 0.31 FID, MDT (1.79 at 1300 epochs) by 0.18 FID at 800 epochs, and REPA (1.80 at 800 epochs) by 0.19 FID at the same budget. Among autoregressive models, ReDi at 800 epochs (FID 1.61) surpasses VAR (1.80) and MagViTv2 (1.78) but falls short of MAR (1.55). ReDi also substantially outperforms earlier latent diffusion methods: LDM (3.60), U-ViT-H/2 (2.29), MaskDiT (2.28), and SD-DiT (3.23). Notably, ReDi achieves its best FID (1.61) with fewer training epochs than most competitors, demonstrating both quality and convergence-speed advantages.

## Relation to Prior Work

ReDi extends the latent diffusion modeling framework (LDM, DiT, SiT) by jointly denoising VAE image latents and DINOv2 semantic features within a single diffusion process, rather than treating them as separate objectives. The most direct prior work is REPA (Yu et al., 2025), which aligns diffusion transformer features to DINOv2 via an auxiliary distillation loss; ReDi departs from this by eliminating the distillation objective entirely and instead generating both modalities from noise simultaneously. The Representation Guidance mechanism at inference is inspired by Classifier-Free Guidance (Ho & Salimans, 2022) and VideoJam's multi-condition CFG variant, repurposing the jointly learned semantic channel as an additional steering signal. The approach also draws on multi-modal generative modeling (CoDi, WVD, GEM) but applies the joint-distribution idea to two complementary views of the same image rather than to genuinely different modalities.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The ~23× convergence speedup over vanilla DiT/SiT and ~6× over REPA is practically significant, reducing training budgets for high-quality image generators by an order of magnitude. The method's simplicity (modality-specific embedding matrices and prediction heads, no auxiliary losses) makes it easy to adopt in existing diffusion pipelines, and its demonstrated complementarity with REPA suggests a path toward stacking representation-aware training strategies. Key limitations include evaluation restricted to ImageNet 256×256, reliance on a single semantic encoder (DINOv2-B), and the somewhat ad hoc PCA compression whose optimal dimensionality (r=8) is dataset-dependent. Open questions include whether multiple complementary semantic encoders can be jointly modeled, whether more principled compression (e.g., learned autoencoders) improves over PCA, and whether the approach transfers to higher resolutions, video, or text-conditional settings.
