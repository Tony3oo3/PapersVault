## Problem

Generating flexible-view 3D scenes (360° rotation, zooming) from a single image is fundamentally ill-posed because a single 2D observation cannot disambiguate occluded or absent 3D content. Existing video-diffusion-based methods fail under large viewpoint changes (e.g., 180° rotations) due to weak base models and suboptimal training data, while image-based approaches accumulate geometric errors, ultimately limiting the range of explorable viewpoints.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/NEURIPS20255732/cropped_page1_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method accepts a single RGB image (or an existing 3DGS scene for extrapolation) and produces a persistent 3D Gaussian splatting scene supporting arbitrary viewpoint rendering, including full 360° panoramas and zooming. Internally, the V2V diffusion model operates at 480×720 resolution on 49-frame video sequences; the initial 3D scaffold is obtained by duplicating the input image as both source and reference views for DUSt3R, and camera trajectories are interpolated (linear/cubic spline for position, slerp for rotation) to match the 49-pose requirement. A full 360° scene requires three iterative expansion passes, taking approximately 30 minutes on a single A800 GPU.

## Method

FlexWorld maintains a persistent 3DGS representation that is progressively expanded through three stages per iteration: (1) novel view synthesis via a video-to-video diffusion model built on CogVideoX-5B-I2V, where the 3D-VAE-encoded incomplete video is channel-wise concatenated with noise latents (replacing the original image conditioning), trained on DL3DV-10K with 3DGS-reconstructed training pairs that avoid the geometric inaccuracies of dense-stereo-based pairs; (2) geometry-aware scene integration using DUSt3R depth estimation on m=6 keyframes, with median-based depth scale alignment to a well-optimized reference view, guided filtering for smooth transitions, and alpha-masked point cloud extraction; (3) 3DGS optimization with L1+SSIM+LPIPS loss, Real-ESRGAN upscaling, and optional SDEdit refinement via FLUX. The key architectural distinction from prior work (e.g., ViewCrafter) is the persistent 3DGS anchor across iterations, which eliminates repeated 3D reconstruction, enables seamless scene extrapolation, and reduces cumulative error under large camera variations.

## Results

On RealEstate10K, FlexWorld achieves the best FID (13.88), FVD (100.41), PSNR (16.62), SSIM (0.612), and LPIPS (0.344) among all six baselines, while also matching the best camera rotation error (R_err = 0.026, tied with See3D). On Tanks and Temples, FlexWorld leads in FID (37.31), FVD (376.49), PSNR (13.20), and SSIM (0.405), with the best translation error (T_err = 0.100). For 3D scene generation (Table 2), FlexWorld achieves PSNR 16.18 / SSIM 0.604 / LPIPS 0.369 on RealEstate10K and PSNR 12.99 / SSIM 0.389 / LPIPS 0.544 on Tanks, outperforming all baselines except a marginal SSIM gap versus See3D (0.389 vs. 0.396) on Tanks.

## Baselines & Comparisons

FlexWorld outperforms all six baselines on the majority of metrics across both datasets. ViewCrafter [10] is the closest competitor, but FlexWorld improves FID by 3.11 (13.88 vs. 16.99) and FVD by 43.48 (100.41 vs. 143.89) on RealEstate10K, and by 3.87 FID and 172.61 FVD on Tanks. See3D [11] achieves comparable camera rotation error (R_err 0.026 on RE10K) but trails FlexWorld by 2.18 PSNR and 0.066 SSIM on the same dataset; on Tanks, See3D edges out FlexWorld on SSIM (0.396 vs. 0.389) for 3D scene generation but loses on PSNR (12.82 vs. 12.99) and LPIPS (0.584 vs. 0.544). DimensionX [13] and MotionCtrl [8] are substantially weaker, with DimensionX showing the worst FID (33.77 on RE10K) and camera errors (R_err 0.864). CameraCtrl [9] achieves the best T_err on RE10K (0.117) but has the worst FVD (381.38) and trails FlexWorld on all quality metrics. LucidDreamer [4], evaluated only for 3D scene generation, is the weakest baseline (PSNR 13.03 on RE10K), confirming that image-diffusion-based approaches accumulate geometric errors relative to video-diffusion-based methods.

## Relation to Prior Work

FlexWorld extends the line of video-diffusion-based novel view synthesis (ViewCrafter, See3D, DimensionX) by replacing their transient per-iteration point cloud representations with a persistent 3D Gaussian Splatting scene that is progressively expanded. It departs from prior V2V approaches in two key ways: (1) it fine-tunes a substantially stronger base model (CogVideoX-5B-I2V) rather than SVD or DynamicRafter, and (2) it constructs training pairs from 3DGS-based reconstructions rather than dense stereo outputs (DUSt3R/MASt3R), closing a domain gap between training and inference conditions. The progressive scene-integration pipeline (depth alignment via median scaling, guided filtering, alpha-masked point cloud merging) builds on DUSt3R's multi-view depth estimation but applies it iteratively to a growing scene rather than a one-shot reconstruction.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

FlexWorld demonstrates that a persistent 3DGS representation combined with a strong V2V diffusion model can achieve 360° scene generation and, uniquely, scene extrapolation from existing reconstructions—capabilities that single-pass video generation methods fundamentally cannot provide. The ~30-minute per-scene runtime and dependence on dense stereo accuracy for depth alignment remain practical bottlenecks, and the paper acknowledges that V2V camera control degrades when input lacks 3D structure. Open questions include whether feed-forward 3DGS reconstruction and distilled video generators can eliminate the iterative optimization loop, and whether the progressive expansion strategy scales beyond 360° to truly unbounded scenes without accumulated geometric drift.
