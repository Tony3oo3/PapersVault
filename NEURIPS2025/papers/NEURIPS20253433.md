## Problem

Semantically constrained adversarial examples (SemanticAEs) generated from natural language instructions suffer from three forms of instruction uncertainty—referring diversity, descriptive incompleteness, and boundary ambiguity—that limit their transferability, scenario adaptability, and evaluation reliability. Existing diffusion-based methods either destabilize adversarial optimization under multi-step sampling or fail to integrate scenario knowledge when instructions are incomplete, and no prior work has addressed 3D SemanticAE generation or provided a principled evaluation protocol.

## Input / Output

The method takes a free-form text instruction (e.g., 'Realistic image of boat, specifically, canoe') as the sole semantic constraint, operating in a strict black-box setting where neither the semantic set S(Text) nor the target model M is known. For 2D generation, it produces an adversarial image and a paired non-adversarial exemplar image; for 3D generation, it produces a Gaussian-splat object whose rendered multi-view video is evaluated against 2D classifiers. The 3D pipeline additionally samples camera parameters from a distribution P_Cam during optimization via expectation-over-transformation, and the coarse structure latent z_0^slat serves as a fixed semantic anchor excluded from the optimization space.

## Method

The InSUR framework addresses instruction uncertainty across three dimensions. First, the ResAdv-DDIM sampler takes k residual shortcut steps (k∈{1,2,3,4}) through the DDIM posterior to coarsely predict x_0 from the current x_t, yielding a more accurate ∇_{x_t} L_ATK estimate than the standard ∇_{x_0} approximation and stabilizing the adversarial direction across denoising stages; an adaptive early-stop mechanism with thresholds ξ_1=0.1 and ξ_2=0.01 further reduces optimization cost. Second, for 2D, a spatial guidance mask M redistributes conditional and unconditional noise predictions to enrich background diversity under tight semantic constraints; for 3D, the pipeline embeds a differentiable Gaussian-splatting renderer (via the Trellis framework) inside the ResAdv-DDIM coarse prediction g_θ, back-propagating attack gradients through the 3D structure while EoT over camera distributions encodes scenario knowledge. Third, a WordNet-based label taxonomy defines an appropriate abstraction level for attack targets, and the ASR_Relative metric requires both adversarial success on x_adv and correct classification of the paired exemplar, eliminating confounding variations in benign generation.

## Results

On the ImageNet label evasion task with ResNet50 as surrogate, InSUR achieves 62.0% average ASR (target accuracy 15.1%), outperforming the strongest baseline SD-NAE (47.4% ASR) by 14.6 percentage points while maintaining far better naturalness (LPIPS 0.031 vs. 0.457). On the proposed Coarse Label Evasion task, InSUR reaches 47.9% ASR (accuracy 35.2%), surpassing VENOM (34.9%) and SD-NAE (31.8%). Across all four surrogate settings and both tasks, InSUR achieves at least 1.19× average ASR and 1.08× minimal ASR over all baselines, with generation time of ~7.3 s per sample on a single 4090 GPU. For 3D SemanticAE (first of its kind), InSUR achieves 92.2% ASR with 2.8% target accuracy, compared to 45.1% ASR without the ResAdv-DDIM component.

## Baselines & Comparisons

InSUR dominates all four baselines on ASR across every surrogate and task setting. Against MI-FGSM (fastest at 1.4 s but weakest attack), InSUR improves ASR by ~20 pp on ImageNet and ~25 pp on Coarse Label Evasion, at roughly 5× the generation time. AdvDiff, while producing the most natural images (LPIPS 0.046), is essentially ineffective as an attacker (4.9% ASR on ImageNet, 1.8% on Coarse), confirming that diffusion-based generation without adversarial optimization fails to produce transferable attacks. SD-NAE is the closest competitor on raw ASR (47.4% vs. 62.0% on ImageNet) but suffers severe naturalness degradation (LPIPS 0.457 vs. 0.031) and is 3.4× slower. VENOM offers a good speed–naturalness trade-off (3.1 s, LPIPS 0.023) but trails InSUR by 12–27 pp in ASR depending on the setting. InSUR's advantage is most pronounced on the harder Coarse Label Evasion task, where the semantic-abstracted evaluation boundary amplifies the gap over all baselines.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the diffusion-based adversarial example generation line (DiffPGD, SD-NAE, AdvDiff, VENOM) by reframing the core difficulty as 'instruction uncertainty' and addressing it through three targeted mechanisms: a residual-guided DDIM sampler (ResAdv-DDIM) that stabilizes adversarial optimization direction across multi-step denoising, guidance masking adapted from DiffEdit for spatial constraint control, and a WordNet-based label taxonomy for evaluation. The 3D extension builds on Trellis and 3D Gaussian Splatting to achieve the first reference-free 3D SemanticAE generation, bridging the gap between 3D generative models and 2D target classifiers via differentiable rendering. Conceptually, the paper departs from prior work by treating the language instruction not merely as a conditioning signal but as an inherently uncertain specification whose three failure modes (referring diversity, descriptive incompleteness, boundary ambiguity) each require distinct technical solutions.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The structured decomposition of instruction uncertainty provides a useful diagnostic framework for understanding why text-to-adversarial-example generation underperforms, and the ResAdv-DDIM sampler is a general-purpose component that could benefit any multi-step diffusion-based adversarial pipeline. The first 3D SemanticAE generation opens a path toward physically grounded adversarial evaluation, though the current 3D quality and limited real-world validation (Blender re-rendering with degraded performance) leave significant room for improvement. Open questions include whether the framework scales to larger VLMs beyond the modest LLaVA/Qwen-VL results, whether the label-taxonomy evaluation generalizes beyond ImageNet, and how the approach performs under adaptive defenses. The ASR_Relative metric and non-adversarial exemplar requirement address a real evaluation gap that the field has lacked.
