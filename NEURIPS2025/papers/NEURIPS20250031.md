## Problem

Existing segmentation-mask-to-image (S2I) generation methods cannot simultaneously achieve semantic consistency (matching regional text prompts to their assigned image regions) and shape consistency (preserving exact entity boundaries). Mask-as-condition approaches (e.g., ControlNet) fail to align text with regions, while masked-attention approaches (e.g., FreestyleNet, PLACE) lose spatial detail because segmentation masks are compressed into low-resolution latent spaces—a problem amplified by the 16× downsampling in modern DiT architectures versus 8× in UNet.

## Input / Output

The method takes a global text prompt, N entity-level text prompts, and N corresponding binary segmentation masks as input, and produces a single RGB image. Masks are first converted into a sparse Entity Contour Map (merged binary contours duplicated to RGB), which is then encoded by a frozen VAE into condition tokens that share 2D RoPE position indices with noisy image tokens. Training resolution is 1024×1024 for the open-set SACap-1M dataset and 512×512 for closed-set ADE20K/COCO-Stuff; the shape guidance strength γ ∈ (0,1] allows flexible control from strict mask adherence to scribble-style loose positioning without retraining.

## Method

Seg2Any is built on FLUX (a multimodal diffusion transformer) and decouples segmentation mask conditions into two complementary channels. For semantic alignment, a Semantic Alignment Attention Mask restricts cross-attention so each entity's text tokens attend only to their corresponding image tokens (and vice versa), while all image tokens attend to each other for global coherence. For shape preservation, the Entity Contour Map is encoded via a frozen VAE into sparse condition tokens that are concatenated with text and noisy image tokens in a unified sequence for multi-modal attention, with LoRA (rank 64, ~594M params) applied to all three branches (condition, image, text) to minimize trainable parameters. A second mechanism, the Attribute Isolation Attention Mask, is applied to the middle layers (20–38) of FLUX to prevent cross-entity attribute leakage by restricting each entity's image tokens to attend only to themselves during self-attention. A bias term modulated by γ controls the influence of condition tokens, and zero-valued condition tokens are filtered out to reduce inference cost.

## Results

On the open-set SACap-Eval benchmark, Seg2Any achieves a class-agnostic MIoU of 94.90, nearly matching the real-image upper bound of 96.03, demonstrating near-perfect shape consistency. On the closed-set COCO-Stuff benchmark, Seg2Any attains the highest MIoU of 45.54 (vs. 42.42 for FreestyleNet and 42.23 for PLACE), while on ADE20K it reaches 54.46 MIoU, approaching the real-image upper bound of 54.41. In region-wise quality on SACap-Eval, Seg2Any scores 93.89% spatial accuracy, 91.52% color accuracy, 88.15% shape accuracy, and 90.12% texture accuracy, with a global FID of 15.53.

## Baselines & Comparisons

On COCO-Stuff, Seg2Any (45.54 MIoU) outperforms all baselines, surpassing FreestyleNet (42.42) by +3.12 and PLACE (42.23) by +3.31, with the largest margin over ControlNet (27.46) and ControlNet++ (34.56). On ADE20K, Seg2Any (54.46 MIoU) beats FreestyleNet (44.42), ControlNet++ (43.64), ControlNet (32.55), GLIGEN (23.78), and UniControl (25.44), but falls short of PLACE (60.20) by 5.74 MIoU, which the authors attribute to a domain gap between FLUX and the ADE20K/COCO-Stuff image distributions. On the open-set SACap-Eval benchmark, Seg2Any achieves 94.90 class-agnostic MIoU (near the 96.03 real-image ceiling), outperforming the FLUX-based baselines DreamRenderer, 3DIS-FLUX, and EliGen, as well as the UNet-based FreestyleNet and PLACE, in both shape consistency and region-wise attribute control. The FID scores for Seg2Any are slightly higher than PLACE and FreestyleNet on closed-set benchmarks, consistent with the domain shift from the FLUX base model.

## Relation to Prior Work

Seg2Any builds on the FLUX multimodal diffusion transformer (MM-DiT) architecture and OminiControl's unified condition-token concatenation paradigm, but departs from prior S2I methods in a key architectural insight: rather than injecting segmentation masks as dense pixel-level conditions (ControlNet, ControlNet++) or as binary/soft attention weights (FreestyleNet, PLACE), it decouples the mask into a sparse Entity Contour Map for shape and a Semantic Alignment Attention Mask for semantics. For multi-entity attribute isolation, it extends DreamRenderer's observation that middle DiT layers handle instance-level rendering by making the attention mask training-based rather than training-free, and it generalizes the task from closed-set category maps to open-set, category-agnostic generation.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The open-set S2I formulation removes the closed-set vocabulary constraint that limited prior methods, and the SACap-1M dataset (1M images, 5.9M entities with VLM-generated regional captions) plus SACap-Eval benchmark provide much-needed infrastructure for this setting. The near-upper-bound shape consistency (94.90 vs. 96.03 class-agnostic MIoU) demonstrates that sparse contour encoding is sufficient for precise spatial control, potentially simplifying future controllable generation pipelines. Open questions include scalability to very large entity counts, the impact of VLM annotation noise on downstream generation quality, and whether the decoupled shape-semantic paradigm transfers to video or 3D generation.
