## Problem

Existing training-free sparse attention methods for Diffusion Transformers can only achieve ~70% sparsity before visual quality degrades significantly, while methods reaching >95% sparsity require costly retraining. The paper identifies the root cause as a distributional shift in softmax statistics: when sparse attention excludes tokens from the denominator, the resulting probability distribution is distorted, and this distortion becomes severe at extreme sparsity levels.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/NEURIPS20251945/cropped_page1_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method operates as a training-free, inference-time modification on pretrained DiT models (CogVideoX-2B, PixArt-α/Σ, Hunyuan-DiT), taking a text prompt and producing 720×480, 6-second videos at 8 fps or 1024×1024 images. It uses a static sliding-window attention mask and requires full attention to be computed during the first 5–15 denoising steps (depending on the model) to cache the necessary softmax statistics before switching to sparse attention for the remaining steps.

## Method

Re-ttention addresses the softmax denominator shift in two stages. First, it observes that the ratio ρ between the sparse and full softmax denominators is relatively stable across neighboring denoising steps, so it caches ρ from a previous step and multiplies the sparse attention scores by ρ to approximate the full-attention distribution (with a small ramp-up parameter λ added each step). Second, because the ρ-reshaped scores no longer sum to one, it caches the residual R — mathematically equivalent to the attention output of the masked tokens at the caching timestep — and adds it back to restore a valid probability distribution. This combination of denominator approximation and residual caching enables 96.9% sparsity (using only ~3.1% of tokens) with negligible quality loss compared to full quadratic attention.

## Results

On T2V (CogVideoX-2B at 96.9% sparsity), Re-ttention achieves PSNR 27.96, SSIM 0.894, and LPIPS 0.059, closely matching full-attention reference quality and outperforming DiTFastAttn (PSNR 27.93, SSIM 0.865, LPIPS 0.098). On T2I, Re-ttention at 96.9% sparsity achieves GenEval 0.512 and HPSv2 27.72 on PixArt-Σ, and GenEval 0.590 and HPSv2 28.89 on Hunyuan-DiT, while DiTFastAttn collapses to GenEval 0.233 and 0.002 respectively. Re-ttention is the only method that maintains positive ImageReward scores across all T2I models at 96.9% sparsity (e.g., IR 0.923 on Hunyuan vs. −2.237 for DiTFastAttn).

## Baselines & Comparisons

Re-ttention outperforms all three baselines across every model and metric at 96.9% sparsity. Against Sparse VideoGen (SVG), which operates at only 75% sparsity, Re-ttention achieves substantially higher GenEval (e.g., 0.512 vs. 0.172 on PixArt-Σ) and HPSv2 (27.72 vs. 18.48), while also maintaining positive ImageReward scores where SVG produces strongly negative values. Against MInference (also 75% sparsity), Re-ttention wins on GenEval, HPSv2, LPIPS, IR, and CLIP at a much higher sparsity level (96.9% vs. 75%). The closest competitor is DiTFastAttn, which also uses caching; on T2V (CogVideoX-2B) the gap is narrow (PSNR 27.96 vs. 27.93, SSIM 0.894 vs. 0.865), but on T2I the advantage is dramatic—on Hunyuan-DiT, Re-ttention achieves GenEval 0.590 and HPSv2 28.89 while DiTFastAttn collapses to GenEval 0.002 and HPSv2 12.28, indicating that DiTFastAttn's caching strategy fails to generalize to this architecture at extreme sparsity.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper sits at the intersection of two lines of work: training-free sparse attention for transformers (MInference, Sparse VideoGen, DiTFastAttn) and DiT caching/reuse techniques (Delta-DiT, Timestep Embedding Tells). It departs from prior sparse attention methods by identifying the softmax denominator shift as the fundamental statistical barrier preventing >95% sparsity, rather than focusing solely on which tokens to include. The method repurposes the temporal redundancy of the denoising process—previously exploited for feature caching—to approximate the full-attention denominator and compensate for the resulting probability mass deficit, a use of denoising-step correlation that prior sparse attention work did not leverage.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

Achieving 96.9% sparsity (retaining only 3.1% of tokens) in a training-free manner is practically significant for the growing compute demands of high-resolution video and image generation, and the softmax-denominator diagnosis provides a clear mechanistic explanation for why prior methods plateau below ~70% sparsity. However, the method is currently limited to static attention masks and non-autoregressive (non-causal) attention, and the improvement over the strongest baseline (DiTFastAttn) at matched sparsity is modest on T2V, with the larger gains appearing over weaker baselines (SVG, MInference) that degrade severely. Open questions include whether the approach extends to dynamically computed masks, autoregressive or LLM-style causal attention, and whether the residual-caching mechanism generalizes to architectures without a sequential denoising process.
