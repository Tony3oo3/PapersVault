## Problem

EEG-based auditory attention detection (AAD) lacks synergistic frameworks that fully exploit complementary spatial and frequency-domain EEG features under the strict energy-efficiency constraints of wearable hearing aids and low-power BCIs. Existing dual-branch networks combine features via isolated learning (simple concatenation or summation) and rely on resource-intensive 3D convolutions, limiting real-world deployability.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/NEURIPS20254224/cropped_page1_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The model consumes 64-channel EEG decision windows (0.1s, 1s, or 2s) preprocessed at 128 Hz, from which two feature embeddings are extracted: CSP spatial-temporal features (C×T matrix) and differential-entropy frequency-spectral features (5×32×32 topographic maps across δ–γ bands). These are expanded into 1D token sequences over T_S=4 spiking time steps and processed through the network to produce a binary attention label (left vs. right speaker). The fixed 0.06M parameter model size is invariant across all decision window lengths, unlike baselines such as M-DBPNet whose size varies with window.

## Method

S²M-Former is a spiking neural network with a symmetric dual-branch architecture: a spatial branch encoder (SBE) using cascaded temporal convolutions and dual-path spatial convolutions, and a frequency branch encoder (FBE) using dilated 3×3 convolutions with progressive channel transformation, both producing unified 1D token representations. A mirrored S²M block then hierarchically refines these tokens through four spike-driven modules: Spiking Channel-wise Self-Attention (SCSA) for long-range intra-branch dependencies, Spiking Multi-Scale Separable Convolution (SMSC) for local fine-grained patterns, Spiking Gated Channel Mixer (SGCM) for cross-branch channel fusion via QK-style gating, and Membrane Potential-aware Token Mixer (MPTM) for token-level complementary integration. A novel Channel-wise Parametric LIF (CPLIF) neuron assigns a learnable membrane time constant per channel to enable adaptive temporal dynamics across inter-module connections. By replacing 3D operations with 1D token sequences and MAC operations with sparse spike-based accumulate operations, the architecture achieves a 14.7× parameter reduction and 5.8× energy reduction over dual-branch ANN baselines while maintaining competitive SOTA accuracy.

## Results

S²M-Former (0.06M params) achieves the highest decoding accuracy in 11 out of 18 conditions across three AAD benchmarks (KUL, DTU, AV-GC) under within-trial and cross-trial settings, with 83.33% Top-3 coverage. On cross-trial evaluation, it reaches SOTA on DTU (76.74% ± 9.96 at 2s) and AV-GC (70.64% ± 18.65 at 2s). In cross-subject (LOSO) validation, it achieves 75.75% on KUL and 59.75% on DTU (2s window), outperforming all baselines. The model is 14.7× smaller than DBPNet and 5.8× more energy-efficient (0.0779 mJ vs. 0.4526 mJ) while maintaining competitive accuracy.

## Baselines & Comparisons

Against the dual-branch ANN baselines, S²M-Former (0.06M) outperforms DBPNet (0.88M) on DTU (85.28% vs. 83.93% at 2s within-trial; 76.74% vs. 76.40% cross-trial) and AV-GC (91.83% vs. 90.78% within-trial; 70.64% vs. 64.77% cross-trial), while being 14.7× smaller and 5.8× more energy-efficient. It also surpasses M-DBPNet (1.32M) on DTU and AV-GC cross-trial (76.74% vs. 76.18%; 70.64% vs. 68.90%) with 22× fewer parameters. Against the single-branch SOTA DARNet (0.08M), S²M-Former wins on DTU (85.28% vs. 81.30% within-trial) and AV-GC (91.83% vs. 89.17% within-trial; 70.64% vs. 64.99% cross-trial), though DARNet edges ahead on KUL 0.1s (87.66% vs. 83.39%). Against SNN baselines (QKFormer 0.29M, SDT 0.37M, Spikformer 0.37M), S²M-Former achieves substantially higher accuracy (e.g., 85.28% vs. 69.54%, 69.44%, 67.28% on DTU within-trial 2s) with 5–6× fewer parameters. The older single-branch baselines SSF-CNN and MBSSFCC are clearly outperformed across all datasets and windows, with MBSSFCC (16.81M) being 280× larger yet only marginally competitive on KUL within-trial.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the spiking neural network line of work (Spikformer, Spike-driven Transformer, QKFormer) into the auditory attention detection domain, where prior SNN methods have shown weaker performance than ANN baselines due to sparse representations and simple architectures. It builds on the parametric LIF neuron (Fang et al., ICCV 2021) by introducing a channel-wise variant (CPLIF) for finer-grained temporal control, and adapts the QK attention mechanism from QKFormer into a spiking gated channel mixer. Conceptually, it departs from recent dual-branch AAD networks (DBPNet, M-DBPNet) that fuse spatial and frequency features via simple concatenation, instead proposing a symmetric mirrored architecture with biologically plausible token-channel mixers that explicitly encourage complementary learning across branches while replacing resource-intensive 3D convolutions with lightweight 1D token operations.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The 14.7× parameter reduction and 5.8× energy reduction make S²M-Former a practical candidate for deployment in battery-constrained wearable hearing aids and low-power brain-computer interfaces, where current dual-branch ANN models are too computationally heavy. However, the authors acknowledge that CSP and DE feature extraction inherently disrupts the temporal dynamics of raw EEG, limiting the full exploitation of SNNs' spatio-temporal strengths, and hardware validation on neuromorphic chips remains future work. An open question is whether the symmetric complementary learning paradigm generalizes beyond AAD to other EEG decoding tasks (e.g., motor imagery, emotion recognition) where multi-domain feature fusion is similarly needed under efficiency constraints.
