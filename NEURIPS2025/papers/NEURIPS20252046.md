## Problem

Multi-Head Self-Attention in Vision Transformers computes an N×N similarity matrix per layer, incurring O(N²C) cost that is largely spent on visually weak or redundant token correlations. Existing linear-complexity remedies either restrict the receptive field (sacrificing global structure) or approximate the full attention map without distinguishing informative from redundant interactions. The paper asks whether an explicit notion of visual contrast—highlighting what separates one region from another—can simultaneously reduce complexity and sharpen discriminative signal.

## Input / Output

For classification, the method consumes 224×224 RGB images patchified into N tokens (e.g., 196 tokens at 16×16 patch size) and outputs a 1000-way class label on ImageNet-1K. For generation, it takes a class label and Gaussian noise as input and produces 256×256 RGB images via diffusion (DiT) or flow (SiT) pipelines. Internally, the module operates on per-head query/key/value tensors of shape N×d and generates n pooled contrast tokens (n≪N, e.g., 64 from an 8×8 grid) that mediate all attention interactions, so the external I/O contract of the backbone is unchanged.

## Method

Visual-Contrast Attention (VCA) is a two-stage drop-in replacement for MHSA that reduces per-layer complexity from O(N²C) to O(NnC). In Stage I (global contrast), each head's H×W query map is average-pooled to a coarse h×w grid, flattened into n tokens, and split into positive and negative streams via two distinct learnable positional embeddings; each stream independently attends to all keys and values, and the two outputs are subtracted, RMS-normalised, and scaled to yield a compact contrast map. In Stage II (patch-wise differential attention), the original N patch queries compute two N×n attention maps against the positive and negative contrast tokens, take their difference (A₁ − λA₂) with a learnable scalar λ, and multiply by the contrast map to produce the final N×d output. The entire module adds fewer than 0.3 M parameters to DeiT-Tiny, introduces no extra FLOPs because the contrast stage reuses existing key-value tensors, and requires no changes to residual paths, layer norms, or training hyper-parameters, making it universally applicable to plain and hierarchical ViT backbones as well as diffusion/flow generative models.

## Results

On ImageNet-1K classification, VCA lifts DeiT-Tiny top-1 accuracy from 72.2% to 75.6% (+3.4 pp) with only 0.3 M extra parameters and no additional FLOPs, and improves PVT-Tiny by +3.1 pp (75.1% → 78.2%). Across hierarchical backbones (Swin, CSwin), gains range from +0.4 to +1.0 pp. On class-conditional ImageNet-1K generation at 256×256, VCA reduces FID-50K by 2.1–5.2 points for DiT and 2.1–4.3 points for SiT across Small and Base scales and patch sizes 8/4/2.

## Baselines & Comparisons

VCA is evaluated as a drop-in replacement for MHSA across seven baseline families. On plain ViTs, it outperforms DeiT [Touvron, ICML 2021] by +3.4 pp (DeiT-T) and +0.9 pp (DeiT-S) at identical FLOPs. On hierarchical backbones, it beats PVT [Wang, ICCV 2021] by up to +3.1 pp (PVT-T), Swin [Liu, ICCV 2021] by +0.4 to +1.0 pp, and CSwin [Dong, CVPR 2022] by +0.4 to +0.6 pp, with parameter counts actually decreasing slightly for PVT and Swin variants. For image generation, VCA lowers FID-50K over DiT [Peebles, ICCV 2023] by 2.3–5.2 points and over SiT [Ma, ECCV 2024] by 2.1–4.3 points across all model sizes and patch resolutions. In the ablation (Table 3), VCA also surpasses the original language-oriented Differential Transformer [Ye, ICLR 2025] applied to both stages by +0.5 pp in classification accuracy and −1.8 FID in generation, confirming that the vision-specific spatial-pooling and dual-embedding design is strictly better than a direct port of the NLP differential attention mechanism.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper adapts the differential attention mechanism from the Differential Transformer (Ye et al., ICLR 2025), which subtracts two softmax attention maps to highlight discriminative signal in language, to the vision domain. Architecturally, it builds on the mediator-token paradigm established by Agent Attention (Han et al., ECCV 2024), Anchored Stripe Attention (Li et al., CVPR 2023), and Efficient DiT (Pu et al., ECCV 2024), all of which insert an auxiliary token set between queries and keys to linearize attention. The key departure is the vision-specific use of spatial average pooling to distill the dense query field into a small set of 'visual contrast tokens' (n ≪ N), exploiting the spatial smoothness of natural images, and the two-stage design where Stage I produces a global contrast map and Stage II lets original patch queries differentially attend to that map.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

VCA offers a practically attractive drop-in replacement for MHSA: it reduces per-layer complexity from O(N²C) to O(NnC) while simultaneously improving accuracy, with fewer than 0.3 M extra parameters and no additional FLOPs. The consistency of gains across plain ViTs, hierarchical backbones (PVT, Swin, CSwin), and both diffusion- and flow-based generators suggests broad applicability. However, the reliance on task-agnostic average pooling may miss edge-rich or high-frequency details, the speed advantage shrinks on small images where N is already modest, and extensions to video, 3D, or language remain unexplored. Whether the contrastive formulation generalizes beyond ImageNet-scale classification and 256×256 generation to more diverse or open-vocabulary settings is an open question.
