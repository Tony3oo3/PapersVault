## Problem

Existing monocular 3D tracking methods suffer from two critical limitations: they assume a static camera and cannot disentangle camera motion from dynamic object motion, and they are restricted to tracking sparse pixels from the first frame, failing to capture newly emerging objects in intermediate frames. This prevents dense, world-centric 3D tracking of all pixels across a full video sequence.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/NEURIPS20254723/cropped_page1_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method consumes a monocular video along with three foundation-model outputs: sparse 2D tracks (from CoTrackerV3 or DELTA at H/s × W/s resolution), monocular depth maps (UniDepth), and coarse foreground dynamic masks (VLM + GroundingSAM or SegmentAnyMotion). It produces dense world-centric 3D trajectories (M_t × 3 per frame) for almost all pixels across all T frames, plus per-frame SE(3) camera poses. The 'almost all' qualifier reflects filtering of noisy outlier tracks for robustness, and the 2D tracks are upsampled from sparse to full H × W resolution before 3D lifting.

## Method

TrackingWorld operates in two stages. First, it generalizes DELTA's upsampler module to lift arbitrary sparse 2D tracks to dense 2D tracks, applying this iteratively to every frame while discarding redundant tracks in regions already covered by prior frames. Second, it lifts dense 2D tracks into world-centric 3D via a three-step optimization: (1) initial camera pose estimation via reprojection loss on static regions, (2) dynamic background refinement that introduces a per-point offset O_static with an L1 'as-static-as-possible' regularizer to identify and absorb misclassified dynamic background points during bundle adjustment, and (3) dynamic object tracking that jointly optimizes 3D coordinates with projection, depth-consistency, as-rigid-as-possible, and temporal-smoothness losses. The key insight is treating all initially-static points as potentially dynamic while constraining them to remain as static as possible, which simultaneously improves camera pose accuracy and recovers background object motion.

## Results

On camera pose estimation, TrackingWorld (DELTA) achieves ATE of 0.088, 0.016, and 0.016 on Sintel, Bonn, and TUM-D respectively, outperforming the best prior method MonST3R (0.111, 0.029, 0.063) by 21%, 45%, and 75% on ATE. For dense 3D track depth accuracy, the method reduces Abs Rel error from 0.636 to 0.218 on Sintel (a 66% reduction) and improves δ<1.25 inlier ratio from 63.1% to 73.3% over DELTA with the same UniDepth prior. On sparse 3D tracking (ADT), TrackingWorld achieves AJ of 23.4 and APD_3D of 32.2, substantially exceeding DELTA (15.3, 22.9) and SpatialTracker (14.3, 22.3). The 2D upsampler achieves EPE of 1.24 and IoU of 80.9 on CVO-Clean, matching or exceeding DELTA's 2D tracking while running ~12× faster.

## Baselines & Comparisons

TrackingWorld outperforms all 15 baselines across the four evaluation dimensions. On camera pose estimation (Table 1), it beats the strongest joint depth-and-pose methods MonST3R (ATE 0.111→0.088 on Sintel, 0.063→0.016 on TUM-D) and Uni4D (0.116→0.088, 0.039→0.016) by 21–75% on ATE, and also surpasses pose-only methods like DROID-SLAM (0.175) and DPVO (0.115) on Sintel. On depth accuracy (Table 2), the optimization-based refinement reduces Abs Rel from 0.636 to 0.218 on Sintel compared to DELTA+UniDepth, a 66% improvement, while also outperforming DELTA with ZoeDepth (0.814) and Depth Pro (0.813). For sparse 3D tracking (Table 3), TrackingWorld (DELTA) achieves AJ 23.4 and APD_3D 32.2 on ADT, beating DELTA (15.3, 22.9), SpatialTracker (14.3, 22.3), CoTrackerV3+UniDepth (13.6, 21.3), and OmniTrackFast (8.6, 18.2); on PStudio (static camera) it ties DELTA on AJ (15.1) and slightly improves APD_3D (25.6 vs 24.6). On dense 2D tracking (Table 4), CoTrackerV3+Up achieves EPE 1.24 and IoU 80.9 on CVO-Clean, beating RAFT (2.48), CoTracker (1.51), SpatialTracker (1.84), and DOT-3D (1.33) on EPE, and exceeding DELTA (1.14) on IoU (80.9 vs 78.9) while DELTA retains a slight EPE edge (1.14 vs 1.24).

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper builds directly on DELTA's tracking upsampler and CoTrackerV3's sparse 2D tracking, repurposing them to track every frame rather than only the first, and on Uni4D's optimization framework for separating static and dynamic scene elements. It departs from prior 3D tracking methods (SpatialTracker, DELTA, OmniMotion) that operate in camera-centric coordinates by explicitly estimating camera poses and lifting tracks into a world-centric frame, and from recent world-centric approaches (St4RTrack, TAPIP3D) by achieving dense tracking of newly emerging objects rather than sparse or drift-prone results. The key conceptual departure is the 'as-static-as-possible' constraint that treats all initially-static points as potentially dynamic, enabling robust camera pose estimation even when foreground masks miss background motion.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

TrackingWorld fills a practical gap: prior 3D tracking methods either assume a static camera or cannot track objects that appear mid-video, limiting their use in motion analysis, novel view synthesis, and video generation pipelines. The ~3× depth accuracy improvement over DELTA+UniDepth (0.636→0.218 Abs Rel on Sintel) demonstrates that optimization-based geometric consistency is a powerful complement to learned depth priors. Open questions include scalability to longer videos (current ~20 min for 30 frames), robustness to highly dynamic scenes with many moving objects, and whether the pipeline can be made real-time for interactive applications.
