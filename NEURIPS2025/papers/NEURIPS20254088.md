## Problem

Existing egocentric video-language pretraining methods learn from 1D text or 2D cues like bounding boxes, which inherently lack 3D spatial understanding. While humans effortlessly infer depth and spatial relationships from 2D visual signals, models pretrained solely on 2D frames and general-purpose text struggle to develop robust 3D awareness. The key challenges are obtaining large-scale depth supervision at low cost and bridging the fundamental modality gap between pixel-level depth regression and text-based contrastive learning.

## Input / Output

During pretraining, the model consumes ~1-second video clips at 224p resolution, original and spatially enriched text captions, and pseudo depth maps at 56p resolution generated by Depth Anything V2-Large. The 3D-aware decoder (only 9M parameters, vs. 768-dim encoders) predicts low-resolution depth maps to maintain a large batch size of 4096 for contrastive learning. At inference time, only the dual encoders are used for downstream tasks, introducing zero additional computational cost; the depth decoder is discarded.

## Method

EgoDTM extends a dual-encoder video-language architecture (ViT video encoder + CLIP-initialized text encoder with flash attention) with a lightweight 3D-aware decoder that predicts depth from the final-layer video features. The decoder comprises a plain feature pyramid projecting to multi-scale outputs, a depth-aware transformer decoder where L learnable depth queries attend to multi-scale features via cross-attention, a bin head predicting adaptive depth interval widths, and a mask head producing per-pixel distributions over depth-bin centers. Training jointly optimizes an InfoNCE video-text contrastive loss (with randomly sampled original or enriched captions) and an L2 depth regression loss against pseudo-depth from a foundation model. A detect-track-generate data pipeline (HOID detection → SAM2 bidirectional tracking → DeepSeek-LLM caption enrichment) produces spatially informed captions describing HOI positions, object shapes, and movements, enabling the text modality to carry 3D-relevant information that complements the depth supervision.

## Results

On zero-shot video-text retrieval (EK100MIR), EgoDTM achieves 33.5% mAP and 33.4% nDCG, outperforming the previous SOTA AVION (32.9% mAP, 32.7% nDCG) by +0.6% and +0.7% respectively. On zero-shot action recognition (EGTEA), EgoDTM reaches 40.2% mean accuracy and 43.2% top-1 accuracy, surpassing AVION (38.6%/42.3%) and LaViLa (30.9%/35.1%). For depth estimation on H2O, EgoDTM attains a scale-aware RMSE of 0.539 and scale-invariant RMSE of 0.481, improving over LaViLa by ~9.8% in scale-aware RMSE. On robot manipulation in Franka Kitchen, EgoDTM achieves 58.6% average success rate, outperforming LaViLa (52%) and CLIP (38.4%) but trailing specialized methods like MPI (75%).

## Baselines & Comparisons

EgoDTM consistently outperforms all text-only and region-aware baselines across the majority of tasks. Against AVION (the strongest prior method), EgoDTM gains +0.9% mAP on EK100MIR, +1.6% mean accuracy on EGTEA, and +1.5% intra accuracy on EgoMCQ, while also leading on NLQ (R5@0.3: 16.11 vs. 14.89) and MQ (R5@0.3: 50.08 vs. 47.3). Against LaViLa, the improvements are more pronounced: +2.7% mAP on EK100MIR, +9.3% mean accuracy on EGTEA, and +6.6% average success rate on robot manipulation. Against region-aware methods HelpingHands and HENASY, EgoDTM surpasses them by +2.3% mAP and +10.8% EGTEA mean accuracy without requiring extra parameters in the visual encoder. On depth estimation, EgoDTM leads all video-language baselines in scale-aware RMSE (0.539 vs. 0.598 for LaViLa) and scale-invariant RMSE (0.481 vs. 0.497 for EgoVLP), though EgoVLP slightly edges out on scale-invariant δ1 (0.853 vs. 0.848). The one area where EgoDTM does not lead is robot manipulation, where self-supervised methods R3M (69.4%) and MPI (75%) still outperform it (58.6%), likely due to their broader pretraining objectives.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the egocentric video-language pretraining paradigm established by EgoVLP and LaViLa, which rely on text-only or 2D region cues (bounding boxes) for supervision. The key departure is introducing monocular depth maps as a third pretraining signal via a lightweight 3D-aware decoder with adaptive bins, drawing on depth estimation techniques (Adabins, FPN) and foundation models (Depth Anything V2, SAM2, HOID) that were previously applied in isolation. Unlike region-aware approaches like HelpingHands and HENASY that add extra encoder parameters for 2D spatial reasoning, EgoDTM embeds 3D awareness directly into the shared video representation through a 9M-parameter decoder that is discarded at inference time.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The work demonstrates that 3D depth supervision during pretraining yields measurable gains across a broad range of downstream tasks (retrieval, action recognition, robot manipulation, long-video localization), suggesting that spatial awareness is a transferable inductive bias for egocentric understanding. The generated dataset of millions of depth maps, HOI masks, and spatially enriched captions is a useful byproduct for the community. However, the authors acknowledge limited generalization beyond egocentric HOI scenarios, and the robot manipulation results, while improved over CLIP/LaViLa, remain well below specialized self-supervised methods like R3M and MPI, leaving open whether depth-aware pretraining can close the gap to purpose-built manipulation representations.
