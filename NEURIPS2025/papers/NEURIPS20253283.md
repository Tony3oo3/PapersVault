## Problem

Recovering editable CAD modeling sequences from raw 3D point clouds remains challenging because existing methods either treat geometry as a monolithic entity (ignoring procedural structure and hierarchical dependencies), suffer from error accumulation in fully autoregressive decoding, or lack explicit mechanisms to align geometric features with stepwise modeling instructions. The core question is how to structure and interpret raw geometry to reflect the underlying design process and guide generation of accurate, semantically meaningful CAD instructions.

## Input / Output

The method takes a 3D point cloud of 2,048 uniformly sampled points (with surface normals) from a normalized CAD model as input. It produces an ordered sequence of up to 10 sketch-extrusion pairs, where each sketch encodes 2D primitives (line, arc, circle) as quantized 8-bit coordinate tokens and each extrusion specifies orientation (Euler angles), translation, scaling, extrusion distance, and Boolean operation type. All continuous parameters are discretized into a 267-token vocabulary, and sequences are padded to 110 tokens for batched training.

## Method

PartCAD is a semi-autoregressive framework with three stages. First, an autoregressive Transformer decoder (4 layers, 8 heads) incrementally generates part-aware latent representations from a global EdgeConv-encoded point cloud feature, with a part discriminator providing auxiliary validity supervision. Second, each part latent predicts a sketch-plane orientation; the point cloud is rotated and projected onto three canonical planes (xy, xz, yz) using an adaptive strategy combining normal-based filtering and grid-constrained resampling, followed by a hierarchical KNN aggregation kernel that progressively refines neighbors by 2D distance, radial distance, and surface normal similarity. Third, a non-autoregressive decoder fuses part-level semantics with point-level and global projection features via cross-attention and self-attention, then two Transformer decoder layers produce complete sketch and extrusion parameters in a single forward pass, supervised by a multi-objective loss combining parameter cross-entropy, rotation cross-entropy, and validity binary cross-entropy.

## Results

On the DeepCAD test set, PartCAD achieves a median Chamfer Distance of 0.238×10³ and an Invalid Rate of 0.91%, dramatically outperforming the strongest sequence-generation baseline TransCAD (median CD 5.67×10³, IR 3.60%) and DeepCAD (median CD 17.49×10³, IR 8.51%). Instruction-level F1 scores reach 82.82 (Line), 56.06 (Arc), 81.06 (Circle), and 95.56 (Extrusion), all leading among sequence-based methods. On the cross-dataset Fusion 360 Gallery evaluation, PartCAD reduces median CD to 1.130×10³ with IR 1.31%, an order-of-magnitude improvement over TransCAD (39.24×10³) and DeepCAD (95.88×10³).

## Baselines & Comparisons

PartCAD outperforms all six baselines across every reported metric. Against sequence-generation methods, it leads TransCAD [43] by 7.79 points on Line F1, 15.54 on Arc F1, 7.17 on Circle F1, and 6.39 on Extrusion F1, while reducing median CD from 5.67 to 0.238 (×10³) and IR from 3.60% to 0.91%. The gap over DeepCAD [42] and its variant DeepCAD* is even larger, with median CD reduced by roughly 70× and 35× respectively. Against primitive-fitting methods, PartCAD achieves the lowest median CD (0.238 vs. 0.355 for SECAD [40], 0.371 for ExtrudeNet [14], 0.493 for Point2Cyl [15], and 0.841 for HNC-CAD [13]) and the lowest IR (0.91% vs. 3.72–24.51%), indicating both superior geometric fidelity and far fewer invalid outputs. On the cross-dataset Fusion 360 Gallery test, PartCAD's median CD of 1.130×10³ remains competitive with the best primitive-fitting method (SECAD at 0.433) while providing full parametric design history, whereas all sequence-generation baselines degrade severely (median CD 39–96×10³).

## Relation to Prior Work

PartCAD sits in the lineage of CAD instruction generation from point clouds, following DeepCAD (autoencoder-based sequence reconstruction) and TransCAD (hierarchical transformer for sequence inference), but departs from both by introducing a semi-autoregressive architecture: autoregressive part-level latent decomposition followed by a single-pass non-autoregressive instruction decoder. The triplane projection guidance with adaptive grid sampling and hierarchical KNN aggregation is a novel geometric interface not present in prior sequence-generation or primitive-fitting methods, bridging the gap between raw 3D geometry and 2D sketch-plane reasoning. The work also draws on EdgeConv-style point cloud encoding and Transformer decoder blocks, repurposing them within a part-aware, projection-conditioned framework.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

PartCAD addresses a practically important gap in reverse engineering: converting unstructured point clouds (e.g., from 3D scans) into editable, parametric CAD programs that encode design intent rather than just final geometry. The large performance margins over prior sequence-based methods (median CD ~24× lower than TransCAD on DeepCAD) and strong cross-dataset generalization suggest the part-aware + projection-guided formulation is a meaningful step forward. Open questions remain around the inherent one-to-many geometry-to-sequence ambiguity, scalability to highly complex or hierarchically unstructured models, and whether the sketch-extrusion-only representation is sufficient for broader design expressiveness. The real-scan experiments hint at practical deployment potential in manufacturing and education, though robustness under severe degradation is still limited.
