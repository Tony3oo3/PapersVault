## Problem

Existing IR and RAG benchmarks are outdated, artificially easy, and static, suffering from contamination, overfitting, and staleness. They rely on short extractive answers or crowd-sourced questions that fail to capture the complexity of real user queries on fast-growing, niche technical domains. FreshStack addresses this by constructing challenging, refreshable evaluation benchmarks from real community-asked questions on recent domains (e.g., LangChain, YOLO v7/v8) where existing retrieval models significantly underperform oracle approaches.

## Input / Output

The framework consumes long-form Stack Overflow questions (350–500 tokens, often containing code snippets and error messages) paired with accepted answers, alongside a corpus of 25K–117K document chunks (max 2048 tokens each) sourced from 4–10 GitHub repositories per domain. It produces evaluation datasets with GPT-4o-generated nuggets (3–4 atomic facts per question), binary nugget-level relevance judgments for top-20 retrieved documents, and retrieval/RAG evaluation scores (α-nDCG@10, Coverage@20, Recall@50, and All Strict nugget recall). The output is structured as per-domain test collections with both inference-setting (question-only) and oracle-setting (answer/nugget-based) retrieval pools.

## Method

FreshStack is a three-stage automatic pipeline: (1) corpus collection clones GitHub repositories, parses files as trees, and chunks them into ≤2048-token segments; (2) nugget generation uses GPT-4o with grading-notes-style prompts to decompose verbose Stack Overflow answers into 3–4 atomic factual nuggets per question; (3) nugget-level support assessment uses GPT-4o with chain-of-thought prompts to judge whether each of the top-20 retrieved documents factually supports each nugget as a binary yes/no. Document pools are constructed via ensemble fusion of four retrieval models (BM25, BGE Gemma-2, E5 Mistral 7B, Voyage-large-2) under both inference (question decomposition or HyDE-style closed-book answer) and oracle (gold answer or concatenated nuggets) settings. The key design choice is replacing traditional document-level relevance with nugget-level support, which reduces ambiguity when judging relevance for long, code-mixed questions. Post-processing removes questions with no supported documents or unsupported nuggets, sacrificing ~46% of questions for higher precision.

## Results

On FreshStack's five niche domains, the best inference-setting system (Fusion of 4 models + Voyage AI rerank-2) achieves α-nDCG@10 scores of 0.397 (LangChain), 0.416 (Yolo v7&v8), 0.319 (Laravel 10&11), 0.318 (Angular 16,17&18), and 0.340 (Godot4), with Recall@50 ranging from 0.488 to 0.614. In contrast, the oracle upper baseline (Stack Overflow Nuggets + Fusion) reaches α-nDCG@10 of 0.519–0.601 and Recall@50 of 0.655–0.825, revealing a substantial accuracy gap that indicates significant headroom for improvement. For RAG evaluation, the oracle setting with GPT-4.1 achieves All Strict (A_strict) scores of 0.651–0.709 across domains, while the best inference setting (Fusion+Rerank + GPT-4.1) reaches 0.591–0.684, with the inference system even surpassing oracle on Yolo v7&v8 (0.684 vs. 0.667).

## Baselines & Comparisons

Fusion of four retrieval models (BM25, BGE Gemma-2, E5 Mistral 7B, Voyage-large-2) consistently outperforms every individual model across all metrics and all five domains, with the sole exception of α-nDCG@10 on Godot4 where E5 Mistral (0.217) edges out Fusion (0.265) in the inference setting. Among individual models, BM25 achieves the best α-nDCG@10 in the pooling stage on all domains, underscoring the continued strength of lexical matching for technical code-mixed content. The Voyage AI rerank-2 reranker provides clear gains over BM25 on all five domains (e.g., LangChain αN@10: 0.230→0.322) and over dense retrievers on LangChain, Yolo v7&v8, and Godot4, but notably degrades performance on Laravel 10&11 and Angular 16,17&18 for BGE, Voyage-large-2, and Fusion—suggesting domain-specific limitations of the neural reranker. In the RAG setting, GPT-4.1 (knowledge cutoff June 2024) outperforms GPT-4o (cutoff October 2023) in closed-book generation across all domains, and the Fusion+Rerank inference pipeline with GPT-4.1 as generator achieves A_strict of 0.684 on Yolo v7&v8, surpassing even the oracle fusion baseline (0.667), indicating that strong retrieval can partially compensate for the absence of gold-answer context.

## Relation to Prior Work

FreshStack extends the nugget-based evaluation lineage from TREC QA 2003 and the TREC 2024 RAG track, repurposing LLM-generated nuggets not just for scoring answers but as the unit of relevance judgment during benchmark construction. It departs from prior IR benchmark construction (BEIR, Natural Questions, XOR-TyDI) by sourcing questions from community-asked Stack Overflow posts on niche, post-2023 domains and building corpora from GitHub technical documentation rather than Wikipedia or curated passages. The pooling methodology draws on standard IR techniques (BM25, dense retrievers, ensemble fusion) and the HyDE/question-decomposition literature, but applies them in an oracle-informed setting to build judgment pools for a new evaluation dataset rather than to answer queries at inference time.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

FreshStack addresses a widely acknowledged gap: existing IR/RAG benchmarks are stale, contaminated, or artificially easy, making it difficult to measure genuine progress in retrieval-augmented systems. The framework's refreshability (re-running on new Stack Overflow posts and updated GitHub repos) and its adoption into the RTEB benchmark suite position it as a potentially long-lived evaluation resource. Key open questions include whether the GPT-4o-based nugget generation and support assessment generalize to domains without clear Stack Overflow communities, whether the observed reranker inconsistency (helpful in 3/5 domains, harmful in 2/5) reflects a fundamental limitation of cross-encoder rerankers on code-mixed content, and how the large oracle-inference gap will narrow as retrieval models improve on niche technical corpora.
