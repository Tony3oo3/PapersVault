## Problem

Current 3D scene understanding methods require either pre-constructed 3D geometry (point clouds, depth maps, meshes) or complete offline multi-view observations with known camera poses, making them impractical for real-world agents that must explore and understand scenes on the fly. No existing framework simultaneously performs online 3D geometric reconstruction and holistic open-world scene understanding from a streaming video without any pose, geometry, or label priors.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/NEURIPS20251833/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

EA3D consumes a streaming RGB video frame-by-frame with no camera poses, depth, or semantic annotations, processing each new frame as it arrives while maintaining a dynamic semantic cache of all observed object categories. Outputs are produced incrementally and simultaneously: photo-realistic novel-view rendering, per-pixel semantic and instance segmentation, 3D bounding boxes, semantic occupancy grids, 3D meshes, depth maps, and object-level physical property descriptions. Every fifth frame is held out as a test view, and the system operates at approximately 0.235 FPS per-frame optimization on a single A100 GPU.

## Method

EA3D integrates three components into a unified online pipeline. First, a VLM (GPT-4o) identifies all objects and physical properties per frame, while multi-level VFMs (CLIP, GroundedSAM, DINOv2) extract pixel-level semantic features; these are fused into a knowledge-integrated feature map that is embedded into 3D Gaussian primitives via a fast feedforward step, with a dynamic semantic cache tracking newly emerging categories over time. Second, online visual odometry (Cut3R + Deep Patch Visual SLAM) iteratively estimates 6-DoF poses and dense point maps, while an online Gaussian updating strategy (extending HiCoM) incrementally adds and adjusts Gaussians per frame using back-projected depth, shared translation/rotation in co-visible regions, and gradient-driven splitting. Third, a recurrent joint optimization module fuses current observations with historical features through a combined loss (photometric L1, depth L1, knowledge-integrated feature L2, and a semantic-aware scale regularization), simultaneously refining geometry, semantics, and camera poses without any post-refinement stage.

## Results

On ScanNet, EA3D (fully pose-free, online) achieves PSNR 25.8, SSIM 0.89, segmentation mIoU 46.3, mAcc 71.8, 3D bbox mAP 39.9, and occupancy mIoU 33.9, outperforming all offline and online baselines across all four task groups. On LeRF under sparse-view online settings, EA3D reaches PSNR 23.2 and mIoU 57.4 at 70 views, surpassing the best offline baseline (OpenGaussian: PSNR 22.7, mIoU 55.8) while operating without any pre-computed poses. The method runs at 0.235 FPS per-frame optimization on a single A100 GPU, roughly 2.3× faster than the HiCoM+VFM baseline (0.102 FPS) while delivering substantially higher quality.

## Baselines & Comparisons

EA3D outperforms all offline reconstruction-based methods (LangSplat, GaussianGrouping, FeatureGS, OpenGaussian, InstanceGaussian) by a wide margin on every metric; for example, it gains +1.9 PSNR and +5.5 mIoU over the strongest offline baseline FeatureGS (23.9/41.1). Against online 2D-to-3D lifting methods (OpenScene, EmbodiedSAM, SAM3D), EA3D achieves higher segmentation mIoU (46.3 vs. 44.2 for EmbodiedSAM) and occupancy mIoU (33.9 vs. 33.0) while requiring no depth input or pre-built point clouds. Among the enhanced online baselines, EA3D surpasses FeatureGS+HiCoM (the closest competitor using semantic features + online updates) by +1.3 PSNR, +5.5 mIoU, and +5.2 mAP, and beats MonoGS+VFM by +1.5 PSNR and +10.0 mIoU. On LeRF sparse-view evaluation, EA3D leads all methods at every view count (10/30/70), achieving 57.4 mIoU at 70 views versus 55.8 for OpenGaussian and 43.3 for HiCoM+VFM, while also being the only method that operates fully pose-free and online. The only metric where EA3D does not hold the top score is 3D bbox AP (57.9 vs. 59.2 for EA3D* with Colmap poses and 58.1 for EmbodiedSAM), indicating a slight trade-off from self-estimated odometry in precise localization.

## Relation to Prior Work

EA3D extends the streaming 3D Gaussian Splatting line (HiCoM, 3DGS-Stream) by removing the dependency on pre-computed poses and multi-view inputs, replacing them with online visual odometry (Cut3R + Deep Patch SLAM) and iterative bundle adjustment. It also builds on the feature-Gaussian paradigm (FeatureGS, Feature3DGS) but departs by embedding open-world knowledge from VLMs (GPT-4o) and multi-level VFMs (CLIP, SAM, GroundedSAM, DINOv2) directly into each Gaussian primitive via a feedforward mechanism, rather than relying on offline pseudo-labels. The key conceptual departure from prior work is the simultaneous, pose-free, online joint optimization of geometry and semantics, whereas prior methods either required complete multi-view observations (LangSplat, GaussianGrouping, FeatureGS) or pre-constructed 3D geometry (OpenScene, SAM3D, EmbodiedSAM).

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

EA3D addresses a practically critical gap for embodied agents: the ability to reconstruct and understand 3D scenes in real time without any geometric or pose priors, which is the natural operating mode for robots exploring unfamiliar environments. The unified framework supporting rendering, segmentation, bounding boxes, occupancy, and mesh generation from a single online pipeline reduces the engineering burden of stitching together separate modules. However, evaluation is limited to two datasets (ScanNet, LeRF) with no error bars, and the method's scalability to large-scale outdoor scenes, dynamic objects, or long-horizon exploration remains untested. Open questions include whether the recurrent joint optimization degrades over very long sequences and how the approach handles adversarial or highly textured environments where visual odometry may fail.
