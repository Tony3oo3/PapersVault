## Problem

Existing protein language models are limited in three key ways: masked-language-model-based approaches cannot score indels or model epistatic interactions in multi-mutation variants; supervised few-shot function learning still lacks data efficiency; and scaling model size primarily improves structure prediction rather than fitness or function prediction. No prior model combines both multimodal (sequence + structure) conditioning and retrieval-augmented in-context learning from homologs in a single architecture.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/NEURIPS20251772/cropped_page2_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The model takes a prompt consisting of a set of homologous protein sequences (context) and an optional partially-specified query protein that constrains generation at a subset of residues. Structure is encoded as 128-bin discretized pairwise Cα–Cα distances plus 36 local backbone atom distances per residue, with per-residue pLDDT confidence scores. For zero-shot variant scoring, the CLM decoder outputs a length-adjusted log-likelihood ratio between variant and wild-type; for supervised prediction, the MLM decoder produces per-residue embeddings that are combined with CLM log-likelihoods in a Gaussian Process kernel. The entire architecture is equivariant to the ordering of proteins in the prompt.

## Method

PoET-2 is a 182M-parameter encoder-decoder Transformer with 12 layers and 1024 hidden dimension, using weight tying across the encoder and both decoders. The encoder employs a two-stage hierarchical attention mechanism (within-protein then across-proteins) that is fully equivariant to protein order, enabling in-context learning from arbitrary homolog sets without memorization. Two decoders operate in parallel: a causal decoder trained with a CLM objective for autoregressive generation and exact joint-probability scoring of variable-length sequences (handling indels and epistasis), and a bidirectional decoder trained with an MLM objective for context-aware residue embeddings. A structure-based attention bias, analogous to T5's relative positional bias but keyed to 3D Cα distance bins rather than linear position, is applied to all intra-protein attention operations. Training uses 62M homologous sequence sets from UniRef50 with optional AlphaFoldDB structures, optimizing a combined loss of MLM encoder, CLM decoder, and MLM decoder terms.

## Results

On the ProteinGym zero-shot DMS substitutions benchmark, PoET-2 (182M params) achieves a Spearman ρ of 0.500 overall, comparable to the state-of-the-art VenusREM (0.519), while substantially outperforming all other single models. On the DMS indels benchmark, PoET-2 achieves ρ = 0.566, a 20% improvement over the next best model (ProGen2 M at 0.466) and 9% over its predecessor PoET-1 (0.517). On the clinical benchmark, PoET-2 sets new state-of-the-art AUROC of 0.928 (substitutions) and 0.952 (indels). In the supervised setting, PoET-2 GP achieves an average Spearman ρ of 0.693 across random, modulo, and contiguous folds, outperforming the previous best Kermut (0.664) by ~4.4%.

## Baselines & Comparisons

PoET-2 outperforms all single-model baselines (ESM-2, ESM C, SaProt, ESM-3 Open, ProSST, MSA Transformer, TranceptEVE L, S3F-MSA) on both DMS substitutions and indels, with the largest margins on indels (0.566 vs. 0.466 for ProGen2 M, the only other model scoring indels besides PoET-1). Against VenusREM, the current state-of-the-art ensemble, PoET-2 trails slightly on overall DMS substitutions (0.500 vs. 0.519, p<1e-3) but dominates on higher-order mutations (3+ substitutions: 0.444 vs. 0.352 for 3 mutations) and is the only model that can score indels; a simple PoET-2+VenusREM ensemble achieves 0.543 overall, surpassing all prior methods. On the clinical benchmark, PoET-2 improves AUROC over PoET-1 by 0.008 (subs.) and 0.018 (indels), and over ESM-1b by 0.036 (subs.). In the supervised setting, PoET-2 GP beats Kermut (0.693 vs. 0.664 avg Spearman ρ) and ProteinNPT (0.693 vs. 0.619) across all three cross-validation folds, with the largest gap on the contiguous fold (0.645 vs. 0.613 for Kermut). Notably, PoET-2 achieves these results with only 182M parameters, compared to 650M–6.4B for ESM-2, ProGen2 XL, and ESM-3 Open.

## Relation to Prior Work

PoET-2 directly extends PoET-1's hierarchical, protein-order-equivariant encoder for processing unaligned homolog sets, but makes the encoder fully bidirectional (PoET-1 was decoder-only) and adds a dual-decoder architecture combining causal and masked language modeling objectives. It departs from structure-aware PLMs (SaProt, ProSST, ESM-3) that tokenize structure discretely, instead operating on continuous backbone atom distances with a structure-based attention bias analogous to T5's relative positional encoding. The retrieval-augmented, family-centric framing builds on PoET-1 and TranceptEVE, while the multimodal conditioning on both sequence and structure homologs is novel in combining these two previously separate lines of work.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

PoET-2's 182M parameter footprint achieving SOTA on indel and higher-order variant prediction—tasks most PLMs cannot handle—makes it a practical, deployable tool for protein engineering where indels and epistatic effects are common. The striking data-efficiency result (100 training points matching ESM C with ~2600 in the contiguous split) suggests retrieval-augmented family-centric representations encode more transferable evolutionary signal per parameter than monolithic sequence-only models. A key open question is why explicit structure conditioning helps zero-shot stability prediction but provides no benefit for supervised learning or clinical variants, implying the embeddings already capture structural information implicitly; unlocking the residual structural signal may require more sophisticated conditioning mechanisms.
