## Problem

Reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards (GRPO) has been widely applied to enhance reasoning in multimodal LLMs, but its application to unified models that jointly perform visual understanding and generation remains largely unexplored. It is unclear whether a single shared policy optimization framework can simultaneously improve both capabilities, or whether task-specific RL is necessary and whether cross-task reward sharing yields synergistic benefits.

## Input / Output

For text-to-image generation, the model takes a natural-language prompt and produces a 384×384 RGB image via fully autoregressive (F-AR) decoding over VQ-quantized visual tokens, using classifier-free guidance (weight 5) at inference. For multimodal understanding, it takes an image paired with a question and outputs a chain-of-thought reasoning trace enclosed in <think>… tags followed by a final answer in <answer>…</answer> tags. During the unified RL stage, each training sample simultaneously provides a real image, a generation prompt, and a QA pair, so the policy generates both a synthesized image and a CoT solution per candidate, and both are scored jointly within the same GRPO group.

## Method

CoRL is a two-stage RL framework built on Janus-Pro-1B that follows a Foundation-then-Specialization paradigm. Stage 1 (Unified RL) jointly optimizes understanding and generation under a single GRPO objective with a composite reward R_Uni-S1 = R_cycle + R_TIM + λ(R_Acc + R_Format), where the bidirectional cycle consistency reward combines LPIPS visual similarity with SPICE re-captioning fidelity, and the text-image matching reward computes symmetric token-level cosine similarity between the ULM's own text and visual embeddings; the KL constraint is omitted in this stage for optimization efficiency. Stage 2 (Refined RL) applies task-specific GRPO with dedicated rewards (R_cycle + R_TIM for generation; R_Acc + R_Format for understanding) and reintroduces the KL penalty for stable fine-tuning. A pilot study across four RL paradigms (separate, merged, cyclic, unified) reveals that unified RL is the only strategy that improves both tasks simultaneously, establishing that shared group-relative advantage estimation enables cross-task synergy that separate or alternating optimization cannot achieve.

## Results

ULM-R1 (built on Janus-Pro-1.5B) achieves an average 23% improvement over its baseline across nine multimodal understanding benchmarks, with standout gains of +15.2 on WeMath (5.9→21.1) and +10.6 on LogicVista (23.9→34.5). On text-to-image generation, ULM-R1 improves GenEval Overall from 0.73 to 0.77, WISE from 0.26 to 0.33, and DPG from 82.63 to 83.92 over Janus-Pro-1.5B. Among unified models, ULM-R1 achieves the best scores on MMStar (47.6), WeMath (21.1), LogicVista (34.5), MathVerse (25.4), and MathVision (22.0), and ranks second on GenEval (0.77) and WISE (0.33) among all unified models.

## Baselines & Comparisons

Against its direct baseline Janus-Pro-1.5B, ULM-R1 wins on all nine understanding benchmarks, with the largest margins on WeMath (+15.2), LogicVista (+10.6), and MathVerse (+11.9), and on all three generation benchmarks (GenEval +0.04, WISE +0.07, DPG +1.29). Among unified models, ULM-R1 outperforms ILLUME+ (44.3 MMMU), Harmon (38.9 MMMU), Show-o (26.7 MMMU), HermesFlow (28.3 MMMU), and Janus-1.3B (30.5 MMMU) on MMMU, and beats all unified baselines on WeMath, LogicVista, MathVerse, and MathVision. On generation, ULM-R1 (GenEval 0.77) surpasses SD3-Medium (0.74), DALL·E 3 (0.67), SimpleAR (0.63), and all other unified models, though it trails SD3-Medium on WISE (0.33 vs 0.42). Compared to understanding-only MLLMs of similar scale (InternVL3, Qwen2.5-VL, LMM-R1), ULM-R1 narrows the gap substantially on math and logic benchmarks (e.g., WeMath 21.1 vs 22.9 for InternVL3) but still lags on MMMU (42.3 vs 48.7) and MMStar (47.6 vs 61.1), reflecting the inherent trade-off of maintaining generation capability within a unified architecture.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the GRPO-based RL paradigm (from DeepSeekMath and DeepSeek-R1) from understanding-only MLLMs to unified multimodal models that perform both visual generation and understanding. It builds directly on Janus-Pro as the base ULM and departs from prior RL-for-generation work (e.g., SimpleAR, which uses GRPO with a single CLIP reward) by introducing a multi-reward unified optimization that jointly ranks generation and understanding outputs within the same group. The two-stage 'unified-then-refined' CoRL procedure is a novel training recipe, but each component (GRPO, verifiable rewards, task-specific fine-tuning) is individually well-established.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

This is the first systematic study showing that RL with verifiable rewards can simultaneously improve both generation and understanding in a unified model without supervised data, and that unified joint optimization outperforms all alternatives (separate RL, cycle RL, weight merging). The 23% average gain on understanding benchmarks is substantial, but the generation improvements (~7%) remain more modest, and the authors acknowledge that a notable performance gap between the two tasks persists. Open questions include whether more sophisticated understanding rewards (beyond binary accuracy and format) can close this gap, and whether the cross-task synergy effect scales linearly with model size (the 7B LoRA experiment suggests diminishing returns on complex reasoning).
