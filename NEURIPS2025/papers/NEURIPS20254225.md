## Problem

Current approaches to elicit meta-thinking in LLMs are either construction-based (SFT on predefined templates, which lack flexibility and fail to generalize out-of-distribution) or single-agent RL (R1-like, which forces the model to learn meta-thinking and detailed reasoning within a single autoregressive pass, leading to inefficient exploration and premature convergence to local optima). No prior work has formally defined and optimized a multi-agent meta-thinking reasoning process through reinforcement learning with a specialized architecture for acquiring meta-thinking.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/NEURIPS20254225/cropped_page1_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The system takes a text prompt (e.g., a math problem or a pair-ranking judgment query) and produces a structured two-phase output: a meta-thinking plan generated by the high-level agent (which can be free-form or structured JSON with actions like DECOMPOSE, REWRITE, or EMPTY), followed by detailed reasoning steps and a final answer from the low-level agent. In multi-turn mode, this high-level/low-level alternation repeats T times, with each turn conditioned on all prior meta-thoughts and reasoning outputs. The final answer is extracted from the last reasoning turn.

## Method

ReMA decomposes the reasoning process into a high-level meta-thinking agent (π_h) that generates strategic oversight and a low-level reasoning agent (π_l) that executes detailed solutions, trained jointly as a Markov Game via block coordinate ascent—alternately freezing one agent while updating the other with aligned reward functions (correctness, format, and consistency rewards). For multi-turn scaling, both agents share identical model weights distinguished only by role-specific system prompts, eliminating GPU model-swapping overhead and enabling simultaneous policy updates. A key algorithmic contribution is a turn-level ratio for GRPO clipping (Eqs. 13–14), which treats all tokens within a turn as a single action for the importance-sampling ratio, preventing the bias and instability that token-level ratios introduce in multi-turn settings where longer turns would disproportionately influence the loss. The convergence analysis frames the iterative updates as TRPO-based block coordinate ascent, guaranteeing monotonic improvement of the joint objective.

## Results

On mathematical reasoning benchmarks, ReMA achieves the highest average pass@1 across three backbone models: 26.73% (Llama3-8B-Instruct, +6.68% over CoT), 36.97% (Llama3.1-8B-Instruct, +1.08% over CoT), and 53.09% (Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, +2.85% over CoT). On LLM-as-a-Judge, ReMA reaches 68.31% average on Llama3.1-8B-Instruct (+7.92% over CoT) and 70.22% on Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct (+1.75% over CoT). The most striking gains appear on out-of-distribution tasks: +20% on AMC23 (Llama3-8B-Instruct), +13.33% on AIME24 (Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct), and +14.23% on RewardBench970 (Llama3.1-8B-Instruct). In multi-turn settings, ReMA improves training accuracy from ~55% to ~70% and gains ~5% on average across seven OOD math benchmarks.

## Baselines & Comparisons

ReMA is compared against three RL baselines: VRP (plain CoT prompting without RL), VRP_RL (single-agent RL on vanilla reasoning), and MRP_RL (single-agent RL with explicit meta-thinking in one autoregressive pass). On math benchmarks, ReMA achieves the best average across all three models, with the largest margin on Llama3-8B-Instruct (+1.28% over MRP_RL, +1.28% over VRP_RL) and Llama3.1-8B-Instruct (+1.42% over MRP_RL, +3.65% over VRP_RL). The advantage is most pronounced on hard OOD problems: ReMA scores 22.50% on AMC23 (Llama3-8B) versus 12.50% for MRP_RL, and 20.00% on AIME24 (Qwen2.5-7B) versus 10.00% for MRP_RL. However, ReMA does not dominate every individual benchmark—VRP_RL wins on GSM8K for Llama3-8B (81.80 vs. 79.38) and MRP_RL wins on Minerva Math for Llama3-8B (18.01 vs. 13.97) and AMC23 for Llama3.1-8B (30.00 vs. 20.00). On LLM-as-a-Judge, ReMA leads on Llama3.1-8B-Instruct (68.31% avg vs. 67.41% VRP_RL and 67.02% MRP_RL) but underperforms MRP_RL on Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct (70.22% vs. 72.44%), suggesting the multi-agent decomposition is most beneficial for weaker or less instruction-tuned backbones. The paper also reports comparisons with inference-time baselines Self-Refine and Multi-Agent Debate (MAD) in Appendix E.1, where ReMA's trained multi-agent system outperforms these prompt-only approaches.

## Relation to Prior Work

ReMA extends the meta-thinking reasoning line (Meta-CoT, DOTS, EvalPlanner) by decomposing the single-agent meta-thinking-then-reasoning pipeline into two hierarchically structured LLM agents trained jointly via multi-agent RL, rather than relying on construction-based SFT with predefined templates or R1-like single-agent RL that must learn both meta-cognition and execution in one autoregressive pass. The multi-turn GRPO with turn-level ratio (Eqs. 12–14) is a direct modification of GRPO's token-level clipping, and the convergence analysis in Appendix C.4 frames the alternating high/low-level updates as block coordinate ascent under TRPO guarantees. The closest prior work—MAPoRL (multi-agent debate with MARL) and COPRY (dual-agent RL)—does not explicitly separate metacognitive planning from plan execution, which is the core structural distinction ReMA introduces.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The framework offers a practical recipe for eliciting meta-thinking in LLMs with better OOD generalization than single-agent RL, and the turn-level ratio is a reusable stabilization technique for multi-turn RL that addresses a known instability in long-horizon credit assignment. However, the multi-turn setting reveals significant sensitivity to rollout hyperparameters (max turns, max tokens per turn) and an 'Echo Trap' where the model collapses into repetitive or empty outputs, limiting immediate deployability. Open questions include whether the approach transfers to tasks that inherently require multi-turn agent interaction (e.g., tool use, embodied reasoning), how to scale inference-time compute across the two agents, and whether the observed role-reversal dynamics under different reward settings can be harnessed deliberately.
