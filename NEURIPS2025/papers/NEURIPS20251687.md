## Problem

Current LVLM-based embodied planners rely on environment-agnostic imitation learning that maps simplified, context-free instructions directly to action sequences, treating task language and surrounding environment as disconnected elements. This causes models to fail on context-sensitive instructions (e.g., 'place the apple on the table near the television'), struggle with long-horizon tasks by resorting to auxiliary feedback signals rather than visual reasoning, and generalize poorly to unfamiliar environments.

## Input / Output

The agent receives a natural language instruction and a complete sequence of egocentric RGB observations (multi-image history, not just the current frame) in a closed-loop setting with no privileged environmental feedback such as action success signals or task progress indicators. At each timestep the model outputs a symbolic action (e.g., 'pick up knife') conditioned on the full observation history, optionally accompanied by a step-wise reasoning annotation that explains the cognitive link between the current visual state and the chosen action.

## Method

WAP (World-Aware Planning Narrative Enhancement) augments original instruction-trajectory pairs along four cognitive dimensions—visual appearance modeling, spatial-relational reasoning, functional abstraction, and syntactic grounding—using a 72B teacher VLM to generate enriched instructions and step-wise rationales, with a semantic-consistency check ensuring task equivalence. The expanded corpus (80,875 pairs from 16,145 ALFRED trajectories) is then used to fine-tune smaller student models (Qwen2.5-VL-7B, InternVL3-8B) via a three-stage curriculum: base action mapping, environmental understanding (visual + spatial augmentations), and conceptual reasoning (functional + syntactic augmentations). The key design choice is that all training and inference operate under strict partial observability—only raw egocentric RGB frames and the instruction—so the model must build its own world representation from visual history rather than relying on privileged environment metadata, which is what enables the large gains in commonsense reasoning (+60.0) and long-horizon planning (+70.0) over baselines.

## Results

On EB-ALFRED (closed-loop, visual-only observations), Qwen2.5-VL-7B with the full WAP curriculum achieves 62.7% average success rate (STD 6.3), a 60.7-point absolute improvement over the base model (2.0%) and a 13.5× relative gain. InternVL3-8B reaches 61.0% average SR under the same protocol. On the VOTA-Bench unseen split, WAP achieves 64.56% overall SR and 59.86% path-length-weighted success, compared to GPT-4o's 20.36% SR and 16.83% PL, demonstrating strong generalization to novel environments and object configurations.

## Baselines & Comparisons

Against GPT-4o, WAP (62.7% avg SR) surpasses the open-loop result (56.3%) by 6.4 points and exceeds the closed-loop result (26.0%) by 36.7 points, indicating that GPT-4o degrades severely without action feedback. Against Claude-3.5-Sonnet, WAP (62.7%) falls 1.3 points short of the open-loop score (64.0%) but exceeds the closed-loop score (57.3%) by 5.4 points, while also achieving a much lower STD (6.3 vs. 13.4), reflecting more balanced performance across task categories. Against Gemini-1.5-Pro (62.3% open-loop), WAP matches within 0.4 points while operating under stricter closed-loop constraints. The base Qwen2.5-VL-7B model scores only 4.7% and InternVL3-8B scores 10.7%, confirming that the gains come from the WAP enhancement pipeline rather than the underlying architecture. On VOTA-Bench unseen environments, WAP (64.56% SR) outperforms GPT-4o (20.36% SR) by 44.2 points across all five task families, demonstrating superior generalization to novel object configurations.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper departs from the dominant environment-agnostic imitation learning paradigm in embodied planning (ALFRED, ALFWorld), where instructions and environmental context are treated as disconnected elements, and from LVLM-based planners that rely on privileged feedback such as action success signals or task progress indicators. Rather than building on a specific prior architecture, WAP repurposes data augmentation and curriculum learning—techniques well-established in NLP and vision—to systematically inject four-dimensional world knowledge (visual, spatial, functional, syntactic) into planning narratives. The closed-loop, visual-only constraint positions the work against both open-loop imitation methods and recent LVLM planners (LLM-Planner, ProgPrompt, VOTA) that assume auxiliary environmental signals unavailable in real deployment.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The result that a 7B open-source model trained with structured data augmentation can match or exceed proprietary systems (GPT-4o, Claude-3.5-Sonnet) under realistic closed-loop, visual-only constraints challenges the assumption that scale or proprietary access is necessary for robust embodied planning. However, the framework is validated only on ALFRED-style household environments with symbolic actions, leaving open questions about generalization to continuous control, industrial or outdoor settings, and mid-execution error recovery. The technique of multi-dimensional instruction augmentation combined with staged curriculum learning is a reusable recipe that could transfer to other embodied or interactive decision-making tasks, but its practical impact will depend on whether the gains hold beyond the ALFRED distribution.
