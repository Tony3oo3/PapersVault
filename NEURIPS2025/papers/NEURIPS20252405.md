## Problem

Existing counterfactual explanation methods require constant access to the predictive model, perform computationally expensive per-instance optimization, and cannot adapt to new user-defined constraints (sparsity, actionability) without retraining. This paper seeks a model-agnostic generative framework that produces multiple diverse counterfactuals in a single forward pass while enabling real-time constraint customization at inference time.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/NEURIPS20252405/cropped_page1_idx2.webp)

## Input / Output

Given a factual tabular data point x₀, a target class label y', a sparsity exponent p (e.g., 0.01 for sparse, 2.0 for Euclidean), and a binary actionability mask m specifying immutable features, DiCoFlex outputs a set of diverse counterfactual instances in one forward pass of a conditional MAF. The method is model-agnostic: the classifier h is only needed during training to label the KNN-sampled neighbors, not at inference. The same trained model serves all (p, m) combinations, so changing constraints requires no retraining.

## Method

DiCoFlex trains a conditional masked autoregressive flow (MAF) to approximate the conditional distribution p_θ(x'|x, y', p, m) by minimizing KL divergence against an empirical distribution q̂ constructed from K nearest neighbors of x in class y' under a masked Lp distance. During training, p and m are sampled from predefined sets and fed as conditioning inputs, so the flow learns to modulate sparsity and actionability behavior. At inference, the user selects p and m freely, and the flow generates multiple diverse counterfactuals in a single forward pass without any access to the classifier. The KNN construction provides theoretical guarantees: validity by construction, proximity bounded by the K-th neighbor distance, in-distribution plausibility, and a formal diversity preservation bound (Theorem A.2) linking expected diversity to the training error ε.

## Results

DiCoFlex achieves 100% validity across all five benchmark datasets (Adult, Bank Marketing, Default, Give Me Some Credit, Lending Club) while generating multiple diverse counterfactuals in a single forward pass. It attains the best hypervolume (diversity) scores on 4 out of 5 datasets (e.g., 13.118 on Lending Club vs. 8.252 for DiCE and 7.448 for CCHVAE) and the best LOF (plausibility) on 4 out of 5 datasets. Continuous proximity is best for DiCoFlex on 3 out of 5 datasets (e.g., 0.459 on Default vs. 1.039 for DiCE). Inference time is 0.12±0.05 seconds per counterfactual, orders of magnitude faster than optimization-based baselines requiring over 1000 seconds.

## Baselines & Comparisons

Against CCHVAE [Pawelczyk et al., 2020], DiCoFlex wins decisively on diversity (hypervolume 13.118 vs. 7.448 on Lending Club; 62.427 vs. 53.706 on Default) and on LOF plausibility on 4 of 5 datasets, while CCHVAE retains an edge on continuous proximity for GMC (0.389 vs. 0.690). Against DiCE [Mothilal et al., 2020], DiCoFlex dominates on proximity (e.g., 0.459 vs. 1.039 on Default; 0.581 vs. 5.096 on Adult) and LOF, but DiCE achieves lower categorical sparsity (e.g., 0.146 vs. 0.412 on Lending Club), reflecting its optimization-based tendency to flip categorical variables less. Against single-explanation baselines, DiCoFlex achieves 100% validity where ReViSE drops to 0.170–0.910 and TABCF collapses to 0.130 on GMC; Wachter never modifies categorical features (sparsity 0.000) and shows the worst proximity (e.g., 8.588 on GMC). The key trade-off is that DiCoFlex's categorical sparsity is higher than DiCE/CCHVAE, but its ε-sparsity on continuous features is competitive or best in 3 of 5 datasets, and the p=0.01 variant further narrows this gap while improving plausibility.

## Relation to Prior Work

DiCoFlex builds on the conditional normalizing flow literature (MAF, NICE, RealNVP) and repurposes it as a primary counterfactual generator rather than the plausibility scorer used in prior flow-based approaches [46, 12]. It departs from optimization-based diverse CF methods (DiCE, Wachter) that require per-instance gradient descent, and from VAE-based generators (CCHVAE, ReViSE) that produce redundant samples, by training the flow on KNN-sampled valid counterfactuals and conditioning on Lp-norm exponent and feature masks at inference time. The key conceptual departure is treating sparsity and actionability as continuous conditioning variables learned during training, enabling real-time constraint adjustment without retraining—a flexibility absent from prior generative or optimization-based CF frameworks.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The orders-of-magnitude speedup (0.12s vs. >1000s per counterfactual) and the ability to dynamically adjust sparsity/actionability at inference time make diverse CF generation practical for real-time interactive settings (e.g., financial advisors, clinical decision support) where optimization-based methods are infeasible. However, the method trades off sparsity against diversity and plausibility, and the KNN-based training distribution may limit generalization to complex, high-dimensional decision boundaries. Open questions include whether the approach scales to very high-dimensional or mixed-type data beyond the five tabular benchmarks tested, and whether the soft constraint mechanism (Lp-norm, masking) can be replaced with hard constraints for regulatory compliance without sacrificing the single-pass efficiency.
