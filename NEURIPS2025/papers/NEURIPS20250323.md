## Problem

Existing diffusion-based virtual try-on methods struggle to simultaneously preserve high-frequency garment textures (plaid patterns, embroidery, seams) and produce physically plausible fabric deformations under diverse human poses. Rigid warping or heuristic geometric transformations cause irreversible texture distortion, while isolated frequency-domain processing fails to synchronize with pose-dependent deformation fields, leaving a gap between data-driven feature learning and continuum-mechanics principles governing real fabric dynamics.

## Input / Output

The method consumes a person image and a garment image at 1024×768 resolution, along with text prompts, pose keypoints, and a mask for the target garment region. Garment features are extracted from the first 10 channels of a pretrained SDXL UNet bottleneck layer and injected into the target diffusion model after pose-aware deformation. The output is a single photorealistic try-on image at the same resolution, generated via 30 denoising steps with classifier-free guidance (scale 2.0) using IP-Adapter for joint low-level and high-level garment conditioning.

## Method

PhysDiff-VTON integrates three physics-inspired modules into an SDXL inpainting backbone. First, a pose-guided deformable warping module predicts spatially variant offsets via cross-attention between pose embeddings and multi-scale garment features, then applies deformable convolution so that stiffer fabrics receive smaller displacements for equivalent joint movements. Second, Haar wavelet decomposition is embedded in UNet skip connections with a frequency-gated attention mechanism that dynamically amplifies directional high-frequency subbands along deformation axes while suppressing orthogonal noise, preserving Nyquist-critical textile microstructures. Third, Potential-Regularized Path Optimization (PRPO) reformulates the denoising trajectory as a variational problem minimizing an action functional with three potentials—inter-timestep smoothness, total variation, and multiscale self-similarity—injecting gradient corrections and annealed noise into each DDPM sampling step to enforce temporal coherence, spatial regularity, and cross-scale structural consistency without retraining the base model.

## Results

On VITON-HD, PhysDiff-VTON achieves the best LPIPS (0.093), FID (6.21), and CLIP-I (0.894) among all compared methods, with SSIM of 0.881. On DressCode, it similarly leads in LPIPS (0.055), FID (8.27), and CLIP-I (0.918), while also achieving the highest SSIM (0.932). The closest competitor, IDM-VITON, trails by 0.009 in LPIPS, 0.08 in FID, and 0.011 in CLIP-I on VITON-HD, and by 0.007 in LPIPS, 0.37 in FID, and 0.014 in CLIP-I on DressCode.

## Baselines & Comparisons

PhysDiff-VTON outperforms all six baselines on LPIPS, FID, and CLIP-I across both VITON-HD and DressCode. The most direct comparison is with IDM-VITON (the base framework), where PhysDiff-VTON improves LPIPS from 0.102 to 0.093 and FID from 6.29 to 6.21 on VITON-HD, and from 0.062 to 0.055 and 8.64 to 8.27 on DressCode. Among GAN-based methods, GP-VTON achieves the highest SSIM on VITON-HD (0.898 vs. 0.881 for PhysDiff-VTON), but its DressCode performance degrades severely (LPIPS 0.484, FID 55.21), indicating poor generalization. Among diffusion-based baselines, StableVITON is the strongest on DressCode SSIM (0.910) but trails PhysDiff-VTON by 0.052 in LPIPS and 6.10 in FID. Overall, PhysDiff-VTON achieves the best or near-best score on every metric, with the sole exception of VITON-HD SSIM where GP-VTON leads by 0.017.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper builds directly on IDM-VTON (Choi et al., ECCV 2024) as its architectural backbone, adopting the dual-encoder (semantic + UNet) garment conditioning and SDXL inpainting base model. It departs from prior warping approaches (GP-VTON's part-based local flow, HR-VTON's affine transforms) by introducing pose-conditioned deformable convolution that learns material-dependent strain offsets, and from isolated frequency-domain methods by embedding Haar wavelet decomposition into UNet skip connections with frequency-gated attention. The most conceptually novel departure is PRPO, which reframes diffusion sampling as a variational action-minimization problem with physics-inspired potential terms (temporal smoothness, total variation, multiscale self-similarity), a formulation not previously applied to VTON or, to our knowledge, to image generation sampling in general.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The PRPO trajectory optimization strategy is potentially transferable beyond virtual try-on to any diffusion-based generation task where physical or structural coherence across timesteps matters (e.g., video generation, 3D-aware synthesis). However, the practical gains are modest (LPIPS 0.093 vs. 0.102 over IDM-VTON on VITON-HD), the paper reports no error bars or statistical significance tests, and evaluation is limited to two static-image datasets with no video or multi-pose consistency testing. The absence of a limitations discussion and the reliance on a single training dataset (VITON-HD) leave open questions about generalization to diverse garment categories, extreme poses, or higher resolutions. The physics-inspired framing, while intellectually appealing, sometimes outpaces the empirical evidence for genuine continuum-mechanics adherence.
