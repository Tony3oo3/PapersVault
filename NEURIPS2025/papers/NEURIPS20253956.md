## Problem

Existing 3D generation pipelines suffer from two compounding bottlenecks: the SDF extraction step used for 3D-supervised VAEs halves effective resolution and discards small geometric components, while the VAE itself introduces a modality mismatch (encoding surface points or DINOv2 features but decoding SDF values) that forces reliance on heavy attention mechanisms and limits reconstruction fidelity. This paper addresses both the remeshing quality gap and the architectural modality gap simultaneously.

## Input / Output

For reconstruction, the method takes a raw (potentially non-watertight, multi-component) 3D mesh and produces a watertight surface represented as a sparse cube grid at up to 1024³ resolution, where each active cube vertex carries a signed distance value and a learnable deformation vector (the Sparcubes format). For generation, a single 2D image conditions a latent diffusion model that samples Sparconv-VAE latents, which are decoded back into the same Sparcubes format and extracted into a fully watertight, 3D-printable mesh. The VAE is modality-consistent: it compresses and reconstructs Sparcubes parameters directly without any intermediate SDF resampling or point-to-field conversion.

## Method

Sparcubes converts raw meshes into watertight surfaces via a four-step pipeline: (1) sparse active-voxel extraction with UDF computation in a narrow band, (2) volumetric flood fill for coarse sign labeling, (3) gradient-based deformation of grid vertices along the UDF gradient to correct spatial alignment of the zero level set, and (4) optional differentiable multi-view rendering refinement. Sparconv-VAE is a modality-consistent VAE whose sparse convolutional encoder (cascade of sparse residual blocks with lightweight local attention at the coarsest scale) compresses Sparcubes parameters into a sparse latent, and whose self-pruning decoder (interleaving sparse residual convolutions with occupancy-predicting upsample blocks) reconstructs the same format. Because input and output share the same modality, no global attention is needed, yielding a lightweight architecture that trains in under two days. The SDF prediction is split into a sign branch (BCE) and a magnitude branch (L2), and the full loss jointly optimizes occupancy, sign, magnitude, deformation, and KL divergence. The framework plugs directly into TRELLIS's latent diffusion pipeline for single-image-to-3D generation.

## Results

On the VAE reconstruction benchmark (Table 2), Sparconv-VAE at 1024³ resolution achieves the best Chamfer Distance on ABO (1.00×10⁻⁴) and Wild (0.46×10⁻⁴), and the best F1 score across all three datasets (85.41, 65.75, 97.12). On the Wild dataset, it reduces CD by 34% over TRELLIS (0.46 vs. 0.70) and by 93% over Dora (0.46 vs. 68.2). For watertight remeshing (Table 1), Sparcubes at 512³ already outperforms Dora-wt at 1024³ on CD (1.01 vs. 1.07 on ABO; 3.09 vs. 4.35 on Objaverse), while completing conversion in ~15 s vs. ~90 s.

## Baselines & Comparisons

Sparconv-VAE outperforms all four baselines on F1 across every dataset, with the largest margin over Dora on Wild (97.12 vs. 62.07) and over XCubes on Objaverse (65.75 vs. 51.65). On ABO, it leads in CD (1.00 vs. 1.32 for TRELLIS, 1.51 for Craftsman) and F1 (85.41 vs. 80.59 for TRELLIS), though Craftsman edges it slightly on ANC (77.46 vs. 77.69 is actually a win for Ours). On Objaverse, Craftsman achieves a lower CD (2.53 vs. 3.00) and higher ANC (77.37 vs. 75.10), but Sparconv-VAE still wins decisively on F1 (65.75 vs. 55.28), indicating better overall shape coverage. On the challenging Wild dataset, Sparconv-VAE dominates all metrics, reducing CD by 34% over TRELLIS and by 93% over Dora, while Dora's CD degrades catastrophically (68.2 vs. 0.46), likely due to its reliance on watertight input meshes. The 512³ variant already matches or exceeds the 1024³ results, confirming that the sparse representation captures geometry efficiently without needing full-resolution grids.

## Relation to Prior Work

Sparc3D extends the deformable isosurface extraction lineage (FlexiCubes, Dual Marching Cubes) by introducing a sparse, near-lossless remeshing pipeline (Sparcubes) that avoids the UDF-to-SDF conversion step used by prior 3D-supervised VAEs (Dora, Craftsman, Hunyuan3D), which halves effective resolution and discards small components. On the generative side, it departs from both VecSet-based VAEs (which compress local point features into global latents) and TRELLIS's sparse-voxel VAE (which encodes DINOv2 features into SDF) by proposing a modality-consistent Sparconv-VAE that encodes and decodes the same sparse SDF + deformation representation, eliminating the input-output modality gap and the need for heavy global attention. The generation stage directly reuses TRELLIS's latent diffusion architecture, fine-tuned on Sparconv-VAE latents.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

By unifying topology-preserving remeshing and modality-consistent latent encoding into a single framework, Sparc3D addresses two practical bottlenecks that have capped the fidelity of 3D generation pipelines: resolution loss from SDF extraction and detail degradation from modality conversion. The 3× remeshing speedup and 4× faster VAE convergence make high-resolution (1024³) training more accessible, and the watertight, 3D-printable outputs broaden applicability to physical fabrication. Key limitations remain: no texture preservation, and hidden internal structures in fully closed meshes are discarded. Open questions include whether the sparse deformable representation can be extended to carry appearance information and whether the self-pruning decoder generalizes to even higher resolutions or multi-object scenes.
