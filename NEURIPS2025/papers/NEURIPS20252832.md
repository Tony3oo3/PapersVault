## Problem

Existing LLM cascade methods for cost-efficient reasoning rely on supervised training with labeled data, offer no theoretical guarantees on generalization error or worst-case cost overruns, and provide limited control over test-time computational budget. C3PO addresses these gaps by introducing a self-supervised (label-free) cascade optimization framework with conformal probabilistic cost bounds and PAC-Bayes generalization guarantees.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/NEURIPS20252832/cropped_page1_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method consumes a text reasoning prompt and, for each of m LLMs in the cascade, the model's predicted answer and a confidence score in [0,1]. During training, it requires only unlabeled model outputs (predictions and confidence scores from all m models on N prompts), partitioned into a self-supervision set for threshold learning and a calibration set for conformal cost verification. At test time, the cascade produces a final answer and an exit decision (which model index to stop at), evaluated by 0-1 regret relative to the most powerful model's prediction rather than ground truth.

## Method

C3PO learns per-model confidence thresholds via discrete grid search over a finite hypothesis class, minimizing empirical 0-1 regret with respect to the most powerful model (MPM) on unlabeled data. The key insight is formulating the learning objective as agreement with the MPM rather than ground truth, which eliminates the need for labels entirely. A conformal prediction step on a held-out calibration set enforces a probabilistic cost constraint, guaranteeing that inference cost exceeds a user-specified budget with probability at most α (Theorem 1). PAC-Bayes analysis then certifies that the learned thresholds generalize, bounding excess test regret in terms of grid size, number of cascade stages, and calibration set size (Theorem 2). The non-differentiable 0-1 loss renders gradient-based optimization ineffective, so the method instead performs exhaustive or heuristic search over the discrete threshold grid, which is computationally tractable for cascades of fewer than 5 models.

## Results

C3PO achieves state-of-the-art accuracy-cost trade-offs across 16 reasoning benchmarks using three LLM cascades (LLaMA, Qwen, GPT). On MATH-500 with the LLaMA cascade, C3PO reaches 62.5% accuracy at $0.0019/question, compared to 57% at $0.0053/question for self-consistency with the MPM alone. Across all 16 datasets, C3PO requires less than 20% of the MPM's cost to achieve within 2–10% accuracy of the MPM (LLaMA cascade), and the conformal cost guarantee is empirically validated with only 1 violation in 300 runs (15 datasets × 2 cascades × 5 budgets × 2 α levels). On SVAMP and MATH-500, C3PO attains top accuracy at roughly 10× lower cost than all cascade baselines.

## Baselines & Comparisons

C3PO outperforms all four cascade baselines (FrugalGPT, MoT, TREACLE, ModelSwitch) and the self-consistency (SC) oracle across most cost budgets on all 16 datasets and all three model families. Against SC using the MPM, C3PO achieves higher accuracy at a fraction of the cost (e.g., 62.5% vs. 57% on MATH-500 at ~3× lower cost). Against TREACLE (the strongest supervised baseline), C3PO matches or exceeds accuracy while requiring no ground-truth labels and operating at 10× lower cost on SVAMP and MATH-500. Against FrugalGPT, C3PO wins on cost-efficiency since FrugalGPT's BERT meta-model requires labeled training data and does not provide formal cost guarantees. Against MoT and ModelSwitch (other unsupervised methods), C3PO's key advantage is the conformal cost bound: it is the only method that provably keeps cost overruns below a user-specified probability α, whereas MoT and ModelSwitch offer no such guarantee. In the distribution-shift experiment (train on GSM8K/SVAMP, test on MATH-500), C3PO degrades least among all methods, confirming robustness to domain change.

## Relation to Prior Work

C3PO sits at the intersection of LLM cascading (FrugalGPT, TREACLE, Mixture-of-Thoughts, ModelSwitch) and statistical learning tools (conformal prediction, PAC-Bayes theory). While prior cascade methods either require labeled data for meta-model training (FrugalGPT, TREACLE) or rely on heuristic self-consistency thresholds without formal guarantees (MoT, ModelSwitch), C3PO repurposes the self-consistency signal by mapping it to agreement probability with the most powerful model rather than ground truth, eliminating the need for labels. The conformal cost bound and PAC-Bayes generalization guarantee are borrowed from statistical learning and applied here for the first time to the LLM cascade setting, providing a theoretical backbone that no prior cascade method offers.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

C3PO demonstrates that principled, label-free cascade optimization can match or exceed supervised methods while providing provable cost and generalization guarantees, which is practically important for deploying LLMs in new domains where labeled data is scarce or expensive. The conformal guarantee held in 299 of 300 empirical runs, validating the theory in practice. A noted limitation is that the conformal cost constraint can be overly conservative, under-utilizing the budget; future work could explore adaptive or distribution-aware cost calibration. Open questions include whether richer confidence surrogates, verification models, or more advanced search heuristics can tighten the accuracy-cost frontier further, and whether the guarantees extend to streaming or non-i.i.d. deployment settings.
