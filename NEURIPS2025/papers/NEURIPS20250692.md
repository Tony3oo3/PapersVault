## Problem

Text-only Chain-of-Thought reasoning, while effective for language tasks, suffers from visual hallucinations and insufficient multimodal integration when applied to vision-language problems. Existing multimodal CoT approaches remain predominantly prompt-driven rather than end-to-end trained with reinforcement learning, leaving a gap in how to make grounded visual reasoning a learned, optimized capability rather than a prompted behavior.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/NEURIPS20250692/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The model takes a chart or plot image at raw resolution paired with a free-form text question and produces a structured output: a <think> block containing interleaved <points x0="..." y0="..."> coordinate annotations (in raw pixel space, 1-based indexing) preceding each reasoning step, followed by an <answer> tag with the final response. The point coordinates are deterministic and parseable via regex, enabling downstream evaluation of both answer correctness and spatial grounding accuracy independently.

## Method

Point-RFT uses a two-stage training pipeline on Qwen2.5-VL-7B. Stage 1 (SFT) fine-tunes the model on a 71K-sample Point-CoT dataset where GPT-4o generates step-by-step rationales and Molmo-7B grounds each referenced visual element to pixel coordinates, with cross-validation discarding mismatched point counts. Stage 2 (RFT) applies GRPO reinforcement learning on 15K ChartQA samples with a dual reward combining format compliance (regex-based structural check) and answer accuracy (exact or ±5% numeric match), plus a KL penalty against the SFT policy. The key insight is that interleaving point-level visual tokens within the reasoning trace forces perception-reasoning alignment, and RL then optimizes which grounded reasoning paths yield correct answers, yielding 90.04% on ChartQA versus 83.92% for text-only CoT RFT.

## Results

On ChartQA (in-domain), Point-RFT achieves 90.04% accuracy, improving over the base Qwen2.5-VL-7B (70.88%), text-only RL baseline Base-RFT (83.92%), and Point-SFT alone (87.21%). Across five out-of-domain benchmarks (CharXiv, PlotQA, IconQA, TabMWP, Counting), Point-RFT attains a 53.16% average, surpassing the base model's 35.94% and Base-RFT's 33.80% by 17.22 and 19.36 percentage points respectively. All improvements are statistically significant (p < 0.01 under t-test).

## Baselines & Comparisons

Against the Qwen2.5-VL-7B backbone without CoT prompting (87.30% ChartQA, 39.06% OOD avg), Point-RFT wins on both in-domain (+2.74 pp) and out-of-domain (+14.10 pp) accuracy. When the backbone is evaluated with CoT prompting (70.88% ChartQA, 35.94% OOD avg), Point-RFT's advantage widens dramatically to +19.16 pp on ChartQA and +17.22 pp on OOD average, indicating that the grounded point-based reasoning format is far more effective than the model's default text-only CoT. Against Molmo-7B (20.48% ChartQA, 9.75% OOD avg), Point-RFT achieves a 69.56 pp margin on ChartQA and a 43.41 pp margin on OOD average, confirming that the grounding model's localization capability, when integrated into a full reasoning pipeline with RL, substantially outperforms using Molmo-7B as a standalone VLM. The text-only RL variant (Base-RFT) actually degrades OOD performance relative to the base model (33.80% vs. 35.94%), whereas Point-RFT consistently improves across all six benchmarks, underscoring that visual grounding is the key differentiator for generalization.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the text-only CoT + RL paradigm (exemplified by DeepSeek-R1 and GRPO) to the multimodal setting by introducing visually grounded point annotations into the reasoning chain. It departs from prior multimodal CoT work (Rose et al., 2023; Hu et al., 2024; Fu et al., 2025), which the authors note is 'predominantly prompt-driven, rather than end-to-end RFT,' by performing full reinforcement finetuning on grounded rationales. The data pipeline repurposes GPT-4o for reasoning trace generation and Molmo-7B for spatial grounding, combining two models with complementary strengths to produce the 71K Point-CoT dataset, while the training backbone is Qwen2.5-VL fine-tuned via a two-stage SFT-then-GRPO pipeline built on Easy-R1.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The paper demonstrates that interleaving point-level visual references into CoT reasoning yields substantially better multimodal reasoning than text-only CoT under identical RL conditions, and that this advantage transfers to out-of-domain benchmarks (19.36% absolute OOD gain). The released 71K Point-CoT dataset and the disentanglement of perception errors from reasoning errors offer practical diagnostic value for building more reliable document-understanding systems. However, the approach is validated primarily on chart/document tasks with a single 7B backbone, and the two-stage pipeline plus sequential grounded reasoning introduces significant inference latency, leaving open whether the gains persist for general VQA, larger models, or latency-sensitive deployments.
