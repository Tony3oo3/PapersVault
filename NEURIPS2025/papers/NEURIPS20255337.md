## Problem

Scene-level novel view synthesis is bottlenecked by the scarcity of clean, multi-view training data with consistent appearances. Existing multi-view diffusion models like CAT3D are fine-tuned on limited curated datasets (synthetic renderings, crowd-sourced object videos), while abundant permissively-licensed in-the-wild photo collections suffer from appearance inconsistencies (varying lighting, weather, aspect ratios, transient occlusions) that break standard multi-view diffusion training. The paper addresses how to unlock training on this scalable, diverse in-the-wild data source.

## Input / Output

The model takes a single 512×512 source scene image (padded to square for non-square aspect ratios), target camera poses encoded as 6-channel Plücker raymaps, and optionally a text prompt or external image for appearance control. It processes v=8 view slots in parallel (1 observed, 7 unobserved; expanded to v=16 for video), producing multiple spatially consistent novel views at 512×512 resolution. Depth maps from DepthAnything (aligned to COLMAP SfM point clouds) are used internally for warp conditioning but are not a user-facing input.

## Method

WildCAT3D extends CAT3D's multi-view diffusion framework with two key mechanisms to handle in-the-wild appearance variation. First, a lightweight fully-convolutional appearance encoder (alternating 3×3 convs and 2×2 max-pooling, outputting an 8-dim vector per view) compresses each VAE latent into a global appearance embedding that is broadcast across spatial positions and concatenated as extra input channels, creating an information bottleneck that lets the model 'peek' at coarse appearance without leaking fine-grained content. Second, a warp conditioning branch unprojects the source view via estimated depth and re-renders it to each target pose, injecting the warped VAE latents as additional channels to resolve single-view scale ambiguity. A custom appearance-aware classifier-free guidance scheme keeps appearance embeddings active in the unconditional branch to prevent oversaturation artifacts. At inference, the source view's appearance embedding is copied to all target slots, enforcing cross-view appearance consistency. The model is trained in two stages: CAT3D pretraining on CO3D + Re10K, then WildCAT3D fine-tuning on MegaScenes + CO3D, with the full input shape v×(2k+d+7)×n×n projected via a 1×1 conv into the LDM denoiser.

## Results

WildCAT3D achieves state-of-the-art single-view NVS across all four benchmarks. On DTU it reaches PSNR 10.77 / FID 57.32, on Mip-NeRF 360 PSNR 14.77 / FID 42.17, on Re10K PSNR 21.58 / FID 24.70, and on MegaScenes PSNR 13.92 / FID 9.87. Notably, it attains these results while training on strictly fewer curated data sources (CO3D, Re10K, MegaScenes) and without the aggressive filtering applied by prior methods to in-the-wild data.

## Baselines & Comparisons

WildCAT3D outperforms all baselines on DTU, Mip-NeRF 360, and Re10K across every reported metric (PSNR, SSIM, LPIPS, FID, KID). On DTU, it improves PSNR by ~2.0 dB over the strongest prior method (MS NVS, 8.80) and reduces FID from 85.96 to 57.32. On Mip-NeRF 360, the scene-level benchmark, it gains 0.71 dB PSNR and cuts FID by ~35% relative to MS NVS. On Re10K the margin is largest: PSNR 21.58 vs. 17.22 for MS NVS and FID 24.70 vs. 60.01. On the in-distribution MegaScenes benchmark, WildCAT3D leads on PSNR (13.92 vs. 13.40), FID (9.87 vs. 11.58), and KID (0.015 vs. 0.040) but trails MS NVS slightly on SSIM (0.439 vs. 0.445) and LPIPS (0.355 vs. 0.344), which the authors attribute to MS NVS being partially overfit to the benchmark's filtered data format. Importantly, WildCAT3D achieves these gains while using fewer training data sources (no Objaverse, no ACID) and no aggressive filtering of in-the-wild MegaScenes data.

## Relation to Prior Work

WildCAT3D extends the CAT3D multi-view diffusion framework (itself adapted from latent diffusion models) by adding a lightweight appearance encoder and warp conditioning to enable training on unfiltered in-the-wild scene data. It departs from prior NVS methods (Zero-1-to-3, ZeroNVS, MegaScenes NVS) that rely on curated multi-view datasets or aggressive filtering to handle appearance consistency, instead explicitly modeling global appearance as a low-dimensional bottleneck. The warp conditioning mechanism is adapted from the MegaScenes pipeline (Tung et al., 2024), and the appearance-aware classifier-free guidance is a novel modification to standard CFG to avoid oversaturation artifacts when conditioning on appearance signals.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

By unlocking training on abundant, permissively-licensed in-the-wild photo collections (430K+ scenes, 2M+ images from MegaScenes), WildCAT3D demonstrates a path toward web-scale NVS training without the curation bottleneck that limits prior methods. The appearance-controlled generation capability (via external image or text-prompted retrieval) opens new applications for scene editing and interpolation. Key limitations include reliance on external depth estimation (error propagation), lack of explicit 3D consistency guarantees, and mild flickering/saturation artifacts; the authors note that video priors, explicit transient occlusion modeling, and fine-grained textual conditioning remain open directions for improvement.
