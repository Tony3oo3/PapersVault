## Problem

Visual Prompt Tuning (VPT) applied to self-supervised backbones suffers from two underexplored failure modes: prompts exhibit near-uniform attention across all input tokens (lacking specialization), and prompt outputs collapse into the pretrained self-attention subspace (failing to introduce novel task-relevant directions). These limitations become especially severe under distribution shift and in few-shot regimes, where selective attention and representational enrichment are most critical for effective adaptation.

## Input / Output

The method operates on standard RGB images processed by a frozen ViT-B/16 backbone (224×224 input, 16×16 patches yielding 196 patch tokens plus 1 CLS token). VIPAMIN itself is a one-time initialization step that consumes a single mini-batch of 256 downstream training images passed through the frozen model to compute prompt weights; no additional learnable parameters are introduced. The final output is a class label produced by the standard VPT classification head after prompt tuning training.

## Method

VIPAMIN initializes visual prompts via two complementary modules requiring only a single forward pass and two lightweight matrix operations. The Matching module projects each randomly sampled prompt and mean-pooled input embeddings into the key subspace W_K of the first transformer block, selects the top-k most cosine-similar tokens, and averages their embeddings to align each prompt with a semantically coherent local region. The Orthogonalizing module computes the SVD of the frozen self-attention output SA(E_0), projects a random prompt through W_V, removes its component in the row subspace of SA(E_0) via the projector (I − VV^T), and reverse-maps through the pseudoinverse of W_V to inject novel representational directions outside the pretrained subspace. The final prompt is a convex combination (1−λ)·p_avg + λ·p_orth, where λ controls the strength of orthogonalization. This initialization is fully unsupervised, adds zero trainable parameters, and integrates as a drop-in replacement for random (Xavier uniform) prompt initialization in any existing VPT pipeline.

## Results

On VTAB-1k with MoCo-v3 pretrained ViT-B/16, VIPAMIN achieves 69.86% mean accuracy, surpassing full fine-tuning (66.23%) by 3.63 points and VPT (57.94%) by 11.92 points. With MAE pretrained ViT-B/16, VIPAMIN reaches 64.09% mean, outperforming full fine-tuning (61.28%) and VPT (40.96%) by 2.81 and 23.13 points respectively. In few-shot FGVC settings (k=1 to k=8), VIPAMIN consistently leads, outperforming VPT by 7.7% at k=1 and 24.7% at k=8 in mean accuracy across five datasets.

## Baselines & Comparisons

VIPAMIN decisively outperforms VPT [27] across all settings: +11.92% mean on MoCo-v3 VTAB-1k and +23.13% on MAE, with the largest gap on Structured tasks (+19.13% MoCo-v3, +29.97% MAE). Against GateVPT [70], VIPAMIN gains +4.06% (MoCo-v3) and +14.87% (MAE) in mean accuracy. The closest competitor, SPT [66], is surpassed by +1.53% (MoCo-v3) and +1.51% (MAE) on VTAB-1k mean, and by 1–2% consistently across all k-shot FGVC settings. In the VPT-Deep comparison (Table 3), VIPAMIN-Deep (71.23%) beats SPT-Deep (70.53%), iVPT [76] (70.21%), DA-VPT [53] (69.13%), and E2VPT [20] (69.67%), while VFPT [72] edges it out by a marginal 0.10% (71.33% vs 71.23%). Against simpler PEFT baselines—Linear Probing [24] (54.69%), Partial-1 [71] (64.61%), Bias Tuning [52] (66.43%), and Adapter [4] (64.82%)—VIPAMIN-Deep leads by 4.8–16.5 points. Notably, VIPAMIN is the first prompt-based method to surpass full fine-tuning on all three VTAB-1k categories under MAE pretraining.

## Relation to Prior Work

VIPAMIN extends Visual Prompt Tuning (VPT) by addressing two empirically identified failure modes—uniform prompt attention and representational subspace collapse—that are particularly pronounced with self-supervised backbones. It departs from prior prompt initialization and enhancement methods (SPT's clustering-based prototypes, GatedPT's learnable gates, iVPT's reinforcement modules, VFPT's Fourier-domain modulation) by requiring no additional parameters, no architectural modifications, and only a single forward pass with two lightweight matrix operations (top-k cosine matching and SVD-based orthogonal projection). Conceptually, it reframes prompt initialization as a geometric problem: aligning prompts with semantically coherent token clusters in the key space while injecting directions orthogonal to the frozen self-attention output subspace.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The paper provides a clean diagnostic framework (attention entropy and subspace projection energy) for understanding why self-supervised VPT underperforms, and offers a parameter-free fix that is trivially deployable in existing pipelines. The finding that a single forward pass initialization can match or exceed methods adding learnable components (GatedPT, iVPT, VFPT) suggests that initialization quality, not architectural complexity, is the binding constraint for prompt-based adaptation. Open questions include whether the principles generalize to text or multimodal settings, whether hyperparameters k and λ admit principled (rather than empirical) selection, and what fundamental representational differences between supervised and self-supervised pretraining drive the observed prompt behavior.
