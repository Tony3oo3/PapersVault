## Problem

Existing event-guided low-light video enhancement methods are tuned for steady low-illumination scenes and assume both RGB and event sensors are always available, causing them to over-enhance under normal lighting or fail entirely when one modality is missing. No unified framework can handle dynamic illumination variation and arbitrary modality availability simultaneously.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/NEURIPS20255007/cropped_page1_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

The method accepts low-light RGB video frames and event sequences as optional conditioning inputs, operating at 480×720 resolution with 49 frames at 8 FPS. RGB frames are encoded to latent space via a pre-trained VAE and concatenated along the frame channel, while events are first converted to 3-channel voxel grids and then encoded through the same VAE. The output is a temporally consistent normal-light video, generated auto-regressively in batches where the last predicted frame of each batch seeds the next.

## Method

EVDiffuser fine-tunes the CogVideoX I2V DiT in a modality-agnostic manner: RGB conditioning is injected via VAE latent concatenation with LoRA (rank 128) on the image branch only, while event conditioning uses a ControlNet-style trainable copy of the first 18 DiT blocks with zero-initialized linear layers that add features back into the denoising path. Training integrates three datasets (SDE event+RGB, V2E2V event-only, SDSD RGB-only) with random illumination degradation applied to normal-light frames, so the model never sees a fixed lighting condition. At inference, a modality-adaptive guidance rescaling (MGR) extends classifier-free guidance with per-modality scales ω_f = α·I_ill and ω_e = α·(1−I_ill), where I_ill is a per-frame illumination prior, dynamically shifting emphasis between RGB and event conditions based on scene brightness.

## Results

On the V-SDE benchmark (varying illumination), EVDiffuser achieves 21.55 dB PSNR and 27.14 dB PSNR* (V-SDE-in), outperforming the best baseline EvLowlight (11.29 dB PSNR, 20.51 dB PSNR*) by 6.63 dB on PSNR*. On the RGB-only V-SDSD benchmark, EVDiffuser reaches 29.10 dB PSNR* and 0.9163 SSIM, surpassing the best baseline LLFlow-L-SKF (20.46 dB PSNR*) by over 8.64 dB. The method also achieves the best temporal consistency scores on VBench metrics (Subject Consistency 0.7007, Motion Smoothness 0.9942) and ranks first in all four user-study preference dimensions.

## Baselines & Comparisons

EVDiffuser decisively outperforms all 12 baselines on the V-SDE varying-illumination benchmark, achieving 6.63 dB higher PSNR* than the strongest multi-modality baseline EvLowlight (27.14 vs. 20.51) and 7.01 dB higher than the best event-only method ETNet (27.14 vs. 16.13). On the RGB-only V-SDSD benchmark, EVDiffuser leads the best image-only baseline LLFlow-L-SKF by 8.64 dB in PSNR* (29.10 vs. 20.46) and 0.27 in SSIM (0.9163 vs. 0.6485). On the original SDE dataset with stable low light (where baselines are specifically designed), EVDiffuser is competitive but trails EvLight++ by ~3.5 dB in PSNR* (22.34 vs. 25.83), confirming that the diffusion-based approach trades a small amount of peak performance in the narrow stable-light regime for massive gains in generalization. Notably, multi-modality baselines (EvLowlight, EvLight++) outperform single-modality image-only methods on PSNR*, but all non-diffusion baselines suffer severe overexposure or structural degradation under illumination variation, a failure mode EVDiffuser avoids through its modality-adaptive guidance rescaling.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper builds on the CogVideoX video diffusion model (DiT-based) as a foundation, repurposing it from text-to-video generation to conditional low-light video enhancement. It departs from prior event-guided LLVE methods (EvLight, EvLowLight, EvLight++) that assume stable low illumination and constant availability of both RGB and event sensors, by treating both modalities as optional conditions in a modality-agnostic diffusion pipeline. The event conditioning mechanism borrows from ControlNet-style feature injection, while the RGB conditioning uses LoRA-adapted VAE latents concatenated along the frame channel. The modality-adaptive guidance rescaling extends Classifier-Free Guidance by making the guidance scales illumination-dependent, a mechanism not present in prior diffusion-based restoration work.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The paper addresses a genuine practical gap: real-world systems experience varying illumination and sensor failures, yet all prior LLVE methods are tuned for stable low-light with both sensors present. The introduced V-SDE/V-SDSD benchmarks for varying illumination fill a notable evaluation gap. However, the 5.63 s/frame inference time and 7.9B parameter count make real-time deployment impractical, and the large PSNR gap over baselines is partly attributable to the new benchmark being inherently unfavorable to methods trained on stable low-light. Open questions include whether the modality-agnostic diffusion paradigm can be made efficient enough for practical use (few-step sampling, distillation) and whether it generalizes beyond LLVE to other event-camera tasks like interpolation or denoising.
