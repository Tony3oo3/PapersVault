## Problem

Existing PointMamba-based methods rely on conventional scanning schemes (random, Hilbert, Z-order) that fragment spatial continuity in point cloud token sequences, and their random masking strategy in self-supervised pre-training fails to exploit semantic similarity between tokens, impairing local semantic modeling and global feature coherence.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/NEURIPS20254648/cropped_page1_idx1.webp)

## Input / Output

The method consumes raw 3D point clouds of 1024 points, which are patchified into 64 patches of 32 points each via KNN grouping. During pre-training, the masked autoencoder reconstructs masked point coordinates using Chamfer distance L2 loss; for downstream tasks, the encoder produces per-point feature representations that are fine-tuned to yield class labels (classification), per-point part labels (segmentation), or few-shot class predictions.

## Method

ZigzagPointMamba introduces two coupled innovations on top of the PointMamba SSM backbone. First, a 3D zigzag scan path extends 2D zigzag traversal to three orthogonal planes (XY, XZ, YZ) by sorting points along one axis, layering them, then applying alternating ascending/descending sorts within segments on the remaining two axes, producing spatially coherent token sequences that preserve local geometric adjacency. Second, the Semantic-Siamese Masking Strategy (SMS) normalizes token feature vectors, computes pairwise cosine similarity, sums each row to obtain a redundancy score, and masks tokens whose score exceeds a batch-adaptive threshold (default 0.8), followed by a secondary random masking pass (ratio 0.6) on the remaining tokens. The encoder comprises 12 vanilla Mamba blocks (384-dim) and the decoder 4 Mamba blocks, trained for 300 epochs with AdamW and cosine annealing on ShapeNetPart, then transferred to classification, segmentation, and few-shot benchmarks.

## Results

On ScanObjectNN, ZigzagPointMamba achieves 94.15% (OBJ-BG), 92.10% (OBJ-ONLY), and 88.65% (PB-T50-RS) classification accuracy, outperforming the PointMamba baseline by 0.19%, 1.22%, and 0.72% respectively. On ModelNet40, it reaches 93.15% overall accuracy (+0.4% over PointMamba). For part segmentation on ShapeNetPart, it attains 84.16% Cls.mIoU and 85.78% Inst.mIoU, improving over PointMamba by 1.59% and 0.5% respectively. The model uses 12.3M parameters and 3.1G FLOPs, matching PointMamba's efficiency.

## Baselines & Comparisons

ZigzagPointMamba consistently outperforms its direct baseline PointMamba across all evaluated benchmarks: +0.19% on OBJ-BG, +1.22% on OBJ-ONLY, +0.72% on PB-T50-RS (ScanObjectNN), +0.4% on ModelNet40, and +1.59% Cls.mIoU on ShapeNetPart. Against Transformer-based methods, it surpasses ACT on OBJ-ONLY (92.10 vs 91.91) and PB-T50-RS (88.65 vs 88.21), and beats GeoMask3D on all three ScanObjectNN subsets. However, it trails ReCon on OBJ-ONLY (92.10 vs 93.12) and PB-T50-RS (88.65 vs 89.73), and loses to GeoMask3D (94.20%) and ACT (93.6%) on ModelNet40. On ShapeNetPart segmentation, it remains below PointM2AE (84.86 Cls.mIoU), ACT (84.66), and GeoMask3D (84.49), though it substantially improves over PointMamba (82.57). In few-shot settings (ModelNetFewShot), it gains +1.5% over PointMamba in the 10-way 10-shot scenario but remains below ReCon (93.3%) and ACT (93.3%) on 10-way 10-shot.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends PointMamba (Liang et al., NeurIPS 2024), which first adapted the Mamba selective state-space model to unordered point clouds, by replacing its ad-hoc token ordering (random, Hilbert, or Z-order) with a structured 3D zigzag scan path and substituting random masking with a Semantic-Siamese Masking Strategy (SMS). The zigzag path generalizes the classical 2D zigzag raster scan to three orthogonal planes (XY, XZ, YZ) with alternating segment sorting, while SMS draws conceptual inspiration from semantic-guided masking approaches like SemMAE and GeoMask3D but operates on cosine-similarity redundancy scores over KNN-grouped tokens. The overall architecture remains a masked autoencoder with Mamba encoder/decoder blocks, placing the work squarely within the self-supervised point cloud pre-training lineage (Point-BERT → PointMAE → Point-M2AE → PointMamba).

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The paper addresses a practical bottleneck in SSM-based point cloud models—how to impose a token order that respects 3D spatial structure without the quadratic cost of attention—and offers a lightweight, parameter-free scanning scheme. However, the gains over PointMamba are modest (0.4% on ModelNet40, 1.59% Cls.mIoU on ShapeNetPart, 0.19–1.22% on ScanObjectNN subsets), and the ablation reveals that the zigzag path alone does not outperform simpler Z-order or Hilbert curves, suggesting the benefit is entangled with the SMS masking rather than the scan order per se. Open questions include whether the fixed SMS threshold (0.8) generalizes across point cloud densities and categories, whether bidirectional or temporal SSM variants could capture multi-directional context, and whether the approach scales to large-scale outdoor scenes beyond the 1024-point benchmark setting.
