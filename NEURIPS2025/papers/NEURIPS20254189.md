## Problem

Existing diffusion-based anime face colorization methods rely on predefined noise schedules (e.g., discrete-cosine in AnimeDiffusion, log-normal in EDM) that produce non-uniform perceptual degradation across timesteps, concentrating difficulty in a narrow mid-noise band and leaving early/late steps underutilized. This uneven pacing complicates reverse-trajectory learning and hinders faithful recovery of fine-grained color textures, particularly under cross-reference conditions where structural and stylistic fidelity must be jointly preserved.

## Input / Output

The method takes a 1-channel anime face sketch (extracted via XDoG edge detection) and a 3-channel reference color image (constructed by applying Thin Plate Spline deformation plus random rotation to the ground truth), channel-concatenated into a 4×H×W conditioning tensor at 256×256 resolution. The conditioning is held fixed throughout the entire reverse diffusion trajectory. The output is a 3-channel RGB colorized anime face image at the same 256×256 resolution, evaluated under both same-reference (reference derived from the same sketch) and cross-reference (reference drawn from a different test image) scenarios.

## Method

SSIMBaD introduces a perceptually grounded sigma-space transformation φ*(σ) = σ/(σ+0.3), selected empirically by maximizing the R² linearity between the transformed noise level and SSIM degradation under additive Gaussian noise. This transformation is applied consistently in both training (uniform sampling of φ*(σ) over [φ*(σ_min), φ*(σ_max)]) and inference (linear interpolation in φ*-space to construct the σ schedule), replacing EDM's default log-normal training sampling and ρ-parameterized inference schedule. The denoiser is a U-Net with EDM-style preconditioned residual architecture, where the conventional log(σ) noise embedding is replaced by c_noise = φ*(σ) to align temporal conditioning with the perceptual trajectory. A lightweight reverse-only trajectory refinement stage then fine-tunes the full N-step denoising path end-to-end with an L2 loss between the final reconstruction and ground truth, exploiting the smoother reverse trajectory enabled by the SSIM-aligned schedule to improve realism without overfitting the forward corruption.

## Results

On the Danbooru AnimeFace benchmark (256×256, 579 test samples), SSIMBaD with trajectory refinement achieves PSNR of 18.92/15.84 dB, MS-SSIM of 0.8512/0.8207, and FID of 34.98/37.10 under same-reference and cross-reference settings, respectively. It attains the best cross-reference scores on PSNR, MS-SSIM, and FID among all compared methods, while remaining competitive on perceptual metrics (LPIPS 0.1174/0.4804, DINOv2-I 0.9644/0.8826). The ablation shows that the EDM backbone alone raises PSNR from 11.39 to 13.30 (same-reference), SSIM-aligned scaling further lifts it to 15.15, and trajectory refinement delivers the final jump to 18.92.

## Baselines & Comparisons

Against the GAN-based SCFT [18], SSIMBaD wins on cross-reference PSNR (15.84 vs. 15.47), MS-SSIM (0.8207 vs. 0.7627), and FID (37.10 vs. 45.18), while SCFT retains a slight edge on same-reference MS-SSIM (0.7833 vs. 0.8512 — actually SSIMBaD wins here too). Against the AnimeDiffusion family [3], SSIMBaD substantially outperforms both the pretrained (PSNR 11.39) and finetuned (PSNR 13.32, FID 135.12) variants on all fidelity metrics, with the finetuned version suffering a severe realism collapse (FID >135). ControlNet [36] achieves competitive same-reference style scores (DINOv2-I 0.9640) but degrades sharply under cross-reference (MS-SSIM drops to 0.2007, FID rises to 50.25), whereas SSIMBaD maintains MS-SSIM 0.8207 and FID 37.10. Among the train-free attention methods, Attention Distillation [39] dominates same-reference columns (PSNR 19.58, FID 32.93, LPIPS 0.1139) but collapses under cross-reference (MS-SSIM 0.1252, FID 94.17), while Cross-Image Attention [1] achieves the best cross-reference LPIPS (0.4569) but trails SSIMBaD on all fidelity metrics in that setting. Overall, SSIMBaD is the only method that remains strong on both fidelity and style under reference mismatch, whereas each baseline excels in one regime and fails in the other.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper builds on the EDM continuous-time diffusion framework (Karras et al., 2022) and the AnimeDiffusion U-Net colorization pipeline (Cao et al., 2024), but departs from both in a key way: it replaces EDM's standard log-normal σ sampling with a perceptually grounded squash transformation φ*(σ) = σ/(σ+0.3) that linearizes SSIM degradation across timesteps. Conceptually, it positions itself against the broader landscape of reference-guided colorization—GAN-based SCFT, train-free attention transfer (Cross-Image Attention, Attention Distillation), and ControlNet—by arguing that perceptual uniformity in the noise schedule, rather than architectural correspondence mechanisms, is the principal driver of structural fidelity under reference mismatch. The trajectory refinement stage is a lightweight reverse-only fine-tuning that exploits the smoother schedule to close the remaining gap without overfitting.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The SSIM-aligned sigma-space scaling is presented as a general principle applicable to any conditional diffusion task requiring structural preservation, not just anime colorization, which gives it potential broader utility. However, validation is confined to a single dataset (Danbooru AnimeFace) at 256×256 resolution, and the paper acknowledges limitations in fine-grained detail recovery under extreme sketch sparsity or large reference-content gaps. Open questions include whether other perceptual metrics (LPIPS, DINOv2 features) could serve as alternative alignment targets, whether the approach transfers to higher resolutions or non-anime domains, and whether the specific constant c=0.3 in the squash function is dataset-dependent. The lightweight, supervised design targeting near real-time inference is practically relevant for production pipelines in webtoon and animation industries.
