## Problem

Energy-guided diffusion models for offline RL require an intermediate energy at each denoising step to steer generation toward high-reward actions, but the exact intermediate energy involves an intractable log-expectation over the posterior distribution of clean actions. Previous methods either use inexact approximations (MSE or DPS) or, as in QGPO, derive the exact log-expectation form without solving it, leaving a gap between the theoretically correct guidance and what is actually computable.

## Input / Output

The method consumes state-action pairs from offline D4RL datasets (Gym-MuJoCo, Pointmaze, Locomotion, Adroit) where states and actions are continuous vectors of varying dimensionality per environment. During inference, given a state vector, the diffusion model iteratively denoises a sampled action vector over T steps, with the intermediate energy network providing a scalar guidance signal at each step. The Q-function and intermediate energy network both take (state, action, timestep) as input and produce scalar outputs, while the noise prediction network outputs a vector of the same dimensionality as the action.

## Method

AEPO derives a closed-form approximation to the intractable log-expectation intermediate energy by (1) applying a first-order Taylor expansion of the Q-function around a reference action to convert implicit action dependence into an explicit linear form, and (2) evaluating the resulting Gaussian expectation via the moment-generating function, yielding a target that depends only on the posterior mean, covariance, and Q-gradient. Two posterior approximation strategies (Posterior 1 using the diffusion model's noise prediction, Posterior 2 using dataset variance) estimate the Gaussian parameters. A separate intermediate energy neural network is trained with MSE loss to match the simplified target, and at inference the score function is the sum of the diffusion model's score and the gradient of the learned intermediate energy. A guidance rescaling step normalizes the combined score magnitude to the unguided score magnitude, making performance robust to the guidance strength hyperparameter ω.

## Results

On the D4RL Gym-MuJoCo benchmark (9 tasks across HalfCheetah, Hopper, and Walker2d with medium, medium-replay, and medium-expert datasets), AEPO achieves a mean normalized score of 87.4 and total score of 786.7, the best among all 28 compared methods. On D4RL Pointmaze (maze2d), AEPO attains a mean sparse score of 132.3 and mean dense score of 127.5, and on Locomotion (antmaze) a mean score of 80.6 (total 483.4). On D4RL Adroit (12 subtasks), AEPO achieves a mean score of 55.6 and total score of 667.5, surpassing the next-best method LD (54.6 / 655.5).

## Baselines & Comparisons

AEPO achieves the best mean score (87.4) and total score (786.7) on the D4RL Gym-MuJoCo benchmark, outperforming the closest diffusion-based competitors QGPO (86.6 / 779.2) and D-QL (86.3 / 777.1) by 0.8 and 1.1 in mean score respectively, and also surpassing non-diffusion methods such as PBRL (84.4) and IQL (77.0). On D4RL Pointmaze, AEPO dominates the dense-reward maze2d tasks (mean dense 127.5 vs. DD's 61.5 and TCD's 48.9) and leads on antmaze (mean 80.6 vs. QGPO's 78.3), though TCD slightly edges out AEPO on maze2d sparse (135.8 vs. 132.3). On D4RL Adroit, AEPO achieves the highest total score (667.5) across 12 subtasks, beating LD (655.5), TAP (622.6), and QGPO (593.9). Among transformer-based baselines, AEPO outperforms TT (78.9), BooT (81.9), and DT (74.3) on Gym-MuJoCo by 8.5, 5.5, and 13.1 in mean score. Among earlier diffusion-based methods, AEPO surpasses Diffuser (77.5), DD (81.8), IDQL (82.1), HDMI (82.6), and DiffuserLite (84.4), confirming that the analytic solution to the intermediate energy provides a consistent advantage over both classifier-guided and classifier-free-guided approaches.

## Relation to Prior Work

This paper extends the energy-guided diffusion sampling framework for offline RL established by QGPO (Lu et al., ICML 2023), which derived the exact intermediate energy formula but left the intractable log-expectation unsolved. AEPO provides a closed-form approximation to that log-expectation by combining first-order Taylor expansion of the Q-function with the moment-generating function of the Gaussian posterior, building on the DDPM framework (Ho et al., 2020) and the conditional generative modeling paradigm for decision-making (Ajay et al., 2022). Unlike classifier-guided and classifier-free-guided methods that use inexact intermediate energy (MSE or DPS approximations), AEPO trains a dedicated intermediate energy network to match the analytically simplified target, and unlike QGPO's contrastive energy prediction, it does not require generating out-of-distribution fake actions.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The theoretical result clarifies that all prior guided-sampling diffusion RL methods operate with inexact intermediate guidance, providing a principled correction that yields consistent (if modest) improvements across 30+ D4RL tasks. The approach is general to any Gaussian diffusion model and avoids the need for manual guidance-strength tuning via its rescaling mechanism. Open questions include whether higher-order Taylor expansions or non-isotropic posterior approximations would yield further gains, and whether the method extends naturally to non-Gaussian diffusion schedules or very high-dimensional continuous action spaces beyond the D4RL benchmarks tested.
