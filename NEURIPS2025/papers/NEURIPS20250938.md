## Problem

Existing video relighting methods (e.g., RelightVid, Light-A-Video) rely on implicit temporal correlations and omit explicit geometric guidance, causing loss of lighting fidelity and coherence whenever scene geometry changes. More broadly, diffusion-based video generation models treat illumination as an uncontrollable implicit factor rather than a first-class conditioning signal, making it difficult to achieve physically plausible light-scene interactions across frames.
![Teaser figure](../imgs/NEURIPS20250938/cropped_page0_idx0.webp)

## Input / Output

At inference, IllumiCraft accepts a text prompt describing the target illumination, an input video (720×480, 49 frames), and an optional static background reference image, producing a temporally coherent relit video. Notably, the HDR environment maps and 3D point tracks used during training are not required at test time; the model has internalized lighting and geometric priors through random dropping during training (50% HDR dropout, 30% 3D tracking dropout), so users steer illumination solely via the text prompt.

## Method

IllumiCraft builds on the Wan2.1 1.3B DiT backbone and fuses three conditioning streams within a single diffusion architecture. Illumination is injected via a compact MLP-Transformer lighting encoder that processes HDR environment maps into 3 learnable illumination tokens, which are concatenated with the CLIP text embedding to form the final prompt; appearance is provided by VAE-encoded relit foreground and background-reference latents concatenated as a 32-channel control latent; and geometry is injected through a ControlNet-style 'condition DiT' that clones the first 4 transformer blocks, encodes 3D tracking video via the VAE, and adds zero-initialized linear projections hierarchically into the main DiT stream. Training minimizes standard diffusion denoising loss with random dropping of each signal (50% HDR, 30% 3D tracking, 10% reference image) so the model generalizes to any subset of inputs at inference. The IllumiPipe pipeline curates 20,170 paired video sequences from Pexels, extracting temporally consistent HDR maps via chrome-ball warping, relit clips via Light-A-Video with 100 diverse lighting prompts, and dense 3D point tracks via SpatialTracker.

## Results

On the text-conditioned video relighting task (49 frames, 720×480), IllumiCraft achieves an FVD of 2186.40, a 43% reduction over the strongest baseline Light-A-Video (3946.71), while also leading on LPIPS (0.5623), PSNR (12.03), text alignment (0.3342), and temporal consistency (0.9948). In the background-conditioned setting, IllumiCraft further reduces FVD to 1072.38 (49 frames) and 1011.08 (16 frames), outperforming RelightVid by 28% and Light-A-Video by 37% on FVD respectively. Inference takes 105.21 seconds per 49-frame sample, roughly 6× faster than Light-A-Video (645.53 s) and 10× faster than AnyV2V+IC-Light (1033.21 s).

## Baselines & Comparisons

IllumiCraft outperforms all four baselines on every reported metric in both evaluation settings. Against IC-Light (frame-wise relighting), IllumiCraft reduces FVD by 55% (2186 vs. 4915) and improves PSNR from 8.55 to 12.03, while IC-Light produces overly smoothed textures and poor temporal coherence (TC 0.951 vs. 0.995). Against AnyV2V+IC-Light, IllumiCraft cuts FVD by 43% and boosts text alignment from 0.278 to 0.334, as AnyV2V introduces color distortions and temporal jitters when propagating single-frame edits. Against Light-A-Video (the strongest text-conditioned baseline), IllumiCraft achieves a 45% FVD reduction (2186 vs. 3947) and higher temporal consistency (0.9948 vs. 0.9910), with Light-A-Video tending to blur fine details and dampen contrast. Against RelightVid (background-conditioned, 16-frame setting), IllumiCraft lowers FVD from 1492 to 1011 (28% reduction) and improves PSNR from 17.19 to 19.78, while RelightVid introduces banding artifacts and unnatural illumination on complex scenes.

## Relation to Prior Work

IllumiCraft extends the video relighting line initiated by IC-Light (single-image) and carried into the video domain by Light-A-Video (cross-frame light attention) and RelightVid (temporal attention layers), both of which modify the 2D U-Net backbone of IC-Light. The key departure is architectural: rather than patching a U-Net with temporal modules, IllumiCraft builds on the DiT-based Wan2.1 video diffusion backbone and introduces two new conditioning channels—HDR environment maps injected as learnable illumination tokens into the text prompt embedding, and 3D point-track videos injected via a ControlNet-style cloned-block pathway. This makes it the first video relighting method to explicitly fuse both illumination and geometry cues within a single transformer diffusion model, rather than relying on implicit temporal correlations for coherence.

## Why It Matters / Open Questions

The joint geometry-illumination formulation addresses a concrete failure mode of prior video relighting methods (loss of lighting fidelity when scene geometry changes), and the 20,170-pair IllumiPipe dataset with synchronized HDR maps and 3D tracks is a practical resource for the broader controllable video generation community. A notable design choice is that HDR and 3D tracking are training-only signals; at inference the model relies on learned priors, making it robust to tracking or HDR estimation failures. Open questions include scalability to longer sequences beyond 49 frames, handling of dynamic occlusions (acknowledged as a failure mode), and whether the approach generalizes to multi-object or multi-light-source scenes. The paper also flags the dual-use risk of more convincing relit videos for deepfakes.
